Mandate & investment process

Objective: beat the S&P 500 over rolling 12-month periods with a concentrated portfolio of 8-15 high-quality businesses bought at attractive valuations, held for their fundamentals (about a 12-month horizon). Paper trading only: $100,000 simulated, in US dollars, no broker.

  1. 1. ScreenEvery week the ~3,800 largest listed companies worldwide are ranked on quality and valuation; cyclicals on peak profits are marked down.
  2. 2. ResearchAn AI analyst writes a note from stockanalysis.com figures and primary sources (filings, company releases), citing every number.
  3. 3. DecideThe bear case is argued before the bull case, ending in BUY or AVOID with conviction 1-5. Only conviction 4+ is bought.
  4. 4. Size7% (conviction 4) or 10% (5), scaled 0.6-1.4x by volatility. Caps: 30% per sector; China/HK 25%; at most 15 holdings.
  5. 5. MonitorDaily re-pricing and thesis triggers, a dip radar for unusual falls, and a full re-check of 3 holdings a week. A cash reserve of 5-35% follows a macro stress score.

Core positions have no price stops: they are sold when the business case breaks, not when the price falls. Sell-side price targets are never used. Every decision is logged and scored against the S&P 500 at 1, 3, 6 and 12 months.
