# Zimmer Biomet Holdings, Inc. (ZBH) — Core initiation — 2026-10-11

## 1. Business
ZBH designs, makes and sells musculoskeletal implants and surgical technology: knee and hip reconstruction ("Recon"), sports medicine/extremities/trauma (S.E.T.), spine, craniomaxillofacial/thoracic (CMFT) and dental, plus the ROSA robotic surgery platform. Revenue is per-procedure device sales to hospitals and surgery centers worldwide, driven by aging-population volume growth, pricing/mix and robotics adoption. [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/zbh/)

## 2. Quality
TTM ROIC is 6.2% and ROCE 7.2%, both roughly flat over five years (+0.6pp/+0.7pp) — a mediocre, not excellent, return profile for a medtech [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/zbh/financials/ratios/). Gross margin has drifted down to 69.5% TTM from 71.3% in FY2021 (-1.6pp) and operating margin to 16.6% (-0.9pp), evidence of mix/cost pressure rather than pricing power [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/zbh/financials/income-statement/). Durable moat elements: surgeon switching costs (instrumentation sets, training), regulatory barriers to entry, and scale in a concentrated oligopoly (Stryker, J&J, Smith+Nephew) that limits new entrants. Q2 2026 organic revenue growth was 4.0% with full-year guidance raised, and management is overhauling the Americas sales-channel leadership to lift commercial execution — a [Likely] positive, not-yet-proven catalyst that is not yet visible in the margin trend (source: https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/zimmer-biomet-announces-americas-leadership-promotions-to-accelerate-commercial-transformation-302872765.html). Altman Z-Score is 2.51 (grey zone) and Piotroski F-Score 6/9 — adequate, not strong [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/zbh/statistics/).

## 3. Valuation
ZBH trades on 21.7x TTM P/E, 9.6x EV/EBITDA, 2.8x EV/Sales, 14.2x P/FCF and 1.3x P/B — below its own 5-year minimum on every multiple (P/E -2%, EV/EBITDA -7%, EV/Sales -9%, P/FCF -6%, P/B -7%) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/zbh/financials/ratios/). Versus peers (SYK, MDT, SNN, ENOV) it is cheaper on EV/EBITDA (-17%), P/FCF (-18%) and especially P/B (-39%), and roughly in line on P/E (-1%) and EV/Sales (-2%) [Certain]. FCF yield is 7.06%. The fact sheet's own-history fair value (inform-only) is $105.35 base, $73.21 bear, $179.17 bull versus the $89.14 close — the market is pricing about 18% below the base case, and the reverse-DCF shows the price implies only ~1.0% year-1 revenue growth fading to terminal, a low bar against the 4.0% organic growth just reported [Certain]/[Likely]. The discount looks more like a quality discount (flat ROIC/ROCE, margin drift) than a clean mispricing [Likely].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROIC (ratios page) falls below 5% TTM, confirming returns are structurally sub-cost-of-capital
- Gross margin (income statement page) falls below 68% TTM, confirming margin drift is structural, not mix/FX
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios/balance sheet page) rises above 3.5x, signalling leverage is crowding out capital return
- Organic revenue growth (earnings release/filings) turns negative or FY guidance is cut, undermining the sales-channel-transformation thesis

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Q2 2026: $252.4m of buybacks (2.9m shares) and $45.8m of dividends ($0.24/share; $0.48/share H1 2026) [Certain] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1136869/000119312526335044/zbh-20260630.htm). TTM FCF margin is 14.1% and FCF conversion 148.8% of net income — strong in absolute terms but down from 362% in FY2022, a declining trend [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/zbh/financials/cash-flow-statement/). Total debt is $7.48bn (current $1.20bn + long-term $6.28bn) against net debt/EBITDA of 2.83x — moderate leverage that still leaves room for both buybacks and the dividend [Certain] (source: 10-Q, above). Shares outstanding fell 1.95% YoY, consistent with net buybacks [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/zbh/statistics/).

## 6. Unusual items
Short interest is 4.51% of float and falling (-2.5% vs prior month) — not a crowded short [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/zbh/statistics/). Altman Z-Score of 2.51 sits in the distress "grey zone" (1.8–3.0), worth monitoring alongside the leverage trigger above [Likely]. Institutional ownership is shown as 106.1% of float on the statistics page — likely a double-counting artefact of overlapping 13F positions rather than a real figure; flagged, not used as a signal [Guessing].
