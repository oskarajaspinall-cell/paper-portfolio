It reports in CNY and the fact sheet shows no foreign revenue segment; China is the home market [Likely]. Segment and country mix not checked against filings [data unavailable].

## 2. Quality
- ROIC is 24.9% on a rolling-12-month basis, up from 21.2% a year earlier, and has risen from 14.0% in FY2021 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sha/603939/financials/ratios/?p=trailing) [Certain].
- Operating margin is 10.4% and net margin 7.1%, both edging up; gross margin is slipping, from 40.1% to 38.5% [Certain].
- FCF conversion is 196.9% of net income. FCF margin is 14.0% but sits 3.9pp below its 2-year high of 17.9% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sha/603939/financials/cash-flow-statement/?p=trailing). Part of the cash conversion probably comes from working capital and lease accounting, which should not be extrapolated [Guessing].
- Net debt/EBITDA is -1.89x, so the company holds net cash; Piotroski F is 6 and Altman Z 3.17 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sha/603939/statistics/) [Certain].
- Moat: local density and purchasing scale in a fragmented market. Regulatory pressure on drug pricing and online competition can erode that [Guessing]. Moat is plausible but narrow.

## 3. Valuation
- P/E is 17.8x against a 5-year median of 28.7x, at 7% of its range. EV/EBITDA of 6.2x is below the 5-year minimum of 8.9x. P/FCF is 8.9x and FCF yield 11.28% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sha/603939/statistics/) [Certain].
- Against peers: P/E is 10% below the peer median (n=2), EV/EBITDA is in line, and EV/Sales, P/FCF and P/B are at large premiums. Peers are cheaper on cash multiples, so the discount is mostly against its own history [Certain].
- The mechanical fair value is 43.46 CNY base (DCF 67%, EV/EBITDA 33%) against a 26.26 close, with a bear value of 24.92 CNY. The reverse DCF implies year-1 revenue shrinking by 7.4%, which looks too pessimistic for a business growing profit [Likely].
