# Macro overlay: SouthState Bank Corporation (SSB) — 2026-11-21
## Assessment
**Weight: secondary.** US-only regional bank (no foreign exposure, per the note), so macro enters through the discount rate and credit cost, not earnings translation. [Likely]

**Chain:** rising rates and wider credit spreads -> deposit costs, securities marks and credit provisions -> earnings and P/B multiple; risk-off -> higher equity risk premium for banks.

**Facts (FRED):** 10y yield 5.04% (2026-11-19, +0.35pp over ~3m); 2y 4.76% (+0.57pp); fed funds 3.88% (+0.25pp); 10y TIPS 2.88% (+0.53pp); high-yield spread 3.52% (+0.77pp, 2026-11-19); VIX 28.44 (+12.4 over ~3m). Source: https://fred.stlouisfed.org (snapshot reports/macro/2026-11-21.md). [Certain]

**Market pricing (proxy):** the 2y sits about 0.9pp above fed funds, consistent with a market pricing further hikes; 10y-2y has flattened to 0.28pp, a squeeze on the spread banks earn. Breakevens fell to 2.15%, so the rise is real-rate driven, not inflation. **(inference)**

**Bull:** spreads stabilise and the curve re-steepens; NIM holds, provisions stay in line with loan growth, P/B (1.1x, near its 5y low) re-rates toward peers' 1.3x.
**Bear:** spreads keep widening and hikes get priced in; provisions rise, securities marks and deposit costs squeeze NIM, ROE slips from 10.3% toward the 8% invalidation level, and the multiple compresses.

**Tilt:** toward bear, small. The shares already trade below their 5y P/E minimum, which discounts part of this; the unreliable fair value (the 10y at 5.04% also feeds the cost of equity) adds no support. [Guessing]
