# SouthState Bank Corporation (SSB) — Core initiation — 2026-11-21
## 1. Business
Regional bank holding company (Winter Haven, Florida) earning net interest income on loans funded by low-cost deposits, plus fee income (service charges, trust, correspondent banking). Eight-state Southeast and Texas/Colorado footprint, US only, so no foreign-country exposure [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/764038/000119312526439976/ssb-20260930.htm). Sector: Financials, Banks - Regional (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/ssb/).

## 2. Quality
- Returns are adequate, not exceptional: ROE 10.3% TTM versus 10.1% in FY2021, flat over five years; net margin 35.0% TTM, flat (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/ssb/financials/ratios/). [Certain]
- ROIC/ROCE are [data unavailable] for banks, so ROE is the quality gauge. [Certain]
- Moat is local deposit franchise and relationship banking in faster-growing Sun Belt markets; this is durable but shared with peers, so I treat it as narrow. [Likely]
- Q3 credit was described as stable on the earnings call (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/ssb/transcripts/715215-q3-2026/). [Likely]
- FCF margin is noisy for a bank (falling over 5 years, rising over the last 12 months, 15.3% to 56.6%); working-capital swings in a bank's cash flow make it unreliable here. [Likely]

## 3. Valuation
P/E is 10.6x, below its 5y minimum of 11.6x and 5% under the peer median of 11.1x (PNFP, WTFC, ONB, CFR, UMBF). P/B is 1.1x, near the bottom of its 5y range (1.0x-1.3x) and 17% under peers' 1.3x. FCF yield 15.62% is flattered by the cash-flow swing noted above. Mechanical fair value base is 131.53 vs close 99.18, but it is flagged unreliable (methods disagree 4.7x); the P/B+ROE method alone gives a base of 104.25, only modest upside. The price implies shrinking revenue in year 1 (reverse DCF), which looks too pessimistic for a bank growing loans. [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/ssb/statistics/)

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROE (ratios page) falls below 8% on a TTM basis.
- Net margin (income statement page) falls below 28% on a TTM basis.
- Shares outstanding growth (statistics page) exceeds 6% YoY without acquisition-backed earnings growth.
- Provision for credit losses rises well above loan-growth needs for two consecutive quarters, per the 10-Q (sec.gov).

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Shares outstanding rose 3.19% YoY (statistics page), which points to share issuance, likely acquisition-related; dilution is a watch item. Management guides to flat support staffing from 2027 while growing revenue producers (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/ssb/transcripts/782476-the-bancanalysts-association-of-boston-conference-2026/). [Likely] Bank FCF is not a reliable conversion measure; earnings quality is better judged by credit provisions.
