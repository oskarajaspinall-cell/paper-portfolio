**Market pricing (proxy):** the 2y sits 0.77pp above fed funds, so the market prices hikes rather than cuts. **(inference)** Higher-for-longer helps asset yields but raises deposit costs and pressure on securities and CRE marks. [Likely]

**Inference:** The slope is modest and positive, which is mildly supportive of NIM. The sharp 1m spread widening is the live change. It is still low in level (3.4%), so it is not yet stress. SSB trades at 11.2x P/E and 1.1x P/B, below its 5y range on P/E (fact sheet), so some risk is priced in. Beta of 0.78 is low.

**Bull:** the curve steepens and spreads stabilise. NIM expands and the P/B multiple re-rates toward the peer 1.3x.
**Bear:** spreads keep widening, the curve re-flattens or inverts. Credit costs rise, the multiple compresses and growth slows.

**Tilt:** neutral, small. Slope support offsets the spread widening; a spread above about 4.5% would turn it bearish.
