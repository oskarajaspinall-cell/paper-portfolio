# SouthState Bank Corporation (SSB) — Core initiation — 2026-10-25
## 1. Business
Regional bank holding company, Winter Haven, Florida. It earns net interest income on loans and securities funded by deposits, plus fee income (deposit service charges, cards, correspondent banking and capital markets). [company-reported] About $68.9 billion of assets across eight states, mainly the Southeast plus Colorado, Texas and Virginia (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/764038/000110465926089026/ssb-20260630x10q.htm). [Certain]

## 2. Quality
Adequate, not exceptional. ROE has been 9.3-10.7% for five years, TTM 10.3%, trend flat (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/ssb/financials/ratios/). Net margin is steady around 30-31%, TTM 35.0% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/ssb/financials/income-statement/). [Certain] Franchise moat is local deposit share and relationships in fast-growing Sun Belt markets; this is a durable but not return-expanding edge. [Likely] Efficiency ratio improved to 50.0% in Q2 2026 as merger costs rolled off [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/764038/000110465926089026/ssb-20260630x10q.htm). Q3 call: loans and deposits both growing, margin stable, credit stable, guidance for stable margin (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/ssb/transcripts/715215-q3-2026/). [Likely] Weak spots: Piotroski F of 4, and shares up 3.19% YoY (acquisition-related dilution) (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/ssb/statistics/). [Certain]

## 3. Valuation
P/E 11.2x is below the 5y minimum of 11.6x and 3% under the peer median of 11.6x; P/B 1.1x sits at 35% of its 5y range and 15% under peers at 1.3x (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/ssb/statistics/). P/FCF 11.9x is 16% above peers but bank FCF is noisy. [Certain] The price implies shrinking revenue (-4.8% in year 1 in reverse DCF), which looks too pessimistic for a growing loan book. [Likely] Blended base fair value is 132.73 vs a close of 105.51 (+25.8%), but the best method, P/B + sustainable ROE, gives 105.19 base, i.e. fair price. The upside rests on the FCFE DCF (187.82), which I distrust for a bank. [Likely] So: cheap against its own history, only modestly cheap against earnings power. Bear case 109.29 is above the price. [Guessing]

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROE (ratios page) falls below 8% on a TTM basis.
- Net margin (income statement page) falls below 27%.
- Shares change YoY (statistics page) exceeds 6% without a matching rise in earnings.
- Piotroski F-score (statistics page) falls to 3 or below.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Growth has been funded partly by acquisitions (the Independent deal closed in Q1 2025 [company-reported], source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/764038/000110465926089026/ssb-20260630x10q.htm) and share count is rising 3.19% YoY. FCF conversion fell from 89.0% in FY2024 to 28.9% in FY2025 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/ssb/financials/cash-flow-statement/). For a bank, operating cash flow is distorted by loan-held-for-sale flows, so I read this as noise rather than distress. [Likely] Dividends continue (source: https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/southstate-bank-corporation-reports-third-quarter-2026-results-declares-quarterly-cash-dividend-302915965.html). [Certain]
