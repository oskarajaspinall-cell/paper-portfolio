## 2. Quality
Profitability is adequate, not superior. ROE was 10.8% TTM versus 12.7% in FY2022, flat over five years [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sha/600919/financials/ratios/). Net margin rose from 47.9% to 52.7% over the same period (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sha/600919/financials/income-statement/) [Certain]. A durable moat is plausible, from a local franchise in a wealthy, industrial province, but I have no evidence on margin, asset quality or provisioning on this fact sheet [Guessing]. ROIC, ROCE and leverage are [data unavailable]. The F-score is 4, which is middling (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sha/600919/statistics/) [Certain]. FCF figures are not meaningful for a bank because deposit and loan flows drive them.

## 3. Valuation
P/E is 6.5x, above its 5y maximum of 5.9x against a median of 4.8x. P/B is 0.6x, at the top of its 0.4x–0.6x range. The stock has re-rated, but it is 6% cheaper than the peer median P/E and 18% cheaper on P/B (peers: Ningbo, Nanjing, Hangzhou, China Merchants) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sha/600919/statistics/). Mechanical base fair value is 16.97 CNY versus a 12.00 close (+41%). Its P/B+ROE leg is 13.55 (+13%), and the FCFE leg uses distorted bank cash flows that I distrust [Likely]. A discount to book with a roughly 11% ROE is not obviously mispriced, as a 0.6x P/B is consistent with a cost of equity near ROE once China risk is priced [Guessing]. The upside rests mostly on a distrusted method.
