# Shanghai Pudong Development Bank Co., Ltd. (SHA:600000) — Core initiation — 2026-10-19

## 1. Business
A Chinese national joint-stock commercial bank (Shanghai-headquartered, SSE-listed), earning net interest income from corporate and retail lending funded by deposits, plus fee income from cards, wealth management and transaction banking [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sha/600000/).

## 2. Quality
ROE has fallen from 8.1% (FY2021) to 6.3% TTM, a -1.7pp five-year trend, and net margin has slipped from 47.4% to 46.7% TTM (-1.2pp) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sha/600000/financials/ratios/, https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sha/600000/financials/income-statement/). Chinese joint-stock banks are rate-regulated by the PBOC with little pricing power and compete largely on scale and state backing, not product differentiation [Likely]. Peers (China Merchants Bank, China CITIC Bank, Industrial Bank, Bank of Jiangsu) trade at a similar P/E (peer median 7.0x vs SPDB's 6.9x), suggesting the market is discounting the sector, not singling SPDB out [Likely] (source: fact sheet valuation table). SPDB's P/B of 0.4x sits well below the peer median of 0.6x (-33%), a market signal of relatively weaker perceived asset quality or capital strength that stockanalysis.com's figures cannot verify directly [Likely]. Piotroski F-Score is a middling 4/9 [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sha/600000/statistics/). No durable moat is visible: this is a commodity-lending business subject to China's credit cycle.

## 3. Valuation
P/E (6.9x) is in line with peers and mid-range of its own 5-year history (51st percentile); P/B (0.4x) is near the bottom of its 5-year range and at a 33% discount to peers [Certain]. FCF-based multiples (P/FCF 0.4x, FCF margin swinging from -349.8% to +674.3% across FY2024-TTM) are not economically meaningful for a deposit-funded bank and should be disregarded [Certain] (source: fact sheet). Base fair value (P/B + sustainable ROE, primary; FCFE DCF, secondary) is 10.65 CNY, only +9.4% above the 9.73 CNY close; bear/bull cases are -22.8%/+26.9% [Certain] (source: fact sheet fair-value table). The reverse DCF implies the market is pricing in a -9.1% year-1 revenue decline, consistent with the observed ROE downtrend rather than an overreaction [Likely]. A single-digit base-case upside, next to a falling-ROE trend, is a thin margin of safety, not a value opportunity.

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROE (ratios page) recovers to and sustains above 8% (the FY2021 level) for two consecutive fiscal years, evidencing the decline was cyclical, not structural.
- Net margin (income statement) stabilizes above 46% rather than extending its five-year downtrend.
- P/B (statistics page) closes materially toward the peer median (currently 0.6x, a 33% premium to SPDB) without a further fall in ROE.
- Base fair-value upside (fair-value table, re-run by scripts/valuation.py) widens meaningfully above the current +9.4%, giving an actual margin of safety.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
FCF margin and FCF/NI conversion are not meaningful for a bank — they swing from -781.7% to +1,445.0% across the five years shown, reflecting loan/deposit balance changes rather than owner earnings, and should not be read as cash generation [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sha/600000/financials/cash-flow-statement/). The dividend-discount estimate (3.84-4.78 CNY) sits far below the 9.73 CNY market price and below the P/B+ROE estimate, consistent with a bank retaining capital to rebuild buffers rather than maximizing payout [Likely] (source: fact sheet fair-value table). Shares outstanding rose 2.71% YoY, a mild per-share dilution headwind [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sha/600000/statistics/).
