# Japan Airlines (TYO:9201) — Core initiation — 2026-11-08
## 1. Business
Full-service airline (Japan's flag carrier) earning revenue from domestic and international passenger and cargo flights, plus a smaller low-cost arm. Profit depends on load factors, yields, fuel and the yen. Foreign-country revenue split: [data unavailable] from the fact sheet; not verified from a primary source. [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tyo/9201/)

## 2. Quality
Post-pandemic recovery is complete: ROIC rose from 2.5% (FY2023) to 11.0% (FY2026), operating margin 9.3%, Piotroski F 8 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tyo/9201/financials/ratios/). [Certain]
However, the last 12 months are deteriorating: TTM ROIC 9.8% vs 11.0% a year earlier, operating margin 7.8% vs 9.3%, 2.2pp below its 2-year high (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tyo/9201/financials/ratios/?p=trailing). H1 profit fell on rising revenue per the 2026-11-02 report headline (https://www.nasdaq.com/articles/japan-airlines-h1-profit-declines-revenue-117), implying cost inflation (labour, fuel, yen) outpacing yield. [Likely]
Moat is moderate: slot-constrained Haneda access, a duopoly with ANA and a strong balance sheet (net cash on TTM, net debt/EBITDA -0.97x). Airlines are cyclical, so the moat does not protect through-cycle returns. [Likely] Altman Z 1.63 is weak. [Certain]

## 3. Valuation
P/E 13.5x vs 5y median 11.9x (21st percentile of range) and peers 14.0x, so earnings are not cheap. EV/EBITDA 3.3x is near its 5y low (3.1x) and 30% below peers; P/B 0.9x vs 1.2x median; FCF yield 11.29%; P/FCF 8.9x vs 7.6x median (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tyo/9201/statistics/). [Certain]
Low EV/EBITDA partly reflects net cash and heavy depreciation/leases from fleet capex, so it flatters. The model fair value (base 11,023.67 JPY vs close 3,277.00) is unreliable: the through-cycle DCF produces a bear of -28,769.45, so I do not rely on it. [Likely] Price is +14.5% above the 200-day average, with a 52-week high just set. The price assumes margins hold near current levels with no recovery; but profits are falling. [Guessing]

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
FCF conversion 145.9% TTM and FCF margin 7.4%, flat year on year, so cash generation is holding while accounting profit slips. Shares outstanding fell 1.24% YoY, a modest buyback. Net cash position gives capacity, but fleet renewal capex is a recurring call on cash. [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tyo/9201/statistics/)
