# Lennox International (LII) — Core initiation — 2026-10-09

## 1. Business
Lennox designs, manufactures and sells HVAC and refrigeration equipment for North American homes and buildings under the Lennox, Ducane and Armstrong Air brands, through company-owned and independent dealer distribution [Certain]. Two segments: Home Comfort Solutions (residential, 59% of 1H26 sales) and Building Climate Solutions (commercial, 41%) (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1069202/000106920226000087/lii-20260630.htm) [Certain]. Revenue mixes new-equipment sales with recurring, higher-margin replacement parts and service.

## 2. Quality
Historically a very high-return business: ROIC ran 35.9%-48.9% and ROCE 43.1%-67.0% over FY2021-24, with Altman Z 7.15 indicating minimal distress risk (source: factsheet RA/ST) [Certain]. But both are now falling — ROIC to 26.4% TTM and ROCE to 33.8% TTM — as residential volumes soften (source: factsheet RA) [Certain]. Structural mix-shift toward commercial and aftermarket has lifted gross margin to 33.3% and operating margin to 19.8% TTM, both up over the 5-year trend (source: factsheet IS) [Certain]. The moat is scale distribution and brand/dealer relationships, not pricing power: a Sherman Act Section 1 class action names Lennox and six other HVAC manufacturers over alleged coordination (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1069202/000106920226000087/lii-20260630.htm) [Certain]. Residential net sales fell 7.3% YoY in Q2 2026 while commercial grew 24%, confirming a genuine, not one-off, bifurcation in demand (source: same 10-Q) [Certain]. Management says residential recovery is delayed to 2027 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/lii/transcripts/725723-deutsche-bank-s-chicago-industrials-summit/) [Likely].

## 3. Valuation
LII trades at a P/E of 16.1x and EV/EBITDA of 12.4x, both below their 5-year minimums (17.1x/14.0x) and far below the peer median (41.6x/24.2x across CARR, TT, JCI, AAON) (source: factsheet RA) [Certain]. P/FCF of 17.0x is also well below its 5y range (26.7x-42.1x) (source: factsheet) [Certain]. The discount partly reflects mix: peers are larger and less residential-exposed. The blended base-case fair value (DCF 67%/EV-EBITDA 33%) is $356.77, within 1.3% of the $361.54 last close — essentially fair on through-cycle fundamentals despite cheap multiples (source: factsheet) [Certain]. The reverse DCF implies the market still prices 11.6% year-1 revenue growth fading to terminal — demanding given residential sales fell 7.3% YoY (source: factsheet, 10-Q) [Likely]. Downside/upside is asymmetric: bear case -57.3% vs bull +8.6% (source: factsheet) [Certain]. P/B of 9.5x is above peers' 7.0x median because buybacks have shrunk the equity base, not from asset-paying demand (source: factsheet) [Certain].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Home Comfort Solutions (residential) segment net sales (10-Q MD&A) fail to return to YoY growth by the quarter covering 1H2027, contradicting management's stated 2027 recovery.
- ROIC (ratios page) falls below 20%, extending the current decline from 26.4% TTM.
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) rises above 2.5x, above the FY2021-22 peak of 2.15x-2.32x.
- Gross margin (income statement) falls below 30%, reversing the structural mix-shift gain of the last three years.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Shares outstanding fell 1.82% YoY on buybacks, and the board raised the quarterly dividend to $1.36/share on 2026-09-18 (source: https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/lennox-declares-quarterly-dividend-302883415.html) [Certain]. FCF conversion (FCF/NI) is 93.7% TTM but has been volatile, as low as 40.5% in FY2022, so near-term conversion strength should not be extrapolated mechanically (source: factsheet CF,IS) [Certain]. Net debt/EBITDA of 1.68x TTM sits mid-range versus the 5-year history (0.94x-2.32x) (source: factsheet) [Certain]. Management is simultaneously investing in organic capacity (a new Fort Lauderdale commercial-HVAC training center opened 2026-09-03) while returning cash to shareholders (source: https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/lennox-opens-new-florida-training-center-to-invest-in-commercial-hvac-workforce-302867946.html) [Certain].

## 6. Unusual items
A Sherman Act Section 1 antitrust class action names Lennox and six other HVAC manufacturers; the company states it cannot yet estimate a possible loss range (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1069202/000106920226000087/lii-20260630.htm) [Certain]. Separately, a plaintiffs' law firm announced on 2026-08-24 it is "investigating" potential shareholder claims following the stock's post-guidance-cut decline; this is a solicitation, not an established finding of wrongdoing (source: https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/lii-investors-have-opportunity-to-join-lennox-international-inc-fraud-investigation-with-sbs-law-302857922.html) [Guessing]. Short interest rose 24.5% in the past month to 6.93% of float, a mildly bearish positioning signal worth monitoring but not yet a fundamental flag (source: factsheet ST) [Likely].
