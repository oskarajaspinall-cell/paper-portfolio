[Certain] The balance sheet is net cash: net debt/EBITDA of -0.64x TTM. FCF margin was 33.6% in FY2025 with 112.8% FCF/NI. Piotroski F is 6. [Certain]

The moat is asset-based (low-cost, long-life mines) and cannot be verified here: there are no cost-curve or reserve data. A gold miner has no pricing power, so a margin jump this large is mostly the gold price rather than structural advantage.
