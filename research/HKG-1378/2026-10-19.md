# China Hongqiao Group Limited (HKG:1378) — Core initiation — 2026-10-19

## 1. Business
China Hongqiao is a Chinese producer in the Materials sector, Aluminum industry (source: stockanalysis.com overview) [Certain]. It is an alumina/primary-aluminum producer whose revenue and margins move with commodity aluminum, alumina and input-cost (coal, bauxite) prices [Likely] — evidenced by gross margin swinging from 26.5% (FY2021) to 13.8% (FY2022) and back to 27.9% (TTM) (source: income statement) [Certain].

## 2. Quality
Returns are strong but cyclical: ROIC ranged 7.8%-19.5% and ROCE 10.2%-23.8% over FY2021-FY2025/TTM as gross margin swung with commodity prices (fact sheet ratios/income statement) [Certain]. Net debt/EBITDA of 0.26x TTM, down from a 1.76x FY2022 peak, gives balance-sheet room to weather the next downturn [Certain]. As a commodity producer, Hongqiao has no pricing power, brand or switching-cost moat; any edge is being lower on the cost curve than peers, which we could not verify — no company IR domain is recorded for this ticker in universe/ir_domains.txt, and stockanalysis.com does not publish segment cost data [Guessing]. Current TTM returns (ROIC 19.5%, ROCE 21.9%, ROE 22.9%) sit above the FY2021-25 range shown, yet the shares fell 24.2% over 12 months (fact sheet price) [Certain], implying the market doubts these returns persist through the cycle rather than disputing current profitability.

## 3. Valuation
P/E of 6.3x sits at the 28th percentile of its own 5y range (3.8x-12.9x, median 4.8x) and 44% below the 4-peer median of 11.3x [Certain]. EV/EBITDA of 3.8x is roughly in line with peers (+4%), while P/FCF of 4.9x is 60% below the peer median of 12.3x (n=3) and P/B of 1.2x is in line with peers [Certain]. FCF yield is 20.58% TTM [Certain]. A cheap P/E and P/FCF alongside a peer-in-line EV/EBITDA suggests the market treats current free cash flow as cyclically elevated rather than the enterprise as mispriced, consistent with the 12-month share-price fall despite steady margins. Base-case fair value of HKD 35.83 (67% through-cycle DCF / 33% EV/EBITDA blend) implies +82.4% upside to the HKD 19.64 close; bear/bull of HKD 2.53/55.63 span the range a commodity cyclical can take (fact sheet fair value) [Certain]. Treated as directional, not a target.

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROIC (ratios page) falls and stays below 10% for two consecutive half-year periods, signalling the cost position is not holding through this part of the cycle.
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios/balance sheet page) rises above 1.0x, rebuilding toward the FY2022 peak of 1.76x.
- Gross margin (income statement) falls back below 15%, matching the FY2022 trough, evidencing margin compression beyond normal cyclicality.
- FCF conversion, FCF/NI (cash flow/income statement), turns negative for two consecutive half-year periods, as it did in FY2022 (-14.1%).
