# Macro overlay: China Hongqiao Group Limited (1378) — 2026-10-19
## Assessment
Hongqiao is a commodity cyclical (alumina/primary aluminum); the thesis is about where aluminum/alumina margins sit in the cycle (fact sheet). FRED-trackable chain: **US dollar / real yields -> USD-priced global aluminum benchmark & the DCF discount rate -> margin and fair-value range**, secondary to China-domestic drivers (SHFE pricing, energy/bauxite costs, capacity caps) our allowlist cannot quantify.

Facts [Certain, FRED, 2026-10-15/16]: broad dollar (DTWEXBGS) +3.72 over 1m to 121.6 (https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=DTWEXBGS, 2026-10-09) — most of the 3m move (+1.1) is this latest month; effective fed funds (DFF) +0.25pp over both 1m and 3m to 3.88%, i.e. the whole quarter's rise is this month's hike; 10y real yield (DFII10) +0.43pp over 3m to 2.78%; breakeven inflation (T10YIE) barely moved (+0.11pp to 2.35%), so the yield rise is real-rate/policy-driven, not an inflation surprise. CNY (DEXCHUS) held firm, -0.08 over 3m to 6.69, despite broad dollar strength — PBOC managing the peg **(inference)**.

Market pricing: 2y Treasury (4.77%) sits 0.89pp above fed funds (3.88%), a proxy that the market still expects further tightening, not cuts **(inference, proxy)**.

Bull: dollar/real-yield rise stalls -> aluminum price firms, discount rate eases -> supports re-rating off the cheap P/E/P/FCF. Bear: dollar and real yields extend this month's surge -> USD aluminum softer, discount rate (already 5.10% risk-free in our DCF) rises further -> fair-value range compresses, reinforcing the 12-month -24% move. Tilt: moderate toward bear — fresh, large dollar/hike move on an already-falling stock, no offsetting CNY weakness.
