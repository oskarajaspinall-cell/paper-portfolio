# Uber Technologies, Inc. (UBER) — Core initiation — 2026-10-09

## 1. Business
Two-sided marketplace connecting riders/eaters/shippers with drivers, couriers and carriers; Uber monetizes gross bookings via a take-rate commission across Mobility, Delivery (Uber Eats) and Freight [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/uber/). Asset-light model: Uber owns no vehicles; its 10-Q describes the model as powered by "a massive network, leading technology, operational excellence and product expertise" [Certain] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1543151/000154315126000032/uber-20260630.htm).

## 2. Quality
Returns have inflected from value-destructive to strong: ROIC TTM 19.2% (from -19.2% FY21), ROCE 13.2%, ROE 37.2%, all still rising on the 5y trend [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/uber/financials/ratios/). Margins are expanding across the board — gross margin 40.8%, operating margin 12.1%, FCF margin 18.3% TTM — evidence of real operating leverage, not a one-off [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/uber/financials/income-statement/). The moat is a two-sided network-effect marketplace (driver/merchant density lowers wait times and raises selection, which attracts more demand) [Likely] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1543151/000154315126000032/uber-20260630.htm), reinforced by scale versus Lyft and regional share versus Grab. Risk to durability: robotaxi partnerships (Pony.ai in London, alongside Wayve) could eventually let OEMs or AV operators bypass Uber's network, or could instead cement it as the demand-aggregation layer — direction unresolved [Guessing] (source: https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/08/uber-and-chinas-pony-ai-plan-to-launch-robotaxis-in-london/). FCF conversion (FCF/NI) fell 52.7pp over 5y to 105.6% TTM, still fully covering earnings in cash [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/uber/financials/cash-flow-statement/).

## 3. Valuation
The market is pricing Uber well below both its own history and listed peers: EV/EBITDA 19.8x sits below the 5y minimum (26.8x) and 65% under the peer median (56.4x, LYFT/DASH/GRAB); P/E 15.3x is -44% vs peer median and near the bottom of its own 5y range; P/FCF 14.2x is also below its 5y min [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/uber/financials/ratios/, https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/uber/statistics/). FCF yield is 7.05% [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/uber/statistics/). P/B (5.2x) and EV/Sales (2.7x) are the exceptions, running above peer medians, consistent with Uber's higher margin structure. The fact sheet's DCF-based base fair value is $76.76 (+9.3% vs the $70.24 close), with bear/bull of -$11.38/$108.78; the reverse DCF implies the price embeds only ~12.5% year-1 revenue growth fading to terminal [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/uber/). The setup is a profitable, compounding platform priced like a mature, low-growth one.

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROIC (ratios page) falls back below 10%, reversing the 5y re-rating in returns.
- FCF margin (cash flow/income statement) drops below 10%, breaking the margin-expansion trend.
- Net debt/EBITDA (site definition, ratios page) rises above 1.5x, signaling the ezCater deal and further M&A are levering the balance sheet.
- Operating margin (income statement) stalls or reverses for two consecutive quarters, indicating competitive take-rate pressure from Lyft/DoorDash/Grab or AV disintermediation.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Uber repurchased 39.74m shares for $3.01bn in H1 2026 (including $517m in Q2 alone), consistent with shares outstanding falling 2.28% YoY [Certain] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1543151/000154315126000032/uber-20260630.htm, https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/uber/statistics/). Balance sheet: cash + ST investments ~$5.39bn vs total debt ~$12.72bn, but net debt/EBITDA (site definition) is just 0.64x TTM given strong EBITDA growth [Certain] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1543151/000154315126000032/uber-20260630.htm). On 2026-10-06 Uber announced an all-cash $2.3bn acquisition of catering platform ezCater, funded from the balance sheet, expanding Uber Eats into B2B catering [Certain] (source: https://www.reuters.com/business/uber-buy-us-catering-platform-ezcater-23-billion-2026-10-06/); any margin benefit is unconfirmed [Guessing].

## 6. Unusual items
None material: short interest is low (2.25% of float, falling 1.5% vs prior month), institutional ownership is high (83.7%), and Altman Z (3.74) / Piotroski F (5) show no distress [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/uber/statistics/).
