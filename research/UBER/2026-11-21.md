# Uber Technologies (UBER) — Core initiation — 2026-11-21
## 1. Business
Uber runs a two-sided platform taking a fee on Mobility (rides), Delivery (food, grocery, retail) and Freight bookings. Revenue is bookings net of driver and courier payments, so reported revenue depends on contract model; a UK Mobility model change from January 2026 reduced reported revenue by $3.2 billion over nine months [company-reported] (https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1543151/000154315126000041/uber-20260930.htm). Revenue split by country: [data unavailable] in the fact sheet; not verified, so the macro overlay should treat the UK as a known exposure [Likely].

## 2. Quality
Returns have risen over five years: ROIC from -19.2% (FY2021) to 14.9% TTM, operating margin 12.4% TTM, FCF margin 18.4% TTM (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/uber/financials/ratios/) [Certain]. Direction change: TTM ROIC fell from 16.8% to 14.9% and sits 5.9pp below its 2-year high; operating margin and ROCE are still rising [Certain]. Net debt/EBITDA (debt less cash) is 0.98x; Altman Z 3.33 [Certain]. Moat: network density in rides and delivery, and scale in demand and supply [Likely]. The durability question is autonomous vehicles: Waymo, Tesla and Zoox are building their own networks, and Uber says it has invested in partners and that AVs may meaningfully change its industries [company-reported] (https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1543151/000154315126000041/uber-20260930.htm). If AV owners sell direct, the aggregation moat weakens [Guessing].

## 3. Valuation
At 63.47 the price is 12.3% below its 200-day average and 29.1% below a year ago (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/uber/history/). EV/EBITDA 17.1x and P/FCF 12.4x are below their 5y minimums and 36% and 32% below peers; FCF yield 8.04% [Certain]. P/E 37.9x is mid-range of its own 5y range but 74% above peers, because net income includes equity-stake revaluations (see 6) [Likely]. Mechanical base fair value 82.35 (+29.8%), but bear is negative and the DCF swing is wide, so treat it as one estimate [Likely]. The reverse DCF implies 7.8% year-1 revenue growth, fading, a low bar for a business growing bookings at 20% in the quarter [company-reported] (https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1543151/000154315126000041/uber-20260930.htm) [Likely].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Operating margin (income statement, TTM) falls below 10%.
- FCF margin (cash flow statement, TTM) falls below 14%.
- ROIC TTM (ratios page, trailing) falls below 12%.
- Trips growth in the 10-Q drops below 8% YoY, or a named AV partner or competitor launches a rival ride network in a top US market without Uber.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Shares outstanding fell 2.79% YoY, so buybacks more than offset stock compensation [Certain]. Management says roughly half of free cash flow is returned (headline summary of its 2026-11-18 conference, not verified from a primary source) [Guessing]. TTM FCF conversion of 300.8% is inflated by low GAAP net income; FY2025 was 97.1% [Certain]. The company holds $10.1 billion of unrestricted cash and short-term investments [company-reported] (https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1543151/000154315126000041/uber-20260930.htm). Large AV and equity-stake investments are the main use of cash to monitor [Likely].
