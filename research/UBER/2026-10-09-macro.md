# Macro overlay: Uber Technologies, Inc. (UBER) — 2026-10-09
## Assessment
Chain: nominal/real yields → discount rate → DCF fair value and multiple. UBER's own fair value is a 10-year DCF using the 10y Treasury as risk-free rate; earnings are domestic-USD with no material macro channel [Likely].

Facts (FRED, 2026-10-07/08): 10y Treasury (DGS10) 5.28%, +72bp/3m; 10y real yield (DFII10) 2.92%, +61bp/3m; 10y breakeven (T10YIE) 2.35%, roughly flat (+12bp/3m); effective fed funds (DFF) 3.88%, +26bp/3m; HY spread (BAMLH0A0HYM2) 3.09%, +39bp/3m but still low in absolute terms (https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=DGS10, id=DFII10, id=T10YIE, id=DFF, id=BAMLH0A0HYM2).

Market pricing (proxy): the 2y Treasury (4.77%) sits ~89bp above effective fed funds (3.88%), suggesting the market isn't pricing near-term cuts [Likely] **(inference)**. Flat breakevens alongside rising real yields point to a hawkish repricing of the policy path/term premium, not an inflation surprise [Likely] **(inference)**.

The DCF already uses the spot 5.28% risk-free rate, so today's fair value isn't stale. The forward risk: further yield rises would mechanically lower cost of equity's output, compressing DCF fair value and deepening the apparent undervaluation with no change to margins or returns; a reversal lower would aid re-rating toward peers (EV/EBITDA 19.8x vs 56.4x peer median). Uber is a profitable, below-peer-multiple compounder, not a long-duration unprofitable grower, so this sensitivity is real but secondary to the fundamental thesis (margin expansion, buybacks, peer discount).

**macro_tilt**: toward bear, small — rates have moved against the stock and pricing shows no near-term relief, but fundamentals (ROIC, margins, peer gap) dominate.
