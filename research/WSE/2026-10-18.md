# Wise Group plc (WSE) — Core initiation — 2026-10-18
## 1. Business
Wise operates a global money-transfer and multi-currency account platform: customers move funds across currencies through local payment rails in each corridor rather than correspondent banking, paying a transfer fee (take rate on volume), plus interest earned on customer balances held in Wise Account/Wise Business and its multi-currency card. [Certain]

## 2. Quality
Returns on capital remain high but have fallen for two straight years: ROCE 45.6% TTM vs a FY2024 peak of 63.3%; ROE 27.2% TTM vs a FY2024 peak of 51.0% [Certain] [RA]. Gross margin 84.2%, operating margin 44.7%, net margin 19.9% — each 3-12pp below FY2024 peaks as take-rate/cost pressure bites [Certain] [IS]. The balance sheet carries net cash, net debt/EBITDA -4.32x [Certain] [BS,IS]. Reported FCF margin (301%) and FCF/NI (>1,500%) are mechanically inflated by customer-balance movements, not underlying cash generation [Likely]. The moat (multi-currency network effects plus money-transfer licensing that undercuts correspondent banking) is being tested: management raised/narrowed FY27 revenue-growth guidance higher after a Q2 beat [Likely] (source: https://www.reuters.com/technology/money-transfer-firm-wise-raises-revenue-growth-outlook-cross-border-volumes-jump-2026-10-16/), but a filed securities class action alleges AML/counter-terrorism-financing compliance failures tied to SEC disclosures — a direct threat to the licensing-based moat if regulators act [Certain] (see Unusual items).

## 3. Valuation
At the 10.98 last close, P/E (22.7x) sits at the very bottom of its own 5-year range (22.3-145.4x) [Certain] [RA,HI]. Vs peers (WU, PYPL, RELY, EEFT median), WSE carries a premium on P/E (+116%), EV/Sales (+109%) and P/B (+156%), but trades below peer-median EV/EBITDA (-11%) and below its own 5y EV/EBITDA range minimum [Certain] [RA]. P/FCF (1.5x) and FCF yield (67%) look extremely cheap but are distorted by the same customer-float effect noted in Quality, so are not a reliable signal [Likely]. The blended DCF/EV-EBITDA fair value (base 108.64, bull 265.73 vs 10.98) implies deep undervaluation, but that model is built on the same inflated FCF/EBITDA margins and should not be read as a clean entry signal [Likely]. Net: on the multiples least distorted by the float effect, the market is paying a premium for WSE versus listed payments/remittance peers despite two years of margin compression.

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Gross margin (income statement page) falls below 80% for two consecutive fiscal years, confirming take-rate/cost pressure continues past FY2026's 84.2%.
- ROCE (ratios page) falls below 35%, well under the current 45.6% and approaching the FY2023 level of 25.6%, signalling the return profile has structurally reset lower.
- The AML/CTF securities litigation or a related regulator action produces a fine, consent order, or loss of a money-transfer licence in a material market, impairing the licensing moat.
- Net debt/EBITDA (balance sheet + income statement) turns positive, showing the net-cash cushion has been spent or impaired.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Net debt/EBITDA is -4.32x: the company holds materially more cash than debt [Certain] [BS,IS]. Shares outstanding fell 1.53% YoY, consistent with net buybacks [Certain] [ST]. Reported FCF margin (301%) and FCF conversion (>1,500% of TTM net income) are mechanically inflated by movements in the customer balances Wise holds as payments float, not by underlying free cash generation; true owner cash conversion cannot be isolated from the fact sheet and is effectively [data unavailable]. [Likely]

## 6. Unusual items
A securities class action was filed (lead plaintiff deadline 2026-09-29) alleging two Wise executives signed/certified SEC disclosures while the company faced undisclosed AML/counter-terrorism-financing compliance risk [Certain] (source: https://www.tipranks.com/news/shareholders-sue-wise-over-aml-and-counter-terrorist-financing-claims). Short interest is low in absolute terms (0.64% of float) but rose 18.4% month-on-month [Certain] [ST], consistent with growing bearish positioning around the litigation/guidance event. Altman Z of 0.60 sits in the statistical distress zone despite the net-cash balance sheet; likely an artifact of Wise's customer-liability-heavy balance-sheet structure rather than true credit risk [Guessing].
