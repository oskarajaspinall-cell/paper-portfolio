# Macro overlay: Wise Group plc (WSE) — 2026-10-18
## Assessment
Two macro channels touch WSE beyond background noise, though the thesis stays fundamentals-led (margin compression, AML/CTF litigation — see note). **Chain 1**: short-term policy rates → interest earned on customer balances (Wise's stated second revenue stream, per the note) → revenue/margin. **Chain 2**: real yields → discount rate → WSE's multiple (P/E 22.7x, bottom of its 5y range but still a premium to payments peers — factsheet).

Facts: effective fed funds 3.88% on 2026-10-15, +0.25pp over 3m [Certain] (https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=DFF, 2026-10-15). 10y real yield (DFII10) 2.78% on 2026-10-15, +0.43pp over 3m [Certain] (https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=DFII10, 2026-10-15). HY credit spread 3.23% on 2026-10-15, +0.52pp over 3m, still a low absolute level [Certain] (https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=BAMLH0A0HYM2, 2026-10-15).

Market pricing (proxy, not fact): the 2y Treasury yield (4.77%, 2026-10-15, https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=DGS2) sitting ~0.9pp above fed funds implies the market prices further tightening or a slower cutting path over two years [Likely] **(inference)** — a tailwind to Wise's float income if realised, but a headwind to its already-rich multiple via real yields.

**macro_bull**: rates stay elevated/rise further — float income offsets take-rate/margin compression; a multiple already at its 5y low has limited further room to de-rate on rates alone. **macro_bear**: rate cuts remove the float-income tailwind just as margins compress for a second straight year, while risk-off (spreads/VIX) adds multiple pressure atop the litigation overhang. **Tilt**: moderate toward bear — rising real yields and modestly wider (still-low) credit spreads compound an already-weakening fundamental trend more than the float-income tailwind currently helps.
