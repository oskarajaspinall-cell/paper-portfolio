# Motiva Infraestrutura de Mobilidade (BVMF:MOTV3) — Core initiation — 2026-11-09
## 1. Business
Brazilian mobility-infrastructure concessionaire (formerly CCR): toll roads, plus rail/urban mobility and airports, earning regulated, inflation-linked tolls and tariffs under long concessions [Likely]. Revenue is domestic; a foreign share is [data unavailable] on the fact sheet. The Q3 2026 release refers to an airport sale that simplified the portfolio and cut leverage [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/bvmf/MOTV3/transcripts/673430-q3-2026/). Currency and rates exposure is BRL (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/bvmf/MOTV3/).

## 2. Quality
Concession assets give quasi-monopoly traffic and contractual pricing, a durable but finite moat (concessions expire) [Likely]. Returns are mediocre: TTM ROIC 9.0%, ROCE 11.0%, versus FY2022 ROIC of 17.5% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/bvmf/MOTV3/financials/ratios/). Operating margin 33.5% TTM is down from 37.7% a year earlier [Certain]. FCF margin fell from 44.4% to 21.8% in twelve months, and FCF/NI is 71.7% TTM, consistent with heavy concession capex [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/bvmf/MOTV3/financials/cash-flow-statement/?p=trailing). Net debt/EBITDA is 3.72x, high for BRL rates; Altman Z is 1.40, Piotroski 6 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/bvmf/MOTV3/statistics/). Net margin of 30.3% TTM probably includes one-off gains from asset sales and acquisitions [Likely]. Good, not great.

## 3. Valuation
P/E is 5.0x, below its 5y minimum of 5.3x and against a peer median of 13.6x, but this is flattered by one-off gains [Likely]. EV/EBITDA is 7.3x, 81% up its 5y range (median 6.9x) and in line with peers at 7.2x. P/FCF is 6.9x, above its 5y maximum of 6.0x. P/B is 1.6x versus a 5y median of 1.9x. FCF yield is 14.49%. Mechanical base fair value is 31.24 BRL against a 14.95 close, but the sheet marks it unreliable (methods disagree 3.0x; DCF 40.21 vs EV/EBITDA 13.29), so weigh it lightly [Guessing]. The reverse DCF implies year-1 revenue shrinking 9.9%, which is too pessimistic for inflation-linked tolls [Likely]. Cheap on earnings, fair on EBITDA, and cash flow is deteriorating. The price is down 12.1% against its 50-day average after a spike to 20.83 and a low of 11.92 (2026-10-26), so volatility is extreme (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/bvmf/MOTV3/history/).

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) rises above 4.5x.
- TTM operating margin (income statement, trailing) falls below 30%.
- TTM ROIC (ratios, trailing) falls below 8%.
