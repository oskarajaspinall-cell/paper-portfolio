# Hua Xia Bank (SHA:600015) — Core initiation — 2026-11-08
## 1. Business
Regional Chinese commercial bank: earns net interest spread on loans plus fee income, funded by deposits (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sha/600015/). Reports in CNY; revenue is essentially domestic China, so the whole book carries China macro, property and policy-rate risk [Likely]. Segment and regional mix are [data unavailable] on the fact sheet.

## 2. Quality
Mediocre, not a moat. ROE was 8.2% in FY2021 and 7.3% in FY2025, with TTM at 6.3%: flat-to-falling, below any cost of equity that includes the owner's 3.5% China premium (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sha/600015/financials/ratios/) [Certain]. Net margin looks stable (38.0% TTM versus 38.8% in FY2021), but it is not a robust earnings-power signal for a bank [Likely]. ROIC, ROCE, operating margin and leverage are [data unavailable], as is normal for banks. Piotroski F is 4 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sha/600015/statistics/), middling. Shares outstanding rose 9.73% YoY, which is dilution [Certain]. Beta of -0.02 reflects a state-influenced, thinly traded profile rather than genuine diversification [Guessing]. Nothing here shows a durable competitive advantage over larger peers such as China Merchants Bank [Guessing].

## 3. Valuation
The price assumes a shrinking bank. P/E is 4.7x, 79% of the way up its 5y range (3.7x to 5.0x, median 4.2x), against a peer median of 7.1x, a 34% discount. P/B is 0.3x, the same as its 5y min, median and max, and 40% below peers at 0.4x (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sha/600015/statistics/) [Certain]. The discount is largely explained by below-peer ROE and dilution [Likely].
Mechanical fair value (inform-only) is 13.00 CNY base against a 6.56 close, with bear 11.11 and bull 13.40; the reverse DCF implies -19.0% revenue growth in year 1. That fair value is heavily driven by P/B + ROE and FCFE inputs; the DDM cross-check gives only 4.37 base, below the price, so the methods disagree widely [Certain]. Bank FCF is not meaningful (FCF yield 12.99%, P/FCF 1.4x) because deposit and loan flows swing it [Likely]. Cheap, but the cheapness is a value-trap pattern until ROE stabilises [Likely].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROE (ratios page) stays below 7.0% in the next annual report, so returns fail to recover.
- Shares outstanding (statistics page) rise a further 5% or more year on year, so dilution continues.
- Net margin (income statement page) falls below 35%, showing spread or credit-cost pressure.
- Piotroski F (statistics page) drops to 3 or lower.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Capital allocation is shareholder-unfriendly on the evidence: shares outstanding up 9.73% YoY [Certain]. FCF/NI ranges from 93.2% in FY2024 to 835.6% in FY2023, and the TTM FCF margin of 115.6% is down 76.3pp from a year earlier (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sha/600015/financials/cash-flow-statement/). For a bank, operating cash flow moves with the deposit and loan balance, so this is noise and not evidence of earnings quality [Likely]. Dividend history was not reviewed here.
