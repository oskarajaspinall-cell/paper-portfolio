# Macro overlay: Prologis, Inc. (PLD) — 2026-10-12
## Assessment
Primary: PLD is a long-duration industrial REIT, highly levered (net debt/EBITDA 5.24x TTM, rising [Certain], https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/pld/financials/ratios/), now funding a ~$18.8-19.2bn debt-and-equity Segro acquisition [Certain, WSJ/Reuters as cited in the note]. Both the discount rate on its lease cash flows and its cost of financing are first-order to the thesis. Chain: real yields -> REIT cap rate/discount rate -> NAV/P-FFO multiple; also real yields/fed funds -> PLD's cost of debt for refinancing and the Segro deal.

Facts [Certain, FRED, 2026-10-08/09]: 10y real yield (DFII10) 2.87%, +44bp/1m, +56bp/3m; 10y Treasury (DGS10) 5.22%, +42bp/1m, +68bp/3m; 10y breakeven inflation (T10YIE) 2.33%, roughly flat (-4bp/1m, +9bp/3m) - a real-rate/term-premium move, not an inflation scare. Market pricing (proxy, **inference**): 2y Treasury (DGS2, 4.75%) sits ~87bp above effective fed funds (DFF, 3.88%), implying the market prices further tightening or "higher-for-longer," not near-term cuts [Likely].

This is already partly reflected in PLD's de-rating (-8.4% 3m, -20.9pp vs SPY 6m [Certain]), and the fair-value model already uses the current 5.22% risk-free rate. **macro_bull**: real yields reverse toward ~2.3-2.5%, cap rates compress, multiple re-rates toward the $161 bull case, Segro financing cheapens. **macro_bear**: real yields grind above 3.0%, cap rates expand further, multiple compresses toward the $113 bear case, financing cost worsens Segro accretion. **Tilt**: moderate toward bear — leverage, dilutive financing and rising real yields all point the same way, reinforcing rather than offsetting the fundamental AVOID.
