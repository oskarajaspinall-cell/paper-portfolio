# Prologis, Inc. (PLD) — Core initiation — 2026-10-12

## 1. Business
Prologis owns and operates logistics/industrial warehouses, leasing space to e-commerce, 3PL and manufacturing tenants on multi-year leases [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/pld/). It also earns fee income managing co-investment ventures ("strategic capital") and is building a data-center platform, and periodically sells developed assets for gains [Likely] (source: https://www.prologis.com, company overview).

## 2. Quality
Scale and infill-land scarcity near population centers give Prologis a locational moat and development-cost edge over smaller peers [Likely]. But returns on capital are modest for a "quality" label: ROIC is 4.0% TTM, flat over five years (+0.4pp), and ROE has fallen from 8.5% (FY21) to 7.7% TTM, a 5-year decline of -2.4pp [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/pld/financials/ratios/). Leverage is rising, not falling: net debt/EBITDA (site definition) is up from 4.89x (FY21) to 5.24x TTM, +0.47x over five years [Certain] (same source). The company is now also absorbing a transformational, debt- and equity-funded acquisition (Segro, see Unusual items), which raises integration and balance-sheet risk at a time when industrial-REIT fundamentals (rent growth, occupancy) are already decelerating, as shown by the stock's underperformance versus SPY (-12.0pp over 3 months, -20.9pp over 6 months) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/pld/history/). Gross/operating/net margins and FCF conversion are not disclosed on stockanalysis.com [data unavailable].

## 3. Valuation
PLD trades near the bottom of its own 5-year multiple range (EV/EBITDA 23.9x vs 22.3x-40.5x, 9th percentile; EV/Sales 16.7x, 7th percentile) [Certain], reflecting the recent de-rating, but still at a premium to peers (EGP, FR, REXR, STAG): EV/EBITDA +26% and EV/Sales +29% above peer medians, P/E +4% above [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/pld/statistics/ and peer statistics pages). P/B is roughly in line (-5%). Base fair value is $146.00 (P/FFO-proxy) versus the last close of $129.49, i.e. +12.8% upside, with bear $112.65 (-13.0%) and bull $161.40 (+24.6%) [Certain] (source: research/PLD/2026-10-12-valuation.md; fact sheet). The market appears to be pricing continued rent-growth deceleration and Segro integration/dilution risk, while still crediting a scale premium over smaller peers; FCF yield is negative (-0.37%), consistent with a debt/equity-funded development and acquisition cycle rather than a cash-generative discount [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/pld/statistics/).

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page, site definition) rises above 6.0x, confirming the Segro deal and development spend are stretching the balance sheet beyond the current rising trend (5.24x TTM).
- ROIC (ratios page) falls below its 5-year low of 3.5%, signalling the core leasing/development business is losing earning power on capital employed.
- ROE (ratios page) extends its decline below 5%, showing shareholder returns are eroding faster than the 5-year trend (-2.4pp to 7.7% TTM).
- Shares outstanding growth (statistics page) accelerates well beyond the current 0.33% YoY pace (e.g., sustained >5% YoY) as further equity is issued to fund the Segro acquisition, diluting per-share value beyond what the base case assumes.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Prologis pays a regular quarterly dividend (declared 2026-09-02) [Certain] (source: https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/prologis-declares-quarterly-dividend-302868158.html, as listed in the fact sheet). FCF margin and FCF/NI conversion are not disclosed on stockanalysis.com [data unavailable], and FCF yield is negative (-0.37%) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/pld/statistics/), consistent with capital-intensive development funded by debt and equity rather than retained cash. On 2026-08-04 Prologis priced and announced a public offering of 15,000,000 new common shares [Certain] (source: https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/prologis-announces-pricing-of-common-stock-offering-302842518.html, as listed in the fact sheet), which is dilutive and appears tied to funding the Segro acquisition.

## 6. Unusual items
On 2026-08-04 Prologis agreed to acquire UK industrial REIT Segro in a stock-and-cash deal worth up to ~$18.8-19.2bn (0.0920 new Prologis shares per Segro share plus a partial cash alternative) [Certain] (source: https://www.wsj.com/business/deals/prologis-to-buy-u-k-s-segro-for-18-8-billion-50b0ddd5 and https://www.reuters.com/business/uks-segro-agrees-prologis-up-192-billion-bid-2026-08-04/, as listed in the fact sheet). This is a large, transformational deal that materially raises execution and integration risk and was accompanied by a dilutive equity raise the same week [Certain]. Short interest rose +20% month-on-month but remains low in absolute terms at 2.0% of float [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/pld/statistics/) — not yet material on its own.

**Decision: AVOID.** Quality trend (falling ROE, rising leverage) is deteriorating into a transformational, dilutive acquisition, and the valuation premium to peers persists even after the de-rating; the +12.8% base-case upside does not compensate for integration/dilution risk, so conviction falls below the bar (4) required for a BUY.
