# Prologis (PLD) — Core initiation — 2026-11-21
## 1. Business
Prologis owns, develops and manages logistics real estate (REIT - Industrial) and earns rent from warehouse customers, plus co-investment venture fees and development gains. It is expanding selectively into data centers (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1045609/000119312526436160/pld-20260930.htm).
