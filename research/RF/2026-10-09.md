# Regions Financial Corporation (RF) — Core initiation — 2026-10-09

## 1. Business
Regions Financial is a Birmingham, Alabama-based regional bank operating as Regions Bank across the South and Midwest [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/rf/). It earns net interest income on loans funded by deposits, plus fee income from wealth management, mortgage and capital-markets/treasury-management services [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/rf/financials/income-statement/).

## 2. Quality
Profitability has deteriorated for five straight years: ROE fell from 13.8% (FY2021) to 11.9% TTM (-2.2pp) and net margin fell from 36.2% to 30.8% (-5.7pp) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/rf/financials/ratios/, https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/rf/financials/income-statement/). The Piotroski F-Score is 3 (of 9), a weak fundamental-quality signal [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/rf/statistics/). ROIC/ROCE/gross and operating margin are not meaningful or disclosed for a bank and are [data unavailable] on the fact sheet. No durable, measurable moat (low-cost deposit franchise, scale cost advantage, pricing power) is evidenced in the available metrics beyond a Southeast/Midwest branch footprint [Guessing]; regional banks generally have limited pricing power and are rate-sensitive [Likely]. Management continuity risk: Treasurer Deron Smithy and separately CFO-adjacent executive Dave Keenan are both retiring in 2026, with several new leadership appointments (Kate Danella as Chief Administrative Officer, John Jordan leading Consumer Banking, Angela Santone to the CEO's team) [Certain] (source: https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260923641920/en/Regions-Financial-Announces-Retirement-of-Deron-Smithy-Names-Allen-Mayer-as-Treasurer/, https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260811365353/en/Regions-Financial-Announces-Upcoming-Retirement-of-Dave-Keenan-and-New-Executive-Leadership-Appointments/).

## 3. Valuation
RF's P/E of 11.0x sits at the 69th percentile of its own 5-year range (8.7x-12.1x) and is 15% below the peer median of 13.0x (KEY, CFG, HBAN, FITB) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/rf/statistics/). But P/B of 1.3x is above its own 5-year maximum (1.0x-1.3x) and 12% above the peer median of 1.2x [Certain] (same source) — the market is paying an above-history, above-peer price for book value even as ROE has fallen for five years, which is an inconsistent signal rather than a clear discount. The fair-value model (P/B+ROE primary, FCFE DCF secondary) puts base fair value at $27.45 versus the last close of $27.07 (+1.4%), with bear/bull of $22.64/$30.48 (-16.4%/+12.6%) [Certain] (source: fact sheet fair-value table). The reverse DCF implies the price assumes revenue shrinks 1% in year 1 before fading to terminal growth [Certain]. Upside is minimal and downside is larger, so there is no margin of safety.

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROE (ratios page) recovers back above 13%, reversing the five-year decline (currently 11.9% TTM)
- Net margin (income statement) stabilizes and holds above 32% (currently 30.8% TTM, down from 36.2% in FY2021)
- Piotroski F-Score (statistics page) rises to 6 or higher from the current 3

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Shares outstanding fell 3.95% year-over-year, consistent with an active buyback program returning capital to holders [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/rf/statistics/). FCF yield is 7.71% [Certain] (same source).
