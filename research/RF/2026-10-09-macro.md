# Macro overlay: Regions Financial Corporation (RF) — 2026-10-09
## Assessment
Chain: policy/long-term rates -> net interest margin (asset repricing vs deposit cost) and -> AOCI/tangible book value (securities mark-to-market) -> P/B multiple and EPS — the textbook "banks and rates" primary link.

Facts [Certain]: effective fed funds 3.88% (2026-10-07, +25bp/1m, +26bp/3m); 10y Treasury 5.28% (+50bp/1m, +72bp/3m); 2y Treasury 4.77% (+40bp/1m, +56bp/3m); 10y real yield (TIPS) 2.92% (+49bp/1m, +61bp/3m); HY credit spread 3.09% (+41bp/1m, +39bp/3m); VIX 15.08, benign (FRED, https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=<series>, dated 2026-10-07/08). RF's own prime-rate hike to 7.00% (effective 2026-09-17) confirms the transmission is live [Certain] (research/RF/factsheet-2026-10-09.md).

Market pricing **(inference)**: the 10y-2y curve steepened to +51bp, 10y rising faster than 2y over 3 months — proxy for "higher-for-longer," not imminent cuts [Likely]. 2y (4.77%) sits ~89bp above fed funds (3.88%), implying the market still prices tightening risk, a hawkish shift vs three months ago [Likely].

Surprise direction: a faster yield back-up hurts RF — it pressures AOCI/tangible book on its securities portfolio just as P/B (1.3x) already sits above its own 5y range, leaving no cushion. A stabilizing curve with contained credit spreads helps (NIM gains, no credit-cost shock).

Bull: curve stays steep, yields plateau, spreads hold <3.5% — NIM expansion flows through, supports P/B. Bear: yields extend higher and/or HY spreads widen through 4%, compressing tangible book and lifting provisions ahead of Q3 earnings (Oct 16).

Tilt: toward bear, moderate — the 3-month real-yield rise (+61bp) is large and P/B offers no safety margin against further book-value erosion.
