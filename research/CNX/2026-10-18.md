# CNX Resources Corporation (CNX) — Core initiation — 2026-10-18
## 1. Business
CNX is an Appalachian Basin natural gas exploration & production company (Marcellus/Utica shale in southwestern Pennsylvania, plus coalbed methane), with owned in-basin gathering/midstream infrastructure, NGL marketing, an active commodity hedging program, and monetization of environmental attributes (e.g. 45Z credits) alongside core gas production [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/cnx/).

## 2. Quality
Returns have improved markedly: ROIC TTM 14.5% and ROCE TTM 14.9%, up from near-zero/negative in FY2021-22 and FY2024 (ratios page) [Certain]. FCF margin TTM is 24.6%, rising for five years (+6.5pp) [Certain], and net debt/EBITDA has fallen to 1.3x from 72.8x in FY2021 [Certain]. Piotroski F-score is 8/9 [Certain]. As a commodity producer CNX has no pricing power over gas itself; its edge is cost position — low-cost Appalachian acreage and owned midstream that lowers transport cost — plus an active hedge book (214.3 Bcf notional hedged in H1 2026) that smooths realized prices but produced a $166m cash-settlement loss YTD vs $136m a year earlier as hedges lag the move in spot [company-reported] (https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1070412/000107041226000058/cnx-20260630.htm) [Certain]. Altman Z-score of 1.71 sits in the model's distress zone (statistics page) [Certain] — a caveat, though the metric is less reliable for asset-heavy commodity producers than for industrials [Likely].

## 3. Valuation
At 35.91 USD, CNX trades at 5.7x P/E, 4.2x EV/EBITDA, 10.1x P/FCF and 1.1x P/B — 26-54% below the median of EQT/RRC/AR/CTRA on every multiple, with a 9.9% FCF yield [Certain]. EV/EBITDA sits near the bottom (1st percentile) of its own 5-year range, though that range is distorted by near-zero EBITDA in FY2021-22 (EV/EBITDA spiked to 173x) [Certain]. The base-case fair-value model (EV/EBITDA, inform-only) implies 76.14 USD, +112% [Certain], but the bear case is negative, which reflects the sensitivity of a reverse EV/EBITDA output to the gas-price assumption rather than a going-concern signal [Likely]. The discount looks consistent with the market pricing a persistent US gas glut into forward multiples (Reuters, 2026-05-01, https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/while-asia-europe-scramble-natural-gas-us-glut-has-nowhere-go-2026-05-01/) [Likely], a discount that looks too wide against CNX's now-moderate leverage and improving returns [Likely].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) rises above 3.0x
- FCF margin (income statement/cash-flow pages) falls below 10% for two consecutive quarters
- ROIC (ratios page) falls below 8% for two consecutive quarters
- Sales volumes (10-Q MD&A) decline more than guided for two consecutive quarters beyond normal depletion

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
FCF conversion (FCF/NI) TTM is 55.4%, improving from negative in FY2022/FY2024 [Certain]. H1 2026 net income was $551m (diluted EPS $3.56) vs $235m (EPS $1.37) in H1 2025, roughly 2.3x higher [company-reported] (https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1070412/000107041226000058/cnx-20260630.htm) [Certain]. Management reaffirmed a disciplined capital-allocation approach on the Q2 2026 call, prioritizing buybacks while keeping capex on guidance (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/cnx/transcripts/658067-q2-2026/) [Likely]. Shares outstanding fell sharply YoY per the statistics page, consistent with an active buyback, though the reported magnitude (-99.9%) looks like a data artifact and should not be relied on [Guessing].

## 6. Unusual items
New CFO Ravi Srivastava appointed Sept 21, 2026 (https://www.tipranks.com/news/the-fly/cnx-resources-names-ravi-srivastava-as-new-cfo-thefly-news) [Certain] — a management-change watch item; no stated reason to doubt continuity [Guessing]. Short interest is elevated at 11.69% of float and rose 13.5% in the past month (statistics page) [Certain], consistent with bearish positioning ahead of the Oct 29 Q3 print; monitor, not a fundamental trigger. No material change to Risk Factors in the latest 10-Q vs the year-ago comparable filing (https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1070412/000107041226000058/cnx-20260630.htm) [Certain].
