# Crocs, Inc. (CROX) — Core initiation — 2026-11-15
## 1. Business
Designs and sells casual footwear through two brands: Crocs (clogs, sandals, Jibbitz) and HEYDUDE (slip-ons), via wholesale, DTC and marketplaces. For nine months 2026, Crocs Brand was $2,640.6M and HEYDUDE $502.9M of $3,143.5M revenue (about 84% / 16%); Crocs Brand International was $1,397.5M, about 44% of the total [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1334036/000133403626000060/crox-20260930.htm). No single foreign country is disclosed, so none is flagged [Certain].

## 2. Quality
Returns are high but eroding. TTM ROIC is 23.6% vs 31.9% a year earlier; operating margin is 21.1% vs 23.1%; FCF margin is 18.4% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/crox/financials/ratios/?p=trailing). FY2025 ROIC of -32.2% reflects the HEYDUDE impairment, a non-cash charge [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/crox/financials/ratios/). The moat is brand and an iconic product, not structural: fashion-cycle brands can fade, and HEYDUDE had to be written down [Likely]. Q3 gross margin of 66.2% was lifted by 630bp of IEEPA tariff refunds, which are non-recurring [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1334036/000133403626000060/crox-20260930.htm). Underlying Q3 revenue growth was modest: Crocs Brand +4.4%, HEYDUDE +6.1% [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1334036/000133403626000060/crox-20260930.htm). Net debt/EBITDA is 1.58x and Piotroski F is 7 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/crox/statistics/). Good, cash-generative business, with margin direction a concern [Likely].

## 3. Valuation
At 163.72 the stock is at a 52-week high, 44.1% above its 200-day average, with a 12-month gain of 119.9% and RSI 73.0 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/crox/history/). P/E of 12.3x sits at 98% of its 5y range (6.7x–12.4x), though 31% below the peer median. P/FCF of 9.9x is 53% below peers and FCF yield is 10.07% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/crox/statistics/). Base fair value is 173.74 (+6.1%), with bear 115.83 and bull 304.32; the reverse DCF implies 1.6% year-1 revenue growth. The market has already re-rated the shares on the Q3 beat and raised guidance [Certain]. Upside to base is thin, but the price still assumes little growth, so a sustained HEYDUDE recovery would give room [Guessing]. One-off refunds flatter trailing earnings, so P/E is likely understated [Likely].

## 4. What would prove this wrong

- Operating margin (income statement, TTM) falls below 18%.
- Crocs Brand revenue (10-Q segment note) declines year on year for two consecutive quarters.
- Net debt/EBITDA (statistics page) rises above 2.5x.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Aggressive buybacks: 4.4 million shares for $500.6M year to date, with $1.7bn authorised [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1334036/000133403626000060/crox-20260930.htm). Shares are down 10.71% year on year, and TTM FCF/net income is 113.5% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/crox/statistics/). Debt is $1,313.0M face value, with $113.0M drawn on a revolver due November 2027 [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1334036/000133403626000060/crox-20260930.htm). Repurchases at a re-rated price lower the return per dollar [Likely].
