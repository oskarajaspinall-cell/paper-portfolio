# SK hynix Inc. (KRX:000660) — Core initiation — 2026-10-07
## 1. Business
SK hynix designs, manufactures and sells memory semiconductors: DRAM (server, mobile, PC, graphics, consumer), NAND flash/SSDs and MCP products, plus a smaller non-memory foundry business, serving servers, networking, mobile, PC, consumer and automotive end markets [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/krx/000660/). It is described as a leading supplier of high-bandwidth memory (HBM) for AI accelerators, with disclosed supply ties to major AI chip makers including Nvidia [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/krx/000660/).

## 2. Quality
Profitability swings hard, not steadily: net margin moved from 22.3% (FY21) to -27.8% (FY23) to 85.6% (TTM); ROIC from 13.4% to -9.7% to 61.6% [Certain] (fact sheet, RA/IS). Memory pricing has no durable floor — SK hynix, Samsung and Micron are price-takers when supply balances, and margins turn to losses in a glut (FY23) [Likely]. Today's extreme profitability (operating margin 68% TTM vs 15–35% pre-AI years) reflects an industry-wide DRAM/HBM shortage tied to AI capex, not a moat [Likely]. HBM carries real barriers — qualification cycles and yield favor incumbents, giving SK hynix a technology lead with Nvidia [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/krx/000660/) — but history shows rivals catch up and price clears near marginal cost once supply catches demand. The balance sheet is a genuine strength now: net debt/EBITDA is -0.47x (net cash) versus 4.48x at the FY23 trough, room to invest or absorb the next downturn [Certain] (fact sheet, BS/IS/RA). Net: cyclically excellent, not all-weather.

## 3. Valuation
TTM P/E of 7.8x sits at the 10th percentile of its 5-year range (6.1x–23.1x) and 45% below the peer median (14.1x, n=3: MU, Samsung Electronics, Nanya); EV/EBITDA (8.8x) is similarly low — 27th percentile, 14% below peers [Certain] (fact sheet). But EV/Sales (6.6x) and P/B (4.9x) sit ABOVE their own 5-year maximums (166%/141% of range), with P/B only 12% below the peer median [Certain] (fact sheet). Cheap earnings multiples beside rich sales/book multiples is a classic cyclical-peak signature: the market is pricing TTM earnings (built on an unsustainable 68% operating margin) near the top, so a low P/E on peak margins can still be a full price on normalized earnings [Likely]. Price action agrees: the stock is +348% over 12 months and still +5.3%/+24.5% above its 50/200-day averages, yet it fell 24.3% in the last 3 months, suggesting the market has begun discounting peak-cycle risk [Certain] (fact sheet, HI/ST).

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Gross margin (income statement page) falls below 40% for two consecutive quarters — signals the AI/HBM pricing premium is eroding back toward the FY22–23 commodity pattern.
- ROIC (ratios page) falls below 15% — loses the cushion above the cost of capital that distinguishes this upcycle from the FY22–23 trough (4.9%/-9.7%).
- Net debt/EBITDA (balance sheet/ratios page) rises back above 1.0x — a return to the leverage build-up (4.48x at the FY23 trough) that destroyed quality last cycle.
- FCF margin (cash flow/income statement page) turns negative — repeats the FY22–23 pattern (-9.5%/-12.4%) of capex outrunning cash generation in an oversupplied market.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
FCF margin is 48.5% TTM, up from 26.6% (FY25) and negative in FY22–23, showing strong operating leverage into the current upcycle [Certain] (fact sheet, CF/IS). FCF conversion (FCF/NI) is 56.6% TTM, down from 70.0% (FY24) and 60.2% (FY25) — net income is outrunning cash generation, consistent with heavy capex to expand HBM capacity [Likely] (fact sheet, CF/IS). Net debt/EBITDA of -0.47x (net cash) is a full swing from 4.48x at the FY23 trough, leaving room to keep investing or return cash through the next downturn [Certain] (fact sheet, BS/IS/RA). Shares outstanding are roughly flat (+0.14% YoY) — no meaningful dilution [Certain] (fact sheet, ST).

## 6. Unusual items
SK hynix disclosed to the SEC on 2026-09-18 that it is "reviewing various measures, including establishing additional production bases," in response to media speculation about a new Japan facility, but stated "no matters have been determined" and committed to further disclosure within three months [Certain] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2120882/000119312526394998/d140793d6k.htm). Beta (5y) is high at 2.39, so the position carries outsized exposure to global risk-off moves and the semiconductor/AI-capex cycle beyond the company-specific thesis [Certain] (fact sheet, ST).
