# SK hynix Inc. (KRX:000660) — Core initiation — 2026-10-07
## 1. Business
SK hynix designs and manufactures memory semiconductors — DRAM (server, mobile, PC, graphics) and NAND flash/SSDs — sold to server, mobile, PC, consumer and automotive customers worldwide, plus a smaller non-memory foundry business [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/krx/000660/). Within DRAM it supplies high-bandwidth memory (HBM) to Nvidia for AI accelerators; stockanalysis.com describes it as "Nvidia's primary HBM supplier" under "a huge supply agreement" [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/krx/000660/).

## 2. Quality
Returns have swung from -9.7% ROIC in FY2023 (memory trough: gross margin -1.6%, net margin -27.8%) to 61.6% ROIC TTM as the AI-driven HBM/DRAM upcycle lifted gross margin to 76.3% and operating margin to 68.0% [Certain] (source: fact sheet ratios/income statement). The balance sheet flipped to net cash (net debt/EBITDA -0.47x, from 4.48x at the FY2023 trough), funding capex through the next downturn [Certain]. The moat is real but narrow: HBM needs multi-year Nvidia qualification and heavy capex, keeping SK hynix and Samsung as the only credentialed suppliers at scale [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/krx/000660/). It remains a commodity-cycle business, though — FCF conversion (FCF/NI) fell from 76.1% (FY2021) to 56.6% TTM even as margins rose, and FY2023 shows how fast returns reverse when memory pricing turns [Certain] (source: fact sheet cash flow/income statement). Rivals cannot easily dislodge SK hynix from HBM near-term, but have taken share in commodity DRAM/NAND before [Likely].

## 3. Valuation
On earnings-based multiples the stock looks inexpensive: P/E 7.8x sits at the 10th percentile of its own 5-year range and 45% below the peer (Samsung, Micron, TSMC) median of 14.1x; EV/EBITDA 8.8x is 14% below peers; P/FCF 14.1x is 30% below peers [Certain] (source: fact sheet valuation table). But EV/Sales (6.6x) and P/B (4.9x) are both above their own 5-year highs (166% and 141% of the 5y max respectively), even though still 20-42% below peer medians [Certain]. Read together, the price assumes today's unusually high margins and ROE persist — cheap on earnings, but paying the richest-ever multiple of sales and book value for this business [Likely]. The peer discount on every earnings multiple implies the market still prices SK hynix's HBM leadership with a bigger mean-reversion risk than Samsung or Micron, or simply has not re-rated it as fast [Likely].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Gross margin (income statement page) falls below 40% for two consecutive quarters, signalling the HBM/DRAM pricing cycle has turned down toward the FY2023 trough.
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) rises back above 1.0x, showing capex or inventory build has outrun internal cash generation.
- ROIC (ratios page) falls below its FY2021 "normal-cycle" level of 13.4%, showing current upcycle returns are collapsing below the prior normal rather than merely mean-reverting.
- FCF conversion, FCF/NI (cash flow and income statement pages), falls and holds below 50%, indicating reported earnings are increasingly non-cash.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Shares outstanding are essentially flat (+0.14% YoY) — no meaningful dilution [Certain] (source: fact sheet statistics page). FCF margin is 48.5% TTM and FCF conversion (FCF/NI) is 56.6% TTM, down from 76.1% in FY2021, as a larger share of profit is reinvested in HBM/DRAM capacity capex during the upcycle [Certain] (source: fact sheet cash flow/income statement). The swing to net cash (-0.47x net debt/EBITDA) shows capex is comfortably funded internally [Certain]. Buyback/dividend policy detail is [data unavailable] on the fact sheet beyond share count.

## 6. Unusual items
Beta of 2.39 is high — this is a cyclical, high-beta position that will swing hard versus the portfolio benchmark [Certain] (source: fact sheet statistics page).
