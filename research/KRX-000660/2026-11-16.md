## 2. Quality
Profitability is at a cyclical extreme: TTM ROIC 77.0%, operating margin 74.5%, gross margin 77.4%, versus ROIC of -9.7% and operating margin of -23.6% in FY2023 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/krx/000660/financials/ratios/). [Certain] Memory is a commodity cycle with three main producers; the FY2021-FY2023 swing from 13.4% ROIC to negative shows returns are not durable through the cycle. [Certain] The moat is a temporary technology lead in high-bandwidth memory plus scale economics. [Likely] Balance sheet is net cash (net debt/EBITDA -0.46x). FCF margin is 46.2% TTM, 2.2pp below its 2-year high of 48.5%. FCF conversion is only 65.8% of net income, falling over 5 years (-15.9pp): heavy capex needs. [Certain] Shares outstanding fell 40.58% YoY per the statistics page, which looks like a data or share-class artefact [Guessing] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/krx/000660/statistics/).
