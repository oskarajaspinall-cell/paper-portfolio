# Boston Scientific Corporation (BSX) — Core initiation — 2026-10-09

## 1. Business
Medical device maker selling cardiovascular devices (incl. WATCHMAN left-atrial-appendage closure, interventional cardiology, electrophysiology ablation), urology, endoscopy and neuromodulation products to hospitals and physicians in ~127 countries, mainly via direct sales [Certain] (source: https://www.bostonscientific.com/en-US/about-us.html). FY2025 net sales ~$20bn; ~$2bn/yr R&D funds a steady cadence of new product launches that drive recurring device replacement [Certain] (source: https://www.bostonscientific.com/en-US/about-us.html).

## 2. Quality
Returns and margins have improved steadily: ROIC 7.4%→11.2% (FY2021→TTM), ROCE 6.5%→11.1%, operating margin 15.3%→20.5%, FCF margin 11.1%→17.3% [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/bsx/financials/ratios/, https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/bsx/financials/income-statement/). Durable edge: regulatory barriers, physician switching costs and R&D scale support recurring use in WATCHMAN and EP ablation [Likely]. But management flagged at the Sept 10 2026 Wells Fargo conference that "WATCHMAN faces market headwinds and new competition, while EP growth moderates," a genuine moat question [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/bsx/transcripts/730622-wells-fargo-21st-annual-healthcare-conference/). Separately, an Aug 25 cyberattack disrupted manufacturing/order processing globally; per the 8-K, BSX is "unlikely to meet" Q3/FY2026 sales-growth and EPS guidance, though operations had "substantially recovered" by the Sept 8 filing and management sees no long-term financial impact [Certain] (source: SEC 8-K, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/885725/000088572526000059/bsx-20260907.htm). Net debt/EBITDA 2.12x TTM, falling over 5 years; Altman Z 4.80 (safe), Piotroski F 7 (strong) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/bsx/financials/ratios/, https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/bsx/statistics/).

## 3. Valuation
P/E 16.8x sits 59% below its own 5y range minimum (48.8x) and 44% below the peer median (30.3x; MDT/SYK/EW/ABT); EV/EBITDA 12.6x is 23% below peer median 16.5x; P/FCF 16.6x is 25% below peer median 22.2x [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/bsx/financials/ratios/ and peer statistics pages). A multiple roughly halved from its 5y norm, against improving margins and returns, suggests the market is pricing a structural de-rating — i.e. that WATCHMAN competition and EP-growth moderation persist — not just the cyberattack [Likely]. The fact sheet's DCF/P/E blend puts fair value at $58.24 base (+38.5% vs last close $42.04), $31.04 bear (-26.2%) and $113.49 bull (+170%); reverse DCF implies the price assumes only 3.8% year-1 revenue growth fading to terminal [Certain] (source: fact sheet fair value section, research/BSX/factsheet-2026-10-09.md). At 16-17x earnings/FCF for a business still compounding ROIC, risk/reward looks skewed higher if the disruption proves transitory, with WATCHMAN/EP commentary the real swing factor [Likely].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Operating margin (income statement page) falls and stays below 17% (the FY2023 level), signalling costs from the cyberattack don't normalize
- ROIC (ratios page) reverses back below 8% (the FY2022 level), undoing the 5-year improving trend
- Net sales growth (income statement page) turns negative for two consecutive quarters, confirming WATCHMAN/EP share loss rather than a one-off disruption
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) rises above 2.6x (the FY2022 level), signalling balance-sheet strain from the incident

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
FCF margin 17.3% TTM, up from 11.1% in FY2021; FCF conversion (FCF/NI) 98.7% TTM versus a 5y average above 125%, likely reflecting cyberattack-related working-capital/timing distortions this year rather than a structural decline [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/bsx/financials/cash-flow-statement/, income statement page). Shares outstanding roughly flat (-0.05% YoY), implying no material buyback or dilution [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/bsx/statistics/). Net debt/EBITDA improved from 2.61x (FY2021) to 1.87x (FY2025) before ticking up to 2.12x TTM, still comfortably within the 5y range [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/bsx/financials/ratios/).

## 6. Unusual items
Short interest rose 68.4% month-over-month to 3.22% of float, consistent with the market pricing in the cyberattack/guidance miss [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/bsx/statistics/). An Oct 7 2026 COO succession (effective Jan 1 2027) is routine and not thesis-relevant [Likely]. The Aug 25 cyberattack (Item 1.05 8-K) is the main swing factor and is already addressed in Quality and the invalidation triggers above.
