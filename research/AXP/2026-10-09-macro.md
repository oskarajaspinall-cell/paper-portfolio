# Macro overlay: American Express Company (AXP) — 2026-10-09
## Assessment
AXP is a closed-loop card network *and* lender (receivables on its own balance sheet), so two macro variables matter: real yields (discount rate / multiple) and credit spreads (consumer-credit risk-appetite proxy driving charge-off provisioning). Chain: `real yields -> cost of equity -> equity multiple` and `credit spreads -> risk appetite -> consumer debt-service stress -> card loan-loss provisions`.

Facts [Certain]: the 10y real yield (DFII10) is 2.92% (2026-10-07), up 0.49pp/1m, 0.61pp/3m; the 10y nominal yield (DGS10) is 5.28%, up 0.72pp/3m — the same risk-free rate the fact sheet's CAPM/fair-value table already uses (2026-10-08). The HY spread (BAMLH0A0HYM2) is 3.09%, up ~0.4pp/1m and /3m, still low versus stressed historical levels (https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=BAMLH0A0HYM2, 2026-10-07). Fed funds (DFF) rose 0.25pp/1m to 3.88%.

Market pricing (proxy) [Likely]: 2y yield (4.77%) above fed funds (3.88%) implies the market still prices further tightening/higher-for-longer, not cuts — consistent with AXP's -21.7pp 12-month underperformance vs SPY already discounting a higher rate **(inference)**.

Bull: real yields plateau/fall, spreads stay contained -> multiple re-rates toward the 5y-range midpoint, fair value toward $344.57. Bear: real yields keep rising and HY spreads widen further -> higher cost of equity plus rising cardholder debt-service stress compress the multiple and lift provisions, fair value toward $245.75. Tilt: **small**, toward bear — both signals point the same way but are market-wide, not AXP-specific, spreads remain off a stressed base, and AXP's variable-APR book partly self-hedges funding-cost rises **(inference)**.
