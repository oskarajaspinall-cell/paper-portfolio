# Macro overlay: NVIDIA Corporation (NVDA) — 2026-10-09
## Assessment
NVDA is high-beta (2.22) [Certain] and long-duration: the reverse DCF shows the $230.48 close already prices 54.4% year-1 revenue growth fading to terminal growth over a decade [Certain] (fact sheet). Two chains matter: (1) real yields → CAPM discount rate → multiple, amplified by beta; (2) credit spreads/financing conditions → customers' ability to debt-fund GPUs → NVDA revenue, highlighted by SpaceX reportedly in talks to borrow $40bn to buy NVDA chips [Likely, headline] (Bloomberg, fact sheet news, 2026-10-08).

Facts: 10y real yield (DFII10) 2.92% (2026-10-07), +49bp ~1m / +61bp ~3m [Certain] (https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=DFII10). Fed funds 3.88%, +25bp ~1m [Certain] (...?id=DFF, 2026-10-07). HY spread 3.09%, +39bp ~3m but still tight; VIX 15.08, near 3m lows [Certain] (FRED, 2026-10-07). Pricing proxy: 2y yield (4.77%) sits ~89bp above fed funds, implying no near-term cuts priced **(inference)**. Real yields have risen sharply, a mechanical headwind to NVDA's discount rate, yet the stock sits near its 52-week high — AI-capex news flow is currently outweighing that drag **(inference)**.

**Bull**: real yields stabilize/ease, credit stays cheap → debt-financed capex continues, multiple holds. **Bear**: real yields/HY spreads keep rising → discount rate rises further and debt-financed chip purchases get harder, hitting multiple and demand with little cushion given how much growth is priced. **Tilt**: toward bear, moderate — the real-yield move is large/recent against a near-peak-priced stock, though tight spreads and low VIX cushion it for now.
