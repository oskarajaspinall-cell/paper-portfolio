# NVIDIA Corporation (NVDA) — Core initiation — 2026-10-09

## 1. Business
NVIDIA designs GPUs and accelerated-computing platforms. The Data Center segment (AI training/inference chips, Mellanox-based networking, CUDA software) is sold mainly to hyperscalers, cloud providers and enterprises and now drives the bulk of revenue and growth; Gaming (GeForce), Professional Visualization and Automotive are smaller segments [Certain] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1045810/000104581026000021/nvda-20260125.htm).

## 2. Quality
TTM ROIC 92.0% and ROCE 71.3%, both up sharply over five years (+80.1pp and +49.5pp) [Certain] (fact sheet RA). Gross margin 74.7%, operating margin 65.2%, both rising [Certain] (fact sheet IS). The moat is CUDA's software lock-in (7.5m+ developers, per the 10-K) plus the integrated GPU+networking stack from Mellanox, raising switching costs for large training workloads [Certain] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1045810/000104581026000021/nvda-20260125.htm). Two risks temper durability: hyperscaler customers are building competing in-house silicon, and the 10-K discloses revenue concentrated in "a limited number of partners and distributors" [Certain] (same source); export controls have left NVIDIA "effectively foreclosed" from China's data-center market and caused a $4.5bn H20 inventory charge in FY2026 [Certain] (same source). FCF conversion (FCF/NI) is falling (-2.9pp over 5y, TTM 65.8%) as capex scales with the build-out [Likely] (fact sheet CF,IS). Balance sheet is net-cash (net debt/EBITDA -0.12x) [Certain] (fact sheet BS,IS).

## 3. Valuation
P/E 30.0x sits below NVIDIA's own 5y range (38.0x-114.7x) and 23% below peer median 38.9x; EV/EBITDA 27.5x is roughly in line with peers (+3%) but below its own 5y minimum of 33.9x; P/FCF 43.8x is 13% below peer median [Certain] (fact sheet RA, peers AMD/AVGO/TSM/QCOM). P/B is a standout at 25.0x vs peer median 12.6x (+99%), reflecting the elevated ROE. The site's own-history fair value puts base case at $81.20 and bull case at $202.05, both below the $230.48 last close (-64.8% and -12.3%) [Certain] (fact sheet fair-value table). The reverse DCF implies the price assumes 54.4% revenue growth in year one fading to terminal growth by year ten [Certain] (fact sheet) — a bar few chipmakers sustain for a decade, sitting on top of the concentration and China-foreclosure risks above. The multiple-vs-peer discount looks more than offset by the growth already priced in.

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROIC (ratios page) falls below 50%, more than halving from the TTM 92.0%.
- Gross margin (income statement page) falls below 65%, reversing the multi-year expansion from pricing power.
- Net debt/EBITDA (balance sheet/ratios page) turns net-debt-positive, signalling capex outrunning cash generation.
- FCF conversion, FCF/NI (cash flow/income statement page) falls below 50%, extending its current declining trend materially.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
TTM FCF margin is 41.9%, still high but down from the FY2025 peak of 46.6% [Certain] (fact sheet CF,IS). FCF conversion (FCF/NI) has fallen each of the last two years to 65.8% TTM as data-center capex and inventory build scale with demand [Likely] (fact sheet CF,IS). The balance sheet remains net-cash (net debt/EBITDA -0.12x) [Certain] (fact sheet BS,IS), and shares outstanding fell 1.04% YoY, consistent with net buybacks [Certain] (fact sheet ST). No dividend metric is shown on the fact sheet [data unavailable].

## 6. Unusual items
Export-control exposure is material and current: the 10-K discloses NVIDIA is "effectively foreclosed from competing in China's data center computing/compute market" and took a $4.5bn charge in FY2026 tied to H20 restrictions [Certain] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1045810/000104581026000021/nvda-20260125.htm). Revenue is concentrated in a limited number of large partners/distributors per the same filing [Certain]. Beta is high at 2.22 and short interest is low at 1.27% of float, down 1.4% vs the prior month, with institutions holding 70.8% [Certain] (fact sheet ST). Recent headlines (customer capex commitments from SpaceX/OpenAI-linked spending, and an Nvidia-backed IPO being shelved) are not new financial facts about NVDA itself and don't change the above [Likely] (fact sheet news section).
