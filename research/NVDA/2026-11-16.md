## 1. Business
Sells accelerated-computing platforms (GPUs, networking, systems, software) mainly for AI data centers; Blackwell is the majority of system shipments and Vera Rubin began production shipments [Certain] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1045810/000104581026000075/nvda-20260726.htm). Compute & Networking was 91.8% of quarterly revenue [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1045810/000104581026000075/nvda-20260726.htm). ## 2. Quality
Exceptional economics [Certain]: TTM ROIC 92.0%, operating margin 65.2%, gross margin 74.7%, net cash (net debt/EBITDA -0.12x) (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/nvda/financials/ratios/). Moat is the CUDA software and systems ecosystem plus annual architecture cadence [Likely].

Direction change, addressed: ROIC fell from 175.0% to 92.0% and ROCE from 82.4% to 71.3% over 12 months, while operating margin rose 7.1pp. Margins are not eroding; the fall is a larger capital base (equity investments, commitments) [Likely]. FCF margin slipped to 41.9% and FCF conversion to 65.8% from 80.5% (FY2026), consistent with the filing's extended customer payment terms [Likely].

New risk language: financial guarantees, customer financing, and an obligation to buy unsold AI-cloud capacity. Supply and capacity commitments rose to $279 billion from $119 billion in one quarter [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1045810/000104581026000075/nvda-20260726.htm). Revenue now partly depends on customers' access to capital [Likely].

## 3. Valuation
P/E 20.1x is below its 5y minimum of 38.0x and 53% under the peer median of 42.6x; EV/EBITDA 19.4x also sits below its 5y range and 16% under peers; FCF yield 3.23% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/nvda/statistics/). The market is pricing a peak-earnings risk [Likely]. The reverse DCF implies 40.4% year-1 revenue growth, fading to terminal.

Mechanical fair value is base 86.02 vs a close of 162.85 (-47.2%); bull 208.52 (+28.0%). The through-cycle DCF treats current margins as above-cycle [Likely], so the price already sits well above this model's base [Certain]. The cheap multiples and the model disagree: the market treats earnings as durable, the model does not. Price is -23.6% vs the 50-day and -20.2% vs the 200-day average, RSI 23.6, 3-month momentum -27.7%, 24.7pp behind SPY over 3 months.

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Operating margin (income statement, TTM) falls below 55%.
- Gross margin (income statement, TTM) falls below 68%.
- FCF conversion (FCF/NI, cash-flow statement, TTM) falls below 55%, i.e. cash tied up in customer financing and commitments.
- Net debt / EBITDA (ratios page) turns above +0.5x, meaning guarantees or purchase commitments are being funded by debt.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
