# NVIDIA Corporation (NVDA) — Core initiation — 2026-10-11

## 1. Business
NVIDIA designs GPUs and data-center AI accelerators (its largest segment) sold mainly to hyperscalers and AI labs, plus gaming and professional-visualization GPUs and networking (InfiniBand/NVLink) [Certain] (source: https://www.nvidia.com). It outsources manufacturing to foundries (e.g., TSMC) and monetizes via hardware plus the CUDA software stack that locks developers in [Certain] (source: https://www.nvidia.com).

## 2. Quality
Returns are exceptional and still rising: TTM ROIC 92.0% (5y +80.1pp), ROCE 71.3% (+49.5pp), gross margin 74.7% (+6.1pp), operating margin 65.2% (+23.1pp) [Certain] (fact sheet: RA, IS). The moat is CUDA's software lock-in plus a full-stack systems advantage (chip+network+software) that is hard for a pure silicon competitor to replicate [Likely] (source: https://www.nvidia.com). Two risks can erode this: customer concentration (recent SEC disclosure shows a small number of large customers account for a disproportionate share of receivables) [Likely] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1045810/000104581026000075/nvda-20260726.htm), and large customers (hyperscalers, OpenAI) building or switching to custom silicon (Google TPU, Amazon Trainium, AMD, Broadcom-designed ASICs), which is the central competitive threat to the current returns [Likely] (source: fact sheet news item on OpenAI revenue disclosure, https://www.tipranks.com/news/nvidia-stock-nvda-struggles-after-openai-revenue-disclosure). FCF conversion (FCF/NI) is drifting down, 90.8% in FY2024 to 65.8% TTM (5y -2.9pp), consistent with heavier capex/working-capital intensity as the data-center buildout scales [Certain] (fact sheet: CF, IS).

## 3. Valuation
NVDA's multiples sit below its own 5-year range: P/E 29.0x vs 5y min 38.0x; EV/EBITDA 27.4x vs 5y min 33.9x; P/FCF 43.6x vs 5y min 47.2x [Certain] (fact sheet: RA). Vs the peer group (AMD, AVGO, TSM, QCOM), P/E is 24% below peer median and P/FCF 14% below, while EV/EBITDA is roughly in line (+2%) and P/B is 94% above peer median, reflecting its asset-light, high-ROE model [Certain] (fact sheet: RA). The fact sheet's own fair-value model (through-cycle DCF blended 67/33 with EV/EBITDA) puts bear/base/bull at $30.90/$85.79/$208.22 — even the bull case sits 9.2% below the last close of $229.28 [Certain] (fact sheet: valuation). The reverse DCF implies the price requires ~50.9% revenue growth in year 1, fading to terminal growth by year 10 [Certain] (fact sheet: valuation) — i.e., the market is pricing continuation of the current AI-capex supercycle for years, not simply cheapness vs history [Likely].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Gross margin (income statement page) falls below 65% for two consecutive quarters — pricing power eroding from competition.
- ROIC (ratios page) falls below 50% — returns being competed away by custom silicon or price competition.
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) turns above 1.0x — organic cash generation no longer funding growth/buybacks.
- FCF margin (cash flow + income statement) falls below 30% — capex outrunning cash generation from the buildout.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
FCF margin is still strong at 41.9% TTM though down from a 46.6% FY2025 peak, and FCF conversion has fallen to 65.8% TTM from 90.8% in FY2024 (5y trend -2.9pp), reflecting rising capex/inventory intensity [Certain] (fact sheet: CF, IS). The balance sheet stays net-cash (net debt/EBITDA -0.12x TTM) though the net-cash cushion has shrunk from -0.43x in FY2024 (5y trend +0.45x) [Certain] (fact sheet: BS, IS). Shares outstanding fell 1.04% YoY, a modest buyback offset to dilution; no core dividend [Certain] (fact sheet: ST).

## 6. Unusual items
Customer concentration: a small number of large customers account for a disproportionate share of receivables per the latest 10-Q [Likely] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1045810/000104581026000075/nvda-20260726.htm). Export-control/legal exposure: a contractor linked to Super Micro pleaded guilty to illegally diverting Nvidia AI chips to China; Nvidia itself is not charged, but this underscores ongoing enforcement risk around China-bound AI chip supply chains [Likely] (source: https://www.reuters.com/legal/government/super-micro-contractor-pleads-guilty-scheme-divert-ai-servers-with-nvidia-chips-2026-10-09/ — fetch blocked, cited as listed in fact sheet). Unconfirmed reports that Nvidia may invest in or acquire AI startup Reflection AI (~$25bn) would be a capital-allocation shift if it materializes [Guessing] (source: https://www.tipranks.com/news/nvidia-nvda-could-buy-25-billion-ai-startup-reflection-ai). Short interest remains low (1.34% of float) despite a 5.8% month-on-month rise — not currently a red flag [Certain] (fact sheet: ST).
