# NVIDIA Corporation (NVDA) — Core initiation — 2026-11-15
## 1. Business
NVIDIA sells AI accelerators, networking and full data-center systems (Blackwell, now Vera Rubin) plus gaming/graphics GPUs. Compute & Networking was $88,299m of $96,221m quarterly revenue to 26 Jul 2026 [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1045810/000104581026000075/nvda-20260726.htm) [Certain]. China revenue share: [data unavailable]; the 10-Q says PRC authorities restricted H20 sales and the company could not sell its H20 inventory [Certain] (source: same filing). No single foreign country confirmed at ~10%+.

## 2. Quality
Exceptional economics: TTM ROIC 92.0%, operating margin 65.2%, gross margin 74.7%, net debt/EBITDA -0.12x (net cash) (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/nvda/financials/ratios/) [Certain]. CUDA software and system-level integration are a durable moat [Likely].

Direction: ROIC fell from 175.0% to 92.0% over 12 months and FCF margin from 43.6% to 41.9%, while operating margin rose 7.1pp (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/nvda/financials/ratios/?p=trailing) [Certain]. Falling ROIC with rising margin means the capital base is growing faster than profit (cash and investments built up) [Likely]. FCF conversion fell to 65.8% from 80.5% [Certain]; the filing says extended customer payment terms will keep affecting operating-cash-flow timing [Certain].

Key-person event: the CEO/co-founder resigned 12 Nov 2026 with immediate effect and the board retained outside counsel to investigate (source: https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news) [Certain]. Reports of the allegations' strength are disputed and two investors have asked the board to leave Huang's return open [Likely] (source: https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/14/nvidia-investors-jensen-huang-return-board-letter.html).

## 3. Valuation
P/E 20.6x is below the 5y minimum 38.0x and 42% below peers (35.6x); EV/EBITDA 19.4x vs 5y range 33.9x–70.2x; FCF yield 3.23% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/nvda/statistics/) [Certain]. P/B 17.2x is 62% above peers [Certain].

Mechanical fair value base 86.01 vs close 162.85 (-47.2%); bull 208.51 (+28.0%). The through-cycle DCF treats TTM margins as peak [Likely]. The reverse DCF implies 40.4% year-1 revenue growth, fading to terminal [Certain]. Price is -24.1% vs the 50-day and -20.2% vs the 200-day average, RSI 24.9, 12-month change -16.0%: heavily sold off into earnings on 17 Nov [Certain]. The market is pricing a durable AI build-out slowdown or a leadership/governance discount; the multiple already assumes sharp earnings normalisation [Likely].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Operating margin (TTM, income statement page) falls below 55%.
- Revenue growth YoY (income statement page) turns negative for two consecutive quarters.
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) rises above 0.5x, i.e. net cash gives way to leverage as commitments and guarantees are drawn.
- FCF conversion (FCF/NI, cash flow page) stays below 55% on a TTM basis for two quarters.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Shares outstanding fell 1.04% YoY (buybacks outpace dilution) [Certain]. The filing delta shows a sharp change in risk posture: supply and capacity commitments rose to $279 billion from $119 billion a quarter earlier; equity investments $99 billion with $25 billion further committed; guarantees to SB Energy for OpenAI-hosting leases capped at $105 billion [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1045810/000104581026000075/nvda-20260726.htm) [Certain]. This is vendor financing of customers: circular-demand risk [Likely].

## 6. Unusual items
- CEO resignation and board investigation (above); successor not named, search reportedly open [Likely] (source: https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/14/nvidia-investors-jensen-huang-return-board-letter.html). Allegations of chip-smuggling routes to China are press reporting only [Guessing] (source: https://www.wsj.com/tech/jensen-huang-backdoor-ai-data-centers-a5e4499d); export-control and regulatory exposure could change.
- New 10-Q risk factors: counterparty failure, AI-cloud capacity buy-back obligations, customer financial distress, power/permitting delays [Certain].
- Beta 2.25; short interest 1.21% of float, low [Certain]. Earnings 17 Nov 2026 [Certain].
