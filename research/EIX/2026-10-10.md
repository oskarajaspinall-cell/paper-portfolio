# Edison International (EIX) — Core initiation — 2026-10-10

## 1. Business
EIX is the holding company for Southern California Edison (SCE), a regulated electric transmission/distribution utility serving ~15m people across central, coastal and Southern California (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/eix/). Revenue is earned on a CPUC-authorized rate base: capex into grid hardening and wildfire mitigation is rolled into rates, so earnings growth tracks approved rate-base growth, not volume [Certain] (source: https://www.edison.com/investors).

## 2. Quality
SCE holds an exclusive, CPUC-regulated transmission/distribution franchise — a durable, legally-protected monopoly competitors cannot contest [Certain]. ROCE has risen steadily to 7.1% TTM from 4.8% FY2021, and net debt/EBITDA has improved to 4.88x from a 6.04x FY2022 peak (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/eix/financials/ratios/) [Certain]. However, net margin jumped to 19.3% TTM/23.1% FY2025 from 7.3% FY2024 and ROE to 19.7% TTM from 7.2% — a step-change the fact sheet cannot explain; it is [Guessing] this reflects one-off wildfire-cost recoveries or regulatory true-ups rather than a durable re-rate, so near-term P/E-based valuation should be treated cautiously. The structural risk to the franchise is California's inverse-condemnation doctrine, which has historically made SCE strictly liable for wildfire losses from its equipment regardless of negligence; this is the main thing that can impair returns that competitors can't replicate away [Certain].

## 3. Valuation
EIX trades at 7.2x EV/EBITDA and 1.2x P/B — both below their own 5-year lows (8.0x and 1.2x floor) and 40%/27% below peer medians (PCG, SRE, AEP, DUK) (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/eix/financials/ratios/, statistics pages). These metrics are less distorted by the net-income anomaly noted above, so the discount looks genuine, not just a P/E artifact [Likely]. P/E of 5.7x (vs 5y median 24.1x, peer median 19.4x) is overstated-cheap given the margin jump flagged in Quality [Guessing]. Base fair value (dividend-discount-weighted 67%, P/E 33%) is $70.31, +27% vs the $55.30 close; bear case $38.22 (-31%) assumes litigation outcomes worsen materially; bull $152.66 (source: research/EIX/2026-10-10-valuation.md inputs). The market is pricing meaningful probability of a severe Eaton-fire liability outcome, well beyond the reaffirmed 2026 core-EPS guidance management gave on the Q2 call [Likely].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) rises back above ~6.0x on a sustained (two-quarter) basis, signalling wildfire-claim costs outpacing rate-base recovery.
- The dividend (edison.com dividend history page) is cut or frozen for two consecutive declarations, reversing the current raise trend — a sign the board no longer sees cash flow as adequate.
- FCF margin (cash-flow/income-statement pages) reverses its improving trend (currently -2.0% TTM, up from -3.9% FY2024) and widens materially, rather than continuing toward breakeven.
- A CPUC rate-case decision materially disallows SCE's wildfire-mitigation capex from rate base (text-only trigger: tracked via CPUC dockets/filings at next review).

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
FCF margin is still negative (-3.7% FY2025, -2.0% TTM) but has improved every year since -36.9% FY2021, as wildfire-mitigation capex is increasingly offset by rate-base cost recovery [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/eix/financials/cash-flow-statement/). The quarterly dividend was raised to $0.8775/share effective January 2026, continuing an annual growth pattern through the litigation overhang (source: https://www.edison.com/investors/stock-information/dividend-history) [Certain]. Shares outstanding are roughly flat YoY (-0.26%), so the dividend is not currently being funded by heavy dilution; given negative FCF it is funded by a mix of debt and regulatory cost-recovery mechanisms typical of a capex-heavy regulated utility [Likely].

## 6. Unusual items
A California judge issued a tentative ruling (Aug 2026) denying a bid to hold SCE automatically (inverse-condemnation) liable for Eaton-fire property losses — a [Likely] positive but not-yet-final de-risking event (source: https://www.reuters.com/legal/litigation/sce-wins-tentative-ruling-eaton-wildfire-liability-case-bloomberg-news-reports-2026-08-11/, as listed in the fact sheet; article itself could not be fetched — allowlist/availability). Separately, California lawmakers killed a wildfire-liability-protection bill in September 2026, leaving AB 1054's wildfire fund and case law as the main backstops, with no new legislative shield [Certain] (source: https://www.barrons.com/articles/pge-stock-edison-international-california-wildfire-bill-4016fab7, as listed in the fact sheet). Short interest rose 17.4% month-over-month to 4.05% of float, consistent with an active litigation overhang [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/eix/statistics/).
