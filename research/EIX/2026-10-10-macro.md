# Macro overlay: Edison International (EIX) — 2026-10-10
## Assessment
Chain: real/Treasury yields → cost of equity/debt in EIX's own DDM fair value and refinancing of a levered (~4.9x net debt/EBITDA), FCF-negative balance sheet → fair value, dividend coverage [Likely] **(inference)**.

Official data (FRED, 2026-10-08/09): real yield (DFII10) 2.87%, +56bp/3m; 10y Treasury (DGS10) 5.22%, +68bp/3m — the same print the fact sheet uses as its risk-free input; fed funds (DFF) 3.88%, +26bp/3m; HY spread (BAMLH0A0HYM2) 3.15%, +45bp/3m; breakevens (T10YIE) flat at 2.33% (+9bp/3m). Market pricing: 2y yield (4.75%) sits ~87bp above fed funds, a proxy implying the market still expects some further tightening, not cuts [Likely] **(inference)**. Flat breakevens alongside rising real yields point to a term-premium/real-rate move, not an inflation scare [Likely] **(inference)**.

This is already captured mechanically in future valuation runs (higher DGS10 lowers the DDM fair value), but the pace (+56bp real yield, +45bp HY spread in 3 months) is large versus EIX's low beta (0.61) and negative FCF, raising refinancing cost on debt-funded capex just as the Eaton-fire litigation overhang separately de-risks (Reuters, 2026-08-11). **Bull**: rates/spreads ease → lower discount rate, fair value drifts toward/above the $70 base case. **Bear**: rates/spreads keep rising → fair value compresses further, refinancing cost pressures negative FCF and dividend coverage. **Tilt**: toward bear, moderate — the rate/spread move works against the stock's own valuation model and leverage even though the dominant bull case (litigation, rate-base recovery) is fundamentals-driven, not macro.
