# Macro overlay: ADT Inc. (ADT) — 2026-10-18
## Assessment
ADT carries $7.4bn long-term debt against net debt/EBITDA of 2.84x (FY2025) and an Altman Z-score of 0.65 — distress zone [Certain] (fact sheet). Two variables transmit directly: (1) policy rate & high-yield credit spreads → refinancing cost on that debt and the equity-risk-premium a distress-zone issuer pays → discount rate / multiple; (2) policy rate → household debt-service burden → non-payment disconnects → attrition (already 13.1% gross in Q2 2026 [company-reported]) → revenue.

Facts (FRED, 2026-10-15): effective fed funds is 3.88%, +0.25pp over both 1m and 3m — a recent hike, not a cut. The US high-yield spread is 3.23%, +0.52pp over 3m — credit conditions tightening [Certain]. The 10y Treasury is 5.10%, +0.53pp over 3m, matching the risk-free rate the fact sheet's own DCF already uses, so today's base case has no incremental surprise baked in [Certain]. Market pricing (proxy, inference): the 2y yield (4.77%) sits 0.89pp above fed funds (3.88%), implying the market expects further tightening ahead [Likely] **(inference)** — a headwind the deleveraging story hasn't yet had to absorb.

Bull: spreads narrow / hikes pause → cheaper refinancing, Z-score stabilizes, re-rating toward peers (31-84% below median multiples today).
Bear: spreads keep widening + further hikes → refinancing cost rises, non-payment attrition accelerates → earnings and discount rate deteriorate together, compounding the existing distress-zone flag.

Tilt: toward bear, moderate — rate and credit direction both point the same way as the balance-sheet risk already flagged in the note, though the stock's extreme discount to peers already prices much of this [Likely].
