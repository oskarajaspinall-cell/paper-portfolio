# Emirates Telecommunications Group (ADX:ETISALAT) — Core initiation — 2026-10-31
## 1. Business
Telecom operator (e&): subscription mobile, fixed and enterprise connectivity revenue, from the UAE home market plus international operations. [Likely] Recurring, usage-based cash flows with heavy network capex; the home market is a regulated, concentrated market. Segment mix is not on stockanalysis.com and was not fetched [data unavailable]. (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/adx/ETISALAT/)

## 2. Quality
- Margins are high for a telco: operating margin 29.6% TTM, up from 24.0% in FY2021; FCF margin 22.9% TTM (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/adx/ETISALAT/financials/income-statement/). [Certain]
- Returns: ROCE 18.5% TTM (rising from 15.2%), but ROIC fell from 19.3% (FY2021) to 13.5% (FY2025) before recovering to 16.2% TTM (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/adx/ETISALAT/financials/ratios/). [Certain]
- Durability: [Likely] licensing, spectrum and scale in a concentrated home market keep competitors from competing the returns away. This is inferred from the margins, not from a primary source.
- Leverage is moderate: net debt/EBITDA 0.67x TTM, versus 1.46x in FY2024. Altman Z is 1.61 and Piotroski F is 5 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/adx/ETISALAT/statistics/). [Certain]
- Beta is 0.34, so the stock is defensive. [Certain]

## 3. Valuation
At 20.94 AED the stock trades below its five-year range on P/E (9.2x versus a 11.1x minimum and 16.6x median), EV/EBITDA (6.1x versus 9.0x median) and EV/Sales. P/FCF is 10.2x and FCF yield is 9.80%. [Certain] Against peers (du, STC, Vodafone, Mobily) it is at a 37% discount on P/E, 19% on EV/EBITDA and 32% on P/FCF. P/B is a 19% premium, which fits its higher ROE. [Certain] The mechanical fair value (own-history DCF and EV/EBITDA blend) is a base of 33.35 AED, with the bear case at 22.96 AED above the price. The reverse DCF implies revenue shrinking 4.4% in year 1. [Likely] The price assumes decline for a business whose margins have been expanding. TTM net margin (25.3%) is well above FY2025 (19.7%), so part of the low P/E may reflect a non-recurring gain [Guessing]; the cash-flow multiples are the cleaner read. (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/adx/ETISALAT/statistics/)
