# Open Text Corporation (OTEX) — Core initiation — 2026-11-08
## 1. Business
Enterprise information-management software sold on subscription, cloud and maintenance: content management, business network, IT operations management, cybersecurity and analytics, deployable on-premise, private, public or sovereign cloud (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1002638/000100263826000098/otex-20260930.htm). Growth historically came through acquisitions; the company is now divesting non-core assets. Revenue by country/region: [data unavailable] in the sources read; no single foreign market is confirmed at ~10%+ here. [Likely]

## 2. Quality
Margins are high and improving: gross margin 77.2% TTM, operating margin 23.0% TTM versus 20.5% a year earlier (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/otex/financials/income-statement/?p=trailing). [Certain]
Returns on capital are mediocre: ROIC 9.9% TTM, flat over the last year (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/otex/financials/ratios/?p=trailing). Net debt/EBITDA is 3.10x and Altman Z is 1.35, so the balance sheet is a constraint. [Certain]
FCF margin is 16.3% TTM, down from 17.5% a year earlier, and the 5y trend is falling (-10.0pp). [Certain]
The moat is switching cost: embedded records and compliance workloads in regulated clients. [Likely] But the latest 10-Q shows total revenue down 4.0% (down 1.7% after adjusting for divestitures), with Cybersecurity, ITOM, ADM and Analytics declining while Content and Business Network grew (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1002638/000100263826000098/otex-20260930.htm) [company-reported]. The core is not yet clearly growing as a whole. [Likely]

## 3. Valuation
The price is 24.85 USD, 34.6% below a year ago. Every multiple sits at the bottom of its own 5y range: P/E 9.4x (range position 2%), EV/EBITDA 6.8x (3%), P/FCF 7.0x (3%), EV/Sales 2.1x (4%). Versus the peer median (CVLT, PEGA, DT, BOX) it is 84% below on EV/EBITDA and 87% below on P/E; those peers are higher-growth, so the gap is partly deserved. [Likely]
FCF yield is 14.34%. The reverse DCF says the price implies -13.4% revenue growth in year 1, fading to terminal growth. That is harsher than the reported decline. [Likely]
Model fair value is 68.50 USD base (bear 42.63), inform-only. Even the bear case is above the price, but the DCF rests on free cash flow whose margin has been falling. [Guessing]

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Operating margin (income statement, TTM) falls below 20%.
- FCF margin (cash flow statement, TTM) falls below 13%.
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) rises above 3.7x.
- Total revenue growth in two consecutive quarterly 10-Qs is below -4% after divestiture adjustments, or the company withdraws its full-year outlook.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Shares outstanding are down 5.26% year on year, so buybacks are real. [Certain] Divestitures of eDOCS and Vertica were used to prepay debt, and in October 2026 the company refinanced its 2027 notes with new secured notes due 2031 and 2033 at higher coupons (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1002638/000100263826000098/otex-20260930.htm) [company-reported]. Cash conversion is strong but declining: FCF/NI 129.6% TTM, from 223.8% in FY2022. Shareholder returns compete with deleveraging. [Likely]

## 6. Unusual items
- Refinancing at higher coupons raises interest cost. [Certain]
- Short interest is 3.84% of float, not a signal. Institutions own 95.7%.
- A multi-year plan is due in February; the full-year outlook was maintained with growth investment weighted to the second half (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/otex/transcripts/726164-q1-2027/). [Likely]
- No earnings-type price reaction in the last 10 sessions.
