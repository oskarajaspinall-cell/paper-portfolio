# Open Text Corporation (OTEX) — Core initiation — 2026-11-09
## 1. Business
Enterprise information-management software (content, business network, IT operations, cybersecurity, analytics), sold as cloud subscriptions, licences and maintenance, built largely by acquisition. [Certain] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1002638/000100263826000098/otex-20260930.htm). The 10-Q reports Americas, EMEA and Asia Pacific only; no single foreign country is disclosed, so no ~10%+ country exposure can be named. [Likely] (same source)

## 2. Quality
Returns are middling, not moat-grade: ROIC 9.9% TTM, ROCE 11.3%, though gross margin 77.2% and operating margin 23.0% are high and rising (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/otex/financials/ratios/). Switching costs in embedded document/records systems are plausible protection [Likely], but revenue is shrinking: Q1 revenue fell 4.0% reported, with Cybersecurity, ITOM and Analytics declining [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1002638/000100263826000098/otex-20260930.htm). FCF margin fell to 16.3% TTM from 17.5% a year earlier (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/otex/financials/cash-flow-statement/?p=trailing). Net debt/EBITDA 3.10x and Altman Z 1.35 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/otex/statistics/). Verdict: cash-generative but ex-growth and levered. [Likely]

## 3. Valuation
The price sits at the bottom of its own 5y range on every multiple: P/E 9.4x (2% position), EV/EBITDA 6.8x (3%), P/FCF 7.0x (3%), against peer medians of 21.8x, 16.6x and 22.0x (n=5; ADBE, AKAM, GEN, PTC, TYL). FCF yield is 14.34%. The reverse DCF implies revenue shrinking 13.4% in year 1 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/otex/). Mechanical fair value (base 68.50 vs 24.85) is flagged unreliable: methods disagree 2.3x and base is beyond +100%; weigh lightly. The market is pricing structural decline from AI disruption and leverage; the discount to peers is partly deserved given shrinking revenue and weaker returns. [Likely]

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Operating margin (income statement, TTM) falls below 20%.
- FCF margin (cash-flow statement, TTM) falls below 13%.
- Net debt/EBITDA (statistics page) rises above 3.75x.
- Company-reported Core revenue growth turns negative in constant currency, or FY27 outlook is cut (quarterly release).

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Q1 free cash flow was $138.5m vs $101.2m a year earlier [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1002638/000100263826000098/otex-20260930.htm). FCF/NI is 129.6% TTM; shares are down 5.26% YoY. The company paid a dividend of $0.28 a share and repurchased $23.4m of stock in the quarter [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1002638/000100263826000098/otex-20260930.htm). Disposal proceeds went to debt paydown, and new secured notes refinanced the 2027 maturities at higher coupons. [Certain]

## 6. Unusual items
Serial divestitures distort reported revenue comparisons. FY27 outlook unchanged; multi-year plan due February (headline: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/otex/transcripts/726164-q1-2027/) [Likely]. No risk-factor changes in the 10-Q. Short interest 3.84% of float; 95.7% institutional.
