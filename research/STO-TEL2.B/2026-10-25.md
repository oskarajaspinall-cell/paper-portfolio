# Tele2 AB (STO:TEL2.B) — Core initiation — 2026-10-25
## 1. Business
Nordic/Baltic mobile and fixed-broadband operator (Sweden and the Baltics), earning recurring subscription revenue from consumers and businesses. Revenue growth is low single digit; value comes from cost discipline, network leadership and cash generation. [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sto/TEL2.B/)

## 2. Quality
Returns are rising: ROIC 7.3% in FY2021 to 11.2% in FY2025, operating margin 18.2% to 24.1%, gross margin 43.6% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sto/TEL2.B/financials/ratios/). [Certain] TTM ROIC 22.6%, operating margin 41.5% and ROE 45.1% jump far above the FY trend; this likely reflects the one-off Baltic Tower transaction gain, so it is not repeatable. [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sto/TEL2.B/financials/income-statement/). FCF margin 30.8% TTM; net debt/EBITDA down from 3.11x to 1.45x. [Certain] The Q3 call reported steady organic growth, record equity-free cash flow and narrowed full-year guidance (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sto/TEL2.B/transcripts/536178-q3-2026/). [Likely] Moat is spectrum and network scale in concentrated markets, but growth is inflation-like and competition persistent. [Guessing] Beta 0.34, defensive. Good, not exceptional.

## 3. Valuation
P/E 10.9x vs 5y median 19.5x and peers 17.4x; EV/EBITDA 7.3x, below its 5y minimum of 8.4x and peers 9.0x. But the P/E is flattered by the one-off gain in TTM earnings, while EV/Sales (4.5x, top of range) and P/B (4.6x, 90th percentile) are high. P/FCF 11.9x is 88% of its 5y range, near peers 12.6x; FCF yield 8.40%. [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sto/TEL2.B/statistics/). Fair value base 160.32 SEK vs close 159.85 SEK (+0.3%); bear 91.34, bull 206.42. The price implies about -0.4% year-1 revenue growth, a modest bar, but there is no margin of safety to base value. [Certain]

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Gross margin (income statement page) falls below 41% for a fiscal year.
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) rises back above 2.5x.
- FCF margin (cash flow/income statement pages) falls below 22% for a fiscal year.
- Organic service revenue turns negative for two consecutive quarters (quarterly report).

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
FCF conversion was 188.8% of net income in FY2025 (heavy non-cash charges) and 91.2% TTM as the gain inflates net income. Leverage fell after the Baltic Tower deal; shares change YoY only 0.11%, so no dilution. The Q4 call cited a sharply higher dividend and Q3 guided capex-to-sales lower. [Likely] (sources: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sto/TEL2.B/transcripts/392498-q4-2025/, https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sto/TEL2.B/transcripts/536178-q3-2026/)

## 6. Unusual items
Freya Investissement, a large shareholder, holds more votes than economic ownership (dual-class) and announced it would raise its economic stake toward its vote share: a control and overhang factor. [Certain] (source: https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/02/06/3234043/0/en/Freya-to-align-its-economic-ownership-in-Tele2-up-to-its-27-share-of-votes.html). Short interest [data unavailable]. Price is 8.5% below its 200-day, 6-month relative strength -22.6pp vs SPY (not a thesis input).
