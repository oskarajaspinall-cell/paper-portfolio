# Macro overlay: Geely Automobile Holdings (HKG:0175) — 2026-10-18
## Assessment
Geely's thesis is a valuation gap (P/E 8.4x vs 14.2x peer) and an improving-returns story, not a macro call, but two chains can move the re-rating path. (1) Rates: HKD's dollar peg held (DEXHKUS 7.8476, +0.01/3m, 2026-10-09) [Certain], so HK/China equity discount rates track US policy. The 10y UST rose to 5.10% (+0.53pp/3m, 2026-10-15) and the 2y to 4.77% (+0.61pp/3m), 0.89pp above effective fed funds (3.88%) — a proxy, not a forecast, for a market still pricing further tightening or term premium **(inference)**. Higher US/HK yields raise the discount rate Geely's own DCF fair value uses (risk-free 5.10%, factsheet), compressing its already-flagged-extreme model upside. (2) FX: the yuan strengthened (DEXCHUS 6.692, -0.085/3m) while the broad dollar jumped (+3.72/1m, DTWEXBGS) — a firmer CNY raises relative export prices as Geely pushes into Canada, the UK and Argentina (factsheet news) [Likely] **(inference)**. High-yield spreads widened (3.23%, +0.52pp/3m) while VIX stayed low (16.46) — mild, not acute, risk-appetite deterioration. Bull: Fed pause/cuts lower US/HK yields and a weaker CNY aid exports, supporting re-rating toward peer multiples. Bear: yields and the dollar keep rising and CNY stays firm, keeping the discount rate elevated and eroding export pricing, stalling re-rating despite the cheap multiple. Tilt: moderate, toward bear — the 3-month trend in yields, the dollar and credit spreads runs against the re-rating catalyst, though the valuation gap is fundamental.
