# Geely Automobile Holdings (HKG:0175) — Core initiation — 2026-10-18

## 1. Business
Designs, manufactures and sells passenger vehicles (ICE, hybrid and NEV) in China and export markets under a multi-brand portfolio, plus auto financing and parts/services [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/0175/). Revenue is overwhelmingly vehicle sales; net margin was 4.9% in FY2025 and 4.5% TTM (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/0175/financials/income-statement/) [Certain].

## 2. Quality
Returns have improved sharply: ROIC rose from 3.6% (FY2021) to 24.3% TTM, ROCE from 2.5% to 13.5%, ROE from 6.5% to 17.4% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/0175/financials/ratios/) [Certain]. Margins stay thin but are rising — gross 17.3% TTM, operating 4.1% TTM vs 1.8%/0.7% in FY2021 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/0175/financials/income-statement/) [Certain] — against a genuine industry headwind: China passenger-car retail sales fell 28.0% YoY in September per a WSJ headline (https://www.wsj.com/business/autos/chinas-auto-sales-fall-despite-traditional-peak-season-bfbe9b1a) [Likely]. FCF conversion is unusually high (283.7% TTM) and the balance sheet carries net cash (net debt/EBITDA -2.65x, source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/0175/financials/ratios/) [Certain], a buffer rivals without captive financing lack. The moat is modest, not durable: auto manufacturing has low switching costs and China NEV competition on price and fast-charging technology is intense, per a Reuters headline on Geely vs. BYD charging tech (https://www.reuters.com/world/asia-pacific/geely-turns-4-minute-ev-charging-ai-battle-with-byd-2026-09-23/, not fetched — allowlisted domain blocked the request) [Guessing]. Scale and brand breadth, not a structural cost or network advantage, are the main defenses.

## 3. Valuation
P/E of 8.4x sits at the floor of its own 5-year range (8.4x-36.8x) and 41% below the peer median of 14.2x (BYD, Great Wall, GAC, Dongfeng) (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/0175/financials/ratios/) [Certain]. EV/EBITDA (3.9x vs 5.8x peer, -33%) and P/FCF (3.0x vs 3.6x peer, -17%) tell the same story; only P/B (1.5x vs 0.8x peer, +77%) looks rich, though it is mid-range of Geely's own 5y P/B band (0.9x-2.5x) [Certain]. The market is pricing continued margin/ROIC give-back from the China price war, not the returns improvement already delivered. The model fair value (bear 41.46 / base 67.90 / bull 105.89 HKD vs the 15.01 close) implies +176%/+352%/+606% upside [Certain] — extreme, likely inflated by the low 5y beta (0.48) and the high trailing FCF conversion; treat with caution, not as the thesis. A peer re-rating (to 5.8x EV/EBITDA) implies a smaller, still material upside case [Likely].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Gross margin (income statement page) falls below 14.1% (the FY2022 trough) for two consecutive quarters — price-war erosion beyond the worst of the last cycle.
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) turns positive (net debt) — the balance-sheet buffer funding R&D/capex through the price war is gone.
- ROIC (ratios page) falls back below 10.5% (the FY2024 level) — the returns improvement proves cyclical/subsidy-driven rather than structural.
- Operating margin (income statement page) falls below 2.5% (FY2024 level) for two consecutive quarters — scale gains from the NEV mix shift reverse.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Net cash (net debt/EBITDA -2.65x) and FCF conversion of 283.7% TTM (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/0175/financials/cash-flow-statement/) show strong self-funding of capex and R&D [Certain]. Shares outstanding rose 5.57% YoY (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/0175/statistics/) [Certain], modest dilution more consistent with incentive comp than value-destructive issuance. Headlines describe further external deal-making (a reported stake in Ford's Spanish unit; a stake in NIO's battery-swap business) — see Unusual items for why these are not used as a capital-allocation input here.

## 6. Unusual items
Several headlines attribute deals to "Geely" or "Geely Holding Group" rather than explicitly to this listed entity (HKG:0175/Geely Automobile Holdings): the approved 34% stake in Ford España (https://www.reuters.com/business/retail-consumer/china-approves-geely-acquisition-ford-espaa-2026-10-12/) and the reported 30% stake in NIO's battery-swap unit, the latter sourced to "Geely Holding Group," the unlisted controlling parent (https://www.tipranks.com/news/why-nio-stock-is-rising-in-pre-market-today-sept-28). Reuters fetches to confirm the entity were blocked, so these are flagged [Guessing] and excluded from the thesis rather than assumed to sit on this company's balance sheet. Ownership is concentrated: institutions hold only 20.5% and insiders 2.62% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/0175/statistics/) [Certain], consistent with majority control sitting with the unlisted Geely Holding Group — standard for this structure, but it raises related-party-transaction risk given the overlapping deal flow above. Altman Z-score of 2.25 sits in the model's traditional "grey zone" despite net cash of -2.65x net debt/EBITDA (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/0175/statistics/) [Certain] — the asset-based Z-score likely understates this net-cash manufacturer's resilience and is not treated as a red flag.
