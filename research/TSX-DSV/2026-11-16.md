## 2. Quality
Returns inflected from deep losses to strong profits: ROIC 131.4% in FY2025 and 90.0% TTM, against -25.2% in FY2024 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tsx/DSV/financials/ratios/). [Certain] Operating margin is 41.1% TTM, up 7.9pp on the year (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tsx/DSV/financials/income-statement/?p=trailing). Direction caveats: ROIC is 19.9pp below its 2-year high of 110.0%, and operating margin is 3.4pp below its high of 44.4%. Both are small slips from a high base. FCF margin fell from 26.9% to 9.5% in twelve months and FCF/NI is 46.3% TTM, consistent with growth capex ahead of Cordero and Timmins [Likely]. Balance sheet is net cash (net debt/EBITDA -0.62x) (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tsx/DSV/statistics/). Moat: gold has no pricing power and ROIC this high mostly reflects the gold price [Likely]. The durable edge is asset scale and permitted pits [Guessing]. Shares outstanding rose 36.27% YoY, so growth has been funded in part by dilution.
