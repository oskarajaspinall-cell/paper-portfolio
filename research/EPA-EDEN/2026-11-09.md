# Edenred SE (EPA:EDEN) — Core initiation — 2026-11-09
## 1. Business
Edenred earns a take-rate on prepaid employee-benefit and fuel/fleet payment volumes: meal vouchers and cards, mobility and complementary solutions. 2025 operating revenue was 65% employee benefits, 26% mobility, 9% complementary solutions [company-reported] (source: https://www.edenred.com/fr/investisseurs-actionnaires/chiffres-et-indicateurs-cles). By zone: Europe 60%, Latin America 30%, rest of world 10% [company-reported] (same URL). Latin America (a Brazil-led emerging region) exceeds 10%, so the macro overlay should check it. [Certain]

## 2. Quality
High-return, asset-light network business: ROIC TTM 18.5%, ROCE 27.2%, gross margin 42.1%, FCF conversion 210.2% of net income (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/epa/EDEN/financials/ratios/). Moat is a two-sided network (employers/clients plus merchants) and regulatory-embedded products. [Likely] Net debt/EBITDA 1.65x is moderate. [Certain]
Weakness: operating margin fell from 34.3% to 29.6% over 12 months and ROCE from 28.6% to 27.2%, though FCF margin rose to 38.2% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/epa/EDEN/financials/income-statement/?p=trailing). Q3 call commentary points to meal-regulation changes in Italy and Brazil as headwinds, with guidance reaffirmed (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/epa/EDEN/transcripts/654778-q3-2026-tu/). [Likely] The margin drop looks regulatory, not competitive, but is unproven. [Guessing]

## 3. Valuation
The price assumes decline: reverse DCF implies -2.9% year-1 revenue growth. P/E 11.9x sits at 8% of its 5y range (median 32.3x); EV/EBITDA 6.8x at 3% of range, 26% below peers' 9.1x; P/FCF 5.4x, 67% below peers; FCF yield 18.51% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/epa/EDEN/statistics/). Mechanical base fair value 38.59 EUR vs 24.60 close (+56.9%); bear 21.15 (-14.0%). [Likely] Fair value rests on FCF that may include working-capital float benefits, so treat it with caution. [Guessing] The stock is -10.9% versus its 50-day average, 3-month momentum -14.9%, after Q3. Regulatory pressure is the main reason for the de-rating. [Likely]

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Operating margin (income statement, TTM) falls below 27%.
- ROIC (ratios page, TTM) falls below 15%.
- FCF conversion (FCF/net income, TTM) falls below 120%.
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) rises above 2.5x.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Cash conversion is strong (FCF margin 38.2% TTM, 210.2% of net income). Shares outstanding fell 0.97% YoY, so buybacks modestly offset dilution. [Certain] Management raised the dividend 10% at the 2026 AGM per the transcript summary (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/epa/EDEN/transcripts/651311-agm-2026/). [Likely] Leverage moved up from 1.13x at FY2025 to 1.65x TTM, worth watching. [Certain]

## 6. Unusual items
Regulatory caps on meal-voucher fees in Italy and Brazil are the key specific risk [Likely]. Altman Z of 0.27 is low but typical of a prepaid-float business with large client-fund liabilities [Guessing]. Next earnings: Mar 2, 2027. Short interest [data unavailable].
