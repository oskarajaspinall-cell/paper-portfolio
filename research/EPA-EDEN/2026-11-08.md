# Edenred SE (EPA:EDEN) — Core initiation — 2026-11-08
## 1. Business
Edenred runs prepaid employee-benefit (meal, food) and fleet/mobility payment platforms, plus corporate payments. It earns fees on face value issued, merchant commissions, and interest on client float held before merchants are paid. [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/epa/EDEN/)

## 2. Quality
Re-check result: the 2026-10-25 "quality is rising" call rested on fiscal-year endpoints and does not survive the trailing data. Operating margin is 29.6% vs 34.3% a year earlier (-4.6pp), ROCE 27.2% vs 28.6%, and ROIC 18.5% vs 19.1% (-0.6pp, flat), all below 2025-09-30 peaks (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/epa/EDEN/financials/ratios/?p=trailing and /income-statement/?p=trailing). [Certain] Management calls 2026 a "rebasing year": Italy and Brazil meal-voucher rule changes, lower float income in Brazil and spending on data/AI weigh on earnings, and FY2026 EBITDA is guided down like-for-like (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/epa/EDEN/transcripts/654778-q3-2026-tu/). [Likely] The margin fall is therefore regulatory and guided, not a surprise, yet it shows the franchise is not immune to rule changes. Returns remain high (ROIC 18.5%) and the network moat is intact. [Likely] FCF margin of 38.2% (+14.6pp YoY) is flattered by float working capital and should not be read as improving quality. [Likely] Reading: stable-high, not rising. [Likely]

## 3. Valuation
P/E 11.9x vs 5y median 32.3x (8th percentile of its range); EV/EBITDA 6.8x vs 19.6x median; P/FCF 5.4x; FCF yield 18.51% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/epa/EDEN/statistics/). [Certain] Versus peers P/E is 42% lower and EV/EBITDA 16% lower. Base fair value 38.59 EUR vs close 24.60 (+56.9%); bear 21.15 (-14.0%). The reverse DCF implies -2.9% revenue growth in year 1 fading to terminal growth, harsher than management's 2027–2028 growth ambitions, which are unproven after a rebasing year. [Likely] The base case extrapolates recent FCF; if float-driven FCF normalises, base value is overstated. [Guessing] Price is 10.9% below the 50-day average with 3-month momentum -14.9%: the market is still marking the derating down.

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Operating margin (income statement page, TTM) falls below 26%.
- ROIC (ratios page, TTM) falls below 14%, or ROCE (ratios page) below 22%.
- FCF margin (cash flow/income statement pages, TTM) falls below 28%.
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) rises above 2.5x.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Shares change is -0.97% YoY, so buybacks modestly offset dilution; a dividend increase was announced at the AGM (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/epa/EDEN/transcripts/651311-agm-2026/). [Likely] Net debt/EBITDA 1.65x vs 1.13x at FY2025: leverage has risen as EBITDA is guided lower. [Certain] FCF conversion of 210.2% of net income is float-assisted and reverses if volumes shrink. [Likely]

## 6. Unusual items
Meal-voucher regulation in Italy and Brazil is the specific risk; an Italian antitrust case was flagged on the Q1 call with no quantified impact (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/epa/EDEN/transcripts/403241-q1-2026-tu/). [Likely] Q3 results reaffirmed FY2026 EBITDA guidance (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/epa/EDEN/transcripts/654778-q3-2026-tu/). [Likely] Piotroski F is 5; Altman Z 0.27 is not meaningful for a float-holding payments firm. Short interest [data unavailable]. Institutions 58.3%, insiders 8.31%.

Conviction view for the evaluator: conviction 4 rests on valuation and a guided, regulatory margin reset, not on rising quality. It holds only if the evaluation drops "rising" and accepts a rebasing-year thesis; otherwise conviction 3. [Likely]
