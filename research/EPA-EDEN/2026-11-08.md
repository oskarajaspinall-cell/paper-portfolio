# Edenred SE (EPA:EDEN) — Core initiation — 2026-11-08
## 1. Business
Edenred issues prepaid meal, food and benefit vouchers and cards, earning a fee on each transaction plus interest on client float. Its Mobility arm (fleet fuel and payments) is the growth engine. Italy and Brazil are material, regulation-exposed markets. Brazil's exact revenue share is [data unavailable]: no primary source was reachable, and the Q3 call did not give it (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/epa/EDEN/transcripts/654778-q3-2026-tu/). [Likely]

## 2. Quality
Returns are high and the cash conversion is strong. TTM ROIC is 18.5%, ROCE 27.2% and FCF margin 38.2%, with FCF at 210.2% of net income (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/epa/EDEN/financials/ratios/). [Certain]

Direction change, operating margin: it fell from 34.3% to 29.6% over the last 12 months, against a 5-year trend that reads flat. Our read is that this is regulation, not competition. The Q3 call blamed meal-regulation effects in Italy and Brazil and strong Mobility growth, and that is consistent with a fee-cap hit. [Likely] ROCE fell from 28.6% to 27.2% and sits 4.6pp below its 2-year high of 31.8%. It is still far above FY2021–23 levels (20.3%, 22.5%, 15.2%). [Certain]

Management guided 2026 EBITDA down (like-for-like), guided 8%–12% growth for 2027–28 and reaffirmed guidance at Q3 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/epa/EDEN/transcripts/654778-q3-2026-tu/). [Likely] The moat is network scale on both sides (merchants and clients) and regulated-voucher incumbency. Regulation is what is eroding it. [Likely]

## 3. Valuation
At 24.60 EUR the stock trades at P/E 11.9x (5y median 32.3x), EV/EBITDA 6.8x (19.6x) and P/FCF 5.4x (14.7x). Each sits in the bottom 3–8% of its 5-year range. FCF yield is 18.51%. Against peers it is 42% below on P/E and 67% below on P/FCF, but 110% above on EV/Sales (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/epa/EDEN/statistics/). [Certain]

Base fair value is 38.59 EUR (+56.9%) and bear is 21.15 EUR (-14.0%). The reverse DCF implies revenue shrinking 2.9% in year 1. [Likely] The price assumes the fee cap and loss of interoperability permanently cut earnings, a bear case already near the 200-day average (23.39). Net debt/EBITDA of 1.65x leaves no balance-sheet stress. The price is down 14.9% over 3 months and 10.9% below its 50-day average, which suggests the market is still repricing the Brazil decree. [Guessing]

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Operating margin (TTM, income statement page) falls below 26%.
- ROCE (TTM, ratios page) falls below 23%.
- FCF margin (TTM, cash flow and income statement pages) falls below 30%, or FCF/NI below 120%.
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) rises above 2.5x.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Cash conversion is exceptional (FCF/NI 210.2%; FCF margin 38.2%, up 14.6pp over 12 months). The share count is falling 0.97% a year, so buybacks are modest. A 10% dividend increase was announced at the AGM (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/epa/EDEN/transcripts/651311-agm-2026/). [Likely] Leverage of 1.65x is up from 1.13x at FY2025, which is worth watching if it comes from buybacks or M&A rather than seasonal working capital. [Guessing] The 2026 FCF conversion guide is lower than history; management attributes this to regulation, which we cannot verify. [Guessing]

## 6. Unusual items
- **Brazil country risk:** The Q3 call said the CFO sees the real moving "very, very fast" around the election. The decree has "not a lot of detail", interoperability is unclear and Open Loop adoption is slower than hoped (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/epa/EDEN/transcripts/654778-q3-2026-tu/). [Likely] Lower Brazilian rates should also reduce float income in Q4. The macro overlay should size the currency and Selic effect. A weaker real translates directly into lower EUR earnings. [Likely]
- **Italy antitrust:** Management expects a remedy outcome on Italian regulation in about two months. This is a dated catalyst, in either direction. [Likely]
- **Altman Z** is 0.27, but this is a structural quirk of a float-funded business, so we do not read it as distress. [Guessing]

- **Conviction:** the Direction-change lines are explained by regulation, the valuation is at the bottom of its 5-year range, and the Brazil risk is real but cheap to the price. Our view is that conviction 4 holds, not 5, because the decree details and the Italy outcome are unresolved. [Guessing]
