**Bull:** Selic stays high and BRL stabilises, so float income and translation stop being a drag.
**Bear:** BRL slides further and Selic cuts accelerate: roughly 1-2pp off revenue growth, plus margin pressure.
**Tilt:** toward bear, small; regulation matters more.
