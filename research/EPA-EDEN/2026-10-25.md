# Edenred SE (EPA:EDEN) — Core initiation — 2026-10-25
## 1. Business
Edenred runs prepaid employee-benefit (meal, food) and fleet/mobility payment platforms, plus corporate payments. It earns a fee on face value issued, merchant commissions, and interest on client float held before merchants are paid. [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/epa/EDEN/)

## 2. Quality
Returns are high and recovering: ROIC 18.5% TTM vs 10.0% in FY2023, ROCE 27.2%, operating margin 29.6%, FCF conversion 210.2% of net income (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/epa/EDEN/financials/ratios/). [Certain] The moat is a two-sided network (employers/fleets on one side, merchants on the other) plus regulated voucher schemes that are costly to replicate. [Likely] The cost is regulatory dependence: meal-voucher rule changes in Italy and Brazil hit Q3 growth, while Mobility offset them; FY2026 EBITDA guidance was reaffirmed and 2027–2028 growth targets were set (headline: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/epa/EDEN/transcripts/654778-q3-2026-tu/). [Likely] FCF conversion is flattered by float working capital, which reverses if volumes shrink. [Likely] Net debt/EBITDA 1.65x is manageable (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/epa/EDEN/financials/balance-sheet/). [Certain]

## 3. Valuation
P/E 12.6x vs 5y median 32.3x (9th percentile of range); EV/EBITDA 7.1x vs 19.6x median; P/FCF 5.7x; FCF yield 17.57% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/epa/EDEN/statistics/). [Certain] Versus peers P/E is 44% lower and EV/EBITDA 14% lower, though EV/Sales is higher on its high margin. Base fair value 37.90 EUR vs close 25.91, +46.3%; bear 20.78 (-19.8%). The reverse DCF implies -1.3% revenue growth in year 1, fading to terminal growth, which is harsher than the reaffirmed guidance. [Likely] The price assumes regulation permanently impairs the voucher franchise and that float income is lost. The valuation reflects a derating, not a broken business. [Likely]

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Operating margin (income statement page) falls below 26% on a TTM basis.
- ROIC (ratios page) falls below 14%.
- FCF margin (cash flow/income statement pages) falls below 28%.
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) rises above 2.5x.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Shares change is -0.97% YoY, so buybacks modestly offset dilution; a 10% dividend increase was announced at the AGM (headline: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/epa/EDEN/transcripts/651311-agm-2026/). [Likely] FCF margin 38.2% TTM and the 17.57% FCF yield give ample room for dividends, buybacks and deleveraging. [Certain] Watch whether FCF is float-driven rather than recurring. [Guessing]

## 6. Unusual items
Meal-voucher regulation in Italy and Brazil is the main specific risk and the reason for the derating. [Certain] Altman Z of 0.27 is not meaningful for a float-holding payments firm, but Piotroski F is only 5. Short interest [data unavailable]. Institutions hold 57.6%, insiders 8.31%.
