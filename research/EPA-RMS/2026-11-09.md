# Hermès International (EPA:RMS) — Core initiation — 2026-11-09
## 1. Business
Vertically integrated luxury house: leather goods (Birkin/Kelly), ready-to-wear, accessories, silk, watches and perfume, sold mainly through its own stores, with production largely in-house and supply deliberately rationed [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/epa/RMS/). Regional revenue mix [data unavailable]: the company IR domain was blocked, so China/Asia's share is unverified. Management names China footfall as a key uncertainty [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/epa/RMS/).

## 2. Quality
Exceptional returns: TTM ROIC 50.1%, ROCE 31.8%, gross margin 71.3%, operating margin 41.8%, net debt/EBITDA -1.42x (net cash) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/epa/RMS/financials/ratios/). Moat is brand scarcity and in-house craftsmanship, which lets it hold margins through cycles: operating margin 40.0% in FY2021 vs 41.8% TTM [Likely].
Direction: ROIC fell from 72.3% (FY2022) to 50.1% TTM and is down 2.5pp in 12 months, and ROCE is down 2.9pp. This reflects a growing cash and capital base rather than weaker margins, since operating margin is flat to up [Likely]. FCF margin is rising over 12 months (26.5% to 31.4%) [Certain].
Q3 2026: revenue rose 6% at constant exchange rates, but ready-to-wear and accessories turned negative for the first time in years (source: https://www.reuters.com/business/retail-consumer/hermes-third-quarter-growth-slows-ready-to-wear-turns-negative-2026-10-22/) [Likely].

## 3. Valuation
Price 1,362.50 EUR. P/E 31.7x vs 5y range 44.9x–65.7x (median 49.2x); EV/EBITDA 17.8x vs 28.7x–41.0x; P/FCF 28.2x vs 40.1x–59.7x; FCF yield 3.55%. Every multiple sits below its 5y minimum [Certain]. Against peers (LVMH, Kering, Richemont, Moncler) it still trades at a +64% P/E premium and +71% EV/EBITDA premium [Certain] (sources: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/epa/RMS/statistics/ and the peer statistics pages in Sources).
Fair value is flagged unreliable: DCF base 804.45 vs P/E base 2,114.48 (2.6x apart); blend base 1,241.13 (-8.9% vs price) [Certain]. The reverse DCF implies 23.0% year-1 revenue growth, which is demanding [Likely]. The price is below its history but above a plain DCF; the de-rating reflects slower growth, not a clear bargain [Likely].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Operating margin (income statement page, TTM) falls below 38%.
- ROCE (ratios page, TTM) falls below 28%.
- FCF margin (cash flow statement, TTM) falls below 24%.
- Revenue growth at constant exchange rates in the next two quarterly releases is below 3%, with leather goods turning negative (earnings release).

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Net cash and no meaningful dilution (shares +0.03% YoY). FCF conversion 112.1% of net income TTM, though the 5y trend is down 19.6pp from 122.8% in FY2021. Investment in production and retail capacity continues per the Q3 call (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/epa/RMS/transcripts/655488-q3-2026-tu/) [Likely]. Piotroski F 6, Altman Z 24.03 [Certain].

## 6. Unusual items
- Sector de-rating: stock is -35.1% over 12 months and -43.6pp vs SPY, with luxury weak on Chinese demand (source: https://www.reuters.com/business/retail-consumer/european-luxurys-350-billion-blowout-2026-10-13/) [Likely].
- Store looting in Marseille prompted tighter security; no financial effect shown (source: https://www.reuters.com/business/retail-consumer/hermes-ramped-up-security-after-french-unrest-cfo-says-2026-10-22/) [Likely].
- Controlled by a family-held partnership (commandite structure); institutions hold only 9.8% [Likely].
