# Hermès International (EPA:RMS) — Core initiation — 2026-11-21
## 1. Business
Hermès makes and sells luxury leather goods (Birkin, Kelly), ready-to-wear, accessories and other métiers, mostly through its own stores, with supply deliberately rationed. Regional revenue mix: [data unavailable] (the company's finance site could not be fetched, so no `[company-reported]` split was obtained). Headlines point to China demand and currency as the key swing factors [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/epa/RMS/).

## 2. Quality
Exceptional, with some erosion. Gross margin 71.3% and operating margin 41.8% (TTM) are essentially unchanged over five years; net debt/EBITDA is -1.42x (net cash); Piotroski F 6, Altman Z 24.03 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/epa/RMS/financials/ratios/). [Certain]
The moat is scarcity plus brand heat that mass-market luxury cannot copy; the margin stability through the 2024-26 luxury slowdown supports it. [Likely]
Direction: ROIC fell from 72.3% (FY2022) to 50.1% TTM, and is down 2.5pp over the last 12 months; ROCE is down 2.9pp to 31.8%. This is mostly dilution of returns from heavy capacity investment (net cash and capex growing), not margin loss, since operating margin is flat to up 0.6pp. [Likely] FCF margin has recovered to 31.4% from 26.5% a year earlier (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/epa/RMS/financials/cash-flow-statement/?p=trailing). [Certain]
The Q3 release showed ready-to-wear turning negative and growth slowing (source: https://www.reuters.com/business/retail-consumer/hermes-third-quarter-growth-slows-ready-to-wear-turns-negative-2026-10-22/). [Likely]

## 3. Valuation
Price fell to 1,225.50 EUR (entry-price hit; 12-month change -41.9%, 52-week low 1,213.00 vs high 2,300.00) after the 22 Oct Q3 release showed slowing growth. [Certain]
P/E is 28.5x against a 5y range of 44.9x-65.7x (median 49.2x); EV/EBITDA 15.9x vs 28.7x-41.0x; P/FCF 25.4x vs 40.1x-59.7x; FCF yield 3.94%. Every multiple sits below its own five-year minimum, so the old premium has been largely removed. [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/epa/RMS/statistics/)
It is still the most expensive name in the peer set: P/E +34% vs peers, EV/EBITDA +72%. [Certain]
Mechanical fair value: base 1,230.29 EUR (bear 1,029.83, bull 1,508.24), so the price sits at base, +0.4%. Flagged unreliable: DCF 788.19 and P/E 2,114.48 disagree 2.7x. Weigh lightly. The reverse DCF implies 20.6% year-1 revenue growth. [Likely]
Trend is broken: -23.2% vs the 200-day average, RSI 32.9, relative strength vs SPY -46.0pp over 12 months. [Certain]

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Operating margin (income statement, TTM) falls below 38%.
- ROIC (ratios page, TTM) falls below 40%.
- FCF margin (cash flow statement, TTM) falls below 25%.
- Gross margin (income statement) falls below 68%, signalling loss of pricing power.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
FCF conversion is 112.1% of net income TTM (FY2025: 103.1%), with net cash and share count change 0.03% YoY, so no dilution. Cash is reinvested in production and retail capacity (Q3 call, per the fact sheet headline). Heavier capex is the reason ROIC is slipping; returns on that spend are the thing to watch. [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/epa/RMS/financials/cash-flow-statement/)
