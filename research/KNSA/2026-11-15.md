# Kiniksa Pharmaceuticals (KNSA) — Core initiation — 2026-11-15
## 1. Business
Commercial-stage biotech. Almost all revenue is net sales of ARCALYST (rilonacept), an IL-1 inhibitor for recurrent pericarditis, sold in the US through specialty pharmacies [Likely] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1730430/000110465926119186/knsa-20260930x10q.htm). Ex-US rights are licensed to Huadong (China and Asia), which gives royalties and shipment revenue only, so no foreign country is near 10% of revenue [Likely] (same filing). Pipeline: KPL-387, a monthly follow-on antibody now in pivotal trials, plus earlier assets.

## 2. Quality
Profitability has turned sharply: TTM ROIC is 68.3% against a negative figure in FY2024, operating margin 13.8% and FCF margin 20.8%, all rising on the rolling-12-month table (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/knsa/financials/ratios/?p=trailing) [Certain]. Net cash (net debt/EBITDA -4.24x), Altman Z 10.42, Piotroski 7 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/knsa/statistics/) [Certain]. Q3 results beat and 2026 ARCALYST guidance was raised (source: https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/10/27/3388879/0/en/kiniksa-pharmaceuticals-reports-third-quarter-2026-financial-results-and-recent-portfolio-execution.html) [Certain]. Weak points: gross margin fell from 74.2% (FY2021) to 54.3% TTM, and the moat is a single-asset, single-indication franchise. Protection rests on ARCALYST's niche position and the KPL-387 follow-on, not on diversification [Guessing].

## 3. Valuation
Price 96.33 is near the 52-week high of 99.31 and 53.3% above the 200-day average (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/knsa/history/) [Certain]. P/E is 72.8x, at the 82nd percentile of its 5y range, and 190% above peers (NBIX, INCY, EXEL, HALO). EV/EBITDA is 52.9x versus a 13.5x peer median. P/B of 10.6x is above its 5y max. FCF yield is only 2.60%. P/FCF is low in its own range (10th percentile), but that range was set when FCF was tiny [Likely]. The mechanical fair value (P/E only; DCF and rNPV unavailable) is base 62.70, 34.9% below the price, with bull 115.96 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/knsa/) [Certain]. The price assumes continued ARCALYST growth plus real KPL-387 success; it leaves little margin for a miss [Likely].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Operating margin (TTM, income statement page) falls below 8%.
- FCF margin (TTM, cash flow page) falls below 12%.
- Gross margin (TTM, income statement page) falls below 48%.
- Company-reported pivotal KPL-387 data (2027 guidance, filings on sec.gov) fails its primary endpoint, or ARCALYST guidance is cut.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
FCF conversion is strong (TTM FCF/NI 180.0%) and the balance sheet is net cash, so growth is self-funded. R&D is rising as KPL-387 enters Phase 3 and headcount grows (source: sec-delta filing above) [Certain]. Share count is up 8.67% YoY, which suggests dilution from stock compensation or issuance (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/knsa/statistics/) [Likely]. No dividend or buyback evidence in the fact sheet.
