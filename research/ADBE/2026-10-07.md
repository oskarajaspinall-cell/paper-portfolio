# Adobe Inc. (ADBE) — Core initiation — 2026-10-07

## 1. Business
Adobe sells subscription software: Digital Media (Creative Cloud — Photoshop, Illustrator, Premiere; Document Cloud — Acrobat, Sign) and Digital Experience (enterprise marketing/analytics software), plus a small Publishing segment. Revenue is predominantly recurring subscription fees, billed via bundled plans to individuals, teams and enterprises (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/adbe/).

## 2. Quality
ROIC is 57.2% TTM, up from 38.2% in FY2021, and ROCE is 52.1% TTM, up from 28.6% — both rising every year of the 5y window [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/adbe/financials/ratios/). Gross margin is 89.3% TTM, also rising [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/adbe/financials/income-statement/). These reflect entrenched creative-professional workflows, high switching costs from file formats/plug-ins embedded in agency pipelines, and subscription lock-in. However, Adobe's own 10-K flags competition from "AI-first creative tools," free/low-cost generative-AI alternatives, and social platforms with built-in editing as a growing threat to Creative and Document Cloud pricing power [Certain] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/796343/000079634326000003/adbe-20251128.htm). Consistent with that risk, operating margin has been flat near 36% for five years despite rising returns on capital, and FCF margin has drifted down from 43.6% to 40.8% TTM [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/adbe/financials/income-statement/, https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/adbe/financials/cash-flow-statement/) — the moat is intact today but is being tested, not expanding.

## 3. Valuation
ADBE trades on 13.3x TTM P/E, below its own 5-year range minimum of 18.8x and 48% below the peer (ADSK/CRM/INTU/MSFT) median of 25.4x. EV/EBITDA (9.5x), EV/Sales (3.6x) and P/FCF (8.7x) all sit below their own 5y minimums and 34-49% below peer medians; FCF yield is 11.45% [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/adbe/financials/ratios/, peer statistics pages cited in the fact sheet). Only P/B (7.9x) is richer than peers (+15%), a function of high ROE, not an expensive absolute multiple. The current price assumes either a structural re-rating of Adobe as an AI-disruption casualty, or a durable step-down in growth — not a continuation of the ROIC/margin trend shown above [Likely]. The stock is down 31.3% over 12 months and sits 8% below both its 50- and 200-day moving averages, which is consistent with the market pricing in competitive risk, but the multiple compression looks larger than the (so far modest) fundamental deterioration [Likely].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Gross margin (income statement page) falls below 85% for two consecutive fiscal years — signals commoditization from free/low-cost AI tools.
- Operating margin (income statement page) falls below 34% for two consecutive fiscal years — sustained pricing/margin erosion.
- ROIC (ratios page) falls below 40% — loss of the pricing power underpinning the thesis.
- Net debt/EBITDA (balance sheet and income statement pages) rises above 1.0x — buybacks outrunning cash generation.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
FCF conversion is 145.4% of net income TTM, though the trend has slipped from 142.7% in FY2021 to 138-145% recently [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/adbe/financials/cash-flow-statement/). Adobe returns cash almost entirely via buybacks: shares outstanding are down 6.22% year over year, and the board approved a new $25bn repurchase program on April 21, 2026 [Certain] (source: https://www.adobe.com/investor-relations.html). There is no dividend. Net debt/EBITDA turned slightly positive (0.12x) for the first time in the 5-year window as buybacks modestly outpaced FCF, but leverage remains very low (Altman Z-score 8.01) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/adbe/financials/balance-sheet/, https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/adbe/statistics/).

## 6. Unusual items
Short interest is 4.77% of float, up 6.5% versus the prior month — elevated but not extreme [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/adbe/statistics/). Adobe's 10-K explicitly names generative-AI-first entrants and free creative tools as an emerging competitive risk to Creative/Document Cloud pricing, which is the key swing factor for this thesis [Certain] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/796343/000079634326000003/adbe-20251128.htm). Otherwise none material.
