# TotalEnergies SE (EPA:TTE) — Core initiation — 2026-11-15
## 1. Business
Integrated oil, gas and LNG major with a growing power and renewables arm, plus refining, petrochemicals and fuel marketing. Profit is driven by hydrocarbon prices and LNG volumes; reports in USD, quotes in EUR [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/epa/TTE/). No single foreign country's revenue share was verified; [data unavailable] on geographic mix.

## 2. Quality
Cyclical commodity price-taker, so returns follow the oil and gas cycle rather than a moat. ROIC 8.0% in FY2025 vs 16.1% in FY2022, but TTM ROIC is 13.9% and TTM operating margin 17.0% vs 11.6% a year earlier [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/epa/TTE/financials/ratios/?p=trailing). The 5-year trend is falling while the last 12 months are rising; this looks like cycle, not structural change [Likely]. Durable advantages are scale, integrated LNG and low leverage: net debt/EBITDA 0.52x TTM, Piotroski F 8 [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/epa/TTE/statistics/). FCF conversion 106.3% of net income TTM, down over five years [Certain]. Recent LNG supply deals with ADNOC Gas and a Chinese buyer support the LNG franchise [Likely] (source: https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20261102990414/en/United-Arab-Emirates-TotalEnergies-Signs-a-Five%E2%80%91year-Liquefied-Natural-Gas-Supply-Agreement-with-ADNOC-Gas/). Quality is good for a major but earnings are near a cyclical high [Likely].

## 3. Valuation
P/E 9.2x vs 5y range 7.5x–10.7x (55% position) and peer median 14.8x; EV/EBITDA 4.5x vs peers 6.0x; P/FCF 8.6x vs peers 10.1x; FCF yield 11.57% [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/epa/TTE/statistics/). P/B 1.6x is above its 5y max of 1.4x. Mechanical DCF base 159.84 EUR vs close 82.17 EUR, but the reverse DCF shows the market pricing a year-1 revenue fall of 15.2%, i.e. a commodity-price normalisation [Certain]. The DCF extrapolates peak-ish TTM cash flow, so the base is unreliable [Likely]. Price is 9.4% above its 200-day average with 12-month change +48.6% [Certain]. Fair-to-cheap against peers, but not cheap on own history [Likely].
