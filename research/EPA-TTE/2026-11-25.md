# TotalEnergies SE (EPA:TTE) — Core initiation — 2026-11-25
## 1. Business
Integrated oil, gas and LNG major with a growing power and renewables arm, plus refining, petrochemicals and fuel marketing. Profit is driven by hydrocarbon prices and LNG volumes; reports in USD, quotes in EUR [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/epa/TTE/). Revenue mix by country is [data unavailable]; no single foreign country was verified at ~10%+.

## 2. Quality
Cyclical commodity price-taker: returns follow the oil and gas cycle, not a moat [Likely]. ROIC was 8.0% in FY2025 vs 16.1% in FY2022, yet TTM ROIC is 14.3% and TTM operating margin 17.0% vs 11.6% a year earlier [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/epa/TTE/financials/ratios/?p=trailing). The 5-year fall alongside the 12-month rise looks like cycle rather than structural change [Likely]. Durable strengths are scale, integrated LNG and low leverage: net debt/EBITDA 0.52x TTM, Piotroski F 8 [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/epa/TTE/statistics/). FCF conversion is 106.3% TTM but falling over five years [Certain]. A new agreement to acquire interests in Continental Resources natural-gas upstream assets adds US gas exposure; terms not verified [Likely] (source: https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20261122060678/en/United-States-TotalEnergies-Strengthens-its-Partnership-with-Continental-Resources-to-Develop-its-Gas-Production/). Good for a major; earnings are near a cyclical high [Likely].
