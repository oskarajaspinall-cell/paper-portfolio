## 2. Quality
Returns are exceptional and rising: ROIC 57.9% TTM vs 46.2% in FY2025 and 44.7% in FY2021; ROE 65.0% TTM (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tadawul/4190/financials/ratios/) [Certain]. Operating margin has been steady near 10% (10.1% TTM, flat on a year earlier; source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tadawul/4190/financials/income-statement/?p=trailing) [Certain]. Over five fiscal years, however, gross margin (-1.1pp) and operating margin (-1.2pp) drifted down, so the ROIC gain is capital-turnover led, not margin led [Likely].

Direction change: FCF margin is falling over five fiscal years (-2.9pp) but rising over the last 12 months, 10.7% to 11.9% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tadawul/4190/financials/cash-flow-statement/?p=trailing). FY2023 at 6.1% looks like a working-capital dip, and FY2025 recovered to 12.1% [Likely].

Moat: scale and brand in the home market are the probable reason returns persist, but I could not verify market share from a primary source [Guessing]. Altman Z 7.47, Piotroski 6, beta 0.27 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tadawul/4190/statistics/) [Certain].

## 3. Valuation
P/E 16.0x sits at the 16th percentile of its 5y range (14.6x–23.8x, median 18.6x) and 3% below the peer median 16.5x. EV/EBITDA 15.1x is at the 22nd percentile but 67% above peers (9.1x); P/FCF 14.8x vs 17.4x median. FCF yield is 6.74%. P/B 11.8x is at the 67th percentile, which is the cost of a 65% ROE [Certain]. 

The mechanical fair value is base 17.46 SAR vs a 17.05 close (+2.4%), bear 10.03, bull 21.96 (blend: DCF 67%, EV/EBITDA 33%). The price therefore assumes about 4.1% year-1 revenue growth fading to terminal growth, with no margin of safety on base but a cheap-for-its-history earnings multiple [Likely]. The peer premium on EV/EBITDA reflects higher returns than BBY or JBH and is not by itself a warning [Guessing]. Source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tadawul/4190/statistics/ and the fact sheet.
