## 2. Quality
This is a cyclical, not a compounder. Fiscal-year ROIC went 24.0% (FY2021), 18.0%, -4.5%, -6.8%, -6.5% (FY2025), then 31.2% in the TTM (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tpe/2337/financials/ratios/) [Certain]. Gross margin went 17.8% in FY2025 to 57.7% TTM, and operating margin from -12.8% to 38.1% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tpe/2337/financials/income-statement/) [Certain]. The 12-month turn (ROIC from -8.8% to 31.2%) is a price-cycle upswing, not evidence of a durable moat [Likely]. Three loss-making years sit inside the five-year record, so there is no proven pricing power through the cycle [Certain]. Balance sheet is now fine (net debt/EBITDA -0.30x TTM) but was 19.72x in FY2024 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tpe/2337/financials/balance-sheet/) [Certain]. Piotroski F is 7 and Altman Z is 4.94 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tpe/2337/statistics/) [Certain]. Current profits are probably peak-cycle [Likely].

## 3. Valuation
P/E is 7.1x against a five-year range of 6.5x–7.0x, so it sits above the range maximum, in line with the peer median of 7.0x (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tpe/2337/financials/ratios/) [Certain]. P/B is 1.9x, above its five-year maximum of 1.7x [Certain]. EV/EBITDA is 4.7x and P/FCF 6.2x, with FCF yield 16.22% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tpe/2337/statistics/) [Certain]. Low multiples on peak earnings are the classic cyclical trap, since TTM earnings are far above the five-year norm [Likely]. The mechanical fair value is flagged unreliable: base 59.61 TWD against a 79.60 close, with the two methods 6.4x apart [Certain]. The reverse DCF says the price implies 34.9% year-1 revenue growth [Certain]. The price already assumes earnings stay near peak [Likely].
