# Amadeus IT Group, S.A. (BME:AMS) — Core initiation — 2026-10-19
## 1. Business
Amadeus runs the leading global distribution system (GDS): airlines and travel agencies pay per-booking transaction fees to search and book flights, hotels and rail through its platform (Air Distribution). It also licenses mission-critical IT (passenger service systems, departure control) to airlines on subscription/usage fees (Air IT), plus hospitality and travel-agency software (Hospitality & Other) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/bme/AMS/).

## 2. Quality
A capital-light, two-sided network: airlines need distribution reach, agencies need inventory breadth, and switching either side's booking infrastructure is costly and slow — a durable moat shared with only Sabre and (private) Travelport in an effective oligopoly [Likely]. ROIC rose from -0.3% (FY2021, COVID) to 19.0% TTM and ROCE to 22.2% TTM, operating margin 27.5% TTM, FCF margin 32.5% TTM with FCF conversion 163.6% of net income — all on a rising 5y trend (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/bme/AMS/financials/ratios/, https://stockanalysis.com/quote/bme/AMS/financials/income-statement/). Net debt/EBITDA fell to 1.06x TTM from 5.51x FY2021 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/bme/AMS/financials/ratios/). Near-term growth is uneven: Air Distribution lagged Air IT and Hospitality & Other in H1 2026 on Middle East air-traffic disruption, per the Q2 2026 earnings call, but management called the disruption's worst past and left FY2026 guidance directionally unchanged, only widening the range [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/bme/AMS/transcripts/550383-q2-2026/).

## 3. Valuation
All core multiples sit below their own 5-year range: P/E 18.0x (5y min 20.4x), EV/EBITDA 10.1x (min 12.1x), EV/Sales 3.9x (min 4.6x), P/FCF 10.7x (min 12.8x); P/B 5.0x is also near its 5y floor (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/bme/AMS/financials/ratios/, https://stockanalysis.com/quote/bme/AMS/history/). Versus peers (SABR, BKNG, EXPE, HKG:0696) it trades at a premium on P/E (+31%) and P/FCF (+37%) but a discount on EV/EBITDA (-7%); the EV/Sales premium (+113%) is distorted by thinner-margin peers, so EV/EBITDA is the fairer cross-check (source: fact sheet valuation table). FCF yield is 9.38%. The reverse DCF in the last close implies -4.6% year-1 revenue decline fading to terminal growth — a disconnect [Likely] from management's own guidance of continued constant-currency growth (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/bme/AMS/transcripts/550383-q2-2026/), consistent with the 12-month share fall of -19.9% outrunning the actual earnings impact. Base fair value of €100.14 implies +84.1% upside to the €54.40 close; even the bear case (€62.04) is +14.0% (source: fact sheet).

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROIC (ratios page) falls below 12% — moat/returns eroding, not just a cyclical dip
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) rises above 2.5x — leverage re-approaching pre-pandemic risk
- Operating margin (income statement) falls below 20%
- Air Distribution segment growth (next earnings release) stays negative for two consecutive quarters, signalling structural bookings loss rather than one-off disruption

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
FCF conversion is 163.6% of net income TTM, FCF margin 32.5% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/bme/AMS/financials/cash-flow-statement/). Shares outstanding fell -3.96% YoY (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/bme/AMS/statistics/), consistent with continued buybacks: the AGM and Q2 2026 earnings-call transcripts both describe completed share-repurchase programmes in 2025 and 2026 alongside an ordinary dividend funded from free cash flow [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/bme/AMS/transcripts/654865-agm-2026/, https://stockanalysis.com/quote/bme/AMS/transcripts/550383-q2-2026/). Management also described ongoing R&D investment funding AI/biometrics without impairing group margins [Likely] (same sources). Capital return plus reinvestment is comfortably covered by the 32.5% FCF margin.
