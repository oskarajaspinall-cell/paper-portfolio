# STERIS plc (STE) — Core initiation — 2026-10-10
## 1. Business
STERIS sells sterilization, infection-prevention and surgical-support equipment and consumables to hospitals, surgery centers, and medical-device/pharma/biotech manufacturers [Certain] (source: https://www.steris.com/about). Revenue mixes recurring consumables (detergents, endoscopy accessories, instruments) and services (maintenance, contract sterilization, repair) with capital equipment (sterilizers, surgical tables, endoscope reprocessors) sales [Certain] (source: https://www.steris.com/about). Reporting currency USD; sector Healthcare, industry Medical Devices (source: research/STE/factsheet-2026-10-10.md [OV]).

## 2. Quality
Returns exceed the cost of capital and are rising: ROIC 7.1%→9.7% TTM, ROCE 6.5%→12.2%, ROE 5.5%→11.4% over five years (source: factsheet [RA]) [Certain]. Operating margin expanded 2.6pp to 19.0% TTM; net margin up 7.4pp to 13.3% (source: factsheet [IS]) [Certain]. Installed capital equipment plus proprietary consumables and service contracts creates repeat-purchase revenue and switching costs once a hospital adopts STERIS equipment, though the company does not quantify a recurring-revenue % (source: https://www.steris.com/about) [Likely]. Net debt/EBITDA fell from 2.64x to 0.95x TTM (source: factsheet [RA/BS/IS]) [Certain]. FCF/NI conversion is declining (369% FY23 to 115% TTM) as net income normalizes off a low base, not a red flag alone [Likely]. Piotroski F-score 7/9 and Altman Z 5.44 show a healthy balance sheet (source: factsheet [ST]) [Certain]. The $600m North Carolina chemistries plant (announced 2026-08-05) signals investment in the stickiest consumables line, not retrenchment (source: factsheet news, 2026-08-05) [Likely].

## 3. Valuation
At 214.40 USD, STE trades on 26.2x P/E, 13.7x EV/EBITDA, 3.7x EV/Sales, 22.6x P/FCF (source: factsheet) [Certain]. P/E, EV/EBITDA and EV/Sales sit below STE's own 5-year minimum (-1%, -9%, -8%) and P/E and EV/Sales below peer (BDX/DHR/SYK/BAX) medians (-17%, -1%); EV/EBITDA roughly in line (+2%); P/FCF and P/B run above peers (+8%, +16%) (source: factsheet) [Certain]. Base DCF fair value is 139.13, base P/E-based fair value 252.43, blending (67/33) to a base fair value of 176.90 — 17.5% below last close, with bear/bull of 87.08/388.11 (source: factsheet) [Certain]. The price implies (reverse-DCF) 15.7% year-one revenue growth fading to terminal growth, above STERIS's reported ~7% organic growth last quarter (source: factsheet news, Q1 FY2027 transcript 2026-08-06) [Likely]. The market prices in more growth than the base case or recent results support, but the multiple sits below its own 5-year floor and broadly in line with peers, limiting downside [Likely].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROIC (ratios page) falls back below 8% (FY2024 level), reversing the five-year improving trend.
- Operating margin (income statement) drops below 17% (FY2023/24 level), undoing the recent expansion.
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) rises back above 1.5x, reversing the deleveraging that has supported the quality case.
- Organic revenue growth reported in quarterly filings falls below mid-single digits for two consecutive quarters, undercutting the growth management has guided to (source: factsheet news, Morgan Stanley conference transcript dated 2026-09-15).

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
FCF margin has risen from 9.4% (FY22) to 15.3% TTM, and FCF/NI conversion remains above 100% (115% TTM) despite declining from 2023's unusually high 369% (source: factsheet [CF/IS]) [Certain]. Shares outstanding fell 0.48% YoY, indicating modest net buybacks rather than dilution (source: factsheet [ST]) [Certain]. The $600m North Carolina plant is a multi-year capex commitment funded from a low (0.95x) leverage base, consistent with reinvesting in the higher-recurring consumables franchise rather than returning all cash to shareholders (source: factsheet news, 2026-08-05) [Likely].

## 6. Unusual items
Institutional ownership is reported at 100.3% of float (source: factsheet [ST]), an artifact of overlapping/duplicated institutional filings rather than a governance concern [Likely]. Short interest is low (2.87% of float) but rose 20.4% versus the prior month (source: factsheet [ST/News]); not large enough on its own to be material [Likely]. No other governance, legal or macro-exposure items in the fact sheet are material to the thesis.
