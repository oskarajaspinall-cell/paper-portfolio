# Meta Platforms (META) — Core initiation — 2026-11-16
## 1. Business
Meta sells targeted advertising across Facebook, Instagram, Messenger and WhatsApp, funded by an engagement base it reaches for free [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/meta/). Reality Labs (headsets, AI glasses, neural interfaces) is a loss-making side bet. Heavy AI and data-centre spend now drives costs [Certain]. No single foreign country was identified as ~10%+ of revenue; geographic mix was not verified [Guessing].

## 2. Quality
High-quality franchise, but the last year has weakened. Gross margin is 81.0% and TTM ROIC 29.6% (rising +1.0pp over 12 months, though falling over five fiscal years) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/meta/financials/ratios/). The moat is network scale plus ad-targeting data, which competitors cannot easily copy [Likely]. Direction flags: ROCE fell to 21.9% from 30.7%, operating margin to 37.8% from 43.2%, and FCF margin to 11.9% from 23.7% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/meta/financials/ratios/?p=trailing). Causes are spending, not demand: infrastructure costs, third-party AI token costs and legal charges [Certain]. Q3 operating income fell 37% year on year, including a $9.89 billion charge for the August 2026 multistate attorneys general agreement [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1326801/000162828026066773/meta-20260930.htm). Excluding that charge, the margin compression is mainly capex-driven depreciation [Likely]. Whether AI spend earns a return is the open question [Guessing].

## 3. Valuation
P/E is 21.5x, 57% of the way through its 5y range and in line with peers at +2% [Certain]. EV/EBITDA is 14.4x, 48% below peers, though the peer set is distorted by AMZN and GOOGL. P/FCF is 57.3x, above its 5y maximum of 36.1x, and FCF yield is only 1.75% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/meta/statistics/). Earnings look fairly priced and cash flow looks expensive, because capex is absorbing the cash. The mechanical fair value is unreliable: the DCF is negative on depressed FCF, and the blended base of 230.28 sits well below the 641.69 close (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/meta/) [Likely]. The price assumes capex converts into growth and that FCF recovers [Likely]. The stock is 1.8% above its 200-day average, 5.6% below its 50-day average, and 3-month relative strength vs SPY is +10.9pp.

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- TTM FCF margin (cash-flow trailing page) stays below 5%.
- TTM ROIC (ratios page) falls below 20%.
- TTM operating margin (income-statement trailing page) falls below 30%.
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) rises above 1.5x.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Cash conversion has collapsed: FCF/net income is 38.1% TTM against 76.3% in FY2025 [Certain]. Capex is the cause. Net debt/EBITDA has moved from -0.34x in FY2024 to 0.28x, as debt rose; interest expense rose sharply on higher long-term debt [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1326801/000162828026066773/meta-20260930.htm). Shares outstanding fell 0.76% year on year, so buybacks are modest [Certain]. Reality Labs is expected to lose about $18 billion to $19 billion in 2026 [company-reported] (same filing URL). The balance sheet is still sound, with an Altman Z of 6.44.

## 6. Unusual items
- Regulatory and legal: Italy fined Meta over Android gallery photos, and Meta says it will appeal [Certain] (source: https://www.theguardian.com/technology/2026/nov/15/italy-fines-meta-887m-for-accessing-android-users-photos-and-videos). The new filing text adds youth and privacy bellwether trials scheduled for 2026 and beyond [Certain] (filing URL above). Further settlements are likely [Likely].
- Short interest is 3.41% of float, up 165.6% on the month from a low base [Certain].
- The filing adds risk language on open-source AI misuse, platform-owner (Apple, Google) restrictions on ad measurement, and consumer-hardware inexperience [Certain].
