# Macro overlay: Partners Group Holding AG (PGHN) — 2026-10-18
## Assessment
PGHN's earnings mix (recurring fees + performance fees/carry) is cyclical to the credit and rate cycle: financing costs and exit windows for portfolio companies drive performance-fee crystallisation, and the fact sheet's own DCF discounts PGHN's cash flows at the FRED 10y Treasury (5.10%, 2026-10-15) plus beta [Certain, https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=DGS10]. Chain: credit spreads/real yields → financing cost & exit activity → performance-fee realisation → earnings; separately, 10y yield → discount rate → fair-value multiple.

Facts: US 10y Treasury 5.10% (+53bp/3m), 10y real yield 2.78% (+43bp/3m), HY credit spread 3.23% (+52bp/3m, still below the 5.0% stress threshold), effective fed funds 3.88% (+25bp/1m and 3m — hiking/holding, not cutting), VIX 16.46 (-0.27/3m, calm) [Certain, FRED, 2026-10-15/16]. Market pricing (proxy): 2y yield (4.77%) sitting ~90bp above effective fed funds implies the market expects no near-term cuts, i.e. "higher for longer" **(inference)**. This is incrementally adverse for deal financing and exits just as reported performance income already fell sharply in H1 2026 (per the researcher's note) — largely company-specific, but rate/spread direction is the macro amplifier.

Bull: yields/spreads ease → exit activity recovers → performance fees rebound → multiple re-rates toward peers (28.9x P/E median vs 13.3x now). Bear: yields/spreads keep rising → DCF discount rate rises and exits stay slow → fair value and performance fees both fall further. Tilt: small, toward bear — the move is real but modest next to the ~40% base-case upside already priced, and VIX/credit are not yet stressed.
