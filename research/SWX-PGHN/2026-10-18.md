# Partners Group Holding AG (SWX:PGHN) — Core initiation — 2026-10-18
## 1. Business
Partners Group is a Swiss-listed alternative asset manager investing institutional and private-wealth client capital across private equity, private credit, infrastructure and real estate [Certain]. It earns recurring management fees on fee-earning AUM plus performance fees (carried interest) crystallised on successful realisations [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/swx/PGHN/).

## 2. Quality
Returns on capital are very high but declining: TTM ROIC 29.6% (down 29.3pp over 5y), ROCE 51.3%, ROE 75.4% [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/swx/PGHN/financials/ratios/). Margins remain high (operating 59.7%, net margin 48.0% TTM) but have drifted down (-2.7pp / -6.6pp over 5y) as performance fees normalise [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/swx/PGHN/financials/income-statement/). FCF conversion has improved sharply to 162.8% of net income TTM, an asset-light, cash-generative model [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/swx/PGHN/financials/cash-flow-statement/). Moat: long-lock fee-earning AUM, deep institutional LP relationships and a multi-decade realised track record are hard to replicate, and allocator switching costs are high [Likely]. Net debt/EBITDA has risen from net cash in FY2021 to 1.76x TTM [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/swx/PGHN/financials/ratios/), worth monitoring. A drop in H1 performance income and a co-CEO transition announced 2026-09-01 underline the business's fee cyclicality and near-term leadership risk [Certain] (source: https://www.wsj.com/finance/investing/partners-group-shares-slide-after-drop-in-performance-fees-ceo-change-82686164).

## 3. Valuation
At 607.40 CHF, PGHN trades on 13.3x TTM P/E, below its own 5y range minimum (20.2x) and the KKR/Blackstone/Apollo/EQT peer median of 28.9x (-54%) [Certain]. EV/EBITDA (12.0x) and P/FCF (8.1x) show similar discounts to both history and the peer medians (15.9x, 24.8x) [Certain]. P/B (10.7x) screens expensive versus the 3.7x peer median, but book value is a weak anchor for a fee-driven, capital-light model; P/E and P/FCF are the more relevant cross-checks [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/swx/PGHN/financials/ratios/ and peer statistics pages). FCF yield is 12.33% [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/swx/PGHN/statistics/). The stock's own model puts base fair value at 854.30 CHF (+40.6% vs last close) with the bear case still above it at 652.93 CHF (+7.5%) [Certain] (source: fact sheet fair value section). The price embeds persistently weak performance fees with no re-rating toward history; the bear-case cushion is the key support for position sizing [Likely].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Net margin (income statement page) falls below 40% TTM, confirming structural rather than cyclical fee compression (currently 48.0%).
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) rises above 2.5x, signalling leverage is funding operations rather than temporary commitments (currently 1.76x).
- ROE (ratios page) falls below 40% TTM, undermining the capital-light return profile (currently 75.4%).
- FCF conversion (FCF/NI, cash-flow and income-statement pages) falls back below 60%, reversing the recent cash-generation improvement (currently 162.8%).

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
FCF margin has risen to 78.2% TTM and FCF conversion to 162.8% of net income [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/swx/PGHN/financials/cash-flow-statement/). Shares outstanding fell 1.06% YoY, consistent with net buybacks rather than dilution [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/swx/PGHN/statistics/). Net debt/EBITDA has risen from net cash (FY2021) to 1.76x TTM, partly funding commitments such as the announced ~$630m Fidelity Life acquisition (2026-09-03) and continuing fund investments [Likely] (source: https://www.nzherald.co.nz/business/partners-group-to-buy-fidelity-life-for-630m-in-deal-that-will-see-an-asian-giant-get-a-larger-slice-of-the-nz-life-insurance-market/premium/2CJS74ZNBFEXJGDU7ONCROADII/). Leverage is not yet at a level that threatens the model but is a trend to track against trigger 2 above.

## 6. Unusual items
A Partners Group-managed, London-listed investment trust is being wound down after an investor vote, per Reuters (2026-10-06) [Certain] (source: https://www.reuters.com/legal/transactional/partners-group-managed-investment-trust-heads-liquidation-after-vote-2026-10-06/); this is a single vehicle, not disclosed as material to group AUM, but a data point on redemption pressure in listed closed-end structures [Likely]. The co-CEO transition announced September 2026 is a leadership-continuity item to monitor [Likely]. Short interest and its monthly change are [data unavailable].
