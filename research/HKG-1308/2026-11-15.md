## 2. Quality
Returns are exceptional for a shipper: TTM ROIC 54.3%, operating margin 33.5%, FCF conversion 99.6% of net income, and net cash (net debt/EBITDA -0.51x) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/1308/financials/ratios/). Piotroski F is 5 and Altman Z is 11.43 [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/1308/statistics/).

Durability is the question. ROIC ranged from 26.7% (FY2023) to 129.8% (FY2022), so returns swing with the freight cycle [Certain]. Rolling-12-month ROIC is down 4.4pp, ROCE down 5.1pp and operating margin down 3.3pp, all below 2-year highs (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/1308/financials/ratios/?p=trailing). The 5-year trend is falling on every return metric. Intra-Asia density and network scale are a plausible but unproven moat; freight is a commodity [Likely]. The fact sheet cannot show it, so I do not claim a structural edge [Guessing].

## 3. Valuation
The price is above the stock's own history and its peers on earnings multiples. P/E is 11.4x against a 5y range of 3.1x–8.7x (above max) and a peer median of 6.7x. P/FCF is 11.5x and FCF yield is 8.67% [Certain].

The mechanical EV/EBITDA fair value is 29.83 HKD base and 31.44 bull, against a close of 42.18 HKD. The base is 29.3% below the price and the bear is 69.9% below [Likely]. The price assumes TTM margins hold near cycle highs. The trend is healthy: +10.6% above the 200-day average and +33.5% over 12 months [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/1308/history/). The quality premium is not earned on this evidence.
