# Kia Corporation (KRX:000270) — Core initiation — 2026-11-08
## 1. Business
Korean volume automaker (Auto Manufacturers) selling internal-combustion, hybrid and electric vehicles globally; profit comes from vehicle sales net of incentives, mix and FX (won weakness helps exports) [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/krx/000270/). US sales are growing: Kia America reported record October sales, a sixth consecutive record month [Likely] (source: https://www.nasdaq.com/articles/kia-america-october-sales-rise-16). Revenue split by country: [data unavailable]; the US is almost certainly above 10%, so the macro overlay should check it [Guessing].

## 2. Quality
Returns are good but cyclical and rolling over. ROIC was 27.2% in FY2024, 16.9% in FY2025 and 14.1% TTM; the five-year "rising" trend hides a TTM fall from 22.0% a year earlier [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/krx/000270/financials/ratios/?p=trailing). Operating margin fell from 10.2% to 6.8% in twelve months, and FCF margin from 7.4% to 5.6% [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/krx/000270/financials/income-statement/?p=trailing). The Q2 call cited incentives and FX pressuring operating profit despite record sales [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/krx/000270/transcripts/654036-q2-2026/). That points to a commodity-like moat: share gains are real but pricing power is thin. The balance sheet is a strength: net cash, net debt/EBITDA -1.91x; Piotroski 6, Altman Z 3.02 [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/krx/000270/statistics/). Margins at 6.8% still beat a typical mass-market peer [Guessing].

## 3. Valuation
The price assumes earnings stay at or below trough levels. P/E is 5.5x against a 5y median of 4.5x (52% of range) and a peer median of 15.4x; EV/EBITDA is 1.5x (14% of range) vs peers 9.7x; P/B is 0.6x, below its five-year minimum; FCF yield is 17.24% [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/krx/000270/statistics/). Korean automakers have structurally traded at a discount, and 5y median P/E is only 4.5x, so the peer gap overstates mispricing [Likely]. Mechanical fair value: bear 175,113, base 322,382.80, bull 430,736.36 KRW vs a 100,300 close; the through-cycle DCF drives it and EV/EBITDA gives only 99,724.72 base [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/krx/000270/financials/ratios/). Even the bear case is above the price [Likely], but it rests on mid-cycle margins that the last twelve months contradict. The price is down 24.9% in three months and 30.7% below its 200-day average [Certain].

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
FCF conversion is 95.2% of net income TTM, recovered from 70.0% in FY2025 but below 126.9% in FY2021 [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/krx/000270/financials/cash-flow-statement/). The share count is down 1.02% YoY, a small buyback [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/krx/000270/statistics/). Net cash is large relative to the 38,982.2bn KRW market cap, but its use (payout, capex for EVs) is not evidenced here [Guessing].
