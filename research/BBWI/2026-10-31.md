# Bath & Body Works (BBWI) — Core initiation — 2026-10-31
## 1. Business
Mall- and street-based specialty retailer of fragrance, body care and home fragrance under one brand. Revenue comes from company stores, direct (online) sales, and a growing wholesale and international channel, which now includes Ulta and Amazon [Certain] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/701985/000070198526000029/bbwi-20260801.htm). Sales are seasonal and promotion-led, with the holiday quarter the largest profit pool [Likely].

## 2. Quality
Returns are high but the top line is shrinking. ROIC is 24.5% TTM and has held in the mid-20s for five years (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/bbwi/financials/ratios/) [Certain]. The moat is brand and proprietary product with a vertically controlled supply chain; it is real but the evidence is mixed, because operating margin has fallen from 25.5% (FY2022) to 14.8% TTM and gross margin from 48.9% to 43.0% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/bbwi/financials/income-statement/) [Certain].

Latest quarter net sales fell 2.3%, with stores down 5.4% on fewer transactions; the operating margin rise to 14.2% came mainly from about $80 million of tariff refunds, a one-off [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/701985/000070198526000029/bbwi-20260801.htm) [Certain]. Management's turnaround plan ("Consumer First Formula") shows early signs: body care improving, direct back to growth, wholesale expanding [Likely]. Not yet proven [Guessing]. Peak-quality it is not; durable-but-eroding is fairer [Likely].

## 3. Valuation
The price assumes structural decline. P/E is 5.9x against a 5y range of 6.9x–12.7x, EV/EBITDA 6.5x (at the 5y minimum) and P/FCF 4.5x, versus peer medians of 14.3x, 10.4x and 13.9x (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/bbwi/financials/ratios/) [Certain]. FCF yield is 22.45% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/bbwi/statistics/) [Certain]. The fact sheet's reverse DCF implies year-1 revenue shrinking 19.5%, far worse than the latest quarter [Certain]. Base fair value is 67.98 against a 22.77 close; the bear case of 37.42 is also above the price [Certain].

Caveat: TTM earnings and FCF are flattered by the tariff refunds and a first-quarter litigation settlement gain [company-reported] (same filing URL), so the true run-rate multiples are higher than shown, and the DCF base case probably overstates value [Likely]. The stock is still cheap on a normalised view [Likely].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Gross margin (income statement page, TTM) falls below 40%
- Operating margin (income statement page, TTM) falls below 12%
- Net debt / EBITDA (ratios page) rises above 3.5x
- Latest 10-Q/10-K MD&A reports Stores net sales declining at a worse rate than the prior quarter for two consecutive quarters, with Direct also declining (sec.gov)

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
FCF conversion is 132% of net income TTM, but partly because of the one-offs above (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/bbwi/financials/cash-flow-statement/) [Certain]. Shares are down 5.35% year on year, yet no buyback was made in the year to date and the outlook assumes none [company-reported] (same filing URL), so deleveraging is the priority [Likely]. Leverage of 3.0x net debt / EBITDA is the main balance-sheet constraint; Altman Z is 2.27 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/bbwi/statistics/) [Certain]. Capex is guided up to about $240 million, to real estate and transformation [company-reported] (same filing URL).

## 6. Unusual items
Short interest is 8.73% of float, up 8.2% on the month, and the 14-day RSI is 71.3 after a rally of 14.1% in three months (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/bbwi/statistics/) [Certain]. Next earnings are estimated for Nov 19, 2026, and management guided the coming quarter's earnings sharply lower [Likely] (source: https://www.tipranks.com/news/the-fly/bath-body-works-sees-q3-adjusted-eps-7c-12c-consensus-26c-thefly-news). The filing also flags Middle East conflict effects on transport and input costs [Likely]. Beta is 1.40.
