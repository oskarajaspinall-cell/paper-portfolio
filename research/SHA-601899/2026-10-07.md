# Zijin Mining Group Company Limited (SHA:601899) — Core initiation — 2026-10-07

## 1. Business
Zijin is a diversified miner extracting and processing copper, gold, zinc, lead, silver, lithium and other concentrates/smelted metals from mines in China and overseas (Serbia, DRC, Argentina among others) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sha/601899/company/). Revenue comes from mineral extraction, smelting/processing, metal and jewelry sales, plus environmental services [Certain] (same source).

## 2. Quality
ROIC, ROCE and ROE have all risen sharply over five years (ROIC 13.5%→24.3% TTM; ROE 23.4%→36.6% TTM), and gross/operating/net margins are each up ~8-12pp since FY2021 [Certain] (source: factsheet RA, IS). FCF conversion jumped to 101% TTM from 38% in FY2021, and net debt/EBITDA fell from 1.88x to 0.44x over the same period — Altman Z 3.61 (safe zone) and Piotroski F 7/9 confirm the balance sheet is genuinely stronger, not leverage masking margin gains [Certain] (source: factsheet RA, ST). The improvement is largely commodity-price driven rather than a widening structural moat: Zijin's scale and reserve diversification give it a real cost advantage over single-asset peers, but it remains a price-taker in copper and gold, so returns will compress if metal prices fall [Likely]. Insider ownership is minimal at 0.09% versus 30.5% institutional, limiting the management-alignment signal [Certain] (source: factsheet ST).

## 3. Valuation
At 12.0x P/E and 7.3x EV/EBITDA, Zijin trades below its own five-year minimum on P/E, EV/EBITDA and P/FCF (5y ranges 12.2x-17.4x, 9.6x-11.5x and 16.2x-66.8x respectively), and 25-58% below the peer median (FCX, BHP, SCCO, NEM) across P/E, EV/EBITDA, EV/Sales, P/FCF and P/B [Certain] (source: factsheet RA and peer statistics pages). FCF yield is 8.83% [Certain] (source: factsheet ST). Part of the peer discount is likely structural — A-share miners typically price below US/London peers for capital-mobility and governance reasons [Likely], something the fact sheet cannot quantify. But the more persuasive part of the case is the gap to Zijin's own trailing range: the market is pricing margins to revert below any level seen in the past five years, not merely applying a China discount [Likely].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROIC (ratios page) falls below 15% for two consecutive fiscal years (vs 19.3%/24.3% in FY2025/TTM)
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) rises back above 1.9x, reversing the FY2023-TTM deleveraging
- Gross margin (income statement) falls below 20% (the FY2024 level), signalling the margin expansion was cyclical rather than structural
- FCF conversion, FCF/NI (cash flow statement + income statement), drops below 40% (the FY2022 level)

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
FCF margin rose from 2.6% (FY2021) to 18.2% (TTM) and FCF conversion from 38% to 101%, so growth and the balance sheet are now being funded without added leverage — net debt/EBITDA fell from 1.88x to 0.44x over the same period [Certain] (source: factsheet CF, IS, RA). Shares outstanding rose only 1.17% YoY, so the margin and cash-flow gains are not being diluted away [Certain] (source: factsheet ST). Dividend policy is [data unavailable] on the fact sheet.

## 6. Unusual items
Margin and return gains are concentrated in gold and copper price moves, a macro factor outside management's control [Likely].
