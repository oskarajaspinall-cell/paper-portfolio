# Macro overlay: XP Inc. (XP) — 2026-10-31
## Assessment
**Gate: secondary.** The variable is Brazil's risk premium and the real. Chain: Lula's re-election -> fiscal/Selic path and BRL -> higher required return on BRL earnings plus weaker USD translation -> lower multiple. [Likely]

**Facts.** BRL was 5.0410 per USD on 2026-10-23 (5.2329 on 2026-10-01) (https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=DEXBZUS). That predates the 2026-10-25/26 election result, so the shock is not in the data. US stress is low: HY spread 3.26%, VIX 17.2 (2026-10-29) (https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=BAMLH0A0HYM2). [Certain]

**Pricing.** The price fell from 32.73 to 14.29 and P/E is 7.3x, below its 5y minimum (fact sheet). The market has priced a large premium; our fair value assumes only 3.0%. The post-election BRL and Selic moves are unmeasured. [Likely]

**Inference.** Earnings are not yet hit (Q2 net income +5%). The channel is the discount rate, not near-term earnings.

- **Bull:** fiscal anchor holds, BRL stable, Nov 16 results show flows intact; the multiple recovers toward the 5y median 12.7x.
- **Bear:** BRL falls and Selic rises; the premium widens and flows shift to fixed income; the multiple compresses even if margins hold.
- **Tilt:** toward bear, small. Much is priced, but further political surprises skew down.
