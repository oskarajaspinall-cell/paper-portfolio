# XP Inc. (XP) — Core initiation — 2026-10-31
## 1. Business
XP is a Brazilian investment platform and broker (Capital Markets, reports in BRL, listed in USD). It earns fees and spreads on client assets: brokerage, distribution of funds and structured products, and banking. The latest call cited client assets up 17% YoY and gross revenues up 8% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/xp/transcripts/673737-q2-2026/) [Likely].

## 2. Quality
- Profitability is high: operating margin 30.5% TTM, net margin 28.5%, ROE 22.6% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/xp/financials/ratios/) [Certain]. Margins are broadly stable over five years (operating margin -1.6pp) [Certain].
- ROIC is 2.8% and falling (-2.7pp over five years); for a balance-sheet-heavy broker this is a weak metric, but it signals no rising returns on capital [Likely].
- FCF conversion of 248.5% of net income is flattered by client-flow and working-capital items at a broker; do not treat the FCF margin of 70.9% as a recurring earnings stream [Likely].
- Moat: scale, advisor network and brand in Brazilian retail and affluent investing. Durable but exposed to rate cycles, product-mix shifts to daily-liquidity products (lower revenue per real) and bank competition [Likely].
- Piotroski F is 6 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/xp/statistics/) [Certain].

## 3. Valuation
Price is 14.29 USD after a collapse from the 6-month high of 32.73 (2026-10-12) to a low of 13.78 (2026-10-26) following the Lula election result (sources: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/xp/history/; https://www.barrons.com/articles/brazil-election-stocks-slump-xp-ambev-f730e840) [Certain].
- P/E 7.3x is below its 5y minimum of 8.7x (median 12.7x) and 53% under the peer median of 15.4x [Certain].
- P/B 1.5x is below its 5y minimum (2.0x), 58% under peers [Certain].
- P/FCF 3.0x and FCF yield 33.50% are distorted by flows, as above [Likely].
- Reverse DCF: price implies -3.5% revenue growth in year 1 [Certain].
- Fair value (inform-only, 3.0% Brazil premium): bear 17.98, base 21.15, bull 26.39, so even the bear case is above the price [Likely].
The market is pricing a political and rate regime shock, not a deterioration in reported earnings, which are not yet reflected in results [Guessing].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
