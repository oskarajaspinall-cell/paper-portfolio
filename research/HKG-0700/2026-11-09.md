# Tencent Holdings (HKG:0700) — Core initiation — 2026-11-09
## 1. Business
Chinese internet platform (Internet Content & Information, reports in CNY, quoted in HKD) (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/0700/). [Likely] It earns from games, advertising on its social/messaging network, and fintech and cloud services. [Likely] Most revenue is mainland China, so China policy and the CNY matter. The country split is [data unavailable]: the company site is not on the allowlist (fetch blocked).

## 2. Quality
- Returns are high and rising: ROIC 18.3% TTM vs 13.0% in FY2021; operating margin 33.3% vs 22.1%; gross margin 56.7% vs 43.9% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/0700/financials/ratios/). [Certain]
- The balance sheet is clean: net debt/EBITDA 0.04x; Altman Z 4.44, Piotroski F 6 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/0700/statistics/). [Certain]
- Moat: [Likely] a network effect from the messaging and social base, which feeds games, ads and payments. Margins widening while revenue grows points to pricing power, not just cyclical gain.
- Direction change: FCF margin fell to 21.3% TTM from 26.6% a year earlier, 13.3pp below its 2-year high (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/0700/financials/cash-flow-statement/?p=trailing). [Certain] Operating profit is rising, so the drop is not an earnings problem. [Guessing] Higher AI capex is the likely cause, but the fact sheet does not show capex.

## 3. Valuation
On trailing figures the stock is cheap against its own history and its peers (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/0700/statistics/). [Certain]
- P/E is 14.3x, below its 5y minimum of 14.8x, and 25% under the peer median of 19.1x.
- EV/EBITDA is 10.9x, below its 5y minimum of 13.9x, and 30% under the peer median.
- P/B is 2.7x, also below its 5y range.
- P/FCF is 19.5x, 60% up its 5y range, because FCF has fallen. FCF yield is 5.12%.

The mechanical fair value is flagged unreliable: DCF base is 228.95 HKD, EV/Revenue base is 553.51 HKD, blended base 337.13 HKD against a 419.80 HKD close. The DCF includes a 3.5% owner-set China risk premium. [Likely] Earnings multiples say cheap, the DCF says rich: the price assumes durable earnings without a wider China discount. Weigh the fair value lightly.

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Operating margin (income statement, TTM) falls below 28%.
- ROIC (ratios page, TTM) falls below 14%.
- FCF margin (cash-flow statement, TTM) falls below 15%, meaning the AI spend is not converting to profit.
- Net debt/EBITDA (statistics page) rises above 1.5x.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Shares outstanding fell 1.10% year on year, so buybacks are modest but real (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/0700/statistics/). [Certain] FCF conversion was 95.9% of net income in FY2025 but 71.5% TTM. Leverage is near zero. [Likely] Capital is being redirected to AI infrastructure; the 2026-11-18 results should show whether returns follow.

## 6. Unusual items
- US–China tech friction: Oracle reportedly cancelled a chip-leasing deal with Tencent after US government pressure (source: https://www.reuters.com/world/china/oracle-abandons-7-billion-tencent-ai-deal-under-trump-pressure-ft-reports-2026-11-04/). [Likely] This limits access to AI compute. It is a risk to AI roadmap speed, not to current earnings.
- Sentiment: the 12-month price change is -33.7%. The stock rose 7.2% on 2026-11-05 on 2.8x volume (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/0700/history/). [Certain]
