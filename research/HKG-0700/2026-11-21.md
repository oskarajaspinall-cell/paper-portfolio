# Tencent Holdings (HKG:0700) — Core initiation — 2026-11-21
## 1. Business
Chinese internet platform: WeChat/Weixin social and payments ecosystem monetised through games, advertising, fintech and business services (cloud) [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/0700/). Reports in CNY, quotes in HKD. Revenue by geography/segment is [data unavailable]: the company's own site is not on the allowlist and could not be fetched. Mainland China is plainly the dominant market [Likely]; the macro overlay should treat it as China exposure.

## 2. Quality
Good business with rising returns on five-year endpoints: ROIC 13.0% (FY2021) to 16.5% TTM, operating margin 22.1% to 32.2%, gross margin 43.9% to 56.6% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/0700/financials/ratios/ and .../income-statement/) [Certain]. Net debt/EBITDA is 0.25x, Altman Z 3.86, shares down 1.04% YoY (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/0700/statistics/) [Certain]. Piotroski F is only 4, however.

Recent direction is mixed. ROIC is -0.9pp vs a year ago and below its 2-year high of 18.1%; operating margin is flat. The material break is FCF margin: 28.5% a year ago to 3.6% TTM (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/0700/financials/cash-flow-statement/?p=trailing) [Certain]. Headlines attribute this to a sharp step-up in AI infrastructure capex that also compressed Q3 profit (source: https://www.scmp.com/tech/big-tech/article/3374902/tencent-profit-dips-sixfold-increase-ai-infrastructure-spending-sacrifices-margin) [Likely]. Whether that spend earns its cost of capital is unproven [Guessing]. Moat (network effects of WeChat) [Likely] durable.

## 3. Valuation
Price fell to 344.00 HKD, 25.7% below the 200-day average, 12-month change -44.7%, RSI 26.8 [Certain]. P/E 11.5x vs 5y range 14.8x–21.6x (below minimum) and peers 15.1x; EV/EBITDA 9.1x vs peers 13.9x (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/0700/financials/ratios/) [Certain]. P/FCF 90.8x is far above its range and FCF yield only 1.10%, so the cheapness is on earnings, not cash [Certain]. Mechanical fair value is EV/Revenue only (DCF fails on negative base cash-flow margin): bear 423.59, base 557.55 HKD, with 3.5% China risk premium; a single-method estimate, treat with caution [Likely]. The price assumes the capex step-up does not earn a return, plus a China/chip-curb discount.

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Operating margin (income statement TTM) falls below 28%.
- ROIC TTM (ratios page) falls below 12%.
- FCF margin TTM (cash-flow page, trailing) still below 10% after two further quarterly reports, i.e. spend not converting to cash.
- Revenue growth stays below 8% YoY for two consecutive quarters (income statement).

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Share count is shrinking (-1.04% YoY), supporting per-share value [Certain]. Cash conversion was 95.9% of net income in FY2025 but 12.3% TTM, as capex surged (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/0700/financials/cash-flow-statement/) [Certain]. Balance sheet can fund it (net debt/EBITDA 0.25x). The question is returns on the AI build-out, not solvency.
