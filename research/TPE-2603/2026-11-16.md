# Evergreen Marine Corporation (Taiwan) (TPE:2603) — Core initiation — 2026-11-16
## 1. Business
Taiwan-listed container shipping line (Marine Shipping, Industrials). It earns freight revenue from carrying containers on liner services; profit is set by freight rates, vessel supply and fuel, so earnings are highly cyclical [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tpe/2603/). Revenue split by country/region was not read from a primary source: [data unavailable]. Trans-Pacific and Asia–Europe lanes are probably the largest exposures [Guessing].

## 2. Quality
Not a durable-returns business; returns follow the freight cycle [Likely]. ROIC was 93.8% in FY2021 and 105.1% in FY2022, then 5.5% in FY2023, 25.9% in FY2024, 11.3% in FY2025 and 12.2% TTM (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tpe/2603/financials/ratios/) [Certain]. Operating margin is 20.7% TTM against 23.6% a year earlier and 34.8% at the March 2025 peak (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tpe/2603/financials/income-statement/?p=trailing). The last 12 months fell on every return and margin measure, so the fact sheet's direction flags apply: ROIC down 3.8pp, FCF margin down 2.9pp [Certain]. Strengths: net cash (net debt/EBITDA -0.08x), FCF conversion 129.2% of net income TTM, and a Piotroski F of 5 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tpe/2603/statistics/). No moat evidence: shipping is a commodity service with scale only [Likely].

## 3. Valuation
P/E 7.0x is 79% up its 5y range (median 3.5x) and EV/EBITDA 4.1x is 75% up (median 2.8x), so earnings are priced above the usual trough-to-peak midpoint [Certain]. Against peers (TPE:2609, TPE:2615, ETR:HLAG) P/E is 6% lower and EV/EBITDA 22% higher. P/B 0.9x is only 21% up its range. FCF yield is 18.42%, but P/FCF of 5.4x is above its 5y maximum, which suggests FCF is near a cyclical high [Likely]. The mechanical fair value, based on EV/EBITDA alone (NAV unavailable), is 157.49 TWD base against a 257.50 close (-38.8%) and 233.29 bull (-9.4%); the price sits above even the bull case [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tpe/2603/). The price assumes margins hold near the current 20.7% rather than mean-revert [Likely]. Trend is strong: +19.0% above the 200-day, +38.5% in 12 months.
