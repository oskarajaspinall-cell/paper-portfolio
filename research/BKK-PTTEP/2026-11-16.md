## 2. Quality
Returns are good but cyclical and below the 2022-23 peak. ROIC is 12.7% TTM vs a 15.9% FY2023 high and 10.5% in FY2025 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/bkk/PTTEP/financials/ratios/) [Certain]. Gross margin of 77.4% shows a low-cost asset base, though the 5y trend is falling [Certain]. Direction change: operating margin is down over 5 years but up over the last 12 months (37.7% to 43.1%), which looks like price/volume recovery rather than structural change [Likely]. Cash conversion is the weak point: FCF/NI is 64.2% TTM vs about 100% in FY2023-24, and FCF margin is 13.8% vs 31.0% at its 2-year high, consistent with heavy capex [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/bkk/PTTEP/financials/cash-flow-statement/?p=trailing). Net debt/EBITDA is 0.26x, so the balance sheet is strong [Certain]. A durable moat is thin: reserves and state-linked access to Thai concessions, not pricing power [Likely].
