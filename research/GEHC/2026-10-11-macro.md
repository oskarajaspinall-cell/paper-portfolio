# Macro overlay: GE HealthCare Technologies Inc. (GEHC) — 2026-10-11
## Assessment
Chain: real yields → DCF discount rate → fair-value multiple; HY credit spreads → cost of debt → refinancing, for a company that just added leverage. [Likely] **(inference)**

Facts: the 10y real yield (TIPS, DFII10) is 2.87% (2026-10-08), +0.56pp/3m; the 10y Treasury (DGS10) is 5.22%, +0.68pp/3m (fred.stlouisfed.org, id=DFII10/DGS10, 2026-10-08). The 10y breakeven (T10YIE) is flat (2.33%, -0.04pp/1m), so the yield rise looks real-rate/term-premium driven, not an inflation surprise. [Certain] The HY credit spread (BAMLH0A0HYM2) widened +0.45pp/3m to 3.15% (2026-10-08). [Certain] The fact sheet's own DCF already uses today's 5.22% risk-free rate, so the current level is priced into the $59.13 base fair value; the risk is a further rise, not yet confirmed either way (proxy: 2y yield 4.75% sits above effective fed funds 3.88%, consistent with more tightening priced than delivered — a proxy, not a certainty). **(inference)**

Fundamentals already show rising net debt/EBITDA (2.36x TTM) ahead of the $945m cash-funded Sofie acquisition (Business Wire, 2026-10-05) and falling margins — macro here is an amplifier, not the driver.

**Bull**: real yields/spreads roll over → discount rate eases → fair value drifts up, narrowing the current 10% premium to base fair value.
**Bear**: real yields and HY spreads keep rising → discount rate and financing cost rise → fair value falls further below the $65.74 close; refinancing the enlarged debt load gets costlier.
**Tilt**: toward bear, moderate — the 3-month rate/spread move is large and stacks on a fundamentals-driven leverage concern, though GEHC's revenue has no direct rate sensitivity. [Likely]
