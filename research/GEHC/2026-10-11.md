# GE HealthCare Technologies Inc. (GEHC) — Core initiation — 2026-10-11

## 1. Business
GEHC designs, makes and services medical imaging and care equipment: CT, MRI, ultrasound, X-ray and molecular-imaging systems (Imaging); patient monitoring, anesthesia and maternal-infant devices (Patient Care Solutions); contrast media and radiopharma tracers (Pharmaceutical Diagnostics); plus enterprise imaging/AI software. Revenue mixes capital-equipment sales with recurring service contracts and consumables. [Likely] (source: https://www.gehealthcare.com/about)

## 2. Quality
Moat rests on a large installed base tied to recurring service/consumable revenue (8,900 field engineers cited) and PDx's regulated, specialist supply chain. [Likely] (source: SEC 10-K, gehc-20251231.htm, Item 1) The 10-K names Siemens Healthineers, Philips, United Imaging, Mindray and Canon as primary competitors, with no backlog or customer-concentration disclosure, so the moat looks contested, particularly from lower-cost Chinese entrants in imaging equipment. [Likely] (same source)
Returns are mixed: ROIC is flat over 5y (11.8% TTM) but ROCE is falling (-3.3pp to 10.3%), operating margin is falling (-1.0pp to 14.0%) and net margin is falling (-2.7pp to 9.3%). [Certain] (source: fact sheet Quality table) Net debt/EBITDA has risen to 2.36x TTM from near-zero in FY2021. [Certain] (same source) Altman Z-score is 1.92 — the distress grey zone — with Piotroski F-score 5/9. [Certain] (source: fact sheet Other table) A decent, not exceptional, business with rising leverage and margin drift.

## 3. Valuation
At 15.1x TTM P/E GEHC sits near the bottom of its own 5y range (11th percentile) and 25% below the peer median (20.3x; PHG, SYK, MDT, BSX). [Certain] (source: fact sheet Valuation table) EV/EBITDA (10.6x) and EV/Sales (1.8x) are also below peer medians (-17%/-49%); P/FCF (18.8x) is roughly in line (+6%) and P/B (2.7x) is below its own 5y minimum but above peer median. [Certain] (same source) The discount looks partly explained by lower, falling margins rather than pure mispricing. [Likely]
Base fair value is $59.13 against a last close of $65.74 — price is already 10.1% above base fair value, with bear/bull at $44.68/$74.31. [Certain] (source: fact sheet Fair value table) The reverse DCF implies ~8.3% year-1 revenue growth fading to terminal, optimistic given the margin/leverage trends above. [Likely] (same source) Not clearly cheap; a mediocre business priced for a reasonable recovery. [Likely]

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) rises above 3.0x — leverage thesis breaks
- Operating margin (income statement) falls below 13% (TTM: 14.0%)
- ROIC (ratios page) falls below 10% (TTM: 11.8%)
- Gross margin (income statement) falls below 38% (TTM: 39.5%), signalling Chinese-competitor price pressure

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
FCF conversion (FCF/NI) has risen to 79.5% TTM from 60.5% in FY2021. [Certain] (source: fact sheet Quality table) The board raised the quarterly dividend 14% to $0.04/share in September 2026. [Certain] (source: fact sheet News & sentiment, https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260922850951/en/GE-HealthCare-announces-cash-dividend-increase-for-third-quarter-of-2026/) On October 5, 2026 GEHC agreed to buy Sofie Biosciences, a US PET-radiopharma CMO, for $945m cash, funded from the balance sheet. [Certain] (source: fact sheet News & sentiment, https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20261005273717/en/GE-HealthCare-to-Acquire-SOFIE-Biosciences-Establishing-a-Final-Mile-Footprint-for-PET-Radiopharmaceutical-Supply-in-the-U.S./) This adds to an already-rising net debt/EBITDA (2.36x TTM) — see trigger 1. Shares outstanding fell only 0.55% YoY: buybacks are modest. [Certain] (source: fact sheet Other table)

## 6. Unusual items
The $945m Sofie Biosciences acquisition (above) is a real, near-term capital-allocation event that raises financial leverage ahead of the thesis check-ins. [Certain] Altman Z-score of 1.92 sits in the distress grey zone and is flagged given the leverage trend; not disqualifying alone, but it narrows the margin for error. [Likely] (source: fact sheet Other table) Institutional ownership is high at 93.6% with short interest a modest 3.76% of float, down 10.8% month-on-month — no crowding or distress signal there. [Certain] (source: fact sheet News & sentiment / Other tables)
