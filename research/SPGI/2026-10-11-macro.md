# Macro overlay: S&P Global Inc. (SPGI) — 2026-10-11
## Assessment
Chain: long-term interest rates → (a) higher CAPM discount rate in SPGI's own DCF fair value, (b) tighter financial conditions → lower bond-issuance volumes → lower Ratings-segment transaction revenue. Secondary, not primary: the core moat (NRSRO status, index-licensing lock-in) is macro-independent [Certain].

Facts: 10y Treasury yield 5.22% on 2026-10-08, up 68bp/3m; 10y real yield (TIPS) 2.87%, up 56bp/3m (FRED DGS10/DFII10, 2026-10-08). HY credit spread 3.15%, up 45bp/3m but still low historically — no issuance-freeze signal yet (FRED BAMLH0A0HYM2, 2026-10-08).

Pricing (proxy, inference): the 2y yield (4.75%) sits 87bp above effective fed funds (3.88%), consistent with the market pricing further tightening or a higher terminal rate rather than near-term cuts [Likely] **(inference)** (FRED DGS2/DFF, 2026-10-08).

The fact sheet's own DCF already uses today's 5.22% risk-free rate, so base fair value ($262.59, -36% vs the $407.82 close) already reflects the higher discount rate [Certain] (factsheet). Open question: do rates keep rising (bear: more compression, softer issuance into Oct 27 earnings) or stabilize/ease (bull: relief lifts fair value, issuance recovers).

**Bull**: yields plateau, breakevens contained → discount rate eases → DCF fair value rises; issuance recovers → Ratings revenue accelerates.
**Bear**: yields extend higher, HY spreads widen → fair value compresses further; issuance windows narrow → Ratings revenue decelerates into the Q3 print.
**Tilt**: moderate toward bear — the 3-month yield move is large and not yet reversing, and SPGI's 12m underperformance vs SPY (-31.1pp, factsheet) is consistent with ongoing discount-rate repricing **(inference)**.
