# S&P Global Inc. (SPGI) — Core initiation — 2026-10-11

## 1. Business
S&P Global earns subscription, transaction and asset-linked fees across four core divisions after the mid-2026 Mobility spin-off: Ratings (credit ratings on debt issuance), Market Intelligence (data/analytics subscriptions), Commodity Insights/Energy (Platts price benchmarks) and Indices (S&P Dow Jones Indices licensing, fees tied to AUM in indexed products) [Likely] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/64040/000006404026000013/spgi-20251231.htm).

## 2. Quality
Ratings' NRSRO status is a regulatory barrier new entrants cannot easily replicate; Indices benefit from switching-cost lock-in once products (ETFs, options) are built on a benchmark [Certain] (source: 10-K, as above). Cboe just signed a 25-year extension of its exclusive S&P 500 options licence to 2051, evidencing that lock-in [Certain] (source: fact sheet headline, 2026-09-29, https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/cboe-and-sp-dow-jones-indices-sign-25-year-extension-of-exclusive-licensing-agreement-through-2051-302892397.html). Margins are high and stable: TTM operating margin 43.0%, net margin 30.5%, FCF margin 34.6%, FCF conversion 113.2% of net income [Certain] (factsheet). Headline ROIC/ROE have collapsed since FY2021 (ROIC 88.9%→11.1%; ROE 73.4%→14.3% TTM) [Certain] (factsheet), but this is mechanical: the 2022 IHS Markit merger enlarged the capital base [Likely]. Net debt/EBITDA rose from net cash to 1.43x [Certain] (factsheet), modest against >100% FCF conversion. Mid-2026 Mobility spin-off removes a smaller, cyclical segment, likely raising the remaining group's margin and recurring-revenue mix [Likely] (source: SEC 8-K, 2026-07-02, item 2.01, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/64040/000110465926080005/0001104659-26-080005-index.htm).

## 3. Valuation
Every multiple sits below SPGI's own 5-year range: P/E 24.8x vs 33.6–53.1x, EV/EBITDA 16.2x vs 22.6–26.9x, EV/Sales 8.2x vs 11.0–14.0x, P/FCF 21.6x vs 27.7–43.4x [Certain] (factsheet). Versus peers (MCO, MSCI, ICE, FDS) it is cheap-to-in-line too: P/E −4%, EV/EBITDA −13%, EV/Sales −21%, P/FCF +0%, P/B −25% vs peer medians [Certain] (factsheet). The DCF/FCFE fair value says the opposite: base $262.59 vs the $407.82 close (−35.6%), bear $151.73 (−62.8%), bull $435.59 (+6.8%) — price sits near the bull case [Certain] (factsheet). A separate P/E-based fair value ($551.67–$658.06) implies the stock is cheap; the fact sheet does not reconcile the two methods and neither can be discarded [Certain] (factsheet). The reverse-DCF implies 19.5% year-1 revenue growth fading to terminal, aggressive against margins near multi-year highs [Certain] (factsheet). Net: multiples say cheap, intrinsic DCF says rich — a genuine conflict, not a clean signal.

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Net margin (income statement page) falls below 25% (the FY2023 trough) for two consecutive quarters
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) rises above 2.5x
- FCF conversion, FCF/net income (cash-flow and income-statement pages) falls below 90% for a full fiscal year
- 10-K/10-Q segment disclosure shows Ratings or Indices losing share (e.g. a named competitor win against an NRSRO rating or an index mandate)

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Shares outstanding fell 2.79% YoY, consistent with ongoing buybacks [Certain] (factsheet). TTM FCF margin of 34.6% and FCF conversion of 113.2% of net income fund both buybacks and the dividend; the board declared a Q4 2026 dividend on 2026-09-29 [Certain] (source: fact sheet headline, https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/sp-global-declares-fourth-quarter-dividend-302893412.html). Net debt/EBITDA of 1.43x is low enough that cash generation, not leverage, funds capital return [Certain] (factsheet). The Mobility disposal (SEC 8-K, 2026-07-02) simplifies the capital base to four higher-margin divisions [Likely] (source as above).

## 6. Unusual items
Mobility segment spin-off executed mid-2026 (SEC 8-K, item 2.01, filed 2026-07-02) changes the revenue mix away from automotive data; full financial detail of the separation was not in the excerpts read [Likely] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/64040/000110465926080005/0001104659-26-080005-index.htm). New Chief Legal Officer (Thomas Kim) starts 2026-10-19, replacing a retiring incumbent — a routine succession, not flagged as thesis-relevant [Certain] (source: fact sheet headline, https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/sp-global-appoints-new-chief-legal-officer-302903448.html). Short interest is low (2.2% of float, down 7.8% month-on-month) and institutional ownership is high (89.4%) [Certain] (factsheet).
