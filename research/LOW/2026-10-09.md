# Lowe's Companies, Inc. (LOW) — Core initiation — 2026-10-09

## 1. Business
Lowe's is the #2 US home-improvement retailer: 1,759 stores plus >540 Pro branch locations and >120 supply-chain facilities, selling to DIY (>60% of sales) and Pro customers in-store, online and via job-site delivery [Certain] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/60667/000006066726000029/low-20260130.htm). Margin is boosted by private brands (Kobalt, allen+roth) [Certain] (same source).

## 2. Quality
Scale (store density, supply chain) and a growing Pro ecosystem are the moat: management cites same-day/next-day appliance delivery nationwide and the only "true digital marketplace" among US home-improvement peers, with five straight quarters of positive comps and share gains while competitors are "down double digits" [Likely] (source: Goldman Sachs conference transcript, https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/low/transcripts/748321-goldman-sachs-global-consumer-and-retail-conference/). The 2025 debt-funded acquisitions of Foundation Building Materials and Artisan Design Group extend reach into larger Pro/commercial jobs, mirroring Home Depot's SRS deal [Likely] (source: 10-K, as above).
But returns on capital are decaying fast: ROIC fell from 38.5% (FY2022) to 22.4% TTM and ROCE from 48.6% to 29.5% TTM (source: ratios page, https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/low/financials/ratios/) [Certain] — largely acquisitions inflating invested capital faster than earnings. Operating margin is flat-to-down (-0.8pp/5y), net margin -1.1pp (source: income statement, https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/low/financials/income-statement/) [Certain]. Returns stay above cost of capital, so the moat holds, but the trend is wrong-way.

## 3. Valuation
LOW trades below its own 5-year multiple range on every metric shown (P/E 16.0x vs 16.4-22.6x range; EV/EBITDA 11.4x vs 12.2-15.6x; EV/Sales 1.6x vs 1.7-2.2x; P/FCF 15.1x vs 19.1-20.4x) and 19-32% below the peer median (HD, FND, TSCO, WSM) (source: ratios/statistics pages, https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/low/financials/ratios/, https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/low/statistics/) [Certain]. That screens cheap. But the fact sheet's own DCF/P/E blend puts base fair value at $183.32 vs the $188.85 close — only -2.9% apart, i.e. essentially fairly valued (bear -30.5%, bull +11.4%) (source: fact sheet fair-value table) [Certain]. The reverse DCF implies the market is only pricing ~2.9% year-1 revenue growth fading to terminal — modest, consistent with management's flat-macro message, not a mispricing. The multiple discount to history/peers looks explained by the leverage step-up and slowing housing cycle rather than a free lunch [Likely].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROIC (ratios page) falls below 15% on a trailing-twelve-month basis, signalling the Pro acquisitions are structurally diluting returns rather than a transition cost.
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios/balance sheet) rises above 3.52x (current FY2026 level) instead of trending toward management's 2.75x target, indicating the deleveraging plan is failing.
- FCF margin (cash flow/income statement) falls below 6% for two consecutive fiscal years, showing cash generation is not funding the stated priorities.
- Gross margin (income statement) falls below 32%, indicating private-brand/Pro mix is not offsetting tariff or promotional pressure.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Cash conversion is strong: FCF/NI is 105.4% TTM and rising (+17.1pp over 5y) (source: cash-flow/income statement) [Certain]. Management's stated priority order is (1) organic/Pro investment, (2) debt reduction to a 2.75x leverage target (expected mid-2027), (3) the dividend (Dividend Aristocrat, maintained), and (4) buyback resumption only once leverage is achieved (source: Goldman Sachs conference transcript, as above) [Likely]. Buybacks are effectively paused while net debt/EBITDA sits at 3.52x on a fiscal-year basis vs that 2.75x target (source: ratios page) [Certain] — a shareholder-return headwind not present at Home Depot.

## 6. Unusual items
Housing-cycle exposure is explicit: management expects H2 to "mirror" a weak H1 with "no improvement," citing elevated mortgage rates and consumer caution, though it says it can "grow in any macro environment" [Likely] (source: Goldman Sachs transcript, as above). A 20-minute drone-delivery pilot with DoorDash/Alphabet's Wing launched Sept 24, 2026 is immaterial to size but the stock fell on the day despite the announcement, suggesting the market is focused on demand, not fulfilment innovation [Likely] (source: https://www.tipranks.com/news/lowes-stock-low-drops-despite-new-drone-delivery-pilot-with-tech-giants). Short interest is low (1.68% of float) and institutional ownership high (79.9%), so this is not a crowded short (source: statistics page) [Certain].

**Decision: AVOID.** The discount to history and peers is real but the fact sheet's own fair value (-2.9% vs close) shows little margin of safety once the leverage step-up and decaying ROIC/ROCE trend are priced in; conviction falls short of the bar needed to buy against a falling-returns, paused-buyback setup in a soft housing cycle.
