# Macro overlay: Lowe's Companies, Inc. (LOW) — 2026-10-09
## Assessment
Chain: 10y Treasury/real yields -> mortgage rates -> existing-home turnover & big-ticket renovation spend -> LOW revenue growth; same 10y yield -> DCF discount rate -> fair value (fact sheet uses 5.28% risk-free, https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=DGS10, 2026-10-07) [Certain].

Facts (FRED, 2026-10-07/08): 10y Treasury 5.28% (+0.50pp 1m, +0.72pp 3m); 10y real yield (DFII10) 2.92% (+0.49pp 1m, +0.61pp 3m); 2y Treasury 4.77% (+0.40pp 1m); fed funds 3.88%; 10y breakeven 2.35% (+0.12pp 3m, flat 1m); HY spread 3.09% (+0.39pp 3m, still low); VIX 15.08 (benign) [Certain].

Market pricing **(inference)**: 2y yield ~89bp above fed funds proxies a higher-for-longer path, not near-term cuts [Likely]. Flat breakevens suggest real-rate/term-premium-driven tightening, not repriced inflation [Likely]. Management's Sept 15 "no improvement, elevated mortgage rates" guidance already reflects weak housing, but most of the yield move (+50bp 10y, +49bp real) came in the trailing month, likely after guidance and this fair value were set — incremental tightening not yet fully reflected **(inference)** [Likely].

Bull: yields roll over (10y toward ~4.5%), mortgage rates ease, turnover stabilizes; revenue beats the priced ~2.9% growth and fair value drifts toward the $210 bull case.
Bear: yields keep rising, turnover stays depressed; revenue undershoots and a higher discount rate pushes fair value toward/below the $131 bear case.
Tilt: small toward bear — yields accelerate just as the stock sits ~3% below fair value, but this is the same housing-rate risk management already flags; it is not the dominant driver of the AVOID call (ROIC decay, paused buybacks are).
