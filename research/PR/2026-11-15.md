# Permian Resources Corporation (PR) — Core initiation — 2026-11-15
## 1. Business
Independent oil and gas exploration and production company (Energy) concentrated in the Delaware Basin of the Permian, selling oil, NGLs and natural gas at market prices (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/pr/). Growth comes from drilling and from bolt-on and grassroots acreage deals next to its existing core position (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1658566/000165856626000124/pr-20260930.htm). All operations are in the US; no foreign-country exposure [Likely].

## 2. Quality
Returns are decent for a commodity producer, not moated. TTM ROIC is 12.0% vs 9.3% a year earlier and ROCE 13.5% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/pr/financials/ratios/?p=trailing) [Certain]. Net debt/EBITDA is 0.71x vs 1.66x in FY2021 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/pr/financials/ratios/) [Certain]. Piotroski F is 7 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/pr/statistics/) [Certain].

Direction: operating margin of 35.4% is 8.5pp below its 2-year high but flat year on year; FCF margin is falling over five fiscal years but rising over the last 12 months (13.6% to 16.2%) [Certain]. The 5-year fall reflects the shift from a cash-harvesting phase to growth spending; I read the recent rise as real but price-driven [Likely].

Durable edge is limited to low-cost core inventory and scale; the price taker position means returns follow oil [Likely]. The company is now investment grade at all three rating agencies, which should lower funding costs [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1658566/000165856626000124/pr-20260930.htm).

## 3. Valuation
P/E is 12.0x (67% of its 5y range; +11% vs the peer median), EV/EBITDA 5.1x (81% of range; -3% vs peers), P/FCF 18.8x (+58% vs peers) and FCF yield 5.33% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/pr/statistics/) [Certain]. Peers: FANG, DVN, EOG, OXY, CTRA. The stock is priced at the top of its own history and not cheap against peers on cash flow.

The mechanical fair value (own-history EV/EBITDA only; reserve NAV is [data unavailable]) is 10.62 base and 23.87 bull against a 23.05 close. The price therefore assumes bull-case multiples and sustained oil prices [Likely]. The case rests on trend, not discount: the close is 10.9% above the 200-day average, 12-month change is +73.2% and relative strength vs SPY is positive (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/pr/history/) [Certain]. The fair-value method is crude for an E&P, so I would not lean on its sign alone [Guessing].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROIC (ratios page, TTM) falls below 9%.
- Net debt / EBITDA (ratios page, TTM) rises above 1.5x.
- FCF margin (trailing cash-flow page) falls below 10%.
- Operating margin (trailing income-statement page) falls below 28%.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
FCF conversion is 64.6% of net income TTM vs 143.7% in FY2021, because growth capex and acquisitions absorb cash (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/pr/financials/ratios/) [Certain]. Nine-month 2026 acquisitions were about $1.1 billion and the base dividend is $0.16 a quarter [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1658566/000165856626000124/pr-20260930.htm). Shares outstanding rose 13.52% YoY (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/pr/statistics/); part is the Class C exchange that removed the noncontrolling interest, but I have not split it from true issuance [Guessing].
