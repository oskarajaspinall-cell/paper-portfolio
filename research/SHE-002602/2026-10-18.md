# Zhejiang Century Huatong Group Co.,Ltd (SHE:002602) — Core initiation — 2026-10-18

## 1. Business
Century Huatong operates across auto-parts manufacturing, internet/mobile gaming and AI cloud data services in China and internationally [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/she/002602/). Gaming now drives the economics: gross margin has risen to 71.5% TTM and operating margin to 21.0% TTM, far above levels typical of the legacy auto-parts base [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/she/002602/financials/income-statement/).

## 2. Quality
Returns have improved sharply and consistently: ROIC rose from 1.8% (FY2021) to 33.1% TTM and ROCE from 2.3% to 24.0% TTM, with net margin recovering from a FY2022 loss year (-61.8%) to 17.4% TTM [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/she/002602/financials/ratios/, https://stockanalysis.com/quote/she/002602/financials/income-statement/). FCF conversion is strong (109.3% of net income TTM) and the balance sheet carries net cash (net debt/EBITDA -1.23x) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/she/002602/financials/cash-flow-statement/). This is a genuine margin and returns inflection, not a stable multi-year record: five of the nine TTM metrics shown moved by double-digit percentage points versus FY2021, so the data window is short [Likely]. Chinese gaming publishing is hit-driven and subject to content-approval processes that are not disclosed on the sources available here, so durability of the current margin level cannot be verified from stockanalysis.com data alone [Guessing].

## 3. Valuation
The stock trades below its own 5-year range on P/E (11.9x vs 22.2x-72.4x) and EV/EBITDA (7.9x vs 9.2x-63.9x), and below the peer median (SHE:002555, SHE:002558, SHE:002624, NTES) on every multiple shown: P/E -20%, EV/EBITDA -22%, EV/Sales -40%, P/FCF -11%, P/B -10% [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/she/002602/financials/ratios/). The reverse DCF implies the market is pricing in only 1.2% revenue growth next year, fading to terminal growth — i.e. the price assumes the FY2024-25 margin and return improvement does not continue [Certain] (source: fact sheet reverse-DCF). Base fair value of CNY 22.04 is 79.7% above the last close of CNY 12.27; the bear case fair value is negative, a modelling artifact from compounding a margin-reversion assumption through the DCF rather than a literal floor, so it should not be read as a liquidation value [Likely].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Gross margin (income statement page) falls back below 60%, reversing the FY2024-25 step-up and signalling the game portfolio is aging without replacement hits.
- ROIC (ratios page) falls back below 10%, undoing the FY2024-25 improvement and indicating the returns inflection was transitory.
- Net margin (income statement page) falls back into single digits, repeating the pre-2024 profitability pattern rather than the TTM 17.4%.
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) turns positive beyond 0.5x, indicating the net-cash position is being spent down without a commensurate return.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Cash generation is strong and self-funded: FCF margin is 19.0% TTM and FCF conversion is 109.3% of net income, both near 5-year highs [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/she/002602/financials/cash-flow-statement/). The balance sheet is net cash (net debt/EBITDA -1.23x TTM), giving flexibility for buybacks, bolt-on studio acquisitions or debt-funded growth without near-term refinancing risk [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/she/002602/financials/ratios/). Shares outstanding grew a modest 1.71% YoY, a small dilution headwind rather than a red flag [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/she/002602/statistics/). Dividend data is not shown on the sources used [data unavailable].

## 6. Unusual items
FY2022 shows a large reported loss (net margin -61.8%), most likely a one-off (e.g. impairment) rather than an ongoing feature, but the cause is not disclosed on the pages used here [Guessing] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/she/002602/financials/income-statement/). Institutional ownership is comparatively low at 19.8% and insider ownership 10.49%, worth monitoring for liquidity/governance but not yet material to the thesis [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/she/002602/statistics/).
