# Zoetis Inc. (ZTS) — Core initiation — 2026-10-07
## 1. Business
Zoetis develops, manufactures and sells medicines, vaccines and diagnostics for companion animals (dogs, cats, horses) and livestock (cattle, swine, poultry, fish) across 100+ countries, monetizing via branded prescription parasiticides, vaccines and diagnostics platforms sold to vets and producers [Certain] (source: https://www.zoetis.com/).

## 2. Quality
Returns are high: ROIC 27.3% TTM (5y range 26-30%), gross margin 71.7% TTM, operating margin 38.1% TTM, both rising over 5y (source: research/ZTS/factsheet-2026-10-07.md, RA/IS). But the moat is being tested: management cut FY2026 guidance to organic revenue growth of -3% to -1% and adjusted net income decline of 9%-5% (~$500m below prior guidance), citing clinic visit declines and value-conscious pet owners [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/zts/transcripts/662326-q2-2026/, Q2 2026 call). US dermatology in-clinic share fell to ~86%, down 5pp sequentially and 10pp YoY, as new entrants used discounting and bundling [Certain] (same source) — evidence competitors can take share cyclically. Livestock's +11% Q2 growth is partly transitory screwworm demand, expected to moderate to mid-single digits in H2 [Likely] (same source). ROE of 64.9% TTM is flattered by net debt/EBITDA rising to 1.86x from 1.03x (FY2021) (source: factsheet RA/BS/IS) — leverage, not operations, drives the equity return. Pricing power has weakened [Likely].

## 3. Valuation
At 71.33 USD (2026-10-06 close), ZTS trades at 11.6x P/E and 9.1x EV/EBITDA, roughly 25-28% below its own 5-year range minimum (20.7x/14.9x) and 36-68% below peer medians (IDXX, ELAN, MRK, PAHC) (source: factsheet, RA/P:IDXX/P:ELAN/P:MRK/P:PAHC). FCF yield is 7.86% (source: ST). The stock is down 50.6% over 12 months and 38.9% over 6 months, 26.9% below its 200-day MA (source: factsheet HI/ST). The depressed multiple already prices in a meaningful, multi-year deceleration in companion-animal growth and some permanent share loss, not just a transitory cut — the valuation gap to peers is wider than the -9% to -5% near-term earnings cut alone would justify, suggesting the market also doubts the durability of returns in Section 2. P/B at 9.4x is the one multiple above peer median (+56%), reflecting leverage rather than cheapness.

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Gross margin (income statement page) falls below 68% (vs. 71.7% TTM), signalling structural pricing power loss, not cyclical softness.
- US companion-animal in-clinic share (reported quarterly on earnings calls) continues falling past the -10pp YoY already seen in dermatology, into a second consecutive quarter.
- ROIC (ratios page) drops below 24% (vs. 27.3% TTM), indicating returns compression beyond the current guidance cut.
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios/balance sheet page) rises above 2.5x, constraining capital return capacity.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
FCF margin is 24.3% TTM and FCF conversion (FCF/NI) is 87.8% TTM, both near 5-year highs (source: factsheet CF/IS) [Certain]. Net debt/EBITDA has risen steadily from 1.03x (FY2021) to 1.86x (TTM) (source: factsheet BS/IS/RA) [Certain] — likely reflecting buybacks against a falling share count (shares down 4.22% YoY, source: ST) rather than distress, given Altman Z of 5.87 and Piotroski F of 6 (source: ST), both healthy.

## 6. Unusual items
Short interest is 4.22% of float, up 3.6% vs. the prior month, with heavy bearish options flow noted on 2026-09-24 (2,600 puts, 1.4x expected volume, concentrated in Oct/Nov-26 65-strike puts) — a market signal of continued near-term skepticism, not a fundamental fact [Guessing] (source: factsheet News & sentiment; headline https://www.tipranks.com/news/the-fly/zoetis-put-volume-heavy-and-directionally-bearish-thefly-news-5). Institutional ownership is high at 98.6% with insiders at 0.10% (source: factsheet, ST). Next earnings Nov 5, 2026 will be the first full quarter test of the cut guidance [Certain] (source: factsheet, ST/OV).
