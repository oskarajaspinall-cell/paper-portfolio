# Zoetis (ZTS) — Core initiation — 2026-11-08

## 1. Business
Global animal-health company: companion-animal (dermatology, parasiticides, pain/mAbs, diagnostics) is about 67.3% of nine-month revenue and livestock about 31.6% `[company-reported]` (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1555280/000155528026000050/zts-20260930.htm). The US is about 50.6% of revenue `[company-reported]` (https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1555280/000155528026000050/zts-20260930.htm). No single foreign country reaches 10%; the largest, Brazil, is about 4.3% `[company-reported]` (https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1555280/000155528026000050/zts-20260930.htm), so no country-risk flag. [Certain]

## 2. Quality
Returns remain exceptional: TTM ROIC 26.4%, ROCE 29.0%, operating margin 37.9%, gross margin 71.6% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/zts/financials/ratios/). Moat: installed vet-clinic relationships, regulatory approvals and brand/clinical data in a category where pets' care is non-discretionary at the margin. [Likely]

Direction change: ROCE is 2.9pp below its 2-year high, but versus a year earlier it is up 2.5pp; ROIC is -0.5pp, operating margin +0.3pp and FCF margin -0.7pp, all flat (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/zts/financials/ratios/?p=trailing). So the ROCE gap is a peak-to-now drift, not a deterioration in profitability yet. [Likely]

The moat is, however, being tested. The 10-Q says companion-animal revenue fell on softer demand and heightened competition in the key dermatology franchise, generic competition on Convenia and Cerenia, and lower Librela and vaccine sales; dermatology fell about 13.5% in nine months `[company-reported]` (https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1555280/000155528026000050/zts-20260930.htm). Reuters reports cautious 2027 planning (https://www.reuters.com/business/healthcare-pharmaceuticals/zoetis-plans-cautiously-2027-pet-owners-cut-back-vet-visits-shares-fall-2026-11-05/). [Likely] Livestock and diagnostics are growing. FY adjusted EPS guidance was raised (source: https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20261104076504/en/Zoetis-Announces-Third-Quarter-2026-Results-Highlights-Progress-Advancing-Innovation-Across-Diversified-Portfolio-and-Narrows-Full-Year-2026-Revenue-Guidance-Raises-Full-Year-2026-Adjusted-EPS-Guidance/). [Likely]

## 3. Valuation
Price 79.40 USD (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/zts/history/). P/E 13.4x, EV/EBITDA 9.9x, P/FCF 14.4x, all below the 5y minimum (20.7x, 14.9x, 24.3x) and below peer medians (45.6x, 14.6x, 36.2x); FCF yield 6.93% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/zts/statistics/). Reverse DCF implies 0.8% year-1 revenue growth fading to terminal. Model fair value: bear 76.01, base 125.91, bull 147.48; the DCF leg alone is 93.14 base. The price is just above the bear case, i.e. it assumes stagnation in a business that is still growing in livestock and diagnostics. The risk is that the companion-animal pricing power is structurally impaired, in which case history-based multiples overstate fair value. [Likely] Trend is poor: 12-month change -34.1%, below the 200-day average; this is information, not a thesis input. [Certain]

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Operating margin (TTM, income statement trailing page) falls below 34%.
- ROIC (TTM, ratios trailing page) falls below 22%.
- Net debt/EBITDA (statistics page) rises above 2.5x.
- Companion-animal revenue declining for two further consecutive quarters, with dermatology share loss cited in the 10-Q MD&A.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
FCF conversion 89.2% of net income and FCF margin 23.5% TTM. Shares are down 5.45% year on year, so buybacks are material. Net debt/EBITDA has risen from 1.03x (FY2021) to 1.92x, partly funding buybacks; interest expense rose on higher debt `[company-reported]` (https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1555280/000155528026000050/zts-20260930.htm). Leverage is acceptable but rising while earnings are flat. [Likely]

## 6. Unusual items
- SEC delta: the 10-Q dropped the tariff and supply-chain risk factors, which lowers disclosed trade risk. [Likely] Fiscal-year alignment removed the international one-month reporting lag and shifted price-increase timing, so year-on-year comparisons are distorted. Restructuring charges and an impairment on PetMedix in-process R&D appear. [Certain] (same 10-Q URL)
- Short interest 4.32% of float, benign (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/zts/statistics/). New board member from 1 December (source: https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20261013131007/en/Zoetis-Appoints-Iris-Knobloch-to-its-Board-of-Directors/); immaterial.
