# Zoetis (ZTS) — Core initiation — 2026-11-23
## 1. Business
Largest pure-play animal-health company: sells vaccines, parasiticides, dermatology (Apoquel, Cytopoint) and monoclonal antibodies to vets and livestock producers, plus diagnostics. Nine-month 2026 revenue was 67.3% companion animal and 31.6% livestock; the U.S. segment was 50.6% [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1555280/000155528026000050/zts-20260930.htm). China was 2.6% of revenue [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1555280/000155528026000050/zts-20260930.htm), so no single foreign country is material. [Certain]

## 2. Quality
Returns are exceptional: TTM ROIC 26.6%, operating margin 37.9%, gross margin 71.6%, FCF margin 23.5% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/zts/financials/ratios/). [Certain] The moat is vet-clinic relationships, regulatory approvals and a brand-led dermatology franchise. [Likely]

The moat is now being tested. The 10-Q says companion-animal revenue fell on softer demand, "an increasingly competitive landscape", heightened pressure on the dermatology franchise, and generic competition on Convenia and Cerenia; lower Librela and vaccine sales added to the decline (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1555280/000155528026000050/zts-20260930.htm). [Certain] Reuters reports management is planning cautiously for 2027 (source: https://www.reuters.com/business/healthcare-pharmaceuticals/zoetis-plans-cautiously-2027-pet-owners-cut-back-vet-visits-shares-fall-2026-11-05/). [Certain]

Direction change flagged: ROIC is 2.4pp and ROCE 2.9pp below their 2-year highs, though both are broadly flat to up on 3 years. [Certain] Margins have not yet cracked; ROIC erosion is modest. Livestock and diagnostics are partial offsets. [Likely]

## 3. Valuation
At 79.11 USD, P/E is 13.4x against a 5y range of 20.7x–56.7x (median 32.3x); EV/EBITDA is 9.9x vs 14.9x–37.1x; every multiple is below its 5y minimum. FCF yield is 6.95%. Versus peers, P/E is -71% and EV/EBITDA -30%, though IDXX/MRK are different businesses (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/zts/statistics/). [Certain]

Reverse DCF: the price implies 1.6% year-1 revenue growth, fading to terminal. Base fair value is 123.31 (bear 74.38, bull 144.55), but it is flagged unreliable: DCF 89.25 vs P/E 191.45, a 2.1x gap. Weigh it lightly; the DCF alone suggests more modest upside. [Likely]

The market is pricing a de-rating of a franchise that has lost pricing power, not a collapse. The price is 12.5% below the 200-day average and the 12-month change is -32.4%, so there is no trend support. [Certain]

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- TTM ROIC (ratios page, trailing) falls below 22%.
- TTM operating margin (income statement, trailing) falls below 34%.
- Company-reported companion-animal revenue declines operationally for two further consecutive quarters in 10-Q MD&A, with dermatology cited as the driver.
- TTM FCF margin (cash flow, trailing) falls below 18%.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
FCF conversion of 89.2% of net income TTM is healthy; shares fell 5.45% YoY (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/zts/statistics/). [Certain] The 10-Q shows nine-month buybacks of 17.7M shares [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1555280/000155528026000050/zts-20260930.htm), with no Q3 dividend declared. Net debt/EBITDA rose to 1.92x from 1.26x at FY2024, and the 10-Q cites higher interest expense. Leverage is still moderate; Altman Z is 5.43. [Certain] Buying back stock while earnings fall is a risk if the decline persists. [Likely]

## 6. Unusual items
- Fiscal-year alignment: from 2026 international subsidiaries dropped their one-month reporting lag, so December 2025 is excluded from 2026 results and prior periods are recast; the 10-Q says price-increase timing shifted. Year-on-year comparisons are noisy (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1555280/000155528026000050/zts-20260930.htm). [Certain]
- The 10-Q removed its tariff and supply-chain risk factors and notes a restructuring/cost programme plus an impairment on PetMedix IPR&D assets (same URL). [Certain]
- Pipeline: USDA licensed Cytopoint Plus on 2026-11-11 (https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20261111108155/en/Zoetis-Receives-USDA-License-for-Cytopoint-Plus%E2%84%A2-The-First-and-Only-Long-Acting-Monoclonal-Antibody-Providing-75-Days-of-Allergic-Itch-Relief-in-Dogs-with-Allergic-Dermatitis/), a possible defence of dermatology. [Likely]
- Short interest 4.12% of float; institutions own 96.4%. [Certain]
