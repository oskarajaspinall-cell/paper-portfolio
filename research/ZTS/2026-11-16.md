# Zoetis Inc. (ZTS) — Core initiation — 2026-11-16
## 1. Business
Zoetis sells veterinary medicines, vaccines and diagnostics for companion animals (dermatology, pain, parasiticides) and livestock. It sells directly to vets and producers and keeps a leading position in most markets [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/zts/). No single foreign country is shown as ~10%+ of revenue in the sources read; not verified against the 10-K [Guessing].

## 2. Quality
High-quality franchise: ROIC 26.4% TTM, operating margin 37.9%, gross margin 71.6%, FCF margin 23.5% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/zts/financials/ratios/). [Certain] Returns are flat over five years, not rising. ROCE sits 2.9pp below its 2-year high, and ROIC is -0.5pp year on year (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/zts/financials/ratios/?p=trailing): modest erosion, not a break. [Certain]

The moat (vet loyalty, regulatory approvals, biologics such as monoclonal antibodies) is being tested. The Q3 10-Q says companion-animal revenue fell on softer demand, a more competitive landscape, heightened pressure on dermatology, generic competition on Convenia and Cerenia, and lower Librela sales (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1555280/000155528026000050/zts-20260930.htm). [Certain] Reuters reports cautious 2027 planning (source: https://www.reuters.com/business/healthcare-pharmaceuticals/zoetis-plans-cautiously-2027-pet-owners-cut-back-vet-visits-shares-fall-2026-11-05/). [Likely] The moat is eroding at the edges but is intact in livestock and diagnostics. [Likely] Net debt/EBITDA is 1.92x, up from 1.03x in 2021. [Certain]

## 3. Valuation
Every multiple is below its own 5-year minimum: P/E 14.1x (5y min 20.7x), EV/EBITDA 10.4x (14.9x), P/FCF 15.3x (24.3x), FCF yield 6.56% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/zts/statistics/). [Certain] Versus peers EV/EBITDA is -34% and P/FCF -53%, but P/B is +27%. The price implies 3.1% year-1 revenue growth fading to terminal growth. [Certain]

The mechanical fair value (base 121.97 vs close 83.82) is flagged unreliable: the DCF (87.23) and P/E (191.45) methods disagree 2.2x. Weigh it lightly; the DCF base is only slightly above the price. [Certain] The price is 8.2% below the 200-day average, and the 12-month change is -30.8%. The market is pricing a lasting growth reset, not a cyclical dip. [Likely]

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROIC (ratios page, TTM) falls below 22%.
- Operating margin (income statement, TTM) falls below 34%.
- FCF margin (cash-flow page, TTM) falls below 20%.
- Revenue (income statement, TTM) declines year on year for two further consecutive quarters, showing the companion-animal share loss is structural.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
FCF conversion is 89.2% of net income, and the share count fell 5.45% year on year, so buybacks are sizeable (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/zts/statistics/). [Certain] Leverage rose to 1.92x net debt/EBITDA, and the 10-Q cites higher average debt and restructuring/impairment charges, including a write-off of acquired PetMedix R&D (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1555280/000155528026000050/zts-20260930.htm). [Certain] Buybacks funded partly by debt while sales are flat is acceptable at this leverage but leaves less room if the downturn persists. [Likely]
