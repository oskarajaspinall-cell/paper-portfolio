# Cinemark Holdings (CNK) — Core initiation — 2026-11-16
## 1. Business
Cinemark is the third-largest U.S. cinema operator (source: https://deadline.com/2026/11/cinemarko-netflix-cliff-booth-theatrical-release-1237212191/) [Likely]. It earns admissions (shared with studios as film rentals), concessions and other revenue (advertising, screen rental, premium formats). International (mainly Latin America) is material: 26.3 million of 63.7 million Q3 patrons [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1385280/000119312526444406/cnk-20260930.htm), but at a far lower ticket price, so its revenue share is below its attendance share. Revenue share by country not obtained [data unavailable]; Argentina is under hyper-inflationary accounting (same URL). [Certain]

## 2. Quality
Returns have recovered from the pandemic: ROIC TTM 12.9% vs 10.4% in FY2025, operating margin 15.1% vs 11.0%, FCF conversion 117.5% of net income (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/cnk/financials/ratios/). All four rolling-12-month measures are rising or flat, with no direction warning. [Certain]
Q3 U.S. attendance rose 12.7% [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1385280/000119312526444406/cnk-20260930.htm). U.S. ticket price rose 6.3% [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1385280/000119312526444406/cnk-20260930.htm). Pricing, premium-format mix and concessions are durable levers; scale gives it studio and landlord leverage. [Likely]
The moat is modest: earnings depend on the film slate and the exclusive theatrical window. The CEO publicly criticised a streaming-first release model (source: https://deadline.com/2026/11/cinemarko-netflix-cliff-booth-theatrical-release-1237212191/). [Likely] Altman Z is 1.71 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/cnk/statistics/).

## 3. Valuation
At 40.80 USD the stock trades at 16.7x P/E (72% of its 5y range), 9.5x EV/EBITDA (1% of range, the maximum inflated by the pandemic) and 13.7x P/FCF (23%), with a FCF yield of 7.28%. EV/EBITDA is 23% below peers and P/FCF 20% below; P/B is 113% above (levered equity base). Source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/cnk/financials/ratios/ [Certain]
The reverse DCF implies 1.3% year-1 revenue growth, fading, so the price assumes little beyond current box office. Base fair value is 58.26 USD (+42.8%), but bear is 14.87 and bull 105.47: the range reflects earnings cyclicality, so treat the base as soft. [Likely] The price is 27.0% above its 200-day average, 6-month momentum +55.2%, near the 52-week high.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Shares outstanding fell 18.66% YoY, and leverage fell from 4.87x (FY2025) to 3.21x TTM while FCF conversion stayed above 100%. A quarterly dividend continues (source: https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20261113224302/en/Cinemark-Declares-Quarterly-Cash-Dividend-of-0.09/). Treasury stock increased over 2026, consistent with ongoing buybacks (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1385280/000119312526444406/cnk-20260930.htm). [Likely] Strong, but results are peak-slate flattered.
