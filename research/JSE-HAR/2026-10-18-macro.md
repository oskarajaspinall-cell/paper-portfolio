Bull: real yields/dollar roll over → gold extends its run → operating leverage pushes HAR's 41% operating margin higher → re-rates from 6.2x P/E (12th percentile of 5y range) toward peers (13.2x).

Tilt: moderate toward bear — the two sharpest, most recent macro moves both point against gold just as HAR's re-rating case rests on sustaining a gold-driven margin peak.
