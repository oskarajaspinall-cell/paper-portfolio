# Harmony Gold Mining Company Limited (JSE:HAR) — Core initiation — 2026-10-18
## 1. Business
Harmony Gold is a South African-domiciled gold miner (JSE primary listing, NYSE ADR) running underground and surface mines in South Africa plus Hidden Valley in Papua New Guinea, selling gold bullion at prevailing/forward prices [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/jse/HAR/). It has diversified into copper via the CSA mine in Australia and the Eva Copper development, adding a second commodity alongside gold [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/jse/HAR/transcripts/739944-h2-2026-q-a/).

## 2. Quality
Returns have improved sharply: ROIC rose from 12.1% (FY2022) to 41.0% TTM, ROE from -3.3% to 48.0%, and operating margin from 8.9% to 41.0%, on a higher gold price and cost discipline [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/jse/HAR/financials/ratios/). FCF conversion (FCF/NI) is volatile — -67.5% (FY2022), 84.5% (FY2024), 53.7% TTM — reflecting heavy capex on CSA integration and Eva Copper [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/jse/HAR/financials/cash-flow-statement/). The balance sheet is near net cash (net debt/EBITDA 0.02x TTM) [Certain]. Gold mining has no pricing power, however: margins track the gold price rather than a durable moat, so the recent margin expansion is cyclical as much as structural [Likely]. Copper diversification and an 11-year unbroken production-guidance record are operating-quality evidence, but not a moat against commodity-price risk [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/jse/HAR/transcripts/739944-h2-2026-q-a/).

## 3. Valuation
At 6.2x P/E (12th percentile of its own 5y range of 5.3x-12.1x) and 3.9x EV/EBITDA (18th percentile of 3.6x-5.6x), HAR sits near the bottom of its own history despite record profitability, and at a 53%/30% discount to peer medians (13.2x P/E, 5.7x EV/EBITDA; AU, GFI, NEM, SSL) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/jse/HAR/financials/ratios/). P/FCF (11.3x) and P/B (2.4x) sit closer to peers (-3%/-35%), and FCF yield is 8.86% [Certain]. The gap to peers implies the market is pricing either a reversal of the current high-margin regime (lower gold price) or a South Africa country-risk/complexity discount (deep underground mines, Eva Copper permitting, rand reporting) [Likely]. The fact sheet's own fair-value model is not computable here (NAV needs reserve data, EV/EBITDA needs an FX input neither on stockanalysis.com), so there is no owner-estimate anchor; the peer/history discount is the only valuation signal available [Certain].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) rises above 1.0x and stays there for two consecutive quarters — capex/dividends outrunning cash generation.
- FCF margin (income statement + cash-flow pages) falls below 8%, roughly half the TTM 15.9% — cost inflation eroding the margin gains.
- ROIC (ratios page) falls below 20%, reversing the structural improvement from the FY2022 low of 12.1%.
- Gold production guidance is missed for a full fiscal year, breaking the 11-year streak, or Eva Copper's first-copper date (management-guided to 2028) slips by more than a year without a replacement growth driver (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/jse/HAR/transcripts/739944-h2-2026-q-a/).

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
FCF conversion was 53.7% of net income TTM, down from 75.0% in FY2025 as capex rose [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/jse/HAR/financials/cash-flow-statement/). Shares outstanding rose only 0.80% YoY, so the CSA and Eva Copper deals were funded mostly without heavy dilution [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/jse/HAR/statistics/). Management described FY2026 as a record dividend year and said most CSA-acquisition one-off costs are now behind the group, implying a cleaner cost base and more FCF available for distributions/deleveraging into FY2027 [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/jse/HAR/transcripts/739944-h2-2026-q-a/). Net debt/EBITDA of 0.02x TTM leaves ample room for further copper growth capex [Certain].

## 6. Unusual items
Eva Copper's first-copper timeline (guided to 2028) carries a flagged environmental/endangered-species permitting risk, per management [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/jse/HAR/transcripts/739944-h2-2026-q-a/). Institutional ownership is high (64.6%) and insider ownership very low (0.15%) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/jse/HAR/statistics/). Short interest is not disclosed on stockanalysis.com [data unavailable]. No other governance item is material beyond the South Africa operating/country-risk exposure already discussed in Valuation.
