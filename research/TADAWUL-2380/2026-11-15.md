TTM to 2026-09-30 flipped sharply: ROIC 21.6%, operating margin 12.1%, FCF margin 21.6%, against -6.8%, -7.4% and 4.4% a year earlier (https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tadawul/2380/financials/ratios/?p=trailing). [Certain] on the numbers. The five-year trend is falling while the last year is rising. [Likely] this is a cycle recovery, not a durable moat: a commodity processor with no pricing power, and the Aramco parent is a feedstock and offtake advantage, not a returns guarantee [Guessing]. Net debt/EBITDA of 1.31x TTM is moderate; the FY negative multiples are artefacts of negative EBITDA and mean nothing.
