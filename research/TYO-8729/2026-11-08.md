# Sony Financial Group (TYO:8729) — Core initiation — 2026-11-08
## 1. Business
Japanese life-insurance-led financial group (Sony Life, with banking and non-life units), spun out of Sony Group. It earns underwriting margin and investment spread on policyholder assets, plus fees and bank interest. Revenue is overwhelmingly yen; no foreign-country share is shown on the fact sheet [data unavailable] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tyo/8729/). [Likely]

## 2. Quality
- ROIC rose from 0.3% in FY2022 to 7.4% in FY2026 and is 16.0% TTM (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tyo/8729/financials/ratios/). [Certain]
- The last year's direction confirms this: TTM ROIC is 16.0% against 11.4% a year earlier, and 16.0% is also the 2-year high (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tyo/8729/financials/ratios/?p=trailing). [Certain]
- ROE is volatile: 6.3%, 18.4%, 6.7%, 12.5%, 8.5%, then 11.8% TTM. That is typical of insurers with market-linked earnings and is not evidence of a durable moat. [Likely]
- Margins, ROCE and FCF margin are [data unavailable]. FCF/NI of 794.6% in FY2026 reflects insurance float (premiums received ahead of claims), not owner earnings. [Likely]
- The history is short and reflects a corporate spin-off, so the 5-year series is not a clean comparable. [Likely]
- Piotroski F is 5 and beta is 0.04 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tyo/8729/statistics/). [Certain]
- A durable edge is plausible in Sony brand distribution, but nothing in the data shows it. [Guessing]

## 3. Valuation
- P/E is 11.2x against a peer median of 9.9x, a 12% premium. P/B is 1.1x against 1.2x, a 12% discount. EV/EBITDA is 5.6x against 5.3x (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tyo/8729/statistics/). [Certain]
- The 5y ranges (P/E 17.3x–450.9x, P/B 1.5x–31.3x) are distorted by the small pre-spin earnings and equity base. Reading "below min" as cheap would be wrong. [Likely]
- P/FCF of 2.1x and FCF yield of 46.55% are artefacts of insurance float, not distributable cash. [Likely]
- The fact-sheet fair value (base 814.24 JPY vs close 141.20) is driven by a P/E method at 2,211.90 JPY base off unstable history. The P/B+ROE method, which suits insurers better, gives a base of 115.41 JPY, below the price. We discount the blend and take the P/B+ROE method. [Likely]
- Net: the price assumes roughly peer-level returns, with no clear margin of safety. [Likely]
