# ANTA Sports Products Limited (HKG:2020) — Core initiation — 2026-10-18

## 1. Business
ANTA designs, manufactures, markets and sells sports footwear, apparel and accessories through a multi-brand portfolio — ANTA (mass-market/professional), FILA (licensed premium sports fashion), DESCENTE, KOLON SPORT, JACK WOLFSKIN and MAIA ACTIVE — plus a consortium stake in Amer Sports (Arc'teryx, Salomon, Wilson), sold via wholesale, owned retail and e-commerce across China and, increasingly, overseas [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/2020/).

## 2. Quality
The multi-brand structure is the moat: ANTA's mass-market scale plus FILA's premium license plus stakes in global performance brands (Amer Sports) give shelf space and price points domestic rivals Li Ning and Xtep cannot easily replicate [Likely]. TTM operating margin (24.3%) and net margin (18.8%) are above their FY2021 level and rising (+1.5pp/+1.3pp over five years); gross margin has held near 62% [Certain] (source: RA, IS). FCF conversion is strong (127.2% TTM, exceeding net income in four of five fiscal years) and the balance sheet sits in net cash (net debt/EBITDA -0.60x TTM) [Certain] (source: CF, RA). The blemish: ROIC has fallen from 36.4% (FY2021) to 23.3% TTM and ROE from 28.6% to 24.8% as the capital base has grown with international/Amer Sports expansion [Likely] — still above a reasonable cost of capital, but worth watching. Piotroski F-score 7/9 and Altman Z 4.63 confirm earnings-quality and balance-sheet strength [Certain] (source: ST).

## 3. Valuation
Current multiples sit below ANTA's own five-year range on every measure in the fact sheet: P/E 11.9x (5y min 13.2x), EV/EBITDA 6.4x (min 10.1x), EV/Sales 2.2x (min 2.6x), P/FCF 9.3x (min 10.5x), P/B 2.4x (min 2.8x) [Certain] (source: RA, HI). Versus the peer median (Li Ning, Xtep, Nike) it is mixed: ANTA is cheaper on P/FCF (9.3x vs 14.3x, -35%) but screens more expensive on P/E, EV/EBITDA, EV/Sales and P/B, where the peer median is pulled down by Li Ning/Xtep's depressed multiples [Likely]. Blended fair value of HKD115.64 implies +48.4% upside to the HKD77.95 close, with bear/bull of +16.2%/+136.8%; the reverse DCF implies the market is pricing roughly flat-to-negative revenue growth in year one [Certain] (source: fact sheet fair-value table), a low bar against the double-digit revenue growth reported at the H1 2026 results [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/2020/transcripts/729402-h1-2026/). The price assumes returns keep fading toward peer-like multiples rather than stabilizing.

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROIC (ratios page) falls below 15% for two consecutive fiscal years, showing the Amer Sports/overseas expansion is structurally diluting returns rather than temporarily building scale.
- Gross margin (income statement) falls below 58% TTM, showing discounting or channel mix overcomes the premiumization strategy.
- FCF margin (cash flow/income statement) falls below 15% TTM, showing the Amer Sports/Puma stakes have become cash drags rather than growth optionality.
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) turns positive and rises past 1.0x, showing the Puma stake and further M&A have eroded the net-cash balance sheet underpinning the moat.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
FCF conversion has exceeded net income in four of five fiscal years (92.9%-180.5%), TTM 127.2%, funding both brand reinvestment and a net-cash balance sheet (net debt/EBITDA -0.60x TTM) [Certain] (source: CF, IS, RA). The announced ~€1.5bn, 29.06%-stake acquisition in Puma SE (completed October 2026) is one of ANTA's largest single capital deployments, shifting toward opportunistic M&A in mature Western markets alongside the earlier Amer Sports consortium investment [Likely] (source: https://www.gurufocus.com/news/9114836/anta-sports-acquires-2906-stake-in-puma-se-for-1505-billion). Shares outstanding fell 0.30% YoY, consistent with no dilution [Certain] (source: ST).

## 6. Unusual items
The Puma stake adds a third major multi-brand platform (after FILA's domestic turnaround and Amer Sports' Western expansion) to integrate simultaneously, raising management-attention and execution risk [Likely]. Separately, US political commentary has linked Amer Sports' American brands (Wilson, Louisville Slugger) to ANTA's Chinese ownership amid US-China tensions; this is opinion/reporting rather than a disclosed regulatory action, but is a geopolitical tail risk for the Amer Sports/US-consumer sleeve worth monitoring [Guessing] (source: https://www.foxnews.com/outkick-sports/two-iconic-american-baseball-equipment-companies-now-owned-chinese-company-associated-ccp). Short-interest data is unavailable; institutional ownership is modest at 17.1% [Certain] (source: ST).
