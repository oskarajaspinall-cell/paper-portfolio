# Hangzhou Hikvision Digital Technology (SHE:002415) — Core initiation — 2026-11-09
## 1. Business
Hikvision designs and sells video-surveillance and IoT hardware, with software and solutions attached, to governments, enterprises and consumers; it earns product margins on volume (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/she/002415/) [Likely]. ## 2. Quality
Returns are high but below FY2021: ROIC 31.3% TTM vs 52.3% in FY2021 and 25.5% in FY2025; operating margin 21.2% TTM vs 16.9% in FY2025 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/she/002415/financials/ratios/) [Certain]. The last 12 months reverse the five-year decline (ROIC 22.5% a year earlier), and gross margin of 48.6% TTM is the highest in the table [Certain]. Whether this is durable or a cyclical or one-off rebound is unproven; scale and cost position in cameras are the likely moat, but Chinese rivals compete on price [Guessing]. FCF margin fell to 16.0% from 21.4% a year earlier [Certain]. Balance sheet is net cash (net debt/EBITDA -1.37x), Piotroski 7 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/she/002415/statistics/) [Certain].

## 3. Valuation
P/E 17.9x and EV/EBITDA 12.8x sit below their 5y minimums (19.3x and 14.5x), and at roughly half the peer medians (41.4x and 25.9x; peers are mostly US security and public-safety names with different growth and geopolitics) (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/she/002415/financials/ratios/) [Certain]. FCF yield is 5.01% [Certain]. The mechanical fair value is flagged unreliable: DCF base 20.74 vs EV/EBITDA base 51.45 CNY; blended base 30.98 is 12.1% below the 35.25 close [Certain]. The reverse DCF implies 19.0% year-1 revenue growth, which looks demanding against that DCF; the cheap multiples may partly reflect peak-margin earnings and a 3.5% China premium [Likely]. Not clearly cheap on cash-flow value despite low multiples [Guessing].
