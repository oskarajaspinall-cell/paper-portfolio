# Gaming and Leisure Properties (GLPI) — Core initiation — 2026-11-08
## 1. Business
Gaming-focused triple-net REIT. It owns casino real estate and leases it under long master leases to operators (PENN, Pinnacle, Boyd, Bally's and others). Rent is mostly fixed with small escalators, plus a percentage-rent component reset periodically (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1575965/000157596526000072/glpi-20260930.htm). Bally's and affiliates supplied 19.4% of cash income in Q3 2026 [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1575965/000157596526000072/glpi-20260930.htm). [Certain] No material foreign revenue was identified.

## 2. Quality
Steady, contractual cash flows, but not a high-return business. ROIC is 9.7% TTM, up from 8.3% in FY2021, and the last year is flat (https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/glpi/financials/ratios/). [Certain] ROE is 18.7% TTM, helped by leverage. Net debt/EBITDA is 5.19x on our measure and 5.09x on the site's definition (same page). Margins and FCF conversion are [data unavailable]. [Certain]

The moat is regulatory: gaming licences make a tenant hard to replace, and master leases cross-bind the tenant's properties. That same feature works against the landlord when a tenant fails, because a replacement operator needs licences and approvals. [Likely]

The main quality issue is Bally's. The new risk factor says Bally's disclosed substantial doubt about its going-concern status in August 2026. GLPI has funded $640.7 million of up to $940.0 million for Bally's Chicago, and rent from that project is deferred for accounting until completion [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1575965/000157596526000072/glpi-20260930.htm). [Certain]

## 3. Valuation
At 38.89 USD the stock trades below its 5y minimum on every multiple (https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/glpi/statistics/). [Certain]
- P/E is 11.9x against a 5y range of 15.3x–22.4x and a peer median of 21.1x.
- EV/EBITDA is 12.3x against a 5y range of 13.3x–16.4x and a peer median of 14.9x.
- FCF yield is 4.42%.
- P/B is 2.3x, a premium to peers of 1.6x.

The mechanical P/FFO-proxy fair value is 48.64 bear, 55.54 base and 60.85 bull, so the base case implies +42.8% (fact sheet, Fair value section). [Likely] This is one model estimate. The price is 12.3% below the 200-day average and the shares are down 13.8% over 12 months, while SPY is up 8.5%. [Certain]

The market is pricing a meaningful chance that Bally's cannot pay, plus higher long-term rates. The 4.84% risk-free rate lifts the discount rate (FRED, https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=DGS10). [Likely] Peers' multiples are not a clean comparison because none has this tenant concentration. [Guessing]

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Bally's share of GLPI cash income, per the 10-Q tenant disclosure, stays above 15% while Bally's remains under a going-concern warning. A Bally's bankruptcy filing or missed rent payment also counts.
- Net debt/EBITDA, site definition (ratios page), rises above 6.0x.
- ROIC TTM (ratios page, ?p=trailing) falls below 8.5%.
- Bally's Chicago opening slips beyond the first half of 2027 as guided on the Q3 call, or further provisions for credit losses are taken on the Bally's-related investments (10-Q).

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Growth comes from sale-leasebacks and development funding. Examples are the Bally's Lincoln acquisition, which closed in February 2026, a funded Virginia Live! development and a Dry Creek delayed-draw loan [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1575965/000157596526000072/glpi-20260930.htm). [Certain] Q3 results and the Joliet JV with Prologis (source: https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/10/28/3390221/29133/en/gaming-and-leisure-properties-enters-joint-venture-with-prologis-inc-to-develop-the-former-hollywood-casino-joliet-site.html) show continued deal flow. [Likely] Shares outstanding rose 2.88% YoY, so growth is partly equity-funded and dilutive per share (https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/glpi/statistics/). [Certain] FCF conversion is [data unavailable]. FCF yield is 4.42%.

## 6. Unusual items
Tenant concentration and Bally's going-concern doubt, as above. The 10-Q also shows a large Q3 provision for credit losses, attributed to the rise in long-term rates and lower projected commercial real estate prices [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1575965/000157596526000072/glpi-20260930.htm). [Certain] Short interest is 3.77% of float, little changed (https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/glpi/statistics/).
