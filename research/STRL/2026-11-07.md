# Sterling Infrastructure (STRL) — Core initiation — 2026-11-07
## 1. Business
Sterling is a US civil and site-development contractor with three segments: E-Infrastructure Solutions, Building Solutions and Transportation Solutions (source: https://www.strlco.com) [Certain]. Growth now comes from large mission-critical site projects, with resources shifting from lower-margin highway work [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/874238/000087423826000125/strl-20260930.htm). Revenue is project-based, with backlog typically completed in 6 to 36 months and contracts terminable on short notice [company-reported] (same filing) [Certain].

## 2. Quality
Returns have risen sharply: TTM ROIC 44.2% vs 11.7% in FY2021, operating margin 18.6% vs 7.8%, gross margin 23.8% vs 14.4% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/strl/financials/ratios/) [Certain]. Net debt/EBITDA is -0.06x, Altman Z 7.33, Piotroski F 8 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/strl/statistics/) [Certain].

Durability is the question. Construction is a contract business with limited structural moat; the margin gain looks like mix shift into scarce-capacity, complex site work [Likely]. Backlog is $4.14bn and combined backlog with unsigned awards $6.14bn, 1.9x book-to-burn over nine months [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/874238/000087423826000125/strl-20260930.htm) [Certain]. Q3 gross margin eased to 24.1% from 24.7% [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/874238/000087423826000125/strl-20260930.htm) [Certain]. Concentration in data-centre-type demand is not quantified in the sources read [Guessing]. Q3 results beat and guidance was raised (source: https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/sterling-reports-record-third-quarter-results-and-raises-full-year-2026-guidance-302922700.html) [Likely].

## 3. Valuation
P/E 14.2x sits at the 21st percentile of its 5y range (9.3x–32.4x) and 50% below the peer median of 28.4x; EV/EBITDA 8.5x is 35% below peers (13.2x). EV/Sales 1.8x and P/B 5.1x are above peers; P/FCF 16.4x is mid-range (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/strl/statistics/) [Certain]. FCF yield is 6.11% [Certain].

The price fell 71.5% in six months from a 1,005.68 high, so the market is pricing sharp earnings decline or a cycle peak, despite the beat-and-raise [Likely]. Mechanical base fair value of 147.91 is 41.5% below the 252.82 close, bull 446.75; the DCF is extremely sensitive to growth fade. Reverse DCF implies 18.3% year-1 revenue growth fading to terminal [Certain]. Beta 1.98 [Certain]. Cheap on earnings multiples, but that follows peak-margin earnings; the base fair value signals no margin of safety [Likely].
