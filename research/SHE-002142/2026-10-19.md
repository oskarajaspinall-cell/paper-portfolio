Net margin has nonetheless risen to 53.0% TTM from 48.3% in FY2021 [Certain] (same source). Piotroski F-Score is 4/9, a middling read [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/she/002142/statistics/). Beta of 0.60 implies low share-price volatility [Certain] (same source). Fact-sheet FCF margin/conversion figures (165%/312% TTM) are not meaningful for a deposit-taking bank, since the cash-flow statement mixes loan-book growth with operating cash flow; ROE and P/B are the better quality lenses [Certain]. The durable edge, to the extent one exists, is a long-standing retail/SME deposit franchise in a wealthier coastal province, but the fact sheet cannot evidence a moat (no NPL ratio, NIM or segment data) [Guessing].

## 3. Valuation
At 7.6x TTM P/E the stock sits at the 28th percentile of its own 5-year range (5.4x-13.5x) — cheap vs its own history — but 17% above the 4-peer median of 6.5x (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/she/002142/financials/ratios/ and peer statistics pages). P/B of 0.8x sits in the 14th percentile of its 5-year range (0.7x-1.7x) and 19% above the peer median of 0.7x [Certain]. Model fair value (P/B + sustainable ROE primary, FCFE DCF secondary) is 45.00 CNY base, +29.6% above the 34.72 CNY last close, bear +8.0%, bull +42.6% (source: fact sheet fair-value table). The reverse-DCF implies -5.7% revenue growth next year fading to terminal growth, pessimistic given the bank has grown net income every year shown. The stock looks cheap on its own history and the fair-value model, despite a small premium to peers [Likely].
