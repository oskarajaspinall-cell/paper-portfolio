# Bank of Hangzhou (SHA:600926) — Core initiation — 2026-10-19
## 1. Business
Bank of Hangzhou is a Chinese city commercial bank headquartered in Zhejiang province. It earns net interest income by taking deposits and lending to corporates, SMEs and retail customers in and around Hangzhou, plus fee income from wealth management and transaction banking [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sha/600926/).

## 2. Quality
Profitability has improved steadily: ROE rose from 10.8% (FY2021) to a peak 13.7% (FY2023/24) before easing to 12.1% TTM, and net margin has risen every year to 57.8% TTM (+8.5pp over five years) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sha/600926/financials/ratios/, https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sha/600926/financials/income-statement/). ROIC/ROCE and gross/operating margin are not meaningful for a bank and the fact sheet shows them as [data unavailable]. Piotroski F-score is a middling 4/9 [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sha/600926/statistics/). The fact sheet has no asset-quality data (NPL ratio, provision coverage) and no primary IR/SEC source is allowlisted for this filer, so credit quality — the real test of a bank's durability through a cycle — cannot be verified here [Guessing]. A regional deposit franchise in one of China's wealthier provinces is a plausible moat, but this is not confirmed by any cited figure.

## 3. Valuation
At 6.3x P/E the stock sits mid-range of its own 5-year history (4.3x–9.0x, 43rd percentile) and 10% below the 4-peer median of 7.0x [Certain]. P/B of 0.7x matches the peer median (0.7x) and sits in the middle of its own range (54th percentile) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sha/600926/financials/ratios/). P/FCF (1.3x) and the 77.8% FCF yield look extreme but for a deposit-taking bank operating cash flow is dominated by deposit inflows/outflows, not distributable owner earnings — these multiples should be read with caution [Likely]. The mechanical fair value model (P/B+ROE primary, FCFE/DDM secondary) puts base fair value at 25.16 CNY vs the 16.83 CNY close (+49.5%), with the bear case still +17.0% [Certain] (source: fact sheet fair-value table). The reverse DCF implies the price assumes year-1 revenue declines of ~10.6%, which looks pessimistic against a bank still growing ROE and net margin.

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROE (ratios page) falls below 10% for two consecutive fiscal years, reversing the five-year improving trend.
- Net margin (income statement) drops below 50%, signalling margin or credit-cost pressure.
- Shares outstanding (statistics page) grow more than 5% YoY, diluting per-share returns beyond the current 0.83% pace.
- Piotroski F-score (statistics page) falls to 2 or below, indicating broad fundamental deterioration.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Share count grew only 0.83% YoY, so dilution has been minimal [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sha/600926/statistics/). Reported FCF margin/conversion figures are very high and volatile (e.g. -301% to +481% FCF margin across FY2021–25) because operating cash flow for a bank reflects deposit and loan-book movements rather than distributable cash; they should not be treated as a cash-conversion signal [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sha/600926/financials/cash-flow-statement/). No dividend or buyback figure is shown on the fact sheet [data unavailable].
