## 2. Quality
Good but not exceptional. ROIC is 12.0% TTM versus 11.8% in FY2025 and 17.9% in FY2023, so the 5-year trend is falling (-1.1pp) (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tyo/4151/financials/ratios/) [Certain]. Over the last 12 months it recovered from 8.1% to 12.0%, still 3.7pp below the 2-year high of 15.7% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tyo/4151/financials/ratios/?p=trailing) [Certain]. Operating margin is 17.9% TTM against a 21.7% FY2023 peak; FCF margin recovered to 13.8% from 7.5% a year earlier but is volatile (8.3% to 22.7% across fiscal years) [Certain]. Net cash (net debt/EBITDA -1.91x site definition), Altman Z 6.03 and Piotroski 7 point to a strong balance sheet (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tyo/4151/statistics/) [Certain]. Moat is patent/biologic exclusivity, which erodes at expiry; the high gross margin is evidence of pricing power now, not of durability [Likely]. Returns are adequate, with lumpy cash conversion (FCF/NI 85.6% TTM) [Certain].

## 3. Valuation
At 2,427 JPY the stock trades at 14.8x P/E, below its 5y minimum of 15.7x and 22% below the peer median of 19.0x (Astellas, Takeda, Chugai, Otsuka) [Certain]. EV/EBITDA is 8.1x (5y range 7.9x–17.0x, median 8.9x) and 18% below peers; P/B is 1.4x, below its 5y minimum. P/FCF of 17.2x is in line with peers (+1%) and FCF yield is 5.80% [Certain]. The price sits at the bottom of its own range, and the reverse DCF implies 3.2% year-1 revenue growth fading to terminal [Certain]. Model fair value is 2,605.76 JPY base (+7.4%), 1,915.41 bear (-21.1%) and 4,521.38 bull (+86.3%) (source: research/TYO-4151/factsheet-2026-11-08.md) [Likely]. Skew is positive but the base case offers little margin of safety. Cheap versus peers partly reflects a lower-growth, patent-cliff-exposed profile [Guessing]. Price is 1.9% below the 200-day average with no earnings reaction (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tyo/4151/history/) [Certain].
