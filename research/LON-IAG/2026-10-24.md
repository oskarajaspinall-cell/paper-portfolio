# International Consolidated Airlines Group (LON:IAG) — Core initiation — 2026-10-24
## 1. Business
IAG owns British Airways, Iberia, Aer Lingus, Vueling and LEVEL, plus IAG Loyalty. Revenue comes from premium long-haul (Heathrow, Madrid hubs), short-haul and low-cost fares, cargo and loyalty income [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/lon/IAG/).

## 2. Quality
Re-research trigger: the latest close of 394.70 GBX reached the entry price set on 2026-10-19 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/lon/IAG/history/). Fundamentals are unchanged since then: ROIC 27.5% TTM (from -24.6% in FY2021), operating margin 15.0%, FCF margin 11.8%, net debt/EBITDA 0.74x (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/lon/IAG/financials/ratios/) [Certain]. Piotroski F is 4 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/lon/IAG/statistics/) [Certain].

Why it fell: the only stock-specific explanation in the headlines is jet-fuel cost fears as crude resumed rallying (source: https://invezz.com/news/2026/10/08/iag-share-price-heres-why-the-british-airways-parent-is-falling-today/) [Likely]. Nothing newer than the cabin-product stories is listed, and no guidance cut, legal action or management change appears [Likely]. The further slide is probably continued fuel/sector pressure; the cause is not confirmed [Guessing]. The price is 9.5pp behind SPY over 3 months and RSI is 32.8 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/lon/IAG/statistics/) [Certain].

Durability rests on Heathrow slots, BA's premium brand and loyalty income, not on cost advantage [Likely].

## 3. Valuation
EV/EBITDA of 3.3x is below its 5y minimum (4.4x) and 14% below peers (3.9x); P/E is 7.1x vs peers' 12.6x; P/FCF is 5.1x vs 11.7x; FCF yield is 19.59% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/lon/IAG/financials/ratios/ and peer pages) [Certain]. The reverse DCF implies -24.4% year-1 revenue growth [Certain]. The through-cycle DCF is unusable (bear -474.35 GBX, base 2,087.98 GBX), so only EV/EBITDA anchors: 395.95 / 440.31 / 459.61 GBX bear/base/bull [Certain]. The price now sits on that method's bear value; its base value is only modestly above the price [Likely]. Cheap, but the skew is not wide, and the multiple is cheap partly because current margins are near a cycle high [Likely].

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
FCF conversion is 128.6% of net income, shares outstanding are down 8.59% YoY and leverage is low [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/lon/IAG/statistics/). A final buyback programme was launched, completing the announced return of excess cash [Likely] (source: https://www.nasdaq.com/articles/iag-launches-final-eu500-mln-share-buyback-program). Once it ends, buyback support for the shares falls away [Guessing].

## 6. Unusual items
Fuel and the dollar are the swing factors; beta is 1.36 [Certain]. Q3 results on Nov 6, 2026 are the first test of whether margins held through the fuel spike; buying before them adds event risk without a better price than after [Likely]. The price is 7.2% below its 50-day and 5.5% below its 200-day average [Certain]. Decision: AVOID at conviction 3; the entry price was reached but the margin confirmation attached to it is still pending. Re-research after Q3 [Likely].
