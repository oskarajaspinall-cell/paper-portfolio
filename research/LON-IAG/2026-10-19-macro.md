# Macro overlay: International Consolidated Airlines Group (LON:IAG) — 2026-10-19

## Assessment
Two variables matter: the dollar (jet fuel, most aircraft leases/debt are USD; IAG reports in EUR/GBP) and the risk-free rate (feeds the DCF discount rate behind the fact sheet's wide bear/base/bull spread). Chain: dollar strength -> higher USD fuel/lease cost in EUR/GBP terms -> margin pressure; real yields -> discount rate -> fair value.

Facts, per reports/macro/2026-10-19.md (FRED): broad dollar index (DTWEXBGS) 121.602 on 2026-10-09, +3.72 over ~1m; 10y Treasury (DGS10) 5.10%, +0.53 over 3m; 10y real yield (DFII10) 2.78%, +0.43 over 3m; fed funds (DFF) 3.88%, +0.25 over 3m; HY spread (BAMLH0A0HYM2) 3.23%, +0.52 over 3m but still calm; VIX 16.46, calm.

Market pricing (proxy): 2y Treasury (4.77%) ~0.9pp above fed funds implies no near-term cuts priced **(inference)**. IAG's 3m underperformance vs SPY (-9.3pp, fact sheet) and headlines on rising jet fuel costs (fact sheet news, 2026-10-08/09-09) suggest some cost/rate pressure is already priced [Likely]; a further leg up in the dollar or rates would still be a fresh headwind [Guessing] **(inference)**.

Bull: dollar/yields ease -> lower fuel/lease costs and discount rate -> supports re-rating. Bear: dollar/rates extend their rise -> renewed margin compression, higher discount rate -> caps re-rating, reinforcing "cheap for a reason" pricing. Tilt: small, toward bear — real but secondary to the margin/buyback thesis driving the base case.
