# Gilead Sciences, Inc. (GILD) — Core initiation — 2026-10-11

## 1. Business
Biopharmaceutical company earning product sales and royalties from HIV therapies (Biktarvy, Descovy, new long-acting Yeztugo/lenacapavir), oncology/cell therapy (Trodelvy, Yescarta, Tecartus, anito-cel in development), and liver disease [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/gild/). Revenue is protected by patents and complex biologics manufacturing (cell therapy) rather than a platform or network effect.

## 2. Quality
Returns are strong and improving: ROIC 34.4% TTM (rising +6.2pp over five years) and ROCE 30.9% TTM (+3.8pp) [Certain] (source: ratios page). Gross margin is stable near 79-80% [Certain] (source: income statement). The moat is real but narrower than headline numbers suggest: HIV franchise dominance (Biktarvy, Descovy) carries high switching costs, and Gilead is extending the cycle with once/twice-yearly lenacapavir (Yeztugo), licensed royalty-free in six markets to broaden access [Likely] (source: https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260916123411/en/Gilead-Accelerates-Global-Access-Planning-for-Investigational-Once-Yearly-Lenacapavir-for-HIV-Prevention/). Operating margin has drifted down (-3.6pp over five years to 38.9% TTM) [Certain] (source: income statement). TTM net margin/ROE are sharply negative (-10.6%/-20.7%) purely from $11.2bn of acquired IPR&D expense plus $1.75bn of IPR&D impairment booked in Q2 2026 against business-development deals, not operating deterioration [Certain] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/882095/000088209526000031/gild-20260630.htm). The question is whether lenacapavir and the oncology pipeline replace Biktarvy/Descovy revenue before generics and pricing pressure erode the HIV franchise.

## 3. Valuation
The stock trades at the top of, or above, its own five-year range on two of four multiples: EV/EBITDA 14.3x (182% of the 5y range, vs peer median 14.1x) and EV/Sales 6.9x (175% of the 5y range, +24% vs peers AMGN/VRTX/BIIB/REGN) [Certain] (source: ratios/statistics pages). P/FCF is cheaper than peers (14.5x vs 20.9x median, -31%) and mid-range of its own history (79%) [Certain]. P/B (15.9x, 479% of its own range) is distorted by a shrinking equity base from years of buybacks (shares down 1.39% YoY) rather than overvaluation alone [Likely]. The DCF-based fair value (best method for this industry) is 151.99 base, essentially equal to the 151.17 last close (+0.5%), with bull only +1.5% but bear -49.3% (76.71) [Certain] (source: fact sheet fair value table). The current price already assumes the lenacapavir/oncology transition succeeds largely as planned, leaving little margin of safety and asymmetric downside if it does not.

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Combined Biktarvy + Descovy revenue growth turns negative for two consecutive quarters (income statement, product revenue) — HIV franchise erosion.
- Gross margin (income statement) falls below 75% — pricing/competitive pressure.
- ROIC (ratios page) falls back below 20% — returns reverting to FY2023 levels.
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page, site definition) rises above 2.0x — capital discipline lost after large business-development spending.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Shares outstanding fell 1.39% YoY, consistent with continued buybacks [Certain] (source: statistics page). Net debt/EBITDA is moderate at 1.46x TTM despite roughly $13bn of IPR&D expense/impairment taken against business-development deals in H1 2026 [Certain] (source: 10-Q above). TTM FCF margin is a healthy 42.5%, but the FCF-conversion ratio is not meaningful this period because net income is negative solely from the non-operating IPR&D charges above [Certain] (source: cash-flow and income statements).

## 6. Unusual items
TTM net margin and ROE are distorted negative by one-off acquired-IPR&D expense and impairment of ~$13bn tied to business-development deals in Q2 2026, not weaker underlying operations [Certain] (source: 10-Q above). Short interest is low (1.76% of float) but rose 19.3% month-over-month — not large enough to be a standalone signal [Likely] (source: statistics page). Institutional ownership is high at 91.2% [Certain] (source: statistics page).
