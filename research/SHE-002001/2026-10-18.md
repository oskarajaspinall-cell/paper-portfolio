# Zhejiang NHU Company Ltd. (SHE:002001) — Core initiation — 2026-10-18

## 1. Business
NHU manufactures and sells nutrition, fragrance/flavor, advanced-materials and pharmaceutical-ingredient chemicals, reported through Pharmaceutical and Chemical, and Other segments [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/she/002001/). Products include animal/human vitamins (A, D3, E, H, C, B5, B6, B12), amino acids, carotenoids and coenzyme Q10; fragrance/flavor compounds for personal care and food; PPS/PPA resins and composite materials; and APIs and intermediates [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/she/002001/company/). It sells domestically and internationally; no segment revenue split is disclosed on either page [Certain].

## 2. Quality
Returns are high and resilient through a visible commodity-chemical cycle: ROIC troughed at 9.2% in FY2023 (a vitamin-pricing downturn, gross margin fell to 31.5%) and recovered to 22.5% TTM, with ROE 22.0% TTM and ROCE up +2.1pp over five years [Certain] (source: RA, IS). Gross margin (42.9% TTM) and operating margin (33.9% TTM) are high for a chemicals producer, consistent with backward-integrated, large-scale vitamin/nutrition production that smaller domestic peers and diversified majors (BASF, DSM-Firmenich) cannot easily match on cost in these lines [Likely] — inferred from margin level and peer comparison, not a disclosed cost-curve figure. The balance sheet is net cash (net debt/EBITDA -0.25x TTM, from 1.02x in FY2023); Piotroski F-score 6 / Altman Z 6.62 show no distress [Certain] (source: RA, ST). Risk to durability: vitamin pricing is a global commodity cycle (China capacity, feed demand) that hit margins hard in FY2022-23 and could again [Likely].

## 3. Valuation
Current multiples sit at or below NHU's own five-year lows: P/E 11.2x (5y min 11.4x), EV/EBITDA 7.4x (min 7.6x), EV/Sales 3.2x (min 3.3x), P/FCF 12.2x (near min); only P/B (2.3x, 13% into range) is off its low [Certain] (source: RA, HI). Versus peers (Zhejiang Medicine, Zhejiang Garden Biochemical, BASF, DSM-Firmenich) it is cheaper on earnings/cash multiples (P/E -50%, EV/EBITDA -9%, P/FCF -56%) but dearer on EV/Sales (+71%) and P/B (+70%) [Certain] (source: RA) — consistent with NHU's higher margins/ROE commanding a premium on sales and book even while cheap on earnings. Base fair value CNY26.50 is only +1.4% above the CNY26.14 close; bull (+55.0%) needs the margin recovery to hold, bear (-83.4%) is a FY2022/23-style collapse persisting [Certain] (source: fact-sheet fair-value table). The reverse DCF implies the market prices only 5.3% year-one growth fading to terminal [Likely].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Gross margin (income statement) falls back below 35% for two consecutive quarters, signalling the vitamin-pricing cycle has turned down again rather than the FY2024/25 recovery being durable.
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) rises above 0.5x, showing capex or a margin downturn has eroded the net-cash position that funds the cycle.
- FCF margin (cash flow/income statement) falls below 10% TTM (vs 27.0% TTM now), showing capacity expansion is no longer converting to cash.
- ROIC (ratios page) falls below 12% for two consecutive fiscal years, showing new Chinese vitamin capacity has structurally eroded returns rather than this being a cyclical trough like FY2023.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
FCF conversion is strong and improving: FCF/NI 91.4% TTM (102.0% in FY2025, up from -15.8% in FY2022's downturn), funding a swing to net cash (net debt/EBITDA -0.25x TTM from 1.02x in FY2023) [Certain] (source: CF, IS, RA). Shares outstanding rose only 0.11% YoY, consistent with no meaningful dilution [Certain] (source: ST).
