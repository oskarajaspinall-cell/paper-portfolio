# Macro overlay: Valterra Platinum Limited (VAL) — 2026-10-19
## Assessment
Chain: VAL's revenue is the globally USD-priced PGM basket (platinum, palladium, rhodium) translated into ZAR, so the dollar and US real yields — not SA-specific factors — are the dominant external drivers of earnings/margin, fitting the "miners & commodities" primary case [Likely] (inference).

Facts (FRED, reports/macro/2026-10-19.md): dollar index (DTWEXBGS) 121.602, 2026-10-09, +3.72 (~3%) 1m, +1.1 3m [Certain]. 10y real yield (DFII10) 2.78%, 2026-10-15, +0.16pp 1m, +0.43pp 3m [Certain]. 2y Treasury (DGS2) 4.77% vs fed funds (DFF) 3.88%, 2026-10-15 — 2y ~0.9pp above fed funds, a proxy for market-expected further tightening, not a fact [Likely] (inference). HY spread (BAMLH0A0HYM2) 3.23% (+0.47pp 1m), still below the 5% "high" threshold [Certain]. VIX 16.46, falling [Certain]: calm risk appetite even as dollar/real-yield moves pressure USD commodity pricing. No ZAR/USD series in the FRED set, so the usual offset (ZAR often weakens with the dollar, cushioning ZAR revenue) can't be sized here [Guessing] (inference) — a real gap.

Bull: dollar/real yields plateau or reverse -> PGM basket firms -> VAL's recovering margin (43.9% TTM ROIC, 32.8% operating margin, factsheet) extends, supporting its premium-to-peers multiple.
Bear: dollar/real-yield rise persists -> USD PGM basket softens; without offsetting ZAR weakness, the margin recovery stalls just as the market already prices a premium to JSE peers (+28% P/E, +55% EV/EBITDA, factsheet), risking multiple compression.
Tilt: small, toward bear — the 1-month moves are concurrently unfavourable but start from benign absolute levels, a watch-item into the Oct 26 earnings release, not a proven regime shift.
