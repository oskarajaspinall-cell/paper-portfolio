# Valterra Platinum Limited (JSE:VAL) — Core initiation — 2026-10-19
## 1. Business
Valterra Platinum (formerly Anglo American Platinum, demerged from Anglo American in 2025) mines and processes platinum-group metals (platinum, palladium, rhodium, ruthenium, iridium, osmium) plus nickel, copper, cobalt sulphate, chrome and gold, mainly in South Africa [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/jse/VAL/). Revenue is overwhelmingly a function of the market PGM basket price and mined volumes; Mogalakwena is described as the world's largest open-pit platinum mine [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/jse/VAL/, headline dated 2026-10-14).

## 2. Quality
PGM mining is a commodity, price-taking business with no pricing power; Valterra's edge is being the lowest-cost, largest-scale producer [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/jse/VAL/, headline dated 2026-10-14), which let ROIC run 136.1%→85.2% in FY2021-22 before the 2022-24 PGM price crash dragged it to 14.4% (FY2024) and operating margin to 14.6% [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/jse/VAL/financials/ratios/, https://stockanalysis.com/quote/jse/VAL/financials/income-statement/). Both have since recovered to 19.8%/20.7% (FY2025) and 43.9%/32.8% TTM [Certain], evidence the cost advantage holds through the cycle rather than a one-off re-rating. Net debt/EBITDA has stayed negative (net cash) every year shown, FY2021-TTM [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/jse/VAL/financials/balance-sheet/) — balance-sheet strength that is the real differentiator from peers, letting it keep investing and paying dividends through troughs. The durable moat is asset quality/cost position, not demand: PGM autocatalyst demand faces a structural EV-substitution risk every producer shares equally [Likely].

## 3. Valuation
At 125,318 ZAc, VAL trades on 9.1x TTM P/E and 5.2x EV/EBITDA, both in the bottom third of its own 5-year range (17th percentile) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/jse/VAL/financials/ratios/) — the market is pricing a cyclical trough-to-mid recovery, not a repeat of the FY2021-22 PGM-price boom (24.0x P/E peak) [Likely]. Versus JSE PGM peers (Impala Platinum, Sibanye-Stillwater, Northam), VAL screens expensive: +28% on P/E, +55% on EV/EBITDA, +74% on EV/Sales, cheaper only on P/FCF (-7%) [Certain] (source: fact sheet valuation table, https://stockanalysis.com/quote/jse/IMP/statistics/, https://stockanalysis.com/quote/jse/SSW/statistics/, https://stockanalysis.com/quote/jse/NPH/statistics/). That premium is consistent with VAL's net-cash balance sheet and a 12.66% FCF yield [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/jse/VAL/statistics/), but no mechanical fair value could be computed here (NAV needs reserve/asset data, EV/EBITDA needs a ZAR FX rate; neither is on stockanalysis.com) [Certain] — this note cannot independently confirm the peer premium is earned, and that gap should weigh on conviction.

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) turns positive (net debt) for two consecutive half-years — the net-cash cushion and cost-curve advantage would be eroding.
- ROIC (ratios page) falls back below 15% on a full fiscal year — the lowest-cost-producer edge is not holding through the cycle.
- FCF margin (cash-flow/income-statement pages) turns negative for a full fiscal year — the FY2023 trough repeats rather than the current recovery extending.
- Operating margin (income-statement page) compresses back below 15% — reversion to the FY2023/24 trough, not a sustained recovery.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Net cash every year FY2021-TTM (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/jse/VAL/financials/balance-sheet/) funded FCF margin of 26.7% and FCF conversion of 114.1% of net income TTM [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/jse/VAL/financials/cash-flow-statement/), with the share count essentially flat (+0.21% YoY) — no dilution [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/jse/VAL/statistics/). Management has flagged a new underground development project to extend mine life, per an October 2026 report [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/jse/VAL/, headline dated 2026-10-14); planned capex is not disclosed on an allowlisted source, so its effect on free cash flow cannot be sized here [Guessing]. Net cash plus high FCF conversion should support dividends through the PGM cycle.

## 6. Unusual items
Operations are concentrated in South Africa, exposing VAL to single-country power-supply (load-shedding), labour and ZAR-currency risk shared with all SA peers [Likely]. Insider ownership is low (0.01%), typical following the 2025 demerger from Anglo American; institutional ownership is 54.1% [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/jse/VAL/statistics/). Short interest is [data unavailable]. Nothing here is material enough to override the quality/valuation picture above.
