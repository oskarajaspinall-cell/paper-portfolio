Bear: further tightening (per the 2y/funds proxy) raises consumer stress and funding costs together -> NCOs reverse higher, reserve builds resume, ROE compresses, the peer discount persists.
Tilt: the note already flags SYF's beta (1.34) and NCO rate near its target ceiling as macro-sensitive; current data contradicts the forward-rate risk, so this is a secondary layer on a fundamentals-led thesis.
