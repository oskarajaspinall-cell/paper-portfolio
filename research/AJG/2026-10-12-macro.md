# Macro overlay: Arthur J. Gallagher & Co. (AJG) — 2026-10-12
## Assessment
AJG is a fee-based brokerage, not rate-sensitive like a bank, but two features make rates relevant: a premium multiple (38.6x TTM P/E vs 19.0x peer median) and a debt-funded acquisition engine (net debt/EBITDA 2.97x TTM, up from net cash in FY2024) [Certain]. Chain: real yields ↑ → equity discount rate ↑ → high-multiple names re-rate down; real yields ↑ → cost of acquisition debt ↑ → pressure on an already-falling ROIC/ROE trend [Likely] **(inference)**.

Facts (FRED, 2026-10-08/09): 10y real yield (DFII10) 2.87%, +0.44pp/1m, +0.56pp/3m; 10y nominal (DGS10) 5.22%, +0.68pp/3m; 2y (DGS2) 4.75%; breakeven (T10YIE) 2.33%, roughly flat (+0.09pp/3m); fed funds (DFF) 3.88%; HY spread 3.15%, +0.45pp/3m. Market pricing (proxy, **inference**): flat breakevens mean the yield rise is mostly real/term-premium, not an inflation scare; DGS2 ~87bp above DFF implies the market prices policy staying higher-for-longer, not near-term cuts.

Already priced: AJG is down -24.2% over 12 months, trailing SPY by -39.9pp [Certain, factsheet], consistent with the market already de-rating it for rising real yields and falling ROIC — not a fresh surprise. But the +103% P/E premium and rising leverage mean a further real-yield surprise still bites before Oct 29 earnings disclose financing costs and organic growth.

Bull: yields stabilise/ease → multiple re-rates up, cheaper refinancing aids M&A. Bear: yields/spreads widen further → more multiple compression plus costlier debt compound the falling-ROIC trend. Tilt: moderate toward bear — the move is real and recent, though much is already in the price **(inference)**.
