# Arthur J. Gallagher & Co. (AJG) — Core initiation — 2026-10-12

## 1. Business
Arthur J. Gallagher is a global insurance brokerage and risk-management services firm. The Brokerage segment (the larger segment) earns commissions, fees, and supplemental/contingent revenue placing property-casualty and employee-benefits risk for corporate and public-entity clients with insurers. The Risk Management segment, mainly Gallagher Bassett, earns fees for third-party claims administration and risk consulting. Growth is organic plus a continuous tuck-in and occasional larger acquisition programme (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/354190/000162828026008662/ajg-20251231.htm) [Certain].

## 2. Quality
The moat rests on broker/client relationships, niche underwriting specialisation and scale that supports a continuous acquisition pipeline (source: 10-K, above) [Certain]. These switching costs are real, but returns on the capital now being deployed are falling: ROIC declined from 10.5% (FY2021) to 6.6% TTM, and ROE from 12.9% to 6.7% TTM (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/ajg/financials/ratios/) [Certain]. Net debt/EBITDA rose to 3.51x in FY2025 and 2.97x TTM, after a brief FY2024 net-cash position, consistent with the 10-K's own description of a recent "larger than typical" debt-part-funded acquisition (senior notes at 2.4%-6.75%, maturities to 2055) (source: 10-K, above) [Certain]. Margins are still healthy and improving (operating margin 19.9% TTM, gross margin 43.2% TTM) [Certain], but FCF conversion fell from 166.8% (FY2024) to 145.7% TTM (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/ajg/financials/cash-flow-statement/, ratios page above) [Certain]. The underlying business is good, but marginal returns on the current roll-up strategy are weakening — the key quality risk.

## 3. Valuation
At 232.70 USD, AJG trades on 38.6x TTM P/E, 16.9x EV/EBITDA, 4.8x EV/Sales and 26.1x P/FCF (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/ajg/financials/ratios/, https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/ajg/statistics/) [Certain]. On P/E that is the cheapest quintile of its own 5-year range (35.7x-50.1x) [Certain], but AJG still commands a +103% P/E, +40% EV/EBITDA, +22% EV/Sales and +54% P/FCF premium to the peer median (MMC, AON, WTW, BRO) [Certain]; only P/B is cheaper than peers (-45%), a low-quality signal for a fee-based brokerage whose book value is mostly M&A goodwill rather than tangible capital [Likely]. The fact sheet's own-history/reverse-DCF model puts base fair value at 236.20 USD, only +1.5% above last close, with bear/bull of 192.54/315.46 (source: fact sheet fair-value model, factsheet-2026-10-12.md) [Certain]. The market is already pricing continued premium multiples and stable margins; there is no valuation cushion to offset the declining-ROIC trend above.

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROIC (ratios page) recovers above 9% (its FY2021-22 average), signalling recent M&A is earning adequate returns.
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) falls back below 2.5x, showing deleveraging after the 2025 acquisition wave.
- FCF conversion (FCF/NI, cash-flow and income-statement pages) recovers above 160%, last seen in FY2024.
- Organic brokerage growth, next disclosed at the Oct 29, 2026 earnings release, reaccelerates to double digits, justifying the peer premium.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Capital is directed almost entirely at M&A: shares outstanding rose 6.58% YoY (stock-funded deals) and net debt/EBITDA rose to 2.97x TTM from a brief FY2024 net-cash position as acquisitions — including one the 10-K calls "larger than typical" — were debt-part-funded (source: 10-K, above; https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/ajg/financials/ratios/) [Certain]. FCF margin fell to 15.1% TTM from 22.3% in FY2024, and FCF conversion to 145.7% from 166.8% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/ajg/financials/cash-flow-statement/) [Certain]. No buyback programme is visible in the data provided; capital discipline looks weaker than the historical roll-up record implies [Likely].

## 6. Unusual items
None material. Short interest is low (3.25% of float) though it rose 23.0% month-on-month, and insider ownership is low (0.97%) but typical for a company of this size (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/ajg/statistics/) [Certain].
