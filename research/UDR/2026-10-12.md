# UDR, Inc. (UDR) — Core initiation — 2026-10-12

## 1. Business
UDR is an apartment REIT owning and operating communities across coastal and Sun Belt U.S. markets, earning rental income from middle/upper-income renters [Certain] (source: https://ir.udr.com/). It also holds stakes in unconsolidated joint ventures with MetLife, LaSalle and Carmel Partners, adding fee/equity income alongside its wholly-owned portfolio [Certain] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/74208/000007420826000073/udr-20260630x10q.htm).

## 2. Quality
Returns are improving: ROIC has risen from 1.5% (FY2021) to 3.8% TTM, and ROE from 3.6% to 13.7% TTM, both 5y highs [Certain] (fact sheet: RA). Net debt/EBITDA has fallen from 7.29x to 5.76x over five years but remains high in absolute terms, typical for the sector [Certain] (fact sheet: RA). Q2 2026 results beat expectations and management raised full-year guidance for revenue, NOI and FFOA per share, with coastal markets leading growth and Sun Belt improving [Certain] (source: https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260726611735/en/UDR-Inc.-Announces-Second-Quarter-2026-Results-and-Raises-Full-Year-2026-Guidance-Ranges/). The durable edge is scale and geographic/price-point diversification across desirable markets plus an investment-grade balance sheet, which cushions local supply shocks [Likely] (source: https://ir.udr.com/). Apartment REITs have limited structural moats beyond location quality and operating scale — new Sun Belt supply can pressure local rents — so returns depend on continued execution, not a hard competitive barrier [Likely].

## 3. Valuation
Signals conflict by multiple. P/E (21.6x) is 37% below the peer median (34.2x) and below UDR's own 5y range minimum; EV/EBITDA (17.0x) is 11% below peers and also below its 5y minimum — both imply the market is pricing UDR cheaply on earnings/EBITDA [Certain] (fact sheet: RA). But P/B (3.8x) is 44% above the peer median and near the top (93%) of its own 5y range, implying the market pays up for UDR's book equity specifically [Certain] (fact sheet: RA, P:EQR/AVB/ESS/CPT/MAA). FCF yield is modest at 2.97% [Certain] (fact sheet: ST). The fair-value model (P/FFO proxy, base case) implies 84.5% upside to 62.96 versus the 34.12 last close [Certain] (fact sheet: IS,BS,CF,RA,ST,HI) — this is far larger than the 11% discount visible on EV/EBITDA versus peers, so the model's upside looks assumption-driven (growth/margin normalization) rather than market-observable; it should be treated as directional, inform-only context, not a sized target.

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) rises above 6.5x, reversing the five-year de-leveraging trend
- ROIC (ratios page) falls back below 2.5% TTM, reversing the five-year improvement
- Piotroski F-Score (statistics page) falls below 5, signalling broad fundamental deterioration
- Short interest (statistics page) keeps rising materially beyond its current 3.44% of float, confirming building bearish positioning against the thesis

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
UDR declared its 220th consecutive monthly common dividend on 2026-09-24, an unbroken record signalling payout discipline [Certain] (source: https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260924534092/en/UDR-Inc.-Announces-220th-Consecutive-Common-Stock-Dividend/). Shares outstanding fell 1.03% year-over-year, consistent with modest net buybacks rather than dilution [Certain] (fact sheet: ST). FCF yield of 2.97% is thin relative to the dividend commitment, so coverage depends on continued NOI/FFO growth rather than a large cash cushion [Likely] (fact sheet: ST). The 10-Q shows development activity increasing versus year-end 2025, a use of capital that adds execution risk if lease-up is slower than planned [Likely] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/74208/000007420826000073/udr-20260630x10q.htm).

## 6. Unusual items
Short interest rose 24.7% month-over-month to 3.44% of float — a notable increase but not yet an extreme level [Likely] (fact sheet: ST). Beta is low at 0.71, consistent with the sector's defensive, rate-sensitive character [Certain] (fact sheet: ST). Institutional ownership is very high at 97.9% versus 0.42% insider ownership, typical for a large-cap REIT and not itself a red flag [Certain] (fact sheet: ST). Otherwise none.
