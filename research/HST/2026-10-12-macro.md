# Macro overlay: Host Hotels & Resorts, Inc. (HST) — 2026-10-12

## Assessment
HST is a leveraged (net debt/EBITDA ~2.2x-3.3x [Certain], source: factsheet-2026-10-12.md) lodging REIT whose CAPM fair value and P/AFFO-style multiple behave like a cap rate. Rates and credit conditions shift the valuation range and refinancing cost, even though the core thesis (RevPAR recovery, deleveraging) is fundamentals-driven, not a macro call — **secondary**, not primary.

**Chain**: real yields & credit spreads -> cost of capital/cap rate -> REIT multiple (P/AFFO) & refinancing cost -> fair value range.

**What's priced** — Facts (FRED, fred.stlouisfed.org, 2026-10-08/09): 10y real yield (DFII10) 2.87%, +44bp/1m, +56bp/3m; 10y Treasury (DGS10) 5.22%, +42bp/1m, +68bp/3m; 2y (DGS2) 4.75% vs effective fed funds (DFF) 3.88% (+87bp, proxy: no near-term cuts priced); 10y breakeven (T10YIE) 2.33%, roughly flat; HY spread (BAMLH0A0HYM2) 3.15%, +48bp/1m, +45bp/3m (below the regime's 5.0% "high" bar). **Inference**: breakevens flat while real yields jumped means the discount-rate rise is a real-rate/term-premium move, compressing cap-rate valuations directly [Likely] **(inference)**. The factsheet's fair value already uses today's 5.22% risk-free rate, so the level is priced; the risk is further deterioration.

**Bull**: yields/spreads ease -> cap-rate compression -> P/AFFO multiple expands off the ~flat base case. **Bear**: real yields and HY spreads keep climbing -> cap-rate expansion plus pricier refinancing -> fair value drifts toward/below the $20.55 bear case already modeled.

**Tilt**: toward bear, moderate — the 3-month FRED moves (real yield +56bp, HY spread +45bp) both point against a rate-sensitive, leveraged REIT with no margin of safety to its $22.67 base fair value (close $22.65) [Likely].
