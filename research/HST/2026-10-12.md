# Host Hotels & Resorts, Inc. (HST) — Core initiation — 2026-10-12
## 1. Business
Host is the largest U.S. lodging REIT: it owns (not operates) 75 luxury/upper-upscale hotels (~41,300 rooms) across 21 top U.S. markets, leased to third-party brand operators under management contracts [Certain] (source: https://www.hosthotels.com). Revenue is hotel operating income driven by RevPAR (room/F&B/other), net of brand management fees, with capital recycled via periodic acquisitions and dispositions [Certain] (source: https://www.hosthotels.com).

## 2. Quality
ROIC rose from -1.1% (FY21) to 6.9% TTM and ROE from -0.2% to 15.6% TTM [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/hst/financials/ratios/). Net debt/EBITDA (site definition) fell from 8.69x (FY21) to 2.18x TTM [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/hst/financials/ratios/) — meaningful post-pandemic deleveraging. Piotroski F-score is 5 [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/hst/statistics/) — moderate, not exceptional. The moat is thin: scale and a supply-constrained, upper-upscale asset base help, but Host does not control the brands/operators driving RevPAR, so it has no structural cost or pricing advantage versus peer hotel REITs (PK, APLE, SHO, DRH); returns largely track the broader lodging cycle [Likely]. Q2 2026: comparable RevPAR +7.0%, Total RevPAR +5.9%, adjusted EBITDAre +5.8% YoY, and FY2026 guidance was raised [Likely] (source: https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/08/05/3339778/0/en/Host-Hotels-Resorts-Inc-Reports-Results-for-the-Second-Quarter-of-2026.html). Net: an improving, better-capitalised balance sheet inside a commodity-like asset class, not a durable competitive edge.

## 3. Valuation
P/E 15.2x sits 27% below its own 5y range minimum (15.9x) and 31% below the peer median (22.1x) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/hst/financials/ratios/) — looks cheap on earnings. EV/EBITDA 11.5x is near the middle of its 5y range (10.6x–32.9x, median 11.4x) and 6% below peer median (12.2x) [Certain] — broadly in line. P/B 2.4x is 221% above its own 5y range max (2.0x) and 93% above peer median (1.3x) [Certain] — stretched, though P/B is a weak gauge for REITs carrying real estate at historical cost. FCF yield is 6.46% [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/hst/statistics/). The fact sheet's own-history fair value (P/AFFO proxy, best method) is $22.67 base versus a last close of $22.65 — essentially 0% upside [Certain] (source: research/HST/factsheet-2026-10-12.md). Bear case is $20.55 (-9.3%); bull case only $23.27 (+2.7%) — the skew is asymmetric to the downside, with the market already pricing HST at roughly fair value.
