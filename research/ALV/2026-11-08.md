# Autoliv (ALV) — Core initiation — 2026-11-08
## 1. Business
Autoliv is the largest supplier of automotive passive safety: airbags, steering wheels and seatbelts, sold to global OEMs on multi-year platform awards and priced with raw-material and tariff pass-through (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/alv/) [Certain]. Revenue follows global light-vehicle production (LVP) plus safety content per vehicle. Q3 sales to Chinese OEMs grew by more than 20% [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1034670/000119312526429588/alv-20260930.htm). China's share of total revenue: [data unavailable] in the fact sheet and not retrieved from the filing excerpt [Certain].

## 2. Quality
Returns are high for Auto Parts: TTM ROIC 18.5%, ROCE 24.8%, operating margin 10.2%, FCF margin 6.6%, FCF conversion 117.0%, net debt/EBITDA 1.21x (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/alv/financials/ratios/) [Certain]. The moat is oligopoly structure with Joyson and ZF, regulatory-driven content, and validation/liability barriers to entry [Likely].
Direction: ROIC is flat year on year but 4.8pp below its 2024 high, and ROCE is down 1.2pp over 12 months (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/alv/financials/ratios/?p=trailing). Q3 adjusted operating margin was 9.7% and guidance is narrowed to the low end of its range, on raw materials, logistics, Thailand flooding and call-off volatility, mostly offset by cost action [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1034670/000119312526429588/alv-20260930.htm). Margins have plateaued; the 5-year rise is real but momentum has stalled [Likely]. Tariffs are mostly passed through to customers [company-reported] (same filing).

## 3. Valuation
P/E 14.8x sits at 37% of its 5y range (11.3x–20.8x); EV/EBITDA 6.9x is near the bottom of its range (6.7x–9.6x); P/FCF 12.2x is below its 5y minimum; FCF yield 8.20% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/alv/statistics/) [Certain]. Peers (APTV, BWA, LEA, GNTX) trade at 10.7x P/E and 5.1x EV/EBITDA, so ALV carries a premium of +38% on P/E and +34% on EV/EBITDA, consistent with higher returns and lower cyclicality [Likely]. Reverse DCF: the price implies 1.8% year-1 revenue growth fading to terminal, a low bar for a share-gaining leader. Fair value base is 141.64 against a 123.47 close (+14.7%); bear 47.22, bull 157.90 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/alv/financials/income-statement/) [Likely]. The bear case is wide because the DCF is sensitive to margin; the EV/EBITDA bear is far tighter [Guessing]. The price assumes flat volumes and stable margins.
