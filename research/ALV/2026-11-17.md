# Autoliv (ALV) — Core initiation — 2026-11-17
## 1. Business
Autoliv is the largest automotive safety supplier: airbags, seatbelts and steering wheels sold to car makers under multi-year programmes, with earnings tied to global light-vehicle production (LVP) and safety content per car [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/alv/). Q3 sales to Chinese OEMs grew by more than 20% [company-reported] (https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1034670/000119312526429588/alv-20260930.htm). China's share of revenue: [data unavailable] (the 10-Q regional note was not readable); the filing says Asia sales exceeded China's in the quarter, and India is growing fast [company-reported] (same URL).

## 2. Quality
Good business with a durable position [Likely]: a small oligopoly in safety (certification, scale, liability and launch track record) makes entry hard. Returns are high and rising on five-year endpoints: ROIC 19.9% in FY2025, 18.5% TTM; ROCE 24.8% TTM; operating margin 10.2%; FCF conversion 117% TTM; net debt/EBITDA 1.21x (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/alv/financials/ratios/).
Direction check: ROIC is 4.8pp below its 2024 high and ROCE fell from 26.0% to 24.8% over 12 months (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/alv/financials/ratios/?p=trailing). Cause: raw-material, logistics and tariff costs, only mostly passed through to customers [company-reported] (https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1034670/000119312526429588/alv-20260930.htm). This is cost pressure, not lost share [Likely]; FCF margin rose from 5.4% to 6.6% [Certain].

## 3. Valuation
Price 117.37 (2026-11-16), near both its 50-day (116.83) and 200-day (117.97) averages, 12-month change -2.1% [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/alv/history/). EV/EBITDA 6.7x is at the bottom of its 5y range (6.7x–9.6x); P/FCF 11.8x is below its 5y minimum; P/E 14.1x sits at 30% of its range; FCF yield 8.48% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/alv/statistics/). Versus peers (APTV, LEA, BWA, GNTX) it trades at a premium on every multiple (P/E +37%, EV/EBITDA +31%), which quality arguably justifies [Likely]. Mechanical base fair value 138.05, +17.6%; the bear value of 46.40 comes from the DCF and is a wide, unreliable range [Likely]. The reverse DCF implies 1.5% year-1 revenue growth, a low bar [Certain]. The drop from the 127.25 swing high (2026-11-05) follows Q3: the full-year adjusted operating margin guidance narrowed to the low end of its range [company-reported] (https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1034670/000119312526429588/alv-20260930.htm) [Certain].
