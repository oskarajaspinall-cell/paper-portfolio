# SBA Communications Corporation (SBAC) — Core initiation — 2026-10-09

## 1. Business
SBA owns and operates wireless communication towers, leasing antenna space to wireless carriers (AT&T, Verizon, T-Mobile and international operators) under long-term contracts with built-in annual escalators; a smaller site-development segment builds/upgrades sites for carriers [Certain]. Beyond the U.S., SBA operates across the Americas (notably Brazil and Central America) and in Africa (source: https://ir.sbasite.com/) [Certain].

## 2. Quality
The tower REIT oligopoly (SBAC, AMT, CCI) has durable structural advantages: new tower construction is constrained by zoning/permitting, and once a carrier's antennas are installed, relocation is costly, so multi-year leases with escalators recur with high renewal rates [Likely]. This can't be verified from margins: gross/operating/net/FCF margins and ROIC/ROCE/ROE are all [data unavailable] — a real gap in checking returns on capital.
Leverage is measurable: net debt/EBITDA (site definition) is 8.12x TTM, down from 9.35x in FY2021 — a five-year deleveraging trend [Certain], but still high for a business with real refinancing needs. Piotroski F-Score is 4/9, middling [Certain]. Management told the RBC conference capital allocation "currently favors share buybacks over debt repayment" (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/sbac/transcripts/752299-rbc-capital-markets-2026-global-communications-and-infrastructure-conference/) [Likely] — favoring buybacks over delevering above 8x is a discipline risk if rates stay elevated. Shares outstanding fell only -1.25% YoY, so the buyback is modest [Certain].

## 3. Valuation
SBAC trades at a discount to its own five-year history and to peers: P/E 18.3x is below its 5y range minimum of 19.6x (peer median 28.1x, -35%); EV/EBITDA 18.0x is below its 5y minimum 19.5x (peer median 19.2x, -7%); EV/Sales 11.5x is below its 5y minimum 12.5x (peer median 12.3x, -7%) [Certain]. Trading below the five-year low on every multiple implies the market is pricing structural deceleration — consistent with management calling the business a post-5G "harvest phase" (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/sbac/transcripts/740230-bank-of-america-2026-media-communications-entertainment-conference/) [Likely], not a temporary dislocation. FCF yield is 5.83%.
The fair-value model (P/AFFO proxy, the only usable method; NAV unavailable) gives base $276 vs the $170.01 close (+62%), bear $188 (+10.7%), bull $318 (+87%) [Guessing] — single-method, no NAV cross-check, wide bear-bull spread: a directional signal, not a target.

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page, site definition) rises back above 9.0x, reversing the five-year deleveraging trend
- Piotroski F-Score (statistics page) falls below 4, confirming deteriorating fundamentals
- Site leasing revenue growth (income statement) turns negative for two consecutive fiscal years, confirming the "harvest phase" is a structural decline, not a plateau
- FCF margin or FCF conversion (cash flow/income statement) cannot be computed due to a sustained drop in operating cash flow

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
FCF yield is 5.83% TTM; FCF margin itself is [data unavailable]. Shares outstanding fell -1.25% YoY, a modest buyback rather than aggressive capital return [Certain]. Management has stated a preference for buybacks over debt repayment despite leverage near 8x (source: RBC transcript above) [Likely] — shareholder-friendly over balance-sheet repair, a risk if refinancing costs keep rising. New debt issuance referenced on the Q2 2026 call strengthened near-term liquidity, per management commentary (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/sbac/transcripts/657376-q2-2026/) [Likely], though no specific figures from that call are used as data here.

## 6. Unusual items
A 2026-10-05 release describes a joint venture (Forum Markets / Edge Node AI) to deploy AI inference compute at some existing powered data sites and towers (~11MW initial capacity) (source: https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/forum-markets-announces-joint-venture-with-edge-node-ai-to-deploy-inference-ai-compute-across-the-us-at-existing-powered-data-sites-and-mobile-communications-towers-302898361.html) [Guessing] — early-stage optionality, not yet visible in any reported financials, and should not be treated as a near-term earnings driver. Short interest is low and stable (3.60% of float, +1.4% MoM) [Certain] — not a crowding risk. Institutional ownership is very high (99.9%), unremarkable for a large-cap REIT.
