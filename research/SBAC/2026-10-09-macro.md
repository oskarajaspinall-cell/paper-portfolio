# Macro overlay: SBA Communications Corporation (SBAC) — 2026-10-09
## Assessment
SBAC is a tower REIT with long escalator leases but 8.12x net debt/EBITDA [Certain] (factsheet, stockanalysis.com ratios page, 2026-10-09) and management preferring buybacks over delevering [Likely] — real-rate/credit-spread sensitivity on top of the bottom-up deceleration thesis. **Chain (inference)**: rising real yields and credit spreads raise the REIT's discount rate (compressing the P/AFFO multiple) and refinancing cost, squeezing AFFO headroom on an already-leveraged balance sheet.

**Facts**: 10y real yield (DFII10) 2.92% on 2026-10-07, +0.49pp ~1m, +0.61pp ~3m; 10y Treasury (DGS10) 5.28% same date, +0.72pp ~3m; high-yield spread (BAMLH0A0HYM2) 3.09%, +0.39pp ~3m; 2y yield 4.77% vs effective fed funds 3.88% (all https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=..., 2026-10-07).

**Market pricing (proxy, inference)**: 2y yield 0.89pp above effective fed funds implies the curve prices further policy tightening or a higher terminal rate, not cuts — the opposite of what a levered REIT needs.

**Already priced**: the fact sheet's DCF risk-free input (5.28%) already uses the current DGS10 level, so today's yield is in the base case ($276); it is the *direction of further change*, not the level, that matters.

**Bull**: real yields/spreads stabilize or ease → discount rate relief → multiple re-rates toward the 5y range. **Bear**: yields/spreads keep rising → discount rate and refinancing cost rise further on 8.12x leverage → fair value and multiple compress below the current 5y-low base. **Tilt**: toward bear, moderate — the stock is already priced for structural deceleration, and a continuing real-yield leg up adds a second, leverage-amplified headwind fundamentals alone don't capture.
