# CF Industries Holdings (CF) — Core initiation — 2026-10-07

## 1. Business
CF Industries is North America's largest nitrogen fertilizer producer, making ammonia, urea, UAN and AN at nine US/UK/Canada complexes, sold mainly to agricultural distributors and industrial customers [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/cf/). Economics hinge on the spread between low-cost North American natural gas feedstock and globally set nitrogen product prices, which track grain economics and international gas/coal-linked supply [Certain]. It is also building low-carbon "blue" ammonia capacity (Blue Point JV) for clean energy/export demand (source: https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260826407037/en/CF-Industries-JERA-Co.-and-Mitsui-Co.-Ltd.-Break-Ground-on-Blue-Point-One-the-World%E2%80%99s-Largest-Low-Carbon-Ammonia-Plant-in-Louisiana/).

## 2. Quality
Returns are strong: TTM ROIC 24.5%, ROE 29.9%, gross margin 42.5% (rising +1.9pp/5y), operating margin 38.6%, net debt/EBITDA only 0.29x [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/cf/financials/ratios/). The moat is North American shale-gas feedstock access versus higher-cost European/Asian producers, durable because new Gulf Coast gas supply is itself scarce and capital-intensive to replicate [Likely]. Risk to durability: CF's own Blue Point ammonia JV (CF 40%/JERA 35%/Mitsui 25%, production from 2029) plus global capacity adds could compress nitrogen spreads over the cycle [Likely] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1324404/000132440426000019/cf-20260630.htm). FCF quality has already softened: FCF margin fell from 36.1% (FY2021) to 24.7% TTM, and FCF/NI conversion fell from 257% to 91%, consistent with heavier Blue Point capex [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/cf/financials/cash-flow-statement/). A September headline on a possible US-Belarus potash deal hit fertilizer stocks including CF, but that concerns potash (Nutrien's product), not CF's nitrogen book, so the direct read-through is limited [Likely] (source: https://www.barrons.com/articles/cf-industries-stock-nutrien-fertilizer-deal-trump-belarus-de7b3beb).

## 3. Valuation
CF trades on 8.6x P/E and 4.8x EV/EBITDA, both inside the bottom half of its own 5y range (31%ile and 42%ile) and well below the peer median (20.5x and 7.0x; peers MOS, NTR, ICL, LXU) — the market is not paying up for CF's earnings or EBITDA [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/cf/financials/ratios/). Yet P/FCF (9.2x) and P/B (3.1x) sit above CF's own 5y maximum (120% and 157% of range), though P/B is still above peer median (+157%) while P/FCF is below it (-40%) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/cf/statistics/). Read together, the price assumes nitrogen margins stay near current elevated levels (supporting the cheap earnings multiple) while discounting that free cash flow conversion keeps falling as Blue Point capex ramps — the P/FCF and P/B stretch versus CF's own history reflects that cash, not earnings, is the scarcer resource right now [Likely].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) rises above 1.5x, showing buybacks/capex are being funded with leverage beyond the current low-debt stance.
- FCF margin (cash flow + income statement) falls below 15% TTM, showing capex and/or price erosion are overwhelming cash generation faster than modeled.
- Gross margin (income statement) falls below 30% TTM on a sustained basis, showing the gas-cost advantage or nitrogen pricing has structurally weakened.
- ROIC (ratios page) falls below 10%, showing returns no longer clear a reasonable cost of capital through the cycle.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
CF is returning cash while self-funding growth: a $750m buyback authorized May 2025 had $247m used by June 30, 2026 (~$503m remaining), and the quarterly dividend was raised 20% to $0.60/share in July 2026 from $0.50 in Q2 2026 [Certain] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1324404/000132440426000019/cf-20260630.htm; https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260708744551/en/CF-Industries-Holdings-Inc.-Announces-20-Increase-in-Quarterly-Dividend/). Blue Point capex is being shared with JV partners (CF contributed $156m of its 40% share in H1 2026) [Certain], and the balance sheet remains light: $3,250m long-term debt, $2,480m cash and an undrawn $750m revolver as of June 30, 2026 [Certain] (same 10-Q source). TTM FCF conversion is still above 1x NI (91%) despite the capex ramp [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/cf/financials/cash-flow-statement/).

## 6. Unusual items
Short interest is 6.50% of float, up 5.8% month-over-month — elevated but not extreme [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/cf/statistics/). No governance or accounting flags found; Altman Z 3.34 and Piotroski F 7 both indicate low distress risk [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/cf/statistics/).
