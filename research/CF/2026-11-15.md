# CF Industries Holdings (CF) — Core initiation — 2026-11-15
## 1. Business
World's largest ammonia producer; makes nitrogen fertilizer (ammonia, urea, UAN, ammonium nitrate) and industrial products (DEF, nitric acid) from North American natural gas, sold to co-ops, retailers, distributors and industrial users [Certain] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1324404/000132440426000025/cf-20260930.htm). Nine-month 2026 net sales: North America 84.1%, "Europe and other" 15.9% [company-reported] (https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1324404/000132440426000025/cf-20260930.htm). No single foreign country is disclosed at ~10%+; the UK plants sit inside the European share [Likely].

## 2. Quality
Strong cyclical-commodity business, not a growth compounder. TTM ROIC 25.4% vs 20.6% in FY2025 and 18.8% a year earlier; operating margin 38.5%; net debt/EBITDA 0.21x; Piotroski 7 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/cf/financials/ratios/). [Certain]
Moat: low-cost US gas feedstock versus marginal European/Asian producers, plus unmatched ammonia distribution and logistics. [Likely]
Direction: ROCE is falling over five fiscal years (-2.2pp) but rising over the last 12 months (16.6% to 20.8%); the five-year fall reflects the 2022 spike (45.2%) rolling off, so the recent rise is the cycle, not a structural change. [Likely] FCF margin is 26.2%, 4.2pp below its 2-year high of 30.5%, and FCF conversion fell to 95.4% of net income, from 143.9% in FY2024, as capex rises (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/cf/financials/cash-flow-statement/?p=trailing). [Likely]
Q3 2026 revenue beat per a headline (https://www.tipranks.com/news/the-fly/cf-industries-reports-q3-eps-2-87-consensus-2-83-thefly-news) [Likely].

## 3. Valuation
Price 125.09 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/cf/history/). P/E 8.8x sits at 33% of its 5y range (median 10.0x) and EV/EBITDA 4.8x at 42% (median 5.7x); both about 45% below the peer median, though peers are few and different (potash, seeds). P/FCF 8.8x and P/B 3.2x are above their 5y maxima, so cash-flow cheapness has narrowed. FCF yield 11.37% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/cf/statistics/). [Certain]
Mechanical fair value base 187.81 (+50.1%), bear 111.56 (-10.8%), bull 264.10; the through-cycle DCF anchors it and the EV/EBITDA leg is 140.42 (source: fact sheet). [Likely] The price implies -13.4% revenue growth in year 1: the market is pricing a nitrogen-price downcycle. Earnings are above mid-cycle (TTM ROIC at a 2-year high), so the DCF upside may be generous. [Guessing]
Trend: 6.2% above the 200-day average, +47.6% over 12 months; healthy, not overextended.

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROIC (ratios page, TTM) falls below 15%, the FY2024 trough area, with no offset from lower capex.
- Operating margin (income-statement page, TTM) falls below 28%.
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) rises above 1.5x.
- FCF margin (cash-flow page, TTM) falls below 18% while buybacks continue.
