# Macro overlay: Mid-America Apartment Communities (MAA) — 2026-10-12
## Assessment
MAA is a Sunbelt residential REIT carrying 4.62x net debt/EBITDA [Certain] (https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/maa/financials/ratios/, 2026-10-12), so real yields are the primary macro channel: real yields → cap rates/cost of capital → P/AFFO multiple, and real yields → refinancing cost → FFO growth **(inference)**.

Facts (FRED, 2026-10-08): 10y real yield (DFII10) 2.87%, +44bp/1m, +56bp/3m (https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=DFII10); 10y nominal (DGS10) 5.22%, +42bp/1m, +68bp/3m; 10y breakeven (T10YIE) 2.33%, flat (+9bp/3m, 2026-10-09); fed funds (DFF) 3.88%, +25bp/1m; 2y yield (DGS2) 4.75%.

Market pricing **(inference)**: flat breakevens with nominal/real yields jumping together mean this is a real-rate/term-premium shock, not inflation — raising discount rates with no rent-growth offset. The 2y yield (4.75%) 87bp above fed funds (3.88%) proxies tighter-for-longer pricing, not cuts (FRED, 2026-10-08).

Already priced **(inference)**: MAA is down 14.1% over 3 months, lagging SPY by 17.6pp, roughly coincident with the yield spike (fact sheet, 2026-10-09); EV/EBITDA (15.8x) already sits below its 5y floor (16.0x) and 17% below peers (19.1x) — some pressure looks reflected.

macro_bull: yields stabilize/ease (contained breakevens, a Fed pause) → cap rates ease → multiple re-rates toward the 19.1x peer median, adding to the +18% base upside.
macro_bear: yields keep rising on a hawkish path → cap rates rise further, refinancing cost on 4.62x leverage increases → multiple stays depressed, FFO stalls, fair value cut.

macro_tilt: toward bear, moderate — a large real-rate-driven move hits an already-levered, underperforming REIT, amplifying rather than offsetting the leverage/oversupply risk already in the note.
