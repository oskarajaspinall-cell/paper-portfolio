# Mediobanca Banca di Credito Finanziario S.p.A. (BIT:MB) — Core initiation — 2026-11-16
## 1. Business
Italian merchant and regional bank, classed Banks - Regional (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/bit/MB/). It earns net interest income, fees and investment-banking income, and reports in EUR. Headlines say Monte dei Paschi (MPS) recently acquired it and that Intesa Sanpaolo now plans a buyout offer on the terms of MPS's failed bid (source: https://www.reuters.com/legal/transactional/intesas-offer-mediobanca-keep-terms-failed-mps-bid-document-shows-2026-11-11/) [Likely]. Revenue mix by country: [data unavailable], no foreign market is documented as ~10%+.

## 2. Quality
Decent but not exceptional bank economics. ROE rose from 8.3% (FY2022) to 11.8% (FY2025), and net margin rose from 36.8% to 40.0%, 41.8% on a TTM basis (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/bit/MB/financials/ratios/) [Certain]. ROIC, ROCE and leverage are [data unavailable], as expected for a bank. Piotroski F is 3, which is weak (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/bit/MB/statistics/) [Certain]. FCF is meaningless for a bank: the FCF margin swings from 207.8% to -110.0% to 95.1% across years, with TTM at 113.3% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/bit/MB/financials/cash-flow-statement/) [Certain]. No durable moat is evidenced in the data. Italian merchant-banking franchises are competitive, and the deal situation matters more than the franchise [Guessing].

## 3. Valuation
The price is 27.37 EUR (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/bit/MB/history/). P/E of 19.1x is above its 5y max of 14.0x (median 9.0x) and 68% above the peer median of 11.4x. P/B of 1.9x is above its 5y max of 1.4x and 20% above peers' 1.5x [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/bit/MB/statistics/). The mechanical fair value (P/B with sustainable ROE, FCFE and DDM) is 16.74 EUR base and 18.24 EUR bull, so the price is 33% to 55% above every case, and the reverse DCF implies 9.9% year-1 growth [Certain]. The trend is strong: +16.2% above the 200-day average, with a 52.1% 12-month change. The market is plausibly pricing a takeover premium rather than standalone value [Likely]. P/FCF of 7.1x and a 13.99% FCF yield are not reliable for a bank.

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROE (ratios page) falls below 9% on a fiscal-year basis.
- Net margin (income statement page) falls below 35% on a TTM basis.
- A completed Intesa or other takeover that removes the stock from listing or changes its capital structure (company or Reuters filings), which would end an independent thesis.
- Shares change YoY (statistics page) turns above +3%, a sign of dilutive issuance.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Shares change YoY is -0.49%, so there is mild net buyback or no dilution (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/bit/MB/statistics/) [Certain]. Bank cash flow is dominated by working capital and funding flows, so FCF conversion (564.4%, -312.0%, 30.5%, -98.1% over FY2022-25) tells us nothing about earnings quality [Likely]. Dividend and payout data are not in this fact sheet [data unavailable].

## 6. Unusual items
- Control and M&A: MPS shareholders rejected a merger of Mediobanca into the group on 2026-10-29 (source: https://www.reuters.com/business/finance/monte-dei-paschi-shareholders-reject-merging-mediobanca-into-group-2026-10-29/) [Likely]. Intesa then said it will launch a buyout offer once the MPS bid is settled (source: https://www.reuters.com/business/finance/intesa-launch-buyout-offer-mediobanca-soon-mps-bid-settled-2026-11-03/) [Likely]. Event risk is high in both directions: the offer terms may cap the upside, or fall away.
- Valuation: price is far above own-history and peer multiples (see section 3).
