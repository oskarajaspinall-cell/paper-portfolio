# Macro overlay: HCL Technologies Limited (HCLTECH) — 2026-10-17
## Assessment
Chain: dollar strength (DTWEXBGS, the only broad-dollar proxy in the FRED allowlist — no direct USD/INR series is tracked) → INR depreciation → translation uplift on HCL's largely USD-billed revenue/earnings into INR → supports the premium multiple HCL carries over peers (fact sheet). Secondary chain: US financial conditions (credit spreads, real yields) → BFSI/enterprise discretionary IT budgets → HCL's bookings momentum, the driver cited in its own Q2 FY26/27 print.

Facts [Certain]: DTWEXBGS 121.602 (2026-10-09), +3.72 (~1m)/+1.1 (~3m) (https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=DTWEXBGS). DGS2 4.77% vs DFF 3.88% (2026-10-15) (https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=DGS2; .../id=DFF). BAMLH0A0HYM2 3.23%, +0.47 (1m)/+0.52 (3m) (2026-10-15) (https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=BAMLH0A0HYM2). VIXCLS 16.46, -0.74 (1m) (2026-10-15) (https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=VIXCLS).

Market pricing **(inference)**: the 2y yield running ~0.9pp above effective fed funds implies the market prices "higher-for-longer" policy, not near-term cuts — a proxy, not a forecast. That keeps the dollar firm near-term, consistent with the gap already visible between HCL's USD/constant-currency guided growth (2.6% YoY) and its INR-reported growth (11.5% YoY; fact sheet, 2026-10-12 transcripts) — part of that gap is FX translation, not demand **(inference)**.

Macro bull: dollar/INR tailwind persists, credit stays easy (VIX 16, HY spreads still low vs history despite the recent uptick) → the post-earnings drift extends, premium holds.
Macro bear: dollar/risk-on reverses (INR firms, VIX or HY spreads jump) → the translation tailwind fades and tighter financial conditions crimp BFSI/enterprise tech budgets → drift fades, premium compresses toward peers.
Tilt: small, toward bull — supportive today but secondary to the fundamental driver (AI-bookings/margin beat) underpinning this tactical trade.
