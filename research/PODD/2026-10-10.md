# Insulet Corporation (PODD) — Core initiation — 2026-10-10

## 1. Business
Insulet makes the Omnipod, a tubeless, disposable wearable insulin pump for type 1 and type 2 diabetes [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/podd/). Revenue is recurring: patients are prescribed the pump platform, then buy consumable pods roughly every three days, sold through pharmacy and durable-medical-equipment channels in the US and internationally [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/podd/).

## 2. Quality
Returns are high and have risen: ROIC 21.3% TTM (from 11.6% FY2021), ROCE 21.3% (from 6.9%), with gross margin 71.1% and operating margin 16.9%, both up over five years [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/podd/financials/ratios/, https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/podd/financials/income-statement/). The moat is real: Omnipod's tubeless form factor and entrenched reimbursement/prescriber relationships create switching costs versus tubed pumps (Medtronic, Tandem) [Likely].

But execution has slipped. Guidance was lowered on higher-than-expected type 2 diabetes attrition, and management describes "four corrective actions" targeting early type 2 drop-off (source: Insulet Transcript, Wells Fargo 21st Annual Healthcare Conference, 2026-09-09, https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/podd/transcripts/740218-wells-fargo-21st-annual-healthcare-conference/; Insulet Transcript, Bernstein Insights Healthcare Forum, 2026-09-24, https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/podd/transcripts/757002-bernstein-insights-healthcare-leaders-and-disruptors-3rd-annual-healthcare-forum/) [Likely]. A securities class action alleges Insulet misled investors on the safety/reliability of its insulin delivery products, referencing "two Omnipod medical device corrections"; allegations are unproven [Likely] (source: GlobeNewsWire, 2026-08-13, https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/08/13/3344840/32719/en/contact-robbins-llp-for-information-about-leading-the-lawsuit-that-alleges-insulet-corporation-misled-investors-regarding-the-safety-and-reliability-of-its-insulin-delivery-product.html). Net debt/EBITDA is low at 0.67x, so the balance sheet is not a near-term constraint [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/podd/financials/ratios/).

## 3. Valuation
Multiples have de-rated sharply alongside a 57.1% 12-month share-price decline (52-week high 354.88, last close 135.28): P/E 25.5x and EV/EBITDA 15.9x both sit below their 5-year range minimums (43.8x and 36.0x) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/podd/financials/ratios/, https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/podd/history/). Versus peers (DXCM, TNDM, ABT, MDT), PODD is cheaper on EV/EBITDA (-7%) and EV/Sales (-18%) but more expensive on P/FCF (+45%) and P/B (+10%) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/podd/statistics/, peer statistics pages).

Critically, the fact sheet's own base-case DCF fair value is 113.48 versus the 135.28 last close — the stock trades 16.1% above that base case, not below it [Certain] (source: fact sheet fair value table). The reverse DCF implies the market is still pricing roughly 31.2% year-1 revenue growth fading to terminal growth — a demanding bar given the attrition issue above [Likely]. Bear (-50.52) and bull (2,040.32) cases are extreme model outputs and not usable as decision inputs [Certain].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROIC (ratios page) falls below 15%, reversing the five-year improving trend.
- Gross margin (income statement) falls below 68%, its FY2021/FY2023 level, signalling the corrective actions are hitting unit economics.
- FCF margin (cash flow + income statement) turns negative again, as in FY2021–FY2022.
- Net debt/EBITDA (balance sheet + income statement) rises above 2.5x, its FY2021/FY2023 level.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
FCF margin improved from -16.4% (FY2021) to 13.9% (FY2025) and 9.6% TTM, with FCF/net income conversion of 78.3% TTM, up from deeply negative in FY2021-22 [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/podd/financials/cash-flow-statement/, https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/podd/financials/income-statement/). Shares outstanding fell 1.19% year-on-year, consistent with net buybacks rather than dilution [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/podd/statistics/). Net debt/EBITDA of 0.67x leaves capacity to keep funding R&D and international expansion without needing equity issuance [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/podd/financials/ratios/).

## 6. Unusual items
Short interest is 7.36% of float and rose 14.3% in the past month, and institutional ownership is reported at 106.0% of float — both consistent with the market positioning for further downside around the litigation/attrition overhang [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/podd/statistics/). An active securities class action alleges misleading disclosure on device safety/reliability; outcome and financial exposure are unresolved [Likely] (source: https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/suewallst-reminds-shareholders-of-a-lead-plaintiff-deadline-of-august-31-2026-in-insulet-corporation-lawsuit---podd-302861346.html).
