Credit spreads and the dollar are immaterial given 0.67x net debt/EBITDA and unquantified FX mix [Likely].

Tilt: small, toward bear — attrition/litigation dominates the -57% 12-month move; the real-yield rise is a secondary, same-direction headwind **(inference)**.
