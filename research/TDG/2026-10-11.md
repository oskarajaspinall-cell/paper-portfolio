# TransDigm Group Incorporated (TDG) — Core initiation — 2026-10-11

## 1. Business
TransDigm designs and supplies highly engineered, proprietary aerospace components (actuation, ignition, cockpit, power and structural parts) sold to aircraft OEMs and as aftermarket replacement parts across commercial and defense aircraft. Roughly 90% of FY2025 net sales came from proprietary products and about 55% from aftermarket, which carries higher margins and recurs over 25-30 year aircraft lifecycles (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1260221/000126022125000081/tdg-20250930.htm). [Certain]

## 2. Quality
ROIC has risen from 9.4% (FY21) to 15.4% TTM and ROCE from 9.7% to 19.3%, with gross margin at 59.8% and operating margin at 46.7% — among the highest in industrials (source: research/TDG/factsheet-2026-10-11.md). [Certain] The moat is structural: ~90% of sales are sole-source/proprietary parts certified to specific airframes, and FAA re-certification costs make switching to a competing part prohibitively expensive once designed in (source: SEC 10-K, above). [Certain] Q3 FY2026 results beat and management raised full-year revenue/EBITDA/EPS guidance, with growth across OEM, aftermarket and defense (source: factsheet news, 2026-08-04 transcript/filings). [Likely] The main offset to quality is leverage: net debt/EBITDA is 6.03x TTM, up from a 4.77x FY24 low, as the company funds continuous bolt-on M&A — 95 deals since 1993, most recently Prince & Izant, closed 2026-09-28 — mostly with debt rather than equity. [Certain]

## 3. Valuation
TDG trades at 18.0x EV/EBITDA, the bottom of its own 5-year range (18.0-25.7x) and 38% below the peer median (29.3x: HEI, HWM, RTX, GE); P/E of 33.6x is below its 5-year minimum (36.5x) and 21% below peers' 42.5x (source: factsheet). [Certain] Despite looking cheap relative to its own history and peers, the fact sheet's DCF-based fair value (base case 803.20) sits 27.5% below the last close of 1,107.99; only the bull case (1,547.89) is above the current price (source: factsheet). [Certain] The reverse DCF implies the price requires 13.4% revenue growth in year 1 fading to terminal growth — a high bar given TDG's growth blends organic aftermarket demand with acquisitions (source: factsheet). [Likely] This conflict suggests the whole aerospace peer set, and TDG's own recent years, may be rerated on cyclical strength rather than TDG being statistically cheap in absolute terms. [Guessing]

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) rises above 7x without a clear deleveraging path, reversing the FY21-24 improvement
- Aftermarket revenue mix (10-K) falls materially below the ~55% FY2025 level, reducing margin durability
- Operating margin (income statement) falls below 44%, the FY2023 level, signalling pricing-power erosion
- FCF conversion, FCF/NI (cash flow and income statement), falls below 85% for two consecutive quarters, below the FY2025/TTM level

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
FCF margin is 19.3% TTM (5y range 15.3-23.7%) and FCF conversion is 90.5% TTM, down from 118.8% in FY2021 (source: factsheet). [Certain] Capital is directed almost entirely to bolt-on M&A — 95 deals since 1993, concentrated in proprietary, aftermarket-heavy aerospace components, most recently Prince & Izant, closed 2026-09-28 (source: SEC 10-K; https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/industrial-growth-partners-completes-sale-of-prince--izant-to-transdigm-302892197.html). [Certain] Shares outstanding fell only 0.21% year on year, so buybacks are not a material use of cash (source: factsheet). [Certain] Debt funds most growth capital, consistent with the leverage trend in section 2. [Certain]

## 6. Unusual items
Altman Z-score is 1.93 (grey/distress zone) and Piotroski F-score is 6/9 (source: factsheet). [Certain] The low Z-score looks structural to TDG's debt-funded model rather than a new deterioration signal, but it leaves little balance-sheet margin for error if aftermarket demand weakens. [Likely] Short interest is low (2.18% of float) but rose 33.4% month on month (source: factsheet). [Certain] One 2026-06-17 headline referencing "TDG" relates to TDG Gold Corp (TSXV:TDG), an unrelated mining company, and is not used here. [Certain]
