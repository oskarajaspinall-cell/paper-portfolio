# Macro overlay: TransDigm Group Incorporated (TDG) — 2026-10-11
## Assessment
TDG's thesis is a proprietary-parts moat, not a macro trade, but 6.03x net debt/EBITDA and debt-funded bolt-on M&A make it sensitive to the cost of debt, and its 33.6x P/E sits above the fact sheet's DCF fair value (base 803.20 vs. close 1,107.99), making the discount rate the swing variable [Likely] **(inference)**. Chain: real yields + credit spreads → cost of debt / DCF discount rate → multiple and M&A funding pace.

Facts: US 10y real yield (DFII10) 2.87%, +0.56pp/3m (2026-10-08); 10y Treasury 5.22%, +0.68pp/3m; 2y Treasury 4.75% vs. fed funds 3.88% (2026-10-08); HY spread (BAMLH0A0HYM2) 3.15%, +0.45pp/3m; 10y breakeven 2.33%, roughly flat (-0.04pp/1m). [Certain]

Market pricing (proxy, **(inference)**): 2y yield ~0.87pp above fed funds suggests the market prices further tightening or higher-for-longer, not cuts; stable breakevens mean the real-yield rise is a rates/term-premium story, not fresh inflation surprise. [Likely] The rate rise and spread widening already happened and TDG is down 14.5%/3m, -18.1pp vs SPY, partly reflecting this, but the 2y/funds gap implies more could be priced. [Guessing]

Bull: rates/spreads stabilize or ease → discount rate falls, multiple re-rates toward bull fair value (1,547.89), M&A funding stays cheap. Bear: real yields and HY spreads keep rising → discount rate and interest cost rise, fair value pressured toward the bear case (494.55), M&A pace slows. Tilt: moderate toward bear — fundamentals are strong, but valuation is already stretched versus DCF and leverage amplifies rate/spread moves.
