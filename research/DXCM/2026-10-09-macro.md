# Macro overlay: DexCom, Inc. (DXCM) — 2026-10-09
## Assessment
DXCM's CGM demand is non-discretionary healthcare spend, largely insulated from the macro cycle [Likely] **(inference)**. The live channel is the discount rate: a premium multiple (P/E 33.3x vs peer median 25.4x) and high beta (1.48) make its cost of equity (10y Treasury + beta x ERP per `[valuation]`) unusually sensitive to Treasury yields [Certain].

Official data: 10y real yield (DFII10) is 2.92% on 2026-10-07, +0.49pp/1m, +0.61pp/3m; 10y Treasury (DGS10) is 5.28%, +0.72pp/3m (fred.stlouisfed.org, 2026-10-07) [Certain]. Using DXCM's beta, cost of equity rose ~70bp over 3m (5.28%+1.48x5% ERP vs ~4.56%+7.4% three months ago) **(inference)**, pressuring the DCF fair value (base $64.89, already -23.1% vs the $84.41 close) further downward, all else equal [Likely].

Market pricing: the 2y yield (4.77%) sits ~89bp above fed funds (3.88%, both 2026-10-07), a proxy implying the market prices further tightening rather than cuts [Likely] **(inference)**. Breakeven inflation (T10YIE) is flat at 2.35% (2026-10-08), so no fresh inflation surprise [Certain]. Despite the yield rise, DXCM rallied +14% over 3m, beating SPY by +10pp — fundamentals (raised guidance, beat) have dominated the rate headwind so far [Likely].

**Bull**: yields ease -> discount rate falls -> fair-value range widens up, narrowing the valuation gap. **Bear**: yields keep rising -> fair value falls further below price, alongside a stronger dollar (DTWEXBGS 121.385, +0.7pt/3m) trimming international revenue translation modestly. **Tilt**: small, toward bear — a real but second-order valuation headwind on an otherwise macro-insensitive earnings stream.
