# DexCom, Inc. (DXCM) — Core initiation — 2026-11-15
## 1. Business
DexCom sells continuous glucose monitoring (CGM) systems (G7, G7 15 Day) and consumables to people with diabetes, mostly through distributors. For the nine months to September 2026 the US was $2,735.5m (71.1% of revenue) and international 28.9% [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1093557/000109355726000195/dxcm-20260930.htm). Distributors were 84.8% of revenue [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1093557/000109355726000195/dxcm-20260930.htm). No single foreign country is disclosed. [Certain]

## 2. Quality
Returns are high and still rising: TTM ROIC 45.9% (FY2021 18.5%), operating margin 24.0% (17.2% a year earlier), net cash (net debt/EBITDA -0.28x) (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/dxcm/financials/ratios/). [Certain]
Moat: a recurring-consumable installed base, sensor/data ecosystem and reimbursement access; Abbott is a well-funded CGM rival. [Likely]
Gross margin is 63.4% TTM vs 68.6% in FY2021, so the 5-year trend is down though recovering from 60.1% in FY2025. Management cites G7 15 Day benefits and manufacturing efficiencies in the 10-Q delta, partly offset by G6 inventory charges. [Likely]
FCF margin is 21.5% vs 23.5% a year earlier and 29.7% at its 2-year high (March 2026): a fade, not a collapse; FCF conversion is still 110.8% of net income. [Certain]
The 10-Q delta dropped the FDA warning-letter risk factors; closure is unconfirmed. [Guessing]

## 3. Valuation
Price 83.33 USD: P/E 32.7x (5y range 31.0x–239.9x, 1st percentile of range), EV/EBITDA 20.5x (below its 5y minimum), P/FCF 28.3x, FCF yield 3.53%. History says cheap, but the multiples compressed as earnings grew. [Certain]
Versus peers it is dearer: P/E +29%, EV/Sales +64%, P/B +136%. [Certain]
Fair value is flagged unreliable (methods disagree 3.0x): DCF base 36.89 vs P/E base 109.30; blend base 61.03 (-26.8%). The price therefore rests on sustained growth and margin gains above the DCF's assumptions. The reverse DCF implies 32.1% year-1 revenue growth, far above the guided pace. [Likely]
Trend is healthy: +11.0% above the 200-day, 12-month +39.8%, though 3-month relative strength is -5.9pp. [Certain]

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Shares are down 3.92% YoY, and a $1bn buyback was authorised on 29 October 2026 (headline: https://www.tipranks.com/news/the-fly/dexcom-announces-1b-share-repurchase-program-thefly-news) [Likely]. The company has no borrowings on its credit facility and funds capex from operations (10-Q: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1093557/000109355726000195/dxcm-20260930.htm). FCF/NI is 110.8% TTM, so earnings are cash-backed. [Certain] Rising SG&A on advertising and facilities is the watch item. [Likely]
