# DexCom, Inc. (DXCM) — Core initiation — 2026-11-17
## 1. Business
Sells continuous glucose monitoring (CGM) systems, the G7 and G7 15 Day, plus disposable sensors, to people with diabetes via pharmacy and distributor channels. Recurring sensor revenue drives the model. The US is the large majority of sales; international is the remainder, with no single foreign country disclosed separately in the 10-Q (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1093557/000109355726000195/dxcm-20260930.htm) [Certain]. No foreign country is shown at ~10%+.

## 2. Quality
A high-return business whose returns are rising: ROIC 45.9% TTM vs 24.1% in FY2024; operating margin 24.0% TTM vs 14.9% in FY2024; net cash (net debt/EBITDA -0.28x) (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/dxcm/financials/ratios/) [Certain]. Gross margin recovered to 63.4% TTM after sliding from 68.6% in FY2021 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/dxcm/financials/income-statement/) [Certain]. The MD&A credits G7 15 Day benefits and manufacturing efficiencies (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1093557/000109355726000195/dxcm-20260930.htm) [Certain]. Moat: recurring sensors, installed user base, clinical and payer access; Abbott is the scaled rival [Likely].
Direction change: FCF margin is 21.5% TTM vs 23.5% a year earlier, 8.2pp below its 2-year high of 29.7%, while operating margin rose. Cash conversion is still 110.8% of net income, so this looks like timing and capex rather than weaker earnings quality [Guessing].
The 10-Q drops the FDA warning-letter risk factors, which suggests the matter is closed, but the filing does not say so [Likely].

## 3. Valuation
P/E 32.0x vs a 5y median of 88.5x (1% of range) and EV/EBITDA 20.0x, below its 5y minimum. Against peers (PODD, ABT, MDT, TNDM) it is dearer: P/E +27%, EV/EBITDA +32%, P/FCF +3% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/dxcm/statistics/) [Certain]. FCF yield is 3.61%. The de-rating comes with much higher returns on capital, so the history flatters the discount [Likely].
Mechanical fair value is flagged unreliable: DCF base 36.71 vs P/E base 109.30, blended base 60.90 against a close of 81.61. Weigh lightly. The reverse DCF implies 31.6% year-1 revenue growth, well above recent growth [Likely]. The price assumes sustained margin gains. Trend: +8.6% vs the 200-day average, -3.0% vs the 50-day, 12-month change +40.6%.

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROIC (ratios page, TTM) falls below 25%.
- Gross margin (income statement page, TTM) falls below 58%.
- Operating margin (trailing income statement page) falls below 18%.
- A new FDA warning letter, recall order or production shutdown (10-Q/8-K at sec.gov).

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Shares are down 3.92% YoY. The 2026 buyback of $1.00B was completed by 30 September (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1093557/000109355726000195/dxcm-20260930.htm) [company-reported]; a new programme was announced 2026-10-29 (source: https://www.tipranks.com/news/the-fly/dexcom-announces-1b-share-repurchase-program-thefly-news) [Likely]. No borrowings are drawn on the credit facility. FCF margin 21.5% TTM, FCF conversion 110.8%. The risk is paying up for buybacks at a rich price, and a cash-funded acquisition in Q2 [Likely].

## 6. Unusual items
Beta 1.44 (high). Short interest 4.29% of float, not material. Q3 results beat and the FY26 revenue range was narrowed on 2026-10-29 (source: https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20261029820206/en/Dexcom-Reports-Third-Quarter-2026-Financial-Results/) [Likely]. The MD&A flags inventory charges for the planned G6 discontinuation and higher advertising spend [Certain]. Pricing-pressure risk language was removed from the 10-Q, which is not proof pricing has eased [Guessing].
