## 2. Quality
Solid, not exceptional. ROIC is 11.5% TTM, flat over five years (-0.7pp), and ROCE 15.0% is flat (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/jse/BVT/financials/ratios/) [Certain]. Operating margin of 9.3% has held within a narrow band, but gross margin has fallen 1.7pp over five years [Certain]. Rolling-12-month ROIC, ROCE, operating margin and FCF margin are flat or rising (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/jse/BVT/financials/ratios/?p=trailing) [Certain]. FCF conversion of 152.9% of net income is well above its 5-year path, which may include working-capital release, so I do not extrapolate it [Likely]. Net debt/EBITDA of 2.33x is below FY2025's 2.71x but above FY2022's 2.01x [Certain]. Beta is 0.24 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/jse/BVT/statistics/). The moat is breadth and operating discipline across many niche businesses, not a single franchise [Guessing].

## 3. Valuation
The price assumes little growth. P/E is 13.7x against a 5-year range of 13.1x–15.2x (29% position), a 12% discount to the peer median of 15.6x (BID, SHP, MRP, SOL). P/FCF is 9.0x against a 13.3x median, and FCF yield is 11.16%, but FCF is flattered by the conversion spike [Likely]. EV/EBITDA of 7.4x is below the 5-year minimum of 8.3x and in line with peers (+1%). P/B of 1.9x sits at the bottom of its range (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/jse/BVT/financials/ratios/). Fair value: [data unavailable] (SOTP needs segment data; DCF has no ZAR rate). Cheap against its own history, though earnings growth is not accelerating [Likely].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROIC (ratios page, TTM) falls below 10.0%.
- Operating margin (income statement page, TTM) falls below 8.5%.
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) rises above 3.0x.
- FCF conversion (cash flow and income statement pages, FY) falls below 80% in the FY2027 report.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Shares outstanding changed -0.11% YoY, so there is no dilution (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/jse/BVT/statistics/) [Certain]. The H1 call cites acquisitions integrated and disposals with further deleveraging [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/jse/BVT/transcripts/381335-h1-2026/). Leverage fell from 2.71x to 2.33x in FY2026. FCF margin rose from 4.9% to 7.2% [Certain].
