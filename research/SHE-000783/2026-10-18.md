# Changjiang Securities Company Limited (SHE:000783) — Core initiation — 2026-10-18
## 1. Business
A Chinese securities firm: brokerage commissions, underwriting/investment banking, asset management and proprietary trading/investment income off its own balance sheet [Certain]. Industry classified Capital Markets (source: fact sheet OV). Revenue and margins move with mainland China equity-market turnover and the firm's trading book, not a steady subscription-style stream [Certain].

## 2. Quality
Returns are improving but still low in absolute terms: ROIC 2.3%→4.5% TTM and ROE 8.0%→12.3% TTM over FY2021–TTM, with the TTM uplift concentrated in the latest period (source: ratios page) [Certain]. Operating margin rose from 22.8% (FY2023) to 51.1% TTM and net margin from 22.7% to 39.2% TTM (source: income statement) [Certain] — a cyclical margin recovery, not demonstrated durability. ROE is well above ROIC, meaning leverage (a securities firm's balance sheet, not a moat) is doing real work [Likely]. Piotroski F-score is 6/9 (source: statistics page), a reasonable but unremarkable fundamental-strength signal [Certain]. China's brokerage industry is licensed but fragmented (dozens of comparably-sized peers) and commission-driven, so there is no clear structural barrier stopping rivals competing away any excess return once equity turnover normalises [Likely]. Net debt/EBITDA is not meaningful for a securities-firm balance sheet and is [data unavailable] on both fact-sheet definitions.

## 3. Valuation
The stock trades at 8.6x P/E, 45% below its own 5-year range (12.9x–22.6x) and in line with the four-peer median of 8.8x (source: ratios/statistics pages, peers SHA:600030, SHA:601688, SHE:000776, SHA:601211) [Certain]. P/B is 1.0x, a 33% premium to the 0.7x peer median; P/FCF (2.0x) is far below peers (3.3x) [Certain], but FCF margin swung from 17.2% to 281.4% TTM over five years (source: cash flow/income statement), so P/FCF and the 50.39% FCF yield reflect trading-book timing, not recurring cash, and shouldn't be read literally [Likely]. Owner's FCFE/DCF base fair value is CNY10.78 (+40.6% vs the CNY7.67 close), bear CNY4.90 (-36.2%), bull CNY16.46 (+114.6%) (source: fact sheet fair-value section) [Certain]. The price assumes the ROE recovery fades and multiples stay compressed vs own history, though it is only in line with peers, not cheap against them, on P/E [Likely].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROE (ratios page) falls back below 5%, reversing the FY2025/TTM recovery toward FY2022–24 levels.
- Net margin (income statement) falls below 25%, undoing the margin expansion from 22.7% (FY2023).
- Piotroski F-score (statistics page) drops below 5, signalling fundamental deterioration.
- Operating margin (income statement) falls below 25%, back toward the FY2022/23 range.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Cash conversion metrics are not reliable here: FCF/NI swung from 48.8% to 1,004.1% over FY2021–24, 414.9% TTM (source: cash flow/income statement) [Certain], consistent with a securities firm whose reported "free cash flow" is dominated by trading-book and client-settlement timing, not distributable cash [Likely]. The fact sheet shows no dividend, buyback or share-count data (shares change YoY [data unavailable]), so capital return can't be assessed; no company-reported figure was used given the limited allowlist for this filer and no IR domain on record.

## 6. Unusual items
Insider ownership is minimal (0.01%) against 17.5% institutional ownership (source: statistics page) [Certain], consistent with a state-linked broker where management holds little direct equity — a governance characteristic to monitor but not a stand-alone red flag. Recent fact-sheet news items are routine quarterly/annual filing-publication notices only, with no operational detail (source: fact sheet OV/filings links); nothing in them changes the thesis. No short-interest data is available for this China A-share listing.
