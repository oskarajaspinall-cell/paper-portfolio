# Changjiang Securities Company Limited (SHE:000783) — Core initiation — 2026-10-19
## 1. Business
Changjiang Securities is a China-based, CSRC-licensed full-service securities broker: commission-based brokerage and margin financing, investment banking/underwriting, asset management and proprietary/trading-book income [Certain] (source: stockanalysis.com overview, Industry: Capital Markets).

## 2. Quality
Returns are cyclical, not structural. ROE moved 8.0%→4.9%→4.7%→5.0%→9.1% over FY2021-25 before jumping to 12.3% TTM, and ROIC stayed near 1-3% for four years before 4.5% TTM [Certain]. Operating margin (29.9%→51.1% FY24-TTM) and net margin (28.0%→39.2%) expanded sharply only in the most recent period, consistent with a China equity-trading/margin-lending upcycle rather than a widened moat [Likely]. Brokerage, margin financing and underwriting are commoditized, competing on licence, balance-sheet size and distribution against CITIC, Huatai, GF and the newly merged Guotai Haitong; none earns a structural advantage over Changjiang [Likely]. Piotroski F-Score is 6/9, unremarkable [Certain]. This is a capital-markets-cycle business: good when China trading volumes and margin balances rise, mediocre otherwise — five-year ROE (never above ~9% before this year) is the base case, not the TTM print.

## 3. Valuation
Trailing P/E of 8.6x sits 45% below even the five-year minimum (12.9x) and roughly in line with the peer median (8.8x), implying the market expects TTM net margin (39.2%, above the 22.7%-35.2% range of FY2021-25) to fade toward cycle-average levels [Likely]. P/B of 1.0x — more relevant given volatile reported earnings — is 33% above the peer median of 0.7x, so book-value investors pay up versus peers even as P/E looks cheap: a genuine mixed signal [Certain]. P/FCF of 2.0x is not meaningful: FCF margin swung from 92% to 33% to 65% to 281% to 17% over FY2021-25, reflecting working-capital swings rather than owner earnings [Certain]. Base fair value of CNY10.78 (+40.6% vs the CNY7.67 close) rests on an FCFE/DCF method built on this volatile FCF base, so the upside carries that caveat; bear/bull fair values of CNY4.90/CNY16.46 bracket the cyclicality.

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROE (ratios page) falls back below 5% for a full fiscal year, confirming the FY2025/TTM improvement was purely cyclical.
- Operating margin (income statement) drops back below 30%, signalling the trading/underwriting upcycle has reversed.
- ROIC (ratios page) fails to clear 3% for two consecutive fiscal years, showing no structural improvement in returns on capital.
- Piotroski F-Score (statistics page) falls below 5, indicating deteriorating balance-sheet or earnings quality.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Reported free cash flow is dominated by changes in client securities, margin loans and the trading book, not distributable owner earnings — FCF margin ranged 17%-281% over FY2021-25, so FCF conversion (414.9% TTM) is not a reliable cash-discipline gauge here [Certain]. Shares change YoY and buyback/dividend detail are [data unavailable]; net debt/EBITDA is [data unavailable] and not a meaningful metric for a broker's funding structure anyway. Capital allocation should really be judged on regulatory capital ratios and margin-book risk, which stockanalysis.com does not publish, so that judgment cannot be made here.

## 6. Unusual items
Institutional ownership is only 17.5% (insiders 0.01%) [Certain], low for a mid-cap financial and consistent with China A-share retail-dominated ownership, which can add volatility around sentiment shifts. The stock is 11.1%/7.1% below its 50-/200-day averages, down 25.4pp vs SPY over 12 months, and RSI is 31.5 (oversold) [Certain] — a weak tape, though this is price action, not a thesis trigger.
