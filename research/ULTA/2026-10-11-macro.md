## Assessment

The fact sheet's own DCF already uses today's 5.22% risk-free rate, so the stretch is already captured — the open question is direction from here. Market pricing (proxy, inference): 2y yield (4.75%) running ~87bp above effective fed funds (3.88%) implies the market is not pricing near-term cuts, consistent with real yields still drifting up rather than down [Likely] (inference). Flat breakevens alongside rising real yields point to a tightening real-rate impulse, not an inflation scare.

Bull: real yields plateau/reverse as the Fed signals cuts resume -> discount rate eases, narrowing the fair-value gap. Bear: real yields extend their rise (restrictive policy persists) -> discount rate rises further, widening the valuation gap, while higher consumer borrowing costs add pressure to the already-softening margin trend (ROIC -5.8pp, operating margin -2.7pp over 5y, note, 2026-10-11). Tilt: toward bear, small — this reinforces, but doesn't create, the fundamental overvaluation call; low beta limits amplification.
