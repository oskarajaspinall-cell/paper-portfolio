# Macro overlay: Illinois Tool Works Inc. (ITW) — 2026-10-09
## Assessment
Chain: real yields → DCF discount rate → fair-value gap, and real yields → industrial-capex cost of capital → ITW's capex-related segments (Welding, Test & Measurement), credited for the Q2 guidance raise [Certain] (fact sheet news, 2026-07-28).

Facts (FRED, 2026-10-07/08): 10y real yield (DFII10) 2.92%, +49bp/1m, +61bp/3m; 10y Treasury (DGS10) 5.28%, +72bp/3m; 2y (DGS2) 4.77% vs effective fed funds (DFF) 3.88%; breakevens (T10YIE) 2.35%, +12bp/3m [Certain].

Market pricing (proxy, **inference**): 2y yield ~89bp above fed funds implies the market prices further tightening, not cuts [Likely] — a hawkish shift vs three months ago. ITW's base-case DCF already uses today's 5.28% risk-free rate, so the -36.8% fair-value gap (fact sheet) partly reflects this; the surprise risk is forward — further yield rises would cut fair value more, a negative surprise if the market still assumes rates stabilize here [Likely] **(inference)**.

**Bull**: yields retrace toward ~2.5% real → discount rate eases, fair-value gap narrows, capex segments keep compounding per guidance.
**Bear**: core CPI stays sticky, yields extend further → fair value falls further, capex customers defer spend, pressuring the just-raised FY26 EPS guidance ($11.35-$11.55) — consistent with the sell-side "Catalyst Call: Sell" flagged in the fact sheet (commentary, not fact).
**Tilt**: toward bear, moderate — yields have moved consistently higher over both 1m and 3m with no stabilization evidence yet, compounding an already-large valuation gap; capped at moderate given ITW's modest leverage (1.84x) and low beta (0.97).
