# Grupo México (BMV:GMEXICO.B) — Core initiation — 2026-11-16
## 1. Business
Holding company with three divisions: copper-led mining (Southern Copper plus Mexican operations, also silver, zinc, molybdenum), a Mexican/US rail network, and infrastructure (the latest call cites Saavi consolidation) [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/bmv/GMEXICO.B/). Earnings are driven by metal prices and mine cost. Revenue share by country (Mexico, Peru, US): [data unavailable]; the company site was blocked, so no ~10%+ foreign share can be confirmed. Reporting currency is USD, shares quote in MXN.

## 2. Quality
Highly profitable, low-leverage miner with a rail annuity [Likely]. TTM ROIC 24.9%, operating margin 53.4%, net margin 31.8%, net debt/EBITDA 0.36x; Piotroski F 6, Altman Z 4.88 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/bmv/GMEXICO.B/financials/ratios/ and /statistics/). Durable advantage: low-cost, long-life copper deposits and a rail franchise hard to replicate [Likely].

Caveat: returns are cyclical. Over five fiscal years ROIC, operating margin and FCF margin are falling, yet each rose over the last 12 months (ROIC 18.7% to 24.9%, operating margin 44.2% to 53.4%) (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/bmv/GMEXICO.B/financials/ratios/?p=trailing). That is mostly higher metal prices and cost gains per the Q3 call [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/bmv/GMEXICO.B/). TTM margins sit near a two-year high, so they are not a through-cycle base [Likely]. FCF conversion TTM 95.7% vs 126.8% in FY2021 [Certain].

## 3. Valuation
P/E 13.1x is at 71% of its 5y range (8.6x–14.9x); EV/EBITDA 7.6x at 87% (max 8.1x); P/B 3.6x and EV/Sales 4.6x are above their 5y maxima. FCF yield 7.24%. Against peers (SCCO, FCX, ANTO) it is cheaper: P/E -47%, EV/EBITDA -16%, P/FCF -59% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/bmv/GMEXICO.B/financials/ratios/). The price assumes peak-like margins persist [Likely].

Mechanical fair value (EV/EBITDA only; NAV [data unavailable]): base 88.08 MXN, bull 210.22 MXN vs close 220.15 MXN, so the price is above even the bull case [Certain]. Fair value is one model estimate, not a sell signal. Trend is healthy: +4.5% above the 200-day, 12-month change +38.5%, but 3-month momentum only +0.8% and RSI 40.2 [Certain].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Operating margin (income statement, TTM) falls below 45%.
- ROIC (ratios page, TTM) falls below 18%.
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) exceeds 1.5x, e.g. through a debt-funded acquisition.
- FCF margin (cash flow page, TTM) falls below 20% for two consecutive quarters.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Share count down 1.73% YoY, so no dilution [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/bmv/GMEXICO.B/statistics/). TTM FCF margin 30.4% and conversion 95.7%, below FY2021-FY2024 (117%–127%), as capex rises with major projects (Q2 call) [Likely]. Grupo México opted out of the Argentine Belgrano Cargas rail bid [Certain] (source: https://www.reuters.com/business/grupo-mexico-opts-out-argentina-cargo-rail-bid-blow-to-milei-privatization-drive-2026-10-21/), which suggests capital discipline [Guessing].

## 6. Unusual items
Insiders own 19.37%, i.e. a controlling family holder, so minority-shareholder governance risk [Likely]. Short interest [data unavailable]. Copper price is the main macro exposure; peso/USD affects the MXN share price vs USD results [Likely]. Any Peru or US country-risk overlay cannot be sized without a revenue split.
