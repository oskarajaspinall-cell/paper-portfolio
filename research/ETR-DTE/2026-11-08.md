# Deutsche Telekom (ETR:DTE) — Core initiation — 2026-11-08
## 1. Business
Integrated telecom operator: German mobile, fixed and broadband; European subsidiaries; and a controlling stake in T-Mobile US, which is the largest profit contributor [Likely]. Revenue is subscription-based and recurring. The US is clearly above 10% of revenue [Likely], so USD and US competition matter; I could not obtain the exact share from a permitted primary source (company site fetch blocked), so [data unavailable]. (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/etr/DTE/)

## 2. Quality
- Returns are improving: ROIC 5.0% (FY2021) to 8.8% TTM, ROCE 5.6% to 10.7%, operating margin 12.6% to 22.2% [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/etr/DTE/financials/ratios/).
- Last-12-month direction is flat: ROIC -0.2pp, operating margin -0.0pp, FCF margin -0.8pp versus a year earlier [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/etr/DTE/financials/ratios/?p=trailing). Gains have plateaued.
- Moat: scale, spectrum and fibre assets in concentrated, regulated markets; low beta of 0.34 [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/etr/DTE/statistics/).
- Q3 2026: guidance raised for the third time this year, driven by T-Mobile US [Likely] (source: https://www.reuters.com/business/deutsche-telekom-lifts-2026-outlook-plans-up-2-billion-buyback-2026-11-05/).
- Weak spots: Net debt/EBITDA 3.13x and Altman Z 1.13 [Certain]; net margin 7.3% versus operating margin 22.2% reflects heavy interest, tax and minorities [Likely].

## 3. Valuation
At 25.91 EUR (2026-11-06), EV/EBITDA is 5.9x, below its 5y minimum of 7.1x and 16% below the peer median (TMUS, VZ, T, ORA) [Certain]. P/FCF is 4.2x, bottom of its 5y range and 49% below peers; FCF yield is shown as 23.64% [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/etr/DTE/statistics/). P/E is 13.9x, above its 5y median of 12.6x and 20% above peers; P/B is 1.4x, top of its range [Certain]. Earnings multiples are not cheap; cash-flow multiples are.

Caution: the mechanical fair value (base 120.67 EUR) rests on FCF at 327.4% of net income. Reported FCF probably omits lease and spectrum outflows [Guessing], so I treat the fair value and the yield as overstated, not decision-grade. The reverse DCF implies year-1 revenue of -24.2%, not credible here [Likely]. The market is pricing a SpaceX mobile threat in the US [Likely] (source: https://www.reuters.com/business/media-telecom/deutsche-telekom-says-investors-already-pricing-spacex-wireless-threat-2026-11-05/).

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Operating margin (income statement, TTM) falls below 20%.
- ROIC (ratios page, TTM) falls below 7.5%.
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) rises above 3.8x.
- FCF margin (cash flow statement, TTM) falls below 20%.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Share count is down 1.34% year on year [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/etr/DTE/statistics/). Management raised the dividend and announced a 2027 buyback [Likely] (source: https://www.wsj.com/business/earnings/deutsche-telekom-raises-outlook-plans-dividend-increase-cbb6085e). Reported FCF margin of 24.0% TTM is high relative to net margin; conversion is 327.4%, falling versus 444.3% in FY2021 [Certain]. Leverage has fallen from 3.98x to 3.13x, but shareholder returns compete with network and spectrum spending [Likely].
