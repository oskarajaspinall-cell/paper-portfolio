# WuXi Biologics (HKG:2269) — Core initiation — 2026-11-16
## 1. Business
Contract research, development and manufacturing organisation (CRDMO) for biologics: it earns fees from drug developers (antibodies, ADCs, bispecifics) across discovery, development and commercial supply, and benefits as clinical programmes advance to commercial manufacture [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/2269/). Reports in CNY, trades in HKD. Revenue split by customer geography (US/Europe/China) [data unavailable]: no allowlisted company domain was available to fetch, so the foreign share is unverified for the country-risk overlay.

## 2. Quality
Returns are recovering, not yet elite. ROIC TTM 13.9% vs 9.7% in FY2024; operating margin 32.4% vs 24.6%; gross margin 47.5% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/2269/financials/ratios/) [Certain]. Rolling-12-month ROIC, operating and FCF margin all rose vs a year earlier (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/2269/financials/ratios/?p=trailing) [Certain]. Net margin fell over five years (21.2% TTM), so earnings quality improved less than operating metrics [Likely]. Net cash (net debt/EBITDA -1.14x), Altman Z 8.62, Piotroski 7 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/2269/statistics/) [Certain]. Moat: integrated discovery-to-commercial platform, regulatory track record (passed another FDA GMP inspection, https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/wuxi-biologics-passes-another-fda-gmp-inspection-reinforcing-its-quality-track-record-and-commercial-supply-reliability-302889266.html) and switching costs once a process is validated [Likely]. Fresenius biosimilar option agreement (https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/fresenius-and-wuxi-biologics-enter-exclusive-development-agreement-to-expand-biosimilars-pipeline-302926065.html) adds pipeline visibility; option rights are not revenue [Likely].

## 3. Valuation
P/E 34.1x sits at 19% of its own five-year range and 44% above the peer median (n=2); EV/EBITDA 17.7x sits at 8% of range and 14% below peers; P/FCF 38.8x is below its five-year minimum; FCF yield 2.58% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/2269/statistics/) [Certain]. Mechanical fair value (EV/Revenue only; rNPV unavailable) is base 46.42 HKD vs close 46.74, bear 26.69, bull 98.36: the price discounts the base case and little more, with wide dispersion [Likely]. Trend is healthy: +15.6% above the 200-day, 12-month +40.2%, ahead of SPY on 3/6/12 months, but 6.0% below the 50-day [Certain]. Next results 25 Nov 2026 [Certain].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Operating margin (income statement, TTM) falls below 27%.
- ROIC (ratios page, TTM) falls below 10%.
- FCF margin (cash flow, TTM) falls below 8%.
- Company-reported revenue growth turns negative in a half-year, or US export or biosecurity restrictions cut customer revenue (company filings/RNS).

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
FCF conversion improved to 85.4% of net income TTM from 54.1% in FY2025, FCF margin 18.1% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/2269/financials/cash-flow-statement/) [Certain]. Capex intensity is falling as the build-out matures [Likely]. Share count down 0.45% YoY, no dilution; net cash balance sheet [Certain].

## 6. Unusual items
- China-based operator with US customers: US-China policy (biosecurity legislation, export controls) is the main tail risk; unquantified here [Likely].
- Short interest [data unavailable]. Institutions hold 40.5%, insiders 1.18% [Certain].
