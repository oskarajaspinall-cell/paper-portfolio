# BB Seguridade Participações (BVMF:BBSE3) — Core initiation — 2026-10-31
## 1. Business
Holding company that earns equity-method income from insurance, pension and brokerage ventures sold mainly through the Banco do Brasil branch network [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/bvmf/BBSE3/). It is capital-light: gross margin 98.2% and operating margin 95.5% TTM (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/bvmf/BBSE3/financials/income-statement/). Results depend on investment income, pension reserve growth and rural (agricultural) insurance claims [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/bvmf/BBSE3/transcripts/552051-q2-2026/).

## 2. Quality
Returns are exceptional: ROE 85.9% TTM against 57.5% in FY2021, ROCE 67.6% TTM, net margin 87.9% TTM (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/bvmf/BBSE3/financials/ratios/) [Certain]. Net debt/EBITDA is negative on the site definition (-0.65x), so there is no leverage risk; Altman Z is 6.02 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/bvmf/BBSE3/statistics/) [Certain].

The moat is probably distribution exclusivity through the Banco do Brasil network, which a competitor cannot easily copy [Likely]. It is contractual and tied to a state-controlled bank, so renewal terms and bank-side policy are the main risk [Guessing].

Weak spot: cash conversion. FCF/net income fell from 71.8% in FY2021 to 37.7% TTM, and FCF margin from 53.1% to 33.1% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/bvmf/BBSE3/financials/cash-flow-statement/) [Certain]. Piotroski F is only 3 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/bvmf/BBSE3/statistics/) [Certain]. Recurring income growth is slow in the latest quarter, with agricultural headwinds [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/bvmf/BBSE3/transcripts/552051-q2-2026/).

## 3. Valuation
P/E is 8.7x against a 5y range of 7.8x–10.9x (median 8.4x), 31% up the range, and 11% above the peer median of 7.9x. EV/EBITDA is 7.4x (median 7.1x), 33% above peers. P/B is 7.4x against 6.8x median, and P/FCF is 23.2x, above the 5y maximum of 20.6x (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/bvmf/BBSE3/financials/ratios/) [Certain]. FCF yield is 4.32% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/bvmf/BBSE3/statistics/).

The price assumes the very high ROE persists and that earnings are cash-backed. Mechanical fair value (blend of P/B+ROE and P/E) is base 22.18 BRL against a 41.32 close, -46.3% (bull 25.50, -38.3%) (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/bvmf/BBSE3/). The P/B+ROE leg caps sustainable ROE, so it is punitive for this stock, but even the P/E leg is not cheap against peers. The stock is fairly priced for quality, with no margin of safety [Likely].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROE (ratios page) falls below 60% on a TTM basis.
- FCF conversion (FCF/net income, cash flow page) falls below 30% TTM.
- Operating margin (income statement page) falls below 90% TTM.
- Shares outstanding (statistics page) rise more than 3% year on year (dilution).
