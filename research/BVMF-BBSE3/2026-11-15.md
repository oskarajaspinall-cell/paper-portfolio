# BB Seguridade Participações (BVMF:BBSE3) — Core initiation — 2026-11-15
## 1. Business
Holding company that earns fees and equity income from insurance, pension and capitalisation products sold through the Banco do Brasil branch network; it largely distributes and shares underwriting economics rather than carrying a balance-sheet-heavy insurer [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/bvmf/BBSE3/). Reports in BRL, Brazil home market; no foreign revenue concentration identified [Likely].

## 2. Quality
Returns are exceptional: ROE 69.7% TTM versus 89.8% in FY2025, operating margin 95.6%, net margin 88.2%, negative net debt/EBITDA (-0.46x on the site definition) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/bvmf/BBSE3/financials/ratios/). The moat is the exclusive distribution agreement with Banco do Brasil's branch network [Likely]; competitors cannot easily replicate that reach.

Direction is the issue. ROIC fell from 220.8% to 123.4% in twelve months, ROCE from 59.1% to 57.2%, FCF margin from 38.0% to 31.4%, and FCF conversion has slid to 35.6% of net income [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/bvmf/BBSE3/financials/ratios/?p=trailing). Q3 headlines report weaker insurance results offset by pensions and a cut to premium guidance [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/bvmf/BBSE3/transcripts/662865-q3-2026/). Earnings quality is falling at the cash level; accounting earnings are not being converted. Rising 5-year but falling 12-month: cyclical/credit-driven softness is plausible, not proven [Guessing].

## 3. Valuation
P/E 7.9x sits near the bottom of its own 5y range (7.8x–10.9x; 3% position) and 3% below the peer median of 8.2x; EV/EBITDA 6.8x is 8% above peers [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/bvmf/BBSE3/statistics/). P/FCF at 22.1x is above its 5y maximum (20.6x), and FCF yield is 4.52%: the cheapness is on earnings, not cash. P/B 5.4x is below its range minimum but far above peers (1.8x) because ROE is far higher.

Mechanical fair value base 24.15 BRL versus a 37.27 close is flagged unreliable (P/B+ROE gives 16.27, P/E gives 39.90) [Certain]. The price assumes earnings stay near current levels and that ROE stays high; a ROE reversion toward peers would not be priced.
