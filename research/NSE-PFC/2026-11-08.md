# Power Finance Corporation (NSE:PFC) — Core initiation — 2026-11-08
## 1. Business
State-controlled non-bank lender to India's power sector (generation, transmission, distribution, renewables), earning net interest spread funded by domestic and offshore bonds [Likely]. Revenue is domestic, so INR and Indian policy drive it; no material foreign revenue region [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/nse/PFC/). A merger with REC Limited was approved by both boards and the President of India in June 2026 [Likely] (source: https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/business/india-business/pfc-rec-merger-approved-what-happens-to-rec-shares-swap-ratio-and-shareholders-now/articleshow/132068103.cms).

## 2. Quality
Returns are steady rather than exceptional. ROE has been flat at 21.1% (FY2022), 21.5% (FY2024) and 20.5% (FY2026), with TTM at 18.7% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/nse/PFC/financials/ratios/) [Certain]. ROIC is a low 2.9% TTM, normal for a leveraged lender and not comparable with industrials [Likely]. The rolling ROIC is flat against a year earlier (3.0%) [Certain].

The moat is structural: sovereign ownership, a specialised mandate and cheap funding, plus the planned REC combination, which would remove the closest competitor [Likely]. Weak points: FCF conversion is negative in FY2023-FY2026 (-471.0%, -496.7%, -405.0%, -18.7%), as expected when loan growth is booked as operating outflow, so cash flow says little about quality [Likely]. Piotroski F is 4 [Certain]. Operating margin, net margin and leverage are [data unavailable], so asset quality and funding cost cannot be tested from the fact sheet. That is a real gap.

## 3. Valuation
At 328.80 INR, P/E is 4.1x against a 5y median of 4.8x (range 2.1x-6.5x, 45th percentile) and a peer median of 10.7x, a 62% discount (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/nse/PFC/financials/ratios/) [Certain]. P/B is 0.6x against 0.7x median and 1.6x for peers, a 64% discount [Certain]. EV/EBITDA is [data unavailable], as is leverage-adjusted comparison.

The price assumes earnings are near peak and that government-owned lender returns will fade, or that the merger dilutes holders [Guessing]. Mechanical base fair value is 385.86 INR (+17.4%), bear 169.27 (-48.5%), bull 474.52 (+44.3%), P/E-only because the DCF methods lacked inputs [Certain]. The bear case is wide and the base upside is moderate. The stock is down 17.0% over 12 months and 21.3% over three months, trading 17.0% below its 200-day average [Certain]. The discount is cheap on paper, but without asset-quality data the margin of safety is unproven.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Shares outstanding are essentially unchanged (0.01% YoY) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/nse/PFC/statistics/). FCF yield is 4.96% and P/FCF 20.1x, but TTM FCF/NI is 20.5% after four negative fiscal years, so FCF is not a reliable payout guide [Certain]. Capital is deployed into loan growth funded by debt, including a USD note programme [Likely] (source: https://www.businessupturn.com/business/power-finance-corporation-launches-300-million-notes-with-5-32-coupon/).
