# UniCredit (BIT:UCG) — Core initiation — 2026-11-08
## 1. Business
Pan-European universal bank (Italy, Germany, Central and Eastern Europe); earns net interest income plus fees on retail, corporate and investment banking. ## 2. Quality
Returns have risen strongly: ROE went from 3.5% (FY2021) to 16.8% (FY2025), 15.8% TTM, and net margin from 14.7% to 44.0% TTM [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/bit/UCG/financials/ratios/). The ROE is high for a European bank, but part of it is the cyclical rate tailwind [Likely]. FCF margin is volatile (-181.7% FY2023, 68.6% TTM), which is normal for banks because working capital swings through deposits and loans, so FCF is a weak quality guide [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/bit/UCG/financials/cash-flow-statement/). Piotroski F is only 4 and ROIC, operating margin and leverage are [data unavailable] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/bit/UCG/statistics/). Moat is scale and funding cost in Italy, but banks are cyclical and the durable reason competitors can't erode returns is modest [Guessing].

## 3. Valuation
P/E is 10.9x versus a 5y median of 6.3x (66% of the 5y range) and peers at 10.5x [Certain]. P/B is 1.6x, at the top of its 5y range (0.7x median) and 33% above peers at 1.2x [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/bit/UCG/statistics/). P/FCF 6.7x is not comparable for a bank. The price therefore already capitalises high ROE as sustainable. Mechanical fair value: base 84.89 EUR vs last close 76.80 (+10.5%), bear 14.32, bull 98.69; the P/B+ROE leg gives 71.54, below the price, and the dividend discount leg 39.53 [Likely]. The reverse DCF implies -1.4% year-1 revenue growth, a cheap-looking but model-dependent signal. Net: modest upside, with a top-of-range P/B and a sharp bear case if ROE normalises. Next results date is Nov 9, 2026.

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROE (ratios page) falls below 11% on a TTM basis.
- Net margin (income statement page) falls below 35% TTM.
- Shares change YoY (statistics page) turns positive above 3% (dilution from funding the Commerzbank deal).
- Piotroski F (statistics page) falls below 3.
