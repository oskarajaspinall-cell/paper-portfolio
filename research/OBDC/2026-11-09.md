# Blue Owl Capital Corporation (OBDC) — Core initiation — 2026-11-09
## 1. Business
OBDC is an externally managed business development company (BDC) lending to US upper-middle-market companies, mostly floating-rate senior loans originated by the Blue Owl direct-lending platform. It earns net investment income (interest and fees minus funding costs and adviser fees) and pays most of it out as dividends under RIC rules. No foreign country is flagged at ~10% of revenue in the filing delta. [Likely] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1655888/000165588826000069/obdc-20260930.htm)

## 2. Quality
- Returns are modest and flat: TTM ROIC 8.1% vs 8.1% a year earlier; ROE has slid to 4.7% from 9.4% in FY2025 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/obdc/financials/ratios/). [Certain]
- Operating margin of 75.1% is high but down 1.0pp over 12 months, against a rising 5-year trend. Net margin fell to 20.8% from 33.9%, so below-the-line items (unrealised marks, costs) are absorbing income (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/obdc/financials/income-statement/). [Certain]
- Moat is scale and incumbency in sponsor-backed lending. The 10-Q says most financings go to existing borrowers. It also says its AI, tariff and rate stress tests show a well-positioned portfolio. That is management's claim, not verified. [Likely]
- Shares outstanding rose 4.53% YoY, which dilutes per-share growth (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/obdc/statistics/). [Certain]

## 3. Valuation
P/B is 0.8x, below the bottom of its 5y range (0.8x to 1.0x) and 20% under the peer median of 1.0x. EV/Sales is 8.0x, at the bottom of its 5y range and 15% under peers. P/E is 16.3x, above the 5y maximum of 10.1x and 8% under peers: depressed earnings, not a cheap price, drive this. [Certain] The mechanical fair value base of 11.90 USD (+8.8% vs the 10.94 close) is flagged unreliable because the DCF and P/E methods differ 2.2x; weigh it lightly. [Guessing] The price implies 2.3% year-1 revenue growth, undemanding for a floating-rate lender if rates stay high. Price is 1.5% under the 200-day average, 12-month change -8.7%. Net: a discount to book with weak earnings conversion; upside depends on credit losses staying low. [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/obdc/statistics/)
