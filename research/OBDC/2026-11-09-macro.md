# Macro overlay: Blue Owl Capital Corporation (OBDC) — 2026-11-09
## Assessment
**Weight: secondary.** OBDC is a floating-rate US direct lender. Two variables matter: the policy-rate path (earnings) and credit spreads/risk appetite (NAV marks and the P/B discount). Chain: policy path -> base rates -> net investment income (NII); and credit spreads -> loan marks and credit-loss fears -> equity risk premium -> P/B (0.8x, fact sheet).

**Facts (FRED):** fed funds 3.88% (2026-11-05), +0.25pp over ~3m (https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=DFF). US high-yield spread 3.48%, +0.45pp over 1m and +0.77pp over 3m (https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=BAMLH0A0HYM2, 2026-11-05). VIX 28.18, +13.2 over 1m (https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=VIXCLS, 2026-11-05). 10y Treasury 4.84%, -0.43pp over 1m (https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=DGS10, 2026-11-05).

**Pricing (proxy):** the 2y at 4.46% sits 0.58pp above fed funds, so the market prices further hikes (a proxy, not a forecast). The Q3 call headline says higher rates and wider spreads should lift earnings (https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/obdc/transcripts/726407-q3-2026/, 2026-11-05). Q3 NII of 33c met consensus, so the earnings benefit looks largely priced [Likely].

**Inference:** the live change is the risk premium: spreads and VIX jumped in a month, yet the price is only 1.5% below its 200-day average (fact sheet), so a credit-stress leg may not be priced. [Guessing]

**Bull:** hikes arrive with stable spreads; NII rises and P/B moves toward the 1.0x peer median.
**Bear:** spreads widen further, non-accruals rise and marks fall; NAV (14.32, fact sheet) erodes and the discount widens.
**Tilt:** toward bear, small.
