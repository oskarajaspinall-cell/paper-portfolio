# Macro overlay: Northrop Grumman Corporation (NOC) — 2026-10-10
## Assessment
NOC's revenue is ~98% US government, fixed-price/cost-type contracts; FX and global-growth channels are immaterial, and its reported 5y beta is -0.11 [Certain] (factsheet ST). The one credible channel is valuation, not earnings: NOC's DCF spans B-21/Sentinel cash flows into the 2030s-40s, so its fair value is sensitive to real yields.

Official data: US 10y real yield (DFII10) 2.87% on 2026-10-08, +56bp/3m, +44bp/1m [Certain] (fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=DFII10, 2026-10-08). US 10y nominal yield (DGS10) 5.22%, +68bp/3m [Certain] (...id=DGS10, 2026-10-08). NOC's own base fair value ($465.24) already uses today's 5.22% risk-free rate, so the current yield level is priced into the published estimate, not a stale input [Certain] (factsheet). Market pricing (inference): equities broadly absorbed this yield rise without de-rating — SPY is +3.6% over 3m [Certain] (factsheet SPY-HI) — while NOC fell -9.7% over the same window and sits ~40pp behind SPY over 12 months [Certain] (factsheet HI). The underperformance looks driven mainly by company-specific overhangs (CAS pension inquiry, FY2023 margin-charge memory, Iran-war headline risk), not by the rate move itself **(inference)**.

Bull: real yields ease back toward ~2.3% (spring 2026 level) -> discount rate falls -> DCF fair value rises, narrowing/reversing the ~3% gap to price. Bear: DFII10 continues toward/above ~3.25% -> cost of equity rises further -> fair value falls further below the already-depressed price, reinforcing the evaluation's "unattractive reward:risk" call. Tilt: small toward bear — the rate channel is real but second-order next to litigation/margin risk and NOC's historically low market beta.
