# Northrop Grumman Corporation (NOC) — Core initiation — 2026-10-10

## 1. Business
Prime defense contractor organized in four segments (Aeronautics, Defense Systems, Mission Systems, Space Systems), selling aircraft (B-21), missiles/missile defense (Sentinel), space and C4ISR systems mainly to the U.S. government under multi-year fixed-price and cost-type contracts [Certain] (source: fact sheet OV/IS).

## 2. Quality
Backlog is $104.7bn, 1.6x TTM revenue; management calls demand "the most robust in 20+ years" across all segments [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/noc/transcripts/750863-morgan-stanley-s-14th-annual-laguna-conference/). Few firms hold the clearances and classified-program track record to compete for Sentinel/B-21-class work — a durable barrier [Likely]. TTM ROIC is 15.0%, ROCE 15.3%, down sharply from FY2021 (25.4%/22.7%); 5y trend falling (ROIC -10.5pp) [Certain] (source: fact sheet RA). Driven by FY2023's operating-margin collapse to 6.7% (vs 21.0% FY2021), consistent with fixed-price development cost charges; margin has since recovered to 13.4% TTM [Certain] (source: fact sheet IS). FCF conversion is improving (29.9%→81.1% FY2022→TTM), and FCF margin is rising (4.0%→8.5%) [Certain] (source: fact sheet CF/IS). Net debt/EBITDA has risen from 1.27x to 2.03x TTM [Certain] (source: fact sheet BS/IS). Risk is repeatable: Sentinel and B-21 remain in development/early production, and FY2023 shows fixed-price cost risk is real, even though the latest 10-Q shows no new reach-forward loss [Likely] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1133421/000113342126000034/noc-20260630.htm).

## 3. Valuation
NOC trades at 15.3x P/E and 11.4x EV/EBITDA, a 31% and 19% discount to the peer median (LMT/RTX/GD/LHX) [Certain] (source: fact sheet). It sits at the 25th percentile of its own 5y P/E and EV/EBITDA range, but below its 5y range on EV/Sales, P/FCF and P/B [Certain] (source: fact sheet). The DCF-based base fair value is $465.24, only 3.2% below the $480.72 close; bull fair value is just 6.1% above the close, while bear fair value is 59.0% below it [Certain] (source: fact sheet). The peer-multiple discount looks attractive, but the DCF — which reverse-engineers the growth the price implies (5.7% year-1 revenue growth fading to terminal) — says the stock is already near fair value, with a shallow bull case and a deep bear tail skewed toward FY2023-style margin-charge risk. Reward:risk from here is unattractive at the current price.

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Operating margin (income statement) falls below 10%, signalling a recurrence of FY2023-style fixed-price development charges.
- FCF margin (cash flow/income statement) falls back below 6%, reversing the FY2022–TTM improving trend.
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) rises above 2.5x.
- Total backlog (10-Q filings) declines year over year, undermining the "record demand" thesis.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Management's stated priority is to invest in programs, then pay a dividend, then return remaining capital to shareholders [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/noc/transcripts/750863-morgan-stanley-s-14th-annual-laguna-conference/). Buybacks are minimal: of a $3.0bn authorization from December 2024, $0.5bn is used, just $68m in H1 2026, per the Q2 2026 10-Q [Certain] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1133421/000113342126000034/noc-20260630.htm). Shares outstanding fell only 1.75% YoY [Certain] (source: fact sheet ST). Management guided 2026 FCF of $3.1-3.5bn despite higher B-21 spend, capex near 4.5% of revenue through 2027-28 [Likely] (same transcript source). FCF conversion (81.1% TTM) and FCF margin (8.5% TTM) continue improving [Certain] (source: fact sheet CF/IS).

## 6. Unusual items
The Q2 2026 10-Q discloses an unresolved CAS pension cost-accounting inquiry (DCMA noncompliance determination, 2013-2019); DOJ's criminal inquiry closed in May 2026 but a civil investigative demand continues, with a "reasonably possible" material adverse effect the company cannot currently quantify [Certain] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1133421/000113342126000034/noc-20260630.htm). Bethpage environmental remediation litigation also continues [Certain] (same source). The stock is down 24.6% over 12 months and trades 9.6%/19.4% below its 50-/200-day averages despite record backlog and improving FCF conversion [Certain] (source: fact sheet HI). Reported 5y beta is -0.11, unusually low for the sector [Certain] (source: fact sheet ST); not treated as material to the thesis.
