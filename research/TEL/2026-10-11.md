# TE Connectivity plc (TEL) — Core initiation — 2026-10-11
## 1. Business
TE Connectivity makes connectors, sensors and cable assemblies that carry electrical signals and power reliably in harsh environments, sold mainly to OEMs across three segments — Transportation (auto/EV), Industrial (factory, aerospace, energy, medical) and Communications (data/appliances) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/tel/). Parts are qualified into customer platforms years before production, creating sticky, multi-year revenue once designed in [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/tel/transcripts/730347-jefferies-global-industrials-conference-2026/).

## 2. Quality
ROIC fell from 18.7% (FY21) to 11.5% (FY25) before rebounding to 17.5% TTM; ROCE stayed roughly flat at 16-18% over five years [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/tel/financials/ratios/). Margins are expanding: gross margin 36.1% TTM vs 32.7% FY21, operating margin 20.4% vs 18.2%, and FCF conversion is strong at 109.9% TTM [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/tel/financials/income-statement/, https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/tel/financials/cash-flow-statement/). Net debt/EBITDA is moderate at 0.92x TTM, up from 0.86x in FY23 [Certain]. Piotroski F-score of 7 and Altman Z of 5.22 indicate low distress risk [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/tel/statistics/). The moat rests on long qualification cycles and high switching costs once a connector is designed into a platform (auto, aerospace, data center), which protects pricing and share [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/tel/transcripts/730346-citi-s-2026-global-tmt-conference/). Management cited AI/data connectivity orders up 70% YoY, a newer, less-proven growth leg next to the mature auto/industrial base [Likely] (same source).

## 3. Valuation
TEL trades at 21.4x TTM P/E — mid-range of its own 5-year history (34th percentile of 14.4x-34.8x) but a 62% discount to the peer median of 56.2x (APH, APTV, GLW, ST) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/tel/financials/ratios/ and peer statistics pages). It is similarly discounted on EV/EBITDA (13.7x vs 17.4x peer, -21%) and EV/Sales (3.5x vs 5.0x, -30%). Our own fair-value model puts base fair value at $153.69 and even the bull case at $173.74 — both below the $218.29 last close (-29.6% and -20.4%) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/tel/ fair value, computed per scripts/valuation.py). The reverse DCF implies the market is pricing 14.7% revenue growth in year 1 fading to terminal, above TEL's recent organic trend [Likely]. This suggests the peer discount reflects TEL's lower structural growth/margin profile versus APH, not that the stock is cheap in absolute terms.

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROIC (ratios page) falls and stays below 12% for two consecutive fiscal years (already touched 11.5% in FY25)
- Gross margin (income statement) falls below 32%, below the FY21-23 trough, signalling lost pricing power
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) rises above 1.5x
- AI/data-communications order growth (management commentary) decelerates sharply from the 70% YoY pace cited, showing the newer growth leg was cyclical rather than structural
