# Rotork plc (LON:ROR) — Core initiation — 2026-11-15
## 1. Business
Rotork makes electric, pneumatic and hydraulic valve actuators and flow-control instruments, and earns recurring service income on the installed base. End markets are oil and gas, water and power, and chemical, process and industrial (CPI). [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/lon/ROR/). The H1 2026 call cited strength in CPI, water and service despite oil and gas disruption [Likely] (source: https://www.marketbeat.com/instant-alerts/rotork-h1-earnings-call-highlights-2026-08-04/). No single foreign country or emerging-market region was verified at ~10%+ of revenue [data unavailable].

## 2. Quality
Excellent. ROIC is 26.3% TTM vs 20.9% in FY2021, ROCE 30.5%, gross margin 50.5% and operating margin 24.1%, all rising over five years [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/lon/ROR/financials/ratios/). Net cash (net debt/EBITDA -0.13x) and Piotroski F of 6 [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/lon/ROR/statistics/). The moat is the installed base, specification lock-in and service revenue [Likely]; the margin trend supports pricing power.
Direction change: FCF margin is rising over five years but fell from 16.0% to 14.3% over the last 12 months, 3.6pp below its 2-year high of 17.9% [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/lon/ROR/financials/cash-flow-statement/?p=trailing). Operating margin rose 3.5pp over the same period, so the decline is working capital or capex, not profitability [Likely]; cause unverified. Net debt/EBITDA has moved from -0.86x to -0.13x as cash is returned [Certain].

## 3. Valuation

P/E is 33.3x (5y median 25.6x, 67% of the range), EV/EBITDA 19.4x (median 15.4x), EV/Sales 5.1x at the top of its range, and P/B 7.2x above its 5y maximum [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/lon/ROR/financials/ratios/). Against peers (LON:SPX, LON:IMI, LON:WEIR, EMR, FLS) the premium is +18% on P/E, +25% on EV/EBITDA and +63% on P/FCF [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/lon/ROR/statistics/).
