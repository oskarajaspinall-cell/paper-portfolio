# Honeywell Aerospace Inc. (HONA) — Core initiation — 2026-10-12

## 1. Business
Designs and supplies avionics, cabin/cockpit systems, navigation/sensors, defense electronics and propulsion to commercial OEMs, airlines (aftermarket parts/services) and defense/space customers [Certain] (source: honeywellaerospace.com). Spun off from Honeywell International as an independent Nasdaq-listed company in 2026 [Certain] (source: honeywellaerospace.com). Commercial aftermarket is the largest and fastest-growing end market (H1 2026: $4.0bn, +7% YoY) [Certain] (source: SEC 10-Q, hona-20260627.htm).

## 2. Quality
Returns on capital are high but the trend is adverse: ROIC 32.0%→33.6% FY24→FY25 but TTM only 18.3%; operating margin fell FY23→TTM from 24.8% to 17.3% (-4.2pp) and net margin from 20.9% to 10.9% (-5.5pp) [Certain] (fact sheet). FCF conversion is strong and rising (128.3% TTM) [Certain] (fact sheet), a genuine positive. Aftermarket servicing on an installed base typically carries switching-cost and certification moats [Likely]. This is a brand-new standalone entity (2026 spin-off) with under one year of public history, no parent balance-sheet support, and net debt/EBITDA of 4.27x against $15.85bn of debt issued at separation ($16bn notes, March 2026) [Certain] (source: SEC 10-Q, hona-20260627.htm). The Aug 6, 2026 guidance cut and ~20% one-day share drop occurred the day after this 10-Q was filed, suggesting the margin deterioration already visible in the data is not yet fully resolved [Likely] (headline, source: invezz.com, as listed in fact sheet).

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
The separation was funded with $16.0bn of senior unsecured notes issued March 16, 2026 (maturities 2028-2066, coupons 3.90%-5.852%), leaving $15.85bn long-term debt outstanding at Q2 2026, plus an undrawn $4.0bn revolver with no financial covenants [Certain] (source: SEC 10-Q, hona-20260627.htm). FCF margin is 13.9% TTM and FCF conversion 128.3% TTM, both cash-generative despite the margin compression [Certain] (fact sheet). No dividend or buyback policy is yet visible in the data available; this is a newly independent company and capital-return history cannot be assessed.

## 6. Unusual items
Four separate law-firm notices (Kaplan Fox, Robbins LLP, Bernstein Liebhard, SBS Law, Aug-Oct 2026) are investigating or have filed securities class actions alleging the company misled investors about its business prospects, following the Aug 6 guidance cut [Likely] (allegations are unproven solicitations, but the overhang and volume are notable so soon after the spin-off; sources: newsfilecorp.com, globenewswire.com, businesswire.com, as listed in fact sheet). Short interest rose 61.9% month-over-month to 2.72% of float [Certain] (fact sheet). A $2.04m DOJ False Claims Act settlement (Sept 2026) is immaterial in size [Certain] but signals compliance scrutiny [Guessing] (source: tipranks.com/thefly, as listed in fact sheet).
