# Macro overlay: McDonald's Corporation (MCD) — 2026-10-08
## Assessment
McDonald's is a mature, high-margin franchisor priced partly as a low-beta (0.45 [Certain], https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/mcd/statistics/), 50-year dividend-growth bond proxy (annualized dividend $7.44/share, yield ~3.2% on the 2026-10-07 close of $230.88) [Certain] (research note; factsheet). Its 12-month -22% share decline and current multiples (18.8x P/E, 14.5x EV/EBITDA, both below 5y range) are driven mainly by idiosyncratic traffic softness and an AI-pricing antitrust suit [Likely] (research note). Over the past month, US real yields jumped: DFII10 (10y TIPS) +0.48pp to 2.91% (2026-10-06) and DGS10 +0.49pp to 5.27%, while breakevens barely moved (T10YIE +0.01pp to 2.36%, 2026-10-07) — the rise is a real-rate move, not an inflation story [Certain] (FRED, https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=DFII10, id=DGS10, id=T10YIE). The 2y yield (4.79%, 2026-10-06) sitting ~91bp above effective fed funds (3.88%, +0.25pp over 1m, 2026-10-06) implies the market is pricing a further, not easier, policy path **(inference, proxy)**.

Chain: rising real yields -> higher discount rate / opportunity cost versus bond-proxy dividend equities -> pressure on MCD's already-trough valuation multiple, on top of fundamentals.

Bull: real yields stabilize or ease -> dividend-yield relative appeal improves -> multiple stabilizes or re-rates off the trough.
Bear: real yields extend their rise (higher-for-longer policy, rising term premium) -> further multiple compression on an already bond-proxy-sensitive, leveraged (~3.6x net debt/EBITDA [Certain], factsheet) name.
Tilt: toward bear, small — the dominant driver of the recent de-rating is company-specific (litigation, US traffic, franchisee cost pushback); the real-yield move adds incremental, not primary, pressure.
