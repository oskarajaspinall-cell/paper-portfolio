# McDonald's Corporation (MCD) — Core initiation — 2026-10-08

## 1. Business
McDonald's is primarily a franchisor: of 45,356 restaurants at year-end 2025, ~95% were franchised, earning royalties, rent and initial fees, plus sales from the ~5% company-operated stores [Certain] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/63908/000006390826000035/mcd-20251231.htm). It owns ~56% of restaurant land and ~80% of buildings, collecting rent largely independent of franchisee profitability [Certain] (same source). Sector: Consumer Discretionary, Restaurants; price and reporting currency USD [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/mcd/).

## 2. Quality
The real-estate ownership model (56% of land, 80% of buildings) is the durable moat: landlord economics are harder to replicate than a recipe and let McDonald's capture value even when franchisee profitability is squeezed [Certain] (source: 10-K, mcd-20251231.htm). ROIC averaged ~18% and ROCE ~23% over FY2021-25, both stable to rising, with TTM operating margin 45.7% and gross margin 57.4%, among the highest in the sample [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/mcd/financials/ratios/, .../income-statement/). FCF margin and FCF/net-income conversion have both declined over five years (FCF margin -3.9pp to 28.0%; conversion -10.2pp to 88.3%) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/mcd/financials/cash-flow-statement/), consistent with reports of softening US traffic despite value-menu deals [Likely] (source: https://www.tipranks.com/news/mcdonalds-turnaround-faces-a-traffic-problem-despite-cheaper-meals). Net debt/EBITDA of ~3.6x is high for the sector though stable [Certain] (fact sheet). A newly filed antitrust suit alleges the company's AI pricing tool lets franchisees exchange non-public price data, a franchise-governance risk, not yet a quantified liability [Likely] (source: https://www.theguardian.com/business/2026/oct/07/mcdonalds-ai-prices-lawsuit).

## 3. Valuation
P/E is 18.8x versus its own 5y range of 25.3x-31.2x (below the 5y min) and a peer median of 18.2x (+3%) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/mcd/financials/ratios/, peer statistics pages). EV/EBITDA is 14.5x versus 5y range 18.6x-20.4x (below min), peer median 16.2x (-10%). EV/Sales is 7.8x, far above the peer median of 4.0x (+95%), reflecting MCD's much higher margins rather than cheapness. P/FCF of 21.1x is roughly in line with the peer median of 21.4x (-1%); FCF yield is 4.75% [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/mcd/statistics/). Together, the market prices MCD near peer multiples on earnings and FCF, yet at the cheapest point in its own five-year history on every available multiple, after a -22% 12-month share-price decline and a -20% gap to its 200-day average [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/mcd/history/). This reads as a de-rating from a historical premium toward peer-average multiples — consistent with traffic and litigation concerns — rather than a mispriced recovery [Likely].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- US comparable sales (next 10-Q/10-K, sec.gov) turn negative for two consecutive quarters, confirming structural traffic loss rather than a value-deal blip.
- Operating margin (income statement page) falls below 43%, below the FY2021-25 low of 43.7%, signalling franchisee-economics pressure overwhelms the royalty model.
- FCF conversion, FCF/net income (cash flow + income statement pages), falls below 75%, extending the five-year decline and undermining the cash-generation thesis.
- The AI-pricing antitrust suit results in class certification or a regulatory (FTC/DOJ) action that forces a material change to the franchise pricing system (per SEC filings/court record).

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
McDonald's has paid dividends for 50 consecutive years; the Q4 2025 dividend was raised 5% to $1.86/quarter ($7.44 annualized) [Certain] (source: 10-K, mcd-20251231.htm). In 2025 it paid $5.1bn of dividends and repurchased $2.0bn of stock, returning $7.1bn total, under a stated priority of growth capex, then dividends, then buybacks from residual free cash flow [Certain] (same source). TTM FCF margin is 28.0% and FCF yield 4.75%, but FCF conversion fell from 94.1% (FY2021) to 88.3% (TTM): more net income now funds the same cash return [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/mcd/financials/cash-flow-statement/, .../statistics/). Net debt/EBITDA of ~3.6x limits further leverage for buybacks [Certain] (fact sheet).

## 6. Unusual items
(a) An antitrust class action filed in Chicago federal court in early October 2026 alleges McDonald's AI pricing tool let franchisees exchange non-public price and sales data [Likely] (source: https://www.theguardian.com/business/2026/oct/07/mcdonalds-ai-prices-lawsuit; https://www.tipranks.com/news/mcdonalds-faces-class-action-over-alleged-ai-powered-price-fixing). (b) Reported franchisee pushback over an ~$800,000 store-revamp bill is a reinvestment-cost/franchisee-relations risk [Likely] (source: https://www.tipranks.com/news/mcdonalds-stock-mcd-flattens-as-franchisees-cant-digest-800000-revamp-bill). (c) 14-day RSI of 24.7 is technically oversold, and short interest, though a modest 1.73% of float, rose 10.3% month-on-month, implying mounting bearish positioning [Certain] (fact sheet statistics page). None is yet reflected in reported financials.
