# Macro overlay: McDonald's Corporation (MCD) — 2026-10-11
## Assessment
Chain: real yields -> DCF discount rate -> MCD's own fair-value range, amplified by 3.6x net debt/EBITDA leverage and its bond-proxy, low-growth profile (beta 0.45) [Likely] **(inference)**.

Facts [Certain]: US 10y real yield (DFII10) 2.87% on 2026-10-08, +44bp ~1m, +56bp ~3m (https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=DFII10, 2026-10-08). 10y nominal (DGS10) 5.22%, +68bp 3m; 2y (DGS2) 4.75%, +59bp 3m; fed funds (DFF) 3.88%, +26bp 3m (same source, 2026-10-08). HY spread (BAMLH0A0HYM2) 3.15%, +45bp 3m (2026-10-08). Breakevens (T10YIE) flat at 2.33% (2026-10-09) — the real-yield rise is a growth/policy repricing, not an inflation scare.

Market pricing proxy **(inference)**: 2y yield (4.75%) above fed funds (3.88%) implies the market prices further tightening or higher-for-longer, versus the cuts many expected earlier in 2026 — a hawkish shift [Likely].

MCD's own fair-value model (factsheet, 2026-10-11) discounts at the 10y Treasury (5.22%) plus beta; its bear case ($172.20, -26.8% vs $235.23 close) implies a higher discount/lower growth, bull ($244.18, +3.8%) a lower one. Further real-yield rises tilt toward the bear case, independent of any change in comps or franchise economics.

Bull: real yields plateau/retreat -> fair value re-rates toward base/bull ($219-244).
Bear: real yields and HY spreads keep rising -> fair value pulled toward bear ($172), extending the de-rating already underway (stock -20.1% over 12m, below both MAs).
Tilt: toward bear, moderate — the 3-month real-yield move is large and recent, not yet in the base case, and leverage amplifies MCD's discount-rate sensitivity versus peers.
