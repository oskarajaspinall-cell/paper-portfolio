# Macro overlay: Yum! Brands, Inc. (YUM) — 2026-10-09
## Assessment
Chain: real yields → discount rate → DCF/multiple. YUM is a low-beta (0.55) [Certain] asset-light franchisor trading at a premium to peers on EV/EBITDA (+14%), EV/Sales (+52%) and P/FCF (+30%) despite sitting below its own 5y multiple range [Certain] (factsheet Valuation). The 10y real yield (DFII10) rose to 2.92% on 2026-10-07, +49bp/~1m and +61bp/~3m (fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=DFII10, 2026-10-07); the 10y nominal yield hit 5.28% (+72bp/3m) while breakeven inflation is flat near 2.35% (fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=T10YIE, 2026-10-08) — so the move is real-rate, not inflation-expectations, driven [Certain, facts]. Market pricing (proxy, inference): the 2y yield (4.77%) sits ~89bp above effective fed funds (3.88%), implying markets price further tightening or a higher-for-longer path, not cuts (inference). The fact sheet's own DCF already uses today's 5.28% risk-free rate, so the base fair value (121.16, -15.3% vs close) already reflects current real yields; the open question is whether yields rise further (not yet priced) or roll over. Further real-yield increases would compress YUM's premium multiple and widen the gap to peers; a reversal (CPI surprise soft, Fed pivot) would relieve pressure and support a re-rate toward fair value. Secondary channel: rising HY spreads (+39bp/3m to 3.09%, fred BAMLH0A0HYM2, 2026-10-07) raise refinancing cost risk given YUM's 4.15x net debt/EBITDA [Certain, factsheet], though no maturity data is available [Guessing]. Macro tilt is small: fundamentals (decelerating ROIC/margins, leverage) already drive the bear case; macro is an incremental headwind, not the thesis.
