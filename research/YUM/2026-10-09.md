# Yum! Brands, Inc. (YUM) — Core initiation — 2026-10-09

## 1. Business
Yum! Brands franchises (and operates a minority of) KFC and Taco Bell restaurants worldwide; it exited the pizza category by completing the sale of Pizza Hut, including its mainland China operations, during 2026 [Certain] (source: SEC 8-K, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1041061/000104106126000187/a8kex991912026.htm). Revenue is mostly franchise/license royalties and fees, plus company-owned restaurant sales; the model is asset-light with high returns on capital [Certain] (source: factsheet Quality table).

## 2. Quality
TTM ROIC (59.7%) and ROCE (64.5%) look exceptional, but the pre-TTM fiscal-year trend was falling: ROIC declined from 46.7% to 33.4% and ROCE from 47.5% to 40.5% across FY2021-FY2025 [Certain] (source: factsheet Quality table, RA). Gross margin eroded from 48.1% to 45.3% over the same five years while operating margin held roughly flat near 33% [Certain]. FCF conversion rose to 105.1% in FY2025 before falling back to 75.8% TTM [Certain]. KFC and Taco Bell are top-tier global QSR brands with franchisee economics, pricing power and marketing scale that smaller peers can't easily replicate [Likely]. Management says resources previously absorbed by the lower-margin Pizza Hut business can now be redirected into KFC and Taco Bell growth [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/yum/transcripts/745389-barclays-19th-annual-global-consumer-conference/). But the portfolio is now less diversified (two brands, not three), and net debt/EBITDA stays elevated at 4.15x TTM despite improving from 5.04x in FY2021 [Certain] (source: factsheet, RA/BS/IS).

## 3. Valuation
At 143.00 USD, YUM trades on 18.0x TTM P/E, in line with the 18.3x peer median (MCD, QSR, DPZ, WEN), but at a premium on EV/EBITDA (16.9x vs 14.8x, +14%), EV/Sales (5.9x vs 3.9x, +52%) and P/FCF (23.2x vs 17.9x, +30%) [Certain] (source: factsheet Valuation table). All four multiples sit below YUM's own 5-year range, reflecting the stock's -13.5%/-10.9%/-2.5% 3m/6m/12m moves and -17 to -25pp underperformance vs SPY [Certain] (source: factsheet Price table). The fact sheet's DCF/EV-EBITDA blend puts base fair value at 121.16 USD, 15.3% below the last close; only the bull case (152.21) sits above the current price, implying the market is paying for execution on the post-divestiture growth plan rather than today's numbers [Certain] (source: factsheet Fair value table). The reverse DCF embeds 10.4% year-1 revenue growth fading to terminal, above the margin trend shown in section 2 [Likely] (source: factsheet Fair value table).

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROIC (ratios page) recovers and holds above 45% for two consecutive fiscal years, reversing the FY2021-FY2025 decline.
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios/balance sheet pages) falls below 4.0x, evidencing real deleveraging post-divestiture.
- FCF margin (cash flow/income statement pages) stays above 20% for two consecutive fiscal years, confirming the leaner two-brand portfolio converts earnings to cash as management implies.
- Gross margin (income statement page) stabilizes or rises from the FY2025 46.2% level rather than extending its 5-year decline.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
YUM remains net-debt funded, at 4.15x EBITDA TTM [Certain] (source: factsheet, RA/BS/IS). It pays a quarterly dividend, most recently declared August 2026; the fact sheet does not show the per-share amount [data unavailable] (source: factsheet, ST). FCF margin was 19.3% TTM and FCF/NI conversion 75.8% TTM, down from 105.1% in FY2025 [Certain] (source: factsheet Quality table). The Pizza Hut divestiture gives balance-sheet capacity for debt paydown or buybacks, but no use of proceeds is disclosed [Guessing] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1041061/000104106126000187/a8kex991912026.htm). Management intends to keep a healthy balance sheet and dividend while returning capital to shareholders, with no revised 2026 guidance [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/yum/transcripts/745389-barclays-19th-annual-global-consumer-conference/).

## 6. Unusual items
Altman Z-score is 2.34, in the grey zone — not unusual for an asset-light franchisor with a small balance sheet, but worth watching given 4.15x leverage [Likely] (source: factsheet, ST). Piotroski F-score is 4/9, middling [Certain]. Short interest rose 14% month-on-month to 3.62% of float, still low in absolute terms [Certain] (source: factsheet, ST). The company just completed a structural simplification (exiting Pizza Hut globally) that sharpens brand focus but reduces diversification [Likely] (source: SEC 8-K above).
