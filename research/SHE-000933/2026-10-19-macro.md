# Macro overlay: Henan Shenhuo Coal Industry and Electricity Power (SHE:000933) — 2026-10-19

## Assessment
Shenhuo is a vertically integrated, price-taking aluminum/coal producer [Certain] (note, §2): earnings swing with the global commodity cycle more than with any valuation input, clearing the bar for `primary` via **dollar/yields → USD-priced commodities (aluminum, thermal coal) → realized price/margin**, plus a smaller **US yield → DCF discount rate** channel (the note's fair value uses the FRED 10y as risk-free rate).

Facts [Certain], FRED, dated 2026-10-15/16/09 (reports/macro/2026-10-19.md): broad dollar index (DTWEXBGS) +3.72 to 121.6 over ~1m, +1.1 over ~3m; 10y Treasury (DGS10) +0.53pp to 5.10% and 10y real yield (DFII10) +0.43pp to 2.78% over ~3m; fed funds (DFF) +0.25pp to 3.88% over both 1m and 3m; HY spread (BAMLH0A0HYM2) +0.52pp to 3.23% over 3m; CNY/USD (DEXCHUS) -0.085 to 6.692 over 3m (yuan firm despite broad-dollar strength — PBOC stability, not a translation tailwind since revenue and costs are both domestic CNY).

Market pricing (proxy) **(inference)**: the 2y yield (4.77%) sitting well above fed funds (3.88%) implies the market prices further tightening/higher-for-longer, consistent with sticky core CPI (lagging, Sep data) rather than near-term cuts. A stronger dollar and higher real yields are typically a headwind for USD-priced metals and raise the discount rate **(inference)**. Partly offsetting: the note's own bear case (25.15 CNY) already sits ~1.8% below last close, i.e. the market already prices something close to a margin trough [Likely] (note, §3), so incremental macro deterioration is only a modest surprise versus what's priced. China demand/capacity policy, outside FRED scope, likely dominates **(inference)**.
