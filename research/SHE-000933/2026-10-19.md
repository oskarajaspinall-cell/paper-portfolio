# Henan Shenhuo Coal Industry and Electricity Power Co. (SHE:000933) — Core initiation — 2026-10-19

## 1. Business
Henan Shenhuo is a China-based, vertically integrated producer of electrolytic aluminum, aluminum alloys and profiles, with in-house coal mining and power generation feeding its smelters, plus a trading arm [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/she/000933/). Captive coal and power supply insulates smelting costs from merchant energy prices, the single biggest input in aluminum economics [Likely].

## 2. Quality
Returns are high but cyclical and falling over five years: ROIC declined from 22.3% (FY21) to 12.6% (FY24) before a TTM rebound to 27.7%; ROCE fell from 49.0% to 18.6% (FY24) before a TTM rebound to 34.9%; gross margin fell from 35.7% (FY21) to 21.1% (FY24) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/she/000933/financials/ratios/). The TTM bounce above FY2025 looks like a commodity upswing, not a structural re-rating [Likely]. The moat is partial: captive coal/power lowers cost volatility versus pure smelters, but aluminum and coal are globally priced, so Shenhuo sets cost, not price [Likely]. The balance sheet is strong and improving: net debt/EBITDA fell to 0.12x TTM from a 5y range of 0.63x–1.57x, and FCF conversion, while declining (289.9%→156.5% TTM), stayed above 100% every year [Certain] (same source). Net margin has been flat (+0.3pp over 5y), consistent with a price-taker [Likely].

## 3. Valuation
At 25.61 CNY, the stock trades on 8.2x P/E (35th percentile of its own 5y range of 4.4x–15.3x) and 4.5x EV/EBITDA (28th percentile of 3.2x–7.8x) — cheap versus its own history but near mid-cycle, not trough, multiples [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/she/000933/financials/ratios/). Versus four listed peers (HKG:1378, SHE:000807, AA, CENX), Shenhuo is slightly pricier on P/E (+22%), EV/EBITDA (+10%) and EV/Sales (+22%), but cheaper on P/FCF (5.3x vs 9.6x peer median, -45%) — the market pays less for Shenhuo's cash than peers' [Certain] (same source). The fact sheet's own-history fair value model shows bear/base/bull of 25.15/48.33/73.32 CNY (-1.8%/+88.7%/+186.3% vs last close), blending through-cycle DCF and EV/EBITDA; the base case assumes margins normalize to their 5y average rather than staying at the current TTM peak, and bear sits almost at today's price, implying the market already prices something close to a trough, not mid-cycle, outcome [Likely].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROIC (ratios page) falls below 10% for two consecutive fiscal years, below the FY2021-25 range of 12.6%–29.2%, signalling the cost advantage from vertical integration is not holding up through the cycle.
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) rises above 1.5x, back toward the FY2021/FY2024 peaks of 1.49x/1.57x, indicating the deleveraging has reversed.
- Gross margin (income statement) falls below 20% for two consecutive fiscal years, below the FY2024 low of 21.1%, indicating the captive coal/power cost edge has eroded.
- FCF conversion, FCF/NI (cash flow/income statement) falls below 100% for a full fiscal year, below the 5y low of 148.4% (FY2024), indicating cash generation is no longer backing reported profit.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
FCF margin has averaged ~24% over five years (27.2%→16.7%→23.6% TTM) and FCF conversion has never dropped below 148% of net income in the period shown, so earnings are well-backed by cash [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/she/000933/financials/cash-flow-statement/). Net debt/EBITDA has fallen to 0.12x TTM, the lowest of the 5y range, so the balance sheet has been strengthened rather than used for aggressive buybacks: shares outstanding are essentially flat (-0.10% YoY), implying capital has gone to debt reduction and reinvestment rather than shareholder returns [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/she/000933/statistics/).
