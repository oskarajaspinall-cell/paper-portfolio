# Henan Shenhuo Coal Industry and Electricity Power (SHE:000933) — Core initiation — 2026-11-08
## 1. Business
China-based, vertically integrated producer of electrolytic aluminum, with captive coal mining and power generation feeding its smelters [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/she/000933/). Captive energy lowers cost volatility, but aluminum is globally priced, so Shenhuo is a price-taker [Likely]. Country revenue mix: [data unavailable] on the fact sheet; the business is China-centred and reports in CNY [Likely].

## 2. Quality
Five-year trend is down, but the last year is sharply up. ROIC fell from 22.3% (FY2021) to 12.6% (FY2024), then TTM jumped to 32.6% from 13.0% a year earlier; operating margin went from 15.8% to 29.8% over the same 12 months, FCF margin from 17.7% to 25.8% [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/she/000933/financials/ratios/?p=trailing). The TTM is the highest in two years and above every fiscal year shown, so it reads as a commodity-price upswing at peak spread, not a structural step-up [Likely]. Net margin over five years is flat, consistent with no pricing power through the cycle [Likely]. Balance sheet is a genuine strength: net cash (-0.20x net debt/EBITDA TTM), Piotroski F 8, Altman Z 3.79 [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/she/000933/statistics/). Q3 2026 results were filed on 2026-10-19 (https://stockanalysis.com/quote/she/000933/filings/3847944/), but the price did not react (no earnings-type reaction), suggesting the market had already seen the strength [Likely]. Moat: partial, cost-based only [Likely].

## 3. Valuation
At 25.26 CNY: P/E 7.0x (23% of own 5y range, 4.4x–15.3x; median 6.4x), EV/EBITDA 3.6x (9% of range; peers 4.3x), P/FCF 4.6x, FCF yield 21.96% [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/she/000933/statistics/). P/E is +3% vs peers, EV/EBITDA -15%. These multiples sit on peak-cycle earnings, so low multiples are what a peak looks like: the market is pricing earnings to fall, not rise, and the price is down 20.4% over 6 months while TTM profit rose [Likely]. The fair-value model's base (55.52 CNY, +119.8%) rests on a through-cycle DCF at 70.19; the EV/EBITDA leg is 26.18, almost the current price [Certain] (source: fact sheet fair-value table). I give the DCF leg little weight given the peak TTM base [Guessing]. Bear 28.92 is above the price, which is positive, but is itself built from the same inflated TTM [Likely]. Cheap, but not demonstrably mispriced.

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROIC (ratios page) below 10% for two consecutive fiscal years.
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) rises above 1.5x.
- Gross margin (income statement) below 20% for two consecutive fiscal years.
- FCF conversion, FCF/NI (cash flow and income statement), below 100% for a full fiscal year.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
FCF conversion has stayed far above 100% every year (196.8% FY2025, 154.8% TTM), so reported profit is cash-backed [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/she/000933/financials/cash-flow-statement/). The balance sheet moved from 1.57x net debt/EBITDA (FY2024) to net cash; shares change is 0.34% YoY, so no buyback support and slight dilution [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/she/000933/statistics/).
