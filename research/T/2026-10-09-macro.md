# Macro overlay: AT&T Inc. (T) — 2026-10-09
## Assessment
Today's ~10% drop is idiosyncratic — SpaceX's ~$8bn spectrum purchase from Grain Management, a competitive-threat headline, not macro [Certain] (source: fact sheet news, 2026-10-09). But T is high-leverage (net debt/EBITDA 3.19-3.30x [Certain], RA), high-payout, so its DCF fair value and refinancing cost are discount-rate sensitive: long real yields are a genuine secondary lever **(inference)**.

**Chain**: real yields -> (i) cost of debt on ~3.2x levered balance sheet at refinancing, (ii) relative appeal of T's dividend vs Treasuries -> cost of equity/discount rate in the DCF -> fair-value range and multiple.

**What's priced**: 10y real yield (DFII10) 2.92%, +0.49pp/~1m, +0.61pp/~3m (FRED, 2026-10-07) [Certain]. 10y nominal (DGS10) 5.28%, +0.72pp/~3m; 2y (DGS2) 4.77% vs fed funds 3.88% — 2y above fed funds, a proxy for no near-term cuts priced [Likely] **(inference, proxy)**. Breakeven inflation (T10YIE) only +0.12pp/3m, so the move looks real-rate/term-premium driven, not an inflation surprise [Likely]. The fact sheet's DCF already uses today's 5.28% risk-free rate; the risk is direction from here. HY spreads widened modestly (+0.39pp/3m) but T is investment-grade, a weaker read-through.

**Bull**: yields plateau/ease -> discount rate falls, dividend more competitive -> DCF fair value (base $43.36) re-rates up. **Bear**: real yields extend their rise -> higher refinancing cost, cost of equity up -> fair value compresses toward the bear case ($25.97).

**Tilt**: small, toward bear — the quarter's real-yield backup adds a modest headwind, secondary to the SpaceX threat actually moving the stock today.
