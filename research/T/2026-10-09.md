# AT&T Inc. (T) — Core initiation — 2026-10-09
## 1. Business
AT&T sells mobile wireless service and devices (Mobility) and fixed broadband/fiber plus business wireline services, after exiting video (DirecTV) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/t/). Revenue is recurring, subscription-based, but the network is capital-intensive and spectrum-dependent [Certain].

## 2. Quality
Returns are only modestly above cost of capital: TTM ROIC 8.9%, ROCE 7.4%, both roughly flat over 5 years [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/t/financials/ratios/). Operating margin has fallen 4.8pp and FCF margin 4.2pp over 5 years; FCF conversion (FCF/NI) has slid from >130% in FY2021 to 81.9% TTM [Certain] (same source). Net debt/EBITDA is still elevated at 3.19-3.30x [Certain]. The moat (spectrum, national network) is being directly tested: on 2026-10-09 AT&T, Verizon and T-Mobile shares fell sharply after SpaceX agreed to buy ~$8bn of nationwide low-band spectrum from Grain Management to expand Starlink Mobile direct-to-device service — a new, well-funded source of wireless competition [Likely] (source: https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/08/spacex-spectrum-license-att-verizon-tmobile.html; source: https://www.tipranks.com/news/spacex-stock-is-rising-while-telecom-stocks-are-down). This is a three-to-four-player commoditized market where AT&T is not the structural winner; durability of returns is not clearly protected from new entrants [Guessing].

## 3. Valuation
P/E (8.2x) sits near the bottom of its 5-year range (2nd percentile) and 13% below the peer median; EV/EBITDA (6.8x) is below its own 5-year minimum but 7% above peers [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/t/financials/ratios/, https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/t/statistics/). P/FCF (8.9x) and P/B (1.5x) are richer: +37% and +15% above peer medians, with P/B above its 5-year max [Certain]. Base-case DCF fair value of $43.36 implies +74% upside to last close ($24.87), but the reverse DCF shows the market price only clears if revenue falls ~9.2% in year 1 before fading to terminal growth [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/t/). That is an unusually wide, binary gap for a mega-cap telecom, and is consistent with the market already pricing real share-loss risk from new satellite-based competition rather than a simple mispricing [Guessing]. Net: statistically cheap multiples look more like a value trap than an opportunity given the fresh competitive threat.

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- TTM ROIC (ratios page) holds at/above ~8.5% over the next two fiscal years rather than declining as new competition bites
- FCF conversion, FCF/NI (cash-flow + income statement pages), recovers back above 100% rather than continuing its 5-year slide
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) continues toward ~3.0x rather than re-levering above the current 3.19-3.30x
- No material wireless revenue/subscriber share loss shows up in filings following the SpaceX spectrum deal

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
FCF margin has fallen from 19.7% (FY2021) to 13.9% TTM, and FCF conversion from >130% to 81.9% TTM, reflecting heavier network capex and a smaller cash cushion over income [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/t/financials/cash-flow-statement/). Net debt/EBITDA of 3.19-3.30x is high for the sector, constraining room to fund fiber/5G capex and shareholder returns simultaneously without further deleveraging [Likely]. Shares outstanding fell 2.15% YoY, consistent with modest buybacks [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/t/statistics/).
