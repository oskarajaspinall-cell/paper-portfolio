# Tapestry, Inc. (TPR) — Core initiation — 2026-10-08

## 1. Business
Tapestry designs and sells premium handbags, accessories and footwear, mainly under Coach (86.4% of FY2026 net sales) and Kate Spade (13.4%); Stuart Weitzman was divested in August 2025 [Certain] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1116132/000111613226000018/tpr-20260627.htm). Revenue comes from owned retail/e-commerce and wholesale channels; handbags are the largest Coach category at 58.1% of Coach sales [Certain] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1116132/000111613226000018/tpr-20260627.htm).

## 2. Quality
ROIC rose from 20.7% (FY22) to 43.5% TTM and ROCE from 20.3% to 36.9%, while gross margin expanded from 69.6% to 76.6% and operating margin from 17.6% to 23.3% [Certain] (source: factsheet, ratios/income-statement pages). FCF margin reached 22.6% TTM with FCF conversion of 118.6% of net income, and net debt/EBITDA is modest and falling, at 1.39x [Certain] (source: factsheet, cash-flow/ratios pages). The durable edge is brand power in accessible-luxury handbags (Coach), now with a simplified two-brand portfolio after exiting Stuart Weitzman, concentrating marketing and product investment [Likely] (source: 10-K, above). The key vulnerability is concentration risk: Coach alone is 86% of sales, so any single-brand misstep (design missteps, promotional creep, tariff/China exposure) has outsized earnings impact, and Kate Spade remains sub-scale at 13% [Likely] (source: 10-K, above).

## 3. Valuation
At 113.42 USD, TPR trades on 15.6x TTM P/E — near the bottom of its own 5-year range (9.1x-98.8x, 7th percentile) and a 25% discount to the peer (RL, CPRI, PVH, VFC) median of 20.9x [Certain] (source: factsheet, ratios/statistics pages). EV/EBITDA of 12.5x is roughly in line with peers (12.6x median) and P/FCF of 12.4x is a 14% discount to peers' 14.4x; FCF yield is 8.05% [Certain] (source: factsheet). EV/Sales (+235% vs peers) and P/B (+491% vs peers) screen expensive, but both are distorted: EV/Sales by TPR's structurally higher margins than CPRI/PVH, and P/B by a buyback-shrunk equity base (TTM ROE of 197.1% confirms a thin equity denominator, not extraordinary economics) [Likely]. On the earnings- and cash-flow-based multiples most relevant for a margin leader, the price assumes continued margin durability but little re-rating from currently depressed sentiment; it is not pricing in Coach brand risk materializing [Likely].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Operating margin (statistics/income-statement page) falls below 20% TTM
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) rises above 2.0x
- FCF margin (cash-flow/income-statement pages) falls below 15% TTM
- Gross margin (income-statement page) falls below 70% TTM

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Shares outstanding fell 5.53% YoY, consistent with an active buyback [Certain] (source: factsheet, statistics page). The board raised the quarterly dividend 16% to 46.25c, payable September 21, 2026 [Certain] (source: fact sheet news, https://www.tipranks.com/news/the-fly/tapestry-raises-quarterly-dividend-16-to-46-25c-per-share-thefly-news). FCF conversion of 118.6% of net income and an 8.05% FCF yield TTM leave ample room to fund both the buyback and the dividend without balance-sheet strain, with net debt/EBITDA at just 1.39x [Certain] (source: factsheet).

## 6. Unusual items
Short interest is elevated at 8.09% of float and rising (+3.2% vs prior month), a sign of active bear positioning against the quality/valuation case above [Certain] (source: factsheet, statistics/news). Reported institutional ownership of 101.7% of shares is a stockanalysis.com data artefact (aggregated 13F double-counting), not a true float figure [Likely] (source: factsheet, statistics page). Tapestry is integrating Coach and Kate Spade into Google's AI shopping ecosystem (Gemini app, AI Mode search checkout), announced 2026-09-16 [Certain] (source: https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260916065964/en/Shoppers-Can-Now-Buy-Coach-and-Kate-Spade-Products-in-the-Gemini-App-and-Google-Search-Including-AI-Mode/) — a modest positive for omnichannel reach, not yet financially material. Next earnings print is November 5, 2026, inside the 12-month core horizon but not itself a thesis trigger [Certain] (source: factsheet, overview/statistics pages).
