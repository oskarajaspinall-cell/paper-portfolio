# Macro overlay: Sezzle Inc. (SEZL) — 2026-11-15
## Assessment
**Weight: secondary.** Two variables matter: real yields / risk appetite (multiple) and consumer credit conditions (loss rates, funding).

**Chain:** real yields and risk appetite -> equity risk premium -> multiple on a stock at 29.9x P/E, above its 5y maximum, with beta 6.60 (fact sheet, stockanalysis.com 2026-11-15) [Certain]. Second chain: consumer stress -> past-dues (already ~15.7% vs ~12.9% at year-end, 10-Q) -> provisions and margin [Likely].

**Facts (FRED):** the 10y TIPS yield is 2.86%, +0.47pp in 3 months (2026-11-12, https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=DFII10). The 2y is 4.86%, +0.71pp in 3 months, and fed funds is 3.88% (DGS2, DFF, same date). The high-yield spread is 3.2%, +0.49pp in 3 months, still low (BAMLH0A0HYM2, 2026-11-12). VIX is 18.7 (VIXCLS, 2026-11-12).

**Pricing (proxy):** the 2y about 1pp above fed funds implies hikes, not cuts **(inference)**. The stock is +169.9% over 12 months, so it shows no sign of pricing these headwinds.

**Bull:** yields plateau and spreads stay tight, so the multiple holds. **Bear:** a real yield above 3.25% or spreads toward 4.5% compress the multiple and raise loss and funding fears.

**Tilt:** toward bear, moderate. Credit risk is mostly company-specific, so macro adds to the fundamental bear case rather than creating it.
