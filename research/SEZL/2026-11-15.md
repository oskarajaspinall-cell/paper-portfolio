# Sezzle Inc. (SEZL) — Core initiation — 2026-11-15
## 1. Business
Buy-now-pay-later platform for US and Canadian consumers. Revenue: merchant fees and fees on instalment receivables (lending-based income, 44.1% of nine-month revenue), subscriptions (24.4%) and card interchange/partner incentives [company-reported] [Certain] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1662991/000166299126000171/szl-20260930.htm). The 10-Q gives no country split and says there is no significant geographic concentration, so no foreign-country share can be named [Certain]. New 2026 products: SezzleCash, Sezzle Send, Sezzle Mobile (same URL).

## 2. Quality
Returns are exceptional but past peak. TTM ROIC 94.4%, operating margin 60.0%, FCF margin 51.2%, net debt/EBITDA 0.09x (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/sezl/financials/ratios/). [Certain]
Direction: ROIC is rising over five years but fell from 118.1% to 94.4% in 12 months, and FCF margin slipped from 54.8% to 51.2% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/sezl/financials/ratios/?p=trailing). Operating margin is still rising. [Certain] The ROIC fall looks like capital growing faster than profit as the receivable book expands. [Guessing]
Moat: thin. BNPL is contestable (Affirm, PayPal, card issuers), and the 5y-trend is a three-year-old turnaround, not a long record. Subscribers approaching one million (headline: https://www.tipranks.com/news/the-fly/sezzle-reports-q3-adjusted-eps-1-37-consensus-1-29-thefly-news) add stickiness. [Likely]
Credit risk is rising: 29+ day past-dues were about 15.7% of receivables at September versus about 12.9% at year-end, and the allowance rose to about 11.1% of gross notes [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1662991/000166299126000171/szl-20260930.htm). [Certain]

## 3. Valuation
Price 154.60 USD. P/E 29.9x versus a 5y range of 16.3x-18.3x; P/B 18.8x versus 5.3x-16.3x; P/FCF 17.6x versus 10.4x-11.0x: every multiple is above its own 5y maximum (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/sezl/financials/ratios/). [Certain] P/E is +165% above the peer median of 11.3x; EV/EBITDA is below peers (15.0x vs 27.3x, n=3). FCF yield 5.70% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/sezl/statistics/). The mechanical base fair value of 79.34 USD is flagged unreliable (methods disagree 2.4x), so weigh it lightly [Certain]. The reverse DCF implies 39.4% year-1 revenue growth fading to terminal growth. [Certain] The price therefore assumes the current growth rate and margins persist with credit losses contained. [Likely] The 12-month trend is strongly positive (+169.9%, above the 200-day average, relative strength vs SPY +159.5pp). [Certain]

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Operating margin (income statement, TTM) falls below 50%.
- ROIC (ratios page, TTM) falls below 60%.
- The 29+ day past-due share of receivables (10-Q) rises above 20%.
- Revenue growth (income statement, quarterly) falls below 25% YoY.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
FCF is 163.0% of net income, but receivable growth is funded from operations. Shares fell 1.26% YoY, so buybacks modestly outweigh stock compensation (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/sezl/statistics/). [Certain] A national bank charter application was filed on 22 October 2026. If approved, it adds capital, compliance and operating cost, and may not be approved [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1662991/000166299126000171/szl-20260930.htm). [Certain] Whether a bank lowers funding cost enough to offset that is unproven. [Guessing]

## 6. Unusual items
- Short interest 23.53% of float; beta 6.60; ATR 7.7% of price. Position sizing and volatility matter more than usual (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/sezl/statistics/). [Certain]
- Insiders own 44.47%, which aligns interests but limits free float. [Certain]
- The Q3 2026 results beat, and guidance was raised (source: https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/11/04/3395777/0/en/sezzle-reports-third-quarter-2026-results.html). The reaction day rose 13.4% on 4.0x volume. [Certain]
