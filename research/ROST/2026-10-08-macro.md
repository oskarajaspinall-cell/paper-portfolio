Proxy: 2y yield (4.79%) sits above fed funds (3.88%, 2026-10-06), implying the market prices the policy rate staying higher for longer, not near-term cuts [Likely] **(inference, proxy)**. This fits the sector rotation already in the factsheet's news (staples ETF beating discretionary, ETF Trends 2026-09-22) [Likely].

ROST trades 108-148% through its own 5y range on EV/EBITDA, EV/Sales and P/B (above the 5y max) and +12-62% vs TJX/BURL/DLTR [Certain, factsheet]. A name already priced at a history-and-peer premium is the one most exposed if real yields keep rising — a risk to the tactical pullback-in-uptrend thesis, independent of ROST's genuinely strong comps (off-price retail is itself a trade-down beneficiary of the same inflation pressure, a partial natural hedge).

Bull: yields/spreads stabilize, rotation fades, VIX stays low → pullback resolves bullish, multiple holds, target reached inside the window. Bear: yields and spreads keep rising, rotation into staples deepens → the stretched multiple de-rates and the stop is hit before the target, regardless of comps. Net: a modest valuation-channel risk to watch, not a fundamental change.
