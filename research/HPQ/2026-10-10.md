# HP Inc. (HPQ) — Core initiation — 2026-10-10

## 1. Business
HP sells Personal Systems (PCs, workstations; ~75% of revenue) and Printing (hardware plus high-margin ink/toner supplies). Personal Systems grew revenue 18% YoY in Q3 FY26 but on falling unit volumes — price/mix (AI-PC premiumization, memory-cost pass-through) drove growth, not demand [Certain] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/47217/000004721726000048/hp73126exhibit991q326.htm). Printing is smaller but far more profitable. [Certain]

## 2. Quality
Printing's ink/toner annuity carries real switching-cost economics (18.1% operating margin in Q3 FY26 vs. 4.6% for Personal Systems) [Certain] (source: 8-K EX-99.1, above). But PCs are a commodity business against Dell and Lenovo with no pricing power, and the moat is structurally eroding: ROIC has fallen from 54.6% (FY21) to 27.6% TTM and ROCE from 59.7% to 32.8%; operating margin from 8.9% to 6.5% and net margin from 10.3% to 4.1% (fact sheet, Quality table). On 2026-09-21 HP withheld FY2027 guidance and disclosed a planning assumption that industry PC unit volumes decline mid-single-digits in CY2027, explicitly flagged as dependent on an uncertain 2H26 [Certain] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/47217/000114036126037170/ef20082499_form8k.htm). This is a margin-compressing, competitively contested business, not one with durable excess returns. [Likely]

## 3. Valuation
HPQ trades at a steep discount to peers (DELL, XRX, HKG:0992): P/E 11.5x vs. peer median 49.8x (n=2, small/skewed sample), EV/EBITDA 7.2x vs. 13.4x, P/FCF 7.0x vs. 27.4x; FCF yield 14.25% (fact sheet, Valuation table). Against its own 5-year history, P/E sits near the top of its range (88th percentile) while P/FCF sits near the bottom (21st percentile) and EV/EBITDA is mid-range (47th) — the absolute multiple is low mainly because the business itself is structurally low-multiple, not because it is unusually cheap versus its own past. Base-case fair value of $40.22 implies +33.2% upside, bear case $26.91 only -10.9% downside, vs. last close $30.20 (fact sheet, Fair value). The reverse DCF implies the market is pricing -9.3% revenue growth in year 1 — consistent with the PC-volume warning already being discounted, which limits (but does not remove) further downside. [Likely]

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROIC (ratios page) continues its 5-year decline and falls below 20%, confirming competitive returns are structurally gone, not cyclically low.
- Operating margin (income statement) falls below 5% TTM (currently 6.5%), confirming margin compression is accelerating past FY2026 levels.
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) rises above 2.0x (currently 1.33x TTM), showing buyback-funded capital return has become unsustainable against a softening FCF base.
- Personal Systems revenue (income statement/segment data) turns negative YoY as the disclosed FY2027 unit-volume decline actually materializes, rather than being offset by price/mix as in FY26.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
FCF conversion is strong and rising (89-158% of net income over FY21-TTM), supporting a steady cash return: $274m dividends ($0.30/share) plus $300m buybacks (~12.2m shares) in Q3 FY26 alone, ~$0.6bn total [Certain] (source: 8-K EX-99.1, above). But net debt/EBITDA has risen from 0.70x (FY21) to 1.33x (TTM), and P/B is unavailable on the fact sheet — consistent with the buybacks having been funded partly by debt against a thin or negative equity base, which raises leverage risk if FCF weakens alongside the warned PC downturn. [Likely]

## 6. Unusual items
Altman Z-score is 1.60, inside the classic distress zone (<1.8), reflecting the thin/negative equity base from years of buybacks rather than imminent default risk, but it leaves little margin for error if cash generation deteriorates [Likely] (source: fact sheet, Other table, stockanalysis.com statistics page). Restructuring charges continue ($48m in Q3 FY26, down from $365m the prior quarter, tied to AI-adoption enablement and employee separation), an ongoing cost-cutting treadmill rather than a one-off [Certain] (source: 8-K EX-99.1, above). Short interest is elevated at 8.07% of float (down 10.6% month-on-month); institutional ownership is 94.3% (fact sheet, News & sentiment table).
