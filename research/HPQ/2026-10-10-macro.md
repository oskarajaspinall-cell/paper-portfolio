Market pricing **(inference)**: 2y yield (4.75%) 87bp above effective fed funds (3.88%) proxies a market pricing further hikes, not cuts, consistent with sticky CPI and widening spreads — tightening conditions, not stale data. The fair-value DCF already uses today's 5.22% risk-free rate; the surprise risk is a further rise.

Bull: inflation cools, yields/spreads retrace → discount rate falls, supports re-rating toward base fair value ($40.22, +33% vs $30.20). Bear: rates/spreads keep rising → discount rate up, fair value compresses, financing cost rises against the Altman Z/leverage profile as guided FY27 PC-unit declines bite. Tilt: toward bear, moderate — macro tightening compounds a company-specific leverage/demand risk already flagged.
