# A. O. Smith Corporation (AOS) — Core initiation — 2026-10-07

## 1. Business
A. O. Smith makes residential and commercial water heaters, boilers, and water treatment/purification systems, sold mainly through plumbing wholesalers and retail into replacement-driven demand in North America, plus a Nanjing-headquartered China operation making water heaters, RO filtration and kitchen appliances [Certain] (source: https://www.aosmith.com/about-us/). North America is a long-standing duopoly with privately-held Rheem [Likely] (source: https://www.aosmith.com/about-us/).

## 2. Quality
ROIC is 23.0% TTM, ROCE 24.9%, ROE 27.1% — well above a typical industrial cost of capital, though ROIC has fallen 4.5pp over five years as pandemic-era returns normalize [Certain] (source: factsheet). Gross and operating margins are rising (38.6%/18.2% TTM) [Certain] (source: factsheet). The moat is North America: ~75%+ of volume there is non-discretionary replacement, the market is effectively a two-player duopoly, and UL certification plus plumber/wholesaler distribution relationships and DOE efficiency-standard compliance are real barriers to entry [Likely] (source: https://www.aosmith.com/about-us/). Rest-of-World/China is the weak link, not the moat: China third-party sales fell 28% YoY in local currency after Beijing ended appliance subsidies, and segment margin compressed to 5.2% from 10.5%; management is running a strategic review of "partnerships and other alternatives" for China [Certain] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/91142/000009114226000098/aos-20260630.htm).

## 3. Valuation
AOS trades at 15.4x P/E, 10.4x EV/EBITDA, 12.0x P/FCF and 4.2x P/B — below both its own 5-year range (P/FCF is 50% under the 5y low of 17.1x) and the WTS/PNR/LII/MAS peer median, with an 8.4% FCF yield [Certain] (source: factsheet). The discount prices in the FY2026 guidance cut (diluted EPS narrowed to $3.60–3.75 from $3.60–3.90) and a prolonged China downturn, giving little credit to the still-growing, higher-margin North America segment (sales +5.3% YoY) [Likely] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/91142/000009114226000098/aos-20260630.htm). At the peer-median EV/EBITDA (11.7x) the stock would be ~11% higher; re-rating needs China to stabilize or North America alone to be credited a normal multiple [Likely].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- North America segment sales (10-Q) decline for two consecutive quarters.
- Gross margin (income statement page) falls below 35% (below the 5y range shown on the factsheet).
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) rises above 1.5x, signalling capital discipline is slipping post the Leonard Valve deal.
- ROIC (ratios page) falls below 20%, below the current 23.0% TTM level.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
FCF conversion is strong (127.8% TTM, generally >100% over five years) despite a dip to 88.8% in FY2024 [Certain] (source: factsheet). Management keeps returning cash: $162.4M of buybacks and $99.8M of dividends in H1 2026, funded from a $525–575M FY2026 FCF guide [Certain] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/91142/000009114226000098/aos-20260630.htm). The Leonard Valve acquisition was debt-funded via a $470M term loan, moving net debt/EBITDA from net-cash to 0.63x and the leverage ratio from 7.7% to 25.7% [Certain] (source: 10-Q) — still low in absolute terms but a trend worth monitoring given continuing buybacks.

## 6. Unusual items
Short interest is elevated at 9.8% of float [Certain] (source: factsheet), consistent with bearish positioning around the China and guidance concerns. Leadership transition: Stephen Shafer (already President/CEO) added the Chairman title as Kevin Wheeler retired (2026-06-22), and a new CFO, Carrie Anderson, started in 2026 — normal-course succession, not a governance red flag [Likely] (source: https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/kevin-wheeler-to-retire-as-executive-chairman-president-and-ceo-stephen-shafer-named-chairman-302806975.html). Management also flagged that FY2026 guidance excludes any impact from recently announced tariff changes, an unquantified downside risk [Certain] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/91142/000009114226000098/aos-20260630.htm).
