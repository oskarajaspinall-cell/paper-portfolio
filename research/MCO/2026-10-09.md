# Moody's Corporation (MCO) — Core initiation — 2026-10-09

## 1. Business
Moody's has two segments: Moody's Investors Service (MIS), credit ratings on corporate, structured, financial-institution and public-sector debt, earning both transaction (issuance) and recurring (monitoring) fees; and Moody's Analytics (MA), subscription data/software for risk, compliance and KYC workflows (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1059556/000162828026009136/mco-20251231.htm) [Certain].

## 2. Quality
MCO and S&P Global form a ratings duopoly protected by NRSRO regulatory status, a structural entry barrier [Certain] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1059556/000162828026009136/mco-20251231.htm). TTM ROIC is 32.0% and ROCE 33.3%, both rising over five years; operating margin is 46.2% and FCF conversion 106.2% of net income, all best-in-class [Certain] (source: factsheet-2026-10-09.md, Quality table). MA is now 99% recurring revenue with 95% retention and 9% organic ARR growth; MIS recurring revenue grew 6% with first-time mandates up 45%, widening the base generating future monitoring fees [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/mco/transcripts/652937-q2-2026/). Management frames AI as a usage expander rather than a threat, citing proprietary data embedded in customer workflows [Guessing] (same source). Net debt/EBITDA is a modest 1.51x and falling. The main quality risk is cyclicality of MIS transaction revenue to debt-issuance volumes, a smaller share of the mix as recurring revenue grows [Likely].

## 3. Valuation
TTM multiples sit below their own 5-year ranges (P/E 29.1x vs 32.8-44.5x; EV/EBITDA 21.1x vs 25.4-31.0x; P/FCF 26.8x vs 34.0-42.8x) but above the peer median on every multiple (P/E +13%, EV/EBITDA +24%, EV/Sales +22%, P/FCF +35% vs SPGI/MSCI/FICO/VRSK) (source: factsheet-2026-10-09.md, Valuation table) [Certain]. The fact sheet's base-case DCF/FCFE fair value is $204.61, 55.4% below the $458.69 last close; even the bull case ($273.78) is 40.3% below [Certain] (source: factsheet-2026-10-09.md, Fair value table). The reverse DCF implies 27.0% revenue growth in year one fading to terminal — above MA's current 9% ARR growth and MIS's high-single-digit recurring growth [Likely], i.e. the market prices re-acceleration beyond recent results. The P/E-based fair value ($584.78 base) is far higher, so methods disagree sharply; DCF/FCFE is the owner's primary method for this industry.

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROIC (ratios page) falls below 20% (vs TTM 32.0%), signalling erosion of the ratings/analytics returns.
- Operating margin (income statement) falls below 40% (vs TTM 46.2%), signalling pricing or cost pressure.
- FCF margin (cash flow/income statement) falls below 28% (vs TTM 36.4%), signalling weaker cash conversion.
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) rises above 2.5x (vs TTM 1.51x), signalling debt-funded M&A/buybacks straining the balance sheet.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
FCF conversion is 106.2% of net income, among the highest in the peer set [Certain] (source: factsheet-2026-10-09.md). Management guided 2026 buybacks up to $3bn (~$2.2bn done year-to-date) and targets returning "more than 130% of free cash flow to shareholders" while preserving balance-sheet flexibility, alongside bolt-on M&A (e.g., a minority stake in PhilRatings) [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/mco/transcripts/652937-q2-2026/; https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260914620177/en/Moody%E2%80%99s-to-Acquire-Minority-Stake-in-Philippine-Rating-Services-Corporation-PhilRatings/). Net debt/EBITDA of 1.51x leaves room for this pace without stressing the balance sheet [Likely].

## 6. Unusual items
None material. Short interest is low (1.78% of float, +10.4% MoM) and institutional ownership is high (70.4%), both unremarkable [Certain] (source: factsheet-2026-10-09.md).
