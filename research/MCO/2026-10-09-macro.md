# Macro overlay: Moody's Corporation (MCO) — 2026-10-09
## Assessment
MCO's own DCF already runs on the risk-free rate: the base fair value uses the FRED 10y Treasury at 5.28% (2026-10-07, https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=DGS10) and still implies $204.61, 55% below the $458.69 close (factsheet) [Certain]. Over the past 3 months the 10y Treasury rose +0.72pp to 5.28% and the 10y real (TIPS) yield rose +0.61pp to 2.92% (2026-10-07, https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=DFII10) [Certain]; most of the move is real, not inflation, since 10y breakeven inflation rose only +0.12pp to 2.35% (2026-10-08, https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=T10YIE) [Certain]. A real-yield-led rate rise lifts MCO's own discount rate directly, consistent with the stock underperforming SPY by -21.8pp over 12 months (factsheet) [Likely] **(inference)**. Second channel: MIS transaction revenue tracks bond-issuance volumes, sensitive to financing costs and credit stress; the HY spread widened +0.39pp to 3.09% over 3 months (2026-10-07, https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=BAMLH0A0HYM2) [Certain] but remains historically tight, and Q2 2026 guidance was raised on 15% revenue growth — no issuance stress yet [Likely]. Market pricing (proxy): the 2y Treasury (4.77%) trading above effective fed funds (3.88%, 2026-10-07, https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=DFF) implies the market is not pricing rate cuts over two years — "higher for longer" is already partly priced [Likely] **(inference)**. Bull: real yields stabilize/fall and issuance holds — multiple re-rates toward peers. Bear: real yields keep rising with spreads widening further — discount rate and issuance headwinds compound the already-large valuation gap.
