# Aldar Properties PJSC (ADX:ALDAR) — Core initiation — 2026-11-08
## 1. Business
Abu Dhabi's largest listed property developer. It earns from selling homes and land (lumpy, backlog-driven development revenue) and from a recurring-income book of investment properties, hospitality and asset management [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/adx/ALDAR/). Home market is the UAE; no foreign country is flagged as ~10%+ of revenue in the sources read, but the segment and country mix was not verified [Guessing].

## 2. Quality
Returns have risen strongly: ROIC 8.2% in FY2021 to 16.1% in FY2025, 14.0% TTM; ROE 19.7% TTM [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/adx/ALDAR/financials/ratios/). Operating margin is flat at 28.6% TTM, but gross margin has slid from 40.9% to 36.0% and net margin from 27.0% to 23.6% [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/adx/ALDAR/financials/income-statement/). The moat is local: land bank access and the Abu Dhabi government-linked ecosystem, which a new entrant cannot copy quickly [Likely]. This is not a proven durable-returns story: returns are at cyclical highs for a developer, and Altman Z is 1.88 with Piotroski 5 [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/adx/ALDAR/statistics/). Management commentary on the Q3 call says guidance was maintained despite softer sales and hospitality weakness [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/adx/ALDAR/transcripts/730633-q3-2026/). Direction: ROIC and ROCE are flat over twelve months, but FCF margin fell from 23.1% to 6.7% [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/adx/ALDAR/financials/cash-flow-statement/?p=trailing).

## 3. Valuation
At 7.98 AED the stock trades on 7.4x P/E, below its 5y minimum of 9.1x (median 11.0x), and 7.7x EV/EBITDA against a 5y range of 8.4x to 14.1x [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/adx/ALDAR/financials/ratios/). P/B is 1.1x, in line with its median. It is more expensive than the three Dubai peers on earnings (peer median P/E 4.8x), though the peer set is thin and mixes different business models [Likely]. Earnings multiples look cheap but cash multiples do not: P/FCF is 25.9x against a 5y maximum of 14.3x, and FCF yield is 3.87% [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/adx/ALDAR/statistics/). Developer earnings are recognised ahead of cash, so P/E flatters. Mechanical fair value is 10.42 AED base (+30.6%), 8.73 bear (+9.4%); the bull case (101.10) is not meaningful. The reverse DCF implies 9.9% year-1 revenue growth, fading [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/adx/ALDAR/). Upside is moderate and the bear case barely sits above the price.

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROIC (ratios page, TTM) falls below 10%
- Net debt / EBITDA (site definition, ratios page) rises above 2.0x
- FCF margin (cash flow statement, TTM) stays below 5% for two consecutive quarters
- Operating margin (income statement, TTM) falls below 22%

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Cash conversion has deteriorated: FCF/NI of 83.7% in FY2025 fell to 28.3% TTM, and net debt/EBITDA rose from -0.09x in FY2021 to 1.17x TTM [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/adx/ALDAR/financials/balance-sheet/). Growth is being funded with leverage, plus outside capital such as a partner investment reported in the press [Likely] (source: https://www.benzinga.com/markets/large-cap/26/02/50743271/apollo-injects-1-billion-into-uae-realty-giant-aldar). Land and project spend ahead of handovers explains part of the cash drag [Guessing]. Share count change is [data unavailable].
