# Macro overlay: United Airlines Holdings (UAL) — 2026-10-11
## Assessment
UAL is a high-beta (1.30, stockanalysis.com statistics) [Certain], leveraged (net debt/EBITDA 2.40x TTM, ratios page) [Certain] cyclical already at a 5y-high EV/EBITDA (7.3x vs 5y max 7.0x, factsheet) [Certain]. Its fair-value model already uses the current risk-free rate (10y Treasury 5.22%, FRED DGS10, 2026-10-08) in CAPM cost of equity, so the *level* isn't a surprise; the *change* is.

Chain: real yields → cost of debt/equity → valuation multiple for a leveraged, capex-heavy cyclical; secondarily, credit spreads → refinancing cost for the $5.8bn of new H1-2026 debt (SEC 10-Q).

Facts (FRED, 2026-10-08/09): 10y real yield (DFII10) 2.87%, +0.56pp/3m; 10y breakeven (T10YIE) 2.33%, roughly flat (+0.09pp/3m) — the yield rise is real-rate/term-premium driven, not an inflation/fuel-cost scare [Certain]. Market pricing (proxy, not fact): 2y yield (4.75%) sits 0.87pp above effective fed funds (3.88%, FRED DGS2/DFF) — implies the market still prices further tightening/term premium, not near-term cuts [Likely] **(inference)**. HY credit spread 3.15%, +0.45pp/3m but still tight; VIX 15.41, falling 1m/3m — risk appetite calm, not a risk-premium shock [Certain].

Bull: yields stabilize/ease, calm VIX/tight spreads persist → multiple holds or re-rates toward $162.58 base fair value. Bear: real yields extend higher and spreads widen further → cost of capital rises for this 2.4x-levered name already at a stretched multiple, compressing it even with margins intact.

Tilt: toward bear, small — the rate move is real but fundamentals (loyalty-status war vs Delta/American, margin recovery, peer-multiple discount) drive the thesis; spreads and VIX aren't yet stressed.
