# United Airlines Holdings (UAL) — Core initiation — 2026-10-11

## 1. Business
UAL operates a hub-and-spoke network (mainline + regional United Express) from hubs including ORD, IAH, DEN, EWR, SFO and IAD, selling passenger seats (domestic, Atlantic, Pacific, Latin America), cargo capacity and MileagePlus loyalty/co-brand credit-card revenue [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/ual/). Costs are dominated by jet fuel, labor and capacity (fleet/capex), making margins cyclical and fuel/demand-sensitive [Certain].

## 2. Quality
Returns have recovered sharply from the pandemic trough: ROIC rose from -15.5% (FY21) to 10.3% TTM, ROE to 23.3% TTM, operating margin to 7.0% TTM (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/ual/financials/ratios/) [Certain]. FCF conversion is volatile (169%, -10%, 122%, 76% FY22-25) reflecting heavy, lumpy capex [Certain]. The moat is network scale and slot/gate constraints at capacity-limited hubs (EWR, ORD, IAD) plus loyalty switching costs, not a cost advantage — airlines remain price-competitive and margins are structurally thin versus most industrials [Likely]. United is now attacking Delta/American directly with a status-match offer to poach their top-tier frequent flyers, paired with Starlink wifi as a differentiator [Likely] (source: https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/01/united-airlines-status-match-delta-american.html); this can defend/grow premium revenue but also raises loyalty-program costs and signals the moat needs active reinforcement, not pure structural protection. Net debt/EBITDA has improved from 4.30x (FY22) to 2.40x TTM but remains a legacy COVID debt load (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/ual/financials/ratios/) [Certain].

## 3. Valuation
At 10.1x P/E (42nd percentile of its own 5y range, 5.2x-16.7x) and 7.3x EV/EBITDA (above its 5y max of 7.0x, so a 5y-high multiple on cyclically elevated earnings), the market is paying up versus UAL's own history even as it trades at a 29-53% discount to peers (DAL/AAL/LUV/ALK median) on most multiples [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/ual/financials/ratios/). The reverse DCF implies the price assumes -0.9% revenue growth in year 1 fading to terminal growth — a low bar that current demand trends (passenger revenue +16.3% H1 2026 YoY, source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/100517/000010051726000139/ual-20260630.htm) comfortably clear today [Certain], but airline demand is cyclical and can reverse quickly. Base fair value of $162.58 (+51% vs the $107.45 close) assumes through-cycle margins persist; bear case ($2.06) assumes a demand/fuel shock resets returns back toward the 2021-22 trough [Guessing].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) rises back above 4x on a sustained basis, reversing the post-COVID deleveraging.
- Operating margin (income statement) falls below 5% for two consecutive quarters.
- ROIC (ratios page) falls below 8%, signalling returns are converging back to peer/cost-of-capital levels rather than staying structurally elevated.
- FCF conversion (cash flow/income statement) turns negative for a full fiscal year outside a one-off capex spike.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Cash and short-term investments rose to $16.7bn at Q2 2026 (from $5.9bn cash at YE2025) on $6.4bn of H1 operating cash flow, but this reflects $5.8bn of new debt issuance against $4.5bn repaid — net debt rose ~$1.3bn, i.e. refinancing and liquidity building, not net deleveraging, in H1 2026 [Certain] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/100517/000010051726000139/ual-20260630.htm). Buybacks were cut sharply to $27m (H1 2026) from $237m (H1 2025 period), prioritising the balance sheet and $3.0bn of H1 capex (fleet) over shareholder returns [Certain] (same source). Shares outstanding still fell 1.48% YoY (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/ual/statistics/).

## 6. Unusual items
Altman Z-score of 1.31 sits in the conventional "distress" zone, though this is structurally common for leveraged, capital-intensive airlines rather than unique to UAL [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/ual/statistics/). Short interest is modest at 5.2% of float and falling [Certain] (same source). Beta of 1.30 means the stock amplifies broad market/macro and fuel-price swings [Certain]. No governance red flags identified.
