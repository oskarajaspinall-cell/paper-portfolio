# East West Bancorp (EWBC) — Core initiation — 2026-11-09
## 1. Business
Pasadena-based bank holding company (East West Bank), regional banks industry. It earns net interest income on commercial real estate, C&I and residential loans, plus fees (wealth, FX, lending). Its franchise is built on cross-border U.S.–China business [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/ewbc/). Revenue share by country: [data unavailable]; the filing names U.S.-China trade and economic conditions in China as a source of uncertainty [Certain] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1069157/000106915726000051/ewbc-20260930.htm). Macro overlay should check China.

## 2. Quality
Returns are high for a bank and steady: ROE 15.9% in FY2025 and 16.6% TTM, flat over five years (+0.2pp), with peak 19.1% in FY2022 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/ewbc/financials/ratios/) [Certain]. Net margin 50.1% TTM, though falling 1.3pp over five years (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/ewbc/financials/income-statement/) [Certain]. Q3 2026 efficiency ratio 34.40% [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1069157/000106915726000051/ewbc-20260930.htm) is the likely moat: low cost base, sticky bilingual cross-border clients, deposit franchise [Likely]. Q3 net income grew year on year, driven by net interest income [company-reported] (same filing). Shares fell 0.41% YoY. Piotroski F is 4 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/ewbc/statistics/): mediocre, but less informative for banks [Guessing]. Q3 call: lower charge-off guidance [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/ewbc/transcripts/647616-q3-2026/).

## 3. Valuation
P/E 12.5x sits at 95% of its 5y range (8.2x–12.8x, median 11.4x) but 9% below the peer median of 13.7x (CFR, WAL, ZION, CMA, PNFP). P/B 2.0x is above its 5y maximum (1.9x) and 59% above peers' 1.2x; P/FCF 9.5x vs 5y median 9.4x; FCF yield 10.58% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/ewbc/statistics/) [Certain]. Fair value is flagged unreliable: methods disagree 3.8x (P/B+ROE base 121.79, FCFE DCF 171.70, DDM 45.04; blended base 138.43 vs close 128.84) [Certain]. The reverse DCF implies -0.9% year-1 revenue growth, undemanding if ROE holds [Likely]. The premium to peers is earned by higher ROE; the price leaves little cushion if credit or China sentiment turns [Likely].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROE (ratios page) TTM falls below 13%.
- Net margin (income statement page) TTM falls below 45%.
- Net interest margin in the 10-Q MD&A falls below 3.2% [company-reported level, 10-Q filings].
- TCE ratio in the 10-Q falls below 9%, or net charge-offs rise materially above the lowered guidance.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
FCF conversion is 113.3% of net income in FY2025, falling 20.5pp over five years but FCF margin is rising over the last 12 months (58.6% to 64.6%) (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/ewbc/financials/cash-flow-statement/?p=trailing) [Certain]. For banks FCF is distorted by working capital items; treat lightly [Likely]. Net share count is shrinking, so buybacks exceed issuance. Book value per share rose 2% in nine months and TCE ratio eased to 10.02% from 10.54% [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1069157/000106915726000051/ewbc-20260930.htm), consistent with growth absorbing capital [Likely].
