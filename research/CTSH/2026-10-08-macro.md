# Macro overlay: Cognizant Technology Solutions (CTSH) — 2026-10-08
## Assessment
CTSH's thesis is idiosyncratic — a cheap, deteriorating-returns IT-services name whose multiple already sits below its own 5-year trough and the peer median [Certain]. The one macro variable that bears on it is the discount rate used to re-rate that multiple. Real yields have risen sharply: the 10y TIPS yield is 2.91% (2026-10-06, FRED DFII10), up +48bp in a month and +61bp in three [Certain]. The 2y Treasury (4.79%, 2026-10-06) sits ~91bp above effective fed funds (3.88%), a proxy that the market prices further policy firmness over two years [Likely] **(inference)**. Breakeven inflation is little changed (2.36%, +1bp/1m), so this looks like a real-rate/term-premium move, not an inflation scare [Likely] **(inference)**. CTSH's bull case needs a hospitable broad discount rate for its depressed multiple to re-rate; a continued real-yield climb would cap that even if ROIC/margin trends stabilize. A plausible offshore-cost tailwind from a stronger dollar cannot be quantified: INR isn't tracked in FRED, and the broad dollar index (DTWEXBGS, +2.9%/1m) diverges from CNY (DEXCHUS, −0.08/1m), so this channel stays unmodeled and low-confidence [Guessing] **(inference)**. Bull: real yields stabilize/ease, removing the discount-rate drag on re-rating. Bear: real yields extend higher, keeping the multiple capped regardless of fundamentals. Given CTSH's low beta (0.85) and a company-specific thesis, the tilt is small.
