# Cognizant Technology Solutions (CTSH) — Core initiation — 2026-10-08

## 1. Business
Cognizant is a global IT services and consulting firm, billing enterprise clients (organized into Financial Services, Health Sciences, Products & Resources, and Communications Media & Technology verticals) mainly for staffed delivery of digital engineering, cloud migration, IT outsourcing and AI-enabled process automation, typically under multi-year managed-services contracts [Certain] (source: https://www.cognizant.com/us/en/about-cognizant; https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1058290/000105829026000031/ctsh-20260630.htm).

## 2. Quality
Returns are deteriorating, not durable: ROIC has fallen from 20.5% (FY2021) to 14.8% TTM and ROE from 18.7% to 14.9%, while gross margin has eroded from 37.3% to 33.4% — consistent with a commoditizing, people-intensive service where offshore peers (INFY, WIT) and consulting-scale peers (ACN, IBM) cap pricing [Likely]. ROCE has held up better (19.7% → 20.3% TTM), and FCF conversion is rising (94.7% of net income in FY2023 to 117% TTM) [Certain] (source: fact sheet RA/CF/IS). Long-dated contracts and switching costs give some client stickiness, but the margin/ROIC trend shows this is not translating into pricing power. Management's own Q2 2026 10-Q discloses a large severance-led restructuring program ("Project Leap") aimed at AI-driven productivity — itself evidence the current cost structure is not defensible without self-disruption [Likely] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1058290/000105829026000031/ctsh-20260630.htm). Net debt/EBITDA has moved from net-cash to a small 0.26x.

## 3. Valuation
Every multiple sits below both its 5-year range and the peer median: P/E 12.5x (5y min 12.8x; peers 13.5x, -7%), EV/EBITDA 6.6x (5y min 8.1x; peers 8.6x, -23%), EV/Sales 1.2x (peers 1.8x, -30%), P/FCF 9.9x (peers 11.8x, -16%), P/B 1.8x (peers 4.0x, -56%, though peer mix includes higher-ROE/leverage names so less comparable) [Certain] (source: fact sheet RA/peers). FCF yield is 10.11%. The price sits below its own trough multiples of the last five years, implying the market expects the ROIC/margin decline above to continue, not stabilize. The stock is down 15.7% over 12 months and trades below both the 50- and 200-day moving averages despite a +29.9% three-month bounce off its June low — the de-rating looks only partially reversed. At these levels, even a thesis that returns merely stabilize would screen cheap versus peers and history.

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROIC (ratios page) falls below 12% (vs 14.8% TTM), confirming the return decline is structural rather than cyclical.
- Gross margin (income statement) falls below 32% (vs 33.4% TTM), evidence that pricing pressure is intensifying rather than stabilizing.
- FCF margin (cash flow/income statement) falls below 8% (vs 12.0% TTM), signalling cash conversion is breaking down alongside margins.
- Net debt/EBITDA (balance sheet/income statement) rises above 1.0x without a corresponding rise in ROIC, signalling capital is being deployed (e.g., debt-funded M&A) without adequate returns.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
FCF conversion is strong and improving (117% of net income TTM, up from 94.7% in FY2023), and FCF margin has held near 10-12% through the restructuring period [Certain] (source: fact sheet CF/IS). Shares outstanding fell 3.24% YoY, indicating ongoing buybacks [Certain] (source: fact sheet ST). Net debt/EBITDA turned slightly positive (0.26x, from net cash of -0.19x a year earlier), consistent with 2026 bolt-on acquisition activity disclosed in the 10-Q, while leverage remains low and the Altman Z-score (5.91) signals no distress [Certain] (source: fact sheet BS/IS/ST; https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1058290/000105829026000031/ctsh-20260630.htm).

## 6. Unusual items
A severance-heavy restructuring program ("Project Leap") was underway as of the Q2 2026 10-Q, targeting AI-led productivity gains; this can pressure near-term operating costs before any benefit shows in margins [Likely] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1058290/000105829026000031/ctsh-20260630.htm). Short interest is 8.02% of float, up 1.2% versus the prior month — elevated but not extreme [Certain] (source: fact sheet ST). Piotroski F-score is a middling 4/9, consistent with the profitability deterioration already captured in the Quality section [Likely] (source: fact sheet ST). News flow is routine new-logo/extension wins (Gilead, SITA, ACI Worldwide) with no disclosed numbers usable as data [Certain] (source: fact sheet News & sentiment).
