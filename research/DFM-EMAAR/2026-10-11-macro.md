# Macro overlay: Emaar Properties PJSC (EMAAR) — 2026-10-11
## Assessment
Chain: US rate path → UAE policy rate (AED is a hard USD peg; UAE tracks the Fed near 1:1) [Certain] (inference, peg mechanism) → Dubai mortgage costs/buyer demand; separately, US 10y Treasury yield → EMAAR's own DCF discount rate → the fact sheet's fair value (risk-free input 5.22% from DGS10) [Certain] (https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=DGS10, 2026-10-08).

Facts: DGS10 5.22%, +0.68pp/3m; DFF 3.88%, +0.26pp/3m; core CPI (CPILFESL) +0.976 index pts/1m vs +1.64/3m — the latest month ran faster than the prior quarterly pace [Likely] (inference); dollar index (DTWEXBGS) 121.385, +2.86/1m (all FRED, 2026-10-02/08/09). Pricing proxy: 2y yield (4.75%) ~87bp above fed funds implies markets expect no near-term cuts [Likely] (inference, proxy).

This reads as a continuing trend, not a fresh surprise: rising US/UAE rates raise Dubai mortgage costs and EMAAR's DCF discount rate (bear for multiple); dollar strength raises AED-priced costs for foreign buyers (bear for volume). EMAAR is net cash (net debt/EBITDA -0.76x), so its own funding cost is unexposed — only buyer demand and the discount rate are. Much is already in the price: 5.2x P/E (-17% below its 5y range), -16.6% 12m, -32pp vs SPY.

**Bull**: rates roll over (2y toward/below funds) → discount rate/mortgage costs ease → multiple re-rates toward 5y/peer median.
**Bear**: rates/dollar keep rising → discount rate and buyer costs stay elevated → multiple stays depressed.
**Tilt**: moderate toward bear — yields, funds rate and dollar all rose together via a real transmission (peg + DCF input), partly offset by net cash and an already-cheap multiple.
