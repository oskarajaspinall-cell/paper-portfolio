# Kinsale Capital Group (KNSL) — Core initiation — 2026-11-16
## 1. Business
Kinsale is a US excess and surplus (E&S) lines specialty insurer. It underwrites hard-to-place commercial property, casualty and some personal risks, written through wholesale brokers. It earns underwriting profit plus investment income on float. [Certain] No foreign country is disclosed as ~10%+ of revenue in the sources read. (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/knsl/)

## 2. Quality
Returns are high. ROIC is 24.3% TTM and ROE 27.8% TTM, against 22.6% and 23.9% in FY2021 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/knsl/financials/ratios/). [Certain] ROIC is flat on a year earlier but 3.0pp below its 2-year high of 27.3%, and 1.4pp lower than 3 years ago. [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/knsl/financials/ratios/?p=trailing)

The moat is underwriting discipline and a technology-led low-cost model in E&S. [Likely] The 10-Q shows the cost: competition is eroding growth. Q3 gross written premiums fell 4.8% [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1669162/000166916226000050/knsl-20260930.htm). Property fell 19.3% and Commercial Property 26.8% on "heightened competition" [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1669162/000166916226000050/knsl-20260930.htm). Casualty grew only 0.9% [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1669162/000166916226000050/knsl-20260930.htm). Reserves look sound: Q3 prior-year development was favourable by $19.7 million, partly offset by adverse construction liability from 2017-18 [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1669162/000166916226000050/knsl-20260930.htm). The Q3 net income drop came from portfolio repositioning, not underwriting. [Likely]

## 3. Valuation
At 395.89 USD, P/E is 17.2x and EV/EBITDA 13.2x, both below their 5y minimums (18.1x and 14.0x; 5y medians 26.1x and 20.5x). P/B is 4.6x, at the bottom of its range. All are still a premium to peers: P/E +21%, P/B +86% (peer medians 14.3x and 2.5x). FCF yield is 10.92%. [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/knsl/statistics/)

The price assumes returns stay far above peers but growth slows. The mechanical base fair value is 297.82 USD (-24.8%); bull is 454.79 (+14.9%). The method (P/B with sustainable ROE) caps ROE and so penalises Kinsale. [Likely] FCF conversion of 185.3% flatters the yield, because float growth fuels operating cash flow, and it is falling as growth slows. [Likely] The shares are 10.0% above the 50-day average and 12.9% above the 200-day. Six-month momentum is +31.8%, but the 12-month change is -0.3%. [Certain]

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROIC TTM (ratios page, ?p=trailing) falls below 20%.
- Casualty gross written premiums (10-Q, MD&A divisional table) decline year on year for two consecutive quarters, meaning softening has spread beyond property.
- Prior-year reserve development (10-Q MD&A) turns net adverse in any quarter, or adverse development appears outside construction liability.
- Net debt / EBITDA (site definition, ratios page) rises above 1.0x.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Shares outstanding fell 1.83% YoY, so buybacks shrink the count while the premium base is flat. [Certain] Leverage is light at 0.33x net debt/EBITDA. [Certain] Q3 net retention rose to 86.2% from 83.3% [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1669162/000166916226000050/knsl-20260930.htm). That keeps more profit but also more risk. [Likely] The portfolio repositioning swapped equities and sold lower-yielding bonds at a loss. It is a one-off. [Likely]
