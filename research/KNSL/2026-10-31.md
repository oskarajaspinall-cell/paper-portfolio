# Kinsale Capital Group (KNSL) — Core initiation — 2026-10-31
## 1. Business
Kinsale writes excess and surplus lines property and casualty insurance: risks standard carriers decline, priced on technical underwriting and a low-cost, technology-driven platform. It earns underwriting profit on premiums plus investment income on float. [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/knsl/)

## 2. Quality
Returns are high: TTM ROIC 24.3%, ROE 27.8% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/knsl/financials/ratios/). Balance sheet is light: net debt/EBITDA 0.33x, and FCF is 185.3% of net income (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/knsl/statistics/). [Certain]

The edge is a cost and data advantage in small-account E&S, which is hard to copy quickly. [Likely] It is cyclical, though, and the cycle is turning. Gross written premiums fell 4.8% in the third quarter, with property down 19.3% and Commercial Property down 26.8% on "heightened competition"; casualty grew 0.9% [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1669162/000166916226000050/knsl-20260930.htm). [Certain] Prior years developed favorably by $19.7 million, partly offset by adverse construction liability development in the 2017 and 2018 accident years [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1669162/000166916226000050/knsl-20260930.htm). [Certain] Net income fell 30.7%, mainly from investment portfolio repositioning rather than underwriting [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1669162/000166916226000050/knsl-20260930.htm). [Certain] 12-month price change is -9.4%, consistent with the market pricing slower growth. [Likely]

## 3. Valuation
P/E is 15.9x against a 5y range of 18.1x–37.9x (median 26.1x), so below its own minimum. P/B is 4.3x against 4.6x–8.1x (median 7.3x), also below the minimum. P/FCF is 8.5x and FCF yield 11.82% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/knsl/statistics/). [Certain]

Against peers (RLI, WRB, AXS, SIGI) it still trades at +18% on P/E and +82% on P/B. [Certain] The price therefore assumes the premium to peers, justified by returns on equity, survives a softening market. Mechanical fair value is bear 237.13, base 296.59, bull 453.55 versus a last close of 365.71 (base -18.9%, bull +24.0%). [Likely] It is cheap against its own history but not against peers or base fair value. Falling premiums argue that the historical multiples were paid for growth now fading. [Likely]

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROE (ratios page) falls below 20% on a TTM basis.
- Casualty gross written premiums (10-Q, divisional table) decline year on year for two consecutive quarters.
- Adverse prior-year reserve development (10-Q MD&A) exceeds favorable development in any quarter.
- Gross written premiums (10-Q) fall more than 10% year on year in any quarter.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Shares outstanding fell 1.83% year on year, so buybacks are shrinking the count (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/knsl/statistics/). [Certain] Leverage is low, and FCF conversion is high but has fallen from 262.8% in FY2021 to 185.3% TTM (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/knsl/financials/cash-flow-statement/). [Certain] The company sold equities and lower-yielding bonds in the third quarter and reinvested to raise expected returns, which cost reported earnings. [Certain] A pro-cyclical buyback at a premium multiple adds little if growth stalls. [Guessing]
