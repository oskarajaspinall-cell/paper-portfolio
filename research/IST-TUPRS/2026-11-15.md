# Türkiye Petrol Rafinerileri A.S. (IST:TUPRS) — Core initiation — 2026-11-15
## 1. Business
Turkey's largest crude refiner and fuel supplier; earns the refining margin (product prices less crude cost) on throughput, sold mainly into the Turkish market [Likely]. Industry: Oil & Gas Refining & Marketing; reports and trades in TRY (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/ist/TUPRS/). ## 2. Quality
Cyclical, margin-driven, not a compounder [Certain]. Fiscal-year ROIC ran 52.4% (FY2021), 99.5%, 69.4%, then 13.8% (FY2024) and 12.0% (FY2025), against 38.9% TTM (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/ist/TUPRS/financials/ratios/). The direction change is real: ROIC, ROCE and operating margin fell over five fiscal years but rose over the last 12 months (ROIC 16.3% to 38.9%; operating margin 6.1% to 13.2%) (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/ist/TUPRS/financials/ratios/?p=trailing). The Q3 2026 call headline cites record utilisation and strong cracks, so the upswing is a margin-cycle recovery [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/ist/TUPRS/transcripts/723818-q3-2026/). Moat is local scale and logistics, not pricing power [Guessing]. Balance sheet is net cash (-1.15x net debt/EBITDA TTM), FCF conversion 132.6%, Piotroski 6, Altman Z 3.28 [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/ist/TUPRS/statistics/). FY-average returns (about 12-14% ROIC in FY2024-25) are the sustainable guide, not TTM [Likely].

## 3. Valuation
P/E 5.6x (34% of its 5y range; peer median 8.7x, -35%), EV/EBITDA 2.8x (51% of range; peers 5.2x, -46%), P/FCF 4.3x (24%), P/B 1.3x (46%; peers 2.2x), FCF yield 23.48% [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/ist/TUPRS/statistics/). Peers: PKN, MOL, VLO, MPC, NESTE. The discount to peers is partly a Turkey/TRY discount and partly a peak-margin discount [Likely]. Mechanical fair value is flagged unreliable (methods disagree 3.6x; bear 57.72, base 637.18 TRY vs a 359.00 close): weigh it lightly [Certain]. The reverse DCF implies -12.5% year-1 revenue growth, so the price assumes margins fall back to mid-cycle [Likely]. Price is 21.6% above the 200-day and 5.1% below the 50-day; 12-month +84.0% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/ist/TUPRS/history/). It is working, not obviously expensive on normalised earnings, but TTM earnings are near a cycle high [Likely].
