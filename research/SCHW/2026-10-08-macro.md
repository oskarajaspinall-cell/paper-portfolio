# Macro overlay: The Charles Schwab Corporation (SCHW) — 2026-10-08

## Assessment
Chain: short-term rates -> deposit "cash sorting" / net interest margin -> net interest revenue (Schwab's largest revenue line, 10-K); long yields -> AFS/HTM securities unrealized losses -> tangible book value/capital ratios -> P/B multiple [Certain] **(inference on mechanism, standard for this business model)**.

Facts: effective fed funds 3.88% (FRED DFF, 2026-10-06), +0.25pp both ~1m and ~3m — a hike, not a cut, in the past month. 10y Treasury 5.27% (DGS10), +0.49pp ~1m/+0.72pp ~3m. 2y Treasury 4.79% (DGS2), +0.42pp ~1m. 10y real yield (DFII10) 2.91%, +0.48pp ~1m. 10y breakeven (T10YIE) only 2.36%, +0.01pp ~1m. HY spread (BAMLH0A0HYM2) 3.03%, +0.35pp ~1m, still low.

Market pricing (proxy, **inference**): 2y yield (4.79%) sits ~91bp above effective fed funds (3.88%), implying the market still prices further tightening/higher-for-longer rather than cuts. Breakeven inflation is nearly flat, so the real-yield surge looks like a term-premium/policy-path repricing, not a fresh inflation surprise.

This is a sharp, broad-based move within one month — unusual, and plausibly not fully priced before it happened.

**macro_bull**: rates stay high, deposit beta low, cash-sorting muted -> NII and ROE keep expanding, multiple re-rates toward peers (currently -16% P/E, [Certain], fact sheet).
**macro_bear**: renewed cash-sorting (2022-23 playbook) compresses NIM; higher long yields deepen AFS/HTM marks, pressuring book value/P/B (near peer parity, +4%).
**Tilt**: SCHW underperformed SPY by -10.2pp over 3m into this yield spike [Certain, fact sheet] — consistent with the bear risk already being partly discounted **(inference)**. Oct 15 earnings will show actual NII/cash-mix trend.
