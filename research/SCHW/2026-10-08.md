# The Charles Schwab Corporation (SCHW) — Core initiation — 2026-10-08
## 1. Business
Schwab is a scale brokerage/bank hybrid: $11.90tn client assets, 38.5m active brokerage accounts [Certain] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/316709/000031670926000009/schw-20251231.htm). Revenue is net interest revenue on swept client cash and bank deposits, plus asset-management/trading fees on a low-cost, high-volume model [Certain] (same source). It targets the >$80tn US investable-wealth pool.

## 2. Quality
ROE is rising sharply (10.4%→20.3% TTM, +7.7pp over 5y) and net margin is 38.8% TTM, up 5.4pp [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/schw/financials/ratios/, https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/schw/financials/income-statement/). ROIC is lower and roughly flat (8.6% TTM, +0.8pp), implying returns are helped by banking-deposit leverage more than capital efficiency — a feature of the sweep model, not a standalone red flag. FCF conversion is volatile year to year but 106.9% TTM [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/schw/financials/cash-flow-statement/). The moat is scale, low-cost structure and a bundled "no trade-offs" offering, but competition is real: the 10-K cites rivals including banks, trust companies and fintechs [Certain] (source: 10-K), and a headline flags AI-driven pressure from Vanguard [Likely] (source: https://www.tipranks.com/news/the-fly/schwab-slips-amid-ai-competition-from-vanguard-ibd-reports-thefly-news). Offsetting this: a reported record net-new-asset quarter [Likely] (source: https://www.tipranks.com/news/charles-schwab-stock-schw-rises-as-net-new-assets-hit-record-high) and new AI/Anthropic partnerships for advisors [Likely] (source: https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260914786358/en/Charles-Schwab-and-Anthropic-to-Bring-Claude-to-Independent-Registered-Investment-Advisors/). Durability is good but not immune to rate-cycle or fee-compression risk.

## 3. Valuation
P/E of 17.4x sits 43% below its own 5y range minimum (21.1x–29.7x) and 16% below peer median (20.6x; IBKR, RJF, LPLA, MS) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/schw/financials/ratios/). P/B of 3.8x is mid-range historically (66th percentile) and roughly in line with peers (+4%) [Certain]. P/FCF (15.4x) is near its 5y low and 31% cheap to peers, though the 5y P/FCF range is distorted by the FY23 outlier. The fact sheet's own fair-value model (FCFE DCF, best method; risk-free 5.27%) puts base case at $93.55 vs last close $95.58 (-2.1%), bear $55.92 (-41.5%), bull $103.68 (+8.5%) [Certain] (source: fact sheet). The price is roughly priced for the base case already — not a deep-value entry, but the P/E discount to both history and peers, against rising ROE, is the more striking signal than the DCF midpoint.

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Net margin (income statement page) falls below 30% for two consecutive quarters, reversing the current trend.
- ROE (ratios page) drops back below 15%, erasing the 5-year improvement.
- FCF conversion (FCF/NI, cash-flow + income statement) falls persistently below 50% outside of a one-off working-capital swing.
- Evidence of sustained net-new-asset deceleration or net outflows (10-Q/press releases), reversing the record-inflow trend cited above.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Shares outstanding fell 3.36% YoY, indicating net buybacks [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/schw/statistics/). TTM FCF margin is 41.5% and FCF conversion 106.9%, both up sharply from FY21-24 levels, though the FY23 figure (372.7% margin) shows these are noisy, balance-sheet-driven numbers for a bank-like model rather than steady industrial FCF [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/schw/financials/cash-flow-statement/).

## 6. Unusual items
Short interest is low (1.19% of float) but rose 14.6% month-on-month, and bearish options flow was flagged in a recent headline [Likely] (source: https://www.tipranks.com/news/the-fly/charles-schwab-put-volume-heavy-and-directionally-bearish-thefly-news-3). Piotroski F-score of 6 is solid; Altman Z is unavailable. Beta of 0.78 is low for the sector. A secondary listing on the Texas Stock Exchange (Oct 1) is a structural/administrative change, not a fundamental one [Certain] (source: https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20261001581919/en/Schwab-Initiates-Secondary-Listing-on-the-Texas-Stock-Exchange/). None of these are individually material to the thesis.
