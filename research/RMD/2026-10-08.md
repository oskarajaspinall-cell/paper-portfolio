# ResMed Inc. (RMD) — Core initiation — 2026-10-08

## 1. Business
ResMed sells flow-generator devices and masks/accessories for sleep apnea and respiratory care, plus a software/digital-health business serving out-of-hospital care providers. Devices are the initial sale; masks and accessories are replaced regularly, giving a recurring, razor/razor-blade revenue stream tied to a large and growing installed patient base (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/rmd/).

## 2. Quality
Returns are high and improving: TTM ROIC 26.2% (FY22: 22.0%) and ROCE 25.4% (FY22: 22.7%), both up over the 5-year window (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/rmd/financials/ratios/). Gross margin has risen to 61.6% and operating margin to 34.0% (both +4-6pp since FY22), with net margin at 26.9% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/rmd/financials/income-statement/). FCF conversion is 108% of net income (FCF margin 29.2%, up from 6.0% in FY22), and the balance sheet is net cash (net debt/EBITDA -0.31x) (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/rmd/financials/cash-flow-statement/, https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/rmd/financials/ratios/). [Certain] The consumables (masks/accessories) stream against a large installed base, plus regulatory/device-approval barriers, are the structural reasons new entrants have not eroded these returns [Likely]; management also points to GLP-1 scripts correlating with higher, not lower, PAP therapy starts and resupply rates, which argues against GLP-1 drugs displacing the device franchise [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/rmd/transcripts/638539-q4-2026/).

## 3. Valuation
All five multiples sit near the bottom of RMD's own 5-year range: P/E 21.7x (15th pct of 18.5x-39.4x), EV/EBITDA 14.9x (12th pct), P/FCF 19.3x (2nd pct), P/B 4.9x (14th pct) (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/rmd/financials/ratios/). [Certain] The stock has de-rated sharply alongside a 12-month price decline of 20.5%, underperforming SPY by 36.2pp over that period (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/rmd/history/, https://stockanalysis.com/etf/spy/history/). Against peers (PHG, INSP, MDT) RMD still trades at a premium on every multiple (P/E +17%, EV/EBITDA +20%, P/FCF +16%, P/B +127%, EV/Sales +198%) (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/rmd/financials/ratios/, peer statistics pages), but the peer set includes a lower-margin diversified device maker (MDT) and a recall-impaired competitor (PHG), so the premium is consistent with RMD's materially higher margins and ROIC rather than pure overvaluation [Likely]. On balance, the price today assumes little further margin expansion and continued mid-single-digit growth, not a re-acceleration.

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Gross margin (income statement page) falls below 57% (reversing the FY22-FY26 expansion trend).
- ROIC (ratios page) falls back below 20% (giving up the 5-year gains).
- FCF conversion (FCF/net income, cash-flow and income-statement pages) drops below 100% for two consecutive fiscal years.
- Core revenue growth falls persistently outside management's guided 5%-7% FY2027 range (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/rmd/transcripts/638539-q4-2026/).

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Management guided to total FY2027 shareholder returns of over $1.85bn, a 75% increase year-over-year: a 10% dividend increase to $0.66/share and a $1.5bn buyback program, including a $450m accelerated tranche funded by MatrixCare divestiture proceeds (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/rmd/transcripts/638539-q4-2026/). [Certain] This is underpinned by FCF conversion above 100% of net income and a net-cash balance sheet (net debt/EBITDA -0.31x) (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/rmd/financials/ratios/), so the return program does not depend on incremental leverage.

## 6. Unusual items
Shares fell on an August 7, 2026 report that fiscal 2027 revenue guidance came in below expectations, tied to suspended ventilator sales and cost pressure (source: https://www.reuters.com/legal/litigation/resmed-shares-tumble-weak-2027-sales-forecast-eclipses-quarterly-profit-beat-2026-08-07/) [Likely]; the article itself could not be fetched to verify detail beyond the headline. Short interest is 10.2% of float and rose 11.0% month-on-month (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/rmd/statistics/) [Certain], an elevated and rising bearish position worth monitoring but not large enough on its own to override the fundamentals above.
