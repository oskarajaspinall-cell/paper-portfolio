# Futu Holdings Limited (FUTU) — Core initiation — 2026-10-18
## 1. Business
Futu operates Moomoo/富途, a Hong Kong-headquartered online brokerage and wealth-management platform serving retail investors in Hong Kong, the US, Singapore, Australia, Malaysia, Canada, Japan and the UK [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/futu/). Revenue is mainly trading commissions, margin-financing/securities-lending interest and distribution fees on wealth-management products sold through its app [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/futu/financials/income-statement/). Funded accounts reached 3,842,667 (+33.6% YoY), client assets HK$1.40tn (+43.6% YoY), as of 2026-06-30 [company-reported] (https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1754581/000110465926098911/tm2623384d1_ex99-1.htm).

## 2. Quality
ROE has risen from 19.2% (FY21) to 30.5% TTM and ROIC from 10.9% to 19.8%, with operating margin expanding to 66.7% TTM [Certain] (https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/futu/financials/ratios/) — strong operating leverage as the platform scales. The moat rests on a lower cost base than incumbent brokers and a multi-jurisdiction licence footprint that is slow for challengers to replicate [Likely]. Funded accounts +33.6% YoY and client assets +43.6% YoY as of 2026-06-30 confirm the growth engine is intact [company-reported] (https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1754581/000110465926098911/tm2623384d1_ex99-1.htm). However, FCF margin swung from -70.5% (FY23) to +257.5% (FY24) [Certain] (https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/futu/financials/cash-flow-statement/), reflecting large client-related balance-sheet items (margin loans, client payables) rather than owner-earnings stability — true cash generation is harder to read than the margin/ROE trend suggests [Likely]. Management's Q2 2026 call flagged "moderate asset outflows" from Mainland China regulatory changes, partly offset by overseas growth [Likely] (https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/futu/transcripts/638657-q2-2026/).

## 3. Valuation
At 10.5x TTM P/E, FUTU trades below its own 5-year range (13.9x-17.6x) and 56% below the peer median of 23.6x (TIGR, IBKR, HOOD, SCHW) [Certain] (https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/futu/financials/ratios/). P/FCF of 2.8x sits at the bottom of its 5-year range and 67% below peers' 8.6x; P/B of 3.0x is 35% below peers' 4.6x median despite the higher ROE [Certain]. The 35.10% TTM FCF yield is partly a function of the volatile client-payable swings noted above, so is not a clean read on sustainable cash return [Likely]. The market is pricing a discount for China/HK regulatory and litigation risk, larger than the earnings/margin trend alone justifies [Likely]. Base-case fair value of $235.71 (+123.5% vs the $105.48 close) and bear case $167.07 (+58.4%) imply room to re-rate absent escalation, though this is inform-only, not a target [Certain] (fact sheet fair-value block).

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Net margin (income statement page, stockanalysis.com) falls and stays below 35% for two consecutive quarters (signals margin compression from competition or regulation).
- ROE (ratios page, stockanalysis.com) drops below 15% for two consecutive quarters, reversing the five-year improving trend.
- Company-reported quarterly funded-account growth (next 6-K) decelerates below 10% YoY, versus the current 33.6%.
- A Chinese-regulator enforcement action or penalty against Futu is confirmed in a company SEC filing (6-K/20-F) or official PRC regulator notice, rather than only alleged in shareholder-lawsuit solicitations.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Shares outstanding rose just 0.42% YoY, so dilution is minimal [Certain] (https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/futu/statistics/). FCF conversion (FCF/NI) ranged from -149.8% (FY23) to 566.4% (FY24) [Certain] (https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/futu/financials/cash-flow-statement/), driven by swings in margin-loan and client-payable balances rather than true owner cash flow — read over several years, not any single one. The fact sheet shows no dividend or buyback data, so capital return cannot be assessed [data unavailable]; profit appears retained to fund balance-sheet growth (margin book, client assets) [Likely].

## 6. Unusual items
Insider ownership is high at 36.82% versus institutional 33.3% [Certain] (https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/futu/statistics/), supporting management alignment [Likely]. Short interest rose 10.4% month-over-month [Certain]. Several US shareholder law firms announced (Aug 2026) class-action-lead-plaintiff solicitations alleging Futu understated the scope of a Chinese regulatory penalty while continuing "rectification measures" operations [Guessing] (https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/suewallst-reminds-shareholders-of-a-lead-plaintiff-deadline-of-august-25-2026-in-futu-holdings-limited-lawsuit---futu-302856680.html). No Futu SEC filing found confirms a penalty — the Q2 2026 earnings release makes no mention of one [Certain] (https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1754581/000110465926098911/tm2623384d1_ex99-1.htm). This is plaintiff-firm solicitation, not a company disclosure or court finding, but is a Section 4 monitoring item.
