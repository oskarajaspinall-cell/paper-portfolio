# Macro overlay: Futu Holdings Limited (FUTU) — 2026-10-18
## Assessment
Chain **(inference)**: Fed policy rate/UST yields → net interest income on client margin loans and idle cash → FUTU revenue mix, since online brokers (IBKR, SCHW, HOOD) typically earn a meaningful interest spread on client cash/margin books [Likely]. Secondary: VIX/risk appetite → retail trading activity → commissions. HKD is pegged to USD (DEXHKUS 7.8476, 2026-10-09, +0.0082 3m — negligible [Certain], https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=DEXHKUS), so FX-translation risk on FUTU's HKD earnings is immaterial.

Facts: Fed funds (DFF) 3.88%, 2026-10-15, +0.25pp over 1m/3m — a hiking, not cutting, quarter [Certain]. 10y Treasury (DGS10) 5.10%, +0.53pp 3m; 2y (DGS2) 4.77%, +0.61pp 3m; 10y real yield (DFII10) 2.78%, +0.43pp 3m [Certain] (https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=DFF, id=DGS10, id=DGS2, id=DFII10, all 2026-10-15). VIX 16.46, -0.27pp 3m (calm) [Certain] (id=VIXCLS, 2026-10-15). HY spread 3.23%, +0.52pp 3m (modest widening, still tight) [Certain] (id=BAMLH0A0HYM2, 2026-10-15).

Market pricing (proxy, not fact): 2y yield (4.77%) sits ~0.9pp above fed funds (3.88%) and rose more over 3m (+0.61pp vs +0.25pp actual), implying the market has priced further tightening beyond what has occurred — a dovish surprise (faster cuts) is the bigger tail risk to NII **(inference)**.

Bull: rates stay higher-for-longer + calm VIX → NII and trading volumes support earnings above the deep valuation discount (10.5x P/E vs 23.6x peers, fact sheet) [Likely]. Bear: a risk-off shock (VIX/credit-spread spike) or faster-than-priced Fed cuts cuts NII and dents trading activity, reinforcing the China/HK discount [Likely]. Tilt: small, toward bull — macro is a secondary layer; the thesis is dominated by idiosyncratic valuation and mainland-regulatory/litigation risk (see note), not macro.
