# Krystal Biotech (KRYS) — Core initiation — 2026-11-23
## 1. Business
Single-product commercial gene-therapy company: VYJUVEK, a topical HSV-1 vector gel for dystrophic epidermolysis bullosa (DEB), sold direct in the US, Germany, France and Japan (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1711279/000171127926000071/krys-20260930.htm) [Certain]. Q3 revenue by region: US $95,323k, Europe $23,256k (about 18%), Japan $8,762k (about 7%), of $127,341k total [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1711279/000171127926000071/krys-20260930.htm). No single foreign country is disclosed at 10%+; Europe is a region, not one country.

## 2. Quality
Excellent economics [Certain]: gross margin 94.3% TTM, operating margin 48.4%, FCF margin 66.0%, ROIC 37.5%, net debt/EBITDA -3.94x (net cash) (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/krys/financials/ratios/). The moat is a first-and-only corrective therapy in DEB with in-house HSV-1 vector manufacturing, orphan-type patient scarcity and regulatory exclusivity [Likely]. Weaknesses [Certain]: one product; US nine-month revenue was $274,445k vs $277,145k a year earlier [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1711279/000171127926000071/krys-20260930.htm), so US is flat and growth is ex-US. Direction change: ROIC is rising over five years but fell from 40.4% to 37.5% in 12 months, 3.9pp below its 2-year high; ROCE, operating and FCF margins are rising (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/krys/financials/ratios/?p=trailing) [Certain]. Customer A was 61% of receivables [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1711279/000171127926000071/krys-20260930.htm), a concentration risk [Likely].
