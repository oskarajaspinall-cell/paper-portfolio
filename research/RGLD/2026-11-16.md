# Royal Gold (RGLD) — Core initiation — 2026-11-16
## 1. Business
Royal Gold buys streams and royalties on other companies' mines, paying upfront for a share of output or revenue [Certain]. Streams were about 68% of revenue and gold about 73% in the nine months to 30 Sep 2026 [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/85535/000008553526000051/rgld-20260930.htm). Pueblo Viejo (Dominican Republic) was about 10.5% of nine-month revenue [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/85535/000008553526000051/rgld-20260930.htm). South & Central America was about 23.2% [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/85535/000008553526000051/rgld-20260930.htm).

## 2. Quality
Business model quality is high [Certain]: gross margin 86.6% and operating margin 63.1% TTM, with minimal capex and costs (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/rgld/financials/income-statement/). The moat is contractual: perpetual, mine-life interests that are hard to replicate, with scale and a cheap cost of capital as the barrier in new deals [Likely].

Returns on capital are modest because upfront deposits are capitalised: ROIC 11.5% TTM, down 2.5pp in 12 months from 14.0% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/rgld/financials/ratios/?p=trailing). Operating margin also slipped from 67.5% to 63.1% over 12 months. I read this as Sandstorm/Horizon acquisition capital (late 2025) not yet fully earning, plus a higher-cost, copper/stream mix [Likely]. FCF margin of 66.6% TTM is a rebound from a negative year caused by the acquisition [Certain]. Q3 delivered record cash flow and the company was debt-free after quarter end [Likely] (source: https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20261104900713/en/Royal-Gold-Reports-Strong-Third-Quarter-Results-Including-Record-Operating-Cash-Flow-and-Gold-Equivalent-Ounce-Sales-Additional-Share-Repurchases-and-Full-Repayment-of-Debt-after-Quarter-End/).

## 3. Valuation
P/E is 25.8x, 4% into its 5y range and below the 27.3x peer median; EV/EBITDA 14.9x is 17% into its range and 25% below peers; P/FCF 18.7x is below its 5y minimum; FCF yield 5.36% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/rgld/statistics/). P/B 2.8x is above its 5y maximum, but peers sit at 4.9x [Certain]. The price is therefore a discount to peers and own history on earnings and cash flow [Likely].

Mechanical fair value gives a base of 113.66 and a bull of 302.49 against a close of 256.89, using EV/EBITDA only because the NAV method is [data unavailable]. The base is far below the price because it extrapolates own-history multiples and ignores the gold price; I treat it as unreliable [Guessing]. The price assumes gold stays high and the new mines ramp [Likely]. Trend is healthy: +7.1% above the 200-day average, 12-month change +32.8%, beta 0.46.

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROIC (ratios page, TTM) stays below 10% for two further quarters.
- Operating margin (income statement, TTM) falls below 58%.
- FCF margin (cash flow statement, TTM) falls below 55%.
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) rises above 1.5x, i.e. debt-funded deals again outrun cash flow.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Large deals in 2025 lifted the share count 27.57% YoY (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/rgld/statistics/) [Certain]. Since then the revolver went from $900.0 million at 31 Dec 2025 to fully repaid on 19 Oct 2026, and a $500 million buyback has repurchased $79.4 million in nine months [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/85535/000008553526000051/rgld-20260930.htm). TTM FCF conversion is 138.8% of net income (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/rgld/financials/cash-flow-statement/). Net debt/EBITDA is -0.03x. Capital discipline is improving [Likely].

## 6. Unusual items
- Filing delta: the 10-Q dropped the debt, integration and Hod Maden joint-venture risk factors, consistent with the debt repayment and the July 2026 restructuring that cut the equity stake to 15% in return for a 2.5% royalty [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/85535/000008553526000051/rgld-20260930.htm).
- Hod Maden still carries a $70 million loan and a funding share [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/85535/000008553526000051/rgld-20260930.htm) [Likely].
- Pueblo Viejo exposure is Dominican Republic country risk, at ~10% of revenue [Likely].
- Short interest is 2.60% of float; institutions own 85.2% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/rgld/statistics/).
