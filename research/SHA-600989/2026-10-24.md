# Ningxia Baofeng Energy (SHA:600989) — Core initiation — 2026-10-24
## 1. Business
Coal-based chemicals producer: converts coal into polyethylene, polypropylene and related products, plus coke-chain by-products [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sha/600989/). Profit depends on the spread between olefin prices and coal cost; the coal-to-olefins cost position is the core of the thesis [Likely]. Reports in CNY, listed in Shanghai (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sha/600989/).

## 2. Quality
Margins are high for a commodity chemical: TTM gross margin 40.5%, operating margin 34.7%, net margin 28.1%, ROIC 22.2% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sha/600989/financials/ratios/) [Certain]. But returns are cyclical: ROIC fell from 23.0% (FY2021) to 11.5% (FY2024), then recovered to 17.0% (FY2025) [Certain]. That swing shows the advantage (low-cost coal feedstock, integration) is real but not immune to product price cycles [Likely]. Piotroski F is 8 and Altman Z is 4.31 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sha/600989/statistics/) [Certain]. Net debt/EBITDA improved from 2.67x (FY2024) to 0.90x TTM [Certain]. The durable-moat case is only moderate: capacity builders elsewhere can compress spreads [Guessing]. Insider ownership is 35.09%, aligned with minorities [Certain].

## 3. Valuation
P/E is 9.2x against a 5y range of 12.6x–19.5x (below the minimum) and a peer median of 12.8x; EV/EBITDA is 7.2x against 9.9x–14.3x and peers at 8.3x; P/FCF is 11.5x, FCF yield 8.66% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sha/600989/statistics/) [Certain]. The price therefore assumes earnings are near a cyclical peak and will fall: TTM margins are the highest in five years [Certain]. The fact sheet's base fair value is 17.79 CNY vs a 21.33 close (-16.6%); bear 12.38, bull 44.02 (source: fact sheet, https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sha/600989/financials/ratios/) [Certain]. Only one method (EV/EBITDA) produced a value, since the through-cycle DCF failed on negative FCF history [Certain]. Cheap on trailing multiples, but not below base fair value, so there is no margin of safety on the mechanical model [Likely].

## 4. What would prove this wrong

- ROIC (ratios page) falls below 12% on a TTM basis.
- FCF conversion (FCF/NI, cash-flow page) falls below 40% TTM.
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) rises above 2.0x.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
FY2022–FY2024 FCF was negative (FCF margin -11.8%, -14.2%, -8.0%) as capex built new capacity, then turned to 21.3% in FY2025 and 22.5% TTM; FCF conversion is 80.4% TTM (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sha/600989/financials/cash-flow-statement/) [Certain]. Share count fell 1.28% YoY [Certain]. Whether growth capex restarts is the key question [Guessing]. The capex phase returned only about 12% ROIC at the trough [Certain].
