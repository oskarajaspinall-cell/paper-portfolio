# Orion Oyj (HEL:ORNBV) — Core initiation — 2026-11-15
## 1. Business
Finnish pharma. It earns from branded and generic medicines, a Finnish-centred consumer/animal health franchise, and increasingly from Innovative Medicines: royalties and tablet supply to Bayer for prostate-cancer drug Nubeqa (darolutamide) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hel/ORNBV/transcripts/539634-q3-2026/). Royalties drove Q3 profit, and the FY26 guidance raise rests mainly on Nubeqa [Likely]. Regional revenue split: [data unavailable]; the call gave no split and no single foreign country is confirmed at ~10%+.

## 2. Quality
Returns are exceptional and rising: TTM ROIC 49.9% vs 32.1% a year earlier, operating margin 38.1% vs 26.7%, gross margin 67.4% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hel/ORNBV/financials/ratios/?p=trailing). Net debt/EBITDA is -0.02x, Altman Z 13.15, Piotroski 7 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hel/ORNBV/statistics/) [Certain].
Moat: Nubeqa is patent-protected and sold by Bayer, so Orion carries no commercial cost on it [Likely]. That makes the margin uplift high-quality but concentrated and not Orion-controlled. Management says the royalty rate peaked in Q2, so royalties now track Bayer's market sales [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hel/ORNBV/transcripts/539634-q3-2026/). FCF conversion is weak: 62.7% of net income TTM, 48.7% in FY2025. FCF margin is falling over five fiscal years (-3.3pp) but rising over the last 12 months (15.3% to 19.0%) [Certain]. Cash lags profit, partly investment in capacity [Guessing].

## 3. Valuation
P/E 22.0x sits at 48% of its 5y range (17.9x–26.4x) and +5% vs peers (21.0x). EV/EBITDA 16.2x is +65% vs peers. P/B 10.3x and EV/Sales 6.6x are above their 5y maxima. P/FCF 35.0x vs a 30.3x median; FCF yield 2.86% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hel/ORNBV/statistics/) [Certain].
The mechanical fair value is base 51.76 EUR (bear 21.06, bull 112.69) against a 103.00 EUR close, so the price sits near the bull case. The reverse DCF implies 24.6% year-1 revenue growth [Certain]. The market is pricing Nubeqa royalties as durable and growing, plus the DASL-HiCaP label expansion. The stock is up 72.0% in 12 months with RSI 81.5, so little is left for error [Likely].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Operating margin (income statement, TTM) falls below 30%.
- ROIC (ratios page, TTM) falls below 30%.
- FCF margin (cash flow statement, TTM) falls below 12%.
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) rises above 1.5x, e.g. through debt-funded M&A.
