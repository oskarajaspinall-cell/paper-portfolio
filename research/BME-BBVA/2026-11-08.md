Reuters describes Mexico as its main market, alongside Spain, with Turkey and South America also contributing [Likely] (source: https://www.reuters.com/business/finance/bbvas-q3-net-profit-rises-19-thanks-mexico-2026-10-29/). ## 2. Quality
Profitability is high and rising: ROE rose from 10.8% (FY2021) to 18.3% (FY2025) and 19.8% TTM, and net margin from 25.0% to 33.2% (FY2025) and 32.6% TTM [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/bme/BBVA/financials/ratios/). ROIC, ROCE and operating margin are [data unavailable], as is usual for banks. The moat is scale and low-cost deposits in Spain and Mexico, plus a digital-first franchise [Likely]. Q3 results showed profit growth led by Mexican and Spanish lending, and full-year ROTE guidance was raised [Likely] (source: https://www.reuters.com/business/finance/bbvas-q3-net-profit-rises-19-thanks-mexico-2026-10-29/). FCF margin is volatile (FY2024 -61.4%, TTM 50.6%) because bank cash flow is dominated by balance-sheet movements, so it is not a quality signal [Likely]. The share count is falling (-3.24% YoY), consistent with buybacks [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/bme/BBVA/statistics/). Emerging-market earnings (Mexico, Turkey) carry currency and policy risk [Likely].

## 3. Valuation
At 22.73 EUR, P/E is 11.3x, above the top of its 5y range (5.5x–11.3x, median 6.3x), and P/B is 2.0x against a 5y range of 0.7x–1.8x (median 0.9x) [Certain]. Versus peers it trades at a 6% premium on P/E (peer median 10.7x) and 41% on P/B (1.4x) [Certain]. The re-rating has already happened; a high ROE justifies some premium. The base fair value is 25.04 EUR (+10.2%), bear 15.99 (-29.7%) and bull 31.23 (+37.4%) [Likely]. The best method (P/B + ROE) gives a base of 19.47, below the price, while the FCFE DCF gives 36.17, so the upside rests on cash-flow assumptions that are noisy for a bank [Guessing]. Skew is roughly balanced: modest upside with a deeper bear case. The stock is 4.7% below its 50-day average and 5.7% above its 200-day (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/bme/BBVA/history/).

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROE (ratios page) falls below 15% on a TTM basis.
- Net margin (income statement page) falls below 28% on a TTM basis.
- Shares outstanding (statistics page) stop shrinking, with YoY change above 0%, which would signal the buyback has ended or capital is being raised.
- Quarterly net profit (income statement page) declines YoY for two consecutive quarters.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
The second tranche of the share buyback is under way [Likely] (source: https://www.nasdaq.com/articles/banco-bilbao-vizcaya-argentaria-sa-purchases-23968633851-euros-shares-second-tranche). The share count fell 3.24% YoY. FCF/NI was 155.0% TTM but swung between -192.8% and 344.5% over FY2021–FY2025, so cash conversion is not a reliable measure for a bank [Certain].
