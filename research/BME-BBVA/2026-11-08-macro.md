**Chain:** peso -> FX translation -> reported EUR profit; risk-off -> equity risk premium -> P/B multiple.

- USD per EUR is 1.1163, down 0.018 in ~1m (https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=DEXUSEU).

**Pricing:** Part of the peso slide and the VIX spike came after or around the Q3 print [Likely]. At 2.0x book versus a 0.9x 5y median, the stock has little cushion if the risk premium rises [Certain, fact sheet].

**Bear:** a further 5-10% peso fall trims EUR earnings growth by a few points, and a higher risk premium compresses the P/B rerating.
