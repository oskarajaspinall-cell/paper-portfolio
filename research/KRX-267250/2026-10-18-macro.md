# Macro overlay: HD Hyundai Co., Ltd. (267250) — 2026-10-18
## Assessment
HD Hyundai's two main segments have offsetting dollar exposure: shipbuilding earns USD-denominated export contracts (won-weakness tailwind on KRW-translated revenue) while HD Hyundai Oilbank imports dollar-priced crude (won-weakness cost headwind to refining margins) [Likely] **(inference)** — the thesis (deep discount to peers on P/E 4.1x, EV/EBITDA 2.8x vs peer medians) is a fundamentals/valuation call, not a macro one.

Facts: the broad trade-weighted dollar index (DTWEXBGS) rose to 121.602 on 2026-10-09, +3.72 (~3.1%) in ~1 month and +1.1 in ~3 months [Certain, https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=DTWEXBGS, 2026-10-09]; effective fed funds rose to 3.88% on 2026-10-15, +0.25pp in both windows (a hike) [Certain, https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=DFF]; the HY credit spread widened to 3.23%, +0.47pp/+0.52pp [Certain, https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=BAMLH0A0HYM2]. Market pricing (proxy, **(inference)**): the 2y Treasury (4.77%, 2026-10-15) sits ~89bp above effective fed funds, consistent with the market pricing further tightening/inflation risk, not cuts [Likely]. KRW/USD itself is not in the tracked FRED set, so won direction is inferred from the broad dollar index, not confirmed directly [Guessing].

**macro_bull**: further dollar strength + won weakness lifts won-translated shipbuilding export revenue faster than refining input costs rise. **macro_bear**: credit-spread widening plus a stronger dollar raises imported-crude costs faster than orderbook translation gains, pressuring the already-recovering refining margin. **Tilt**: small, because the two channels largely net off and neither is confirmed by a Korea-specific official series.
