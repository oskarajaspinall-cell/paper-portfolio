# HD Hyundai Co., Ltd. (KRX:267250) — Core initiation — 2026-10-18
## 1. Business
HD Hyundai is the listed holding company atop the HD Hyundai group, consolidating controlling stakes in Korean shipbuilders and HD Hyundai Oilbank's refining/petrochemicals business, plus construction and electrical-equipment makers; stockanalysis.com classifies it under Oil & Gas Refining & Marketing (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/krx/267250/) [Likely]. It earns through consolidated subsidiary profit rather than operating assets of its own [Likely].

## 2. Quality
Group returns have risen sharply: ROIC 1.5%→13.1% FY2021-FY2025, TTM 21.5%; ROE TTM 24.1%; operating margin from a 3.3% FY2023 trough to 13.5% TTM [Certain]. Net debt/EBITDA fell from 6.59x (FY2021) to 0.14x TTM, and the Piotroski F-Score is 8 [Certain]. Set against this, Altman Z-Score is only 1.63 — in the traditional distress zone (<1.8) — a reminder the group still carries heavy, capital-intensive shipbuilding and refining balance sheets even as leverage falls [Certain]. As a diversified chaebol holding company, moat quality is uneven across subsidiaries: shipbuilding (orderbook scale, LNG/dual-fuel specialisation) has real barriers to entry, while refining and construction/electrical equipment are commodity-cyclical with little pricing power [Likely]. The TTM margin surge most likely reflects a cyclical upswing rather than a structural shift, so returns could revert once the order or crack-spread cycle turns [Likely].

## 3. Valuation
At the last close the stock trades on P/E 4.1x, EV/EBITDA 2.8x, EV/Sales 0.5x and P/FCF 1.7x — 60–86% below the four-peer median (KRX:000150, KRX:000880, KRX:034730, KRX:028260) and at or below its own 5-year range low on every multiple except P/B (0.3x, near peers, 55th percentile of its own range) [Certain]. The market is pricing either a durable derating of the holdco structure, or deep skepticism that TTM returns (ROIC 21.5%, net margin 3.5%) hold up [Likely]. The model's own fair-value range is unusually wide — base +892% and bull +3,029% above the 157,400 KRW close, while the bear case is negative — a sign the mechanical through-cycle DCF breaks down for this capital-intensive, cyclical holdco and should be read as directional only, never a trading level [Certain]. Even allowing a large margin of error, the discount to peers looks wider than cyclicality alone justifies [Likely].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) rises back above 2x, undoing the FY2021–FY2025 deleveraging
- TTM operating margin (income statement) falls back below its FY2021–FY2023 average of roughly 4%, confirming the current level was a cyclical peak, not a new baseline
- ROIC (ratios page) falls back under 8%, its FY2022 level, for two consecutive reported quarters
- Piotroski F-Score (statistics page) drops below 6, signalling broad fundamental deterioration

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
FCF margin TTM is 8.4%, recovered from -6.9% in FY2021, though FCF/net-income conversion has swung widely (1,323% FY2021, 12% FY2022, 1,137% FY2024, 243% TTM), consistent with lumpy working capital and subsidiary capex/dividend timing in a shipbuilding-refining group [Certain]. Net debt/EBITDA of 0.14x TTM shows balance-sheet capacity has been rebuilt rather than spent on buybacks or renewed leverage [Certain].
