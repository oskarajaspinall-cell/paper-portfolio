# FedEx Corporation (FDX) — Core initiation — 2026-10-09

## 1. Business
FedEx runs an integrated global air-and-ground network for express/priority and ground parcel delivery plus logistics, billing by weight, distance and speed tier; express shipments carry the highest yield [Likely]. Following the FY2026 spin-off of FedEx Freight (LTL trucking) into a standalone company [Certain] (source: AGM transcript, https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/fdx/transcripts/736080-agm-2026/), FedEx now centers on Express, Ground and Services, consolidated under the Network 2.0 integration.

## 2. Quality
Operating margin expanded from 6.2% (FY22) to 7.9% TTM and ROIC from 8.3% to 9.2% TTM, both on a rising 5y trend [Certain] (source: factsheet, ratios/income-statement pages). Network 2.0 — merging previously separate Express and Ground networks — is ~62% complete and management frames it as structural cost-out (DRIVE program), not one-off [Likely] (source: Citi TMT conference transcript, https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/fdx/transcripts/741918-citi-s-2026-global-tmt-conference/). FCF conversion jumped to 115.4% TTM from 72.8% in FY2025 [Certain] (factsheet), but this coincides with capex falling to the lowest level in company history as a % of revenue [Certain] (AGM transcript) — part of the jump is capex timing, not pure efficiency [Likely]. Net debt/EBITDA fell from 3.10x to 2.48x [Certain]. The moat is network density and air-ground integration, which is costly to replicate, but the industry remains competitive (UPS, large retailers' own logistics) and margins, while improving, are still below best-in-class peers [Likely].

## 3. Valuation
FDX trades at a steep discount to the peer median on every multiple (P/E 15.7x vs 29.7x, EV/EBITDA 8.3x vs 15.9x, EV/Sales 1.0x vs 1.5x) and near the bottom of its own 5y range (19-31st percentile); P/FCF (13.4x) is below its own 5y low (17.5x) [Certain] (factsheet). But the factsheet's blended DCF/EV-EBITDA fair value sits below the last close even in the bull case: base $259.21 (-11.1%), bull $272.70 (-6.5%) vs close $291.73 [Certain] (factsheet). Conflict: peer multiples say cheap, own-history DCF says rich. Three of four peers (XPO, CHRW, JBHT) are asset-light brokers that structurally trade on higher multiples than an asset-heavy carrier, likely inflating the peer median [Likely]; the reverse-DCF reading (4.7% year-1 growth fading to terminal) better fits FedEx's trajectory and should carry more weight. Net: not demonstrably cheap against FedEx's own cash-flow history.

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Operating margin (income statement page) falls back below 7.0% for two consecutive quarters, signalling the Network 2.0 cost-out has stalled.
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) rises back above 2.9x, reversing the deleveraging trend.
- FCF margin (cash flow/income statement pages) falls back below 4%, showing the FY2026 jump to 5.4% was capex timing rather than structural.
- ROIC (ratios page) falls back below 9.0%, reversing the 5y improving trend.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
FY2026 capex fell to the lowest level in company history as a % of revenue, supporting record adjusted FCF; FedEx returned about $2.2bn to shareholders via buybacks and dividends [Certain] (AGM transcript). A quarterly dividend of $1.22/share was declared in August 2026 [Certain] (factsheet news). FCF conversion (FCF/NI) was 115.4% TTM vs 72.8% in FY2025 [Certain] (factsheet) — a sharp step-up that should be monitored given it coincides with reduced capex, not solely efficiency. Shares outstanding fell 1.65% YoY, consistent with continued buybacks [Certain] (factsheet).

## 6. Unusual items
FedEx completed the spin-off of FedEx Freight (LTL trucking) in FY2026 [Certain] (AGM transcript) — this breaks clean comparability of the pre-FY2026 margin/ROIC trend data in the fact sheet, which may blend the divested segment into FY2022–25 figures. Three shareholder proposals (independent board chair, lower special-meeting threshold, a report on abortion-drug distribution risk) were all rejected at the September 2026 AGM — no governance red flag [Certain] (AGM transcript). A FedEx/Advent-led consortium tendered for over 89% of Polish parcel-locker operator InPost in September 2026 per the factsheet's headline feed [Likely] (source: https://www.reuters.com/business/fedex-advent-led-consortium-secures-over-89-inpost-shares-takeover-offer-2026-09-18/, listed in factsheet; the article itself could not be fetched to verify deal terms). Short interest is low and falling (2.21% of float, -3.8% month-on-month) — no crowded short [Certain] (factsheet).
