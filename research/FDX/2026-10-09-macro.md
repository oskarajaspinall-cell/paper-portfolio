# Macro overlay: FedEx Corporation (FDX) — 2026-10-09
## Assessment
FDX is a global, leveraged (net debt/EBITDA 2.48x), capital-intensive carrier with meaningful international Express revenue, so two variables matter: the dollar and the long-end real yield. **Chain**: dollar strength → international revenue translation drag + softer cross-border demand; rising real yields → higher DCF discount rate → lower fair value, reinforcing that FDX's own-history DCF (base $259, bull $273) already sits below the $291.73 close [Certain] (factsheet).

**Official data** [Certain] (fred.stlouisfed.org, 2026-10-02/07): the broad dollar index rose +2.86 in the past month to 121.385 (vs +0.70 over 3m — most of the move is recent); the 10y real yield (DFII10) rose +0.61pp over 3m to 2.92%; the 10y nominal yield rose +0.72pp to 5.28%; HY spreads widened +0.39pp to 3.09% (still a low absolute level). **Market pricing (proxy, inference)**: the 2y Treasury yield (4.77%) sits ~0.89pp above effective fed funds (3.88%) [Likely], implying the market isn't pricing near-term cuts and may price further tightening — a "higher-for-longer" backdrop for a leveraged cyclical. This is a recent change, not just a level.

**Bull**: dollar stabilizes/weakens and real yields plateau → translation drag fades, discount rate eases, narrowing the fair-value gap. **Bear**: dollar extends its 1-month surge and real yields keep rising → further FX drag on international Express plus a lower DCF fair value, widening the already-negative base-case gap. **Tilt**: toward bear, small — real but modest relative to the -11% base-case overvaluation already identified on fundamentals alone **(inference)**.
