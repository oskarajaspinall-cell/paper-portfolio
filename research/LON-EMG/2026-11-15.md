# Man Group (LON:EMG) — Core initiation — 2026-11-15
## 1. Business
Listed active asset manager (systematic and discretionary hedge-fund strategies, plus a growing private-credit offer). Revenue is management fees on AUM plus performance fees, so earnings are geared to AUM, flows and fund returns. Reports in USD, quoted in GBX (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/lon/EMG/). Regional revenue split: [data unavailable] (no allowlisted primary source fetched). [Certain]

## 2. Quality
Returns are high but volatile, not steady. ROIC TTM 21.3% vs 13.1% in FY2025 and 46.0% in FY2022; operating margin 27.3% TTM vs 20.1% FY2025 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/lon/EMG/financials/ratios/). The five-year trend is falling, but the last 12 months are rising (ROIC +8.0pp, operating margin +7.7pp, FCF margin +6.9pp), driven by performance fees and AUM gains. [Likely] This is cyclical fee income, not a proven step-change. Net cash (net debt/EBITDA -0.02x), Piotroski F 8, shares down 1.67% YoY, FCF conversion 158.2% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/lon/EMG/statistics/). Moat: track record, systematic-research scale and distribution, which is real but eroded by fee competition and performance dependence. [Likely] Record AUM was reported on 16 Oct 2026 and Meiji Yasuda expanded its commitment to the private-credit strategy with a possible stake (source: https://www.reuters.com/world/asia-pacific/uks-man-group-deepens-meiji-yasuda-tie-up-with-128-bln-commitment-potential-5-2026-10-26/). [Likely] Beta 0.55.

## 3. Valuation
The price assumes the current earnings peak holds. P/E 14.9x sits at 67% of its 5y range (median 10.4x) and is 15% above peers; EV/EBITDA 9.1x vs 8.1x median; P/B 2.9x is above its 5y maximum of 2.5x (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/lon/EMG/financials/ratios/). The offset is P/FCF 9.0x (41% of range) and FCF yield 11.11%, which is flattered by 158.2% conversion that is unlikely to persist. [Likely] Mechanical fair value base 265.54 GBX vs close 314.80 GBX (-15.6%), bear 140.61, bull 456.14; the reverse DCF implies 1.4% year-1 revenue growth, a low bar if AUM growth continues (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/lon/EMG/). Price is +8.0% above the 200-day average, 12-month change +51.6%. Fairly to fully priced on earnings, not cheap. [Likely]

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Operating margin (income statement, TTM) falls below 20%.
- ROIC TTM (ratios page, trailing) falls below 13%.
- Net debt / EBITDA (statistics page) rises above 1.5x, i.e. the net cash position is lost to debt-funded balance sheet use.
- Shares outstanding (statistics page, YoY change) turn to dilution above +3%.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Net cash, a falling share count (-1.67% YoY) and FCF conversion of 158.2% TTM (211.4% in FY2024, 126.3% in FY2025) point to cash returned rather than consumed. [Certain] Timing of performance-fee cash makes conversion lumpy. Dividend and buyback detail: [data unavailable] in the fact sheet. Meiji Yasuda's capital commitment is to the strategy, which supports growth with limited balance sheet use. [Guessing]
