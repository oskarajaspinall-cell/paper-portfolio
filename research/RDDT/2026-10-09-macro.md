# Macro overlay: Reddit, Inc. (RDDT) — 2026-10-09
## Assessment
RDDT is USD-reporting, net-cash, no debt, domestic ad/licensing revenue — credit spreads and FX are not transmission channels [Certain]. The one live channel: real yields → discount rate → multiple. RDDT trades on 36.5x P/E / 34.1x EV/EBITDA (34-43% above peer median) with a 2.05 beta, more sensitive than peers to a rising cost of equity **(inference)** (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/rddt/statistics/).

**Facts**: 10y real yield (DFII10) 2.92% on 2026-10-07, +0.49pp ~1m, +0.61pp ~3m; fed funds (DFF) 3.88%, +0.26pp ~3m; 10y breakeven (T10YIE) 2.35%, roughly flat ~1m, +0.12pp ~3m; VIX 15.08, falling (FRED, DFII10/DFF/T10YIE/VIXCLS, 2026-10-07/08).

**Market pricing (proxy, inference)**: 10y yield rose more than the 2y over 3m (+0.72pp vs +0.56pp) and fed funds itself rose, consistent with a hawkish "higher-for-longer" repricing, not a cooling-inflation story, since breakevens are nearly flat — a real-rate move, not an inflation surprise.

RDDT fell 19.9% in 3 months, more than the ~0.6pp real-yield rise alone explains — stock-specific news (Google licensing-renewal doubt, Anthropic litigation) likely dominates [Likely] **(inference)**. Further real-yield rises add incremental compression on top of those catalysts; a plateau/retreat removes a headwind and aids re-rating toward peers.

**Bull**: real yields stabilize/fall → ERP eases → premium multiple partly re-rates, aided by the FY2025 margin inflection. **Bear**: real yields keep rising → discount rate keeps climbing → further compression even if margins hold. **Tilt**: moderate toward bear — rates still rose in the latest month and RDDT's beta/premium multiple amplifies that vs peers, though stock-specific catalysts remain the bigger swing factor.
