# Reddit, Inc. (RDDT) — Core initiation — 2026-11-02
## 1. Business
Reddit monetises community-generated content, mainly through advertising on conversation pages and feeds, plus content licensing to AI and search companies. Revenue was $895.0m in Q3, up 53% [company-reported] (https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1713445/000171344526000116/rddt-20260930.htm). Search-referred visitors are largely logged-out and are monetised mainly through conversation-page and feed ads [company-reported] (same filing). [Certain]

## 2. Quality
Financials are excellent: gross margin 91.5%, operating margin 29.4%, TTM FCF margin 38.8%, net cash (net debt/EBITDA -3.25x), FCF/NI 120.8% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/rddt/financials/ratios/). [Certain]

The moat is the weaker part, and the sell-off questions it. Q3 ARPU grew 36% on ad pricing up about 34%, but global DAUq was flat sequentially, with US DAUq down 5% sequentially and 2% year on year; growth came from rest of world, which monetises at far lower ARPU [company-reported] (same filing). Management says search-engine changes, including AI summaries, make referral traffic volatile. New Risk Factors name Google, Meta, OpenAI and Anthropic as rivals for attention, and warn that licensing deals may not be renewed [company-reported] (same filing). [Certain] Dependence on Google traffic looks structural, not a one-quarter wobble. [Likely]

Headlines attribute the plunge to weak guidance and lower search traffic (https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/30/reddit-stock-q3-earnings.html). [Likely]

## 3. Valuation
At 91.73, P/E is 19.3x against a peer median of 21.1x, EV/EBITDA 15.9x against 13.8x, P/FCF 14.8x against 10.8x, and FCF yield 6.76% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/rddt/statistics/). [Certain] The price now assumes growth slows sharply, not that the business is impaired. The fact-sheet fair value (base 316.29, EV/Revenue method only; DCF unavailable) rests on pre-reset revenue growth and is not reliable here. The 5y multiple range is IPO-era and uninformative. [Guessing] The multiples are undemanding only if the US user base stabilises. With beta 2.05 and RSI 19.2, momentum is washed out, and short interest of 15.28% of float is up 8.8% on the month. Cheap against history, but not clearly cheap for a business whose traffic source is being disintermediated. [Likely]

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- US DAUq (10-Q MD&A) declines year on year for a second consecutive quarter, or the logged-in-only experiment lowers global DAUq year on year.
- Revenue growth (income statement page) falls below 25% year on year in any quarter.
- Operating margin (income statement page) falls below 20% on a TTM basis.
- Disclosure of a non-renewed or materially repriced content-licensing deal (10-Q or 8-K).

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Cash conversion is strong (FCF/NI 120.8% TTM) and the company has net cash. Share count rose 3.49% year on year, so stock compensation dilutes owners (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/rddt/statistics/). [Certain] The 10-Q says marketing spend on user acquisition drove user growth and may be cut or redirected, so some DAUq growth is paid for [company-reported] (https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1713445/000171344526000116/rddt-20260930.htm). It also flags a possible US deferred-tax valuation allowance release within 12 months, which would inflate reported net income without cash [company-reported] (same filing). [Likely]

## 6. Unusual items
- Plaintiff-firm "fraud investigation" solicitation after the fall (https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20261030543777/en/RDDT-Investors-Have-Opportunity-to-Join-Reddit-Inc.-Fraud-Investigation-with-SBS-Law/); solicitation only, not a filed case. [Likely]
- Reddit is moving to a logged-in-only experience (https://www.businessinsider.com/reddits-logged-in-google-ai-overviews-traffic-2026-10); this may cut logged-out traffic that supplies much of the ad inventory. [Likely]
- Conflict: the caller's brief quotes a different base fair value from the fact sheet's 316.29. Neither is a decision input.
- Verdict from this note: the fall is not an overreaction to a one-off. It reflects a structural question about the traffic source, and the obstacle behind the earlier AVOID has not been removed. Lean AVOID. [Likely]
