# Reddit, Inc. (RDDT) — Core initiation — 2026-10-09
## 1. Business
Reddit runs topic-based discussion communities (subreddits) built on user-generated content, monetizing in two ways: (1) advertising (display/CPM and conversion formats targeted on first-party interest signals) and (2) data licensing — selling API/content access to AI developers for model training [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/rddt/). The model is asset-light: 91%+ gross margin [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/rddt/financials/income-statement/).

## 2. Quality
FY2025 was an inflection year: operating margin flipped from -43.1% (FY2024) to +20.1% (FY2025), reaching 28.2% TTM; net margin 31.3% TTM; FCF margin 36.7% TTM; ROE 30.7% TTM; ROCE 23.8% TTM — all rising sharply [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/rddt/financials/ratios/). Gross margin is structurally high (91.4% TTM, +6.1pp over 5y) [Certain], showing strong operating leverage as revenue scales. The moat is network effects — breadth/depth of niche communities and a long content archive that is hard to replicate — now being monetized directly via data-licensing deals, a second revenue stream peers Meta/Snap/Pinterest largely lack [Likely]. Reddit ending free RSS feeds/API access to block AI-bot scraping (TechCrunch, 2026-09-30) supports pricing power over that licensed-data moat [Likely] (source: https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/30/reddit-is-killing-rss-feeds-ending-public-api-access-because-of-ai-bots/). Key risk: one licensing counterparty (reportedly Google) may not renew on current terms (see Unusual items), and the core ad business still competes against much larger-scale peers.

## 3. Valuation
At 156.47, RDDT trades on 36.5x P/E and 34.1x EV/EBITDA, 34-43% above the peer median (META/PINS/SNAP/GOOGL: 27.2x / 23.9x) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/rddt/statistics/), pricing in continued above-peer growth and margin expansion. EV/Sales (9.8x) and P/FCF (29.6x) sit far below Reddit's own 5-year range (EV/Sales 18.8-20.7x; P/FCF 63.7-133x), but this is mostly mechanical — revenue and FCF grew off a much smaller/negative base, not a cheaper business [Likely]. P/FCF is in line with peers (29.6x vs 29.4x) despite faster growth, more genuinely supportive. The fact sheet's model fair value (EV/Revenue-based; DCF unavailable, FCF history too short) shows a base case of 284.26 vs the 156.47 close (+82%), but rests on one method with no DCF cross-check, so it should carry little weight (source: fact sheet fair-value block). Net: the price assumes Reddit sustains ~30%+ margins and keeps outgrowing larger peers — demanding but not unreasonable given the FY2025 inflection.

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Operating margin (income-statement page) falls back below 15%, reversing the FY2025 inflection
- Gross margin (income-statement page) falls below 85%
- FCF margin (cash-flow/income-statement pages) turns negative again
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) rises back above 3x, ending the current net-cash position

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Reddit holds net cash (net debt/EBITDA -3.45x TTM) and converts earnings to cash well (FCF/NI 116.9% TTM), though that ratio has been volatile (-44.6% FY2024, 129.2% FY2025) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/rddt/financials/cash-flow-statement/). There is no dividend; the main drag is dilution — shares outstanding rose 8.43% YoY [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/rddt/statistics/), consistent with heavy stock-based compensation typical of a 2024 IPO. Investors should watch this converge toward peer-level dilution as the business matures.

## 6. Unusual items
Short interest is elevated and rising: 14.11% of float, up 17.6% month-on-month [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/rddt/statistics/) — a crowded short that can amplify moves either way. Beta is high (2.05) [Certain]. A sell-side note reported via headline (not used as a rating/target input) flagged a lower probability that Reddit's data-licensing arrangement with Google renews on current terms (TheFly/TipRanks, 2026-09-15) [Guessing] — Google is a plausible large licensing counterparty and non-renewal would hit the newer, higher-margin revenue stream (source: https://www.tipranks.com/news/the-fly/wells-fargo-sees-lower-probability-of-successful-reddit-google-ai-deal-renewal-thefly-news). Reddit won a contract-claim ruling against Anthropic in its AI-scraping litigation (Barron's, 2026-09-21) [Likely], modestly supportive of licensing leverage (source: https://www.barrons.com/articles/reddit-stock-anthropic-case-ai-e821d5e0).
