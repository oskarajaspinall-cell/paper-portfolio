# Reddit, Inc. (RDDT) — Core initiation — 2026-10-31
## 1. Business
Reddit monetises community forums mainly through advertising (conversation-page and feed ads), plus a smaller content-licensing line selling access to its text to AI companies [Certain] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1713445/000171344526000116/rddt-20260930.htm). Revenue depends on daily visitors, a large share of whom arrive logged-out from search engines, and on ad pricing [Certain].

## 2. Quality
Why it fell: the 10-Q filed 2026-10-30 adds risk factors on falling search-engine referral traffic, AI summaries and agents substituting for visits, scraping that undermines licensing, and an upcoming logged-in-only experiment (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1713445/000171344526000116/rddt-20260930.htm) [Certain]. Headlines tie the plunge to weak guidance and "down meaningfully" Q3 search traffic (source: https://deadline.com/2026/10/reddit-shares-plunge-q3-search-traffic-earnings-1237209538/) [Likely].
Reported: global DAUq +12% year on year but flat quarter on quarter, US DAUq -5% quarter on quarter [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1713445/000171344526000116/rddt-20260930.htm). Ad price rose about 34% [company-reported] (same URL), so growth is price-led, not audience-led [Likely].
Financials are excellent: gross margin 91.5%, operating margin 29.4%, FCF margin 38.8%, net cash (net debt/EBITDA -3.25x) (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/rddt/financials/ratios/). The moat (unique human discussion) is real, but its distribution is controlled by Google and AI answer engines [Likely]. Durability of returns is uncertain [Likely].

## 3. Valuation
P/E 18.7x versus peer median 43.5x; EV/EBITDA 15.9x versus 25.2x; P/FCF 14.8x versus 12.8x; FCF yield 6.76% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/rddt/statistics/). Five-year history (fiscal-year-end, from IPO-era loss years) shows 14.8x P/FCF far below its range, but that range is not a usable anchor [Likely]. The fact-sheet fair value (base 316.29, EV/Revenue only; DCF returned nothing) rests on those inflated sales multiples, so Conflict: it implies +244.8% upside while the market prices a business whose audience may erode. I give it no weight [Likely]. The price assumes either flat-to-declining earnings or a sharp traffic loss; it is cheap on trailing profits, which were earned before the traffic shift is visible in numbers [Guessing].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- US DAUq (10-Q, MD&A) declines more than 5% quarter on quarter again in either of the next two quarters.
- Revenue growth (income statement page) falls below 25% year on year in any quarter of the next four.
- Operating margin (statistics page) falls below 20% on a TTM basis.
- Content-licensing deals not renewed or disclosed as lost (10-Q Risk Factors / MD&A).

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
FCF conversion is 120.8% of net income and the balance sheet is net cash (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/rddt/financials/cash-flow-statement/). Shares outstanding rose 3.49% year on year, so stock compensation dilutes [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/rddt/statistics/). The 10-Q says a US tax valuation allowance release is possible within 12 months, a non-cash gain that will flatter net income [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1713445/000171344526000116/rddt-20260930.htm).

## 6. Unusual items
Short interest 15.36% of float, up 8.8% on the month; beta 2.05; 14-day RSI 17.5, so very oversold (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/rddt/statistics/) [Certain]. Law-firm "fraud investigation" solicitations were published on 2026-10-30; these are promotional, not findings [Likely]. A logged-in-only change could cut traffic near term before it lifts monetisation [Guessing]. Verdict lean: AVOID; the thesis (durable audience) is under live challenge and the price gives no evidence it has stabilised [Likely].
