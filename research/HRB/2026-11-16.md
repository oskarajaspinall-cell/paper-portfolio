# H&R Block (HRB) — Core initiation — 2026-11-16
## 1. Business
H&R Block earns fees from assisted tax preparation (company-owned and franchised offices), do-it-yourself tax software, and adjacent products: tax protection, identity services, refund-related loans and small-business services. Revenue is highly seasonal and concentrated in the January–April filing season, so the September quarter is a loss quarter. It operates mainly in the U.S., with smaller Canada and Australia operations. No foreign country was verified at ~10% of revenue [Likely]. (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/hrb/)

## 2. Quality
Returns on capital are very high and rising: ROIC TTM 48.4% vs 47.8% in FY2022, ROCE 47.0% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/hrb/financials/ratios/) [Certain]. Operating margin is steady at 22.3% TTM against 21.5% in FY2022; gross margin has drifted down 1.3pp to 43.7% [Certain]. Net debt/EBITDA is 1.69x TTM, up from 1.11x at FY2026 year-end, which is seasonal [Likely]. The durable edge is brand, a large human-assisted client base and the trust placed in the preparer in a regulated, repeat-use service [Likely]; the threat is DIY and free-filing substitution, and the filed Q1 text shows rising spend on retention and technology [Likely] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/12659/000001265926000036/hrb-20260930.htm). Direction change: FCF margin is falling over 5 fiscal years (-2.4pp) but rose over the last 12 months, from 15.3% to 18.8%. The recovery is real but not yet back to the 21.6% of FY2022 [Certain]. Beta is only 0.41 [Certain].

## 3. Valuation
P/E is 8.9x vs a 5y median of 10.2x (37% of its range); EV/EBITDA is 7.7x vs 7.9x (53%); P/FCF is 8.1x vs 7.5x (29%). FCF yield is 12.39% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/hrb/statistics/) [Certain]. Peers (INTU, PAYX, ADP) trade at a 61% P/E discount, but they are software and payroll franchises with recurring revenue, so the gap is partly deserved [Likely]. The reverse DCF implies year-1 revenue shrinking 11.6%, which is far from the Q1 results, where revenue rose and FY2027 guidance was reaffirmed [Likely] (source: https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/11/09/3398946/0/en/h-r-block-reports-fiscal-2027-first-quarter-results-and-reaffirms-fiscal-2027-outlook.html). Fair value is flagged unreliable (methods disagree 2.2x): DCF base 107.86 vs EV/EBITDA 49.15 against a 50.01 close, so I weigh it lightly. The price is above its 200-day average (+25.7%) with a 3-month fall of 6.2% [Certain]. Price assumes a flat-to-shrinking franchise [Likely].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROIC TTM (ratios page, trailing) falls below 35%.
- Operating margin TTM (income statement, trailing) falls below 19%.
- FCF margin TTM (cash-flow statement, trailing) falls below 14%.
- Filed 10-K/10-Q reports a decline in U.S. assisted tax-prep returns or revenue year on year, or guidance is cut below the reaffirmed range (sec.gov / IR releases).

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
FCF/NI is 105.1% TTM, down from 134.8% in FY2022 but stable near 100% for three years [Certain]. Shares fell 6.36% YoY, so buybacks are the main use of cash; the company repurchased shares in Q1 under its full-year return plan [Certain] (source: https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/11/09/3398946/0/en/h-r-block-reports-fiscal-2027-first-quarter-results-and-reaffirms-fiscal-2027-outlook.html). Leverage is moderate (Altman Z 3.34). An Investor Day on 3 December 2026 may reset medium-term targets [Certain] (source: https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/11/02/3393834/0/en/h-r-block-to-host-investor-day-on-december-3-2026.html).

- New CEO from 1 January 2026 and a new chief accounting officer from October 2025 [Certain] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/12659/000001265925000016/hrb-20250930.htm).
