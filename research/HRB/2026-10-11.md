# H&R Block, Inc. (HRB) — Core initiation — 2026-10-11

## 1. Business
H&R Block prepares tax returns through company-owned and franchised retail offices (franchisees pay ~30% of gross tax-prep revenue as royalties), DIY desktop/online software, and Wave (small-business accounting/payments). Revenue is highly seasonal, earned mostly February–April; the company runs at a loss in H1 of its fiscal year (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/12659/000001265926000026/hrb-20260630.htm). [Certain]

## 2. Quality
HRB leads the "assisted" tax-prep category (>55% of the category) and says it held that share in FY2026, citing 70 years of brand trust and an omni-channel model [Likely] (source: Q4 FY26 earnings call, https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/hrb/transcripts/661703-q4-2026/). TTM ROIC is 46.6% and ROCE 40.1%, up over 5 years (+17.6pp and +7.3pp), operating margin 23.0%, FCF conversion 103.1% of net income [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/hrb/financials/ratios/). The main structural threats are free government filing (IRS Direct File/Free File, through Oct-2029) and AI-enabled DIY tools, both flagged by HRB itself as risks that could "reduce the need for third-party tax preparation services" [Likely] (source: 10-K, same SEC link above). HRB positions AI (Sidekick/AI Tax Assist, 4.2m interactions, engagement doubling YoY) as a tool for its tax pros, not a replacement [Likely] (same link). Gross margin has drifted down slightly over 5 years (-1.3pp to 44.3%), not a trend break [Certain] (source: ratios/income-statement pages above).

## 3. Valuation
HRB trades at 7.7x P/E, 6.4x EV/EBITDA, 1.7x EV/Sales and 7.2x P/FCF — all in the bottom 14–18% of its own 5-year range and 49–66% below the peer median (INTU, ADP, PAYX, EFX) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/hrb/financials/ratios/ and peer statistics pages). FCF yield is 13.9%. P/B looks like an outlier at 46.6x vs. a 7.1x peer median, but this reflects a buyback-shrunk equity base (shares down 6.2% YoY), not overvaluation [Likely]. The fact sheet's own-history DCF/EV-EBITDA blend puts base fair value at $92.38 vs. a $44.05 last close (+109.7%), implying the market prices roughly -15.4% revenue decline in year one — well below management's FY2027 guidance of +3.9–5.1% revenue growth [Likely] (fair value is inform-only, never a buy trigger; source: research/HRB/2026-10-11-valuation.md, derived from stockanalysis.com statements/ratios). The multiple compression looks more attributable to structural disruption fears than to current fundamentals, which keep improving.

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Assisted tax-prep revenue (income statement/segment disclosure) declines for two consecutive quarters, evidencing real share loss to free/DIY alternatives.
- Gross margin (income statement page) breaks its 5-year low, falling below 43% (vs. 44.3% TTM).
- FCF conversion (FCF/NI, cash flow + income statement pages) falls below 90% for a full fiscal year, signalling deteriorating earnings quality.
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) rises above 2.0x (vs. 1.11x TTM), signalling buybacks/dividends outrunning cash generation.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
FCF conversion is 103.1% of net income (TTM), supporting an aggressive but currently affordable return-of-capital program: $500m of buybacks in FY2026 (7.9% of shares outstanding) plus a 10% dividend increase to $0.46/quarter (64th consecutive year of dividends), for $714m total shareholder returns in FY2026; management guides to a further $400m of buybacks in FY2027 [Certain] (source: Q4 FY26 earnings call/release, https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/hrb/transcripts/661703-q4-2026/). Net debt/EBITDA is 1.11x, essentially flat over 5 years, so leverage has not been used to fund the buyback [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/hrb/financials/ratios/).

## 6. Unusual items
Short interest is elevated at 15.5% of float (down 2.2% vs. the prior month), consistent with a live bear case around free-filing/AI disruption rather than a balance-sheet or governance concern [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/hrb/statistics/). Beta is low (0.39), institutional ownership high (96.2%). Altman Z (3.24) and Piotroski F (6/9) show no distress signal.
