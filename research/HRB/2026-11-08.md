# H&R Block (HRB) — Core initiation — 2026-11-08
## 1. Business
Seasonal tax-preparation franchisor and operator: assisted preparation by tax professionals (company-owned and franchised offices, with royalties), DIY online filing, and Wave small-business software and payments. [Certain] It handled 11.3m US assisted returns in FY2026 and 3.6m paid DIY returns [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/12659/000001265926000026/hrb-20260630.htm). Canada and Australia are the foreign operations; the filing gives no country share and the MD&A treats them as a small revenue line, so no foreign country appears to reach ~10% [Likely] (same filing).

## 2. Quality
Returns are high and stable. TTM ROIC is 46.6% against 46.4% a year earlier; the FY2026 65.4% is a different basis [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/hrb/financials/ratios/?p=trailing). Operating margin is 23.0% (21.5% in FY2022); gross margin eased from 45.7% to 44.3% [Certain].
Direction changes: ROCE fell from 42.1% to 40.1% in 12 months, 8.7pp below its 2025-03-31 peak of 48.8%. Operating margin is flat to up, so I read it as capital growth (franchise-office purchases) not weaker economics [Likely]. FCF margin rose from 15.9% to 19.2% but is below FY2022's 21.6%; FCF conversion is 103.1% against 134.8% [Certain].
Moat: brand and assisted-preparation habit; risks are free filing and AI. The new 10-K adds language on free offerings and AI substitution, and says the IRS free-filing programme is suspended, not ended [Certain] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/12659/000001265926000026/hrb-20260630.htm). Real but contestable [Likely].

## 3. Valuation
Price 52.69 is up from a 6-month low of 29.12, yet the 12-month change is only +4.2%: the market first priced a collapse and then retraced [Certain]. P/E is 9.3x (5y median 10.2x), EV/EBITDA 7.4x (7.9x) and P/FCF 8.6x (7.5x); FCF yield 11.64%. Peers (INTU, PAYX, ADP) trade at a P/E of 23.2x, so the discount is 60% [Certain], but those are subscription/payroll models with recurring revenue, a weak comparison [Likely].
Reverse DCF: price implies -12.8% revenue growth in year 1 [Certain]. Fair value base is 95.54 (bear 67.22), mostly from the DCF; the EV/EBITDA leg is 54.02, near the price [Certain]. Bottom line: the price assumes a structural decline that no reported figure shows, but multiples are at the 5y middle, not a trough. Earnings are due 2026-11-09 (seasonally minor quarter) and an Investor Day follows (source: https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/11/02/3393834/0/en/h-r-block-to-host-investor-day-on-december-3-2026.html) [Certain].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROCE (ratios page, trailing) falls below 35%.
- Operating margin (income statement, trailing) falls below 20%.
- FCF margin (cash-flow page, trailing) falls below 14%, or FCF conversion below 85%.
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) rises above 1.75x.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Shares are down 6.17% YoY; 10.5m were repurchased in FY2026 and dividends declared were $1.68 a share, up 12.0% [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/12659/000001265926000026/hrb-20260630.htm). Net debt/EBITDA is 1.11x, flat; the filing shows new senior notes replacing maturing ones and an extended revolver [Certain] (same URL). A discrete tax benefit from an IRS settlement lowered the FY2026 tax rate, so the 18.6% net margin is flattered by a one-off [Certain] (same URL). Use operating margin as the run-rate. Buybacks at 9x earnings are accretive [Likely].

## 6. Unusual items
