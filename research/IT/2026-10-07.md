# Gartner, Inc. (IT) — Core initiation — 2026-10-07

## 1. Business
Gartner sells subscription research and advisory to enterprise IT, business and government leaders, priced on contract value with high renewal intensity (Insights segment) [Likely]. It supplements this with paid Conferences (events, sponsorships) and project-based Consulting. Insights is the largest, highest-margin segment; Consulting is the smallest and has been shrinking [Certain] (source: 10-Q, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/749251/000074925126000245/it-20260630.htm).

## 2. Quality
ROIC and ROCE have risen over five years to 36.0%/35.8% TTM, among the highest of any services business, implying real pricing power rather than financial engineering [Certain] (source: fact sheet RA). FCF conversion is strong (166% TTM) and net debt/EBITDA is modest (1.33x), giving balance-sheet flexibility [Certain] (source: fact sheet CF,IS,BS). The moat is switching costs: IT and procurement teams renew multi-year research contracts because Gartner's frameworks get referenced internally, making it costly to re-platform decision processes onto a rival's research [Likely]. Offsetting this, gross, operating and net margins each declined over five years (gross -1.1pp, operating -2.4pp, net -5.5pp) [Certain] (source: fact sheet IS), and the 10-Q shows Consulting contracting while Conferences grows, a lower-recurring-quality mix shift [Certain] (source: 10-Q). The subscription moat looks intact, but lower-quality segments face more pressure than headline ROIC implies [Likely].

## 3. Valuation
At 17.1x P/E, 9.8x EV/EBITDA, 2.1x EV/Sales and 9.1x P/FCF, IT trades below its own five-year range low on every multiple (-28% to -53%) and 33-74% below the peer median of FORR/SPGI/MCO/VRSK [Certain] (source: fact sheet RA, P:FORR, P:SPGI, P:MCO, P:VRSK). An 11.0% FCF yield (source: fact sheet ST) prices in a structural growth slowdown, a sector-wide de-rating of information-services multiples, or specific skepticism about the shrinking Consulting mix [Likely]. The stock is down 28.1% over 12 months but has rallied 36.9% over three months and sits just above both its 50- and 200-day averages, so the price already reflects a partial re-rating, not a trough [Certain] (source: fact sheet HI,ST). SPGI/MCO/VRSK arguably have stronger moats than Gartner, so the peer gap likely overstates cheapness on a quality-adjusted basis, but the discount to its own history is also wide [Likely].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROIC (ratios page) falls below its 5y low of 25.4% — core research pricing power eroding.
- FCF conversion, FCF/NI (cash flow + income statement) falls below its 5y low of 110% — cash quality deteriorating.
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) rises back above its 5y high of 2.22x — balance-sheet discipline reversing.
- Gross margin (income statement) falls below 67% — mix shift spreading beyond Consulting into core Research.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Capital return is buyback-led: shares outstanding are down 8.83% YoY (source: fact sheet ST), consistent with 166% TTM FCF conversion funding repurchases rather than dividends (fact sheet shows no dividend line) [Certain]. Net debt/EBITDA of 1.33x TTM, up from a 2024 low of 0.74x but still below the five-year average, suggests room for more buybacks or bolt-on deals without stressing the balance sheet [Likely] (source: fact sheet RA,BS). FCF margin of 19.9% TTM is below the 5y high of 26.5% (FY2021) (source: fact sheet CF,IS); cash conversion has structurally cooled as segment mix has shifted.

## 6. Unusual items
Short interest is elevated and rising: 14.53% of float, up 13.6% month-over-month (source: fact sheet ST), unusually bearish positioning for a stock already down 28% over 12 months — this should temper conviction in the valuation gap above [Likely]. Reported institutional ownership of 113.7% (source: fact sheet ST) reflects double-counting across overlapping fund structures, not true free-float ownership [Likely]. Altman Z of 4.10 and Piotroski F of 6 show no distress (source: fact sheet ST).
