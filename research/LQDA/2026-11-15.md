# Liquidia Corporation (LQDA) — Core initiation — 2026-11-15
## 1. Business
Single-product commercial biotech. YUTREPIA (inhaled dry-powder treprostinil) has been sold since June 2025 for pulmonary arterial hypertension (PAH) and competes with United Therapeutics' Tyvaso. Pipeline: L606 (nebulised treprostinil) and Raynaud's/other lung indications (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1819576/000110465926124752/lqda-20260930x10q.htm). Revenue is US; no foreign country is near 10% [Likely]. [Certain]

## 2. Quality
Early-stage but inflecting. Gross margin is 92.7% TTM vs 58.0% in FY2024; operating margin is 47.1% TTM vs -155.0% a year earlier; FCF margin is 46.8% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/lqda/financials/income-statement/, https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/lqda/financials/ratios/?p=trailing). TTM ROIC of 306.2% is a first-profit-year artefact on a tiny capital base, not a durable level [Likely]. Net margin is still -15.1% TTM, which does not square with the 47.1% operating margin; the gap is probably litigation-settlement accounting and interest [Guessing]. Net cash: net debt/EBITDA -0.77x (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/lqda/statistics/). Piotroski 6, Altman Z 4.48.

Moat is thin and narrower after the settlement: YUTREPIA's PH-ILD label and promotion were given up, so the drug is confined to PAH [Certain] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1819576/000110465926124752/lqda-20260930x10q.htm). Edge is a differentiated delivery device and dose range, against a larger incumbent that has just taken a settlement [Likely].

## 3. Valuation
EV/Sales is 8.0x, below its 5y minimum of 16.0x (median 21.2x), and 11% above the peer median of 7.2x (UTHR, HALO, SUPN, KRYS) (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/lqda/statistics/). Own history is pre-profit and not a useful anchor. FCF yield is 5.63%. P/E, EV/EBITDA and P/FCF are [data unavailable], and no fair value is computable (DCF and P/E blocked by negative trailing net income/short history). The price therefore embeds continued PAH share gains and L606 success with no mechanical cross-check [Likely].

Price: 57.41 USD, +102.8% over 12 months but -20.4% over 3 months (peak 93.61 in August, low 22.31 on 2026-10-01) and above its 200-day average by 4.9%. Extremely volatile around the litigation outcome [Certain].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Gross margin (income statement page, TTM) falls below 80%.
- Operating margin (TTM, income statement page) falls below 20% excluding settlement items.
- FCF margin (cash flow page, TTM) turns negative.
- Net debt/EBITDA (statistics page) rises above 1.5x, i.e. the cash pile is consumed by settlement payments and debt.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Settlement payments to United Therapeutics are [company-reported] at $185.0 million within thirty business days of 13 October 2026, then $145.4 million in two instalments through April 2027 (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1819576/000110465926124752/lqda-20260930x10q.htm). These will absorb a large share of cash for the next several quarters [Likely]. Shares outstanding rose 3.45% YoY: dilution is modest but has been the funding route [Certain]. FCF conversion of net income is -309.1% TTM, distorted by settlement items.
