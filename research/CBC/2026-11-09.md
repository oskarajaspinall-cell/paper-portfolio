# Central Bancompany (CBC) — Core initiation — 2026-11-09
## 1. Business
Bank holding company for The Central Trust Bank (Jefferson City, Missouri): consumer banking, commercial banking and wealth management. Earnings come mainly from net interest income, plus wealth-management and fee income [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/cbc/). No foreign country is a material revenue share [Likely]; it is a domestic US regional bank.

## 2. Quality
ROE rose from 10.2% (FY2021) to 11.3% (FY2025) and 12.6% TTM; net margin rose from 32.3% to 38.6% and 40.8% TTM [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/cbc/financials/ratios/). ROIC, ROCE and leverage are not published for banks [data unavailable]. Q3 2026 management commentary: NIM up year on year, credit "benign", capital high [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/cbc/transcripts/723782-q3-2026/). Returns are good for a regional bank, but a durable moat is not shown by the data: it rests on deposit franchise and local density [Guessing]. FCF margin fell over five years (FY2025 FCF/NI 59.2%) but TTM rebounded to 44.6% from 28.1% a year earlier; bank cash flow is noisy and I give it little weight [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/cbc/financials/cash-flow-statement/?p=trailing). Beta is 0.06 [Certain].

## 3. Valuation
P/E 20.0x versus a peer median of 12.5x (+60%); P/B 2.4x versus 1.6x (+46%); P/FCF 18.9x versus 9.9x [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/cbc/statistics/). The 5y range (median P/E 344.4x) is distorted by a short, recent listing history and is not a usable anchor [Likely]. The fair value is flagged unreliable: base 25.08 versus a 37.75 close, a gap of -33.6%; methods disagree 3.8x (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/cbc/). The P/B + ROE method gives 20.37 base. The price already embeds a premium to peers, and a reverse DCF implies 7.6% year-1 revenue growth [Likely]. The stock is +77.7% over 12 months with RSI 76.7, so the market has re-rated it well ahead of the earnings improvement [Certain].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROE (ratios page) falls below 10.0% on a TTM basis.
- Net margin (income statement page) falls below 34.0% for a fiscal year.
- Shares change YoY (statistics page) stays above 7.56% in the next fiscal year, i.e. dilution continues instead of buybacks.
- Credit quality: the Q3 "benign credit" description is reversed in a later earnings release (text trigger; company release).

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
A new $100M buyback was authorised in Q2 2026, and management speaks of "selective buybacks" [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/cbc/transcripts/656118-q2-2026/). Yet the share count rose 7.56% YoY, consistent with a recent IPO issuance [Likely]. FY2025 FCF conversion was 59.2%, against 110.6% in FY2024, before the TTM rebound [Certain].

## 6. Unusual items
Short interest is 11.08% of float, down 3.0% on the prior month. Insiders own 31.19% and institutions 18.3%, so the free float is thin and the controlling holders' alignment is strong, but so is the overhang risk from later share sales [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/cbc/statistics/). Q3 EPS of 48c came in just under consensus per a news headline (source: https://www.tipranks.com/news/the-fly/central-bancompany-reports-q3-eps-48c-consensus-49c-thefly-news) [Likely]; no figure is used from it. Short listing history limits the multiples' comparability.
