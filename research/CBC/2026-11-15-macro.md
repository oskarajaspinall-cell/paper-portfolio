# Macro overlay: Central Bancompany (CBC) — 2026-11-15
## Assessment
**Chain:** a pure US regional bank, so rates drive it: policy path -> funding costs and the 10y-2y slope -> net interest margin; credit spreads -> loan losses and the equity risk premium -> the P/B multiple [Likely].

**Facts (FRED, 2026-11-12/13):** fed funds 3.88% (+0.25pp over 3m), 2y 4.86% (+0.71pp 3m), 10y 5.10% (+0.47pp 3m), so the 10y-2y slope is only about 0.24pp. HY spread is 3.2% (+0.49pp 3m, still low), VIX 18.7 (+4.1 over 3m). Sources: https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=DGS2, https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=DGS10, https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=BAMLH0A0HYM2, https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=VIXCLS.

**Pricing (proxy):** a 2y yield about 1pp above fed funds implies the market expects hikes, or at least no cuts **(inference)**. Company Q3 (2026-10-27): NIM up 12bps YoY, credit benign, "rate uncertainty" acknowledged (stockanalysis.com transcript, https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/cbc/transcripts/723782-q3-2026/) [Likely]. The stock is +81% over 12 months with RSI 73.7, so a higher-for-longer path with a flat curve is largely in the price **(inference)**.

**Bull:** curve steepens through a higher 10y with stable funding costs and spreads near 3%: NIM keeps widening, ROE above 12.6% supports the 2.4x P/B.
**Bear:** the 2y keeps rising (flatter or inverted curve, deposit costs up) and spreads widen: margin gain stalls, credit costs rise, and the multiple compresses from a stretched 20x P/E.

**Tilt:** toward bear, small. Macro is a mild headwind to an already expensive multiple, but the current data (benign credit, low spreads) don't contradict the fundamentals.
