# Central Bancompany (CBC) — Core initiation — 2026-11-15
## 1. Business
Central Bancompany is a Missouri-based bank holding company (The Central Trust Bank) and listed on Nasdaq. It earns net interest income on loans funded by deposits, plus fee income including wealth management [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/cbc/; https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/10/27/3388910/0/en/central-bancompany-inc-reports-third-quarter-2026-results.html). Revenue is a US regional bank mix; no foreign country is ~10%+ [Likely]. Segment mix [data unavailable].

## 2. Quality
ROE is modest but improving: 10.2% in FY2021 to 11.3% in FY2025 and 12.6% TTM [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/cbc/financials/ratios/). Net margin rose from 32.3% to 38.6% in FY2025 and 40.8% TTM [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/cbc/financials/income-statement/). Management's Q3 call cited a wider net interest margin and benign credit [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/cbc/transcripts/723782-q3-2026/). Moat is local deposit franchise density, a typical regional-bank advantage rather than a strong one [Guessing]. ROIC, ROCE and leverage are [data unavailable], as banks lack them. FCF conversion fell from 110.6% in FY2024 to 59.2% in FY2025, but TTM FCF margin recovered to 44.6% from 28.1% a year earlier, so the 5-year decline is a lumpy-cash-flow effect, not a trend [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/cbc/financials/cash-flow-statement/?p=trailing). Piotroski F is only 4 [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/cbc/statistics/).

## 3. Valuation
P/E is 20.2x vs a peer median of 11.9x (+70%), P/B 2.4x vs 1.4x (+66%), P/FCF 19.1x vs 7.5x [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/cbc/statistics/). The 5y own-history range (P/E median 344.4x) is not meaningful: the company only recently listed, so that history is distorted [Likely]. FCF yield is 5.24% [Certain]. Mechanical fair value (base 24.09 vs close 38.15) is flagged unreliable: methods disagree 3.8x, and the P/B + ROE leg (19.57) sits far below the price [Certain] (source: research/CBC/factsheet-2026-11-15.md). The price assumes sustained ROE expansion well beyond 12.6% and the premium persisting; the reverse DCF implies 8.9% year-1 revenue growth, high for a bank [Certain]. Price is +30.0% above the 200-day MA with 12-month change +81.2% and RSI 73.7: stretched [Certain]. Not cheap on any measure that exists.

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROE (ratios page) falls below 10% on a TTM basis.
- Net margin (income statement page) falls below 33% on a TTM basis.
- TTM FCF margin (trailing cash flow page) falls back below 28%.
- Shares change YoY (statistics page) stays above 7% for another year, i.e. further dilution.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Shares rose 7.56% YoY [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/cbc/statistics/), consistent with post-IPO issuance [Likely]. A new buyback was authorised with Q2 results, and management called buybacks "selective" on the Q3 call [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/cbc/transcripts/656118-q2-2026/). Cash conversion was 59.2% in FY2025 but TTM FCF margin is 44.6% [Certain].

## 6. Unusual items
Short interest is 11.16% of float, up 0.7% on the prior month [Certain]. Insiders own 31.19% and institutions 20.7%, so the free float is small and an overhang from insider or pre-IPO holder sales is possible [Guessing]. Beta (5y) of 0.06 reflects a short listing history and is not reliable [Likely]. Q3 EPS was reported just under consensus on 2026-10-27 [Likely] (source: https://www.tipranks.com/news/the-fly/central-bancompany-reports-q3-eps-48c-consensus-49c-thefly-news).
