# Macro overlay: Central Bancompany (CBC) — 2026-11-09
## Assessment
**Weight: secondary.** CBC is a domestic US regional bank, so macro acts through net interest income, credit and the multiple.

**Chain:** policy path and curve -> deposit and loan repricing -> NIM -> earnings. Second chain: credit spreads and risk appetite -> equity risk premium -> a 20.0x P/E against a 12.5x peer median.

**Facts (FRED, 2026-11-05):**
- 2y 4.46%, 10y 4.84% (https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=DGS10). Both fell over ~1m, so the curve is about +0.38pp and slightly flatter.
- Fed funds 3.88% (https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=DFF).
- HY spread 3.48%, +0.45pp over 1m (https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=BAMLH0A0HYM2).
- VIX 28.18, +13.2 over 1m (https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=VIXCLS).

**Pricing (proxy):** the 2y sits about 0.6pp above fed funds, so no cuts are priced **(inference)**.

**Inference:** the baseline is NIM up and credit "benign" (https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/cbc/transcripts/723782-q3-2026/), so the surprises that hurt are wider spreads and a flatter curve [Likely]. A steeper curve with stable spreads helps.

**Bull:** spreads retrace, curve steepens; NIM growth continues and the multiple holds.
**Bear:** spreads toward 4.5%+, credit costs rise, curve flattens; earnings growth slows and the multiple compresses toward peers (the larger effect).
**Tilt:** toward bear, small. Fundamentals are strong and priced; macro mainly threatens the multiple premium.
