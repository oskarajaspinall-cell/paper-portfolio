## Assessment
**Weight: secondary.** ASB is a US-only regional bank, so the exposure is credit conditions and risk appetite. Chain: credit spreads / VIX -> equity risk premium and expected provisions -> P/B and P/E multiple [Likely].

**Facts (FRED, 2026-11-05):** HY spread 3.48% (+0.45pp 1m, +0.77pp 3m) (https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=BAMLH0A0HYM2). VIX 28.18 (+13.2 in 1m) (https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=VIXCLS). 10y 4.84%, 2y 4.46%, so the slope is about +0.38pp (https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=DGS10, https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=DGS2). Fed funds 3.88% (https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=DFF).

**Pricing (proxy):** the 2y sits above fed funds, so hikes are priced **(inference)**. Breakeven 2.23% is stable; the real yield drives yields.

**Inference:** the hurtful surprise is risk-off: VIX doubling with spreads still low is a risk-premium shock, not a credit event. Multiples compress first, provisions later. ASB's P/E of 10.8x is at 6% of its 5y range [Certain] (research/ASB/factsheet-2026-11-09.md), so much is priced. Country risk: none material [Likely].

**Bull:** VIX normalises, spreads stay below about 4%; multiple re-rates toward peers (11.6x), NIM keeps expanding.
**Bear:** spreads above about 4.5% and VIX above 30; multiple compresses, provisions rise from a low base.
**Tilt:** toward bear, small: new shock, but the multiple is near its floor and beta is 0.81.
