# Associated Banc-Corp (ASB) — Core initiation — 2026-11-09
## 1. Business
Midwest regional bank holding company (Green Bay, Wisconsin). Earns net interest income on commercial, consumer and auto loans funded by deposits, plus fees from deposit accounts, cards, wealth management and capital markets (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/asb/). It is remixing away from residential mortgages toward commercial lending and acquired American National in Q2 2026 (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/7789/000000778926000260/asb-20260930.htm). No foreign revenue region is material [Likely].

## 2. Quality
Mediocre-to-adequate, not a moat business. ROE is 10.2% TTM against 8.6% in FY2021, with FY2023-24 depressed to 4.5% and 2.8% by the mortgage-portfolio repositioning (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/asb/financials/ratios/) [Certain]. ROIC/ROCE are not published for banks [data unavailable]. Advantage is local commercial relationships and deposit franchise, which is modest and replicable by peers [Likely]. Margin direction is improving: the 10-Q attributes higher net interest income to the acquisition, better asset mix and cheaper funding, with net interest margin 3.14% for nine months vs 3.02% [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/7789/000000778926000260/asb-20260930.htm). Q3 results beat per headline reporting (source: https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/associated-banc-corp-reports-third-quarter-2026-earnings-of-0-76-per-common-share-or-0-81-per-common-share-excluding-nonrecurring-items-recognized-during-the-quarter-302917404.html) [Likely]. FCF margin is 4.1pp below its 2-year high; for a bank FCF is distorted by loan and trading flows, so it is a weak signal [Likely].

## 3. Valuation
P/E 10.8x sits at 6% of its 5y range (9.1x-35.0x, median 10.0x), 7% below peers (11.6x) [Certain]. P/FCF 6.7x is 27% below peers; FCF yield 15.30% [Certain]. P/B 1.1x is 18% below peers (1.3x); the site's 5y P/B range is flat at 0.8x, so the "above max" reading is unreliable [Likely]. Mechanical base fair value 33.85 is +11.5% against the 30.36 close, but it is flagged unreliable: P/B+ROE gives 26.47, FCFE DCF 48.61, DDM 15.32. Weigh it lightly. The price is a fair-to-modest discount to peers, not a deep one [Likely]. Price is above both moving averages with +19.5% over 12 months, so the discount is partly re-rated already.

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROE (ratios page) TTM falls below 8%.
- Net margin (income statement page) TTM falls below 25%.
- Net interest margin in the 10-Q/10-K MD&A falls below 3.00% for a quarter (company filings, sec.gov).
- Provision for credit losses in the 10-Q rises above 100 million USD for a nine-month period (sec.gov).

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Dividend raised to 25c from 24c (source: https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/associated-banc-corp-announces-increase-in-common-stock-dividend-302921219.html) [Certain]. Share count rose 7.92% YoY, reflecting acquisition issuance, which dilutes per-share returns unless integration delivers [Certain]. Management says the American National conversion is completed (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/asb/transcripts/714244-q3-2026/) [Likely]. FCF/NI of 161.8% TTM is not a clean cash-conversion read for a bank [Likely].

## 6. Unusual items
Risk Factors in the latest 10-Q show no substantive change vs a year earlier [Certain] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/7789/000000778926000260/asb-20260930.htm). 10-Q notes nonrecurring severance, retention and contract-termination costs from the deal, and a lower acquired-loan provision after adopting ASU 2025-08, which flatters near-term provisions [Likely]. Short interest 0.01% of float; insiders 10.00%.
