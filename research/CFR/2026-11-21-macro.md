# Macro overlay: Cullen/Frost Bankers (CFR) — 2026-11-21
## Assessment
**Weight: secondary.** Chain: rate path -> net interest income on a deposit base that is 32.9% non-interest-bearing [company-reported] (research note, section 1), and credit spreads / VIX -> equity risk premium -> the bank's P/B multiple (2.3x vs 1.3x peer median). [Likely]

**Facts (FRED):** 2y yield 4.76% (+0.57pp over ~3m) vs effective fed funds 3.88% (2026-11-19, https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=DGS2). 10y yield 5.04%, real yield 2.88% (+0.53pp ~3m, https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=DFII10). HY spread 3.52% (+0.77pp ~3m, https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=BAMLH0A0HYM2). VIX 28.4 (+12.4 ~3m, https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=VIXCLS). [Certain]

**Market pricing (proxy):** a 2y yield about 0.9pp above fed funds implies the market prices hikes, not cuts. The company's own 10-Q cites Fed projections implying a possible rise (research note, section 6). Rates higher for longer are already in the price and in raised NII guidance. [Likely]

**Inference:** the 12% three-month fall (RSI 26) with no company news looks like risk-premium widening, not an earnings cut. The earnings channel is mildly positive (asset-sensitive, cheap deposits), offset by deposit competition. The surprise that hurts is a credit or risk-off shock (spreads widening, loan-loss fears); a calmer VIX helps the multiple. **(inference)**

**Bull:** rates stay high, VIX normalises, multiple re-rates toward the 15x median P/E (about +10%), NII beats guidance.
**Bear:** spreads widen toward 5% and the 2y falls on growth fear: NII guidance cut and P/B compresses toward 1.8x (about -20%).
**Tilt:** neutral to slightly toward bear, small. Earnings are supported, but the multiple is exposed to risk appetite.
