- **Added**: Our accounting policies related to the allowance for credit losses on loans and off-balance-sheet credit exposures are considered critical because they involve considerable judgment and estimation by management. The allowances are calculated in accordance with Accounting Standards Codification (“ASC”) Topic 326, “Financial Instruments - Credit Losses” (“ASC 326”). The allowance for credit losses on loans is a contra-asset valuation account that is deducted from the amortized cost basis of loans to present the net amount expected to be collected. The allowance for credit losses on off-balance-sheet credit exposures is a liability account reported as a component of accrued interest payable and other liabilities in our consolidated balance sheets.
