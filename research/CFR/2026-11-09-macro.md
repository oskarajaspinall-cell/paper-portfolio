# Macro overlay: Cullen/Frost Bankers (CFR) — 2026-11-09
## Assessment
**Weight: secondary.** CFR is a Texas bank with about 33% non-interest-bearing deposits (note section 1), so rates and credit conditions move its earnings and multiple. There is no material foreign exposure. [Likely]

**Chain:** policy path and the 2y yield -> deposit-funded asset yields -> net interest income; credit spreads and VIX -> risk premium -> the P/B multiple (2.5x, 77% of its 5y range, per the fact sheet).

- HY spread 3.48% (+0.45 in 1m); VIX 28.18 (+13.2 in 1m).

**Pricing (proxy):** the 2y sits about 58bp above fed funds, so some tightening is priced. **(inference)** Higher-for-longer helps NII, and the filing flags possible rate rises (note, section 6), but that is largely priced. The risk is a spread and VIX jump hitting bank multiples and credit-cost expectations.

**Bull:** rates stay high, spreads stabilise, NII guidance holds, P/B holds near 2.5x.
**Bear:** HY spread near 5% and VIX above 35 compress P/B toward 1.8-2.0x. The price is not cheap against peers (P/B +104%), so de-rating risk is real.

**Tilt:** toward bear, small. Valuation is full, and a risk-off move hits it before the NII benefit shows. [Guessing]
