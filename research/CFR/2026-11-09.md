# Cullen/Frost Bankers (CFR) — Core initiation — 2026-11-09
## 1. Business
Texas-focused commercial and consumer bank (Frost Bank). Earns net interest income on a deposit base funded heavily by non-interest-bearing demand deposits, about 32.9% of total deposits [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/39263/000003926326000056/cfr-20260930.htm), plus fee income. No foreign country is ~10% of revenue [Likely]. Sector: Financials, Banks - Regional (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/cfr/).

## 2. Quality
- ROE is 16.3% TTM vs 10.1% in FY2021, rising 5.2pp over five years and steady at 15.3% in FY2024 and FY2025 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/cfr/financials/ratios/). [Certain]
- Net margin 30.0% TTM, down 2.7pp over five years but still high for a bank (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/cfr/financials/income-statement/). [Certain]
- Durable edge: a cheap, sticky core-deposit franchise, so the bank earns more as rates rise. Beta is 0.51 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/cfr/statistics/). [Likely]
- Shares fell 2.26% YoY, so buybacks are shrinking the count. [Certain]
- Management raised loan growth and NII guidance on the latest call (headline: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/cfr/transcripts/720163-q3-2026/). [Likely]
- FCF margin is noisy: 5.8% in FY2025, 25.2% TTM. It is a poor bank metric. [Likely]
- ROIC and operating margin are [data unavailable].

## 3. Valuation
- P/E is 14.7x, 42% of the way up its 5y range (11.9x-18.6x) and below the 15.1x median, but 26% above the peer median of 11.7x. [Certain]
- P/B is 2.5x, 77% of the way up its 5y range (1.8x-2.7x) and 104% above the peer median of 1.2x. [Certain]
- FCF yield is 5.99% and P/FCF is 17.1x, 13% of the way up its 5y range. [Certain]
- Mechanical fair value (base 153.19 vs close 155.23) is flagged unreliable, as methods disagree 3.2x. P/B + ROE gives 124.56 and the FCFE DCF gives 210.46. [Certain]
- The reverse DCF implies -0.7% year-1 revenue growth. [Certain]
- Sources: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/cfr/statistics/ and the fact sheet.
- Read: the price is full against peers and fair on its own history. It already pays for quality, so upside depends on earnings growth. [Likely]

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
It repurchased 1,266,798 shares for $178,349 thousand in the nine months to September 2026 [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/39263/000003926326000056/cfr-20260930.htm). It also pays common and preferred dividends (headline: https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/cullenfrost-reports-third-quarter-results-302923708.html). Cash conversion (FCF/NI) was 83.9% TTM, with large year-to-year swings (19.6% in FY2025, 147.9% in FY2024). That is normal for a bank, where working-capital items distort it. [Likely]
