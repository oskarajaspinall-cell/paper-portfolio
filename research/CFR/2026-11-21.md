# Cullen/Frost Bankers (CFR) — Core initiation — 2026-11-21
## 1. Business
Texas-focused commercial and consumer bank (Frost Bank). It earns net interest income on a deposit base funded heavily by non-interest-bearing demand deposits, about 32.9% of total deposits [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/39263/000003926326000056/cfr-20260930.htm), plus fee income. No foreign country is ~10% of revenue [Likely]. Sector: Financials, Banks - Regional (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/cfr/).

## 2. Quality
- ROE is 16.3% TTM vs 10.1% in FY2021, rising 5.2pp over five years, and 15.3% in both FY2024 and FY2025 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/cfr/financials/ratios/). [Certain]
- Net margin is 30.0% TTM, down 2.7pp over five years but still high for a bank (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/cfr/financials/income-statement/). [Certain]
- Durable edge: a cheap, sticky core-deposit franchise. Beta is 0.51 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/cfr/statistics/). [Likely]
- Shares fell 2.26% YoY, so buybacks are shrinking the count. [Certain]
- Q3 results: loan growth and a record pipeline, with loan growth and net interest income guidance raised (headline: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/cfr/transcripts/720163-q3-2026/). [Likely]
- Direction: FCF margin is falling over five years but rose from 0.8% to 25.2% over the last 12 months. It is a noisy bank metric, so I do not lean on it. [Likely]
- ROIC and operating margin are [data unavailable].

It also pays common and preferred dividends (headline: https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/cullenfrost-reports-third-quarter-results-302923708.html).
