# Dropbox, Inc. (DBX) — Core initiation — 2026-11-08
## 1. Business
Subscription file-sync, collaboration and document workflow software sold to individuals, families and teams. More than 90% of revenue is self-serve, with 18.20 million paying users at 30 Sep 2026 [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1467623/000146762326000061/dbx-20260930.htm) [Certain]. Growth is the issue: the filing says growth has declined, with negative growth in past periods. Mix is shifting to cheaper plans; Dash (AI search) and DBX Agent Cloud are the new bets.

## 2. Quality
A highly cash-generative, mature franchise: gross margin 79.9%, operating margin 26.6% and FCF margin 36.1% TTM, with operating margin up from 14.2% in FY2021 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/dbx/financials/income-statement/) [Certain]. Moat is habit, switching costs and word-of-mouth self-serve distribution, which keeps acquisition costs low [Likely]. Durability is unproven: AI-native tools and bundled suites can commoditise storage, and the filing adds new risk language on declining growth, paying-user conversion and renewals [Likely].
Direction change: ROIC fell to 33.7% from 64.9% a year earlier and ROCE to 41.4% from 74.5%, though operating margin is flat (+0.3pp) and FCF margin flat (-0.1pp) (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/dbx/financials/ratios/?p=trailing) [Certain]. The ROIC drop likely reflects a shrunken, debt-funded capital base rather than falling profit [Guessing].

## 3. Valuation
The price implies shrinkage: reverse DCF implies -6.4% revenue growth in year 1 fading to terminal growth [Certain]. P/E 18.8x vs 5y median 20.0x; EV/EBITDA 12.4x vs 16.9x; P/FCF 8.2x vs 10.4x; FCF yield 12.27% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/dbx/statistics/) [Certain]. Versus peers (BOX, ZM, DOCU, TWLO) P/E is -48% and EV/EBITDA -67%, but peers are growth-priced, so the comparison is weak [Likely].
Model fair value: base 49.40, bear 23.69, bull 52.84 USD vs last close 34.66 USD (+42.5% to base). The DCF carries 67% weight and assumes slow decay; EV/Revenue alone gives 31.03, below the price [Certain]. Upside needs revenue to stabilise near flat [Likely]. The market prices a melting ice cube; the cash yield pays you to wait if decline is no worse [Likely].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Paying users (company 10-Q key metrics) fall below 17.5 million for two consecutive quarters, excluding the FormSwift wind-down.
- Operating margin (income statement, TTM page) falls below 23%.
- FCF margin (cash flow statement, TTM page) falls below 32%.
- Net debt / EBITDA (ratios page) exceeds 4.0x.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
FCF is 210.3% of net income TTM, and shares outstanding are down 18.02% YoY, so buybacks are aggressive (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/dbx/statistics/) [Certain]. Net debt / EBITDA rose from 1.14x (FY2023) to 3.41x TTM; the term loan is fully drawn and higher interest cut nine-month FCF [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1467623/000146762326000061/dbx-20260930.htm) [Certain]. A further workforce cut of about 9% was announced 28 Oct 2026 (same source) [Certain]. The Tigris Data acquisition adds M&A risk to a levered balance sheet (source: https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20261105637007/en/Dropbox-Announces-Plans-to-Acquire-Tigris-Data-Inc.-and-Unveils-DBX-Agent-Cloud-as-Part-of-AI-Powered-Product-Evolution/) [Likely].

## 6. Unusual items
- Short interest 17.75% of float (down 7.4% vs prior month); insiders own 40.06%, so control is concentrated (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/dbx/statistics/) [Certain].
- Altman Z 0.53 and Piotroski F 5: weak distress measures from leverage [Certain].
- Q3 was a beat-and-raise, but no earnings-type price reaction registered, so no drift setup (source: https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20261105521246/en/Dropbox-Announces-Third-Quarter-2026-Results/) [Likely].
- Litigation from the 2024 Dropbox Sign breach continues (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1467623/000146762326000061/dbx-20260930.htm) [Certain].
