# Caixa Seguridade Participações (BVMF:CXSE3) — Core initiation — 2026-11-15
## 1. Business
Insurance-distribution holding that earns fees and equity income from products sold through the Caixa Econômica Federal branch network: mortgage insurance, private pensions, premium bonds and credit-related life cover [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/bvmf/CXSE3/). Capital-light, so it earns very high margins and returns. Revenue-by-country data is [data unavailable]; it is a Brazil-only business [Likely], so the macro overlay should check BRL, the Selic rate and the state-bank link. Financials sector, BRL reporter.

## 2. Quality
Returns are exceptional and rising: ROIC 19.4% in FY2021 to 34.5% TTM, ROE 33.3% TTM (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/bvmf/CXSE3/financials/ratios/) [Certain]. Net debt/EBITDA is -0.26x, so there is net cash, and Altman Z is 26.29 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/bvmf/CXSE3/statistics/) [Certain].

The recent direction is softer. ROIC is 8.3pp below its 2-year high of 42.9%, and ROCE is 10.0pp below its 45.4% high. Operating margin fell 2.3pp in the last 12 months (83.8% to 81.5%) and is 7.7pp lower than in FY2021 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/bvmf/CXSE3/financials/income-statement/?p=trailing) [Certain]. The Q3 2026 call headline cites pressure on Credit Life and branch sales from high rates and a temporary strike, with managerial net income growing only modestly (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/bvmf/CXSE3/transcripts/668432-q3-2026/) [Likely]. So the peak has probably passed, but returns remain far above cost of capital.

The moat is the distribution channel into a very large bank customer base. It is contractual, but it depends on one counterparty that is also the controlling parent [Likely].

## 3. Valuation
The price assumes returns hold and the channel keeps its economics. P/E is 14.1x against a 5y range of 8.3x–13.2x, above the maximum. EV/EBITDA is 12.8x (5y max 11.7x). P/B is 4.6x (5y max 3.7x). Against peers (BBSE3, PSSA3, WIZC3) it trades at +79% on P/E and +160% on P/B (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/bvmf/CXSE3/statistics/) [Certain]. Part of the premium is deserved by the capital-light model [Likely].

Mechanical fair value (P/B+ROE blended with P/E) is BRL 12.89 base, bear 9.26, bull 13.02, against a last close of 21.28. Even the bull case is 38.8% below the price [Certain]. FCF yield is [data unavailable].

The trend is strong: 10.6% above the 200-day average, +40.9% over 12 months, beating SPY by 30.5pp [Certain]. A good business that is working, but paid for in full.
