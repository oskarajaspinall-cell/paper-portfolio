# United Bankshares, Inc. (UBSI) — Core initiation — 2026-11-16
## 1. Business
Regional bank holding company (Banks - Regional, Financials). It earns net interest income on commercial real estate, residential and consumer loans funded by core deposits, plus fee income [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/ubsi/). The loan book is led by commercial real estate [Certain] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/729986/000119312526450993/d882885d10q.htm). Footprint is US only (West Virginia base, Atlanta added via the Piedmont deal, de novo Florida entry announced) [Likely]. No foreign country is a material revenue share.

## 2. Quality
Ordinary-to-decent bank, not a high-return franchise. ROE is 9.4% TTM, 8.9% FY2025, and flat over five years (7.6%-8.9%, source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/ubsi/financials/ratios/) [Certain]. ROIC/ROCE are [data unavailable] for banks. Net margin is rising, 36.9% FY2024 to 40.8% TTM (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/ubsi/financials/income-statement/) [Certain]. Q3 2026 EPS reportedly met expectations, with a higher net interest margin than Q2 and risk-based capital described as solid (source: https://www.tipranks.com/news/the-fly/united-bankshares-reports-q3-eps-94c-consensus-93c-thefly-news) [Likely].
Moat: low-cost deposit base and a scale position in its home markets [Guessing]; nothing in the fact sheet proves pricing power. Cash-flow direction: FCF margin falls over five years (-16.8pp) but is rising over 12 months (42.1% to 43.7%); FY2021-22 were inflated by bank working-capital swings, so the long decline is mostly normalisation [Likely]. Beta 0.74 is defensive.

## 3. Valuation
P/E 13.1x sits at 54% of its 5y range (11.6x-14.4x), +17% above peers (11.2x). P/B 1.2x is at 97% of its range, in line with peers. P/FCF 11.7x is at 96% of range, +33% above peers [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/ubsi/statistics/). FCF yield is 8.51%, but bank FCF is noisy and should be weighed lightly [Likely].
Mechanical fair value is 41.95 base versus a 48.15 close (-12.9%), flagged unreliable: methods disagree 2.5x (P/B+ROE 33.74, FCFE DCF 58.38). The price assumes ROE holds around the current level with no decline. It is a fair-to-full price, not a discount [Likely]. The case rests on trend: +31.4% over 12 months, +21.1pp vs SPY, 6.7% above the 200-day average [Certain].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROE (ratios page) falls below 7.5% on a TTM basis, i.e. below the FY2024 low.
- Net margin (income statement page, TTM) falls below 35.0%.
- Shares outstanding change YoY (statistics page) turns above +3% on dilution, reversing the current -1.12% buyback shrink.
- FCF margin (rolling 12-month table) falls below 35%.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Share count is shrinking (-1.12% YoY) and a new repurchase programme was authorised alongside the Q3 dividend (source: https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260824105013/en/United-Bankshares-Declares-Third-Quarter-Dividend-and-Announces-New-Stock-Repurchase-Program/) [Certain]. FCF/NI is 107.2% TTM, above 100% for five years [Certain]. The 2025 Piedmont acquisition was absorbed; recent growth is organic, loans up in commercial real estate [Certain] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/729986/000119312526450993/d882885d10q.htm). The Florida de novo is a small, low-risk expansion [Likely] (source: https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20261008370877/en/United-Bankshares-Inc.-Announces-Florida-Expansion/).

## 6. Unusual items
Filing delta: Q3 10-Q Risk Factors show no substantive change [Certain]. The MD&A changes are balance-sheet movements. Growth skews to commercial real estate, so credit quality in that book is the key risk [Likely]. Short interest is 4.69% of float, down 9.9% on the month; institutions own 76.2% [Certain].
