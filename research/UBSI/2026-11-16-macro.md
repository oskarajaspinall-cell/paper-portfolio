# Macro overlay: United Bankshares (UBSI) — 2026-11-16
## Assessment
**Chain:** rates path -> curve and long yields -> net interest margin, securities marks and CRE credit/valuation -> bank multiple. UBSI is US-only with a CRE-led loan book (research note, sec.gov 10-Q), so the link is real: secondary [Likely].

**Facts (FRED, https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=DGS2, 2026-11-12/13):** fed funds 3.88% (+0.25pp over ~3m). 2y yield 4.86% (+0.71pp ~3m). 10y yield 5.10% (+0.47pp ~3m). 10y real yield 2.86% (+0.47pp). Breakeven 2.24% (-0.03pp). HY spread 3.20% (+0.49pp ~3m, still low). VIX 18.7 (+4.07 ~3m).

**Market pricing (proxy):** the 2y sits about 100bp above fed funds, so the market prices hikes **(inference)**. The 10y-2y slope is only +0.24pp, so the curve has flattened. The rise is real yields, not inflation breakevens, which are flat.

**Inference:** higher yields help asset repricing, but a flat curve limits margin gains. Rising real yields also hurt CRE valuations and bank multiples. Credit spreads are not yet signalling stress. The stock is at 97% of its 5y P/B range, so there is little cushion.

**Bull:** curve re-steepens with stable spreads; NIM keeps rising, ROE drifts above 9.4%.
**Bear:** hikes plus wider spreads (HY above ~4.5%) hit CRE credit and compress the multiple; ROE falls toward the 7.5% invalidation line.
**Tilt:** toward bear, small: the price is full and rate-driven risks are building, but credit has not turned.

Country risk: none material (US only).
