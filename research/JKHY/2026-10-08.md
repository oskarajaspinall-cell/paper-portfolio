# Jack Henry & Associates, Inc. (JKHY) — Core initiation — 2026-10-08

## 1. Business
Jack Henry sells core banking/processing software and payments to community banks (900+) and credit unions (700+) on long-term (~6-year) outsourcing/SaaS and license contracts, plus recurring support fees (~20% of license fees) [Certain] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/779152/000077915226000067/jkhy-20260630.htm). Revenue is heavily recurring; growth also comes from 36 acquisitions since 1999 [Certain] (source: same 10-K).

## 2. Quality
High switching costs (deep core-system integration, multi-year contracts) support ROIC 22.7% TTM, ROCE 24.8% TTM and FCF margin 27.3% TTM, rising +3.1pp over 5 years, with FCF conversion 138% [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/jkhy/financials/ratios/, https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/jkhy/financials/cash-flow-statement/). Net debt/EBITDA is 0.09x — minimal leverage [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/jkhy/financials/ratios/). The 10-K discloses "price compression between the former and renegotiated contracts" on certain renewals, and warns that fragmentation from niche fintech point-solutions and nontraditional providers "may reduce demand for broader integrated offerings" [Likely] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/779152/000077915226000067/jkhy-20260630.htm). ROE has drifted down -2.8pp over 5 years despite margin expansion, consistent with acquisition-driven goodwill diluting returns [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/jkhy/financials/ratios/). Net: a durable, high-retention business, but moat is being tested at the margin by cheaper point solutions, not yet visible in the numbers.

## 3. Valuation
P/E of 20.9x sits near the bottom of its own 5-year range (8th percentile: 19.5x–36.1x), i.e. the stock has already de-rated sharply [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/jkhy/financials/ratios/). It still trades at a +31% P/E, +13% EV/EBITDA and +28% EV/Sales premium to the median of FI, FIS, ACIW and QTWO [Certain] (source: fact sheet peer statistics pages). Base-case fair value of $189.78 (DCF 67%/EV-EBITDA 33% blend) implies +30.3% upside to the $145.69 close; bear $107.21 (-26.4%), bull $203.00 (+39.3%) [Likely] (source: factsheet fair-value section). The reverse DCF shows the price implies only 0.4% year-1 revenue growth fading to terminal — well below both the 7.3% FY2026 growth and management's 7%-8% FY2029 guidance given at the September 2026 Investor Day [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/jkhy/transcripts/750018-investor-day-2026/). The valuation case rests on this gap closing.

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Revenue growth (income statement) falls below 5% for two consecutive fiscal years, undercutting the FY2029 7-8% guidance.
- FCF margin (cash flow + income statement) falls below 20%, reversing the 5-year rising trend (now 27.3%).
- Net margin (income statement) falls below 17% (the FY2024 trough), signalling renewal price compression is biting.
- ROIC (ratios page) falls below 19% (the FY2024 trough) for two consecutive years, evidencing moat erosion.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
FCF conversion of 138% (FCF/NI) TTM shows low capital intensity [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/jkhy/financials/cash-flow-statement/). Shares outstanding fell -1.37% YoY, consistent with net buybacks [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/jkhy/statistics/).
