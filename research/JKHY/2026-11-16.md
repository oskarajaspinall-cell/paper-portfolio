# Jack Henry & Associates (JKHY) — Core initiation — 2026-11-16
## 1. Business
Core banking, payments and digital software and services for US community banks and credit unions. Revenue is recurring: "services and support" (cloud hosting, on-premise support, licences, implementation) and "processing" (card, remittance, faster payments, digital) (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/779152/000077915226000098/jkhy-20260930.htm) [Certain]. Contracts are long-dated. Deconversion fees are lumpy one-offs when clients leave. No foreign country is a material share of revenue [Likely].

## 2. Quality
Strong. TTM ROIC 23.8% (5y fiscal trend "falling", but rising over the last 12 months from 21.8%), operating margin 25.6%, FCF margin 27.6%, FCF/NI 138.2%, net debt/EBITDA 0.13x (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/jkhy/financials/ratios/). [Certain]
Direction change: the 5-year ROIC slide (24.9% in FY2022 to 21.7%, 19.1%, then recovery to 23.6%) bottomed in FY2024. The last year is a recovery, not a fresh decline. [Likely]
Moat: switching costs. Replacing a core processor is multi-year and risky for a small bank, so clients stay for long terms. [Likely] The Q1 FY27 results showed continued core wins and cloud migration; the 10-Q describes processing growth driven by card, digital and faster payments, and cost growth from AI, cloud and security spend (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/779152/000077915226000098/jkhy-20260930.htm). [Likely] Risk factors show no substantive change since last year's filing. [Certain]
