# Jack Henry & Associates (JKHY) — Core initiation — 2026-11-15
## 1. Business
Sells core banking, payments and digital-banking technology to US community banks and credit unions. Revenue has two streams: services and support (cloud hosting, licences, on-premise support) and processing (card, remittance, faster payments, digital). Contracts are long and recurring, and hosting and processing volumes grow with clients' usage [Certain] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/779152/000077915226000098/jkhy-20260930.htm). No foreign country is disclosed as ~10% of revenue; the business is US-centred [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/jkhy/).

## 2. Quality
High-return, cash-generative franchise [Certain]. TTM ROIC is 23.8% and FCF margin 27.6%, with FCF conversion of 138.2% of net income (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/jkhy/financials/ratios/). Net debt/EBITDA is 0.13x.

Direction change: ROIC is down over five fiscal years (24.9% to 23.6%) but up over the last 12 months (21.8% to 23.8%) as margins recovered. Operating margin is 25.6%, against a 2-year high of 26.0%. The recovery is real, not a fiscal-year-end artefact [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/jkhy/financials/ratios/?p=trailing).

The moat is switching cost: replacing a bank's core system is slow and risky, so churn is low [Likely]. Q1 FY27 processing revenue grew 10.7% and services and support 7.5% (5.1% excluding deconversion revenue) [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/779152/000077915226000098/jkhy-20260930.htm). Management cites new-core wins and shorter implementations [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/jkhy/transcripts/795731-citi-s-15th-annual-fintech-conference/).

Watch costs: Q1 R&D rose 16.6% [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/779152/000077915226000098/jkhy-20260930.htm), and cost of revenue includes higher cloud, AI and security spend.

## 3. Valuation
The price (177.67 USD) sits 30% up the 5y range on P/E (24.4x vs 31.7x median), 29% on EV/EBITDA (17.1x) and 15% on P/FCF (17.2x); the FCF yield is 5.80% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/jkhy/statistics/). It trades at a premium to the payments and software peer median (EV/EBITDA +79%, P/E +15%), a reasonable premium for lower beta (0.54) and higher returns [Likely].

The mechanical fair value disagrees: base 123.97 USD (-30.2%), bull 135.98, from FCFE DCF and DCF. The P/E method gives 230.66 base. The methods differ by almost 2x, so treat the fair value as low-reliability. The reverse DCF implies 13.6% year-1 revenue growth fading to terminal, well above the roughly 8% framework management cites [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/jkhy/transcripts/796197-kbw-fintech-ecosystem-conference-2026/). Price is +15.4% above the 200-day MA after a 6-month gain of 25.6%, and RSI is 67.4. Own-history multiples are undemanding; DCF says the price needs above-framework growth [Likely].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- TTM ROIC (ratios page, ?p=trailing) falls below 18%.
- TTM FCF margin (cash-flow page, ?p=trailing) falls below 22%.
- Gross margin (income-statement page) falls below 41%, i.e. cloud, AI and security costs overwhelm pricing.
- Processing revenue growth in the 10-Q MD&A falls below 5% for two consecutive quarters, or core client losses outnumber wins.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Cash conversion is strong: FCF/NI is 138.2% and net debt/EBITDA is 0.13x, so there is little leverage risk. Shares fell 2.22% YoY, a modest net buyback with no dilution [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/jkhy/statistics/). A small acquisition was made in the prior fiscal year [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/779152/000077915226000098/jkhy-20260930.htm). Piotroski F is 6.
