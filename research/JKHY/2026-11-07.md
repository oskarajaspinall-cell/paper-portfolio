# Jack Henry & Associates (JKHY) — Core initiation — 2026-11-07
## 1. Business
Core banking, payments and digital software for US community banks and credit unions. Revenue is "services and support" (cloud hosting on long contracts, licences, maintenance) and "processing" (cards, remittance, faster payments, digital transactions), per the latest 10-Q (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/779152/000077915226000098/jkhy-20260930.htm). Most revenue recurs on multi-year contracts [Certain].

## 2. Quality
Returns remain high and have recovered: ROIC 23.6% in FY2026 (TTM 24.3%) after a low of 19.1% in FY2024; operating margin 25.3% (TTM 25.6%) vs 22.1% in FY2024; FCF conversion 138.2% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/jkhy/financials/ratios/) [Certain]. The moat is switching cost: replacing a bank's core system is slow and risky, so clients rarely leave [Likely]. Q1 FY2027 confirms momentum: processing revenue +10.7% and services and support +7.5% (+5.1% excluding deconversion fees, which are lumpy one-off contract-exit payments) [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/779152/000077915226000098/jkhy-20260930.htm). Headlines report raised FY27 guidance and core wins (source: https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/jack-henry--associates-inc-reports-first-quarter-fiscal-2027-results-302928417.html) [Likely]. Offset: R&D grew faster than revenue, and cloud, AI and security costs are a margin headwind [company-reported, same 10-Q]. Risk Factors show no substantive change [Certain].

## 3. Valuation
At 173.23 USD the stock trades at 23.8x P/E (26th percentile of its 5y range, median 31.7x), 17.6x EV/EBITDA (median 22.5x) and 17.4x P/FCF (median 23.7x); FCF yield 5.76% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/jkhy/statistics/). It is well above peers (EV/EBITDA +93%), but they are lower-quality or ex-growth [Likely]. The mechanical base fair value is now 129.12 USD (bull 141.70), so the price is above even the bull case; the reverse DCF needs 11.9% year-1 revenue growth, above recent reported growth [Likely]. The earlier base value was reached because the price rose, not because the model moved up. Against that, multiples are still below own history and quality has improved. Opportunity is largely spent on a 12-month view but not broken [Guessing]. Verdict: HOLD, no add at this price.

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Operating margin (income statement page) falls below 22% on a fiscal-year basis
- ROIC (ratios page) falls below 19%
- FCF conversion (FCF/NI, ratios/cash flow pages) falls below 100%
- Processing revenue growth (10-Q MD&A) falls below 5% year on year for two consecutive quarters

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
FCF margin 27.3% in FY2026 with conversion of 138.2%. Net debt/EBITDA 0.09x, so the balance sheet is essentially unlevered. Shares outstanding fell 2.22% year on year, so buybacks more than offset dilution [Certain]. Reinvestment (R&D, cloud) is rising, which is appropriate if returns hold [Likely].

## 6. Unusual items
Short interest 6.34% of float, down 7.8% on the month; RSI 64.9 after a 21.2% six-month gain, so near-term momentum is stretched [Certain]. Beta 0.54 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/jkhy/statistics/). Deconversion revenue inflates reported growth and is unpredictable [Likely].
