## 2. Quality
Turnaround, not yet a proven compounder. Operating margin rose from -2.5% (FY2022) to 12.5% TTM, gross margin 56.0%, FCF margin 20.0%, TTM ROIC 23.9% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/tal/financials/ratios/) [Certain]. But ROIC was negative or near zero in four of five fiscal years, so the 5y record is poor [Certain]. Net margin of 28.4% far exceeds the 12.5% operating margin, implying large non-operating income (investment gains/interest) that is unlikely to recur at this level [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/tal/financials/income-statement/). TTM FCF/NI is 70.4%, falling. Net debt/EBITDA is -5.13x (net cash) and Altman Z 2.54 / Piotroski 5 are middling (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/tal/statistics/). A durable moat is not evident: tutoring is fragmented and policy-dependent [Likely].

## 3. Valuation
At 10.17 USD, P/E 6.3x, EV/EBITDA 6.6x and P/FCF 8.8x are all below their 5y minimums (12.1x / 9.0x / 12.6x), and well below peer medians (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/tal/financials/ratios/) [Certain]. FCF yield is 11.32% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/tal/statistics/). The reverse DCF implies revenue falling 21.2% in year 1, a harsh assumption against the recent growth in the earnings headlines [Likely]. Model base fair value 23.92 (+135%) rests mostly on a DCF with a wide range (bear -8.94, bull 56.80) and inform-only status [Guessing]. Part of the cheap earnings is inflated by non-operating gains, so P/E flatters; P/FCF and EV/EBITDA are the cleaner reads. The discount likely reflects China regulatory risk and a sceptical market, not just mispricing [Likely].

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Shares outstanding fell 8.26% YoY, consistent with the buyback mentioned in the Q1 FY2027 call summary (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/tal/statistics/; headline listed in the fact sheet) [Likely]. Net cash and an 11.32% FCF yield give room to keep returning capital. Conversion is solid at 70.4% FCF/NI TTM, but flattered by the gap between net and operating income.
