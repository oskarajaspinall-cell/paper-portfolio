# Booking Holdings Inc. (BKNG) — Core initiation — 2026-10-07

## 1. Business
Booking Holdings runs online travel marketplaces — Booking.com, Priceline, Agoda, KAYAK, OpenTable, Rentalcars.com, FareHarbor — connecting travelers with hotels, flights, rentals and restaurants. Revenue is mainly commission/merchant fees on bookings plus advertising (KAYAK, BKNG Ads) and OpenTable/FareHarbor subscription-agency fees [Certain] (source: https://www.bookingholdings.com/).

## 2. Quality
An asset-light marketplace with best-in-class returns: TTM ROIC 92.5% and ROCE 114.3%, both rising over five years (+28.3pp and +60.4pp respectively) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/bkng/financials/ratios/). Gross margin (87.2% TTM) and operating margin (34.9% TTM) have expanded every year since FY2021 [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/bkng/financials/income-statement/), consistent with scale economics in a global accommodation-supply network and the Genius loyalty program that lifts direct (lower-cost) bookings. The main moat risk is AI-agent disintermediation: multiple recent headlines flag Meta's "Muse" AI agent as a threat to OTA economics, with CNBC citing a "consumer inertia" trade hitting Booking and Airbnb shares [Likely] (source: https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/29/jim-cramer-meta-muse-consumer-inertia.html). Booking is responding with its own AI layer — the Lola assistant launched Sept 29, 2026, and Agoda's AI room-selection bot — but it is too early to say whether this defends share [Guessing] (source: https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260929615712/en/Lola-the-AI-Assistant-for-Experiences-Debuts-With-New-Membership-Plans-and-Travel-and-Lifestyle-Partners/). FCF conversion, while still well above 100% (132.3% TTM), has declined from 216.0% in FY2021, a trend worth watching [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/bkng/financials/cash-flow-statement/).

## 3. Valuation
BKNG trades at 17.4x P/E, 11.7x EV/EBITDA, 4.3x EV/Sales and 12.4x P/FCF, each below its own five-year range minimum (-14%, -16%, -4%, -1% respectively) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/bkng/financials/ratios/). On P/E and P/FCF it also trades at a discount to the EXPE/ABNB/TCOM/TRIP peer median (-34% and vs 9.7x median, resp.), but on EV/EBITDA and EV/Sales it screens at a premium (+17% and +140%) because the peer basket mixes lower-margin names (EXPE, TCOM) with higher-growth ABNB [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/bkng/statistics/). FCF yield is 8.05% [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/bkng/statistics/). Net: the price assumes durably slower growth and/or margin pressure from AI disruption versus BKNG's own history — a reasonable discount for the risk in Section 2, not an extreme one, given leverage and quality metrics remain intact.

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Operating margin (income statement page) falls below 30% for a full fiscal year, from 35.2% in FY2025 — would signal competitive share loss.
- ROIC (ratios page) falls below 50%, from 92.5% TTM — would signal returns being competed away.
- FCF conversion, FCF/NI (cash flow + income statement pages), falls below 100% — would signal earnings-quality deterioration.
- Net debt/EBITDA (balance sheet + income statement pages, site definition) rises above 1.0x, from 0.33x TTM — would signal balance-sheet stress limiting buyback capacity.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Shares outstanding fell 4.16% year-on-year, implying substantial buybacks funded from FCF [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/bkng/statistics/). TTM FCF margin is 33.8% and FCF conversion 132.3% of net income, both strongly cash-generative [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/bkng/financials/cash-flow-statement/). Net debt/EBITDA is low at 0.33-0.36x depending on definition [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/bkng/financials/ratios/), leaving headroom to continue repurchases without releveraging. No dividend is evident in the fact sheet.

## 6. Unusual items
None. Short interest is low at 2.62% of float and falling (-2.6% month-on-month); institutional ownership is high at 98.2% [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/bkng/statistics/). Altman Z-score 6.49 and Piotroski F-score 6 show no distress or accounting red flags [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/bkng/statistics/).
