# PTC Inc. (PTC) — Core initiation — 2026-10-08
## 1. Business
PTC sells industrial design and lifecycle software (Creo CAD, Windchill PLM) and IoT/AR platforms (ThingWorx, Kepware) to manufacturers, mostly on multi-year subscription contracts [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/ptc/). Classified Software - Application (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/ptc/).

## 2. Quality
Profitability is strong and improving: ROIC 19.7% TTM (up from 13.9% FY2021), ROCE 22.7% TTM (+8.1pp over 5y), gross margin 84.5% TTM, operating margin 39.8% TTM (+14.8pp over 5y), FCF margin 31.7% TTM (+12.2pp) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/ptc/financials/ratios/, https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/ptc/financials/income-statement/). Net debt/EBITDA fell to 0.98x from 2.74x FY2021, and Piotroski F-Score is 6 / Altman Z 5.71, both consistent with a sound balance sheet [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/ptc/financials/ratios/, https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/ptc/statistics/). This margin trajectory is consistent with switching-cost economics typical of embedded engineering software [Likely].
However, PTC signed a definitive merger agreement to be acquired by Schneider Electric, filed as an 8-K (Item 1.01) on 2026-10-05 (source: https://www.sec.gov/cgi-bin/browse-edgar?action=getcompany&company=PTC+Inc&type=8-K). Standalone business quality is no longer the dominant driver of 12-month return; deal completion risk is [Certain].

## 3. Valuation
Current multiples look statistically cheap vs history and peers (P/E 18.8x, below the 5y min of 29.5x and peer median 80.0x; EV/EBITDA 17.4x vs 5y min 23.0x and peer median 42.0x) only because TTM earnings surged on margin expansion, not because the shares are mispriced (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/ptc/financials/ratios/). The fact sheet's own-history fair value (DCF-weighted) is base 133.15, bear 75.82, bull 157.73 — all below the 193.60 last close (-31%/-61%/-19%) (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/ptc/, computed in research/PTC/factsheet-2026-10-08.md). Even the bull standalone case sits below the market price, so the entire current valuation rests on the pending acquisition closing near current levels, not on organic cash flow [Certain]. The reverse DCF implies the price embeds 23.9% year-1 revenue growth fading to terminal — far above what the quality metrics above support on a standalone basis [Likely].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- The merger agreement is terminated (confirmed by an SEC 8-K) and standalone ROIC (ratios page) holds above 15%, evidencing the quality case survives independent of deal pricing.
- Gross margin (income statement) falls below 75%, signalling pricing or competitive erosion in the core CAD/PLM franchise.
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) rises back above 2.0x, reversing the deleveraging that underpins the balance-sheet quality score.
- FCF margin (cash flow/income statement) falls below 25%, reversing the cash-conversion improvement cited above.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
FCF margin 31.7% TTM with FCF/NI conversion of 76.4% TTM (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/ptc/financials/cash-flow-statement/). Shares outstanding fell 1.95% YoY, consistent with historical buybacks, and net debt/EBITDA improved to 0.98x (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/ptc/statistics/, https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/ptc/financials/ratios/) [Certain]. Capital-allocation discretion is customarily constrained while a merger agreement is pending, so this history has limited forward relevance [Likely].

## 6. Unusual items
PTC agreed to be acquired by Schneider Electric; the SEC shows an 8-K (Item 1.01, material definitive agreement) filed 2026-10-05 (source: https://www.sec.gov/cgi-bin/browse-edgar?action=getcompany&company=PTC+Inc&type=8-K), consistent with the fact sheet's news headlines reporting the deal the same day [Certain]. The stock jumped 33.5% on 19.8x average volume that session and has held 103% of the move since, indicating the market has priced in deal completion (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/ptc/history/) [Certain]. The fact sheet also lists two shareholder-rights law-firm "investigations" into deal fairness (Halper Sadeh, Brodsky & Smith); these are standard notice solicitations that accompany most announced U.S. mergers, not evidence of a specific problem, but add headline and timing risk (sources: https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/10/07/3376169/0/en/halper-sadeh-llc-is-investigating-whether-ptc-and-opch-are-obtaining-fair-deals-for-their-shareholders.html, https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/10/05/3374952/0/en/brodsky-smith-shareholder-update-notifying-investors-of-the-following-investigations-rxo-inc-nyse-rxo-ptc-inc-nasdaq-ptc-lifecore-biomedical-inc-nasdaq-lfcr-wafd-inc-nasdaq-wafd.html) [Likely]. Short interest is 5.51% of float and rose 4.4% month-on-month, consistent with arbitrage-related positioning around the deal (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/ptc/statistics/) [Likely].
