# Deckers Outdoor (DECK) — Core initiation — 2026-11-15
## 1. Business
Designs and sells footwear and apparel through two growth brands, HOKA (performance running) and UGG (lifestyle), all made by independent manufacturers. Wholesale is about 70% of six-month sales and direct-to-consumer the rest [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/910521/000162828026068116/deck-20260930.htm). International sales grew faster than domestic, and the filing names no single foreign country [Certain] (same filing, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/910521/000162828026068116/deck-20260930.htm).

## 2. Quality
Returns are exceptional: TTM ROIC 71.9%, ROCE 45.7%, gross margin 58.9%, operating margin 23.6%, FCF conversion 99.3% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/deck/financials/ratios/) [Certain]. Net cash (net debt/EBITDA -0.48x) [Certain]. The moat is brand heat and full-price discipline, not structure: both brands are fashion/performance-cycle dependent [Likely].

Direction: ROIC is rising over five fiscal years but down from 78.5% to 71.9% over 12 months, 46.9pp below its 2-year high; ROCE, operating margin and FCF margin are flat to rising (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/deck/financials/ratios/?p=trailing). Part of the ROIC dip likely reflects a seasonal inventory build in the 10-Q (https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/910521/000162828026068116/deck-20260930.htm) [Likely]. Margins are holding, so the dip is not yet a franchise problem [Likely].

## 3. Valuation
At 86.82 USD the stock trades at 11.5x P/E, 7.9x EV/EBITDA and 11.0x P/FCF, all below the bottom of their 5y ranges (13.9x, 9.3x, 12.9x) and 31-47% under peers on those measures (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/deck/statistics/) [Certain]. FCF yield is 9.08% [Certain]. The reverse DCF implies -0.3% revenue growth in year 1 [Certain]. Mechanical base fair value is 110.02 USD (+26.7%), bear 37.63, bull 178.57, a wide range; DCF and P/E disagree on the base (122.61 vs 84.84) [Likely].

The market is pricing a durable end to growth. Trend is not supportive: -12.0% vs the 200-day average, 3m/6m relative strength -3.9pp / -9.2pp vs SPY [Certain]. Cheap, but not yet working.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Buybacks are heavy and shares are down 6.97% YoY (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/deck/statistics/) [Certain]. With net cash and about 99% FCF conversion this is well covered, and repurchases below historical multiples create value [Likely]. The 10-Q cash flow statement confirms large six-month repurchases (https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/910521/000162828026068116/deck-20260930.htm) [Certain].

## 6. Unusual items
- Q2 results (22 Oct) came with a cut to full-year sales guidance, blamed on rival discounting and slower HOKA/UGG growth (source: https://www.wsj.com/business/earnings/deckers-cuts-sales-outlook-amid-slower-hoka-ugg-growth-84f46d5c; https://www.reuters.com/business/deckers-results-show-aggressive-discounts-rivals-are-hitting-premium-brands-2026-10-22/) [Likely]. The 10-Q MD&A adds: softer domestic DTC traffic and conversion, higher promotional intensity, and a failed European 3PL transition raising chargeback reserves (https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/910521/000162828026068116/deck-20260930.htm) [Certain].
- New in the 10-Q: refunds of previously paid IEEPA tariffs were booked as a cost-of-sales reduction, so near-term margins are flattered by a one-off; management says mitigation will not fully offset incremental tariffs [Certain] (same filing URL as above).
- Short interest 8.34% of float, down 6.9% on the month (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/deck/statistics/) [Certain].
