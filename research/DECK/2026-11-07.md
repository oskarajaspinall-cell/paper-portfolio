# Deckers Outdoor (DECK) — Core initiation — 2026-11-07
## 1. Business
Designs and sells footwear and apparel through independent manufacturers, mainly HOKA (performance running/outdoor) and UGG (iconic casual/seasonal). Sells via wholesale retailers and distributors and direct-to-consumer (e-commerce and stores). Economics rest on brand pull enabling full-price selling and high gross margin (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/910521/000162828026068116/deck-20260930.htm) [Certain].
## 2. Quality
Returns are exceptional and stable: TTM ROIC 71.9%, ROCE 45.7%, operating margin 23.6%, gross margin 58.9%, with net cash (net debt/EBITDA -0.48x) and FCF conversion 99.3% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/deck/financials/ratios/) [Certain]. The moat is brand heat plus full-price discipline, not scale; it is real but fashion-dependent [Likely]. Evidence of pressure is now visible: the 2026-10-22 results showed discounting by rivals hurting premium brands and a cut to full-year sales guidance (source: https://www.reuters.com/business/deckers-results-show-aggressive-discounts-rivals-are-hitting-premium-brands-2026-10-22/) [Likely]. The filing cites softer domestic DTC traffic and mixed product updates, while management held full-price selling (SEC delta, source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/910521/000162828026068116/deck-20260930.htm) [Certain]. Earnings still beat the quarter's expectations, so margins, not the brand's franchise, are intact so far [Likely]. Growth is slowing; returns are not yet [Likely].
## 3. Valuation
At 86.71 USD the stock trades below its own 5y minimum on P/E (11.4x vs 13.9x min, 17.6x median), EV/EBITDA (7.9x vs 9.3x min) and P/FCF (11.0x), with FCF yield 9.09% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/deck/statistics/) [Certain]. It is 37% below the peer median P/E and 32% below on EV/EBITDA [Certain]. The reverse DCF implies -1.2% revenue growth in year 1, fading to terminal growth (fact sheet fair value) [Certain]. Base fair value 112.86 (+30.2%), bear 38.06, bull 182.96; the bear case is wide and reflects a fade of margins [Likely]. The price assumes the brands stall, which is harsher than current results; the risk is that HOKA share loss is structural [Guessing]. Shares are down 6.97% YoY from buybacks [Certain].
## 4. What would prove this wrong
- TTM gross margin (income statement page) falls below 54%, showing full-price discipline is failing.
- TTM operating margin (income statement page) falls below 19%.
- Annual revenue (income statement page) declines year on year in FY2027, i.e. HOKA and UGG both shrink.
- TTM FCF conversion (cash-flow page) falls below 75% for two consecutive reports, signalling inventory build or earnings quality problems.
## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Net cash, no leverage, FCF conversion about 99% of net income and a 6.97% YoY reduction in share count; buybacks at a sub-historical multiple are accretive [Likely]. No dilution or acquisitions of concern [Likely]. Cash conversion has been strong since FY2023 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/deck/financials/cash-flow-statement/) [Certain].
## 6. Unusual items
Short interest 8.19% of float, up 14.3% month on month (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/deck/statistics/) [Certain]. Filing flags a European logistics transition that disrupted shipments and raised customer chargeback reserves, plus tariff cost exposure partly offset by IEEPA tariff refunds that flatter reported cost of sales [Likely] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/910521/000162828026068116/deck-20260930.htm). Guidance cut on 2026-10-22 (source: https://www.wsj.com/business/earnings/deckers-cuts-sales-outlook-amid-slower-hoka-ugg-growth-84f46d5c) [Likely]. Decision lean: HOLD; thesis intact, cheap, but growth deceleration is unresolved [Guessing].
