# Deckers Outdoor (DECK) — Core initiation — 2026-11-16
## 1. Business
Designs and sells footwear and apparel through two brands, UGG (casual, seasonal) and HOKA (performance running), made entirely by independent manufacturers. Sales go via wholesale and direct-to-consumer. International was $644,712k of $1,480,554k quarterly net sales, about 44% (`[company-reported]`, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/910521/000162828026068116/deck-20260930.htm); no single foreign country is disclosed there, so no country overlay trigger [Likely].

## 2. Quality
Excellent economics [Certain]: TTM gross margin 58.9%, operating margin 23.6%, ROCE 45.7%, ROE 44.3%, FCF conversion 99.3%, and net cash (net debt/EBITDA -0.48x) (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/deck/financials/ratios/). The moat is brand heat, not structure [Likely]: HOKA and UGG are fashion-exposed, and rivals' discounting is reaching them. The 10-Q says domestic DTC traffic and conversion softened and consumers bunched purchases into promotional periods, while the company held full-price discipline (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/910521/000162828026068116/deck-20260930.htm) [Certain]. Management cut full-year sales guidance on 2026-10-22 (source: https://www.wsj.com/business/earnings/deckers-cuts-sales-outlook-amid-slower-hoka-ugg-growth-84f46d5c) [Likely].
Direction change: ROIC is 71.9% TTM vs 78.5% a year earlier and 118.8% at its 2-year high. Operating margin (+0.0pp) and FCF margin (+0.2pp) are flat, so the ROIC fall comes mainly from a larger capital base after cash was deployed, not from weaker margins [Likely]. Short interest is 8.34% of float.

## 3. Valuation
The price assumes a no-growth business [Certain]: the reverse DCF implies -0.4% year-1 revenue growth. P/E is 11.0x against a 5y range of 13.9x–31.8x (below the minimum) and a peer median of 16.3x; EV/EBITDA is 7.9x vs 12.0x median; FCF yield is 9.08%. Base fair value is 110.15 vs a 86.82 close (+26.9%); bear 37.65, bull 178.77 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/deck/statistics/). The wide bear/bull range shows how much depends on whether growth merely slows or reverses [Likely]. Trend is weak: the price is 11.8% below its 200-day average, 12-month change +0.5%, and it trails SPY by 9.9pp over 12 months. This is a cheap quality name still earning its derating, not yet a working trend [Likely].

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Shares fell 6.97% YoY through buybacks, funded by FCF margin of 18.9% TTM [Certain]. Net cash narrowed from -1.14x to -0.48x net debt/EBITDA, so repurchases are running ahead of the cash generated or timing differs [Likely]. Tariff refunds (IEEPA) flatter the near-term cost of sales and are one-off: about $46,000k recorded in the half-year (`[company-reported]`, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/910521/000162828026068116/deck-20260930.htm). Treat reported margins as inflated by that [Likely].

## 6. Unusual items
- A transition to a new European third-party logistics provider disrupted fulfilment in peak season and raised chargeback reserves; management reports improvement but residual impact may persist (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/910521/000162828026068116/deck-20260930.htm) [Certain].
- Tariff exposure persists: management does not expect mitigation to fully offset incremental tariff costs this year (same filing) [Certain].
- Board appointment on 2026-10-22 (source: https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20261022057565/en/Deckers-Brands-Appoints-Rati-Sahi-Levesque-to-Board-of-Directors/); immaterial.
