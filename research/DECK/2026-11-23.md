# Deckers Outdoor (DECK) — Core initiation — 2026-11-23
## 1. Business
Designs and sells UGG and HOKA footwear, apparel and accessories, made entirely by independent manufacturers. For the six months to 30 Sep 2026 wholesale was 70.4% of net sales and DTC 29.6%; international was 45.9% and domestic 54.1% [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/910521/000162828026068116/deck-20260930.htm). No single foreign country or region is broken out, so none is named. [Certain]

## 2. Quality
Very high returns on capital: FY2026 ROIC 104.4%, TTM ROE 44.3%, gross margin 58.9%, operating margin 23.6%, net cash (net debt/EBITDA -0.48x) (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/deck/financials/ratios/). FCF conversion is about 99% of net income, so earnings are cash-backed. [Certain]

Moat: two brands with pricing power (full-price selling, gross margin up 6.7pp over five years). It is a brand moat, not structural, and fashion-driven. [Likely]

Direction change: TTM ROIC fell to 66.5% from 72.7% a year earlier, versus a 2-year high of 131.4% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/deck/financials/ratios/?p=trailing). Operating margin is flat year on year and ROCE rose, so the ROIC drop looks like normalising invested capital and cash build, not margin erosion. [Likely]

The Q2 10-Q delta adds: weaker domestic DTC traffic and conversion, heavier industry promotion, mixed product updates and a European 3PL transition that raised chargeback reserves (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/910521/000162828026068116/deck-20260930.htm). Management cut its sales outlook (source: https://www.wsj.com/business/earnings/deckers-cuts-sales-outlook-amid-slower-hoka-ugg-growth-84f46d5c). [Certain]

## 3. Valuation
At 77.94 USD, P/E is 10.3x against a 5y range of 13.9x–31.8x, EV/EBITDA 7.0x (5y min 9.3x) and FCF yield 10.12%. Every multiple is below its 5y minimum. Against peers (NKE, ONON, CROX, BIRK) it is 32% cheaper on EV/EBITDA and 48% cheaper on P/FCF, but 5% dearer on P/B. The reverse DCF implies revenue shrinking 3.4% in year 1, which is harsher than the guidance cut suggests. Mechanical base fair value is 111.00 (+42.4%); bear 37.78, bull 180.08. The range is wide, the DCF bear case is very low, and the base leans on a through-cycle DCF. [Likely]

The price is 20.3% below its 200-day average and 6-month relative strength versus SPY is -13.7pp: the market is pricing a fading brand cycle. [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/deck/statistics/)

## 4. What would prove this wrong

- Operating margin (income statement, TTM) falls below 20%.

- FCF conversion (FCF/NI, cash flow page, fiscal year) falls below 80%.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Net cash balance sheet and shares down 6.97% YoY. Buybacks were $637,463 thousand in the six months to 30 Sep 2026 [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/910521/000162828026068116/deck-20260930.htm). Buying back stock at a 10.3x P/E is value-accretive if the brand holds. FCF margin is 18.9% TTM, flat on a year earlier. [Certain]
