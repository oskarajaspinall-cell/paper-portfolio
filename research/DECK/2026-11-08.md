# Deckers Outdoor (DECK) — Core initiation — 2026-11-08
## 1. Business
Designs and sells footwear through independent manufacturers: HOKA (performance running) and UGG (iconic casual), plus smaller brands, via wholesale and DTC. [Certain] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/910521/000162828026068116/deck-20260930.htm) International was $1,146,815k of $2,500,085k six-month net sales, about 46% [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/910521/000162828026068116/deck-20260930.htm); the filing gives no single country or region share, so no one foreign market is shown at ~10%+.

## 2. Quality
Two powerful brands earn high returns: TTM ROCE 45.7%, ROE 44.3%, gross margin 58.9%, operating margin 23.6%, FCF conversion 99.3% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/deck/financials/ratios/). [Certain] Margins have risen over five years, and net cash remains. [Certain]

Direction change addressed: TTM ROIC fell from 78.5% to 71.9% and sits far below its 2-year high of 118.8%, but ROCE rose to 45.7% and operating and FCF margins are flat (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/deck/financials/ratios/?p=trailing). The ROIC slide therefore reflects a larger invested-capital base (inventory build) rather than margin erosion. [Likely] Management reported softer US DTC demand, heavier industry promotions in athletic, mixed product updates and a European 3PL disruption (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/910521/000162828026068116/deck-20260930.htm). [Certain] Reuters reports rival discounting is hitting premium brands (source: https://www.reuters.com/business/deckers-results-show-aggressive-discounts-rivals-are-hitting-premium-brands-2026-10-22/); the sales outlook was cut (source: https://www.wsj.com/business/earnings/deckers-cuts-sales-outlook-amid-slower-hoka-ugg-growth-84f46d5c). [Likely] Moat is brand strength, which is only partly tested by this soft patch. [Guessing]

## 3. Valuation
At 86.71 USD, P/E is 11.4x, below its 5y minimum of 13.9x and 30% under the peer median of 16.5x; EV/EBITDA 7.9x versus a 5y median of 12.0x; P/FCF 11.0x versus 25.6x median; FCF yield 9.09% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/deck/statistics/). Reverse DCF implies -1.2% revenue growth in year 1, fading to terminal growth. [Certain] Base fair value is 112.86, 30.2% above the price; bear 38.06, bull 182.96, so the range is wide (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/deck/). The price assumes the growth stall persists and returns normalise lower. [Likely] Price is 12.3% below the 200-day average with relative strength of -16.8pp over six months: the market has de-rated on slowing growth, not on a collapse in profitability. [Likely]

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Gross margin (TTM, income statement page) falls below 55%.
- ROCE (ratios page, TTM) falls below 38%.
- FCF margin (TTM, cash flow page) falls below 14%.
- Net sales (income statement page) decline year on year for two consecutive quarters, signalling brand fade rather than a soft patch.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Buybacks are the main use of cash: about 6.45 million shares for roughly $637.5 million over six months [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/910521/000162828026068116/deck-20260930.htm); shares are down 6.97% year on year (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/deck/statistics/). [Certain] Repurchases at 11x earnings look accretive. [Likely] Inventory rose from $487.0 million to $860.8 million in six months [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/910521/000162828026068116/deck-20260930.htm); partly seasonal but ahead of softer demand, so markdown risk is real. [Likely] TTM net debt/EBITDA is -0.48x, versus -1.14x at FY2026, as buybacks outpaced cash flow. [Certain]
