# Deckers Outdoor Corporation (DECK) — Core initiation — 2026-10-07

## 1. Business
Deckers designs, markets and distributes footwear and apparel through two scale brands — HOKA (performance running) and UGG (casual/lifestyle) — plus smaller Teva and Koolaburra lines, sold via wholesale and direct-to-consumer channels [Certain] (source: https://www.deckers.com). HOKA's growth has historically offset UGG's seasonal (winter-skewed) sales pattern [Likely] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/910521/000162828026037664/deck-20260331.htm).

## 2. Quality
Returns on capital are exceptional and still rising: TTM ROIC 90.6%, ROCE 44.7%, ROE 42.6%, each up materially over the 5-year trend (ROIC +43.6pp) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/deck/financials/ratios/). Gross margin has expanded to 57.8% TTM (+6.7pp over 5y) with FCF conversion at 110% of net income, funded from a net-cash balance sheet (net debt/EBITDA -0.85x) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/deck/financials/ratios/). The moat is brand equity (HOKA's running-technology credibility; UGG's lifestyle-icon status), not low-cost manufacturing — the 10-K flags that "barriers to entry have been reduced by access to offshore manufacturing" [Likely] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/910521/000162828026037664/deck-20260331.htm). Concentration risk is real: two brands drive the business, one customer carries 18.5% of trade receivables, and management warns of "pricing and promotional pressure across our brands" [Likely] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/910521/000162828026037664/deck-20260331.htm). This has not yet shown in the numbers — margins and returns still climb — but it is the risk to track.

## 3. Valuation
The stock trades below its own 5-year multiple range on every earnings/cash-flow metric: P/E 11.3x (5y range 13.9x–31.8x), EV/EBITDA 7.5x (5y min 9.3x), P/FCF 9.9x (5y min 12.9x) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/deck/financials/ratios/). It is also cheaper than peers (CROX, ONON, NKE, VFC) on P/E (-40%), EV/EBITDA (-32%) and P/FCF (-33%), though EV/Sales and P/B are above peer median (+21%/+28%), reflecting Deckers' higher margins and asset-light balance sheet [Certain] (source: fact sheet peer statistics pages). The shares are down 20.6% over 12 months, 37pp behind SPY, and sit near 6-month/52-week lows (77.20) after a run of lower swing highs (104.88→93.09→83.00) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/deck/history/). The price is therefore assuming a meaningful, durable slowdown in HOKA/UGG growth and/or margin compression — not merely a pause — which is inconsistent with still-rising ROIC, ROCE and gross margin in the FY2026 numbers [Likely].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Gross margin (income statement page) falls below 55% for two consecutive quarters, signalling the competitive/tariff pressure flagged in the 10-K is biting.
- Operating margin (income statement page) drops below 20%, reversing the 5-year expansion trend.
- ROIC (ratios page) falls below 70%, indicating the brand moat is eroding faster than the market has priced.
- Net debt/EBITDA (balance sheet + income statement) turns positive and rises, showing capital discipline has broken down.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
FCF conversion is 110% of net income (TTM), and the share count is down 5.7% year-over-year, consistent with ongoing buybacks funded entirely from operating cash flow on a net-cash balance sheet [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/deck/statistics/, https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/deck/financials/cash-flow-statement/). No dividend is evident in the fact sheet. This is a self-funding compounder with no leverage risk.

## 6. Unusual items
Short interest is elevated and rising — 7.02% of float, up 20.4% month-over-month — indicating a genuine bear/bull disagreement, not a consensus call [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/deck/statistics/). A widely reported investor took a new position in early October 2026 per a third-party headline; this is noted for context only and is not used as a decision input [Guessing] (source: https://www.tipranks.com/news/deckers-outdoor-stock-deck-jumps-as-michael-burry-opens-position-in-the-shoe-company). Next earnings is October 22, 2026, shortly after this initiation [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/deck/).
