## 2. Quality
A good business whose returns have risen but are now rolling over [Certain].
- ROIC 6.4% (FY2021) to 12.7% TTM; operating margin 9.8% to 18.0% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tyo/4578/financials/ratios/).
- Direction change: ROIC TTM fell from 16.9% to 12.7% in a year, 5.1pp below its 2-year high; operating margin fell from 19.1% to 18.0% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tyo/4578/financials/ratios/?p=trailing). Management flagged a profit decline for FY2026 from generic competition, offset by launches (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tyo/4578/transcripts/406289-q4-2025/) [Likely]. The July call revised full-year forecasts upward (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tyo/4578/transcripts/638303-q2-2026/) [Likely].
- Moat is patent-based and finite: the margin gain is partly a patent-protected franchise, which generics erode [Likely]. Net cash (net debt/EBITDA -0.46x), Piotroski 7, Altman Z 5.17 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tyo/4578/statistics/) [Certain].

## 3. Valuation
Price 12,300 JPY after +49.3% in 12 months, +10.5% above the 200-day (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tyo/4578/statistics/) [Certain].
- P/E 17.0x sits at 38% of its 5y range (12.9x-23.6x) and 21% below the peer median of 21.4x [Certain].
- EV/EBITDA 9.8x, P/FCF 20.7x and P/B 2.0x are all above their 5y maxima (8.9x, 17.8x, 1.7x); EV/EBITDA is still 21% below peers [Certain]. The cheapness is relative to peers, not to its own history.
- FCF yield 4.83%.
- Mechanical fair value base 9,960.25 (-19% vs close), bear 6,770.09, bull 14,511.31 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tyo/4578/). The reverse DCF implies 9.6% year-1 revenue growth, demanding for a company facing generic erosion [Likely].
The price assumes the launches fully replace the lost products [Likely].
