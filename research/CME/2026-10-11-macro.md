# Macro overlay: CME Group Inc. (CME) — 2026-10-11

## Assessment
Chain: fed funds/short rates → interest income on clearing-member cash collateral (a direct CME revenue line) and rate volatility → trading volume in CME's rates complex (largest franchise, record ADV) → revenue/margin **(inference)**; discount-rate channel is muted by CME's low beta (0.27) [Certain, factsheet].

Facts (FRED, 2026-10-08/09): 10y real yield (DFII10) 2.87%, +44bp/1m, +56bp/3m; 10y Treasury (DGS10) 5.22%, +42bp/+68bp; 2y (DGS2) 4.75%, +36bp/+59bp; fed funds (DFF) 3.88%, +25bp/+26bp; breakevens (T10YIE) 2.33%, roughly flat; HY spread 3.15%, +48bp/1m; VIX 15.4, low/falling.

Market pricing (proxy, [Likely]): 2y yield (4.75%) above effective fed funds (3.88%) reads as "no near-term cuts priced"; the back-up is real-rate-led, not breakeven-led. **(inference)** Record Q3 ADV (+22% international) and above-peer EV/EBITDA/EV/Sales multiples suggest this higher-for-longer, higher-volatility backdrop is already substantially priced.

Macro bull: continued yield back-up/elevated short rates sustain record collateral interest income and rates-complex volume, supporting earnings and the premium multiple. Macro bear: a dovish pivot (DFF/yields fall, vol subsides) curtails interest income and normalizes volume off records just as EV/EBITDA (+34%) and EV/Sales (+49%) vs peers leave no margin of safety — a double hit.

Tilt: small, toward bull — CME's volume/interest-income engine benefits from rate volatility in either direction, an offset pure valuation work underweights, but it's mostly already visible in record ADV and the rich multiple, so incremental upside from here is limited.
