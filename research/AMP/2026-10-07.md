# Ameriprise Financial, Inc. (AMP) — Core initiation — 2026-10-07

## 1. Business
Diversified financial services holding company. Advice & Wealth Management (financial advisors charging fees on client assets) is the core earnings driver; Asset Management (Columbia Threadneedle) earns management fees on third-party AUM; Retirement & Protection sells annuities and insurance. Revenue is predominantly recurring, asset/fee-based rather than transactional [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/amp/).

## 2. Quality
The model is asset-light and highly cash-generative: TTM ROE is 63.4% and FCF conversion (FCF/net income) is 204% [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/amp/financials/ratios/, https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/amp/financials/cash-flow-statement/). The durable part of the moat is the advisor network and embedded client relationships, which carry switching costs, plus the Columbia Threadneedle brand. However, profitability is eroding at the margin: gross margin has fallen 5.5pp and net margin 6.7pp over five years [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/amp/financials/income-statement/), consistent with fee-rate and/or advisor-comp pressure. A 2026-09-24 TheFly/Citywire item reports Ameriprise is offering bonuses and fee discounts to stem advisor attrition [Likely] (source: https://www.tipranks.com/news/the-fly/ameriprise-offering-bonuses-to-try-to-stem-recent-attrition-citywire-says-thefly-news-2) — a direct threat to the fee-generating asset base, and a plausible driver of the margin trend. ROCE (3.9% TTM) is a structural artifact of the fee-revenue model, not a moat read. Net: a good, scaled, recurring-revenue business whose core asset (the advisor channel) is under competitive pressure [Likely].

## 3. Valuation
At 12.0x TTM P/E, AMP sits at the 40th percentile of its own five-year range (9.9x–15.2x) — in line with history, not stretched [Certain]. Against peers (LPLA, SCHW, RJF, TROW) it trades at a 24% P/E discount (12.0x vs 15.8x median) and a 65% P/FCF discount (5.4x vs 15.5x median) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/amp/financials/ratios/ and peer statistics pages). P/B is 122% above the peer median (6.9x vs 3.1x), but this reflects a buyback-depleted book rather than overvaluation in an asset-light fee business where P/B is a weak metric. The combined read is a market pricing in continued margin/fee erosion (and the advisor-attrition risk above) rather than paying for the superior cash conversion; price already carries a 15.5pp 12-month relative-strength shortfall to SPY [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/amp/history/).

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Net margin (income statement page) falls below its 5-year low of 15.9%, confirming fee/margin erosion is accelerating rather than stabilizing.
- ROE (ratios page) falls below its 5-year low of 57.9%, signalling core profitability is breaking down, not just normalizing.
- FCF conversion, FCF/net income (cash flow + income statement pages) falls below its 5-year low of 93.8%, indicating cash-generation quality is deteriorating.
- Shares outstanding YoY (statistics page) turns positive (buybacks stop/reverse), indicating management itself has lost confidence in the cash-generation thesis.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
FCF margin is 40.6% TTM and rising (+19.2pp over five years), with FCF running at over double net income [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/amp/financials/cash-flow-statement/). Management is returning this aggressively: shares outstanding are down 5.22% YoY [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/amp/statistics/), and the board authorized an additional $5.5bn buyback through September 2028 on 2026-09-29 [Certain] (source: https://www.tipranks.com/news/the-fly/ameriprise-announces-additional-5-5b-share-repurchase-authorization-thefly-news). This is a capital-light business that needs little reinvestment, so nearly all FCF is available for distribution.

## 6. Unusual items
Short interest is 3.10% of float, up 10.5% month-on-month — a modest increase, not yet a crowded short [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/amp/statistics/). Altman Z-score (0.71) looks distressed but is not a reliable signal for a financial-services balance sheet (the model is built for industrials); Piotroski F-Score is a middling 5. No governance or accounting red flags identified. Otherwise none material beyond the advisor-attrition item already covered in Quality.
