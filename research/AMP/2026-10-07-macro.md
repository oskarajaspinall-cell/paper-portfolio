# Macro overlay: Ameriprise Financial, Inc. (AMP) — 2026-10-07
## Assessment
AMP's fee revenue (Advice & Wealth Management, Columbia Threadneedle) scales with AUM, a function of equity/bond market levels, so the rates/risk-appetite backdrop is a secondary driver of both revenue and AMP's multiple, on top of the advisor-attrition/fee-compression thesis already in the note [Likely] **(inference)**.

Facts (FRED, 2026-10-05/06): 10y real yield (DFII10) 2.95%, +0.52pp (1m)/+0.71pp (3m); 10y nominal (DGS10) 5.31%, +0.83pp (3m); fed funds (DFF) 3.88%, +0.25pp (1m and 3m); 10y breakeven (T10YIE) 2.36%, +0.11pp (3m, essentially flat); HY credit spread (BAMLH0A0HYM2) 3.12%, +0.4pp (3m); VIX 15.52, flat (3m) [Certain]. Market pricing: 2y yield (4.84%) sits ~0.96pp above effective fed funds (3.88%), a proxy suggesting the market prices further tightening/term-premium rather than cuts [Likely, proxy] **(inference)**. Since breakevens are flat, the yield rise is a real-rate/term-premium repricing, not an inflation surprise — the kind of move that compresses equity multiples and AUM growth without lifting nominal fee bases.

Bull: real yields stabilize or retrace, credit spreads stay contained, AUM growth resumes, AMP's 12.0x P/E re-rates toward the 15.8x peer median. Bear: real yields extend higher on hawkish Fed repricing (DFF already +0.25pp), credit spreads widen further, risk-off marks down AUM and lifts the equity risk premium on AMP's already-discounted multiple. Tilt: moderate toward bear — the 3m facts (real yields +0.71pp, HY spreads +0.4pp, dollar +2.86 1m) show tightening already underway, consistent with AMP's own 3m underperformance (-2.1% vs SPY +3.7%, factsheet) [Likely] **(inference)**.
