# Ameriprise Financial (AMP) — Core initiation — 2026-11-23
## 1. Business
US wealth manager and asset manager. Fee income from advisor-led wealth management (advisory wrap fees, transaction fees, bank and cash-sweep spread), from asset management (Columbia Threadneedle, Seligman) and from legacy annuity and insurance books (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/amp/). No foreign country is identified as ~10%+ of revenue in the sources read [Likely]. Industry: Asset Management; Financials sector (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/amp/).

## 2. Quality
A high-return, cash-generative franchise [Likely]. ROE is 72.0% TTM and FCF margin 36.4% TTM; operating margin 34.9% is flat over five years (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/amp/financials/ratios/). The moat is advisor relationships and platform stickiness: management says long-tenured advisor retention is high, and a third-party bank (Huntington) moved its advisors onto the platform in October [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/amp/transcripts/713968-q3-2026/).

Direction change, addressed: ROIC is falling over 12 months (498.1% to 457.8%) and FCF margin sits 10.5pp below its 2-year high of 46.9%. Neither is a collapse. ROIC is inflated by a tiny equity base, so ROCE (4.1%, flat) is the cleaner read [Likely]. Operating margin has slipped 1.2pp in a year, and gross margin is falling (54.6% vs 61.7% in FY2021) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/amp/financials/income-statement/?p=trailing). Altman Z 0.73 is not meaningful for a financial [Guessing].

## 3. Valuation
P/E is 11.1x, at 24% of its own 5y range (9.9x–15.2x) and 19% below the peer median of 13.7x. P/FCF is 5.7x vs 17.6x for peers; FCF yield is 17.59% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/amp/statistics/). The reverse DCF implies revenue growth of -1.8% in year 1 [Certain]. Base fair value 566.06 vs the 480.29 close (+17.9%); bear 395.11, bull 851.85 [Likely]. FCF includes insurance and bank flows that may not be fully distributable [Guessing]. The price is 4.7% below the 50-day average and 2.4% below the 200-day; 3-month momentum is -12.2%, 12-month +11.1%.

Why it fell: no stock-specific shock in the headlines. Third-quarter results were strong per management, yet the stock drifted to its 29 Oct low (478.81) [Likely]. Analysts asked about press reports on advisor recruiting packages; management denied any program [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/amp/transcripts/713968-q3-2026/). Recruiting competition and lower short rates are the likely worries [Guessing].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROCE (ratios page, TTM) falls below 3.5%.
- FCF margin (cash-flow page, TTM) falls below 28%.
- Operating margin (income-statement page, TTM) falls below 32%.
- Shares change YoY (statistics page) turns positive, i.e. buybacks stop shrinking the count.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Shares are down 5.85% YoY, and FCF conversion is 176.2% of net income TTM (229.0% in FY2025), so buybacks are well covered [Certain]. Management announced a new multi-year repurchase authorization on the third-quarter call [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/amp/transcripts/713968-q3-2026/). Caution: FCF includes insurance and bank balance-sheet flows, so conversion above 100% overstates distributable earnings [Guessing].

## 6. Unusual items

- MD&A: an unfavorable actuarial unlocking charge of $91 million in the quarter vs a $22 million favorable item a year ago, driven by variable annuity surrender and utilization, universal life surrender and long-term care assumptions [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/820027/000082002726000054/amp-20260930.htm).
