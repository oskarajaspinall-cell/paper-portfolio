# Range Resources Corporation (RRC) — Core initiation — 2026-11-15
## 1. Business
Appalachian natural gas, NGL and oil producer; revenue is commodity sales net of its own price realisation, so earnings track Henry Hub and NGL prices (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/rrc/) [Certain]. No foreign country is ~10%+ of revenue: US only [Likely]. Key fact: on October 25, 2026 it agreed an all-stock merger with EQT at a fixed 0.987 EQT shares per RRC share [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/315852/000119312526434351/rrc-20260930.htm) [Certain]. RRC now trades as a proxy for EQT stock.

## 2. Quality
Returns are good but cyclical. ROIC TTM 17.1% vs 7.3% in FY2024 and 29.6% in FY2022; operating margin 37.0% vs 16.9% FY2024 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/rrc/financials/ratios/) [Certain]. Net debt/EBITDA 0.55x, Piotroski 8, Altman Z 2.90 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/rrc/statistics/) [Certain]. Direction is up over 12 months (ROIC +4.5pp) but operating margin is 3.2pp and FCF margin 3.8pp below their March 2026 highs, so the peak was last spring [Certain]. Moat is low-cost acreage and scale, not pricing power; gas is a price-taker [Likely]. Q3 results beat per headlines, but the deal, not the quarter, now sets the price (source: https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/10/27/3389652/0/en/range-announces-third-quarter-2026-results.html) [Likely].

## 3. Valuation
The price is anchored to the deal, not to RRC fundamentals. Last close 51.83 USD is 17.4% above the 50-day and 25.8% above the 200-day average, with 12-month change +34.8% after the announcement-day jump (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/rrc/history/) [Certain]. P/E 13.8x vs 5y median 11.1x and peer median 8.7x (+57%); EV/EBITDA 7.8x vs 7.1x median and peers +38%; P/B 2.5x is above its 5y maximum; FCF yield 6.00% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/rrc/statistics/) [Certain]. Mechanical base fair value 46.32 USD is 10.6% below the close; bull 49.18 is also below it [Certain]. The market already pays a premium to peers and to the stock's own history; upside now depends on EQT's share price, because the exchange ratio is fixed [Likely]. Owning RRC is owning EQT at a ratio, with deal-break risk (a fall back toward standalone value) added [Likely].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROIC TTM (ratios page, trailing) falls below 10%.
- Operating margin TTM (income statement, trailing) falls below 28%.
- Net debt / EBITDA (ratios page) rises above 2.0x.
- Merger Agreement terminated or not approved, shown by a Form 8-K on sec.gov (then re-underwrite RRC standalone).

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
FCF conversion 81.6% of net income (FY2025: 80.5%); shares down 1.77% YoY (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/rrc/financials/cash-flow-statement/) [Certain]. It redeemed $600 million of 8.25% notes due 2029 using the credit facility [company-reported] and has roughly $1.4 billion of buyback authorisation left [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/315852/000119312526434351/rrc-20260930.htm) [Certain]. Merger covenants limit buybacks, M&A and other strategic action until closing [Likely].

## 6. Unusual items
Merger: needs RRC and EQT shareholder votes and Hart-Scott-Rodino clearance; a $423 million termination fee is payable by RRC in certain terminations [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/315852/000119312526434351/rrc-20260930.htm) [Certain]. Antitrust review of two large Appalachian gas producers could delay or condition closing [Likely]. Law-firm "investigations" of deal value are solicitation, not evidence (source: https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/11/12/3401562/0/en/rrc-shareholders-amparo-law-firm-pllc-is-investigating-whether-the-eqt-deal-delivers-the-best-value-for-range-resources-shareholders.html) [Likely]. Short interest 7.18% of float, down 36.8% on the prior month, consistent with deal-spread unwinding [Likely]. The "Next earnings date" on the fact sheet is stale (Oct 27 already reported) [Certain].
