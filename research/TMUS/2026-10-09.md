# T-Mobile US, Inc. (TMUS) — Core initiation — 2026-10-09

## 1. Business
TMUS is the second-largest US wireless carrier, monetizing a subscriber base through recurring postpaid/prepaid service plans (T-Mobile, Metro, Mint MVNO), equipment financing, fixed wireless access (home broadband) and growing enterprise/fiber businesses [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/tmus/).

## 2. Quality
Returns on capital have risen every year for five years: ROIC 5.4%→8.9% TTM, ROCE 5.5%→10.7% TTM, FCF margin to 20.0% TTM and FCF conversion to 174% TTM, while net debt/EBITDA has fallen to 3.42x from a 4.08x FY2022 peak [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/tmus/financials/ratios/). None of these moved today; the last completed close is still 171.31 on 2026-10-08 [Certain]. The catalyst is an Oct 8 headline that a SpaceX spectrum-license deal hit AT&T/Verizon/T-Mobile shares together [Certain] (source: https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/08/spacex-spectrum-license-att-verizon-tmobile.html, as listed in the fact sheet) — sector-wide, not TMUS-specific. Neither TMUS nor AT&T filed a new 8-K on 2026-10-08/09 [Certain] (source: https://www.sec.gov/cgi-bin/browse-edgar?action=getcompany&CIK=0001283699&type=8-K; https://www.sec.gov/cgi-bin/browse-edgar?action=getcompany&CIK=0000732717&type=8-K), and T-Mobile's IR/press pages were unreachable (403) [Certain]. The CFO has separately dismissed satellite-to-device competition [Likely] (source: https://www.businessinsider.com/starlink-elon-musk-strategy-cellphones-no-threat-t-mobile-cfo-2026-9). Absent a filed fundamental change, the slide reads as repricing a long-dated competitive narrative, not evidence the Sprint-merger spectrum moat has actually been breached [Likely].

## 3. Valuation
At the fact sheet's confirmed close of 171.31, TMUS traded at a premium to peers (P/E +119%, EV/EBITDA +24%, P/FCF +12% vs VZ/T/CHTR median) but cheap to its own 5-year range (P/E -6% vs 5y min, EV/EBITDA -29%) [Certain]. DCF fair value was $275.91 base/$290.55 bull vs 171.31 (+61%/+70%); the $38.93 bear case rests on an unsupported -6.1% reverse-DCF growth assumption [Certain]. The owner reports a further intraday decline to ~149.85 (Yahoo live quote, 12:31 ET); context only, not a stockanalysis.com figure, excluded from the math above, and could partly reverse by the close [Likely]. If it holds, it widens the gap to base fair value toward +84%, on a business whose returns on capital, margins and leverage are all still improving with no filed fundamental trigger — sentiment, not a reassessment of TMUS's own economics. Consistent with BUY, not AVOID, pending the official close.

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROIC (ratios page) reverses its 5-year rising trend, falling back toward the FY2021 level of 5.4%
- FCF margin (cash flow/income statement) drops below ~15%, giving back over a quarter of the current 20.0% TTM level
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) resumes rising back above its FY2022 peak of 4.08x
- A filed 8-K or FCC ruling shows T-Mobile's own mid-band spectrum position materially diluted (not just a competitor's spectrum deal)

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
H1 2026: $7.1bn of buybacks (34.75m shares), cutting shares outstanding -3.93% YoY, plus a dividend raised 15% on Sept 24, 2026 [Certain] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1283699/000128369926000101/tmus-20260630.htm; https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260924651626/en/T-Mobile-Announces-a-15-Quarterly-Dividend-Increase/). Net debt/EBITDA of 3.42x is still falling, and FCF conversion (174% TTM) funds both buybacks and fiber-JV expansion (i3 Broadband, GoNetspeed/Greenlight) [Certain].

## 6. Unusual items
Short interest is low (3.72% of float, +5.7% month-on-month), not a crowding risk; Altman Z of 1.80 is a poor fit for a stable, contracted-revenue telecom (Piotroski F 5/9) [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/tmus/statistics/). The two-day, three-carrier spectrum sell-off is itself unusual (sector-wide, not stock-specific) and is the reason for this re-initiation; next earnings (Oct 28, 2026) is the next hard data point [Certain].
