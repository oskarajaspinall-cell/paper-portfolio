TTM operating margin is 25.5%, up 4.0pp on a year earlier. ROIC is 17.0% TTM against 10.5% in FY2021 [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/epa/DSY/financials/ratios/). Direction change: FCF margin is down over five fiscal years (31.1% to 23.6%) but up over the last 12 months (23.3% to 27.3%) (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/epa/DSY/financials/cash-flow-statement/?p=trailing). The FY2021 level was likely timing-inflated, so the recent rise is a recovery, not a new trend [Guessing].
