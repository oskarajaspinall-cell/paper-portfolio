# Vinci SA (EPA:DG) — Core initiation — 2026-11-08
## 1. Business
Vinci is a French concessions and contracting group: toll roads and airports (long-life infrastructure concessions), plus energy services and construction. Concessions supply the high margins and cash; contracting supplies scale and order backlog [Likely]. Geographic and segment revenue shares are [data unavailable]: the company website (vinci.com) is not on the fetch allowlist, so no country share could be checked for the macro overlay (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/epa/DG/).

## 2. Quality
Returns are good and stable for a contractor. ROIC 10.3% TTM (5y: 5.6% to 11.5%), ROCE 12.5%, operating margin 12.3% (up from 9.0% in FY2021) (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/epa/DG/financials/ratios/) [Certain]. The last 12 months show no deterioration: ROIC +0.2pp, operating margin +0.4pp, FCF margin +0.7pp year on year (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/epa/DG/financials/ratios/?p=trailing) [Certain]. FCF margin 12.2% and FCF/net income 182% are far above a typical contractor, consistent with depreciation-heavy concessions plus contractor working capital [Likely]. Moat: concession contracts on toll roads and airports are hard to replicate and are inflation-linked [Likely]. Balance sheet is leveraged: net debt/EBITDA 2.02x, Altman Z 1.13 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/epa/DG/statistics/), typical for concession funding but a watch item [Likely]. Q3 2026 call coverage: revenue growth accelerated, backlog expanded, guidance reaffirmed (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/epa/DG/transcripts/779382-q3-2026-tu/) [Likely].

## 3. Valuation
The price is 111.60 EUR. P/E 12.4x sits at 9% of its 5y range (11.5x–20.3x), EV/EBITDA 6.6x is below its 5y minimum of 7.3x, and P/FCF 6.7x is 20% up its range; FCF yield is 14.96% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/epa/DG/financials/ratios/) [Certain]. Peer medians are P/E 24.8x and EV/EBITDA 10.2x (n=4: Bouygues, Ferrovial, Hochtief, Saint-Gobain), so Vinci is 50% and 36% cheaper; peers have different mixes, so the gap overstates mispricing [Likely]. The mechanical fair value (base 327.83 EUR; DCF 421.20, EV/EBITDA 141.07) is dominated by the DCF, which capitalises reported FCF; the reverse DCF says the price implies -18.3% revenue growth in year 1 [Certain]. I discount the DCF: concession capex and debt-funded growth may make FCF flatter than owner earnings [Guessing]. Even on the EV/EBITDA leg alone, base is 141.07 EUR, above the price [Certain]. The price assumes decline, not a steady compounder [Likely].

## 4. What would prove this wrong

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Shares outstanding fell 1.32% year on year, so buybacks are ongoing (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/epa/DG/statistics/; buyback disclosure: https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/09/01/3354435/0/en/vinci-disclosure-of-transactions-on-shares-from-august-24-2026-to-august-28-2026.html) [Certain]. FCF conversion of 182% of net income is strong, though down 72.8pp over five years from an elevated FY2021 level (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/epa/DG/financials/cash-flow-statement/) [Certain]. Leverage fell from 3.19x in FY2021 to 2.02x, with a slight rise from 1.80x in FY2025 [Certain]. Management reaffirmed 2026 guidance and said liquidity supports selective investment and buybacks (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/epa/DG/transcripts/779382-q3-2026-tu/) [Likely].
