# Tianshan Aluminum Group (SHE:002532) — Core initiation — 2026-10-24

Tianshan Aluminum is a Chinese primary aluminium producer classified in Aluminum, Materials (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/she/002532/). Revenue is aluminium sold at a cyclical spread over power and raw-material costs, so earnings follow the aluminium price and input costs. ## 2. Quality
Returns are cyclical, not durable-moat. ROIC fell from 17.8% (FY2021) to 7.5% (FY2023) before recovering to 18.5% TTM; operating margin ran 10.6%–28.5% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/she/002532/financials/ratios/). [Certain] Margins sit at a cycle high now (gross 33.4% TTM vs 14.1% FY2023), and any moat is cost-position based and not verifiable from the fact sheet. [Guessing] Positives: FCF conversion 105.5% TTM, net debt/EBITDA 0.50x, Piotroski 8, Altman Z 3.69 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/she/002532/statistics/). Beta 0.74. [Certain] Net: good balance sheet and strong current economics, but peak-looking margins in a commodity business. [Likely]

## 3. Valuation
P/E 7.5x, EV/EBITDA 5.2x and P/FCF 7.1x are all below their 5y minimums (8.2x / 5.9x / 9.2x); FCF yield 14.07% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/she/002532/statistics/). [Certain] Against peers P/E is 21% lower and P/FCF 20% lower, but EV/EBITDA is 8% higher and EV/Sales 53% higher. The low multiples sit on peak margins, so they likely price in mean reversion. Model fair value: base 8.28 CNY vs close 11.29 (-26.7%), bear 1.26, bull 17.66; the through-cycle DCF base is 7.59. The reverse DCF implies 9.4% year-1 revenue growth. [Likely] Price is above base fair value, so there is no margin of safety on normalised earnings.
