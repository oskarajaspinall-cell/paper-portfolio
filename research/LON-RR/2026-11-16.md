## 2. Quality
Turnaround is real in the numbers. [Certain] Operating margin rose from 4.1% (FY2021) to 19.7% (FY2025), 20.7% TTM; FCF margin 19.3% TTM; ROCE 24.8% TTM; net debt/EBITDA -0.41x (net cash). Every metric in the rolling-12-month table is rising (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/lon/RR/financials/ratios/?p=trailing). TTM ROIC of 470.6% and ROE of 114.5% are distorted by tiny invested capital/equity after past losses; ignore them as a level [Likely]. Moat: [Likely] duopoly-like installed base on widebody engines creates decades of service revenue competitors cannot easily displace. Reasons for caution: net margin 13.1% TTM vs 27.5% FY2025 (FY2025 likely flattered by non-operating gains) [Guessing]; Altman Z 2.78, Piotroski F 6 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/lon/RR/statistics/).

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Operating margin (income statement, TTM) falls below 16%.
- FCF margin (cash flow statement, TTM) falls below 14%.
- Net debt/EBITDA (statistics page) turns positive above 1.0x.
- ROCE (ratios page, TTM) falls below 18%.
