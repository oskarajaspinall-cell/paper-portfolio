# Textron Inc. (TXT) — Core initiation — 2026-10-09
## 1. Business
Textron is a diversified industrial/defense conglomerate. Textron Aviation (Cessna/Beechcraft) sells business jets and turboprops plus aftermarket parts and service. Bell makes military and commercial helicopters. Textron Systems supplies unmanned systems, marine craft and munitions to defense customers. Industrial makes specialized vehicles (E-Z-GO golf carts, Arctic Cat). Textron Finance supports aircraft/vehicle sales. [Certain] (source: https://www.textron.com)

## 2. Quality
ROIC is 9.6% TTM, ROCE 9.5%, both roughly flat to rising over five years [Certain] (source: RA page). But cash conversion is deteriorating: FCF margin fell from 9.9% (FY21) to 5.0% TTM, and FCF/net-income conversion fell from 164% to 81% over the same period [Certain] (source: CF/IS pages) — a clear quality red flag. Moat: Bell's defense-helicopter franchise and Textron Systems' qualified-supplier status give long-cycle, high-switching-cost revenue [Likely]; Cessna's brand and dealer/parts network support an aftermarket annuity [Likely]. Aviation and Industrial remain cyclical. Net debt/EBITDA (TTM 1.65x) has risen from a 1.28x FY23 low [Certain]. Piotroski F-Score 6/9 and Altman Z 2.74 [Certain] (source: ST page) show adequate balance-sheet health. Net margin is flat near 6%. On balance: real defense moats, offset by a five-year cash-conversion trend moving the wrong way.

## 3. Valuation
TXT trades well below both its own 5y range and peers (GD, LMT, LHX, HII) on P/E (14.0x vs 5y min 16.7x, peer median 19.5x, -28%), EV/EBITDA (9.2x vs peer 13.4x, -32%), EV/Sales (1.0x vs peer 1.7x, -42%) and P/B (1.6x vs peer 2.8x, -44%) [Certain]. On P/FCF it trades ABOVE peers (16.7x vs peer 13.8x, +21%) [Certain] — FCF has compressed faster than earnings, so part of the multiple discount reflects genuine cash deterioration, not simple mispricing. The fact sheet's DCF-weighted fair value is $76.68 base, only 4.7% above the $73.22 close; bear $65.21 (-10.9%), bull $134.64 (+83.9%) [Certain]. A ~5% margin of safety against a steep peer discount suggests the market is already pricing continued margin/cash pressure, not offering a clean value gap. FCF yield is 5.98% [Certain] (source: ST page).

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- FCF margin (ratios/cash-flow pages) recovers above 7% TTM, confirming the cash-conversion trend has reversed.
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) rises above 2.0x, signalling balance-sheet strain funding buybacks.
- ROIC (ratios page) falls below 8%, confirming the defense/aviation moat is not holding returns.
- Aviation or Bell backlog disclosed in the 10-Q falls materially year-on-year (text-only trigger; checked at each filing).

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Shares outstanding fell 4.3% YoY [Certain] (source: ST page), so buybacks are the main capital return; the fact sheet shows no dividend figure. FCF conversion fell from 164% to 81% TTM [Certain] — buybacks are increasingly funded off a shrinking FCF base (margin just 5.0% TTM), which caps future repurchase capacity unless margins recover [Likely]. Net debt/EBITDA of 1.65x remains below 2x but is rising from its 1.28x FY23 trough [Certain].
