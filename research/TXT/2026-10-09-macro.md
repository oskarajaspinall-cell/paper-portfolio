# Macro overlay: Textron Inc. (TXT) — 2026-10-09
## Assessment
Chain **(inference)**: rising long-term real yields raise TXT's DCF discount rate and, secondarily, raise financing costs for buyers of Cessna/Beechcraft business jets (often debt/lease-financed) — a demand lever for Textron Aviation, TXT's largest segment [Likely]. Bell and Textron Systems (defense, ~mid-40s% of revenue) are appropriations-driven and macro-insensitive [Likely].

Facts: US 10y real yield (DFII10) 2.92% on 2026-10-07, +49bp ~1m, +61bp ~3m (fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=DFII10). 10y breakeven inflation (T10YIE) 2.35% on 2026-10-08, flat 1m (-2bp), +12bp 3m (id=T10YIE) — the real-yield rise is a real-rate/term-premium move, not inflation fear, feeding straight into the discount rate. HY credit spread (BAMLH0A0HYM2) 3.09%, +39bp 3m but still low; VIX 15.08, -1.82 3m — no broad risk-off (both 2026-10-07).

Market pricing: TXT fell -18.5% over 3 months vs SPY +3.8% (factsheet, 2026-10-08) — far larger than a rate move alone explains, so most of the drop is company-specific (FCF-conversion deterioration), not macro **(inference)**. The DCF already discounts at today's 5.28% risk-free rate, so the current level is in the $76.68 base fair value; further CHANGE in real yields is the open variable.

Bull: real yields retrace toward ~2.5% → discount rate and financing costs ease → fair value drifts toward $76.68-$134.64. Bear: real yields climb past ~3.25% → discount rate and Aviation financing costs rise further → fair value pressured below the $65.21 bear case. Tilt: moderate toward bear — the real-yield uptrend is still active, adding an external headwind to the FCF/margin deterioration already flagged.
