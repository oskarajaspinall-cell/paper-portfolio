# China Pacific Insurance (Group) Co., Ltd. (SHA:601601) — Core initiation — 2026-10-18

## 1. Business
CPIC is one of China's largest composite insurers, writing life insurance (protection/savings products sold via tied agents and bancassurance, measured by new business value) and property & casualty insurance (auto and non-auto commercial lines) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sha/601601/). Profit comes from underwriting margin plus net investment income earned on the policyholder float it invests in bonds, equities and alternatives [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sha/601601/transcripts/657669-h1-2026/).

## 2. Quality
ROE has risen from 12.2% (FY21) to 17.5% TTM, while ROIC has stayed flat around 10.9% over five years — a widening gap that suggests leverage/mix, not just capital efficiency, is driving the ROE improvement [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sha/601601/financials/ratios/). Management's H1 2026 call described double-digit new-business-value growth in life, an improving P&C combined ratio, and life interest-rate-hedging ratios above management's target, alongside a newly introduced interim dividend [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sha/601601/transcripts/657669-h1-2026/). The durable edge is regulatory: China restricts new composite-insurer licences, and CPIC is one of a handful of nationally scaled underwriters with an agency/bancassurance distribution network peers can't quickly replicate [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sha/601601/transcripts/657669-h1-2026/). The risk to that edge is structural: management flagged falling long-term rates compressing the life segment's investment spread and climate volatility (typhoons) as a growing P&C claims risk [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sha/601601/transcripts/657669-h1-2026/); Net debt/EBITDA (site definition) has risen from 1.33x (FY21) to 2.47x (FY25) over the cycle [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sha/601601/financials/ratios/).

## 3. Valuation
At 29.32 CNY, CPIC trades on 5.2x P/E — below its own 5-year range (5.6x–8.7x) and the peer median of 6.2x (Ping An, New China Life, AIA) — and 0.8x P/B, at the bottom of its own range and 37% below the peer median of 1.2x [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sha/601601/financials/ratios/). The stock is 39% below its 52-week high and has underperformed SPY by 35.3pp over 12 months [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sha/601601/history/). The owner's P/B+ROE/P/E blend puts base fair value at 42.28 CNY (+44.2%); even the bear case (31.07 CNY) sits above the current price [Certain] (source: research/SHA-601601/2026-10-18-valuation.md). The price likely assumes either continued compression in China's long rates squeezing investment spread, or a broad discount on Chinese financials not fully captured by the model's 3.5pp country-risk premium [Guessing]. FCF yield of 71% (ST) is not a reliable signal here — insurer "FCF" is distorted by float, so it is excluded from the thesis [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sha/601601/statistics/).

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROE (ratios page) falls back below 13% for a full fiscal year, reversing the five-year improvement the re-rating case depends on.
- New business value / life segment growth turns negative in a reported quarter (company filings/transcripts), signalling loss of distribution momentum.
- Net debt/EBITDA, site definition (ratios page), rises above 3.0x and continues climbing beyond its current 5-year high of 2.71x (FY24).
- P&C combined ratio deteriorates above 100% in a reported period (company's quarterly/annual filings), eroding the second core underwriting engine.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
FCF conversion (FCF/NI) ranged 331.3%–491.2% over the last five years (fact sheet), reflecting investment/float cash flows typical of an insurer, not discretionary free cash flow, so it is not a reliable capital-allocation signal here [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sha/601601/financials/cash-flow-statement/). CPIC introduced an interim dividend for the first time in H1 2026, anchored to operating profit and solvency, alongside its existing annual payout [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sha/601601/transcripts/657669-h1-2026/). Shares outstanding rose 3.34% YoY, a modest dilution [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sha/601601/statistics/). Net debt/EBITDA (both definitions) has trended up over five years but remains at moderate absolute levels (2.5x–3.3x) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sha/601601/financials/ratios/).

## 6. Unusual items
Insider ownership is just 0.01% against 36.2% institutional ownership, typical of a Chinese SOE-linked insurer but limited management alignment via equity [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sha/601601/statistics/). Management flagged new, "very comprehensive" asset-liability management regulation with a "profound impact" on the sector — a potential near-term compliance cost, though one that may favour scaled incumbents like CPIC over smaller rivals [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sha/601601/transcripts/657669-h1-2026/). Beta (5y) of 0.45 is low for a Chinese equity and should not be read as low fundamental risk given the rate/claims exposure above [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sha/601601/statistics/).
