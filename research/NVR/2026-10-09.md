# NVR, Inc. (NVR) — Core initiation — 2026-10-09

## 1. Business
NVR builds and sells single-family homes under the Ryan Homes, NVHomes and Heartland Homes brands across 37 metro areas in 16 states [Certain] (source: https://www.nvrinc.com). A vertically integrated building-products manufacturing arm supplies structural components to its own sites, and NVR Mortgage/NVR Settlement Services serve only NVR buyers [Certain] (source: https://www.nvrinc.com). Revenue is overwhelmingly homebuilding; mortgage banking is a smaller fee adjunct.

## 2. Quality
NVR's moat is capital-light land sourcing: at FY2025-end it controlled 180,100 lots, 94% via fixed-price lot purchase agreement (LPA) deposits (~$920.1m cash) rather than owned/developed land [Certain] (source: SEC 10-K, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/906163/000090616326000018/nvr-20251231.htm). This avoids the land-development capital and write-down risk heavier owned-land peers (DHI, LEN, PHM, TOL) carry in downturns, driving ROIC (36.7% TTM) and ROCE (31.6% TTM, rising over 5y) far above a typical builder [Certain] (source: fact sheet, https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/nvr/financials/ratios/). Returns are nonetheless declining — ROIC down 16.4pp and ROE down 7.3pp over five years — alongside margin compression (gross -0.7pp TTM, operating -1.2pp), consistent with a cooling market and rate-driven affordability pressure, not a loss of competitive position [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/nvr/financials/income-statement/; SEC 10-K risk factors cite mortgage-rate effects on affordability). Net debt is negative and Altman Z of 13.62 signals very low distress risk; Piotroski F of 4/9 is mediocre and flags the margin/return erosion [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/nvr/statistics/).

## 3. Valuation
At 15.5x TTM P/E, NVR sits at the 86th percentile of its own 5-year range (8.5x–16.6x) and 27% above peer median (12.2x); EV/EBITDA (10.3x, +10%), EV/Sales (1.6x, +26%) and P/FCF (15.2x, +18%) all carry premiums, and P/B (4.7x) is 210% above peer median, reflecting NVR's structurally higher ROE, not cheapness [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/nvr/financials/ratios/, peers DHI/LEN/PHM/TOL). The base-case fair value is $5,141.87 versus last close $6,002.55, implying -14.3% downside; bear is -37.7%, bull +27.9% [Likely] (source: fact sheet valuation table; reverse-DCF implies the price assumes 7.3% year-1 revenue growth fading to terminal growth). The stock is down 23.9% over 12 months, below its 50-day (-3.9%) and 200-day (-9.8%) averages — some slowdown is priced — but on every multiple but one it still sits above both its own history and peers, leaving little margin of safety [Likely].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Gross margin (income statement page) falls below 20% for two consecutive quarters
- ROIC (ratios page) falls below 25%
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios/balance sheet page) turns net-debt positive beyond 0.5x, signalling the land-light model is being funded with leverage
- LPA deposits at risk of forfeiture rise materially as a share of total lots controlled without a corresponding rise in closings (next 10-K/10-Q), signalling the capital-light sourcing model is deteriorating

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
NVR returns essentially all excess cash via buybacks and pays no dividend: shares outstanding fell 7.92% YoY, and the board authorized two new $750m repurchase programs in 2025 (May, August), with $549.6m still available at FY2025-end [Certain] (source: SEC 10-K; fact sheet ST). FCF conversion is solid at 91.7% TTM of net income, though down from above 100% in 2021-2022, consistent with margin compression rather than a cash-collection problem [Likely] (source: fact sheet CF/IS).

## 6. Unusual items
Short interest rose 19.4% in the past month to 4.99% of float — a modestly bearish tilt worth monitoring but not yet large [Certain] (source: fact sheet ST). Institutional ownership is high at 91.0%, insiders 1.14%. No governance issues identified.
