# Universal Health Services (UHS) — Core initiation — 2026-11-16
## 1. Business
UHS operates acute-care hospitals and behavioral-health facilities in the US (plus UK behavioral sites), paid by commercial insurers, Medicare and Medicaid. Medicaid is large: the prior 10-Q named 14 states each yielding roughly $100 million or more [company-reported] (https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/352915/000119312525272385/uhs-20250930.htm). In August 2026 it bought Talkspace, a virtual behavioral-health firm, for about $820 million net of cash [company-reported] (https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/352915/000119312526451866/uhs-20260930.htm). UK share of revenue: [data unavailable]. [Likely]

## 2. Quality
Returns are decent, not exceptional. ROIC TTM 11.5% (FY2021 10.6%, FY2025 12.7%), ROE 19.7%, gross margin 44.5% and net debt/EBITDA 2.27x (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/uhs/financials/ratios/) [Certain]. Moat is local: licensed-bed scarcity and certificate-of-need regimes in hospital markets, and behavioral capacity that is hard to replicate [Likely]. It is a payer-dependent, labor-intensive business, so returns are policy-exposed, not protected.

Direction change: ROCE has risen over five fiscal years but fell from 16.0% to 13.7% over the last 12 months, and operating margin slipped 0.6pp to 10.8% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/uhs/financials/ratios/?p=trailing) [Certain]. The filing cites California's June 2026 acute-psychiatric staffing standards as a cost addition [Certain] (source: sec-delta above). Management said three facilities were loss-making and could be a 2027 tailwind [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/uhs/transcripts/789940-ubs-global-healthcare-conference/).

## 3. Valuation
At 194.07 USD the stock trades at P/E 8.3x and EV/EBITDA 6.5x, both below their 5-year minimums (9.2x and 7.2x), and EV/EBITDA is 29% below peers (HCA, THC, CYH, ACHC); P/E is 4% below peers (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/uhs/statistics/) [Certain]. FCF yield is 9.82% [Certain]. The reverse DCF implies -1.2% revenue growth in year 1 [Certain]. The mechanical base fair value is 269.70 (+39%), but the bear case is 10.37, so the DCF is sensitive to cash-flow assumptions [Likely]. Price is 10.0% above the 200-day MA after a +10.9% earnings reaction on 2026-11-04 and the midterm-driven hospital rally; the 12-month change remains -12.8% [Certain]. A WSJ piece (https://www.wsj.com/finance/stocks/hospital-stocks-are-feeling-blue-in-a-good-way-f4d73ed2) claims hospitals trade above historical averages, but UHS is below its own range [Likely]. The market prices in policy-driven margin pressure, not growth [Guessing].

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Shares are down 6.83% year on year, i.e. real buybacks at low multiples [Certain]. FCF conversion is 76.3% of net income TTM, up from 38.8% in FY2022 but below 98.4% in FY2024 [Certain]. The debt-funded Talkspace deal is a bet outside the core hospital franchise; integration, provider retention and payer relationships are named risks [Certain] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/352915/000119312526451866/uhs-20260930.htm). Leverage was 1.92x at FY2025 and is 2.27x TTM [Certain].

## 6. Unusual items
- DC hospitals: the District of Columbia Attorney General is reviewing the George Washington University Hospital arrangement; UHS disputes jurisdiction, and the new physician entity's operating losses may exceed expectations [Certain] (source: sec-delta filing above).
- The 10-Q removed its explicit Medicaid-state sensitivity and shareholder-litigation risk factors; the Medicaid exposure itself remains, and the filing adds the One Big Beautiful Bill Act provider-fee limits and enrollment limits [Certain].
- A federal funding lapse is possible after 2026-12-11 [Certain].
- Short interest is 5.37% of float; institutions own 92.8% [Certain].
- The fact sheet's next-earnings row (Oct 27, 2026) is already past; results were published 2026-10-27 (https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/10/27/3389648/0/en/universal-health-services-inc-announces-financial-results-for-the-three-and-nine-month-periods-ended-september-30-2026.html).
