# Roper Technologies, Inc. (ROP) — Core initiation — 2026-10-09

## 1. Business
Roper owns ~30 niche software and technology businesses across three segments [Certain]: Application Software (57% of FY2025 revenue — vertical SaaS in legal, healthcare, education, insurance), Network Software (20% — marketplaces/connectivity: freight matching, construction, supply chain) and Technology Enabled Products (23% — water meters, medical devices, sensors) (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/882835/000088283526000009/rop-20251231.htm). It grows mainly by acquiring and holding niche-leading businesses, run on a decentralized model [Certain] (same source).

## 2. Quality
Roper's current returns are modest for a "quality compounder": ROIC 6.7% TTM and ROCE 7.3%, both rising slowly over five years (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/rop/financials/ratios/). Operating margin is high and stable at 28.0% TTM (5y range 27.6%-28.4%) [Certain] (https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/rop/financials/income-statement/). The moat case: niche market leadership, high switching costs from mission-critical embedded workflows, and a decentralized model letting ~30 units act entrepreneurially [Likely] (SEC 10-K, above). Remaining performance obligations of $5.2bn and 12-month backlog of $3.4bn support revenue visibility [Likely] (same source, qualitative only). The 10-K flags a real risk: AI could become commoditized, eroding software differentiation, with cost pass-through risk from third-party cloud/AI platforms [Likely] (same source). FCF conversion (FCF/NI) fell sharply to 104.7% TTM from 142-172% in FY2021-24, and net debt/EBITDA rose back to 3.38x from 2.53x in FY2023 [Certain] (https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/rop/financials/cash-flow-statement/, https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/rop/financials/balance-sheet/) — leverage funds the M&A engine the moat depends on.

## 3. Valuation
Roper is priced well below its own history and peers. TTM P/E of 15.3x sits at the 15th percentile of its 5-year range (10.1x-45.0x) and 54% below the peer median (33.0x, n=4: AME, DHR, FTV, DOV) (https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/rop/financials/ratios/). EV/EBITDA (14.5x), P/FCF (13.7x) and P/B (1.9x) sit below their own 5-year minimums and well under peer medians; EV/Sales (5.7x) is roughly in line with peers (-3%) (same source). The reverse-DCF read is that price implies only 2.5% revenue growth in year one fading to terminal growth — a low bar given Roper's M&A-funded growth history [Likely]. Fair value (DCF-weighted, inform-only) is $534.52 base vs a $364.23 last close [Likely] (fair value table, factsheet). The 52-week change of -28.7% vs SPY's +15.7% is a large de-rating the multiples corroborate [Certain] (https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/rop/statistics/).

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROIC (ratios page) reverses its five-year uptrend and falls back below 6.0% (FY2023 level), signalling M&A capital no longer earns its cost.
- FCF conversion (FCF/NI, cash-flow + income-statement pages) falls further below the 100% already seen TTM, undermining the self-funding-compounder thesis.
- Net debt/EBITDA (balance sheet + income statement) rises materially above the current 3.38x without a deleveraging plan, constraining the bolt-on M&A engine.
- Operating margin (income statement) breaks below its 5-year floor of ~27.6%, evidence AI commoditization or cloud-pricing pressure is eroding software economics.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Capital allocation is acquisition-led: ~$8.96bn deployed on M&A over 2023-2025 (CentralReach $1.85bn, Subsplash $0.8bn in 2025; Procare $1.86bn, Transact Campus $1.6bn in 2024), funded by debt and operating cash flow [Certain] (SEC 10-K). Debt stood at $9.36bn with $2.64bn undrawn revolver capacity at FY2025-end; management prioritizes cash generation to cut debt and interest [Certain] (same source). Shares outstanding fell 2.63% YoY — net buybacks alongside the dividend [Certain] (https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/rop/statistics/). FCF margin remains solid at 31.7% TTM despite the recent conversion dip (https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/rop/financials/cash-flow-statement/).
