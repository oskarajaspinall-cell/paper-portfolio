# Macro overlay: Roper Technologies, Inc. (ROP) — 2026-10-09
## Assessment
Chain: real yields → DCF discount rate (and cost of acquisition debt) → fair value / multiple for a debt-funded, long-duration serial acquirer. Roper's fair value is built on risk-free 5.28% + ERP (fact sheet) and the business runs on $9.36bn debt at 3.38x net debt/EBITDA to fund M&A [Certain].

Facts (FRED, all series dated 2026-10-07/08): 10y real yield (DFII10) 2.92%, +0.49pp ~1m, +0.61pp ~3m [Certain] (https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=DFII10). 10y nominal (DGS10) 5.28%, +0.72pp ~3m (https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=DGS10); breakeven inflation (T10YIE) 2.35%, only +0.12pp ~3m [Certain] (https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=T10YIE) — so the nominal-yield rise is overwhelmingly a real-rate move, not inflation expectations **(inference)**. 2y yield (4.77%, https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=DGS2) sits 0.89pp above effective fed funds (3.88%, https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=DFF) — a proxy, not fact, that the market isn't pricing near-term cuts **(inference)**. HY credit spread (BAMLH0A0HYM2) 3.09%, +0.39pp ~3m but still historically tight — no acute funding stress [Likely] (https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=BAMLH0A0HYM2).

A sustained ~0.6pp rise in real yields mechanically lowers Roper's DCF fair value and raises the hurdle for accretive M&A **(inference)**; this is consistent with ROP's -28.7% 12-month return vs SPY's +15.7% — much of this de-rating looks already priced **(inference)**.

**Bull**: real yields stabilize/ease → discount rate relief → fair value re-rates up, cheaper M&A financing. **Bear**: real yields keep rising → discount rate climbs further, M&A funding costs rise against existing leverage → multiple stays depressed. **Tilt**: toward bear, small — incremental, not acute, since credit remains tight and much of the move is already reflected in the stock's cheap multiples.
