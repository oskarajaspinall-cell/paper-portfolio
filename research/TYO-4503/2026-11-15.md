# Astellas Pharma (TYO:4503) — Core initiation — 2026-11-15
## 1. Business
Japanese research-based drugmaker selling branded prescription drugs. The franchise is led by XTANDI (prostate cancer, co-promoted with Pfizer, profits shared in the US), with newer "strategic brands" such as PADCEV and VYLOY taking over growth [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tyo/4503/transcripts/705801-q2-2027/). Regional revenue split (US, Japan, China): [data unavailable] in the fact sheet and not confirmed from a primary source; the US is plausibly well above 10% [Guessing], so the macro overlay should check USD/JPY.

## 2. Quality
Gross margin is steady (80.8% TTM vs 80.5% in FY2022) [Certain]. Returns have recovered from the FY2024 trough: ROIC 17.3% TTM (4.7% in FY2024), operating margin 21.1% (7.8%) (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tyo/4503/financials/ratios/). Net debt/EBITDA is 0.27x [Certain]. Direction change: FCF margin is 20.6% TTM, 2.8pp below its 23.5% peak (2026-06-30), but still 2.4pp above a year ago; FCF conversion has fallen to 127.5% as earnings caught up with cash flow, not because cash collapsed [Likely]. Moat is patent-based and finite: management guides that XTANDI sales decline from the fiscal third quarter on US price negotiation (IRA) and then US generic entry in 2027, which the rising profit margin has not yet absorbed [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tyo/4503/transcripts/705801-q2-2027/). Durability depends on strategic brands replacing XTANDI [Guessing].

## 3. Valuation
At 2,699.00 JPY the stock trades at 12.6x P/E and 6.6x EV/EBITDA, both below the bottom of their 5y ranges (15.5x and 7.2x), 33% and 43% below peers (n=5 Japanese pharma) [Certain]. P/FCF is 9.8x and FCF yield 10.15%. P/B of 2.4x is 97% up its range, above peers by 22% [Certain]. The reverse DCF implies revenue falling 7.2% in year 1, so the market already prices the XTANDI cliff [Likely]. Mechanical fair value (base 4,138.87 JPY) is flagged unreliable: DCF and P/E disagree 2.3x, so weigh it lightly. The price is 15.1% above the 200-day average with a 12-month gain of 45.6%: a working stock, though the discount partly reflects peak-looking earnings on one drug [Likely].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Operating margin (income statement, TTM) falls below 15%.
- ROIC (ratios page, TTM) falls below 10%.
- FCF margin (cash flow page, TTM) falls below 12%.
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) rises above 2.0x, e.g. from debt-funded licensing deals or a failed pipeline replacement.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
FCF conversion of 127.5% of net income and a 10.15% FCF yield give ample capacity [Certain]. Share count is stable (0.02% YoY change) [Certain]. Management reaffirmed a rising dividend and keeps buying pipeline assets, e.g. an immuno-oncology option and licence deal with Audax (source: https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/astellas-and-audax-biosciences-enter-exclusive-option-and-license-agreement-for-dax-044-a-novel-immune-targeted-il-12-antibody-cytokine-fusion-for-immuno-oncology-302920594.html) [Likely]. Net debt was 2.38x EBITDA in FY2024, so the balance sheet shows past deal-led leverage; the path back to 0.27x is a positive [Certain].

## 6. Unusual items
