# Extra Space Storage Inc. (EXR) — Core initiation — 2026-10-12
## 1. Business
Extra Space Storage is the largest self-storage REIT in the US, earning month-to-month rental income from 4,281 owned/JV stores (~330m sq ft) [Certain] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1289490/000128949026000011/exr-20251231.htm), management fees from 1,856 third-party-managed stores, and interest/fee income from a $1.5bn bridge-loan and preferred-equity platform [Certain] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1289490/000128949026000011/exr-20251231.htm).

## 2. Quality
Scale (largest US operator) in a fragmented market of mostly small local operators supports a fee-generating management platform that doubles as an acquisition pipeline, plus proprietary pricing/marketing technology [Certain] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1289490/000128949026000011/exr-20251231.htm). But returns on capital have deteriorated, not just cycled: ROIC fell from 8.9% (FY21) to 5.4% TTM and ROE from 26.1% to 7.0% over the same period [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/exr/financials/ratios/). Net debt/EBITDA (site definition) rose from 5.55x to 5.94x TTM, among the higher levels in the peer set [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/exr/financials/ratios/), even as management still calls the balance sheet "low leverage" with ~$2bn of revolver capacity [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/exr/transcripts/657057-q2-2026/). New supply and competitive bidding for acquisitions are cited risks that could cap future occupancy/rate gains [Likely] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1289490/000128949026000011/exr-20251231.htm). Net: a durable operating platform, but structurally lower returns on capital than five years ago.

## 3. Valuation
At 134.65 USD, EXR trades at 18.8x EV/EBITDA, the 2nd percentile of its own 5y range (18.5x-33.0x) and in line with peers (19.3x median, -2%) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/exr/financials/ratios/). P/E of 29.8x sits in the lower third of its own range (35th percentile) but 8% above peer median (27.7x) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/exr/financials/ratios/). P/B of 2.1x is near the bottom of its own range and 64% below peer median (5.9x), reflecting a different book-leverage mix than Public Storage [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/exr/financials/ratios/). FCF yield is 3.58% [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/exr/statistics/). The fact sheet's base fair value (P/FFO proxy, best method) is 151.54 USD, 12.5% above last close; bear 127.96 (-5.0%), bull 202.61 (+50.5%) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/exr/). Multiple compression to a 5y-range low in EV/EBITDA, with price ~5% below both the 50- and 200-day averages, suggests the market has already priced the ROIC decline rather than assuming further deterioration.

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROIC (ratios page, https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/exr/financials/ratios/) falls below 4.5%, extending the current decline
- Net debt/EBITDA, site definition (same ratios page) rises above 6.5x
- Same-store revenue growth reported in the quarterly earnings release (https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/exr/) turns negative, reversing FY2026 guidance of 1-2% growth
- Occupancy reported in the quarterly earnings release falls below 92%, versus 94.2% in Q2 2026

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
EXR funds growth via its bridge-loan/preferred-equity platform ($1.5bn outstanding, a level management calls "comfortable") [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/exr/transcripts/657057-q2-2026/) and ~$2bn of revolver capacity, recently terming out debt with a $550m note priced at 4.9% [Certain] (source: https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/extra-space-announces-pricing-of-550-million-of-4-900-senior-notes-due-2032--302809868.html). Shares outstanding grew 2.02% YoY, typical REIT equity funding for acquisitions [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/exr/statistics/). A 3.58% FCF yield is modest for a REIT still reliant on external growth to offset lower returns in the existing portfolio [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/exr/statistics/).

## 6. Unusual items
CEO succession: Joe Margolis to retire, Noah Springer to become CEO effective January 1, 2027 [Certain] (source: https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/extra-space-storage-announces-executive-leadership-transition-302857841.html). A planned handover at a scale operator, not itself a red flag, but worth monitoring for execution continuity. Short interest is 2.33% of float, not elevated [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/exr/statistics/). No other governance or macro items material.
