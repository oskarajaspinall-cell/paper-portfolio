# Macro overlay: Extra Space Storage Inc. (EXR) — 2026-10-12
## Assessment
Chain: real yields -> REIT discount rate/cap rate -> valuation multiple & fair value range; credit spreads -> refinancing cost -> margin on EXR's externally funded, 6x-leveraged growth model.

Market pricing — facts: 10y real yield (DFII10) 2.87% on 2026-10-08, +44bp/1m, +56bp/3m [Certain] (https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=DFII10). 10y Treasury (DGS10) 5.22% same date, +42bp/+68bp [Certain] (https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=DGS10) — this exact 5.22% is already the fact sheet's risk-free input behind base fair value 151.54, so today's level is priced, not a surprise (research/EXR/factsheet-2026-10-12.md). Breakeven inflation (T10YIE) 2.33%, roughly flat [Certain] (https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=T10YIE, 2026-10-09) — the move is real-rate/term-premium, not inflation **(inference)**. 2y yield (DGS2) 4.75% sits above fed funds 3.88% (https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=DGS2, DFF, 2026-10-08), a rough proxy that near-term cuts aren't priced **(inference)**. HY spread 3.15%, +45-48bp/1m-3m (https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=BAMLH0A0HYM2) raises EXR's marginal bond-funding cost versus June's $550m note priced at 4.90%.

Still rising, not stabilizing, yields/spreads are a negative surprise for discount rate and refinancing cost — though EXR's EV/EBITDA is already at a 5y-low (2nd percentile) and trails SPY by 9.2pp over 3m, so part is already reflected **(inference)**.

Bull: yields/spreads roll over -> multiple re-rates toward 5y-median EV/EBITDA, fair value drifts toward/above base case.
Bear: yields/spreads keep rising -> discount rate and refinancing cost climb further, pressuring the range below the current bear case and squeezing externally funded growth.
Tilt: toward bear, moderate — leverage and external-funding reliance make EXR unusually exposed to the current direction of rates/spreads, adding downside beyond the ROIC-deterioration thesis already in the note.
