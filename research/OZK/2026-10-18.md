# Bank OZK (OZK) — Core initiation — 2026-10-18
## 1. Business
Bank OZK is a Little Rock, Arkansas commercial bank [Certain]. It earns net interest income by funding a loan book — historically concentrated in large commercial real-estate construction loans originated by its Real Estate Specialties Group (RESG), alongside a growing corporate/institutional banking (CIB) book — with low-cost deposits, plus modest fee income [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/ozk/).

## 2. Quality
Returns have been solid and fairly stable: ROE 11.3% TTM, within a 11.8%-14.1% range across FY2021 to FY2025 (flat, -0.6pp over 5 years) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/ozk/financials/ratios/). Net margin is high (44.5% TTM) though falling (-3.7pp over 5y), while FCF conversion is strong and rising (110.3% TTM, +16.9pp over 5y) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/ozk/financials/cash-flow-statement/, https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/ozk/financials/income-statement/). Piotroski F-score is only 4/9 [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/ozk/statistics/), a middling read on balance-sheet trend quality. The durable edge is RESG's underwriting niche in large, complex construction loans few regional banks can structure or hold at scale [Likely]. The offsetting weakness is concentration: a large share of RESG exposure sits in a small number of outsized credits, and news flow shows at least one "largest credit" had its maturity extended and was called "more critical" by sell-side commentary [Likely] (source: https://www.tipranks.com/news/the-fly/citi-says-915m-bank-ozk-loan-in-more-critical-situation-thefly-news, https://www.tipranks.com/news/the-fly/bank-ozk-provides-info-on-long-term-extension-of-largest-credit-thefly-news). That single-name concentration, not competition, is the real threat to returns [Likely].

## 3. Valuation
The market prices in meaningful credit risk. OZK trades at P/E 7.6x (13th percentile of its own 5y 7.2x-10.3x range) versus a 10.8x peer median (EWBC, WAL, CFR, SNV), -29%; P/B 0.8x versus a 1.6x peer median, -47%, near the bottom (2nd percentile) of its own 5y 0.8x-1.2x range; and P/FCF 6.6x versus 10.4x peers, -37%, also near its 5y low [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/ozk/financials/ratios/, peer statistics pages). FCF yield is 15.2% [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/ozk/statistics/). The reverse DCF implies the price assumes revenue shrinks 12.6% in year 1 before fading to terminal growth — a scenario consistent with real, not merely feared, credit losses [Certain] (source: research/OZK/2026-10-18-valuation.md). Base fair value of $84 (+82% vs the $46.12 close) assumes the concentration risk does not crystallize beyond history; it is one model estimate, not a signal, and the peer discount suggests the market won't pay up until that risk clears [Likely].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROE (ratios page, https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/ozk/financials/ratios/) falls below 10% for two consecutive quarters — provisioning outrunning earnings power.
- Net margin (income statement, https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/ozk/financials/income-statement/) falls below 40% — large-credit losses reaching the P&L.
- FCF conversion, FCF/NI (cash flow and income statement, https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/ozk/financials/cash-flow-statement/) falls below 80% — earnings quality deteriorating as nonaccruals build.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Cash conversion is a strength: FCF margin 47-64% and FCF/NI over 100% in four of the last five fiscal years [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/ozk/financials/cash-flow-statement/). Shares outstanding fell 2.16% year-over-year, consistent with buybacks [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/ozk/statistics/). Management also continues a long run of consecutive quarterly common dividend increases [Certain] (source: https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/10/01/3373369/0/en/bank-ozk-announces-increase-to-quarterly-common-stock-dividend-and-announces-preferred-stock-dividend.html), signalling confidence capital generation can keep funding both buybacks and the dividend without impairing the capital base [Likely].

## 6. Unusual items
Short interest is unusually elevated at 17.5% of float and still rising (+6.6% versus the prior month) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/ozk/statistics/), consistent with the market actively betting on the RESG concentration risk discussed above. Institutional ownership is high at 94.6% [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/ozk/statistics/). Next earnings (Oct 20, 2026) fall just after this note and will be the first test of whether the extended "largest credit" and broader RESG book are performing as management expects [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/ozk/).
