# Macro overlay: Bank OZK (OZK) — 2026-10-18

## Assessment
OZK's bear case rests on RESG construction-loan concentration (the $915M "largest credit" extension); rates are a real but secondary lever on that risk, not the thesis itself. **Chain**: policy rate path & long yields -> CRE borrowers' refinancing economics (and OZK's own funding/NIM) -> credit costs and earnings; risk-free rate -> CAPM discount rate -> fair-value multiple.

**Facts** [Certain] (FRED, https://fred.stlouisfed.org/, 2026-10-15/16): fed funds 3.88% (+0.25pp 1m/3m — a hike just delivered); 10y Treasury 5.10% (+0.53pp 3m); 2y Treasury 4.77% (+0.61pp 3m); 10y real yield (DFII10) 2.78% (+0.43pp 3m); 10y breakeven 2.35% (+0.02pp 1m, broadly anchored); US HY credit spread 3.23% (+0.47pp 1m, +0.52pp 3m, widening); VIX 16.46 (-0.74 1m, calm).

**Market pricing (inference)**: the 2y yield sitting ~89bp above fed funds is a *proxy* for a market still pricing further tightening/no near-term cuts. With breakevens flat, the yield rise is a real-rate move, lifting the risk-free input (5.10%) already used in OZK's fair value — a further rise would cut it directly. HY spreads widening while VIX stays low is a credit-specific, not broad-risk, repricing — consistent with (not proof of) the market's existing 0.47x P/B peer discount on OZK.

**Bull**: rates plateau, HY spreads narrow -> RESG refinances without loss, asset-sensitive NIM benefits from the hike already delivered -> re-rate toward peer multiples.
**Bear**: higher-for-longer rates + continued spread widening -> CRE refinancing stress intensifies, provisions rise, ROE risks the <10% invalidation trigger, discount rate rises -> fair value drifts toward/below the $74.95 bear case.
