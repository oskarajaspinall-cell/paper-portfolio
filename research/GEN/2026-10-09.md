# Gen Digital Inc. (GEN) — Core initiation — 2026-10-09

## 1. Business
Gen sells consumer subscriptions under Norton, Avast, LifeLock, Avira/AVG and MoneyLion: antivirus, identity protection and financial-wellness tools, cross-sold on a common platform to ~500m users (79m paying) in 150+ countries [Likely] (source: https://www.gendigital.com). It also runs Engine, an embedded-finance/insurance marketplace, now a $500m annualised-revenue line per Q1 FY27 commentary [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/gen/transcripts/662803-q1-2027/). Revenue is almost entirely recurring auto-renewing subscriptions.

## 2. Quality
Returns remain solid but compressing: ROIC fell from 20.5% (FY22) to 14.4% TTM, ROCE from 26.7% to 16.8% [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/gen/financials/ratios/). Operating margin is high and rising (42.8% TTM, +6.0pp over 5y) but gross margin is eroding (78.0% TTM, -6.9pp) as lower-margin marketplace revenue (Engine) scales — management flagged "slightly lower gross margin due to revenue share" in its fastest-growing categories [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/gen/transcripts/662803-q1-2027/). The moat is switching-cost/habit-driven: auto-renewal subscriptions, 90% LifeLock retention, NPS >70, and rising cross-sell (27% Norton households multi-product, ARPU up 8-10% in two years) [Likely] (same source). Eleven straight quarters of paid-customer growth (now 81m) support durability [Likely] (same source). Leverage is a constraint: net debt/EBITDA 3.17x TTM [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/gen/financials/ratios/) and Altman Z of 1.42 sits in the distress zone despite strong cash generation [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/gen/statistics/) — returns on capital are partly flattered by debt-funded buybacks, not pure operating gains.

## 3. Valuation
GEN trades at a large discount to the enterprise-security peer set (FTNT, CHKP, PANW, ZS): P/E 13.3x vs 66.8x peer median, EV/EBITDA 8.8x vs 130.4x, P/FCF 8.8x vs 43.0x [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/gen/statistics/). That gap is not fully like-for-like — peers are faster-growing enterprise cybersecurity names, GEN is a mature, slower-growth consumer subscription business — but even against its own five-year range GEN sits near the bottom (P/E 30th percentile, EV/EBITDA 9th percentile) [Certain]. The reverse DCF implies the market is pricing roughly -2.6% revenue growth in year one fading to terminal growth, against actual Q1 FY27 bookings growth of 11% and a raised FY27 revenue guide of 9-11% [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/gen/transcripts/662803-q1-2027/). Base-case fair value of $42.15 (+85% vs last close $22.75) assumes continued mid-to-high-single-digit growth and margin stability; bear case ($24.83, +9%) assumes growth and margins both deteriorate toward recent troughs [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/gen/).

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Paid customer count (company transcripts/filings) falls for two consecutive quarters, reversing the current 11-quarter growth streak.
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) rises above 4.0x, signalling buybacks/M&A are outpacing free cash flow rather than being funded by it.
- Gross margin (income statement) falls below 75%, confirming marketplace/Engine mix shift is structurally eroding profitability rather than being offset by scale.
- ROIC (ratios page) falls below 10%, confirming the five-year downtrend has broken through a level consistent with a durable moat.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
FCF conversion is strong (TTM FCF/NI 147%, FCF margin 30.5%) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/gen/financials/cash-flow-statement/). In Q1 FY27 Gen returned $181m to shareholders ($100m buybacks, $81m dividends), paid down $45m of debt, and generated $430m FCF, holding net leverage at 2.95x — just below its stated <3x target [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/gen/transcripts/662803-q1-2027/). Share count is shrinking (-1.52% YoY) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/gen/statistics/), but leverage remains elevated enough (3.17x TTM per the financials) that capital return is competing with deleveraging, not purely additive.

## 6. Unusual items
Short interest is elevated at 7.8% of float and rising (+1.5% month-on-month) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/gen/statistics/). At the September 2026 AGM, shareholders failed to pass the advisory vote on executive compensation — a governance flag worth monitoring but not yet a financial one [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/gen/transcripts/746596-agm-2026/). Altman Z of 1.42 (distress zone) alongside 3.17x net leverage is a balance-sheet risk factor given the business is otherwise highly cash-generative [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/gen/statistics/).
