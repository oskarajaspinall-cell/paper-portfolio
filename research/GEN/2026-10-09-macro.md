# Macro overlay: Gen Digital Inc. (GEN) — 2026-10-09
## Assessment
GEN is a USD-reporting consumer-subscription software business with limited macro linkage through earnings — revenue is recurring, defensive, non-cyclical [Likely]. The channel that matters is leverage: net debt/EBITDA is 3.17x TTM and Altman Z is 1.42, distress zone [Certain] (stockanalysis.com ratios/statistics, 2026-10-09), so debt cost/availability affects refinancing, buybacks and the equity risk premium.

**Chain**: rising real yields/credit spreads → higher refinancing cost on ~3.2x leverage → less FCF for buybacks/dividends → higher equity risk premium → multiple pressure, independent of bookings growth.

**What's priced**: 10y real yield (DFII10) 2.92% (2026-10-07), +0.49pp ~1m/+0.61pp ~3m; 10y breakeven (T10YIE) flat at 2.35% (-0.02pp 1m) [Certain, FRED] — the rise is real-rate, not inflation-expectations, driven **(inference)**. 2y yield (4.77%) sits above effective fed funds (3.88%) [Certain, FRED], a proxy suggesting cuts aren't yet priced **(inference)**, adverse for refinancing relief. HY credit spread is 3.09%, +0.39pp 3m [Certain, FRED] — widening but still historically tight; no stress signal yet.

**Bull**: yields/spreads ease → refinancing risk fades → re-rate toward base/bull fair value ($42-97 vs $22.75). **Bear**: yields/spreads keep rising → leverage constraint tightens, buybacks curtailed, risk premium rises → pulls toward bear case ($24.83) with unchanged fundamentals. **Tilt**: toward bear, small — real but HY spreads stay tight and VIX is low (15.08, falling) [Certain, FRED]; thesis still rests on fundamentals/valuation discount.
