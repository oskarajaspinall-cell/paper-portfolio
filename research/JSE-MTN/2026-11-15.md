Naira and rand exposure matters for the macro overlay.

## 2. Quality
Good, cash-generative, but not a clean compounder [Likely].
- TTM operating margin 32.2% and ROCE 26.9% are both at 2-year highs; ROIC 15.5% is 2.4pp below its 2-year high of 17.9% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/jse/MTN/financials/ratios/?p=trailing).
- FCF margin is falling over five fiscal years (-2.9pp) but rising over the last year (15.5% to 21.7%): a recovery from a trough, not a long uptrend [Likely].
- FY2023-24 shows fragility: ROIC 8.3% and ROE -7.6% in FY2024, with net margin -5.0% [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/jse/MTN/financials/ratios/). That fits currency shocks in Nigeria [Guessing].
- Net debt/EBITDA 1.13x; Piotroski F 7 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/jse/MTN/statistics/).
- The moat is licensed spectrum and scale in markets with few rivals [Likely]. Returns are exposed to FX and regulation.
