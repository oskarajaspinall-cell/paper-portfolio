# MTN Group (JSE:MTN) — Core initiation — 2026-11-08
## 1. Business
MTN is a pan-African and Middle East mobile operator earning voice, data, fintech (mobile money) and infrastructure revenue in emerging markets; Nigeria and Ghana were named strong contributors in the latest results call (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/jse/MTN/transcripts/665948-q2-2026/) [Likely]. Revenue share by country: [data unavailable]; Nigeria is probably ~10%+ of revenue, for the macro overlay to check [Guessing]. Reports in ZAR.

## 2. Quality
Profitability is good and recovering, but not yet proven durable [Likely]. Operating margin is 32.2% TTM against 23.2% in FY2024 and 30.9% in FY2022; gross margin is 70.3% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/jse/MTN/financials/income-statement/). ROIC is 15.5% TTM, flat over five years (14.9% in FY2021) and 2.4pp below its 2-year high of 17.9%; it was 8.3% in FY2023 and net margin was negative in FY2024 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/jse/MTN/financials/ratios/). The last 12 months improved sharply: ROIC +6.7pp, FCF margin 15.5% to 21.7% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/jse/MTN/financials/ratios/?p=trailing). The FY2023-24 trough shows earnings are exposed to currency and macro shocks in its markets, so the recovery may be partly cyclical [Likely]. Moat: scale and spectrum licences, leading positions in concentrated mobile markets [Likely]. Net debt/EBITDA 1.13x; Piotroski F 7 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/jse/MTN/statistics/).

## 3. Valuation
Cash flow looks cheap, earnings do not [Likely]. FCF yield is 13.15% and P/FCF 7.6x, but that is 95% of its 5y range (6.3x–7.7x) and in line with peers (7.3x). EV/EBITDA is 4.8x vs 4.4x 5y median (62% of range) and 17% below the 5.8x peer median. P/E is 22.1x vs 18.8x 5y median and 13.5x peers (+64%); P/B is 2.5x, 90% of range, +24% vs peers (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/jse/MTN/statistics/). Peers: Vodacom, Airtel Africa, Orange, Telkom. The price therefore assumes the recent margin and cash-flow peak is sustained, with little margin for a reversal. Fair value: [data unavailable] (no ZAR FX rate for the model). Price is 9.3% above its 50-day average, 25.1% higher over 12 months, RSI 67.3: not a washed-out entry [Certain].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Operating margin (income statement TTM) falls below 27%
- ROIC (ratios page TTM) falls below 12%
- FCF margin (cash-flow TTM) falls below 15%
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) rises above 2.0x

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
FCF conversion is high at 288.8% of net income TTM (232.1% FY2025) because net margin (7.5%) is depressed by finance and non-cash costs; FCF margin is 21.7% [Likely]. Leverage is moderate at 1.13x. Shares outstanding rose 1.42% YoY, mild dilution (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/jse/MTN/statistics/). Management reported a new capital allocation framework and a fintech separation (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/jse/MTN/transcripts/405402-q4-2025/) [Likely]; details unverified.

## 6. Unusual items
Emerging-market currency and regulatory exposure (ZAR reporter, Nigeria/Ghana operations) matters for country risk [Likely]. Beta 0.86; institutions own 57.7%, insiders 0.11%; short interest [data unavailable]. No dated legal or M&A catalyst in the headlines beyond the fintech separation and IHS transaction mentioned in the Q1 2026 call (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/jse/MTN/transcripts/609625-q1-2026/) [Likely].
