# Public Storage (PSA) — Core initiation — 2026-10-12

## 1. Business
PSA owns and operates self-storage facilities, renting units to consumers and businesses monthly with no long-term lease commitment; ancillary revenue comes from tenant insurance/merchandise and third-party property management fees [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/psa/). In 2026 it closed acquisitions of National Storage Affiliates (22 Jul) and Public Storage Canada (1 Sep), adding scale and a new country [Certain] (source: https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260722542295/en/Public-Storage-Announces-Closing-of-National-Storage-Affiliates-Acquisition/, https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260901151661/en/Public-Storage-Completes-Acquisition-of-Public-Storage-Canada/).

## 2. Quality
ROIC fell from 13.5% (FY21) to 11.6–11.7% TTM, a -1.9pp five-year decline [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/psa/financials/ratios/). ROE swings between 18.8% and 44.6% over the last five fiscal years (21.9% TTM), consistent with M&A/gain-on-sale accounting rather than steady operating gains [Likely] (same source). Net debt/EBITDA (site definition) has risen from 2.56x to 2.89x, +0.33x over five years, funded partly by debt for the NSA and Canada deals plus a new C$400m senior-notes offering priced 9 Sep 2026 [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/psa/financials/ratios/, https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260909653598/en/Public-Storage-Prices-Public-Offering-of-C400-Million-Senior-Notes-in-its-Inaugural-Offering-in-the-Canadian-Market/). Piotroski F-score is 5, unremarkable [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/psa/statistics/). Scale and local density give PSA a real cost advantage over smaller operators, but tenant switching costs are low and the US industry has absorbed heavy new supply, so pricing power depends on local occupancy tightness, not a durable moat [Likely]. Margin and FCF-conversion lines are not shown on the income-statement/cash-flow pages for this REIT [data unavailable].

## 3. Valuation
At 289.86 USD, PSA trades on 27.7x TTM P/E, 19.3x EV/EBITDA and 13.5x EV/Sales [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/psa/financials/ratios/). Against its own five-year range, P/E sits mid-band (61st percentile) while EV/EBITDA is near the five-year low (16th percentile): the market pays near-trough EBITDA multiples versus a mid-cycle earnings multiple [Likely]. Versus the EXR/CUBE/NSA peer median, PSA carries a 3% EV/EBITDA premium and a 12% EV/Sales premium but a 7% P/E discount [Certain] (same source; peer statistics pages). P/B of 10.5x is far above its own five-year range (max 7.0x) and the peer median (3.3x); book value understates replacement cost for an old REIT portfolio, weakening P/B as a signal [Likely]. Base fair value (P/FFO-proxy; NAV unavailable) is 330.19, +13.9% above last close; bear 179.69 (-38.0%), bull 345.34 (+19.1%) [Certain] (source: fair value table, factsheet-2026-10-12.md).

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROIC (ratios page, https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/psa/financials/ratios/) falls below 10%, extending the current five-year decline from 13.5% to 11.6%.
- Net debt/EBITDA, site definition (ratios page, same URL) rises above 3.5x from the current 2.89x.
- Piotroski F-score (statistics page, https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/psa/statistics/) falls below 4 from the current 5.
- A subsequent quarterly filing (https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/psa/filings/) cuts full-year core FFO or occupancy guidance below the raise disclosed with Q2 2026 results.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
The board declared a $3.00/share quarterly dividend in August 2026 [Certain] (source: https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260806612593/en/Public-Storage-Declares-Third-Quarter-2026-Dividends/). FCF yield is 3.03% [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/psa/statistics/). Shares outstanding are roughly flat (+0.14% YoY), so growth is being funded with debt rather than buybacks or material equity issuance [Certain] (same source) — consistent with the rising net debt/EBITDA in Quality above, as capital has gone into the NSA and Canada acquisitions rather than shareholder returns beyond the dividend. FCF margin and FCF-to-net-income conversion are not disclosed on the cash-flow/income-statement pages for this REIT [data unavailable].

## 6. Unusual items
Short interest is 3.65% of float, up 15.4% month-over-month [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/psa/statistics/) — modest but rising, consistent with bearish positioning on self-storage supply/demand, not yet at a distress level. Institutional ownership is 83.0% [Certain] (same source). The peer set used for Valuation includes NSA, now a PSA subsidiary (closed 22 Jul 2026), so its standalone multiple may be stale and overstate the peer-median comparison [Likely]. Otherwise none material.
