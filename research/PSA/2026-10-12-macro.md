# Macro overlay: Public Storage (PSA) — 2026-10-12
## Assessment
PSA's fair-value model discounts cash flows at the 10y Treasury yield plus a beta-scaled equity risk premium, and the REIT just added debt (NSA, Canada; a C$400m senior-notes offering priced 2026-09-09) that must eventually be refinanced [Certain] (source: research/PSA/factsheet-2026-10-12.md). Official data: the 10y real yield (FRED DFII10) is 2.87% (2026-10-08), up 44bp/1m, 56bp/3m; the 10y Treasury (DGS10) is 5.22%, up 68bp/3m; the HY credit spread (BAMLH0A0HYM2) is 3.15%, up 45bp/3m [Certain] (https://fred.stlouisfed.org). Breakeven inflation (T10YIE) is flat (2.33%, -4bp/1m), so the yield rise looks real-rate/term-premium driven, not inflation re-pricing **(inference)**. Market pricing: the 2y yield (4.75%, DGS2) sits 87bp above effective fed funds (3.88%, DFF), a proxy for the market expecting policy to stay tight or tighten further, not cut soon **(inference, proxy)**. PSA fell -9.2% over 3 months, underperforming SPY by -12.8pp, coincident with this yield back-up **(inference)**. Chain: real yields & spreads -> cost of equity/debt -> REIT multiple (P/FFO) & refinancing cost -> fair-value range, on top of fundamentals (supply/occupancy) that remain the primary driver. Bull: yields stabilize/retrace -> multiple expands toward the 330 base fair value. Bear: real yields extend past ~3.25% with wider spreads -> multiple compresses further, refinancing the C$400m notes gets costlier, pressuring an already-levered balance sheet (net debt/EBITDA 2.89x, rising). Tilt: toward bear, moderate — rising leverage into a rising-rate, widening-spread backdrop is an incremental risk beyond the fundamentals case already flagged in the note.
