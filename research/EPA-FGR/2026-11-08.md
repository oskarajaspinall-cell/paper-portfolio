# Eiffage SA (EPA:FGR) — Core initiation — 2026-11-08
## 1. Business
French construction, infrastructure and energy-systems contractor with a concessions arm (motorways, rail, airports) that supplies recurring toll cash flow [Likely]. Contracting is project-based and low-margin; concessions carry the cash conversion and the debt. Revenue mix by country/region: [data unavailable]; the company site was not on the allowlist, so no foreign-exposure share is stated (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/epa/FGR/).

## 2. Quality
- Returns are solid for a contractor: ROIC 8.6%, ROCE 11.2%, ROE 17.7% TTM, with ROCE rising 2.2pp over five years (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/epa/FGR/financials/ratios/) [Certain].
- Operating margin is stable at 9.9% TTM (9.5% in FY2021) and net margin 4.0%; no margin erosion [Certain]. Gross margin of 83.9% is a site classification quirk and not comparable with contractors' usual figures [Likely].
- Leverage is falling: net debt/EBITDA 2.81x TTM from 3.80x in FY2021, though it rose from 2.60x at FY2025 [Certain]. Altman Z is 1.04 (weak, but concession debt distorts it) and Piotroski F is 7 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/epa/FGR/statistics/) [Likely].
- Moat: long-dated concessions and a record order book are a durable, regulated base [Likely]; the contracting side has no durable moat [Likely]. Last year's FCF margin direction is falling (10.9% vs 12.0% a year earlier) (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/epa/FGR/financials/cash-flow-statement/?p=trailing) [Certain].

## 3. Valuation
P/E is 9.7x against a 5y median of 9.8x, so earnings are priced in line with history, but EV/EBITDA of 5.2x is below its 5y minimum of 6.1x and 30% below the peer median of 7.5x [Certain]. P/B is 1.2x and P/FCF 3.6x (peers 8.0x). FCF yield of 27.70% is not credible as distributable cash: FCF conversion of 269.4% of net income likely includes concession and working-capital items [Guessing]. The mechanical fair value of 748.57 EUR against a 106.00 EUR close (DCF 1,048.84) is a model artefact driven by that FCF, and I place no weight on it. The reverse DCF implying -29.2% year-1 revenue growth is likewise a symptom of an inflated FCF base [Likely]. On earnings the stock is fairly valued to modestly cheap: roughly a 10x P/E for a stable, 9.9%-operating-margin compounder with falling leverage (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/epa/FGR/statistics/) [Likely].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Operating margin (income statement, TTM) falls below 9.0%.
- ROIC (ratios page, TTM) falls below 7.5%.
- Net debt / EBITDA (ratios page) rises above 3.2x.
- FCF margin (cash flow statement, TTM) falls below 8.0%.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Deleveraging is the main use of cash: net debt/EBITDA down 1.19x in five years. Shares outstanding rose 1.98% year on year (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/epa/FGR/statistics/), mild dilution, possibly employee share plans [Guessing]. Cash conversion is very high at 269.4% FCF/NI, but falling from 321.8% in FY2021 and likely flattered by concession accounting [Likely]. The H1 2026 call reported a record order book and lower light-vehicle traffic on concessions (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/epa/FGR/transcripts/575544-h1-2026/) [Likely].
