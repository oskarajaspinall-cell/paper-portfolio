# Cirrus Logic, Inc. (CRUS) — Core initiation — 2026-10-18
## 1. Business
Cirrus designs mixed-signal and audio semiconductors — low-power audio codecs, boosted amplifiers, haptics drivers and camera controllers (audio) plus power ICs and smart codecs (HPMS) — sold mainly to smartphone OEMs via multi-year co-design cycles, with growing PC, professional-audio, automotive, industrial and metrology sales [Certain].

## 2. Quality
Returns are strong and improving: ROIC 28.4% TTM, ROCE 20.0% TTM, operating margin 23.0% TTM (+2.5pp over 5y), FCF margin 27.9% TTM, FCF conversion 132.9% TTM, net cash (net debt/EBITDA -1.45x) [Certain]. The moat is narrow but durable while it holds: deep IP in low-power mixed-signal design embeds Cirrus in a customer's architecture for a design cycle's life, hard for a rival to displace mid-cycle [Likely]. The flip side is customer concentration — revenue is dominated by one large smartphone customer (not quantified by stockanalysis.com) — so a lost design win would be material; this is the biggest quality risk [Likely]. Diversification is underway: Q1 FY2027 HPMS net sales rose $43.5m y/y on smartphone and PC content, including design wins tied to NVIDIA's RTX Spark PC platform [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/772406/000077240626000037/crus-20260627.htm), still small relative to the core audio franchise [Likely].

## 3. Valuation
The stock trades at a steep discount to its own history and peers (SWKS, QRVO, SYNA, DLB): P/E 13.2x vs 5y range 15.4–33.0x (below the 5y min) and peer median 26.4x (-50%); EV/EBITDA 8.3x vs peer median 13.8x (-40%); P/FCF 9.4x vs peer median 21.6x (-56%); FCF yield 10.58% [Certain]. P/B (2.5x) is roughly in line with peers (2.5x). Base fair value is $111.01 vs the $107.87 close (+2.9%); bear $58.18 (-46.1%); bull $149.68 (+38.8%); the reverse DCF implies the price embeds only 5.1% year-1 revenue growth fading to terminal [Likely]. The stock has fallen 34.6% in six months and sits 22.6% below its 200-day average, reading as the market pricing in customer-concentration/content-loss risk even though margins and returns have not deteriorated — the crux of the call. Little margin of safety in the base case against material upside if diversification holds [Likely].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Net sales growth (10-Q MD&A) decelerates below high-single digits for two consecutive quarters as HPMS/PC content fails to offset audio ASP declines.
- Gross margin (income statement page) falls below 50% for two consecutive quarters, signalling an unfavourable mix shift.
- ROIC (ratios page) falls below 15%, signalling the mixed-signal design-cycle moat has eroded.
- A disclosed design-win loss at the primary smartphone customer (10-Q risk factors or earnings-call transcript).

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
No amounts are drawn under the Third Amended Credit Agreement; net cash position (net debt/EBITDA, site definition, -1.98x) [Certain]. FCF conversion of 132.9% TTM funds both R&D and bolt-on M&A without leverage [Certain]. Cirrus acquired INVENTVM Semiconductor, an Italian fabless mixed-signal power/audio IC designer, on 2026-10-06 [Certain] (source: https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20261006608573/en/Cirrus-Logic-Announces-Acquisition-of-INVENTVM-Semiconductor/). Separately, on 2026-10-13 Cirrus confirmed it had approached Synaptics (SYNA) about a deal but is "not currently pursuing a transaction" [Certain] (source: https://www.tipranks.com/news/the-fly/cirrus-logic-confirms-making-synaptics-bid-currently-not-pursuing-a-transaction-thefly-news) — appetite for consolidation that could resurface.

## 6. Unusual items
Fiscal 2017-19 federal returns are under IRS examination for US/UK transfer pricing; the Revenue Agent's Report proposes roughly $168.3m of additional tax (excl. interest) plus roughly $63.7m of penalties, which Cirrus disputes via IRS Appeals [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/772406/000077240626000037/crus-20260627.htm). The outcome of the dispute is uncertain [Guessing]. A new GlobalFoundries wafer-supply commitment through 2028 (newly disclosed risk factor) obligates fixed quarterly wafer purchases regardless of demand, risking excess inventory if smartphone volumes soften [Likely] (same filing). Short interest is 6.98% of float, down 3.3% month-on-month; institutional ownership is reported at 103.7% of float [Certain].
