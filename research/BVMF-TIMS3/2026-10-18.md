# TIM S.A. (BVMF:TIMS3) — Core initiation — 2026-10-18
## 1. Business
TIM S.A. is Brazil's third-largest mobile operator, also selling fixed broadband (Ultrafibra) and B2B/IoT services; postpaid is now ~70% of mobile service revenue [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/bvmf/TIMS3/transcripts/548708-q2-2026/). It is a subsidiary of TIM Brasil Serviços e Participações S.A. [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/bvmf/TIMS3/).

## 2. Quality
Returns are rising and already attractive: ROIC 17.6% TTM (+7.0pp over 5y), ROCE 16.6% (+7.7pp), ROE 17.3% [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/bvmf/TIMS3/financials/ratios/). Operating margin has expanded to 25.8% and FCF margin to 33.4% [Certain] (same source). Brazil's mobile market has rationalised to three scaled operators (TIM, Vivo, Claro), giving pricing power management is testing via front-book increases and convergent bundles [Likely] (source: transcript below). Diversification into broadband and B2B, now the fastest-growing segments [Likely] (same source), reduces reliance on mobile, where growth has slowed on price-driven churn [Likely] (same source). Leverage is modest (net debt/EBITDA 1.05x TTM) [Certain] (ratios page), though the I-Systems deal lifted debt somewhat, addressed via a subsidiary capital increase [Likely] (transcript). Piotroski F-Score is 7/9 [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/bvmf/TIMS3/statistics/). The edge is rational industry structure and scale behind high capex barriers, not a technology moat — a risk if a new entrant reopens price competition [Likely] (transcript: a "below-the-line" promotional shift is already underway).

## 3. Valuation
TTM P/E of 10.5x sits below its own 5-year minimum (10.8x) and 32% below peer median (15.4x, n=3: VIVT3, AMX, TIGO); EV/EBITDA 4.4x is 24% below peers; P/FCF 4.9x is 48% below peers' 9.5x [Certain] (source: fact sheet valuation table). The reverse-DCF implied growth is -15.5% in year 1, fading to terminal growth by year 10 — the market is pricing a revenue decline despite recent mid-single-digit growth [Certain] (source: fact sheet fair-value section). Base fair value of BRL41.25 (DCF 67%/EV-EBITDA 33% blend) implies +117.9% upside to the BRL18.93 close; even the bear case (BRL24.30) implies +28.4% [Certain] (same source). The stock has also underperformed SPY by 32.8pp over 12 months [Certain] (source: fact sheet price table), suggesting sentiment, not fundamentals, is driving the discount — consistent with cyclically promotional competition rather than structural deterioration.

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROIC (ratios page) falls back below 13% (FY2024 level), signalling the margin-expansion story has reversed.
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) rises above 2.0x, signalling M&A or capex is funded with leverage beyond the cash generation management described.
- Mobile postpaid net adds turn structurally negative for two consecutive quarters (10-K/20-F or earnings release), evidencing a competitive price war rather than a cyclical promotional phase.
- FCF margin (cash flow/income statement) falls below 20%, evidencing the cash conversion quality has broken down.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
FCF conversion is 211% of net income TTM (FCF margin 33.4% vs net margin 15.8%) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/bvmf/TIMS3/financials/cash-flow-statement/, income-statement/), funding both dividends and the I-Systems/V8 bolt-on acquisitions without net debt/EBITDA exceeding 1.05x [Certain] (ratios page). Shares outstanding fell 0.89% YoY, indicating no dilutive issuance [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/bvmf/TIMS3/statistics/). Management says it has "enough cash" for both capex and shareholder remuneration [Likely] (transcript).

## 6. Unusual items
Controlling-shareholder structure: TIM S.A. is a subsidiary of TIM Brasil Serviços e Participações S.A. [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/bvmf/TIMS3/), a related-party/minority-protection consideration typical of Brazilian corporate structures. Institutional ownership is low at 16.4% (insiders 0.05%) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/bvmf/TIMS3/statistics/). Altman Z-score of 2.18 sits in the grey zone, though this is typical for capital-intensive telecoms rather than a distress signal on its own [Likely]. Short interest is [data unavailable].
