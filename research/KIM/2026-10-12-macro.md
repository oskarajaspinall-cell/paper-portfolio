# Macro overlay: Kimco Realty Corporation (KIM) — 2026-10-12
## Assessment
KIM is a ~6.1x net-debt/EBITDA retail REIT [Certain]: `real yields -> REIT discount rate/cap rates & cost of debt -> valuation multiple and FFO growth` [Likely] **(inference)**.

Facts (FRED, 2026-10-08/09): US 10y real yield (DFII10) 2.87%, +0.44pp 1m/+0.56pp 3m (fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=DFII10); 10y nominal (DGS10) 5.22%, +0.68pp 3m; 2y (DGS2) 4.75%, +0.59pp 3m; fed funds (DFF) 3.88%, +0.26pp 3m; 10y breakeven (T10YIE) 2.33%, roughly flat. Core CPI index is a lagging August read (+0.98pp 1m), not a forward signal.

Market pricing **(inference)**: DGS2 ~87bp above DFF is a proxy — not a forecast — for the market still leaning toward further tightening over cuts [Likely]; firmer breakevens over 3m imply inflation risk is seen as skewed up, unresolved.

Bull: inflation cools, Fed signals hikes are done, DFII10 eases toward ~2.3-2.5% -> cap rates compress -> KIM re-rates toward bull fair value 31.17 [Likely].
Bear: sticky inflation + further hikes push DFII10 above 3.0% and DGS10 above 5.5% -> higher refinancing cost on ~6.1x leverage -> multiple compresses toward bear fair value 21.18 [Likely].

Tilt: yields have already risen across the curve over 1-3m while the 2y/fed-funds spread implies further tightening is leaned toward, a live headwind for this levered REIT, whose stock already underperformed SPY by ~15pp over 3m — rates look like a priced-in but still-moving risk, not resolved **(inference)**.
