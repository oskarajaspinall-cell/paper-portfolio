# Kimco Realty Corporation (KIM) — Core initiation — 2026-10-12

## 1. Business
Kimco is a US REIT owning open-air, grocery-anchored shopping centers and mixed-use assets concentrated in major coastal/Sun Belt metros [Certain]. Revenue is base rent, percentage rent and expense recoveries from grocers, service retailers and "small shop" tenants, with incremental growth from re-leasing spreads, redevelopment and capital recycling (selling mature assets, redeploying into mixed-use) [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/kim/transcripts/658099-q2-2026/).

## 2. Quality
Grocery-anchored centers in supply-constrained metros are a scarce asset — zoning and land scarcity limit new competing supply; management cites "record small shop occupancy" and "limited new supply supporting rent growth" [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/kim/transcripts/752153-bofa-ny-global-real-estate-conference-2026/). But the moat is not clearly showing up in returns on capital: ROIC is only 3.1%→4.0% TTM over five years, and ROE fell from 10.7% (FY2021) to 5.8% TTM, a -5.2pp five-year trend [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/kim/financials/ratios/) — both likely below what a REIT's implied cost of capital requires to create equity value, so the scarcity benefits occupancy and rent, not obviously shareholder returns. Leverage has improved but stays elevated: net debt/EBITDA (site definition) is 6.12x TTM, down from 8.81x in FY2021 [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/kim/financials/ratios/), a real constraint if rates stay higher for longer. Piotroski F-score is 5/9 — middling, no clear tell either way [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/kim/statistics/).

## 3. Valuation
At 22.16, KIM trades on 26.0x P/E (6th percentile of its own 5y range, whose 132.9x ceiling is an outlier) and 17.3x EV/EBITDA (4th percentile) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/kim/financials/ratios/) — low percentile reflects a skewed history, not obvious cheapness. Versus peers (REG, FRT, BRX, SKT) KIM is roughly in line to slightly rich on EV/EBITDA (+2%) and EV/Sales (+4%), and 14% above peer median P/E, but at a 49% discount to peer median P/B (1.4x vs 2.8x) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/kim/statistics/) — more a reflection of below-peer ROE than mispricing. The fact sheet's own-history fair value (P/FFO proxy, the owner's best method here) puts base fair value at 22.61 — near spot (+2.1%) — with bear 21.18 (-4.4%) and bull 31.17 (+40.7%) [Certain] (source: research/KIM/factsheet-2026-10-12.md). The price already embeds close to the base case; there is little margin of safety, and the bear case sits barely below spot.

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROIC (ratios page) falls back below 3% for two consecutive fiscal years, reversing the FY2021–2025 recovery.
- Net debt/EBITDA, site definition (ratios page), rises back above 7x, undoing the FY2021–2025 deleveraging.
- ROE (ratios page) continues its five-year decline and drops below 4%.
- Piotroski F-score (statistics page) falls to 3 or below, signalling broad fundamental deterioration.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Shares outstanding fell -0.23% YoY [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/kim/statistics/) — a modest buyback offset to dilution, not a large capital-return program. FCF yield is 4.58% [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/kim/statistics/), but FCF margin, FCF conversion and standard income-statement margins are [data unavailable] on stockanalysis.com for this REIT, so true cash conversion can't be verified from the fact sheet. Management's own Q2 2026 commentary describes continued capital recycling (selling mature assets, redeploying into mixed-use/redevelopment) and a raised 2026 outlook alongside a dividend increase (figures not used as data; headline only) [Likely] (source: https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/08/04/3338124/27734/en/Kimco-Realty-Announces-Second-Quarter-2026-Results.html, as listed in the fact sheet's news feed).

## 6. Unusual items
Short interest is 5.40% of float, up 22.3% month-over-month [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/kim/statistics/) — a moderate but rising bearish bet, not yet distress-level but worth monitoring. Institutional ownership is reported at 100.8% (ETF/overlap double-counting is common for large-cap REITs) and insider ownership is 2.24% [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/kim/statistics/) — neither is a governance red flag on its own.
