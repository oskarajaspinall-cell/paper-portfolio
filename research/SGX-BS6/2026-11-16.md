Singapore-listed, China-based shipbuilder (financials in CNY, Industrials sector) that earns from building commercial vessels on long-dated order books, plus an investment portfolio earning financial income. ## 2. Quality
Profitability has risen sharply: ROCE 26.4% (FY2025) to 30.5% (TTM), gross margin 13.8% (FY2021) to 35.2% (TTM), operating margin 32.1% TTM (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sgx/BS6/financials/ratios/) [Certain]. Net cash (net debt/EBITDA -1.05x) and Altman Z 4.16 show a strong balance sheet [Certain].
Direction conflict: TTM ROIC fell to 40.1% from 65.9% a year earlier (2-year high 112.1%), and FCF margin fell to 8.5% from 21.5% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sgx/BS6/financials/ratios/?p=trailing). ROCE and operating margin still rose over the same year, so the ROIC fall looks driven by the capital base and financial-income mix rather than weaker ship margins [Guessing]. Shipbuilding margins at this level are cyclical; the durable moat is capacity and yard position, not proven pricing power [Likely].

## 3. Valuation
P/E 9.3x sits at 84% of its 5y range (6.7x–9.8x) but 31% below the peer median 13.5x; EV/EBITDA 6.4x is 23% below peers (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sgx/BS6/statistics/) [Certain]. P/FCF 31.7x and P/B 2.7x are above the 5y maximum, and FCF yield is 3.16% [Certain]. Peers are Korean and Chinese shipbuilders, which are cyclical and not fully comparable.
Mechanical fair value base 2.12 SGD vs close 4.32, bear 1.73 and bull 10.01 SGD; the reverse DCF implies 33.2% year-1 revenue growth, so the price needs margins to stay at peak (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sgx/BS6/) [Likely]. Price is 3.2% above the 200-day average, 12.6% below the 50-day, and 12-month change is +29.0% [Certain]. Cheap versus peers on earnings, not versus its own history or cash flow.

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Operating margin (income statement, TTM) falls below 26%.
- ROCE (ratios page, TTM) falls below 20%.
- FCF margin (cash flow page, TTM) stays below 8.5% for the next two reported periods, i.e. cash conversion does not recover.
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) rises above 0x (net cash lost).
