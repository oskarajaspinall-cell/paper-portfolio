# AAK AB (STO:AAK) — Core initiation — 2026-11-08
## 1. Business
AAK makes specialty vegetable oils and fats, sold as ingredients to food, chocolate/confectionery, dairy-alternative and food-service customers, earning a processing and formulation margin over raw materials [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sto/AAK/). It reports in SEK. Revenue split by country/region: [data unavailable]; the company's IR domain was not fetchable (blocked), so no foreign share ≥10% could be confirmed or ruled out.

## 2. Quality
Returns are good and well above 2021-22: ROIC 15.1% TTM vs 12.0% in FY2021 and a 17.7% peak in FY2024; operating margin 10.5% vs 6.6% [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sto/AAK/financials/ratios/). A moat from customer-specific formulations and scale is plausible but unproven by the fact sheet [Guessing].

Direction: the last year is softer. ROCE fell from 22.4% to 19.1% and ROIC sits 2.7pp below its 2-year high (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sto/AAK/financials/ratios/?p=trailing) [Certain]. The Q3 headline cites pricing pressure across segments [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sto/AAK/transcripts/532943-q3-2026/). That suggests FY2024's margin peak was partly cyclical [Likely]. Net debt/EBITDA of 0.60x is low [Certain].

## 3. Valuation
At 182.60 SEK the shares trade at 13.4x P/E against a 5y range of 19.8x-35.1x, and 9.0x EV/EBITDA against 12.8x-18.5x, both below the 5y minimum [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sto/AAK/financials/ratios/). Versus peers (BG, DAR, CRDA, CRBN) P/E is -36% and EV/EBITDA -17%; P/B is +26% [Certain]. FCF yield is 6.01% [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sto/AAK/statistics/).

Mechanical base fair value is 202.39 SEK (+10.8%), but it blends a DCF of 146.06 and a P/E of 315.06, so the two methods disagree widely; the bear case is far below the price [Certain]. The reverse DCF implies 7.3% year-1 revenue growth [Certain]. The shares are down 31.0% over 12 months and sit 18.3% below the 200-day average, so the market is pricing in margin normalisation [Likely]. Whether the de-rating overshoots depends on how much of the margin gain is structural [Guessing].

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
TTM FCF conversion is 80.8% and FCF margin 6.2%, but FY2025 FCF was negative (-0.9% margin) and FY2022 deeply so, from working-capital swings with raw-material prices [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sto/AAK/financials/cash-flow-statement/). Share count is up 0.11% YoY (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sto/AAK/statistics/). The Q4 2025 headline mentions higher dividends and a buyback [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sto/AAK/transcripts/236226-q4-2025/). Leverage is modest.
