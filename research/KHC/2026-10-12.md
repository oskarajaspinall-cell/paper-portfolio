# The Kraft Heinz Company (KHC) — Core initiation — 2026-10-12

## 1. Business
Kraft Heinz makes and markets packaged food and condiments (Heinz ketchup, Kraft, Oscar Mayer, Philadelphia, Velveeta, Lunchables) sold mainly through North American and international grocery, retail and foodservice channels, earning revenue on volume and price/mix across condiments, cheese, meals and meats [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/khc/).

## 2. Quality
Scale brands give some pricing power, but returns have deteriorated. ROE swung from +5.5% (FY2024) to -12.8% (FY2025) and -8.8% (TTM) [Certain], and net margin collapsed from +10.6% to -23.4% (FY2025) / -13.6% (TTM) while operating margin fell only modestly (21.4%→18.0% TTM) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/khc/financials/ratios/, https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/khc/financials/income-statement/) — the gap is consistent with a large non-operating or impairment-type charge, not a core-margin collapse [Likely]. ROIC improved to 8.1% TTM but ROCE is flat near 7% [Certain], short of a clear double-digit-return moat. FCF margin is actually rising (12.2%→15.3% TTM) [Certain], so cash generation held up despite the earnings hit, but net debt/EBITDA (~3.0x) [Certain] limits flexibility (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/khc/financials/ratios/). Short interest is elevated and rising (11.52% of float, +14.8% month-on-month) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/khc/statistics/), consistent with management's own admission of "green shoots, but we need to do better" on Q2 trends (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/khc/ — Aug 5 2026 headline) [Certain].

## 3. Valuation
KHC trades at a discount to its own 5y range and to peers (GIS, CPB, MDLZ, CAG) on EV/EBITDA (7.8x vs 5y range 8.2x–11.6x, peer median 8.4x) and P/FCF (6.9x vs peer 9.8x, below its 5y minimum of 7.8x) [Certain], with a 14.45% FCF yield [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/khc/financials/ratios/, https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/khc/statistics/). P/B at 0.7x is far below peers' 1.9x [Certain] — more consistent with doubt about book-value quality (goodwill/brand carrying values) than genuine cheapness. EV/Sales is a premium to peers (1.7x vs 1.5x) [Certain], reflecting leverage inflating EV relative to sales. Base fair value ($60.80, +173% vs the $22.27 close) implies the market prices roughly -15.5% year-one revenue decline fading to terminal growth [Certain] — a discount that looks inflated by KHC's unusually low reported 5y beta (0.11) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/khc/statistics/), understating the true cost of equity. Treat the fair value range with caution; it is inform-only, never a buy signal alone.

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROE (ratios page) returns to positive and ROIC holds above 8% for two consecutive fiscal years, confirming the FY2025 hit was a one-off, not structural decline.
- Net margin (income statement) recovers above 8% without a further impairment-type charge.
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) falls below 2.8x, showing real deleveraging progress.
- FCF margin (cash flow statement) holds above 14%, confirming cash conversion is resilient regardless of reported earnings volatility.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
FCF margin is rising (11.1% FY2023 → 15.3% TTM) [Certain] and FCF yield is 14.45% [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/khc/financials/cash-flow-statement/, https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/khc/statistics/), funding the dividend and debt service, but net debt/EBITDA near 3.0x [Certain] constrains room for buybacks. Shares outstanding fell only -1.13% year-on-year [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/khc/statistics/), so capital return skews toward debt paydown over repurchases. FCF conversion vs net income reads as deeply negative (-112.4% TTM) [Certain] purely because net income itself is negative; cash generation, not the accounting earnings line, is the metric to track here.

## 6. Unusual items
FY2025/TTM net income and ROE turned sharply negative while operating margin fell only modestly — consistent with a large non-operating or impairment-type charge that the fact sheet cannot itself confirm; the next 10-K should be checked for the specific line [Likely]. Short interest at 11.52% of float and rising [Certain] is elevated for a consumer staple (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/khc/statistics/). The General Counsel resigned effective October 20, 2026 "to pursue other opportunities," with no successor yet named [Certain] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1637459/000163745926000067/khc-20261002.htm) — routine, not thesis-moving on its own. The company is transferring its exchange listing from Nasdaq to NYSE [Certain] (source: https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260826560484/en/Kraft-Heinz-to-Transfer-Stock-Exchange-Listing-to-NYSE/, as listed in the fact sheet) — administrative only.
