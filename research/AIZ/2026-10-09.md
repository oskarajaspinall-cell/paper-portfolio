# Assurant, Inc. (AIZ) — Core initiation — 2026-10-09

## 1. Business
Assurant provides niche insurance and protection/support services embedded inside other companies' products: mobile-device protection and extended service contracts sold through carriers and retailers (Global Lifestyle), and lender-placed and renters insurance sold through mortgage servicers and property managers (Global Housing), plus smaller automotive, commercial-equipment and financial-services protection lines [Certain] (source: https://www.assurant.com). It earns premiums, fees and float income through long-term B2B contracts rather than direct-to-consumer distribution [Certain] (source: https://www.assurant.com).

## 2. Quality
Returns are rising and well above cost of capital: ROE climbed from 10.6% (FY21) to 18.3% TTM and ROIC from 9.0% to 14.4% TTM [Certain] (fact sheet: RA). FCF conversion has stayed above 140% of net income since FY2022 (155.7% TTM), and net debt/EBITDA has fallen to 0.29x (site definition) [Certain] (fact sheet: CF,IS,BS,RA). The moat is relational: Assurant is embedded inside OEM, carrier, retailer and mortgage-servicer workflows, with 20+ year client tenures, 325m+ end customers and claims infrastructure across 21 countries that is costly for a client to re-tender [Likely] (source: https://www.assurant.com). Q2 2026 was a record quarter, with adjusted EBITDA +18% and EPS +19% y/y ex-catastrophes, and management raised full-year guidance [Certain] (fact sheet news, 2026-08-05 transcript, /stocks/aiz/transcripts/661339-q2-2026/). Piotroski F-Score is only middling (5/9) [Certain] (fact sheet: ST), and Housing carries catastrophe-weather exposure that can spike losses in any single quarter [Likely].

## 3. Valuation
At $272.86 the stock sits close to its own 5y history in absolute terms but at a premium to peers. P/E (13.1x) is at the 38th percentile of its 5y range yet +68% above the ALL/PGR/KMPR/CINF peer median (7.8x); EV/EBITDA (8.0x) is below its own 5y minimum but +12% above peers; P/FCF (8.1x) is near its own 5y floor (7th percentile) yet +33% above peers; P/B (2.2x) is above its own 5y max and +31% above peers [Certain] (fact sheet: RA,ST,P:ALL,P:PGR,P:KMPR,P:CINF). The fact sheet's own-history fair value (P/B+ROE primary, P/E secondary) is $263.95 base and $283.91 bull — the current price is only 3.3% above the base case and 4.1% below the bull case [Certain] (fact sheet: IS,BS,CF,RA,ST,HI). The market is already paying for the elevated-ROE, raised-guidance trajectory; there is limited margin of safety against the base case despite the peer-relative premium.

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROE (ratios page) falls back below 12% (the FY2024 level), unwinding the 5y improvement
- FCF conversion (FCF/net income, cash-flow and income-statement pages) drops below 100%
- Net debt/EBITDA, site definition (ratios page) rises above 0.5x
- Piotroski F-Score (statistics page) falls below 4

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
FCF conversion has averaged above 140% of net income since FY2022 (155.7% TTM) [Certain] (fact sheet: CF,IS), reflecting insurance float and light capex rather than working-capital drawdown. Shares outstanding fell 1.49% y/y [Certain] (fact sheet: ST), consistent with an active buyback; Assurant's Q2 2026 10-Q confirms ongoing share repurchases and a quarterly common dividend paid in H1 2026, without disclosing a remaining authorization figure [Certain] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1267238/000126723826000041/aiz-20260630.htm). Net debt/EBITDA is low and falling (0.29x, site definition), leaving room to keep funding both buybacks and the dividend from operating cash flow [Likely].

## 6. Unusual items
Institutional ownership is reported at 101.2% of float, a float-definition artifact rather than a governance flag [Certain] (fact sheet: ST). Short interest is low (2.70% of float) but rose 14.9% month-over-month [Certain] (fact sheet: ST) — not large enough on its own to be material. Otherwise none.
