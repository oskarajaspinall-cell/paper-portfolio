## Bear case

EV/EBITDA of 110.0x is far above AMD's own 5y maximum of 61.0x and the 31.9x peer median; FCF yield is 0.79% [Certain]. Momentum is already slowing: 3-month momentum is +17.6% against +193.2% over six months [Certain]. The nearest swing-low support, 491.12, sits far below the stop [Certain].
## Bull case
