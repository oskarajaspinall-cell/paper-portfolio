# Evaluation: Advanced Micro Devices, Inc. (AMD) — 2026-10-07
## Bear case
The setup is crowded going into a binary event. The stock trades 25.2% above its 50-day MA and 71.3% above its 200-day MA, within reach of the 52-week high of 658.52, after a +193.2% six-month run [Certain]. EV/EBITDA of 110.0x and EV/Sales of 25.5x sit above their own 5y maxima [Certain]. The fundamentals have not caught up: the TTM operating margin of 15.8% is still below FY2021's 22.2%, and ROIC is only 9.8% [Certain]. The Anthropic, Saudi and World Labs announcements are already in the price, so the Nov 3 print has to beat expectations that are now very high [Likely]. With a beta of 2.45, a guidance miss could gap the shares straight through a −10% stop. That makes the realised loss larger than planned, and the payoff is worse than the roughly 1:1 the target and stop imply [Likely].
## Bull case

The fundamentals are getting better: gross margin is 55.7% TTM vs 52.5% in FY2025, the FCF margin is 20.3%, the company holds net cash (−0.92x net debt/EBITDA) and its Piotroski F-score is 8 [Certain]. The Anthropic MI450 deal of up to 2GW could lift data-center GPU guidance [Likely]. Short interest is low at 2.46%, so there is little forced selling [Certain]. The portfolio is all cash, so position limits are not a constraint [Certain].
