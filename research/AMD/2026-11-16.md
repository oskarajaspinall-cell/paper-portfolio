# Advanced Micro Devices (AMD) — Core initiation — 2026-11-16
## 1. Business
Fabless designer of x86 CPUs (EPYC server, Ryzen PC), Instinct AI accelerators, Radeon/semi-custom console chips and embedded FPGAs; sells to hyperscalers, PC OEMs, console makers and industrial customers. Data Center (EPYC plus Instinct MI450 launch) and Embedded drove Q3 net revenue of $13.4 billion, up 45%; Client and Gaming fell on lower semi-custom demand [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2488/000000248826000191/amd-20260926.htm). Country revenue split: [data unavailable] in the sources read; China/export-control exposure is flagged as a risk [Certain].

## 2. Quality
Margins are recovering, but returns are not yet high-quality. TTM gross margin is 56.0% (5y trend rising), operating margin 17.8% vs 9.4% a year earlier, ROIC 12.8% vs 5.2% a year earlier, though ROIC is still far below FY2021's 72.8% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/amd/financials/ratios/). [Certain] FCF margin is 15.5%, down 1.5pp on a year ago and 7.4pp below its 2-year high; FCF conversion is 92.6% vs a 5y rising trend. Net cash (net debt/EBITDA -0.59x), Piotroski 7, Altman Z 13.65 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/amd/statistics/). Moat: CPU share against Intel is durable [Likely]; in GPUs Nvidia's software ecosystem is the barrier, and the 10-Q newly names both rivals' pricing and ecosystem tactics as risks [Certain]. Customer warrants and capacity commitments (below) suggest some demand is being bought [Likely].

## 3. Valuation
The price assumes a lot. P/E 111.4x sits at 25% of its 5y range (the range peaked at 278.9x on depressed earnings), but EV/EBITDA 78.0x and EV/Sales 19.1x are above their 5y maxima and 316% and 115% above peers (NVDA, INTC, AVGO, QCOM). P/FCF 124.1x, FCF yield 0.81% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/amd/statistics/). [Certain] The mechanical fair value base of 66.47 USD vs a 534.06 close is flagged unreliable (methods disagree 4.1x), so I weigh it lightly; even the EV/EBITDA method's base of 134.56 is far below the price [Certain]. The price is a bet on AI-accelerator share gains and earnings several times today's. Price is 25.8% above the 200-day average, 107.7% up over 12 months, beta 2.54; Friday's sharp fall followed Nvidia CEO news (source: https://invezz.com/news/2026/11/13/amd-falls-9-intel-drops-6-as-chip-stocks-slide-on-friday/) [Likely].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Gross margin (income statement page, TTM) falls below 50%.
- Operating margin (income statement, trailing) falls below 12%.
- ROIC (ratios page, trailing) falls back below 8%.
- Data Center segment revenue growth turns negative year on year in a 10-Q (company filing), or net cash turns to net debt (net debt/EBITDA above 0x on the balance-sheet/ratios pages).

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Shares outstanding rose 1.36% YoY (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/amd/statistics/), so dilution persists. Q3 filing shows $4.75 billion of new senior notes in August 2026, $875 million of notes repaid, and agreements to buy Taalas and World Labs, the latter completed today [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2488/000000248826000191/amd-20260926.htm; https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/11/16/3403615/0/en/amd-completes-acquisition-of-world-labs.html) [Certain]. Cash conversion has slipped from the FY2025 155.4% to 92.6% TTM [Certain].

## 6. Unusual items
- New 10-Q language: long-term capacity purchase agreements, cloud GPU compute commitments (AMD buys capacity it may not place with third parties), lease guarantees, and warrants issued to customers valued on judgmental probabilities of exercisability. These are off-the-usual-path commitments that could weigh on cash if AI demand softens [Likely] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2488/000000248826000191/amd-20260926.htm).
- Added risks: export controls/tariffs, and rack-scale Helios products AMD licenses but doesn't build, adding supply-chain complexity [Certain].
- Gaming revenue fell 40% in Q3 on semi-custom weakness [company-reported] (same filing).
- Headline: TensorWave signed a six-year OpenAI agreement for AMD-powered AI infrastructure, backed by AMD Ventures [Likely] (source: https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20261116130015/en/TensorWave-Announces-Agreement-with-OpenAI-to-Deliver-More-Than-450MW-of-AMD-Powered-AI-Infrastructure/).
- Chip selloff on AI-spending doubts; Commerce closed a remote-access loophole for Chinese firms [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/news/commerce-closes-remote-access-chip-loophole-china/). Short interest 2.37% of float.
