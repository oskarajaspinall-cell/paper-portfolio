The increase in net revenue was driven by an increase in Data Center segment revenue primarily driven by the strong demand for our AMD EPYC™ processors and the launch of our AMD Instinct™ MI450 Series GPUs, and an increase in Embedded segment revenue as demand strengthened across end markets, partially offset by a decrease in our Client and Gaming segment revenue primarily due to lower demand for semi-custom products.
