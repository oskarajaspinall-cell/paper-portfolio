# HD Korea Shipbuilding & Offshore Engineering (KRX:009540) — Core initiation — 2026-10-17
## 1. Business
Intermediate holding company atop HD Hyundai's shipbuilding group (Hyundai Heavy Industries, Hyundai Mipo, Hyundai Samho, HD Hyundai Engine), building commercial vessels (container ships, tankers, LNG/gas carriers), offshore platforms, naval/defense ships and marine engines [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/krx/009540/transcripts/694086-q3-2025/). Revenue is billed on multi-year newbuild contracts as construction milestones are met, so results track contracted vessel pricing, mix and yard productivity [Likely].

## 2. Quality
Returns have inflected sharply: ROIC rose from -10.4% (FY2021) to 31.0% (FY2025) and 44.0% TTM; ROCE to 23.3% TTM; operating margin to 15.5% TTM from -9.0% (FY2021) [Certain] (ratios/income-statement pages). Management attributes this to higher vessel prices, productivity and FX, with a mix shift toward high-value gas carriers and order intake tracking the annual target each recent quarter [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/krx/009540/transcripts/694078-q1-2026/, https://stockanalysis.com/quote/krx/009540/transcripts/694124-q2-2026/). The balance sheet is net cash (net debt/EBITDA -1.77x TTM) [Certain]. The moat is structural scale: LNG/gas-carrier construction is concentrated among a handful of Korean/Japanese/Chinese yards with multi-year order books, and naval/defense ties (reported Palantir and HII collaborations with the Hyundai Heavy Industries subsidiary) add a second franchise competitors can't quickly replicate [Likely] (sources: https://www.gurufocus.com/news/6811189/palantir-expands-partnership-with-hyundai-heavy-industries, https://www.gurufocus.com/news/3160987/hii-collaborates-with-hyundai-heavy-industries-to-enhance-uskorea-shipbuilding-ties). Risk: FCF conversion is lumpy (568% FY2023, 160% FY2025, reflecting milestone billing) and Altman Z is 2.36, the grey zone rather than the safe zone, despite net cash [Certain].

## 3. Valuation
Every multiple sits far below its 5-year range and peers: P/E 5.4x vs a 5y range of 13.3x-38.6x and peer median 27.1x (-80%); EV/EBITDA 2.2x vs peer 13.6x (-84%); P/FCF 4.0x vs peer 30.6x (-87%); P/B 1.0x vs peer 3.3x (-70%); FCF yield 25.11% [Certain]. The price assumes the current margin and ROIC won't hold — either vessel-pricing tailwinds fade, or the market discounts TTM returns (44% ROIC) as a cyclical peak versus the FY2025 run-rate (31%) [Likely]. Base fair value of 1,008,525.51 KRW (+280.6% vs the 265,000 KRW close) [Certain] is highly sensitive to how much of the TTM margin persists; the gap's size argues for treating it as a ceiling, not a base case, until more quarters confirm the margin is structural rather than cyclical [Guessing]. Even the bear case (-47.1%) is cheaper than peers on every multiple shown [Likely].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Operating margin (income statement page) falls below 8%, signalling the margin upcycle has reversed toward FY2024-type levels.
- ROIC (ratios page) falls below 15%, suggesting returns are reverting from the current peak faster than the thesis assumes.
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page, site definition) turns net-debt positive (currently net cash at -1.77x TTM), indicating the balance-sheet strength underpinning the re-rating case is eroding.
- FCF margin (cash flow and income statement pages) falls below 5%, indicating cash conversion is not sustaining recent levels.
