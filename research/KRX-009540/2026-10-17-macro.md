# Macro overlay: HD Korea Shipbuilding & Offshore Engineering (KRX:009540) — 2026-10-17

## Assessment
Chain: dollar strength -> KRW depreciation (proxy, not directly observed — FRED's approved set covers CNY/HKD, not KRW) -> FX translation gain on USD-denominated newbuild contracts -> reported KRW revenue/margin **(inference)**. Management has already named FX, alongside vessel pricing and productivity, as a driver of the margin inflection in the Q1/Q2 2026 calls [Likely] (https://stockanalysis.com/quote/krx/009540/transcripts/694078-q1-2026/, https://stockanalysis.com/quote/krx/009540/transcripts/694124-q2-2026/).

Facts: the broad trade-weighted dollar index rose to 121.602 on 2026-10-09, +3.72 over ~1m and +1.1 over ~3m [Certain] (https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=DTWEXBGS, 2026-10-09). The 10y Treasury (used as the DCF risk-free rate) rose to 5.10% on 2026-10-15, +0.53pp over ~3m [Certain] (https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=DGS10, 2026-10-15); against a fair-value gap of +280% base/-47% bear, a ~50bp rate move is second-order.

Market pricing: the stock is down 38.0% over 12 months and trades at -80% P/E and -84% EV/EBITDA discounts to peers [Certain] (factsheet), which **(inference)** looks dominated by fear that the 44% TTM ROIC is a cyclical peak (order-cycle/margin-normalization risk), not by FX or rates.

Bull: continued dollar strength sustains the FX tailwind, helping the current margin persist longer than priced. Bear: a dollar reversal removes one of three cited drivers, reinforcing the margin-peak case. Tilt: toward bull, small — FX is a real but secondary input next to the dominant, non-macro order-cycle debate.
