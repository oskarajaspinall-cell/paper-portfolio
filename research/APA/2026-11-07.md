# APA Corporation (APA) — Core initiation — 2026-11-07
## 1. Business
Independent oil and gas explorer and producer, selling crude, gas and NGLs from the U.S. (Permian), Egypt and the North Sea, with exploration in Suriname, Alaska and Uruguay [Certain] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1841666/000184166626000071/apa-20260930.htm). Earnings follow commodity prices and unit costs. Egypt volumes under the production sharing contract fall as prices rise [Certain] (same filing).

## 2. Quality
Mediocre through the cycle; no durable moat [Likely]. ROIC was 14.4% in FY2024 and 14.0% in FY2025, against 43.8% in FY2023 and 23.0% TTM (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/apa/financials/ratios/). That swing shows returns follow the oil price, not any structural advantage. Operating margin is 43.3% TTM against 21.8% in FY2024 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/apa/financials/income-statement/). FCF conversion is 96.0% of net income TTM, and 124.1% in FY2025. Leverage is low at 0.53x net debt/EBITDA TTM (down from 1.67x in FY2021), and Piotroski F is 8 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/apa/statistics/). Altman Z of 2.02 is grey-zone [Likely]. Beta is 0.28 [Certain]. The 10-Q shows the North Sea declining naturally and Egypt net oil volumes down on price-driven cost recovery [Certain] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1841666/000184166626000071/apa-20260930.htm). Decommissioning liabilities are a real tail risk, and the 10-Q dropped the dedicated risk factor on them, which I read as a disclosure change, not a risk reduction [Guessing].

## 3. Valuation
At 44.63 USD the stock trades at 7.3x P/E (5y median 6.0x) and 2.9x EV/EBITDA, near the bottom of its 5y range (14th percentile, median 3.2x) (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/apa/financials/ratios/). That is 41% below the peer median of 4.9x. P/FCF is 7.2x against a peer median of 12.4x, and the FCF yield is 13.91%. P/B of 2.1x is 25% above peers. The price therefore assumes earnings close to current levels and no durable reset of oil prices [Likely]. The 12-month change is +106.0%, so much of the recovery has happened. Fair value (inform-only) is 48.80 base (+9.3%), 38.46 bear (-13.8%) and 52.86 bull (+18.4%). The reserve NAV method is [data unavailable], so this rests on EV/EBITDA alone [Certain]. Upside to base is modest relative to the commodity risk [Likely].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) rises above 1.5x.
- FCF margin (cash flow and income statement pages) falls below 10% on a TTM basis.
- ROIC (ratios page) falls below 10% on a TTM basis.
- Shares outstanding (statistics page) stop shrinking: shares change YoY turns positive.
