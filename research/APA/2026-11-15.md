## 2. Quality
Returns are cyclical, not structural [Likely]. ROIC fell from 41.6% (FY2022) to 14.0% (FY2025), then recovered to 23.0% TTM; operating margin is 43.3% TTM vs 31.3% a year earlier (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/apa/financials/ratios/). The five-year "falling" label is a peak-to-trough artefact; the last 12 months are rising, which tracks commodity prices and cost cuts rather than a durable moat [Likely]. Management targets annualised cost savings of $500 million by end-2026 [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1841666/000184166626000071/apa-20260930.htm), a real but one-off lever. Egypt net production fell as higher price realisations cut cost-recovery volumes under the contract (same filing) [Certain]: price upside is partly given back. Net debt/EBITDA is 0.53x TTM, FCF conversion 96.0%, Piotroski 8 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/apa/statistics/). No moat beyond cost position [Likely].

## 3. Valuation
Price 48.44 USD sits at the fair-value base of 48.80 (bear 38.46, bull 52.86); only the EV/EBITDA method ran, as reserve NAV is [data unavailable] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/apa/statistics/). EV/EBITDA is 3.1x, 27% up its 5y range, 43% below peers; P/E 7.9x, 39% below peers; P/FCF 7.8x; FCF yield 12.81% [Certain]. P/B 2.3x is 55% above peers. TTM earnings are near a cycle high, so low multiples flatter value [Likely]. The stock has doubled over 12 months (+102.8%), trades 25.0% above its 200-day average and has beaten SPY by 92.4pp. Discount to peers is largely the market pricing leverage to Egypt and a declining North Sea [Guessing]. Verdict: fairly priced and in a strong trend, not cheap on the model.
