# Zhaojin Mining Industry Company (HKG:1818) — Core initiation — 2026-11-08
## 1. Business
Gold miner and producer, classified Materials / Gold (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/1818/). Revenue is driven by the gold price times ounces sold, less mining cost [Certain]. Operations are almost certainly mainland China, and it reports in CNY with HKD-quoted shares [Likely]. No segment or country split was fetched, so the China share is [data unavailable]. Profit is therefore a leveraged call on the gold price and on China policy.

## 2. Quality
Returns have risen sharply from a low base: ROIC 1.7% in FY2021 to 10.4% TTM, ROCE 12.3% TTM, and operating margin 11-12% to 28.5% TTM (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/1818/financials/ratios/) [Certain]. Balance sheet repaired: net debt/EBITDA 7.31x in FY2021 to 0.82x TTM [Certain]. Share count is down 5.09% YoY, so no dilution [Certain].

Direction change: ROIC, ROCE, operating margin and FCF margin are all below their 2-year highs set 2026-06-30 (ROIC 10.4% vs 13.6%; FCF margin 18.8% vs 29.6%), though each is above a year earlier (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/1818/financials/ratios/?p=trailing) [Certain]. The peak was probably the gold price at its height [Likely]. A miner has no durable moat: returns depend on the commodity price and on ore grade/cost position, which I could not verify [Guessing]. Piotroski F is 6 and Altman Z is 2.67, so the business is sound but not distinguished [Certain].

## 3. Valuation
At 17.41 HKD the stock trades at P/E 16.2x, EV/EBITDA 8.4x and P/FCF 14.6x, all below their 5y minimums (29.0x / 14.3x / 26.3x) (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/1818/financials/ratios/) [Certain]. But FY-end history was set on depressed earnings, so the discount to history mostly reflects earnings growth [Likely]. Against three A-share gold peers (SHA:601899, SHA:600547, SHA:600489) it is about 12% dearer on P/E and EV/EBITDA, but 34% cheaper on P/FCF and 32% cheaper on P/B (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sha/601899/statistics/) [Certain]. FCF yield is 6.84%.

Mechanical fair value (EV/EBITDA only; NAV unavailable) is a base of 17.75 HKD, only 1.9% above the price, with a bear of 14.56 and a very wide bull of 51.06 [Certain]. The price assumes TTM earnings are roughly sustained, with no cushion if gold or margins slip. The stock is down 38.6% over 12 months, 47.1pp behind SPY [Certain].

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
FCF conversion is about 104% of net income TTM, up from 41% in FY2023 [Certain]. Cash has gone to deleveraging, from 4.95x to 0.82x net debt/EBITDA over FY2023 to TTM, and the share count is shrinking (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/1818/statistics/) [Certain]. The FY2022 FCF margin was negative at -9.7%, which shows capex swings can overwhelm cash flow [Certain]. Mine reserve spending and dividends were not reviewed [data unavailable].
