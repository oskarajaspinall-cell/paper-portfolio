# The Hartford Insurance Group, Inc. (HIG) — Core initiation — 2026-10-07

## 1. Business
Diversified P&C and group benefits insurer: Business Insurance (commercial P&C, small/mid-market), Personal Insurance (auto/home), and Employee Benefits (group life/disability). Earns underwriting profit plus investment income on float [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/hig/).

## 2. Quality
Returns are high and rising: ROE 22.1% TTM, up from 13.0% in FY2021; ROIC 18.0% TTM, up from 10.9% [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/hig/financials/ratios/). FCF conversion (FCF/NI) is strong at 132.6% TTM but has declined from 167-211% in FY2021-24 [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/hig/financials/cash-flow-statement/, https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/hig/financials/income-statement/). Net debt/EBITDA has fallen steadily to 0.76-0.78x, a conservative insurer balance sheet [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/hig/financials/ratios/). Piotroski F-score of 6/9 corroborates improving fundamentals [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/hig/statistics/). The moat is scale/underwriting discipline in commercial lines and group benefits, harder to replicate than personal auto; management's Q2 2026 transcript cites "disciplined underwriting" and "proactive reserving" sustaining results [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/hig/transcripts/654385-q2-2026/). A CEO transition (President A. Morris Tooker to succeed Christopher Swift, announced 2026-09-30) is an internal promotion, not an outsider hire, limiting strategy-discontinuity risk [Likely] (confirmed via SEC 8-K Item 5.02: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/874766/000087476626000067/0000874766-26-000067-index.htm).

## 3. Valuation
At 8.7x P/E, HIG trades below its own 5-year range (min 9.7x) and in line with peer median (8.7x; TRV, CB, ALL, CINF) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/hig/financials/ratios/). On EV/EBITDA (6.9x vs 5y range 7.7-9.5x, peer median 6.4x) the stock is also below its own history and at a modest 8% premium to peers [Certain]. P/B of 1.8x sits at the top of its own 5y range (1.3-2.0x) but only 2% above peer median, implying the market is paying for the improved ROE rather than for growth [Certain]. P/FCF of 5.9x and an FCF yield of 16.9% are inexpensive in absolute terms [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/hig/statistics/). Net: the price assumes current high returns (ROE ~20%+) persist but does not pay up versus peers for them — the re-rating risk is asymmetric if returns normalize down, but current multiples already look cheap relative to HIG's own re-rated earnings power [Likely].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROE (ratios page) falls below 15% for two consecutive quarters, signalling the underwriting-cycle tailwind has reversed.
- FCF conversion (FCF/NI, cash-flow and income-statement pages) falls below 100%, indicating earnings quality is deteriorating faster than the FY2021-25 trend shows.
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) rises back above 1.2x, reversing the deleveraging trend.
- Piotroski F-score (statistics page) drops below 5, indicating broad-based fundamental deterioration.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
FCF conversion is strong (132.6% TTM, though down from 150-211% in FY2021-24) and shares outstanding have fallen 3.96% YoY, indicating active buybacks [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/hig/statistics/). Management's Q2 2026 transcript references a $4.2bn share-repurchase plan and the announced sale of Hartford Funds, alongside the agreed acquisition of Equitable's Employee Benefits business (announced 2026-08-04) [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/hig/transcripts/654385-q2-2026/; source: https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260804930783/en/The-Hartford-To-Acquire-Equitable%E2%80%99s-Employee-Benefits-Business/). This is a mix of returning capital (buybacks) and reallocating toward the higher-return Employee Benefits segment — consistent with the ROE/ROIC uptrend already visible in the data [Likely].

## 6. Unusual items
CEO succession announced 2026-09-30 (internal promotion, confirmed by SEC 8-K Item 5.02) [Certain]. New independent board director (Priscilla Almodovar, former Fannie Mae CEO) appointed effective 2026-09-01 [Certain] (source: https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260811180836/en/The-Hartford-Appoints-Priscilla-Almodovar-To-Its-Board-of-Directors/). Short interest is negligible (0.01% of float, down 56% month-on-month) and institutional ownership is high at 95.3%, both unremarkable for a large-cap insurer [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/hig/statistics/). Beta of 0.49 indicates low systematic market exposure [Certain]. None of these are individually material to the thesis.
