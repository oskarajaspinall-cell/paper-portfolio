# Macro overlay: The Hartford Insurance Group (HIG) — 2026-10-07
## Assessment
HIG earns underwriting profit plus investment income on a large fixed-income float [Certain] (research/HIG/2026-10-07.md). The variable that matters: `rates -> reinvestment yield on float -> net investment income (earnings)` and, separately, `rates -> mark-to-market on the AFS bond book -> AOCI/book value -> P/B multiple`.

Facts: the 10y Treasury yield (DGS10) is 5.31% (2026-10-05), up +0.83pp/3m; the 10y real yield (DFII10) is 2.95%, up +0.71pp/3m, while 10y breakeven inflation (T10YIE) is 2.36%, up only +0.11pp/3m (https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=DGS10, id=DFII10, id=T10YIE; 2026-10-05/06). High-yield credit spreads (BAMLH0A0HYM2) widened +0.44pp/1m to 3.12% (https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=BAMLH0A0HYM2, 2026-10-05). **(inference)** A real-rate-led move with flat breakevens reads as a term-premium backup, not an inflation surprise — a cleaner NII tailwind than an inflation-driven move, since claims-cost inflation isn't rising in step. The 2y yield (4.84%) above fed funds (3.88%) is a proxy that cuts aren't priced near-term.

**Bull**: rates stay higher, breakevens anchored → reinvestment income keeps rising, reinforcing the ROE uptrend (22.1% TTM) without matching claims inflation. **Bear**: a credit-spread shock or recession-led rate reversal hits bond-portfolio value and future NII together, just as P/B (1.8x) sits at the top of its 5y range. **Tilt**: neutral, small — NII tailwind and AOCI/credit-spread drag roughly offset, and beta of 0.49 shows limited historical sensitivity to macro swings [Likely].
