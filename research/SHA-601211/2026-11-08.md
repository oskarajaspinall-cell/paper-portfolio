# Guotai Haitong Securities (SHA:601211) — Core initiation — 2026-11-08
## 1. Business
China's largest state-backed brokerage, formed by the Guotai Junan–Haitong merger. It earns commissions, investment banking fees, asset management fees, margin-lending interest and proprietary trading gains, so earnings track onshore equity-market turnover and levels [Likely] (source: https://www.scmp.com/business/china-business/article/3292700/china-spur-mergers-us16-trillion-stockbroking-sector-after-guotai-haitong-deal). Revenue split by segment and by region (including the Hong Kong arm) is [data unavailable] in the fact sheet. Reporting currency is CNY (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sha/601211/).

## 2. Quality
Mediocre returns, cyclical margins. ROIC is 2.2% TTM and ROE 8.8% TTM, against 10.3% ROE in FY2021: flat over five years [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sha/601211/financials/ratios/). Operating margin is 54.7% TTM versus 32.6% in FY2023, a rising trend, but it swings with market activity and is not evidence of a moat [Likely]. FCF margin of 207.0% TTM is not meaningful for a broker: it reflects working-capital and client-balance swings, and it is 68.7pp below its 2-year high of 275.7% [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sha/601211/financials/cash-flow-statement/?p=trailing). The durable advantage is scale and state backing plus a licence-limited industry, which supports share but not clearly above-cost-of-capital returns [Guessing]. Shares outstanding rose 32.86% year on year, so per-share growth lags earnings growth [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sha/601211/statistics/).

## 3. Valuation
The stock is cheap on its own history and peers. P/E is 9.6x, below its 5y minimum of 10.0x (median 12.4x) and 3% below the peer median of 10.0x [Certain]. P/B is 0.7x, at the bottom of its 5y range (0.7x–1.0x) and 13% below peers' 0.8x. P/FCF of 1.7x is not a reliable signal for a broker. The price implies about 0.2% year-1 revenue growth in the reverse DCF [Certain]. Mechanical base fair value is 22.96 CNY against a 16.46 close (+39.5%), bear 12.16, bull 39.73; the bear is 26.1% below the close [Likely]. The DCFs rest on cash flow that is distorted by client balances, so I rely more on the P/E method (base 21.25) [Guessing]. Equity-market-dependent earnings at 9.6x TTM, a peak-ish 54.7% operating margin and a weak 12-month trend (-15.6%) argue the discount is partly earned [Likely]. (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sha/601211/statistics/)

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Capital allocation is driven by the state owner and the merger. The share count rose 32.86% year on year, consistent with merger share issuance, so there is no buyback tailwind [Likely]. Dividend and payout figures are not in the fact sheet [data unavailable]. FCF/NI of 509.7% TTM is a broker working-capital artefact, not cash earnings power, and I do not credit it [Likely]. Debt metrics are [data unavailable], so balance-sheet leverage cannot be assessed from the fact sheet.
