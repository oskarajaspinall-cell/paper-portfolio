# Swissquote Group Holding SA (SWX:SQN) — Core initiation — 2026-11-08
## 1. Business
Swiss online bank and broker: earns trading commissions, FX and currency-conversion margins, net interest on client deposits, custody and fund fees, plus a digital-banking (Yuh) joint venture [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/swx/SQN/). Revenue is largely Swiss-domiciled, but the country/region split is [data unavailable]; no foreign market confirmed at ~10%+.

## 2. Quality
Profitable and high-return: ROE 28.0% and ROIC 18.0% (TTM), operating margin 46.3% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/swx/SQN/financials/ratios/). [Certain]
But direction is adverse. ROIC is down from 20.7% a year ago and from a 2-year high of 23.9%; operating margin fell from 49.4% to 46.3% over the same 12 months (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/swx/SQN/financials/ratios/?p=trailing). Over five fiscal years ROIC fell from 32.3% to 19.5%, though net margin rose to 47.0%. [Certain]
A moat from scale, brand and a vertically integrated platform (own bank, broker, custody) is plausible, but retail trading volumes and rate-sensitive interest income are cyclical, and the Piotroski F-score is only 4. [Likely]
Cash-flow ratios (FCF margin 159.6%, FCF/NI 339.4%) are distorted by client-deposit flows in a bank and are not evidence of quality. [Likely]
Management restated 2026 guidance between the March and August results calls (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/swx/SQN/transcripts/609798-h1-2026/); revenue growth looks to be slowing. [Likely]

## 3. Valuation
P/E 14.1x sits at the 20th percentile of its five-year range (12.7x to 19.9x, median 15.4x) and 4% below the peer median of 14.6x (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/swx/SQN/financials/ratios/). P/B of 3.6x is 90% above peers at 1.9x, which reflects its far higher ROE. [Certain]
Reported P/FCF of 4.1x and FCF yield of 24.24% are bank-cash-flow artefacts and shouldn't be relied on. [Likely]
Mechanical base fair value is CHF 41.49 against a CHF 33.80 close (+22.8%); bear is CHF 25.98 (-23.1%) and bull CHF 67.08. The reverse DCF implies 4.2% year-1 revenue growth fading to terminal. [Certain]
The price is -29.0% over 12 months and 14.8% below the 200-day average. It discounts slower growth, but the multiple is not a clear bargain given falling returns. The skew is modestly positive, not compelling. [Likely]
