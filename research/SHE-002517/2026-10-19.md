# Kingnet Network Co., Ltd. (SHE:002517) — Core initiation — 2026-10-19

## 1. Business
Chinese mobile/web game developer and publisher, monetizing live-service titles through in-app purchases; sector Communication Services, industry Electronic Gaming & Multimedia [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/she/002517/).

## 2. Quality
Returns on capital are very high: ROIC 39.6% TTM (34.0–65.6% over FY21–25), ROE 26.6% TTM [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/she/002517/financials/ratios/). Gross margin has risen to 77.8% TTM (70.8% in FY21) and net margin to 31.6% TTM, consistent with a high-margin, scalable IP/live-service model [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/she/002517/financials/income-statement/). FCF conversion has run above net income every year since FY22, reaching 120.5% TTM, and the balance sheet is net cash, with net debt/EBITDA at -2.52x TTM and deepening each year [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/she/002517/financials/cash-flow-statement/, https://stockanalysis.com/quote/she/002517/financials/balance-sheet/). This looks like a durable, cash-generative franchise [Likely]. The caveat: ROCE (-4.6pp) and ROE (-1.9pp) have trended down over five years even as margins rose, suggesting returns on incremental capital are past their peak and growth is slowing [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/she/002517/financials/ratios/). Whether that reflects a maturing portfolio of titles or weaker new-IP pipeline cannot be judged from the numbers alone [Guessing].

## 3. Valuation
P/E of 13.6x sits at the 1st percentile of its own 5-year range (13.5x–24.5x) and EV/EBITDA 10.9x at the 16th percentile — both near historical lows [Certain]. Against four listed peers (SHE:002555, SHE:002558, SHA:603444, NTES), Kingnet trades at a premium on every multiple: P/E +13%, EV/EBITDA +25%, EV/Sales +18%, P/FCF +11%, P/B +4% vs peer medians [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/she/002517/financials/ratios/). FCF yield is 8.95% [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/she/002517/statistics/). The fact sheet's DCF/EV-Revenue blend puts base fair value at 19.27 CNY vs the 15.12 CNY close, +27.4% upside (bear -41.3%, bull +76.6%); the reverse DCF implies the price assumes only 3.0% revenue growth fading to terminal growth [Certain] (source: fact sheet fair-value table). Read together: the market prices Kingnet as a mature, slow-growth cash generator, cheap versus its own history but at a premium to peers — the case rests on holding current margins and cash conversion, not a growth re-rating [Likely].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROIC (ratios page) falls below 25% (vs 39.6% TTM), signalling the moat is eroding.
- Gross margin (income statement) falls below 70% (vs 77.8% TTM, 5y low 70.8%), signalling pricing or IP-cost pressure.
- FCF margin (cash flow + income statement) falls below 20% for two consecutive quarters (vs 38.1% TTM), signalling cash generation is breaking down.
- Net debt/EBITDA (balance sheet + income statement) turns net-debt positive (vs -2.52x net cash TTM), signalling capital misallocation or an earnings shortfall severe enough to consume the cash buffer.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
FCF conversion has run well above net income every year since FY22 (131.8%, 103.7%, 91.5%, 111.9%, 120.5% TTM) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/she/002517/financials/cash-flow-statement/). The net-cash position has deepened every year (net debt/EBITDA -1.56x FY21 to -2.52x TTM), so retained cash is accumulating on the balance sheet rather than funding leverage [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/she/002517/financials/balance-sheet/). Shares outstanding rose 2.06% YoY, a modest dilution rather than a buyback-funded shrink [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/she/002517/statistics/).
