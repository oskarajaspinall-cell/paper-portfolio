# Aluminum Corporation of China Limited (HKG:2600) — Core initiation — 2026-10-18

## 1. Business
Chalco is China's largest integrated alumina/primary-aluminum producer: it mines bauxite and coal, refines bauxite into alumina, smelts primary aluminum, and trades aluminum and related products domestically and for export [Certain]. Revenue is overwhelmingly commodity-price driven, not differentiated by product (source: stockanalysis.com overview, https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/2600/).

## 2. Quality
Returns have risen sharply: ROIC 13.0%→23.6% TTM, ROE 14.1%→24.5% TTM, and net debt/EBITDA has fallen from 2.12x (FY2021) to 0.18x (TTM), alongside margin expansion (gross margin 12.1%→23.0% TTM, operating margin 8.7%→17.4% TTM) (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/2600/financials/ratios/, https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/2600/financials/income-statement/) [Certain]. This is a scale/integration advantage, not a pricing-power moat: Chalco is a commodity producer with a 5-year beta of 1.56 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/2600/statistics/), so returns track the alumina/aluminum cycle rather than company-specific execution [Likely]. Margins, ROIC and leverage are all at 5-year bests, but a commodity producer's current-cycle returns need not reflect mid-cycle economics: the 5-year history shows ROIC troughed near 10% in FY2022–23 [Likely]. Institutional ownership is modest at 27.4% and insider ownership is negligible at 0.01% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/2600/statistics/), consistent with majority state control typical of Chinese SOE producers [Likely] — governance incentives may not align with minority shareholders.

## 3. Valuation
The stock trades below its own 5-year multiple range and below peers on every multiple shown: P/E 6.2x (5y range 9.2x–17.4x, below the floor) and 9% below peers' (AA, RIO, HKG:1378, CENX) 6.8x median; EV/EBITDA 3.8x vs a 5y range of 4.7x–7.0x (also below the floor) and 33% below peers' 5.6x; P/FCF 3.8x vs peers' 17.6x (-79%) (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/2600/financials/ratios/, peer statistics pages) [Certain]. The reverse DCF implies the price is pricing a 19.2% revenue decline in year 1 fading to terminal growth — bearish relative to the improving margin/ROIC trend above [Likely]. Base fair value is HK$14.40 (+98.1% vs the HK$7.27 close); even the bear case (HK$10.91) implies +50% (source: fact sheet fair-value table). The price has derated hard as fundamentals improved: -30.8% vs the 200-day average, -44.7% over 6 months, underperforming SPY by 53.5pp over 6 months (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/2600/history/) [Guessing].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) rises back above 1.5x, signalling the deleveraging trend has reversed.
- Gross margin (income statement) falls below 15% for a full fiscal year, signalling the commodity cycle has turned against realized prices/costs.
- ROIC (ratios page) falls below 10%, reverting to the FY2022–23 trough and undermining the "quality improved" thesis.
- FCF margin (cash flow + income statement) falls below 5%, signalling cash generation is deteriorating faster than reported earnings.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
FCF margin is 15.2% TTM; FCF conversion (FCF/NI) is still high at 217% TTM, though down from 552% in FY2021 as net income has caught up to cash flow rather than cash flow deteriorating (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/2600/financials/cash-flow-statement/) [Likely]. Shares outstanding are effectively flat (+0.23% YoY): no dilution overhang (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/2600/statistics/) [Certain]. Capital has clearly gone to debt paydown: net debt/EBITDA fell from 2.12x to 0.18x over five years (source: ratios page) [Certain]. A GuruFocus dividend headline (2026-09-09, https://www.gurufocus.com/news/9072157/aluminum-corp-of-china-ltds-dividend-analysis) appears in the fact sheet but carries no usable figure under house rules; stockanalysis.com shows no separate payout-ratio figure [data unavailable].
