# Macro overlay: Aluminum Corporation of China Limited (HKG:2600) — 2026-10-18
## Assessment
Chalco is a commodity producer (bauxite/alumina/primary aluminum) whose revenue is "overwhelmingly commodity-price driven" (research/HKG-2600/2026-10-18.md §1) [Certain], so the dollar-commodity channel is live even though leverage is now minimal (net debt/EBITDA 0.18x TTM, factsheet) and the discount-rate channel is weak.

Facts: the broad trade-weighted dollar index rose to 121.602 on 2026-10-09, +3.72 over ~1m and +1.1 over ~3m (https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=DTWEXBGS, 2026-10-09) [Certain]. US 10y real yields rose to 2.78% on 2026-10-15, +0.43pp over 3m (https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=DFII10) [Certain]. CNY/USD eased to 6.692 on 2026-10-09, -0.0139 over 1m (https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=DEXCHUS) — the yuan firmed slightly even as the broad dollar rose [Certain], implying PBOC is keeping it stable, which dampens FX pass-through into Chalco's mostly domestic CNY-priced sales **(inference)**.

A stronger dollar and higher real yields typically pressure USD-priced global aluminum/alumina benchmarks **(inference, no LME series in our dataset)**. But the stock has already fallen 44.7% over 6 months and 53.5pp vs SPY (factsheet, https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/2600/history/, through 2026-10-16), with the reverse DCF pricing a 19.2% year-1 revenue decline (factsheet) — a very bearish starting point that likely reflects China property/demand worries more than the past month's dollar move **(inference)**. Marginal bear case from further dollar strength is real but secondary to the fundamental (quality/valuation) thesis.
