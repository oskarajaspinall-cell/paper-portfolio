# Zions Bancorporation (ZION) — Core initiation — 2026-11-15
## 1. Business
Western US regional bank (Zions Bank, California Bank & Trust and others) earning mainly net interest income on commercial, CRE and consumer loans funded by customer deposits, plus customer fees (wealth, capital markets, retail and business banking) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/zion/). No foreign country is ~10% of revenue [Likely]; macro exposure is US rates and Western US credit.

## 2. Quality
ROE is 16.7% TTM against 13.5% in FY2025 and 14.7% in FY2021, so the five-year trend is falling but the latest year is the best of the series (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/zion/financials/ratios/) [Certain]. Net margin is 32.2% TTM vs 27.1% in FY2025 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/zion/financials/income-statement/) [Certain]. FCF margin and FCF/NI are inflated by bank cash-flow items and are not a reliable signal [Likely].

Q3 earnings growth was driven by credit: the provision was a benefit against a charge a year ago tied to two related borrowers, and net interest margin slipped on lower asset yields [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/109380/000010938026000127/zions-20260930.htm). That makes part of the ROE recovery non-recurring, and the efficiency ratio worsened year on year [company-reported] (same URL). Moat is a local deposit franchise, modest rather than wide [Likely]. Piotroski F is 4 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/zion/statistics/) [Certain]. ROIC and leverage are [data unavailable].

## 3. Valuation
P/E is 8.1x, below its 5y minimum of 8.5x (median 9.8x) and 31% below the peer median of 11.8x (CMA, KEY, RF, HBAN, WAL) [Certain]. P/B is 1.3x, equal to its 5y median and 12% below peers at 1.4x [Certain]. The price therefore assumes TTM earnings are not sustained: a P/E at a five-year low with ROE at a five-year high is the market discounting peak credit conditions [Likely].

Fair value is flagged unreliable, with methods disagreeing 4.3x. The P/B + ROE method gives 81.88 base against a 66.34 close; the dividend discount gives 27.83 and FCFE DCF 118.81 [Certain]. Rely on P/B + ROE only, and treat the blend (base 94.19) lightly [Guessing]. Trend: the price is 3.4% above its 200-day average, up 26.5% over 12 months, with 3-month momentum of -7.4% [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/zion/history/).

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROE (ratios page) TTM falls below 12%.
- Net margin (income statement page) TTM falls below 25%.
- Nonperforming assets in the 10-Q rise above 0.75% of loans [company-reported baseline in the Q3 10-Q: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/109380/000010938026000127/zions-20260930.htm].
- Shares change YoY (statistics page) turns positive by more than 2%, i.e. dilution to fund losses.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Buybacks are shrinking the share count (-0.57% YoY) and the board authorised a further repurchase for Q4 alongside dividends (source: https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/zions-bancorporations-board-announces-approval-of-share-repurchase-and-declares-dividends-on-common-and-preferred-stock-302925166.html) [Certain]. The company also bought the Basis Multifamily Finance agency lending platform, adding integration cost [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/109380/000010938026000127/zions-20260930.htm). Bank FCF conversion is not meaningful [Likely].
