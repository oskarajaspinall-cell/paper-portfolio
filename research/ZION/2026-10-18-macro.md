# Macro overlay: Zions Bancorporation (ZION) — 2026-10-18
## Assessment
ZION's thesis is bottom-up (cheap multiple, fading one-off Visa/SBIC gains, ROE reversion) [Likely], but as a regional bank rates are a real secondary lever: **short rates -> funding costs -> NIM -> NII**, and **long real yields -> securities mark-to-market (AOCI) & cost of equity -> book value and P/B multiple** **(inference)**.

What's priced: fed funds rose to 3.88% (FRED DFF, 2026-10-15), +0.25pp/1m and /3m — a recent hike, not a cut [Certain]. 10y rose to 5.10% (+0.53pp/3m), 2y to 4.77% (+0.61pp/3m) [Certain, DGS10/DGS2, 2026-10-15]; 2y sitting ~89bp above funds implies the market prices higher-for-longer, not cuts **(inference, proxy)**. Most of the move is real yield (DFII10 +0.43pp/3m to 2.78%, 2026-10-15) vs breakevens +0.11pp/3m to 2.35% (T10YIE, 2026-10-16) — a hawkish-growth repricing, not inflation fear [Certain/inference]. HY spreads widened +0.52pp/3m to 3.23% (BAMLH0A0HYM2, 2026-10-15) but stay historically tight; VIX fell to 16.46 (VIXCLS, 2026-10-15) — no stress priced [Certain]. The fact sheet's cost-of-equity already uses today's 5.10% 10y; a further rise is the surprise that hurts (funding-cost re-acceleration, wider AOCI losses); a pause/cut helps (extends the NIM tailwind the note's bear trigger watches for reversal).

Bull: rates ease, funding costs fall, multiple re-rates toward peers (1.4x P/B). Bear: hikes continue, funding costs and AOCI losses offset repricing, multiple stays depressed. Tilt: small, roughly neutral — the 3-month rate backup is real but secondary to a thesis built on gain-fade and valuation, not rates.
