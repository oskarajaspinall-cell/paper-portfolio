# Macro overlay: Zions Bancorporation (ZION) — 2026-11-15
## Assessment
**Chain:** rates and credit -> deposit costs, loan repricing and provisions -> net interest margin and credit cost -> ROE -> P/B and P/E. Credit spreads -> Western US credit losses -> equity risk premium is the second link. No foreign country is material (note section 1), so country risk is n/a.

**Facts (FRED, 2026-11-12/13):** 2y Treasury 4.86% (+0.71pp over 3m), 10y 5.10% (+0.47pp), fed funds 3.88% (+0.25pp), so 10y-2y is only +0.24pp (flat). Real 10y yield is 2.86% (+0.47pp) and breakevens are 2.24% (-0.03pp), so the rise is real yield, not inflation. High-yield spread is 3.2% (+0.49pp over 3m), still low in absolute terms. VIX is 18.7. Sources: https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=DGS2, DFII10, BAMLH0A0HYM2 (macro snapshot 2026-11-15).

**Pricing (proxy):** the 2y sits ~1pp above fed funds, so the market prices further hikes **(inference)** [Likely]. The stock trades at a 5y-low P/E (8.1x) with the Q3 provision benefit flagged non-recurring (note section 2), so credit normalisation is partly priced **(inference)** [Guessing].

**Bull:** rates stay high but spreads stay near 3%. Asset repricing offsets deposit costs, NIM stabilises and the multiple recovers toward peers.
**Bear:** a rise in the real yield or a hawkish surprise flattens or inverts the curve, raises deposit betas and widens spreads. Provisions revert and ROE falls toward the 12% invalidation level.

The hurting surprise is wider credit spreads, not the rate level, which is already moved and mostly known.

**Tilt:** toward bear, small. Spreads and real yields are drifting the wrong way, but both are small moves from calm levels.
