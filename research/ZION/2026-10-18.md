# Zions Bancorporation, National Association (ZION) — Core initiation — 2026-10-18

## 1. Business
Zions is a Western US regional bank (Salt Lake City) operating through affiliate brands across AZ, CA, CO, ID, NV, NM, OR, TX, UT, WA [Certain]. It earns net interest income on commercial, consumer and real-estate loans funded mainly by core deposits, plus fee income from capital markets, wealth management and, since August 2026, an acquired agency multifamily (Fannie Mae/Freddie Mac) lending platform (source: https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/zions-bancorporation-completes-acquisition-of-fannie-mae-and-freddie-mac-multifamily-business-line-from-basis-investment-group-302840262.html) [Certain].

## 2. Quality
TTM ROE is 16.4%, above the FY2021-25 range of 12.8-14.7% (fact sheet, ratios page) [Certain], but inflated: Q2 2026 diluted EPS of $3.05 (vs $1.63 a year earlier) included $252m of pre-tax noncustomer gains — a $215m Visa Class B-1 sale and $37m SBIC unrealized gains, worth ~$1.31/share after tax — [company-reported] (https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/109380/000010938026000111/zions-20260630.htm). Underlying: net interest margin rose to 3.27% from 3.17% a year ago on lower funding costs [company-reported] (https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/109380/000010938026000111/zions-20260630.htm), but the efficiency ratio was flat at 62.2% y/y [company-reported] (https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/109380/000010938026000111/zions-20260630.htm) — average for a regional bank, not a cost edge. Net margin trended down over five years (35.4% FY2021 to 27.1% FY2025, fact sheet income statement) before a TTM one-off bounce to 31.8%. Regional banking is a scale/funding-cost business with limited moat; Zions' edge is a low-cost Western deposit franchise, not pricing power. [Likely]

## 3. Valuation
ZION trades at 8.1x P/E, below its own 5-year range (8.5x-11.0x) and 39% below the peer (WAL, CMA, KEY, CFR) median of 13.2x; P/FCF of 5.5x is at the bottom of its 5-year range and 69% below peers; P/B of 1.2x is 13% below the peer median of 1.4x but mid-range on its own history (fact sheet valuation table) [Certain]. The price implies -11.8% revenue growth in year one fading to terminal growth (reverse DCF, fact sheet) — a bar average loans of $61.9bn, up 2% q/q, already clears [company-reported] (https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/109380/000010938026000111/zions-20260630.htm). Base fair value of $92.81 is 46.5% above the $63.37 close; even the bear case ($74.96) implies 18.3% upside (fact sheet fair value table) [Likely]. The market is pricing a weaker, cheaper bank than the fundamentals here support.

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROE (ratios page, stockanalysis.com) falls below 12% for two consecutive quarters, showing the TTM strength was entirely the one-off gains, not an improving core.
- Net interest margin (10-Q MD&A, company-reported, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/109380/000010938026000111/zions-20260630.htm) falls back below the 3.17% year-ago level, reversing the funding-cost tailwind.
- Efficiency ratio (10-Q MD&A, company-reported, same filing) rises above 65%, confirming expense growth is outrunning revenue.
- Net margin (income statement page, stockanalysis.com) falls below 25% once the Visa/SBIC gains roll off, below the FY2025 run-rate.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
The board authorized up to $75m of Q3 2026 buybacks and declared common and preferred dividends (source: https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/zions-bancorporations-board-announces-approval-of-share-repurchase-and-declares-dividends-on-common-and-preferred-stock-302839563.html) [Certain]; shares outstanding fell only 0.23% y/y (fact sheet), so the buyback is modest relative to the float. Zions priced $500m of senior notes in July 2026 (source: https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/zions-bancorporation-announces-pricing-of-senior-notes-302837057.html) [Certain], funding growth/liability management rather than signaling distress. TTM FCF margin (45.6%) and FCF conversion (143.5%, fact sheet) are high, but for a bank these cash-flow-statement figures are driven by deposit/loan balance changes, not a reliable quality signal, and should be read with caution. [Likely]
