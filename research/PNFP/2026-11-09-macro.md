# Macro overlay: Pinnacle Financial Partners (PNFP) — 2026-11-09
## Assessment
**Weight: secondary.** A US regional bank: earnings turn on the rate path and credit, and the multiple (P/B 1.1x, at its 5y median) turns on risk appetite. No material foreign exposure (note, section 1).

**Chain:** credit spreads / VIX -> equity risk premium -> bank P/B multiple; secondarily policy path -> deposit costs and curve -> NIM.

**Facts (FRED, 2026-11-05/06):** HY spread 3.48%, +0.45pp in ~1m, +0.77pp in ~3m. VIX 28.18, +13.2 in ~1m. Fed funds 3.88%, +0.25pp in ~3m. 2y yield 4.46%, 10y 4.84%, so the 2s10s curve is about +0.38pp. The 10y fell 0.43pp in ~1m, and the 10y real yield is 2.60%. Breakeven 2.23% is flat (https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=BAMLH0A0HYM2, ...VIXCLS, ...DFF, ...DGS2, ...DGS10).

**Pricing (proxy):** the 2y sits about 58bp above fed funds, so the market prices further hikes **(inference)**. Flat breakevens suggest the move is real-rate and risk driven, not an inflation scare [Likely]. The spread is still modest in absolute terms (the regime's calm anchor is 3.0%), but the VIX jump is a fast change in risk appetite, not just a level [Likely].

**Hurts PNFP:** a further credit-spread or VIX spike (de-rates regional banks, raises credit-loss worries, hits the integration story) and a higher-for-longer path that lifts deposit costs. **Helps:** a steeper curve with stable credit, which supports NIM.
