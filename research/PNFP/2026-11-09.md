# Pinnacle Financial Partners (PNFP) — Core initiation — 2026-11-09
## 1. Business
Southeast US regional bank holding company (Banks - Regional, Financials) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/pnfp/). It earns net interest income on commercial and consumer loans funded by core deposits, plus fees. Growth comes from hiring experienced bankers who bring client books [Likely] (source: https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20261022287827/en/Pinnacle-Financial-Partners-adds-96-revenue-producing-team-members-in-3Q26-extending-recruiting-momentum-with-experienced-hires-across-Southeast/). Share count is far higher after the merger (shares change YoY 71.92%) (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/pnfp/statistics/). No single foreign market identified.

## 2. Quality
Returns are mediocre, not franchise-grade. ROE was 10.3% in FY2021 and 9.6% TTM, flat over five years (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/pnfp/financials/ratios/) [Certain]. Net margin fell from 40.2% to 25.5% TTM (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/pnfp/financials/income-statement/) [Certain]; some of this is merger-related dilution and costs [Guessing]. ROIC, ROCE and operating margin are [data unavailable] (not meaningful for banks). Moat: relationship banking, talent-led share gains in high-growth Southeast metros; that is real but replicable by rivals who can hire away bankers [Likely]. Management cites strong loan and deposit growth with stable credit, per the Q3 call (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/pnfp/transcripts/713966-q3-2026/) [Likely]. Piotroski F is 4 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/pnfp/statistics/), middling.

## 3. Valuation
P/E 13.7x vs 5y median 12.1x (40% of range), 12% above the peer median 12.2x; P/B 1.1x at its 5y median, 17% below peers' 1.3x (peers WBS, ZION, CFR, EWBC, WAL) (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/pnfp/financials/ratios/) [Certain]. P/FCF 10.5x is near the bottom of its range, FCF yield 9.49% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/pnfp/statistics/), but bank FCF is noisy [Likely]. Mechanical fair value base 100.44 vs close 100.64 (-0.2%), bear 78.13, bull 140.47; flagged unreliable as methods disagree 4.5x [Certain]. The price implies 1.3% year-1 revenue growth, which looks undemanding if hiring-led growth persists [Likely]. Overall: fairly priced, no margin of safety [Likely].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROE (ratios page) TTM falls below 8.0%.
- Net margin (income statement page) TTM falls below 22%.
- Merger systems conversion slips beyond the guided March 2027 window, or FY26 guidance is cut (company releases).
- Credit: a sustained rise in charge-offs or non-performing loans in the quarterly release.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
FCF/NI is 140.1% TTM, 105.8% in FY2025 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/pnfp/financials/cash-flow-statement/) [Certain]. FCF margin fell 20.5pp in 12 months to 35.8%, 26.0pp below its 2-year high; bank cash flow reflects working-capital swings, so this is a caution rather than a signal [Likely]. Common and preferred dividends continue (source: https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20261026683964/en/Pinnacle-Financial-Partners-announces-common-and-preferred-stock-dividends/). Equity issuance funded the merger; buybacks are not evident [Likely].

## 6. Unusual items
Merger integration risk until conversion [Likely]. Trading was halted briefly on 2026-10-21 pending news ahead of results (source: https://www.tipranks.com/news/the-fly/pinnacle-financial-trading-halted-news-pending-thefly-news); cause not verified [Guessing]. Short interest 3.76% of float, up 12.2% on the month; institutions own 90.5% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/pnfp/statistics/).
