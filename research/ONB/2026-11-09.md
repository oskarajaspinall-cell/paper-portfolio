# Old National Bancorp (ONB) — Core initiation — 2026-11-09
## 1. Business
Regional bank holding company (Evansville, Indiana) earning net interest income on commercial, commercial real estate and consumer loans, plus fees from wealth management, capital markets and banking. Sixth-largest Midwest-headquartered commercial bank, with $74.4 billion of assets, operating mainly in the Midwest and Southeast US [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/707179/000070717926000067/onb-20260930.htm). No foreign revenue of note [Likely]. Bremer was acquired during the past year [Certain].

## 2. Quality
Average, not exceptional [Likely]. ROE is 11.5% TTM against 9.0% in FY2025 and a flat five-year trend (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/onb/financials/ratios/). ROIC and ROCE are [data unavailable], as is leverage, which is normal for banks. Net margin is 36.0% TTM, but its five-year trend is falling (-4.2pp) (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/onb/financials/income-statement/). Direction change: FCF margin fell over five fiscal years but has risen over the last 12 months, from 34.5% to 36.8% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/onb/financials/cash-flow-statement/?p=trailing). The recovery is partly Bremer scale and lower merger costs [Likely]. Merger costs were $0.7 million in Q3 against $12.1 million in Q2 [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/707179/000070717926000067/onb-20260930.htm). Moat is a local-deposit franchise and commercial relationships, which is real but common among regional banks [Likely]. Shares outstanding rose 10.97% YoY, from deal issuance [Certain].

## 3. Valuation
P/E is 10.2x, at the 31% position of its 5y range (8.7x-13.3x, median 12.7x) and 3% below the peer median of 10.5x (WTFC, FNB, CADE, ASB, UMBF) (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/onb/statistics/). P/B is 1.2x, above its 5y maximum of 1.1x, and 9% below peers' 1.3x, so book value is no longer cheap against its own history [Certain]. P/FCF is 9.6x, 26% above peers, and FCF yield is 10.39%. The mechanical base fair value is 31.60 against a 24.93 close, but it is flagged unreliable (methods disagree 6.3x; P/B + ROE gives 20.70 base, below the price) so I weight it lightly [Certain]. The price is roughly fair on earnings and fair-to-full on book; upside depends on EPS growth, not re-rating [Likely]. Short interest is 6.56% of float.

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROE (ratios page, TTM) falls below 9%.
- Net margin (income statement page, TTM) falls below 30%.
- Quarterly provision for credit losses in the 10-Q exceeds $60 million (company-reported; Q3 was $33.6 million, source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/707179/000070717926000067/onb-20260930.htm).
- Shares outstanding growth (statistics page) stays above 5% a year beyond FY2027 without matching EPS growth.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
FCF conversion is 102.3% of net income TTM, versus 95.2% in FY2025, with a falling five-year trend (-6.3pp) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/onb/financials/cash-flow-statement/). Management has used M&A (Bremer) funded partly by equity, which diluted the share count. Q3 expenses excluding merger costs were $354.7 million against $376.5 million a year earlier [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/707179/000070717926000067/onb-20260930.htm). Integration discipline is the main allocation test [Likely].

## 6. Unusual items
