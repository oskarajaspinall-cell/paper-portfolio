# Macro overlay: Old National Bancorp (ONB) — 2026-11-09
## Assessment
**Weight: secondary.** ONB is a US regional bank with no material foreign revenue, so country risk does not apply [Likely].

**Chain:** policy path and curve -> deposit costs vs asset yields -> net interest margin (the Q3 10-Q says margin narrowed as asset yields fell faster than deposit costs). Second: credit spreads and VIX -> risk premium -> P/B multiple.

**Facts (FRED snapshot, 2026-11-05 data):** fed funds 3.88% (+0.25pp over ~3m); 2y 4.46%; 10y 4.84%; 10y-2y gap 0.38pp, about 0.10pp flatter over 1m; high-yield spread 3.48% (+0.45pp 1m); VIX 28.18 (+13.2 1m).

**Pricing (proxy):** the 2y sits about 58bp above fed funds, suggesting further hikes are priced **(inference)**. Spread levels are still modest, so the change is in risk appetite, not yet credit stress.

**Inference:** at 1.2x book, above its 5y maximum, the multiple is exposed to a higher risk premium. Hawkish surprises that flatten the curve and lift deposit costs hurt; a pause and steeper curve help. Q3 provisions ($33.6m [company-reported]) show no macro credit problem yet.

**Bull:** pause, steeper curve, VIX under 20: margin stabilises, multiple moves toward peers' 1.3x book.
**Bear:** hikes, HY spread above 5%: NIM squeeze, higher provisions, P/B compresses.

**Tilt:** toward bear, small. Much is visible in the data and fundamentals dominate.
