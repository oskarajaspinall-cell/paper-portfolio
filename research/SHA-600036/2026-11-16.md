# China Merchants Bank (SHA:600036) — Core initiation — 2026-11-16
## 1. Business
Chinese joint-stock commercial bank, reporting in CNY. It earns net interest income on retail and corporate loans, plus fees from wealth management and cards. Its retail and wealth franchise is its identifying feature among Chinese banks [Likely]. Revenue is overwhelmingly domestic, so the exposure is China itself rather than a foreign region; the macro overlay should treat China/HK country risk as the main exposure (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sha/600036/).

## 2. Quality
- ROE has drifted down: 15.1% (FY2021) to 12.0% (FY2025) and 11.5% TTM, a 3.1pp fall over five years [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sha/600036/financials/ratios/). ROIC and ROCE are [data unavailable], as expected for a bank.
- Net margin rose from 45.2% to 50.8% TTM, so the ROE decline is not a margin problem [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sha/600036/financials/income-statement/). It more likely reflects a larger equity base and lower rates.
- FCF margin and conversion (296.9% of net income TTM) are not meaningful for a bank: deposit and loan flows drive operating cash flow. I do not rely on them [Likely]. The "Direction change" flag on FCF margin is therefore noise.
- Q3 call: management expects continued net interest margin pressure and slower loan growth, and stresses prudent risk and wealth management [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sha/600036/transcripts/741728-q3-2026/).
- The moat is brand and retail deposit and wealth franchise. It is durable but is being eroded by falling rates [Likely]. Beta is 0.41 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sha/600036/statistics/).

## 3. Valuation
- P/E is 7.4x against a 5y median of 7.1x (45% of the range) and a peer median of 6.5x (+13%) (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sha/600036/statistics/). P/B is 0.8x, equal to its 5y median, at 20% of its range, but above the peer median of 0.4x [Certain].
- Peers are mid-sized joint-stock banks; the large state banks have no page. P/FCF and FCF yield are not meaningful for a bank.
- The price is 8.9% above its 200-day average, and 12-month change is -0.1% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sha/600036/history/).
- Fair value base is 57.59 CNY against a 42.93 close, but it is flagged unreliable: methods disagree 3.0x, with the dividend discount model at 20.36 and P/B + ROE at 55.74. The reverse DCF implies -6.8% year-1 revenue growth: the market prices a shrinking bank [Likely]. The discount depends on ROE holding near 11-12% [Guessing].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROE (ratios page) falls below 10%.
- Net margin (income statement page) falls below 45%.
- Shares change YoY (statistics page) rises above +3%, i.e. dilution from a recapitalisation.
- Piotroski F-score (statistics page) falls below 3.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Share count is shrinking by 0.63% YoY, a modest buyback or no dilution signal [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sha/600036/statistics/). Management says it prioritises internally generated capital, which argues against a raise [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sha/600036/transcripts/741728-q3-2026/). Cash conversion metrics are not meaningful for banks. Dividend data is not in the fact sheet.
