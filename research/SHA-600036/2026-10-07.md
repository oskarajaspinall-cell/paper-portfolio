# China Merchants Bank Co., Ltd. (SHA:600036) — Core initiation — 2026-10-07

## 1. Business
China Merchants Bank is a Chinese joint-stock commercial bank (founded 1987, ~122,000 employees) listed in Shanghai [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sha/600036/). It operates Wholesale Finance, Retail Finance and Other Business segments, earning net interest income on retail mortgage, auto, consumer and micro-business loans plus corporate lending, and fee income from wealth management, cards and agency sales of funds, trusts and insurance [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sha/600036/). Retail AUM reached roughly CNY 16.6tn in Q3 2025 [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sha/600036/transcripts/381323-q3-2025/).

## 2. Quality
ROE fell from 15.1% (FY2021) to 11.5% TTM, a 3.1pp decline over five years [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sha/600036/financials/ratios/), reflecting system-wide margin compression, not a CMB-specific issue — management guided NIM to 1.87% for FY2025 with further, smaller declines in 2026 [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sha/600036/transcripts/400576-q4-2025/). Asset quality is the key differentiator: NPL ratio stable at 0.94%, though coverage slipped 20pp to 391.79% as retail microloan/consumption NPLs rose CNY 2.6bn [Likely] (same source). Core Tier 1 CAR is 14.16%, down 70bp, above regulatory minimums [Likely] (same source). Net margin is structurally high and rising (45.2% → 50.5%, +5.3pp over 5y) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sha/600036/financials/income-statement/), helped by a fee rebound — fee income posted its first YoY growth since 2022, with retail AUM up 11% YoY [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sha/600036/transcripts/381323-q3-2025/). The durable edge is a low-cost retail/wealth franchise and best-in-class NPLs versus domestic peers, but Piotroski F-score is only 4/9 [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sha/600036/statistics/) and the ROE advantage is narrowing as the sector absorbs PBOC rate cuts.

## 3. Valuation
P/E of 7.1x sits at the midpoint of its own 5-year range (4.8x–10.6x, 40th percentile) but is 21% above the peer median of 5.9x (Industrial Bank, Ping An Bank, China CITIC Bank, China Minsheng Bank) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sha/600036/financials/ratios/). P/B of 0.8x is near the low end of its own 5-year range (18th percentile, 0.6x–1.4x) yet 93% above the peer median of 0.4x [Certain] (same source) — CMB has long traded at a premium to peer joint-stock banks for superior ROE and asset quality, a gap the market still assumes persists even as ROE converges toward peers. P/FCF (1.8x vs. peer 0.6x) is not a reliable anchor for a bank given balance-sheet-driven cash-flow swings, so it is noted but discounted. The price assumes the market keeps paying up for CMB's franchise quality rather than for cheapness; the key variable is whether that quality gap over peers keeps narrowing.

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROE (ratios page) falls below 10% — would mean the quality premium over the peer median (currently justified by superior profitability) is no longer earned.
- NPL ratio (ratios/statistics page) rises above 1.2% — would signal the asset-quality advantage over peers is eroding, not just a sector-wide retail-credit soft patch.
- Core Tier 1 CAR (ratios page) falls below 12% — would indicate capital buffer no longer comfortably exceeds regulatory minimums.
- Net margin (income statement) falls below 45% — would reverse the fee/wealth-management mix shift that has been offsetting NIM compression.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Shares outstanding rose 1.27% YoY [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sha/600036/statistics/), mild dilution with no offsetting buyback evident. FCF conversion (FCF/NI) is 381% TTM, up 150pp over five years [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sha/600036/financials/cash-flow-statement/), but for a bank this largely reflects deposit-funding and balance-sheet mechanics rather than distributable owner earnings, so should not be read as surplus capital. Core Tier 1 CAR of 14.16% remained strong even after funding an interim dividend [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sha/600036/transcripts/400576-q4-2025/), but management gave no explicit forward payout guidance [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sha/600036/transcripts/381323-q3-2025/).

## 6. Unusual items
Institutional ownership is only 25.0% and insider ownership 0.01% [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sha/600036/statistics/), typical for a Chinese A-share bank but limited insider alignment. Earnings are concentrated in PBOC rate policy and the domestic retail-credit cycle — a single-regulator, single-country macro exposure [Likely]. Short-interest data is unavailable. No M&A, legal or management-change items appear in recent filings/transcripts.
