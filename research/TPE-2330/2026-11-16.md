Reports in TWD. ## 2. Quality
Exceptional and still improving [Certain]. TTM ROIC 60.3% (FY2021 37.4%), operating margin 58.0% (FY2021 41.0%), gross margin 65.8%, net margin 51.1% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tpe/2330/financials/ratios/). Recent direction confirms it: ROIC +13.2pp and operating margin +8.4pp over the last year (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tpe/2330/financials/ratios/?p=trailing). Net cash: net debt/EBITDA -0.89x. Piotroski F 7 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tpe/2330/statistics/).
Moat: near-monopoly in advanced AI-chip manufacturing, with scale and process lead that rivals have not closed [Likely]. A challenger, Rapidus, promises cheaper 2nm chips but has yet to prove it can manufacture them (source: https://www.fool.com/investing/2026/11/14/why-a-rising-tsmc-challenger-could-reshape-the-chi/) [Likely]. Returns this high are cyclical-peak-like in FY terms; the 5y ROIC low was 32.0% (FY2023) [Certain].

P/E 22.7x is 72% up its 5y range (median 23.7x) and 20% below the peer median 28.3x [Certain]. EV/EBITDA 15.0x is above the 5y max (14.7x) yet 25% below peers; EV/Sales 11.0x and P/B 8.2x are above their 5y maxima [Certain]. FCF yield only 2.58%; P/FCF 38.8x (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tpe/2330/statistics/).
The mechanical fair value is base 905.97 TWD vs close 2,350.00 (-61.4%), bull 1,729.27 (-26.4%). The reverse DCF needs 43.1% year-1 revenue growth [Certain]. The model fades growth from history and almost certainly understates a business whose margins keep expanding [Likely], but the price is not cheap: earnings must keep compounding for the multiple to hold. Price is 5.9% above its 200-day average, 2.7% below the 50-day; 3-month momentum -3.5%, 12-month +54.9%, RSI 38.8 [Certain]. Fair-priced for an intact thesis, no margin of safety [Likely].
