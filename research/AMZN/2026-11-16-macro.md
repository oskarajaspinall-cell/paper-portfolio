# Macro overlay: Amazon.com, Inc. (AMZN) — 2026-11-16
## Assessment
**Weight: secondary.** Chain: real yields -> discount rate on distant AI/AWS cash flows -> multiple; credit spreads -> capex funding is the second link. TTM FCF margin is -5.9% and nine-month capex is $175.7bn [company-reported] (https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1018724/000101872426000040/amzn-20260930.htm), so value rests on far-out cash flows [Likely]. Beta is 1.50 (https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/amzn/statistics/).

**Facts** (FRED, https://fred.stlouisfed.org): 10y TIPS 2.86% (2026-11-12), +0.47pp over ~3m, -0.02pp over ~1m. 2y 4.86% (+0.71pp over 3m), fed funds 3.88%. 10y breakeven 2.24% (2026-11-13). HY spread 3.20% (+0.49pp over 3m). VIX 18.7.

**Pricing (proxy):** the 2y sits 98bp above fed funds, so further hikes look priced **(inference)**. The 10y rise is real, not inflation: a discount-rate shock. Credit is calm, so funding is not yet a constraint.

**Bull:** real yields ease below 2.5% and spreads stay under 4%; the multiple holds. **Bear:** real yield above 3.25% or HY above 4% compresses the multiple, with the price (266.66) already above the mechanical bull fair value (254.91, EV/Revenue only, a weak anchor).

**Tilt:** toward bear, small. The 3m rise is partly priced (stock +11.5% over 12m); a further surprise matters, not the level.

Country risk: no foreign country is shown at ~10%+ of revenue (split unconfirmed in the note), so no country check was made.
