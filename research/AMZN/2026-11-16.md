# Amazon.com, Inc. (AMZN) — Core initiation — 2026-11-16
## 1. Business
Amazon earns money from three engines: retail (first-party and marketplace sales, fulfilment and Prime), advertising, and AWS cloud and AI infrastructure. AWS and advertising drive the margin. Retail drives scale and traffic. Segment mix and country split are [data unavailable]: the 10-Q text I could read did not show them (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1018724/000101872426000040/amzn-20260930.htm). No single foreign country is shown at ~10%+ [Guessing]; Germany, UK and Japan are the likely largest.

## 2. Quality
Operating margin has risen from 5.3% (FY2021) to 13.1% TTM, and gross margin from 42.0% to 51.4%, which shows the mix shifting to advertising and AWS [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/amzn/financials/income-statement/). The moat is logistics scale, Prime's installed base and AWS switching costs [Likely]. Contracted AWS demand is large: unrecognised commitments of about $578 billion, 6.1-year average life [company-reported] (https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1018724/000101872426000040/amzn-20260930.htm).

Direction change, which must be addressed: TTM ROIC fell to 10.9% from 15.0% a year earlier, ROCE to 10.6% from 14.3%, and FCF margin to -5.9% from 1.5% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/amzn/financials/ratios/?p=trailing). Operating margin rose over the same period, so the decline is the capital base growing faster than returns: nine-month capex was $175.7bn against $92.3bn a year earlier [company-reported] (https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1018724/000101872426000040/amzn-20260930.htm). Whether it pays back depends on AI-capacity utilisation [Likely]. Not yet proven.

## 3. Valuation
P/E is 21.1x against a 5y range of 31.8x–51.6x and a peer median of 36.9x. That flatters earnings: net margin (16.9%) exceeds operating margin (13.1%) because of Anthropic mark-ups; other income was $68.4bn over nine months [company-reported] (https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1018724/000101872426000040/amzn-20260930.htm) [Certain]. EV/EBITDA of 16.7x is at the bottom of its range and 24% under peers. EV/Sales (3.9x) is above its 5y maximum. FCF yield is -1.63% and P/FCF is [data unavailable]. The mechanical fair value rests on EV/Revenue alone because the DCF failed on negative FCF: base 241.22, bull 254.91, bear 119.62 against a 266.66 close, so the price sits above even the bull value [Certain]. The price assumes capex converts to AWS and AI revenue at current returns [Likely]. Trend is healthy: +9.0% above the 200-day average, with 12-month change of +11.5% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/amzn/statistics/).

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Operating margin (TTM, income statement trailing page) falls below 11%.
- TTM ROIC (ratios trailing page) falls below 8% while capex still grows.
- Net debt / EBITDA (ratios page) exceeds 2.5x, which would mean the capex is debt-funded.
- Gross margin (income statement page) falls below 48%.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Cash conversion has broken: FCF margin is -5.9% TTM and FCF/NI is -34.8%, as capex outruns operating cash flow [Certain]. Net debt / EBITDA is 1.04x, up from 0.38x at FY2025, and shares change +0.83% YoY (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/amzn/financials/ratios/). The company invested $50.0bn in OpenAI preferred stock and $10.0bn in Anthropic preferred stock in nine months [company-reported] (https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1018724/000101872426000040/amzn-20260930.htm). Blackstone Digital Infrastructure Trust is to buy a majority of a data-centre joint venture, so Amazon is recycling capital [Likely] (https://www.tipranks.com/news/the-fly/blackstone-digital-confirms-acquisition-of-amazon-data-centers-for-5b-thefly-news).
