**Bull:** real yields fall back below ~2.5% and spreads stay tight. The multiple holds near 5y median (75x P/E) while growth continues.
**Bear:** real yields above ~3.2% or a risk-off move (VIX, spreads). The multiple compresses toward peers, which is a large drop since P/E is +185% vs peers, with no change to earnings.

**Tilt:** toward bear, small. Rates raise downside sensitivity of the multiple; fundamentals (Q3 beat and raise) are unaffected. [Guessing]
