# Manhattan Associates (MANH) — Core initiation — 2026-11-15
## 1. Business
Sells cloud-native supply chain execution, warehouse/transport management, order management and store/POS software to retailers, wholesalers, manufacturers and logistics firms, mainly via recurring cloud subscriptions plus related services; legacy perpetual licences and maintenance are shrinking as customers convert [Certain] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1056696/000119312526441094/manh-20260930.htm). ## 2. Quality
Very high-quality economics [Certain]: ROCE 106.2% TTM, gross margin 55.9%, operating margin 24.9%, FCF margin 32.8%, FCF conversion 178.3% of net income, net cash (net debt/EBITDA -0.50x) (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/manh/financials/ratios/, https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/manh/statistics/). Moat is mission-critical, embedded warehouse/order systems with high switching cost and a versionless cloud architecture [Likely]. Q3 cloud subscription revenue grew about 28% [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1056696/000119312526441094/manh-20260930.htm), with record bookings and raised FY outlook per the call summary [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/manh/). Direction check: operating margin is 24.9% vs 25.9% a year earlier (-1.1pp), the only softening metric; ROIC is inflated by negligible invested capital and not informative [Likely]. Beta 0.83.

## 3. Valuation
The price assumes sustained premium growth [Certain]. P/E 72.1x sits at 59% of its own 5y range (median 75.1x), EV/EBITDA 50.3x at 46%, P/FCF 39.3x at 38%; vs peers (DSGX, SAP, ORCL, PEGA) P/E is +185% and EV/EBITDA +141% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/manh/statistics/). FCF yield is only 2.54%. Mechanical fair value is flagged unreliable: DCF base 79.72 vs EV/Revenue base 275.98 against a 257.15 close; the reverse DCF implies 38.7% year-1 revenue growth, which is not credible against about 11% reported growth [Likely]. It is not cheap vs peers or the DCF; it is only mid-range against its own history. The stock is in a strong uptrend: +50.6% above the 200-day, +46.7% over 12 months, RSI 67.7, relative strength vs SPY +36.3pp over 12 months (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/manh/history/).

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Operating margin (TTM, income statement page) falls below 22%.
- FCF margin (TTM, cash flow page) falls below 28%.
- Cloud subscription revenue growth (10-Q/earnings release) falls below 18% year on year.
- Net cash turns to net debt / EBITDA above 0x (balance sheet page), i.e. cash flow diverted to debt-funded buybacks or M&A.
