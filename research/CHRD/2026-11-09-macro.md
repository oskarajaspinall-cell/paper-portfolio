# Macro overlay: Chord Energy (CHRD) — 2026-11-09
## Assessment
**Chain:** WTI price -> realized price -> margin and FCF (~100% oil, Bakken) [Certain: note §1]. Secondary: real yields/credit spreads -> equity risk premium -> multiple.

**Facts (official):** WTI was $99.64 on 2026-11-03, $99.77 on 2026-10-01, $81.96 on 2026-08-03, with a peak of $110.01 on 2026-10-19 (FRED DCOILWTICO, https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=DCOILWTICO). The 10-Q cites the Hormuz closure and OPEC+ as drivers (https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1486159/000148615926000047/chrd-20260930.htm). 10y real yield 2.60%, -0.31 in 1m (2026-11-05); HY spread 3.48%, +0.45 in 1m; VIX 28.18, +13.2 in 1m (https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=DFII10, BAMLH0A0HYM2, VIXCLS; snapshot reports/macro/2026-11-09.md).

**Pricing (proxy/inference):** At 2.9x EV/EBITDA and a 19.4% FCF yield (fact sheet), the equity discounts a fall in oil from ~$100 **(inference)**. Falling real yields help the multiple slightly; widening spreads and a VIX spike offset that. [Likely]

**Bull:** oil holds above $90 into 2027: margins and FCF stay near TTM (22% FCF margin), re-rating toward the base fair value of 155. **Bear:** a Hormuz de-escalation takes WTI back to the ~$80 of August: revenue and margin fall sharply and TTM peak earnings roll off; the multiple does not offset it.

**Tilt:** toward bear, moderate: the oil price is geopolitically elevated (+$18 in three months) and the market already pays only a trough multiple, so asymmetry is to the downside on a resolution. [Guessing] on timing. No material foreign country exposure.
