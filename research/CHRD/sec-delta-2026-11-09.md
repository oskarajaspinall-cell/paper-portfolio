- **Added**: Crude oil revenues. Our crude oil revenues decreased $108.3 million to $1,306.6 million for the three months ended September 30, 2026 as compared to the three months ended June 30, 2026. The decrease was primarily due to lower crude oil average realized prices quarter over quarter resulting in a $123.3 million decrease, partially offset by an increase of $15.0 million due to higher crude oil production volumes sold quarter over quarter. Average crude oil sales prices, without derivative settlements, decreased by $8.19 per barrel quarter over quarter to an average of $85.80 per barrel for the three months ended September 30, 2026 primarily due to a decrease in the average NYMEX WTI quarter over quarter.
- **Added**: Natural gas revenues. Our natural gas revenues increased $6.5 million to $41.3 million for the three months ended September 30, 2026 as compared to the three months ended June 30, 2026. The increase was primarily due to higher natural gas average realized prices quarter over quarter resulting in a $4.8 million increase quarter over quarter, coupled with an increase of $1.7 million due to higher natural gas production volumes sold quarter over quarter. Average natural gas sales prices, without derivative settlements, increased by $0.13 per Mcf quarter over quarter to $1.07 per Mcf for the three months ended September 30, 2026 primarily due to increases in the corresponding natural gas index prices quarter over quarter.
- **Added**: Crude oil revenues. Our crude oil revenues increased $972.0 million to $3,717.9 million for the nine months ended September 30, 2026 as compared to the nine months ended September 30, 2025. The increase was primarily due to higher crude oil average realized prices period over period resulting in a $797.8 million increase, coupled with an increase of $174.2 million due to higher crude oil production volumes sold period over period. Average crude oil sales prices, without derivative settlements, increased by $18.81 per barrel period over period to an average of $83.54 per barrel for the nine months ended September 30, 2026 primarily due to an increase in the average NYMEX WTI period over period.
