# Chord Energy (CHRD) — Core initiation — 2026-11-09
## 1. Business
Independent oil-weighted E&P, operating mainly in the Williston Basin (Bakken). Revenue is almost all crude oil, sold at WTI-linked prices, so earnings follow the oil price and drilling cost. [Certain] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1486159/000148615926000047/chrd-20260930.htm). It is selling its non-operated Marcellus gas interest, which leaves it more oil-focused. [Certain] No foreign country is material; it is a US business.

## 2. Quality
A cyclical price-taker with no pricing power. Returns follow WTI: ROIC was 46.0% in FY2022, 1.7% in FY2025 and 10.3% on a TTM basis (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/chrd/financials/ratios/). [Certain]
Direction: ROIC is up over the last 12 months (4.8% to 10.3%) but 11.8pp below three years ago. Operating margin has slipped over 12 months (20.3% to 18.9%) and is 11.3pp under its 2-year high (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/chrd/financials/ratios/?p=trailing). FCF margin is rising over 12 months (17.6% to 22.3%). [Certain]
The balance sheet is a strength: net debt/EBITDA is 0.26x. [Certain] Its durable edge is low-cost Bakken acreage and longer laterals, which is a cost advantage and not a moat. [Likely] Altman Z is 1.76 and Piotroski F is 5, so the quality is only middling. [Certain]
New CEO from 19 Oct 2026 (source: https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/chord-energy-announces-leadership-transition-302912087.html). [Certain]

## 3. Valuation
At 136.49 USD, P/E is 8.7x and EV/EBITDA 2.9x, at 5% and 1% of their 5y ranges. P/FCF is 5.1x, 38% up its range, and FCF yield is 19.42%. [Certain]
Against peers (FANG, DVN, OVV, EOG), it trades 22% below on P/E, 41% below on EV/EBITDA and 53% below on P/FCF. [Certain]
The mechanical fair value rests on EV/EBITDA alone, because the NAV method needs reserve data that is [data unavailable]. Base is 155.31 (+13.8%), bear 114.28 (-16.3%). [Likely]
The multiples look cheap, but the 5y median comes from a period of lower oil prices. Realized oil prices were boosted by the Middle East conflict and are already falling quarter on quarter (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1486159/000148615926000047/chrd-20260930.htm). TTM earnings may therefore be near a peak. [Likely] The price assumes oil stays elevated. [Guessing]

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROIC (ratios page, TTM) falls below 6%.
- FCF margin (cash-flow statement, TTM) falls below 15%.
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) rises above 1.5x.
- Shares outstanding (statistics page) rise more than 3% year on year, through dilutive M&A or issuance.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
TTM FCF conversion is 156.7% and FCF margin 22.3%. Management says more than 75% of Q3 free cash flow goes to shareholders (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/chrd/transcripts/726505-q3-2026/). [Likely] Share count change is 0.33% year on year, so buybacks are not shrinking it much. [Certain] The $550.0 million Marcellus sale is expected to close in Q4 2026 [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1486159/000148615926000047/chrd-20260930.htm). The filing also lists a longer-lateral well program as a strategy. [Certain]
