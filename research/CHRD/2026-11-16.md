# Chord Energy (CHRD) — Core initiation — 2026-11-16
## 1. Business
Independent oil and gas E&P, mainly Williston Basin (Bakken) in the US. Revenue is overwhelmingly crude oil sold at WTI-linked prices, with small gas and NGL contributions, so earnings track oil prices and drilling efficiency [Certain] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1486159/000148615926000047/chrd-20260930.htm). Single-country (US) business; no foreign revenue exposure of macro-overlay relevance [Likely]. Sector: Energy (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/chrd/).

## 2. Quality
Cyclical commodity producer, not a compounder. ROIC is 10.3% TTM, up from a year earlier but 11.8pp below three years ago; operating margin is 18.9% TTM versus 20.3% a year earlier and a 30.2% two-year high, so the 5y "rising" label rests on a depressed FY2021 start (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/chrd/financials/ratios/?p=trailing) [Certain]. FCF margin rose to 22.3% TTM from 17.6%, the best in two years, though it is below the FY2021 level [Certain]. Balance sheet is strong: net debt/EBITDA 0.26x TTM (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/chrd/financials/ratios/). Moat is limited to Bakken acreage, scale and low costs; returns are set by oil prices [Likely]. Piotroski F 5, Altman Z 1.76 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/chrd/statistics/).

## 3. Valuation
At 149.93 USD the stock trades at 3.2x EV/EBITDA (2% of its 5y range, 40% below peers MTDR/NOG/FANG/DVN), 9.5x P/E (5% of range, 12% below peers), 1.0x P/B and a 17.68% FCF yield (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/chrd/financials/ratios/) [Certain]. Mechanical fair value (EV/EBITDA only; reserve NAV [data unavailable]) is 155.31 base, 114.28 bear, 159.44 bull, so the base case offers only +3.6% [Certain]. The price already discounts roughly the current oil strip: cheap on trailing earnings that include elevated realised oil prices, so the multiples flatter if oil mean-reverts [Likely]. The stock is 12.3% above its 200-day average with 12-month change +67.4% [Certain]. Cheap against peers, not against mid-cycle earnings [Guessing].

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Management directed over 75% of Q3 free cash flow to shareholders, with higher production guidance and raised EBITDA outlook (source: https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/chord-energy-reports-third-quarter-2026-financial-and-operating-results-declares-base-dividend-and-updates-2026-outlook-302929809.html) [Likely]. Share count change is 0.33% YoY, so buybacks barely offset issuance [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/chrd/statistics/). It agreed to sell its non-operated Marcellus interest for $550.0 million gross [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1486159/000148615926000047/chrd-20260930.htm), a sensible simplification [Likely]. FCF/NI of 156.7% TTM reflects depletion-heavy accounting and a low FY2025 net income [Likely].
