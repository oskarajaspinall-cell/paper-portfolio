# Macro overlay: Chord Energy (CHRD) — 2026-11-16
## Assessment
**Chain:** WTI oil price (with a Middle East / Hormuz premium) -> realised price -> revenue and FCF -> multiple. Secondary: 10y yield -> cost of equity -> fair value. [Likely]

**Facts:** WTI was 89.62 USD on 2026-11-11, against 98.73 on 2026-10-09 and 84.77 on 2026-08-11. It peaked at 110.01 on 2026-10-19 and was 69.60 on 2026-07-06 (https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=DCOILWTICO, read 2026-11-16). The 10y yield is 5.10% (+0.47pp in 3m) and the 10y real yield is 2.86% (https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=DGS10, DFII10; 2026-11-12). The 2y yield is 4.86% against fed funds at 3.88% (DGS2, DFF). The high-yield spread is 3.2% (BAMLH0A0HYM2), so credit stress is low.

**Pricing (proxy/inference):** a 2y yield about 1pp above fed funds suggests the market prices hikes **(inference)**. The base fair value of 155.31 against 149.93 (+3.6%) rests on EBITDA that includes recent elevated oil, and the stock is up 67.4% over 12 months, so the premium looks largely priced [Likely].

**Bull:** oil holds near 90 or higher, FCF margin stays near 22% and the multiple holds.
**Bear:** the geopolitical premium fades, WTI drifts towards the ~70 July low and EBITDA falls. Higher long yields lift the cost of equity, and the 114.28 bear fair value applies.

**Tilt:** toward bear, moderate. Oil is about 19% off its October peak while the stock sits near its highs with thin upside. [Guessing]

Country risk: none material; the business is US-only.
