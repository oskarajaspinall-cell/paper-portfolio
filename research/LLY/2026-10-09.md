# Eli Lilly and Company (LLY) — Core initiation — 2026-10-09

## 1. Business
Eli Lilly discovers, develops and sells branded prescription medicines globally: diabetes and obesity care (Mounjaro, Zepbound, Trulicity, Jardiance — tirzepatide-based incretins), oncology (Verzenio, Jaypirca), immunology (Taltz, Ebglyss, Omvoh) and neuroscience [Likely]. Revenue is earned from patent-protected drugs sold through wholesalers and pharmacies, reimbursed by payers; recent growth has concentrated in the GLP-1/incretin franchise for diabetes and obesity [Likely].

## 2. Quality
Returns are exceptional and improving: TTM ROIC 42.2% (+7.3pp over 5y), ROE 102.3%, operating margin 49.7% (+16.3pp), gross margin 83.4% [Certain]. Durability rests on patent protection and first-mover scale in the GLP-1 class (Mounjaro/Zepbound), where Lilly and Novo Nordisk lead; Lilly's 10-K cites "mass-compounded incretins," payer formulary restrictions and new oral-GLP-1 entrants as threats to that franchise, without disclosing the revenue share it represents (source: sec.gov 10-K, lly-20251231.htm) [Certain]. Patent cliffs are a real medium-term risk: Trulicity's US compound patent and data protection both expire 2027; Jardiance 2029; Taltz compound patent 2030 (data protection 2028); Mounjaro/Zepbound's compound patent runs to 2036 but US tirzepatide data protection expires 2027 (source: sec.gov 10-K) [Certain]. FCF conversion has deteriorated sharply — 108.5% of net income in FY2021 to 68.1% TTM — as manufacturing capacity is built; a capital-intensity issue, not yet a demand one [Likely].

## 3. Valuation
Multiples give conflicting signals. TTM P/E (39.3x) and EV/EBITDA (26.1x) sit below Lilly's own 5-year minimums (44.9x, 26.7x) — cheap versus its own history — and P/E is 26% below the peer median (53.3x; NVO/JNJ/MRK/ABBV) [Certain]. But EV/EBITDA is 65% above the peer median, EV/Sales +117%, P/FCF +137% and P/B +323% [Certain]: Lilly trades at a large premium to peers on every multiple except P/E, which net income (depressed by weak FCF conversion) distorts. More decisive: the fact sheet's DCF-led fair value is a $508.24 blended base (DCF $464.67, best method) — 56.5% below the $1,169.60 last close — and even the bull case ($1,126.00) sits 3.7% below the price [Certain]. The reverse DCF shows the price implies 37.6% revenue growth in year 1, fading to terminal growth — an aggressive bar that leaves little room for a patent-cliff disappointment or incretin-competition setback.

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Gross margin (ratios/income statement page) falls below 78% (its FY2022 level), signalling payer/compounded-incretin price pressure
- FCF conversion, FCF/NI (cash-flow + income statement pages), fails to recover above 50% within two fiscal years as capex normalizes
- ROIC (ratios page) falls below 30% (its FY2023 level), indicating incretin-franchise returns are being competed away
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) rises above 2.0x without a matching step-up in operating cash flow, indicating the capacity build is debt-funded without a demand payoff

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Net debt/EBITDA is low and falling (1.10x TTM own-definition, 1.08x site-definition) [Certain], leaving balance-sheet room to keep funding manufacturing capacity and bolt-on licensing — e.g. the Gate Bioscience drug-discovery collaboration, expanded 2026-10-05 to a deal potentially worth more than $870 million (source: https://www.reuters.com/legal/litigation/gate-bioscience-expands-lilly-drug-discovery-deal-worth-more-than-870-million-2026-10-05/) [Likely]. But cash conversion has weakened materially: FCF margin fell from 21.4% (FY2021) to 2.3% (FY2023) before recovering to 22.8% TTM, and FCF/NI is 68.1% TTM versus 108.5% in FY2021 [Certain] — profit is outrunning cash as capacity is built, worth monitoring though ROIC/ROCE trends remain positive.

## 6. Unusual items
Short interest rose 14.6% versus the prior month, to a still-low 0.85% of float [Certain]. Otherwise none material: institutional ownership is high (89.3%), Piotroski F-score strong (7), Altman Z safely in the safe zone (6.55), beta low (0.45, 5y) [Certain].
