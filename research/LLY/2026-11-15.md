# Eli Lilly and Company (LLY) — Core initiation — 2026-11-15
## 1. Business
Global pharma maker; profits are driven by incretin drugs (tirzepatide as Mounjaro/Zepbound, orforglipron as Foundayo), plus Verzenio, Taltz, Jardiance and others. Mounjaro, Zepbound and Foundayo were 66 percent of revenue for the nine months to 30 Sep 2026 [company-reported] [Certain] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/59478/000005947826000108/lly-20260930.htm). Country revenue mix: [data unavailable] in the fact sheet; I did not fetch it. China is an expanding market (orforglipron approved there, first GLP-1 pill) [Likely] (source: https://www.reuters.com/business/healthcare-pharmaceuticals/china-approves-eli-lillys-weight-loss-pill-orforglipron-2026-11-12/).

## 2. Quality
Exceptional and still improving. [Certain] TTM ROIC 42.7% (a year earlier 38.3%), operating margin 51.3% (43.8%), gross margin 83.6% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/lly/financials/ratios/?p=trailing). Net debt/EBITDA 0.96x, Altman Z 7.27, Piotroski 6 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/lly/statistics/).

Direction change: FCF margin is down over five fiscal years (heavy capacity build; FY2023 2.3%) but up over the last year, 15.6% to 22.7% [Certain]. Capacity spending continues (a $3.5bn Pennsylvania site) [Certain] (source: https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/lilly-breaks-ground-in-pennsylvanias-lehigh-valley-on-a-3-5-billion-site-to-make-next-generation-obesity-medicines-the-latest-of-10-new-us-sites-since-2020--302938234.html).

Moat [Likely]: scale in manufacturing, a deep incretin pipeline (oral and combination assets) and outcomes data. Concentration is the weak point: realised U.S. prices fell on Zepbound and Mounjaro, offset by volume [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/59478/000005947826000108/lly-20260930.htm).

## 3. Valuation
Price 1,443.50 USD, a record, +30.1% above its 200-day average (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/lly/history/). P/E 46.8x sits at 4% of its own five-year range, yet EV/Sales 15.4x is at 90%. Against peers: P/E +8%, EV/EBITDA +107% (28.6x vs 13.8x), P/FCF +165%. FCF yield is 1.53%. [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/lly/statistics/).

Mechanical fair value: base 547.40, bull 1,206.26, below the price even in the bull case; the reverse DCF needs 40.3% year-1 revenue growth [Certain]. Fair value is unreliable for a business growing this fast, but the price assumes continued earnings compounding and no price-regulation shock [Likely]. It is priced as a working, rising business rather than a discount; a trend and quality case, not a value case [Likely].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Operating margin (TTM, income statement page) falls below 45%.
- ROIC (TTM, ratios page) falls below 35%.
- FCF margin (TTM, cash-flow page) falls below 15%.
- Incretin share of revenue (10-Q MD&A) rises further while U.S. realised price declines outpace volume growth, shown by quarterly revenue growth turning negative year on year.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Capital goes to capacity and R&D. Total debt was $49.5 billion at 30 Sep 2026, up $7.0 billion since December 2025 [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/59478/000005947826000108/lly-20260930.htm). Share count is shrinking slightly (-0.58% YoY). FCF conversion is 71.3% TTM versus 43.5% in FY2025, recovering but below the 108.5% of FY2021 [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/lly/financials/cash-flow-statement/).

## 6. Unusual items
SEC delta (10-Q filed 2026-10-29): Risk Factors show no substantive change. MD&A adds U.S. voluntary pricing agreements, a Medicare GLP-1 bridge programme through 2027 with unknown uptake, IRA price-setting for Trulicity and Verzenio from 2028 and an expectation that more products will be selected, compounded incretins, and euro currency risk. A U.S. heart-failure (HFpEF) tirzepatide application was withdrawn [Certain] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/59478/000005947826000108/lly-20260930.htm). Policy and pricing is the main risk [Likely].

Sentiment: RSI 69.7, near overbought; short interest 0.81% of float; beta 0.39 [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/lly/statistics/). Rally followed the China approval and a rotation out of tech [Likely].
