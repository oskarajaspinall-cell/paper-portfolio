# PayPal Holdings, Inc. (PYPL) — Core initiation — 2026-11-20
## 1. Business
Two-sided payments network: transaction fees on checkout (PayPal, Braintree processing, Venmo), plus interest on customer balances, consumer credit and partner-institution revenue share. Branded checkout is losing mix to lower-margin Braintree. US is the larger market; no single foreign country exceeds 10% of revenue [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1633917/000163391726000088/pypl-20260930.htm). [Certain]

## 2. Quality
Returns are high and stable: ROIC 21.8% TTM, rising over five fiscal years; operating margin 17.7%; FCF margin 18.4%, rising over the last 12 months but below its 2-year high of 22.4% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/pypl/financials/ratios/). [Certain] Gross margin is the weak point, falling 5.5pp over five years to 40.5%. Per the 10-Q, Braintree and Venmo revenue grew while PayPal-branded revenue fell on mix, merchant incentives and lower FX fees, and operating margin fell on transaction expense [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1633917/000163391726000088/pypl-20260930.htm). [Certain] The moat (a large two-sided network) is narrower than the card networks': checkout share is contestable. [Likely] Net debt/EBITDA is 0.61x, rising but low. A cost programme targets at least $1.5 billion in run-rate savings [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1633917/000163391726000088/pypl-20260930.htm). [Certain]

## 3. Valuation
P/E 10.5x is at its 5y minimum (median 20.6x); EV/EBITDA 7.8x, P/FCF 7.4x and P/B 2.4x are all below their 5y minimums; peers trade 69-86% higher on these multiples, though the peer set (V, MA, XYZ, AFRM) is richer-growth. FCF yield is 13.45%. The mechanical base fair value is 67.80 vs a last close of 56.37 (+20.3%); bear 38.17, bull 88.66. The reverse DCF implies roughly zero revenue growth in year 1, fading to terminal. [Likely] The price assumes branded-checkout erosion offsets Braintree and Venmo growth indefinitely. Trend: 11.8% above the 200-day average, 3-month momentum -8.0%, 12-month -8.6%; RSI 38.8. (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/pypl/statistics/)

## 4. What would prove this wrong

- TTM ROIC (ratios page) falls below 15%.

- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) rises above 1.5x.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
FCF conversion is 133.3% of net income TTM (5y trend falling). Shares are down 8.79% YoY; the 10-Q shows large buybacks funded by cash flow and a May 2026 debt issuance [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1633917/000163391726000088/pypl-20260930.htm). [Certain] Buybacks at a 13% FCF yield are accretive if the thesis holds. Restructuring costs are real, recurring and need to stop appearing. [Likely]
