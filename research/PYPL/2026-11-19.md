# PayPal Holdings (PYPL) — Core initiation — 2026-11-19
## 1. Business
PayPal earns transaction fees on payment volume across branded checkout, Braintree (unbranded processing), Venmo and PayPal's other consumer products. It adds interest on customer balances and loan income. The Q3 10-Q says Braintree and Venmo revenue grew while PayPal-branded revenue fell on product and geographic mix, merchant incentives and lower FX fees [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1633917/000163391726000088/pypl-20260930.htm). ## 2. Quality
- Returns are high for a payments company: TTM ROIC 21.8% and operating margin 17.7%, with 5-year ROIC trend rising (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/pypl/financials/ratios/). [Certain]
- Last 12 months: ROIC -0.8pp and operating margin -1.0pp year on year, both flat on the site's trend test. [Certain]
- FCF margin is falling over 5 fiscal years but rose from 16.9% to 18.4% over the last 12 months. It is 4.0pp below its 2-year high. [Certain]
- Gross margin has fallen 5.5pp over 5 years. The filing blames mix shift toward lower-margin Braintree volume and merchant incentives. [Likely]
- Moat: two-sided network, Venmo and checkout habit. It is being eroded at branded checkout, since the filing shows PayPal-branded revenue falling while Braintree grows. [Likely]
- Leverage is low: net debt/EBITDA 0.61x. Altman Z is 1.93 and Piotroski F is 6 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/pypl/statistics/). [Certain]
- Overall: a good cash generator with a contested moat. [Likely]
