# Avanza Bank Holding (STO:AZA) — Core initiation — 2026-11-09
## 1. Business
Swedish online savings platform and bank: earns net interest income on customer cash and lending, plus brokerage, fund and trading commissions on savings capital [Likely]. Revenue is driven by customer growth, fund inflows and trading activity; Q3 2026 operating profit was SEK 920 million, the second-highest quarter ever [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sto/AZA/transcripts/530433-q3-2026/). Revenue is overwhelmingly Swedish [Guessing]; no foreign country is shown at ~10%+ [data unavailable].

## 2. Quality
Exceptional returns for a bank: ROE 36.9% TTM and ROIC 34.2% TTM, operating margin 62.8% TTM (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sto/AZA/financials/ratios/) [Certain]. Returns have been stable at roughly 35-37% since FY2022 but are below FY2021's 52.0% ROE; ROIC is 4.6pp under its 2-year high of 38.8% and down 1.7pp on a year earlier [Certain]. This is a mild fade, not a break, and FY2021 was a retail-trading boom [Likely]. Moat: low-cost platform, brand and sticky savings balances, with customer growth continuing [Likely]. Risk: trading income is volatile; lower volatility weakened it in Q3 [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sto/AZA/transcripts/530433-q3-2026/). Balance sheet metrics [data unavailable]; Piotroski F is 4 [Certain].

## 3. Valuation
P/E 20.0x versus a 5y range of 18.5x-25.2x (22% position), so near the cheap end of its own history, but +41% against the peer median 14.2x (SEB, Swedbank, Handelsbanken, Nordnet) [Certain]. P/B 6.7x versus peers 1.9x, reflecting ROE roughly triple a typical bank [Certain]. FCF yield 18.98% and P/FCF 5.3x are not meaningful for a bank, since cash flow includes customer-deposit movements [Likely]. Mechanical fair value is SEK 186.81 base versus a close of SEK 368.50 (-49%), but it is flagged unreliable (methods disagree 2.1x: P/B+ROE 135.11, FCFE 290.20) [Certain]. The price implies sustained high returns and growth (reverse DCF: 12.7% year-1 revenue growth) [Certain]. Priced for continued excellence with little margin of safety on the model; the history-based cheapness is the only support [Likely].
