# Vallourec S.A. (EPA:VK) — Core initiation — 2026-11-24
## 1. Business
Vallourec makes premium seamless steel tubes and VAM threaded connections for oil and gas wells, plus industrial and geothermal uses, and has a smaller Mine & Forest iron-ore and wood segment [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/epa/VK/transcripts/679824-q3-2026/). North America and the Middle East are the two largest Tubes regions, and the Q3 call blamed Middle East exposure for part of the gap to Tenaris [Likely] (same source). ## 2. Quality
Returns are high but slipping. ROIC is 14.7% TTM vs 18.3% in FY2025 and a 2-year high of 18.3% (2025-12-31), and ROCE is 15.0% vs 19.2% at its 2-year high (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/epa/VK/financials/ratios/?p=trailing) [Certain]. Both are rising over five fiscal years but falling over the last 12 months, so the 2026-11-08 note's "rising" reading was too generous [Certain]. Operating margin is 14.8% TTM (16.4% high in 2026-03), FCF margin 12.6% and net debt/EBITDA 0.15x (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/epa/VK/statistics/) [Certain]. The Q3 call showed lower Tubes margin, a US subcontractor problem, and Middle East port delays expected to cost money again in Q4 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/epa/VK/transcripts/679824-q3-2026/) [Likely]. These look like execution and logistics problems, not collapsing demand, since management said US pricing was still rising [Likely]. The moat (proprietary VAM threads, customer qualification) is intact but has not stopped margin erosion [Likely].

## 3. Valuation
At 14.81 EUR: P/E 13.2x (15% of its 5y range, median 9.4x), EV/EBITDA 4.9x (46% of range, median 5.2x), P/FCF 7.6x (19% of range) and FCF yield 13.09% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/epa/VK/statistics/) [Certain]. Peer medians (TS, NOV, OII) put it 39% below on EV/EBITDA and 47% below on P/FCF [Certain]. Fair value base is 18.91 EUR (+27.7%), bull 31.10; the bear case is negative, so the range is very wide and rests on a through-cycle DCF [Certain]. The reverse DCF implies -6.7% revenue growth in year 1, a deeper downturn than the 2026-11-08 reading [Certain]. The price is 28.1% below its 200-day average, RSI is 22.5 and relative strength vs SPY over 6 months is -35.3pp [Certain]. Cheap on cash flow and EV/EBITDA, but the market is pricing a lasting earnings reset [Likely].

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
FCF conversion is 161.7% TTM and the company is close to net cash (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/epa/VK/financials/ratios/) [Certain]. Shares outstanding are down 2.48% YoY, a reversal from the mild dilution seen on 2026-11-08 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/epa/VK/statistics/) [Certain]. The call said a large part of Q3 cash generation came from warrant proceeds, and that cash returned in the quarter included an extraordinary interim dividend [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/epa/VK/transcripts/679824-q3-2026/). So underlying Q3 cash generation was weak and the TTM FCF margin overstates the run rate [Guessing].
