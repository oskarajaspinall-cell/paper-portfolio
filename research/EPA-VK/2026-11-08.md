# Vallourec S.A. (EPA:VK) — Core initiation — 2026-11-08
## 1. Business
Vallourec makes premium seamless steel tubes and threaded connections (VAM) for oil and gas wells, plus industrial and growing new-energy (geothermal) uses; it earns a premium for engineered products, not commodity pipe [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/epa/VK/). Management targets 10%-15% of group EBITDA from New Energies by 2030 [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/epa/VK/transcripts/684543-investor-update/). ## 2. Quality
Returns are high and recovered from a weak 2021: ROIC 17.7% TTM vs 2.5% in FY2021, operating margin 16.2%, FCF margin 12.0% TTM, FCF conversion 124.3% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/epa/VK/financials/ratios/) [Certain]. The balance sheet is net cash (net debt/EBITDA -0.18x) vs 2.13x in FY2021 [Certain]. Piotroski F is 8 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/epa/VK/statistics/) [Certain]. The last 12 months are rising on ROIC, ROCE, operating and FCF margins. ROCE of 16.1% sits 4.4pp below its 2-year high of 20.5% (2024-06-30), so returns are near, not at, peak [Certain]. Peak-cycle risk is real: ROIC was 2.5% and ROE -20.8% in FY2021-22, so these are cyclical returns and the premium-connection moat (proprietary VAM threads, qualification barriers) is the argument for a higher floor [Likely]. The Q2 call cited offshore contract wins and strong US dynamics despite Middle East disruption to volumes (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/epa/VK/transcripts/659211-q2-2026/) [Likely].

## 3. Valuation
At 18.91 EUR: P/E 13.6x (16% of its 5y range, above the 9.4x median), EV/EBITDA 5.4x (62% of range, median 5.2x), P/FCF 9.9x (median 9.6x), FCF yield 10.12% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/epa/VK/statistics/) [Certain]. EV/Sales and P/B are above their 5y maxima, reflecting higher margins [Likely]. Versus peers (TS, NOV, SLB, WFRD) it trades 27-41% below on every multiple [Certain]. Fair value base is 19.97 EUR, +5.6% above the close; the bear case is negative and the bull 30.64 EUR, so the range is wide and the through-cycle DCF is the main driver [Certain]. The reverse DCF implies -2.3% revenue growth in year 1, i.e. a market already pricing a downturn [Certain]. The price sits 8.3% below the 200-day average and 6-month momentum is -23.9%, meaning the market has de-rated on cycle fears since the May high [Certain]. Cheap versus peers, fairly valued versus own-model base; little margin of safety on the base case [Likely].
