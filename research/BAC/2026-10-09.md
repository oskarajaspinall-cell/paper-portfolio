# Bank of America Corporation (BAC) — Core initiation — 2026-10-09

## 1. Business
Bank of America is a diversified US bank: Consumer Banking (deposits, cards, mortgages), Global Wealth & Investment Management (Merrill, Private Bank), Global Banking (corporate lending, treasury, investment banking) and Global Markets (trading); industry classification is Banks – Diversified [Certain]. Revenue is mainly net interest income on loans funded by deposits, plus fee income from wealth management, trading and investment banking (source: factsheet OV).

## 2. Quality
ROE fell from 11.8% (FY21) to a 9.2% trough (FY24), then recovered to 10.2% (FY25) and 11.2% TTM [Certain]. Net margin shows the same pattern: 34.1% (FY21) down to 26.7% (FY23), back to 29.5% TTM [Certain]. As a scaled, diversified bank, BAC's mix across consumer, wealth management, banking and markets gives more balance than narrower peers [Likely]. But the ROE recovery looks mostly cyclical (rate/credit cycle), not a widening moat: BAC trades at 1.4x P/B versus a 2.0x peer median (JPM/WFC/C/GS) [Certain] — the market already prices its returns as structurally below best-in-class peers such as JPM. ROIC, ROCE, gross/operating/FCF margin and net debt/EBITDA are not meaningful or unavailable for a deposit-funded bank's balance sheet [data unavailable]. Piotroski F-score is 4/9, mid-table [Certain]. Net: a serviceable, well-diversified franchise without a clear durable edge over peers; returns track the rate/credit cycle more than structural moat.

## 3. Valuation
P/E of 12.4x sits at the 60th percentile of its own 5y range (10.2x–13.8x) and 10% below the 13.7x peer median [Certain]. P/B of 1.4x is above its own 5y high (0.9x–1.3x) yet still 30% below the 2.0x peer median [Certain] — cheap versus JPM/WFC/C/GS on book value, consistent with lower ROE. The fact sheet's own-history fair value (CAPM, FRED 10y 5.28%; blend 67% P/B+ROE / 33% FCFE DCF) gives a base case of $40.56, 24.3% below the $53.61 last close; even the bull case ($45.08) is 15.9% below it [Certain]. The two methods diverge sharply — P/B+ROE implies $29.84–34.30, FCFE DCF implies $42.54–66.64 — so the blend leans on the more optimistic DCF carrying only a third weight; on the ROE-based method alone the stock looks notably more expensive. Reverse-DCF implied growth (3.3% yr-1, fading to terminal) is not aggressive, yet price sits above every fair-value scenario [Certain].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROE (ratios page) falls back below 9% for two consecutive fiscal quarters — the post-2024 recovery would be confirmed as purely cyclical.
- Net margin (income statement) drops below 26% TTM — credit costs or NII compression eroding profitability faster than peers.
- Piotroski F-score (statistics page) falls to 3 or below — multiple fundamental red flags appearing together.
- Shares outstanding (statistics page) stop shrinking or start rising — the buyback currently supporting EPS (-3.72% YoY shares) reverses.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Shares outstanding are down 3.72% YoY [Certain], consistent with an active buyback supporting EPS. The statistics page shows a 25.35% FCF yield [Certain], but this is not economically comparable to an industrial company's FCF yield: for a deposit-funded bank, reported operating/financing cash flow swings mechanically with loan and deposit balances [Likely], so it should not be read as a cash-conversion signal. ROIC, ROCE, FCF margin, FCF conversion and net debt/EBITDA are not meaningful or unavailable for a bank's balance sheet [data unavailable]. The clearest read on capital efficiency remains ROE, recovering to 11.2% TTM [Certain].

## 6. Unusual items
Merrill Lynch (a BAC unit) agreed to pay $39m to settle a class action over low interest paid on customers' idle cash sweep balances (source: https://www.reuters.com/business/finance/bank-america-pay-39-million-settle-customer-claims-over-low-interest-rates-cash-2026-09-30/, as listed in the fact sheet) [Certain]. The amount is immaterial against a $374.9bn market cap, but signals continuing regulatory/legal scrutiny of cash-sweep practices industry-wide [Likely]. Separately, the Fed finalized a stress-test overhaul on 2026-09-30 intended to make the annual exam "more transparent and less volatile" (source: https://www.reuters.com/business/finance/fed-finalizes-bank-stress-test-overhaul-2026-09-30/, as listed in the fact sheet) [Likely], which could reduce capital-planning uncertainty for large banks including BAC. Next earnings are due 2026-10-14, five days after this note [Certain].
