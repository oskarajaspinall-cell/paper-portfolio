# Macro overlay: Bank of America Corporation (BAC) — 2026-10-09
## Assessment
Banks + rates is a primary macro link (CLAUDE.md taxonomy) [Certain]. Chain: fed funds/long yields → net interest margin and securities mark-to-market (AOCI) → BAC's net interest income and capital → equity risk premium for financials → P/B multiple.

**Facts** (FRED, https://fred.stlouisfed.org/, 2026-10-07/08): 10y Treasury (DGS10) 5.28%, +0.72pp/3m; 10y real yield (DFII10) 2.92%, +0.61pp/3m; 2y yield (DGS2) 4.77%, +0.56pp/3m; fed funds (DFF) 3.88%, +0.26pp/3m; 10y breakeven (T10YIE) 2.35%, +0.12pp/3m; HY spread (BAMLH0A0HYM2) 3.09%, +0.39pp/3m; VIX 15.08, benign.

**Market pricing (proxy, inference):** 2y yield sits ~0.89pp above fed funds, implying the market still prices further tightening [Likely] **(inference)**. Nominal yields rose mostly via real yield, not breakevens, so the move is policy-path/term-premium driven, not an inflation scare **(inference)**. This matches the WSJ's framing of banks facing "a new rate-hiking cycle" (2026-10-05, in fact sheet) and the sector's -11.9pp 3m underperformance vs SPY — the rate worry looks largely priced in already [Likely].

**Bull:** rates stay higher without a credit event; BAC's loans reprice faster than deposit costs, NIM/NII beat, multiple re-rates toward the 2.0x peer P/B.
**Bear:** the fast real-yield back-up (+0.61pp/3m) pressures AFS/HTM securities (AOCI) echoing 2023, while HY spreads keep widening, compressing NIM and capital.
**Tilt:** toward bear, small — the curve is upward-sloping (no 2023-style inversion) and HY spread (3.09%) is well below the 5.0% stress level, but the real-yield pace is a live, checkable risk into Oct 14 earnings.
