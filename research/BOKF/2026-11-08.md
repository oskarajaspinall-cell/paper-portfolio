# BOK Financial Corporation (BOKF) — Core initiation — 2026-11-08
## 1. Business
Tulsa-based regional bank holding company (Financials, Banks - Regional). It earns net interest income on commercial, energy and consumer loans, plus fee income from trading, asset management, transaction cards and mortgage activity [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/bokf/). No single foreign country is evidenced as a material revenue share; the business is US-centred [Likely].

## 2. Quality
Mediocre-to-adequate franchise, not a compounder. ROE ran 11.6% (FY2021), 10.3%, 10.8%, 9.8%, 10.1% (FY2025), TTM 11.2%: a falling five-year trend, with a TTM recovery (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/bokf/financials/ratios/) [Certain]. Net margin slid from 31.3% to 25.8% (FY2024), 26.6% (FY2025), TTM 29.2% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/bokf/financials/income-statement/) [Certain]. ROIC/ROCE are not meaningful for banks [data unavailable]. Piotroski F is 4, i.e. middling (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/bokf/statistics/) [Certain].

Recent headlines point to improving operations: Q3 results were reported ahead of consensus, loan growth strong, net interest margin up, full-year guidance maintained, and the quarterly dividend raised [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/bokf/transcripts/711347-q3-2026/; https://www.tipranks.com/news/the-fly/bok-financial-increases-quarterly-dividend-24-to-78c-per-share-thefly-news). Moat is local relationships and fee businesses, but returns have not exceeded roughly 11-12% [Guessing].

Direction change: FCF margin fell from 64.8% (TTM a year earlier) to 31.4%. For a bank, FCF is dominated by working-capital and trading-asset swings, so this is weak evidence either way [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/bokf/financials/cash-flow-statement/?p=trailing).

## 3. Valuation
The price assumes little growth. P/E is 11.7x, 36% of the way through its 5y range (10.7x to 13.5x, median 13.1x) and 7% below the peer median of 12.7x (CFR, PNFP, WTFC, UMBF). P/B is 1.3x, at its 5y median and 6% below peers at 1.4x. P/FCF of 10.9x sits at 82% of its range and 12% above peers, but is unreliable for a bank [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/bokf/statistics/). FCF yield is 9.15%.

Fair value (blend of P/B + ROE and FCFE DCF; own-history assumptions): bear 116.35, base 136.71, bull 152.99 against a 129.48 close, i.e. -10.1% / +5.6% / +18.2% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/bokf/history/) [Likely]. The reverse DCF implies -4.3% year-1 revenue growth. Skew is modest: a discount to peers and history, but the best method (P/B + ROE) gives 115.70 base, below the price [Likely].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROE (ratios page, TTM) falls below 9.0%.
- Net margin (income-statement page, TTM) falls below 24%.
- Shares outstanding change YoY (statistics page) turns positive above +2%, i.e. buybacks reverse into dilution.
- Piotroski F-score (statistics page) falls below 3.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Share count fell 4.73% YoY, so buybacks are shrinking the base [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/bokf/statistics/). The dividend was raised sharply [Likely] (source: https://www.tipranks.com/news/the-fly/bok-financial-increases-quarterly-dividend-24-to-78c-per-share-thefly-news). Cash conversion (FCF/NI) was 99.5% in FY2025 and 107.6% TTM, but swung wildly in earlier years (-630.4% in FY2021, 943.2% in FY2022), so it is noisy and not a quality signal for a bank [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/bokf/financials/cash-flow-statement/).

## 6. Unusual items
