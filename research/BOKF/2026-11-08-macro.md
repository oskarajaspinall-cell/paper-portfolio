# Macro overlay: BOK Financial Corporation (BOKF) — 2026-11-08
## Assessment
**Weight: secondary.** Chain: credit spreads / risk appetite -> bank equity risk premium -> P/B multiple. A slower link runs through the policy path and curve to net interest margin (NIM) [Likely].

**Facts (FRED):** The high-yield spread is 3.48% (2026-11-05), up 0.77pp in ~3m (https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=BAMLH0A0HYM2). VIX is 28.18, up 13.2 in ~1m (https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=VIXCLS). The 10y is 4.84% (https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=DGS10) and the 2y 4.46% (https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=DGS2), a slope of about 0.38pp. Fed funds is 3.88% (https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=DFF).

**Pricing (proxy):** The 2y about 58bp above fed funds implies a higher policy path, not cuts **(inference)**. BOKF is down 10.4% over 3m against SPY at -4.3% (fact sheet), so part of the risk-premium shock looks priced **(inference)**.

**Company link:** Q3 NIM was 3.0% (up 9bp), loan growth 11.5%, provisions guided under $10M (https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/bokf/transcripts/711347-q3-2026/). Earnings are healthy now. The hurt would be a credit surprise: provisions up and P/B down. A flat curve with sticky inflation is a mild NIM headwind.

**Bull:** Spreads retrace, curve steepens; NIM expands and the multiple moves toward peer P/B 1.4x (about +5-10%).
**Bear:** Spreads past 5%, provisions rise; multiple compresses toward the 1.1x 5y low, earnings hit modestly.
**Tilt:** toward bear, small. Fundamentals are strong and credit data show no deterioration yet.

No material foreign revenue share is evidenced (note section 1), so no country risk.
