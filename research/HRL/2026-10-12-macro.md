# Macro overlay: Hormel Foods Corporation (HRL) — 2026-10-12
## Assessment
HRL's thesis is idiosyncratic (margin/ROE deterioration, the debt-funded Brakebush deal) and beta is low (0.29) [Certain] (https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/hrl/statistics/), so broad risk appetite isn't the driver. One channel is live: rates/credit, feeding both the DCF discount rate (67% weight in base fair value) and the financing cost of the ~$1.06bn debt-funded Brakebush deal management must de-lever after [Likely].

**Facts**: US 10y Treasury (DGS10) 5.22% on 2026-10-08, +0.68pp over 3m; 10y real yield (DFII10) 2.87%, +0.56pp over 3m; 10y breakeven (T10YIE) 2.33%, roughly flat (+0.09pp 3m) — nearly all the nominal-yield rise is real, not inflation expectations [Certain] (https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=DGS10, id=DFII10, id=T10YIE, 2026-10-08/09). HY credit spread (BAMLH0A0HYM2) 3.15%, +0.45pp/3m, still below stress levels [Certain] (https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=BAMLH0A0HYM2).

**Market pricing (proxy, inference)**: 2y Treasury (4.75%) above effective fed funds (3.88%) implies the market prices no near-term cuts **(inference)**. The fact sheet's DCF already uses today's 5.22% risk-free rate, so the level is priced; the surprise risk is direction from here.

**Bull**: real yields/spreads ease → lower discount rate lifts fair value toward the $25.32 bull case; cheaper Brakebush financing speeds de-leveraging.
**Bear**: real yields/spreads keep rising → fair value compresses toward/below the $12.03 bear case; costlier acquisition debt slows de-leveraging, adding to existing margin pressure.
**Tilt**: toward bear, small — rates amplify an already company-specific bear case rather than driving it.
