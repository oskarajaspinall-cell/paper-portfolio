# Hormel Foods Corporation (HRL) — Core initiation — 2026-10-12

## 1. Business
Branded packaged-food maker (Spam, Skippy, Planters, Applegate, Jennie-O) selling through retail, foodservice and deli/specialty channels in the US and internationally [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/hrl/). Revenue is protein- and pantry-brand driven; margins depend on input-cost pass-through (pork, turkey, peanuts) and mix toward value-added/branded items [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/hrl/financials/income-statement/).

## 2. Quality
Returns are deteriorating across every measure: ROIC fell from 11.4% (FY21) to 6.6% TTM, ROCE from 9.9% to 8.7%, ROE from 13.6% to 4.3% [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/hrl/financials/ratios/). Gross margin slipped 1.3pp and operating margin 1.9pp over five years, with net margin nearly halved to 2.8% TTM, pointing to persistent input-cost and competitive pressure [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/hrl/financials/income-statement/). Brand strength (Spam, Skippy, Planters) gives pricing power and shelf-space durability, but the trend shows this moat is not preventing margin erosion versus peers [Likely]. The 2026-09-30 agreement to buy Brakebush Brothers for ~$1.055bn adds scale in value-added chicken and foodservice; management expects adjusted EPS accretion from FY2028 and plans to "de-lever following the acquisition," implying debt funding and higher near-term leverage [Likely] (source: https://www.hormelfoods.com/newsroom/hormel-foods-announces-definitive-agreement-to-acquire-brakebush-a-leading-value-added-chicken-company/). Net debt/EBITDA is a moderate 1.57x TTM, giving headroom, but integration risk is real [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/hrl/financials/ratios/).

## 3. Valuation
HRL trades at 30.9x TTM P/E, 156% of its own 5-year range and near the peer median of 33.0x [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/hrl/financials/ratios/, peers GIS/TSN/CAG/KHC statistics pages). Yet cash-flow multiples look cheap: EV/EBITDA 9.9x is below its own 5y minimum (12.5x) though 17% above peers; P/FCF 13.5x is below its 5y minimum but 46% above the peer median; FCF yield is 7.4% [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/hrl/statistics/). Blended fair value is $19.87 (base), near the $19.19 close (+3.5%), with bear/bull of $12.03/$25.32 [Certain] (source: research/HRL/factsheet-2026-10-12.md). The reverse-DCF shows the price assumes -1.3% revenue growth in year one fading to terminal growth — the market has priced in continued deterioration with little recovery credited [Likely]. The gap between an elevated P/E and a below-average EV/EBITDA suggests earnings, not cash flow, are depressed — consistent with a margin-recovery rather than secular-decline story, though unproven in the data.

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROIC (ratios page) falls below 5% for two consecutive fiscal years, confirming returns have structurally broken rather than cyclically dipped.
- Gross margin (income statement) falls below 14% (below the FY2025 trough of 15.7%), showing input-cost/pricing pressure is still worsening.
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) rises above 3.0x post-Brakebush close without a clear de-leveraging path, contradicting management's stated plan.
- FCF conversion (FCF/NI, cash flow + income statement) falls below 80% for two consecutive years, showing cash quality of earnings has deteriorated from today's strong 227.9% TTM level.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
FCF conversion is strong and rising (227.9% TTM vs 84.7% in FY21), and FCF margin is 6.4% TTM [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/hrl/financials/cash-flow-statement/, income-statement/). Shares outstanding are essentially flat (+0.13% YoY), so capital return is not diluting holders [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/hrl/statistics/). The Brakebush deal ($1.055bn, ~10% of market cap) is the dominant near-term capital-allocation decision: debt-funded, expected to be EPS-accretive only from FY2028, with management committing to de-lever afterward [Likely] (source: https://www.hormelfoods.com/newsroom/hormel-foods-announces-definitive-agreement-to-acquire-brakebush-a-leading-value-added-chicken-company/). The company also continues to pay a quarterly dividend, declared 2026-09-28 [Certain] (source: factsheet news; https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/hormel-foods-corporation-declares-quarterly-dividend-302892098.html).

## 6. Unusual items
Short interest is 7.17% of float and rose 21.7% versus the prior month, a meaningfully elevated and rising bearish position for a staples name [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/hrl/statistics/). The stock is down 19.7% over 12 months and underperformed SPY by 35.3pp over that period, with 14-day RSI at 25.0 (oversold), consistent with continued selling pressure into the quality deterioration above rather than a stabilising base [Likely] (source: research/HRL/factsheet-2026-10-12.md). Beta is low (0.29), so the stock's own fundamentals, not market beta, are driving the move [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/hrl/statistics/).
