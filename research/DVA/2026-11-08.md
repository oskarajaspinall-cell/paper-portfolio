# DaVita Inc. (DVA) — Core initiation — 2026-11-08
## 1. Business
Largest US dialysis provider: it earns per-treatment fees from Medicare/Medicaid and, disproportionately for profit, commercial insurers, across a chronic, non-discretionary patient base (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/dva/). Profit depends on treatment volume, commercial mix and revenue per treatment [Likely]. No foreign country is shown as ~10%+ of revenue on the fact sheet [data unavailable].

## 2. Quality
Returns are decent and stable, not exceptional. TTM ROIC 12.2% and ROCE 14.4%, both up on a year earlier (+0.6pp, +0.9pp); operating margin 15.2%, flat (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/dva/financials/ratios/?p=trailing) [Certain]. Moat is scale and regional density in an oligopoly with Fresenius [Likely], but the moat does not protect pricing: payers and the state set the economics. Q3 volumes rose on census growth; ACA exchange enrolment fell after enhanced premium tax credits expired, hurting commercial mix and revenue per treatment (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/927066/000092706626000134/dva-20260930.htm) [Certain]. FCF margin 9.1% is 4.4pp below its 2-year high (13.6%) and 0.6pp below a year ago (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/dva/financials/cash-flow-statement/?p=trailing): the decline is real, not just endpoint noise [Certain]. Management says tech spend growth will decelerate (10-Q above) [Likely]. Piotroski 7, Altman Z 1.68 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/dva/statistics/).

## 3. Valuation
Cheap on cash earnings, not on every measure. FCF yield 10.74%; P/FCF 9.3x versus peer median 17.0x (-45%); EV/EBITDA 8.7x, at 40% of its 5y range and 19% under peers; P/E 14.1x, above its 5y max of 13.8x (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/dva/). P/B 10.7x is meaningless given buyback-shrunk equity (shares -14.08% YoY) [Likely]. Mechanical base fair value 380.01 versus close 191.35 (+98.6%), but it is 67% DCF, which is sensitive to a free-cash-flow base now falling; the EV/EBITDA leg gives 205.51, only modestly above price [Certain]. Reverse DCF: the price implies -8.4% year-1 revenue growth, so the market prices a policy hit, not stagnation [Certain]. The stock is +57.9% over 12 months against a 52-week high of 247.49 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/dva/history/). Upside is large if the policy risk is contained; the multiple leg says the re-rating has partly happened [Likely].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Operating margin TTM (income-statement trailing page) falls below 13.5%.
- FCF margin TTM (cash-flow trailing page) falls below 7%.
- Net debt / EBITDA (ratios page) rises above 5.0x.
- Text trigger: a final federal rule or law restricting charitable premium assistance, or a cut to commercial exchange enrolment that the 10-Q shows reducing revenue per treatment further.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Aggressive buybacks cut the share count 14.08% in a year, funded by FCF that converts at 140.6% of net income TTM (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/dva/financials/cash-flow-statement/) [Certain]. But leverage is high, at 4.51x net debt/EBITDA, against 4.19x at FY2024, and buying back stock at this leverage leaves little room for a reimbursement shock [Likely]. Conversion of 140.6% is down from 175.5% at FY2025 [Certain].

## 6. Unusual items
- Policy: HHS published a Kidney Care National Plan with a charitable-activity review recommendation; sell-side commentary calls it the largest near-term risk. Treat as [Likely] a real overhang on commercial mix (source: https://www.tipranks.com/news/the-fly/td-says-hhs-charitable-activity-review-recommendation-near-term-risk-for-davita-thefly-news). The headline is third-party reporting; its numbers are not used.
- Q3 print: adjusted EPS beat and FY26 guidance narrowed, with higher technology costs and ACA mix pressure noted (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/dva/transcripts/724019-q3-2026/) [Likely]. The reaction was +5.1% on 3.2x volume.
- Short interest 14.67% of float, +14.1% on the prior month (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/dva/statistics/): the market is positioned for a policy hit [Certain].
- Law-firm "investigation" notices after a share-price decline are solicitations, not findings [Likely] (source: https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20261104281242/en/Did-You-Lose-Money-on-DaVita-Inc.-Stock-The-Law-Offices-of-Howard-G.-Smith-Announces-Securities-Fraud-Investigation-After-6.23-Share-Price-Decline/).
