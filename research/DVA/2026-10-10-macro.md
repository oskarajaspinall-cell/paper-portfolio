# Macro overlay: DaVita Inc. (DVA) — 2026-10-10
## Assessment
DVA's dialysis volumes are non-discretionary and its beta is low (0.80) [Certain] (fact sheet, stockanalysis.com/stocks/dva/statistics/), so demand is largely macro-insensitive. The one live channel is capital cost: net debt/EBITDA is 4.5x [Certain] (fact sheet, ratios page) and the fair-value model discounts cash flows at the 10y Treasury (5.22%) plus beta×ERP [Certain] (fact sheet). Chain: real yields/credit spreads → cost of equity and refinancing cost → DCF fair value and interest expense for this leveraged buyback model.

Facts (FRED, 2026-10-08/09): the 10y real yield (DFII10) is 2.87%, +56bp over 3 months; the 10y nominal yield (DGS10) is 5.22%, +68bp; breakeven inflation (T10YIE) is only +9bp — so the move is real-rate, not inflation, driven [Certain]. HY credit spreads (BAMLH0A0HYM2) are 3.15%, +45bp over 3 months, still low in absolute terms [Certain]. Market pricing (proxy): the 2y yield (4.75%) sits ~87bp above effective fed funds (3.88%), implying the market prices further tightening or term premium, not imminent cuts [Likely] **(inference)**. DVA fell -21.9% over 3 months, underperforming SPY by -25.5pp, coincident with this real-yield rise [Likely] **(inference)**.

Macro bull: real yields/HY spreads stabilize or fall → discount rate eases, DCF fair value expands, refinancing cheaper, multiple re-rates toward the already-wide discount to the model's base fair value ($428). Macro bear: real yields/spreads keep rising → fair value compresses and interest expense rises on the 4.5x-levered balance sheet, pressuring the buyback-driven EPS story. Tilt: moderate, toward bear — leverage amplifies rate sensitivity and the recent underperformance already shows the headwind biting **(inference)**.
