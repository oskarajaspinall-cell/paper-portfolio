# First Solar, Inc. (FSLR) — Core initiation — 2026-11-20
## 1. Business
Makes thin-film (CdTe) PV modules, sold mainly to US utility-scale developers and owners. Profit rests on module price per watt plus the US advanced manufacturing production credit, which cuts cost of sales. The US is its primary market [Certain] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1274494/000127449426000212/fslr-20260930.htm). Factories are in the US, India, Malaysia and Vietnam. No single foreign country's revenue share is disclosed in the sources read [Likely].

## 2. Quality
Returns are high and have risen over five years: TTM ROIC 19.8%, operating margin 37.7%, net cash (net debt/EBITDA -0.52x) (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/fslr/financials/ratios/) [Certain]. The moat is regulatory plus technological: US-made, non-Chinese-polysilicon supply is favoured by tariffs and foreign-entity credit limits, and thin film is excluded from crystalline-silicon trade remedies [Likely] (source: sec-delta, 10-Q above). Much of the margin is a policy subsidy, not price advantage [Likely]. Direction flag: FCF margin is rising over five years but fell from 12.2% to 4.6% over the last 12 months, 26.1pp below its 2-year high. The 10-Q shows operating cash flow of -$402.7m for nine months, driven by a $1,285.8m rise in government grants receivable, plus inventory and receivables build [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1274494/000127449426000212/fslr-20260930.htm). Profit is not yet converting to cash [Certain].

## 3. Valuation
P/E 8.6x and EV/EBITDA 6.0x are both below their 5-year minimums (14.6x and 10.1x) and about half the peer EV/EBITDA median of 12.0x. P/FCF is 69.2x and FCF yield 1.45%, so the cheapness is on accounting earnings, not cash [Certain]. The price assumes earnings are at a peak, or that the credit and tariff protection fade: gross margin is 49.3% against a fiscal-year high of 44.2% [Likely]. Mechanical fair value (EV/EBITDA only; DCF unusable on negative FCF) is bear 148.49, base 173.44, bull 303.53 versus a last close of 151.95 [Certain]. Trend is poor: 28.0% below the 200-day, -40.2% over 12 months, RSI 30.2.

Why it fell: the Q3 call cited moderated bookings from contracting discipline and trade-investigation uncertainty (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/fslr/transcripts/720237-q3-2026/) [Likely]. A post-election rally reversed within days (source: https://www.barrons.com/articles/first-solar-stock-price-9bf61d5f) [Likely]. Q3 revenue fell 22.8% while gross margin rose to 57.9% [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1274494/000127449426000212/fslr-20260930.htm).

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROIC TTM (ratios page, ?p=trailing) falls below 15%.
- FCF margin TTM (cash flow page, ?p=trailing) turns negative, i.e. grants receivable keep outrunning cash receipts.
- Gross margin TTM (income statement page) falls below 40%, i.e. the credit benefit or pricing erodes.
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) rises above 1.0x, i.e. net cash is spent on buybacks and capex.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Total debt fell to $107.7m, all India facilities, with cash and securities of $1,624.1m, and nine-month capex was $434.7m [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1274494/000127449426000212/fslr-20260930.htm). A buyback was announced with Q3 results (source: https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20261029415483/en/First-Solar-Inc.-Announces-Third-Quarter-2026-Financial-Results-Updates-Guidance-and-Announces-500-Million-Share-Repurchase-Program/) [Certain]. Cash fell over the nine months, so the buyback is not funded by operations [Likely]. Share count is flat (+0.15% YoY).

## 6. Unusual items
- Policy dependence: the 10-Q adds new tariff regimes after the Supreme Court struck down the IEEPA tariffs, and a polysilicon trade investigation outcome. The company expects US module prices to rise [Likely] (source: sec-delta, 10-Q above).
- Patent litigation: its TOPCon suit against JinkoSolar is now in risk factors, and a separate suit involving Mundra (Adani) is also named [Certain] (source: same 10-Q). Separate tracker patent suits are covered by an insurance receivable matching the accrual [Certain].
- Short interest 11.04% of float, beta 1.71 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/fslr/statistics/).
