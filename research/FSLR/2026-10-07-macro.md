# Macro overlay: First Solar, Inc. (FSLR) — 2026-10-07
## Assessment
FSLR's customers (utility-scale developers) finance builds with debt, and FSLR's own equity is valued off multi-year earnings — both are real-rate sensitive [Likely] **(inference)**. Chain: real yields ↑ → developer financing cost/IRR ↑ → order/ASP risk; and discount rate on FSLR's long-dated earnings ↑ → multiple compression, independent of fundamentals.

Official data: US 10y real yield (DFII10) is 2.95% (2026-10-05), up +0.52pp in ~1m and +0.71pp in ~3m; nominal 10y (DGS10) 5.31%, up +0.83pp/3m; effective fed funds (DFF) 3.88%, up +0.25pp/3m — tightening, not a cutting cycle (https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=DFII10, id=DGS10, id=DFF, all 2026-10-05). Breakevens (T10YIE) barely moved (+0.11pp/3m to 2.36%, 2026-10-06), so the move is a real-rate/funding-cost story, not an inflation story [Certain]. HY credit spreads widened +0.44pp/1m to 3.12% (2026-10-05), a mild tightening in financing conditions relevant to leveraged solar developers [Certain] (https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=BAMLH0A0HYM2).

Market pricing: FSLR fell -22.9% over 3m, underperforming SPY by -26.6pp; sell-side/commentator pieces (interpretation, not fact) attribute the drop to rising yields/borrowing costs [Guessing]. The rate move is likely already substantially priced **(inference)**.

**Bull**: real yields roll over (DFII10 back toward ~2.6%) → funding eases, multiple re-rates toward peers/own 5y average (EV/EBITDA 7.5x → ~10x). **Bear**: real yields extend higher → developer IRR/order risk plus further discount-rate drag on an already-cheap multiple. **Tilt**: moderate toward bear near-term — the yield move is large and fresh and not yet stabilizing, even though much is already priced.
