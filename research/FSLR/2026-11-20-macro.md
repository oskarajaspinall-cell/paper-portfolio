# Macro overlay: First Solar (FSLR) — 2026-11-20
## Assessment
**Weight: secondary, and rates and risk appetite are the only macro links.** Policy (tariffs, the manufacturing credit, the trade investigation) is the main driver and is not macro data.
**Chain:** real yields -> developers' project financing cost and the discount rate on long-dated cash flows -> bookings and the multiple. VIX and credit spreads -> equity risk premium -> a beta-1.71 stock [Likely].
**Facts (FRED):** the 10y TIPS yield is 2.88%, +0.53pp in 3 months (https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=DFII10, 2026-11-18). The 10y Treasury is 5.07%, +0.42pp in 3 months (https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=DGS10, 2026-11-18). VIX is 28.44, +11.1 in 1 month (https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=VIXCLS, 2026-11-19). The high-yield spread is 3.52%, +0.77pp in 3 months (https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=BAMLH0A0HYM2, 2026-11-19).
**Pricing (proxy):** the 2y yield is 4.80% against fed funds at 3.88% (DGS2, DFF, 2026-11-18). The market is pricing no cuts and some risk of hikes **(inference)**. The stock is already -31.7% over 3 months against SPY -6.9% (fact sheet), at 8.6x P/E, so much of the rate and risk-premium rise looks priced. Because it fell about 5x as far as the market, most of the fall is not explained by macro **(inference)**.
**Surprise direction:** a further real-yield rise or a spread blowout hurts bookings and the multiple. A VIX normalisation or falling real yields helps. Breakevens fell (2.16%, -0.18pp), so the rise is real-rate, not inflation-driven [Likely].
**Country risk:** US is the primary market. Plants in India, Malaysia and Vietnam are cost-side, and no foreign revenue share is disclosed [Likely].
