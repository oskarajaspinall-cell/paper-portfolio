# First Solar, Inc. (FSLR) — Core initiation — 2026-10-07
## 1. Business
First Solar designs and manufactures thin-film CdTe solar modules in the US (and Malaysia/Vietnam/India), selling under multi-year contracts mostly to utility-scale project developers and utilities [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/fslr/). US output qualifies for Section 45X advanced-manufacturing production tax credits, which the company monetizes partly via forward sale agreements [Certain] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1274494/000127449426000170/fslr-20260630.htm). Expected 2026 global nameplate capacity is ~25GW [Likely] (source: https://investor.firstsolar.com/).

## 2. Quality
Returns are high and improving: ROIC rose from 8.2% (FY21) to 20.6% TTM, ROCE to 16.0% TTM, with gross margin at 44.0% TTM (+15.7pp over five years) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/fslr/financials/ratios/, https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/fslr/financials/income-statement/). The moat is CdTe thin-film technology that sits outside crystalline-silicon anti-dumping/countervailing duty regimes, combined with 100%-US-eligible 45X credits that structurally favor domestic capacity over Chinese-linked peers (CSIQ, JKS) [Likely] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1274494/000127449426000170/fslr-20260630.htm). Net cash position (net debt/EBITDA -0.64x TTM) and Altman Z of 6.65/Piotroski F of 7 indicate low distress risk [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/fslr/statistics/). Key risks to durability: a securities class action filed June 23, 2026 alleging false statements, and active patent litigation on both sides (FSLR suing JA Solar/American Panel Solutions; defending a tracker-patent claim from a third party) that could carry cost or distraction [Certain] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1274494/000127449426000170/fslr-20260630.htm). Section 232/tariff policy on polysilicon stockpiling is an ongoing regulatory variable [Likely] (source: https://www.tipranks.com/news/the-fly/commerce-tfr-a-positive-for-first-solar-t1-energy-and-toyo-says-roth-capital-thefly-news).

## 3. Valuation
At 11.1x P/E and 7.5x EV/EBITDA, FSLR trades below its own five-year low (14.6x / 10.1x) and at a 25-50% discount to peer medians on these earnings-based multiples [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/fslr/financials/ratios/). EV/Sales (3.3x) and P/B (1.9x) look rich versus peers (+197%/+56%), but most peers (CSIQ, JKS, ARRY) run thin or negative margins, so sales/book multiples understate FSLR's profitability gap — P/E and EV/EBITDA are the more comparable reads here [Likely]. The depressed multiple implies the market is pricing continued tariff/regulatory uncertainty and a margin step-down that has not yet shown up in trailing results; TTM gross margin (44.0%) and FCF margin (27.9%) are both near five-year highs [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/fslr/financials/ratios/). This is a valuation gap between current fundamentals and a de-rated price, not a growth re-rating story.

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Gross margin (income statement) falls below 35% (vs. 44.0% TTM)
- ROIC (ratios page) falls below 15% (vs. 20.6% TTM)
- FCF margin (cash flow + income statement) turns negative again (vs. +27.9% TTM)
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) rises above 1.0x net leverage (vs. -0.64x net cash TTM)

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
FCF margin flipped from -7.3% (FY24) to +22.7% (FY25) and +27.9% TTM, with FCF conversion (FCF/NI) at 85.9% TTM, aided by 45X credit monetization: $117.6m received in H1 2026 and ~$2.15bn of future credits already sold forward under three agreements [Certain] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1274494/000127449426000170/fslr-20260630.htm). Shares outstanding are essentially flat (+0.12% YoY), so there is no dilution drag [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/fslr/statistics/).

## 6. Unusual items
Short interest is elevated at 10.35% of float and rose 11.6% month-on-month, indicating active bearish positioning tied to tariff/trade-policy headlines [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/fslr/statistics/). A securities class action (filed June 23, 2026) and two active patent suits are live legal overhangs; neither has a quantified liability yet [Certain] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1274494/000127449426000170/fslr-20260630.htm).
