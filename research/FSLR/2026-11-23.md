# First Solar (FSLR) — Core initiation — 2026-11-23

## 1. Business
First Solar makes cadmium-telluride thin-film PV modules, sold mainly to US utility-scale developers and independent power producers under multi-year contracts. Profit leans on the US advanced manufacturing production credit and on tariffs and tax-credit rules that favour US-made modules. Manufacturing spans the US, India, Malaysia and Vietnam; the US is the primary market (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1274494/000127449426000212/fslr-20260930.htm) [Certain]. No foreign country is stated as ~10%+ of revenue in the text I read [Guessing].

## 2. Quality
Returns are high and rising on five-year endpoints: ROIC 19.8% TTM vs 8.2% in FY2021, operating margin 37.7% TTM (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/fslr/financials/ratios/) [Certain]. Net cash (net debt/EBITDA -0.52x), Altman Z 6.61 [Certain]. The moat is policy-made: a non-Chinese supply chain, differentiated thin-film technology and production credits [Likely]. Q3 sales fell 22.8% as module volume fell 22.0% and customer contracts were terminated; gross margin rose to 57.9% [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1274494/000127449426000212/fslr-20260930.htm).

Direction change: FCF margin fell from 12.2% to 4.6% in twelve months. Nine-month operating cash flow was negative on a build in government grants receivable, inventories and trade receivables (10-Q above) [Certain]. Earnings are not yet cash; collection of the credit receivable is the test [Likely].

## 3. Valuation
P/E 8.4x and EV/EBITDA 5.8x are both below their 5-year minimums (14.6x, 10.1x) and 65% and 56% below peer medians (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/fslr/statistics/) [Certain]. But P/FCF is 67.9x and FCF yield 1.47% [Certain], so cheapness rests on credit-boosted earnings. Mechanical fair value (EV/EBITDA only; the DCF failed on negative FCF) is bear 148.49, base 173.44, bull 303.53 against a last close of 137.59 [Likely]. The close is under the bear case: the market prices a policy or bookings cliff [Likely].

Trend is broken: 22.6% below the 50-day average, 34.7% below the 200-day, 3-month momentum -35.7%, RSI 33.0 [Certain].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Gross margin (statistics page, TTM) falls below 40%.
- FCF margin (cash flow page, TTM) is below 0%, i.e. credit receivables are not converting to cash.
- ROIC (ratios page, TTM) falls below 15%.
- Quarterly net sales (income statement page) fall below $1.0bn with further customer contract terminations disclosed in the 10-Q.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Capex is heavy: South Carolina phase one started production in September 2026 [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1274494/000127449426000212/fslr-20260930.htm). Debt is only India working-capital lines. Shares rose 0.15% YoY despite a buyback announced on 2026-10-29 (https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20261029415483/en/First-Solar-Inc.-Announces-Third-Quarter-2026-Financial-Results-Updates-Guidance-and-Announces-500-Million-Share-Repurchase-Program/) [Certain]. FCF/NI is 12.5% TTM vs 77.7% in FY2025 [Certain].

## 6. Unusual items
- Why the price fell: Q3 results on 2026-10-29 came with updated guidance; the call cited contracting discipline and Section 232 uncertainty moderating bookings (https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/fslr/transcripts/720237-q3-2026/) [Likely]. The stock rallied after the 2026-11-04 elections, then reversed (https://www.barrons.com/articles/first-solar-stock-price-9bf61d5f) [Likely].
- Policy: the 10-Q shows IEEPA tariffs ruled unlawful and replaced by other tariff actions; Section 232 is cited as likely to lift US module prices (10-Q above) [Certain]. Customer-loss and tax-incentive risk-factor paragraphs were removed from the filing, though the risks remain in the business [Guessing].
- Short interest 11.04% of float; beta 1.71 [Certain].
- Patent suit against JinkoSolar and a Series 7 warranty liability (10-Q above) [Certain].
- CEO testified on trade enforcement on 2026-11-20 (https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20261120395166/en/First-Solar-CEO-Urges-Bipartisan-Support-for-Industrial-Strategy-Enforcement-of-US-Trade-and-Tax-Policy-at-House-Select-Committee-on-China-Hearing/) [Certain].
