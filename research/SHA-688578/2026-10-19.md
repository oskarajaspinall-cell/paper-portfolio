# Shanghai Allist Pharmaceuticals (SHA:688578) — Core initiation — 2026-10-19

## 1. Business
Allist is a China-based, Shanghai STAR Market-listed biopharmaceutical company (Healthcare/Biotechnology) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sha/688578/). It earns revenue from sales of patented prescription drugs in China; TTM gross margin of 95.7% and net margin of 43.7% point to a branded, patent-protected product mix rather than generics [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sha/688578/financials/ratios/). Product-level segment mix and pipeline detail are not published by stockanalysis.com [data unavailable].

## 2. Quality
Returns have improved sharply: ROIC rose from -13.9% (FY21) to 76.0% (FY25, TTM 52.1%) and ROCE from -2.3% to 32.4% (TTM 31.4%), while gross margin stayed between 94.0% and 96.8% across FY21-25 [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sha/688578/financials/ratios/). A gross margin this high and stable, alongside rising capital returns, is consistent with patent-protected, single-source drugs where rivals cannot easily undercut price, not a commoditised generics business [Likely]. The durability of that edge depends on remaining patent life and reimbursement approval in China, neither published by stockanalysis.com [data unavailable]; China's volume-based procurement system is a sector risk to branded-drug pricing that could apply to Allist as to any China pharma [Guessing], best tracked via the margin trend below. FCF conversion is strong and rising (97.6% FY25, 95.1% TTM, from -891.7%/-40.0% in FY21-22) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sha/688578/financials/cash-flow-statement/), evidence margin is converting to real cash.

## 3. Valuation
At 81.79 CNY the stock trades on 13.8x TTM P/E, 12.0x EV/EBITDA, 5.3x EV/Sales and 14.4x P/FCF — all below Allist's own 5-year range minimum (18.9x / 15.8x / 6.7x / 20.8x respectively) and far below the peer median (75.5x / 49.9x / 8.0x / 32.4x; peer group Hengrui, Innovent, Junshi and Zai Lab, see Sources) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sha/688578/). P/B at 4.3x sits 41% through its 5y range and close to the peer median (-5%) [Certain]. The multiple gap implies the market is pricing in either a sharp near-term earnings reset or a liquidity/ownership discount typical of thinly-held China A-shares (institutional ownership only 16.1%) [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sha/688578/statistics/). The model's own base fair value of 123.66 CNY (+51.2% vs last close) rests on a single method (EV/Revenue; rNPV unavailable as pipeline probabilities aren't published) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sha/688578/), so it is a sanity check on the multiple gap, not a target in itself.

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Gross margin (ratios page) falls below 90% for two consecutive quarters — would signal pricing pressure eroding the patent-protected margin this thesis depends on.
- TTM ROIC (ratios page) falls below 30% — would show the FY23-25 step-up in returns was not durable.
- TTM revenue growth (income statement) turns negative — would indicate the core drug franchise is losing share rather than just decelerating off a high base.
- FCF conversion, FCF/NI (cash-flow statement, income statement) falls below 50% TTM — would show margin gains are not converting to cash.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Allist holds net cash (net debt/EBITDA of -1.52x FY25, -1.56x TTM) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sha/688578/financials/ratios/). FCF conversion is high and improving (97.6% FY25, 95.1% TTM) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sha/688578/financials/cash-flow-statement/), so earnings growth has been largely self-funded rather than reliant on financing. Shares outstanding rose only 0.34% YoY [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sha/688578/statistics/), i.e. minimal dilution. Dividend policy is not shown on the fact sheet pages used [data unavailable]. The combination of no leverage and strong cash conversion gives flexibility to fund R&D and commercialisation without external capital [Likely].
