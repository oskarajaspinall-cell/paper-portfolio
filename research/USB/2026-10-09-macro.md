# Macro overlay: U.S. Bancorp (USB) — 2026-10-09
## Assessment
USB is a rate-sensitive regional bank, so macro is `primary`: **rates → net interest margin and cost of equity → earnings and fair-value multiple**. [Certain] The 10y Treasury yield rose to 5.28% on 2026-10-07, +0.72pp/3m, driven mostly by the *real* yield (DFII10 2.92%, +0.61pp/3m) rather than inflation expectations (T10YIE 2.35%, -0.02pp/1m) (fred.stlouisfed.org, 2026-10-07/08) [Certain]. Fed funds rose to 3.88% (+0.26pp/3m) while the 2y yield (4.77%, +0.56pp) sits ~0.9pp above it — a proxy that the market prices further tightening, not cuts **(inference)**. This cuts both ways: higher short rates raise deposit costs faster than loans reprice (near-term NIM pressure) [Likely], but the positive 2s10s slope (~0.5pp) aids term-transformation over time [Likely]. Mechanically, the fact sheet's fair value uses the 10y as its CAPM risk-free rate (5.28%); a further back-up in real yields lowers the FCFE fair value directly, while USB's P/B (1.5x, 87th percentile of its own 5y range) already carries a premium against a compressing ROE, leaving little room to absorb a higher discount rate [Likely]. HY credit spreads (3.09%, +0.39pp/3m) widen modestly but remain historically tight — not yet a funding-stress signal. **Bull**: rates stabilize/ease with growth intact → NIM holds, fair value supported. **Bear**: real yields keep rising → funding squeeze plus a higher discount rate compress earnings and multiple, widening the existing downside skew (bear $39.73 vs bull $61.22). **Tilt**: toward bear, moderate — the discount-rate input has already moved materially against a stock priced near the top of its own P/B range.
