# U.S. Bancorp (USB) — Core initiation — 2026-10-09

## 1. Business
U.S. Bancorp is a top-five U.S. commercial bank, earning net interest income from deposit-funded consumer, commercial and CRE lending, plus fee income from payments processing (Elavon merchant acquiring), corporate trust, wealth management and capital markets (BTIG) [Certain]. It also recently piloted USBDC, a proprietary USD-backed stablecoin for payments settlement [Certain] (source: https://www.reuters.com/legal/transactional/us-bancorp-executes-first-live-transaction-using-dollar-backed-usbdc-stablecoin-2026-09-09/).

## 2. Quality
ROE has fallen from 14.6% (FY2021) to a 10.2%-12.6% range (FY2023-TTM), a 2.5pp five-year decline [Certain], reflecting higher post-2023 capital requirements for large regional banks and margin pressure rather than a one-off. Net margin compressed similarly, from 33.3% to 29.9% TTM, a 4.6pp five-year fall [Certain]. The Piotroski F-score of 4/9 signals mediocre balance-sheet/earnings quality [Certain]. USB's scale in payments (Elavon) and corporate trust gives fee diversification some peers lack, but core banking returns are not clearly defensible against PNC, TFC, WFC and COF: USB's P/B of 1.5x sits 10% above the four-peer median of 1.3x [Certain] even as its ROE has converged toward peer levels [Likely]. No durable moat is evident beyond scale and deposit-franchise stickiness; returns remain cyclical and rate-sensitive rather than structurally protected from competition.

## 3. Valuation
At 11.4x P/E, USB sits near the bottom of its own 5-year range (18th percentile; 11.0x-13.3x) and 4% below the peer median of 11.8x [Certain]. On P/B — the more relevant metric for a bank — USB trades at 1.5x, near the top of its 5-year range (87th percentile; 1.2x-1.5x) and 10% above the peer median of 1.3x [Certain], implying the market still pays a premium for USB's historically higher ROE even as that ROE has compressed toward peer levels. The fact sheet's own-history fair value (blended P/B+ROE and FCFE DCF) puts base case at $51.84, 9.1% below the $57.03 last close, with bear $39.73 (-30.3%) against bull only $61.22 (+7.3%) [Certain] — a downside-skewed risk/reward. The reverse DCF shows the price implies just -2.0% year-1 revenue growth fading to terminal: the market isn't pricing aggressive growth, but the asymmetry argues against paying up here.

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROE (ratios page) sustains above 13% for two consecutive quarters, reversing the five-year compression from 14.6% (FY2021) toward 10.2%-12.6% (FY2023-TTM).
- Net margin (income statement) resumes a multi-quarter uptrend back toward the 33% FY2021 level rather than continuing its 4.6pp five-year decline.
- Piotroski F-score (statistics page) falls below 4, signaling further balance-sheet/earnings quality deterioration from an already-mediocre level.
- Shares outstanding (statistics page) stop shrinking (currently -0.35% YoY), indicating buybacks have been curtailed — a sign management sees less value in the stock, or capital is being redirected to defend capital ratios.
