# Macro overlay: Salesforce, Inc. (CRM) — 2026-10-08
## Assessment
CRM is a tactical pullback-in-uptrend position, not a classic long-duration growth name (P/E 20.8x, FCF yield 8.2%), but still a software multiple sensitive to the same discount-rate factor as peers. **Chain**: real yields → discount rate → equity multiple for software/growth names. **Facts** (FRED, 2026-10-06/07): the 10y real yield (DFII10) rose +0.48pp (~1m), +0.61pp (~3m) to 2.91%; the 10y nominal yield (DGS10) rose +0.49pp (~1m) to 5.27%; breakeven inflation (T10YIE) was flat (+0.01pp, 1m) at 2.36%. Almost all of the nominal-yield rise is a *real*-yield move, not an inflation surprise — the textbook channel for multiple compression. High-yield spreads (BAMLH0A0HYM2) widened +0.35pp to 3.03%, a mild risk-off signal. **Market pricing (proxy, inference)**: the 2y yield (4.79%) sits ~0.9pp above fed funds (3.88%), consistent with the market pricing further tightening [Likely] **(inference)**. Despite the yield rise, software stocks (CRM +32.5% 3m) have re-rated higher on an AI-fears-fading narrative (Reuters, 2026-10-06, commentary) — the market is currently pricing sector AI optimism *over* the discount-rate drag. That decoupling is the risk: a continued real-yield rise without fresh AI-narrative support would hit software multiples broadly, CRM included, regardless of fundamentals. **Bull**: yields stabilize → multiple re-expansion continues, target hit faster. **Bear**: yields keep climbing (hot core CPI) → sector-wide compression, pressuring CRM toward its stop before the 3-month window. **Tilt**: toward bear, small — CRM's below-peer valuation cushions it, and the horizon is short.
