# Salesforce (CRM) — Core initiation — 2026-11-17
## 1. Business
Subscription CRM and enterprise software: Agentforce apps (Sales, Service, Marketing, Commerce, Slack) plus the data platform and Informatica, sold mainly on recurring contracts. Subscription and support was ~95% of revenue in the July 2026 quarter [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1108524/000110852426000190/crm-20260731.htm). Europe was ~24% [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1108524/000110852426000190/crm-20260731.htm) and Asia Pacific ~10% [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1108524/000110852426000190/crm-20260731.htm) by contracting-entity region; the filing states no single non-US country exceeds 10% [company-reported]. No emerging-market concentration. [Certain]

## 2. Quality
A good business with a sticky installed base and switching costs (data, workflows, integrations). [Likely] Margins have expanded materially: operating margin 21.5% TTM vs 2.1% in FY2022, FCF margin 34.5%, gross margin 77.3% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/crm/financials/ratios/). Returns are only moderate: ROIC 11.0%, ROCE 11.4%, partly diluted by acquisitions (Informatica). Direction change: ROIC rose over five fiscal years but fell over the last 12 months (12.2% to 11.0%, https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/crm/financials/ratios/?p=trailing), while operating margin (+0.3pp) and FCF margin (+2.9pp) held or rose. So the dip in ROIC reflects a larger capital base post-deal, not margin erosion. [Likely] Leverage rose: net debt/EBITDA 2.40x TTM from -0.18x in FY2025, the funding of the buyback and Informatica. The moat against AI-native agents and Microsoft is the key debate. [Guessing]

## 3. Valuation
At the 2026-11-16 close of 214.83, P/E is 18.0x, EV/EBITDA 14.8x and P/FCF 10.5x, each below its own five-year minimum, and 21-41% below peers (NOW, ADBE, ORCL, MSFT, WDAY). FCF yield is 9.50% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/crm/statistics/). Mechanical base fair value is 248.79 (+15.8%); bear 90.51, bull 297.34, with a wide DCF vs EV/Revenue gap (197.65 vs 351.07), so treat it as imprecise. The reverse DCF implies 8.4% year-1 revenue growth fading to terminal. The price embeds substantial AI-disruption risk to seat-based software. Five-year range minimums reflect a period of inflated GAAP earnings; the discount to history is partly structural de-rating. [Likely] The stock fell sharply today after the 12-month change of -20.2%.

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Operating margin (income statement, TTM) falls below 19%.
- ROIC (ratios page, TTM) falls below 9% for two consecutive quarters, i.e. acquisitions failing to earn their cost.
- FCF margin (cash flow statement, TTM) falls below 30%.
- Net debt / EBITDA (ratios page) rises above 3.0x.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Shares outstanding fell 7.44% YoY (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/crm/statistics/), driven by a large accelerated buyback that started in March 2026 [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1108524/000110852426000190/crm-20260731.htm). Cash conversion is strong but FCF/NI fell to 156.8% TTM as net income was lifted by investment gains [Likely].
