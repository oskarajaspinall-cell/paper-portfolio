# Atour Lifestyle Holdings (ATAT) — Core initiation — 2026-11-07
## 1. Business
Atour is a China-based mid-to-upscale "lifestyle" hotel group (Lodging). It earns fees and related revenue from a hotel network largely run on a manachised (asset-light) model, plus a growing retail arm selling hotel-branded products. The latest headlines describe retail growing faster than hotels, with retail guidance raised [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/atat/transcripts/667938-q2-2026/). A reported split by segment is [data unavailable]: the company site was blocked.

## 2. Quality
Returns are high and have risen: ROCE 56.9% TTM vs 16.4% in FY2021, ROE 59.1% TTM (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/atat/financials/ratios/) [Certain]. Operating margin 24.3% TTM vs 9.1% in FY2021, gross margin 43.7% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/atat/financials/income-statement/) [Certain]. Net cash: net debt/EBITDA -1.52x; Altman Z 7.39 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/atat/statistics/) [Certain]. FCF margin 19.7% TTM, FCF/net income 117.2%, so profits are cash-backed, though conversion has fallen from 264.1% in FY2023 as the business matured [Certain]. Piotroski F is a middling 5 [Certain].
Moat: an asset-light brand and franchisee network, scale and a retail channel into the guest base. The durability is [Likely], not proven: China mid-scale lodging has many competitors and franchisee economics can be competed away. Margins held through FY2025 and TTM, which is supportive [Likely].

## 3. Valuation
P/E 17.2x vs a 5y range of 21.2x–165.2x, below the minimum; EV/EBITDA 10.0x vs a 14.0x–68.8x range, below the minimum; P/FCF 14.3x is 10% up its range. Against peers (HTHT, MAR, HLT, TCOM), P/E is 46% lower and EV/EBITDA 49% lower; P/B of 9.8x is above peers (n=2) but reflects ROE near 59% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/atat/statistics/) [Certain]. FCF yield is 6.99% [Certain].
Reverse DCF: the price implies 8.2% year-1 revenue growth, fading to terminal. Fair value base is 43.29 USD vs a 35.91 close (+20.6%); bear 16.10 and bull 101.22 show a wide range (fact sheet Fair value section; inform-only) [Likely]. The market prices in a sharp slowdown and a China discount; the stock has fallen 10.9% in 12 months [Likely].
