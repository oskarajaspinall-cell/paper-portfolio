# Macro overlay: Novo Nordisk A/S (NOVO.B) — 2026-10-18
## Assessment
Novo reports in DKK but earns most GLP-1/obesity revenue in the US (USD) [Likely] **(inference, general industry knowledge — not a stockanalysis.com figure)**. A stronger dollar lifts DKK-translated US revenue/margin; a weaker dollar drags it. This is secondary, not the thesis: the ~-29% 12-month share fall and sub-10x P/E reflect a company-specific re-rating (Eli Lilly's oral GLP-1 pill winning EU committee backing 2026-10-16, Reuters; falling FCF conversion), not a macro repricing. Novo's 5y beta is 0.38 [Certain] (stockanalysis.com statistics page), so risk-premium/discount-rate transmission from real yields is structurally weak here.

Facts: broad dollar index 121.602 on 2026-10-09, +3.72 (~1m) [Certain] (FRED DTWEXBGS). US 10y real yield 2.78% on 2026-10-15, +0.43pp (~3m) [Certain] (FRED DFII10). 2y Treasury (4.77%) sits 0.89pp above fed funds (3.88%) [Certain] (FRED DGS2, DFF) — a proxy that near-term rate cuts aren't priced [Likely] **(inference, proxy)**. Net: recent dollar strength is a mild earnings-translation tailwind; the real-yield rise is a mild discount-rate headwind to the DCF fair value; both are small next to the competitive-share story.

**Bull**: dollar firm / real yields stable → modest DKK revenue/EPS translation lift, no multiple effect given low beta.
**Bear**: dollar reverses lower and/or real yields keep rising → FX drag compounds weak FCF conversion and trims the DCF fair value, reinforcing the "value trap" bear case.
**Tilt**: neutral — channels roughly offset, small versus the dominant non-macro competitive thesis.
