# Novo Nordisk A/S (CPH:NOVO.B) — Core initiation — 2026-10-18
## 1. Business
Novo Nordisk is a Danish pharmaceutical manufacturer whose revenue is concentrated in GLP-1 diabetes and obesity therapies (Ozempic, Rybelsus, Wegovy), plus a smaller rare-disease franchise (haemophilia, growth hormone) [Certain]. It earns through patented branded drugs sold globally via payers/distributors, with pricing power from clinical differentiation and scarce GLP-1 manufacturing capacity [Likely].

## 2. Quality
TTM ROIC is 39.2%, ROE 59.8%, ROCE 45.4% — down sharply from FY2021 (68.0% / 71.2% / 62.3%) but still exceptional for any industry [Certain] (RA). Operating margin has actually risen to 49.0% TTM from 42.1% in FY2021, and gross margin is stable near 82% [Certain] (IS) — the decline in capital returns looks more like a heavier asset base (capacity build-out) diluting returns than margin erosion [Likely]. Net debt/EBITDA moved from net cash to 0.55x TTM, still low [Certain] (BS,IS). The moat is narrowing: Eli Lilly's oral GLP-1 pill won EU regulatory-committee backing on 2026-10-16, becoming "the second oral weight-loss drug" after Novo's own, directly contesting Wegovy/Rybelsus (source: https://www.reuters.com/legal/litigation/eli-lillys-weight-loss-pill-wins-backing-europe-2026-10-16/) [Likely]. The scarcity-pricing phase of the GLP-1 duopoly looks to be ending in favour of genuine two-player competition [Likely].

## 3. Valuation
P/E of 9.9x sits below Novo's own 5y range (14.1x–38.2x) and 69% under the peer median of 31.8x (LLY, AZN, SAN, JNJ); EV/EBITDA (7.1x) and P/FCF (15.1x) show the same pattern against both history and peers [Certain] (RA, peer statistics pages). The DCF-based base fair value of 515.35 DKK (+98.5% vs the 259.60 DKK close) implies the price assumes roughly the reverse-DCF 7.4% year-1 growth fading quickly to terminal, below Novo's own trailing trend; even the bear case (278.08 DKK) sits above the last close [Certain] (fact sheet fair value section). The market is pricing a structural de-rating — share loss to Lilly and weaker cash conversion — rather than a cyclical dip [Likely]; at under 10x TTM earnings with still-elite margins, meaningful further competitive erosion already looks priced in [Likely].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- FCF conversion (FCF/NI, cash-flow/ratios pages) falls below 50% for two consecutive fiscal years, from 65.1% TTM, showing capex structurally outrunning earnings quality.
- Gross margin (income statement) falls below 78%, from 82.0% TTM, showing pricing/mix damage from oral GLP-1 competition.
- Net debt/EBITDA (balance sheet + income statement) rises above 1.5x, from 0.55x TTM, showing the capacity build-out is being funded beyond plan with debt.
- Operating margin (income statement) falls below 40% for two consecutive quarters, from 49.0% TTM, showing competitive pricing pressure is hitting profitability, not just capital efficiency.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
FCF margin has fallen to 23.0% TTM from 34.6% in FY2021, and FCF conversion (FCF/NI) to 65.1% from 101.9%, driven by rising capex to expand GLP-1 manufacturing capacity rather than margin loss, since operating margin is up over the same period [Likely] (CF,IS). Net debt/EBITDA has moved from net cash to 0.55x — still conservative — funding that expansion [Certain] (BS,IS). Shares outstanding fell 0.14% YoY, consistent with buybacks/cancellations rather than dilution [Certain] (ST).

## 6. Unusual items
A products-liability suit alleges Ozempic caused vision loss (NAION); the fact sheet shows no quantified financial exposure [Guessing] (source: https://www.foxnews.com/health/man-claims-went-blind-after-taking-ozempic-shares-warning-other-users). Eli Lilly's oral GLP-1 pill won EU committee backing on 2026-10-16, intensifying direct obesity-market competition [Likely] (source: https://www.reuters.com/legal/litigation/eli-lillys-weight-loss-pill-wins-backing-europe-2026-10-16/). Institutional ownership is modest at 27.7% and insider ownership near zero at 0.02%; short interest is [data unavailable] [Certain] (ST).
