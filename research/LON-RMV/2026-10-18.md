# Rightmove plc (LON:RMV) — Core initiation — 2026-10-18

## 1. Business
Rightmove operates the UK's largest residential property portal by traffic and agent listings, classified by stockanalysis.com as Internet Content & Information within Communication Services [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/lon/RMV/). It monetises agent and new-homes-developer demand for buyer/renter leads, which shows up as near-100% gross margin and no inventory or receivables-heavy model (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/lon/RMV/financials/income-statement/) [Certain].

## 2. Quality
Returns are exceptional: TTM ROIC 497.4%, ROCE 379.6%, ROE 282.4%, reflecting a capital-light marketplace with net cash (net debt/EBITDA -0.10x) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/lon/RMV/financials/ratios/). The moat is a two-sided network effect in a UK portal market long a duopoly with Zoopla [Likely]. That moat is being tested: operating margin fell from 73.4% (FY2021) to 66.7% (TTM) and net margin from 60.1% to 49.7%, a steady five-year drift, not a one-off [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/lon/RMV/financials/income-statement/). CoStar Group (peer CSGP) acquired OnTheMarket, "the operator of onthemarket.com, a U.K. residential property portal," in December 2023, run within its Residential Real Estate segment; its FY2025 10-K gives no explicit competitive strategy versus Rightmove, but a well-capitalised third challenger threatens the duopoly's pricing power over the thesis horizon [Likely] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1057352/000105735226000020/csgp-20251231.htm). Altman Z of 63.24 and Piotroski F of 4 show no distress risk [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/lon/RMV/statistics/).

## 3. Valuation
TTM multiples sit at or below their own five-year lows: P/E 18.9x (5y range 18.2x–36.7x, 4th percentile), EV/EBITDA 13.1x (below the 13.5x minimum), P/FCF 16.4x (below the 16.8x minimum) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/lon/RMV/financials/ratios/). Against peers (AUTO, CSGP, REA, G24), it trades 27–32% below on P/E, EV/EBITDA and P/FCF, and only 11% above on EV/Sales; P/B (+600% vs peers) is not meaningful given the near-zero capital base [Certain] (source: fact sheet valuation table). The stockanalysis.com DCF and EV/Revenue models could not be computed here, a missing FX rate for GBP, so no fair-value cross-check is available this cycle [Certain] — a data gap, not a view. The 541.40p close is 22% below the 693.80p 52-week high and includes an unconfirmed takeover-rumour jump (see Unusual items); excluding that, the stock still reads cheap versus its own history and peers on earnings/cash multiples.

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Operating margin (income statement page) falls below 60%, versus 66.7% TTM and a five-year high of 73.4% — would show the duopoly's pricing power eroding structurally, not cyclically.
- ROIC (ratios page) falls below 400% for two consecutive fiscal years, versus 497.4% TTM — capital returns compressing on a sustained basis.
- FCF conversion, FCF/NI (cash flow and income statement pages), falls below 90%, versus 109.1% TTM — cash quality deteriorating.
- Shares outstanding (statistics page) stop shrinking and start rising, versus -2.63% YoY now — buyback discipline abandoned.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
FCF conversion has stayed above 100% in every one of the last five fiscal years (106.1%–109.1%) and sits at 109.1% TTM, so reported earnings are fully backed by cash [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/lon/RMV/financials/cash-flow-statement/). The balance sheet carries net cash (net debt/EBITDA -0.10x to -0.16x across the period), and shares outstanding fell 2.63% year-on-year, consistent with an active buyback returning surplus cash rather than funding growth with debt [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/lon/RMV/financials/balance-sheet/, https://stockanalysis.com/quote/lon/RMV/statistics/).
