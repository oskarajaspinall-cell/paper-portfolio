# Rightmove plc (RMV) — Core initiation — 2026-10-19
## 1. Business
Rightmove runs the UK's largest online property portal: home-buyers and renters browse free, while estate agents, lettings agents and new-home developers pay subscription/membership and advertising fees for listings and data products [Certain]. The model is agent-funded, not transactional, which is why gross margin is 100% (source: income statement).

## 2. Quality
TTM ROIC 497.4%, ROCE 379.6%, ROE 282.4% (ratios page) [Certain] — an exceptional return profile from a two-sided network-effect marketplace with near-zero marginal cost per listing (100% gross margin) [Likely]. Returns look durable: agents need to be where buyer traffic already is, and buyers go where agent inventory is deepest, a reinforcing moat [Likely]. That said, operating margin has fallen 5.7pp over five years (73.4%→66.7%) and net margin 9.0pp (60.1%→49.7%, income statement) [Certain], and ROIC itself has drifted down 35.3pp even as ROCE (+48.7pp) and ROE (+77.0pp) rose (ratios page) [Certain] — consistent with a growing capital/cost base rather than lost pricing power, but worth tracking. FCF conversion stayed above 100% of net income in every one of the last five years (cash flow/income statement) [Certain], confirming earnings quality. Piotroski F-score is 4/9 (statistics page) [Certain] — middling for a business this profitable, a modest watch item rather than a red flag.

## 3. Valuation
At 541.40 GBX, RMV trades on 18.9x P/E, 13.1x EV/EBITDA and 16.4x P/FCF (ratios/statistics pages) [Certain] — P/E sits at the 4th percentile of its own 5-year range (18.2x–36.7x), and EV/EBITDA and P/FCF are both below their 5-year minimums [Certain]. Versus peers (LON:AUTO, ASX:REA, ETR:G24, CSGP), RMV trades 27-32% cheaper on P/E, EV/EBITDA and P/FCF, though 11% dearer on EV/Sales [Certain]. P/B of 53.7x looks extreme next to the 7.7x peer median, but is not a useful cross-check for an asset-light, high-ROE balance sheet [Likely]; FCF yield is 6.09% (statistics page) [Certain]. Shares are down 20.7% over 12 months (statistics page) [Certain] even as H1 2026 revenue grew 7% (stockanalysis.com news/transcript listing) [Likely] — price has de-rated faster than the business, the case for entry. stockanalysis.com's own DCF/EV-Revenue fair-value models could not be computed (no GBP FX rate), so no independent fair-value check is available [data unavailable].
