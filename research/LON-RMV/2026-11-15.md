# Rightmove plc (LON:RMV) — Core initiation — 2026-11-15
## 1. Business
UK's dominant online property portal. It earns subscription fees from estate agents and new-homes developers (revenue per advertiser times advertiser count), plus smaller other services. Revenue is essentially all UK [Likely]; no foreign market is near 10% on the evidence available (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/lon/RMV/). Growth comes from price rises and product upsell, not volume [Likely].

## 2. Quality
Very high quality [Certain]: gross margin 100.0%, operating margin 66.7% TTM, FCF margin 54.3%, FCF/net income 109.1%, net cash (net debt/EBITDA -0.10x) (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/lon/RMV/financials/ratios/). ROIC 497.4% TTM is inflated by a tiny capital base, so read it as "asset-light", not literally.
Moat: network effect, as agents must list where buyers are [Likely].
Direction: 5-year margins have drifted down (operating margin -5.7pp, FCF margin -8.4pp), but the last 12 months are stable (operating margin -1.5pp, FCF margin +0.4pp, ROIC +30.3pp) (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/lon/RMV/financials/income-statement/?p=trailing). The fall is modest and likely reflects reinvestment (AI product spend) [Guessing]. Piotroski F is only 4 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/lon/RMV/statistics/).

## 3. Valuation
P/E 20.4x sits at 12% of its 5y range (18.2x–36.7x), EV/EBITDA 14.1x at 4%, P/FCF 17.7x at 5%, FCF yield 5.66%. P/E is 20% below the peer median 25.4x (Auto Trader, REA, Zillow, Scout24); EV/EBITDA is 30% below (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/lon/RMV/statistics/) [Certain]. EV/Sales is 13% above peers because Rightmove's margins are far higher [Likely].
Fair value: [data unavailable] (no GBP FX rate for the model).
The price is 26.3% above its 200-day average, up 41.8% in six months, with RSI 68.7, yet 12-month change is only +3.3%. The rally has partly priced in bid speculation [Likely]. Even so, the market still values the stock at the cheap end of its own history, so it assumes slow growth.

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Operating margin (income statement, TTM) falls below 62%.
- FCF margin (cash flow statement, TTM) falls below 49%.
- ROIC TTM (ratios page) falls below 350%.
- Company-reported guidance for revenue growth is cut again, or agent/developer advertiser numbers fall in two consecutive reports (text trigger, RNS).

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
FCF conversion 109.1% of net income; net cash; shares down 2.63% YoY, so buybacks are the main return of capital [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/lon/RMV/statistics/). Buybacks shrink equity, which inflates ROE (282.4%) and P/B (57.7x), so ignore those two [Likely]. Good capital discipline, with no M&A risk visible [Likely].
