# Rightmove plc (LON:RMV) — Core initiation — 2026-11-23

## 1. Business
UK online property portal. Revenue is subscriptions from estate agents (average revenue per advertiser times membership) and from new-homes developers, plus smaller services. Reported in GBP, essentially UK-only; no foreign country or emerging-market region is a material share of revenue that the fact sheet shows [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/lon/RMV/). Housebuilding activity is the weak segment, per the 6 November trading update (source: https://www.reuters.com/world/uk/uks-rightmove-sees-2027-revenue-growth-similar-2026-due-homebuilding-slump-2026-11-06/).

## 2. Quality
Exceptional economics, narrow but real moat: the dominant UK portal's audience attracts agents, and agents' listings attract the audience [Likely]. Gross margin 100.0%, operating margin 66.7% TTM, FCF margin 54.3%, ROIC 497.4% TTM, net cash (net debt/EBITDA -0.10x) (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/lon/RMV/financials/ratios/). ROIC is inflated by a tiny capital base; read it as "asset-light", not as a growth rate [Certain].

Direction: operating margin is down from 73.4% (FY2021) to 66.7% TTM, 2.3pp below its 2-year high of 69.0%; FCF margin is 2.3pp below its 2-year high of 56.5%. ROIC falls over five fiscal years but rose over the last 12 months (467.2% to 497.4%). Returns are intact; margin erosion is gradual, consistent with product and AI investment [Guessing]. 2027 revenue growth is guided "similar to 2026" (Reuters headline, no figure used) [Likely].

## 3. Valuation
P/E 18.8x vs a 5y range of 18.2x / 23.1x / 36.7x; EV/EBITDA 12.9x and P/FCF 16.2x are both below their 5y minimums; FCF yield 6.18% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/lon/RMV/statistics/). Versus peers P/E is 10% below the median, EV/EBITDA 17% above; the peer set is loose, so little weight [Likely]. Fair value: [data unavailable] (no GBP FX rate).

Price 533.40 GBX is 15.0% above the 200-day average; 52-week high 612.40 (13 November). The market prices a quality compounder slowed by the housing cycle, at the bottom of its own multiple history [Likely].

Why it fell: the high coincided with a report that activist Sachem Head wants a sale process (https://www.reuters.com/legal/transactional/sachem-head-wants-rightmove-consider-sale-talk-with-potential-buyers-sources-say-2026-11-13/). The slide since is most simply that bid premium fading, on top of the 6 November outlook [Guessing]. Both Reuters articles were blocked.

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Operating margin (income statement page, TTM) falls below 62%.
- ROIC (ratios page, TTM) falls below 300%.
- FCF conversion, FCF/NI (cash flow page), falls below 90%.
- Shares outstanding change YoY (statistics page) turns positive by more than 2%, i.e. buybacks replaced by dilution.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
FCF conversion 109.1% TTM (108.4% FY2025): profit converts fully to cash. Shares outstanding fell 2.63% YoY, so cash goes to buybacks and the balance sheet is net cash [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/lon/RMV/statistics/). The H1 call headline cites substantial capital returns (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/lon/RMV/transcripts/574251-h1-2026/). Piotroski F is 4, a mild blemish driven by margin drift [Likely].

## 6. Unusual items
- Activist: Sachem Head reportedly pressing for a sale, unconfirmed [Likely]. Could add upside or, if it fades, remove support.
- UK housing: the November asking-price report points to a weak market (source: https://www.standard.co.uk/business/money/rightmove-britain-matt-smith-john-d-wood-london-b1304971.html) [Likely].
