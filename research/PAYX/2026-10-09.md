# Paychex, Inc. (PAYX) — Core initiation — 2026-10-09
## 1. Business
Paychex provides outsourced payroll processing, HR administration, benefits and retirement-plan recordkeeping to ~840,000 small/mid-sized US clients (Management Solutions), plus co-employment PEO services and insurance brokerage for clients outsourcing HR entirely (PEO and Insurance Solutions). Revenue is recurring: per-employee, per-payroll-run fees plus float income on client funds held between payroll and tax remittance. The Paycor acquisition adds a cloud HCM platform aimed at the mid-market, funded with debt. [Certain] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/723531/000072353126000006/payx-20260831.htm)

## 2. Quality
TTM ROE is 47.1%, gross margin 74.4%, operating margin 39.9% and FCF margin 30.5%, with FCF conversion (FCF/NI) 111.7% and rising [Certain] (source: fact sheet, RA/IS/CF). The core moat is durable: mandatory payroll-tax compliance embeds Paychex in client workflows, switching costs are high, and 84.6% institutional ownership reflects a stable franchise [Likely]. But ROIC/ROCE have nearly halved over five years (ROIC 55.7%→27.4%, ROCE 42.9%→28.3%), mainly reflecting the debt-funded Paycor deal enlarging the capital base rather than core-business deterioration [Likely] (source: RA). Growth is decelerating: Management Solutions revenue grew only ~4.3% YoY in Q1 FY27, FY27 guidance is just 5-6% revenue growth, and management flagged Management Solutions "trending toward the low end of guidance" on the Q1 call [Likely] (source: https://www.tipranks.com/news/the-fly/paychex-sees-management-solutions-trending-toward-low-end-of-guidance-thefly-news; 10-Q segment data). Net debt/EBITDA swung from net cash through FY2024 to 1.2x TTM, a real increase in integration risk.

## 3. Valuation
P/E 20.7x sits at the 7th percentile of its own 5y range (19.7x-34.3x) and 25% below peer median 27.6x; EV/EBITDA 13.3x is also at the 7th percentile and 29% below peers. P/FCF 18.4x and EV/Sales 6.2x run above peer medians (+13%, +37%), but PAYX's superior margins justify some EV/Sales premium to ADP/PCTY/NSP. P/B 10.0x is 42% below peers, consistent with ROE compressing versus its own history even though it still exceeds peers in absolute terms. Blended fair value (DCF 67% / EV-Revenue 33%) is $109.72, 5.0% above the $104.46 close; bear/bull are -20.7%/+36.4%, an asymmetric skew [Guessing] (model-dependent). The reverse DCF implies ~7.7% year-1 revenue growth priced in, above the 5-6% FY27 guide, so the market is pricing a structurally slower-growing franchise plus leverage/integration risk, not a broken one. (source: fact sheet valuation section)

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Management Solutions segment revenue growth (10-Q segment note) turns negative or stays below ~3% for two consecutive quarters, confirming a structural slowdown in the core payroll franchise.
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) rises above 2.0x with no visible deleveraging path, showing the Paycor deal is consuming more balance-sheet capacity than planned.
- ROIC (ratios page) falls below 20%, showing returns on the combined capital base are deteriorating, not just mix-shifting.
- FCF conversion (FCF/net income, cash-flow/ratios page) drops below 90%, indicating cash-generation quality is weakening alongside reported earnings.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
TTM FCF margin 30.5% and FCF conversion 111.7% underpin continued buybacks (shares outstanding down 0.92% YoY) even as the balance sheet absorbed the Paycor deal: long-term debt reached $4.56bn and cash fell to $600.9m at Q1 FY27-end from $1.09bn five months earlier [Certain] (source: 10-Q). Net debt/EBITDA moved from net cash through FY2024 to 1.2x TTM (source: RA). Cash generation remains strong enough to fund buybacks alongside gradual deleveraging, but the added debt leaves less margin for error on Paycor integration than the pre-acquisition balance sheet did. [Likely]

## 6. Unusual items
Short interest is 6.2% of float (elevated for a low-beta compounder, though down 3.0% month-on-month); beta 0.85 and Altman Z 3.22/Piotroski F 6 show no distress [Certain] (source: fact sheet ST). The stock fell ~8% on the Sep 23 Q1 print after management flagged Management Solutions trending toward the low end of guidance [Certain] (source: https://www.tipranks.com/news/the-fly/paychex-sees-management-solutions-trending-toward-low-end-of-guidance-thefly-news) — the clearest driver of 12-month underperformance vs SPY (-33.4pp).
