Core CPI (CPILFESL) rose +0.49% over 3m to 2026-08-01 (https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=CPILFESL) and the 10y breakeven (T10YIE) ticked up to 2.35% on 2026-10-08 (https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=T10YIE) — mild, lagging inflation pressure. Market pricing (proxy): the 2y Treasury (4.77%, 2026-10-07) sits ~90bp above fed funds (3.88%), implying the market isn't pricing near-term cuts and may expect further tightening **(inference)**. Management's FY27 guide (5-6% revenue, 7-9% EPS, 2026-09-23) predates this month's fed-funds rise, so float income looks like a modest, currently-unpriced tailwind to that guide **(inference)**. The 10y yield (5.28%) matches the risk-free rate already in the fact sheet's DCF, so the discount rate isn't newly moving fair value. Bear: cuts resume, float income fades, compounding the Management Solutions growth-deceleration case. Bull: higher-for-longer rates let float income offset slower core growth. Secondary, not the core thesis (client growth/Paycor integration dominates).
