# Macro overlay: Broadcom Inc. (AVGO) — 2026-10-08
## Assessment
AVGO trades at a growth multiple (P/E 48.1x, EV/EBITDA 34.4x) [Certain] (stockanalysis.com, 2026-10-08) and is, per the research note's newsflow, in talks to raise $50-60bn in private debt for OpenAI/Anthropic custom-chip capacity [Likely] (research/AVGO/2026-10-08.md, citing WSJ 2026-10-07). Two macro variables matter: (1) real yields, the discount rate on a high-multiple growth name, and (2) HY credit spreads, the marginal cost of that debt-funded capex.

**Facts** (FRED, dated): 10y real yield (DFII10) 2.91%, 2026-10-06, +48bp/1m, +61bp/3m; 10y nominal (DGS10) 5.27%, +72bp/3m; 2y (DGS2) 4.79%, +60bp/3m; HY spread (BAMLH0A0HYM2) 3.03%, +36bp/3m; VIX 15.0, -1.1pt/3m (fred.stlouisfed.org, all 2026-10-06).

**Market pricing (proxy, inference)**: 2y yield (4.79%) sits ~91bp above fed funds (3.88%, DFF), implying the market is not pricing near-term cuts — a hawkish repricing versus a cutting path [Likely] **(inference)**. Core CPI (CPILFESL) is lagging (Aug data), not a forward signal.

**Bull**: yields/spreads stabilize — financing for the AI-chip debt deal prices tighter than feared, multiple holds or re-rates toward peers (P/E still -22% vs NVDA/AMD/QCOM/MRVL median).
**Bear**: real yields and HY spreads keep widening together — the debt-funded capex narrative gets scrutinized, high-multiple semis de-rate, raising odds the 2.5x-ATR stop ($350.59) is hit before the $428.35 target.
**Tilt**: the simultaneous 3-month back-up in real yields and HY spreads, landing exactly as AVGO seeks a record debt raise, moderately skews the 8-week tactical setup toward the bear case versus fundamentals alone.
