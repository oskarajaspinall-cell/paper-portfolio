# Broadcom Inc. (AVGO) — Core initiation — 2026-11-16
## 1. Business
Broadcom sells custom AI accelerators (XPUs), AI networking and other semiconductors, plus infrastructure software (VMware). Asia Pacific was 70.8% of fiscal Q3 2026 revenue and Americas 18.6% [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1730168/000173016826000080/avgo-20260802.htm). The filing gives no single-country split [Certain]. Top five end customers were about 55% of Q3 revenue, against about 40% a year earlier [company-reported] (same URL).

## 2. Quality
Quality is high and rising [Certain]. TTM ROIC is 30.7% against 18.1% a year earlier, operating margin is 48.8% and gross margin is 75.5% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/avgo/financials/ratios/?p=trailing). Net debt/EBITDA is 0.68x and Piotroski F is 7 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/avgo/statistics/).

Direction change: FCF margin is falling over five fiscal years (-6.4pp) but rose over the last 12 months, from 41.6% to 44.2% [Certain]. FCF conversion has fallen to 103.0% of net income. The five-year ROIC trend rests on FY2024's trough (11.1%, a post-VMware year), so the rise is partly a recovery [Likely]. Custom XPU design wins and switching costs are a durable moat [Likely]. The risk is concentration. The company says customers are asking to lease XPUs and racks instead of buying them (source: SEC delta, same 10-Q URL) [Certain].

## 3. Valuation
The price is 347.01 USD. P/E is 42.9x, at the 22% position of its 5y range (17.1x–133.8x), and 22% above the peer median of 35.0x. EV/EBITDA is 30.7x, 10% below peers, and P/FCF is 39.9x, 28% below peers. FCF yield is only 2.51% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/avgo/statistics/).

Mechanical fair value is base 108.77, bear 71.57 and bull 266.18, so the price is above even the bull case [Certain]. The reverse DCF implies 40.4% year-1 revenue growth, fading to terminal growth by year 10 [Certain]. The through-cycle DCF probably undervalues an AI super-cycle, but it shows how much growth the price already assumes [Guessing]. The shares are 5.6% below the 200-day average and 3-month momentum is -16.9%, so the trend is not strong.

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Operating margin (income statement, TTM) falls below 40%.
- TTM ROIC (ratios page) falls below 20%.
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) rises above 1.5x.
- Top-five customer share (10-Q, MD&A) rises further, or a top-five customer cuts its AI spending plans, leaving quarterly revenue lower year on year.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
FCF margin is 44.2% TTM, but conversion has slipped to 103.0% of net income. Shares outstanding rose 0.99% over the year, so stock-based pay dilutes slightly (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/avgo/statistics/). The filing describes an "AI XPV" financing platform. Broadcom has guaranteed customer lease obligations to a financial partner, with maximum undiscounted exposure of about $29 billion and no payments made to date [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1730168/000173016826000080/avgo-20260802.htm). That is off-balance-sheet credit risk tied to frontier AI labs [Likely].

## 6. Unusual items
- The 10-Q now says the AI upturn "may not be sustainable" because of a market correction or a perceived AI spending "bubble" [Certain]. It also discloses regulatory inquiries in Korea, Japan and the EU (source: SEC delta, 10-Q URL above).
- Beta is 1.51, so expect large swings. Relative strength against SPY is -13.9pp over 3 months (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/avgo/history/).
- Short interest is low at 1.10% of float.
- Headlines link recent chip-sector swings to a reported Nvidia CEO change (source: https://invezz.com/news/2026/11/16/why-are-amd-and-broadcom-stocks-falling-in-premarket-trading-on-monday/). It is unverified third-party reporting [Guessing].
