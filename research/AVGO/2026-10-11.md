# Broadcom Inc. (AVGO) — Core initiation — 2026-10-11

## 1. Business
Broadcom designs and sells semiconductors — networking/switching chips, custom AI accelerators (XPUs) for hyperscalers, broadband, storage and wireless — mostly through distributors and a handful of large direct customers, plus Infrastructure Software (VMware virtualization, mainframe, cybersecurity) sold on subscription/perpetual licence. [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/avgo/)

## 2. Quality
Returns are high and rising: ROIC 30.7% TTM vs 15.6% FY21, ROCE 26.0%, operating margin 48.8% TTM (+9.6pp over 5y), net margin 42.9% [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/avgo/financials/ratios/, https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/avgo/financials/income-statement/). FCF margin is strong but normalizing: 44.2% TTM, down 6.4pp over 5y, and FCF/NI conversion fell to 103% from 330% in FY2024 [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/avgo/financials/cash-flow-statement/). The moat is real but concentrated: custom-ASIC co-design relationships with a small number of hyperscalers plus VMware's high switching costs. The company's own 10-K discloses the top five end customers account for ~40% of net revenue in both FY2024 and FY2025, and warns a loss of, or demand drop from, any of them would be material [Certain] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1730168/000173016825000121/avgo-20251102.htm). Balance sheet is clean (net debt/EBITDA 0.68x, Altman Z 13.08, Piotroski F 7) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/avgo/statistics/, https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/avgo/financials/ratios/).

## 3. Valuation
On relative multiples the stock looks inexpensive: P/E 46.1x sits at the 25th percentile of its own 5y range and 24% below the semiconductor-peer median (60.7x); EV/EBITDA is 40% below peers and P/FCF 46% below [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/avgo/financials/ratios/). But the own-history DCF/EV-EBITDA fair-value blend puts base fair value at 108.49 and even the bull case at 265.65 — both well below the 361.54 close — and the reverse DCF shows the price implies 41.7% revenue growth in year one fading to terminal growth [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/avgo/, computed in research/AVGO/2026-10-11-valuation.md). The gap exists because peers (notably NVDA) trade at even richer multiples, while AVGO's own 10-year base rates don't yet capture the AI-ASIC ramp. The price assumes that ramp continues at a pace well above Broadcom's historical growth for years, with no margin of safety if AI capex decelerates. [Likely]

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROIC (ratios page) falls below 20%, signalling AI-ASIC returns are being competed away.
- Net margin (income statement) falls back below 25% (FY2021-24 range), indicating the AI mix shift is not sticking.
- Net debt/EBITDA (balance sheet, income statement) rises above 2.5x as reported AI-chip financing commitments (~$50-60bn per WSJ/Bloomberg) convert to on-balance-sheet debt.
- The next 10-K's top-five-customer concentration disclosure (SEC filing) rises materially above 40% or discloses the loss of a named hyperscaler customer.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
FCF conversion remains comfortably above 100% of net income (103% TTM), though it is normalizing down from the VMware-driven 330% in FY2024 [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/avgo/financials/cash-flow-statement/). Net debt/EBITDA has fallen to 0.68x from 2.31x in FY2024, giving real headroom [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/avgo/financials/ratios/). Shares outstanding rose only 0.99% YoY — modest stock-comp dilution, not debt-funded buybacks [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/avgo/statistics/). Press reports (Oct 2026) say Broadcom is arranging $50-60bn of financing to fund custom AI-chip builds for OpenAI/Anthropic; this is not yet reflected in the TTM leverage figures and would raise net debt materially if consummated. [Likely] (source: https://www.wsj.com/tech/oracle-broadcom-and-spacex-seek-blockbuster-debt-deals-to-pay-for-ai-chips-848e8032)

## 6. Unusual items
Customer concentration is the key non-standard risk: top five end customers are ~40% of net revenue per the FY2025 10-K [Certain] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1730168/000173016825000121/avgo-20251102.htm). Reported large AI-chip debt-financing talks with OpenAI/Anthropic (~$50-60bn) could materially raise leverage if they proceed [Likely] (source: https://www.wsj.com/tech/oracle-broadcom-and-spacex-seek-blockbuster-debt-deals-to-pay-for-ai-chips-848e8032). Short interest is low (1.10% of float, +2.9% MoM) and institutional ownership is high (79.8%) — not material on their own [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/avgo/statistics/).
