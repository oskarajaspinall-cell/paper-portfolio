# Macro overlay: Broadcom Inc. (AVGO) — 2026-10-11

## Assessment
AVGO trades at 361.54 vs its own base fair value of 108.49 (reverse DCF implies ~41.7% year-1 revenue growth), so the multiple is unusually sensitive to the discount rate, and the thesis note flags a reported $50-60bn AI-chip debt financing (OpenAI/Anthropic) that would raise leverage if consummated [Certain] (research/AVGO/2026-10-11.md; factsheet). Chain: real yields -> DCF discount rate -> fair-value range/multiple; credit spreads -> cost of the reported debt raise -> capex pace and leverage.

Facts (FRED, 2026-10-08/09): 10y real yield (DFII10) 2.87%, +56bp 3m; 10y nominal (DGS10) 5.22%, +68bp 3m; 10y breakeven (T10YIE) 2.33%, roughly flat (+9bp 3m) — the nominal rise is real-yield-, not inflation-, driven [Certain]. HY spread (BAMLH0A0HYM2) 3.15%, +45bp 3m, widening even as VIX fell to 15.41 (-43bp 3m) [Certain] — this divergence **(inference)** may reflect heavy AI-debt supply (Broadcom/Oracle/SpaceX) rather than broad risk-off. 2y yield (4.75%) above fed funds (3.88%) is a rough proxy for a market still pricing higher-for-longer, not cuts **(inference)**.

AVGO has de-rated 27% off its June high and trails SPY by 11-13pp over 3/6/12m, partly consistent with rising real yields compressing high-multiple AI names [Likely]. Further yield/spread rises would widen the price-to-fair-value gap and could complicate the AI-chip financing; a reversal would ease both. **Tilt: toward bear, moderate** — rates and spreads are moving against the stock's richest input (discount rate) as it tests credit markets for tens of billions.
