**Pricing (proxy):** the 2y sits about 1pp above fed funds, so the market prices hikes **(inference)** [Likely]. Flat breakevens with rising real yields mean a real-rate repricing, not an inflation scare **(inference)** [Likely]. The stock is already -16.9% over 3m, so part of this is priced [Likely]. Higher real yields or wider spreads hurt; lower real yields help.

**Bull:** real yields stabilise below 3% and spreads stay tight, so the multiple holds while growth carries it. **Bear:** real yield above 3.25% and HY above 4% trigger risk-off in a high-beta (1.51) name, and the multiple falls before earnings do.

**Tilt:** toward bear, small. Rates press on the multiple, but the thesis rests on AI capex and customer concentration.
