## 2. Quality
Historically a high-return utility-like franchise: FY2021–FY2025 ROCE 16.8–19.9% and ROE 26.8–30.4%, gross margin 43–44.5% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hel/ELISA/financials/ratios/) [Certain]. Beta is 0.45 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hel/ELISA/statistics/). Cash conversion is strong: TTM FCF/NI 112.4%, FCF margin 16.5% [Certain].

But the last year is deteriorating. TTM ROIC is 12.6% against 15.5% a year earlier, ROCE 17.0% against 21.2%, operating margin 20.0% against 22.3% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hel/ELISA/financials/ratios/?p=trailing) [Certain]. Net debt/EBITDA has risen from 1.75x (FY2023) to 2.14x [Certain]. The Q3 results call described flat revenue, lower comparable EBITDA on mobile competition and lower-margin mix, offsetting savings; guidance unchanged (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hel/ELISA/transcripts/382120-q3-2026/) [Likely]. Moat (spectrum, scale, three-player market) is intact but returns are leaking [Likely]. Piotroski F is 5 [Certain].

## 3. Valuation
Every multiple sits below its own 5y minimum: P/E 16.5x (5y median 18.7x), EV/EBITDA 9.4x (11.0x), P/FCF 14.7x (19.6x), FCF yield 6.79% [Certain]. Against peers (Telia, Telenor, KPN, Orange) it is at a premium on P/E (+5%), EV/EBITDA (+15%), P/FCF (+31%) and P/B (+45%) [Certain]. The de-rating therefore tracks falling returns rather than clear mispricing, and the premium to peers is small against its own history.

Mechanical fair value is 37.55 EUR base (bear 29.12, bull 44.19) against a 34.00 close, +10.4% to base and -14.3% to bear (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hel/ELISA/). The reverse DCF implies only 1.1% year-one revenue growth, which is undemanding for a flat-revenue business [Likely]. The upside is modest, and the downside to bear is comparable in size [Likely].
