# Macro overlay: ONEOK, Inc. (OKE) — 2026-10-12
## Assessment
Chain: rates/credit spreads → discount rate (DCF) and refinancing cost → valuation multiple and FCF, for a highly levered (net debt/EBITDA 4.28x TTM [Certain], source: fact sheet, stockanalysis.com ratios page) midstream operator funding a $4.425bn acquisition partly with debt alongside $9bn of Apollo structured equity [Likely] (source: research/OKE/2026-10-12.md).

Facts (FRED, https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=DFII10, 2026-10-08/09): 10y real yield (DFII10) 2.87%, +0.44pp 1m, +0.56pp 3m [Certain]. 10y nominal (DGS10) 5.22%, +0.42pp 1m, +0.68pp 3m [Certain] — this is the risk-free rate already used in OKE's fair-value DCF. 2y yield (DGS2) 4.75% vs effective fed funds (DFF) 3.88% [Certain]. HY credit spread (BAMLH0A0HYM2) 3.15%, +0.45pp 3m [Certain]. 10y breakeven (T10YIE) 2.33%, roughly flat [Certain].

Market pricing (inference): 2y yield above fed funds is a proxy that the market expects further tightening, not an easing path **(inference)**. The simultaneous 3-month rise in real yields and HY spreads is a genuine repricing of "higher for longer," not stale data, and it is a headwind for bond-proxy, leveraged midstream equity **(inference)**.

Bull: yields/spreads plateau or ease → lower discount rate, cheaper refinancing of the Apollo/debt-funded deal → fair value re-rates up from the 104.43 base case.
Bear: yields/spreads extend their 3-month rise → higher cost of equity and refinancing cost on a 4.28x-levered balance sheet already seeing FCF conversion fall to 79.5% TTM → fair value compresses toward the 70.73 bear case.
Tilt: the fundamental case (fee-based volumes, M&A synergies) dominates; macro only shifts the valuation range at the margin.
