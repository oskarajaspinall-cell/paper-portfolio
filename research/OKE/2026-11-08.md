# ONEOK, Inc. (OKE) — Core initiation — 2026-11-08
## 1. Business
US midstream operator with four segments: Natural Gas Liquids, Refined Products & Crude, Natural Gas Gathering & Processing, and Natural Gas Pipelines. It earns mostly take-or-pay and volume-based fees, about 90% fee-based in 2026 [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1039684/000103968426000039/oke-20260930.htm) [Likely]. Operations are domestic; no foreign country is a material revenue share [Likely]. The Brazos Midland Permian gathering and processing purchase closed on 2026-10-06 [Certain] (source: https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/10/06/3376015/0/en/oneok-completes-acquisition-of-brazos-midstream-s-permian-midland-basin-assets.html).

## 2. Quality
A durable network moat (hard-to-replicate pipelines, long investment-grade contracts) [Likely], but the numbers show no widening of returns. TTM ROIC is 8.8%, up 0.9pp on a year earlier, but only back to its 2-year high; ROE has fallen from 24.9% (FY21) to 15.0% [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/oke/financials/ratios/). Recent direction is the concern: operating margin fell from 18.0% to 14.2% over 12 months and FCF margin from 9.3% to 5.9% [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/oke/financials/income-statement/?p=trailing). FCF conversion is 62.2% TTM vs 94.5% in FY24 [Certain]. The 10-Q attributes segment profit declines partly to higher operating costs and unfractionated NGL inventory build [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1039684/000103968426000039/oke-20260930.htm) [Likely]; part of the cash gap may be working capital that reverses, but this is unproven [Guessing]. Q3 volumes and guidance rose [Likely] (source: https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/10/27/3389643/0/en/oneok-announces-higher-third-quarter-2026-earnings-and-increases-2026-financial-guidance.html). Headline Q3 net income includes a one-time noncash tax benefit from the Apollo transaction, so it overstates earning power [company-reported] (same 10-Q).

## 3. Valuation
At 85.19 USD: P/E 13.2x, EV/EBITDA 10.7x, both at or below the 5y minimum (13.6x / 10.7x) and 29% / 19% below the peer median (18.6x / 13.1x) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/oke/statistics/). But P/E is flattered by the one-off tax benefit, and P/FCF of 21.2x is above its 5y maximum (20.5x) and level with peers (21.4x); FCF yield is 4.71% [Certain]. Cheapness therefore rests on EBITDA, not cash. Mechanical fair value base is 101.12 USD (+18.7%), bear 77.94 (-8.5%); the 381.97 bull is not credible [Certain] (source: fact sheet fair-value section). The reverse DCF needs only 3.3% year-1 revenue growth [Certain]. Price is 7.8% below its 50-day and 4.4% below its 200-day average, RSI 33.2 [Certain]: a sell-off after a guidance raise, which suggests the market is questioning cash conversion and Brazos integration [Guessing]. Upside is positive but modest versus a bear case that is -8.5% with deteriorating cash trends.

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- FCF conversion (FCF/NI, cash-flow and income-statement pages) falls below 50% on a TTM basis
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) is above 4.5x on a TTM basis once Brazos is in the numbers (3.68x at 2026-09-30, before the acquisition's debt)
- Operating margin (income statement, TTM) falls below 12%
- ROIC (ratios page, TTM) falls below 7%

Note: the prior note's 70% FCF conversion trigger has already been breached (62.2%); levels above are reset.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Apollo contributed $9 billion for Class B units with a capped return of 7.01% for the first period, structurally subordinated to debt but ahead of common at the operating level [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1039684/000103968426000039/oke-20260930.htm) [Certain]. Proceeds funded debt tenders and most of the $4.6 billion Brazos price [company-reported] (same URL); S&P and Fitch upgraded to BBB+ [company-reported] [Certain]. Shares outstanding rose 2.75% YoY [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/oke/statistics/). Quarterly dividend held at $1.07 [Certain] (source: https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/10/14/3381152/0/en/oneok-declares-quarterly-dividend.html). Cash conversion is the weak point: FCF margin has fallen while growth capex (Permian plants, NGL expansion) continues [Likely].

## 6. Unusual items
Apollo Class B ranks ahead of common equity's residual cash flows and is a permanent complexity discount [Likely]. Short interest is 3.75% of float, down 13.6% month on month [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/oke/statistics/). Middle East disruption has lifted refined-products volumes and crack spreads, a possibly transient tailwind [company-reported] (same 10-Q) [Likely].

Verdict for the evaluator: evidence improved (guidance raise, upgrade, cheaper entry) but cash conversion and margins worsened; supports keeping conviction at 3, not 4.
