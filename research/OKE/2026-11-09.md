# ONEOK, Inc. (OKE) — Core initiation — 2026-11-09
## 1. Business
Fee-based midstream operator: natural gas gathering and processing, NGL transport and fractionation, refined products and crude, and natural gas pipelines, across the Rocky Mountain, Mid-Continent, Permian and Gulf Coast regions. Management expects about 90% of 2026 earnings to be fee-based, supported by minimum-volume and take-or-pay contracts [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1039684/000103968426000039/oke-20260930.htm). Reports in USD; no foreign country is a material share of revenue [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/oke/).

## 2. Quality
Adequate, not exceptional. Returns are mid-single to low-double digit: ROIC 8.8% TTM, ROCE 9.2%, ROE 15.0%, all below FY2021-22 levels (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/oke/financials/ratios/). [Certain]
Durable edge is physical: integrated gathering-to-export network and long-term contracts with investment-grade counterparties, which are hard to replicate [Likely] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1039684/000103968426000039/oke-20260930.htm).
Direction change, addressed: operating margin fell from 18.0% to 14.2% over 12 months and FCF margin from 9.3% to 5.9% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/oke/financials/income-statement/?p=trailing). Part of this is the acquisition-heavy, growth-capex phase and commodity-linked revenue mix, not pricing erosion; ROIC rose 0.9pp over the same period [Likely]. FCF conversion of 62.2% of net income is the weak point [Certain].

## 3. Valuation
The price (85.19 USD) sits below the 5y range on P/E (13.2x vs min 13.6x), EV/EBITDA (10.7x vs min 10.7x) and EV/Sales, and at a discount to peers (WMB, KMI, TRGP, MPLX) of 37% on P/E and 19% on EV/EBITDA [Certain]. P/FCF is the exception: 21.2x, above its 5y maximum, with a FCF yield of 4.71% [Certain]. A one-time noncash tax benefit lifted Q3 net income [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1039684/000103968426000039/oke-20260930.htm), so P/E flatters [Likely].
Mechanical fair value is flagged unreliable (DCF base 126.03 vs EV/EBITDA base 51.31); the blended base of 101.12 is a loose guide only. The reverse DCF implies 3.3% year-one revenue growth, a modest hurdle [Likely]. (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/oke/)

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- TTM ROIC (ratios page, trailing) falls below 7.5%.
- Net debt / EBITDA (statistics/ratios page) rises back above 4.5x after the planned debt repayments.
- TTM operating margin (income statement, trailing) falls below 12%.
- FCF conversion (FCF/net income, cash flow statement) stays below 60% for FY2026.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Heavy growth spending: the Brazos Midland acquisition closed on 6 October 2026 for roughly $4.6 billion cash [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1039684/000103968426000039/oke-20260930.htm). It was funded from the Apollo-backed ONEOK Holdings transaction and short-term borrowings; Apollo's Class B units carry a capped return of 7.01% at first [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1039684/000103968426000039/oke-20260930.htm). S&P and Fitch lifted ratings to BBB+ [company-reported] (same URL). The Apollo capital is a structurally junior, equity-like and costly claim [Likely]. Shares outstanding rose 2.75% YoY [Certain]; FCF conversion is declining (section 2). Dividend held at $1.07 per quarter (source: https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/10/14/3381152/0/en/oneok-declares-quarterly-dividend.html).
