# Macro overlay: W. R. Berkley Corporation (WRB) — 2026-10-08
## Assessment
WRB is a specialty P&C insurer whose earnings blend underwriting margin with investment income on float held mostly in fixed income; equity beta is very low (0.27) [Certain] (fact sheet, Other). The macro variable that moves the thesis is the yield path: rising nominal/real rates raise the reinvestment yield on WRB's bond portfolio as holdings roll off, lifting investment income/ROE (tailwind), while the same rise lifts the risk-free rate in cost-of-equity (discount-rate headwind on the multiple) **(inference)**.

**Facts** [Certain] (FRED, fredgraph.csv?id=DGS10/DFII10/DFF, 2026-10-06): 10y Treasury 5.27% (+0.72pp/3m); 10y real yield 2.91% (+0.61pp/3m); fed funds 3.88% (+0.25pp/1m, a recent hike); breakevens roughly flat (T10YIE 2.36%, +0.11pp/3m) — the move is real-rate led, not an inflation scare. **Market pricing (proxy, inference)**: 2y yield (4.79%) sits ~0.9pp above fed funds, consistent with the market pricing further tightening or term premium, not a pause. HY spreads (3.03%, +0.36pp/3m) ticked up but remain low — no stress signal yet.

**Bull**: real yields keep climbing on growth/term premium (not recession); float reinvests faster than reserves roll off, investment income/EPS/ROE keep beating, multiple holds despite higher Rf.
**Bear**: the rise turns credit/liquidity-driven, spreads widen sharply, financials' risk premium re-rates up faster than the earnings tailwind, compressing WRB's already-premium 2.8x P/B.
**Tilt**: low beta, a not-extreme valuation versus own history, and rate-up historically favoring P&C float income tilt macro **toward bull, small** — orderly, real-rate-led yield rises have helped this name's earnings more than they've hurt its multiple **(inference)**.
