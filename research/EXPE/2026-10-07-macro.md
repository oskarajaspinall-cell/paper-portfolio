# Macro overlay: Expedia Group, Inc. (EXPE) — 2026-10-07
## Assessment
Chain: real yields/financial conditions -> consumer discretionary spending power (wealth effect, credit cost) -> travel bookings growth -> EXPE gross bookings/take-rate revenue; secondarily, dollar strength -> USD translation of international bookings **(inference; fact sheet does not disclose EXPE's geographic revenue split)**.

Facts [Certain] (FRED, reports/macro/2026-10-07.md): 10y real yield (DFII10) 2.95% on 2026-10-05, +0.71pp/3m; fed funds (DFF) 3.88%, +0.25pp/3m (a hike, not a cut); HY credit spread (BAMLH0A0HYM2) 3.12%, +0.44pp in the last month alone; broad dollar index (DTWEXBGS) +2.86% in 1m; VIX only 15.52, flat. Breakeven inflation (T10YIE) rose just +0.11pp/3m, so the yield move is mostly real-rate/term-premium, not an inflation surprise.

Market pricing (proxy, **(inference)**): 2y Treasury (4.84%) sits ~1pp above fed funds (3.88%), implying the market prices further tightening ahead, not easing — a hawkish surprise versus a plausible prior expectation of cuts.

EXPE-specific [Certain] (factsheet): beta 1.36, 3m return -6.2pp vs SPY; net cash balance sheet (no funding risk) but high demand elasticity to consumer financial conditions.

Bull: rate/spread pressure stalls, bookings growth holds, EXPE's depressed multiple (16.2x P/E vs 26.9x peer median) gets room to re-rate. Bear: real yields/HY spreads keep widening, travel demand softens, dollar strength adds a translation headwind, compounding the existing AI-disintermediation discount. Tilt: small toward bear — EXPE's thesis is idiosyncratic (AI agents, buybacks) and valuation already discounts weak sentiment, but the speed of the real-yield/credit-spread move is new and unaddressed by the fundamental case.
