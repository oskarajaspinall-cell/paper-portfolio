# Expedia Group, Inc. (EXPE) — Core initiation — 2026-10-07
## 1. Business
Online travel platform spanning B2C brands (Expedia, Hotels.com, Vrbo), a B2B white-label booking technology business for travel partners, an advertising/media segment monetizing traffic across its sites, and trivago metasearch. Revenue is a take rate on gross bookings plus B2B platform fees and ad sales [Certain] (source: stockanalysis.com/stocks/expe/).

## 2. Quality
Returns have improved every year since 2021: ROIC 3.0%→43.1% (FY2025), ROE 0.4%→48.7%, operating margin 3.0%→14.7%, gross margin 82.3%→90.1% [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/expe/financials/ratios/, https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/expe/financials/income-statement/). The balance sheet flipped from 9.4x net debt/EBITDA (2021) to net cash (-0.50x TTM) [Certain] (same source). Moat rests on scale/marketing efficiency and the One Key loyalty program unifying its brands, plus B2B distribution deals that are stickier than B2C traffic. The clearest emerging threat is AI trip-planning agents disintermediating OTA search: Expedia is joining Meta's "Muse" agent for trip planning (announced 2026-09-22), which pressured peer brand trivago roughly 10% on the news [Likely] (source: https://www.tipranks.com/news/the-fly/trivago-under-pressure-as-expedia-announces-muse-integration-thefly-news). Whether partnering with Muse protects or cannibalizes Expedia's own traffic/take-rate economics is unresolved [Guessing]. FCF margin, while still solid at 28.4% TTM, has structurally declined from an unusually high 35.8% pandemic-recovery base in 2021 [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/expe/financials/cash-flow-statement/).

## 3. Valuation
At 16.2x P/E, EXPE trades 17% below its own 5-year low (19.4x) and 40% below the 4-peer (BKNG, ABNB, TCOM, TRIP) median of 26.9x [Certain]. EV/Sales (1.9x) and P/FCF (7.0x) are 37% and 42% below peer medians respectively; EV/EBITDA (10.3x) is roughly in line with peers (-4%) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/expe/, peer statistics pages cited in fact sheet). The market is pricing a travel OTA with little re-rating credit for the ROIC/margin improvement already delivered, likely reflecting the AI-disintermediation overhang rather than fundamentals. P/B of 25.8x looks extreme (222% above its own 5y max) but is distorted by a book-equity base shrunk by years of buybacks, not by the business [Likely] — this multiple is not a reliable valuation signal here.

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROIC (ratios page) falls below 20% on a full fiscal year, reversing the 2021-2025 improvement.
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) moves back above 1.0x net debt, reversing the deleveraging to net cash.
- Gross margin (income statement page) falls below 85% on a full fiscal year, signalling take-rate erosion (e.g., from AI-agent disintermediation).
- FCF margin (cash flow/income statement pages) falls below 15% on a full fiscal year.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Shares outstanding fell 6.17% YoY on buybacks [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/expe/statistics/). Per its Q2 2026 10-Q, Expedia repurchased 4.2m shares for $900m in H1 2026 (avg. $214.86) with $5.7bn of authorization remaining (no expiry) [Certain] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1324424/000132442426000053/expe-20260630.htm). It paid two $0.48/share quarterly dividends in H1 2026 (up from $0.40 prior year) and declared another $0.48 dividend for September 2026 [Certain] (same source). Liquidity is strong: $6.7bn cash, $2.4bn restricted cash, undrawn $2.5bn revolver to 2031, no near-term debt maturities (next: $749m notes due 2027) [Certain] (same source). FCF conversion (FCF/NI) remains very high at 219% TTM, though down from distorted 2021-22 levels [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/expe/financials/cash-flow-statement/).

## 6. Unusual items
Altman Z-score is 1.45, nominally in the classic "distress" zone, while Piotroski F-score is a strong 7/9 and net debt is negative — the low Z-score looks like an artifact of a buyback-shrunk book-equity base rather than genuine distress, but it is unusual enough to flag and monitor [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/expe/statistics/). Short interest is 5.54% of float and rose 20.9% month-over-month, indicating building bearish positioning, plausibly tied to the AI-disintermediation narrative [Likely] (same source).
