# Focus Media Information Technology (SHE:002027) — Core initiation — 2026-10-18

## 1. Business
Focus Media operates China's largest out-of-home "life circle media" advertising network, selling ad space on elevator LCD-TV and digital-frame displays, poster frames and cinema screens to brand advertisers (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/she/002027/) [Certain]. Revenue is leveraged off owned/leased screen and frame inventory, so margin tracks advertiser demand and ad-slot utilization/pricing [Likely].

## 2. Quality
Returns on capital are high: ROIC 60.4% TTM (42.9% FY21 → 41.0% FY25), ROCE rising to 37.5% TTM from 33.0% FY21, gross margin 71.6% TTM, FCF margin ~61% TTM with FCF conversion 226% of net income (source: ratios/income-statement/cash-flow pages) [Certain]. The balance sheet carries net cash (net debt/EBITDA -1.46x TTM) [Certain]. The moat is the density of its elevator/building network inside residential and office "life circle" spaces, which would be costly for a rival to replicate at scale (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/she/002027/) [Likely]. Flag: net margin fell from 42.0% (FY2024) to 23.1% (FY2025) and ROE from 28.7% to 18.3%, before both partly recovered to 27.0%/21.8% TTM (source: ratios/income-statement pages) [Certain]; the cause is not disclosed on the fact sheet, so whether the FY2025 dip was one-off or structural is [Guessing].

## 3. Valuation
At 5.16 CNY, the stock trades well below its own 5-year multiple range: EV/EBITDA 8.0x vs a 5y range of 16.1x-29.7x (-60% below the 5y min), EV/Sales 5.2x (-96% below min), P/FCF 9.7x (-71% below min), and P/B near the bottom of its 5y range (4th percentile) (source: ratios page) [Certain]. Versus peers (CCO, LAMR, JCDecaux/EPA:DEC), it is cheaper on EV/EBITDA (-45%), P/FCF (-34%) and P/B (-42%), roughly in line on P/E (-1%), and richer only on EV/Sales (+17%) (source: ratios/statistics pages) [Certain]. The blended DCF/EV-EBITDA fair value is 6.57 CNY base (+27.2% upside), 8.63 CNY bull (+67.2%), and 5.05 CNY bear (-2.1%, i.e. limited downside even in the bear case) (source: fact-sheet fair-value section) [Likely]. The reverse DCF implies the price assumes -1.5% revenue growth in year 1 fading to terminal growth, i.e. continued stagnation (source: fact-sheet fair-value section) [Likely].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Net margin (income statement, ratios page) fails to recover above ~30% for two consecutive quarters, confirming the FY2025 drop is structural, not one-off.
- ROIC (ratios page) falls below 25% for a full fiscal year, signalling the elevator-network moat is eroding to competition.
- FCF conversion, FCF/NI (cash-flow and income-statement pages) falls below 100% for a full fiscal year, indicating earnings quality has deteriorated.
- Net debt/EBITDA (balance sheet/ratios page) turns positive (the net-cash position is lost), indicating capital discipline has broken down.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
FCF conversion is very high (226% of net income TTM; 154%-239% across FY21-25) (source: cash-flow/income-statement pages) [Certain], and the balance sheet has been net cash in every year since FY21 (net debt/EBITDA -1.46x TTM) (source: balance-sheet/ratios pages) [Certain]. Shares outstanding are essentially flat (+0.02% YoY) (source: statistics page), implying no meaningful dilution [Certain]. The fact sheet does not break out the split of cash returned via dividends vs buybacks, so that mix is [data unavailable].
