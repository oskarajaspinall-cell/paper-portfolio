# Macro overlay: AngloGold Ashanti plc (AU) — 2026-10-24
## Assessment
**Primary** [Likely]. AU is a gold miner (https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/au/, 2026-10-24) with a 49.9% TTM operating margin, so earnings are highly geared to the gold price. **Chain:** real yields / dollar -> gold price -> revenue and margin -> multiple.

**Facts (FRED, 2026-10-22; https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=DFII10):** 10y TIPS 2.64% (+0.21pp 3m); 2y 4.65% vs fed funds 3.88%; breakeven 2.34% (+0.08pp); broad dollar +2.48 in ~1m (2026-10-16).

**Pricing (proxy):** the 2y-over-fed-funds gap implies tightening priced. **(inference)** Rising real yields with a stronger dollar is the combination most hostile to gold. The shares are 17.2% below the 50-day average, RSI 31.6 (fact sheet), so part of this is priced. Surprise that hurts: hawkish repricing. Surprise that helps: falling real yields.

**Bull:** real yields and the dollar ease; gold and the multiple recover (P/E 11.2x is below its 5y minimum).
**Bear:** real yields keep rising; gold falls and margins retreat from peak toward their 5y median.

**Tilt:** toward bear, small. Adverse 1-3m moves, but largely priced, and the snapshot has no gold price to confirm the transmission.
