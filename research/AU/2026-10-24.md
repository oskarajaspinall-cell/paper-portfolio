# AngloGold Ashanti (AU) — Core initiation — 2026-10-24
## 1. Business
Gold miner with a multi-country portfolio and growth projects in Nevada and Colombia. Revenue is production volume times the gold price; profit is the spread over all-in cost. It is a price-taker, so margins track the gold price and cost control (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/au/transcripts/751941-mining-forum-americas-2026/) [Likely].

## 2. Quality
Returns have stepped up sharply: TTM ROIC is 45.0% against 8.5% in FY2022 and -38.7% in FY2023, and operating margin is 49.9% against 12.4% in FY2023 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/au/financials/ratios/). This is cyclical leverage to the gold price, not proof of a moat [Likely]. A gold miner's only durable edge is low-cost, long-life ore bodies. Management stresses Tier 1 assets and capital discipline (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/au/transcripts/751941-mining-forum-americas-2026/) [Guessing]. The balance sheet is a net cash position (net debt/EBITDA -0.14x) and FCF conversion is 117.4% of net income (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/au/financials/cash-flow-statement/) [Certain]. Share count is up 7.69% YoY, which dilutes holders (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/au/statistics/) [Certain]. Quality is high at today's gold price but unproven through a downcycle: the FY2021-23 history shows how fast it erodes [Likely].

## 3. Valuation
P/E is 11.2x, below its 5y minimum of 11.6x, and EV/EBITDA is 5.8x at the bottom of its 5y range (8.0x median). FCF yield is 10.48%. That looks cheap, but these multiples sit on peak-margin earnings, so they flatter [Likely]. Against peers, P/E is 12% lower and EV/EBITDA 6% lower, but P/B (4.8x) is above the 5y max and 55% above peers (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/au/financials/ratios/). The mechanical fair value is bear 27.76, base 37.76 and bull 115.79 against a last close of 83.59 (source: fact sheet, Fair value). The base case is 54.8% below the price and uses normalised history, so the price assumes today's gold-driven margins persist [Likely]. NAV was [data unavailable]. There is no margin of safety on normalised earnings.

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Gross margin (statistics page) falls below 40%.
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) rises above 1.0x.
- FCF margin (cash flow statement page) falls below 20%.
- ROIC (ratios page) falls below 20%.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
FCF conversion has exceeded 100% in FY2022, FY2025 and TTM, and the company has moved to net cash. It announced a share repurchase programme, put to a shareholder meeting (source: https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260701239405/en/AngloGold-Ashanti-Publishes-Notice-of-General-Meeting-in-Relation-to-Proposed-Share-Repurchase-Programme/) [Certain]. Yet the share count rose 7.69% YoY, so buybacks have not yet offset dilution [Likely]. Growth capex in Nevada and Colombia will absorb cash [Likely].

## 6. Unusual items
Environmental permitting is the main project risk (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/au/transcripts/751941-mining-forum-americas-2026/) [Likely]. The stock is 17.2% below its 50-day average with RSI 31.6, so it is selling off despite results; the cause is not in the sources read [Guessing]. Institutions own 69.2%. Earnings are due Nov 5, 2026. Lean: AVOID, as quality is cyclical and valuation offers no margin of safety on normalised earnings.
