## Risk Factors
- **Added**: The following risk factors supplement and/or update the risk factors previously disclosed in our Annual Report on Form 10-K for the year ended December 31, 2025:
- **Added**: The accounting method for the 2032 Exchangeable Notes could adversely affect our financial condition and consolidated financial results.
- **Added**: The accounting method for reflecting the 2032 Exchangeable Notes on our Condensed Consolidated Balance Sheets, recognizing amortized interest expense, and reflecting the underlying shares of common stock in diluted earnings per share could adversely affect our reported earnings, financial condition, and liquidity.
- **Added**: In accordance with GAAP, the 2032 Exchangeable Notes, are reflected as a liability on our Condensed Consolidated Balance Sheets, with an initial carrying amount equal to the principal amount of the 2032 Exchangeable Notes, net of issuance costs. The issuance costs are treated as a debt discount for accounting purposes, and amortized into interest expense over the term of the 2032 Exchangeable Notes. As a result of this amortization, the interest expense recognized for accounting purposes will be greater than the cash interest payments on the 2032 Exchangeable Notes, which will result in lower reported income.
- **Added**: In addition, we expect that the shares underlying the 2032 Exchangeable Notes will be reflected in our diluted earnings per share using the “if-converted” method. Under that method, diluted earnings per share would generally be calculated assuming that all of the 2032 Exchangeable Notes were exchanged solely into shares of common stock at the beginning of the reporting period, unless the result would be anti-dilutive. The application of the if-converted method may reduce our reported diluted earnings per share.
