# PBF Energy Inc. (PBF) — Core initiation — 2026-11-15
## 1. Business
Independent US refiner (Oil & Gas Refining & Marketing, Energy sector). It buys crude, runs it through its refineries (including Torrance) and sells gasoline, diesel and other products; earnings follow the refining margin (product price less crude cost), not volume. No foreign country or emerging-market region is identified as ~10%+ of revenue in the sources read [Likely]. (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/pbf/)

## 2. Quality
Not a durable-returns business yet [Likely]. Refining is a commodity margin business with no pricing power; returns swing with the cycle. ROIC went from 60.2% (FY2022) to -9.8% (FY2025) and is 25.3% on a TTM basis (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/pbf/financials/ratios/). The five-year trend is falling, but the last twelve months rose sharply (-11.8% to 25.3%), so the current figure is a cyclical peak, not a new base [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/pbf/financials/ratios/?p=trailing). Operating margin is 7.1% TTM against -2.5% in FY2025. The balance sheet has turned: net debt/EBITDA is 0.05x, Altman Z 3.80, Piotroski 8 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/pbf/statistics/) [Certain]. The Q3 call credits tight product markets, which is a cyclical, not structural, driver (headline: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/pbf/transcripts/719862-q3-2026/) [Likely]. Freight-driven heavy-crude discounts help US refiners [Guessing] (https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/soaring-freight-rates-give-us-refiners-edge-crude-discounts-widen-2026-11-06/).

## 3. Valuation
The price assumes earnings fall back toward the cycle average. P/E 3.1x is at 28% of its 5y range (2.5x median) and 63% below the peer median of 8.4x. EV/EBITDA 2.9x is at 6% of its range and 46% below peers. FCF yield is 29.61%, P/FCF 3.4x. P/B 1.1x is above its 5y maximum, so book value is no longer the cheap leg. The reverse DCF implies revenue falling 4.2% in year 1. Base fair value is 73.08 against a 71.28 close (+2.5%), with a bear case that is negative and a very wide bull case, so the fair-value range is unreliable [Certain]. Cheap multiples on peak profits are the classic refining trap [Likely]; upside rests on margins staying elevated. Trend is healthy: +28.3% above the 200-day average, 12-month +84.1%, but 7.7% below the 50-day (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/pbf/history/).

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Operating margin (TTM, income-statement trailing page) falls below 3%.
- ROIC (TTM, ratios trailing page) falls below 10%.
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) rises above 1.5x.
- FCF margin (TTM, cash-flow trailing page) turns negative.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
FCF conversion is 95.1% TTM (FY2025's 494.1% reflects near-zero profit) and FCF margin 6.6%. Debt has been cleaned up: the company redeemed higher-coupon senior notes with cash and refinanced part with a zero-coupon exchangeable issue, and extended and enlarged its revolver [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1534504/000153450426000041/pbf-20260930.htm) [Certain]. It bought the Torrance hydrogen plants from a lessor (same filing). The dividend was raised 18% [Likely] (https://www.tipranks.com/news/the-fly/pbf-energy-increases-quarterly-dividend-18-to-32-5c-per-share-thefly-news). Shares outstanding rose 5.39% YoY, so dilution, not buybacks, is the record (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/pbf/statistics/).

## 6. Unusual items
- New risk factors on the exchangeable notes: the notes dilute diluted EPS under the if-converted method and capped-call counterparties carry credit risk [company-reported] (https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1534504/000153450426000041/pbf-20260930.htm) [Certain]; modest.
- Short interest 10.54% of float, down 6.3% on the month (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/pbf/statistics/). Institutions own 91.0%.
- Political risk: the administration has convened refiners over fuel prices before the midterms [Likely] (https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/trump-meet-refiners-fuel-retailers-iran-war-pressures-gas-prices-ahead-midterms-2026-08-27/). Margins depend on the Iran conflict's effect on crude and freight [Guessing].
