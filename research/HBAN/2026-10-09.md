# Huntington Bancshares Incorporated (HBAN) — Core initiation — 2026-10-09

## 1. Business
Huntington National Bank is a Midwest/mid-Atlantic regional bank: it earns net interest income on the spread between loans (commercial, auto, consumer, mortgage) and deposits, plus fee income from payments, wealth management, capital markets and insurance [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/hban/).

## 2. Quality
ROE rose from 8.0% (FY21) to 10.1% (FY25) but slipped to 9.0% TTM, and net margin similarly rose 5y (21.7%→28.7%) but eased to 26.1% TTM [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/hban/financials/ratios/, https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/hban/financials/income-statement/). FCF conversion (FCF/NI) has fallen from 140-170% in FY21-22 to 87-112% in FY24-TTM [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/hban/financials/cash-flow-statement/). Piotroski F-score is only 4 (of 9), a mediocre fundamental-health read [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/hban/statistics/). Shares outstanding grew 18.3% YoY, unusually dilutive for a bank and a direct drag on per-share returns [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/hban/statistics/). At a Barclays conference (Sept 16, 2026) management cut FY27 EPS guidance to $1.75-$1.83 (from $1.90-$1.93), citing persistent NIM pressure and "competitive dynamics," and trimmed FY26 implied revenue growth to 34% (from 37%) and NII growth to 35% (from 39-43%) [Likely] (source: https://www.tipranks.com/news/the-fly/huntington-bancshares-cuts-fy27-eps-view-to-1-75-1-83-consensus-1-88-thefly-news; https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/hban/transcripts/749853-barclays-24th-annual-global-financial-services-conference/). Management explicitly naming competition as a margin drag argues against a durable moat; this is a scale deposit franchise, not a differentiated one.

## 3. Valuation
P/E of 12.0x sits at the 25th percentile of its own 5-year range (9.6x-19.3x) and 6% below the peer median of 12.8x; P/B of 1.0x is 18% below the 1.3x peer median [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/hban/statistics/, peers KEY/FITB/RF/CFG). P/FCF of 11.6x is 20% cheap to peers (14.4x) but at the 72nd percentile of its own 5y range, i.e. expensive versus its own history on a cash basis, consistent with the FCF-conversion slide above [Certain]. The fair-value model (P/B+ROE primary, FCFE/DDM secondary) gives base $16.20 (+5.4% vs last close $15.37), bear $11.69 (-23.9%) and bull $19.31 (+25.6%); the reverse DCF implies the market is pricing in -1.8% revenue growth in year 1 fading to terminal growth [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/hban/). The skew is roughly symmetric around a near-flat base case — cheap on headline multiples, but not a clear margin of safety given the quality concerns above.

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROE (ratios page) falls back below 8% (the FY2021 trough) for two consecutive fiscal years.
- Net margin (income statement) falls below 24%, below the FY2024 level.
- FCF conversion / FCF-NI (cash flow + income statement) stays below 80% for a full fiscal year.
- Piotroski F-score (statistics page) falls below 4.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
TTM FCF margin is 29.2% and FCF conversion 111.9%, both down from 5y highs (54.8% margin, 170.4% conversion in FY22) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/hban/financials/cash-flow-statement/). Shares outstanding rose 18.3% YoY — the fact sheet does not show dividend or buyback cash flows, so the net capital-return picture is [data unavailable]; this dilution alone is a meaningful offset to any buyback activity [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/hban/statistics/).

## 6. Unusual items
Shares outstanding +18.3% YoY is unusually high for a bank and dilutes per-share metrics [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/hban/statistics/). Management cut FY27 EPS guidance and FY26 revenue/NII growth outlook on Sept 16, 2026, citing NIM pressure and competition, alongside raising planned repurchases to $1.3bn-$1.4bn (reported by TheFly, not independently confirmed on the fact sheet) [Likely] (source: https://www.tipranks.com/news/the-fly/huntington-bancshares-cuts-fy27-eps-view-to-1-75-1-83-consensus-1-88-thefly-news; https://www.tipranks.com/news/the-fly/huntington-bancshares-sees-fy26-implied-revenue-growth-34-vs-prior-37-view-thefly-news). Stock has underperformed SPY by 23.9pp over 12 months, partly reflecting this [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/hban/history/). Short interest is low (2.61% of float, +1.5% m/m) — not a crowding risk [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/hban/statistics/). Q3 2026 earnings are due Oct 22, 2026, shortly after this note [Certain] (source: https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/huntington-bancshares-incorporated-announces-third-quarter-2026-earnings-call-details-302894994.html).
