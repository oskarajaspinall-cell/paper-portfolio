# Macro overlay: Huntington Bancshares (HBAN) — 2026-10-09
## Assessment
Chain: policy rate path -> deposit cost (funding) vs loan/securities yield -> net interest margin (NIM) -> EPS; secondarily credit spreads -> provisioning -> margin [Likely] **(inference)**. This is the "banks and rates" transmission the mandate treats as primary.

Facts (FRED, https://fred.stlouisfed.org/, 2026-10-07/08): effective fed funds 3.88%, +0.25pp (~1m), +0.26pp (~3m) — a confirmed hike, not a proxy [Certain]. 2y Treasury 4.77%, +0.4pp/+0.56pp; 10y Treasury 5.28%, +0.5pp/+0.72pp; 10y real yield 2.92%, +0.49pp/+0.61pp; HY credit spread 3.09%, +0.41pp/+0.39pp; VIX 15.08, -1.82pp (3m) [Certain].

Market pricing (proxy, 2y vs fed funds = implied near-term path): 2y (4.77%) sits 0.89pp above fed funds (3.88%), implying the market prices further tightening/no near-term cuts [Likely] **(inference)**. On Sept 16, 2026 management cut FY27 EPS guidance ($1.75-$1.83) and FY26 NII growth (to 35%), citing NIM pressure and deposit competition (per the fact sheet's cited commentary) [Likely]. The fed-funds hike and front-end rise since then post-date that guide, so funding costs this quarter are running incrementally worse than what the cut guidance assumed [Likely] **(inference)**; the modest 10y-2y steepening is a slower, offsetting tailwind for asset repricing.

Bull: Fed pauses/curve steepens with a stable front end -> deposit-cost relief, NIM troughs, guidance stabilizes; P/E (25th pct of 5y range) re-rates toward peers. Bear: fed funds and the 2y keep rising -> deposit costs outrun asset yields -> NIM/EPS cut again below the Sept guide, compounded by widening HY spreads -> higher provisioning. Tilt: moderate toward bear — a real, already-executed hike plus a rising 2y extends the company's own flagged risk, not just sentiment.
