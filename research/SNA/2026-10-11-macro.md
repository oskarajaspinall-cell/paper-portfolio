# Macro overlay: Snap-on Incorporated (SNA) — 2026-10-11
## Assessment
Chain: rising rates -> higher funding/installment-credit cost for Snap-on's own Financial Services arm, which finances franchisee vans and financed technician tool purchases, pressuring Tools/RS&I volume and FS margin; secondarily, higher risk-free rate -> DCF discount rate -> fair-value multiple **(inference)**.

Facts [Certain] (FRED, 2026-10-08/09, https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=DGS10): 10y Treasury 5.22%, +0.68pp/3m; 10y real yield 2.87%, +0.56pp/3m; fed funds 3.88%, +0.26pp/3m; HY credit spread 3.15%, +0.45pp/3m; 10y breakeven 2.33%, roughly flat (+0.09pp/3m). The back-up is mostly real-rate driven, not an inflation-expectations shift.

Market pricing (proxy) **(inference)**: 2y yield (4.75%) sits ~0.9pp above fed funds (3.88%), consistent with the curve pricing higher-for-longer rather than near-term cuts — a headwind for rate-sensitive financing demand, not yet reversing.

Already partly visible: SNA has underperformed SPY by -13.5pp/-20.6pp over 3m/6m [Certain] (fact sheet, 2026-10-09) and ROIC/ROCE drifted down 0.7-2.6pp over five years, attributed by the note to slowing Tools/RS&I demand [Likely] — rate-driven affordability pressure is a plausible contributor, not separable from idiosyncratic softness.

Bull: rates ease (10y toward ~4.5%) -> cheaper financing, technician demand re-accelerates, discount rate falls -> fair value re-rates up from base $320.61.
Bear: rates/credit spreads stay elevated or widen further -> FS funding cost and franchisee credit risk rise, Tools growth slows further -> fair value pressured toward bear case $204.27.
Tilt: toward bear, small — net cash and low beta (0.70) cushion the equity side, and financing is one of several demand channels for this moat-led business; the thesis isn't macro-dependent.
