# Grupo Aeroportuario del Sureste (BMV:ASUR.B) — Core initiation — 2026-11-08
## 1. Business
Airport concession operator: aeronautical fees per passenger plus commercial income (retail, parking, rentals) at Cancún and other Mexican airports, San Juan (Puerto Rico) and Colombia. The September 2026 Motiva deal added airports in Brazil, Ecuador, Costa Rica and Curaçao [Certain] (source: https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/asur-closes-acquisition-of-motivas-interests-in-airports-in-brazil-ecuador-costa-rica-and-curacao-302866774.html). Mexico is likely the largest revenue share [Likely]; exact country shares: [data unavailable]. Macro overlay should check MXN, COP and BRL.

## 2. Quality
Structurally strong: concession monopolies at leisure gateways like Cancún, with fees regulated but assets hard to replicate [Likely]. Operating margin was 55.9% in FY2024 and 45.6% in FY2025 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/bmv/ASUR.B/financials/income-statement/); TTM is 40.9%.

Direction is negative. ROIC is 13.4% TTM vs 26.3% a year earlier, and operating margin is down 9.3pp (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/bmv/ASUR.B/financials/ratios/?p=trailing). Part is acquisition dilution (new capital, lower-margin assets; net debt/EBITDA 2.89x vs -0.34x in FY2024) [Likely]. Part is weak Mexican traffic: Mexico traffic fell year on year in August, September and October (source: https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/asur-announces-total-passenger-traffic-for-october-2026-302931336.html) [Certain]. Q3 revenue rose on consolidation while the EBITDA margin contracted (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/bmv/ASUR.B/transcripts/715198-q3-2026/) [Likely]. Whether Mexican weakness is cyclical or structural is unproven [Guessing]. Beta 0.13 understates real risk.

## 3. Valuation
P/E 14.8x sits at 31% of its 5y range (median 14.6x), 22% below peers (18.8x, n=4: PAC, OMAB, AENA, ADP). EV/EBITDA 10.6x is mid-range (51%), 6% below peers. P/FCF 15.2x is near its median; FCF yield 6.57%. P/B 2.8x equals its median (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/bmv/ASUR.B/statistics/). The price is a fair multiple of depressed earnings, not a trough.

Mechanical fair value is wide: bear 216.51, base 794.01, bull 1,488.52 MXN vs a 491.55 close; base +61.5%, bear -56.0%. Base leans on a through-cycle DCF (988.86) that embeds the earlier high-margin years; the EV/EBITDA method gives 404.31, below the price [Likely]. The reverse DCF implies -3.5% year-1 revenue growth, a low bar given consolidation [Likely]. The peer discount is partly deserved given falling returns. Price is 5.2% below its 200-day average; 12-month change -14.9%.

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- TTM ROIC (ratios page, trailing view) stays below 10% at two consecutive quarterly reports.
- TTM operating margin (income statement, trailing view) falls below 35%.
- Net debt / EBITDA (statistics page) rises above 3.5x.
- TTM FCF margin (cash flow, trailing view) falls below 15%.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
The Motiva acquisition (20 airports) is a large step into Brazil and others, financed partly with debt: the Cancún subsidiary placed senior notes in October (source: https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/asur-announces-the-successful-placement-by-its-subsidiary-aeropuerto-de-cancun-sa-de-cv-of-6-711-senior-notes-due-2031-and-7-491-senior-notes-due-2036--302902737.html) [Certain]. Management plans next-year investment mainly funded by regional cash flow (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/bmv/ASUR.B/transcripts/715198-q3-2026/) [Likely]. FCF conversion was 43.3% in FY2025 (heavy capex) and 97.1% TTM. Share-count change: [data unavailable]. Integration risk is high [Likely].
