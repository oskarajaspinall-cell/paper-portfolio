# Match Group (MTCH) — Core initiation — 2026-11-07
## 1. Business
Match Group runs dating apps (Tinder, Hinge, Match, Azar, others). Revenue is mostly subscriptions and paid features bought in-app, so Apple and Google in-app fees are a major cost. Tinder is the largest brand but has lost users; Hinge is the growth engine. Since the second quarter of 2026 it reports three segments: Tinder, Hinge and "Everyone Everywhere" (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/891103/000089110326000163/mtch-20260930.htm). [Certain]

## 2. Quality
Returns are high: TTM ROIC 24.7%, ROCE 30.3%, gross margin 75.9%, operating margin 31.1%, FCF margin 32.7% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/mtch/financials/ratios/). FCF runs at 158.0% of net income. [Certain]

Moat is network effects (users go where other users are) plus brand. It is leaking, not impregnable: Tinder's daily users fell for over three years before returning to growth in September (source: https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/match-group-announces-third-quarter-results-302926778.html). [Likely] Revenue is guided flat to slightly down in Q4 (source: https://www.tipranks.com/news/the-fly/match-group-sees-q4-revenue-down-1-to-flat-at-865m-875m-thefly-news), and management flags an in-app fee margin headwind in 2027 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/mtch/transcripts/707185-q3-2026/). [Likely] Platform dependence is real: Apple pulled the Azar app for several weeks in 2026 and monetisation fell on the relaunch (SEC delta, 10-Q above). [Certain] Hinge growth offsets Tinder. [Likely]

## 3. Valuation
P/E 13.3x, EV/EBITDA 10.1x, P/FCF 7.8x and FCF yield 12.84%. Each sits at the bottom of its 5y range (P/E 1% position, EV/EBITDA 0%, P/FCF 1%; medians 15.2x, 12.8x, 12.0x) (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/mtch/financials/ratios/). Peers are noisy (P/E peer set n=2); P/FCF is 20% below the peer median of 9.8x, while EV/Sales is above peers on higher margins. The reverse DCF says the price implies year-1 revenue growth of -2.3%, fading to terminal growth, i.e. continued shrinkage with no turnaround. Model base fair value is 48.83 against a 39.03 close (+25.1%); bear 17.98, bull 54.56. The bear case shows how sensitive the DCF is to a revenue decline. [Likely] Share count is down 6.86% YoY, a buyback tailwind. (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/mtch/statistics/)

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Tinder payers or daily users (company quarterly release) fall year on year again for two consecutive quarters.
- Hinge revenue growth (company quarterly release) drops below 10% year on year.
- Operating margin (income statement page) falls below 24%, or FCF margin (cash flow page) below 25%.
- Net debt / EBITDA (ratios page) rises above 3.0x.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Cash conversion is strong and net debt/EBITDA has fallen from 3.50x in FY2021 to 2.51x TTM. The share count is shrinking, so FCF goes mostly to buybacks and debt service. Debt is mostly fixed-rate senior notes maturing 2027 to 2033 plus a 2030 exchangeable (SEC delta, 10-Q above), so refinancing in 2027 and 2028 is a watch item. [Likely] Altman Z is weak at 1.13, though Piotroski F is 7. (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/mtch/statistics/)

## 6. Unusual items
Short interest is 5.30% of float, up 5.1% on the month; beta is 1.46 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/mtch/statistics/). The price is 7.2% below its 50-day average after the Q3 print. The Azar App Store removal shows Apple and Google policy risk. [Likely] Reuters reported Tinder US daily users back to growth in October (source: https://www.reuters.com/technology/tinders-us-daily-active-users-return-growth-october-match-ramps-up-ai-efforts-2026-11-02/).
