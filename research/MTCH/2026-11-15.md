# Match Group (MTCH) — Core initiation — 2026-11-15
## 1. Business
Subscription dating apps; revenue is mostly direct payer subscriptions and in-app purchases, plus a small indirect (advertising) line. Q3 2026 segment revenue: Tinder 480,019 and Hinge 220,586 of 891,232 (USD thousands), Everyone Everywhere (Match, Azar, Pairs and others) 176,019 [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/891103/000089110326000163/mtch-20260930.htm). Geographic split: [data unavailable] in the sources read; no single foreign country is confirmed at ~10%+ [Guessing].

## 2. Quality
Profitability is high and rising: TTM ROIC 24.7%, ROCE 30.3%, gross margin 75.9%, FCF margin 32.7% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/mtch/financials/ratios/) [Certain]. Operating margin is down over five fiscal years but up over the last 12 months (26.4% to 31.1%), so the five-year "falling" label is stale [Certain]. Net debt/EBITDA is 2.51x and falling; Piotroski F is 7 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/mtch/statistics/) [Certain].
Moat: network effects in Tinder and Hinge. Tinder's US daily users returned to growth after a long decline, and Hinge grows at a double-digit rate (source: https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/match-group-announces-third-quarter-results-302926778.html) [Likely]. The moat is real but not impregnable: Tinder shrank for years, and Apple and Google take app-store fees and have shown they can change the rules, as with Azar's removal from the App Store [Likely].

## 3. Valuation
At 41.63 USD, P/E is 14.2x, EV/EBITDA 10.6x, P/FCF 8.3x and FCF yield 12.03%. Each sits in the bottom 1-5% of its own five-year range, and P/E and EV/EBITDA are about 62-63% below the peer median (BMBL, SPOT, PINS, GOOGL; the peers are richer, growthier businesses) [Certain]. The reverse DCF says the price implies 0.0% year-1 revenue growth, fading to terminal growth [Certain]. Base fair value is 46.75 (+12.3%), bear 17.24, bull 52.38 [Likely]. The bear case comes from a DCF that is very sensitive to the growth haircut [Guessing]. The price is above the 200-day average (+12.5%) with 12-month relative strength of +15.8pp versus SPY. The market is paying for a stable payer base, not a recovery [Likely].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Operating margin (TTM, income statement page) falls below 26%.
- FCF margin (TTM, cash flow page) falls below 25%.
- Net debt/EBITDA (statistics page) rises above 3.5x.
- Tinder US daily-active-user growth (company results release) turns negative again for two consecutive quarters.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
FCF conversion is 158% of net income TTM, and FCF margin is up 4.9pp in a year (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/mtch/financials/cash-flow-statement/) [Certain]. Shares outstanding are down 6.86% YoY, so buybacks are running at scale [Certain]. Leverage is falling. The senior notes are staggered, with maturities starting December 2027, and the revolver has $499.4 million of availability [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/891103/000089110326000163/mtch-20260930.htm) [Certain]. Altman Z of 1.13 is distorted by buyback-depleted equity [Guessing].

## 6. Unusual items
- The 10-Q filing delta shows the brand reorganisation into three segments (Evergreen and Emerging and MG Asia merged into E&E), so segment history is restated. It also shows the Azar removal from the App Store and its lower monetisation after relaunch (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/891103/000089110326000163/mtch-20260930.htm) [Certain].
- Management flags in-app-fee changes as a 2027 margin headwind (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/mtch/transcripts/707185-q3-2026/) [Likely].
- Q3 revenue fell year over year while EPS rose (source: https://www.tipranks.com/news/the-fly/match-group-reports-q3-eps-73c-vs-62c-last-year-thefly-news) [Likely]. Growth is not yet back.
- Beta is 1.46. Short interest is 5.20% of float.
