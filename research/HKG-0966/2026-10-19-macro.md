## Assessment
China Taiping's TTM ROE surge (9.5%→28.9%) and wide bear/bull fair-value range (HKD 12.36–31.79) are driven mainly by investment gains on its RMB-denominated float, not underwriting [Likely] **(inference** from the ratios page, https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/0966/financials/ratios/). Bull: China stimulus/equity rally sustains investment gains, US yields plateau → fair value drifts toward the bull case.
