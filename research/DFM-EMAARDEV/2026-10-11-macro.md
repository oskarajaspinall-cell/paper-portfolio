# Macro overlay: Emaar Development PJSC (EMAARDEV) — 2026-10-11
## Assessment
Chain: US rates -> AED peg (fixed since AED tracks USD) -> UAE mortgage/EIBOR rates -> Dubai buyer affordability/absorption -> off-plan sales velocity and pricing, with a second channel: dollar strength -> foreign (non-USD) buyer purchasing power -> demand [Likely] **(inference: peg transmission to local lending rates)**.

Market pricing (facts): US 10y Treasury 5.22%, +68bp over 3m; 10y real yield (TIPS) 2.87%, +56bp over 3m; fed funds 3.88%, +26bp over 3m; core CPI +0.98 index pts over 1m (FRED, https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=DGS10/DFII10/DFF/CPILFESL, 2026-10-08/2026-08-01) [Certain]. 2y yield (4.75%) sits ~87bp above fed funds — a proxy that the market expects further tightening/no near-term cuts rather than easing [Likely, proxy]. Broad dollar index 121.4, +2.9% over 1m (FRED DTWEXBGS, 2026-10-02) [Certain]. This is a genuine hawkish repricing versus three months ago, not a lagging read: both channels (local rates, dollar) have moved against Dubai housing demand at the margin **(inference)**.

Bull: US/UAE rates plateau or ease, dollar stabilizes → mortgage affordability and foreign-buyer demand hold, supporting the revenue-growth assumptions behind the valuation re-rating case.
Bear: rates keep rising and dollar keeps strengthening → buyer financing costs and foreign purchasing power erode → slower launch absorption/pricing power, delaying the multiple re-rating thesis.
Tilt: **toward bear, small** — both channels point the same way, but Emaar Development itself is net-cash (no financing exposure) and much of Dubai's buyer base is cash, not mortgage-dependent [Likely] **(inference)**; the peg also removes any AED translation risk to reported earnings, a macro positive.
