# H. Lundbeck A/S (CPH:HLUN.B) — Core initiation — 2026-11-15
## 1. Business
Copenhagen-listed specialty pharma selling branded neuroscience drugs (migraine, psychiatry, neurology), led by Vyepti and Rexulti, with a Parkinson's/MSA pipeline. Revenue grew across all regions in the nine months to September; Vyepti in the US was the main driver [Likely] (source: https://www.reuters.com/business/healthcare-pharmaceuticals/lundbeck-hikes-annual-forecasts-migraine-drug-powers-earnings-beat-2026-11-10/). I could not fetch a primary-source regional split, so the US share is [data unavailable]; a US-heavy mix is [Guessing].

## 2. Quality
Returns are decent and improving on the fiscal-year view. TTM ROIC is 13.7% (FY2021 8.8%), and operating margin is 24.4% (FY2021 13.8%) with gross margin 81.2% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/cph/HLUN.B/financials/ratios/) [Certain]. FCF margin is 20.3% and FCF/NI 129.3%. Net debt/EBITDA is 0.61x, down from 2.00x in FY2024 [Certain].

Direction check: TTM ROIC is 3.7pp below its 2-year high of 17.4%, and FCF margin 3.8pp below its 24.1% high (both September 2024). Over the last year ROIC is flat and operating margin is flat, so the FY trend overstates current momentum. FCF margin is up 8.5pp year on year [Certain].

Moat: patent-protected branded drugs, not a structural moat. Durability depends on Vyepti exclusivity and pipeline replacement [Likely]. The Q3 release raised full-year guidance [Certain] (source: https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/lundbeck-raises-financial-guidance-for-2026-302934778.html).

## 3. Valuation
P/E is 14.3x, above its 5y max of 13.9x (range 12.5-13.9x). EV/EBITDA is 7.6x against a 5.7-6.6x range, so it has re-rated. Against peers it is cheap: P/E is 60% below the peer median and EV/EBITDA 29% below [Certain]. FCF yield is 9.10% and P/FCF 11.0x, mid-range at 49% of the 5y band.

The price implies -0.5% revenue growth in year 1, fading to terminal growth, which is undemanding if Vyepti keeps growing [Likely]. The mechanical fair value (base 64.65 DKK, +8.9% vs 59.35 close) is flagged unreliable: DCF and P/E disagree 2.6x. Weigh it lightly [Certain].

Price is +37.7% above the 200-day average, RSI is 73.6, and the stock jumped 6.1% on 3.3x volume on 2026-11-10 after results. Much of the re-rating has already happened [Likely].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Operating margin (income statement, TTM) falls below 20%.
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) rises above 2.0x.
- FCF margin (cash flow statement, TTM) falls below 12%.
- Vyepti growth stalls or full-year guidance is cut at a later report (company release).

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Cash conversion is strong: FCF/NI is 129.3% TTM, with FY2024 (89.7%) the exception, when leverage rose to 2.00x [Certain]. Leverage has since fallen to 0.61x. Management cites deleveraging and pipeline investment (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/cph/HLUN.B/transcripts/777133-q3-2026/) [Likely]. Share count is flat (-0.03% YoY), so there is no dilution and no buyback support [Certain].
