# Guangdong Investment (HKG:0270) — Core initiation — 2026-11-09
## 1. Business
Hong Kong-listed holding company, subsidiary of GDH Limited. Segments: water resources (water distribution, sewage treatment), property investment, department stores, hotels, coal-fired power and toll roads (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/0270/company/) [Certain]. Water is the core earner [Likely]. Operations are Hong Kong and mainland China (Guangdong); the exact revenue split by region is [data unavailable] in the fact sheet and not verified from a primary source. Reports in HKD (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/0270/).

## 2. Quality
Returns are modest but stable: ROIC 8.1% TTM, ROE 9.2% FY2025, both flat over five years (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/0270/financials/ratios/) [Certain]. Operating margin rose from 26.9% to 39.2% (FY2021 to FY2025) and is 38.0% TTM, flat over the last year (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/0270/financials/income-statement/) [Certain]. Net debt/EBITDA fell from 3.96x (FY2022) to 0.60x TTM [Certain]. Piotroski F is 6 and beta 0.63 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/0270/statistics/) [Certain].
Moat: regulated or contracted water supply is hard to replicate [Likely], but returns sit near cost of capital, so the moat protects stability, not excess returns [Likely].
Direction flag: FCF margin is rising over five years but fell from 46.8% to 41.6% over the last 12 months, 15.0pp below its 2-year high of 56.5%. FY2023-24 FCF conversion above 300% looks like a working-capital or capex swing, not a run rate [Likely].

## 3. Valuation
P/E 10.9x versus a 5y median of 11.9x (30th percentile of range) and a peer median of 10.3x. EV/EBITDA 7.4x sits at the 9th percentile of its 5y range and 38% below peers (peer median 11.9x). P/B 0.9x. FCF yield 15.14%, but P/FCF at 6.6x is above its 5y maximum of 5.6x (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/0270/statistics/) [Certain].
Mechanical fair value is flagged unreliable: DCF base 16.63 HKD, dividend discount 6.00 HKD, P/E 9.01 HKD, against a last close of 8.16 HKD. Weigh it lightly [Certain]. The price implies negative year-1 revenue growth in the reverse DCF [Likely]. On earnings multiples the stock is fairly priced to cheap; EV/EBITDA is the only clear discount [Likely].

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Deleveraging has been the priority: net debt/EBITDA from 3.96x to 0.60x. FY2025 FCF conversion was 170.4% of net income, down from 321.5% in FY2024 [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/0270/financials/cash-flow-statement/). The company has a conglomerate structure with low-return department store, hotel and property assets alongside water [Likely]. Shares change and payout detail: [data unavailable].
