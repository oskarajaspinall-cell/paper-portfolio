# The National Shipping Company of Saudi Arabia (TADAWUL:4030) — Core initiation — 2026-10-24
## 1. Business
Bahri is a Saudi marine shipper (stockanalysis.com classifies it Marine Shipping) and earns charter and freight revenue from its fleet. Crude tankers are the core, which is why the peer set is listed tanker owners [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tadawul/4030/). It bought nine VLCCs in 2024, which shows it is growing the tanker fleet [Likely] (source: https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/saudis-bahri-signs-almost-1-billion-deal-nine-oil-tankers-2024-08-20/). I have not confirmed the segment mix from a primary source [Guessing].

## 2. Quality
Returns have risen strongly: ROIC went from 1.9% (FY2021) to 10.9% (FY2025) and 24.7% TTM; operating margin went from 7.9% to 26.3% and 39.0% TTM (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tadawul/4030/financials/ratios/). Net debt/EBITDA fell from 5.79x to 0.80x TTM; Piotroski F is 7 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tadawul/4030/statistics/). [Certain]

The weakness is durability. Tanker charter rates are a commodity cycle, and the TTM margins are well above any FY level, so they probably sit near a cycle high [Likely]. Without a moat, high returns should attract new ships and compete themselves away [Likely]. Bahri's one possible edge is its Saudi relationship, which I have not verified [Guessing]. FCF was negative in FY2024 (-21.8%) and FY2025 (-9.9%) because fleet capex outran earnings (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tadawul/4030/financials/cash-flow-statement/) [Certain].

## 3. Valuation
The price is 34.80 SAR (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tadawul/4030/history/). On TTM, P/E is 5.0x against a 5y median of 11.0x and a peer median of 7.5x. EV/EBITDA is 4.3x against 8.8x and 7.2x (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tadawul/4030/financials/ratios/). FCF yield is 13.82% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tadawul/4030/statistics/). [Certain]

The market is pricing peak earnings as temporary. The mechanical base fair value is 21.53 SAR, 38.1% below the price, and the bear case is 16.81. The bull case is 77.57 (source: fact sheet, research/TADAWUL-4030/factsheet-2026-10-24.md) [Certain]. P/B is 1.6x, at the top of its 1.3–1.6x 5y range, so the discount rests entirely on earnings that may not last. The base case says the stock is not cheap on normalised earnings [Likely].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Operating margin (income statement page) falls below 25%, i.e. back to the FY2025 level, which would confirm the TTM peak was transient.
- ROIC (ratios page) falls below 10%.
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) rises above 2.5x.
- FCF margin (cash flow page) stays negative for the next two reported fiscal years.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Capex has been heavy: FCF/NI was -95.1% in FY2024 and -42.3% in FY2025, against 69.5% TTM. Leverage is manageable at 0.80x TTM (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tadawul/4030/statistics/).
