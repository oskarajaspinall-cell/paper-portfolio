# HCA Healthcare, Inc. (HCA) — Core initiation — 2026-10-09

## 1. Business
HCA operates ~190 acute-care hospitals plus surgery centers, imaging and freestanding ERs across 20 US states and the UK, earning fee-for-service revenue from admissions, outpatient visits and procedures paid by commercial insurers, Medicare/Medicaid and self-pay patients [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/hca/). Medicare and Managed Medicare were ~32.7% of 2025 revenue [Certain] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/860730/000119312526044769/hca-20251231.htm). Scale lets it cluster facilities densely within metro markets to negotiate payer rates and spread capex.

## 2. Quality
ROIC (21.8% TTM) and ROCE (26.3% TTM, +3.5pp over 5y) are high and improving — evidence of pricing power and scale [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/hca/financials/ratios/). The durable edge is largely regulatory: many states run Certificate-of-Need laws restricting new hospital supply, and HCA's market-density strategy raises the capital/permitting bar for new entrants [Likely] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/860730/000119312526044769/hca-20251231.htm). But margin trend is weakening below the gross line: net margin fell 2.9pp over 5y to 8.8% TTM even as gross margin rose 3.2pp to 42.6% [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/hca/financials/income-statement/). This matches the Q2 2026 transcript's disclosure of a $400m EBITDA headwind from a 15% drop in exchange-plan patients, partly offset by Medicaid supplemental payments [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/hca/transcripts/654467-q2-2026/). Net debt/EBITDA is elevated and rising (3.20x TTM, +0.26x over 5y) and Altman Z (2.64) sits in the distress "grey zone," leaving limited balance-sheet slack if payer mix keeps shifting adverse [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/hca/statistics/).

## 3. Valuation
At 445.01 USD, HCA trades on 14.9x TTM P/E (81st percentile of its own 5y range of 11.5x–15.7x) and 9.3x EV/EBITDA (47th percentile of 8.7x–10.0x) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/hca/financials/ratios/). Versus peers UHS, THC, CYH and ENSG, HCA is priced well above the group on every multiple: P/E +73%, EV/EBITDA +29%, EV/Sales +61%, P/FCF +31% [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/uhs/statistics/, https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/thc/statistics/, https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/cyh/statistics/, https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/ensg/statistics/). The fact sheet's own-history DCF (best method) puts base fair value at 396.35 USD, 10.9% below last close; bear 298.05 USD (-33.0%), bull 571.97 USD (+28.5%) [Certain] (source: research/HCA/factsheet-2026-10-09.md). The reverse DCF shows the price already assumes 6.8% revenue growth in year 1 fading to terminal growth — optimistic given the exchange-patient headwind above [Likely] (source: research/HCA/factsheet-2026-10-09.md). Shares outstanding fell 8.97% YoY, so recent EPS growth has been flattered by buybacks rather than purely organic profit growth [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/hca/statistics/).

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Net margin (income statement page) falls below 7% TTM, confirming payer-mix/exchange headwinds are structural, not transitory.
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) rises above 3.5x, signalling leverage is eroding the balance-sheet cushion.
- ROIC or ROCE (ratios page) falls below its 5y low (18.0% / 21.3%), indicating the pricing-power/scale moat is eroding.
- Piotroski F-Score (statistics page) falls below 5, indicating broad fundamental deterioration.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
FCF conversion (FCF/NI) is strong and rising: 87.7% TTM (+36.0pp over 5y), with FY2025 conversion above 100% [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/hca/financials/cash-flow-statement/). FCF yield is 6.22% [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/hca/statistics/). Capital is returned mainly via buybacks (shares down 8.97% YoY), which has driven book equity to near-zero/negative (ROE in the hundreds of percent FY2021–24; FY2025 [data unavailable]) rather than through deleveraging, even as net debt/EBITDA keeps rising [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/hca/financials/ratios/). The 10-K cautions there is "no assurance" dividends continue [Likely] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/860730/000119312526044769/hca-20251231.htm).

## 6. Unusual items
None material. Short interest (4.49% of float, +5.8% month-on-month) and institutional ownership (62.2%) are unremarkable [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/hca/statistics/).
