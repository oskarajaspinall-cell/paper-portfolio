# Macro overlay: HCA Healthcare, Inc. (HCA) — 2026-10-09
## Assessment
HCA's thesis (regulated-moat hospital operator, premium multiple, 3.20x net debt/EBITDA and rising) makes it rate-sensitive on the discount-rate leg, even though demand/payer-mix (ACA exchange attrition, Medicaid supplements) is the dominant earnings driver, not macro [Likely] **(inference)**. Chain: real/nominal yields and credit spreads -> WACC and refinancing cost -> DCF fair value and the premium multiple the market pays. Facts [Certain] (FRED, https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=DFII10, DGS10, DGS2, DFF, BAMLH0A0HYM2, 2026-10-07): 10y real yield 2.92% (+0.49pt/1m, +0.61pt/3m); 10y nominal 5.28% (+0.50pt/1m); effective fed funds 3.88% (+0.25pt/1m, i.e. a hike just happened); HY credit spread 3.09% (+0.41pt/1m). Market pricing proxy: 2y yield (4.77%) sits ~0.89pt above fed funds (3.88%), implying the market is not pricing near-term cuts and may expect further tightening — a genuine shift versus a prior easing-path assumption **(inference)**. The fact sheet's fair value (298/396/572) already uses today's 5.28% risk-free rate, so the back-up is partly embedded; further yield/spread widening would still compress it further given HCA's leverage. **Macro bull**: yields/spreads roll over -> WACC falls -> fair value and the ~30-70% peer premium hold or extend toward 572. **Macro bear**: real yields/HY spreads keep rising -> WACC up, refinancing cost up -> fair value compresses toward 298 and the premium multiple mean-reverts toward peers. **Tilt**: moderate toward bear — the 1-month real-yield and fed-funds moves are large, recent and go the wrong way for a levered, premium-multiple name, though company-specific payer-mix trends still dominate the overall thesis [Likely].
