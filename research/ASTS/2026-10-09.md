# AST SpaceMobile, Inc. (ASTS) — Core initiation — 2026-10-09

## 1. Business
AST SpaceMobile is building a low-earth-orbit satellite constellation (BlueWalker/BlueBird) that connects standard, unmodified smartphones directly to broadband 4G/5G, monetised via revenue-share agreements with mobile network operators rather than direct billing. It has partnered with "over 60" operators covering a claimed 3bn+ subscribers, including AT&T, Verizon, Google, Rakuten, Bell and STC [Likely] (source: https://ast-science.com/). The company is still pre-commercial-scale: TTM revenue is a small fraction of its cost base (operating margin -410% TTM) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/asts/financials/income-statement/).

## 2. Quality
This is not yet a good business by any profitability test: ROCE -8.4% TTM (improving from -70.7% in FY2023), ROE -45.6% TTM, net margin -536.7% TTM, and FCF margin -1,420.7% TTM, all deeply negative [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/asts/financials/ratios/). The 5y trend shows margins improving off a worse base, but FCF conversion is deteriorating (-109.2pp over 5y) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/asts/financials/cash-flow-statement/). The potential moat is real but unproven: direct-to-device connectivity needs scarce spectrum priority rights and a dense, expensive LEO constellation — high capital and regulatory barriers that make replication hard for a pure new entrant [Likely] (source: https://ast-science.com/). But SpaceX's Starlink is expanding its own direct-to-cell service and is a far better-capitalised rival already in commercial service with T-Mobile, which directly threatens the carrier-partnership moat ASTS is counting on [Likely] (source: https://www.tipranks.com/news/ast-spacemobile-stock-plunges-today-but-dont-panic-says-top-investor). Shares outstanding grew 39.7% YoY, diluting any eventual per-share value [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/asts/statistics/).

## 3. Valuation
The market is pricing a successful, large-scale buildout. EV/Sales of 198.3x is 760% above the peer median (23.0x, n=4: GSAT, IRDM, VSAT, RKLB), though only the 19th percentile of its own 5y range (25.7x-932.2x) — the whole peer group has re-rated, not just ASTS [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/asts/statistics/). P/B of 9.0x is above its 5y max (8.5x) but 19% below the peer median (11.2x) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/asts/statistics/). P/E, EV/EBITDA and P/FCF are unavailable (earnings, EBITDA and FCF are all negative) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/asts/financials/ratios/). No fair value is computable: negative FCF and negative TTM EBITDA rule out both the DCF and EV/EBITDA methods [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/asts/financials/ratios/). Investors must underwrite a multi-year path to profitable scale with no valuation anchor available.

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROCE (ratios page) fails to continue improving from -8.4% TTM over the next two fiscal years (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/asts/financials/ratios/)
- FCF margin (cash flow/income statement pages) does not narrow from -1,420.7% TTM as the constellation reaches commercial scale (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/asts/financials/cash-flow-statement/)
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) reverts from net-cash (-1.74x TTM) to sustained high leverage without a matching revenue ramp (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/asts/financials/ratios/)
- A confirmed loss of a major carrier partnership (e.g., AT&T or Verizon) to a competing direct-to-cell provider, reported in a filing or company release

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
As of 2026-06-30, AST SpaceMobile held approximately $2.29bn in cash and cash equivalents against roughly $2.97bn of total debt, carried mainly as convertible notes maturing 2032-2036 (coupons 1.625%-4.25%) [Certain] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1780312/000119312526342550/asts-20260630.htm). Property and equipment rose from $1.4bn (FY2025-end) to $2.07bn (2026-06-30), consistent with continued satellite construction spend [Likely] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1780312/000119312526342550/asts-20260630.htm). FCF conversion is not a meaningful metric here: the company is self-funding a capital-intensive buildout from its balance sheet and capital markets (shares outstanding +39.7% YoY), not distributing cash [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/asts/statistics/).

## 6. Unusual items
Short interest is high at 24.49% of float (rising +1.7% vs prior month), and beta is elevated at 2.71, both consistent with a volatile, heavily-debated story stock [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/asts/statistics/). Multiple shareholder law firms (Kaplan Fox, Robbins LLP, Bernstein Liebhard, Grabar Law) have filed or are soliciting plaintiffs for securities class actions alleging AST SpaceMobile misled investors about its "competitive position in the satellite D2C market," with a lead-plaintiff deadline of 2026-11-13 [Certain] (source: https://www.newsfilecorp.com/release/315973/Robbins-LLP-is-Investigating-Allegations-that-AST-SpaceMobile-Inc.-Misled-Investors-Regarding-its-Competitive-Position-in-the-Satellite-D2C-Market). The allegations go directly to the competitive-moat claim underpinning this thesis and are a material legal overhang [Likely]. Separately, the stock fell as much as -15.7% intraday on 2026-10-09 on reports that SpaceX is expanding its own direct-to-cell wireless ambitions, the clearest near-term evidence of the competitive threat in Section 2 [Certain] (source: https://www.tipranks.com/news/the-fly/ast-spacemobile-falls-15-7-thefly-news).
