# EOG Resources (EOG) — Core initiation — 2026-11-21
## 1. Business
Independent US shale oil and gas producer; it earns money by selling crude, NGLs and natural gas from the Delaware Basin, Utica and Eagle Ford, with smaller Trinidad and Middle East (UAE, Bahrain) operations. Revenue follows commodity prices and volume. No single foreign country is shown as ~10%+ of revenue in the sources read [Likely]. Trinidad and the Gulf programmes are early or small (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/821189/000082118926000227/eog-20260930.htm).

## 2. Quality
Good operator, but returns are commodity-cyclical, not moat-protected [Likely]. TTM ROIC is 22.7% vs 16.0% in FY2025 and 32.6% in FY2022: falling over five years, rising over the last 12 months (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/eog/financials/ratios/?p=trailing). Operating margin 35.5% and FCF margin 25.3%, both up on a year ago and FCF margin at its 2-year high. Net debt/EBITDA is 0.16x, so the balance sheet is a real strength; Altman Z 3.86 and Piotroski 7 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/eog/statistics/) [Certain]. Durable edge is low-cost, high-return inventory and discipline, which is real but shared with the best peers [Likely]. The 5-year decline in ROIC shows returns follow prices, so the TTM rebound should not be extrapolated [Likely].

## 3. Valuation
P/E 9.6x vs a 5y range of 9.3x-11.4x (16th percentile) and peers at 12.0x (-20%). EV/EBITDA 5.0x is below its 5y minimum of 5.3x, and 6% under peers. P/FCF 10.4x is below its 5y minimum, and FCF yield is 9.58% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/eog/statistics/). P/B 2.3x sits well above peers (+66%), reflecting higher returns [Likely]. The mechanical fair value is 154.15 vs a 148.60 close (+3.7%), a single method (EV/EBITDA) because the reserve NAV is [data unavailable]; bear-to-bull range is narrow, so low reliability [Likely]. The price assumes mid-cycle commodity earnings roughly in line with TTM, not a peak premium; it is cheap against own history on earnings and cash flow but not against fair value.

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROIC (ratios page, TTM) falls below 15% for two consecutive quarters.
- FCF margin (cash-flow statement, TTM) falls below 15%.
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) rises above 1.0x without a matching acquisition.
- Shares outstanding change YoY (statistics page) turns positive, i.e. buybacks stop shrinking the count.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
FCF conversion is 90.2% of net income TTM vs 69.3% in FY2025. Shares are down 3.50% YoY (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/eog/statistics/). The board lifted the buyback authorization from $10 billion to $20 billion effective May 20, 2026 [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/821189/000082118926000227/eog-20260930.htm). Q3 results came with an 8% dividend increase (source: https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/eog-resources-reports-third-quarter-2026-results-and-raises-regular-dividend-8-302931110.html) [Certain]. EOG sold its Barnett Shale and Woodford Oil Window interests, both closed, which is consistent with focus on top-return plays [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/821189/000082118926000227/eog-20260930.htm) [Likely]. Cash rose over the first nine months of 2026 [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/821189/000082118926000227/eog-20260930.htm).
