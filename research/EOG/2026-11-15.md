# EOG Resources (EOG) — Core initiation — 2026-11-15
## 1. Business
Independent oil and gas explorer and producer. Revenue comes from selling crude oil, NGLs and natural gas, mainly from US shale (Delaware Basin, Eagle Ford, Utica), plus gas in Trinidad and early UAE/Bahrain programmes [Likely] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/821189/000082118926000227/eog-20260930.htm). Earnings follow commodity prices, so realised price and unit cost drive margins. No single foreign country is evidently ~10% of revenue, but I have not verified the split [Guessing].

## 2. Quality
Good, but cyclical, business. TTM ROIC is 22.7% and operating margin 35.5%, against 16.0% and 28.7% in FY2025 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/eog/financials/ratios/). The 5-year fiscal trend is falling (ROIC -4.7pp), yet the last 12 months rose (ROIC 17.6% to 22.7%) and sit 2.7pp below the 2-year high. Most of the recovery is higher commodity prices plus record volumes, so it is cyclical, not structural [Likely]. The durable edge is low-cost premium inventory and a net-cash-like balance sheet (net debt/EBITDA 0.16x; Piotroski 7) [Likely]. Margins above 25% FCF are mid-cycle-plus, not a floor [Likely]. Q3 beat on revenue and production, and guidance was held (source: https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/us-shale-producer-eog-resources-beats-third-quarter-profit-estimates-2026-11-05/) [Likely].

## 3. Valuation
Price 158.32 USD. P/E 10.3x is 46% through its 5y range and 21% below the peer median (COP, DVN, FANG, OXY). EV/EBITDA 5.3x is at the bottom of its narrow range and 5% below peers. P/FCF 11.1x is at 10% of its range; FCF yield 8.99%. P/B is above peers, which reflects higher returns [Likely]. The mechanical base fair value (EV/EBITDA method only, as reserve NAV is unavailable) is 154.15, 2.6% below the price, so there is no discount. The stock is 13.7% above its 200-day average with 12-month change +45.6%. The price assumes earnings stay near current peak-ish levels; it pays for a working business, not a bargain [Likely].

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
FCF conversion is 90.2% TTM, up from 69.3% in FY2025. Shares fell 3.50% YoY. The board lifted the repurchase authorisation to $20 billion in May 2026 [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/821189/000082118926000227/eog-20260930.htm) and raised the dividend 8% [Certain] (source: https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/eog-resources-reports-third-quarter-2026-results-and-raises-regular-dividend-8-302931110.html). Disposals of non-core Barnett Shale and Woodford Oil Window closed in Sept/Oct 2026, concentrating capital on the best plays [Certain] (same 10-Q). Net debt/EBITDA rose from -0.10x in FY2024 to 0.49x in FY2025 after the Encino acquisition, and is 0.16x now [Likely].
