# Macro overlay: EOG Resources (EOG) — 2026-10-08

## Assessment
EOG's thesis is bottom-up (low-cost multi-basin position, buybacks, valuation discount to peers), but revenue is explicitly commodity-price-driven [Certain] (research/EOG/2026-10-08.md). The macro snapshot has no direct oil/gas price series, so the only usable channel is indirect: **dollar strength & real-yield repricing → USD-priced commodity pricing → EOG realized price/revenue**, plus a minor discount-rate effect on the multiple.

What's priced: 10y real yield (DFII10) jumped +0.48pp in one month to 2.91% (2026-10-06), nearly all of the 10y nominal move (DGS10 5.27%, +0.49pp/1m), while breakeven inflation was flat (T10YIE 2.36%, +0.01pp/1m) [Certain, FRED]. That split signals a real-rate/growth repricing, not an inflation scare. The 2y yield (4.79%) ~91bp above fed funds (3.88%) proxies a market pricing higher-for-longer policy [Likely] **(inference, proxy)**. The dollar index rose +2.86 points (~2.4%) in one month to 121.385 (2026-10-02) [Certain, FRED]; dollar strength is historically a headwind for USD-priced oil, though pass-through is inferred, not confirmed by a crude series **(inference)**.

Bull: dollar/real-yield pressure fades, oil holds/firms, FCF (8.74% yield) sustains buybacks, multiple re-rates toward the 16.1x peer median. Bear: dollar strength plus higher-for-longer real rates weigh on oil pricing, further compressing FCF margin (25.0%→15.2%, FY21-25) and keeping the multiple near 5y lows. Tilt: small, toward bear — the move is real but the oil linkage is a proxy, and EOG's low leverage (0.23x net debt/EBITDA) and low beta (0.37) cushion the equity.
