## 2. Quality
Cyclical and capital-hungry, not a compounder [Likely]. ROIC went 13.1% (FY2022), 4.8% (FY2024), -7.8% (FY2025), 12.3% (FY2026); operating margin 22.7% in FY2026 after -14.8% in FY2025 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/asx/MIN/financials/ratios/). FCF margin was negative for four straight years before turning to 13.5%, and FCF/NI is 82.1% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/asx/MIN/financials/cash-flow-statement/). The rebound is real and still rising on a rolling-12-month basis, but the history shows returns are commodity-cycle driven, not protected by a durable moat [Likely]. Management says the recovery was helped by record services and lithium volumes and de-leveraging [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/asx/MIN/transcripts/549710-q4-2026/). Net debt/EBITDA fell from 7.15x to 1.74x (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/asx/MIN/financials/balance-sheet/); Altman Z 2.01 is grey-zone (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/asx/MIN/statistics/).

## 3. Valuation
P/E 8.7x and EV/EBITDA 5.5x both sit below their 5y minimums (11.5x and 7.2x), and 23-42% below peers' medians (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/asx/MIN/financials/ratios/). FCF yield is 9.51% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/asx/MIN/statistics/). But FY2026 earnings are near a recovery high after a loss year, so low multiples on cyclical profit may be a peak-earnings signal [Likely]. Base fair value 50.84 AUD is only +9.1% above the 46.59 close; bear 27.79 (-40.4%), bull 139.88; the NAV method was unavailable so EV/EBITDA alone drives it [Certain]. The stock is -20.8% under its 200-day average with 6-month momentum of -32.8%, and 12-month relative strength lags SPY (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/asx/MIN/history/). The market is pricing a commodity downturn; the fair value shows little margin of safety against it [Likely].

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Past years burned cash on heavy growth capex: FCF margin was -50.9% in FY2024 and -58.8% in FY2025, funded by debt. FY2026 reversed this and cut leverage. Shares outstanding rose 0.91% YoY (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/asx/MIN/statistics/). Management guides to brownfield expansion and a copper focus [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/asx/MIN/transcripts/578124-h2-2026/), which risks a return to heavy spending; the discipline is unproven through a cycle [Guessing].
