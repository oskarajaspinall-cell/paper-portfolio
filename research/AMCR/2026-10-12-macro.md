# Macro overlay: Amcor plc (AMCR) — 2026-10-12
## Assessment
AMCR's packaging revenue is largely contract/pass-through and macro-insensitive [Likely], but the balance sheet is not: net debt/EBITDA 3.97x TTM and an Altman Z-score of 1.37 (distress zone) are already flagged thesis risks (fact sheet, ratios/statistics pages). Chain: **rising real yields & widening credit spreads → higher cost of capital/refinancing → discount rate and equity risk premium on a leveraged, low-growth, bond-proxy equity (beta 0.57) → valuation multiple and interest expense**.

What's priced: US 10y real yield (TIPS) is 2.87% (2026-10-08), up +0.56pp over 3 months [Certain, FRED DFII10]; US HY credit spread is 3.15%, up +0.45pp over 3 months [Certain, FRED BAMLH0A0HYM2]; the 2y Treasury (4.75%) sits above effective fed funds (3.88%), a proxy suggesting the market is not pricing near-term cuts **(inference)**. AMCR has underperformed SPY by -14.6pp (6m) and -11.0pp (12m) (fact sheet, HI/SPY-HI), which plausibly reflects this tightening already working through a levered, high-payout stock **(inference)**; commentator pieces framing AMCR as a bond-proxy ("yields 5.5%", Barron's 2026-08-12, cited only to describe market framing, not as fact) support this read.

Bull: yields/spreads ease → cost of capital falls, multiple re-rates toward peers, deleveraging accelerates. Bear: further hawkish repricing or spread widening → multiple compresses further, refinancing cost rises on an already distress-flagged balance sheet. Tilt: moderate toward bear — the combination of rising real yields and widening HY spreads hits a specifically leverage-flagged name, though merger-synergy execution remains the dominant driver.
