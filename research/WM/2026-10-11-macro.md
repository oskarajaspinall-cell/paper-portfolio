# Macro overlay: Waste Management, Inc. (WM) — 2026-10-11
## Assessment
WM's revenue is fee-based and structurally inelastic (landfill scarcity, permitting barriers) [Likely], so macro acts almost entirely via the discount rate on its premium, bond-like multiple (beta 0.43, fact sheet ST [Certain]), not via earnings. Chain: real yields -> cost of equity -> DCF fair value -> multiple. Fact: the US 10y real yield (DFII10) is 2.87% on 2026-10-08, up 56bp over 3 months (FRED, https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=DFII10); the 10y Treasury (DGS10) is 5.22%, up 68bp over 3 months (https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=DGS10). Market pricing, proxy **(inference)**: the 2y yield (4.75%, DGS2) sits above the effective fed funds rate (3.88%, DFF; both 2026-10-08), implying the market is not pricing near-term cuts and may expect policy to stay firm - unhelpful for a long-duration defensive at a premium multiple. The fact sheet's own CAPM/DCF already uses today's 5.22% risk-free rate and still puts the bull case ($201.13) below last close ($208.83); WM has lagged SPY by 13.4pp over 3 months as real yields rose (fact sheet HI/SPY-HI) - rate-driven multiple compression already underway, not exhausted if yields keep climbing. **Bull (inference):** real yields roll over -> discount rate eases -> the fair-value gap to price narrows, multiple re-rates up. **Bear:** real yields extend higher -> DCF fair value falls further below an already-stretched price, widening downside. **Tilt:** toward bear, moderate - rates are moving the direction that hurts WM's multiple, reinforcing (not offsetting) the fundamental case that price already exceeds the bull scenario.
