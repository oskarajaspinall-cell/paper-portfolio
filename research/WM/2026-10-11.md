# Waste Management, Inc. (WM) — Core initiation — 2026-10-11
## 1. Business
WM is North America's largest vertically integrated waste company: it collects, transfers, recycles and disposes of municipal, commercial and industrial waste through owned collection fleets, transfer stations, recycling facilities and landfills, plus renewable natural gas (RNG) and Healthcare Solutions (regulated medical waste, ex-Stericycle). Revenue is fee-based collection and disposal, supplemented by recyclables and RNG sales (source: fact sheet OV).

## 2. Quality
Moat: WM owns/controls most of the economically viable landfill capacity in its markets; new-landfill permitting is near-impossible, giving disposal pricing power that's structural [Likely]. Vertical integration (collection-transfer-disposal-RNG) captures margin non-integrated haulers can't. ROIC is flat at 11.6% TTM vs 10.8% FY21 (+0.3pp/5y); ROE rose to 29.8% TTM from 24.9% FY21 (+4.8pp), partly via rising leverage (net debt/EBITDA up to 2.93x TTM from 2.79x FY21) rather than pure returns gains [Likely]. Margins are genuinely expanding: gross margin +2.4pp, operating margin +2.1pp over 5y to 40.6%/18.9% TTM, consistent with pricing power, not mix [Certain]. FCF conversion is volatile (125.2% TTM, falling 5y trend -30pp), reflecting lumpy capex and the Stericycle integration, not core cash deterioration [Likely]. CEO transition - Jim Fish retiring, John Morris named next CEO, announced 2026-08-26 (SEC 8-K Item 5.02) - is a leadership change to monitor, with no disclosed strategy shift [Likely].

## 3. Valuation
Current P/E 29.5x sits at the 8th percentile of its own 5y range (28.8x-38.4x) [Certain]; EV/EBITDA 13.7x and P/FCF 23.4x sit below their 5y minimums (-28% and -49%), unusual for a quality compounder. Against peers (RSG, WCN, GFL, CWST), WM trades at a discount on every earnings/cash multiple: P/E -20%, EV/EBITDA -13%, EV/Sales -12%, P/FCF -43% [Certain]. The exception is P/B at +104% above peer median, reflecting WM's higher ROE (29.8%, vs peers' lower leverage), not overvaluation [Likely]. The fact sheet's own DCF/EV-EBITDA fair value puts base case at $180.01 (-13.8% vs the $208.83 last close); even bull case $201.13 is below last close (-3.7%) [Certain]. So while WM looks cheap on relative multiples and its own history, the reverse-DCF fair value implies the price assumes execution above the bull scenario - relative and intrinsic valuation conflict, which sizing must reconcile.

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) rises above 3.5x, showing the Stericycle/Healthcare Solutions integration is being funded with more leverage than the core waste business can service.
- FCF conversion, FCF/NI (cash-flow & income statement) falls below 70% for two consecutive fiscal years, showing cash generation is structurally impaired, not just lumpy capex.
- Operating margin (income statement) falls below 17% for two consecutive quarters, indicating pricing power or cost discipline is eroding.
- ROIC (ratios page) falls below 10%, indicating returns on the landfill/collection asset base are being diluted by lower-return acquisitions.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
FCF margin is 13.9% TTM, though the 5y trend is falling (-2.4pp) and FCF conversion swings between 78.6% (FY24) and 134.0% (FY21), reflecting heavy, lumpy capex (collection fleet, landfill cells, RNG facilities) and the Stericycle acquisition [Certain] (source: fact sheet CF). Net debt/EBITDA has risen to 2.93x TTM from 2.79x FY21 as M&A was debt-funded [Certain]. Shares outstanding are flat (+0.03% YoY), so shareholder returns come via the dividend ($0.945/quarter, declared 2026-08-24) rather than buybacks [Certain]. Institutional ownership is high at 84.7% [Certain] (source: fact sheet ST).

## 6. Unusual items
CEO transition: Jim Fish (CEO) to retire; John Morris named next CEO, announced 2026-08-26 and filed same day as an SEC 8-K Item 5.02 [Certain] (source: https://www.sec.gov/cgi-bin/browse-edgar?action=getcompany&CIK=WM&type=8-K&dateb=&owner=include&count=10). No disclosed strategy change accompanies it [Likely]. Short interest rose +34.6% month-over-month to 1.96% of float - still low in absolute terms but a directional shift worth monitoring [Likely] (source: fact sheet ST/News).
