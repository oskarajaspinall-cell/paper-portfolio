# Macro overlay: Solventum Corporation (SOLV) — 2026-10-12
## Assessment
SOLV's revenue (hospital/dental consumables, biopharma filtration) is not materially macro-cyclical, but its balance sheet is: net debt/EBITDA is 4.29x (post-2024 3M spin-off) and Altman Z is 1.74 (distress zone), while TTM FCF margin is negative and management kept buying back stock ($288m in Q2 2026) [Certain] (fact sheet BS/IS/ST). Chain: rising rates and widening credit spreads → higher refinancing cost on existing leverage → margin/FCF pressure and a higher equity risk premium → multiple **(inference)**.

What's priced: the 10y Treasury yield is 5.22% (2026-10-08), up 68bp/3m and 42bp/1m; the 10y real yield (TIPS) is 2.87%, up 56bp/44bp over the same windows [Certain] (fred.stlouisfed.org, DGS10/DFII10, 2026-10-08). Fed funds rose to 3.88% (+26bp/3m) and the 2y Treasury (4.75%) sits above fed funds — a proxy the market expects policy to stay tight, not ease [Likely] **(inference)**. The US HY credit spread is 3.15%, up 45bp/3m (BAMLH0A0HYM2, 2026-10-08) — well below stress (>5%) but moving the wrong way for a levered, cash-negative issuer. Breakevens (2.33%) are roughly flat, so this is a real-rate/credit move, not an inflation-expectations shift.

Bull: spreads/yields stabilize or retreat → refinancing cost eases, multiple holds. Bear: yields/spreads keep rising toward the 5% HY-stress level → refinancing gets materially costlier while FCF is negative → margin and multiple compression, Altman Z deteriorates further. Fundamentals (HIS separation execution, one-off tariff refund inflating TTM margin) remain the primary driver; macro tilts the balance-of-risk toward the bear case given the leverage.
