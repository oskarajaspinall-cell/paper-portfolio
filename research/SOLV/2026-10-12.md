# Solventum Corporation (SOLV) — Core initiation — 2026-10-12

## 1. Business
Solventum makes and sells medical, dental and purification products across four segments: MedSurg (wound care, infection prevention, patient monitoring — V.A.C. therapy, Littmann), Dental (adhesives, impression materials, Clarity aligners), Health Information Systems (clinical documentation/coding software, now being separated), and Purification & Filtration (biopharma, industrial water, food, residential). It earns revenue selling consumables, devices and software/services to hospitals, dental professionals, biopharma manufacturers and water customers globally. [Certain] (source: https://www.solventum.com/en-us/home/our-company/)

## 2. Quality
ROIC fell from 12.0% (FY2021) to 5.5% TTM and ROCE from 14.8% to 6.0%; gross margin fell from 60.2% to 54.8% and operating margin from 22.9% to 7.6% [Certain] (source: fact sheet RA/IS). FCF margin turned negative (-1.4% TTM) versus consistently >100% conversion through FY2023 [Certain] (source: fact sheet CF/IS). Net debt/EBITDA rose from near-zero to 4.29x, debt taken on at the 2024 spin-off from 3M [Certain] (source: fact sheet BS/IS). MedSurg's V.A.C. wound-therapy franchise and Dental's Clarity aligners (15.2% organic growth) show real brand share, but no segment has shown pricing power to defend margins against tariffs, inflation and spin-off dis-synergies [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/solv/transcripts/660958-q2-2026/). Management is also carving out the faster-growing, higher-margin HIS unit, leaving RemainCo roughly 400bps more margin-dilutive before offsets [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/solv/transcripts/744369-wells-fargo-21st-annual-healthcare-conference/). Altman Z of 1.74 sits in the distress-risk zone [Certain] (source: fact sheet ST).

## 3. Valuation
P/E of 10.4x sits at the 11th percentile of its own 5y range and 61% below the peer median [Certain], but TTM net margin (17.3%) is inflated by a one-off $100m tariff refund, not normalized earnings power [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/solv/transcripts/660958-q2-2026/). EV/EBITDA (17.3x, above the 5y max, +71% vs peer median) and EV/Sales (2.4x, above the 5y max) show the market paying a premium to both history and peers [Certain] (source: fact sheet RA). P/B of 3.1x is 83% above the peer median of 1.7x despite lower and falling ROE/ROIC [Certain]. FCF yield is negative (-0.79%) [Certain] (source: fact sheet ST). The base fair value of $133.79 (+53%) rests entirely on the P/E method, DCF being unusable with a negative base FCF margin [Certain] (source: fact sheet); since that P/E is flattered by the one-off refund, the upside likely overstates fair value [Likely]. On a cash-flow and enterprise-value basis the stock looks expensive, not cheap.

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROIC (ratios page) falls further below 5%, confirming no floor under returns on capital.
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) rises above 5x, showing spin-off leverage is not being repaid.
- FCF margin (cash flow + income statement) stays negative for two more consecutive fiscal years, showing the cash drag is structural, not separation-related.
- RemainCo organic growth (next earnings release) falls below the 4%-5% 2028 target management reiterated at the Wells Fargo conference.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Shares outstanding are roughly flat (+0.20% YoY) [Certain] (source: fact sheet ST), yet the company repurchased $288m of stock in Q2 2026 while TTM FCF margin was negative and net debt/EBITDA stood at 4.29x [Certain]/[Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/solv/transcripts/660958-q2-2026/). Separation costs (TSA set-up, dis-synergies) add further temporary cash demands this year, and management has not updated its long-range operating-margin target (23%-25%) pending the separation's final structure [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/solv/transcripts/744369-wells-fargo-21st-annual-healthcare-conference/). Buying back stock ahead of deleveraging, while FCF is negative, is poor sequencing given leverage taken on at the 2024 spin-off.

## 6. Unusual items
Net debt/EBITDA jumped from near-zero to 4.29x at the 2024 spin-off from 3M and has kept rising [Certain] (source: fact sheet BS/IS). Altman Z-score of 1.74 is in the distress-risk zone, and short interest rose 40.1% month-on-month to 3.38% of float, though institutional ownership remains high at 77.6% [Certain] (source: fact sheet ST). Solventum is mid-process marketing its HIS unit for sale (initial bids collected, per Axios) [Likely] (headline source as listed in fact sheet: https://www.tipranks.com/news/the-fly/solventum-collects-initial-bids-for-health-information-systems-unit-axios-says-thefly-news) — a near-term catalyst but also a source of execution risk to the RemainCo margin and growth targets above.
