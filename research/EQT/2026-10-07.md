# EQT Corporation (EQT) — Core initiation — 2026-10-07

## 1. Business
EQT is the largest US natural gas producer, concentrated in Appalachia (6% of US gas output), vertically integrated after acquiring Equitrans Midstream, giving it control of gathering, transmission and storage alongside production (source: https://www.eqt.com). It sells gas into regional and interstate markets, increasingly exposed to LNG-export and data-center demand [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/eqt/).

## 2. Quality
Returns have recovered sharply: ROIC rose from -6.8% (FY21) to 9.7% TTM, ROCE to 10.9% TTM, both still rising on a 5y trend (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/eqt/financials/ratios/) [Certain]. Operating margin TTM is 46.2% and FCF margin 40.4%, both near 5y highs, with FCF conversion of NI at 138.6% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/eqt/financials/ratios/, income statement, cash-flow statement) [Certain]. The durable edge is scale plus midstream integration: EQT controls gathering/transmission for its own gas, cutting basis-differential and takeaway risk peers face (source: https://www.eqt.com) [Likely]. Appalachian gas can trade near $4 while New England spikes toward $20 on pipeline bottlenecks; EQT's midstream ownership partly insulates it from that constraint [Likely] (source: https://www.foxbusiness.com/media/energy-ceo-says-americans-arent-benefiting-from-low-prices-because-one-major-roadblock). This remains gas-price cyclical: net margin swung from -16.7% (FY21) to 34.2% (FY23) to 4.6% (FY24) (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/eqt/financials/income-statement/) [Certain]. Net debt/EBITDA fell to 0.79x TTM from 7.89x in FY21, giving balance-sheet room peers with higher leverage lack (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/eqt/financials/ratios/) [Certain].

## 3. Valuation
At 5.5x EV/EBITDA and 8.7x P/FCF, EQT trades below its peer median (6.1x, 13.1x) and near the bottom of its own 5y range (10th and 6th percentile respectively), implying the market is not paying up for the midstream-integration/moat story relative to pure-play peers EXE, AR, RRC and CTRA (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/eqt/financials/ratios/, peer statistics pages) [Certain]. P/E (12.1x) and EV/Sales (4.1x) screen above peer medians, but these are more distorted by gas-price-driven earnings volatility than cash-flow multiples [Likely]. FCF yield is 11.45% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/eqt/statistics/) [Certain]. The gap between a cheap EV/EBITDA/P-FCF and an expensive P/E suggests the current price assumes near-term EPS understates normalized cash generation, consistent with a commodity producer trading on a cyclically depressed earnings base [Likely].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) rises back above 3x, eroding the balance-sheet advantage versus peers.
- FCF margin (income statement/cash-flow statement) falls below 20%, signalling the integration/cost advantage is not holding through the cycle.
- ROIC (ratios page) falls back below peer-median levels for two consecutive fiscal years, undermining the "returns are structurally improving" thesis.
- Piotroski F-Score (statistics page) drops below 5, indicating broad fundamental deterioration.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
TTM FCF conversion of net income is 138.6% and FCF margin is 40.4%, both near 5y highs, showing cash generation currently well exceeds accounting earnings (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/eqt/financials/cash-flow-statement/, income-statement) [Certain]. Net debt/EBITDA of 0.79x TTM (down from 1.28x FY25 and 3.68x FY24) gives balance-sheet flexibility for further deleveraging, buybacks or continued midstream investment (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/eqt/financials/ratios/) [Certain]. Shares outstanding grew 5.93% YoY, a dilution headwind to watch given it works against per-share cash-flow growth (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/eqt/statistics/) [Certain]; this is consistent with stock-funded M&A (Equitrans) rather than buyback-led allocation [Likely].

## 6. Unusual items
Short interest is 3.61% of float, up 16.6% versus the prior month, and institutional ownership is high at 95.1% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/eqt/statistics/) [Certain] — not large enough to be a standalone red flag but worth monitoring given the rate of change. A 2026-09-04 Business Wire headline referencing "EQT" acquiring McGill and Partners (a UK specialty insurance broker) for $2.0bn "from Warburg Pincus," naming "EQT X," refers to EQT AB, the unrelated Swedish private-equity firm (fund EQT X), not EQT Corporation; it is excluded as a name collision [Certain] (source: https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260904761876/en/EQT-to-acquire-McGill-and-Partners-a-leading-specialty-re-insurance-broker-for-USD-2.0-billion-from-Warburg-Pincus/). Q2 2026 results missed Wall Street profit estimates on weaker gas prices (EPS $0.39 vs $0.42 consensus) even as the company raised FY26 volume guidance and lowered planned capex, underscoring the commodity-price sensitivity already captured in the quality section [Certain] (source: https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/eqt-misses-quarterly-profit-estimates-weaker-natural-gas-prices-2026-07-21/).
