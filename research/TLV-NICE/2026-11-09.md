# NICE Ltd. (TLV:NICE) — Core initiation — 2026-11-09
## 1. Business
NICE sells cloud software for contact centres (CCaaS), AI customer-service tools and financial-crime/compliance analytics, mostly as recurring subscriptions, with gross margin of 64.0% TTM (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tlv/NICE/financials/income-statement/). Reports in USD, quoted in Tel Aviv in agorot (ILA). Revenue split by country/region: [data unavailable] (company IR domain not fetchable under the allowlist). [Likely] North America is the largest market, but this is unverified.

## 2. Quality
- Five-year record is good: ROIC rose from 10.3% (FY2021) to 18.7% (FY2025), operating margin from 13.8% to 22.2% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tlv/NICE/financials/ratios/). [Certain]
- The last 12 months contradict that: ROIC fell from 16.7% to 10.2%, operating margin from 21.9% to 17.2%, FCF margin from 26.5% to 17.6% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tlv/NICE/financials/ratios/?p=trailing). [Certain] Gross margin is also slipping (66.4% FY2025, 64.0% TTM). [Certain]
- Management cited slower AI ramps and softness in non-core streams, and announced a workforce reduction (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tlv/NICE/). [Likely] This reads as cloud competition and mix pressure, not a collapse; the Gartner CCaaS Leader placement suggests the franchise is intact (source: https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20261021683157/en/NiCE-Named-a-Leader-in-the-2026-Gartner%C2%AE-Magic-Quadrant%E2%84%A2-for-Contact-Center-as-a-Service.-Delivering-AI-Powered-Customer-Service-at-Enterprise-Scale/). [Guessing]
- Moat is moderate: switching costs, but AI-native entrants threaten seat-based pricing. [Guessing]

## 3. Valuation
P/E is 16.5x against a 5y range of 11.3x–96.9x (median 37.7x), at the 6th percentile; EV/EBITDA 7.3x and EV/Sales 1.8x sit below their 5y minimums; P/FCF is 10.9x with a 9.16% FCF yield (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tlv/NICE/statistics/). [Certain] Against peers (FIVN, TWLO, PEGA, CRM) it trades 26% below on P/E and 62% below on EV/EBITDA, but only 7% below on P/FCF. [Certain] The mechanical fair value (+160% base) is flagged unreliable and is ignored. The reverse DCF implies year-1 revenue shrinking 14.2%, so the price embeds a bleak AI-disruption outcome. [Likely] The 12-month change is -26.0%. Cheap, but TTM earnings are falling, so the multiples flatter. [Likely]

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Shares outstanding fell 5.35% year on year, so buybacks are meaningful (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tlv/NICE/statistics/). [Certain] FCF conversion was 146.8% of net income TTM, down from 180.2% in FY2024 but still strong. Net cash is small: net debt/EBITDA is -0.34x TTM versus -1.40x in FY2024, so the cushion has thinned. [Certain]
