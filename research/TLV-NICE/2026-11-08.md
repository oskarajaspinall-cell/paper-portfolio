# NICE Ltd. (TLV:NICE) — Core initiation — 2026-11-08
## 1. Business
NICE sells enterprise software for customer-experience and contact centres (cloud CCaaS and AI-driven analytics/automation) plus financial-crime and compliance tools, mostly on subscription. Revenue is reported in USD; the shares trade in Tel Aviv in agorot (ILA). Revenue split by country/region: [data unavailable] (the company site was not on the allowlist, so no primary source was read) (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tlv/NICE/). [Likely] a majority of sales is outside Israel, so the overlay should check the largest foreign region.

## 2. Quality
- Five-year trend is good: ROIC 10.3% (FY2021) to 18.7% (FY2025); operating margin 13.8% to 22.2%; net cash every year (net debt/EBITDA -0.34x TTM) (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tlv/NICE/financials/ratios/). [Certain]
- The last 12 months contradict that trend: ROIC 16.7% to 10.2%, operating margin 21.9% to 17.2%, FCF margin 26.5% to 17.6% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tlv/NICE/financials/ratios/?p=trailing). Gross margin slid from 66.4% (FY2025) to 64.0% TTM (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tlv/NICE/financials/income-statement/). [Certain] Falling margins alongside slower cloud growth point to pricing/mix pressure from AI-native rivals and heavier spend, not just noise. [Likely]
- Q3 revenue and adjusted EPS beat guidance and FY26 EPS guidance rose, but management cited slower AI ramps, soft non-core streams, and a restructuring to cut management layers (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tlv/NICE/transcripts/724977-q3-2026/). [Likely]
- Moat: installed base, Gartner CCaaS Leader placement (source: https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20261021683157/en/NiCE-Named-a-Leader-in-the-2026-Gartner%C2%AE-Magic-Quadrant%E2%84%A2-for-Contact-Center-as-a-Service.-Delivering-AI-Powered-Customer-Service-at-Enterprise-Scale/). Whether switching costs survive generative-AI agents is unproven. [Guessing]

## 3. Valuation
At 31,520 ILA ($98.94) the stock trades at P/E 16.5x, EV/EBITDA 7.3x, P/FCF 10.9x and a 9.16% FCF yield. EV/EBITDA, EV/Sales and P/B sit below their own 5y minimums; P/E is at the 6th percentile of its 5y range (median 37.7x). Versus peers (FIVN, TWLO, PEGA, CRM) it is 26% cheaper on P/E, 62% on EV/EBITDA, but only 7% on P/FCF (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tlv/NICE/statistics/). [Certain]

The reverse DCF implies revenue shrinking 14.2% in year 1, fading to terminal growth (source: fact sheet fair value section). Revenue is growing, so the price embeds an AI-disruption discount, not a current decline. [Likely] Mechanical base fair value is 82,023.60 ILA, +160% above the close; this is a model estimate on a falling FCF base and is not a signal. [Guessing] The stock is -26.0% over 12 months, -6.4% below its 50-day average, RSI 36.9. A value-trap outcome is plausible if margins keep falling.

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Operating margin (income statement, TTM) falls below 15%
- FCF margin (cash flow statement, TTM) falls below 14%
- Gross margin (income statement, TTM) falls below 62%
- ROIC (ratios page, TTM) falls below 8%

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Net cash balance sheet and shares outstanding down 5.35% YoY, so buybacks are shrinking the count (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tlv/NICE/statistics/). [Certain] FCF/NI was 114.0% in FY2025 and 146.8% TTM, still above 100% but down from 180.2% in FY2024. [Certain] Buying back stock at a P/FCF near the 5y low is value-accretive if FCF stabilises.
