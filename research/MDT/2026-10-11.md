# Medtronic plc (MDT) — Core initiation — 2026-10-11

## 1. Business
Medtronic designs, makes and sells medical devices across Cardiovascular, Neuroscience and Medical Surgical franchises — pacemakers/defibrillators, cardiac mapping/ablation, spinal and robotic surgery (Hugo), and neuromodulation [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/mdt/). Revenue is device sales plus recurring consumables to hospitals globally. On 2026-10-07 it completed the split-off of its former Diabetes unit, MiniMed [Certain] (source: https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/medtronic-announces-final-exchange-ratio-of-4-5939-in-split-off-of-minimed-group-inc-302901675.html).

## 2. Quality
ROIC TTM is 8.6%, down from 9.3% in FY2022 (-1.0pp over 5y); ROCE 9.3% and ROE 10.6% TTM are roughly flat [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/mdt/financials/ratios/). Margins have eroded steadily: gross margin 68.4%→65.3% FY2022-FY2026 (-3.0pp), operating margin 21.6%→20.0% (-1.6pp), net margin 15.9%→13.2% (-2.7pp) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/mdt/financials/income-statement/). FCF margin fell 3.9pp to 16.3% TTM and FCF conversion fell 5.6pp to 117% (still >100%) [Certain]. Net debt/EBITDA rose from 1.51x to 1.86x-1.97x [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/mdt/financials/ratios/). Piotroski F-Score is a solid 7/9, but Altman Z of 2.98 sits in the distress "grey zone" [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/mdt/statistics/). Scale, regulatory approvals and hospital relationships give Medtronic durable share in cardiac rhythm, spine and neuromodulation, but five years of margin compression show pricing/mix pressure from Boston Scientific, Stryker and Abbott is eating returns, not a widening moat [Likely].

## 3. Valuation
At 88.48 USD, MDT trades on 21.8x TTM P/E, below its own 5y range minimum (22.3x), and 18.5x P/FCF, 19% below its 5y floor (19.7x); EV/EBITDA (12.7x) and EV/Sales (3.5x) also sit near the bottom of their 5y ranges [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/mdt/financials/ratios/). Versus peers (SYK, BSX, ABT, ZBH), MDT is cheaper on every multiple: P/E -14%, EV/EBITDA -12%, EV/Sales -10%, P/FCF -6%, P/B -23% [Certain]. FCF yield is 5.42%. The fact sheet's own-history DCF/P/E blend puts base fair value at 84.90 (-4.0% vs last close), bear 64.21 (-27.4%), bull 102.00 (+15.3%) [Certain] (source: fact sheet fair value table). Reverse DCF implies the price assumes ~7.9% year-1 revenue growth fading to terminal — a reasonable, not aggressive, assumption. A sector-wide discount plus a below-5y-range multiple suggests the market already prices in the margin erosion, limiting downside but capping upside until margins stabilize [Likely].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Operating margin (income statement page) falls below 18% TTM, below the FY2023 trough — pricing pressure is structural, not cyclical.
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) rises above 2.3x, above the FY2025 peak — balance-sheet strain post-MiniMed split.
- ROIC (ratios page) falls below 7%, below the FY2023 low — returns are being competed away.
- FCF conversion (cash flow/income statement) falls below 100% for two consecutive fiscal years.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
FCF conversion stays above 100% (117% TTM) despite the 5y decline, so earnings convert to cash, not just accrual [Certain]. Shares outstanding were flat YoY (0.00%), implying buybacks roughly offset dilution [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/mdt/statistics/). Net debt/EBITDA of 1.86x-1.97x is moderate for an investment-grade medtech but has risen for five straight years [Certain]. The MiniMed split-off (completed 2026-10-07) removes the former Diabetes segment's assets and liabilities and simplifies the portfolio toward higher-margin cardiovascular, neuroscience and surgical franchises, though FY2026 fact-sheet figures may not fully reflect the post-split structure [Likely] (source: https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/medtronic-announces-final-exchange-ratio-of-4-5939-in-split-off-of-minimed-group-inc-302901675.html).

## 6. Unusual items
Insider ownership is only 0.04% (institutions hold 87.1%) — low insider alignment, though typical for a large-cap [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/mdt/statistics/). Short interest is low (1.35% of float) but rose 20.1% versus the prior month — a modest rise in bearish positioning, not yet a red flag [Certain]. The MiniMed split-off (final exchange ratio 4.5939 Medtronic-for-MiniMed shares) is a material restructuring; the next reported quarter (earnings due 2026-11-17) should show a cleaner, higher-margin base [Likely] (source: https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/medtronic-announces-final-exchange-ratio-of-4-5939-in-split-off-of-minimed-group-inc-302901675.html). Recent FDA/CE clearances (Affera Prism-2 mapping software, LigaSure RAS Maryland on Hugo, Altaviva/Symplicity Spyral CPT codes) extend the pipeline in cardiac ablation, robotic surgery and renal denervation [Likely] (source: https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/medtronic-announces-regulatory-milestones-for-newest-cardiac-mapping-and-ablation-software-302893542.html).
