# PT Telekomunikasi Indonesia Tbk (IDX:TLKM) — Core initiation — 2026-10-18

## 1. Business
Indonesia's dominant integrated telecom operator: fixed-line and fixed broadband, mobile voice/data, enterprise/wholesale data, and digital services, majority state-owned. Revenue mix by segment is not shown on stockanalysis.com [data unavailable]. [Likely]

## 2. Quality
Returns and margins have declined every year for five straight years: ROIC 19.8%→14.4% (TTM 16.2%, -5.4pp), ROCE 20.8%→16.6% (TTM 18.0%, -4.2pp), ROE 25.6%→16.1% (TTM 17.7%, -9.6pp), operating margin 30.2%→24.1% (TTM 24.2%, -6.1pp), net margin 17.4%→12.1% (TTM 12.0%, -5.2pp) (source: ratios/income statement pages). [Certain] The business is still profitable with mid-to-high-teens returns on capital, but a five-year monotonic decline in every return and margin metric is not a one-off, and is consistent with intensifying competitive pressure eroding pricing power. [Likely] The one offsetting positive: FCF margin rose 26.8%→28.9% (TTM) and FCF conversion rose from 154.5% to 241.0% TTM, so cash generation has not deteriorated as fast as accounting profitability (source: cash flow/income statement pages). [Certain] No durable moat evidence (regulatory licence, network scale) is available from stockanalysis.com; the trend argues competitors ARE taking share of returns, not that they can't. [Likely]

## 3. Valuation
Current multiples sit below both the stock's own 5-year range and peers (IDX:ISAT, IDX:EXCL, IDX:FREN) on most measures: EV/EBITDA 3.3x (5y min 4.9x; peer median 4.8x, -32%), P/FCF 4.3x (5y min 7.5x; peer median 5.5x, -21%), EV/Sales 1.5x (peer median 1.9x, -20%); P/E 10.5x is below its own 5y min of 12.0x but above a single peer (n=1) at 7.6x; P/B 1.4x is below its own 5y min (1.7x) but above peer median 1.1x. FCF yield is 23.1%. Base-case fair value of 5,914 IDR implies +211% upside to the 1,900 IDR last close, and the reverse DCF shows the price already embeds a -26% revenue decline in year 1 fading to terminal growth — a distress scenario the quality metrics (still mid-to-high-teens ROIC/ROCE) don't yet corroborate. [Likely] The peer set is thin (n=1 or n=3, all smaller Indonesian operators), limiting the comparison's weight. [Certain]

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROIC (ratios page) falls below 10% (TTM 16.2%, down from 19.8% five years ago) — would confirm the return erosion is accelerating past a sustainable level.
- Operating margin (income statement) falls below 20% (TTM 24.2%, down from 30.2%) — a further large step down would confirm structural, not cyclical, margin pressure.
- Net debt/EBITDA (balance sheet/income statement) rises above 1.5x (TTM 0.38x, but the trend rose 0.40x→0.61x before easing) — sustained releveraging would signal capex or dividend-policy stress.
- FCF margin (cash flow/income statement) falls below 15% (TTM 28.9%, currently rising) — reversal would remove the one metric offsetting the profitability decline.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
FCF margin is rising (26.8%→28.9% TTM) and FCF conversion (FCF/NI) has jumped from 154.5% to 241.0% TTM, so cash profitability is holding up better than reported earnings (source: cash flow/income statement pages). [Certain] Net debt/EBITDA is low at 0.38x TTM despite rising across 2021-2024 (0.40x→0.62x) before easing — balance sheet is not currently stretched. [Certain] Shares outstanding are essentially flat (-0.13% YoY), so no dilution (source: statistics page). [Certain] Dividend/buyback detail is not shown on stockanalysis.com [data unavailable].
