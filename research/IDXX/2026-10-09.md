# IDEXX Laboratories, Inc. (IDXX) — Core initiation — 2026-10-09
## 1. Business
IDEXX sells diagnostics to companion-animal veterinary practices: point-of-care instruments (Catalyst, ProCyte, SNAP) placed at low upfront cost that lock in recurring sales of proprietary reagents/consumables and reference-lab testing — over 80% of Companion Animal Group revenue is recurring [Certain]. VetConnect PLUS software and new cytology/cancer platforms (inVue Dx, Cancer Dx) add stickiness. A smaller livestock/poultry diagnostics and water-testing segment rounds out revenue [Certain].

## 2. Quality
A high-quality niche leader: TTM ROIC 47.2%, ROCE 68.1% (+6.1pp over 5y), gross margin 62.4%, FCF conversion 110% [Certain] reflect an integrated hardware+reagent+software model management calls unusual to match across all three (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/idxx/transcripts/674071-investor-day-2026/). Net Promoter Scores above 50 on major platforms, and management's observation that multi-platform customers "grow materially faster," point to high switching costs once an instrument is placed [Likely] (same source). Diagnostics drive ~80% of practice activity and are the highest-EBITDA category for vets, a source of pricing power [Likely]. Competitive risk: Zoetis's diagnostics push and Mars-owned Heska/Antech, though IDEXX still leads companion-animal point-of-care share [Likely]. ROE fell 46.4pp over 5y (112.6%→74.2% TTM) mechanically from deleveraging (net debt/EBITDA 1.34x→0.55x), not from weaker returns on capital, which stayed above 40% throughout [Likely].

## 3. Valuation
Every multiple sits at or below its own 5y minimum — P/E 36.6x (5y range 38.1-75.0x), EV/EBITDA 25.5x (range 26.2-54.6x), P/FCF 32.2x (range 41.9-87.8x) — so the stock is cheap only versus its own history, not in absolute terms [Certain]. Versus peers (ZTS, ELAN, PAHC, PDCO) it trades 138-305% above median on P/E, EV/EBITDA and EV/Sales, reflecting materially higher returns on capital rather than true comparability [Likely]. The fact sheet's own-history DCF/EV-Revenue blend puts fair value at $353.57 base and $371.07 bull versus last close $513.14 — even the bull case is 28% below price [Certain]. The reverse DCF implies the market is pricing ~33% revenue growth in year 1 fading to terminal growth, above IDEXX's double-digit organic-growth target and the soft-visit headwind (-1.5% to -2%) management flagged [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/idxx/transcripts/743337-barclays-24th-annual-global-financial-services-conference/). The price assumes flawless, extended execution of the inVue Dx/Cancer Dx ramp and international expansion.

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Companion Animal Group recurring revenue organic growth (income statement/ratios page) falls below high-single-digit for two consecutive quarters, versus management's 10%+ target.
- Gross margin (income statement page) falls below 60%, signalling pricing or mix pressure.
- ROIC (ratios page) falls below 35%, breaking the 5y trend of returns above 40%.
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) rises above 1.5x without a comparably accretive acquisition funding it.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
No dividend; capital is returned via buybacks (shares outstanding -2.40% YoY) [Certain]. FCF margin is 27.5% TTM with FCF conversion of 110% of net income, funding continued bolt-on M&A — most recently CoVetAI, an AI-native veterinary workflow software provider (terms undisclosed) — without raising leverage (net debt/EBITDA 0.55x, down from 1.34x in FY2022) [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/idxx/).

## 6. Unusual items
Institutional ownership is very high at 96.9% versus insider ownership of just 0.26% [Certain]. Short interest is low and falling (2.44% of float, -8.4% month-on-month) [Certain], so there is no crowded-short signal either way. Beta of 1.55 means the stock is more volatile than the market on broad drawdowns [Certain]. None of these are decision-changing on their own.
