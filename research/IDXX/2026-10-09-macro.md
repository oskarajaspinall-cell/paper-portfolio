# Macro overlay: IDEXX Laboratories, Inc. (IDXX) — 2026-10-09
## Assessment
IDXX's thesis is fundamental — priced 31-61% above the fact sheet's own-history DCF fair value [Certain] — not macro-driven. But its 36.6x P/E (below its own 5y minimum of 38.1x) and 1.55 beta make its multiple unusually sensitive to the discount rate, so macro earns secondary weight.

Chain: real yields -> cost of equity (CAPM: 10y Treasury + beta x ERP, the fact sheet's own DCF input) -> fair-value ceiling / multiple.

Facts: 10y real yield (DFII10) 2.92% on 2026-10-07, +0.49pp ~1m, +0.61pp ~3m; nominal 10y (DGS10) 5.28%, +0.72pp ~3m; breakeven inflation (T10YIE) flat at 2.35% (-0.02pp ~1m) (fred.stlouisfed.org, 2026-10-07/08). VIX calm at 15.08 (-1.82pp ~3m); HY spread still low at 3.09% though +0.39pp ~3m (fred.stlouisfed.org, 2026-10-07).

Market pricing **(inference)**: 2y yield (4.77%) sits 0.89pp above fed funds (3.88%), a proxy for the market pricing no near-term cuts/further tightening risk over two years. Flat breakevens suggest the rise is real-rate-, not inflation-, driven — it still lifts IDXX's discount rate either way.

Bull: real yields plateau/retreat -> cost of equity eases -> room for a multiple re-rating from today's below-5y-minimum levels, with no change in fundamentals.
Bear: real yields extend their 3-month rise -> cost of equity rises further -> the DCF fair value (already far below price) ratchets lower, adding to de-rating risk.
Tilt: toward bear, small — recurring diagnostics revenue is not macro-sensitive, and much of the real-yield move is likely already reflected in the stock's -17.6% 12-month de-rating **(inference)**.
