# Globe Life Inc. (GL) — Core initiation — 2026-10-09
## 1. Business
Globe Life underwrites individual life and supplemental health (accident/health) insurance for middle-income households, sold mainly through exclusive career-agency networks (American Income Life, Liberty National) plus a direct-to-consumer channel [Certain] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/320335/000032033526000090/gl-20251231.htm). Revenue is premiums less benefits/claims, plus net investment income earned on the float backing long-duration policy reserves [Certain] (same source).

## 2. Quality
Profitability is strong and improving: ROIC rose from 10.3% (FY21) to 14.1% TTM; ROE rose from 11.8% to 20.9% TTM [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/gl/financials/ratios/). The exclusive-agency distribution model is a real switching-cost moat — agents are recruited, trained and financed inside a captive network, which is slow for rivals to replicate [Likely] (source: 10-K, as above). But cash quality is softening: FCF/NI conversion fell from 155.9% (FY22) to 101.7% TTM, and net debt/EBITDA (site definition) rose from 1.40x to 1.89x [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/gl/financials/ratios/). Short interest is low in absolute terms (2.71% of float) but rose 15% month-on-month, and a PRNewswire solicitation ad (Aug 25, 2026) raised unspecified fiduciary-duty allegations against insiders; a check of GL's 2024–2026 8-K filings on SEC EDGAR found no disclosed DOJ/SEC investigation item, so this reads as an unverified law-firm solicitation rather than a confirmed issue, but it is a watch item [Guessing] (source: https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/did-globe-life-inc-insiders-breach-their-fiduciary-duties-to-shareholders-302859259.html; https://www.sec.gov/cgi-bin/browse-edgar?action=getcompany&CIK=0000320335&type=8-K&dateb=&owner=include&count=40).

## 3. Valuation
GL trades at a clear discount to its direct peer group: P/E 11.0x vs. peer median 15.6x (-29%) and EV/EBITDA 9.5x vs. 12.9x (-26%), while P/B is roughly in line (2.1x vs. 2.1x) despite a 20.9% TTM ROE [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/gl/statistics/, peer statistics pages). Against its own five-year range GL's P/E (52nd percentile) and EV/EBITDA (43rd percentile) sit near the middle, but P/FCF (10.4x) is above its own five-year maximum, consistent with the recent fall in cash conversion [Certain] (source: ratios/cash-flow pages). The stockanalysis.com own-history fair value puts base case at $190.29 (+14.6% vs. last close $166.08), bear $117.33 (-29.4%), bull $211.47 (+27.3%) [Certain] (source: factsheet, blend of P/B+ROE and P/E methods). The price, 13% below its 52-week high, suggests the market is partly pricing the cash-conversion slowdown and governance overhang into an otherwise peer-cheap, high-ROE franchise [Likely].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROE (ratios page) falls and holds below 15% for two consecutive quarters.
- FCF/NI conversion (cash-flow and income-statement pages) keeps falling and drops below 90% TTM.
- Net debt/EBITDA, site definition (ratios page), rises above 2.5x.
- Shares outstanding (statistics page) stop shrinking YoY, signalling buybacks have been curtailed.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Shares outstanding fell 5.42% YoY, consistent with sustained buybacks funded from operating cash flow [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/gl/statistics/). FCF conversion remains above 100% of net income TTM (101.7%) even after its multi-year decline, so capital returns are still cash-funded rather than debt-funded on a TTM basis, though the declining trend (155.9% → 101.7% since FY22) bears watching [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/gl/financials/cash-flow-statement/, ratios page).

## 6. Unusual items
Short interest rose 15% month-on-month to 2.71% of float, and an Aug-2026 PRNewswire item solicits shareholders over unspecified alleged fiduciary-duty breaches by insiders; GL's 8-K filings on EDGAR for 2024–2026 show no disclosed DOJ/SEC investigation item, so this cannot be corroborated from primary filings and is flagged as an unresolved governance watch item rather than a confirmed risk [Guessing] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/gl/statistics/; https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/did-globe-life-inc-insiders-breach-their-fiduciary-duties-to-shareholders-302859259.html). Beta is low (0.44), reducing market-wide macro sensitivity relative to peers [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/gl/statistics/).
