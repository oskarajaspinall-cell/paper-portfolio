# Globe Life Inc. (GL) — Core initiation — 2026-11-08
## 1. Business
Writes life insurance (about two-thirds of premium) and supplemental health, sold mainly through exclusive career agencies (American Income, Liberty National, Family Heritage) plus direct-to-consumer; earns underwriting margin plus investment income on policy reserves. American Income is 54% of life premium [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/320335/000032033526000231/gl-20260930.htm). Predominantly US; no foreign country is material [Likely].

## 2. Quality
Returns are high for a life insurer: ROE 19.3% TTM and ROIC 13.4% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/gl/financials/ratios/) [Certain]. Management describes operating ROE as "generally in the mid-teens" excluding AOCI [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/320335/000032033526000231/gl-20260930.htm). The moat is the captive agency and affinity-group (union, military) distribution plus low-ticket, high-persistency products [Likely].

Direction change: ROIC is rising over five fiscal years (+4.4pp) but fell from 14.8% to 13.4% over the last 12 months, below its 2-year high of 15.3% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/gl/financials/ratios/?p=trailing) [Certain]. The Q3 10-Q shows why: American Income producing agents fell 6% on weak new-agent retention, and health margin slipped on group claims [company-reported] (same 10-Q URL). Life remeasurement gains were smaller than a year ago, so earnings quality is slightly weaker [Likely]. Net debt/EBITDA is rising to 2.09x (balance sheet page) [Certain]. Q3 EPS missed consensus per headlines (https://www.tipranks.com/news/the-fly/globe-life-reports-q3-eps-4-84-consensus-4-89-thefly-news) [Likely]. Risk Factors: no substantive change in the SEC delta.

## 3. Valuation
At 157.18 the stock trades on 10.5x P/E versus a 5y median of 9.6x (range 8.7x–13.1x), and 32% below peers (15.4x; AFL, PRI, UNM, MET) (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/gl/statistics/) [Certain]. EV/EBITDA is 9.1x (peers 13.8x); P/B is 1.8x against its 1.9x median. P/FCF is 8.9x, at the top of its 5y range, and FCF yield is 11.25% [Certain]. Blended base fair value is 213.21 (+35.6%), but it leans on P/B + ROE (248.26); the P/E method gives only 143.09 (below the price) and the bear case is 128.97 [Certain]. Upside therefore rests on sustaining a high ROE, which is now trending down. The peer discount is partly justified by the earnings-quality and agent-count questions [Guessing]. Shares outstanding fell 4.84% YoY (buybacks) [Certain].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROIC (ratios page, TTM) falls below 12%.
- Net debt/EBITDA (site definition, ratios page) rises above 2.5x.
- American Income average producing agent count (10-Q MD&A) declines more than 6% year on year for a second quarter running.
- Health underwriting margin as a percent of premium (10-Q MD&A) falls below 22%.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
FCF conversion is 112.6% of net income TTM (falling from 155.9% in FY2022) [Certain]. A new buyback authorization was announced in August (https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/globe-life-announces-new-share-repurchase-authorization-302846588.html) [Certain], and a quarterly dividend was declared on 5 November (https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/globe-life-inc-declares-dividend-302931392.html) [Certain]. Buybacks lifted per-share growth, but leverage is creeping up [Likely].

## 6. Unusual items
Law-firm "investigation" notices followed a share drop after the company disclosed excess prior-period Medicare Supplement claims tied to a federal CMS processing delay (https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/globe-life-inc-investigation-notice-suewallst-notifies-investors-of-pending-investigation-into-globe-life-inc-gl-302919923.html) [Likely]; these are solicitations, not filed findings [Guessing]. The 10-Q notes UA General Agency (Medicare Supplement via independent agents) was removed from the segment description [company-reported] (same 10-Q URL). Short interest is 0.04% of float (statistics page) [Certain]. Price is 15.5% below three months ago with RSI 38.4 [Certain].
