# Macro overlay: Ralph Lauren Corporation (RL) — 2026-10-10
## Assessment
RL's thesis is fundamentally a valuation call: the stock is priced for ~20.5% year-1 revenue growth (reverse DCF) against FY2026 results of low-double-digit growth [Certain]. Two macro variables still move the range: real yields (discount rate) and the dollar (FX translation on ~59% non-US revenue: Europe 31%, Asia 26%, SEC 10-K 2026-05-21) **(inference)**.

Facts (FRED, https://fred.stlouisfed.org/): US 10y real yield (DFII10) 2.87%, 2026-10-08, +56bp/3m; nominal 10y (DGS10) 5.22%, 2026-10-08, +68bp/3m [Certain]. 10y breakeven inflation (T10YIE) 2.33%, 2026-10-09, -4bp/1m — the yield rise is almost entirely real, not an inflation scare [Likely]. Broad dollar index (DTWEXBGS) 121.4, 2026-10-02, +2.4%/1m [Certain].

Market pricing (proxy, inference): 2y Treasury (DGS2, 4.75%) sits 87bp above effective fed funds (DFF, 3.88%, both 2026-10-08), implying the market is not pricing near-term cuts [Likely].

The fact sheet's DCF already uses the current 5.22% risk-free rate, so today's level is baked into the $194.98 base/$267.39 bull fair value — both already 28-47% below the $371.28 close [Certain]. Further real-yield or dollar strength would compress that range and add FX drag; a reversal would lift it modestly — neither changes the overvaluation conclusion.

macro_bull: real yields/dollar retrace -> fair value nudges up, gap narrows slightly.
macro_bear: real yields/dollar keep rising -> fair value compresses further, FX drag caps growth, re-rating risk rises.
Tilt: toward bear, small — macro reinforces, doesn't drive, the valuation-led bear case.
