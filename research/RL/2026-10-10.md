# Ralph Lauren Corporation (RL) — Core initiation — 2026-10-10

## 1. Business
Ralph Lauren designs and markets lifestyle apparel, accessories, fragrances and home goods under the Ralph Lauren, Polo and related brands. Revenue comes from retail/DTC (594 full-price + 307 outlet stores, digital commerce), wholesale (~9,500 doors) and licensing royalties. FY2026 mix: North America 41%, Europe 31%, Asia 26%; ~59% of revenue is earned outside the US [Certain] (source: SEC 10-K, filed 2026-05-21, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1037038/000162828026037074/rl-20260328.htm).

## 2. Quality
A high and improving-return business: TTM ROIC 28.4% (rising +14.4pp over 5 years), ROCE 23.5%, ROE 37.5% [Certain] (fact sheet ratios page). Gross margin expanded every year to 70.3% TTM and operating margin to 16.2%, despite the 10-K's own warning of a "highly promotional retail environment" [Certain]/[Likely] (fact sheet income statement; 10-K risk factors). The moat is ~60 years of brand equity plus a diversified channel/geography mix the 10-K cites as differentiating versus peers [Likely]. A WSJ piece corroborates pricing power via affordable entry products ("$12 tennis socks") without diluting the brand, naming RL as still growing [Likely] (wsj.com, cited via fact sheet). Q1 FY2027 revenue grew 13% YoY with a raised outlook, per the transcript on the fact sheet [Likely]. Net debt/EBITDA is low at 0.67x; FCF conversion is 106% of net income TTM [Certain]. Peers (TPR, PVH, CPRI, LEVI) earn structurally lower returns.

## 3. Valuation
The market is paying up for that quality, and then some. Every multiple sits above its own 5-year maximum and well above the peer median: P/E 23.4x (5y max 21.1x; peers 16.0x, +46%), EV/EBITDA 14.6x (5y max 13.5x; peers 11.0x, +32%), EV/Sales 2.8x (peers 1.1x, +156%), P/B 8.1x (peers 7.6x) [Certain] (fact sheet valuation table). Fair value (DCF-led, inform-only) is $194.98 base and $267.39 bull versus a last close of $371.28 — even the bull case is 28% below the current price [Certain] (fact sheet fair value). The reverse DCF shows the price embeds ~20.5% revenue growth in year 1 fading to terminal growth, well above the mid-teens growth the business has actually delivered [Certain] (fact sheet fair value). This is a good business priced for a growth rate it has not shown; the gap between price and even the bull-case model is too wide to underwrite.

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Gross margin (income statement page) falls below its 5-year low of 64.9% — pricing power/mix thesis breaking.
- ROIC (ratios page) falls below 20%, reversing the 5-year rising trend — the return-on-capital moat eroding.
- FCF/net income (cash flow + income statement pages) stays below 80% for two consecutive fiscal years — cash conversion no longer tracking reported profit.
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) rises above 1.5x — balance-sheet discipline that currently supports the quality thesis deteriorating.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
The 10-K confirms an active quarterly cash dividend and Class A share repurchase program [Certain] (source: 10-K, same URL as above); shares outstanding fell 2.32% YoY, consistent with net buybacks [Certain] (fact sheet statistics page). TTM FCF margin is 12.5% and FCF conversion 106% of net income, though both are down from the FY2024/25 peak (140%/137%), a gap mainly from working-capital timing rather than margin deterioration given operating margin kept expanding [Certain]/[Likely] (fact sheet cash flow/income statement). Low leverage (0.67x net debt/EBITDA) leaves room to keep funding both growth capex and distributions [Certain] (fact sheet ratios page).

## 6. Unusual items
Short interest is 7.49% of float, elevated for a large-cap and consistent with valuation skepticism, though it fell 2.7% versus the prior month [Certain] (fact sheet statistics/news). No governance, accounting or distress flags: Altman Z 6.04, Piotroski F 7/9 [Certain] (fact sheet statistics page). The 10-K's own risk factors (tariffs, China supply-chain/UFLPA exposure, promotional pressure) are standard apparel-sector disclosures, not new information [Likely] (10-K, same URL as above). Otherwise none material.
