# Luckin Coffee (OTC:LKNCY) — Core initiation — 2026-11-08
## 1. Business
Luckin operates a digital-first, mostly takeaway coffee chain, earning revenue from company-operated stores and from partner-store supplies and fees, with ordering through its app. Revenue is overwhelmingly mainland China, so China/CNY is effectively the whole exposure [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/otc/LKNCY/). It is expanding overseas, for example Singapore and a possible Gulf entry [Likely] (source: https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/29/luckin-coffee-mubadala-middle-east-temasek-centurium-capital.html). The ADR trades OTC; financials are in CNY.

## 2. Quality
Returns are high: ROIC 24.7% TTM against 22.2% in FY2024, and ROCE 26.4% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/otc/LKNCY/financials/ratios/). Gross margin is 60.8% and operating margin 11.0% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/otc/LKNCY/financials/income-statement/). Net cash (net debt/EBITDA -0.24x) and FCF conversion of 125.6% of net income show the earnings are real [Certain]. Altman Z is 4.73, though Piotroski F is only 4 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/otc/LKNCY/statistics/).

Direction change: ROIC fell from 26.7% to 24.7% over 12 months and FCF margin from 10.5% to 9.4%. Operating margin is flat (-0.5pp). The Q3 call summary points to subsidy-driven same-store-sales comparisons while store expansion drives growth [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/otc/LKNCY/). So returns are drifting down from a peak and growth is coming from unit count, not from existing stores [Likely]. The moat is scale and density of stores plus app data and purchasing; the 2020 accounting fraud history shows governance has been a weakness [Certain]. Price competition in China coffee is the main threat to the returns [Likely].

## 3. Valuation
P/E is 16.7x, below the 5y minimum of 20.2x and 16% below the peer median of 20.0x. EV/EBITDA is 3.3x against a 5y range of 11.3x–45.8x and peers at 16.7x; P/FCF is 13.4x versus 21.6x minimum and 24.5x for peers; FCF yield is 7.45% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/otc/LKNCY/statistics/). EV/EBITDA is flattered by net cash, so P/E and P/FCF are the cleaner read [Likely].

Base fair value is 45.09 USD, +32.6% against the 34.00 close (bull 75.11 USD). The bear case prints a negative -27.70 USD, which is unusable [Likely]. The reverse DCF implies 7.8% year-1 revenue growth fading to terminal, modest for a unit-growth business, and the fair value already carries a 3.5% CNY country-risk premium. The price assumes growth slows and returns keep eroding [Likely].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROIC (ratios page, TTM) falls below 18%.
- Operating margin (income statement, TTM) falls below 8%.
- FCF margin (cash flow page, TTM) falls below 5%.
- Net debt/EBITDA (statistics page) rises above 1.0x.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Shares outstanding are down 0.52% YoY and the board raised the buyback authorisation in September [Certain] (source: https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/09/01/3353892/0/en/luckin-coffee-announces-upsizing-of-share-repurchase-program.html). The balance sheet is net cash. FCF conversion improved from 5.3% in FY2023 to 96.3% in FY2025 and 125.6% TTM, though TTM FCF margin has slipped from its peak. Growth capex for stores is funded from internal cash [Likely].

## 6. Unusual items
- Governance: a new director was appointed 2026-09-29 (source: https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/09/29/3370646/0/en/luckin-coffee-announces-appointment-of-new-director.html). Past fraud and OTC delisting from Nasdaq are a standing discount [Certain].
- OTC ADR: thinner and less protected than a US exchange listing; short interest [data unavailable].
- China regulatory and macro risk: CNY-reporting, so a weaker yuan lowers the USD value of the ADR [Likely].
