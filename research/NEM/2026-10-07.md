# Newmont Corporation (NEM) — Core initiation — 2026-10-07

## 1. Business
Newmont is the world's largest gold producer, operating roughly a dozen mines across the Americas, Africa and Asia-Pacific — including Boddington, Cadia, Tanami, Lihir, Peñasquito, Pueblo Viejo and the Nevada Gold Mines joint venture [Certain] (source: https://www.newmont.com/about-us/). It sells gold plus copper, silver, zinc and lead by-products at prevailing market prices, so revenue tracks metal prices and mined volumes rather than any differentiated product pricing [Certain] (source: https://www.newmont.com/about-us/).

## 2. Quality
Returns have inflected sharply: ROIC rose from 1.2% (FY21) to 27.9% TTM, ROE from 0.8% to 25.9%, and operating margin from 16.3% to 55.2% (RA, IS). Most of this is gold-price beta compressing cost ratios, not a new cost structure, and could reverse in a lower-price regime [Likely]. The durable edge is scale and asset quality: NEM is the only S&P 500-listed pure-gold producer, with long-life, large ore bodies (Boddington, Cadia, Lihir, Nevada JV) that are not easily replicated, plus by-product credits that lower net costs [Certain] (source: https://www.newmont.com/about-us/). Management is actively high-grading the portfolio — e.g. completing the sale of the non-core Northumberland project on 2026-10-06 [Certain] (source: https://www.newsfilecorp.com/release/317695/StrikePoint-Completes-Acquisition-of-the-Northumberland-Gold-Project-from-Newmont-Corporation) — and the balance sheet is now net-cash (net debt/EBITDA -0.20x TTM vs 2.10x in FY23) [Certain] (RA, BS), which cushions downside if gold corrects. Piotroski F-score 8/9 supports balance-sheet quality [Certain] (ST).

## 3. Valuation
Multiples are mixed, not cheap across the board. EV/EBITDA (6.9x) sits near the bottom of its 5y range (14th percentile) and 7% below peers (7.5x) — the market is not paying up for the margin inflection [Likely]. P/FCF (12.5x) is below its entire 5y range, implying the market doubts current FCF is sustained, yet it's still 26% above the peer median (10.0x). Conversely, EV/Sales (4.6x, 91st percentile) and P/B (3.5x, above the 5y max of 3.2x) are both rich versus NEM's own history and versus peers (+29%/+13%), consistent with the market capitalising the recent book-value and margin gains rather than discounting them [Likely]. P/E (14.7x) is near the bottom of its own range but 13% above peers. Net: the price assumes today's elevated gold-price margins and net-cash balance sheet persist, with some scepticism already priced into cash-flow multiples but not yet into book-value multiples [Likely].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROIC (ratios page) falls back below 10% for two consecutive quarters, signalling the margin inflection was purely price-driven and reversing.
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) rises back above 1.0x, undoing the net-cash position that currently supports quality.
- FCF margin (cash-flow/income statement) falls below 15%, reverting toward the FY21-23 average and undermining the cash-conversion thesis.
- Piotroski F-score (statistics page) drops below 6, indicating fundamental deterioration beyond price effects.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Cash conversion is strong: FCF margin 37.8% TTM and FCF/NI 113.2% (CF, IS), with H1 2026 free cash flow of $5.3bn per management [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/nem/transcripts/751948-mining-forum-americas-2026/). Management returned $4.6bn to shareholders via dividends and buybacks through H1 2026, cutting share count by roughly 9% [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/nem/transcripts/751948-mining-forum-americas-2026/), consistent with the fact sheet's -3.94% YoY shares outstanding (ST). A further $6bn buyback authorisation and a proposed 8% dividend increase to $0.27/share were flagged by management [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/nem/transcripts/751948-mining-forum-americas-2026/), pending formal board approval. Combined with net-cash leverage, capital return looks well-funded from operating cash flow rather than debt.

## 6. Unusual items
Board refresh (Peter Beaven appointed non-executive director, effective 2026-09-01) and a new CEO (Natascha Viljoen, since January 2026) are leadership-transition items, not yet a governance concern [Guessing] (source: https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260820394004/en/Newmont-Appoints-Peter-Beaven-to-Board-of-Directors/). Short interest is low and falling (1.60% of float, -11.4% month-over-month) (ST), so no crowding risk. Otherwise none.
