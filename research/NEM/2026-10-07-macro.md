# Macro overlay: Newmont Corporation (NEM) — 2026-10-07
## Assessment
NEM sells gold (plus by-products) at prevailing market prices with no differentiated pricing [Certain] (research/NEM/2026-10-07.md), so the dominant chain is **real yields + dollar → gold price → NEM revenue and margin (high operating leverage) → multiple**. Primary, not background.

Facts (FRED, 2026-10-05/06): 10y real yield (DFII10) is 2.95%, up +0.52pp ~1m / +0.71pp ~3m; dollar index (DTWEXBGS) 121.4, up +2.86 ~1m; breakeven inflation (T10YIE) flat at 2.36% (+0.01pp ~1m) — a real-rate/dollar shock, not an inflation story [Certain]. Policy-path proxy (market pricing, not fact): DGS2 (4.84%) sits well above fed funds (3.88%), implying the market still prices a higher/longer path **(inference)**.

This is a notable divergence: both classic gold headwinds (real yields, dollar) rose sharply over 1-3 months, yet NEM is up +18.5% over 3m and +34% over 12m, with EV/Sales and P/B already above their 5y ranges [Likely] (factsheet). The rally looks driven by gold demand independent of real rates (safe-haven/central-bank buying, per news flow) rather than falling real yields **(inference)** — the stock's richest multiples have not yet absorbed a real-yield/dollar headwind already live in official data.

**Bull**: dollar/real-yield rally stalls (dovish pivot, inflation re-accelerates) → gold holds/extends → margin and rich multiples sustained.
**Bear**: real yields and dollar keep climbing → gold corrects → high fixed-cost leverage compresses margin from TTM highs and EV/Sales, P/B de-rate from above-range levels.
**Tilt**: moderate, toward bear — elevated book-value/sales multiples sit atop a real-rate/dollar move that hasn't yet forced a gold correction.
