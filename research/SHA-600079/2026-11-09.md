# Humanwell Healthcare (SHA:600079) — Core initiation — 2026-11-09
## 1. Business
Chinese specialty and generic drug maker (stockanalysis.com industry: Drug Manufacturers - Specialty & Generic). [Likely] Its profits come from anaesthesia and analgesic (CNS) drugs, reproductive-health products and traditional Chinese medicine, plus a US generics subsidiary. Foreign revenue share: [data unavailable]; no primary source was fetched, so the US share is unverified. Reports and trades in CNY. (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sha/600079/)

## 2. Quality
[Certain] Returns are decent and improving: ROIC 12.3% TTM vs 8.8% a year earlier; ROCE 14.1% TTM; operating margin 15.6%, up 3.4pp over five years; gross margin 49.5%, up 5.4pp (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sha/600079/financials/ratios/). FCF margin rose from 2.3% to 8.6%, FCF conversion 81.6% of net income, though FCF margin is 2.1pp below its 2-year high of 10.7%. Net debt/EBITDA is 0.47x. Piotroski F is 7 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sha/600079/statistics/).
[Likely] The moat is regulatory: licences and approvals for controlled anaesthetic and opioid drugs limit entry. Volume-based procurement price cuts in China are the standing threat to that margin. [Guessing] The moat is narrower than a global branded franchise.

[Likely] The price assumes margin gains persist and the market already prices China regulatory and country risk. Beta is 0.32.
