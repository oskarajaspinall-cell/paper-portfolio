# CITIC Securities (SHA:600030) — Core initiation — 2026-10-25
## 1. Business
China's largest listed brokerage by market value: earns fees from wealth management, investment banking and asset management, plus trading and principal investing, interest on margin lending, and international business. Earnings are geared to Chinese equity-market turnover, issuance and the level of the market. [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sha/600030/)

## 2. Quality
Margins have risen, but returns remain modest and cyclical. Operating margin is 59.1% TTM vs 42.1% in FY2023; net margin 43.6% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sha/600030/financials/income-statement/). ROE is 12.2% TTM vs 7.7% in FY2023 and 12.0% in FY2021, so it is back at the top of its own range, not structurally higher; ROIC is only 3.6% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sha/600030/financials/ratios/). [Likely] Moat is scale, licences and franchise breadth in a regulated, state-influenced industry; these protect share, not returns, as fee rates and market activity drive profit. Free cash flow (FCF margin swinging from -71.7% to 317.5%) is distorted by trading and client balances and says nothing about earning power [Certain]. Piotroski F is 5 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sha/600030/statistics/). Verdict: decent franchise, earnings near a cyclical peak. [Likely]

## 3. Valuation
P/E is 9.8x, below the 5y minimum of 13.4x (median 14.3x), but TTM earnings are elevated, so the low multiple partly reflects peak profit [Likely]. P/B is 1.0x, at the bottom of its 1.0x–1.5x range; versus peers P/E is +15% and P/B +30% (peer medians 8.5x and 0.8x) (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sha/600030/statistics/). Mechanical base fair value is 28.27 CNY vs a 25.45 close (+11.1%), bear 18.51 (-27.3%), bull 31.13 (+22.3%). The reverse DCF implies 0.5% year-1 revenue growth, but FCFE for a broker is unreliable, so that is weak evidence [Likely]. Upside is modest relative to the downside if turnover fades; the stock is at a premium to cheaper peers. Price is below both moving averages and 12-month relative strength is weak. Results are due 2026-10-26. (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sha/600030/)

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROE (ratios page) falls below 8% on a TTM basis.
- Operating margin (income statement page) falls below 45% on a TTM basis.
- Net margin (income statement page) falls below 35% on a TTM basis.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Reported FCF is not meaningful for a securities firm: client funds, trading assets and financing flows dominate the cash flow statement (FCF/NI 372.7% TTM, -215.1% in FY2023) [Certain]. Judge by ROE and book value, not cash conversion. Buyback and dividend data are [data unavailable] in the fact sheet. (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sha/600030/financials/cash-flow-statement/)

## 6. Unusual items
