# Truist Financial Corporation (TFC) — Core initiation — 2026-10-09

## 1. Business
Truist is a US regional bank serving the Southeast/Mid-Atlantic, formed from the 2019 BB&T-SunTrust merger [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/tfc/). It earns net interest income on loans funded by low-cost deposits, plus fee income from wealth management, capital markets and insurance brokerage. TTM net margin is 30.7% [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/tfc/financials/income-statement/).

## 2. Quality
Reported profitability has been volatile: ROE ran 9.2% (FY21) then -2.5% (FY23) and -0.1% (FY24) before normalizing to 9.1% TTM; net margin swung from -6.1% (FY23) to 42.2% (FY24) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/tfc/financials/ratios/, https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/tfc/financials/income-statement/). That pattern points to merger-integration and divestiture charges distorting single years rather than a stable run-rate, and even the TTM ROE of 9.1% sits below what a well-run regional bank should earn [Likely]. New CEO Mike Lyons is now executing a portfolio clean-up — a $5.5bn sale of near-prime auto loans to exit a less-profitable, non-core line, with guidance reaffirmed for modest NIM improvement and fee-income growth [Likely] (source: https://www.reuters.com/legal/transactional/truist-financial-sell-55-billion-auto-loans-amid-overhaul-2026-09-15/, https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/tfc/transcripts/740243-barclays-24th-annual-global-financial-services-conference/). Regional banking is a scale/deposit-funding-cost business with limited pricing power and no clear moat beyond franchise size; TFC's post-merger execution record argues for "prove it first," not a durable-compounder rating [Guessing].

## 3. Valuation
TFC trades at 10.5x TTM P/E (5y range 9.6x-13.0x, 26th percentile; peer median 11.7x, -10%), 0.9x P/B (5y range 0.8x-1.1x, 40th percentile; peer median 1.4x, -34%) and 9.7x P/FCF (peer median 14.4x, -33%) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/tfc/financials/ratios/, https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/usb/statistics/, https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/pnc/statistics/, https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/rf/statistics/, https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/fitb/statistics/). That discount broadly tracks TFC's lower 9.1% TTM ROE against higher-ROE peers carrying richer P/B — not an obvious mispricing [Likely]. The fact sheet's own base-case fair value is $47.05, only +1.9% above the $46.18 last close; bear is -52.5%, bull +20.0% [Certain] (source: fact sheet fair value table). The reverse DCF implies the market is pricing -5.5% year-1 revenue growth fading to terminal growth — consistent with the ongoing loan-book shrinkage (auto sale, shares -3.67% YoY) rather than a growth re-rating [Likely].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROE (ratios page) fails to sustain above 8% for two consecutive quarters after the auto-loan sale closes, showing the clean-up didn't restore profitability.
- FCF conversion, FCF/NI (cash flow + income statement pages), falls back below 50% as in FY2023/FY2024, signalling renewed earnings-quality deterioration.
- Net interest margin (ratios page) declines rather than improves over the next two quarters, contradicting management's reaffirmed guidance.
- Net margin (income statement) swings negative again outside a clearly flagged one-off, repeating the FY2023 pattern.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
TTM FCF margin is 30.5% with FCF/NI conversion of 99.3%, though FY2023 was deeply negative (-789.6%) on one-off items, so the TTM run-rate is the cleaner guide [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/tfc/financials/cash-flow-statement/, https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/tfc/financials/income-statement/). Shares outstanding fell 3.67% YoY, consistent with buybacks [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/tfc/statistics/). The September 2026 redemption of $850mm subordinated notes and the pending $5.5bn auto-loan sale free up balance-sheet capacity rather than signal distress [Likely] (source: https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/truist-announces-redemption-of-subordinated-notes-due-october-2026-302882455.html).

## 6. Unusual items
Short interest is low (2.16% of float, up 6.3% month-on-month) and institutions own 78.3% — no crowding or distress signal [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/tfc/statistics/). An August 2026 report that Truist and another bank paused selling Delaware Life insurance products amid a federal probe into Delaware Life's parent is a distribution-channel issue, not a disclosed TFC balance-sheet exposure [Guessing] (source: https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/28/mark-walter-delaware-life-truist-fifth-third-banks.html). Otherwise none.
