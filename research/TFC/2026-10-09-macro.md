# Macro overlay: Truist Financial Corporation (TFC) — 2026-10-09
## Assessment
TFC is a regional bank; its NIM is set by the rate curve and its credit costs by the risk-premium cycle — a "banks and rates" primary link **(inference)**.

**Facts (FRED, 2026-10-07/08):** 10y Treasury (DGS10) 5.28%, +0.50pp/1m, +0.72pp/3m; 2y (DGS2) 4.77%, +0.40pp/+0.56pp; 10y real yield (DFII10) 2.92%, +0.49pp/+0.61pp; effective fed funds (DFF) 3.88%, +0.25pp/+0.26pp; HY credit spread (BAMLH0A0HYM2) 3.09%, +0.41pp/+0.39pp (fredgraph.csv?id=DGS10/DGS2/DFII10/DFF/BAMLH0A0HYM2). **Computed [Certain]:** 2s10s spread is +0.51pp now vs ~+0.35pp 3m ago — the curve has steepened, usually a NIM tailwind as assets reprice above liabilities.

**Market pricing (proxy, inference):** 2y yield (4.77%) sitting above effective fed funds (3.88%) implies the market prices further tightening or a funds-rate catch-up — a proxy, not a Fed statement. The simultaneous rise in real yields (+0.61pp/3m) and HY spreads (+0.39pp/3m) suggests this is partly a "higher-for-longer/credit-risk" repricing, a less clean positive for banks than pure curve steepening.

**Bull:** steepening continues without added credit stress → NIM improves per management's guidance → P/B re-rates toward peers' 1.4x from TFC's 0.9x. **Bear:** deposit funding costs catch up to the real-yield move before assets reprice, and HY spreads widen further → NIM/credit guidance disappoints into Oct 16 earnings, keeping the discount. **Tilt:** small, toward bear — real yields and HY spreads rising together is a modest, untested headwind to management's reaffirmed "modest NIM improvement" guidance **(inference)**; Q3 earnings (Oct 16) is the near-term verifier.
