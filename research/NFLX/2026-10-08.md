# Netflix, Inc. (NFLX) — Core initiation — 2026-10-08

## 1. Business
Netflix earns almost all revenue from recurring monthly subscription fees paid by members across four regions (UCAN, EMEA, LATAM, APAC) on ad-free and ad-supported tiers [Certain] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1065280/000106528026000212/nflx-20260630.htm). Six-month 2026 revenue of $24.8bn grew across all four regions (UCAN +11.8%, EMEA +15.7%, LATAM +19.9%, APAC +17.7%) [Certain] (same source). It reinvests recurring cash flow into an owned-and-licensed content library, monetized via a global, software-driven distribution and recommendation platform.

## 2. Quality
This is a high-quality, improving business [Certain]. TTM ROIC is 32.4%, up from 22.0% in FY2021; ROE is 49.5%; operating margin 29.7% and gross margin 49.1%, both five-year highs; net debt/EBITDA has fallen to 0.51x from 1.89x (fact sheet Quality table). The moat is a scale flywheel: the largest subscriber base funds the largest content budget, which funds content that sustains the base, at near-zero marginal distribution cost once content is produced — an edge loss-making or lower-margin peers cannot replicate at this scale [Likely]. Disney's decision to license titles ("Ice Age," "Will Trent") to Netflix is evidence this platform power persists even versus direct competitors [Likely] (source: https://www.reuters.com/business/media-telecom/disney-licenses-lineup-movies-tv-shows-netflix-including-ice-age-will-trent-2026-10-02/). The risk is growth, not returns: co-CEO Sarandos said in October 2026 Netflix is "not growing as fast as I want" [Likely] (headline in fact sheet) — a deceleration risk not yet visible in margins or returns.

## 3. Valuation
NFLX looks cheap on its own five-year history: P/E of 22.0x is 32% below its five-year minimum (29.2x); P/FCF of 26.0x is 9% below its minimum (30.8x); EV/EBITDA of 20.2x is 18% below its minimum (23.7x) [Certain]. FCF yield is 3.84%. Against peers (DIS, WBD, PARA, SPOT), NFLX trades below the peer median on P/E (-13%) and P/FCF (-12%) but far above on EV/Sales (+136%) and P/B (+380%), reflecting Netflix's structurally higher margins and ROE rather than comparable businesses [Likely]. The DCF/EV-EBITDA blend puts base fair value at $39.38 (-43.5% vs the $69.70 close) and bull at $64.74 (-7.1%); the reverse DCF implies the price assumes 30.2% year-one revenue growth, above the ~15% six-month 2026 pace delivered [Certain]. The price has fallen sharply (-40.5% over 12 months, -56.3pp vs SPY) but still prices in more growth than the base case supports.

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Revenue growth (income statement) falls below the ~15% six-month 2026 pace for two consecutive quarters, confirming the co-CEO's growth-deceleration comment is structural.
- Operating margin (income statement) falls below the FY2024 level of 26.7%, reversing the five-year margin-expansion trend.
- ROIC (ratios page) falls below 25%, erasing more than half the improvement recorded since FY2021.
- FCF margin (cash flow/income statement) falls below 15%, reversing the gains from -0.4% in FY2021 to 23.1% TTM.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
FCF margin is 23.1% TTM, up from -0.4% in FY2021, with FCF conversion (FCF/net income) of 81.7% [Certain] (fact sheet). Net debt/EBITDA has fallen to 0.51x from 1.89x over five years, and shares outstanding declined 1.48% year-on-year, consistent with buyback-funded capital return rather than reinvestment need [Certain] (fact sheet). Content is now funded from operating cash flow rather than incremental debt, a sign of capital discipline [Likely] (source: SEC 10-Q, qualitative only, no dollar figure used).

## 6. Unusual items
Netflix had agreed to acquire Warner Bros. Discovery's studio/streaming assets, but WBD terminated that agreement in February 2026 to pursue Paramount Skydance instead, and Netflix received a cash termination fee [Certain] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1065280/000106528026000212/nflx-20260630.htm; dollar amount not used, as it is not shown on stockanalysis.com). This removes a potential inorganic content/IP catalyst but is consistent with capital discipline. Beta is elevated at 1.61 [Certain] (fact sheet); short interest is low at 2.28% of float, up 3.0% month-on-month; institutional ownership is high at 83.1% and insider ownership low at 0.58% (fact sheet).
