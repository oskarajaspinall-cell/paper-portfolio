# Macro overlay: Netflix, Inc. (NFLX) — 2026-10-08
## Assessment
NFLX's 12-month slide (-40.5%, fact sheet) is primarily company-specific — co-CEO Sarandos said growth is slowing (TipRanks/Bloomberg headlines, 2026-10-01) — but a secondary macro channel amplifies it: rising real yields raise the discount rate for a beta-1.61 [Certain] (fact sheet), premium-multiple name whose fair value is built on the 10y Treasury. **Chain**: real yields -> cost of equity -> DCF/EV-multiple fair value (amplified by beta).

**Facts**: US 10y real yield (DFII10) 2.91%, 2026-10-06, +0.48pp ~1m / +0.61pp ~3m; 10y nominal (DGS10) 5.27%, same date, +0.49pp/+0.72pp; 10y breakeven (T10YIE) 2.36%, 2026-10-07, nearly flat (+0.01pp ~1m) — the yield rise is real-rate-led, not an inflation scare (fred.stlouisfed.org). HY credit spread 3.03%, +0.35pp ~1m, still low; VIX 15.0, benign.

**Market pricing (proxy)**: 2y Treasury (4.79%) sits ~91bp above effective fed funds (3.88%), consistent **(inference)** with the market pricing restrictive policy persisting, not near-term cuts.

The fact sheet's DCF already uses today's 5.27% risk-free rate, so base fair value ($39.38) is current. **Bull**: real yields retrace toward ~2.3% (3-month-ago level) -> cost of equity falls -> fair value re-rates up, reinforcing the "cheap vs history" case. **Bear**: real yields push toward ~3.3%+ -> cost of equity rises further, beta amplifies it -> fair value compresses below $39, undermining valuation support even if growth stabilizes. **Tilt**: moderate toward bear — growth deceleration is the main driver, but a large real-rate-led yield move compounds de-rating risk for this specific high-beta name.
