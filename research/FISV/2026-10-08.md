# Fiserv, Inc. (FISV) — Core initiation — 2026-10-08

## 1. Business
Fiserv provides payments and financial-services technology through two segments: Merchant Solutions (the Clover point-of-sale/commerce platform and acquiring) and Financial Solutions (core banking, digital banking and card-issuer processing for banks and credit unions) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/fisv/). Revenue is mostly recurring processing/subscription fees plus transaction-based merchant fees.

## 2. Quality
ROIC rose from 3.4% (FY21) to 8.7% (FY25) but has slipped to 6.8% TTM; ROCE similarly eased to 7.9% TTM from a 10.1% FY25 peak [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/fisv/financials/ratios/). Gross/operating margins remain near 5-year highs (56.2%/21.4% TTM) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/fisv/financials/income-statement/). But net debt/EBITDA has climbed from 2.72x (FY2024) to 3.54x TTM [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/fisv/financials/ratios/), and the Altman Z-score is 1.47 — inside the distress zone (<1.8) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/fisv/statistics/). Shares outstanding are down 5.2% YoY on buybacks [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/fisv/statistics/). Activist Jana Partners is pushing Fiserv to more than double planned cost cuts and adopt Palantir tooling, per Reuters reporting (2026-09-28) [Likely] (source: https://www.tipranks.com/news/the-fly/jana-urges-fiserv-to-boost-cost-cuts-use-palantir-products-reuters-reports-thefly-news). Fiserv filed an 8-K (Item 5.02, 2026-09-22) on exit/disposal costs, alongside a "Project Elevate" savings plan flagged at a September conference [Certain] (source: https://www.sec.gov/cgi-bin/browse-edgar?action=getcompany&CIK=0000798354&type=8-K). The switching-cost moat is real, but margin and leverage trends have turned, and restructuring suggests more strain than trailing ratios show.

## 3. Valuation
At 45.31 USD, FISV trades at 8.7x P/E, 6.7x EV/EBITDA, 2.5x EV/Sales and 0.9x P/B — all below its own 5-year range minimum and 20-46% below the FI/GPN/JKHY/PYPL peer median; FCF yield is 16.3% [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/fisv/financials/ratios/ and peer statistics pages). The fact sheet's reverse DCF implies the market is pricing roughly a 15% revenue decline in year one, fading to terminal growth — i.e. a structural decline not yet visible in TTM revenue or margins [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/fisv/financials/income-statement/). The base-case DCF/EV-Revenue blend fair value is 175.20 USD, +287% above the last close [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/fisv/). A gap this wide, alongside a -64.5% 52-week decline through the entire 5-year multiple range, more plausibly reflects information the market has priced that trailing financials don't yet show (activist pressure, restructuring, rising leverage) than a genuine 3-4x mispricing [Likely].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) falls back below 3.0x within two quarters, reversing the FY25→TTM deterioration.
- ROIC (ratios page) resumes its five-year uptrend and clears 8% TTM again.
- Altman Z-score (statistics page) rises back above 1.8, out of the distress band.
- Operating margin (income statement) holds at or above the FY2024-25 level (~27-29%) as Project Elevate cost cuts are delivered.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
FCF margin is 18.8% TTM, but FCF/net-income conversion has fallen from 215% (FY21) to 140% TTM [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/fisv/financials/cash-flow-statement/). The company continues buying back stock (shares down 5.2% YoY) even as net debt/EBITDA has risen to 3.54x TTM [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/fisv/statistics/) — returning capital to shareholders while leverage climbs is a flag given the parallel restructuring charge and activist pressure for deeper cost cuts.

## 6. Unusual items
Altman Z-score of 1.47 sits in the distress zone (<1.8) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/fisv/statistics/). Activist Jana Partners is pressing for far larger cost cuts and a Palantir-led technology overhaul, per Reuters reporting (2026-09-28) [Likely] (source: https://www.tipranks.com/news/the-fly/jana-urges-fiserv-to-boost-cost-cuts-use-palantir-products-reuters-reports-thefly-news). An SEC 8-K (Item 5.02, filed 2026-09-22) discloses costs associated with an exit/disposal activity, consistent with restructuring [Certain] (source: https://www.sec.gov/cgi-bin/browse-edgar?action=getcompany&CIK=0000798354&type=8-K). Short interest is 5.66% of float, down 9.8% month-on-month [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/fisv/statistics/). The stock is down 64.5% over 12 months and -14% over 3 months while SPY is +3.9% [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/fisv/history/).
