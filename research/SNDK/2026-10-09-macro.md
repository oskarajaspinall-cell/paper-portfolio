# Macro overlay: Sandisk Corporation (SNDK) — 2026-10-09

## Assessment
SNDK's thesis is a NAND-cycle story — Samsung's weak preliminary results drove the Oct 6-8 sell-off (fact sheet news) [Likely] — but valuation is stretched enough (EV/EBITDA 77th percentile, P/B 92nd percentile of its 5y range; reverse DCF implies 14.9% year-1 growth) that a discount-rate move can shift the fair-value range **(inference)**: secondary, not primary.

Chain: real yields -> discount rate -> valuation multiple on a stock priced for peak-cycle growth.

Facts (FRED): 10y real yield (DFII10) 2.92% (2026-10-07), up +0.49pp/1m and +0.61pp/3m (https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=DFII10); 10y nominal (DGS10) 5.28%, +0.72pp/3m; HY credit spread (BAMLH0A0HYM2) 3.09%, +0.39pp/3m; VIX a low 15.08, -1.82pp/3m — risk appetite not yet stressed.

Market pricing (proxy) **(inference)**: the 2y Treasury (4.77%) sits 0.89pp above effective fed funds (3.88%, both 2026-10-07, FRED DGS2/DFF), implying the market expects more tightening, not cuts — a headwind for duration-sensitive multiples. Press coverage of the sell-off cited "rising Treasury yields" alongside Samsung's results (commentator view, not fact).

Bull: real yields stabilize/retreat, risk appetite holds -> compression pauses, re-rating toward the 2,126.61 bull fair value.
Bear: real yields keep rising, spreads widen further -> discount rate up, compressing an already top-decile P/B further toward the 1,415 base/bear range even if NAND pricing holds through Oct 29 earnings.
Tilt: moderate toward bear — the rate move is large and recent, hitting a stock priced for continued high growth, though fundamentals (Oct 29 earnings) stay the dominant swing factor.
