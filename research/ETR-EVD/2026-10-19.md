# CTS Eventim AG & Co. KGaA (ETR:EVD) — Core initiation — 2026-10-19

## 1. Business
CTS Eventim is Germany's leading ticketing and live-entertainment group, operating two segments [Certain]: Ticketing (an online marketplace and ticketing-systems software connecting venues/promoters with fans, earning fees/commissions) and Live Entertainment (promoting, staging and touring concerts/festivals and operating venues across Europe, earning ticket, sponsorship and F&B revenue). The ticketing marketplace is a two-sided network; live promotion is comparatively asset-light (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/etr/EVD/).

## 2. Quality
A durable #1/#2 European ticketing position with network effects (venues and fans concentrate on the largest marketplace) underpins high, rising returns on capital: ROCE 25.2%→31.2% TTM (+36.2pp over 5y), ROE 17.4%→29.8% TTM [Certain] (source: ratios page). The balance sheet is net-cash every year shown, net debt/EBITDA -2.3x to -4.3x [Certain] (source: ratios/balance sheet), giving resilience and M&A optionality smaller rivals lack. The crack: FCF margin fell from 120.3% (FY21, an outlier) to 4.2% (FY25), recovering to 8.3% TTM; FCF/NI fell from 557.9% to 46.4% (FY25) before recovering to 89.0% TTM [Certain] (source: cash flow/income statement) — likely presale-float working-capital timing, not proven margin erosion, unconfirmed over a full clean year [Guessing]. Majority insider/founder ownership (49.34%) [Certain] (source: statistics page) aligns management; two reported director/shareholder purchases (2026-09-30, 2026-10-01) reinforce this [Likely] (https://www.wallstreet-online.de/nachricht/21456141-eqs-dd-cts-eventim-ag-co-kgaa-kps-stiftung-buy). The CFO's abrupt, unexplained exit (2026-09-30) is a governance flag, not yet a thesis break [Likely] (https://www.finanznachrichten.de/nachrichten-2026-09/69719126-cts-eventim-cfo-william-willms-leaves-executive-board-020.htm).

## 3. Valuation
At €61.75, EVD trades on 19.2x TTM P/E and 8.7x EV/EBITDA, both below its own 5-year range (min 21.9x / 13.1x) and below peer medians (56.3x P/E, n=1; 23.7x EV/EBITDA, n=2) [Certain] (source: ratios page vs LYV/EB/MSGE statistics pages) — though the small, imperfect peer set (one pure ticketer, one events-tech platform, one arena operator) limits read-through. EV/Sales (1.5x) sits in line with peers (1.6x, n=3); P/FCF (21.6x) is above the peer median (13.6x, n=3), reflecting the recent dip in FCF conversion rather than weaker earnings [Certain]. The DCF-weighted base fair value of €78.54 implies +27.2% upside, and the reverse DCF shows the current price assumes only ~15.3% year-1 revenue growth fading to terminal — a modest bar against the double-digit growth the company has recently reported [Certain] (source: factsheet fair-value table). Bear case (€48.80, -21.0%) assumes further margin/growth disappointment.

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- FCF margin (cash flow/income statement pages) stays below 10% for two consecutive full fiscal years, confirming a structural (not presale-timing) decline in cash conversion.
- Net margin (income statement page) falls and holds below 8%, indicating ticketing/promotion economics are eroding under competition.
- ROCE (ratios page) falls below 20%, showing the marketplace moat no longer commands a scale premium.
- Net debt/EBITDA (balance sheet/ratios page) turns positive and exceeds 1x, removing the net-cash cushion the thesis relies on.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
The net-cash balance sheet (net debt/EBITDA -2.3x to -4.3x every year shown) [Certain] funds bolt-on M&A in ticketing and live promotion without external financing. FCF conversion weakened from FY21-23 levels (FCF/NI 558%→179%) to FY25's 46.4%, before recovering to 89.0% TTM [Certain] (source: cash flow/income statement pages) — consistent with the ticketing advance-sale deposit float moving working capital year to year, though the fact sheet does not confirm this driver [Guessing]. A further clean year of FCF/NI near or above 100% would support the thesis; a repeat sub-50% year would not.

## 6. Unusual items
CFO Dr William Willms left the Executive Board "with immediate effect" on 2026-09-30 with no stated reason in reported headlines [Certain] (https://www.wallstreet-online.de/nachricht/21454707-eqs-adhoc-cts-eventim-cfo-dr-william-willms-leaves-the-executive-board-with-immediate-effect) — a governance item to monitor for cause, partly offset by concurrent insider buying from a director and a major shareholder [Likely] (https://www.wallstreet-online.de/nachricht/21462468-eqs-dd-cts-eventim-ag-co-kgaa-dr-cornelius-baur-buy). Altman Z-Score 2.87 and Piotroski F-Score 6/9 [Certain] (source: statistics page) show no distress but a moderate financial-health score given the net-cash position. Beta 1.25 [Certain] indicates above-market cyclicality to consumer/event spending.
