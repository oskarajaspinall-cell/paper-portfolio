# Primerica, Inc. (PRI) — Core initiation — 2026-11-16
## 1. Business
Primerica is a distribution company: a commissioned independent sales force sells term life insurance to middle-income households in the US and Canada, plus third-party mutual funds, managed accounts and annuities (Investment and Savings Products, ISP). Revenue is premiums plus commissions and fees [Certain] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1475922/000119312526449402/pri-20260930.htm). Canada's share of revenue: [data unavailable] in the sources read; not confirmed as ~10%+, so no country overlay is evidenced [Guessing].

## 2. Quality
Returns are high and rising: ROIC 19.3% TTM, up from 15.6% in FY2021; ROE 31.2% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/pri/financials/ratios/) [Certain]. FCF conversion is 116.8% of net income, though down 16.0pp over five years. Net debt/EBITDA (site definition) fell from 2.02x to 0.85x; Piotroski F is 7; shares outstanding fell 4.86% YoY (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/pri/statistics/) [Certain]. The moat is the captive recruiting/sales network and the agent's relationship with a middle-income client, a capital-light model [Likely]. The weakness is the front end: the 10-Q says new recruits, new life-licensed representatives and new policies issued all fell, and the life-licensed force shrank versus December; lapse rates stay above historical levels [Certain] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1475922/000119312526449402/pri-20260930.htm). ISP is the offset, with strong client asset values and flows [Likely]. Recruiting is the lead indicator for term growth [Likely].

## 3. Valuation
P/E is 11.6x, at 7% of its 5y range (11.0x–19.3x); EV/EBITDA 8.8x at 25%; P/B 3.2x at 41%; FCF yield 10.47% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/pri/financials/ratios/). The price is near its five-year low multiple while ROIC is at a high, so the market is discounting term-life franchise erosion [Likely]. Versus peers (GL, AFL, UNM, MET, PRU) P/E is -14% and EV/EBITDA -42%, but P/B is +71%; those peers are balance-sheet insurers, so the comparison is loose [Likely]. Mechanical fair value is base 261.22 (-11.0% vs the 293.39 close), bull 304.27 (+3.7%); the P/B + ROE leg is 295.31 and the P/E leg 193.06, so the fair value is a model estimate that splits sharply by method [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/pri/statistics/). Price is 4.7% above the 200-day MA, 12-month change +13.3%, 3-month momentum -6.2%. It is a fair price, not a clear discount [Likely].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROIC (ratios page, TTM) falls below 15%.
- FCF conversion (FCF/NI, cash flow page) falls below 100% for TTM.
- Net debt / EBITDA (site definition, ratios page) rises above 2.0x.
- Life-licensed sales force count (10-Q MD&A) is lower than in the prior year's 10-Q for two consecutive quarters, with new policies issued still falling.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Capital is returned heavily: the share count is down 4.86% YoY (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/pri/statistics/) [Certain]. Nine-month repurchases charged to retained earnings were $377.8M and dividends $112.7M [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1475922/000119312526449402/pri-20260930.htm). The FCF yield of 10.47% and leverage of 0.85x leave room for this to continue [Likely]. Cash conversion above 100% of net income supports it [Certain].

## 6. Unusual items
The 10-Q drops the "Risks Related to Legislative and Regulatory Changes and Government Policy Uncertainty" heading and its first risk factor, and incorporates the 2025 Annual Report's risk factors by reference. That reads as a restructure, not a dropped risk, but I did not read the replacement text [Guessing]. Higher interest rates in the third quarter increased unrealized investment losses, with no income-statement loss recognised [Certain] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1475922/000119312526449402/pri-20260930.htm). Short interest is 4.45% of float; beta 0.84 [Certain]. Latest results (headline only, no figures used): Q3 revenue and EPS rose, with record investment sales, while term life production and recruiting stayed soft [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/pri/transcripts/726469-q3-2026/).
