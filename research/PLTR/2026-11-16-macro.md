HY spread 3.2%, +0.49pp over ~3m (BAMLH0A0HYM2, 2026-11-12). VIX 18.7 (VIXCLS, 2026-11-12). [Certain]

**Pricing (proxy):** the 2y about 1pp above fed funds suggests no near-term easing is priced; this is a proxy. Breakevens are flat (-0.03pp over ~3m), so the rise is real-rate driven, the type that compresses long-duration multiples. **(inference)** The seven-session sell-off may already reflect part of it. [Likely]

**Surprise direction:** further real-yield rises or spread widening hurt; falling real yields help.

**Bull:** real yield below ~2.4%, spreads calm: the multiple holds. **Bear:** real yield above ~3.25% or HY spread above 4%: a de-rating, amplified by beta 1.61. Revenue and margin are unchanged in both.

**Tilt:** toward bear, small. Macro adds downside variance to a stretched multiple but leaves the fundamental thesis alone. [Guessing] on magnitude.
