# Fidelity National Information Services (FIS) — Core initiation — 2026-11-15
## 1. Business
FIS sells core banking, payments and capital-markets software and processing to banks and other financial institutions, mostly under multi-year recurring contracts [Certain] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1136893/000113689326000063/fis-20260930.htm). Banking Solutions was $2,548 million of $3,454 million Q3 revenue; Capital Market Solutions $823 million [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1136893/000113689326000063/fis-20260930.htm). Revenue outside North America was $768 million of that $3,454 million [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1136893/000113689326000063/fis-20260930.htm); the filing names no single foreign country above 10%. Issuer Solutions (bought from Global Payments, closed 9 January 2026) now sits in Banking.

## 2. Quality
Mixed, and improving only on a lagging basis. [Likely]
- Returns are low for a software-and-processing franchise: ROIC TTM 6.9%, up from 3.2% a year earlier, but ROCE has slipped (9.2% to 8.1%) (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/fis/financials/ratios/?p=trailing). Fiscal-year ROIC rose 0.9% to 5.3%, largely deleveraging after the Worldpay disposal.
- Operating margin 21.5% TTM, down from 22.6%; FCF margin 21.5%, down from 24.7% and 6.5pp below its 2-year high (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/fis/financials/income-statement/?p=trailing). Direction is currently negative on both.
- Moat: switching costs on core banking platforms and recurring contracts [Likely]. At the KBW conference, management said bank demand stays strong, but client-directed implementation delays and higher cyber investment weigh on 2027 margins (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/fis/transcripts/796331-kbw-fintech-ecosystem-conference-2026/) [Likely]. Pricing power is limited: the 10-Q says vendor cost increases can't be fully offset (source: research/FIS/sec-delta-2026-11-15.md) [Certain].
- Net debt/EBITDA 5.43x (4.06x FY2025); Altman Z 0.31 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/fis/statistics/).

## 3. Valuation
The price assumes little. EV/EBITDA 9.5x vs a 5y range of 14.9x–22.0x (below its minimum), +13% above the peer median of 8.4x. P/FCF 5.5x vs a 5y median of 12.9x; FCF yield 18.26% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/fis/statistics/). [Certain]

P/E 4.4x is distorted: TTM net margin of 26.8% and ROE of 23.0% include the one-off Worldpay stake sale gain, so ignore it. The model fair value is flagged unreliable (+231% base) and should be weighed lightly. The reverse DCF implies revenue shrinking about 25% in year 1, far harsher than recurring contracts suggest [Likely]. Leverage explains part of the discount: equity is a thin slice of an EV carrying 5.4x net debt/EBITDA, so small EBITDA misses move the equity a lot. The shares are down 55.4% over 12 months and 30.1% below the 200-day average; the market prices a guidance cut, a possible margin reset and integration risk [Likely].

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
The Issuer Solutions deal was funded with about $7.7 billion of new debt plus the Worldpay stake sale [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1136893/000113689326000063/fis-20260930.htm). The dividend is $0.44 per share a quarter, with $688 million declared over nine months, against buybacks of just $92 million [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1136893/000113689326000063/fis-20260930.htm). Net share count is down 1.75% YoY. FCF conversion of net income is 80.3% TTM; FCF margin is falling. Leverage rose from 4.06x to 5.43x, so debt paydown is the real priority and the dividend consumes much of the free cash [Likely].

## 6. Unusual items
- Why the dip: on 4 November the company lowered its 2026 outlook and its finance chief left abruptly (https://www.barrons.com/articles/fidelity-national-information-services-earnings-stock-price-444a4652) [Likely]. A law-firm "investigation" notice followed (https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/fis-shareholder-investigation-suewallst-notifies-investors-of-potential-securities-claims-involving-fidelity-national-information-services-302930334.html); solicitation, not a filed claim [Likely].
- The CFO exit raises a credibility question: management had previously voiced high visibility [Likely].
- The 10-Q Risk Factors show no substantive change; one integration-difficulty bullet was removed, consistent with the deal closing (source: research/FIS/sec-delta-2026-11-15.md) [Likely].
- Short interest 4.09% of float, up 7.5% on the month (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/fis/statistics/).
