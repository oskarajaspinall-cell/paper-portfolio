# Fidelity National Information Services (FIS) — Core initiation — 2026-10-09

## 1. Business
FIS sells financial institutions and capital-markets firms the software that runs their core operations: Banking Solutions (core processing, digital banking, card/item processing, fraud) and Capital Market Solutions (trading, asset servicing, treasury/risk) [Certain] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1136893/000113689326000013/fis-20251231.htm). It just exited merchant payments (Worldpay) and re-entered card issuing, buying Issuer Solutions from Global Payments while selling its remaining Worldpay stake to GPN, closed 2026-01-09 [Certain] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1136893/000113689326000013/fis-20251231.htm).

## 2. Quality
Both segments run on multi-year contracts the 10-K describes as generating "high client retention rates" because clients' systems of record and mission-critical back-office functions sit inside FIS's platforms — real switching costs [Likely] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1136893/000113689326000013/fis-20251231.htm). Operating margin rose from 15.3% (FY21) to 21.7% (TTM) and ROIC from 0.9% to 6.6% (TTM) [Certain] (source: factsheet). But net margin swung from -172.3% (FY22) to +27.6% (TTM) on Worldpay impairments/gains, not operations, and FCF margin fell from 48.1% to 23.1% (TTM) [Certain] (source: factsheet). Net debt/EBITDA is 5.82x TTM, up from 4.06x at FY25, after ~$7.7bn of new debt funded the Issuer Solutions deal [Certain] (source: factsheet; https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1136893/000113689326000013/fis-20251231.htm). Altman Z is 0.35, Piotroski F 4/9 — the Z-score sits deep in the distress band, a genuine flag even allowing the model fits asset-light software poorly [Likely] (source: factsheet). A durable, recurring-revenue business whose balance sheet has just been re-levered via another large portfolio swap.

## 3. Valuation
FIS trades at 5.3x P/E, 10.8x EV/EBITDA, 6.3x P/FCF and 1.1x P/B — below its own 5-year range and below the FI/GPN/JKHY/ACIW peer median on every multiple (EV/EBITDA -9%, P/FCF -60%, P/B -50% vs peers) [Certain] (source: factsheet). FCF yield is 15.94% [Certain] (source: factsheet). Blended fair value (base 99.35, bear 59.49, bull 183.24) sits far above the 34.34 last close; the reverse DCF implies the price assumes -11.9% revenue decline in year one fading to terminal growth — pricing structural decline, not just leverage risk [Certain] (source: factsheet). That gap is too large to take at face value given the distress-zone Altman Z and fresh debt: the market is pricing Issuer Solutions integration risk a pure historical-cash-flow DCF misses, so the fair-value gap is a ceiling, not a target.

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) fails to fall back toward ~4x within four quarters of the Issuer Solutions close
- Banking Solutions or Capital Market Solutions revenue growth (income statement, segment disclosure) turns negative for two consecutive quarters, undermining the recurring-revenue thesis
- FCF conversion (FCF/NI, cash flow + income statement) stays below 50% for two more fiscal years, well under its FY21-23 norm
- Altman Z-score (statistics page) falls further rather than recovering as integration costs run off

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
FCF margin has nearly halved since FY21 (48.1% → 23.1% TTM) and FCF conversion has been erratic (-22% to +697% across the last four fiscal years) as Worldpay-related items distorted net income [Certain] (source: factsheet). Shares outstanding fell -2.80% YoY, implying continued buybacks even as the company raised ~$7.7bn of new debt for M&A — allocating cash to both debt-funded growth and shareholder returns simultaneously [Certain] (source: factsheet; https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1136893/000113689326000013/fis-20251231.htm). That combination needs the Issuer Solutions integration to go cleanly for leverage to normalize.

## 6. Unusual items
Two large portfolio transactions sit inside the five-year numbers: the 2024 sale of a 55% Worldpay stake to GTCR and the January 2026 sale of the remaining stake plus purchase of Issuer Solutions from Global Payments, funded substantially with new debt [Certain] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1136893/000113689326000013/fis-20251231.htm). These explain the wild net-margin and FCF-conversion swings above and the current 5.82x leverage; they are not one-off noise to ignore but the key reason the balance sheet now carries more risk than the operating metrics alone suggest. Short interest rose +16.5% month-over-month to 3.66% of float, consistent with rising market skepticism around the re-leveraging [Likely] (source: factsheet). Recent headlines (IDC FinTech #1 ranking, new core-banking wins, Gartner recognition) are incremental commercial validation but not thesis-moving (source: https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20261007270746/en/FIS-Ranks-First-in-2026-IDC-FinTech-Rankings-Top-100/).
