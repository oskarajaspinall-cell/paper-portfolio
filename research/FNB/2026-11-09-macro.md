**Chain:** credit spreads / risk appetite -> expected credit losses and equity risk premium -> P/B multiple; second, policy path -> deposit costs -> NIM.

**Facts (FRED, 2026-11-05):** HY spread 3.48%, +0.77pp in 3m; VIX 28.18, +13.2 in 1m; fed funds 3.88%, +0.25pp in 3m; 2y 4.46%, -0.33 in 1m (https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=BAMLH0A0HYM2, https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=VIXCLS, https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=DGS2).

**Pricing (proxy):** the 2y sits about 58bp above fed funds, so some tightening is still priced, but fading over the month. **(inference)** The new information is the risk-off move, not the rate level.

**Inference:** wider spreads hurt FNB through provisions (charge-offs only 0.12% [company-reported], room to rise) and a P/B multiple already above its 5y max. A steeper curve helps NIM (3.29%).

**Bull:** spreads retrace, NIM drifts up, the multiple holds. **Bear:** HY above about 5% and VIX above 35 push provisions up and the multiple toward the bear fair value of 16.34 (-8.7%), mostly a multiple effect.

**Tilt:** toward bear, small. Beta is 0.91 and the stock is -1.3pp vs SPY over 3m, so some is priced.
