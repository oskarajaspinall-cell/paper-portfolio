# F.N.B. Corporation (FNB) — Core initiation — 2026-11-09
## 1. Business
Pennsylvania-based regional bank holding company (Banks - Regional). It earns net interest income on consumer and commercial loans and leases funded by deposits, plus fee income (wealth, capital markets, equipment leasing, mortgage). Third-quarter net interest income was $376.5m against $92.6m non-interest income [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/37808/000003780826000029/fnb-20260930.htm), so most revenue is spread income [Certain]. No foreign revenue exposure [Likely]; US-only footprint.

## 2. Quality
Average, not exceptional. ROE is 9.0% TTM, flat over five years (8.0% to 9.0%), with net margin 34.4% TTM, also flat (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/fnb/financials/ratios/). ROIC and ROCE are [data unavailable]. A regional deposit franchise has a modest moat: local relationships and low-cost deposits, but no pricing power over rates [Likely]. Third quarter showed record net interest income, net interest margin 3.29% (up 4bp year-on-year), loan growth 8.5% and deposit growth 8.9% annualized linked-quarter, net charge-offs 0.12% annualized, and non-performing loans plus OREO 0.33% [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/37808/000003780826000029/fnb-20260930.htm). Non-interest expense grew 5.5% year-on-year against revenue growth of 2.6% [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/37808/000003780826000029/fnb-20260930.htm), so operating leverage was negative in the quarter [Certain]; management guides to positive operating leverage for the year (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/fnb/transcripts/709996-q3-2026/) [Likely]. Piotroski F is 4, middling (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/fnb/statistics/).

## 3. Valuation
P/E 10.8x sits at 60% of its 5y range (9.7x to 11.6x, median 10.6x), a 17% discount to the peer median 13.1x (HBAN, CFG, RF, FITB, WBS). P/B 0.9x is above its 5y max yet 30% below peers at 1.3x, so the discount is to peers, not to its own history. P/FCF 8.9x (39% of range) and FCF yield 11.51% are noisy for a bank (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/fnb/financials/ratios/). Mechanical fair value base 19.47 vs close 17.90 (+8.8%; bear 16.34, bull 20.99), but flagged unreliable: methods disagree 3.6x (P/B+ROE base 15.54, FCFE 27.34, DDM 7.58). Weigh lightly [Guessing]. The price looks roughly fair for a bank earning a ~9% ROE; the discount to peers likely reflects lower returns than those peers [Likely]. Reverse DCF implies -5.2% year-1 revenue growth, undemanding.

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROE (ratios page) falls below 7.5% on a TTM basis.
- Net margin (income statement) falls below 30% on a TTM basis.
- Non-performing loans plus OREO to loans exceeds 0.60% in a quarterly 10-Q (sec.gov).
- Net interest margin (FTE) below 3.10% in a quarterly release, i.e. spread compression despite growth.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Buybacks plus a $0.13 quarterly dividend (source: https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/fnb-corporation-declares-cash-dividend-of-0-13-on-common-stock-302917380.html). Year-to-date repurchases were $133m, 2.1% of opening shares [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/37808/000003780826000029/fnb-20260930.htm); shares fell 1.13% year-on-year (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/fnb/statistics/). FCF conversion is 119.1% TTM but volatile (66.5% in FY2025, 255.8% in FY2022), falling over five years (-50.0pp) though FCF margin rose over 12 months, 35.0% to 41.0%; bank cash flow swings with working capital, so it is not a quality signal [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/fnb/financials/cash-flow-statement/).
