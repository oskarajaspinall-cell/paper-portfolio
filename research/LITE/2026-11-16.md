# Lumentum Holdings (LITE) — Core initiation — 2026-11-16
## 1. Business
Lumentum sells optical and photonic components (laser chips, laser assemblies, line subsystems) and Systems (cloud transceivers, optical circuit switches) to AI/cloud and telecom networks. Growth is driven by data-centre build-outs; management says demand is outpacing supply [Certain] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1633978/000162828026069615/lite-20260926.htm). Geographic mix is not in the fact sheet [data unavailable]; no single foreign country was confirmed at ~10%+.

## 2. Quality
Profitability has turned sharply: TTM ROIC 23.0% vs -3.6% a year earlier, operating margin 23.4% vs -5.7%, gross margin 48.0% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/lite/financials/ratios/?p=trailing). The 5-year fiscal trend is falling (ROIC -4.1pp) because FY2023-25 were loss years, so this is a cyclical recovery from a trough, not a record of steady returns [Likely]. Last quarter's gross margin rose on mix, utilisation and lower amortisation [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1633978/000162828026069615/lite-20260926.htm). Moat is capacity and laser-chip know-how in a supply-constrained niche; durability through a capex cycle is unproven [Guessing]. TTM FCF margin 9.5% is well below operating margin, because capex is heavy [Likely]. Net debt/EBITDA -0.48x: net cash [Certain]. Beta 1.65.

## 3. Valuation
EV/EBITDA 43.5x, 27% through its 5y range (9.9x–134.6x), +59% vs peers (COHR, CIEN, FN, AAOI); EV/Sales 13.4x (+133% vs peers); P/FCF 141.9x; FCF yield 0.70%; P/E [data unavailable] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/lite/statistics/). The mechanical base fair value is 254.21 USD vs a 596.62 close (-57.4%); bull 1,148.66 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/lite/). [Likely] The price discounts several more years of steep growth and margin expansion, and the fair-value model rests on one method (DCF unusable: history too short). The stock is 30.7% under its 50-day average after a 32.2% 3-month fall, but is still +138.5% over 12 months.

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Gross margin (income statement page) falls below 40% on a TTM basis.
- Operating margin (TTM, ratios/income statement) falls below 15%.
- TTM FCF margin (cash flow page) turns negative as capex outruns cash generation.
- Net debt/EBITDA (statistics page) moves above +1.5x.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Shares outstanding rose 11.16% YoY (dilution) (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/lite/statistics/). Convertible notes are being converted early and partly settled in cash, and a preferred issue funded capacity [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1633978/000162828026069615/lite-20260926.htm). FCF/NI is -5.4% TTM, so earnings are not yet converting to cash [Certain].

## 6. Unusual items
- **Pending all-stock takeover by Marvell [Certain]**: a merger agreement was signed on 1 Nov 2026 at a fixed exchange ratio of 4.500 Marvell shares per LITE share [company-reported]; termination fee payable by LITE in some cases $2.9bn [company-reported]; closing needs both shareholder votes, antitrust and other approvals, and Marvell need not accept materially adverse remedies (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1633978/000162828026069615/lite-20260926.htm). Because the ratio is fixed, LITE's price is largely a derivative of Marvell's, not of Lumentum's fundamentals [Likely]. Both stocks fell on the announcement and shareholders criticised the terms (source: https://www.marketwatch.com/story/marvell-and-lumentum-shareholders-unite-in-panning-merger-deal-its-a-trash-offer-53a9f307; https://www.barrons.com/articles/marvell-lumentum-stock-merger-risks-58a5d5e8). If the deal fails, LITE's price could fall further [Guessing]; if it closes, the holding becomes Marvell exposure.
- **FCC risk [Likely]**: the FCC is reportedly considering putting optical transceivers on its Covered List; no final rule, timing uncertain (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1633978/000162828026069615/lite-20260926.htm).
- Short interest 8.99% of float, up 4.8% on the month; beta 1.65 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/lite/statistics/).
- Executive retirement drove a stock-compensation spike in R&D [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1633978/000162828026069615/lite-20260926.htm).
