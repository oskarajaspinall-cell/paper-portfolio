Under the fixed 4.500-for-1 Marvell exchange ratio, LITE's price follows Marvell's multiple (research note section 6).

**Pricing (proxy):** a 2y yield about 1pp above fed funds implies hikes are priced **(inference)**. The yield rise is real rather than inflation-driven, which hurts long-duration equities most. The stock is 30.7% below its 50-day average after a 32.2% 3m fall, so part of this is priced, but I can't separate it from the merger reaction **(inference)**.

**Bull:** real yields below 2.5%, spreads near 3%: the multiple stabilises.
**Bear:** real yields above 3.25% or HY above 4.5%: multiple compression with no earnings change; deal repricing risk rises.
**Tilt:** toward bear, small. Merger terms and FCC risk dominate.

No material foreign-country revenue share is confirmed, so no country-risk check applies.
