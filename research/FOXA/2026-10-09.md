# Fox Corporation (FOXA) — Core initiation — 2026-10-09

## 1. Business
Fox owns cable networks (Fox News, Fox Business, FS1, Big Ten Network), the Fox broadcast network and owned-and-operated local TV stations, plus Tubi (free ad-supported streaming) and the newer FOX One direct-to-consumer service. Revenue is affiliate/subscription fees, linear and digital advertising, and licensing of live news and NFL/MLB/college-sports rights [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/foxa/).

## 2. Quality
ROIC and ROCE have risen for several straight years to 17.4%/17.5% TTM, operating margin to 20.3%, with Piotroski F-score 7 and Altman Z 3.31 confirming balance-sheet and earnings strength; net debt/EBITDA is modest at 0.87x [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/foxa/financials/ratios/, https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/foxa/statistics/). Durable moat: Fox News is the long-standing #1 cable-news franchise and Fox holds exclusive NFL/MLB/Big Ten rights, both scarce and hard for competitors to replicate [Likely]. Against this, FCF margin fell from 18.4% to 8.6% and FCF conversion (FCF/NI) from 132% to 87% in FY2026 [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/foxa/financials/cash-flow-statement/) — an earnings-quality red flag coinciding with costs tied to the pending Roku deal. The business also faces structural pay-TV subscriber decline, only partly offset by Tubi/FOX One digital growth [Likely].

## 3. Valuation
At 16.6x P/E and 18.1x P/FCF, FOXA trades above the top of its own 5-year range (154%/165% of range), yet at a 34-38% discount to peer (WBD, PARA, DIS, NWSA) multiples on P/E and EV/EBITDA; EV/EBITDA at 7.7x sits mid-range historically (86%) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/foxa/financials/ratios/). The fact sheet's DCF-based base-case fair value is $58.52 vs last close $63.57 — price is roughly 8.6% above the base estimate; bear case is -20.2%, bull (which embeds Roku upside) is +77.1% [Likely]. Reverse DCF implies only 4.9% year-1 revenue growth fading to terminal — a modest, plausible assumption, not a heroic one [Likely]. Net: cheap vs. media peers, but no longer cheap vs. its own history or a conservative DCF — the re-rating from depressed multiples has largely already happened.

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROIC (ratios page) falls below 12% for two consecutive fiscal years
- FCF conversion, FCF/NI (cash-flow + income statement) stays below 90% for two more fiscal years
- Net debt/EBITDA (balance sheet + income statement) rises above 2.0x
- Operating margin (income statement) falls below 17% for two consecutive fiscal years, signalling cord-cutting is no longer offset by Tubi/FOX One

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Shares outstanding fell 4.77% YoY, consistent with ongoing buybacks funded historically by strong FCF [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/foxa/statistics/). FY2026 FCF margin dropped to 8.6% from 18.4% a year earlier and conversion to 87% from 132% [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/foxa/financials/cash-flow-statement/) — a one-year deterioration worth monitoring given the pending $22bn Roku acquisition, which would add leverage not yet reflected in the balance sheet since the deal has not closed [Likely] (source: fact sheet news item, https://deadline.com/2026/09/fox-corp-roku-merger-doj-review-1237072899/).

## 6. Unusual items
Two pending, material events are not yet in the numbers. First, Fox's $22bn Roku acquisition is under expanded DOJ antitrust review (a "second request") as of September 2026, with no confirmed closing date [Certain] (source: https://www.reuters.com/legal/litigation/us-justice-department-widens-probe-into-foxs-roku-deal-semafor-reports-2026-09-08/, as listed in the fact sheet); a block or delay would remove the bull-case upside embedded in fair value, while a contested closing adds integration and leverage risk. Second, Reuters reported (Aug 27, 2026) the Murdoch family is weighing recombining Fox Corp with News Corp, an unconfirmed governance development that could alter capital structure or the family's control premium [Guessing] (source: https://www.reuters.com/world/rupert-murdoch-considering-re-merger-fox-corp-news-corp-court-documents-video-2026-08-27/, as listed in the fact sheet). Short interest is elevated at 13.66% of float, though it fell slightly month-on-month [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/foxa/statistics/).
