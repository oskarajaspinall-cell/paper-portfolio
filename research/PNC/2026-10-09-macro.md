## Assessment
Chain: policy/long-end rates -> (a) asset repricing -> net interest margin/NII, and (b) the 10y Treasury as PNC's CAPM discount rate -> DCF/DDM fair value; credit spreads -> provision for credit losses -> net margin and equity risk premium [Likely] **(inference)**.

Facts (FRED, 2026-10-07/08): DGS10 5.28%, +0.50pp 1m, +0.72pp 3m; DFII10 (real) 2.92%, +0.49pp 1m, +0.61pp 3m; DFF 3.88%, +0.25pp 1m (effective funds rate has risen, not fallen); T10YIE 2.35%, roughly flat (-0.02pp 1m); BAMLH0A0HYM2 (HY spread) 3.09%, +0.41pp 1m; VIX 15.08, low/falling. Real yields rose with breakevens flat, so the move is a tighter-policy/term-premium signal, not an inflation surprise [Likely] **(inference)**. Market pricing: DGS2 (4.77%) above effective DFF (3.88%) is a rough proxy that the market still expects some further tightening, not imminent cuts [Likely] **(inference, proxy)**. This corroborates PNC's own Sept 17 prime-rate hike to 7.00% (fact sheet), which lifts loan-book yields now.

The fact sheet's own DCF already uses DGS10=5.28% and shows PNC trading above even its bull fair value ($219.68 vs $210.19) [Certain]. Continued yield rises push the DCF band lower, deepening the overvaluation gap; a pullback in yields or spreads would narrow it. HY spreads (+0.41pp 1m) are an early, not yet alarming, provisioning-risk signal (still far below stress levels).

**Bull**: yields/spreads ease -> discount rate falls -> fair value range rises toward price. **Bear**: yields and HY spreads keep rising -> fair value falls further below price and provisions risk rises. **Tilt**: toward bear, small — reinforces the existing valuation call rather than creating a new one.
