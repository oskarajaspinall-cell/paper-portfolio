# Macro overlay: American Tower Corporation (AMT) — 2026-10-12

## Assessment
AMT is a highly levered (net debt/EBITDA 6.1x [Certain], factsheet RA) REIT whose DCF fair value and refinancing cost both run off the risk-free rate. **Chain**: real yields → cost of equity (discount rate) → P/AFFO-proxy fair value; also real yields/credit spreads → refinancing cost on AMT's rolling debt (new senior notes priced 2026-09-09, Business Wire) → AFFO growth.

**Facts**: US 10y real yield (DFII10) 2.87%, +44bp ~1m, +56bp ~3m (FRED, 2026-10-08). 10y nominal (DGS10) 5.22%, +68bp 3m; breakeven inflation (T10YIE) only +9bp 3m (FRED, 2026-10-09) — the move is mostly a real-rate repricing, not inflation fear. HY credit spread (BAMLH0A0HYM2) 3.15%, +45bp 3m (FRED, 2026-10-08). The fact sheet's fair value already uses today's 5.22% risk-free rate, so the level is priced in; what matters is further change.

**Market pricing (proxy, inference)**: 2y Treasury (4.75%) sits ~87bp above effective fed funds (3.88%, FRED, 2026-10-08) [Likely] **(inference)** — consistent with pricing further tightening, matching the ETF Trends headline noting a hike delivered and another possible by year-end (2026-09-30, factsheet news).

**Bull**: real yields stabilize/fall → lower discount rate, re-rating toward the $234-267 fair value, cheaper refinancing, aiding the 2027 AFFO inflection management flagged. **Bear**: real yields/HY spreads keep rising → discount rate rises further, fair value compresses below $234, refinancing cost rises, delaying deleveraging. **Tilt**: moderate toward bear — leverage and the discount-rate valuation make AMT more exposed to a hawkish surprise than the peer-discount case implies alone.
