# American Tower Corporation (AMT) — Core initiation — 2026-10-12

## 1. Business
American Tower owns and leases space on communications towers to wireless carriers (long-term contracts with built-in rent escalators), plus a data-center colocation business (CoreSite), across the US and international markets [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/amt/). Market cap $84.9bn [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/amt/).

## 2. Quality
ROIC is rising (6.3%→8.0% TTM over five years) but ROE has fallen (37.1%→33.9% TTM, -10.8pp trend) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/amt/financials/ratios/). Leverage remains high but improving: net debt/EBITDA (site definition) fell from 8.50x to 6.13x [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/amt/financials/ratios/). Piotroski F-Score of 5 is mid-range [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/amt/statistics/). The moat is structural: towers and data-center sites are costly to replicate and tenants face high switching costs, supporting escalator-driven pricing power [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/amt/). Management's Q2 2026 call flagged DISH-related tenant churn and refinancing costs as headwinds, expected to subside into 2027 [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/amt/transcripts/656096-q2-2026/). AMT's 10-Q shows active refinancing of near-term maturities in H1 2026, consistent with managing a large debt load at still-elevated rates [Certain] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1053507/000105350726000133/amt-20260630.htm). Net: a durable, oligopoly-like infrastructure asset with improving but still modest returns, and real near-term churn/refinancing risk.

## 3. Valuation
AMT trades below its own 5-year range and peer medians on P/E (25.1x vs 5y 32.5-67.8x and peer 49.2x, -49%), EV/EBITDA (18.2x vs 5y 19.1-29.9x and peer 23.8x, -24%) and EV/Sales (11.7x vs 5y 12.4-18.8x and peer 12.6x, -7%) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/amt/financials/ratios/, peer statistics pages). P/B of 22.8x looks extreme (221% above its 5y max; peer median 4.8x, n=2), but tower/data-center REITs often carry low or negative book equity after buybacks and depreciation, so it is not a reliable cross-check [Likely] (source: fact sheet valuation table). FCF yield is 4.66% [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/amt/statistics/). Model fair value (P/AFFO-proxy) shows upside in every scenario versus the $182.24 close: bear +15.5% ($210.40), base +28.5% ($234.10), bull +46.5% ($266.91) [Certain] (source: fact sheet fair value table; inform-only). The market is pricing in a stalled leverage/margin recovery; the discount to peers looks too wide given the improving ROIC and leverage trends in Quality.

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROIC (ratios page) falls back below 6%, its FY2022 trough, reversing the five-year improving trend.
- Net debt/EBITDA, site definition (ratios page), rises back above 7x, reversing the deleveraging trend.
- ROE (ratios page) falls below 12%, below its FY2023 trough, signaling returns compressing further.
- Piotroski F-Score (statistics page) drops below 5, its current level, indicating financial deterioration.

AMT priced a new senior notes offering in September 2026 to refinance maturities, and its 10-Q shows elevated near-term debt maturities versus year-end 2025 — a refinancing item to monitor given still-high leverage, though not unusual for this REIT [Certain] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1053507/000105350726000133/amt-20260630.htm; headline: https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260909933334/en/American-Tower-Corporation-Prices-Senior-Notes-Offering/).
