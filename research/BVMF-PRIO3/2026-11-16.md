# Prio S.A. (BVMF:PRIO3) — Core initiation — 2026-11-16
## 1. Business
Independent Brazilian offshore oil producer (Oil & Gas E&P). It buys mature or under-developed fields (Peregrino, Wahoo) and cuts lifting costs; revenue is crude sales priced off Brent. It reports in USD but is quoted in BRL. Revenue split by country or buyer: [data unavailable] on the fact sheet; not verified from a primary source. (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/bvmf/PRIO3/) [Likely]

## 2. Quality
Returns are high but volatile and have fallen: ROIC was 35.0% in FY2021 and 48.6% in FY2022, then 4.8% in FY2025, and is 16.2% TTM (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/bvmf/PRIO3/financials/ratios/) [Certain]. The last year contradicts the 5-year fall: ROIC rose from 7.7% to 16.2% and operating margin from 23.4% to 36.9%, still below the 2-year-high of 50.5% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/bvmf/PRIO3/financials/ratios/?p=trailing) [Certain]. The FY2025 trough looks like acquisition-year distortion plus weaker oil prices, not a broken model [Guessing]. A moat is thin: it is a price-taker whose edge is cost and execution on acquired assets, not a durable barrier [Likely]. Management reported record production in the Q3 2026 call (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/bvmf/PRIO3/transcripts/664368-q3-2026/) [Likely]. Piotroski F is 7 and Altman Z is 2.07 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/bvmf/PRIO3/statistics/) [Certain].

## 3. Valuation
The price is 63.29 BRL (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/bvmf/PRIO3/history/). P/E is 8.1x, at 42% of its 5y range (3.3x to 14.9x), but EV/EBITDA of 4.1x is below its 5y minimum of 4.5x and EV/Sales of 2.7x is below its minimum of 2.9x (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/bvmf/PRIO3/financials/ratios/) [Certain]. Against peers (PETR4, BRAV3, RECV3) it trades at a premium: P/E +75%, EV/EBITDA +17%, P/B +130% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/bvmf/PETR4/statistics/) [Certain]. The premium reflects higher returns and growth, but leaves little for a lower oil price [Likely]. FCF yield is -1.22%, so earnings are not yet cash (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/bvmf/PRIO3/statistics/). Fair value: [data unavailable]; the NAV method needs reserve data and EBITDA-based fallback failed. The stock is 3.4% above its 200-day average with 12-month change of +58.2% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/bvmf/PRIO3/statistics/) [Certain].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROIC (ratios page, TTM) falls below 10%.
- Operating margin (income statement, TTM) falls below 25%.
- Net debt / EBITDA (ratios page) rises above 2.5x.
- FCF margin (cash flow, TTM) is still negative after two further quarterly reports, i.e. growth capex never converts to cash.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Weak conversion. FCF margin was -46.9% in FY2023, -35.7% in FY2024 and -57.9% in FY2025, so heavy acquisition and development spending was funded by debt: net debt/EBITDA went from -0.26x (FY2021) to 3.24x (FY2025). It has since improved to 1.14x TTM and TTM FCF margin is -2.4% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/bvmf/PRIO3/financials/cash-flow-statement/) [Certain]. Shares change YoY: [data unavailable]. The case rests on spending turning into free cash from here [Likely].

## 6. Unusual items
- Country risk: operations are in Brazil, shares are quoted in BRL, financials in USD; the macro overlay should check BRL and Selic [Likely].
- Cyclicality: oil-price exposure is direct and unhedged on the fact sheet [Guessing].
- Beta (5y) is 0.04, so beta understates the oil risk (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/bvmf/PRIO3/statistics/) [Certain].
- Fact sheet data conflict: next earnings shows Nov 4, 2026, but Q3 results were listed on 2026-11-05 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/bvmf/PRIO3/) [Certain].
