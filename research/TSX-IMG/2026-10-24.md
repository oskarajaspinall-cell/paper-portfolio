# IAMGOLD (TSX:IMG) — Core initiation — 2026-10-24
## 1. Business
IAMGOLD is a Canadian gold miner (Materials / Gold; reports in USD, quoted in CAD). Earnings are gold price times ounces sold, less mine costs. The Côté Gold mine in Québec is the main driver, and the company also has Essakane and the Nelligan development project [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tsx/IMG/, https://www.nasdaq.com/articles/iamgold-corporation-reports-preliminary-q3-2026-production-64400-attributable-ounces-cote).

## 2. Quality
Returns have turned sharply, but the record is short. ROIC was 0.0% in FY2023 and 30.5% on a TTM basis; gross margin rose from 12.6% (FY2023) to 50.3% TTM, and FCF margin from -82.7% to 42.9% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tsx/IMG/financials/ratios/). [Certain] The swing reflects a build-out phase ending and a high gold price, so the figures are cyclical as well as structural. [Likely]

A gold miner has no durable moat: price is set by the market, and the edge is a low-cost, long-life asset. Côté Gold appears to be that, and management says it is prioritising optimisation and mine-life extension over M&A (source: https://www.marketbeat.com/instant-alerts/event-iamgold-eyes-ct-gold-optimization-essakane-extension-over-ma-2026-09-28/). [Likely] The balance sheet is clean: net debt/EBITDA is 0.02x, Altman Z 5.46 and Piotroski F 8 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tsx/IMG/statistics/). [Certain] Beta is 2.35, so the stock amplifies gold and equity swings. [Certain]

## 3. Valuation
P/E is 8.1x against a 5y median of 12.9x, and EV/EBITDA is 4.4x against 10.0x (12th percentile of its own range). Against peers it is in line: P/E -4%, EV/EBITDA -2%, P/B -0% (peer median 2.1x), EV/Sales -13%. FCF yield is 17.30% and P/FCF 5.8x versus a peer median of 19.0x [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tsx/IMG/financials/ratios/).

The 5y history is mostly a loss-making build phase, so "cheap versus history" overstates the discount. In line with peers on earnings multiples, the price assumes current gold-driven margins roughly persist. The mechanical base fair value is 13.76 CAD against a 22.72 CAD close (-39.5%). Bear is 4.08 and bull 53.19. The Mine NAV method is [data unavailable]. Fair value is a single estimate, built entirely on EV/EBITDA. [Likely] That is a wide, unflattering range, and the price implies no margin of safety on it.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
FCF conversion is 136.7% of net income (TTM), so earnings are backed by cash. Leverage fell from 5.08x net debt/EBITDA (FY2023) to 0.02x. Shares outstanding rose 1.16% year on year, mild dilution. Management favours organic growth over M&A, and major expansions are postponed [Likely] (source: https://www.marketbeat.com/instant-alerts/event-iamgold-eyes-ct-gold-optimization-essakane-extension-over-ma-2026-09-28/).
