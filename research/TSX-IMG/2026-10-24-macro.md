# Macro overlay: IAMGOLD Corporation (TSX:IMG) — 2026-10-24
## Assessment
**Gate: primary [Likely].** IAMGOLD is a gold producer (USD reporter; https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tsx/IMG/). Its TTM operating margin is 47.4% and its net debt/EBITDA is ~0.0x (https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tsx/IMG/financials/ratios/, 2026-10-24). Earnings are therefore mostly a function of the gold price, with little financing leverage.

**Chain:** US real yields and the dollar -> gold price -> realised price per ounce -> margin and FCF (**inference**). IMG trades at 4.4x EV/EBITDA, 12% of its 5y range (fact sheet).

**Facts (FRED):**
- 10y TIPS yield 2.64% on 2026-10-22, +0.21pp over ~3m (https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=DFII10).
- Broad dollar index 121.4 on 2026-10-16, +2.48 over ~1m (https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=DTWEXBGS).
- 2y yield 4.65% against fed funds 3.88% (https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=DGS2; https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=DFF).

**Pricing (proxy):** The 2y above fed funds implies no cuts priced. **(inference)** Rising real yields and a firmer dollar are a headwind for gold.

**Not in the snapshot:** the gold price. I could not verify it, so the transmission is inferred.

**Bull:** Real yields roll over or the dollar weakens, or risk-off flows lift gold. Margin and FCF expand, and the multiple re-rates toward the peer/5y median.
**Bear:** Real yields above 2.75% with a stronger dollar push gold lower. Margins compress from 47%, and the multiple stays near its trough.

**Tilt:** toward bear, small. Rates moved against gold over 3m, but the 10y yield is flat over 1m. The Côté ramp-up dominates the near-term path.
