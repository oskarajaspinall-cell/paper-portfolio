## 1. Business
Gold producer and developer; revenue is gold sales from its operating mines, with Oko West (Guyana) under construction, first gold expected next September per a management talk (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tsx/GMIN/transcripts/634674-mining-forum-americas-2026/) [Likely]. ## 2. Quality
Returns have turned sharply: ROIC 23.8% TTM vs -3.8% in FY2023, operating margin 65.9%, gross margin 68.9% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tsx/GMIN/financials/ratios/) [Certain]. Net debt/EBITDA is -0.40x (net cash) and Piotroski F is 8 [Certain]. The moat is low-cost ore bodies, not a competitive barrier; the price of gold sets returns, so margins are cyclical [Likely]. Direction change: FCF margin fell from 6.5% to -16.3% over twelve months, consistent with Oko West construction capex rather than operating weakness [Likely]. Shares rose 4.36% YoY (acquisition and funding dilution) [Certain], partly offset by a new buyback programme announced 2026-08-28 (source: https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/08/28/3352608/0/en/g-mining-ventures-announces-normal-course-issuer-bid-for-common-shares.html) [Certain].
## 3. Valuation
P/E is 22.0x, below its 5y minimum of 23.9x, but EV/EBITDA is 20.0x against a 5y range of 15.9x-17.5x, and EV/Sales is 14.9x. Peers trade on 12.5x P/E and 6.9x EV/EBITDA, so GMIN is +77% and +190% above them (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tsx/GMIN/statistics/) [Certain]. The 5y range is short and covers the build-out years, so it is a weak anchor [Likely]. Mechanical fair value rests on EV/EBITDA alone, because NAV needs mine-level data the site lacks: base 49.78 CAD against a 47.21 CAD close, +5.5% [Certain]. The price already assumes current gold prices persist and Oko West is delivered on time [Likely]. FCF yield is -1.07% [Certain].
## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROIC (ratios page, TTM) falls below 12%.
- Operating margin (income statement, TTM) falls below 55%.
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) rises above 1.5x.
- Oko West first gold is delayed beyond the first half of 2028, per company releases.
## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Cash conversion is poor for now: FCF margin is -16.3% TTM and FCF/NI is -28.8%, as growth capex outruns operating cash [Certain]. Net cash and a repurchase programme are positives, but the share count is still growing [Certain]. Returns on the Oko West spend are the key test [Likely].
