# Genpact Limited (G) — Core initiation — 2026-10-18
## 1. Business
Genpact runs outsourced, technology-enabled business processes (finance, supply chain, banking, insurance) for large enterprises, positioning itself as an "Agentic Operations" company layering AI/agentic tools onto core services (source: sec-delta-2026-10-18.md, 10-Q MD&A). Revenue splits between Core Business Services and Advanced Technology Solutions, sold across Financial Services, Consumer/Healthcare and High Tech/Manufacturing verticals [Certain].

## 2. Quality
Returns are rising and already attractive: ROIC 12.7%→17.7% TTM, ROCE 13.9%→18.2% TTM, ROE 19.8%→22.4% TTM over FY2021–TTM, with operating margin up to 15.0% TTM from 12.7% (fact sheet, ratios/income statement) [Certain]. The moat is switching costs: multi-year contracts embed Genpact into clients' core finance/ops workflows, deepened by a proprietary agentic-AI layer the 10-Q highlights as a key competitive factor (source: sec-delta-2026-10-18.md) [Likely]. Leverage has fallen steadily (net debt/EBITDA 1.63x FY21 → 0.66x FY25, 1.02x TTM) and Altman Z (3.56) / Piotroski F (7) show no distress [Certain]. FCF conversion is strong but decelerating (173.5% FY21 → 98.2% TTM, FY25 133.0%) [Certain]. A new CFO (Sumita Pandit, effective 2026-09-09, succeeding Michael Weiner) started six weeks ago — standard transition risk (source: https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/genpact-appoints-sumita-pandit-as-chief-financial-officer-302872147.html) [Certain].

## 3. Valuation
G trades at a 33-43% discount to peers (EXLS, WNS, ACN, CTSH) on every multiple: P/E 11.1x vs 19.1x, EV/EBITDA 8.1x vs 12.8x, EV/Sales 1.4x vs 2.2x, P/FCF 10.9x vs 16.3x, P/B 2.4x vs 4.2x, and sits at/below its own 5-year range on EV/EBITDA, EV/Sales, P/FCF and P/B (fact sheet) [Certain]. FCF yield is 9.14% [Certain]. Base fair value is $50.60 (+35.8% vs the $37.26 close), bear $39.20 (+5.2%), bull $80.40 (+115.8%) — even the bear case shows upside [Certain]. The reverse DCF implies the price assumes -2.8% year-1 revenue growth fading to terminal — more pessimistic than the company's own reported Q2 2026 segment growth (Financial Services +3.3%, Consumer/Healthcare +9.5%, High Tech/Manufacturing +7.6% y/y) [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1398659/000139865926000014/g-20260630.htm) [Likely]. The market is pricing stagnation despite rising margins and ROIC.

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROIC (ratios page) falls below 14%, reversing the 5-year rising trend.
- Operating margin (income statement) falls below 13%, reversing the margin-expansion trend.
- Net revenue growth (10-Q/10-K MD&A) turns negative for two consecutive quarters.
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) rises back above 1.3x, reversing the deleveraging trend.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Deleveraging is the dominant capital-allocation story: net debt/EBITDA fell from 1.63x (FY21) to 0.66x (FY25), 1.02x TTM [Certain]. Shares outstanding fell 2.80% y/y, implying net buybacks [Certain]. The board declared its regular quarterly cash dividend on 2026-10-15 (source: https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/genpact-limited-board-declares-quarterly-cash-dividend-302910246.html) [Certain] (amount is a headline figure, not used as data per house rules). FCF conversion remains comfortably above 100% of net income on a 5y average basis despite the TTM dip to 98.2%, consistent with a capital-light services model funding both buybacks and the dividend without releveraging [Certain].

## 6. Unusual items
Leadership transition: new CFO Sumita Pandit started 2026-09-09; a new Chief Product/Platform Officer (ex-ASAPP CEO) also joined in August 2026 — two senior hires within two months is a cluster worth monitoring but not yet a red flag [Likely] (source: https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/genpact-names-priya-vijayarajendran-chief-product-and-platform-officer-302864514.html). CEO BK Kalra publicly flagged that enterprise AI readiness is lagging ambition (2026-09-17, CNBC), a demand-pacing risk for Genpact's agentic-AI growth pitch [Guessing]. Short interest is moderate at 7.67% of float, falling 3.5% m/m — no stress signal [Certain].
