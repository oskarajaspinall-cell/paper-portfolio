# Pandora A/S (CPH:PNDORA) — Core initiation — 2026-11-15
## 1. Business
Designs, makes and sells branded affordable-luxury jewellery (charms, bracelets, rings), mostly through its own stores and online plus wholesale partners. Production is in Thailand, with a new Vietnam factory opened 2026-10-01 (source: https://www.reuters.com/business/retail-consumer/denmarks-pandora-opens-worlds-largest-jewellery-factory-vietnam-2026-10-01/) [Likely]. Silver is the key input; 2027 needs are largely hedged (source: https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/13/pandora-platinum-plated-jewelry-silver-prices.html) [Likely]. Revenue share by country: [data unavailable]; the US is a major market with weak consumer sentiment (source: https://www.reuters.com/business/retail-consumer/pandora-ceo-says-new-jewellery-collections-driving-growth-2026-08-13/) [Likely].

## 2. Quality
Gross margin of 78.9% (TTM) and operating margin of 23.2% show real brand pricing power; gross margin is up 3.2pp over five years (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/cph/PNDORA/financials/income-statement/) [Certain]. Returns are high but eroding: ROIC fell from 43.8% (FY2021) to 27.3% TTM, ROCE is 38.6% TTM versus 50.3% a year earlier, and FCF margin is 17.6% versus 23.3% at its 2024 peak (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/cph/PNDORA/financials/ratios/?p=trailing) [Certain]. Net debt/EBITDA rose from 0.44x to 1.90x, driven by buybacks (shares -4.10% YoY) [Likely]. Q3 organic growth was 6% (LFL 4%, network 2%) and guidance was narrowed to the upper end (source: https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/11/04/3395013/0/en/pandora-delivers-6-organic-growth-in-q3-guidance-narrowed-towards-upper-end.html) [Likely]. The moat is brand and scale in an easily imitated category, so it is moderate rather than wide [Guessing]. Direction is mixed: the business is still excellent, but every return metric is lower than a year ago [Certain].

## 3. Valuation
Price 933.00 DKK; P/E 13.5x versus a 5y median of 16.2x (43% of range), EV/EBITDA 8.3x (22% of range), P/FCF 12.0x (60%), FCF yield 8.31% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/cph/PNDORA/statistics/) [Certain]. Versus peers (SIG, CFR, UHR, RMS) P/E is -52% and EV/EBITDA -48%, but those peers are mostly higher-end luxury, so the discount is partly deserved [Likely]. Mechanical fair value is 1,023.24 DKK base (+9.7%), 542.46 bear (-41.9%), 1,436.03 bull (+53.9%). The reverse DCF implies 4.3% year-1 revenue growth, fading [Certain]. The stock is 38.4% above its 200-day average after a +80.7% six-month run, so much of the re-rating has happened. It is fairly priced with modest upside and a skewed-down bear case [Likely].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROIC TTM (ratios trailing page) falls below 22%.
- Gross margin (income statement page) falls below 76% for a TTM period, showing silver or platinum costs are not passed on.
- Net debt/EBITDA (statistics/ratios page) rises above 2.5x.
- FCF margin (cash flow page, TTM) falls below 14%.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
FCF conversion is 111.9% of net income (TTM), down from 142.6% in FY2021. The share count is shrinking 4.10% a year, so buybacks are the main use of cash and leverage has risen to 1.90x [Certain]. ROE of 131.8% reflects leverage and shrinking equity rather than operating improvement [Likely]. Capex is rising with the Vietnam plant [Likely].
