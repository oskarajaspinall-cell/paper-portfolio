# Occidental Petroleum Corporation (OXY) — Core initiation — 2026-10-10

## 1. Business
OXY is a US oil & gas producer (Permian, DJ Basin, Gulf of Mexico, Middle East) selling crude, NGLs and gas at market prices [Certain]. OxyChem makes chlor-alkali/PVC and other basic chemicals, sold on contract/spot [Certain] (source: https://www.oxy.com). Low Carbon Ventures runs CO2 enhanced-oil-recovery and the STRATOS direct-air-capture plant, an emerging, not yet material, revenue stream [Likely] (source: https://www.oxy.com).

## 2. Quality
A capital-intensive, price-taking commodity producer: no pricing power, returns tracking the oil cycle, not a moat [Certain]. ROIC fell from 6.3% (FY21) to 4.3% (FY25), TTM 8.6% on the recent oil-price bounce; ROCE and ROE show the same falling 5y trend (-1.6pp, -8.4pp) (source: stockanalysis.com ratios page) [Certain]. FCF margin fell from 29.1% (FY21) to 17.7% (FY25), TTM 18.9% (-11.4pp over 5y) (source: stockanalysis.com cash-flow/income statement) [Certain]. The one genuine structural edge is low-cost Permian acreage plus proprietary CO2-EOR infrastructure, which lowers the cost curve versus peers without CO2 flood capability, but this is a cost advantage, not pricing power, and DAC/STRATOS is unproven at scale [Likely] (source: https://www.oxy.com). Piotroski F-score is strong at 7/9, but Altman Z of 1.83 sits in the distress "grey zone" (<1.8 = distress) (source: stockanalysis.com statistics page) [Certain].

## 3. Valuation
The market prices OXY roughly in line with its own history, mixed versus peers (COP, DVN, EOG, CVX). P/E 17.7x is at the 64th percentile of its 5y range (4.6x-25.1x), +20% above the peer median (14.7x) [Certain]. EV/EBITDA is cheap both ways: 5.1x, 30th percentile of its own range, -27% below peers (7.0x) [Certain]. P/FCF (13.3x) exceeds its own 5y max (3.6x-10.6x) yet is -15% below peers, reflecting depressed FCF this year rather than a cheap multiple [Certain]. P/B (1.8x) is -22% below peers despite the 87th percentile of its own range [Certain]. FCF yield is 7.53% (source: stockanalysis.com statistics page). The fact sheet's own-history/EV-EBITDAX fair value (NAV unavailable) is $74.38 base (+23.7% vs the $60.11 close), $47.35 bear (-21.2%), $84.13 bull (+40.0%) [Likely]. The price assumes mid-cycle margins persist without crediting the deleveraging under way.

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) rises back above 2.0x, reversing the deleveraging trend (was 0.76x TTM, down from 2.16x FY21).
- FCF margin (cash-flow/income statement) falls below 15% for two consecutive fiscal years, signalling the cost base is structurally higher.
- ROIC (ratios page) falls back below 5% for two consecutive fiscal years, undoing the TTM recovery.
- Management abandons the stated flat 2027 capex/production plan in favour of higher spending without a corresponding ROIC improvement (source: fact sheet news item, 2026-08-06 Reuters headline).

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Management's stated 2027 priority is flat capital spending and flat output while continuing debt reduction (source: fact sheet news item, 2026-08-06 Reuters headline) [Likely]. Net debt/EBITDA has fallen from 2.16x (FY21) to 0.76x TTM, the clearest capital-discipline signal in the data [Certain]. FCF conversion (FCF/NI) has fallen sharply to 62.4% TTM from 164-326% in FY21-FY25, i.e. reported earnings are currently running ahead of cash generation, worth monitoring next quarter [Certain] (source: stockanalysis.com cash-flow/income statement). Shares outstanding rose 1.82% YoY, a mild dilution headwind rather than buyback-driven shrinkage [Certain] (source: stockanalysis.com statistics page).

## 6. Unusual items
Altman Z-score of 1.83 is in the distress/grey zone (<1.8 = distress), worth watching alongside the debt-reduction narrative even though net debt/EBITDA is now low [Likely]. Short interest is low and fell further (2.17% of float, -4.1% MoM), and institutional ownership is high (57.6%) — no crowding or squeeze risk [Certain] (source: stockanalysis.com statistics page). Goldman Sachs added OXY to its Conviction List (2026-10-01); this is a sell-side opinion, not used as an input to this thesis [Certain] (source: fact sheet news item).
