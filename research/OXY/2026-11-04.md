# Occidental Petroleum (OXY) — Core initiation — 2026-11-04
## 1. Business
Oil and gas exploration and production, led by the Permian Basin, plus midstream and marketing and low-carbon projects. Earnings follow crude prices: realized prices rose year on year in Q3 (source: https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/occidental-oil-prices-jump-supply-concerns-lift-crude-2026-10-13/) [Likely]. OxyChem was sold on 2026-01-02 and the proceeds repaid debt [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/797468/000162828026053388/oxy-20260630.htm) [Certain]. Sector: Energy (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/oxy/).

## 2. Quality
Returns are cyclical, not durable: ROIC was 26.1% in FY2022 and 4.3% in FY2025, with TTM at 8.6% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/oxy/financials/ratios/) [Certain]. Gross margin is rising, 73.3% TTM, and net debt/EBITDA is down to 0.76x TTM from 1.90x in FY2025, so deleveraging is real [Certain]. FCF margin is 18.9% TTM against 29.1% in FY2021 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/oxy/financials/cash-flow-statement/) [Certain]. As a price-taker with no durable moat, OXY earns only what crude gives it; the Permian position and lower debt make it a lower-risk price-taker, not a compounder [Likely]. Piotroski F is 7 and Altman Z is 1.83 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/oxy/statistics/) [Certain]. Beta is 0.24, which understates oil sensitivity [Likely].

## 3. Valuation
P/E is 8.9x, at 21% of its 5y range, and 47% below the peer median (XOM, CVX, COP, DVN) [Certain]. EV/EBITDA is 5.2x, at 31% of its range, 32% below peers [Certain]. P/FCF is 13.3x, above its 5y maximum, though 21% below peers; the FCF yield is 7.49% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/oxy/statistics/) [Certain]. The cheap earnings multiples rest on peak-like TTM earnings: net margin 30.3% includes the OxyChem gain, and the Q2 oil price was elevated by Iran-related risk premia [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/797468/000162828026053388/oxy-20260630.htm) [Likely]. So the price assumes crude stays near current levels; the FCF multiple, richer than history, says there is little cushion if crude falls [Likely]. ## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) rises above 1.9x, reversing the deleveraging.
- FCF margin (cash-flow statement and income statement pages) falls below 12% on a TTM basis.
- Gross margin (income statement page) falls below 65% on a TTM basis.
- Management reverses the flat 2027 spending and output plan or the debt-reduction priority in a filing (source: https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/occidental-sees-flat-spending-output-2027-keeps-focus-debt-reduction-2026-08-06/).

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Debt of roughly $8.6bn was repaid in H1 2026 from OxyChem proceeds and operating cash [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/797468/000162828026053388/oxy-20260630.htm) [Certain]. The quarterly dividend was raised 8% to $0.28 per share [company-reported] (same URL) and re-declared on 2026-10-26 (source: https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/10/26/3388440/0/en/occidental-announces-dividend.html) [Certain]. Shares outstanding rose 1.82% year on year, which is mild dilution [Certain]. TTM FCF conversion is 62.4%, down from 164.4% in FY2025, mainly because of the gain-inflated net income [Likely].
