# Central Japan Railway Company (TYO:9022) — Core initiation — 2026-11-09
## 1. Business
Operates the Tokaido Shinkansen (Tokyo–Nagoya–Osaka), Japan's main intercity rail corridor, plus conventional lines and related retail/real-estate. Revenue is fares, so earnings follow business and leisure travel volumes on one line. Home market is Japan; no foreign-revenue disclosure above ~10% is shown [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tyo/9022/).

## 2. Quality
- Margins are very high for a transport company: operating margin 40.4% TTM, gross margin 51.5% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tyo/9022/financials/income-statement/) [Certain]. The moat is structural: a single high-speed trunk line with no practical road, air or rail substitute at scale [Likely].
- Returns are only moderate because the asset base is huge: ROIC 6.0%, ROCE 8.1%, ROE 10.7% TTM (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tyo/9022/financials/ratios/) [Certain]. Returns recovered strongly from FY2022 (ROIC 0.0%) to 6.8% in FY2026 [Certain].
- Direction: ROIC and operating margin are flat over the last year (6.5% -> 6.0%; 40.8% -> 40.4%), and FCF margin fell from 14.9% to 10.1% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tyo/9022/financials/cash-flow-statement/?p=trailing). Capex is the likely driver (Chuo Shinkansen build) [Guessing]; recovery has plateaued [Likely].
- Balance sheet: net debt/EBITDA 4.25x TTM, Altman Z 1.58 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tyo/9022/statistics/) [Certain]. Leverage is the weak point.

## 3. Valuation
Price 4,172 JPY. P/E 7.7x (19th percentile of its 5y range of 6.1x–14.2x), EV/EBITDA 8.3x (3rd percentile), P/FCF 20.1x (18th percentile), P/B 0.8x (60th) (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tyo/9022/financials/ratios/) [Certain]. Against peers P/E is 43% below the median (13.5x) but EV/EBITDA is 41% above (5.9x); the peer set mixes railways with two airlines, so the comparison is loose [Likely]. FCF yield 4.98% [Certain]. The gap between a cheap P/E and a richer EV/EBITDA reflects heavy debt and depreciation [Likely].

Mechanical fair value base is 4,992 JPY (+19.7%), but the bear case is negative (-17,100) and the bull 16,471: the DCF is hypersensitive to capex and is unreliable as a point estimate [Likely]. The reverse DCF implies 7.5% year-1 revenue growth fading to terminal, demanding for a mature railway [Likely]. The share count is shrinking 2.52% YoY (buybacks) [Certain].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Operating margin (income statement, TTM) falls below 36%.
- ROIC (ratios page, TTM) falls below 5%.
- Net debt/EBITDA (fact sheet, TTM) rises above 5.5x.
- FCF margin (cash flow statement, TTM) falls below 5%.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
FCF conversion is weak at 37.9% of net income TTM (46.1% in FY2026), as capex absorbs earnings [Certain]. Management still reduces shares by 2.52% a year [Certain]. Priority is deleveraging from 18.14x in FY2022 to 3.80x in FY2026, which has stalled at 4.25x TTM [Certain]. Capex discipline on the megaproject is the key variable [Likely].
