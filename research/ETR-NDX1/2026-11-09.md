# Nordex SE (ETR:NDX1) — Core initiation — 2026-11-09
## 1. Business
Nordex designs, builds and services onshore wind turbines. Revenue comes from project sales (turbine supply and installation, recognised as projects execute) and from multi-year service contracts, which carry the better margin [Likely]. Recent order flow came from Turkey and a recovering US market [Likely] (source: https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/wind-turbine-maker-nordex-q3-orders-rise-turkish-demand-us-recovery-2026-10-12/). ## 2. Quality
Margins have recovered from losses to solid profits. Operating margin was -9.8% in FY2022 and is 10.1% TTM; gross margin is 27.1% TTM, from 8.1% in FY2022; ROCE is 27.2% TTM (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/etr/NDX1/financials/ratios/) [Certain]. The last-year direction is up on every tracked metric, with no contradiction of the five-year trend [Certain]. The balance sheet is net cash (net debt/EBITDA -2.41x) [Certain].

Durability is the open question. Wind turbines are competitive and cyclical, and the five-year history includes four years of losses or near-zero profit [Certain]. The moat is a growing service base and an installed fleet; I see no pricing power proven through a downturn [Guessing]. Management cites execution, permitting and policy uncertainty (source: Q3 2026 call, headline in fact sheet) [Likely]. Q3 results beat and 2026 margin guidance was raised [Likely] (source: https://www.wallstreet-online.de/nachricht/21532114-eqs-adhoc-nordex-se-nordex-group-reports-strong-preliminary-q3-2026-results-and-raises-ebitda-guidance-for-full-year-2026).

## 3. Valuation
At 42.98 EUR the stock trades on 8.4x EV/EBITDA, 30% up its own 5y range (8.0x–9.1x) and 54% below the peer median of 18.4x (VWS, ENR, GEV, n=3) [Certain]. P/FCF is 10.3x, FCF yield 9.71%, P/E 22.4x versus a 14% discount to peers [Certain]. The 5y P/E and EV/Sales history is distorted by loss years and is no guide. P/B of 6.4x is above its 5y max, reflecting a recapitalised, now profitable equity base [Likely].

The mechanical base fair value is 55.81 EUR (+29.8%); the bear case is negative, so the range is wide [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/etr/NDX1/statistics/). The reverse DCF implies -5.9% year-1 revenue growth, so the price already discounts a downturn, which suits a cyclical at peak-like margins [Likely]. The discount to peers partly reflects Nordex being a pure onshore OEM versus GEV/ENR's grid and gas exposure [Guessing].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Operating margin (income statement, TTM) falls below 6%
- Gross margin (income statement, TTM) falls below 20%
- FCF margin (cash flow statement, TTM) falls below 3% for two consecutive quarters
- Net debt / EBITDA (ratios page) rises above 1.0x

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
TTM FCF margin is 11.7% and FCF/net income 200.6%, well above earnings [Certain]. That likely reflects customer prepayments and working-capital inflow that reverse when order growth slows [Guessing]. Shares outstanding rose 12.14% year on year, so shareholders were diluted funding the recovery [Certain]. Net cash gives cover; no dividend or buyback evidence is in the fact sheet [data unavailable].
