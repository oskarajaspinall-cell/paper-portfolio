# Macro overlay: Vipshop Holdings (VIPS) — 2026-10-19
## Assessment
VIPS's thesis is dominated by China consumer/competitive dynamics (customer decline, REIT one-off gain, PDD/Taobao competition — see note), not US macro; beta is low at 0.54 [Certain] (fact sheet ST). One secondary channel: the house DCF uses risk-free rate + country risk premium as cost of equity, so US Treasury yields mechanically move VIPS's fair value. **Facts**: US 10y Treasury yield 5.10% on 2026-10-15, +53bp/3m, +10bp/1m; 10y real yield (TIPS) 2.78%, +43bp/3m (FRED DGS10, DFII10, https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=DGS10, 2026-10-15). 2y yield 4.77% (+61bp/3m) vs effective fed funds 3.88% (+25bp/3m) (FRED DGS2, DFF). CNY 6.692/USD, yuan +1.2%/3m (FRED DEXCHUS, 2026-10-09). US HY spread 3.23%, +52bp/3m, still near "calm" (FRED BAMLH0A0HYM2).

**Market pricing (proxy, inference)**: 2y yield ~89bp above fed funds suggests markets price further tightening or "higher for longer", not cuts [Guessing] — a trend continuing, not a fresh surprise.

**Chain**: US 10y yield → DCF cost of equity → VIPS fair value. The bear case ($13.06) sits only 1.5% above the last close ($12.86), so a further yield rise could push the mechanical bear fair value below price with no change in China fundamentals.

**Bull**: yields plateau/fall → cost of equity eases → base/bear fair value lifts a few points. **Bear**: 10y pushes toward/above 5.5% (firm core CPI, +1.13 index pts last month, FRED CPILFESL) → cost of equity rises further → bear-case fair value could dip below price. **Tilt**: small, toward bear — a real but secondary lever on a stock whose swings are overwhelmingly company/China-driven.
