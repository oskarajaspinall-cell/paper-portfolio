# Macro overlay: Align Technology, Inc. (ALGN) — 2026-10-09
## Assessment
Gate: secondary. ALGN's DCF base is $76 vs a $141 close (fact sheet); its CAPM discount rate uses the 10y Treasury plus a 1.67 beta (stockanalysis.com) — an unusually discount-rate-sensitive valuation range. Invisalign cases are often patient-financed, and the faster-growing EMEA/APAC segment is FX-translated. [Likely] **(inference)**

Chain: real yields -> cost of equity (CAPM) -> DCF fair value/multiple; real yields -> financing cost -> elective case starts; dollar -> FX translation -> international revenue.

What's priced — facts [Certain]: 10y real yield (DFII10) 2.92% on 2026-10-07, +0.61pp/3m, https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=DFII10 (2026-10-09). Nominal 10y (DGS10) 5.28%, +0.72pp/3m vs breakeven (T10YIE) 2.35%, +0.12pp/3m, https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=T10YIE (2026-10-09) — most of the 3-month yield rise is real, not an inflation-expectations move. Dollar index (DTWEXBGS) 121.385 on 2026-10-02, +0.70pp/3m (2026-10-09): a mild translation headwind. Proxy [Likely] **(inference)**: 2y yield 4.77% sits 0.89pp above fed funds (3.88%, DFF, 2026-10-07) — no near-term cuts priced.

Bull: real yields ease, dollar softens -> discount-rate relief narrows the DCF gap, multiple re-rates toward peers off below-5y-minimum levels. Bear: real yields and the dollar keep rising -> DCF value falls further below $76, financing costs weigh on case starts, FX drags EMEA/APAC growth -> reinforces the note's "value trap" read.

Tilt: toward bear, moderate — the move is real-rate- not inflation-driven, hitting a high-beta, DCF-sensitive name already flagged as possibly overvalued, plus FX headwind on its fastest-growing segment. **(inference)**
