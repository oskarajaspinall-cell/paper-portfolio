# Align Technology, Inc. (ALGN) — Core initiation — 2026-10-09

## 1. Business
Align sells the Invisalign clear-aligner system (TTM 82% of revenue; Clear Aligner +7.8% H1 2026) and iTero scanners/services ("Systems and Services", 18%; -5.3% H1) (source: SEC 10-Q https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1097149/000109714926000060/algn-20260630.htm). [Certain] International markets (EMEA/APAC) now outgrow the US, as wires/brackets remain more entrenched there (source: stockanalysis.com transcript https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/algn/transcripts/748329-2026-global-healthcare-conference/). [Likely]

## 2. Quality
Five-year returns have fallen steadily: ROIC 30.2%→17.2% TTM, ROE 22.5%→10.2%, gross margin 74.3%→70.7%, operating margin 24.7%→18.5% (fact sheet Quality table, source: ratios/income statement). [Certain] The moat is patent-based, not structural, and is actively contested: the 10-Q discloses a June 2026 China first-instance loss on two patents (under appeal) alongside the Angelalign win the headlines cite (source: SEC 10-Q; https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260811741339/en/Align-Technology-Prevails-in-China-Patent-Infringement-Action-Against-Angelalign/). [Certain] Systems and Services (iTero) revenue fell -10.8% YoY in Q2 2026, a second leg no longer supporting growth (source: SEC 10-Q). [Certain] The balance sheet is net-cash (net debt/EBITDA -1.06x), Altman Z 4.94, Piotroski F 7: no distress, but this reads as a maturing, increasingly competed category rather than a durable compounder. [Likely]

## 3. Valuation
All five multiples sit below their own 5-year minimum and 33-57% below the XRAY/HSIC/ISRG/IDXX peer median (P/E 24.5x vs 36.5x; EV/EBITDA 9.8x vs 18.8x; P/FCF 15.8x vs 24.3x) (fact sheet Valuation table). [Certain] On relative multiples the stock screens cheap. But the fact sheet's pure DCF (the industry's best method) values it at $76.08 base case, 47% below the $141.03 close, and the reverse DCF shows the price still embeds 20.9% year-1 revenue growth fading to terminal - well above the mid-single-digit case growth management guided to for H2 2026 (source: stockanalysis.com transcript, Global Healthcare Conference). [Likely] The blended $138.14 base fair value (near-flat to the close) is pulled up mainly by the P/E method ($262, peer multiples); stripped of that, the DCF alone implies a materially overvalued stock relative to decelerating fundamentals - a possible value trap rather than a genuine discount. [Likely]

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Systems and Services revenue (income statement page) returns to growth for two consecutive quarters, reversing the -5.3% H1 2026 decline.
- Gross margin (income statement page) stabilizes at or above 70% rather than continuing its 5-year decline (74.3%→69.9% FY2021-FY2025).
- ROIC (ratios page) resumes an upward trend rather than falling further below the TTM 17.2%.
- Align wins or settles the pending China/Angelalign patent appeals without a durable loss of IP protection in a core market.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
FCF conversion is strong and rising (FCF/NI 153.2% TTM, up from 99.9% FY2021) despite margin compression, and FCF margin held at 15.3% TTM (fact sheet Quality table, source: cash flow/income statement). [Certain] The balance sheet carries net cash (net debt/EBITDA -1.06x) (source: ratios/balance sheet). [Certain] Shares outstanding fell -2.75% YoY, consistent with buybacks funded by that cash generation rather than new debt (source: statistics page). [Certain] Capital discipline is a genuine positive, but it returns cash from a business whose returns on capital are still declining, not reinvesting into renewed growth. [Likely]

## 6. Unusual items
Activist investor Elliott Investment Management engaged with the board; Align added independent directors (including Quentin Blackford, announced 2026-08-31) and launched an operations/strategy review (announced 2026-07-29) (source: stockanalysis.com news headlines, https://www.reuters.com/technology/align-technology-overhaul-board-following-engagement-with-elliott-2026-07-29/ and https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260831593240/en/Quentin-Blackford-to-Join-Align-Technology-Board-of-Directors/). [Certain] Short interest is moderate (5.84% of float, +1.2% vs prior month) and not a standalone flag (source: statistics page). [Certain] The activist involvement is a governance catalyst that could unlock value, or could reflect the board's own concern about the trends in Section 2; outcome is unclear pending the review. [Guessing]
