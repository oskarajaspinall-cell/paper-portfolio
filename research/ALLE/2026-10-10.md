# Allegion plc (ALLE) — Core initiation — 2026-10-10

## 1. Business
Allegion manufactures security-around-the-door hardware — mechanical locks, electronic access control and software — under brands including Schlage, Von Duprin, LCN and CISA, sold via a specification-driven channel to architects/contractors (commercial) and retail/e-commerce (residential) [Certain] (source: https://www.allegion.com).

## 2. Quality
A good, not great, business. Returns remain high but are eroding: ROIC fell from 26.5% (FY21) to 18.8% TTM and ROE from 60.6% to 33.7% (source: research/ALLE/factsheet-2026-10-10.md, RA) [Certain]. Operating and gross margin are still expanding (+2.6pp, +3.2pp over 5y) and FCF conversion is strong (101.9% TTM), so the margin story is intact even as capital efficiency normalizes [Certain]. The moat rests on specification selling (architects design Allegion hardware into building plans, creating switching friction), century-old brands (Von Duprin 1908, Schlage 1920, LCN/CISA 1926) and a broad channel network across 37 production facilities [Likely] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1579241/000157924126000007/alle-20251231.htm). The 10-K flags risks to it: private-label retail competition, e-commerce disintermediation, and AI/non-traditional entrants [Likely] (same source). Net debt/EBITDA of 1.81x is conservative; Piotroski F-score 6 and Altman Z 4.63 show no distress [Certain] (source: factsheet, RA/ST).

## 3. Valuation
The market is not paying up for this business: P/E of 19.7x sits at the 13th percentile of its 5-year range (19.0x–24.6x) and 45% below the peer median 35.7x (MAS, FBIN, CARR, JCI) [Certain] (source: factsheet, RA). EV/EBITDA (14.0x) and P/FCF (19.1x) are near their 5-year lows. EV/Sales (3.4x, +45% vs peers) and P/B (6.0x, +73% vs peers) run high, reflecting the asset-light, high-ROE model rather than overvaluation on cash metrics [Likely]. Base-case fair value (DCF-weighted) is $142.10, only 5.6% below the $150.48 close — the price already assumes most of the base case, with bull ($168.36, +12%) needing sustained electronics growth and bear ($105.17, -30%) a real margin/multiple reset [Certain] (source: factsheet). Reverse DCF implies ~8.5% year-1 revenue growth fading to terminal, in line with the double-digit segment growth reported for Q2 2026, so the embedded growth looks achievable, not aggressive [Likely] (source: factsheet news, https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/alle/transcripts/656029-q2-2026/).

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROIC (ratios page) falls below 15% for two consecutive fiscal years, signalling the specification/brand moat is being competed away rather than just normalizing off a high base.
- Gross margin (income statement) reverses below 42% (FY2022 level), indicating private-label/e-commerce price competition is biting as the 10-K warns.
- FCF conversion (cash flow vs income statement) drops below 85% for a full fiscal year, indicating cash quality deterioration.
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) rises above 3.0x without a matching step-up in ROIC, indicating M&A is destroying rather than adding value.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
FCF margin is rising (15.5%→16.9% FY21–FY25, 15.6% TTM) with FCF conversion above 100% TTM, so earnings are high quality and cash-backed [Certain] (source: factsheet, CF/IS). Shares outstanding fell 0.57% YoY, consistent with net buybacks, and the board just declared a $0.55 quarterly dividend [Certain] (source: factsheet news, https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260902022636/en/Allegion-Declares-Quarterly-Dividend/). The company continues bolt-on M&A (Overly Door Company, Oct 2026) funded from this cash generation rather than stretching the balance sheet, consistent with net debt/EBITDA holding flat at ~1.8x [Likely] (source: factsheet news; https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20261004342891/en/Allegion-Acquires-Overly-Door-Company/). The 10-K does not disclose a specific capital-allocation framework (buyback authorization size, dividend policy target) [Guessing] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1579241/000157924126000007/alle-20251231.htm).

## 6. Unusual items
Institutional ownership is shown at 103.4% of float (likely a data/short-interest double-count artifact common to heavily-lent large caps, not a governance flag) [Guessing] (source: factsheet, ST). Short interest is modest at 3.55% of float and falling (-23.1% vs prior month), no distress signal [Certain] (source: factsheet, ST). Leadership change: CEO of International, Tim Eckersley, is retiring, replaced by Serge Zappone — a succession event to monitor but not thesis-changing on its own [Likely] (source: https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260908884356/en/Allegion-Announces-Tim-Eckersley%E2%80%99s-Retirement-Names-Serge-Zappone-Leader-of-Allegion-International/). Q3 2026 results due Oct 22, 2026, shortly after this initiation [Certain] (source: factsheet, ST/OV).
