# Delivery Hero SE (ETR:DHER) — Tactical initiation — 2026-10-11

## 1. Catalyst and why now
The weekly screen flagged a pullback-in-uptrend (price near its 50-day average after a strong 12-month run). But the real driver of that run is a pending acquisition, not organic momentum: Delivery Hero agreed to support Uber's takeover offer, with CEO Niklas Östberg staying on through the deal (source: https://www.wallstreet-online.de/nachricht/21425676-eqs-adhoc-delivery-hero-se-niklas-oestberg-to-continue-as-chairman-of-the-management-board-ceo-of-delivery-hero-se-beyond-31-march-2027, company ad-hoc disclosure, 2026-09-24) [Certain]. South Korea's FTC opened a merger review of the deal on 2026-09-22 (source: https://www.ibtimes.com.au/south-korea-uber-delivery-hero-baemin-merger-review-1875743) [Certain]. The "pullback" is therefore a deal-arbitrage trading range, not a dip to buy into a continuing trend.

## 2. What the price already assumes
EV/EBITDA (47.4x) sits near the peer median (48.8x, n=3), but EV/Sales (0.9x) is 53% below peers (2.0x, n=4) and P/FCF (153.8x) is 965% above peers (14.4x, n=3) [Certain]. The fair-value model's DCF leg is unusable (FCF negative/too short a history); the base case (41.89 EUR, +14.5%) rests solely on EV/Revenue, a low-confidence single method here [Certain]. None of this prices a takeover: with a live, regulator-reviewed deal, the quote is better read as a probability-weighted blend of deal consideration and standalone value, discounted for break/delay risk [Likely].

## 4. Proposed exit plan
Recommendation: AVOID — do not enter. For reference, the screen's mechanical levels are stop 35.93 EUR (last close − 2.5×ATR(14) = 36.60 − 0.67) and target 37.93 EUR (2× that 0.67 EUR stop distance above last close), time limit the next earnings date or 3 months, whichever is sooner [Certain].
