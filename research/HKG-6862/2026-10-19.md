# Haidilao International Holding Ltd. (HKG:6862) — Core initiation — 2026-10-19

## 1. Business
Haidilao operates branded hot pot restaurants across mainland China, Hong Kong, Macau and Taiwan, earning revenue mainly from dine-in table turns, with growing delivery sales and a multi-brand incubator ("Pomegranate Plan") [Certain]. Nearly all restaurants are company-operated (no franchising); a scaled, company-run procurement and supply chain supports consistent food quality and service, the brand's main differentiator [Certain].

## 2. Quality
Returns have recovered sharply post-pandemic: ROIC rose from 3.5% (FY2021) to 48.8% TTM, ROCE to 44.6%, ROE to 41.9%, with gross, operating and net margins all expanding over the period (operating margin 1.6%→12.3% TTM) [Certain]. FCF conversion is strong (88.6% of net income TTM) and the balance sheet carries net cash (net debt/EBITDA -0.27x) [Certain]. The moat looks softer than the returns suggest: hot pot has low technological differentiation and Haidilao competes against many national and regional chains (peers Jiumaojiu, Xiabuxiabu) on price and service; its edge is execution — scaled central-kitchen procurement, a staff-incentive culture and brand recognition — rather than a structural barrier competitors cannot replicate [Likely]. Management's H1 2026 call referenced continued delivery and new-brand growth alongside FX losses and regionally uneven same-store trends; the specific figures quoted are inconsistent with the company's reported revenue scale, so they are not used as data (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/6862/transcripts/613065-h1-2026/) [Guessing].

## 3. Valuation
At 9.37 HKD, the stock trades at 10.8x TTM P/E, below its own 5-year range (16.4x–80.4x) and 32% below the peer median (16.0x, n=2) [Certain]. EV/EBITDA (5.8x), EV/Sales (1.0x), P/FCF (12.6x) and P/B (4.6x) are each below the stock's own 5-year minimum, yet far above the peer medians (+98%, +142%, +591%, +891%) — a thin, two-to-three-name peer set (YUMC, HKG:9922, HKG:0520) trading at depressed multiples, which limits how much weight that comparison deserves [Likely]. The blended base fair value is 11.49 HKD (+22.6% vs last close), bear 4.48 HKD (-52.1%), bull 32.56 HKD (+247.7%) [Certain]. The reverse DCF implies the market is pricing in only ~4% revenue growth in year one fading to terminal growth — a low bar given current double-digit ROIC/ROCE, consistent with a price set for continued same-store softness rather than for the business's post-recovery returns [Likely].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROIC (ratios page) falls below 25%, roughly half its TTM level, signalling the post-pandemic return recovery is reversing.
- Net margin (income statement) turns negative again, as in FY2021, showing unit economics deteriorating beyond a cyclical dip.
- FCF margin (cash flow/income statement) falls below 5%, versus 8.0% TTM, showing cash generation weakening materially.
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) turns net-debt positive and exceeds 1.0x, showing expansion being funded with leverage rather than net cash.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Haidilao converts earnings into cash well: FCF margin 8.0% TTM and FCF/NI 88.6%, down from FY2022's unusually high 387.6% as the restaurant base normalised post-COVID [Certain]. The balance sheet holds net cash (net debt/EBITDA -0.27x TTM), funding continued store openings without new debt [Certain]. Shares outstanding are flat (+0.01% YoY), so growth is not being funded by dilution [Certain]. A declared dividend policy is [data unavailable] on the fact sheet; capital appears to be redeployed into the restaurant network and newer brands rather than returned [Likely].
