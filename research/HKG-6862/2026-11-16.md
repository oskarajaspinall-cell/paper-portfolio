# Haidilao International (HKG:6862) — Core initiation — 2026-11-16
## 1. Business
Operator of Haidilao hotpot restaurants, plus delivery and newer restaurant brands; revenue comes from table turns, delivery and other restaurant operations (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/6862/) [Likely]. It reports in CNY and is overwhelmingly a China-mainland business [Likely]. The mainland vs overseas split is [data unavailable]: the company site was not on the allowlist. China exposure is therefore the macro overlay's main check: CNY reporter, HKD-listed.

## 2. Quality
Returns are exceptional for a restaurant chain: TTM ROIC 48.8%, ROCE 44.6%, ROE 41.9% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/6862/financials/ratios/) [Certain]. Net cash (net debt/EBITDA -0.27x), Altman Z 7.50, Piotroski F 5, flat share count (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/6862/statistics/) [Certain].

Direction is the issue. Over 12 months ROIC fell 61.3% -> 48.8%, operating margin 13.4% -> 12.3%, FCF margin 11.9% -> 8.0% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/6862/financials/ratios/?p=trailing) [Certain]. ROCE is flat. Five-year trends are rising, but off a COVID trough, so they overstate durability [Likely].

Moat: brand and service-led traffic, with scale in sourcing [Likely]. Restaurants are easy to copy and consumer-sensitive, so returns this high probably mean-revert somewhat [Guessing]. Results headline (H1 2026): traffic and expense ratios improved, but FX losses produced a net loss (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/6862/transcripts/613065-h1-2026/) [Likely].

## 3. Valuation
P/E 12.1x, EV/EBITDA 6.5x and EV/Sales 1.1x all sit below their 5y minimums (16.4x, 9.3x, 1.6x) and 29-35% below the peer median (YUMC, HKG:9922, MCD). P/FCF 14.0x is mid-range (45% of its 5y range). FCF yield is 7.13% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/6862/statistics/) [Certain].

Mechanical fair value (HKD): bear 4.49, base 11.52, bull 32.67 vs close 9.92; base is +16.2% (source: fact sheet, https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/6862/financials/ratios/) [Certain]. The 3.5% China risk premium is an owner assumption. The reverse DCF implies 5.5% year-1 revenue growth, fading [Certain]. The price therefore assumes margins keep slipping, not growth [Likely]. The bear case at 4.49 shows how wide the range is [Certain].

Trend: 22.1% below the 200-day average, 12-month change -25.3%, and 11.4pp, 32.0pp and 35.7pp behind SPY over 3m, 6m and 12m [Certain]. This is cheap but not "working".

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
FCF conversion has slid from 190.0% (FY2023) to 98.6% (FY2025) and 88.6% TTM, so cash is converging on earnings as capex for openings rises [Likely]. Net cash balance sheet, shares flat (0.01%), no dilution [Certain]. Double-digit second-half openings are planned (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/6862/transcripts/613065-h1-2026/) [Likely]; new-store returns decide whether ROIC holds [Guessing].
