# Medpace Holdings (MEDP) — Core initiation — 2026-11-16
## 1. Business
Medpace is a full-service clinical research organisation (CRO). It runs Phase I–IV trials for small and mid-size biotech and pharma sponsors, billing per contract as work is performed (cost-to-cost) with advance billings funding working capital. Revenue is recognised from a backlog of awarded studies [Certain] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1668397/000166839726000036/medp-20260930.htm). The filing gives revenue by therapeutic area, not by country, so no single foreign market is confirmed at ~10%+ [Likely].

## 2. Quality
Strong. Gross margin is 72.2% TTM, up from 61.4% in FY2021, and operating margin is 21.4% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/medp/financials/income-statement/) [Certain]. ROIC is 793.6% TTM and ROCE 75.3% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/medp/financials/ratios/); ROIC is inflated by buybacks shrinking invested capital, so ROCE is the cleaner read [Likely]. Net cash (net debt/EBITDA -0.84x), Piotroski 7, Altman Z 7.00 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/medp/statistics/) [Certain].

Direction change, addressed: ROCE fell from 108.1% to 75.3% over 12 months and FCF margin from 28.7% to 23.5%, while operating margin is flat at 21.4%. The profit engine is intact; the FCF drop is cash timing (the 10-Q says receivable and advance-billing timing "can vary significantly") (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1668397/000166839726000036/medp-20260930.htm) [Likely]. FCF conversion is still 128.9% of net income [Certain].

Moat: sponsor switching costs mid-trial and a biotech-focused niche; but biotech funding cycles make awards cyclical [Likely]. Backlog is $3,167.2 million, +5.6% YoY, and Q3 net awards were $873.4 million [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1668397/000166839726000036/medp-20260930.htm).

## 3. Valuation
The price is not cheap against fundamentals. P/E 36.3x is at 61% of its 5y range (25.5x–43.1x) and +4% vs peers; EV/EBITDA 28.0x is +35% and EV/Sales 6.3x is +88% vs peers (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/medp/financials/ratios/) [Certain]. FCF yield is 3.65% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/medp/statistics/) [Certain]. The mechanical fair value is base 458.31 USD (bear 380.35, bull 684.07) against a 653.94 close, so the price sits near the bull case [Certain]. Reverse DCF implies 23.1% year-1 revenue growth fading to terminal; that is well above the recent pace of backlog growth in section 2 [Likely]. Support is trend, not discount: 22.9% above the 200-day, +54.3% over 6 months, +15.4pp vs SPY over 3 months (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/medp/history/) [Certain]. A premium multiple needs awards to keep accelerating [Likely].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Operating margin (income statement, TTM) falls below 19%.
- FCF margin (cash-flow statement, TTM) falls below 18%, i.e. not just timing.
- Backlog in the 10-Q falls below $2,900 million, or net awards in a quarter fall below $700 million, for two quarters running.
- ROCE (ratios page, TTM) falls below 50%.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Shares are down 5.07% YoY (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/medp/statistics/) [Certain]. The company bought back $294.7 million in the nine months to September 2026 but none in Q3, and on October 20, 2026 the Board approved a new $1.0 billion authorisation [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1668397/000166839726000036/medp-20260930.htm). Buybacks at 36x earnings earn modest returns [Likely]. The company is net cash with no debt burden and FCF/NI of 128.9% [Certain].

## 6. Unusual items
Short interest is 6.09% of float, down 14.1% on the month; institutions own 87.3% and insiders 18.66% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/medp/statistics/) [Certain]. The Q3 call headline reports a raised FY2026 guide and record awards, but 2027 is sensitive to Q4 awards and cancellations (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/medp/transcripts/714073-q3-2026/) [Likely]. The 10-Q delta shows no substantive Risk Factor change [Certain]. Customer concentration is not disclosed in the text read, so biotech sponsor funding risk remains [Guessing].
