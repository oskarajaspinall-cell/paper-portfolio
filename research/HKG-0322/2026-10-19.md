# Tingyi (Cayman Islands) Holding Corp. (HKG:0322) — Core initiation — 2026-10-19

## 1. Business
Tingyi makes and distributes packaged food in China under the Master Kong brand: instant noodles and ready-to-drink beverages (tea, bottled water, juice), the latter including a PepsiCo bottling relationship [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/0322/, sector/industry tag). Sold through an extensive mainland China distribution network; results report in CNY. [Likely]

## 2. Quality
Returns are high and have been rising for five years: ROIC 16.7%→22.3% FY21-FY25 (35.6% TTM), ROE 18.6%→28.7% (33.3% TTM), ROCE 13.5%→24.3% (28.8% TTM) (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/0322/financials/ratios/). [Certain] Gross margin rose 30.4%→34.7% FY21-FY25 (35.4% TTM) and operating margin 5.5%→7.8% (8.4% TTM), while net margin is flatter (+0.6pp over 5y to 5.9% TTM), showing most of the gain still sits above the operating line (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/0322/financials/income-statement/). [Certain] Scale, brand leadership in Chinese instant noodles/RTD tea and a nationwide distribution network are the likely moat, but the category faces real competitive pressure — Nongfu Spring and others have disrupted bottled water/tea pricing — so the margin trend, not the brand alone, is the evidence to track. [Likely] Net debt/EBITDA is negative (net cash, -0.23x TTM), giving balance-sheet flexibility peers with more leverage lack (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/0322/financials/ratios/). [Certain]

## 3. Valuation
At 13.0x TTM P/E and 5.9x EV/EBITDA, the stock trades below its own 5-year range (5y min 13.3x P/E, 6.8x EV/EBITDA) and at discounts of 29% and 26% to the peer median (Uni-President China, Want Want China, Mengniu, Nongfu Spring) (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/0322/financials/ratios/). [Certain] P/FCF of 10.4x is also below the 5y minimum (11.3x) despite FCF conversion of 124% of net income TTM. [Certain] The fact sheet's reverse DCF implies the price assumes revenue shrinks ~3.7% in year 1 before fading to terminal growth — a materially more pessimistic growth path than the margin trend above supports, which is the gap this position is pricing. [Likely] P/B (3.7x) screens expensive vs peers (+101%) but is consistent with Tingyi's much higher ROE, so is not read as a standalone warning. Base fair value HKD15.23 vs the HKD12.37 close is +23.1% upside; bear/bull are -48.2%/+32.8% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/0322/, DCF+P/E blend). [Guessing]

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Gross margin (income statement page) falls back below 30% (below the FY2022 trough of 28.6%), signalling the input-cost/pricing gains have reversed.
- ROIC (ratios page) drops below 15% for a full fiscal year, giving up most of the five-year improvement.
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) rises above 1.0x, ending the net-cash position and signalling weaker capital discipline.
- Revenue (income statement page) declines in two consecutive fiscal years, confirming the market's reverse-DCF shrinkage case rather than a one-off soft year.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
FCF conversion is strong and improving (51-65% FY21-23, 114-127% FY24-TTM of net income), and the balance sheet carries net cash rather than net debt (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/0322/financials/cash-flow-statement/, https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/0322/financials/ratios/). [Certain] Shares outstanding are essentially flat (+0.02% YoY), so there is no dilution drag, but also no buyback support to the multiple from the fact sheet's data (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/0322/statistics/). [Certain] Dividend policy is not shown on the fact sheet pages used; [data unavailable].

## 6. Unusual items
Beta (5y) is unusually low at 0.13 and institutional ownership is modest at 13.4%, consistent with a stock less correlated to global markets and with lower foreign institutional attention — a liquidity/ownership characteristic, not a fundamental flag (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/0322/statistics/). [Likely] Recent news items on the fact sheet are routine results/filing announcements (H1 2026, H2 2025) with no new disclosed items to act on (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/0322/filings/3522929/).
