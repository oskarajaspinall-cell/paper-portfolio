# Tingyi (Cayman Islands) Holding Corp. (HKG:0322) — Core initiation — 2026-11-16
## 1. Business
Shanghai-based maker of instant noodles (Master Kong) and beverages (ready-to-drink tea, juice, water, soft drinks), plus a small property/consulting "Others" segment. Operations are mainly in mainland China, so effectively all revenue is China (CNY reporter; China-risk premium applies) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/0322/company/). Sold through distributors to consumers; profit driven by input costs (palm oil, wheat, PET) and beverage mix.

## 2. Quality
Returns are high and improving: ROIC 35.6% TTM vs 22.3% FY2025 and 16.7% FY2021; gross margin 35.4% vs 30.4% FY2021; operating margin 8.4% vs 5.5% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/0322/financials/ratios/) [Certain]. FCF conversion 124.2% TTM, net cash (net debt/EBITDA -0.23x), no dilution (shares +0.02% YoY) (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/0322/statistics/) [Certain]. Moat is scale distribution and brand in a staple category: durable but narrow-margin [Likely]. Direction flag: ROCE is rising over five fiscal years but fell from 31.0% to 28.8% over the last 12 months; ROIC, operating and FCF margins all rose over the same period, so the ROCE dip looks like a larger capital base, not a margin problem [Likely]. Margin gains partly reflect benign input costs, which may reverse [Guessing]. Piotroski F 6.

## 3. Valuation
Price 13.63 HKD. P/E 14.4x sits at the 9th percentile of its 5y range (13.3x-26.1x, median 15.6x) and 21% below peers (18.2x). EV/EBITDA 6.6x is below its 5y minimum and in line with peers. FCF yield 8.61%; P/FCF 11.6x vs 25.2x median. P/B 5.2x is above its 5y max, reflecting the ROE jump to 33.3%. Mechanical base fair value 15.08 HKD (+10.6%), bear 6.39, bull 16.28; DCF and P/E agree near 15 (source: fact sheet). The reverse DCF says the price implies -0.8% year-one revenue growth, so a flat top line is priced in [Likely]. Margins are near peak, so earnings-based multiples flatter [Likely]. The stock is 11.0% above its 50-day average with RSI 76.3 (overbought), 12-month change +14.8% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/0322/history/) [Certain]. Upside is modest relative to the bear case.
