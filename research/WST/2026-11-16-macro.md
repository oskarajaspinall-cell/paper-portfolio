# Macro overlay: West Pharmaceutical Services (WST) — 2026-11-16
## Assessment
**Chain:** real yields -> discount rate -> multiple on a long-duration compounder. WST trades at 46.8x P/E and a 1.56% FCF yield (fact sheet, stockanalysis.com), so most of its value sits in distant cash flows. Earnings are not macro-sensitive: demand comes from injectable-drug volumes under regulatory-locked specifications, and no single foreign country is disclosed at 10%+ (note section 1). [Likely]

**Facts (FRED):** the 10y TIPS yield was 2.86% on 2026-11-12, up 0.47pp in 3 months and flat over 1 month (https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=DFII10). The 2y yield was 4.86%, up 0.71pp in 3 months, against fed funds at 3.88% (DGS2, DFF, same dates). The high-yield spread was 3.2%, up 0.49pp in 3 months (BAMLH0A0HYM2).

**Pricing (proxy):** a 2y yield about 1pp above fed funds implies the market prices no cuts or hikes. **(inference)** The stock is up 52% in 12 months despite rising real yields, so the multiple has not de-rated. Higher real yields are already absorbed; further upward surprises are the risk. [Likely]

**Bull:** real yields fall back below ~2.4% and spreads stay tight. The multiple holds.
**Bear:** real yields above ~3.25% or spreads widening. A 5-10% multiple compression with no change in earnings. A weaker euro (EMEA 45% of sales) is a small translation drag.

**Tilt:** toward bear, small. Macro adds multiple risk to a fully priced stock but leaves the earnings thesis unchanged.
