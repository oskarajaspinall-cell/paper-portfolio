# West Pharmaceutical Services (WST) — Core initiation — 2026-11-16
## 1. Business
West makes elastomer stoppers, seals and plungers (high-value-product, "HVP", components) plus injection devices for injectable drugs, and sells them to drug makers. The drug-maker's regulatory filing specifies West's component, so switching is slow. A contract-manufacturing arm, West Vantage, was 17% of nine-month sales [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/105770/000010577026000121/wst-20260930.htm). Americas 45%, EMEA 45%, Asia Pacific 10% [company-reported] (same URL). No single foreign country is disclosed at 10%+ [Likely].

## 2. Quality
Strong but below its peak. ROIC 21.6% TTM against 38.1% in FY2021; operating margin 22.9% against 27.1% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/wst/financials/ratios/). [Certain]
The 5-year slide is real, but the last 12 months turned: ROIC rose from 18.6% to 21.6% and operating margin from 21.1% to 22.9% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/wst/financials/ratios/?p=trailing). [Certain] The decline looks like post-COVID destocking and mix, now reversing. [Likely]
Moat: regulatory qualification of containment components, plus a cost of failure far above the part's price. [Likely]
Q3 organic growth was driven by HVP components, and FY guidance was raised (headline: https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/west-reports-third-quarter-2026-results-302922802.html). [Likely] West Vantage is shrinking from a customer contract expiry (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/105770/000010577026000121/wst-20260930.htm). [Likely]
Balance sheet is net cash (net debt/EBITDA -0.16x). [Certain]

## 3. Valuation
The price is demanding. P/E 46.8x sits at 75% of its 5-year range (median 43.9x), a +11% premium to peers (42.2x). EV/EBITDA 30.0x is near its own median (30.2x) but +109% over peers. FCF yield is 1.56% and P/FCF 64.0x (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/wst/statistics/). [Certain]
The mechanical fair value is flagged unreliable: DCF 75.91 versus P/E 390.89, against a 416.42 close. The reverse DCF needs 48.5% year-1 growth, which is not credible, so the DCF is probably too harsh on a compounder. [Likely] Even the P/E method puts the base below the price. [Certain]
The price assumes margins and returns recover toward past levels with HVP growth sustained. The stock is 29.6% above its 200-day average with 12-month gain of 52.1%, so the trend is strong but extended. [Certain] Expect a good business at a full price, with little margin of safety. [Likely]

## 4. What would prove this wrong

- Gross margin TTM (statistics page) falls below 34%.
