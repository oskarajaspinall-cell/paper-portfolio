# Federal Realty Investment Trust (FRT) — Core initiation — 2026-10-12

## 1. Business
Federal Realty owns and operates 103 grocery-anchored shopping centers and mixed-use retail/residential assets (28.8m sq ft, ~3,700 tenants, ~2,500 residential units) concentrated in nine high-income, supply-constrained metros (Boston, NY, DC, Bay Area, SoCal, Miami, Phoenix) [Certain] (source: https://www.federalrealty.com/about). Revenue is rent from retail/mixed-use leases; growth comes from rent escalators, releasing spreads, redevelopment and selective acquisitions, e.g. The Summit, Birmingham AL, Oct-2026 [Certain] (source: https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/federal-realty-acquires-the-summit-alabamas-premier-retail-destination-302896582.html).

## 2. Quality
The moat is locational: affluent, infill, high-barrier-to-entry sites that are hard to replicate given zoning and land scarcity [Likely] (source: https://www.federalrealty.com/about). Federal Realty has raised its dividend for 59 consecutive years, the longest streak among US REITs [Certain] (source: https://www.federalrealty.com/investors), evidence of durable cash generation through cycles. ROE rose from 9.7% (FY21) to 12.6% TTM and net debt/EBITDA fell from 7.26x to 5.79x TTM — a five-year deleveraging trend [Certain] (source: fact sheet RA). Q2 2026 results showed FFO/share +7% YoY to $1.88, record leasing and 96% occupancy, with 2026 guidance raised [Likely] (source: PRNewswire release cited in fact sheet News & sentiment, https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/federal-realty-investment-trust-reports-second-quarter-2026-results-and-guidance-raise-302839726.html). ROIC, ROCE and margin metrics are [data unavailable] on stockanalysis.com for this REIT structure. Absolute leverage (~5.8x net debt/EBITDA) remains a risk if rates stay elevated for longer.

## 3. Valuation
P/E 21.4x sits at the low end of its own 5-year range (5y min 21.6x) and 15% below the peer median of 25.1x; EV/EBITDA 16.7x and EV/Sales 10.5x are also below their 5y minimums and roughly in line with peers (+3% / -2%) [Certain] (source: fact sheet). P/B 2.9x looks rich vs peers (+68% vs 1.7x median) but is a weak proxy here since REIT property is carried at historical cost, not marked to market [Likely]. The mechanical base fair value (P/FFO proxy, the best available method; NAV unavailable on stockanalysis.com) is $147.71, +39.1% above the last close; even the bear case ($109.35) implies a slight +3.0%, a shallow downside case [Likely] (model-derived from fact sheet). That spread, alongside a 15.6pp underperformance vs SPY over 3 months despite raised FFO guidance, suggests the market is pricing sector/rate pressure rather than a company-specific problem.

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Net debt/EBITDA (financials/ratios page) rises back above 7.0x (the FY21 level), reversing the five-year deleveraging trend
- Occupancy (quarterly earnings release/transcript) falls below ~93%, ending the current 96% record-leasing trend
- The dividend-increase streak breaks — no increase declared in a fiscal year, ending the 59-year record (federalrealty.com investor page)
- ROE (ratios page) falls back below 9% TTM for two consecutive quarters, undoing the FY21→TTM improvement

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Growth is funded by a mix of debt, equity and asset recycling: a $400m exchangeable senior notes offering priced August 2026, and the ~$508m Summit acquisition funded partly via "non-dilutive asset sales" per management [Likely] (source: https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/federal-realty-announces-pricing-of-400-million-of-exchangeable-senior-notes-302845798.html). Net debt/EBITDA has fallen every year for five years (7.26x → 5.79x TTM) while still acquiring [Certain] (source: fact sheet). FCF margin/conversion are [data unavailable] on stockanalysis.com for this REIT; TTM FCF yield is -4.00% [Certain] (source: fact sheet ST), consistent with heavy reinvestment rather than distress.

## 6. Unusual items
The $400m exchangeable senior notes (Aug 2026) create potential future share dilution if conversion triggers are met [Likely] (source: PRNewswire release above). Short interest is 5.87% of float, up 25.9% month-over-month [Certain] (source: fact sheet) — worth monitoring, not large. No governance red flags found.
