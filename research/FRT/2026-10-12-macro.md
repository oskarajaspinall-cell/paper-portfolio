# Macro overlay: Federal Realty Investment Trust (FRT) — 2026-10-12
## Assessment
Chain: real yields → REIT discount rate/cap rates & cost of capital → FFO multiple and fair value **(inference)**; real yields → refinancing cost on debt-funded growth (net debt/EBITDA 5.79x TTM, $400m exchangeable notes, Aug-2026) [Certain, fact sheet].

Facts (FRED, 2026-10-08/09): 10y real yield (DFII10) 2.87%, +0.56pp/3m; 10y Treasury (DGS10) 5.22%, +0.68pp/3m; effective fed funds (DFF) 3.88%, +0.26pp/3m (rising, not falling); 10y breakeven (T10YIE) 2.33%, roughly flat (+0.09pp/3m) (fred.stlouisfed.org, DFII10/DGS10/DFF/T10YIE). Market pricing **(inference, proxy)**: 2y yield (4.75%) sits ~0.9pp above effective fed funds, implying the market no longer prices the cuts assumed earlier this year — a "higher-for-longer" real-rate path, driven by growth/term premium rather than inflation (breakevens flat).

Largely already priced: FRT's P/E (21.4x) sits at the bottom of its 5y range and it has underperformed SPY by 15.6pp over 3 months despite FFO +7% YoY and raised guidance [Certain] — the market compressed the multiple as yields rose. The base fair value ($147.71) already uses the current 5.22% risk-free rate, so further real-yield rises would cut that estimate further **(inference)**.

**Bull**: real yields ease (DFII10 toward ~2.5%) → cap rates ease, multiple re-rates toward peer median. **Bear**: real yields keep rising (DFII10 >3.25%, spreads widen) → further multiple compression and costlier refinancing, pressuring fair value despite solid operations. **Tilt**: moderate toward bear — the 3-month real-yield uptrend is intact and the fed-funds/2y spread shows the market has stopped pricing cuts, only partly reflected in the stock's underperformance.
