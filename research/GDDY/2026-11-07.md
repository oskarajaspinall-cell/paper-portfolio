# GoDaddy (GDDY) — Core initiation — 2026-11-07
## 1. Business
GoDaddy sells domains, hosting, websites, email/productivity and commerce tools to small businesses and entrepreneurs. Revenue is largely recurring subscriptions, plus domain aftermarket sales. Two segments: Applications & Commerce (A&C) and Core (domains, hosting). [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/gddy/)

## 2. Quality
Returns have risen: ROIC 12.5% in FY2021 to 28.7% TTM, operating margin 12.1% to 26.1%, FCF margin 20.4% to 33.7% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/gddy/financials/ratios/). Gross margin is flat at 63.7%, so the gain is operating leverage, not pricing alone. [Certain]
Q3 2026 total bookings grew 5.7% and A&C revenue 9.0% [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1609711/000160971126000120/gddy-20260930.htm).
Nine-month Core revenue grew only 3.7% [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1609711/000160971126000120/gddy-20260930.htm).
Bookings growth was helped by pricing actions, partly offset by email customers renewing with fewer seats (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1609711/000160971126000120/gddy-20260930.htm). [Likely] some growth is price, not volume.
Moat: domain registration is a sticky, high-switching-cost installed base of small customers; the moat against AI site builders is [Guessing] moderate. Net debt/EBITDA fell from 4.35x to 1.88x. Piotroski F 8 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/gddy/statistics/).

## 3. Valuation
At 114.38 USD the stock trades on P/E 15.7x (5y median 29.6x, 10% up its range), EV/EBITDA 11.6x (below 5y min 15.7x), P/FCF 8.0x (below 5y min 10.6x) and a 12.57% FCF yield. Peer medians are 33.2x P/E and 24.1x EV/EBITDA, so a 52-53% discount on both (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/gddy/statistics/). Peers (VeriSign, Wix, Shopify, Akamai) are only loose comparables: [Likely] faster growth or monopoly-registry economics explain part of the gap.
The reverse DCF implies -7.5% revenue growth in year 1; fair value base is 187.26 USD, bear 102.86 (fact sheet fair-value section). The price therefore assumes AI disintermediation of the franchise, which current bookings do not show. [Likely]
The share is +24.0% above its 200-day average after a +26.6% 3-month rise, yet 12-month change is -14.1%.

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROIC (ratios page) falls below 20% on a TTM basis.
- FCF margin (cash flow page, derived on fact sheet) falls below 25% TTM.
- Total bookings growth (10-Q MD&A) turns negative year on year for two consecutive quarters.
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) rises above 3.0x.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
FCF conversion is 181.3% of net income, TTM, and shares outstanding fell 7.62% year on year, so buybacks are shrinking the count materially (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/gddy/statistics/). Leverage is falling. Return of cash is funded by FCF, not debt. [Certain] Investing outflow rose on registry application fees for ICANN's 2026 new gTLD programme (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1609711/000160971126000120/gddy-20260930.htm); [Guessing] a modest optional bet, not a distraction. Amortisation is falling as intangibles end their lives, which lifts reported earnings but not cash. [Certain]

This is the central bear case and is now explicitly disclosed.
