# GoDaddy Inc. (GDDY) — Core initiation — 2026-11-08
## 1. Business
GoDaddy sells domains, hosting, email/productivity, websites and commerce tools to small businesses on recurring subscriptions. Core Platform (domains, hosting, aftermarket) is about 61% of nine-month revenue and Applications & Commerce about 39% [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1609711/000160971126000120/gddy-20260930.htm). International is about 33% of revenue [company-reported] (same URL); no single foreign country is disclosed in the figures I read, so the macro overlay should treat it as a broad mix [Likely].

## 2. Quality
Good, cash-generative, with a moat that is habit and switching cost rather than technology. Operating margin is 26.1% TTM, up from 12.1% in FY2021; FCF margin is 33.7%; gross margin is flat at 63.7% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/gddy/financials/income-statement/) [Certain]. Domain registrations renew annually and are costly to migrate, and 10-Q language credits pricing actions in productivity products for bookings growth [Likely].

Direction change (ROIC): TTM ROIC is 28.7% against 30.8% a year earlier and a 2-year high of 37.3% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/gddy/financials/ratios/?p=trailing). Over the same 12 months ROCE (+6.5pp), operating margin (+3.8pp) and FCF margin (+2.1pp) all rose. The 10-Q attributes a higher tax provision to higher pre-tax income and fewer stock-compensation tax benefits, so I read the ROIC dip as a tax effect on rising pre-tax returns, not operating erosion [Likely]. It still matters because ROIC is no longer improving [Certain].

## 3. Valuation
The price assumes decline. P/E is 15.7x (5y median 29.6x; peers 33.2x), EV/EBITDA 11.6x (below the 5y minimum of 15.7x; peers 24.1x), P/FCF 8.0x (5y median 14.9x) and FCF yield 12.57% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/gddy/statistics/) [Certain]. The reverse DCF implies -7.5% year-1 revenue growth (fact sheet fair-value section). Base fair value is 187.26 against a 114.38 close; the bear case is 102.86, 10.1% below the price [Likely]. Peers (VRSN, WIX, SHOP, AKAM) are imperfect comparables, so I weight GDDY's own history more. The market is pricing AI-driven disintermediation of website building and domains. The 10-Q added explicit risk language on this (see section 6) [Likely]. The fundamentals reported so far do not show it: bookings and revenue grew about 6% in Q3 and guidance sits at the high end of the range (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/gddy/) [Likely].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Operating margin (income-statement TTM page) falls below 22%.
- FCF margin (cash-flow TTM page) falls below 28%.
- TTM ROIC (ratios trailing page) falls below 22%.
- Net debt / EBITDA (ratios page) rises above 3.0x.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
FCF conversion is 181.3% of net income. Net debt/EBITDA has fallen from 4.35x in FY2021 to 1.88x (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/gddy/financials/balance-sheet/). Share count is down 7.62% year on year. Management repurchased about $1,311.0M of stock in nine months, with $854.2M authorised remaining [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1609711/000160971126000120/gddy-20260930.htm). Buybacks at a 12.57% FCF yield are accretive [Likely]. The revolver is undrawn [company-reported] (same URL). Altman Z of 1.44 reflects leverage and intangibles, and Piotroski F is 8 [Certain].

## 6. Unusual items
- Securities class action: a law-firm notice reports a suit against GoDaddy and certain officers (source: https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/10/21/3385291/0/en/gddy-loss-notice-godaddy-investors-are-reminded-of-the-october-26-court-deadline-contact-bfa-law-if-you-lost-money.html). It is promotional third-party text; merits and size are unknown [Guessing].
- 10-Q delta: new risk factors on AI tools that let customers build sites on third-party platforms, which could cut demand or pricing for GoDaddy's tools, hosting and domains [Certain]. The 10-Q also notes participation in ICANN's 2026 new gTLD program, with application fees in investing cash flow [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1609711/000160971126000120/gddy-20260930.htm).
- Short interest is 5.97% of float, down 7.8% on the month [Certain]. Beta is 0.94.
