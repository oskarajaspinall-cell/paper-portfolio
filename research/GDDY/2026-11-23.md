# GoDaddy Inc. (GDDY) — Core initiation — 2026-11-23
## 1. Business
GoDaddy sells domain names, hosting, websites, email/productivity tools and commerce software to small businesses and entrepreneurs. Two segments: Core (domain registration, add-ons, aftermarket) and Applications & Commerce (A&C, subscription products) [Certain] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1609711/000160971126000120/gddy-20260930.htm). Revenue by country or region: [data unavailable]; no foreign region was verified at ~10%+.

## 2. Quality
Strong economics, but the moat faces an AI test. ROIC TTM 28.7%, operating margin 26.1% and FCF margin 33.7% are all well above FY2021 levels (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/gddy/financials/ratios/) [Certain]. Domains are a recurring, sticky registration base with low churn risk [Likely]. Net debt/EBITDA has fallen to 1.88x [Certain].

Direction change: ROIC is rising over five fiscal years but fell over the last 12 months (30.8% to 28.7%) and sits 8.6pp below its 2-year high (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/gddy/financials/ratios/?p=trailing). Operating margin, ROCE and FCF margin all rose over the same period, so the ROIC dip looks like a higher capital base, not weaker profitability [Likely].

Q3 2026 results: Q3 earnings were reported on 2026-10-29 with guidance at the higher end of the range, and the transcript headline says Airo is now incrementally additive to bookings (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/gddy/transcripts/720314-q3-2026/) [Likely].

## 3. Valuation
P/E 13.7x sits at 6% of its 5y range. EV/EBITDA 10.3x, EV/Sales 2.8x and P/FCF 6.9x are all below their 5y minimums. Peers trade far higher, but they are faster-growing, so the comparison flatters GDDY [Likely]. FCF yield is 14.45% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/gddy/statistics/). Share count is down 7.62% YoY.

The mechanical base fair value is 186.07 against a 99.53 close (+87.0%), and bear is 102.26 (+2.7%). The reverse DCF says the price implies -10.0% revenue growth in year 1. In effect the market is pricing GDDY as a business that AI disintermediates [Likely]. The bear case at about the current price indicates limited downside if the franchise holds [Guessing]. The price is 7.4% above its 200-day average but 12-month change is -19.9%, so this is a recovery from the June low, not a clean uptrend [Certain].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Operating margin (TTM, income-statement trailing page) falls below 22%.
- ROIC (TTM, ratios trailing page) falls below 22%.
- FCF margin (TTM, cash-flow trailing page) falls below 28%.
- Total bookings growth turns negative for two consecutive quarters (10-Q MD&A), showing AI builders are displacing domains, hosting or website tools. [company-reported] bookings rose 5.7% in Q3 2026 (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1609711/000160971126000120/gddy-20260930.htm).

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
FCF conversion is 181.3% of net income, helped by non-cash amortization and deferred revenue from prepaid renewals [Likely]. FCF margin has risen from 20.4% in FY2021 to 33.7%. Leverage is falling and the share count is shrinking, so buybacks are the main use of cash [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/gddy/statistics/). Altman Z is 1.44, which reflects the heavy buyback-driven equity base and not distress given Piotroski F of 8 [Likely]. The filing also shows registry-rights application fees for ICANN's 2026 new gTLD program: a small new use of cash [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1609711/000160971126000120/gddy-20260930.htm).

## 6. Unusual items
- 10-Q delta: new risk factors say AI website builders, closed ecosystems and third-party platforms could cut demand for GoDaddy's website, hosting and domain products or add pricing pressure. This is the central bear thesis [Certain] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1609711/000160971126000120/gddy-20260930.htm).
- Same filing: lower email seat volume at renewal, offset by price rises [Certain]. Price-led growth is less durable than volume [Guessing].
- Management is pushing Airo, an AI-native builder (source: https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/new-godaddy-airo-ai-builder-lets-small-businesses-turn-ideas-into-websites-and-apps-302940437.html) [Certain].
- Short interest 5.63% of float, not crowded [Certain].
