# GoDaddy Inc. (GDDY) — Core initiation — 2026-10-07

## 1. Business
GoDaddy sells domain registration, web hosting, website/e-commerce tools and productivity software (Microsoft 365 reseller) to ~20.5m small-business customers [Certain] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1609711/000160971126000087/gddyex991-20260630xq2earni.htm). Revenue is subscription/renewal-based across two segments: slower-growing Core Platform (domains, hosting) and faster-growing, higher-margin Applications & Commerce [Certain] (same source).

## 2. Quality
GoDaddy is the largest domain registrar, with high switching costs — migrating a domain, site or business email is painful — supporting a stable ~64% gross margin [Certain] (source: factsheet IS). Returns on capital have more than doubled over five years: ROIC 12.5%→37.3% TTM, ROCE 9.2%→26.4% TTM, driven by operating margin expanding from 12.1% to 25.2% [Certain] (source: factsheet RA/IS). FCF margin is 33.4% TTM, among the strongest in the peer set [Certain]. The moat is real but narrow: pricing power sits mostly in renewals, not new-customer acquisition, where Wix, Shopify, Squarespace and low-cost registrars compete hard on price [Likely]. Net debt/EBITDA has fallen from 4.35x (FY21) to 1.91x (TTM), cutting financial risk [Certain] (source: factsheet BS/IS). ROE (442.9% TTM) and P/B (1,855.9x) are distorted by a near-zero equity base left by years of buybacks — not a genuine quality or distress signal either way [Certain].

## 3. Valuation
At 98.18 USD, GDDY trades on 14.6x TTM P/E, 10.9x EV/EBITDA, 3.0x EV/Sales and 7.3x P/FCF — EV/EBITDA, EV/Sales and P/FCF are all below their own 5-year minimum, and P/E sits at the 8th percentile of its 5-year range [Certain] (source: factsheet RA/HI). Versus peers (WIX, SHOP, VRSN, TCX) it is 66-80% cheaper on every multiple except P/B [Certain] (source: factsheet, peer pages). The 13.7% FCF yield implies the market is pricing continued deceleration, litigation risk or margin pressure, not the current trend of rising ROIC/ROCE and margin [Likely]. Peer comparability is weak: WIX and SHOP are faster-growing comps on thin samples (n=1-4), overstating GDDY's relative cheapness; VRSN (registry, not registrar) is the closer moat analogue and also screens expensive [Likely]. The gap between improving fundamentals and a 5-year-low multiple has a dated cause: the September 2026 securities-fraud allegations over promotional-discount disclosure (see Unusual items) [Likely].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROIC (ratios page) falls below 20% for two consecutive quarters, reversing the five-year improvement trend.
- Operating margin (income statement) drops below 20%, erasing the post-2023 step-up.
- Total bookings growth (SEC 8-K earnings release) falls below 4% y/y for two consecutive quarters, indicating the promotional-discount allegations reflect a real demand problem rather than a disclosure dispute.
- Net debt/EBITDA (balance sheet/ratios page) rises back above 3.0x.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
GoDaddy has retired 6.24% of its shares outstanding over the past year [Certain] (source: factsheet ST), funded by FCF margin of 33.4% TTM [Certain]. FCF conversion (FCF/NI) has swung from 73% to 321% across FY21-25 [Certain] (source: factsheet CF/IS), reflecting volatility in reported net income (tax items), not in underlying cash generation, which has grown steadily. Net debt/EBITDA fell from 4.35x to 1.91x over the same period even while buying back stock, so buybacks are being funded by FCF, not added leverage [Certain] (source: factsheet BS/IS).

## 6. Unusual items
Since late September 2026, at least six plaintiffs' law firms have filed or noticed securities-fraud class actions alleging GoDaddy failed to disclose a promotional discount that materially hurt total-bookings growth, with lead-plaintiff deadlines of Oct 20 and Oct 26, 2026 [Likely] (law-firm notices are solicitations, not proof, but the specific and consistent bookings-growth allegation across filings is a real near-term risk to monitor) (source: https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/10/05/3374315/0/en/gddy-court-notification-godaddy-has-been-sued-for-securities-fraud-over-customer-acquisition-issues-bfa-law-reminds-investors-of-the-october-26-court-deadline.html, https://www.newsfilecorp.com/release/316716/Robbins-LLP-is-Investigating-Allegations-that-GoDaddy-Inc.-Failed-to-Disclose-to-Investors-That-it-had-Initiated-an-Undisclosed-Promotional-Discount-that-had-a-Material-Adverse-Effect-on-Total-Bookings-Growth). Short interest rose 43.9% month-on-month to 6.19% of float [Certain] (source: factsheet ST), consistent with the market positioning for an adverse outcome. GoDaddy's own Q2 2026 earnings release discloses no promotional-pricing commentary and no bookings deceleration — total bookings grew 6% y/y [Certain] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1609711/000160971126000087/gddyex991-20260630xq2earni.htm), so the allegations are not yet corroborated in reported numbers. Separately, the Financial Times reportedly described a preliminary takeover approach from Gen Digital on 2026-09-24, which the stock rallied on; this is unconfirmed and is not a basis for the thesis [Guessing] (source: https://www.barrons.com/articles/godaddy-stock-gen-digital-acquisition-report-6ceb940e). P/B of 1,855.9x reflects near-zero book equity from buybacks, not distress; Altman Z of 1.63 is low but typical for an asset-light, high-FCF buyback compounder rather than a going-concern signal [Likely] (source: factsheet ST).
