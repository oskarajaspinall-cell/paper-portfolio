# GoDaddy Inc. (GDDY) — Core initiation — 2026-11-16
## 1. Business
Sells domains, hosting, email/productivity, websites and commerce tools to small businesses and entrepreneurs, mostly on renewing subscriptions. Core (domains, hosting) was 60.6% and Applications & Commerce 39.3% of nine-month revenue [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1609711/000160971126000120/gddy-20260930.htm). International was 32.7% of revenue [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1609711/000160971126000120/gddy-20260930.htm), but the filing says no country outside the U.S. exceeds 10%, so no single-country overlay is needed. [Certain]

## 2. Quality
Strong and improving cash economics. TTM ROIC 28.7%, operating margin 26.1% and FCF margin 33.7%, all well above FY2021 (ROIC 12.5%, operating margin 12.1%, FCF margin 20.4%) (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/gddy/financials/ratios/). Gross margin is flat at 63.7%. [Certain]
Moat: domain registration and renewal habits, switching friction and a large small-business base with low acquisition cost [Likely]. Direction flag: ROIC fell over 12 months (30.8% to 28.7%, 8.6pp below its 2-year high of 37.3%) while ROCE, operating margin and FCF margin all rose (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/gddy/financials/ratios/?p=trailing). That fits buyback-shrunk capital and a mix shift rather than eroding returns [Likely], though I can't prove it from the data [Guessing].
Key risk: AI site builders disintermediating website/hosting demand, which the new 10-Q risk factors name explicitly (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1609711/000160971126000120/gddy-20260930.htm) [Certain]. Q3 bookings grew on domains and aftermarket, offset by fewer email seats (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1609711/000160971126000120/gddy-20260930.htm) [Certain]. Q3 beat and 2026 revenue guided to the high end of range (source: https://www.tipranks.com/news/the-fly/godaddy-sees-2026-revenue-at-higher-end-of-5-215b-5-255b-consensus-5-24b-thefly-news) [Likely].

## 3. Valuation
The price implies shrinking revenue: reverse DCF needs -6.7% year-1 growth (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/gddy/). P/E 15.7x is at the 10th percentile of its 5y range (median 29.6x); EV/EBITDA 11.6x, EV/Sales 3.2x and P/FCF 7.9x are all below their 5y minimums. FCF yield 12.60% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/gddy/statistics/). Peer medians (WIX, VRSN, SHOP, NET) are far higher, but those are faster-growth or monopoly-registry businesses, so the gap overstates the discount [Likely].
Mechanical fair value: base 181.10 vs close 114.12, bear 99.77, bull 196.97 (DCF-led). The bear case sits only 12.6% below the price, so the market is already pricing much of the AI-disruption risk [Likely]. The stock is +23.3% above its 200-day average and up 24.9% in 6 months, yet down 11.6% over 12 months [Certain].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- TTM operating margin (income-statement trailing page) falls below 22%.
- TTM FCF margin (cash-flow trailing page) falls below 28%.
- TTM ROIC (ratios trailing page) falls below 20%.
- Gross margin (income statement) falls below 60%, or net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) rises above 3.0x.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
FCF is 181.3% of net income, and shares fell 7.62% year on year. Nine-month buybacks were $1,311.0m [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1609711/000160971126000120/gddy-20260930.htm). Net debt/EBITDA fell from 4.35x (FY2021) to 1.88x. Debt maturities are staggered and the revolver is undrawn (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1609711/000160971126000120/gddy-20260930.htm) [Certain]. Registry-rights fees for ICANN's new-gTLD round are a small new investing outflow (same filing) [Certain].

## 6. Unusual items
Altman Z is 1.44, likely a quirk of buyback-depleted equity and not distress, given the 1.88x leverage [Likely]. Short interest is 5.26% of float [Certain]. The Airo AI builder is a key swing factor; management says it began adding to bookings (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/gddy/transcripts/720314-q3-2026/) [Likely], with competitive pricing tests ahead [Likely].
