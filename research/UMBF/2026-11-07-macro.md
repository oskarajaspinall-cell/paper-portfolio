**Inference:** the change is in the risk premium, not earnings. UMBF has de-rated (-7.8% over 3m, https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/umbf/history/), so some is priced. A 3.48% HY spread is not yet credit stress. [Likely]

**Macro bear:** spreads above 5% and a risk-off move hit regional banks. P/B compresses toward the 5y low (1.1x, about -15%) and the El Dorado thrift-style book carries credit risk.

**Tilt:** toward bear, small. The VIX and spread moves are sharp but levels are not stressed, and the P/B + ROE anchor (146.83) already sits close to the price.
