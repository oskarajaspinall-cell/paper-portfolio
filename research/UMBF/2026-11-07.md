# UMB Financial Corporation (UMBF) — Core initiation — 2026-11-07
## 1. Business
Kansas City regional bank holding company. Earns net interest income on commercial and institutional loans, plus fee income from asset management, brokerage, bankcard, healthcare services and treasury management. [Certain] Fees were 30.1% of Q3 2026 revenue [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/101382/000119312526437096/umbf-20260930.htm). Fee mix is richer than a typical regional peer. [Likely]

## 2. Quality
ROE is 13.5% TTM against 11.5% in FY2021, rising (+1.1pp over five years) and stable at 12-15% through the cycle (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/umbf/financials/ratios/). [Certain] Net margin is 35.6% TTM vs 28.1% in FY2025 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/umbf/financials/income-statement/). FCF conversion is 110.6% TTM, though it was only 46.5% in FY2024, so cash flow is lumpy (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/umbf/financials/cash-flow-statement/). [Certain]
Q3 net interest income rose 13.7% year on year, driven by loan growth and cheaper deposit repricing [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/101382/000119312526437096/umbf-20260930.htm). [Certain] Q3 EPS beat expectations per headline (source: https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20261027744364/en/UMB-Financial-Corporation-Reports-Third-Quarter-2026-Results/). [Likely]
Moat is moderate: sticky institutional and fiduciary deposits and fee lines, not a unique franchise. Piotroski F of 4 is mediocre (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/umbf/statistics/). [Likely] Beta 0.85. The 10-Q adds a competitor-risk line about rivals under different regulatory standards. [Likely]

## 3. Valuation
P/E is 10.1x, near the low of its 5y range (9.3x-14.5x, median 12.5x) and 20% below the peer median (12.7x; CFR, WAL, PNFP, BOKF). P/FCF 8.9x vs 9.0x median, 19% below peers. P/B is 1.3x, 42% up its 5y range and 11% above peers, reflecting the superior ROE. FCF yield is 11.24% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/umbf/statistics/). [Certain]
Fair value base is 193.36 USD vs 133.77 close (+44.5%); bear 144.35 (+7.9%). The blend leans on the FCFE DCF (286.42), which is generous for a bank. The P/B + ROE method gives 146.83, only modestly above the close, and is the more credible anchor. The reverse DCF implies -7.3% year-1 revenue growth, i.e. the price assumes shrinkage. [Likely] Cheap on earnings, fair on book. Upside is real but less than headline fair value suggests. [Likely]

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROE (ratios page) falls below 10% on a TTM basis.
- Net margin (income statement page) falls below 25% TTM.
- FCF conversion (cash flow and income statement pages) stays below 80% TTM for two consecutive quarters.
- Shares change YoY (statistics page) stays above 20% on a rolling basis, signalling dilution-funded growth.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Acquisitive: HTLF integration costs ran through 2025, and shares outstanding rose 14.35% YoY (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/umbf/statistics/). [Certain] On 2026-10-27 it agreed to buy El Dorado Savings Bank, valued at about $506 million, 52% cash and 48% stock, adding low-cost deposits in California and Nevada [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/101382/000119312526437096/umbf-20260930.htm). [Certain] Closing needs regulatory and El Dorado shareholder approval. [Certain] FCF conversion is above 100% TTM. Further dilution is the main risk to per-share returns. [Likely]

## 6. Unusual items
Short interest is 5.55% of float, up 4.0% on the prior month (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/umbf/statistics/). [Certain] Institutions own 92.5%. The El Dorado deal is a dated catalyst with integration and credit-quality risk in a thrift-style book. [Likely] Q3 securities gains were non-recurring, so core earnings are less than reported.
