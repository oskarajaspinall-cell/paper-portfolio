## 2. Quality
Good but not exceptional, cyclical. TTM ROIC 11.4%, ROCE 11.8%, operating margin 9.4%, net cash (net debt/EBITDA -0.28x), Piotroski 6 [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tyo/7269/financials/ratios/). Over five fiscal years returns rose (ROIC 8.1% to 12.0%) but the last 12 months fell: ROIC 12.5% to 11.4%, ROCE 14.3% to 11.8%, operating margin 10.8% to 9.4% [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tyo/7269/financials/ratios/?p=trailing). Recent direction is therefore down from a peak (ROIC 14.4% in FY2025), not rising. Moat: dominance in small cars in India and low-cost engineering; hybrids are gaining there [Likely] (source: https://www.cnbc.com/2026/04/23/japanese-carmakers-deepen-hold-in-india-with-hybrids.html). The Reuters piece suggests buyers now demand more features, a risk to the no-frills position [Likely]. Beta 0.41 [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tyo/7269/statistics/).
