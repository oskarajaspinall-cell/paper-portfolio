# Hamilton Lane (HLNE) — Core initiation — 2026-10-31
## 1. Business
Private-markets investment manager and adviser. It earns management and advisory fees on customised separate accounts and commingled specialised funds (drawdown and evergreen), plus incentive fees and carried interest. Growth is increasingly from wealth-channel evergreen funds sold through intermediaries [Certain] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1433642/000143364226000042/hlne-20260630.htm). Specialised funds were $51.9 billion of AUM at 30 June 2026 [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1433642/000143364226000042/hlne-20260630.htm).

## 2. Quality
Good, not exceptional, and slipping at the margin. TTM ROIC is 19.8% and ROE 32.4%, but operating margin has fallen from 46.1% (FY2022) to 43.6% TTM, and gross margin from 64.9% to 60.7% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/hlne/financials/ratios/). FCF conversion is 133.0% of net income TTM, with net debt/EBITDA about zero, so earnings are cash-backed and the balance sheet is clean [Certain]. The moat is relationships and data: a long-dated, sticky fee base and a gatekeeper position with institutions [Likely]. The weaker point is wealth-channel competition from far larger alternatives managers, which can erode fee rates [Likely]. The latest 10-Q shows evergreen funds driving fee growth and a higher bonus accrual from stronger operating performance [Certain]. Risk Factors are unchanged [Certain] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1433642/000143364226000042/hlne-20260630.htm).

## 3. Valuation
The price assumes little. P/E is 12.8x against a 5y range of 17.5x–32.4x (median 25.3x) and a peer median of 34.5x; P/FCF is 12.6x against a median of 17.9x; FCF yield is 7.93% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/hlne/statistics/). EV/EBITDA of 12.1x is below its 5y minimum. Against peers P/B is 36% lower, though EV/Sales is 29% higher [Certain]. The model fair value is bear 54.97, base 92.11, bull 166.38 against a close of 83.91, so base upside is only 9.8%. The DCF leg (82.50) is below the price. The reverse DCF implies 9.8% year-one revenue growth, fading [Likely]. The cheapness is largely historical-multiple compression after a 27.3% 12-month fall, not a clear margin of safety. Peers are larger, so the comparison flatters [Guessing].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Operating margin (income statement page) falls below 38% on a TTM basis.
- TTM ROIC (ratios page) falls below 14%.
- FCF conversion (FCF/NI, cash-flow page) falls below 80% TTM, signalling earnings not turning to cash.
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) rises above 1.5x.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Cash conversion is strong: FCF margin 42.7% TTM. Leverage is minimal. The company repurchased $50.0 million of stock in the June quarter [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1433642/000143364226000042/hlne-20260630.htm). The fact sheet shows shares up 34.50% YoY (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/hlne/statistics/); this looks inconsistent with buybacks, possibly a share-class or data effect, so dilution is unresolved [Guessing].
