# Shandong Hongqiao Aluminum Industry Holding Company Limited (SHE:002379) — Core initiation — 2026-10-18

## 1. Business
Hongqiao is a China-based, vertically integrated aluminum producer — alumina refining through primary aluminum smelting into aluminum products — classified by stockanalysis.com under Materials/Aluminum [Certain] (source: fact sheet overview). Revenue is driven by shipped volumes and realized aluminum/alumina prices, with margin set by the spread between aluminum selling prices and input costs (alumina, energy, carbon) [Likely].

## 2. Quality
This is a commodity cyclical, not a business with a clearly durable moat: ROIC, ROE and margins swung from losses in FY2021/FY2023 to very high returns in FY2024-FY2025 (ROIC -10.8%→64.1%→43.5% TTM; ROE -8.5%→77.4%→84.1% TTM; gross margin -1.4%→19.9%→22.0% TTM) [Certain]. That pattern fits a cost-curve producer riding an aluminum/alumina price upcycle rather than structural pricing power — aluminum is largely undifferentiated, so returns depend on relative input-cost position, which the fact sheet cannot verify [Guessing]. Cash conversion is now strong (FCF margin 16.0% TTM, FCF/NI 107.4%) and leverage is low (net debt/EBITDA 0.14x TTM, vs 13.24x in the FY2023 trough) [Certain]. Altman Z 4.73 (safe) and Piotroski F 5/9 (middling) [Certain]. The five-year history — two loss years out of five — is itself the clearest evidence that competitors and commodity cycles can take these returns away; today's numbers look like a cyclical peak, not an entrenched advantage [Likely].

## 3. Valuation
At 7.5x TTM P/E and 5.0x EV/EBITDA the stock screens cheap against its own 5-year range (4th/10th percentile), but that range is distorted by the FY2021/FY2023 loss years' extreme multiples, so the percentile is unreliable [Likely]. Versus three aluminum peers (AA, HKG:2600, ASX:S32), Hongqiao is roughly in line on P/E (+4%) but at a premium on EV/EBITDA (+31%), EV/Sales (+38%) and, most starkly, P/B (3.2x vs 1.3x peer median, +140%, above its own 5-year max) [Certain]. P/B is least distorted by the earnings swing, so the market already pays up for the current 84% ROE being durable. FCF yield is high at 14.36% [Certain]. Base fair value of CNY 21.92 implies +54.8% upside from the CNY 14.16 close (through-cycle DCF 67%, EV/EBITDA 33%); the bear case is negative, reflecting the downcycle's earnings risk [Certain]. The price assumes the margin step-up persists; the peer P/B premium argues much is priced in.

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Gross margin (income statement page) falls back below 10%, signalling the alumina/aluminum spread that drove the FY2024-25 re-rating has compressed toward FY2021-23 levels.
- ROIC (ratios page) falls below 15%, indicating the 43.5% TTM level was cyclical peak earnings, not sustainable.
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) rises back above 1.0x, showing leverage is being rebuilt after the post-FY2023 deleveraging.
- FCF conversion (FCF/NI, cash flow/ratios pages) drops persistently below 80%, indicating earnings are no longer backed by cash.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Cash generation is strong: FCF margin 16.0% TTM and FCF/NI conversion of 107.4% mean free cash flow is covering (and exceeding) reported net income [Certain]. The balance sheet has swung from net debt/EBITDA of 13.24x in the FY2023 trough to 0.14x TTM — effectively net-cash-like [Certain]. Shares outstanding fell 31.33% year-on-year (statistics page) [Certain]; the fact sheet does not disclose the mechanism (buyback vs. another corporate action), so this is not yet a confirmed shareholder-friendly tailwind — see Unusual items.

## 6. Unusual items
The 31.33% YoY drop in shares outstanding is large enough that its cause needs confirming before treating it as a buyback-driven per-share tailwind [Guessing]. Institutional ownership is low at 4.1%, typical for a China A-share with concentrated non-institutional/state-linked holdings [Certain]. Beta (5y) is essentially zero (-0.01), so this stock's risk is dominated by commodity and China-specific factors rather than global-equity correlation [Certain] — relevant for diversification but not reflected in vol-scaled sizing calibrated on US-style betas. No usable qualitative news was shown in the fact sheet's News & sentiment section [data unavailable]; this is a Shenzhen-listed, non-US-SEC filer, so no SEC filing delta applies. Next earnings are due 2026-10-23, five days after this initiation, a near-term print that could move the shares before the cyclical-durability thesis plays out [Certain].
