# Macro overlay: Shandong Hongqiao Aluminum Industry Holding Company Limited (SHE:002379) — 2026-10-18

## Assessment
Hongqiao's thesis hinges on whether the FY2024-25 margin step-up (gross margin 22.0% TTM vs ~0-4% FY2021-22) persists — a cost-curve/commodity-cycle call, not a China-domestic financing or FX-translation story (revenue and most costs are CNY) [Certain, fact sheet]. The one external macro lever available in our data is the US dollar, via two indirect channels: (1) alumina/bauxite are imported and USD-priced, so dollar strength raises CNY input costs **(inference)**; (2) a firmer dollar typically pressures USD-denominated global commodity benchmarks (LME aluminum), which China's SHFE price loosely tracks **(inference)**. Facts: the broad dollar index (DTWEXBGS) rose to 121.602 on 2026-10-09, +3.72 (~1m) / +1.1 (~3m) [Certain, https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=DTWEXBGS]; effective fed funds rose to 3.88%, +0.25 (~1m) [Certain, https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=DFF]; core CPI (CPILFESL) rose to 338.896 (2026-09-01), +2.83 over 3m (~+0.84%, above a 2% annualized pace) [Certain, https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=CPILFESL] — consistent with sticky inflation keeping the Fed hawkish and the dollar firm. Market pricing: the stock already trades at a 140% P/B premium to peers (3.2x vs 1.3x, fact sheet, stockanalysis.com ratios/statistics pages, 2026-10-16) **(inference: the market is pricing margin durability, not a dollar-driven reversal)**. Beta (5y) is -0.01 and institutional ownership is 4.1% (fact sheet, 2026-10-16) [Certain], so this A-share's multiple is dominated by domestic/commodity factors, not global risk sentiment — dampening, not removing, the dollar channel.
