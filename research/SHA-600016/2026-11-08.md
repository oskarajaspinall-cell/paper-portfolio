## 2. Quality
Returns are low and falling. ROE slid from 6.2% (FY2021) to 4.5% (FY2025) and 3.7% TTM; net margin fell from 38.0% to 31.7% TTM (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sha/600016/financials/ratios/). [Certain] Piotroski F-score is 2 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sha/600016/statistics/). [Certain] Shares outstanding rose 8.46% YoY, i.e. dilution (same source). [Certain] ROIC, ROCE and margins other than net are [data unavailable]. FCF margin is deeply negative, but for a bank, deposit and loan flows run through operating cash flow, so it is not informative; it is the fact sheet's flagged direction change and I give it no weight. [Likely] No evident durable moat: the peers are state-backed and similar, and the declining ROE says the franchise is being competed away. [Likely]

## 3. Valuation
P/E is 6.7x, above its own fiscal-year range (max 6.2x) and in line with the peer median of 6.7x (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sha/600016/financials/ratios/). [Certain] P/B is 0.2x, at the bottom of its range and a 51% discount to peers' 0.4x. [Certain] The discount is explained by sub-5% ROE: a bank earning below its cost of equity deserves less than book. Mechanical fair value: base 4.77 CNY vs the 3.40 close (+40.2%), bear 3.42 (+0.7%), bull 5.33. The bear case only matches the current price, but the base rests on a P/B + ROE method that uses a sustainable ROE the trailing trend does not support, and the cost of equity includes the owner-set 3.5% China premium. [Likely] Reverse DCF implies -11.9% year-1 revenue growth, so the price already discounts shrinkage. [Likely] Cheap for a reason; the price sits below both the 50-day and 200-day averages, with the 12-month change at -15.6%. [Certain]

## 4. What would prove this wrong
