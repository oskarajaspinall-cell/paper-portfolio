# Macro overlay: American International Group, Inc. (AIG) — 2026-10-12
## Assessment
AIG's earnings and valuation both run through rates: reinvestment yield on its large fixed-income portfolio (backing General Insurance float and Life & Retirement reserves) drives net investment income, while the 10y Treasury is the risk-free rate in the fact sheet's own CAPM cost of equity, setting the fair-value ceiling. Facts: 10y Treasury 5.22% on 2026-10-08, +0.68pp/3m; 10y real yield (TIPS) 2.87%, +0.56pp/3m; fed funds 3.88%, +0.26pp/3m (FRED DGS10/DFII10/DFF, 2026-10-08) [Certain]. Breakeven inflation flat (2.33%, -0.04pp/1m, T10YIE) [Certain]; HY spreads widened to 3.15% (+0.45pp/3m) and the dollar firmed (+0.70pp/3m, DTWEXBGS) [Certain].

Flat breakevens with rising real yields and fed funds point to a growth/policy-path repricing, not an inflation surprise **(inference)** [Likely]. The note's own fair value already uses today's 5.22% risk-free rate, so this level is priced into its $41 base estimate, not a fresh shock [Likely]. Concurrent HY-spread widening and dollar strength suggest tightening conditions that typically compress financial-sector multiples faster than an NII tailwind lifts earnings **(inference)** [Guessing].

Bull: yields hold/rise on growth, not credit stress → NII keeps growing, supporting the note's ROE-recovery trigger. Bear: yields fall on a growth scare while spreads widen further → NII tailwind reverses, AOCI pressure and a wider risk premium compress the multiple, reinforcing the fundamental bear case (falling ROE/ROIC, price above fair value). Tilt: small, toward bear — spread/dollar tightening looks more likely to pressure the multiple than to close the ROE gap driving the thesis.
