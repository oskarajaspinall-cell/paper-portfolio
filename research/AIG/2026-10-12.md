# American International Group, Inc. (AIG) — Core initiation — 2026-10-12

## 1. Business
AIG is a global insurer operating two segments: General Insurance (commercial, specialty and personal property-casualty underwriting, including cyber) and Life & Retirement (annuities, life insurance, institutional products). Revenue is earned premiums plus net investment income on the float; profit is the underwriting margin (premiums less claims and expenses) plus investment returns. Buybacks have steadily shrunk the share count [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/aig/statistics/).

## 2. Quality
ROE has fallen from 16.0% (FY2021) to 7.2% TTM and ROIC from 9.8% to 7.7% TTM [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/aig/financials/ratios/) — a multi-year decline, not one bad year. Multi-line scale and specialty/cyber underwriting are real capabilities, but returns are now only modestly above AIG's own cost of capital, and the peer group prices this gap explicitly: peer median P/B is 2.1x against AIG's 1.0x [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/aig/statistics/), i.e. the market itself assigns AIG a materially lower quality premium rather than overlooking a bargain. Net debt/EBITDA is low and falling (1.12x TTM) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/aig/financials/ratios/), giving balance-sheet flexibility but not evidence of a competitive moat. Four leadership changes landed within six weeks — Executive Chairman Peter Zaffino departing for Palantir, a new Chief Risk Officer, a new CEO for Americas & Global Personal Insurance, and the retirement of the General Insurance CEO [Certain] (source: fact sheet News & sentiment, https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20261001046294/en/AIG-Appoints-Turab-Hussain-Chief-Risk-Officer/, https://www.wsj.com/business/c-suite/aigs-zaffino-stepping-down-as-chairman-joining-palantir-4ee5b7f6) — adding execution risk at a business that depends on underwriting discipline [Likely].

## 3. Valuation
P/E of 14.2x sits at the 93rd percentile of AIG's own 5-year range (4.6x–14.9x) and is 29% above the peer median of 11.0x [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/aig/statistics/). EV/EBITDA (5.9x, 25th percentile, 34% below peers) looks cheap, but EV/Sales (1.8x) is 22% above peers [Certain] (same source) — a mixed multiple picture, not a clean discount. P/B of 1.0x is 52% below the peer median of 2.1x, but that is consistent with AIG's much lower ROE rather than mispricing [Likely]. The fact sheet's own blended fair value (P/B+ROE best method, P/E second) puts base fair value at $41.33 and even the bull case at $61.48, both below the $77.04 last close (-46% and -20% respectively) (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/aig/statistics/, ratios and financials pages). At $77.04, the market is paying for a return toward the 2021 level of profitability (16% ROE) that has not recurred since, through a period of unusual management turnover [Likely].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROE (ratios page) recovers to and sustains above 10% for two consecutive quarters, closing the gap with peers that currently justifies the P/B discount.
- ROIC (ratios page) resumes an upward trend rather than extending its 5-year, -3.0pp fall.
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) stays below 1.5x through the leadership transition, evidencing underwriting discipline was not compromised.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Shares outstanding fell 11.1% YoY [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/aig/statistics/), and AIG's Q2 2026 10-Q shows treasury shares rising by roughly 13.5 million in the six months to June 30, 2026, confirming buybacks remain the primary capital-return lever [Certain] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/5272/000000527226000076/aig-20260630.htm). FCF margin and FCF conversion are [data unavailable] on stockanalysis.com. Net debt/EBITDA is low and falling (1.12x TTM, 0.94x site definition) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/aig/financials/ratios/), leaving room to keep repurchasing without balance-sheet stress, but at a sub-10% ROE each retained dollar earns a return close to AIG's cost of capital, limiting how much buybacks alone can re-rate the multiple [Likely].

## 6. Unusual items
Short interest rose 33.2% month-on-month, though it remains low in absolute terms (2.02% of float) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/aig/statistics/). Four leadership changes in six weeks (Executive Chairman, CRO, Americas/Personal Insurance CEO, General Insurance CEO) are unusual turnover for an underwriting-driven business and warrant monitoring into Q3 results on November 3, 2026 [Likely] (sources: https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20261001046294/en/AIG-Appoints-Turab-Hussain-Chief-Risk-Officer/, https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260921688711/en/AIG-Appoints-Sierra-Signorelli-CEO-of-Americas-and-Global-Personal-Insurance/, https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260916185348/en/Jon-Hancock-to-Retire-from-AIG/, https://www.wsj.com/business/c-suite/aigs-zaffino-stepping-down-as-chairman-joining-palantir-4ee5b7f6).
