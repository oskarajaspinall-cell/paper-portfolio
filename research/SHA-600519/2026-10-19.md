# Kweichow Moutai Co., Ltd. (SHA:600519) — Core initiation — 2026-10-19

## 1. Business
Produces and sells Moutai-brand baijiu (premium sorghum liquor), China's top prestige spirits brand, through a wholesale distributor network plus direct retail/e-commerce, alongside a lower-priced "series liquor" range. [Certain] State-controlled via its parent group. Earns through pricing power on a fixed-release, multi-year-aged product with regional-appellation scarcity. (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sha/600519/)

## 2. Quality
Returns remain exceptional: ROIC 37.3% TTM (down from a 51.1% FY2024 peak), ROE 33.0% TTM, gross margin 90.3%, operating margin 64.5% [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sha/600519/financials/ratios/). The moat looks real — scarcity, ageing requirements and a gifting/collector brand peers haven't matched: Moutai trades at 14.4x EV/EBITDA vs a 3-peer median of 8.5x [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sha/600519/statistics/). But the trend has turned: FY2025 was the company's first annual revenue/profit decline on record, and the 1H2026 report showed a further net-profit drop (CNBC, 2026-08-18: https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/19/china-economy-moutai-ai-property.html) [Likely]. China's state-backed "national team" funds have reportedly been reducing Moutai holdings (SCMP, 2026-08-18: https://www.scmp.com/business/china-business/article/3364299/chinas-national-team-ditches-kweichow-moutai-move-adds-valuation-woes) [Likely]. The 5y trend columns still read "rising" over the full window, but ROIC, ROCE and ROE have all fallen from their FY2024 peak [Certain] (source: ratios page above). The swing factor is whether pricing power holds, not today's still-high profitability.

## 3. Valuation
Every multiple sits at or below its own 5-year minimum (P/E 20.4x vs 5y range 20.9x–49.1x; EV/EBITDA 14.4x vs 14.8x–35.1x; P/FCF 14.2x vs 21.8x–69.1x) — the cheapest the stock has screened in five years [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sha/600519/statistics/). Yet it still trades well above the 3-peer median on most multiples (P/E +45%, EV/EBITDA +70%, EV/Sales +164%, P/B +174%), though P/FCF is -72% below peers [Certain] (same source, peers SHE:000858, SHE:000568, SHA:600809). The DCF/EV-EBITDA blended base-case fair value is 1,398.49 CNY, only +5.2% above the 1,329.00 CNY close; bear is -47.2%, bull +26.9% [Certain] (source: factsheet fair value section, built on https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sha/600519/financials/ and statistics pages). The reverse DCF shows the price still assumes 11.1% revenue growth in year one, fading to terminal growth — a demanding bar given the first-ever annual decline just reported [Likely]. The market is paying a quality premium that recent revenue trends no longer obviously support.

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Revenue growth (income statement page) posts a second consecutive full-year decline, confirming a structural problem rather than one-off destocking.
- Gross margin (income statement page) falls below 88%, signalling discounting or channel stuffing that erodes pricing power.
- ROIC (ratios page) falls below 30%, i.e. below FY2021's level, showing the moat eroding rather than merely normalising from a cycle peak.
- Net margin (income statement page) falls below 45%, confirming the 1H2026 profit drop is not a one-off.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Balance sheet is net-cash (net debt/EBITDA -0.49x TTM) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sha/600519/financials/ratios/). Shares outstanding fell 0.28% YoY, consistent with buybacks/cancellation rather than dilution [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sha/600519/statistics/). FCF conversion (FCF/NI) has swung sharply — 115.6% (FY2021) down to 50.1% (FY2022), back up to 143.3% TTM [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sha/600519/financials/cash-flow-statement/) — likely working-capital timing (receivables/inventory) around the demand slowdown rather than a change in the cash-generative model [Likely]. TTM FCF margin of 68.6% is well above the FY2021-25 average, pointing the same way [Likely].

## 6. Unusual items
State-backed "national team" funds reportedly reducing Moutai holdings (SCMP, 2026-08-18: https://www.scmp.com/business/china-business/article/3364299/chinas-national-team-ditches-kweichow-moutai-move-adds-valuation-woes) [Likely] — a sentiment/ownership overhang on a stock where institutional ownership is only 13.5% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sha/600519/statistics/); insider ownership and short interest are [data unavailable]. Broader read-through: a domestic fund rotation out of consumer staples and into AI/tech names (SCMP, 2026-07-23: https://www.scmp.com/business/china-business/article/3361544/chinas-star-fund-manager-dumps-long-held-consumer-bets-pivot-ai-trade; SCMP, 2026-07-06: https://www.scmp.com/business/china-business/article/3359285/tech-rallies-and-consumer-shares-lag-chinas-stock-market-forms-microcosm-economy) [Likely] — a macro/flow risk beyond company fundamentals. No governance or accounting red flags identified.
