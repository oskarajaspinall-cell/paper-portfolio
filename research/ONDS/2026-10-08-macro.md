# Macro overlay: Ondas Inc. (ONDS) — 2026-10-08
## Assessment
ONDS's thesis is fundamentals (backlog, dilution, integration risk), but its funding model makes it unusually macro-sensitive: net debt/EBITDA 7.44x TTM, shares up 297% YoY to fund acquisitions, beta 2.92, EV/Sales 15.2x (132% above peers) [Certain] (fact sheet RA,ST,BS,IS). Chain: real yields and credit spreads → cost of capital for a debt-laden, equity-funded micro-cap → dilution terms on future raises and the multiple paid for growth, amplified by beta **(inference)**.

What's priced: 10y real yield (TIPS) 2.91% (2026-10-06), up +48bp ~1m/+61bp ~3m [Certain] (https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=DFII10); HY credit spread 3.03%, up +35bp/+36bp over the same windows [Certain] (https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=BAMLH0A0HYM2, 2026-10-06). 2y yield (4.79%) sits ~91bp above fed funds (3.88%) [Certain] (https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=DGS2, https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=DFF, 2026-10-06), a proxy suggesting the market isn't pricing imminent cuts **(inference)**. VIX is low (15.01, near a 3m low) [Certain] (https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=VIXCLS) — risk appetite hasn't caught up to tightening real yields/spreads yet **(inference)**.

Bull: real yields/spreads roll over, risk appetite stays calm → cheaper equity financing, less dilution, multiple holds or re-rates up. Bear: real yields/spreads keep rising and VIX catches up → costlier financing, harsher dilution, multiple compression amplified by beta 2.92. Tilt: moderate toward bear — the synchronized rise in real yields and HY spreads taxes the equity-funding model the bull case depends on, though much idiosyncratic risk is already reflected in the stock's -37% 12-month move.
