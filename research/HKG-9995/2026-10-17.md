# RemeGen Co., Ltd. (HKG:9995) — Core initiation — 2026-10-17

## 1. Business
RemeGen is a Chinese biopharma that discovers, manufactures and sells its own biologics in China: telitacicept (anti-BLyS/APRIL fusion protein) for autoimmune disease (lupus, myasthenia gravis, Sjögren's) and disitamab vedotin (a HER2 antibody-drug conjugate) in oncology (source: stockanalysis.com overview, https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/9995/). It supplements domestic product sales with out-licensing deals for ex-China rights to its pipeline, including a January-2026 collaboration with AbbVie on a solid-tumour asset [Likely] (source: https://www.reuters.com/business/healthcare-pharmaceuticals/abbvie-remegen-partner-experimental-solid-tumor-treatment-2026-01-12/).

## 2. Quality
The record is volatile, not durable: operating margin was -153.6%/-142.7%/-83.0% in FY2022-24 and net margin -129.4%/-139.5%/-85.5%, before swinging to +4.3%/+21.8% in FY2025 and a TTM operating/net margin of 61.2%/72.7% [Certain] (source: fact sheet income-statement rows). TTM ROIC 144.9% and ROE 109.1%, versus an 8.7%/7.8% FY2021 peak, look implausible as a run rate; the swing coincides with the January-2026 AbbVie licensing deal, so the TTM profit jump looks largely one-off licensing income, not recurring product economics [Likely] (source: Reuters URL above; fact sheet ratios page). The moat is patent-protected biologics approved in China, but RemeGen faces deeper-pocketed domestic rivals (Innovent, Akeso, BeiGene) with broader pipelines, and periodic national reimbursement (NRDL) negotiations that can compress list prices, unquantifiable here [Likely]. Four of five years of deep losses argue competitors have historically eroded, not ceded, returns here.

## 3. Valuation
Current multiples look dirt cheap: P/E 5.5x is 94% below its 5-year range (57.2x-112.5x) and EV/EBITDA 8.3x is 188% below range (106.1x-158.1x) [Certain] (source: fact sheet ratios/valuation table). That is misleading: the TTM earnings base behind both ratios is inflated by the same one-off licensing income flagged in section 2, so a P/E this far outside history signals a distorted "E", not unusual cheapness [Likely]. EV/Sales (5.4x, -37% vs a peer median 8.6x, n=4: HKG:1801, HKG:9926, HKG:6160, HKG:1877) is cleaner but still carries licensing-revenue recognition [Likely]. The fact sheet's base fair value of HKD 359.42 (+445% vs the HKD 65.90 close) should not be relied on: its best method (rNPV) is [data unavailable], so the blend is 100% EV/Revenue off the same distorted base — unreliable, not a reason to buy (source: fact-sheet fair-value table).

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Net margin (income statement, next published FY/TTM) falls back below 10%, confirming the FY2025/TTM swing to profit was one-off licensing income, not recurring product economics.
- Gross margin (income statement) breaks below its 5-year range (currently 64.8%-95.3%) to under 76%, consistent with NRDL price cuts or rising production costs on the lead biologics.
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) rises above 2.0x, showing the cash cushion built by upfront licensing payments has eroded without further deal flow.
- ROIC (ratios page), once FY2026 is reported, comes in below the FY2021 peak of 8.7%, confirming the TTM 144.9% reading was not a sustainable return on capital.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Shares outstanding rose 3.88% YoY — modest dilution, not heavy (source: fact sheet statistics page) [Certain]. Net debt/EBITDA is -0.94x (net cash), but FCF margin (54.4% TTM) and FCF conversion (74.9% TTM) are a reversal from deeply negative FCF margins in FY2021-24 (-24.7% to -260.7%), again consistent with one-off licensing cash inflows rather than a structural improvement in operating cash generation [Likely]. No dividend; capital is being reinvested in R&D and commercialisation of the two lead franchises.

## 6. Unusual items
Earnings quality: the FY2025/TTM profit and FCF turn appear largely attributable to non-recurring licensing/milestone income (AbbVie deal, source: Reuters URL above) rather than the underlying China biologics business, which was loss-making in FY2022-24 [Likely]. This makes both the cheap trailing multiples and the model fair value unreliable guides to intrinsic value, and the business is exposed to binary clinical/regulatory readouts and China drug-pricing policy that this fact sheet cannot quantify.
