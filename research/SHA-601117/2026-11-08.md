## 2. Quality
Mediocre-to-adequate, not a moat business [Likely]. EPC is project-based and competitive, and the customers are largely state-linked, so there is no pricing power evidence beyond a modest gross margin rise from 9.3% (FY2021) to 11.1% (TTM) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sha/601117/financials/ratios/). ROCE is steady at 8.9% and ROE 9.4%, but ROIC fell from 35.3% (FY2021) to 12.4% TTM [Certain]. Rolling-12-month ROIC is 12.4% versus 13.2% a year earlier, and 5.5pp below its 2-year high of 18.0%, so the decline is stabilising but not recovering [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sha/601117/financials/ratios/?p=trailing). Falling ROIC alongside flat ROCE suggests the net-cash working-capital float is shrinking: net debt/EBITDA moved from -6.46x to -2.19x [Likely]. Cash flow is lumpy: FCF margin was -0.6% in FY2025 and 3.2% TTM, 3.5pp below its 2-year high of 6.8% [Certain]. Altman Z is 1.39 and Piotroski F is 5 [Certain]. A durable competitive advantage is not evident [Likely].

## 3. Valuation
The price assumes steep decline [Certain]. P/E is 6.8x, below its 5y minimum of 7.1x (median 8.9x), and 18% below the peer median of 8.3x. EV/EBITDA is 3.2x versus a 5y median of 3.3x and a peer median of 11.9x. P/B is 0.6x, at its 5y minimum. FCF yield is 13.83% [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sha/601117/statistics/). The reverse DCF implies year-1 revenue shrinking by 24.6% [Certain]. The mechanical base fair value is 14.14 CNY versus a last close of 7.30, but it rests on a DCF that is highly sensitive to working-capital swings, and the bear case is 0.84 [Certain]. The EV/EBITDA leg alone gives 8.68 in the base case, a far more modest upside [Certain]. Cheapness is partly real, partly a state-owned, China-discount, low-return contractor penalty [Likely]. Price is 8.8% below its 200-day average, with 12-month relative strength of -15.9pp vs SPY [Certain].
