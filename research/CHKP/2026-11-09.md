# Check Point Software Technologies (CHKP) — Core initiation — 2026-11-09
## 1. Business
Sells network, cloud and AI security: firewall appliances and gateways plus recurring subscriptions (threat prevention, support, cloud and management software). Gross margin is about 87% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/chkp/financials/income-statement/). Regional revenue mix was not retrievable from the fetched IR page, so no foreign country can be named [data unavailable]. [Certain]

## 2. Quality
Highly cash-generative, asset-light franchise: TTM gross margin 87.2%, FCF margin 40.9%, FCF conversion 128.7% of net income (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/chkp/financials/cash-flow-statement/). Net cash on the site definition (-2.63x net debt/EBITDA); Altman Z 5.89. [Certain]
Direction is the concern. Operating margin has fallen from 41.9% (FY2021) to 28.3% TTM; TTM ROIC 29.9% vs 58.4% a year earlier; ROCE 13.7% vs 21.4% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/chkp/financials/ratios/?p=trailing). Operating margin erosion over five years looks structural (reinvestment, platform shift) rather than cyclical. [Likely] The ROIC halving is partly a capital-base effect, not only profit decline. [Guessing]
Moat: installed base and switching costs in enterprise firewalls; the Q3 transcript headline describes Q3 as a trough with subscription growth outpacing total revenue (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/chkp/transcripts/719828-q3-2026/). [Likely]

## 3. Valuation
P/E 17.3x is below its 5y minimum (18.8x; median 19.9x); EV/Sales 4.6x is below its range; EV/EBITDA 15.4x sits mid-range (43%); P/FCF 13.1x is at the bottom of its range; FCF yield 7.65% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/chkp/statistics/). Peer medians are distorted by high-growth, low-profit peers (PANW, FTNT, CYBR, CRWD), so the discount is not informative on its own. [Likely]
Mechanical fair value: base 165.93 vs close 144.17 (+15.1%); bear 146.60, bull 240.97. The reverse DCF implies only 1.6% year-1 revenue growth fading to terminal. The price therefore assumes stagnation; the key question is whether falling margins stop. [Likely] Price is 26.2% below a year ago.

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Operating margin (TTM, income-statement trailing page) falls below 25%.
- FCF margin (TTM, cash-flow trailing page) falls below 36%.
- Revenue growth (income-statement page) turns negative for two consecutive fiscal-year-over-year quarters after Q4 2026, i.e. the stated trough does not hold.
- Gross margin (statistics page) falls below 84%.
