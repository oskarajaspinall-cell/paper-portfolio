# Mizrahi Tefahot Bank (TLV:MZTF) — Core initiation — 2026-11-09
## 1. Business
Israeli bank (Banks - Regional) earning net interest and fee income on mortgage, corporate and retail lending, funded by deposits. Reports in ILS, quoted in agorot (ILA) on Tel Aviv (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tlv/MZTF/). Revenue is overwhelmingly Israeli; no foreign country was shown at ~10%+ in the fact sheet [Likely]. Segment mix [data unavailable] (no primary source fetched).

## 2. Quality
- ROE has stayed high: 15.9% (FY2021), 19.8% (FY2022), 19.0% (FY2023), 18.5% (FY2024), 16.9% (FY2025), 16.0% TTM, so the 5-year trend is nominally rising but it has eased from the FY2022 peak [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tlv/MZTF/financials/ratios/).
- Net margin rose from 30.1% to 39.2% in FY2025 and 38.7% TTM [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tlv/MZTF/financials/income-statement/).
- Management cites ROE of 16% in Q2 2026 despite a special tax, client relief measures and lower rates [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tlv/MZTF/transcripts/787722-q2-2026/). The special tax and relief are a political/war-related cost; they signal that the profit pool is shared with the state [Guessing].
- Moat: scale in Israeli mortgages and a concentrated domestic banking oligopoly [Likely]; not independently verified. Peak-cycle margins from high rates are the main durability question [Likely].
- Piotroski F is 4; ROIC and leverage are not meaningful for a bank [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tlv/MZTF/statistics/).

## 3. Valuation
P/E is 10.5x vs a 5-year range of 6.5x to 10.3x (median 7.5x): above the top of its own history [Certain]. P/B is 1.5x vs 1.2x to 1.6x (median 1.3x), 90% of the range [Certain]. Versus peers (LUMI, POLI, DSCT, FIBI) P/E is +2% and P/B +6% against the median: in line to slightly dearer [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tlv/MZTF/financials/ratios/). The price therefore assumes ROE stays near 16% with no margin erosion as rates fall.
The fact sheet's fair value is flagged unreliable (base 135.15 vs a price of 22,450 ILA: a unit mismatch between ILS and agorot likely, and ILS FX was missing for two methods); I disregard it [Likely]. P/FCF of 4.3x and FCF yield of 23.32% are not meaningful for a bank, where deposit and loan flows drive cash flow [Likely]. Short of a discount to history or peers, valuation offers no margin of safety.

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROE (ratios page) falls below 13% on a TTM basis.
- Net margin (income statement page) falls below 33%.
- Shares outstanding change YoY (statistics page) exceeds 3%, indicating dilution to fund growth or capital.
- Piotroski F (statistics page) falls to 2 or lower.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Management says it distributes a fixed share of each quarter's profit as dividends and targets a three-year average cost-income ratio below a stated ceiling [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tlv/MZTF/transcripts/787725-q4-2025/). Share count change is small at 0.26% YoY [Certain]. FCF/NI of 245.7% TTM, and the drop in FCF margin from 134.1% to 95.1% over 12 months, reflect balance-sheet working-capital swings and say little about earnings quality in a bank [Likely].

## 6. Unusual items
- Special tax and client relief measures have cut reported returns (Q1 ROE 14.1% in management's account) [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tlv/MZTF/transcripts/787731-q1-2026/). Regulatory/political reach is a recurring risk.
- Israel geopolitical and shekel exposure: macro overlay should check country risk [Likely].
- Direction change flagged: FCF margin falling over 12 months; judged not decision-relevant for a bank, but evaluator must address it.
- Beta 0.14; next earnings Nov 17, 2026 [Certain].
