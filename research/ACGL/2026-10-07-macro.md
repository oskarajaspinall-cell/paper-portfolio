## Assessment

US 10y real yield (TIPS) is 2.95% on 2026-10-05, up 71bp over three months [Certain] (source: https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=DFII10, 2026-10-05); the 10y nominal yield is 5.31%, up 83bp over three months, while 10y breakevens rose only 11bp to 2.36% [Certain] (same source series DGS10/T10YIE, 2026-10-05/06) — the move is dominated by real yields/term premium, not an inflation re-rating **(inference)**. Market pricing proxy: the 2y Treasury yield (4.84%, 2026-10-05) sits above effective fed funds (3.88%, 2026-10-05), implying markets expect the policy rate to stay well above, or move back toward, current levels rather than cut further **(inference, proxy only)**. This multi-month rate climb is largely already in the price, given ACGL's low beta (0.26) [Certain] (factsheet) and because the stock's 25% P/E discount to peers reflects softening Insurance-segment underwriting (combined ratio 98.5% Q2-26 vs 93.4% a year ago), not a macro gap [Likely]. Bull: continued/higher real yields keep lifting reinvestment income on the float, cushioning underwriting softness. Bear: a rate-cut resumption or credit-spread widening (HY spread +44bp over 1m to 3.12%) curbs NII growth just as underwriting margin is already compressing.
