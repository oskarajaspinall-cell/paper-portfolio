# Arch Capital Group (ACGL) — Core initiation — 2026-11-16
## 1. Business
Bermuda-based specialty insurer with three segments: insurance (casualty, specialty, property lines), reinsurance (property catastrophe, casualty) and mortgage insurance (mainly U.S. primary). It earns underwriting income plus investment income on a short-duration, high-grade bond portfolio [Certain] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/947484/000094748426000152/acgl-20260930.htm). No single foreign country's revenue share was verified in the sources read; not checked for the macro overlay [Guessing].

## 2. Quality
Returns are high for an insurer: ROE 18.7% TTM and ROIC 15.9% TTM, versus 11.2% and 8.9% in FY2022 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/acgl/financials/ratios/) [Certain]. Net debt/EBITDA is 0.82x and FCF/NI is 128.6% [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/acgl/financials/cash-flow-statement/). Moat is underwriting selection and diversification across segments, so one soft line does not sink returns [Likely].

Direction: ROIC is flat on a year earlier but 4.8pp below its 2024 peak of 20.7% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/acgl/financials/ratios/?p=trailing) [Certain]. Management says reinsurance net premiums written fell about 9% in Q3 on lower rates and abundant capacity, and expects continued property-catastrophe pricing pressure. Mortgage and casualty-focused insurance partly offset this [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/947484/000094748426000152/acgl-20260930.htm). The cycle is softening, so ROIC more likely drifts lower than higher [Likely]. Q3 benefited from light catastrophe activity (same filing) [Certain].

## 3. Valuation
P/E is 8.9x against a 5y median of 8.1x, 27% up its range, and 15% below the peer median of 10.6x. EV/EBITDA is 7.4x, at the 7th percentile of its range (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/acgl/statistics/) [Certain]. P/B of 1.6x is 18% above peers, which fits a higher ROE [Likely]. FCF yield is 15.85%, but P/FCF is above its 5y max; insurance float flows make FCF a poor guide [Likely].

The price assumes ROE stays high but does not rise. Mechanical base fair value is 150.47 against a 109.61 close, with bear 85.47 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/acgl/) [Certain]. The base leans on a P/B + ROE method that capitalises today's ROE, which is likely near cycle peak, so I discount that upside [Likely]. Shares are 6.92% fewer than a year ago, so buybacks add to per-share growth [Certain].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROIC (ratios page, trailing) falls below 12%.
- ROE (ratios page) falls below 14%.
- Net debt/EBITDA (fact sheet quality table) rises above 1.5x.
- Reinsurance net premiums written decline by more than 15% year on year for two consecutive quarters (10-Q, sec.gov).

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Buybacks are the main use of capital: the Q3 call summary cites a large repurchase total (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/acgl/transcripts/718509-q3-2026/), and share count is down 6.92% [Likely]. FCF/NI of 128.6% is above 100% [Certain]. Reinsurance premium shrinkage is a deliberate return of capital rather than chasing volume [Likely]. A shelf registration was filed on 2026-11-10 (source: https://www.tipranks.com/news/the-fly/arch-capital-files-automatic-mixed-securities-shelf-thefly-news); use is unknown [Guessing].
