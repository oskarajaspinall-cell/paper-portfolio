# Arch Capital Group (ACGL) — Core initiation — 2026-11-23
## 1. Business
Bermuda-based specialty insurer and reinsurer with three segments: insurance (U.S. casualty, E&S, London market), reinsurance (property catastrophe, casualty) and mortgage insurance. Earnings come from underwriting income plus investment income on a short-duration, A1-rated fixed income book. Q3 underwriting income: reinsurance $393 million, mortgage $201 million, insurance $108 million [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/947484/000094748426000152/acgl-20260930.htm). Country revenue split not retrieved [data unavailable]. [Certain]

## 2. Quality
Returns are strong: ROIC 16.5% TTM and ROE 18.7% TTM, with ROIC rising over five years from 12.3% (FY2021) to 17.9% (FY2025) (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/acgl/financials/ratios/). [Certain] Net debt/EBITDA is 0.60x on the site definition, so leverage is low. [Certain]

Direction change: TTM ROIC is 6.9pp below its 2-year high of 23.3% (2024-09-30) and 2.2pp lower over three years (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/acgl/financials/ratios/?p=trailing). The peak was a benign-catastrophe, hard-market year; the current level is the more normal run-rate. [Likely]

Moat is underwriting discipline and diversification, not structural. Reinsurance net premiums written fell 9.3% in Q3 on abundant capacity and lower rates [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/947484/000094748426000152/acgl-20260930.htm), and management expects continued pricing pressure in property catastrophe. [Likely] Mortgage is a steady diversifier. Light catastrophe activity flattered Q3 reinsurance, so it overstates the run-rate. [Likely]

## 3. Valuation
At 108.82 USD the stock trades at 8.9x P/E (27th percentile of its 5y range, 6.3x–16.1x) and 7.4x EV/EBITDA (7th percentile), a 14% and 37% discount to the peer median (EG, RNR, WRB, CB, TRV) (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/acgl/statistics/). P/B is 1.6x versus a 1.5x median, 18% below peers. [Certain] FCF yield is 15.96%, but insurance float cash flow is not owner earnings. [Likely] The model base fair value is 151.55 (+39.3%), bear 86.01 (-21.0%); the P/B + ROE leg drives it and assumes a sustainable ROE above today's level, so treat it with caution. [Guessing] The price assumes earnings fade as reinsurance pricing softens: cheap on earnings, fair on book. The stock is above its 200-day (+11.2%) and its 12-month change is +19.4% against SPY +4.1%, so the trend is healthy. [Certain]

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROIC (ratios page, TTM) falls below 12%.
- ROE (ratios page, TTM) falls below 14%.
- Net debt/EBITDA (site definition, ratios page) rises above 1.5x.
- Insurance or reinsurance underwriting income turns negative in two consecutive 10-Qs (sec.gov).

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Buybacks are the main use of capital: shares outstanding fell 6.92% YoY (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/acgl/statistics/). [Likely] FCF conversion is 128.6% of net income TTM, down from 156.9% in FY2021 (falling 17.6pp). [Certain] An automatic mixed securities shelf was filed 2026-11-10; routine, but watch for issuance against the buyback (https://www.tipranks.com/news/the-fly/arch-capital-files-automatic-mixed-securities-shelf-thefly-news).
