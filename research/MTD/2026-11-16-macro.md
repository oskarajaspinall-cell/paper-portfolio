# Macro overlay: Mettler-Toledo International (MTD) — 2026-11-16
## Assessment
**Chain:** real yields -> discount rate -> multiple. MTD's price (P/E 37.7x, FCF yield 2.52%, fact sheet) rests on multiple persistence, not cash flows (note section 3) [Likely]. Secondary: macro barely changes earnings but can move the multiple.

**Facts (FRED, snapshot 2026-11-16):** 10y TIPS 2.86% (2026-11-12), +0.47pp in ~3m, -0.02pp in ~1m. 2y 4.86% vs fed funds 3.88%. 10y breakeven 2.24%, flat in 3m. HY spread 3.2%, +0.49pp in 3m. Source: https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=DFII10 and the other series in reports/macro/2026-11-16.md.

**Pricing (proxy):** a 2y yield 0.98pp above fed funds implies hikes are priced **(inference)**. The stock rose +69.6% over 6m (note) while real yields rose, so the market has looked through higher discount rates [Likely]. A further rise in real yields or wider spreads hurts; falling yields help.

**Country risk (China):** about 17.6% of sales (10-Q, company-reported, per note). The yuan is 6.676 per USD (2026-11-06), 0.07 stronger over ~3m: a small tailwind [Certain]. The Chinese policy rate was not checked. Section 301 tariffs (12.5% on Switzerland and China since 2026-07-23) are company-reported; nothing else is verified.

**Bull:** real yields fall below ~2.5% and the multiple holds. **Bear:** real yields above 3.25% or a risk-off spread widening compress the multiple several turns, growth unchanged.

**Tilt:** toward bear, small. The multiple is the exposed part.
