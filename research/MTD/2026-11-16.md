# Mettler-Toledo International (MTD) — Core initiation — 2026-11-16
## 1. Business
Precision weighing, analytical and inspection instruments (lab, industrial, food retail), sold with a recurring service and consumables tail. Service net sales were $817.2m of $3,068.7m for nine months [company-reported] [Certain] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1037646/000103764626000074/mtd-20260930.htm). Chinese Operations segment sales were $541.5m, about 17.6% of the total [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1037646/000103764626000074/mtd-20260930.htm), a segment measure not customer destination [Likely]. China therefore needs a country-risk check.

## 2. Quality
Excellent returns: TTM ROIC 46.6% and ROCE 47.8%, gross margin 59.2%, operating margin 27.8%, FCF margin 20.7%, net debt/EBITDA 1.58x (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/mtd/financials/ratios/) [Certain]. Moat: installed base, service density and regulated-workflow switching costs [Likely].

Direction: ROIC is down over five fiscal years but up over the last 12 months (39.2% to 46.6%), so the long decline is not the current trend [Certain]. ROCE is 7.0pp and FCF margin 2.9pp below 2-year highs; operating margin is 0.6pp lower year on year (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/mtd/financials/ratios/?p=trailing) [Certain]. Margins are drifting, not collapsing [Likely].

Q3 sales grew 6%, with China up 13% locally; guidance was raised (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/mtd/transcripts/721311-q3-2026/) [Likely]. Nine-month results include a one-time tariff-refund gain (section 6).

## 3. Valuation
The price is demanding. P/E 37.7x sits at 37% of its 5y range (29.9x to 50.7x) and in line with peers (37.5x). EV/EBITDA 29.2x is 19% above peers; EV/Sales 8.7x is 22% above; P/FCF 39.7x; FCF yield 2.52% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/mtd/statistics/) [Certain].

The mechanical fair value (base 810.08 vs close 1,738.91) is flagged unreliable: DCF and EV/Revenue disagree 3.1x. The reverse DCF implies 32.2% year-1 revenue growth, which no 5-6% grower delivers [Certain]. The premium rests on franchise quality and multiple persistence, not cash flows [Likely]. Trend is strong: +28.6% above the 200-day, 6-month momentum +69.6% against SPY +1.6% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/mtd/history/) [Certain]. Entering after a run-up with RSI 64.1 leaves little margin for a growth miss [Likely].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROIC (ratios page, TTM) falls below 38%.
- Operating margin (income statement, TTM) falls below 26%.
- FCF conversion (FCF/NI) falls below 85% for TTM.
- Chinese Operations segment sales (10-Q) decline year on year for two consecutive quarters [company-reported].

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Buybacks are the main use of cash: $638.7m for 490,368 shares at an average $1,302.57 over nine months [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1037646/000103764626000074/mtd-20260930.htm) [Certain]. Shares fell 2.84% year on year (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/mtd/statistics/) [Certain]. FCF conversion is 93.2% TTM, down from 104.2% in FY2021 [Certain]. Leverage is moderate.

## 6. Unusual items
- One-time tariff gain: $52m IEEPA refunds reduced cost of sales, partly offset by $28m customer refunds cutting sales [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1037646/000103764626000074/mtd-20260930.htm); underlying margins are lower than reported [Likely].
- New Section 301 tariffs from July 23, 2026 (12.5% on Switzerland and China) [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1037646/000103764626000074/mtd-20260930.htm); further tariffs and Middle East war risk to demand are flagged there [Likely]. No risk-factor text changed [Certain].
- Short interest 3.12% of float, down 13.0% month on month [Certain].
