## 2. Quality
Mixed: margins and cash are improving, but returns on capital are only moderate and net income is falling. [Certain]
- Operating margin is 14.9% TTM versus 9.0% in FY2021, and gross margin is 42.3% versus 39.2% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/0291/financials/income-statement/).
- FCF margin is 15.3% TTM, up 2.4pp year on year. FCF conversion is 213.2% of net income (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/0291/financials/cash-flow-statement/).
- ROIC is 11.9% TTM versus 16.0% in FY2022, and flat over the last year. ROE has fallen to 6.3% from 20.1% in FY2021, and net margin to 7.2% from 13.7% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/0291/financials/ratios/). The gap between operating margin and net margin suggests below-the-line items. I did not research the cause [Guessing].
- Net cash is 1.55x EBITDA. Altman Z is 2.50 and Piotroski F is 5 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/0291/statistics/).
A durable moat (scale, distribution, brand) is plausible but not evidenced here [Guessing].

## 3. Valuation
The price assumes shrinking revenue. The reverse DCF implies -7.0% revenue growth in year 1 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/0291/). [Likely]
- P/E is 20.9x, 23% up its 5y range (min 16.2x, median 22.7x), and in line with peers at 20.6x.
- EV/EBITDA is 6.0x versus a 5y minimum of 9.1x, and 7% below peers.
- P/FCF is 9.8x versus a 5y minimum of 14.3x. FCF yield is 10.20%.
- The mechanical base fair value is 34.15 HKD against a 20.46 HKD close. The bear case is 16.74 HKD. It is an inform-only estimate, with a 3.5% country risk premium (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/0291/).
The price is 28.5% below a year ago and 11.9% below the 200-day average. Earnings are depressed against cash flow, so the P/E overstates the cheapness [Likely].
