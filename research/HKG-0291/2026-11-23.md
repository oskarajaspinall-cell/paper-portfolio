## 2. Quality
- Profitability is mixed. Gross margin rose to 42.3% TTM and operating margin to 14.9% from 9.0% in FY2021, which suggests pricing power or mix [Likely]. (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/0291/financials/income-statement/)
- Returns are not rising. ROIC is 10.8% TTM against 12.4% in FY2021 and 12.6% a year earlier. ROE fell to 6.3% from 20.1%. (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/0291/financials/ratios/)
- Net margin (7.2%) fell while operating margin rose. Below-the-line items, probably impairments or lower other income, are eating earnings [Guessing]. Not investigated.
- Cash is strong: FCF margin 15.3% TTM, FCF/NI 213.2%, net cash (net debt/EBITDA -1.55x). Piotroski F is 5 and Altman Z 2.50. (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/0291/statistics/)
- A moat from scale and distribution is plausible but unproven. ROIC near 10% is only moderate [Likely].

## 3. Valuation
- At 20.78 HKD the shares trade at 21.2x P/E, near the 5y median of 22.7x and 27% above peers at 16.7x. The P/E is inflated by depressed net income.
- On cash and enterprise value the stock is cheap. EV/EBITDA is 6.1x against a 5y minimum of 9.1x, P/FCF is 10.0x against a 5y minimum of 14.3x, and FCF yield is 10.04%. Peer EV/EBITDA is 6.5x and peer P/FCF is 20.5x. [Certain]
- The mechanical fair value is base 34.20 HKD, +64.6%, with a bear case of 16.74 (-19.5%). The reverse DCF implies -6.6% year-1 revenue growth, so the price assumes decline [Likely]. The model leans on a falling-ROIC history and a 3.5% country risk premium. (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/0291/statistics/)
- Trend is poor: -10.1% vs the 200-day, 12-month change -25.6%, 50-day 15.8% below the 200-day [Certain].
