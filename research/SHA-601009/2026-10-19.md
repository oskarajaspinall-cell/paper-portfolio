## 2. Quality
ROE has fallen every year shown: 13.8% (FY21) → 10.7% TTM, -2.9pp, consistent with sector-wide net interest margin compression in China [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sha/601009/financials/ratios/). Net margin has risen (+2.4pp to 53.5% TTM) [Certain], but FCF margin/conversion figures (456.7%/854.3% TTM) are not a quality signal for a bank: lenders' cash-flow statements are dominated by deposit/loan financing flows, not operating cash generation, so these should be disregarded [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sha/601009/financials/cash-flow-statement/). Piotroski F-Score is 4/9 — middling [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sha/601009/statistics/). The moat, such as it is, rests on a regional licence and entrenched Jiangsu local-government/SOE relationships, one of China's wealthier provinces; this is not clearly durable against other city/joint-stock banks competing for the same deposits and loans, and the falling ROE suggests competitive/regulatory pressure is winning [Likely]. No leverage/credit ratios are available from stockanalysis.com for a bank balance sheet, so credit quality can't be independently verified here [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sha/601009/financials/balance-sheet/).
