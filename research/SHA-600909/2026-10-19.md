# Huaan Securities Co., Ltd. (SHA:600909) — Core initiation — 2026-10-19

## 1. Business
Huaan Securities is a Chinese Capital Markets intermediary [Certain]: brokerage commissions, margin financing, investment banking/underwriting, asset management and proprietary trading/investment income are its revenue lines (source: fact sheet industry classification). Revenue tracks China's trading volumes, IPO/bond issuance and market sentiment rather than a recurring subscription base [Likely].

## 2. Quality
Returns on invested capital have been persistently low: ROIC ran 3.0% (FY2021) to 2.2% (FY2022-24), recovering only to 2.9% (FY2025) and 4.4% TTM [Certain] — likely below the firm's own cost of capital, a sign it doesn't earn attractive returns on retained capital [Likely]. ROE improved from 8.0% (FY2021) to 12.5% TTM [Certain], but FCF/NI swung from -415.9% (FY2023) to +297.8% (FY2025) [Certain], reflecting trading-book and margin-lending cash swings rather than stable conversion of earnings to cash [Likely]. Chinese brokerage is a licensed but fragmented, commission-competitive industry with low switching costs and no visible brand or scale moat for a mid-sized player like this one [Likely]; returns move with market turnover and capital-markets activity, not pricing power. Net margin of 47.8% TTM [Certain] reflects fee/trading-income accounting, not durable pricing power [Likely].

## 3. Valuation
Current P/E of 11.2x sits below its own 5-year range (15.0x-19.2x) [Certain], but that apparent cheapness disappears against peers: P/E is 17% above the peer median of 9.6x (CITIC, Everbright, Guotai Junan, Huatai) [Certain], P/FCF is 71% above the peer median of 2.8x, and P/B is 79% above the peer median of 0.7x, with P/B at 97% of its own 5-year range (near the top) [Certain]. The market prices this broker richer than larger, more diversified peers despite no quality edge shown in section 2 [Likely]. Base-case fair value of CNY7.83 is only 8.8% above the CNY7.19 last close [Certain], while the bear case is -32.6% [Certain] — asymmetric to the downside. The price assumes the recent ROE recovery persists and the peer-relative premium is justified; neither is well evidenced [Likely].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROE (ratios page) falls back below 8% (the FY2021 level), showing the recent improvement was cyclical, not structural.
- Net margin (income statement) falls below 35% (the FY2023 trough) on fee competition or trading losses.
- FCF conversion (FCF/NI, cash-flow + income statement) stays negative for two consecutive fiscal years, confirming earnings aren't backed by cash.
- Shares outstanding (statistics page) rise, reversing the current -2.0% YoY trend, signalling a capital raise to cover balance-sheet strain.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Shares outstanding fell 2.01% YoY [Certain], consistent with buybacks/no dilution rather than capital raising. FCF conversion is extremely volatile (FCF/NI from -415.9% FY2023 to +297.8% FY2025, 235.8% TTM) [Certain], typical of a broker-dealer whose cash flow is driven by trading-book and margin-lending balances rather than a clean, reinvestable FCF stream [Likely]. Reported FCF yield is 20.69% [Certain], but given that volatility it should be read as a one-period snapshot, not a durable yield [Likely]. Institutional ownership is only 13.5% [Certain], suggesting limited institutional validation of how capital here is being allocated.
