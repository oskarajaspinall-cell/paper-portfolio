# Torex Gold Resources (TSX:TXR) — Core initiation — 2026-11-07
## 1. Business
Gold producer; the Morelos complex in Mexico is the operating asset, with the Los Reyes development project and the San Miguel corridor exploration as growth options [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tsx/TXR/). Revenue is gold-price times ounces sold, so margins are leveraged to the gold price. Financials are reported in USD, the shares quote in CAD. The company is renaming itself Torex Resources, still gold-focused (source: https://www.newsfilecorp.com/release/321439/Torex-Gold-Changes-Name-to-Torex-Resources) [Likely].

## 2. Quality
Returns are currently strong: TTM ROIC 25.9%, ROCE 33.7%, operating margin 47.7%, gross margin 64.6% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tsx/TXR/financials/ratios/) [Certain]. Balance sheet is clean: net debt/EBITDA -0.11x, Altman Z 6.76, Piotroski 7 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tsx/TXR/statistics/) [Certain].

But durability is weak. A miner has no pricing power; it is a price-taker on gold. ROIC was 9.0% in FY2024 and operating margin 24.6% in FY2023, so current margins largely reflect the commodity price, not a moat [Likely]. The only durable edge is low cost per ounce at Morelos, which this fact sheet cannot verify (no AISC or reserve data) [data unavailable]. Mexico concentration is a single-jurisdiction risk [Likely].

## 3. Valuation
P/E 7.8x (5y range 4.7x-12.6x, 40th percentile) and EV/EBITDA 4.0x (1.3x-6.7x, 50th) are mid-range and in line with peers at 7.5x and 4.0x. P/FCF 7.9x is at the bottom of its range and half the peer median of 16.5x; FCF yield 12.60% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tsx/TXR/statistics/) [Certain]. P/B 1.8x is near its range top (96th percentile).

The price assumes today's gold-driven earnings persist. Mechanical fair value is base 16.23 CAD versus a 75.91 CAD close; even the bull case, 55.96, is below the price. NAV was [data unavailable], so this is EV/EBITDA on own history only, anchored on a 1.3x-1.8x historical median [Likely]. Cheap on FCF, not on asset value or history, and the history was low-gold-price earnings [Guessing].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Operating margin (income statement page) falls below 35% on a TTM basis.
- FCF margin (cash flow and income statement pages) falls below 15% TTM.
- Net debt / EBITDA (ratios page) rises above 1.0x.
- Shares outstanding change YoY (statistics page) exceeds 10%.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
TTM FCF conversion is 94.5% of net income, a recovery from -83.4% in FY2024 and 33.4% in FY2025, which came from the Los Reyes build spend [Likely]. Management cites capital returns alongside growth (source: https://www.marketbeat.com/instant-alerts/event-torex-gold-targets-growth-c350m-returns-and-morelos-expansion-2026-10-03/) [Likely]. Yet shares outstanding rose 7.85% YoY, so buybacks have not offset dilution [Certain]. Net cash is a strength; the capex cycle for Los Reyes is the swing factor.
