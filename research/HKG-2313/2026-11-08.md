# Shenzhou International (HKG:2313) — Core initiation — 2026-11-08
## 1. Business
Vertically integrated knitwear manufacturer (fabric, dyeing, cutting, sewing) making sportswear and casual apparel for global brands under volume-driven supply relationships [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/2313/). Revenue by customer and by country is not published on stockanalysis.com and no allowlisted company source was available: [data unavailable]. Customer concentration in a few sportswear brands and an export-heavy mix are probable but unverified [Guessing]. Reports in CNY, so the country-risk overlay should treat it as a China reporter.

## 2. Quality
Returns are good but have turned down. ROIC was 21.2% in FY2025 and is 18.3% TTM; operating margin was 20.4% in FY2024 and is 16.4% TTM (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/2313/financials/ratios/) [Certain]. Direction change: the 5-year trend is up, but the last 12 months fell (ROIC -2.9pp, operating margin -3.1pp, FCF margin 12.0% to 8.7%) (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/2313/financials/ratios/?p=trailing) [Certain]. FCF conversion is weak at 57.5% of net income TTM against 97.0% in FY2023. The balance sheet is strong: net cash, net debt/EBITDA -2.06x, Altman Z 4.69, Piotroski F 5 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/2313/statistics/) [Certain]. A moat from scale and integration is plausible, but the margin slide suggests customers or competitors are taking some of the returns [Likely]. The 12-month price change of -36.3% fits a cyclical or pricing squeeze rather than a broken franchise [Guessing].

## 3. Valuation
At 42.12 HKD the stock is below its 5y minimum on every multiple: P/E 12.0x (5y 14.0x–54.4x, median 24.1x), EV/EBITDA 6.5x (median 19.9x), P/B 1.5x (median 3.3x) (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/2313/financials/ratios/) [Certain]. Against peers it is only 8–10% cheaper on P/E and EV/EBITDA, and 53% dearer on P/FCF (21.0x vs 13.7x) (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/2313/statistics/) [Certain]. FCF yield is 4.77%. Model fair value is base 54.78 HKD (+30.1%), bear 35.41 (-15.9%), bull 79.18; the DCF leg alone gives a base of 27.73, below the price, and the EV/EBITDA leg (108.88) drives the blend, so the upside leans on mean reversion to historical multiples [Likely]. The reverse DCF implies 19.5% year-one revenue growth, which looks demanding for a falling-margin business [Likely]. The price is not obviously cheap on cash flow once the lower margins persist.
