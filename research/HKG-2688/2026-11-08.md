# ENN Energy Holdings (HKG:2688) — Core initiation — 2026-11-08
## 1. Business
Regulated-style Chinese city-gas distributor (stockanalysis.com: Utilities - Regulated Gas): earns connection fees from new users and a gas-sales margin (retail volumes to residential, commercial and industrial customers) plus ancillary energy services (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/2688/). Reports in CNY, quotes in HKD. Revenue is essentially all Mainland China [Likely]; the macro overlay should treat it as China exposure. Segment mix is [data unavailable] in the fact sheet.

## 2. Quality
Mediocre and slipping, not a moat compounder. [Certain] ROCE fell from 15.8% (FY2021) to 11.5% (FY2025), ROE from 23.0% to 13.0%, net margin from 8.3% to 5.3% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/2688/financials/ratios/). Piotroski F is 4 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/2688/statistics/). ROIC, gross and operating margin are [data unavailable].
Offsets: [Certain] the last 12 months improved: ROCE 12.6% vs 11.6% a year earlier, FCF margin 4.3% vs 2.4% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/2688/financials/ratios/?p=trailing). This addresses the Direction-change flag: the five-year FCF decline is real, but the recent rebound is a one-year move after three weak years; [Likely] partly working-capital timing. Franchise exclusivity in concession cities is a durable local advantage [Likely], but regulated pricing caps the spread, and returns have been eroding. Net debt/EBITDA (site definition) 0.94x is moderate.

## 3. Valuation
[Certain] Cheap on every multiple vs its own history: P/E 7.6x vs a 5y minimum of 8.6x; EV/EBITDA 5.0x vs 6.7x; P/FCF 9.7x vs 14.3x; FCF yield 10.29%. Versus peers (HKG:1193, HKG:1083, HKG:2380) P/E is 28% lower and EV/EBITDA 35% lower, though P/B 0.9x is above the peer median 0.4x (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/2688/statistics/).
Fair value is unreliable: base HKD 50.43 vs close 48.72 (+3.5%), bear 27.61 (-43.3%), bull 58.64 (+20.4%); DCF base 60.06 vs dividend discount 31.16. The reverse DCF implies -1.6% revenue growth in year 1 [Certain]. The price already assumes stagnation, but the base-case upside is thin and the bear case is deep. A 3.5% country premium is built in. [Likely] the discount reflects China risk, falling returns and the failed buyout.
