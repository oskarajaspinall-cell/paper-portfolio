# Dollar General Corporation (DG) — Core initiation — 2026-10-12

## 1. Business
Dollar General operates roughly 20,000 small-box discount stores concentrated in rural and small-town America, selling consumable staples (food, health, cleaning) alongside general merchandise at low price points [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/dg/). Revenue comes from store sales; margin is driven by private-label mix, supply-chain scale and a low-rent, low-cost rural footprint that limits big-box competition [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/dg/financials/income-statement/).

## 2. Quality
The rural small-box footprint is a real moat: store density in towns too small for Walmart or Target gives DG local share only Dollar Tree contests [Likely]. But returns have compressed sharply: ROIC fell from 12.9% (FY2022) to 8.1% TTM and ROCE from 15.8% to 10.0%; net margin fell from 7.0% to 3.9% TTM, operating margin from 9.4% to 5.7% TTM (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/dg/financials/ratios/, income statement) [Certain]. The latest quarter shows a genuine inflection: Q2 FY2026 net sales +5.2%, same-store sales +3.5%, operating profit +29.2% YoY, guidance raised for the full year [Certain] (source: https://investor.dollargeneral.com/news-releases). That is a bounce off a depressed FY2025 base (operating margin trough 4.8%), so one quarter does not yet prove durability [Likely]. Net debt/EBITDA is elevated at 3.96x TTM, having peaked at 5.64x in FY2025 (source: ratios page) [Certain]. Piotroski F 7/9 is respectable; Altman Z 2.59 sits in the grey zone, not the safe zone (source: statistics page) [Certain].

## 3. Valuation
DG trades at a discount on every multiple versus its four-peer group (DLTR, WMT, TGT, FIVE): P/E 16.5x (-6%), EV/EBITDA 11.8x (-13%), EV/Sales 1.0x (-29%), P/FCF 14.0x (-21%), P/B 3.0x (-46%) (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/dg/financials/ratios/ and peer statistics pages) [Certain]. Within its own 5-year range the P/E sits at the 36th percentile and EV/EBITDA at only the 12th percentile — cheap versus its own history, not just peers [Certain]. FCF yield is 7.16% (source: statistics page). Base fair value of $146.67 implies +15.3% upside to the $127.23 close; bull $196.36 (+54.3%); bear $27.23 (-78.6%), reflecting what elevated leverage could do to equity value if margins slip again (source: fact sheet fair-value model) [Likely]. The reverse DCF implies the market is pricing only 1.8% year-one revenue growth fading to terminal growth — modest expectations, consistent with a peer-discount rather than a re-rated growth story [Likely].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Operating margin (income statement / ratios page) falls back below the FY2025 trough of 4.8% for two consecutive quarters — the margin recovery stalling.
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) rises back above the FY2025 peak of 5.64x — leverage going the wrong way.
- ROIC (ratios page) falls below the FY2025 trough of 6.3% — returns resuming their multi-year decline.
- Same-store sales growth turns negative in a quarterly release (investor.dollargeneral.com) — the Q2 FY2026 inflection proving a one-off.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
FCF margin has recovered to 4.6% TTM and FCF conversion (FCF/net income) to 118% TTM, up sharply from a 17.5% trough in FY2023 — cash generation is improving faster than reported earnings [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/dg/financials/cash-flow-statement/, income statement). Shares outstanding are roughly flat (+0.49% YoY), consistent with leverage reduction taking priority over buybacks while net debt/EBITDA remains above its FY2022 level of 3.60x [Likely] (source: statistics page).

## 6. Unusual items
None material. Short interest is 3.04% of float, up 13% month-on-month but not elevated in absolute terms (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/dg/statistics/) [Likely]. General Counsel retirement announced Aug 28, 2026 is routine and not thesis-relevant [Certain] (source: https://investor.dollargeneral.com/news-releases).
