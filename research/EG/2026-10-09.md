# Everest Group, Ltd. (EG) — Core initiation — 2026-10-09

## 1. Business
Everest is a Bermuda-based global reinsurer and specialty insurer serving customers in 115+ countries [Likely] (source: https://www.everestglobal.com/our-business). Reinsurance (treaty property/casualty, facultative, structured solutions) is the larger segment; Insurance (accident & health, specialty, programs) is being reshaped — Everest completed the sale of its Canadian retail insurance unit (Oct 1) [Certain] (source: https://www.tipranks.com/news/the-fly/everest-group-completes-sale-of-canadian-retail-insurance-operations-thefly-news) and agreed to sell its Mexico insurance unit to Fairfax (Aug 5) [Certain] (source: https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260805729128/en/Everest-Announces-Agreement-to-Sell-Mexico-Insurance-Operations-to-Fairfax/). Revenue is underwriting premium plus investment income on float.

## 2. Quality
Reinsurance underwriting needs scale, diversified risk pools and reserving discipline — Everest cites a 50-year track record [Likely] (source: https://www.everestglobal.com/our-business). TTM ROIC is 11.0% and ROE 12.6%, both above cost of capital, but both have fallen over five years (ROIC -1.6pp, ROE -3.0pp) [Certain] (fact sheet: ratios page). Management is deliberately shrinking gross premium (-7% YoY in Q2) to defend underwriting margins rather than chase volume [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/eg/transcripts/657888-q2-2026/) — a quality signal — but Q2 also saw roughly $200m of North America casualty reserve strengthening for adverse loss emergence in older accident years [Likely] (same source), a recurring, industry-wide (social inflation) risk that can recur. Piotroski F-score is a middling 4/9 [Certain] (fact sheet: statistics page). Beta of 0.26 [Certain] (fact sheet) reflects reinsurance's low correlation to equities, not operating durability.

## 3. Valuation
At 373.10 USD, EG trades on 7.9x TTM P/E — the 11th percentile of its own 5-year range (6.2x-22.0x) and 15% below the peer median of 9.4x (RNR, RGA, ACGL, CB) [Certain] (fact sheet). P/B is 0.9x, near the bottom (2nd percentile) of its 5y range and 30% below the 1.3x peer median [Certain] (fact sheet) — the market prices EG below book despite a 12.6% TTM ROE, implying it expects further reserve charges or a structural step-down in returns. EV/Sales of 1.0x is 37% below peers [Certain] (fact sheet). The fact sheet's own-history fair value (inform-only) ranges from 250.43 (bear, -32.9%) to 468.98 (base, +25.7%) to 625.81 (bull, +67.7%) [Certain] (fact sheet) — asymmetric upside if ROE merely holds near its TTM level rather than reverting toward the depressed FY2022 trough (ROE 6.4%, ROIC 6.3%).

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROE (ratios page) falls back toward the FY2022 trough of ~6.4% for a full fiscal year, signalling reserve adequacy has broken down again.
- ROIC (ratios page) falls below its FY2022 trough of 6.3% for a full fiscal year.
- Gross premium (income statement) keeps shrinking double-digits YoY alongside margin pressure rather than stabilising — cycle management turning into a durable loss of franchise.
- Piotroski F-score (statistics page) falls below 3, signalling broad-based balance-sheet/earnings deterioration.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Everest is returning capital rather than chasing growth: shares outstanding fell 4.73% YoY via buybacks [Certain] (fact sheet), and the board declared a $2.00/share dividend on Aug 12 [Certain] (source: https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260812407317/en/Everest-Group-Announces-Dividend/). Management called buybacks the "top priority for capital allocation" on the Q2 call, with an 81% payout ratio that quarter [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/eg/transcripts/657888-q2-2026/). FCF margin/conversion are [data unavailable] on stockanalysis.com for an insurer (cash flow is investment-portfolio driven, not comparable to industrials); TTM ROE of 12.6% and the buyback pace are the cleanest capital-discipline signals the fact sheet provides.

## 6. Unusual items
Two 2026 divestitures (Canadian retail insurance, completed; Mexico insurance, agreed) read as portfolio simplification, not distress [Likely] (sources above). Short interest is low in absolute terms (5.33% of float) but rose 32.3% month-on-month [Certain] (fact sheet) — worth monitoring into the Oct 28 Q3 print. Institutional ownership is high at 98.0% [Certain] (fact sheet). A new Group Chief Claims Officer was appointed Sept 29, succeeding a retiring incumbent — routine succession, not a governance flag [Likely] (source: https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260929498554/en/Everest-Appoints-Nandini-Mani-SVP-Group-Chief-Claims-Officer/).
