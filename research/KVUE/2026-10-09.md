# Kenvue Inc. (KVUE) — Core initiation — 2026-10-09
## 1. Business
Kenvue (spun off from J&J in 2023) sells branded over-the-counter self-care, skin/beauty and essential-health consumer products (Tylenol, Listerine, Band-Aid, Neutrogena, among others) through retail and e-commerce channels worldwide [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/kvue/).

## 2. Quality
ROIC 12.3% TTM (up from 11.8% FY2021), ROCE 14.4% TTM, ROE 15.6% TTM and rising (RA) [Certain]. Gross margin is rising to 58.4% TTM (+2.1pp over 5y), but operating margin (19.6% TTM) and net margin (10.8% TTM) have both fallen over the same period (-2.5pp and -4.1pp) (IS) [Certain]. FCF margin/conversion are strong and improving (12.4%/115.4% TTM) (CF,IS) [Certain]. Branded OTC/self-care franchises historically carry pricing power and shelf-space advantages, but this is moot as a 12-month fundamentals thesis: Kenvue agreed on 2025-11-03 to be acquired by Kimberly-Clark for 0.14625 KMB shares plus $3.50 cash per Kenvue share, with closing conditional on antitrust clearances still being obtained [Certain] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1944048/000110465925105216/tm2529895d1_8k.htm). Net debt/EBITDA has risen sharply to 2.16-2.35x from net cash in FY2021-22 (RA,BS,IS) [Certain].

## 3. Valuation
Every multiple sits below both KVUE's own 5y range and the peer median: P/E 20.8x (-9% vs own 5y min, -23% vs peer median 26.9x), EV/EBITDA 11.9x (-22% vs own 5y min, -22% vs peers), EV/Sales 2.7x (-30% vs peers), P/FCF 17.8x (-9% vs peers) (RA,P:CL,P:KMB,P:CHD,P:PG) [Certain]. This is not a standalone-business discount: the fixed stock-plus-cash merger consideration ties KVUE's economics to Kimberly-Clark's own share price and to deal-completion risk and timing, not to Kenvue's fundamentals. The fact sheet's own-history base DCF fair value of $16.78 is only 5.4% below the $17.73 last close, i.e. standalone intrinsic value roughly matches the market price rather than offering a margin of safety — the real spread an investor is pricing is versus the merger consideration, which the fact sheet cannot compute (no KMB share price on this fact sheet) [Certain].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Gross margin (income statement page) falls below 56% (FY2021-23 range), signalling brand pricing power eroding.
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) rises above 2.5x, beyond the current 2.16-2.35x band.
- FCF margin (cash flow/income statement) falls below 8% (last breached in FY2024 at 8.6%).
- ROIC (ratios page) falls below 10%, below the 5y range (10.2%-12.3%).

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
FCF conversion is strong at 115.4% of net income TTM, up from a near-zero 1.9% in FY2021 (CF,IS) [Certain], comfortably covering debt service despite net debt/EBITDA rising to 2.16-2.35x from a net-cash position two years ago (RA,BS,IS) [Certain]. Capital-allocation discretion is now constrained by the pending Kimberly-Clark merger agreement, which typically restricts major independent capital decisions (large buybacks, M&A, dividend changes) pending close [Likely] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1944048/000110465925105216/tm2529895d1_8k.htm); the fact sheet shows no dividend-yield figure to assess directly [data unavailable].

## 6. Unusual items
Kenvue is a pending M&A target, not a standalone 12-month equity story. Under the 2025-11-03 merger agreement, Kimberly-Clark must close by 2026-11-02 or extend to 2027-05-03 if still pursuing regulatory approvals; a termination fee of $1.136bn is payable by either side in specified break scenarios [Certain] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1944048/000110465925105216/tm2529895d1_8k.htm). Antitrust clearance is not yet complete: Australia cleared the deal on 2026-09-01 conditional on divesting the Carefree/Stayfree brands, and Kimberly-Clark was still offering EU remedies as of 2026-09-23, per headlines on the fact sheet [Certain] (sources: https://www.reuters.com/legal/litigation/australia-clears-kimberly-clarks-acquisition-kenvue-requires-carefree-stayfree-2026-09-02/, https://www.reuters.com/legal/transactional/kimberly-clark-offers-remedies-bid-eu-approval-kenvue-deal-2026-09-23/). KVUE's next reported-earnings date (Nov 2, 2026) coincides with the merger's initial outside closing date [Certain] (ST,OV) — a standalone quarterly print may not recur. Separately, a 2026-09-09 Reuters-reported study raised a possible (unproven, per the article) link between prenatal Tylenol use and reproductive effects in daughters — a litigation/reputational tail risk the article itself does not establish as causal [Guessing] (source: https://www.reuters.com/business/healthcare-pharmaceuticals/tylenol-use-pregnancy-may-be-linked-with-uterus-ovary-changes-daughters-2026-09-09/). Given the pending-merger overlay, a core fundamentals thesis is the wrong framework for sizing this name; conviction here should rest on deal-completion odds and the KVUE/KMB-implied spread, not on Kenvue's standalone business quality.
