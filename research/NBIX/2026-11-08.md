# Neurocrine Biosciences (NBIX) — Core initiation — 2026-11-08
## 1. Business
Commercial neuroscience/endocrine drugmaker with no manufacturing of its own (third-party suppliers). Nine-month 2026 net product sales were $2,884.3m [company-reported], of which INGREZZA (tardive dyskinesia, Huntington's chorea) $2,174.8m, CRENESSITY (classic CAH) $547.6m and VYKAT XR (Prader-Willi hyperphagia, acquired May 2026) $161.0m (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/914475/000091447526000054/nbix-20260930.htm). Sales are U.S.-based; the ex-U.S. unit was sold in January 2026, so no foreign country reaches ~10% [Likely]. No geographic split was found [data unavailable].

## 2. Quality
- Returns are high and improving: TTM ROIC 20.2% and ROCE 21.2%, operating margin 26.6% (+6.0pp year on year), FCF margin 28.1% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/nbix/financials/ratios/?p=trailing) [Certain].
- Direction change: ROIC is 5.0pp below its 2-year high of 25.2%, but it is up 3.7pp over the last year. The dip was the FY2025 trough, now reversing as operating leverage returns [Likely].
- Gross margin has slipped from 69.8% (FY2021) to 64.7% TTM, partly from the acquired VYKAT XR and the CRENESSITY mix [Guessing].
- Moat: INGREZZA is a first-in-class, entrenched franchise and by far the largest product in the nine-month mix above [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/914475/000091447526000054/nbix-20260930.htm). CRENESSITY has no approved direct competitor in CAH, so durability is mostly regulatory and clinical, not structural [Likely].
- Concentration and price risk remain. Management expects 2027 growth to offset pricing pressure [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/nbix/transcripts/720213-q3-2026/).

## 3. Valuation
At 164.63 the stock trades on 24.4x P/E, below its 5y minimum of 29.5x (median 51.8x). EV/EBITDA is 15.3x (5y range 20.3x–41.4x) and P/FCF is 16.0x, with a FCF yield of 6.24% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/nbix/statistics/) [Certain]. Peers are mixed: P/E is well below the peer median of 75.8x, but EV/EBITDA is 32% above the peer median of 11.6x (INCY, JAZZ, BIIB, ALKS), and the P/E peer comparison is distorted by depressed peer earnings [Likely].

Base fair value is 173.37 (+5.3%), blending DCF 67% and P/E 33%. The bear case is 63.94 and the bull case is 365.44, a very wide range. The price implies 9.8% year-one revenue growth fading to terminal (source: fact sheet, https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/nbix/). The market already prices continued CRENESSITY and VYKAT XR growth with INGREZZA stable. The stock has risen 13.4% on 3.2x volume since the Q3 beat (2026-11-03) [Certain], which leaves little margin of safety against base value [Likely].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Gross margin (income statement page) falls below 58%.
- TTM ROIC (ratios page, trailing) falls below 15%.
- TTM FCF margin (cash flow page, trailing) falls below 20%.
- INGREZZA quarterly net sales (10-Q) fall year on year, or CRENESSITY quarterly net sales fall sequentially for two quarters.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
FCF conversion is 148% of net income TTM, down from 260% in FY2021 but still strong [Certain]. Management spent about $2.9bn of equity value on the all-cash Soleno acquisition in May 2026, funded partly by a revolver of which $600.0m was drawn and repaid by June [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/914475/000091447526000054/nbix-20260930.htm). Net debt/EBITDA is -0.27x, a net cash position that has narrowed from -1.51x in FY2025. Shares outstanding rose 1.98% year on year, so dilution persists [Certain]. Integration of Soleno is a new, unproven risk [Likely].

## 6. Unusual items
- New risk factor in the 10-Q: patient advocacy groups raised serious adverse event reports for VYKAT XR in August 2026, with no causal link established [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/914475/000091447526000054/nbix-20260930.htm). A label change could hit the acquired asset's value [Likely].
- Short interest is 6.45% of float, up 10.2% on the prior month. RSI is 69.4, i.e. extended [Certain].
- Q3 results: revenue and EPS beat, INGREZZA 2026 guidance raised (source: https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/neurocrine-biosciences-reports-third-quarter-2026-financial-results-302926995.html) [Likely].
