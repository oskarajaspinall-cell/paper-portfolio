# Macro overlay: Chipotle Mexican Grill, Inc. (CMG) — 2026-10-10

## Assessment
CMG is domestic, all-USD, lightly-levered (net debt/EBITDA 2.08x), so FX, the dollar and credit spreads are not material channels [Certain] (factsheet RA, ST). The live channel is rates: CMG's DCF fair value uses the 10y Treasury as risk-free rate (5.22%, giving bear/base/bull $17.62/$29.31/$34.09) [Certain] (factsheet Fair value). **Chain**: real yields -> DCF discount rate -> fair-value range/multiple, plus a weaker secondary channel (higher-for-longer rates -> discretionary spending -> traffic).

**Facts** (fred.stlouisfed.org, 2026-10-08 unless noted): 10y real yield (DFII10) 2.87%, +0.44pp 1m/+0.56pp 3m; 10y nominal (DGS10) 5.22%, +0.42pp/+0.68pp; 2y (DGS2) 4.75%, +0.36pp/+0.59pp; effective fed funds (DFF) 3.88%. **Market pricing**: 2y yield 0.87pp above fed funds implies the market is not pricing near-term cuts **(inference, proxy)**. The 5.22% risk-free input already in the factsheet DCF matches the latest FRED print, so today's level is already priced; the open question is direction from here.

**Bull**: yields ease (cooler core CPI, dovish Fed) -> discount rate falls -> fair-value range re-rates toward the $34 bull case [Likely]. **Bear**: yields extend the 3-month rise -> discount rate rises further -> fair-value range compresses toward the $17.62 bear case, compounding the existing company-specific (food-safety/traffic) de-rating [Likely]. **Tilt**: small, toward bear — the dominant driver is company-specific, but the 3-month rate trend is currently an adverse, incremental headwind to the valuation range **(inference)**.
