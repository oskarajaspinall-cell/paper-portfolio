# Chipotle Mexican Grill, Inc. (CMG) — Core initiation — 2026-10-10
## 1. Business
Company-owned fast-casual Mexican restaurants ("Food with Integrity" positioning), virtually all revenue from food and beverage sales at its own units [Certain] (source: factsheet OV). Opened 100 company-owned restaurants in Q2 2026, 80 with a Chipotlane; digital sales rose to 38.3% of food and beverage revenue from 35.5% a year earlier [Certain] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1058090/000105809026000066/cmg-20260630.htm).

## 2. Quality
Returns are high and have risen for five straight years: ROIC 14.8%→22.7% (TTM 21.0%), ROCE 14.3%→25.9% (TTM 25.1%), ROE 30.2%→47.4% (TTM 49.6%) [Certain] (source: factsheet RA). Operating margin expanded FY2021-FY2024 (10.9%→17.3%) but has since slipped to 15.3% TTM [Certain] (source: factsheet IS). Management attributes part of the recent softness to demand: Q2 2026 comp sales grew only 2.2% (check +1.2%, traffic +1.0%), and the 10-Q cites "low-single-digit headwinds...tied to...recent U.S. food safety concerns and geopolitical developments" [Certain] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1058090/000105809026000066/cmg-20260630.htm). The moat is throughput/digital execution (Chipotlane density, 38.3% digital mix) and brand trust built over years — the food-safety headwind is a direct test of that trust rather than a one-off. Balance sheet is conservative (Altman Z 6.53, net debt/EBITDA 2.08x TTM, beta 0.96) [Certain] (source: factsheet ST, RA), giving room to absorb a soft patch.

## 3. Valuation
Every multiple sits below its own 5-year range minimum: P/E 29.3x (min 31.9x), EV/EBITDA 19.7x (min 22.0x), EV/Sales 3.6x (min 4.4x), P/FCF 25.5x (min 33.8x) [Certain] (source: factsheet valuation table) — a multi-year low, after a -22.9% 12-month decline (SPY +15.7%) [Certain] (source: factsheet Price). Vs peers (WING, CAVA, SG, YUM): cheaper on P/E (-50%) and EV/Sales (-32%), roughly in line on EV/EBITDA (+3%) and P/FCF (-2%), far richer on P/B (+268%, reflecting higher ROE, not a premium) [Certain] (source: factsheet Valuation). Blended base fair value $29.31, 7.2% below the $31.59 last close, bear/bull $17.62/$34.09; reverse DCF implies 21.7% year-1 revenue growth fading to terminal [Likely] (source: factsheet Fair value). Net: de-rated to a multi-year low, but base case still implies the stock sits modestly above fair value, not a clear bargain.

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Comparable restaurant sales (10-Q/earnings release) turn negative for two consecutive quarters.
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) rises above 3.0x.
- Operating margin (income statement) falls below 14%.
- ROIC (ratios page) falls below 15%.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
FCF margin 12.6% TTM, FCF/NI conversion 110.5% TTM, no dividend [Certain] (source: factsheet Quality table). Shares outstanding fell 3.92% YoY, i.e. buybacks are the main return of capital [Certain] (source: factsheet ST). Net debt/EBITDA rose from 1.36x (FY2024) to 2.08x (TTM) — leverage is increasing off a low base, worth watching but not yet a red flag [Likely] (source: factsheet RA/IS).

## 6. Unusual items
Unconfirmed M&A speculation: the Financial Times reported Starbucks had explored a takeover of CMG; Starbucks's only public response was that it is "laser-focused on executing our...strategy," which is not a denial but also not a confirmation [Guessing] (source: https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/08/starbucks-chipotle-acquisition.html). This is not used as a valuation input and should not change position sizing on its own. Short interest is modest at 3.21% of float, up 5.2% month-on-month [Certain] (source: factsheet News & sentiment).
