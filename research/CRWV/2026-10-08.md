# CoreWeave, Inc. (CRWV) — Core initiation — 2026-10-08

## 1. Business
CoreWeave operates a hyperscale cloud built on Nvidia GPUs, renting AI compute capacity (training and inference) to hyperscalers, AI labs and enterprises under multi-year contracts. Growth comes from building new data-center capacity, funded mostly by debt, then filling it against a large pre-committed order book [Certain] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1769628/000176962826000366/crwv-20260630.htm).

## 2. Quality
No durable moat beyond early, large Nvidia GPU allocations and pre-built capacity; MSFT, AMZN and GOOGL are building their own AI capacity and silicon, and NBIS competes for the same GPUs [Likely]. Returns are poor despite scale: TTM ROCE -0.4%, ROE -43.6%, net margin -25.4% even with 67.4% gross margin — depreciation and interest on debt-funded capex erase gross profit [Certain] (fact sheet Quality table). Customer concentration is extreme: the two largest customers were 40% and 23% of H1 2026 revenue (63% combined) [Certain] (sec.gov 10-Q), leaving pricing power with the customer. Leverage is severe — net debt/EBITDA 12.16x TTM and total debt ~$35.1bn vs a $44.8bn market cap [Certain] (fact sheet; sec.gov 10-Q); Altman Z-score 0.29 sits in the distress zone [Certain] (fact sheet). The $103.7bn backlog gives multi-year revenue visibility [Certain] (sec.gov 10-Q), but this is a capital-intensive, commoditizing rental model, not a business with protectable excess returns.

## 3. Valuation
Current multiples sit at a premium to the profitable, far-less-levered hyperscaler peer set: EV/EBITDA 24.4x (+19% vs peer median 20.5x), EV/Sales 12.2x (+30% vs 9.3x), P/B 9.7x (+41% vs 6.9x) [Certain] (fact sheet Valuation table). P/E and P/FCF are unavailable since earnings and free cash flow are negative; FCF yield is -29.51% TTM [Certain] (fact sheet). Own 5-year range comparisons are unavailable given CoreWeave's short trading history [Certain]. The fact sheet's fair-value model could not compute a DCF (FCF negative/too short a history) or an EV/Revenue second method (fewer than two positive years), so there is no stockanalysis.com-derived valuation anchor [Certain] (fact sheet Fair value section). The market is pricing CoreWeave on AI-capacity growth and backlog conversion, not on current returns, leverage or cash generation; that premium needs the backlog to convert profitably and leverage to fall — neither is yet visible [Likely].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) fails to fall materially from 12.16x over the next two fiscal years.
- Revenue concentration (10-Q disclosure) stays at or above ~60% from the top two customers with no new large customer added.
- Gross margin (income statement) falls below 60% (from 67.4% TTM), signalling GPU-rental pricing pressure.
- FCF margin (cash flow/income statement) fails to improve from -179.9% TTM as committed capacity finishes building out.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Growth is funded almost entirely by debt: $16.7bn of net debt proceeds in H1 2026 took total debt to ~$35.1bn [Certain] (sec.gov 10-Q). Operating cash flow was positive ($3.66bn in H1 2026) [Certain] (sec.gov 10-Q), but TTM FCF margin is -179.9% as data-center capex far exceeds operating cash flow [Certain] (fact sheet). FCF conversion (FCF/NI) of 708.2% TTM is distorted by a deeply negative net-income base, not genuine cash strength [Certain] (fact sheet). Shares outstanding grew 75.59% YoY, including stock issued to a large customer under a commercial agreement, diluting holders [Certain] (fact sheet; sec.gov 10-Q).

## 6. Unusual items
Short interest is high at 16.73% of float, consistent with a market split on the leveraged growth story [Certain] (fact sheet, ST). Altman Z-score 0.29 and Piotroski F-score 2 both flag distress-zone balance-sheet and weak-fundamentals signals [Certain] (fact sheet, ST). Shares outstanding rose 75.59% YoY, a structural dilution risk [Certain] (fact sheet, ST).
