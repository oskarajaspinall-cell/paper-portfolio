# Macro overlay: CoreWeave, Inc. (CRWV) — 2026-10-08
## Assessment
CRWV is a primary macro dependency: it funds AI-capacity build-out almost entirely with debt (net debt/EBITDA 12.16x TTM, Altman Z 0.29 — distress zone [Certain], fact sheet) while FCF is deeply negative, so its cost/availability of capital decides whether the ~$103.7bn backlog converts without dilutive equity or refinancing stress.

Chain: Fed policy path → real yields and HY credit spreads → CRWV's marginal cost of debt and equity discount rate on an unprofitable, long-duration name → valuation multiple and refinancing risk.

Facts (FRED, 2026-10-06/07): 10y real yield (DFII10) 2.91%, +0.48pp/1m, +0.61pp/3m; fed funds (DFF) 3.88%, +0.25pp over both windows (a hike that has held); HY spread (BAMLH0A0HYM2) 3.03%, +0.35pp/1m; 10y breakeven (T10YIE) only +0.11pp/3m. Proxy: 2y yield (4.79%) sits ~0.9pp above fed funds, implying no near-term cuts priced **(inference)**. Flat breakevens against a jump in nominal/real yields point to a real-rate "higher-for-longer" repricing, not an inflation scare — hostile to expensive, levered duration names like CRWV **(inference, [Likely])**. HY-spread widening is credit stress CRWV is unusually exposed to; its debt's fixed/floating mix isn't disclosed on stockanalysis.com **[Guessing]**.

Bull: yields/spreads stabilize or reverse → cheaper refinancing, lower discount rate, multiple re-rates up. Bear: hikes/sticky CPI keep yields and spreads rising → interest expense erodes negative FCF further, multiple compresses, refinancing a $35bn, 12x-EBITDA load gets harder. Tilt: toward bear, moderate — amplifies a leverage/distress risk fundamentals already flag, though the 34% 12-month drawdown and 16.7% short interest show much is already priced **(inference)**.
