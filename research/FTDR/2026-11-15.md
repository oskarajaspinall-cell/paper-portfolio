# Frontdoor (FTDR) — Core initiation — 2026-11-15
## 1. Business
Frontdoor sells annual home-warranty plans (American Home Shield, HSA, OneGuard, Landmark, 2-10 HBW) covering repair or replacement of home systems and appliances, plus a growing HVAC-upgrade and non-warranty line. Renewals were 75 percent of nine-month revenue, real-estate-transaction sales seven percent, direct-to-consumer eight percent and non-warranty and other 11 percent [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1727263/000172726326000022/ftdr-20260930.htm). No foreign country is ~10%+ of revenue [Likely]; US business.

## 2. Quality
Good business with a recurring base [Likely]. Over the last 12 months ROIC is 38.5% (2-year high 60.1%), operating margin 20.3% and FCF margin 18.0%, with FCF conversion 137.9% of net income (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/ftdr/financials/ratios/). Direction: ROIC and ROCE are rising over the last year (ROIC +5.6pp, ROCE +2.3pp) after falling from the 2024 peak, so the peak-to-now decline is a normalisation from an unusually high 2024 base, not a fresh deterioration [Likely]. Gross margin rose from 48.9% to 55.0% over five years [Certain]. Management attributes revenue growth to price realisation on renewals; a durable moat is brand scale and claims-cost data, but the business is exposed to repair-cost inflation and real-estate volume, so the moat is moderate [Guessing]. Net debt/EBITDA 1.12x, Piotroski 6, Altman Z 3.93 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/ftdr/statistics/) [Certain].

## 3. Valuation
The price is demanding versus its own history: P/E 24.4x against a 5y median of 17.6x and a max of 24.2x, EV/EBITDA 13.5x against a 12.7x max, and roughly 31-32% above the peer median on both (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/ftdr/financials/ratios/) [Certain]. P/FCF 16.6x is at its own median and below peers, FCF yield 6.02% [Certain]. The mechanical fair value base is 57.55 USD (bull 76.90) against a 96.64 close; the reverse DCF implies 14.2% year-1 revenue growth, far above the recent 6 percent reported growth [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1727263/000172726326000022/ftdr-20260930.htm) [Certain]. The stock is +35.8% above its 200-day average, +88.5% over 12 months, RSI 67.7: it is working but priced for more than current growth [Likely]. Cash-flow multiples are the case for value; earnings multiples are the case against [Likely].

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Shares are down 5.51% year on year (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/ftdr/statistics/) [Certain]. The board approved a new repurchase authorisation of up to $1 billion on October 30, 2026 [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1727263/000172726326000022/ftdr-20260930.htm). Cash conversion is strong (FCF/NI 137.9%), so buybacks are funded from cash flow and low leverage [Likely]. The authorisation is large relative to a 6.5bn USD market cap; buying near highs would destroy value if the price falls back toward fair value [Guessing].
