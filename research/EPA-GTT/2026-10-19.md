# Gaztransport & Technigaz SA (EPA:GTT) — Core initiation — 2026-10-19
## 1. Business
GTT designs and licenses patented cryogenic membrane containment systems for LNG and other liquefied-gas carriers, floating storage units and onshore tanks [Certain]. Shipyards (mainly South Korean and Chinese) pay GTT a royalty/license fee per vessel built under its technology, plus engineering, training and digital-service fees [Likely]. The model is asset-light: revenue tracks the multi-year LNG carrier new-build order cycle rather than GTT's own capex, consistent with its near-zero net debt (source: fact sheet quality table) [Certain].
## 2. Quality
GTT earns extraordinary returns on a tiny asset base: TTM ROIC 144.8%, ROCE 79.4%, ROE 79.6%, all rising over five years (RA) [Certain]. Gross margin 96.8% and operating margin 70.1% TTM, also rising, reflect royalty/licensing economics with minimal incremental cost (IS) [Certain]. The balance sheet carries net cash (net debt/EBITDA -0.52x TTM) and FCF conversion of 102.9% of net income (CF,IS) [Certain]. Five years of >95% gross margin with no scaled rival emerging points to a durable patent/technology moat in cryogenic LNG containment, not a cyclical blip [Likely]. Stockanalysis.com's news feed shows record order intake and confirmed guidance across Q1 2026, Q2 2026 and FY2025 results, consistent with a multi-year order book rather than one-off demand (source: /quote/epa/GTT/transcripts/548715-q2-2026/, /quote/epa/GTT/transcripts/402945-q1-2026/, /quote/epa/GTT/transcripts/378373-q4-2025/) [Likely]. The main threat to the moat is a shipyard- or customer-backed rival containment technology winning commercial orders; nothing in the data shows this yet [Guessing].
## 3. Valuation
At 211.60 EUR, GTT trades on 17.7x TTM P/E (27th percentile of its own 13.7x-28.7x 5y range) and 14.0x EV/EBITDA (33rd percentile of 10.2x-21.8x) (RA) [Certain]. Both sit below the midpoint of GTT's own history despite ROIC, ROE and operating margin near multi-year highs, implying the market is not extrapolating current profitability or order intake indefinitely [Likely]. Peers (Samsung Heavy Industries, Kongsberg Gruppen, Wartsila - capital-intensive shipbuilders/engineers, imperfect comps for an asset-light licensor) show GTT cheaper on P/E (-36%) and EV/EBITDA (-17%) but far pricier on EV/Sales (+314%) and P/B (+86%), which mechanically reflects GTT's much higher margin/ROE structure rather than mispricing [Likely]. The fact sheet's own-history DCF/EV-EBITDA blend puts base fair value at 263.54 EUR (+24.5% vs last close), bear 147.43 (-30.3%), bull 408.83 (+93.2%) [Certain]. The reverse DCF implies the price embeds 6.2% year-one revenue growth, below GTT's recent order-intake momentum [Likely].
## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Gross margin (income statement page) falls below 90% (TTM: 96.8%) - royalty economics eroding.
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) exceeds +1.0x (TTM: -0.52x net cash) - cash generation or capital allocation deteriorating.
- Revenue (income statement page) declines YoY in two consecutive fiscal years - the order book running down faster than new orders replace it.
- ROIC (ratios page) falls below 60% (TTM: 144.8%; 5y low 169.9% in FY2021) - competitive or pricing pressure on licensing fees.
## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
FCF margin is 57.0% TTM (46.7%-62.4% over five years) with FCF conversion (FCF/NI) of 102.9% TTM, confirming reported earnings convert to cash (CF,IS) [Certain]. Net debt/EBITDA rose from -1.16x (FY2021) to -0.52x (TTM) - still net cash, but the cushion has narrowed even as margins rose, consistent with cash returned to shareholders or working-capital build against the order book; the fact sheet shows no dividend/buyback line, so the exact use is [data unavailable]. Shares outstanding rose only 0.08% YoY (ST), so dilution is not a factor [Certain].
## 6. Unusual items
