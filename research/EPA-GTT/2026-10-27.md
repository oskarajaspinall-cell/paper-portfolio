# Gaztransport & Technigaz (EPA:GTT) — Core initiation — 2026-10-27
## 1. Business
GTT licenses patented membrane containment systems for LNG carriers and storage; shipyards pay a royalty per vessel, plus engineering and digital-service fees [Certain]. Revenue is recognised over vessel construction, so it follows LNG carrier orders placed one to three years earlier, not current demand [Likely]. The model is asset-light and net cash (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/epa/GTT/financials/ratios/) [Certain].
## 2. Quality
Unchanged since 2026-10-19: TTM ROIC 144.8%, ROCE 79.4%, gross margin 96.8%, operating margin 70.1%, FCF conversion 102.9%, net debt/EBITDA -0.52x (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/epa/GTT/financials/ratios/) [Certain]. The moat (patented technology, no scaled rival visible) is intact; nothing in the Q3 material points to a rival system winning orders [Likely].
Why it fell: the Q3 2026 call showed nine-month revenue down year on year, as weak 2025 order intake means fewer carriers under construction. Management declined to guide Q4, reaffirmed 2026 guidance without raising it, and would not be "too bullish" on 2027. The CEO called order timing unpredictable, and spot charter rates are weak (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/epa/GTT/transcripts/655575-q3-2026/) [Likely]. Offsetting: year-to-date orders well above last year [Likely]. This looks like a timing dip in a lumpy cycle, not a broken business [Likely].
## 3. Valuation
At 181.10 EUR, P/E is 15.5x (12th percentile of its 13.7x-28.7x 5y range) and EV/EBITDA 11.9x (15th percentile), against peer medians of 26.2x and 16.3x. P/FCF 14.7x is below its 5y minimum (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/epa/GTT/financials/ratios/) [Certain]. The reverse DCF implies only 1.9% year-one revenue growth, well below the prior note's figure [Certain]. Base fair value is 267.15 EUR (+47.5%), bear 149.29 (-17.6%); the EV/EBITDA leg (141.15) is still below the price, and the DCF leg capitalises a 70.1% margin at a five-year high [Certain]. The multiple has de-rated toward the prior entry marker (P/E at its 5y low) but has not reached it [Likely]. The market is treating the revenue decline as the start of an earnings downturn [Guessing].
