# Macro overlay: K92 Mining Inc. (TSX:KNT) — 2026-10-17
## Assessment
K92 is a single-asset gold-copper-silver producer and pure price-taker [Certain] (factsheet §Business), so the dominant channel is **real yields & dollar → metal price → KNT revenue/margin → multiple** — the "miners and commodities" case the gate names explicitly.

Facts [Certain, FRED, 2026-10-15/16]: 10y real yield (DFII10) 2.78%, +0.16pp 1m / +0.43pp 3m; broad dollar index (DTWEXBGS) 121.6, +3.72 1m; 10y breakeven (T10YIE) 2.35%, roughly flat; 2y yield (4.77%) sits ~0.89pp above fed funds (3.88%), a proxy that the market still prices "higher for longer" rather than near-term cuts. Core CPI's move is lagging, not forward-looking.

**Inference** [Likely], not official data: rising real yields and dollar strength are classic gold-price headwinds (higher opportunity cost of bullion, costlier bullion for non-USD buyers). KNT has already re-rated hard (+35% 12m, +34% 3m, factsheet §Price); its EV/EBITDA and P/E sit near 5y lows in percentile terms only because earnings outran price, i.e. the market is pricing continued elevated metal prices, not a reversal. The recent acceleration in real yields/dollar is a potential negative surprise versus that priced path.

**Bull**: real yields fall / dollar weakens → metal-price tailwind → multiple re-rates toward bull fair value (CAD52.97). **Bear**: both keep rising → metal-price headwind → multiple compresses toward base/bear fair value (CAD14.57/8.54). **Tilt**: toward bear, moderate — the 1m move in both variables works against the elevated metal-price assumption in KNT's premium multiple, reinforcing the -47% base-case valuation gap from fundamentals alone.
