# Remitly Global (RELY) — Core initiation — 2026-11-17
## 1. Business
Digital cross-border remittance: fees and FX margin on transfers sent mainly from the US, Canada, UK and Europe to India, Mexico, the Philippines and others; adding cards and business payments [Certain]. Q3 2026 revenue by sender country: US 68.4%, Canada 7.9%, rest of world 23.7% `[company-reported]` (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1782170/000162828026070142/rely-20260930.htm). No foreign send country is ~10%. Receive shares undisclosed.

## 2. Quality
Turnaround now earning strong returns: TTM ROIC 45.5% vs -11.6% in FY2024, operating margin 13.0%, FCF margin 20.8%, gross margin 62.5%, all rising over the last year (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/rely/financials/ratios/?p=trailing) [Certain]. Net cash (net debt/EBITDA -2.45x), Altman Z 7.69 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/rely/statistics/). Moat is modest [Likely]: scale, the partner network (more than 5,700 corridors `[company-reported]`, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1782170/000162828026070142/rely-20260930.htm) and brand help, but price competition from Wise, Western Union and banks is persistent, and profit history is short (positive ROIC only since FY2025). Net margin of 19.1% sits well above operating margin of 13.0%, so part of TTM earnings is probably non-operating or tax [Guessing]; I rely on operating and FCF margins.

## 3. Valuation
P/E 12.4x vs peer median 11.0x (+12%); P/FCF 11.1x sits at 3% of its own 5y range (median 42.3x); EV/Sales 2.0x at 10% of range; FCF yield 8.98% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/rely/statistics/). EV/EBITDA 14.6x is 127% above peers (n=2). The price assumes the margin step-up is largely a peak and growth fades [Likely]. Mechanical base fair value 31.39 vs last close 21.93 is EV/Revenue only (DCF unavailable), so low reliability [Likely]; bear case 15.83 shows downside if margins revert. The 5y history is short and covers loss years, so "cheap vs own range" is flattering [Likely].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Operating margin (TTM, income statement page) falls below 8%.
- ROIC (TTM, ratios page) falls below 20%.
- FCF margin (TTM, cash flow page) falls below 12%.
- Regulatory action (e.g. AUSTRAC outcome, US remittance rules) that restricts a major corridor, reported in a filing.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
FCF conversion 108.5% of net income TTM, FCF margin 20.8%. Buybacks active: board raised the authorization from $200M to $500M on 2026-10-28 `[company-reported]` (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1782170/000162828026070142/rely-20260930.htm), yet shares are up 2.75% YoY (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/rely/statistics/), so repurchases mostly offset stock compensation [Likely]. No debt burden; cash-rich.
