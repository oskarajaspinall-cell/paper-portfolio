# Remitly Global (RELY) — Core initiation — 2026-11-23
## 1. Business
Digital cross-border remittance: it takes a fee and an FX spread on money sent from the US, Canada, UK and Europe to receivers worldwide, with India, Mexico and the Philippines the largest receive countries by volume (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1782170/000162828026070142/rely-20260930.htm) [Certain]. By sending country, the US is 68.4% of 9M revenue [company-reported] and Canada 8.7% [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1782170/000162828026070142/rely-20260930.htm). No foreign country is 10%+; the UK sits in "rest of world". Cards and business payments are new products [Likely].

## 2. Quality
Profitability has turned sharply: TTM ROIC 51.9% against -11.6% in FY2024, operating margin 13.0%, FCF margin 20.8%, all rising on the trailing table (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/rely/financials/ratios/?p=trailing). Gross margin has risen every year, from 48.3% to 62.5% TTM [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/rely/financials/income-statement/). Net cash (net debt/EBITDA -2.45x); Piotroski 6.
Moat is moderate [Likely]: a wide disbursement network with direct partner integrations, and non-discretionary, repeat usage (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1782170/000162828026070142/rely-20260930.htm). Digital rivals (Wise, Western Union digital, banks) compete on price, so pricing power is limited [Guessing]. No customer is 10% or more of revenue (same 10-Q). The ROIC jump is partly early-stage operating leverage, so TTM levels are not a steady state [Likely].

## 3. Valuation
Close 19.83 USD, 13.5% below the 50-day average and 6.6% below the 200-day, after a 28.00 high on 2026-11-04 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/rely/history/). P/E 12.0x, EV/EBITDA 14.0x, P/FCF 10.8x, FCF yield 9.30%. P/FCF sits at 2% of its 5y range (median 42.3x) and P/B at 4%; EV/Sales at 8% [Certain]. Versus peers (WU, PYPL, FOUR, EEFT) it is at a premium on every multiple (P/E +16%, EV/EBITDA +131%), but those peers are slower-growth or legacy [Likely].
Mechanical fair value rests on EV/Revenue alone because the DCF could not run: base 31.39 (+58.3%), bear 15.83 (-20.2%) [Likely]. The price assumes growth fades well below the high-teens early FY2027 view in the Q3 fireside-chat headline (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/rely/transcripts/797424-fireside-chat/) [Likely].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Gross margin (income-statement page, TTM) falls below 58%.
- Operating margin (income-statement page, TTM) falls below 8%.
- FCF margin (cash-flow page, TTM) falls below 14%.
- A regulator action that restricts licences or fines materially (the AUSTRAC investigation, or a US remittance-tax or licensing change), disclosed in a 10-Q/8-K, ending the growth thesis.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
FCF conversion was 108.5% of net income TTM [Certain]. Shares still rose 2.75% YoY, so stock compensation outpaces buybacks (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/rely/statistics/) [Likely]. In the 9M the company repurchased 4.5 million shares for $80.0 million [company-reported]; the board lifted the authorisation from $200.0 million to $500.0 million [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1782170/000162828026070142/rely-20260930.htm). Net cash balance sheet; no leverage risk [Certain].
