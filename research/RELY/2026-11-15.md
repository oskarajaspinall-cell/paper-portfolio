# Remitly Global (RELY) — Core initiation — 2026-11-15
## 1. Business
Digital cross-border money transfer: it earns fees and FX spread on consumer remittances funded by bank, card or wallet and paid out through partners, with business and card products added [Certain] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1782170/000162828026070142/rely-20260930.htm). Revenue is attributed by sending country: United States 67.4% of nine-month 2026 revenue, Canada 8.7% [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1782170/000162828026070142/rely-20260930.htm). No single foreign country reaches ~10%. Biggest receive countries are India, Mexico and the Philippines [Certain] (same filing).

## 2. Quality
Operating economics have turned sharply: TTM operating margin 13.0% vs 2.2% a year earlier, gross margin 62.5% (rising from 48.3% in FY2021), FCF margin 20.8% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/rely/financials/income-statement/). All four rolling-12-month metrics are rising, so there is no direction conflict (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/rely/financials/ratios/?p=trailing) [Certain]. TTM ROIC of 45.5% and net margin of 19.1% overstate steady-state returns; net margin sits above operating margin, which suggests a tax or other non-operating benefit [Guessing]. Moat: a digital-first network of direct pay-in and payout partners, repeat usage and non-discretionary demand [Likely] (source: sec-delta filing above). Competition on price (Wise, Western Union, banks) is the durable risk; the gross margin trend shows no price war so far [Likely]. Balance sheet is net cash (net debt/EBITDA -2.45x) and Altman Z is 7.69 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/rely/statistics/) [Certain].

## 3. Valuation
At 22.55 USD: P/E 13.6x, P/FCF 12.2x (5% of its 5y range, 9.8x–56.2x), EV/Sales 2.2x (14% of range), FCF yield 8.18% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/rely/statistics/) [Certain]. Against peers it is dearer: EV/EBITDA 16.2x vs 6.6x, P/B 3.9x vs 2.5x, P/E +21% [Certain]. So the price assumes profitability is largely durable but not much further expansion; it is cheap vs its own history only because earnings only recently arrived [Likely]. Fair value base 31.39 USD (+39.2%) rests on EV/Revenue alone because the DCF was unavailable, so treat it as soft [Likely]. Trend: +7.2% above the 200-day, 3-month momentum -12.0%, and a drop from the 28.00 high of 2026-11-04 despite a beat-and-raise quarter [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/rely/history/).

## 4. What would prove this wrong

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
FCF conversion is 108.5% of net income TTM, so earnings are cash-backed [Certain]. Buybacks: 80.0 million USD in nine months 2026, with the board lifting authorisation by 300.0 million USD to 500.0 million USD on October 28 [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1782170/000162828026070142/rely-20260930.htm). Share count still rose 2.75% YoY (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/rely/statistics/), so buybacks mostly offset stock compensation [Likely]. Net cash gives room.
