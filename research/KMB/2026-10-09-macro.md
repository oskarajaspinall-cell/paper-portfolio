# Macro overlay: Kimberly-Clark Corporation (KMB) — 2026-10-09
## Assessment
KMB's thesis risk is idiosyncratic: the ~$40bn debt-funded Kenvue acquisition (vs KMB's own $32.5bn market cap), still awaiting EU antitrust clearance **(inference, from the note)**. Macro enters mainly via financing cost: `real yields & credit spreads -> cost of debt for the Kenvue acquisition -> post-close leverage/interest expense -> EPS accretion math and equity risk premium`. Facts [Certain]: US 10y real yield (DFII10) 2.92% on 2026-10-07, +61bp over 3m; US HY credit spread (BAMLH0A0HYM2) 3.09% on 2026-10-07, +39bp over 3m; fed funds (DFF) 3.88%, +26bp over 3m (all FRED, https://fred.stlouisfed.org/, snapshot dated 2026-10-09). The 2y Treasury (4.77%) sitting above effective fed funds (3.88%) is a rough proxy that no near-term cuts are priced **(inference)**, a change from earlier this year. Rising real yields and a modestly wider HY spread raise the funding cost of any new debt around KMB's pending assumption of Kenvue's notes [Likely, per the note's exchange-offer disclosure], tightening the leverage math the note already flags as unproven. KMB's low beta (0.28, stockanalysis.com) mutes the discount-rate-on-multiple channel for the standalone business otherwise. **Bull:** rates/spreads stabilize or ease, post-close leverage stays near-manageable, re-rating proceeds toward the base DCF ($97.75). **Bear:** real yields/spreads keep rising into close, interest expense erodes the accretion case, adding to deal-leverage risk already in the AVOID call. **Tilt:** toward bear, small — rates are a second-order amplifier of a risk the note already prices as the dominant reason to avoid, not a new driver.
