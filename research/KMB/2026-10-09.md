# Kimberly-Clark Corporation (KMB) — Core initiation — 2026-10-09

## 1. Business
KMB makes and sells personal care and tissue consumer products (Huggies, Kleenex, Scott, Cottonelle, Depend) globally through retail and away-from-home channels [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/kmb/). It is mid-transformation: it agreed to sell 51% of its international Family Care & Professional tissue business into a joint venture with Suzano (~$1.7bn), recast as discontinued operations [Certain] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/55785/000162828025055209/kmb-20251204.htm), and is separately trying to close a ~$40bn acquisition of Kenvue (Tylenol, Band-Aid, Neutrogena) [Likely] (source: https://www.reuters.com/legal/transactional/kimberly-clark-offers-remedies-bid-eu-approval-kenvue-deal-2026-09-23/).

## 2. Quality
Returns are strong on paper: ROIC ~25% and ROCE ~25% TTM, gross margin 37.6% and operating margin 17.4%, both rising over 5 years [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/kmb/financials/ratios/, https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/kmb/financials/income-statement/). But the margin trend partly reflects removing the lower-margin international tissue business via the Suzano JV, so it is not fully like-for-like with history [Likely]. FCF conversion is solid (93.1% TTM) and net debt/EBITDA is a moderate 1.60x TTM, down from 2.47x in FY2021 [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/kmb/financials/ratios/). None of this reflects the pending Kenvue deal: KMB (market cap $32.5bn) is trying to absorb a roughly $40bn target, is assuming Kenvue's existing notes via exchange offers [Certain] (source: https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/kimberly-clark-corporation-announces-commencement-of-exchange-offers-and-consent-solicitations-for-kenvue-notes-302891811.html), and still needs EU antitrust clearance, for which it has offered asset-sale remedies [Likely] (source: https://www.reuters.com/legal/transactional/kimberly-clark-offers-remedies-bid-eu-approval-kenvue-deal-2026-09-23/). The brand moat (Huggies/Kleenex/Scott) is real, but post-deal returns on a much larger, more indebted balance sheet are unproven.

## 3. Valuation
P/E 16.7x sits at the 1st percentile of its own 5y range (16.6x–26.5x) and 38% below the peer median (26.9x, PG/CL/CLX/CHD); EV/EBITDA 10.9x is below its own 5y minimum and 28% below peers; P/FCF 17.9x is 19% below peers; FCF yield is 5.6% [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/kmb/, peers https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/pg/statistics/, https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/cl/statistics/, https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/clx/statistics/, https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/chd/statistics/). The own-history DCF-based base fair value ($97.75) is in line with last close ($97.74), implying the market already prices roughly zero re-rating or growth [Certain]. Crucially, both the 5y multiple history and the DCF inputs predate the IFP/Suzano divestiture and the pending Kenvue deal, so "cheap vs history" is not comparing like with like [Likely]. The stock has underperformed SPY by 35.8pp over 12 months and short interest is elevated and rising (15.85% of float, +4.8% month-over-month) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/kmb/statistics/) — consistent with the market actively discounting deal/leverage risk rather than simply mispricing a stable business.

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) stays below 3.0x once the Kenvue deal closes and reported in a subsequent quarter — would show leverage risk did not materialize.
- ROIC (ratios page) holds above 18% for two consecutive fiscal years post-close — would show returns survive the larger, more indebted base.
- Gross margin (income statement) holds above 36% once IFP is fully deconsolidated and Kenvue is combined — would show the margin gain is real, not an IFP-divestiture artifact.
- EU antitrust clearance is obtained without remedies beyond those already offered — would remove the remaining deal-completion risk this note cannot size.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
FCF margin is 11.0% TTM with 93.1% FCF/net-income conversion [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/kmb/financials/cash-flow-statement/). Shares outstanding are roughly flat (-0.40% YoY), so buybacks are not material currently [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/kmb/statistics/) — capital is being held back for, or already committed to, the Kenvue deal rather than returned to shareholders at scale. Net debt/EBITDA of 1.60x is pre-deal; financing includes assuming Kenvue's existing notes via exchange offers, which will raise leverage once it closes [Certain] (source: https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/kimberly-clark-corporation-announces-commencement-of-exchange-offers-and-consent-solicitations-for-kenvue-notes-302891811.html).

## 6. Unusual items
Pending ~$40bn Kenvue acquisition (vs KMB's own $32.5bn market cap), still awaiting EU clearance with remedies offered [Likely] (source: https://www.reuters.com/legal/transactional/kimberly-clark-offers-remedies-bid-eu-approval-kenvue-deal-2026-09-23/); debt-funded via assumption of Kenvue notes [Certain] (source: https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/kimberly-clark-corporation-announces-commencement-of-exchange-offers-and-consent-solicitations-for-kenvue-notes-302891811.html); planned post-closing executive transitions including the CEO's departure [Certain] (source: https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/kimberly-clark-announces-planned-executive-transitions-and-updates-to-post-closing-leadership-team-302895877.html); recent divestiture of 51% of the international tissue business to Suzano, now discontinued operations [Certain] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/55785/000162828025055209/kmb-20251204.htm); short interest 15.85% of float, rising [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/kmb/statistics/).

**Decision: AVOID.** The quality and valuation metrics here are pre-transformation: a company KMB's own size is trying to absorb a larger, debt-funded acquisition still pending antitrust clearance, while simultaneously having just divested a chunk of its existing business. The cheap multiples plausibly reflect the market correctly pricing leverage/integration risk the trailing fact-sheet data cannot size, not a mispriced stable business. Conviction does not clear the bar for BUY.
