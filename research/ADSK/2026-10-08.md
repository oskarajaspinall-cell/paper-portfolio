# Autodesk, Inc. (ADSK) — Core initiation — 2026-10-08
## 1. Business
Autodesk sells subscription design/engineering software (desktop + cloud) across three verticals: AEC (AutoCAD, Revit, Civil 3D, Build), manufacturing (Fusion, Inventor, Vault) and media & entertainment (Maya, 3ds Max) [Certain] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/769397/000076939726000015/adsk-20260131.htm). FY2026 revenue was ~37% indirect via ~1,170 resellers/distributors, with TD Synnex now only 14% of revenue (down from 39% in FY2024) as it shifts to direct relationships [Certain] (same source).

## 2. Quality
A genuinely high-return franchise: TTM ROIC 57.6%, ROCE 34.4% (rising +10.9pp over 5y), gross margin 92.5%, operating margin 28.1% (rising +8.0pp) [Certain] (source: factsheet RA/IS). FCF margin is strong at 36.4% TTM, though FCF/NI conversion has fallen from ~297% (FY2022) to 172.9% TTM as deferred-revenue tailwinds normalize [Certain] (source: factsheet CF/IS). The moat rests on switching costs (embedded workflows, Vault-managed project data) and AutoCAD's DWG file-format dominance, reinforced by an API ecosystem locking in third-party developers [Certain] (source: 10-K). The company itself flags that "the software industry has limited barriers to entry," naming Adobe, Bentley, Dassault Systèmes and PTC as competitors capable of disrupting with AI-native tools [Likely] (same source). A newly named AEC leader (Diana Colella, 2026-10-06) is tasked with the Forma/AI strategy — a change to watch, not yet evidence of outcome [Guessing] (source: https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/autodesk-names-diana-colella-to-lead-architecture-engineering-and-construction-business-302899132.html).

## 3. Valuation
ADSK trades at 30.5x P/E and 21.2x EV/EBITDA, ~40-45% below its own 5-year minimum (47.6x / 28.1x) and 41-44% below the peer median (PTC, CDNS, SNPS, BSY) [Certain] (source: factsheet RA + peer statistics pages). EV/Sales (6.2x) and P/FCF (17.3x) show the same pattern: below the stock's entire 5-year range and peers. Only P/B (14.5x) sits above peer median (+92%), reflecting a buyback-shrunk book value, not a premium. The de-rating looks severe vs history, but the stock's own reverse-DCF fair value (base 219.04, -6.8% vs last close 235.05) suggests compression is largely explained by a lower growth path (price implies ~20.6% year-1 revenue growth, fading) rather than pure mispricing [Likely] (source: factsheet fair-value block). Bear/bull fair value (125.15 / 259.84) brackets the last close: cheap vs history/peers, roughly fair vs own cash-flow math.

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Operating margin (income statement page) reverses below 23% (the FY2025 level), signalling the margin expansion trend has broken.
- ROIC (ratios page) falls below 40% (below every year in the FY2022-FY2025 range shown), indicating capital-return erosion.
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) rises back above 1.0x, reversing the multi-year deleveraging to a net-cash position.
- Gross margin (income statement page) falls below 90%, breaking a run that has stayed above that level for 5 straight fiscal years.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Shares outstanding fell -1.62% YoY, consistent with net buybacks, while the balance sheet is now net-cash (net debt/EBITDA -0.20x to -0.28x TTM depending on definition) [Certain] (source: factsheet, ST/RA/BS). FCF margin of 36.4% TTM funds this comfortably; conversion above 100% of net income (172.9% TTM) means cash generation still outpaces reported earnings despite the multi-year decline in that ratio [Certain] (source: factsheet, CF/IS). No dividend; the 10-K references an active stock-repurchase plan without disclosing a fixed annual commitment [Certain] (source: 10-K).

## 6. Unusual items
Q2 FY2027 results (reported ~2026-08-28) beat on both revenue and EPS, but FY and Q3 guidance fell short of Street expectations, and the stock fell ~4% that day — a guidance-driven de-rating, not a demand problem per the reported beat [Likely] (source: https://www.barrons.com/articles/autodesk-earnings-stock-price-a695556f). Short interest is modest (4.00% of float, down 0.8% vs prior month) — not a crowding risk [Certain] (source: factsheet, ST).
