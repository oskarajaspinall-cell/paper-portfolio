# Mastercard Incorporated (MA) — Core initiation — 2026-11-16
## 1. Business
Global card network: earns fees on payment volume, cross-border activity and transaction switching, plus value-added services and solutions (security, data, AI, advisory). It takes no credit risk. [Certain] Value-added services were 41.24% of nine-month 2026 net revenue `[company-reported]` (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1141391/000114139126000124/ma-20260930.htm). Americas were 42.77% `[company-reported]` (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1141391/000114139126000124/ma-20260930.htm); the remainder is Asia Pacific, Europe, Middle East and Africa. No single foreign country is disclosed at ~10%+ in that excerpt [Likely].

## 2. Quality
Exceptional. ROIC is 91.0% TTM (rising +20.3pp over five fiscal years), operating margin 60.1% TTM, net debt/EBITDA 0.59x (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/ma/financials/ratios/). [Certain] The moat is the two-sided network effect (issuers, merchants, consumers) plus scale, which competitors have not eroded; returns have risen, not fallen. [Likely]

Direction flags: ROIC and ROCE are about 4pp below their March 2026 highs, and FCF margin fell from 54.0% to 44.3% over twelve months (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/ma/financials/cash-flow-statement/?p=trailing). Operating margin hit a two-year high in the same period, so the FCF drop is not an earnings problem. The litigation cash outflow of $537 million in nine months `[company-reported]` (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1141391/000114139126000124/ma-20260930.htm) is a plausible cause, but I have not reconciled it to the cash flow statement [Guessing]. This needs monitoring.

## 3. Valuation
Expensive in absolute and peer terms, but not versus its own history. P/E 34.6x sits at the 13th percentile of its 5y range (33.7x–40.6x). EV/EBITDA is 25.6x (3rd percentile). Peer-median P/E is 18.1x, so MA trades at +91% [Certain]. P/FCF 35.8x is at the 65th percentile, and FCF yield is only 2.79%. P/B is above its 5y maximum, which is distorted by buybacks [Likely].

The mechanical fair value (FCFE DCF 67%, DCF 33%) is 365.29 USD base, 471.90 bull, against a 661.19 close. The reverse DCF implies 23.5% year-1 revenue growth fading to terminal, which is demanding [Certain]. The price is +22.0% above the 200-day average, 3-month momentum is +16.6%, and RSI is 64.4. This is a quality-compounder multiple with a momentum premium, not a discount [Likely].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROIC (ratios page, TTM) falls below 80%.
- Operating margin (income statement, TTM) falls below 56%.
- FCF margin (cash flow statement, TTM) stays below 44% for two more quarters, or FCF conversion (FCF/NI) stays below 90%.
- Net debt/EBITDA (statistics page) rises above 1.5x.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Shares fell 2.63% YoY, so buybacks are steady and net debt is low [Certain]. FY2025 FCF conversion was 114.6%, but TTM is 94.8%, so recent conversion is weaker; watch it. The restructuring charge of $202 million in nine months 2026 `[company-reported]` (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1141391/000114139126000124/ma-20260930.htm) is intended to fund reinvestment [Likely].

## 6. Unusual items
- Litigation: the filing delta (10-Q, filed 2026-10-29) adds charges for settlements with merchants who opted out of the U.S. merchant class litigation, plus an ATM non-discrimination surcharge provision. It still carries UK and European interchange claims. These are recurring cash costs rather than a thesis break [Likely] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1141391/000114139126000124/ma-20260930.htm).
- Agentic commerce: Mastercard has an OpenAI partnership (source: https://www.pymnts.com/partnerships/2026/mastercard-forms-openai-and-tencent-partnerships-to-drive-agentic-commerce/). Management calls it additive and still small (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/ma/transcripts/786340-kbw-fintech-ecosystem-conference-2026/). OpenAI's wallet accepts Visa, Amex and Mastercard cards, so it does not favour MA [Likely]. Stablecoin and AI-wallet rails are a disintermediation risk to monitor [Guessing].
- Short interest is 0.82% of float; institutions own 89.4% [Certain].
