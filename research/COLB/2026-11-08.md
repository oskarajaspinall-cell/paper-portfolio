# Columbia Banking System (COLB) — Core initiation — 2026-11-08
## 1. Business
Regional commercial bank, Tacoma-based, with offices in nine western states (Arizona, California, Colorado, Idaho, Nevada, Oregon, Texas, Utah, Washington) [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/887343/000088734326000188/colb-20260930.htm). Earns net interest income on commercial, SBA and leasing loans funded by customer deposits, plus wealth, trust and mortgage fees. Pacific Premier was acquired on 31 August 2025 [company-reported] (same filing). No foreign country is material. [Certain]

## 2. Quality
- ROE has fallen from 15.4% (FY2021) to 8.5% (FY2025), but TTM is 11.2% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/colb/financials/ratios/). [Certain]
- The latest quarter shows the merger synergies arriving. Net interest margin was 4.11% versus 3.93% in the prior quarter, as costly wholesale funding was replaced by customer deposits [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/887343/000088734326000188/colb-20260930.htm). [Likely]
- Integration risk language was removed from the 10-Q risk factors after the systems conversion (same filing). [Certain]
- Moat is modest: a deposit franchise in western metro markets, with no evidence of pricing power beyond peers. Loans are shrinking while low-yield transactional loans run off, and management guides to subdued loan growth into H1 2027 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/colb/transcripts/785964-the-bancanalysts-association-of-boston-conference-2026/). [Likely]
- Piotroski F is 4 (https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/colb/statistics/); ROIC and operating margin are [data unavailable]. [Certain]

## 3. Valuation
- P/E is 10.6x, at the 11% point of its 5y range (9.9x to 15.9x, median 11.5x) and in line with peers at 10.5x. [Certain]
- P/B is 1.2x against a 1.1x 5y median, 9% above the peer median. The stock is not cheap on book. [Certain]
- P/FCF of 6.0x and the 16.56% FCF yield are unreliable for a bank, because FCF includes deposit and loan flows. [Likely]
- Base fair value is 42.94 against a last close of 30.48 (+40.9%), but that blend leans on FCFE DCF (63.66). The P/B + ROE method gives 32.57 (+6.9% vs the close), and the dividend discount model gives 23.93. Bear is 26.77. [Certain]
- Reverse DCF implies -7.5% year-1 growth. The market already prices little growth, but the credible upside is the P/B + ROE value, not the headline base. [Likely]
- Shares outstanding rose 33.02% YoY from the deal, so per-share comparisons with history are diluted. [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/colb/statistics/)

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Net interest margin (10-Q MD&A, tax-equivalent) falls below 3.70%.
- ROE (ratios page, TTM) falls below 9%.
- Total loans and leases (10-Q) fall below $44bn without a rebound in relationship commercial loans.
- Diluted EPS (income statement page, quarterly) falls below $0.65.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Management guides to continued buybacks (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/colb/transcripts/714256-q3-2026/). [Likely] Post-deal, share count is up 33.02% YoY, so the buyback only starts to offset the dilution. FCF conversion of 167.1% of net income (TTM) is flattered by bank balance-sheet flows and I do not rely on it. FCF margin is 52.5% TTM versus 35.2% a year earlier, but the 5y trend is falling. [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/colb/financials/cash-flow-statement/)

## 6. Unusual items
