# Bio-Techne Corporation (TECH) — Core initiation — 2026-10-12

## 1. Business
Bio-Techne develops, manufactures and sells biological reagents, antibodies, immunoassay kits and instruments, and genomic/spatial-biology tools for life-science research, biopharma and diagnostics customers (Sector: Healthcare, Industry: Biotechnology) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/tech/). Revenue is largely recurring reagent/consumable sales, similar to peers BIO, RGEN and AVTR [Likely].

## 2. Quality
Returns and margins have fallen for five years: ROIC 13.7%→9.7% TTM, ROCE 13.2%→11.3%, ROE 16.1%→9.0%, gross margin 68.6%→66.2%, operating margin 25.2%→22.6% [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/tech/financials/ratios/, https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/tech/financials/income-statement/). Cash conversion is strong (FCF margin 21.7% TTM; FCF/NI 144.7% TTM) and the balance sheet is net-cash-like (net debt/EBITDA 0.07x) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/tech/financials/cash-flow-statement/, https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/tech/financials/ratios/).

But whether this is a durable business is secondary: on 2026-06-23 Bio-Techne signed a merger agreement to be acquired by Merck KGaA for $73.00 cash/share; the only remaining closing condition is antitrust clearance (shareholder approval already obtained) [Certain] (source: sec.gov 8-K filed 2026-06-26, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/842023/000199937126013527/tech-8k_062326.htm; shareholder approval per PRNewswire headline dated 2026-09-23 in the fact sheet, https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/bio-techne-shareholders-approve-acquisition-by-merck-kgaa-darmstadt-germany-302887726.html). A standalone moat judgment is not decision-relevant for a 12-month core thesis: holders are expected to be cashed out at a fixed price within the horizon.

## 3. Valuation
On standalone multiples the stock looks expensive: P/E 62.5x (+41% vs peer median), EV/EBITDA 34.1x (+56%), EV/Sales 9.4x (+74%), P/FCF 43.2x (+54%), P/B 5.4x (+123%) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/tech/financials/ratios/ and peer statistics pages). But the price no longer reflects fundamentals: last close $72.46 sits roughly 0.7% below the $73.00 cash deal price, i.e. the market is pricing near-certain deal completion [Certain] (source: sec.gov 8-K as above; https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/tech/history/). The fact sheet's own standalone fair value (base $77.57, +7.1%; bear $52.82, -27.1%; bull $89.50, +23.5%) is a DCF/EV-Revenue estimate that assumes continued independent operation and is superseded by the fixed merger consideration [Certain]. Upside from here is capped near the remaining ~0.7% spread; downside is the bear-case standalone value (-27.1%) if the deal breaks on antitrust grounds.

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- The merger agreement is terminated or abandoned (sec.gov 8-K) and standalone ROIC (ratios page) recovers back above its 3-year average (~11%), re-establishing a fundamentals case.
- If the deal breaks, FCF margin (cash flow/income statement pages) sustains above 20% and the five-year gross-margin decline (currently -2.3pp) stabilises, confirming the moat is intact.
- Antitrust/regulatory clearance is formally denied (sec.gov 8-K/press release), removing the deal-price ceiling and requiring a fresh standalone valuation.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Shares outstanding fell 1.70% YoY (buybacks) and leverage is minimal (net debt/EBITDA 0.07x, down from 0.97x a year earlier) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/tech/statistics/, https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/tech/financials/ratios/). Pending the merger, capital allocation is constrained by customary interim covenants that require compliance by both parties ahead of closing [Certain] (source: sec.gov 8-K); further buybacks or M&A optionality are likely frozen until the deal closes or breaks [Likely].

## 6. Unusual items
Pending all-cash acquisition by Merck KGaA at $73.00/share; shareholders approved on 2026-09-23; remaining condition is antitrust clearance; outside date 2026-03-25, extendable to 2026-09-25 [Certain] (source: sec.gov 8-K, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/842023/000199937126013527/tech-8k_062326.htm). Termination fee of $230.5m payable by Bio-Techne if it accepts a superior offer; reverse fee of $576.1m payable by Merck if antitrust approval fails [Certain] (same source). Short interest rose 11.4% month-on-month to 4.73% of float and institutional ownership is shown at 113.4% [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/tech/statistics/) — both consistent with merger-arbitrage positioning rather than a bearish fundamental signal [Likely]. ATR is just 0.2% of the last close, an unusually tight range typical of a deal-anchored stock [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/tech/history/).
