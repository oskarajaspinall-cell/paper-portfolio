# Paycom Software (PAYC) — Core initiation — 2026-11-15
## 1. Business
Single-database cloud HCM/payroll software sold to US employers. Payroll is the required foundation and historically the majority of revenue; clients add non-payroll modules over time [Certain] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1590955/000119312526449828/payc-20260930.htm). Revenue is recurring and also earns interest on client funds held. No foreign country is flagged as ~10%+ of revenue [Likely]; the fact sheet reports in USD.

## 2. Quality
High and improving. TTM ROIC is 39.3% against 29.9% a year earlier (3-year change +4.8pp), operating margin 33.9% (+6.0pp) and FCF margin 30.1% (+10.4pp) (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/payc/financials/ratios/?p=trailing) [Certain]. Gross margin is 88.4% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/payc/financials/income-statement/) [Certain]. Part of the TTM jump comes from lower headcount: nine-month operating expenses fell while employee-related costs dropped (source: the 10-Q above) [Likely], so margin gains are partly cost-driven, not only growth. The moat is switching cost and product breadth in payroll [Likely]; the 5y FY ROIC was flat (30.7% in FY2025 vs 31.3% in FY2021), so TTM is a step-up, not a long trend [Certain]. FCF conversion is 129.2% TTM but fell 11.4pp over 5 FY [Certain]. Piotroski F is 5 [Certain].

## 3. Valuation
P/E 24.0x sits at 5% of its 5y range and 23% below the peer median (ADP, PAYX, PCTY, WDAY); EV/EBITDA 14.0x is at 1% of range and 31% below peers; P/FCF 16.7x is below its 5y minimum; FCF yield is 5.98% [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/payc/statistics/). P/B is 59% above peers, reflecting buybacks shrinking equity [Likely]. Mechanical base fair value is 261.42 vs last close 248.86 (+5.0%), range bear 100.62 to bull 424.59; the reverse DCF implies 9.4% year-1 revenue growth [Certain]. The shares are up 84.5% in six months and 47.2% above the 200-day average, so a rerating has already begun and the upside to fair value is thin [Certain]. Price assumes the margin step-up persists.

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Operating margin (TTM, income-statement trailing page) falls below 28%.
- ROIC (TTM, ratios trailing page) falls below 30%.
- FCF margin (TTM, cash-flow trailing page) falls below 20%.
- Net debt / EBITDA (statistics page) rises above 2.0x.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Shares outstanding fell 12.61% YoY, an aggressive buyback [Certain]. Since 2026 it is partly debt-funded: $900.0 million was drawn on the revolver for repurchases and $750.0 million remained outstanding at 2026-09-30 [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1590955/000119312526449828/payc-20260930.htm). Net debt / EBITDA moved from -0.44x in FY2025 to 0.75x TTM [Certain]. Leverage is modest, but buying back at a rerated price with borrowed money raises the risk [Likely]. A dividend is paid [Certain] (source: https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20261102962302/en/Paycom-Announces-Quarterly-Cash-Dividend/).
