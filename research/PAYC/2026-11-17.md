# Paycom Software (PAYC) — Core initiation — 2026-11-17
## 1. Business
Cloud human-capital-management software sold direct to US employers. Payroll is the base application every client must use; other modules are layered on it, and the company says most revenue has historically come from payroll (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1590955/000119312526449828/payc-20260930.htm) [Certain]. Revenue is recurring, with interest earned on client funds as a side stream. No foreign country is disclosed as ~10%+ of revenue in the materials reviewed [Likely].

## 2. Quality
High-quality economics: gross margin 88.4% TTM, operating margin 33.9% and ROIC 39.3%, all above the five-year fiscal-year range (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/payc/financials/ratios/) [Certain]. The rolling-12-month table shows ROIC +9.4pp and FCF margin +10.4pp versus a year earlier, so direction is rising, not just on fiscal-year endpoints [Certain]. The 10-Q says operating expenses fell year on year on lower headcount, so part of the margin gain is cost cutting and automation, not only revenue growth [Likely] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1590955/000119312526449828/payc-20260930.htm). Moat is switching cost: payroll is embedded in the client's workflow, and the single-database design is hard to replicate [Likely]. Weaker points: FCF conversion has fallen 11.4pp over five years, though TTM is 129.2% [Certain]; Piotroski F is 5 and Altman Z 2.30 [Certain].

## 3. Valuation
P/E is 23.9x and EV/EBITDA 14.1x, at 4% and 1% of their five-year ranges, and 13% and 30% below peers (ADP, PAYX, PCTY, DAY). P/FCF is 16.9x, below its five-year minimum. FCF yield is 5.91% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/payc/statistics/) [Certain]. The mechanical base fair value is 260.07 against a close of 254.75, only +2.1%; bear is 100.23 and bull 422.60. The price implies 10.2% year-1 revenue growth fading to terminal [Certain]. The shares are 49.7% above the 200-day average after a 6-month gain of 83.0%, so cheap history reflects a recovered price from a depressed base, not a deep discount today [Likely]. Peak-margin risk: TTM margins sit at their two-year highs, so the multiples flatter if margins revert [Likely].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Operating margin (income statement, TTM page) falls below 27% [Likely].
- ROIC (ratios page, TTM) falls below 30%.
- Net debt / EBITDA (statistics page) rises above 2.0x as buybacks are debt-funded.
- Gross margin (income statement) falls below 84%, showing pricing or mix pressure.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Shares are down 12.61% year on year, a heavy buyback [Certain]. For 2026 the company borrowed to fund repurchases, with $750.0 million [company-reported] outstanding on the revolver at 2026-09-30 (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1590955/000119312526449828/payc-20260930.htm). It had funded buybacks from cash before 2026. Net debt / EBITDA moved from -0.44x at FY2025 to 0.75x TTM. Buying back stock near a fair-value estimate with borrowed money raises risk; leverage is still modest [Likely]. A quarterly dividend continues (source: https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20261102962302/en/Paycom-Announces-Quarterly-Cash-Dividend/).
