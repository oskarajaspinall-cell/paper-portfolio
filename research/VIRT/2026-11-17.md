# Virtu Financial (VIRT) — Core initiation — 2026-11-17
## 1. Business
Electronic market maker: it earns the spread and trading income on equities, fixed income, FX and commodities across global venues, funded by its own capital. A smaller agency brokerage and technology unit (POSIT crossing, analytics) earns commissions and fees. Profit scales with market volumes and volatility, not with asset balances. No single foreign country was disclosed as ~10%+ of revenue in the sources read [Likely] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1592386/000159238626000034/virt-20260930.htm).

## 2. Quality
Profitable but cyclical, so not a clean compounder [Likely]. TTM ROIC is 10.5% and operating margin 42.4%, versus ROIC of 4.5% in FY2023: returns follow volatility, not a stable franchise (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/virt/financials/ratios/). The 12-month trend is up (ROIC +1.6pp, operating margin +8.1pp), which contradicts the falling five-year ROIC trend; FY2021 was the peak, FY2023 the trough [Certain]. ROE of 57.0% is flattered by leverage. Scale, technology and multi-asset breadth are real advantages, but a durable moat is only partly evidenced: market-making spreads are competed away and the moat is not shown in margins through the cycle [Guessing]. Balance-sheet leverage is [data unavailable] from the fact sheet. The latest 10-Q adds new risk language on growth investment and on digital assets and event contracts (source: research/VIRT/sec-delta-2026-11-17.md) [Certain].

## 3. Valuation
P/E is 8.4x, near the 5y median of 8.0x (26th percentile of the range) and 68% below the peer median of 26.3x, but peers are brokers/exchanges with steadier earnings, so the discount is partly deserved [Likely]. P/FCF of 10.1x and P/B of 2.6x are above their 5y maxima. FCF yield is 9.94%. The reverse DCF implies revenue falling 12.6% in year 1, i.e. the market treats current earnings as peak-cycle [Likely]. The mechanical fair value (base 123.12 vs close 58.30) is flagged unreliable (methods disagree 4.9x); weigh it lightly. The price is +9.5% above the 200-day average, 12-month change +61.8%, and relative strength is positive: a working business at a cyclical-multiple price, not a deep bargain [Likely]. All from https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/virt/statistics/.

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Operating margin (income statement, TTM) falls below 30%, i.e. back to the FY2024 level.
- ROIC (ratios page, TTM) falls below 8%.
- FCF margin (cash flow statement, TTM) falls below 14%, or FCF conversion (FCF/NI) below 100%.
- A disclosed loss-making trading quarter or a risk-limit breach in a 10-Q/8-K.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
FCF conversion is 140.3% TTM (FY2025: 105.8%) and FCF margin 24.7% (low of 14.2% a year earlier), so cash tracks earnings but is volatile with working capital at a market maker [Likely]. Shares outstanding rose 1.30% YoY, so buybacks are not shrinking the count. The 10-Q describes term-loan funding and a debt-service plus dividend demand on cash flow [Certain] (source: research/VIRT/sec-delta-2026-11-17.md). Reported nine-month revenue growth was 29.2% [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1592386/000159238626000034/virt-20260930.htm).

## 6. Unusual items
