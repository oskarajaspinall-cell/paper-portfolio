# Macro overlay: Cboe Global Markets (CBOE) — 2026-10-08
## Assessment
Cboe's revenue is volume-driven (proprietary VIX/SPX index options, futures, clearing), so the realized/implied **volatility regime** is the macro variable that mechanically moves transaction revenue, Cboe's largest line **(inference)**. Chain: VIX level/regime → options & futures volumes → transaction & clearing revenue → earnings growth; secondarily, the recent back-up in Treasury yields raises the market-wide discount rate on equity multiples, muted by CBOE's low beta (0.41, https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/cboe/statistics/, 2026-10-08) [Certain].

What's priced: VIX is 15.01 on 2026-10-06, +0.48 ~1m, -1.12 ~3m (https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=VIXCLS) [Certain] — low, cooling volatility, implying the options market is not pricing elevated near-term vol, a headwind to recent volume-driven beats **(inference)**. The 10y real yield (DFII10) is 2.91%, +0.48pp ~1m, +0.61pp ~3m (https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=DFII10, 2026-10-06) [Certain]; the 2y yield (4.79%) sits 0.91pp above fed funds (3.88%) (https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=DGS2, https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=DFF, 2026-10-06) [Certain] — a proxy for market-implied further tightening, which raises the equity discount rate generally **(inference, proxy)**.

Bull: a volatility shock lifts VIX/SPX options and clearing volumes; with 36% TTM operating margin, revenue upside flows largely to earnings, and a "volatility winner" narrative can lift the multiple. Bear: volatility stays low/falls further while real yields keep rising — transaction-revenue growth decelerates below the pace embedded in the 22.0x P/E, and a higher discount rate is a mild multiple headwind even for a low-beta name.

Tilt: toward bear, small — low/falling VIX plus rising real yields are a modest double headwind, but growing recurring data/clearing revenue and low beta limit the read-through, and the fundamental (Kalshi/regulatory) case already dominates the thesis.
