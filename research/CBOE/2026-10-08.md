# Cboe Global Markets, Inc. (CBOE) — Core initiation — 2026-10-08
## 1. Business
Cboe operates exchange networks for options, futures, cash equities and FX across North America, Europe and Asia-Pacific, plus data (Cboe Data Vantage, DataShop) and clearing (Cboe Clear U.S., Cboe Clear Europe) [Certain] (source: https://www.cboe.com/about/). Revenue is transaction fees, recurring data/technology subscriptions and clearing fees; the fastest-growing product line is proprietary index and event/prediction-market derivatives (e.g. VIX, SPX, new KPI contracts with Robinhood) [Certain] (source: https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/cboe-and-robinhood-announce-planned-launch-of-new-kpi-contracts-further-expanding-prediction-market-offerings-302894389.html).

## 2. Quality
Returns are high and rising: TTM ROIC 26.9% and ROCE 26.7%, both up from ~12-15% in FY2021 [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/cboe/financials/ratios/). Margins expanded similarly — TTM operating margin 36.0% vs 23.4% in FY2021, FCF margin 37.0% vs 15.6% — and FCF conversion is consistently >100% of net income (138.5% TTM) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/cboe/financials/income-statement/, https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/cboe/financials/cash-flow-statement/). The moat is structural: proprietary, cash-settled index options (SPX, VIX) can only be listed on Cboe, and the recurring data/clearing revenue is sticky once market infrastructure is built on it [Likely] (source: https://www.cboe.com/about/). Leverage is now net-cash (net debt/EBITDA -0.40x TTM) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/cboe/financials/ratios/). The main competitive threat is event-contract/prediction-market entrants (Kalshi) contesting Cboe's regulatory path for new KPI products — Kalshi has asked the SEC to slow Cboe's product launches, an unresolved regulatory dispute that could delay a growth product line [Likely] (source: https://www.pymnts.com/legal/2026/kalshi-asks-sec-to-slow-product-launches-from-rival-cboe/). Piotroski F-score 7/9 and Altman Z 5.21 indicate a financially healthy balance sheet [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/cboe/statistics/).

## 3. Valuation
At 281.57 on 2026-10-07, CBOE trades on 22.0x P/E, below its own 5y range minimum (24.0x) and in line with peer median (22.2x, -1%) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/cboe/financials/ratios/, https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/cme/statistics/, https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/ice/statistics/, https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/ndaq/statistics/, https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/mktx/statistics/). EV/EBITDA (14.8x) and P/FCF (15.7x) sit near the bottom of their 5y ranges and 12%/37% below peer medians, while EV/Sales (5.7x) and P/B (5.2x) sit above their 5y maxima and above peer medians (-45% and +31% vs peers respectively, reflecting Cboe's asset-light balance sheet rather than a rich valuation) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/cboe/financials/ratios/). Taken together, the earnings- and cash-flow-based multiples (P/E, EV/EBITDA, P/FCF) — the more reliable cross-sectional comparators for an asset-light exchange — all price CBOE cheaper than both its own history and peers, despite superior and still-rising ROIC/ROCE. The market appears to be paying for current profitability without yet crediting the margin/returns trajectory of the last five years [Likely]. FCF yield is 6.38% [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/cboe/statistics/).

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROIC (ratios page) falls below 15% for two consecutive fiscal years, reversing the five-year improvement trend.
- Operating margin (income statement) drops below 28% (FY2024 level), signaling fee or cost pressure.
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) rises back above 1.0x, indicating a shift away from the current net-cash, self-funded model.
- FCF conversion (FCF/NI, cash flow + income statement) falls below 100% for two consecutive years, indicating deteriorating cash quality of earnings.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
FCF conversion has exceeded 100% of net income every year since FY2021 (TTM 138.5%), underpinning the firm's 16th consecutive annual dividend increase (Q3 2026 dividend raised 19% to $0.86/share) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/cboe/financials/cash-flow-statement/, https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/cboe-global-markets-declares-increased-third-quarter-2026-dividend-302851370.html). The balance sheet is net-cash (net debt/EBITDA -0.40x TTM), and management has been actively reshaping the portfolio — completing the sale of Cboe Australia to TMX Group in August 2026 — consistent with recycling capital toward higher-return derivatives, data and clearing businesses [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/cboe/financials/ratios/, https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/cboe-completes-sale-of-cboe-australia-to-tmx-group-302840649.html).

## 6. Unusual items
None material. Short interest is low (2.45% of float, down 1.1% month-on-month) and institutional ownership is high (90.6%), consistent with a stable, widely-held large-cap [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/cboe/statistics/). The Kalshi/SEC dispute over KPI/event-contract product launches (noted in Section 2) is a regulatory overhang on one growth line but is not yet a financial or governance issue [Likely] (source: https://www.pymnts.com/legal/2026/kalshi-asks-sec-to-slow-product-launches-from-rival-cboe/).
