# Tradeweb Markets Inc. (TW) — Core initiation — 2026-11-16
## 1. Business
Tradeweb operates electronic marketplaces for rates, credit, equities and money markets, serving institutional, wholesale and retail clients. It earns mainly transaction fees and commissions, fixed (subscription) and variable (volume-linked), plus market data fees, including from LSEG (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1758730/000175873026000144/tw-20260930.htm) [Certain]. Revenue by country is [data unavailable] from the fact sheet; the filing delta shows no foreign-country share, so none is named.

## 2. Quality
Returns are low in absolute terms but improving: ROIC 10.5% TTM versus 9.5% in FY2025 and 5.2% in FY2021 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/tw/financials/ratios/) [Certain]. Operating margin is 44.1% TTM versus 33.9% in FY2021 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/tw/financials/income-statement/) [Certain]. The moat is network liquidity: dealers and asset managers trade where counterparties already are, so scale begets scale [Likely]. Low ROIC mostly reflects acquisition goodwill on the balance sheet, not weak economics [Likely].
Direction change: FCF margin is 50.6% TTM, down from 52.6% a year earlier and below the 55.0% FY2025 level (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/tw/financials/cash-flow-statement/?p=trailing) [Certain]. FCF/NI has also fallen to 127.0%, partly as net margin rose to 39.9% [Likely]. Still a very high-margin franchise.
Q3 2026 results showed growth across credit, wholesale and market data (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1758730/000175873026000144/tw-20260930.htm) [Certain].

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Shares outstanding fell 0.95% year on year, so buybacks outpace dilution (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/tw/statistics/) [Certain]. FCF conversion remains well above 100% of net income at 127.0% [Certain]. The company agreed to buy Trumid Holdings, announced in the Q3 10-Q as a subsequent event (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1758730/000175873026000144/tw-20260930.htm) [Certain]. Terms are not in the fact sheet, so the return on this deal is unknown [Guessing]. A large deal could dilute ROIC again [Likely].
