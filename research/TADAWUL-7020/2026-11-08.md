# Etihad Etisalat Company / Mobily (TADAWUL:7020) — Core initiation — 2026-11-08
## 1. Business
Saudi Arabia's number-two mobile operator (Mobily), selling consumer and enterprise mobile, fixed broadband and wholesale connectivity, with revenue in SAR from the home market (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tadawul/7020/) [Likely]. No foreign country is flagged as a material revenue share in the fact sheet. [Guessing] on any international share; not verified from a primary source.

## 2. Quality
Returns have improved sharply: ROIC rose from 5.7% (FY2021) to 14.7% (TTM), ROE to 18.3%, operating margin from 11.2% to 19.8% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tadawul/7020/financials/ratios/) [Certain]. Leverage fell to 0.81x net debt/EBITDA from 2.13x, and Piotroski F is 7 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tadawul/7020/statistics/) [Certain]. A three-player licensed market with high spectrum and network barriers is a plausible durable moat [Likely], but the improvement may owe much to post-restructuring cost cuts and is not proof of pricing power [Guessing].

Direction change: the last year is flat on ROIC (14.7% vs 14.8%) and operating margin (19.8% vs 20.0%), so the margin gains have plateaued. FCF margin fell from 20.2% to 16.1% over twelve months and FCF conversion from 242.4% (FY2021) to 89.9% (TTM), below net income (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tadawul/7020/financials/cash-flow-statement/?p=trailing) [Certain]. Earnings are of falling cash quality; probably capex/working capital, cause unverified [Guessing].

## 3. Valuation
P/E of 12.4x and EV/EBITDA of 6.3x are both below their 5y minimums (13.2x, 6.5x), yet P/FCF of 13.8x sits at 98% of its 5y range (8.0x–13.9x) because FCF is shrinking [Certain]. Versus peers (n=3), P/E is 21% above the median 10.3x, EV/EBITDA 4% below, P/B 7% below (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tadawul/7020/statistics/) [Certain]. FCF yield is 7.26%.

Mechanical fair value: base 65.99 SAR vs close 59.05 (+11.8%), bear 57.37 (-2.8%), bull 99.04 (+67.7%); the reverse DCF implies just 1.9% year-1 revenue growth [Certain]. The bull case rests on DCF extrapolation of recent margin gains, which have stalled. Cheap on earnings, fair on cash. Price is 8.1% below its 200-day average with 12-month change of -11.2%, i.e. unloved but not distressed [Certain]. Upside to base is modest and the cash-conversion trend argues against paying for it [Likely].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Operating margin (income statement, TTM) falls below 17.5%.
- ROIC (ratios page, TTM) falls below 12%.
- FCF margin (cash flow statement, TTM) falls below 12%, i.e. conversion keeps eroding.
