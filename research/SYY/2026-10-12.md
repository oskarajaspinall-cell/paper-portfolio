# Sysco Corporation (SYY) — Core initiation — 2026-10-12

## 1. Business
Sysco is the largest foodservice distributor in North America and parts of Europe: it buys food and non-food products from suppliers and delivers them from its own warehouses and trucks to restaurants, healthcare, education and hospitality customers [Certain]. Margins are structurally thin (gross margin 18.5% TTM); profit comes from route density and scale rather than pricing power (operating margin 4.2% TTM) (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/syy/financials/income-statement/).

## 2. Quality
Returns are solid and stable: ROIC 17.1% TTM (flat over 5y, +0.9pp) and ROCE 19.7% TTM (rising, +2.0pp) (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/syy/financials/ratios/) [Certain]. The moat is route density and warehouse scale in a capital-intensive, low-margin industry — hard for a new entrant to replicate, and Sysco is the largest of the major listed distributors [Likely]. FCF conversion is strong (110.3% of net income TTM, rising +25.1pp over 5y) (source: cash-flow/income statement) [Certain]. Set against this, the balance sheet already carries net debt/EBITDA of 2.95x TTM (site definition) before any effect of the pending debt-funded acquisition disclosed in section 6 [Certain], and the business itself offers limited structural growth (TTM net margin 2.1%, essentially flat for 5 years) [Likely]. Beta of 0.64 (source: statistics page) reflects its defensive-staple character, but that defensiveness is being tested by the leverage event below.

## 3. Valuation
On P/E, SYY trades at 21.3x TTM, near the bottom of its own 5y range (18.2x–32.4x, 22nd percentile) and 48% below the peer (USFD, PFGC, CHEF) median of 41.4x (source: ratios/statistics pages) [Certain] — peers are smaller, faster-growing names that typically carry richer multiples, so the gap partly reflects growth, not mispricing [Likely]. EV/EBITDA, capital-structure-neutral, is more comparable: 11.5x, below the 5y range minimum (11.6x) and 22% below the peer median (14.8x) [Certain]. P/B of 14.0x looks expensive next to peers' 4.9x, but this is distorted by Sysco's buyback-shrunk equity base (ROE 77.7% TTM) and is not a useful cross-check [Likely]. Own-history fair value: base $76.51 vs last close $78.14 (-2.1%), bear $53.84 (-31.1%), bull $95.22 (+21.9%) (source: fact sheet fair value table) [Certain] — the market prices SYY almost exactly at base intrinsic value, with little margin of safety before the pending acquisition's leverage effect is even known.

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROIC (ratios page) falls below 14% TTM (vs 17.1% now), signalling the scale/network advantage is eroding.
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page, site definition) rises above 4.5x TTM once the pending acquisition closes, meaning balance-sheet risk has displaced the quality thesis.
- Gross margin (income statement) falls below 17.5% TTM, meaning the distribution network is losing scale/pricing power to peers.
- FCF conversion (cash flow vs income statement) falls below 80% of net income TTM for two consecutive years, meaning the historically strong cash generation has broken down.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
FCF conversion has averaged above 100% of net income since FY2023 (TTM 110.3%), typical of a low-capex distribution model (source: cash-flow/income statement) [Certain]. Sysco has historically returned cash via buybacks rather than retaining equity (shares down 1.88% YoY), leaving ROE at 77.7% TTM and P/B at 14.0x (source: statistics/ratios pages) [Certain]. That model is reversing: in September-October 2026 Sysco issued ~$1bn of new common stock and ~$15.75bn-equivalent of new notes to help fund a pending acquisition, diluting shareholders and adding debt service ahead of any confirmed synergy (source: SEC 8-K, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/96021/000110465926114079/tm2625144d27_8k.htm) [Certain].

## 6. Unusual items
A SEC Form 8-K filed 2026-10-06 discloses Sysco Corporation and Sysco Holdings Corporation issued ~$10.75bn of USD senior notes, ~$3.9bn of USD junior subordinated notes and €1.0bn of euro junior subordinated notes, stating net proceeds will fund "a portion of the cash consideration for the pending acquisition of JRD Unico, Inc. ... and Warehouse Realty, LLC," with a special mandatory redemption if the deal does not close (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/96021/000110465926114079/tm2625144d27_8k.htm) [Certain]. This followed a C$1.5bn senior notes offering closed 2026-09-25 and a $1bn common stock offering (12.3m shares at $81.00) in mid-September 2026 (source: fact sheet news items, https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/09/25/3369319/2867/en/sysco-announces-closing-of-c-1-5-billion-senior-notes-offering.html, https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/09/15/3361631/2867/en/sysco-announces-pricing-of-common-stock-offering.html) [Certain]. Together this is over $18bn of new financing — roughly half of Sysco's $38.4bn market cap — for an acquisition whose target, rationale and price could not be established from allowlisted sources [Guessing]. This raises leverage and integration risk on top of an already 2.95x net debt/EBITDA balance sheet; the quality/valuation case in sections 2-3 should be read as pre-deal, and a re-initiation is warranted once disclosures quantify the acquired business.
