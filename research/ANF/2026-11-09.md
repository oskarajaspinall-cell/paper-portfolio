# Abercrombie & Fitch Co. (ANF) — Core initiation — 2026-11-09
## 1. Business
Multi-brand apparel retailer (Abercrombie, Hollister, YPB) selling through owned stores and digital, with growing franchise/wholesale/licensing in new markets (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1018840/000101884026000047/anf-20260801.htm). Q2 net sales by segment: Americas 80.6%, EMEA 15.9%, APAC 3.5% [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1018840/000101884026000047/anf-20260801.htm). EMEA is the one foreign region above 10% [Certain].

## 2. Quality
Strong but cyclical-fashion exposed. TTM ROIC 26.7%, ROCE 30.1%, operating margin 14.1%, net debt/EBITDA 0.71x (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/anf/financials/ratios/). [Certain]
Moat is brand heat, not structure: FY2023 ROIC was 1.3% and operating margin 2.9%, so returns can collapse when the brand cools. [Certain]
Direction: ROIC fell from 29.7% to 26.7% over 12 months and sits well below its 2-year high of 38.9% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/anf/financials/ratios/?p=trailing). Margin flat, FCF margin up 3.8pp. [Certain]
Q2 gross margin was flattered by a tariff refund of approximately $100 million booked in cost of sales [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1018840/000101884026000047/anf-20260801.htm); TTM margins are therefore above run-rate. [Likely]

## 3. Valuation
P/E 13.3x sits at the 1% point of its 5y range, but that range is distorted by a 475.2x FY2023 trough; vs peers it is +13% (peer median 11.8x). EV/EBITDA 7.6x is 57% of range and -18% vs peers; EV/Sales 1.3x is 89% of range. P/FCF 11.2x is at the low end (10%); FCF yield 8.93% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/anf/statistics/). [Certain]
The price has doubled in 12 months (+112.4%), so the re-rating is done. Mechanical fair value: base 175.86 vs close 149.54 (+17.6%), but the bear case is negative and methods diverge (DCF 202.27 vs EV/EBITDA 123.04), so it is low-reliability. The reverse DCF implies roughly no revenue growth, which looks undemanding if earnings hold. [Likely]
Cheap on cash flow only if TTM margins are sustainable. [Guessing]

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Operating margin (income statement, TTM) falls below 11%.
- ROIC (ratios page, TTM) falls below 20%.
- Net debt / EBITDA (ratios page) rises above 2.0x.
- Gross margin (income statement, TTM) falls below 58%.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Aggressive buybacks: shares down 9.81% YoY; Q2 repurchases of $179.2M [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1018840/000101884026000047/anf-20260801.htm). TTM FCF conversion 105.7% of net income and FCF margin 10.6%. Buying near highs earns less, but leverage stays low. Inventory is flat versus year-end [company-reported] (same filing). [Likely]
