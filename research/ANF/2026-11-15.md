# Abercrombie & Fitch Co. (ANF) — Core initiation — 2026-11-15
## 1. Business
Global multi-brand specialty apparel retailer (Abercrombie, Hollister, YPB activewear), selling through owned stores and digital, with franchise/wholesale growth in new markets [Certain] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1018840/000101884026000047/anf-20260801.htm). Q2 net sales mix: Americas 80.6%, EMEA 15.9%, APAC 3.5% [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1018840/000101884026000047/anf-20260801.htm). EMEA is the only foreign region above 10%; no single emerging-market country is disclosed at that level [Likely].

## 2. Quality
Strong, but cyclical and fashion-dependent. Five-year ROIC is rising, though FY2023 collapsed to 1.3% before recovering, so returns are not smooth (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/anf/financials/ratios/) [Certain]. TTM ROIC is 26.7%, operating margin 14.1% and FCF margin 10.6%; net debt/EBITDA is 0.71x, so the balance sheet is not a risk [Certain].

Direction: TTM ROIC fell from 29.7% to 26.7% over 12 months and sits 12.2pp below its 2-year high; ROCE fell from 31.5% to 30.1% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/anf/financials/ratios/?p=trailing). Operating margin was flat (+0.1pp) and FCF margin rose, so the decline is mostly capital base growth and normalisation from a peak, not margin erosion [Likely].

Moat is brand heat with Gen-Z/millennial customers, which is renewable only through constant merchandising execution: not a durable structural moat [Likely]. Buybacks cut the share count 9.81% YoY [Certain].

## 3. Valuation
At 148.88 USD the stock trades on 12.8x P/E (peers 12.4x) and 7.6x EV/EBITDA against a 6.9x own 5y median and 9.7x peer median; EV/Sales (1.3x) and P/B (4.7x) are near the top of the own 5y range (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/anf/financials/ratios/) [Certain]. The 1% P/E position is distorted by a 475x trough-earnings outlier [Likely]. FCF yield is 8.97% [Certain].

The reverse DCF implies 0.8% year-1 revenue growth, a low bar. Mechanical base fair value is 170.30 (+14.4%), but the bear case is negative and the two methods differ widely (193.93 vs 123.04): low reliability [Likely]. TTM margins are flattered by about $100 million of IEEPA tariff refunds booked against cost of sales in Q2 [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1018840/000101884026000047/anf-20260801.htm); that is non-recurring, so the P/E looks cheaper than it is [Certain]. The stock is up 109.0% in 12 months: a good business that is working, fairly priced [Likely].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Operating margin (income statement page, TTM) falls below 11% excluding the tariff-refund benefit.
- ROIC (ratios page, TTM) falls below 20%.
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) rises above 2.0x.
- Gross margin (income statement page, TTM) falls below 58%.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
FCF conversion is 105.7% TTM (FY2026: 74.6%). Capital goes mainly to buybacks: $179,184K in Q2 and $284,738K year-to-date [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1018840/000101884026000047/anf-20260801.htm). With the stock doubled, repurchases at higher prices earn lower returns [Likely]. Inventories were roughly flat since January (same source) [Certain]. Reinvestment is modest (new stores, systems, ERP).

## 6. Unusual items
- Short interest is 12.09% of float, up 6.8% on the month; a crowded short on a strong run raises volatility around the Nov 24 results [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/anf/statistics/).
- SEC delta: the 10-Q adds new language on tariff refunds, other tariffs remaining in effect, ERP implementation risk, AI initiatives and Middle East conflict raising freight costs and hurting EMEA demand; the Risk Factors section is unchanged [Certain] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1018840/000101884026000047/anf-20260801.htm). The size of the EMEA Middle East exposure is unquantified [Guessing].
