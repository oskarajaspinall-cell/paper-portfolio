# Abercrombie & Fitch Co. (ANF) — Core initiation — 2026-11-17
## 1. Business
Global multi-brand apparel retailer (Abercrombie, Hollister, YPB activewear) selling through owned stores and digital. Q2 net sales: Americas about 80.6%, EMEA about 15.9% ($201,990 thousand of $1,266,689 thousand), APAC about 3.5% [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1018840/000101884026000047/anf-20260801.htm). EMEA (~10%+) is the foreign region the macro overlay should check; the filing flags Middle East conflict as hurting demand in affected markets. [Certain]

## 2. Quality
Strong returns: TTM ROIC 26.7%, ROE 40.6%, operating margin 14.1%, FCF margin 10.6%, net debt/EBITDA 0.71x, Altman Z 4.98 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/anf/financials/ratios/). [Certain]
Direction: ROIC is rising over 5 fiscal years but fell 3.0pp over the last 12 months (29.7% to 26.7%) and sits 12.2pp below its 2-year high; ROCE likewise (31.5% to 30.1%). Margin is flat year on year (+0.1pp) while FCF margin rose 3.8pp. [Certain]
Moat is thin: fashion-led apparel, brand heat is the only edge and it is cyclical (FY2023 ROIC 1.3%). [Likely]
TTM margins are flattered by about $100 million of IEEPA tariff refunds booked in cost of sales in Q2 [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1018840/000101884026000047/anf-20260801.htm); underlying margin is lower. [Likely]

## 3. Valuation
P/E 12.4x sits at the 1% point of its 5y range (7.8x–475.2x, distorted by the FY2023 trough) and +4% vs peers (12.0x). EV/EBITDA 7.5x is mid-range (53%) and 21% below peers; EV/Sales 1.3x is 85% of range. P/FCF 10.9x is below its 5y minimum; FCF yield 9.16% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/anf/statistics/). [Certain]
Mechanical base fair value 169.82 vs close 145.77 (+16.5%), DCF 193.21, EV/EBITDA 123.04; the bear case is negative and the methods disagree widely, so treat it as low reliability. Reverse DCF implies 0.4% year-1 revenue growth. The price assumes margins hold near current (refund-aided) levels with little growth. [Likely]

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Operating margin (TTM, income statement page) falls below 11%.
- ROIC (TTM, ratios page) falls below 20%.
- FCF margin (TTM, cash flow page) falls below 6%.
- Quarterly net sales growth (earnings release, company-reported) turns negative for two consecutive quarters.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Heavy buybacks: shares down 9.81% YoY; the company repurchased about $179 million of stock in Q2 [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1018840/000101884026000047/anf-20260801.htm). FCF conversion 105.7% TTM against 74.6% in FY2026. Leverage low. Buying back at a 12x P/E after a 107.7% 12-month rise is value-neutral at best. [Likely]
