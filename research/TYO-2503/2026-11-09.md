## 2. Quality
Returns are modest but improving. TTM ROIC is 9.5% against 7.6% in FY2025 and 5.5% in FY2024; ROE is 15.1% [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tyo/2503/financials/ratios/). Operating margin is 11.8% against 8.1% in FY2024, gross margin 48.4% [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tyo/2503/financials/income-statement/). The rolling-12-month table confirms the rise: ROIC +4.6pp and operating margin +4.3pp year on year [Certain].

Direction change: FCF margin fell over five fiscal years (7.3% to 4.9%) but rose in the last 12 months (0.7% to 7.8%); FCF conversion is 100.2% TTM [Certain]. Part of the TTM jump may reflect working-capital timing or disposal effects rather than structural change [Guessing].

Moat: entrenched Japanese brand and distribution positions in a mature, shrinking-volume category [Likely]. That supports stable margins, not high returns; an ROIC near 10% is only just above a plausible cost of capital [Likely]. Piotroski F is 8; Altman Z is 2.24 [Certain].

## 3. Valuation
At 2,967.00 JPY the stock trades at P/E 12.8x, below its 5y minimum of 12.9x (median 14.8x), and EV/EBITDA 7.9x versus a 5y range of 9.0x–9.6x [Certain]. Versus the median of four Japanese food and beverage peers it is at a discount: P/E -23%, EV/EBITDA -36%, P/FCF -55% [Certain]. FCF yield is 7.92% [Certain]. P/B of 1.5x is above its 5y max of 1.3x, and EV/Sales is above range, so the discount comes from earnings recovery rather than assets [Certain].

Mechanical base fair value is 4,053.05 JPY (+36.6%); bear 1,370.60, bull 4,466.52 [Certain]. The bear case is dominated by a collapsed DCF input and the two methods diverge widely, so treat it with caution [Likely]. The reverse DCF implies revenue shrinking 1.5% in year 1 [Certain]. The price already discounts a lot of margin mean-reversion; the risk is that TTM margins are a peak [Guessing]. Next earnings are Nov 11, 2026 [Certain].
