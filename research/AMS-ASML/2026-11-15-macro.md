# Macro overlay: ASML Holding N.V. (AMS:ASML) — 2026-11-15
## Assessment
**Chain:** real yields -> discount rate -> multiple. ASML trades at 38.3x P/E against a 5y median of 35.7x, and its mechanical fair value (581.45 EUR) is far below the 1,338.80 EUR price (fact sheet, 2026-11-15). The multiple is the macro-sensitive part [Likely].

**Facts (FRED):** the 10y TIPS yield is 2.86%, +0.47pp over ~3m and -0.02pp over ~1m (https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=DFII10, 2026-11-12). The 2y is 4.86% against fed funds at 3.88% (https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=DGS2, 2026-11-12). Breakeven is 2.24%, flat over 3m (https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=T10YIE, 2026-11-13). The high-yield spread is 3.2% (https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=BAMLH0A0HYM2, 2026-11-12) [Certain].

**Pricing (proxy):** the 2y sits about 1pp above fed funds, so hikes are priced. Flat breakevens mean the 3m rise is real-rate driven, which hurts long-duration equities most **(inference)**. The stock is already -16.8% over 3m, so part of this is priced [Likely].

**Bull:** real yields fall below ~2.5%, so the multiple holds near its median while AI capex lifts revenue.
**Bear:** real yields above ~3.25% or wider spreads, so the multiple compresses toward the 5y low (34.3x) on unchanged revenue.

**Tilt:** toward bear, small. The price is already above the mechanical bull value, and macro adds rate pressure without a fresh surprise.

**Country risk:** China export-control exposure is unquantified (note section 1) and unverified; no official source was read.
