## 2. Quality
Exceptional returns on capital: ROIC 92.1% TTM vs 59.9% a year earlier, ROCE 44.7%, ROE 65.6% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/ams/ASML/financials/ratios/) [Certain]. Operating margin 38.6% TTM, up 3.7pp in 12 months; gross margin 54.3% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/ams/ASML/financials/income-statement/) [Certain]. Net cash (net debt/EBITDA -0.67x) and Piotroski F of 8 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/ams/ASML/statistics/) [Certain]. The durable reason returns persist is its EUV monopoly, which no competitor replicates [Likely]. Direction change: FCF margin fell over 5 fiscal years (33.9% FY2025, 11.9% FY2023 trough) but is 53.6% TTM vs 27.8% a year earlier. FCF conversion of 157.1% TTM is above its history and likely reflects customer prepayments/working-capital timing rather than a permanent step-up [Guessing]; treat the 53.6% as peak-like. Headlines report a Q3 beat and raised 2026 guidance (https://www.nasdaq.com/articles/asml-raises-2026-guidance-ai-demand-accelerates-capacity-expansion) [Likely].

The price assumes AI-driven capacity demand sustains far above history; own-history multiples look cheap only because the 5y range included 2021 and 2024 peaks [Likely].
