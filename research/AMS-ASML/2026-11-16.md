# ASML Holding N.V. (AMS:ASML) — Core initiation — 2026-11-16
## 1. Business
ASML sells lithography systems (and service) to chipmakers; it is the dominant supplier of the equipment used to print advanced chips [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/ams/ASML/). Revenue follows customers' capacity spending, with AI-driven demand cited in recent headlines [Likely] (source: https://www.nasdaq.com/articles/asml-raises-2026-guidance-ai-demand-accelerates-capacity-expansion). ## 2. Quality
Exceptional returns: ROIC 92.1% TTM vs 85.8% in FY2025 and 59.9% in FY2021; ROCE 44.7% TTM [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/ams/ASML/financials/ratios/). Operating margin 38.6% TTM and gross margin 54.3% TTM, both up on a year earlier [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/ams/ASML/financials/income-statement/). Net cash: net debt/EBITDA -0.67x. Piotroski F 8 [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/ams/ASML/statistics/).
Direction change: FCF margin fell over five fiscal years (FY2021 53.4% to FY2025 33.9%, with a 11.9% trough in FY2023) but TTM is 53.6% vs 27.8% a year earlier. Cash flow is lumpy (customer prepayments, working capital), so I treat the TTM as cyclically high rather than a new base [Likely].
Durable moat: a near-monopoly in the equipment that advanced chip production depends on, with returns that have stayed high through the cycle [Likely].

## 3. Valuation
P/E 37.4x sits at 21% of its 5y range (34.3x–48.8x), EV/EBITDA 28.6x at 12% of its range, both in the lower part of own history; vs peers (AMAT, LRCX, KLAC) P/E is +17% and EV/EBITDA +4%. P/FCF 23.6x is below its 5y minimum, but TTM FCF is cyclically high. FCF yield 4.24% [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/ams/ASML/statistics/).
The mechanical fair value (through-cycle DCF + EV/EBITDA) is 581.47 EUR base, 1,100.35 EUR bull, vs a 1,338.80 EUR close: base -56.6%, bull -17.8%. The reverse DCF needs 35.4% year-1 revenue growth [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/ams/ASML/). The price assumes a sustained AI capex up-cycle well beyond history [Likely]. Quality is superb; the price already pays for it.
Trend: 4.4% below the 200-day, 3m momentum -16.8%, 12m +49.5%.
