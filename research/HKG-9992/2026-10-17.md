# Pop Mart International Group (HKG:9992) — Core initiation — 2026-10-17

## 1. Business
Pop Mart designs and retails collectible "blind box" toys and figures built on owned/licensed IP (The Monsters/Labubu, Molly, and others), made by outsourced manufacturers [Certain]. It sells through company-owned stores, vending "roboshops," e-commerce and a secondary resale marketplace, with international store expansion into the US, Europe and Asia under way [Likely] (headline, not fetched: https://www.reuters.com/business/retail-consumer/pop-mart-brings-labubu-dessert-table-global-bakery-push-2026-07-29/). Revenue mix by product/geography [data unavailable].

## 2. Quality
Returns are exceptional and still rising: ROIC 108.4% TTM, ROE 71.2% TTM, ROCE 70.5% TTM, all up sharply over five years; gross margin 71.7% TTM (+10.7pp vs FY21) and operating margin 46.0% TTM (+20.5pp) [Certain] (https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/9992/financials/ratios/, https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/9992/financials/income-statement/). The moat is owned IP plus a design/retail/supply-chain flywheel that is hard for legacy toy makers to replicate quickly [Likely]. The main durability risk is concentration in a small number of characters (Labubu/The Monsters): press repeatedly frames growth as hinging on the "next blockbuster," no numbers taken from these headlines [Likely] (https://www.wsj.com/business/earnings/labubu-makers-revenue-momentum-slows-after-explosive-growth-7d32c266; https://www.cnbc.com/2026/03/25/pop-mart-labubu-sluggish-crybaby-monster-family-sales-.html). FCF conversion fell to 54.5% TTM from 146.6% FY2024 and 77.3% FY2025 [Certain] (https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/9992/financials/cash-flow-statement/), consistent with capex/working-capital build for expansion rather than a margin problem, but worth tracking. Balance sheet is net cash (net debt/EBITDA −0.53x) and Altman Z is 25.24 — no distress [Certain] (https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/9992/financials/balance-sheet/, https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/9992/statistics/).

## 3. Valuation
Trailing multiples look cheap: P/E 12.4x is below its own 5-year minimum (17.5x) and 26% below the peer median of 16.7x (MNSO, FNKO, HAS, MAT); EV/EBITDA 7.6x is below its 5y minimum (12.1x) and 28% below the 10.4x peer median [Certain] (https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/9992/financials/ratios/). But EV/Sales (3.8x) and P/B (6.9x) sit far above peer medians (+209%/+210%) — Hasbro and Mattel are low-growth, low-margin legacy toy businesses, limiting the comparison's value [Likely]. The DCF base fair value is 119.17 HKD, 16.2% below the last close of 142.20 HKD; only the bull case (315.39 HKD) clears the price, while the bear case (56.27 HKD) is far below [Certain]. The reverse DCF implies ~24.6% year-one revenue growth priced in [Certain]. Net: statistically cheap vs history and peers, but the DCF base case says price already assumes growth doesn't decelerate much further.

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Gross margin (income statement page, stockanalysis.com) falls below 60% for two consecutive reporting periods — pricing/IP power eroding beyond normal mix shift.
- FCF margin (cash flow + income statement pages) falls below 12% TTM — conversion deterioration (already 35.1%→17.9% TTM) becoming structural, not expansion-driven.
- ROIC (ratios page) falls below 40% TTM — a sharp reversion would show the IP moat eroding, not just normalizing.
- Net debt/EBITDA (balance sheet + income statement pages) turns net-debt positive — would signal retail/theme-park expansion outstripping returns.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Shares outstanding are essentially flat (−0.24% YoY): no material dilution [Certain] (https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/9992/statistics/). The balance sheet is net cash (net debt/EBITDA −0.53x TTM, vs −3.45x FY2021 — the cash cushion relative to EBITDA has shrunk as EBITDA grew) [Certain] (https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/9992/financials/balance-sheet/). FCF margin is 17.9% TTM, down from a 35.1% FY2024 peak, and FCF yield is 4.46% [Certain] (https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/9992/statistics/), consistent with reinvestment into global store build-out and new formats [Likely] (headlines, not fetched). No dividend or buyback data is shown on the fact sheet [data unavailable].

## 6. Unusual items
IP concentration (principally Labubu/The Monsters) is the single most-cited risk in press coverage of growth deceleration [Likely] (https://www.wsj.com/business/earnings/labubu-makers-revenue-momentum-slows-after-explosive-growth-7d32c266). Press describes the stock as one of Hong Kong's most shorted despite shorts losing money historically; stockanalysis.com itself shows short interest as [data unavailable], so this is unverified [Guessing] (https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/30/short-sellers-betting-against-toymaker-pop-mart-even-though-they-are-losing-money.html). Insider ownership is high at 45.29% vs institutional ownership of 20.6% [Certain] (https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/9992/statistics/) — alignment, but also control concentration. Price is down 47.9% over 12 months, near its 52-week low, with RSI at 28.5 (oversold) [Certain] (https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/9992/history/) — sentiment context, not a trigger.
