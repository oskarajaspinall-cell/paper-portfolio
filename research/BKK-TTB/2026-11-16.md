# TMBThanachart Bank (BKK:TTB) — Core initiation — 2026-11-16
## 1. Business
Thai commercial bank (ttb), reporting in THB; revenue is net interest and fee income from retail and corporate lending (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/bkk/TTB/) [Likely]. Segment and country mix are [data unavailable]; the home market is Thailand, so the exposure is Thai rates, credit and the baht [Certain]. ING, a large shareholder, has been selling down its stake through placements and a buyback (source: https://www.bangkokpost.com/business/general/3304715/ing-share-sale-cuts-ttb-stake-to-116) [Certain].

## 2. Quality
Returns are modest but improving. ROE rose from 5.0% (FY2021) to 8.6% (FY2025) and is 9.1% TTM; net margin rose from 23.8% to 41.5% TTM (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/bkk/TTB/financials/ratios/) [Certain]. ROIC, ROCE, operating margin and leverage are [data unavailable]; a bank's leverage cannot be judged from this sheet. Piotroski F is 4, middling (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/bkk/TTB/statistics/) [Certain]. Beta is 0.19 [Certain]. The moat is Thai scale and a retail deposit franchise, but a ROE near 9% is no better than average. A local report says Thai banks can no longer rely on strong interest income or high ROE (source: https://www.bangkokpost.com/business/general/3218110/thai-banks-struggling-to-make-money) [Likely]. Margin gains look mostly harvested [Guessing].

## 3. Valuation
P/E is 11.7x, at 61% of its 5y range (8.6x–13.6x) and 5% below the peer median of 12.2x. P/B is 1.0x against a 5y max of 0.8x, so it is above its own history, and 2% below peers at 1.1x (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/bkk/TTB/financials/ratios/) [Certain]. FCF yield (20.99%) and P/FCF are distorted by bank cash-flow volatility and should be ignored [Likely]. Mechanical fair value: base 2.24 THB, bear 1.04, bull 2.33 against a 2.72 close, so the price is 17.7% above base and 14.2% above bull. The reverse DCF implies 0.5% year-1 revenue growth (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/bkk/TTB/) [Certain]. The price already pays for the ROE gains. There is no valuation cushion [Likely].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROE (ratios page) falls below 8%.
- Net margin (income statement page) falls below 38%.
- Piotroski F (statistics page) falls to 3 or below.
- Shares outstanding change YoY (statistics page) turns positive, meaning dilution replaces the buyback.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Shares are down 5.19% YoY, so the buyback is real (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/bkk/TTB/statistics/) [Certain]. A transcript headline mentions a higher interim dividend (source: /quote/bkk/TTB/transcripts/710457-q3-2026/) [Likely]. Bank FCF is not meaningful: the FCF margin was 348.1% in FY2025 and 97.0% TTM, versus 237.8% a year earlier. This is the flagged direction change. It reflects working-capital and deposit swings, not earnings power [Likely].

## 6. Unusual items

Fitch kept the subordinated rating steady (source: https://www.bangkokpost.com/business/general/3308065/fitch-keeps-ttb-ratings-steady-following-ing-stake-sale) [Certain].
