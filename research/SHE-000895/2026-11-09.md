## 2. Quality
Returns are high: TTM ROIC 20.6%, ROE 22.5%, ROCE 28.1% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/she/000895/financials/ratios/). Gross margin rose from 13.6% (FY2021) to 18.8% TTM; operating margin 11.3% [Certain]. The moat is brand, scale and cold-chain distribution in processed meat, which is harder to replicate than commodity slaughter [Likely].
Direction is mixed: ROIC is 3.7pp below its 2-year high, ROCE fell from 32.1% to 28.1% in 12 months, and FCF margin fell from 10.8% to 6.7% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/she/000895/financials/ratios/?p=trailing). Margins held while returns eased, so the decline looks capital-driven (net debt/EBITDA moved from -0.31x to 0.72x), not a margin collapse [Likely]. Cause not confirmed with a primary source [Guessing].
