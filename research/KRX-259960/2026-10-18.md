# KRAFTON, Inc. (KRX:259960) — Core initiation — 2026-10-18

## 1. Business
Krafton develops and publishes video games, monetised mainly through live-service in-game purchases on PC, console and mobile [Certain]. Revenue is heavily concentrated in the PUBG franchise (PUBG: Battlegrounds and India's BGMI), supplemented by newer titles such as Subnautica 2 and third-party publishing/incubation deals [Certain] (source: news section of the fact sheet).

## 2. Quality
Unit economics are excellent: 100% gross margin, TTM operating margin 29.7% and FCF margin 21.4%, with FCF conversion at 113.8% of net income TTM — cash generation comfortably exceeds accounting profit [Certain]. The balance sheet is net cash (net debt/EBITDA -1.61x TTM) [Certain]. But returns are deteriorating: ROIC has fallen from 40.3% (FY2021) to 18.3% TTM and ROE from 18.2% to 11.7% over the same period, as the PUBG franchise matures and growth slows [Certain]. The moat is IP-based rather than structural — battle-royale/live-service gaming has low switching costs, and peers (NCsoft, Netmarble, Pearl Abyss, Com2uS) show the same hit-driven, franchise-dependent economics [Likely]. Single-IP revenue concentration in PUBG is the key quality risk: there is no durable reason competitors can't erode returns once the next hit title arrives [Likely].

## 3. Valuation
Every multiple sits at or below its own 5-year trough: P/E 10.3x (5y range 10.9x-41.4x), EV/EBITDA 4.3x (5.5x-25.1x), P/FCF 8.9x (11.8x-39.4x), P/B 1.1x (1.5x-4.7x) [Certain]. Versus the four-stock Korean gaming peer group the picture is mixed: Krafton is cheaper on EV/EBITDA (-21%) and P/FCF (-18%) but more expensive on P/E (+31%), EV/Sales (+23%) and P/B (+45%), reflecting its higher margins and net-cash balance sheet [Certain]. The base-case fair-value model implies +113.9% upside to 415,111 KRW; the reverse DCF shows the current price already assumes -14.5% revenue growth in year 1 fading to terminal growth — i.e. the market is pricing a continued PUBG decline, not stabilisation [Certain]. Fair value is one model estimate and not itself a buy signal, but the gap is unusually wide for a profitable, net-cash business.

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Net margin (income statement page) falls and stays below 15% TTM, beyond the FY2025/TTM step-down already seen (22.1%→18.8%).
- ROIC (ratios page) falls below 15% TTM, extending the five-year decline from 40.3% to 18.3%.
- FCF margin (income statement/cash-flow pages) falls below 15% TTM, from 21.4% now.
- Revenue (income statement page) declines YoY for two consecutive quarters, confirming PUBG-concentration risk is crystallising rather than merely priced in.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Krafton is net cash (net debt/EBITDA -1.61x TTM) and shares outstanding fell 3.05% YoY, consistent with buybacks [Certain]. FCF conversion of 113.8% of net income TTM means cash generation is funding this without leverage [Certain]. Combined with a falling ROIC, the capital-allocation question is whether management redeploys this cash into new IP (Subnautica 2, incubation deals) at adequate returns or simply defends the existing franchise; the fact sheet does not show a dividend or buyback-rate figure to assess pace [data unavailable].

## 6. Unusual items
Governance flag [Likely]: a Delaware court ordered Krafton's CEO to reinstate the ousted leadership of Unknown Worlds (the Subnautica 2 studio) after a plan to avoid a bonus obligation to the studio head was revealed in court (source: https://www.theguardian.com/technology/2026/mar/18/subnautica-2-publisher-krafton-ceo-reinstated-ai-chatgpt-failed-bid-avoid-paying-bonus). Krafton's own Q2 2026 earnings-call summary cites a net loss despite record first-half revenue and operating profit (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/krx/259960/transcripts/657616-q2-2026/), consistent with a one-off charge tied to that dispute; TTM net margin is nonetheless positive at 18.8% [Certain] — no conflict, but worth monitoring for further fallout at the Nov 4, 2026 print.
