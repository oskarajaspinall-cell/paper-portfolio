# RELX PLC (LON:REL) — Core initiation — 2026-11-16
## 1. Business
RELX sells information-based analytics and decision tools to professional customers through four areas: Risk (LexisNexis Risk Solutions), Scientific, Technical & Medical (Elsevier), Legal (LexisNexis) and Exhibitions. Revenue is largely subscription and transaction based, so it recurs. [Likely] Segment and geographic mix: [data unavailable] (the company site was blocked, so no North America share is stated). The macro overlay should treat the US as a probable ~10%+ exposure. [Guessing] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/lon/REL/)

## 2. Quality
A high-return, cash-generative franchise. ROIC is 23.6% TTM against 16.4% in FY2021, and operating margin is 31.7% against 25.5% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/lon/REL/financials/ratios/) [Certain]. FCF margin is 29.4% and FCF/net income is 126.4%, so earnings convert to cash (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/lon/REL/financials/cash-flow-statement/) [Certain]. The last year is flat to rising on every rolling metric, with no direction change flagged [Certain]. The moat is proprietary content and data embedded in customer workflows (legal, risk, research), so switching costs are high [Likely]. Management reported underlying revenue growth improving and unchanged full-year guidance at the Q3 update (source: https://www.nasdaq.com/articles/relx-plc-reports-7-year-date-underlying-revenue-growth-and-reaffirms-full-year-outlook) [Likely]. Net debt/EBITDA is 2.64x, up from 2.23x at FY2025, which is moderate [Certain]. ROE of 131.9% reflects buybacks and a thin equity base, not operating quality [Likely].

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Shares outstanding fell 2.63% year on year, so cash is returned through buybacks (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/lon/REL/statistics/) [Certain]. FCF conversion has stayed between 133.8% and 144.7% across FY2021-FY2025, with 126.4% TTM [Certain]. Leverage has crept up from 2.11x at FY2024 to 2.64x, consistent with buybacks and bolt-on spending exceeding FCF growth [Guessing].
