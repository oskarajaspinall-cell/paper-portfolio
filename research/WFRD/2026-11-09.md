# Weatherford International plc (WFRD) — Core initiation — 2026-11-09
## 1. Business
Oil & gas equipment and services: well construction, drilling and evaluation, completions and production/intervention, sold to national and international oil companies, mostly outside North America. Revenue follows customer E&P spending and rig activity [Certain] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1603923/000160392326000122/wfrd-20260930.htm). Russia was 9% of nine-month 2026 revenue [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1603923/000160392326000122/wfrd-20260930.htm), up from 7%, partly because Middle East revenue fell with the Iran Conflict. Russia is a sanctions and ruble exposure the macro overlay should check [Certain].

## 2. Quality
Returns are high: TTM ROIC 24.6% vs 4.8% in FY2021, and TTM FCF margin 12.3% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/wfrd/financials/ratios/). The direction is softer: ROCE fell 21.4% → 16.9% and operating margin 15.5% → 13.2% over 12 months; ROIC is 10.0pp below its 2-year high [Certain]. Cause is falling revenue (down 6% over nine months), Middle East disruption, weaker North America and higher SG&A [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1603923/000160392326000122/wfrd-20260930.htm). Moat is modest: oilfield services is cyclical, with scale and installed-base advantages in artificial lift and completions, but not durable pricing power [Likely]. Net debt/EBITDA is 0.52x, so the balance sheet is sound [Certain]. Altman Z 2.55 and Piotroski F 5 are middling (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/wfrd/statistics/).

## 3. Valuation
P/E 15.0x is at its 5y median and 25% below peers; P/FCF 10.6x is 36% below peers and at 28% of its 5y range. EV/EBITDA 7.3x is above its 5y maximum yet 35% below peers; P/B 3.1x is below its 5y minimum (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/wfrd/financials/ratios/). FCF yield is 9.46%. Base fair value is 117.55 (+39.2% vs the 84.43 close), bear 67.48, bull 144.33; DCF drives it, EV/EBITDA gives 72.60 [Likely]. The reverse DCF implies -6.9% revenue growth in year 1, so the price already prices a downturn [Likely]. Earnings are possibly near a peak (margins off highs), which makes the DCF base optimistic [Guessing].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Operating margin (income statement, TTM) falls below 11%.
- FCF margin (cash flow statement, TTM) falls below 8%.
- ROIC (ratios page, TTM) falls below 18%.
- Net debt / EBITDA (ratios page) rises above 1.5x.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
FCF conversion is 143.0% of net income TTM, shares are down 1.30% year on year, and the company is redeeming its 8.625% 2030 notes (source: https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/10/30/3392208/0/en/weatherford-announces-redemption-of-8-625-senior-notes-due-2030.html) [Certain]. It completed the NCS Multistage acquisition for shares and cash (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1603923/000160392326000122/wfrd-20260930.htm) [Certain]. Deleveraging plus buybacks is shareholder-friendly; acquisition integration is a watch item [Likely].

## 6. Unusual items
