# Bank of Changsha Co., Ltd. (SHA:601577) — Core initiation — 2026-10-18
## 1. Business
Bank of Changsha is a Chinese city commercial bank classified as Banks – Regional (source: fact sheet, stockanalysis.com) [Certain]. It earns net interest income from the spread between loans/investments and deposits, plus fee income from wealth management, cards, settlement and interbank services [Likely], typical of a regional lender serving corporate and retail customers in its home province.

## 2. Quality
ROE has fallen every year from 12.8% (FY2021) to 10.0% TTM (-2.8pp) [Certain], even as net margin expanded to 46.6% TTM (+2.0pp) [Certain] — consistent with sector-wide net interest margin compression offset by cost/provision efficiency [Likely]. ROIC, ROCE and EV/EBITDA are not computed for a bank, and the reported FCF margin (305.6% TTM) and FCF/NI conversion (656.0% TTM) are not meaningful quality signals for a deposit-funded lender [Certain] — deposit inflows inflate operating cash flow. Piotroski F-score is 4 of 9, middling [Certain]. A regional banking licence and provincial deposit franchise are real but modest barriers to entry [Likely]; the bank competes with much larger national and joint-stock banks for the same Hunan customers, capping pricing power [Likely]. The fact sheet carries no NPL ratio, provision coverage or capital-adequacy data — a material gap for judging a bank's durability that stockanalysis.com does not publish [Certain].

P/B of 0.4x sits 30% below the peer median of 0.6x and near the low end of its own 5y range (30th percentile, 0.4x–0.6x) [Certain].
