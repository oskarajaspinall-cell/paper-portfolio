# Yunnan Aluminium Co., Ltd. (SHE:000807) — Core initiation — 2026-10-17

## 1. Business
Yunnan Aluminium produces and sells aluminum in China through three segments: Alumina, Primary Aluminum and Other (cast/wrought alloy ingots, aluminum home furnishings, welding wire, refined remelt ingots) (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/she/000807/). Revenue is earned selling these products into domestic Chinese markets; profitability tracks the alumina/primary-aluminum price cycle. [Certain]

## 2. Quality
Returns are currently excellent: TTM ROIC 47.9%, ROCE 35.0%, ROE 33.6%, all well above the FY2021-25 averages (source: factsheet Quality table). But the jump is driven by a cyclical margin spike — TTM gross margin 25.9% and net margin 16.6% vs FY2025's 16.8%/10.1% — not by a step-change in the underlying business [Likely]. Aluminum smelting is a scale commodity business; Yunnan Aluminium's integration from alumina to primary metal and a net-cash balance sheet (net debt/EBITDA -0.89x TTM) support resilience through the cycle, but there is no evidence here of pricing power or a structural barrier that stops competitors re-earning these returns once the cycle turns [Likely]. Piotroski F-score 8/9 and Altman Z 10.25 both indicate low near-term financial-distress risk (source: factsheet Other table). The quality picture is therefore: a financially strong, well-run cyclical producer at a cycle high, not a franchise with a durable moat. [Likely]

## 3. Valuation
TTM P/E (8.0x) and EV/EBITDA (4.4x) both sit below their own 5-year minimums (8.4x / 5.8x), i.e. the stock looks cheaper than at any point in five years on trailing multiples (source: factsheet Valuation table). But those trailing earnings are themselves a cycle peak (section 2), so the "cheap" signal is partly a denominator effect, not a genuine re-rating opportunity [Likely]. Versus peers (AA, CENX, KALU, CSTM) the picture is mixed: +16% premium on P/E but -24% discount on EV/EBITDA and -64% discount on P/FCF — no consistent read [Likely]. The through-cycle DCF + EV/EBITDA blend gives a base fair value of CNY 27.97 vs the CNY 25.21 close, +11% (bear -4.2%, bull +88.7%) (source: factsheet Fair value table) [Certain]. The price assumes the cycle holds near today's level, not a collapse back to FY2025 margins or a repeat of this year's upside. [Likely]

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Net margin (income statement page) falls back toward the FY2021-25 average of ~8-9%, confirming the TTM spike was a cycle peak now reversing.
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) turns positive and keeps rising, showing the net-cash cushion is being spent on capex or shareholder returns faster than cash is generated.
- ROIC (ratios page) falls back below its FY2021-24 average of ~19%, removing the current-return premium the base case assumes.
- FCF conversion (FCF/NI, cash-flow and income-statement pages) keeps falling below 100%, showing reported earnings are no longer backed by cash.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
FCF margin is a healthy 19.2% TTM and FCF conversion 115% (still >100%, though down from 129-171% in FY2021-24) (source: factsheet Quality table). The balance sheet is net cash (net debt/EBITDA -0.89x TTM), giving flexibility to fund capex or distributions without raising debt [Certain]. Shares outstanding fell 0.51% YoY, a modest net reduction rather than dilution (source: factsheet Other table). [Certain]

## 6. Unusual items
Single-country, single-currency exposure: all revenue and the cost base are in China/CNY, so the position carries China macro, commodity-policy and CNY-FX risk beyond the operating business itself [Likely]. Institutional ownership is a reported 20.0%, with insider ownership and short interest both unavailable on stockanalysis.com [data unavailable]. No governance or distress red flags in the data available (Altman Z 10.25, Piotroski F 8/9). Recent news on the fact sheet is limited to routine quarterly/annual filing notices, none of which changes the thesis.
