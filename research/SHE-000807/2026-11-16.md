# Yunnan Aluminium (SHE:000807) — Core initiation — 2026-11-16
## 1. Business
Integrated Chinese aluminium producer (Materials / Aluminum): it earns the spread between the aluminium price and its power, alumina and carbon input costs [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/she/000807/). Reports in CNY and sells mainly in China [Likely]; no foreign country was identified as ~10%+ of revenue, and none was verified [Guessing]. Earnings are therefore a leveraged bet on the aluminium price and on power costs.

## 2. Quality
Returns are strong but cyclical. ROIC was 28.0% in FY2025 and 59.1% on a rolling 12-month basis, against 17.0% at the FY2023 trough [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/she/000807/financials/ratios/). Operating margin is 26.8% TTM against 11.1% in FY2024, and FCF margin is 22.1% [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/she/000807/financials/income-statement/). The five-year fiscal-year trend in ROCE and operating margin is falling; the last year reversed it sharply (ROCE 17.9% to 38.0%). That is a commodity-price upswing, not evidence of a moat [Likely]. A commodity producer has no durable pricing power; the edge is cost position, which the fact sheet cannot show [Guessing]. The balance sheet is a strength: net cash (net debt/EBITDA -1.05x), Altman Z 10.53, Piotroski 8 [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/she/000807/statistics/). The 2026-10-29 Q3 filing exists but its content was not read [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/she/000807/filings/3903177/).

## 3. Valuation
At 23.04 CNY the stock trades on 6.0x P/E and 3.1x EV/EBITDA, both below the bottom of their fiscal-year-end five-year ranges (8.4x and 5.8x), with a 19.13% FCF yield [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/she/000807/statistics/). Peer median P/E is 8.3x and EV/EBITDA 4.4x, so the discount is about 28-29% [Certain]. P/B of 1.9x is above peers (1.5x) [Certain]. The reverse DCF implies revenue falls 8.3% in year 1 [Certain]. The mechanical base fair value is 30.43 CNY (+32.1%), bear 26.45, bull 57.60; NAV was unavailable and a through-cycle DCF carries the blend [Certain] (source: research/SHE-000807/factsheet-2026-11-16.md). The market is pricing a peak-earnings reversal, which is rational for a cyclical on TTM margins double the history [Likely]. Cheapness is flattered by peak profit.
