# Macro overlay: Brown & Brown, Inc. (BRO) — 2026-10-09
## Assessment
BRO is a fee-based insurance broker with no underwriting risk [Certain]; direct macro sensitivity is low (beta 0.59 [Certain], source: fact sheet). The real link: BRO funds its roll-up with debt (net debt/EBITDA 3.45x FY25, up from 1.52x FY21 [Certain]); the note's own bear trigger is net debt/EBITDA >4.0x. Chain: **real yields & high-yield credit spreads -> cost of debt for BRO's M&A-funded growth -> leverage/ROIC trajectory -> equity multiple**, with a smaller offsetting channel (short-term rates -> fiduciary float income -> modest revenue tailwind).

Facts (FRED, 2026-10-07/08): 10y real yield (DFII10) 2.92%, +0.49 (1m), +0.61 (3m); HY spread (BAMLH0A0HYM2) 3.09%, +0.41 (1m), +0.39 (3m); 10y breakeven (T10YIE) 2.35%, roughly flat (-0.02/+0.12) (https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=DFII10, id=BAMLH0A0HYM2, id=T10YIE). **(inference)** Flat breakevens with a sharp real-yield rise suggest a term-premium/policy-driven back-up, not an inflation surprise. 2y yield (4.77%) above fed funds (3.88%) proxies a market still pricing further tightening or a higher terminal rate **(inference, proxy)**.

BRO already trades 24-32% below peer/own-history EV/EBITDA and P/E after a -33% 12m decline — much de-rating is already priced [Likely]. **macro_bull**: yields/spreads roll over -> cheaper M&A funding, faster leverage repair, modest multiple relief. **macro_bear**: yields/spreads keep rising -> leverage lingers near the 4.0x bear trigger, ROIC compression persists, multiple stays capped. **Tilt**: toward bear, small — reinforces an already-identified fundamental risk but isn't the core driver; low beta and fee-based model cap sensitivity.
