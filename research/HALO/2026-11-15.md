# Halozyme Therapeutics (HALO) — Core initiation — 2026-11-15
## 1. Business
Halozyme licenses ENHANZE (rHuPH20 enzyme) to big pharma so IV biologics can be given subcutaneously, earning upfronts, milestones and royalties; it also sells auto-injector products. Q3 2026 revenue was $492,974k, of which royalties $341,001k, product sales $137,328k, collaboration $14,645k [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1159036/000115903626000145/halo-20260930.htm). Royalties come from ten commercial products [company-reported] (same URL). No single foreign country split was found [data unavailable].

## 2. Quality
Very high-return, royalty-driven model. TTM ROIC 29.8%, gross margin 76.0%, operating margin 56.7%, FCF margin 49.1%, FCF/NI 182.5% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/halo/financials/ratios/) [Certain]. Moat is patent and regulatory incumbency: partners' products are co-formulated and approved with ENHANZE, so switching is costly [Likely]. Q3 royalties rose strongly and guidance was raised (source: https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/halozyme-reports-third-quarter-2026-results-and-raises-full-year-2026-financial-guidance-302931338.html) [Certain].
Direction: ROIC fell from 44.2% to 29.8% and ROCE from 53.3% to 34.1% over 12 months, operating margin by 2.7pp. This is more likely acquisition-related capital growth (Elektrofi, Surf Bio) and a larger debt-funded base than weaker economics, since FCF margin rose 0.7pp [Likely]. Concentration in a few partners (Roche, Janssen, argenx) is the main weakness [Likely].

## 3. Valuation
Price 100.27 USD after a drop from a 134.00 high on 2026-10-30; RSI 26.7, 12.5% below the 50-day average but 17.4% above the 200-day (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/halo/history/). P/E 25.3x is in line with peers (24.9x, +2%) and 47% of its 5y range; EV/EBITDA 12.0x (13% of range, -21% vs peers); P/FCF 12.9x (3% of range, -21% vs peers); FCF yield 7.75% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/halo/statistics/) [Certain]. The price assumes slower royalty growth than the recent run. Mechanical fair value is bear 74.30, base 87.33, bull 172.15, EV/Revenue only (rNPV unavailable), so the base is 12.9% below the price; low reliability for a royalty stream [Likely].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Gross margin (statistics page) falls below 70% on a TTM basis.
- FCF margin (cash-flow page, TTM) falls below 40%.
- Net debt / EBITDA (ratios page) rises above 3.0x.
- Company-reported royalty revenue (10-Q) declines year on year, or the Merck subcutaneous Keytruda MDASE litigation is lost with invalidity of the patent.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Cash conversion is excellent (FCF/NI 182.5%). Shares fell 2.62% YoY; $400,000k was repurchased in the first nine months of 2026 [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1159036/000115903626000145/halo-20260930.htm). Net debt/EBITDA 1.93x, up from 1.45x in FY2024; Q3 included a new 2033 convertible issue and repayment of older notes [company-reported] (same URL). Bolt-on M&A (Elektrofi) widens the platform to Hypercon/Aerocon; a partner decision on returning a Hypercon target is due by February 2027 [company-reported] (same URL) [Likely].

## 6. Unusual items
- Short interest 12.11% of float (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/halo/statistics/) [Certain].
- IRA/CMS drug-price rules: new Risk Factor language on combination-product treatment; management says zero to minimal royalty impact (source: https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/halozyme-reaffirms-it-projects-zero-to-minimal-impact-to-royalty-revenue-through-at-least-2035-based-on-medicare-drug-price-negotiation-program-final-rule-for-ipay-2029-302932131.html) [Likely]. The cause of the post-October fall is not confirmed by a primary source; CMS-rule worry is a possibility [Guessing].
- Merck patent suit: a Dutch court found infringement of one MDASE patent and issued an injunction on Keytruda Qlex in several European countries [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1159036/000115903626000145/halo-20260930.htm). The 10-Q dropped its prior IP-uncertainty risk paragraphs, so a US outcome stays a key swing factor [Likely].
