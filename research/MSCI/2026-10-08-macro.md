# Macro overlay: MSCI Inc. (MSCI) — 2026-10-08
## Assessment
Two channels: (1) real yields -> discount rate -> valuation multiple — MSCI trades at 30.4x P/E / 23.7x EV/EBITDA, +16%/+51% above its 4-peer median, and reverse-DCF implies the price embeds ~28% year-1 revenue growth [Certain] (factsheet), a growth premium sensitive to real rates. (2) risk appetite/equity levels -> AUM in MSCI-linked products -> asset-based-fee (ABF) revenue, 26.6% of H1 2026 revenue [Certain] (10-Q, 2026-08).

Facts: US 10y real yield (TIPS) 2.91%, +0.61pp/3m, +0.48pp/1m [Certain] (FRED DFII10, 2026-10-06). US 10y nominal 5.27%, +0.72pp/3m [Certain] (FRED DGS10, 2026-10-06). 2y yield 4.79% vs effective fed funds 3.88% — a ~0.9pp premium, a proxy that the market isn't pricing near-term cuts **(inference)**. VIX 15.01, calm, -1.12pp/3m [Certain] (FRED VIXCLS). HY spread 3.03%, +0.36pp/3m but still low [Certain] (FRED BAMLH0A0HYM2). SPY +3.9%/3m while MSCI fell -8.7%, -12.6pp vs SPY [Certain] (factsheet HI). The AUM channel looks benign; the rate-driven compression channel is plausibly already firing **(inference)**, since the real-yield rise coincides with underperformance despite calm markets.

**Bull**: real yields plateau/fall, equity levels hold -> ABF reaccelerates, multiple re-rates toward peers. **Bear**: real yields keep rising and/or equities correct -> discount rate and ABF pressured together, with no cushion (base DCF fair value already 55% below price). **Tilt**: toward bear, moderate — the observed rate rise already lines up with relative weakness, and the valuation gap leaves little room to absorb a further move.
