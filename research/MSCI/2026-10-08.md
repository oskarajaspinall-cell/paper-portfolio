# MSCI Inc. (MSCI) — Core initiation — 2026-10-08
## 1. Business
MSCI licenses indexes, analytics, ESG/climate ratings and real-estate data mainly to asset managers and owners. Recurring subscriptions are ~70.6% of H1 2026 revenue [Certain] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1408198/000140819826000046/msci-20260630.htm). The remainder is asset-based fees (ABF, ~26.6% of H1 2026 revenue) tied to AUM in products benchmarked to MSCI indexes, so this slice moves mechanically with market levels [Certain] (same source).

## 2. Quality
ROIC is 39.9% TTM, up from 25.1% in FY2021 (+10.5pp); ROCE is 46.4%, up +18.3pp; operating margin 55.7% and gross margin 83.0% are both flat-to-rising over 5 years [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/msci/financials/ratios/, https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/msci/financials/income-statement/). FCF conversion (FCF/NI) has stayed above 100% every year for 5 years (117.7% TTM) — low capital intensity [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/msci/financials/cash-flow-statement/). Moat: indexes are embedded in client mandates, prospectuses and benchmarks, making switching costly and slow, which supports the subscription base's recurring-revenue visibility [Likely]. Two durability risks: ABF (26.6% of revenue) is not insulated from a market downturn [Certain] (10-Q, as above); and BlackRock alone is 11.8% of total revenue and 19.8% of Index-segment revenue [Certain] (same 10-Q) — a single-client concentration unusual for a "quality compounder." Net debt/EBITDA has risen from 2.46x (FY2021) to 3.16x (TTM) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/msci/financials/ratios/).

## 3. Valuation
At the last close of $555.38, TTM multiples (P/E 30.4x, EV/EBITDA 23.7x, EV/Sales 14.0x, P/FCF 25.3x) sit 9-16% below MSCI's own 5-year range minimums, but 16-110% above the 4-peer median (SPGI, MCO, FDS, LON:LSEG) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/msci/financials/ratios/). The fact sheet's base-case DCF/FCFE fair value is $249.65 (-55% vs last close); even the bull case ($334.18) implies -40% downside; only the secondary P/E method ($774.17) sits above price [Certain] (source: research/MSCI/factsheet-2026-10-08.md fair-value section). Reverse DCF shows the price embeds ~28% year-1 revenue growth fading to terminal by year 10 [Certain] (same source) — above the 5-year margin trend shown above. A September 2026 conference transcript cites management saying recurring net new sales (not revenue) were up 24-25% year-to-date [Likely] (source: /stocks/msci/transcripts/745231-barclays-24th-annual-global-financial-services-conference/), a different metric, not validating the DCF's growth assumption.

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROIC (ratios page) falls below 25% (the 5-year low) for two consecutive quarters — erosion of competitive positioning.
- FCF conversion, FCF/NI (cash-flow and income-statement pages) falls below 100% for a full fiscal year — cash economics weakening.
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) rises above 4.0x — leverage no longer supported by the recurring-revenue base.
- BlackRock's share of consolidated revenue or Index-segment revenue (10-Q disclosure) rises further, or BlackRock discloses in-sourcing/dual-sourcing of index benchmarks — direct evidence the switching-cost moat is failing.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Shares outstanding fell 4.85% YoY, showing active buybacks [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/msci/statistics/). FCF margin is 47.9% TTM with FCF conversion of 117.7%, funding both buybacks and dividends from internally generated cash [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/msci/financials/cash-flow-statement/). Leverage has risen alongside this (net debt/EBITDA 2.46x → 3.16x over 5 years), implying some of the shareholder return has been debt-funded rather than pure FCF [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/msci/financials/ratios/). MSCI completed the acquisition of First Street (climate-risk data) on 2026-08-03, a small bolt-on extending its climate-analytics product [Certain] (source: https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260803530906/en/MSCI-Completes-Acquisition-of-First-Street/, as listed in the fact sheet).

## 6. Unusual items
Short interest rose 24.7% versus the prior month to 1.94% of float — small in absolute terms but a large relative jump worth monitoring [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/msci/statistics/). BlackRock's client concentration (11.8% of total revenue, 19.8% of Index-segment revenue) is atypical and is treated above as an invalidation trigger rather than background [Certain] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1408198/000140819826000046/msci-20260630.htm). Otherwise none.
