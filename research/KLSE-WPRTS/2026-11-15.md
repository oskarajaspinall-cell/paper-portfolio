# Westports Holdings Berhad (KLSE:WPRTS) — Core initiation — 2026-11-15
## 1. Business
Westports operates the container terminals at Port Klang, Malaysia, under a long-term concession. It earns handling and storage fees on gateway (Malaysian import/export) and transhipment (carrier hub) containers [Likely]. This was not verified in a filing this run. ## 2. Quality
High-returning, cash-generative infrastructure-like asset [Certain]. TTM ROIC is 19.8% and ROCE 22.3%. Both fell over five fiscal years but rose over the last 12 months (ROIC 16.6% to 19.8%), so the long-run slide has reversed for now [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/klse/WPRTS/financials/ratios/?p=trailing). Operating margin is 46.1%, down 3.0pp in a year and 10.0pp over five fiscal years, so pricing or cost pressure is the open question [Certain]. FCF margin is 44.2% with FCF/net income at 131.9%, which shows strong cash conversion [Certain]. Net debt/EBITDA is 1.23x, up from 0.35x in FY2023, after FY2024 debt-funded investment [Likely]. Beta is 0.12 and Piotroski F is 6 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/klse/WPRTS/statistics/). The durable reason is a scarce concession-backed port with high capital intensity and local scale, which limits new entrants [Likely]. Margin erosion suggests this moat is not fully insulated from carriers' bargaining power or rival ports [Guessing].

## 3. Valuation
The price (MYR 7.32) assumes sustained high returns [Likely]. P/E is 19.2x, at the 94th percentile of its 5y range (16.0x-19.4x), and EV/EBITDA is 13.3x at the 91st percentile. Both are 25-29% above the peer median (15.4x and 10.3x; peers HKG:0144, HKG:1199, NSE:ADANIPORTS, PSE:ICT). P/B is 5.7x, above its 5y maximum of 4.7x. P/FCF is 14.7x, at the 24th percentile of its range, and FCF yield is 6.80%, which is the one cheap-looking measure. The reverse DCF implies 4.6% year-1 revenue growth, fading to terminal growth. Fair value is flagged unreliable because methods disagree 2.2x. The base is MYR 6.89 (-5.8%) and the range is bear 4.61 to bull 11.02 [Likely]. The stock is not cheap, and the case rests on trend and cash yield (12-month change +34.3%, 200-day average +15.8% below price) (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/klse/WPRTS/).
