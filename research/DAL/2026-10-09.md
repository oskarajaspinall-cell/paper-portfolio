# Delta Air Lines, Inc. (DAL) — Core initiation — 2026-10-09
## 1. Business
Delta runs a global hub-and-spoke passenger airline, selling economy and premium tickets, cargo and MRO services. A growing share of earnings comes from its SkyMiles loyalty program, monetized through co-brand remuneration from American Express (projected >$9bn in 2026), diversifying revenue away from pure ticket pricing [Likely] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/27904/000002790426000035/deltaairlinesannouncessept.htm).

## 2. Quality
ROIC rose from -5.8% (FY21) to 12.3% (FY25), 10.0% TTM; ROCE 9.7% TTM [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/dal/financials/ratios/). FCF conversion improved to 91.9% TTM and net debt/EBITDA fell from 4.38x (FY22) to 2.19x TTM [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/dal/financials/ratios/). Piotroski F-score 7 [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/dal/statistics/). Moat: scale, slot-constrained hubs and the premium/loyalty revenue mix give Delta cost and pricing advantages peers (AAL, UAL, LUV) can't easily replicate [Likely]. But the business is still commodity-cost-exposed: Q3 2026 adjusted fuel expense rose 62% YoY to $4.1bn, cutting adjusted pre-tax margin 120bp to 8.5%, and management cut FY26 EPS guidance to $5.10-$5.60 from $6.50-$7.50 and FY26 FCF guidance to ~$2.5bn from $3-4bn [Likely] (source: https://www.tipranks.com/news/the-fly/delta-air-lines-cuts-fy26-eps-view-to-5-10-5-60-from-6-50-7-50-thefly-news; source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/27904/000002790426000035/deltaairlinesannouncessept.htm). That confirms returns, while rising on a 5y view, are not yet insulated from fuel-cost shocks.

## 3. Valuation
Base fair value is $59.87 vs last close $82.14 — the price sits 27% ABOVE Delta's own-history DCF/EV-EBITDA blended fair value, i.e. the market already prices in more than the base case [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/dal/). The bull case ($158.43) needs 14.4% year-1 revenue growth fading to terminal (reverse DCF) [Certain]. EV/EBITDA of 9.5x is above its own 5y maximum (8.1x) — 191% of the 5-year range — though 8% below the peer median (10.3x) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/dal/financials/ratios/). P/E (13.6x) is 24% below peer median but only the 10th percentile of its own 5y range, and P/FCF (17.8x) sits near a range skewed by a prior distressed year. Net: cheap versus peers on earnings/FCF, expensive versus its own history on EV/EBITDA, and above its own DCF-based fair value — with the fuel-driven guidance cut just delivered, there is no margin of safety for a 12-month core position here.

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Operating margin (income statement) recovers above 9% for two consecutive quarters, showing fuel costs are being passed through via fares rather than compressing returns.
- FCF conversion (cash flow/income statement) holds above 80%, confirming cash generation is resilient through the cost spike rather than a one-off.
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) continues falling below 2.0x, confirming the deleveraging trend survives the guidance cut.
- Loyalty/co-brand revenue growth (filings) sustains mid-teens % growth, confirming the non-ticket diversification thesis.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Delta plans to repay over $2bn of debt in 2026; adjusted net debt fell $950m to $13.4bn versus year-end 2025 [Likely] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/27904/000002790426000035/deltaairlinesannouncessept.htm). Q3 capex was $1.4bn funding fleet modernization (13 deliveries) [Likely] (same source). Dividends of $141m were paid in Q3, a modest payout alongside debt paydown; no buybacks disclosed, consistent with management's priority of protecting the investment-grade balance sheet [Likely] (same source). Liquidity was $6.9bn including a $3.1bn undrawn revolver [Likely] (same source). FCF conversion has risen to 91.9% TTM [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/dal/financials/ratios/), though FY26 FCF guidance was just cut to ~$2.5bn from $3-4bn [Likely] (source: https://www.tipranks.com/news/the-fly/delta-air-lines-cuts-fy26-eps-view-to-5-10-5-60-from-6-50-7-50-thefly-news).

## 6. Unusual items
Short interest is 4.04% of float, up 16.3% month-over-month [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/dal/statistics/) — not yet large, but rising. Delta is weighing whether to launch its Riyadh route this month after Houthi attacks near the airport; CEO Ed Bastian said "safety is going to make the call" [Likely] (source: https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/09/delta-riyadh-flight-safety-houthi-attacks.html) — a single-route decision, immaterial to group earnings. No governance or accounting red flags identified.
