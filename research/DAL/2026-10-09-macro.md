Market pricing/inference: breakevens barely moved while real yields rose ~0.6pp in 3m — a growth/term-premium move, not an inflation scare **(inference)**. Delta's own DCF already uses 5.28% (current 10y) as risk-free, so today's level is priced; the risk is further rise.

**Bull**: real yields ease -> discount rate falls -> fair value (base $59.87) drifts toward price, narrowing the 27% gap. **Bear**: real yields extend their rise -> discount rate and debt refinancing costs rise further -> fair value falls further below price on a beta-1.34, 2.19x-levered name. **Tilt**: toward bear, small — rates reinforce, but don't drive, an AVOID thesis already built on the fuel-cost guidance cut and overvaluation.
