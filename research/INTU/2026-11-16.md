# Intuit (INTU) — Core initiation — 2026-11-16
## 1. Business
Intuit sells subscription and transaction-based financial software: QuickBooks, payroll, payments and Mailchimp to small businesses (Global Business Solutions), and TurboTax and Credit Karma to consumers. Revenue comes from software subscriptions, payment and lending fees, assisted tax and Credit Karma referral fees (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/intu/). Segment growth: Global Business Solutions +16%, Consumer +11% in fiscal 2026 [company-reported] (https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/896878/000089687826000037/intu-20260731.htm). No foreign country is flagged at ~10% of revenue in the sources read [Likely].

## 2. Quality
High and improving [Certain]. ROIC TTM 22.9%, up from 18.5% a year earlier; operating margin 28.8% (up 2.6pp); FCF margin 40.4% (up 7.9pp); FCF/net income 189.7%; net debt/EBITDA 0.18x (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/intu/financials/ratios/, https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/intu/financials/ratios/?p=trailing). Moat: QuickBooks and TurboTax data and switching costs, the small-business ecosystem (payroll, payments, lending) [Likely]. Threats are real: the new filing adds free and low-cost competition and government filing risk (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/896878/000089687826000037/intu-20260731.htm) [Certain]; AI-native entrants could erode pricing power [Guessing]. Fewer TurboTax federal units were offset by assisted tax and Credit Karma growth [company-reported] (same filing). Credit Karma Tax now offers free federal filing, which may cannibalise paid tiers [Likely] (https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20261020041718/en/Intuit-Launches-Credit-Karma-Tax-Free-Federal-Filing-With-Faster-Refunds-Expanding-Its-Daily-AI-Powered-Financial-Assistance/).

## 3. Valuation
The price has fallen 47.4% over 12 months and trades at 21.1x P/E against a 5y median of 60.1x, in the bottom 5% of its own range on P/E, EV/EBITDA and P/FCF (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/intu/financials/ratios/) [Certain]. Versus peers it is at -2% on P/E, -11% on EV/EBITDA and -33% on P/FCF. FCF yield is 9.30% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/intu/statistics/). Reverse DCF: the price implies only 1.5% year-1 revenue growth, fading to terminal growth, against recent double-digit growth. Mechanical base fair value is 568.43 against a 341.70 close; the bear case is 249.85 [Likely]. The market is pricing a material AI/free-product disruption of the franchise. The stock trades 2.4% below its 200-day average, with a 50-day average 9.1% below the 200-day, so the trend has not yet turned. Results are due Nov 19, 2026.

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Operating margin (income statement page, TTM) falls below 24%.
- FCF margin (cash-flow page, TTM) falls below 30%.
- ROIC (ratios page, TTM) falls below 16%.
- Global Business Solutions revenue growth (10-K/10-Q segment note, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/896878/000089687826000037/intu-20260731.htm) falls below 8% for two consecutive quarters.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Cash conversion is excellent, at 189.7% of net income. Shares fell 2.12% YoY, so buybacks more than offset stock compensation [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/intu/statistics/). Leverage is minimal. A May 2026 reorganisation is expected to cost about $315 million, with $293 million booked in fiscal 2026 [company-reported] (https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/896878/000089687826000037/intu-20260731.htm). The Sequence acquisition looks small [Likely] (https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20261015895580/en/Intuit-to-Acquire-AI-Native-Revenue-Management-Platform-Sequence/).

## 6. Unusual items
- Credit Karma user data is under an FTC order requiring security assessments through 2034 [company-reported] (filing above). Fraud, including AI-driven fraud, is a flagged risk, and tax authorities could block e-filing [Likely].
- Mailchimp becomes a separate segment from fiscal 2027, which gives less segment clarity until then [Certain].
- Short interest is 3.40% of float, up 10.7% on the prior month [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/intu/statistics/).
- The 52-week high is 682.27 and the low is 252.84; earnings on Nov 19, 2026 are a near-term event risk [Certain].
