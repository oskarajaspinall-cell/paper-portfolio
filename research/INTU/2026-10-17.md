# Intuit Inc. (INTU) — Core initiation — 2026-10-17

## 1. Business
Intuit sells subscription/usage-based financial software: QuickBooks (small-business accounting, payroll, payments), TurboTax/assisted tax, Credit Karma (financial matching), Mailchimp (marketing) and Intuit Enterprise Suite (mid-market). Revenue is recurring subscription fees, payment take-rate and advertising. Effective August 2025 Intuit combined Consumer, Credit Karma and ProTax into one Consumer segment; from fiscal 2027 Mailchimp reports as its own segment, separate from Global Business Solutions (source: 10-K MD&A, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/896878/000089687826000037/intu-20260731.htm) [company-reported] [Certain].

## 2. Quality
Returns keep rising: ROIC 22.9% TTM vs 14.7% in FY2022, ROE 23.6%, operating margin 28.8% (+8.1pp over five years), FCF margin 40.4%, FCF conversion 190% of net income (fact sheet, Quality table) [Certain]. Operating margin still expanded in FY2026 even after a restructuring charge under the "2026 Plan" (workforce reduction and site closures, expected substantially complete by Q1 FY2027) (10-K MD&A, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/896878/000089687826000037/intu-20260731.htm) [company-reported] [Certain] — cost discipline, not distress (Altman Z 6.82, Piotroski F 7). The FY2026 10-K adds new risk-factor language on free/low-cost competitors, public-sector free tax-filing programs, and AI-enabled fraud (10-K Risk Factors, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/896878/000089687826000037/intu-20260731.htm) [company-reported] [Likely] — a reminder that price, not AI-native share loss, remains the top reason DIY tax customers leave, as management said at the September investor day. Gross margin has slipped 1.2pp over five years, the one soft metric [Certain]. The embedded accounting/payroll/tax/payments ledger moat looks intact [Likely].

## 3. Valuation
At 330.17 (close 2026-10-15), well above the price at the prior initiation, every multiple still sits near the floor of INTU's own 5-year range: P/E 20.1x (3rd percentile of 18.9x–62.3x), EV/EBITDA 13.1x (1st percentile), P/FCF 10.2x (1st percentile), P/B 4.7x (2nd percentile) (fact sheet, Valuation table) [Certain]. Against ADP, PAYX, GPN and CRM, INTU trades 18–51% below peer medians on every multiple, with a 9.82% FCF yield [Certain]. The rally has not closed the gap: the base DCF+EV/Revenue fair value is 557.68 (+68.9% vs last close), and even the bear case (244.65) sits only 25.9% below it (fact sheet, Fair value table) [Certain]. The market is still pricing the guided fiscal 2027 "reset" close to a permanent step-down rather than a temporary deceleration, a harsher read than rising ROIC and margins support [Likely].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROIC (ratios page) falls below 15%, the FY2022 level, for two consecutive fiscal years.
- Operating margin (income statement) falls below 23%, the FY2024 level, showing restructuring and AI spend aren't converting to efficiency.
- FCF margin (cash flow/income statement) falls below 29%, the FY2024 level, meaning the FY2026 cash step-up was transitory.
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) rises above 1.0x, signalling a shift away from the current net-cash balance sheet.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
FCF conversion is 190% of net income and FCF margin 40.4%, against net debt/EBITDA of just 0.18x (fact sheet, Quality table) [Certain]. Shares outstanding fell 2.12% YoY, so buybacks continue to outrun stock-comp dilution (fact sheet, Other table) [Certain]. The balance sheet absorbed the FY2026 restructuring charge without leverage rising, and still funded the Sequence acquisition announced October 15, 2026 (deal terms undisclosed) (10-K MD&A, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/896878/000089687826000037/intu-20260731.htm) [company-reported] [Certain]. Capital allocation remains self-funded and shareholder-friendly [Likely].

## 6. Unusual items
The FY2026 10-K's segment changes (Consumer combined, Mailchimp split out from fiscal 2027) improve disclosure but break some year-on-year comparability (10-K MD&A, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/896878/000089687826000037/intu-20260731.htm) [company-reported] [Certain]. Credit Karma remains under an FTC order requiring a security program and biennial assessments through 2034 (10-K Risk Factors, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/896878/000089687826000037/intu-20260731.htm) [company-reported] [Certain]. Short interest is low (2.79% of float, +3.7% month-on-month) and institutional ownership is 92.3% (fact sheet, Other/News & sentiment) [Certain]. None of this is thesis-threatening today, but the new risk-factor language on free/public-sector tax competition and AI-enabled fraud is worth tracking through the fiscal 2027 reset [Likely].
