# Intuit (INTU) — Core initiation — 2026-11-08

## 1. Business
Subscription, transaction and advertising revenue from financial software. Global Business Solutions (QuickBooks, payments, payroll, Mailchimp) is 60% of fiscal 2026 revenue and Consumer (TurboTax, Credit Karma) 40% [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/896878/000089687826000037/intu-20260731.htm). Outside the US is about 8% of revenue, with no country at ~10% [company-reported] (same filing), so no country-risk overlay is needed. [Certain]

## 2. Quality
Returns are rising on both the fiscal-year and rolling-12-month views. TTM ROIC is 22.9% against 18.5% a year earlier, and operating margin is 28.8% against 26.2%. FCF margin is 40.4% against 32.5%, with FCF at 189.7% of net income (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/intu/financials/ratios/?p=trailing). There is no Direction change line: the last year confirms the five-year trend. [Certain]

The moat is the QuickBooks small-business ledger with payments and payroll attached, plus the TurboTax brand and data. Gross margin is 81.0% and net debt/EBITDA 0.18x (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/intu/statistics/). [Likely]

The 10-K delta adds risk language on free competitors, government filing systems and AI. TurboTax federal units fell [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/896878/000089687826000037/intu-20260731.htm). [Likely]

Free federal filing under Credit Karma Tax launched on 2026-10-20 (source: https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20261020041718/en/Intuit-Launches-Credit-Karma-Tax-Free-Federal-Filing-With-Faster-Refunds-Expanding-Its-Daily-AI-Powered-Financial-Assistance/). It is defensive and may cannibalise paid TurboTax. [Guessing]

## 3. Valuation
The price assumes almost no growth. The reverse DCF implies 0.6% revenue growth in year 1, fading to terminal growth (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/intu/). The stock is at the bottom of its own 5y range on every multiple:
- P/E 20.7x, against a 5y median of 60.1x.
- EV/EBITDA 13.5x, against 39.3x.
- P/FCF 10.5x, against 34.5x.

Against the peer median (ADBE, CRM, NOW, ADP, WDAY), P/E is 23% lower, EV/EBITDA 27% lower and P/FCF 36% lower. FCF yield is 9.49%. Base fair value is 581.05 against a 341.50 close; bear is 255.95. The 12-month change is -47.9%. The market is pricing an AI-driven loss of franchise value, which results to date contradict. [Likely]

Results are due on Nov 19, 2026, a near-term test. [Certain]

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Global Business Solutions segment revenue growth (10-Q segment note) falls below 10% for a full year; fiscal 2026 was 16%.
- TTM FCF margin (cash flow page, `?p=trailing`) falls below 30%.
- TTM ROIC (ratios page, `?p=trailing`) falls below 16%.
- Consumer segment revenue (10-Q) declines year on year for two consecutive reported periods, which would show free filing and AI eroding the base.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Cash conversion is excellent, with FCF at 189.7% of net income. Share count fell 2.12% in the last year (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/intu/statistics/), so buybacks more than offset stock compensation. At the current multiples, repurchases are accretive. Leverage is minimal. Management is reorganising: a May 2026 plan reduces headcount, with about $315 million of total cost [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/896878/000089687826000037/intu-20260731.htm). The announced bolt-on, Sequence, is a small Series A company (source: https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20261015895580/en/Intuit-to-Acquire-AI-Native-Revenue-Management-Platform-Sequence/). [Likely]

## 6. Unusual items
- Disclosure: the Credit Karma user data is under an FTC order requiring a security program through 2034 [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/896878/000089687826000037/intu-20260731.htm). [Certain]
- Seasonality: results on Nov 19 are fiscal Q1, a seasonally small quarter, so read them for QuickBooks and guidance rather than tax. [Likely]
