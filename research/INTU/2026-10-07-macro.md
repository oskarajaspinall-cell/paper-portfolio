# Macro overlay: Intuit Inc. (INTU) — 2026-10-07

## Assessment
INTU is a domestic, USD-only, debt-light (net debt/EBITDA 0.18x) SaaS business [Certain]; earnings are not macro-sensitive — no FX translation, no floating-rate funding, no commodity input. The one live channel is the discount rate: **real yields → equity risk premium → SaaS multiple**, because the bull case here is partly a multiple re-rating (P/E 17.3x vs its own 5y median 60.1x and peer median 22.0x) **(inference)**.

**Official data**: US 10y real yield (DFII10) 2.95% on 2026-10-05, up 52bp/~1m and 71bp/~3m; US 10y Treasury 5.31% (+83bp/3m); 10y breakeven inflation only +11bp/3m; effective fed funds 3.88% (+25bp/1m and /3m) (fred.stlouisfed.org, 2026-10-05/06) [Certain]. Flat breakevens alongside the real-yield jump mean the nominal move is real-rate/term-premium driven, not an inflation scare [Likely] — the channel that hits duration-sensitive multiples hardest.

**Market pricing (proxy, inference)**: 2y Treasury (4.84%) ~96bp above effective fed funds (3.88%) implies the market prices further tightening or a higher terminal rate, not the cuts priced earlier [Likely]. HY spreads widened 44bp/1m to 3.12%, a mild risk-appetite wobble; VIX 15.52 stays low — no broad risk-off [Certain].

**Bull**: real yields plateau/retreat (DFII10 toward ~2.5%) → ERP eases → multiple re-rates toward peer median alongside fundamentals. **Bear**: real yields climb past ~3.25% on a hawkish repricing → ERP stays elevated → multiple re-rating caps out even as ROIC/margins improve; FCF/buybacks unaffected.

**Tilt**: `small` — the thesis rests on fundamentals (rising ROIC/margins, trough valuation) and a debt-free balance sheet; rates affect re-rating speed, not thesis validity [Likely].
