# Macro overlay: Intuit Inc. (INTU) — 2026-10-17
## Assessment
INTU is domestically-earning (USD-only) and net-cash, so FX and credit spreads aren't transmission variables **(inference)**. The variable that matters: real yields -> discount rate -> DCF/multiple, since the fact sheet's fair value (base 557.68, +68.9% vs the 330.17 close) uses the 10y Treasury as its CAPM risk-free input [Certain, factsheet].

Facts: US 10y real yield (DFII10) 2.78% on 2026-10-15, +0.43pp/3m; nominal 10y (DGS10) 5.10%, +0.53pp/3m; 2y (DGS2) 4.77% vs effective fed funds 3.88% (https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=DFII10 etc., 2026-10-15) [Certain]. Market pricing: 2y yield ~90bp above fed funds is a proxy for the market pricing little further easing, not a cutting path **(inference)**; core CPI rose 2.83 index-pts/3m and breakevens drifted up only +0.11pp, so no fresh inflation surprise is priced **(inference)**.

INTU already de-rated from a 60x median P/E to 20x (3rd percentile of its 5y range) over the past year, overlapping this real-yield climb — rates look like a prior drag mostly already in the price [Likely] **(inference)**. Residual sensitivity: further real-yield rises mechanically compress the DCF fair value, capping how much of the 557.68 base-case gap can close; a stabilization/reversal is a plausible catalyst independent of fundamentals.

**Bull**: real yields stabilize/fall -> multiple re-rates toward peer median on unchanged earnings. **Bear**: real yields grind higher -> DCF fair value drifts down, delaying re-rating. **Tilt**: small, toward bear — the fundamental mispricing thesis (fiscal 2027 "reset" read too harshly) dominates; rates are a secondary swing factor on the multiple, not the earnings case.
