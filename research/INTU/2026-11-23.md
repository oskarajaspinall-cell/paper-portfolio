# Intuit Inc. (INTU) — Core initiation — 2026-11-23
## 1. Business
Intuit sells subscription and transaction-based financial software: QuickBooks and payroll/payments for small and mid-market businesses, TurboTax and Credit Karma for consumers, and (until sale) Mailchimp marketing tools. Revenue peaks in the January and April quarters. [Certain] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/896878/000089687826000045/intu-20261031.htm). Mailchimp is being sold to Bending Spoons for about $3.68 billion, closing early calendar 2027 [company-reported] [Certain] (same URL). No foreign country is shown as ~10%+ of revenue in the text read [Guessing].

## 2. Quality
Strong on the numbers. TTM ROIC is 22.7% (FY2022: 14.7%), operating margin 29.8%, FCF margin 41.3%, FCF conversion 188.0% of net income, net debt/EBITDA 0.33x, all rising or stable over five years and the last twelve months (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/intu/financials/ratios/, https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/intu/financials/ratios/?p=trailing). [Certain] Gross margin is 80.6%, slightly down over five years. Moat: tax and accounting data, brand and switching costs in QuickBooks. [Likely]
The moat is being questioned, not the past results. The filing adds risk language on free and low-cost competitors, government free-filing programmes, and AI-enabled fraud (source: research/INTU/sec-delta-2026-11-23.md). [Certain] Press reports senior departures including TurboTax's chief product officer and the QuickBooks general manager (source: https://www.businessinsider.com/intuit-executive-departures-stock-slides-ai-2026-11). [Likely] Whether AI agents become "front doors" that disintermediate TurboTax is unresolved. [Guessing]

## 3. Valuation
The price assumes a lot of damage. P/E is 15.4x, below the 5y minimum of 18.9x (median 60.1x); EV/EBITDA 10.3x vs 12.6x minimum; P/FCF 7.9x; FCF yield 12.68%. Versus peers (ADBE, CRM, ADSK, WDAY) it trades 36% below on P/E and 43% below on EV/EBITDA (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/intu/statistics/). [Certain] The reverse DCF implies revenue shrinking 5.8% in year 1. [Certain] The mechanical base fair value (604.53 vs close 270.44) is flagged unreliable; weigh it lightly. [Certain]
Price is 22.1% below its 200-day average, 12-month change -58.4%, RSI 26.8: a falling knife, with guidance cut and a tech sell-off (source: https://www.marketwatch.com/story/intuits-downbeat-outlook-deepens-wall-streets-anxiety-about-software-companies-15b878e5; https://invezz.com/news/2026/11/20/intuit-stock-falls-9-on-fy27-revenue-forecast-cut-mailchimp-sale-analysts-cut-pts/).
