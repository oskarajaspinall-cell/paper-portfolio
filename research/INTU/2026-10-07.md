# Intuit Inc. (INTU) — Core initiation — 2026-10-07

## 1. Business
Intuit sells subscription and usage-based financial software: QuickBooks (small-business accounting, payroll, payments), TurboTax/assisted tax filing, Credit Karma (financial matching/lead-gen), Mailchimp (marketing), and Intuit Enterprise Suite (mid-market). Revenue is recurring subscription fees, payment/transaction take-rate and advertising. Its "big bets" (AI-driven assisted tax, the money platform, mid-market expansion) are now ~30% of revenue and each growing over 30% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/intu/transcripts/612260-investor-day-2026/) [Certain].

## 2. Quality
Returns are rising: ROIC 22.9% TTM vs 14.7% in FY2022, ROE 23.6%, operating margin up 8.1pp over five years to 28.8%, FCF margin 40.4%, FCF conversion 190% of net income (source: fact sheet, Quality table) [Certain]. The moat is an embedded small-business/tax ledger (accounting, payroll, payments, tax in one system) that is costly to switch out of, reinforced by proprietary data and brand: management says Intuit saw no meaningful share loss to AI-native tax entrants this season (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/intu/transcripts/745412-goldman-sachs-communacopia-technology-conference-2026/) [Likely]. One real crack: management said the top reason DIY tax customers left was price, and guided fiscal 2027 to a slower "reset year" while still committing to high-teens EPS growth longer term (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/intu/transcripts/612260-investor-day-2026/) [Certain]. That is a growth-rate risk, not a returns-on-capital one — ROIC/ROE/margins are still expanding through the deceleration [Likely].

## 3. Valuation
Every multiple sits below its own 5-year trough: P/E 17.3x vs a 5y range of 18.9x–62.3x, EV/EBITDA 11.5x vs 12.6x–40.8x, P/FCF 8.9x vs 10.0x–38.6x, P/B 4.0x vs 4.6x–11.1x (source: fact sheet, Valuation table) [Certain]. Against peers ADP, PAYX, ADBE and WDAY, INTU is cheaper on every measure: P/E -22%, EV/EBITDA -22%, EV/Sales -21%, P/FCF -47%, P/B -54% to peer medians, with an 11.2% FCF yield [Certain]. The stock is down 57% over 12 months and trades 25.7%/10.2% below its 200/50-day moving averages (source: fact sheet, Price table) [Certain]. The price is assuming the growth deceleration flagged at the September investor day is a durable impairment of the franchise, not a temporary reset — a harsher read than the still-rising returns and margin data support [Likely].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROIC (ratios page) falls back below 15% (the FY2022 level) for two consecutive fiscal years.
- Operating margin (income statement) falls below 23% (the FY2024 level), showing AI/mid-market investment isn't converting to efficiency.
- FCF margin (cash flow/income statement) falls below 29% (the FY2024 level).
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) rises above 1.0x, signalling a shift away from the current conservative balance sheet.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
FCF conversion is 190% of net income and FCF margin is 40.4%, against net debt/EBITDA of just 0.18x (source: fact sheet, Quality table) [Certain]. Shares outstanding fell 2.12% year-on-year, so buybacks are outrunning any stock-comp dilution (source: fact sheet, Other table) [Certain]. High returns, minimal leverage and a shrinking share count give management room to keep self-funding the AI/mid-market "big bets" while still returning cash [Likely].

## 6. Unusual items
Two linked items drove this initiation's timing: management's September 17, 2026 investor day guidance to a slower FY2027 "reset year," and its own comment that price (not AI-native competition) is the top reason DIY tax customers churn — both are the probable cause of the stock's de-rating (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/intu/transcripts/612260-investor-day-2026/, https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/intu/transcripts/745412-goldman-sachs-communacopia-technology-conference-2026/) [Likely]. Otherwise clean: short interest is only 2.69% of float and fell 11.8% month-on-month, institutional ownership is 92.4%, and Altman Z / Piotroski F are 6.82 / 7 (source: fact sheet, Other/News & sentiment tables) [Certain].
