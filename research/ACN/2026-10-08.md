# Accenture plc (ACN) — Core initiation — 2026-10-08

## 1. Business
Accenture sells IT and management consulting, technology implementation (cloud, AI/data, digital engineering, cybersecurity) and managed/outsourced operations to ~9,000 clients in 120+ countries, with 814k employees across 52 countries (source: https://www.accenture.com/us-en/about/company-index) [Certain]. Revenue is largely project- and contract-based (bookings), billed on time-and-materials or fixed-fee/managed-services terms [Likely].

## 2. Quality
Accenture's talent pool, delivery network and 350+ partner ecosystem let it bundle strategy-to-execution work few single-point competitors can match (source: https://www.accenture.com/us-en/about/company-index) [Likely]. Returns are still high (TTM ROIC 26.8%, ROCE 22.8%, ROE 26.0%) but have declined steadily over five years (ROIC -14.4pp, ROCE -8.7pp since FY2022), consistent with margin pressure as AI tooling commoditizes parts of delivery work (fact sheet Quality table, source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/acn/financials/ratios/) [Likely]. Operating margin has held flat-to-up (15.2%→15.8%) and FCF conversion is strong and rising (138.9% of net income TTM), so the moat — switching costs from embedded systems work, plus scale/brand — is eroding on returns but not profitability [Likely]. Balance sheet is effectively net-cash (net debt/EBITDA 0.04x). The 2026-10-07 news that an Accenture contractor was removed from an FBI engagement over a data-breach role (fact sheet News & sentiment, source: https://www.tipranks.com/news/the-fly/fbi-removes-accenture-contractor-over-role-in-data-breach-reuters-says-thefly-news) is a reputational/governance flag, but scope and financial impact are unconfirmed [Guessing].

## 3. Valuation
At 14.5x P/E and 8.4x EV/EBITDA, ACN trades near the bottom of its own 5-year range (4% and 1% percentile respectively, vs 5y medians of 26.5x/17.3x), implying the market is pricing continued margin/ROIC compression rather than a return to prior multiples (fact sheet Valuation table, source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/acn/financials/ratios/) [Likely]. Versus peers (IBM, CTSH, INFY, EPAM), ACN is slightly expensive on earnings/EBITDA multiples (P/E +7%, EV/EBITDA +8% vs peer median) but cheap on cash generation (P/FCF 10.1x vs 11.3x peer median, -11%; FCF yield 9.91%) (fact sheet) [Likely]. The combination — a 5y-low multiple on a still-high-ROIC, net-cash, highly cash-generative business — suggests the de-rating already discounts a meaningful slowdown, leaving asymmetric upside if revenue growth stabilizes, but the declining-ROIC trend means this is a "cheap for a reason" situation, not an obvious re-rating catalyst [Likely].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROIC (ratios page) falls below 20% (TTM is 26.8%, already down 14.4pp over 5y) — would confirm the moat is being competed away, not just mean-reverting.
- FCF margin (cash flow/income statement) falls below 12% (TTM 15.7%) — cash-generation quality deteriorating.
- Net margin (income statement) falls below 9% (TTM 11.3%) — pricing/cost pressure spreading to the P&L, not just returns.
- Net debt/EBITDA (balance sheet/income statement) rises above 1.0x (TTM 0.04x) — balance-sheet discipline breaking down.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Shares outstanding fell 2.36% year-on-year (fact sheet Other table, source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/acn/statistics/), consistent with active buybacks funded by free cash flow rather than debt, given the near-zero net debt/EBITDA (0.04x) [Likely]. FCF conversion of 138.9% of net income (TTM) means cash generation comfortably exceeds reported earnings, giving room for buybacks/dividends without leverage; specific dividend-per-share and total payout figures are [data unavailable] on the fact sheet pages used.

## 6. Unusual items
Short interest is low (4.17% of float) but rose 6.4% month-on-month, and institutional ownership is high (83.8%) (fact sheet News & sentiment) [Likely] — no distress signal.
