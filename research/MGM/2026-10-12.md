# MGM Resorts International (MGM) — Core initiation — 2026-10-12
## 1. Business
MGM owns and operates Las Vegas Strip and regional U.S. casino resorts (gaming, rooms, food & beverage, entertainment) but leases most of that real estate back from VICI Properties under a 25-year master lease at ~$736m/yr rent [Certain] (source: sec.gov 10-Q). It also holds a 50% stake in BetMGM, a U.S. online sports-betting/iGaming joint venture [Certain] (source: sec.gov 10-Q). Revenue is resort operating income plus equity income from BetMGM.

## 2. Quality
ROIC is rising but still low (3.6% TTM, from -1.2% FY22), and net margin has compressed to 2.4% TTM from 13.3% FY21 even as FCF margin held at 8.4% [Certain] (source: fact sheet, ratios/income statement pages). The headline 350.9% FCF/NI conversion is inflated by depressed net income, not stronger cash generation — not a genuine quality signal [Likely]. BetMGM turned equity-income positive in H1 2026 ($30.9m, vs years of losses), a real structural improvement [Certain] (source: sec.gov 10-Q). Las Vegas Strip scarcity and regulated gaming licenses give some pricing power, but the business carries a $23.9bn operating lease liability to VICI fixing ~$736m/yr of rent for 25 years, versus only $6.1bn of funded debt [Certain] (source: sec.gov 10-Q) — any revenue downturn hits equity holders disproportionately given this fixed cost. With ROIC still below a reasonable cost of capital, the "durable good business" case is not yet proven.

## 3. Valuation
P/E (18.3x) is above the peer median (14.0x, +31%) but in the cheap half of its own 5y range (24th percentile) [Certain] (source: fact sheet). EV/EBITDA (15.5x) is 88% above the peer median even though below its own 5y low, because the lease-heavy EV is not comparable to peers with less sale-leaseback debt [Likely]. P/FCF (4.9x) and EV/Sales (2.0x) screen cheap vs peers (-49%/-22%), and FCF yield is 20.3%, consistent with the market pricing material downside — reinforced by a reverse DCF implying -7.7% revenue decline in year 1 fading to terminal growth (source: fact sheet). The model's base fair value of $129.85 (+343.6% vs the $29.27 close) looks implausible given how the lease liability distorts DCF/EV inputs; treat as [Guessing], not a usable base case. Net: multiples conflict across methods — cheap on cash-flow measures, expensive on EV/EBITDA — with an unusually wide, unreliable fair-value range.

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROIC (ratios page) falls back below 2% for two consecutive quarters, reversing the current recovery.
- Net debt/EBITDA, site definition (ratios page) rises above 13x, showing the fixed lease burden outpacing EBITDA growth.
- BetMGM equity income (10-Q/earnings release) turns negative again for two consecutive quarters, reversing H1 2026 profitability.
- Gross margin (income statement) falls below 44%, extending its 5-year decline.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
The board authorized $2.0bn buyback programs in Nov 2023 and Apr 2025; $1.4bn remains available after ~7m shares were repurchased for $253m in H1 2026, consistent with the -9.1% YoY share count [Certain] (source: sec.gov 10-Q, fact sheet). FCF margin is 8.4% TTM, but the elevated FCF/NI conversion reflects thin net income rather than stronger incremental cash generation [Likely]. No dividend. Capital returns via buybacks continue while the balance sheet carries $6.1bn of funded debt plus the $23.9bn VICI lease liability, leaving limited room for a large, unrelated acquisition (see Unusual items) [Likely].

## 6. Unusual items
Media company People Inc. reportedly withdrew a $48.30/share proposal to buy MGM, and management has since discussed MGM instead bidding for People Inc. [Likely] (source: https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/30/mgm-barry-diller-people-inc-casino-dealmaking.html). A reverse acquisition of an unrelated media business would be a capital-allocation red flag if pursued [Guessing]; no 8-K confirms a signed deal (source: sec.gov EDGAR browse, CIK 0000789570). Securities-law "investigation" notices tied to the withdrawn offer are standard solicitation, not evidence of wrongdoing [Likely] (source: https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/10/07/3376289/0/en/mgm-investor-reminder-bfa-law-reminds-mgm-resorts-shareholders-of-the-ongoing-investigation-into-the-48-30-per-share-offer.html). Short interest is elevated at 10.51% of float and still rising [Certain] (source: fact sheet, stockanalysis.com statistics page); RSI of 13 shows a deeply oversold state at a fresh 52-week low, consistent with event-driven uncertainty rather than a deteriorating core business [Likely].
