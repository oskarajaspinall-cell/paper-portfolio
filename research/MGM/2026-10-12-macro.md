# Macro overlay: MGM Resorts International (MGM) — 2026-10-12
## Assessment
MGM carries ~$6.1bn of funded debt plus a $23.9bn VICI lease liability (fixed ~$736m/yr rent), making its discount rate and refinancing cost more rate-sensitive than typical consumer-discretionary peers [Certain] (source: research note, sec.gov 10-Q). Chain: rising real yields and widening high-yield spreads → higher discount rate/refinancing cost → wider equity risk premium → multiple compression for this highly-levered, fixed-cost balance sheet.

**Facts** (FRED, 2026-10-08/09): 10y real yield (DFII10) 2.87%, +56bp/3m; 10y nominal (DGS10) 5.22%, +68bp/3m; 2y (DGS2) 4.75%, +59bp/3m; fed funds (DFF) 3.88%, +26bp/3m; HY spread (BAMLH0A0HYM2) 3.15%, +45bp/3m; breakeven (T10YIE) 2.33%, flat (-4bp/1m); VIX 15.41, -0.43pt/3m.

**Market pricing** (proxy): 2y yield ~87bp above fed funds implies markets price a higher-for-longer path, not cuts [Likely] **(inference)**. Flat breakevens with rising real yields point to a real-rate/term-premium repricing, not an inflation shock [Likely] **(inference)**.

**Surprise direction**: a hawkish surprise (yields/spreads widen further) raises MGM's discount rate and refinancing cost — bear. A dovish surprise (yields/spreads ease) lowers financing costs, supportive of re-rating off distressed multiples (P/FCF 4.9x, FCF yield 20.3%) — bull.

VIX near cycle lows shows risk appetite is calm even as MGM fell -37% in 3 months, confirming the crash is dominated by idiosyncratic factors (withdrawn Diller/People Inc. bid, litigation) rather than systemic macro stress [Likely] **(inference)** — macro is secondary, not primary, here.
