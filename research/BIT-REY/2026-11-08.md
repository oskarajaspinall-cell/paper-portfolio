## 2. Quality
Returns are high and stable: ROIC 24.3% TTM against 26.0% in FY2025 and 26.9% in FY2021; operating margin 15.2% TTM (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/bit/REY/financials/ratios/) [Certain]. Net debt/EBITDA is -0.92x, so there is net cash, and the Altman Z is 4.36 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/bit/REY/statistics/) [Certain]. A margin of that size in a labour-based services industry suggests specialist positioning and pricing power rather than commodity staffing [Likely]. The moat is soft, though: client relationships and talent, not scale or IP, and competitors can hire the same people [Guessing]. Gross margin is flat (17.1% TTM against 17.5% in FY2021), so there is no evidence of widening differentiation. Piotroski F is 6. The Q1 2026 results showed higher revenue, EBITDA and EBIT than a year earlier (source: https://www.tipranks.com/news/the-fly/reply-spa-reports-q1-revenue-eur-645m-vs-eur-607-5m-last-year-thefly-news) [Likely].

## 3. Valuation
At 130.60 EUR the shares trade on 18.7x P/E, 9.3x EV/EBITDA and 18.1x P/FCF (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/bit/REY/financials/ratios/). All are in the bottom of their own 5y ranges (P/E 6th percentile, EV/EBITDA 1st, P/FCF 12th) [Certain]. Against peers (CAP, SOP, ACN, EPAM) the P/E (+6%) and EV/EBITDA (+2%) are in line, but P/FCF (+56%) and P/B (+53%) carry a premium that the higher returns largely justify [Likely]. FCF yield is 5.54%. Base fair value is 139.20 EUR (+6.6%), with a bear of 96.55 (-26.1%) and a bull of 191.85 (+46.9%): limited base upside and a skewed-down bear case (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/bit/REY/). The reverse DCF implies 6.6% year-1 revenue growth, which leaves little margin for error against recent growth [Likely]. The price is up 31.1% in six months, so some re-rating has already happened [Certain].

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
FCF conversion is 102.4% TTM (111.7% in FY2025), so earnings are backed by cash [Certain]. The share count is down 1.18% year on year, a modest buyback or no dilution [Likely]. Cash exceeds debt (net debt/EBITDA -0.92x), leaving room for bolt-on acquisitions, which are the likely use in this model [Guessing].
