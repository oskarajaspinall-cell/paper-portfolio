# National Aluminium Company (NSE:NATIONALUM) — Core initiation — 2026-10-24
## 1. Business
State-controlled integrated bauxite-alumina-aluminium producer in Odisha. It earns money by selling alumina and primary aluminium at LME-linked prices against a largely captive, low-cost bauxite base, so margins track commodity prices more than volumes [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/nse/NATIONALUM/). The Q4 FY2026 call said alumina prices are pressured by global oversupply while aluminium prices are elevated but expected to normalize [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/nse/NATIONALUM/transcripts/543695-q4-25-26/).
## 2. Quality
Returns are excellent at today's prices but cyclical. ROIC was 13.6% in FY2024 and 42.5% in FY2026 (TTM 48.1%); operating margin went from 16.2% to 40.4% (TTM 43.5%) (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/nse/NATIONALUM/financials/ratios/) [Certain]. FY2023 ROIC of 14.3% and FCF margin of -2.8% show what the trough looks like (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/nse/NATIONALUM/financials/income-statement/). Net cash is -1.06x EBITDA net debt in FY2026, so the balance sheet is a buffer [Certain]. The durable edge is a captive, low-cost resource position; the moat is cost position, not pricing power, and I see no protection from a price fall [Likely]. Q1 FY2027 was reported as record revenue and profit (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/nse/NATIONALUM/transcripts/658051-q1-26-27/) [Likely]. Piotroski F is only 5 [Certain].
## 3. Valuation
P/E is 8.2x (5y range 6.1x–14.1x, median 10.0x), EV/EBITDA 5.1x (median 4.6x), P/FCF 12.6x (median 11.9x), FCF yield 7.94%. Versus peers it is cheaper on P/E (-8%) and EV/EBITDA (-16%) but dearer on P/B (+65%) and EV/Sales (+55%) (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/nse/NATIONALUM/statistics/) [Certain]. Multiples are on peak-margin earnings, which are cheap-looking by construction. Mechanical base fair value is 263.86 INR vs a 301.75 close (-12.6%); bear 50.95, bull 451.88. The reverse DCF implies 4.0% year-1 revenue growth. The through-cycle DCF base (308.85) is roughly the price; EV/EBITDA on own history gives 173.87 [Likely]. The price assumes margins largely hold near peak, leaving no margin of safety.
## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Operating margin (income-statement page) falls below 30% on a TTM basis.
- ROIC (ratios page) falls below 25% TTM.
- FCF margin (cash-flow and income-statement pages) turns negative in any fiscal year.
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) rises above 0.0x, i.e. net cash is lost.

FCF conversion was 75.9% of net income in FY2026, down from 87.9% (falling 21.3pp over five years) as capex rose [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/nse/NATIONALUM/financials/cash-flow-statement/). Share count is flat (-0.01% YoY).
