## 2. Quality
Returns stepped up after FY2022 and have held: ROIC 17.2% TTM vs 4.0% in FY2022; ROE 19.2% TTM (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tpe/2618/financials/ratios/) [Certain]. Leverage fell from 3.63x to 0.36x net debt/EBITDA, and Piotroski F is 7 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tpe/2618/statistics/) [Certain].

Airlines are cyclical, so durability is the question. Peak-cycle margins are the risk: operating margin is 14.2% TTM vs 16.6% a year earlier, ROCE 12.4% vs 13.5%, and ROIC is 2.6pp below its 2-year high of 19.9% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tpe/2618/financials/ratios/?p=trailing) [Certain]. Rising fiscal-year trends hide a 12-month fade; ROIC is flat over the year [Certain]. A premium-cabin reputation and Taiwan hub position are a plausible but unproven moat [Guessing]. Beta is 0.51 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tpe/2618/statistics/) [Certain]. Altman Z of 1.83 is in the grey zone [Certain]. Quality is good but not moat-proof.

## 3. Valuation
The price assumes earnings fall back toward mid-cycle. P/E is 9.8x vs a 5y median of 8.3x, at the 16th percentile of its range, and 20% below the peer median of 12.2x. EV/EBITDA of 4.5x is below its 5y minimum and in line with peers (4.7x). P/FCF is 5.8x; FCF yield 17.39% [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tpe/2618/statistics/). P/B is 1.8x, at the top of its 5y range and 28% above peers, so the book multiple is not cheap [Certain].

The mechanical fair value (base 171.20 TWD) is flagged unreliable: methods disagree 5.0x. Weigh it lightly [Certain]. The reverse DCF implying year-1 revenue growth of -24.2% suggests a trough-earnings view is priced [Likely]. The stock is up 38.6% in 6 months and 21.4% above its 200-day average (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tpe/2618/history/), so cheapness on earnings multiples is partly being recognised [Likely]. Earnings are due Nov 17, 2026 [Certain].
