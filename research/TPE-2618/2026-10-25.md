# EVA Airways (TPE:2618) — Core initiation — 2026-10-25
## 1. Business
Taiwan's second-largest full-service carrier: passenger revenue (long-haul premium cabins, North America and Europe from Taipei) plus air cargo, with the Hello Kitty and Star Alliance brands as differentiators. Earnings are driven by yield, load factor, fuel and cargo rates; the currency is TWD (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tpe/2618/). [Likely]

## 2. Quality
Returns have stepped up: TTM ROIC 17.1% against 3.8% in FY2021, operating margin 14.5%, net debt/EBITDA 0.30x from 3.52x (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tpe/2618/financials/ratios/). [Certain] But this is an airline: ROIC was 4.0% in FY2022 and margins have eased from the FY2024 peak (operating margin 17.5% to 14.5%). [Certain] A durable moat is limited to Taipei hub position, a premium brand and Taiwan's tech-driven cargo flow; capacity, fuel and cargo rates are not controllable. [Likely] Piotroski F is 5 and Altman Z 1.69, mediocre (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tpe/2618/statistics/). [Certain] Current returns look closer to a cycle peak than a through-cycle norm. [Likely]

## 3. Valuation
P/E 9.0x sits at the low end of its 5y range (7.5x-21.7x, median 8.3x) and 6% below the peer median of 9.6x; EV/EBITDA 4.1x is below its 5y minimum and 5% under peers; FCF yield is 19.77% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tpe/2618/statistics/). [Certain] The multiples are on peak-like earnings, so cheapness is partly cyclical. The mechanical base fair value of 177.35 TWD (through-cycle DCF, 67% weight) against the 42.20 close is very wide and rests on a DCF whose bull case is extreme; the EV/EBITDA leg gives 45.98 base, close to the price. The reverse DCF implies -27.4% year-1 revenue growth, i.e. a downcycle is priced. [Likely] Treat the DCF upside with caution. [Guessing]

## 4. What would prove this wrong

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
FCF conversion is 175.7% TTM and 106.1% in FY2025, so profits are backed by cash, although airline capex (fleet renewal) is lumpy; FCF margin fell from FY2022.
