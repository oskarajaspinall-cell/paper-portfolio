# Atlantic Union Bankshares (AUB) — Core initiation — 2026-11-09
## 1. Business
Regional bank holding company (Richmond, Virginia) earning net interest income on commercial, construction and consumer loans funded by deposits, plus fee income. Loan mix is commercial real estate, commercial and industrial, and consumer [Certain] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/883948/000088394826000087/aub-20260930x10q.htm). No foreign-country revenue of ~10% or more; US domestic [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/aub/).

## 2. Quality
Mediocre-to-adequate, not a franchise with proven excess returns. ROE fell every year from 9.7% (FY2021) to 6.7% (FY2025), then recovered to 10.7% TTM; net margin followed (22.2% FY2025 to 34.7% TTM) (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/aub/financials/ratios/). The FY2025 dip and rebound look like one-offs rather than structural change [Guessing]. ROIC, operating margin and leverage ratios are [data unavailable] (normal for banks). Moat is local deposit and commercial relationships across Virginia and the mid-Atlantic [Likely], but 10.7% ROE is roughly a cost-of-equity return, so the moat is not demonstrated. Q3 2026 showed strong loan growth and solid credit, but margin pressure from rising funding costs; full-year loan growth and key income outlooks sit toward the lower end of guidance [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/aub/transcripts/711276-q3-2026/). FCF margin (35.9% TTM vs 210.9% a year earlier) is distorted by bank cash-flow items and is not decision-useful [Likely]. Piotroski F is 4.

## 3. Valuation
P/E 10.7x is below its 5y minimum (11.2x; median 14.4x) and about 3% below the peer median of 11.1x. P/B is 1.1x, in line with its own narrow 5y range (1.0x-1.1x) but 23% below peers at 1.4x; the discount reflects a lower-return profile [Likely]. FCF yield 9.97% and P/FCF 10.0x (86th percentile of own range) are unreliable for a bank. Mechanical fair value base is 46.43 USD (+18.0% vs the 39.34 close) but is flagged unreliable: methods disagree 3.2x (P/B+ROE base 30.55; FCFE DCF 78.20; DDM 24.49). The best method (P/B+ROE) is below the price, so the price already assumes ROE holds near current levels [Likely]. Cheap on earnings, fair on book [Likely]. ## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROE (ratios page) falls back below 8% on a TTM basis.
- Net margin (income statement, TTM) falls below 28%.
- Shares outstanding (statistics page) rise a further 5% or more year on year without matching earnings growth.
- Q3-style funding-cost pressure continues: the company cuts FY26 loan growth or income guidance below the stated range in a quarterly release (https://www.atlanticunionbank.com).

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Shares rose 20.64% YoY (statistics page), consistent with dilution from an acquisition [Likely]. The board authorised a $250.0 million buyback in May 2026 [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/883948/000088394826000087/aub-20260930x10q.htm), which should offset some dilution [Likely]. It also issued Tier 2 subordinated notes in Q3 [company-reported] (same URL), adding leverage. Dividend declared 2026-10-29 (https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20261029641532/en/Atlantic-Union-Bankshares-Corporation-Declares-Quarterly-Common-Stock-Dividend-and-Preferred-Stock-Dividend/).
