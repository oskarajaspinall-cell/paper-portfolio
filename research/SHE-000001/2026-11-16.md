# Ping An Bank (SHE:000001) — Core initiation — 2026-11-16
## 1. Business
Shenzhen-listed Chinese joint-stock commercial bank (Banks - Regional). It earns net interest income on corporate and retail loans plus fees from wealth management and cards. Operations are in mainland China and it reports in CNY, so there is no foreign-country revenue concentration (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/she/000001/) [Likely]. ## 2. Quality
Mediocre and slipping. ROE fell from 11.0% (FY2022) to 8.2% (FY2025) and 8.1% TTM, a falling trend (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/she/000001/financials/ratios/) [Certain]. Net margin rose to 47.2% TTM, which suggests cost control rather than growth [Likely]. The FY2025 call transcript summary says revenue and net profit declined while asset quality, cost control and retail transformation improved (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/she/000001/transcripts/403365-h2-2025/) [Likely]. No durable moat is evident: it is one of many national banks competing on price and young-customer acquisition [Guessing]. ROIC, ROCE and leverage are [data unavailable]. FCF margin and conversion are meaningless for a bank because deposit flows swamp them. Piotroski F is 4 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/she/000001/statistics/) [Certain]. Beta is a low 0.46 [Certain].

## 3. Valuation
P/E is 5.7x against a 5y median of 5.5x and a 29% position in the range, so it is not cheap against its own history. It is at a 22% discount to the peer median of 7.4x (n=4) [Certain]. P/B is 0.4x, at the bottom of its 5y range (8% position) and 15% below peers [Certain]. The fair-value model's base is 18.49 CNY against the 12.07 close, but it is flagged unreliable because its methods disagree 3.5x (base 6.05 to 21.08) [Certain]. I give it little weight. It trades near its 52-week high, 8.2% above its 200-day average, with +3.3% over 12 months and -7.1pp against SPY [Certain]. The market prices a low-ROE, no-growth bank. A discount to book is justified while ROE is under 9%, and the fair value is not reliable enough to argue otherwise [Likely]. (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/she/000001/statistics/)
