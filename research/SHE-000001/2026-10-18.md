# Ping An Bank Co., Ltd. (SHE:000001) — Core initiation — 2026-10-18
## 1. Business
Ping An Bank is a Chinese joint-stock commercial bank taking retail and corporate deposits and lending to households and businesses, earning net interest income plus fee income from wealth management and cards [Certain]. It is affiliated with the Ping An Insurance group ecosystem for cross-selling [Likely].

## 2. Quality
ROE has fallen every year shown, from 9.6% (FY2021) to 8.2% (FY2025/TTM), a -1.4pp trend (ratios page) [Certain] — earning less on equity each year, matching the sector-wide Chinese bank margin squeeze. Net margin rose (37.6%→47.3% TTM, income statement) [Certain], but for a bank this reflects provisioning/mix, not core profitability, while ROE falls. Piotroski F-score is a middling 4/9 (statistics page) [Certain]. FCF margin/conversion is extremely volatile (-206% to +385% over 5 years, cash-flow/income statement) [Certain] — for a deposit-taking bank this reflects balance-sheet deposit/loan flows, not economic earnings, so it carries little weight. The moat case rests on scale, licence and the Ping An ecosystem for cross-sell [Likely]; it is not obviously defensible against other large joint-stock and state banks facing the same property-sector and rate pressure [Likely].

## 3. Valuation
At 5.4x TTM P/E, the stock sits near the bottom of its own 5-year range (4.2x–9.6x, 23rd percentile) and 27% below the peer median of 7.4x (ratios/statistics pages, peers SHA:600036, SHE:002142, SHA:601166, SHA:601998) [Certain]. P/B of 0.4x is near its 5-year low (5th percentile of a 0.4x–0.8x range) and 39% below the 0.7x peer median [Certain]. The market is pricing continued ROE erosion and asset-quality risk rather than a going-concern discount, given the P/B sits above the 5-year floor [Likely]. The fact sheet's base fair value (17.98 CNY, P/B+ROE/FCFE blend) implies +56% upside and even the bear case (13.55 CNY) implies +18% [Certain]; this is one mechanical model and not a target — it is consistent with cheapness versus history and peers but should not be treated as a probability-weighted certainty [Likely].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROE (ratios page, TTM) falls below 7% — a further leg down beyond the current -1.4pp 5-year trend would mean margin/asset-quality pressure is still worsening, not stabilising.
- Net margin (income statement, TTM) falls below 40% — a reversal of the recent rising trend would signal credit-provisioning or funding-cost pressure overwhelming the bank.
- Piotroski F-score (statistics page) falls below 3 — a weaker fundamental-quality score than today's 4 would confirm deteriorating balance-sheet health.
- Shares outstanding (statistics page, YoY) grows above 5% for a second consecutive year with no matching ROE recovery — continued dilution compounding against a falling ROE erodes per-share value.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Shares outstanding grew 4.18% YoY (statistics page) [Certain], a modest dilutive drag on per-share returns that is not yet offset by the falling ROE trend. Reported FCF margin and FCF/NI conversion are large and swing wildly year to year (cash-flow/income statement) [Certain]; as noted in Quality, this reflects deposit and loan balance movements rather than distributable cash, so it is not a reliable guide to capital return capacity for this business. The fact sheet shows no dividend-yield figure, so payout policy cannot be assessed from stockanalysis.com data [data unavailable].

## 6. Unusual items
Institutional ownership is low at 7.9% (statistics page) [Certain], consistent with a concentrated, strategic ownership structure typical of Ping An Group-affiliated banks [Likely] — a governance factor worth monitoring but not independently verified here. Sector-level reporting cited in the fact sheet's news feed describes Chinese banks increasingly using satellite monitoring to track borrower collateral amid rising bad-loan risk (source: https://www.scmp.com/economy/china-economy/article/3351315/default-our-stars-chinas-banks-use-satellites-track-assets-sky-high-scrutiny) [Guessing] — directionally consistent with the ROE decline above but not company-specific evidence.
