# Macro overlay: Ping An Bank Co., Ltd. (SHE:000001) — 2026-10-18
## Assessment
ROE has fallen every year since FY2021 (9.6%→8.2% TTM) and the note ties this to a "sector-wide Chinese bank margin squeeze" [Certain] — a China domestic-policy/property-credit story, not a US-macro one. Chain: PBOC policy rate (LPR/RRR) and property-sector credit quality → net interest margin and loan-loss provisioning → bank earnings/ROE **(inference)**.

What's priced: the stock trades at 5.4x P/E (23rd pct of its own 5y range, -27% vs peers) and 0.4x P/B (5th pct, -39% vs peers; both fact-sheet, 2026-10-18) [Certain] — the market already prices continued margin erosion as structural, not an acute credit event (P/B sits just above its 5y floor) [Likely] **(inference)**. PBOC's own LPR/RRR page (pbc.gov.cn) listed only titles, no rate levels, newest dated 2025-12-22, so no fresh policy reading is confirmable [data unavailable]. FRED facts: the US 10y yield rose to 5.10% (+0.53pp/3m, 2026-10-15, fred.stlouisfed.org/DGS10) and the dollar index rose to 121.6 (+1.1%/3m, 2026-10-09, DTWEXBGS), yet CNY/USD *appreciated* to 6.692 (-0.085/3m, 2026-10-09, DEXCHUS) — mild evidence against capital-outflow stress despite US tightening [Likely] **(inference)**.

Bull: policy easing / property stabilization lifts NIM and credit costs faster than priced → ROE re-stabilizes, multiple re-rates toward peers (0.4x→0.7x P/B). Bear: policy stays tight or property stress deepens → ROE breaks below 7% (an existing invalidation trigger) → further de-rating even off already-low multiples. Tilt: neutral/small — no confirmed fresh policy surprise, and the margin-squeeze risk is largely already in the price.
