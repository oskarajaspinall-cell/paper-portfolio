# Norsk Hydro ASA (OSL:NHY) — Core initiation — 2026-11-09
## 1. Business
Integrated aluminium producer: bauxite and alumina (Alunorte, Brazil), Norwegian hydro-powered smelting, rolled and extruded products, recycling and renewable power. Earnings are driven by the LME aluminium price, alumina and carbon costs, and Norwegian power contracts. ## 2. Quality
Good returns for a commodity producer: TTM ROIC 23.9% and ROCE 26.7%, with operating margin 21.7% and net debt/EBITDA 0.28x (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/osl/NHY/financials/ratios/) [Certain]. Cash conversion is weak and lumpy: FCF margin 3.2% TTM, down from 4.6% a year earlier, and 2.4pp below its 2-year high (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/osl/NHY/financials/cash-flow-statement/?p=trailing). ROCE is down 2.7pp over 12 months although the 5-year trend is flat [Certain]. The moat is cost position (low-carbon hydropower, integrated alumina) rather than pricing power, so returns remain cyclical [Likely]. ROIC 5y trend is falling (-8.8pp) from a 2022 peak of 38.2% [Certain]. Alunorte gas supply costs are a live operating pressure (source: https://www.wsj.com/finance/commodities-futures/norsk-hydro-expects-further-hit-from-gas-supply-challenges-at-brazilian-alumina-plant-6e57d9e9) [Likely].
