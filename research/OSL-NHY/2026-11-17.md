# Norsk Hydro ASA (OSL:NHY) — Core initiation — 2026-11-17
## 1. Business
Integrated aluminium producer: bauxite and alumina (Alunorte, Brazil), power-based primary smelting in Norway, rolled and extruded products, recycling, plus energy and renewables. Earnings follow the aluminium price, alumina/carbon costs and Norwegian power; downstream and recycling smooth the cycle. ## 2. Quality
Returns are good for a commodity producer: TTM ROIC 23.9% and ROCE 26.7%, with net debt/EBITDA 0.28x, so balance-sheet risk is low [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/osl/NHY/financials/ratios/). Through the cycle, ROIC fell from 29.2% (FY2021) to 20.3% (FY2025) and ROE is 12.0% TTM, so returns are cyclical rather than structural [Certain]. The durable edge is low-cost, low-carbon Norwegian hydropower-based smelting and recycling [Likely]; this is not a moat against aluminium price swings.
Direction check: ROCE is down 2.7pp over 12 months (29.4% to 26.7%) and FCF margin is down 1.4pp (4.6% to 3.2%), 2.4pp below its 2-year high; ROIC is flat (-0.5pp) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/osl/NHY/financials/ratios/?p=trailing). Q3 core profit missed market expectations, with higher costs and lower realised prices guided [Likely] (source: https://www.reuters.com/business/aluminium-producer-norsk-hydros-quarterly-core-profit-misses-market-view-2026-10-23/).
