# Rollins, Inc. (ROL) — Core initiation — 2026-10-09
## 1. Business
Rollins runs 60+ pest-control brands (Orkin, HomeTeam, Fox, Critter Control) via ~800 company-owned and franchised branches in ~70 countries, serving 2.8m residential and commercial customers [Likely] (source: https://www.rollins.com/about-us/). Revenue mix is mostly recurring treatment contracts (termite, rodent, insect) plus one-time jobs; commercial and termite/ancillary lines are growing fastest, residential one-time/digital-lead demand is the current soft spot [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/rol/transcripts/653570-q2-2026/).

## 2. Quality
Returns are excellent and stable: ROIC ~24-26% and ROCE ~29-31% across FY2021-25, ROE rising to 37-39% [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/rol/financials/ratios/). Gross margin ~52-53% and FCF conversion >115% of net income show pricing power [Certain] (source: fact sheet IS/CF). The moat is route density/brand trust (99 consecutive quarters of revenue growth) [Likely] (source: https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/rollins-inc-reports-second-quarter-2026-financial-results-302832503.html), durable against small competitors but not the structural shift in residential lead generation. Google's May-2026 shift to AI Overviews drove ~50-60% of a swing from mid-single-digit to double-digit digital lead declines, now spanning Google, Gemini, ChatGPT and Claude [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/rol/transcripts/754827-u-s-all-stars-conference/). Door-to-door/builder brands (Fox, HomeTeam) kept growing double-digit, so the hit is channel-specific, not demand destruction, but a genuine risk to the flagship brand's funnel [Likely]. Leverage stays low (net debt/EBITDA 1.16x TTM) [Certain] (source: RA/BS/IS). A law-firm "investor fraud" solicitation is legal advertising, not a confirmed finding [Guessing] (source: https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260803486844/en/ROL-Investors-Have-Opportunity-to-Join-Rollins-Inc.-Fraud-Investigation-with-SBS-Law/).

## 3. Valuation
TTM P/E 29.3x and EV/EBITDA 19.0x sit far below ROL's own 5-year medians (48.6x, 31.8x), reflecting the ~45% 52-week price decline, not a re-rated range [Certain] (source: fact sheet valuation table). Versus peers (RTO, ECL, CTAS, ABM), P/E is in line (29.4x median) and P/FCF slightly below (25.0x vs 26.2x), but EV/EBITDA and EV/Sales are ~18% above peer medians and P/B 125% above [Certain] (source: fact sheet). Base fair value $32.78 is 1.6% above last close ($32.26); bear/bull range -8.8%/+17.7% [Likely] (source: fact sheet fair-value table). Reverse-DCF implied growth (16.2% year-1, fading to terminal) exceeds the reaffirmed 6% FY26 guide, so price likely assumes a return to the 7-8% medium-term target, not today's run-rate [Likely] (source: fact sheet; https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/rol/transcripts/754827-u-s-all-stars-conference/). Price is roughly fair value, not a bargain: re-rating has closed the gap to intrinsic value, leaving little margin of safety for an unresolved lead problem.

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Organic revenue growth (income statement, quarterly) falls below the reaffirmed "at least 6%" FY26 guide in Q3/Q4 2026.
- Gross margin (income statement) falls below 51% (vs 52.4% TTM), signaling the people/fuel cost pressure is not transitory.
- Residential digital-lead decline widens further or fails to stabilize for two more consecutive quarters, per management disclosure in earnings calls/transcripts.
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) rises above 1.5x, indicating leverage is being used to mask organic weakness.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
FCF margin is strong and rising (15.5%→17.3% FY21-25, 15.8% TTM) with FCF/net income conversion of 116-124% [Certain] (source: CF/IS). Share count fell 0.38% YoY, consistent with buybacks funding modest offset to dividend growth; the board declared its regular quarterly dividend in July 2026 [Certain] (source: https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/rollins-inc-announces-regular-quarterly-cash-dividend-302831193.html). Leverage remains conservative (net debt/EBITDA 1.16x TTM), leaving room for continued bolt-on M&A, the company's historical growth lever in a fragmented industry [Likely].

## 6. Unusual items
Short interest rose 37.8% month-on-month to 5.92% of float, a meaningful increase in bearish positioning following the lead-generation disclosure [Likely] (source: fact sheet Other/News tables). RSI 45.6 and price 34% below the 200-day MA reflect a severe, not yet stabilized, drawdown [Certain] (source: fact sheet Price table). The SBS Law investor-fraud solicitation is attorney advertising seeking plaintiffs, not an adjudicated claim, and is not itself evidence of wrongdoing [Guessing].
