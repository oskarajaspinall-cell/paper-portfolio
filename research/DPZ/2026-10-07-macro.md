# Macro overlay: Domino's Pizza, Inc. (DPZ) — 2026-10-07
## Assessment
**Chain**: real yields/credit spreads -> equity discount rate & refinancing cost -> valuation multiple and buyback-funded EPS growth. DPZ's bull case rests on a trough-multiple re-rating (P/E 17.4x, 40% below its own 5y low; source: factsheet, RA) funded by buybacks against 4.84x net debt/EBITDA [Certain]; that re-rating is sensitive to the market-wide risk-free rate and risk appetite, not DPZ-specific fundamentals.

**Facts** (FRED, 2026-10-05/06): 10y real yield (DFII10) 2.95%, +52bp ~1m / +71bp ~3m. 2y Treasury (DGS2) 4.84% vs fed funds (DFF) 3.88%. 10y breakeven (T10YIE) 2.36%, roughly flat (+1bp/1m, +11bp/3m). HY credit spread (BAMLH0A0HYM2) 3.12%, +44bp/1m. [Certain]

**Market pricing (proxy, inference)**: 2y yield ~96bp above fed funds implies the market prices further tightening, not cuts (proxy, inference). Flat breakevens with a sharp real-yield rise suggest a real-rate/term-premium repricing rather than an inflation scare — raising the discount rate for all equities; widening HY spreads add a tightening-financial-conditions signal (inference).

**Bull**: real yields/spreads stabilize or retrace -> discount-rate relief lets DPZ's trough multiple re-rate toward the 21.6x peer median with no fundamental change.
**Bear**: "higher for longer" real rates and wider spreads keep the discount rate/ERP elevated, capping multiple recovery and raising refinancing cost on DPZ's leverage, slowing buyback-funded EPS growth (inference; no DPZ debt-maturity data available).
**Tilt**: moderate, toward bear — the 3-month real-yield move is large and recent, and works directly against the stock's main re-rating catalyst.
