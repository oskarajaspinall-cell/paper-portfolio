# Domino's Pizza, Inc. (DPZ) — Core initiation — 2026-10-07

## 1. Business
Domino's is the world's largest pizza company by store count, operating an asset-light franchise model [Certain]. Revenue comes from franchise royalties/fees, company-owned store sales, and supply-chain sales of food and equipment to franchisees domestically and internationally (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/dpz/).

## 2. Quality
Returns on capital are high and improving: ROIC 62.2% TTM (up from 58.5% FY2021) and ROCE 82.5% TTM, with operating margin rising to 19.3% TTM from 17.9% FY2021 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/dpz/financials/ratios/, https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/dpz/financials/income-statement/) [Certain]. The moat is delivery density, a proprietary digital-ordering platform and a supply-chain network that smaller rivals (PZZA) and diversified peers (YUM's Pizza Hut) cannot easily replicate [Likely]. A new insider CEO, Joe Jordan (15-year Domino's veteran, prior COO/President US), succeeded Russell Weiner on Oct 1, 2026, with Weiner moving to Executive Chairman — continuity-focused succession, low strategic-disruption risk (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1286681/000119312526277510/d50178dex991.htm) [Certain]. Press reports (Sept 2026) describe roughly 13 store closures in one state tied to a franchisee's financial struggles; scale versus Domino's ~7,000+ US units appears immaterial but is a watch item for franchisee-system health (source: https://www.fastcompany.com/91605086/dominos-pizza-store-closures-list-doomed-franchise-locations, https://nypost.com/2026/09/22/business/dominos-shutters-13-restaurants-in-a-single-state/) [Likely].

## 3. Valuation
The stock prices in a growth/quality derate that current fundamentals don't yet support. P/E of 17.4x sits 40% below its own 5-year low (23.9x) and 19% below the peer median (21.6x); EV/EBITDA of 14.6x is 38% below its 5-year low and 10% below peers; P/FCF of 15.3x is 34% below the peer median of 23.3x (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/dpz/financials/ratios/). FCF yield is 6.52% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/dpz/statistics/). The shares are down 27.4% over 12 months and trade below both the 50-day and 200-day moving averages, consistent with the market pricing in structural concerns — unit-growth slowdown, franchisee stress, leverage — rather than a temporary setback [Likely]. With ROIC and margins still expanding, the de-rating looks more sentiment- than fundamentals-driven, leaving re-rating optionality if growth stabilizes.

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Operating margin (income statement page) falls below 17% for two consecutive quarters
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) rises back above 6.0x, last seen in FY2022
- FCF margin (cash-flow/income-statement pages) falls below 10%
- ROIC (ratios page) falls below 50%

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
FCF conversion (FCF/NI) is 109.5% TTM and has exceeded 85% in every year since FY2021, reflecting the asset-light franchise model's high earnings quality (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/dpz/financials/cash-flow-statement/). Net debt/EBITDA of 4.84x is structurally elevated, consistent with debt-funded buybacks typical of restaurant franchisors, not operating distress: Altman Z of 3.15 (safe zone) and Piotroski F of 7/9 support this (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/dpz/statistics/). Shares outstanding fell 2.45% YoY, confirming ongoing buyback-led capital return (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/dpz/statistics/) [Certain].

## 6. Unusual items
Short interest is 10.79% of float and rose 12.5% month-over-month — an elevated, rising bearish position worth monitoring (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/dpz/statistics/) [Certain]. Reported institutional ownership of 104.2% of float likely reflects overlapping 13F/securities-lending double-counting rather than a governance flag [Guessing]. Next earnings are due Oct 13, 2026, six days after this note — expect volatility around the print (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/dpz/) [Certain].
