# Kasikornbank (BKK:KBANK) — Core initiation — 2026-11-16
## 1. Business
Thailand's second-largest lender (source: https://www.reuters.com/world/asia-pacific/kasikornbank-turns-ai-prudent-lending-thailands-economy-slows-chief-exec-says-2025-11-13/) [Likely]. It earns net interest and fee income from retail, SME and corporate lending and deposits. Reporting currency is THB (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/bkk/KBANK/). A segment and geographic split was not fetched; no foreign country is shown as ~10% of revenue in the fact sheet [Guessing].

## 2. Quality
Returns are ordinary, not exceptional: ROE 8.5% TTM, flat over five years (+0.2pp), range 6.9%–8.6% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/bkk/KBANK/financials/ratios/) [Certain]. Net margin is 33.0% TTM, up 1.2pp over five years (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/bkk/KBANK/financials/income-statement/) [Certain]. ROIC, ROCE and operating margin are [data unavailable], as is leverage, which is normal for a bank but leaves capital adequacy unverified here.
FCF margin was 54.0% in FY2025, but bank cash flow is dominated by deposit and loan movements and is not a clean quality signal [Likely]. Rolling FCF margin is 48.5% against 47.2% a year earlier, yet 94.0pp below its 2-year high of 142.5% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/bkk/KBANK/financials/cash-flow-statement/?p=trailing) [Certain]: the earlier peak looks like a working-capital swing, not earnings power [Likely].
A durable moat rests on scale, a deposit franchise and digital leadership, as management states it (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/bkk/KBANK/transcripts/746310-investor-update/) [Likely]. Nothing in the data shows returns rising above the cost of equity.

## 3. Valuation
P/E is 12.1x against a 5y range of 7.5x–9.8x (median 8.8x), above the old maximum. P/B is 1.0x against 0.5x–0.7x, also far above it. P/FCF is 7.9x against 5.5x–6.9x (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/bkk/KBANK/financials/ratios/) [Certain]. Against Thai peers the P/E is +1% and P/B -3% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/bkk/KBANK/statistics/) [Certain], so the re-rating is sector-wide, not specific to KBANK [Likely].
The mechanical base fair value is 216.69 THB against a 254.00 close, -14.7% (bull 236.77, -6.8%). It blends P/B with sustainable ROE (weight 67%) and FCFE DCF (33%) (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/bkk/KBANK/). The reverse DCF implies 0.7% year-1 growth, which is undemanding, but even the bull value sits below the price [Certain].
The trend is strong: +16.7% above the 200-day average, +41.8% over 12 months and +31.4pp ahead of SPY (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/bkk/KBANK/history/). The price assumes the multiple expansion holds with ROE unchanged at 8.5% [Likely]. FCF yield is 12.68% but is distorted by the bank cash-flow caveat above.

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROE (ratios page) TTM falls below 7.0%, the bottom of its five-year range.
- Net margin (income statement page) TTM falls below 29%.
- Rolling FCF margin (trailing cash flow page) turns negative.
- Shares outstanding change (statistics page) turns positive by more than 2% YoY, signalling dilution.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Shares outstanding fell 0.60% YoY, a small net buyback (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/bkk/KBANK/statistics/) [Certain]. Management describes strong CET1 and active shareholder-return measures (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/bkk/KBANK/transcripts/746310-investor-update/) [Likely]. FCF/NI was 168.4% in FY2025 after a negative FY2022, so conversion is erratic and not a basis for valuation [Certain].

## 6. Unusual items
Beta (5y) is 0.10 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/bkk/KBANK/statistics/) [Certain]; it understates risk in a domestic-economy bank. The CEO cites slowing Thai growth and fraud risks (source: https://www.reuters.com/world/asia-pacific/kasikornbank-turns-ai-prudent-lending-thailands-economy-slows-chief-exec-says-2025-11-13/) [Likely]. Q3 2026 results were published on 2026-10-21 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/bkk/KBANK/filings/3862005/); their content was not read. Piotroski F is 4. Thailand is the home market, so the macro overlay should check the THB and the Bank of Thailand policy rate [Likely]. Short interest is [data unavailable].
