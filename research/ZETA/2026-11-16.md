# Zeta Global (ZETA) — Core initiation — 2026-11-16
## 1. Business
Zeta sells the Zeta Marketing Platform (ZMP), a cloud suite combining proprietary consumer data, identity resolution (SuperGraph) and AI-driven campaign orchestration across email, SMS, web, CTV and social. Revenue is mostly usage- and spend-based from enterprise brands and agencies [Likely] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1851003/000119312526453696/zeta-20260930.htm). No foreign country was identified at ~10%+ of revenue in the sources read [Guessing]; not verified.

## 2. Quality
Profitability has turned, but only recently. Operating margin rose from -53.6% (FY2021) to 5.1% TTM; ROCE 6.8% TTM; FCF margin 15.1% TTM, up from 13.0% a year earlier (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/zeta/financials/ratios/) [Certain]. Gross margin is drifting down, 60.6% FY2025 to 59.5% TTM [Certain]. Balance sheet is net cash (net debt/EBITDA -0.49x) [Certain].
Evidence of customer depth: super-scaled customers rose to 212 from 180, with ARPU up 22% [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1851003/000119312526453696/zeta-20260930.htm) [Likely].
Weakness: FCF is flattered by stock-based compensation, which the 10-Q puts at $155.1 million for nine months [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1851003/000119312526453696/zeta-20260930.htm) [Certain]. Operating cash flow over the same period was $210.3 million [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1851003/000119312526453696/zeta-20260930.htm) [Certain]. Shares rose 15.82% YoY (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/zeta/statistics/) [Certain]. A durable moat is not proven: identity data is differentiated, but competition from large suites is intense [Guessing]. ROIC [data unavailable].

## 3. Valuation
The price assumes continued rapid growth and margin expansion. EV/Sales is 5.4x, above its 5y maximum of 4.0x, and 44% above the peer median; EV/EBITDA is 60.1x vs 16.3x for peers; P/FCF is 36.0x (43% of its 5y range) vs 12.2x for peers; FCF yield 2.78%; P/E [data unavailable] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/zeta/statistics/) [Certain].
Mechanical fair value: base 25.99 USD, bull 26.49, against a close of 36.65; the DCF could not run, so it rests on EV/Revenue alone [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/zeta/). That is a weak method for a business whose margins are rising, so treat the gap as a caution, not a verdict [Likely].
The stock is +57.9% above its 200-day average and +93.2% over 12 months, with relative strength +82.8pp vs SPY [Certain]. This is a working business priced for continuation.

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROCE TTM (ratios page, trailing view) falls below 3%.
- FCF margin TTM (cash flow and income statement pages, trailing view) falls below 11%.
- Gross margin TTM (income statement page) falls below 56%.
- Shares change YoY (statistics page) exceeds 20%, i.e. dilution outrunning growth.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
The company is growing through acquisition (Marigold integrated; LiveIntent earn-out payments continue) [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1851003/000119312526453696/zeta-20260930.htm) [Likely]. Cash conversion is strong on an FCF basis (15.1% margin), but heavy stock compensation and a 15.82% rise in share count mean per-share gains lag. The 10-Q discloses restructuring charges of $2.6 million for the quarter [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1851003/000119312526453696/zeta-20260930.htm) [Certain]. No buyback evidence in the sources read [Guessing].
