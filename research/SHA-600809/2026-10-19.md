# Shanxi Xinghuacun Fen Wine Factory Co.,Ltd. (SHA:600809) — Core initiation — 2026-10-19
## 1. Business
Shanxi Fen Wine produces and sells branded Chinese grain liquor (baijiu) across mass-to-ultra-premium price tiers, mainly through a distributor/wholesale network with growing direct and e-commerce sales [Certain]. It is classified as Beverages - Wineries & Distilleries [Certain]. Profitability is brand/price-driven rather than cost-driven: gross margin is 73.9% TTM versus operating margin of 38.4% TTM, i.e. most of the spread is pricing power over grain/packaging cost, not operating leverage alone [Certain].

## 2. Quality
Returns on capital remain very high even after a multi-year decline: ROIC 55.4% TTM (down from 120-227% FY21-23), ROCE 35.5% TTM (-3.3pp over 5y), ROE 27.3% TTM (-9.4pp over 5y) [Certain]. Gross margin has stayed flat at 73-76% across five fiscal years while operating margin rose 7.8pp to 38.4% TTM, consistent with durable pricing power rather than a cost tailwind [Likely]. The weaker signal is cash quality: FCF margin nearly halved from 37.5% (FY21) to 20.2% (FY25)/24.1% TTM, and FCF conversion (FCF/NI) fell from 140.9% to 63.8%/84.8% TTM [Certain] — profit is no longer converting to cash as reliably, likely working-capital or channel-inventory build [Likely]. Piotroski F-score is only 3/9 [Certain], a weak reading that corroborates the margin/cash deterioration [Likely]. Balance-sheet durability is a genuine offset: net debt/EBITDA is -2.53x TTM (net cash, strengthening from -1.72x FY21) [Certain], so the business can absorb further normalisation without financial stress.

## 3. Valuation
Nearly every multiple sits below its own five-year range and peers (Moutai, Wuliangye, Luzhou Laojiao, Yanghe): P/E 14.0x vs 5y range 17.1-72.5x (below min) and peer median 20.9x (-33%); EV/EBITDA 7.5x vs 5y min 11.9x and peer median 12.4x (-39%); EV/Sales 3.0x, -24% vs peers; P/FCF 16.5x, -67% vs peer median (n=2, thin) [Certain]. P/B is the exception at 3.7x, +60% above peer median 2.3x but below its 5y min 5.2x, unsurprising for a >25%-ROE business [Likely]. FCF yield is 6.07% [Certain]. Base-case fair value of CNY 166.26 (reverse DCF implies ~8.3% year-1 growth fading to terminal) is +42.5% above the CNY 116.70 close; the bear case (CNY 117.69) is near the current price [Certain]. The 52-week decline of -39.6%, close below the 50- and 200-day averages, has pushed valuation below what the still-high returns and net cash justify, if Quality erosion stabilises [Likely].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROIC (ratios page) falls below 30% (from 55.4% TTM) — the pricing-power moat eroding rather than just normalising off very high prior-year levels.
- FCF conversion, FCF/NI (cash-flow and income-statement pages), stays below 60% for two consecutive fiscal years — reported profit no longer backed by cash.
- Gross margin (income-statement page) falls below 68% (from 73.9% TTM) — loss of pricing power or discounting to move channel inventory.
- Net margin (income-statement page) falls below 20% (from 28.4% TTM) — operating-leverage gains in section 2 reversing.
