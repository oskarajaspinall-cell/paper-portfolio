# Macro overlay: Space Exploration Technologies Corp. (SPCX) — 2026-10-11

## Assessment
SPCX is a textbook long-duration growth stock: FCF margin -141.1% TTM, no computable P/E/EV-EBITDA/P/FCF, and a $2,206.5bn market cap the note says is "supported entirely by expectations for future cash flows" [Certain] (research/SPCX/2026-10-11.md). With no cash flows to discount today, the discount rate set by real yields is the one lever that moves a valuation anchor the market can't otherwise test. Chain: real yields -> discount rate on distant Starship/Starlink/Mobile cash flows -> ceiling on the premium.

**Facts**: US 10y real yield (TIPS, DFII10) is 2.87% on 2026-10-08, up +0.44pp/~1m and +0.56pp/~3m [Certain] (fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=DFII10, 2026-10-08). 10y breakeven inflation is 2.33%, roughly flat (-0.04pp/1m, +0.09pp/3m) [Certain] (…T10YIE, 2026-10-09) — the real-yield rise is tightening, not inflation. 2y Treasury (4.75%) sits ~87bp above fed funds (3.88%) [Certain] (…DGS2, DFF, 2026-10-08), a proxy that near-term cuts aren't priced.

**Market pricing (inference)**: despite this tightening, SPCX outperformed SPY by +3.3pp over 3 months on idiosyncratic newsflow (Starlink Mobile spectrum deal, India dispute) [Likely] — company catalysts are currently dominating the rate story.

**Bull**: real yields roll over -> lower discount rate -> premium easier to justify. **Bear**: real yields extend their rise -> higher hurdle on an unverifiable cash-flow story -> the ~$2.2T anchor gets harder to defend, with no earnings proof point until 17 Nov 2026. **Tilt**: moderate toward bear — rising real yields add a second headwind atop the fundamental one (negative FCF, dilution) already flagged, though newsflow currently masks it.
