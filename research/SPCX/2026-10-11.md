# Space Exploration Technologies Corp. (SPCX) — Core initiation — 2026-10-11
## 1. Business
SpaceX designs, builds and launches reusable rockets (Falcon 9, Starship) for commercial and government payloads, and operates the Starlink satellite broadband constellation, reporting across Space, Connectivity and AI segments (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/spcx/) [Certain]. It is also expanding into direct-to-device "Starlink Mobile" wireless service (source: fact sheet News & sentiment, TipRanks 2026-10-09) [Likely].

## 2. Quality
Gross margin has risen for five straight years (41.2%→51.9% TTM, +8.2pp), showing genuine unit-cost improvement from reusability [Certain] (source: income statement, factsheet). But operating margin has collapsed from +5.3% (FY2024) to -14.9% TTM and FCF margin to -141.1% TTM, the worst points in the five-year trend [Certain] (factsheet, IS/CF). ROCE turned negative (-2.0% TTM) [Certain]. Piotroski F-Score is weak at 3/9 despite a strong Altman Z of 20.77 (low bankruptcy risk, likely a large net-cash position: net debt/EBITDA -10.23x TTM) [Certain] (factsheet, statistics/ratios). Listed only 12 Jun 2026, so there is barely four months of public history — too short to judge durability through a cycle [Certain]. Falcon/Starship reuse cost advantage and Starlink's scale are real moats, but current numbers show aggressive capex/opex ahead of profit, including a reported ~$8bn spectrum purchase, unverified by stockanalysis.com [Guessing] (source: TipRanks 2026-10-09, https://www.tipranks.com/news/spacexs-starlink-mobile-is-becoming-a-real-threat-to-telecom-giants-evercore-says).

## 3. Valuation
No P/E, EV/EBITDA, EV/Sales, P/FCF or P/B multiple is computable from stockanalysis.com for SPCX or any of the four peers (ASTS, RKLB, LUNR, RDW) — the whole peer set is earnings/FCF-negative with too short a history [Certain] (factsheet Valuation table). FCF yield is -1.47% TTM, i.e. the business is a net cash consumer at a $2,206.5bn market cap [Certain] (factsheet, ST). The fair-value model (DCF, then EV/EBITDA as 2nd method) returns [data unavailable]: FCF is negative/too short a history for a DCF, and fewer than two positive EBITDA years exist for a multiple [Certain] (factsheet Fair value). With no computable multiple and no fair-value anchor, the $2.2T price is supported entirely by expectations for future Starship/Starlink/Starshield/Mobile cash flows that cannot yet be tested against stockanalysis.com data [Certain].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Operating margin (income statement page) fails to recover above -5% within the next two fiscal years, showing the FY2025/TTM collapse (+5.3%→-14.9%) is structural, not a temporary capex ramp.
- FCF margin (cash flow statement page) stays below -100% for two more consecutive quarters after the next earnings report (17 Nov 2026), showing cash burn isn't moderating.
- Gross margin (income statement page) falls below 45%, reversing the five-year improving trend (41.2%→51.9%).
- Shares outstanding (statistics page) grow a further >10% within a year, extending the dilution already seen (+41.79% YoY).

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
FCF margin is -141.1% TTM and operating margin -14.9% TTM, so the business is currently funding heavy investment (rocket/Starship development, Starlink constellation and ground infrastructure, and a reported spectrum purchase) from its balance sheet rather than operating cash flow [Certain] (factsheet IS/CF). Net debt/EBITDA is deeply negative (-10.23x TTM) and Altman Z is 20.77, consistent with a large net-cash position funding the capex rather than debt [Certain] (factsheet, BS/RA). Shares outstanding grew 41.79% YoY, a material dilution for existing holders, likely tied to the June 2026 listing and ongoing equity-funded investment [Certain] (factsheet, ST).

## 6. Unusual items
Newly listed (IPO 12 Jun 2026): only ~4 months of public trading history and one fiscal year of data, so trend conclusions above carry limited sample size [Certain]. Short interest is 7.27% of float, down 6.5% versus the prior month [Certain] (factsheet, ST). Headlines flag a public dispute between CEO Elon Musk and Reliance Jio's Mukesh Ambani over delayed Starlink India market access, a key-person/regulatory-access risk given Musk's outsized public role [Likely] (source: https://www.tipranks.com/news/elon-musk-is-impatiently-trying-to-offer-cheap-starlink-internet-in-india-ahead-of-record-jio-public-offering-how-could-this-impact-spacex-stock; https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/09/elon-musk-intensifies-attack-on-ambani-over-starlink-india-launch-delay/). A reported ~$8bn low-band spectrum acquisition for Starlink Mobile, if confirmed, would materially raise near-term capex intensity and competes directly with legacy telecom incumbents, who sold off on the news; the dollar figure is a headline claim, not a stockanalysis.com figure [Guessing] (source: https://www.tipranks.com/news/spacexs-starlink-mobile-is-becoming-a-real-threat-to-telecom-giants-evercore-says).
