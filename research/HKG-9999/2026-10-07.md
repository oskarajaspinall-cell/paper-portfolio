# NetEase, Inc. (HKG:9999) — Core initiation — 2026-10-07

## 1. Business
NetEase earns most of its revenue from self-developed online games (97.3% of the Games & Value-Added Services segment in FY2025) monetized through in-game purchases and expansions across PC, mobile and console [Certain] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1110646/000110465926043468/ntes-20251231x20f.htm). Smaller contributions come from Youdao (education, NYSE-listed), Cloud Music (streaming, HKEX 9899) and advertising/e-commerce "innovative businesses."

## 2. Quality
Returns and margins have improved steadily: ROIC 368.3% TTM vs 59.9% FY2021, ROCE 23.3% TTM vs 15.9% FY2021, operating margin 35.2% TTM vs 18.7% FY2021, FCF margin 43.5% TTM vs 26.6% FY2021 (source: fact sheet, ratios/income statement pages) [Certain]. The moat is an in-house development model plus a durable IP catalogue (Fantasy Westward Journey, Identity V, Marvel Rivals, Where Winds Meet) that extends monetizable game lifecycles and supports repeat engagement [Likely] (source: 20-F, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1110646/000110465926043468/ntes-20251231x20f.htm). Durability is constrained by two structural risks the 20-F flags: VIEs held 85.2% of FY2025 revenue, so ownership rests on contracts PRC authorities could challenge [Certain]; and tightening PRC minors'-playtime, loot-box and game-approval rules can cap monetization [Certain] (both source: 20-F). Net debt/EBITDA of -3.76x (net cash) gives balance-sheet resilience against either risk crystallizing [Certain] (source: fact sheet, ratios page).

## 3. Valuation
At 16.0x P/E the stock sits at the 21st percentile of its own 5-year range (13.6x-24.8x) and 27% below the peer median (22.0x, n=4) [Certain] (source: fact sheet, ratios/peer statistics pages). EV/EBITDA (8.2x) and P/FCF (10.1x) are both below their own 5-year minimums (9.3x and 10.7x) and 5-28% below peer medians, despite margins and ROIC having roughly doubled since FY2021 [Certain]. The outlier is EV/Sales (3.1x, +100% vs peer median), but that mixes NetEase's higher-margin games model against lower-margin peer revenue bases, so it is not read as a standalone warning. Taken together, the current price assumes the regulatory/VIE overhang persists and the recent margin expansion will not be sustained or re-rated, rather than pricing in the quality improvement already delivered [Likely].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Operating margin (income statement page) falls below 28%, the FY2024 level, reversing the five-year expansion.
- FCF margin (cash flow/income statement pages) falls below 36% (FY2024 level), signalling cash generation is not keeping pace with reported profit.
- ROCE (ratios page) falls below 20%, reversing the five-year rising trend.
- New PRC regulation materially restricts game monetization (e.g., mandatory playtime/loot-box curbs beyond current rules) or a VIE-related regulatory action forces deconsolidation, per future SEC 20-F/6-K filings.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
FCF conversion (FCF/NI) is 156.0% TTM, up from 138.4% in FY2021, confirming reported earnings are backed by cash [Certain] (source: fact sheet, cash-flow/income-statement pages). Management runs an active buyback and dividend programme [Likely] (source: Q2 2026 earnings call transcript, https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/9999/transcripts/676480-q2-2026/), yet shares outstanding still rose 0.39% year-over-year [Certain] (source: fact sheet, statistics page), implying stock-based compensation is offsetting most repurchase activity. The -3.76x net debt/EBITDA (net cash) position means capital return is a choice, not a balance-sheet necessity [Certain].

## 6. Unusual items
VIE revenue concentration (85.2% of FY2025 net revenue) and PRC gaming-regulation risk are structural, not one-off, and are the main reason the stock trades below its own history and peers despite stronger fundamentals [Certain] (source: 20-F). HFCAA audit-inspection risk is currently dormant (PCAOB regained PRC inspection access in December 2022) but could resurface [Guessing] (source: 20-F).
