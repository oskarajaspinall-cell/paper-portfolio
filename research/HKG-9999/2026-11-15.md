# NetEase (HKG:9999) — Core initiation — 2026-11-15
## 1. Business
NetEase earns most of its revenue from online games: in-house live-service titles plus licensed Western franchises. Smaller lines are Youdao (AI learning), Cloud Music and innovative businesses. Revenue is recurring in-game spend against a largely fixed development cost base, so incremental margins are high. ## 2. Quality
Good and still improving. Operating margin rose from 18.7% (FY2021) to 35.2% TTM, gross margin to 67.2%, ROCE 23.3% TTM (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/9999/financials/ratios/). FCF margin is 43.5%, FCF/NI 156.0%, net debt/EBITDA -3.76x, i.e. large net cash (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/9999/financials/cash-flow-statement/). [Certain]

Recent direction: no Direction change line fired; ROCE +1.5pp, operating margin +3.8pp and FCF margin +2.3pp over the last year (FCF margin 43.5% vs a 45.0% two-year high). Trailing ROIC (340.3%) reflects a tiny invested-capital base; I rely on ROCE. [Certain]

Moat: a diversified portfolio of long-lived franchises and in-house studios, not a single-hit dependency. Not unassailable: new-title success is uncertain and Chinese licensing rules can change. [Likely] Piotroski F 6, Altman Z 8.53 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/9999/statistics/). [Certain]

## 3. Valuation
P/E is 16.4x vs a 5y range of 13.6x–24.8x (25% up the range); the peer median is 13.2x but n=1, so weak evidence. EV/EBITDA 8.5x and P/FCF 10.4x are both below their 5y minimums; FCF yield is 9.61% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/9999/statistics/). [Certain]

The reverse DCF implies year-1 revenue growth of -3.1%, fading to terminal growth: the price assumes growth stops while margins have expanded. Base fair value is 217.18 HKD (+14.1% vs the 190.40 close), bear 144.01 (-24.4%), bull 244.11 (+28.2%) (source: research/HKG-9999/2026-11-15-valuation.md). The 12-month change is -14.1% (about 24.5pp behind SPY) and price sits at its 50- and 200-day averages: no trend strength, so the case rests on value. The bear case embeds the owner-set 3.5% China premium. [Likely]
