# NetEase (HKG:9999) — Core initiation — 2026-11-07
## 1. Business
NetEase earns most of its revenue from online games (own-developed live-service titles plus licensed Western franchises), supplemented by Youdao (education/AI learning), Cloud Music and innovative businesses. Revenue is recurring in-game spend; cost is largely development staff and licensing, hence high incremental margins. [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/9999/)

## 2. Quality
A good business, and still improving. Operating margin has risen from 18.7% (FY2021) to 35.2% (TTM), gross margin to 67.2%, and ROCE is 23.3% TTM (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/9999/financials/ratios/). FCF conversion is 156.0% of net income and FCF margin 43.5%; net cash is large (net debt/EBITDA -3.76x) (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/9999/financials/cash-flow-statement/). [Certain]

Moat: a portfolio of long-lived franchises and in-house studios, with data on player engagement; hit-driven risk is mitigated by many live titles rather than one. [Likely] This is not an unassailable moat: new-title success is not guaranteed and Chinese regulators can change the rules. [Likely] Piotroski F is 6, Altman Z 8.53 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/9999/statistics/). [Certain]

## 3. Valuation
P/E is 17.2x against a 5y range of 13.6x–24.8x (33% of the way up) and a peer median of 22.8x (-25%). EV/EBITDA of 9.1x sits below its 5y minimum (9.3x) and 48% under peers; P/FCF is 11.0x (near the 5y low of 10.7x), FCF yield 9.13% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/9999/statistics/). [Certain]

The reverse DCF says the price implies revenue growth of -1.8% in year 1, fading to terminal growth: the market is pricing a business that stops growing even as margins have expanded. Base fair value is 220.11 HKD (+9.8% vs the 200.40 close), bear 145.25 (-27.5%), bull 247.35 (+23.4%) (source: research/HKG-9999/2026-11-07-valuation.md). The skew is moderately favourable, not large; the base case upside is limited and the bear case carries a 3.5% owner-set China premium. [Likely]

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Operating margin (income statement page, TTM) falls below 28%, i.e. back to the FY2024 level.
- FCF conversion (FCF/NI, cash-flow and income statement pages) falls below 100% for a full fiscal year.
- ROCE (ratios page, TTM) falls below 17%.
- Net cash turns to net debt (net debt/EBITDA above 0x, balance sheet page) through acquisitions or a shift in capital return policy.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Cash conversion is excellent (156.0% of net income) and the balance sheet is net cash.
