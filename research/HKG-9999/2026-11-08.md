# NetEase (HKG:9999) — Core initiation — 2026-11-08
## 1. Business
NetEase earns most of its revenue from online games: in-house live-service titles plus licensed Western franchises. Smaller lines are Youdao (AI learning), Cloud Music and innovative businesses. Revenue is recurring in-game spend against a largely fixed development cost base, so incremental margins are high. [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/9999/) Geographic split: [data unavailable] in the fact sheet; the business is China-centred and reports in CNY. [Likely]

## 2. Quality
Good and still improving. Operating margin rose from 18.7% (FY2021) to 35.2% TTM, gross margin to 67.2%, ROCE is 23.3% TTM (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/9999/financials/ratios/). FCF margin is 43.5% and FCF/NI 156.0%; net debt/EBITDA is -3.76x, i.e. large net cash (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/9999/financials/cash-flow-statement/). [Certain]

Recent direction: no Direction change line fired. Over the last year ROCE is +1.5pp, operating margin +3.8pp and FCF margin +2.3pp, all at or near 2-year highs (FCF margin 43.5% vs a 45.0% high, within 2pp). Trailing ROIC (340.3%) is distorted by a very small invested-capital base; I rely on ROCE instead. [Certain]

Moat: many long-lived franchises and in-house studios give a diversified live-service portfolio, not a single-hit dependency. It is not unassailable: new-title success is uncertain and Chinese licensing rules can change. [Likely] Piotroski F is 6, Altman Z 8.53 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/9999/statistics/). [Certain]

## 3. Valuation
P/E is 17.2x vs a 5y range of 13.6x–24.8x (33% up the range) and a peer median of 14.3x (+21% premium, n=3). On cash-based measures it is cheap: EV/EBITDA 9.1x is below its 5y minimum (9.3x) and 56% under peers; P/FCF 11.0x is near the 5y low of 10.7x; FCF yield is 9.13% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/9999/statistics/). [Certain]

The reverse DCF implies year-1 revenue growth of -1.8%, fading to terminal growth: the price assumes growth stops while margins have expanded. Base fair value is 220.11 HKD (+9.8% vs the 200.40 close), bear 145.25 (-27.5%), bull 247.35 (+23.4%) (source: research/HKG-9999/2026-11-08-valuation.md). Upside is modest and the bear case embeds the owner-set 3.5% China premium, so the skew is only moderately favourable. [Likely]
