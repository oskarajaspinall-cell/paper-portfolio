# NetEase, Inc. (HKG:9999) — Core initiation — 2026-11-23
## 1. Business
NetEase earns its money mainly from online games (self-developed PC and mobile titles, plus licensed titles), with smaller education, music streaming and innovative-business segments [Likely]. It reports in CNY and is China-centred; I have not verified a country revenue split from a primary source, so the exact share is unknown [Guessing]. Games gross margin expanded year on year in Q3 on lower revenue-sharing costs, while revenue dipped (source: https://www.fool.com/earnings/takeaways/2026/11/19/netease-3-key-takeaways-for-long-term-investors/) [Likely]. Mainland China is the key country exposure [Likely].

## 2. Quality
Strong business. TTM operating margin is 36.6% against 28.1% in FY2024 and 18.7% in FY2021; gross margin is 68.2%; FCF margin is 41.7%; FCF conversion is 149.9% of net income (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/9999/financials/income-statement/, https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/9999/financials/cash-flow-statement/) [Certain]. Net debt/EBITDA is -3.84x, i.e. a large net cash position [Certain]. ROCE is 23.2% and rising (+5.3pp over five years); ROE is 19.4% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/9999/financials/ratios/) [Certain].

Direction change: ROIC is 33.7% TTM versus 144.6% a year earlier and a 2-year high of 237.1%. That swing reflects a tiny invested-capital base on a net-cash balance sheet, not collapsing profits: operating margin rose 5.0pp over the same period while ROCE rose [Likely]. FCF margin is 0.9pp below a year ago, 3.3pp below its peak [Certain]. Moat: hit-driven content, live-service player communities and in-house studios; durable but not immune to a weak release slate [Likely]. Q3 revenue and EPS missed consensus per headlines (source: https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/netease-investigation-notice-suewallst-notifies-investors-of-pending-investigation-into-netease-ntes-302946204.html) [Likely].

## 3. Valuation
At 154.00 HKD the stock trades below its own 5-year minimum on every multiple: P/E 13.1x (5y range 13.6x–24.8x, median 15.4x), EV/EBITDA 5.7x (9.3x–18.3x), P/FCF 8.7x (10.7x–17.9x), P/B 2.4x (2.9x–4.2x). FCF yield is 11.49% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/9999/statistics/) [Certain]. Versus the four China-internet peers, P/E is +12% and EV/EBITDA -2%; the EV/Sales and P/B premia reflect NetEase's superior margins and cash [Likely].

Mechanical fair value base is 218.17 HKD (+41.7%), bear 147.03, bull 244.87; reverse DCF implies -11.7% year-1 revenue growth, so the price already discounts a shrinking game business [Certain]. The base uses a 3.5% China country premium. Price trend is poor: 18.0% below the 50-day and 18.5% below the 200-day average, 12-month change -28.6%, RSI 26.2 [Certain]. A value case, not a trend case; the market is pricing a content-cycle downturn [Likely].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Operating margin (income-statement page, TTM) falls below 28%.
- FCF margin (cash-flow page, TTM) falls below 30%.
- FCF conversion (FCF/net income, TTM) falls below 100%.
- Net cash turns to net debt: net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) above 0x.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Cash conversion is excellent (FCF 149.9% of net income) and net cash is about 3.8x EBITDA [Certain]. Share count is up 0.39% YoY, so buybacks are not shrinking it materially (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/9999/statistics/) [Certain]. Management cited a dividend and ongoing buybacks on the Q3 call (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/9999/transcripts/736905-q3-2026/) [Likely]. Insiders own 45.32% [Certain].
