# NetEase, Inc. (HKG:9999) — Core initiation — 2026-11-16
## 1. Business
NetEase earns most of its revenue from online games (live-service titles, plus licensed Western franchises), with smaller streaming (Cloud Music), education (Youdao) and innovative-business segments [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/9999/). It is a China-based company reporting in CNY, so the home market is the main exposure; no separate foreign-country share was verified [Guessing]. Ordinary shares trade in Hong Kong and as US-listed shares.

## 2. Quality
Strong and improving [Certain]. TTM ROCE is 23.3% (FY2021: 15.9%), TTM operating margin 35.2% (FY2021: 18.7%) and gross margin 67.2% (FY2021: 53.6%) (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/9999/financials/ratios/). The latest 12 months are above a year earlier on every tracked measure, with no direction-change flag (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/9999/financials/ratios/?p=trailing). FCF margin is 43.5% and FCF/net income is 156.0%, so earnings convert to cash [Certain]. Net debt/EBITDA is -3.76x, a large net cash position, and Piotroski F is 6 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/9999/statistics/). ROIC is [data unavailable] for FY2025 and TTM, so ROCE and ROE (20.4%) carry the return judgement. The moat is a hit-driven game franchise and in-house live-service operations; it is not structural like a network effect, and one weak release cycle could dent it [Likely].

Against peers (Tencent, Kuaishou, EA, Take-Two) EV/EBITDA is 57% below the median, while P/E is 25% above it [Likely]. ## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
The company runs a large net cash position and converts well over 100% of net income to FCF [Certain]. It repurchases shares on both the Nasdaq and Hong Kong lines (source: https://www.nasdaq.com/articles/netease-inc-reports-share-repurchases-176855-ordinary-shares-october-2026). Even so, the share count is up 0.39% YoY, so buybacks are not yet shrinking it [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/9999/statistics/). Cash conversion is a clear strength.
