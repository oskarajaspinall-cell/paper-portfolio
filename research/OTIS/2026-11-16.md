# Otis Worldwide (OTIS) — Core initiation — 2026-11-16
## 1. Business
Otis makes and installs elevators and escalators (New Equipment) and, more profitably, maintains, repairs and modernises its installed base (Service). Service was $7,613m of $11,339m nine-month net sales, about 67% [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1781335/000178133526000127/otis-20260930.htm). Total RPO was approximately $19.6 billion [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1781335/000178133526000127/otis-20260930.htm). China share of revenue: [data unavailable] in sources read; China is a known exposure (the Q3 call cites China weakness), so the macro overlay should check it [Likely].

## 2. Quality
Returns are exceptional: TTM ROIC 58.0%, ROCE 71.9%, gross margin 30.0%, operating margin 15.5% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/otis/financials/ratios/) [Certain]. The moat is the installed base: maintenance contracts are recurring, switching vendors on proprietary equipment is hard, and service growing faster than new equipment lifts mix [Likely]. Caveats the evaluation must address: ROIC is 37.7pp below its 2-year high of 95.6%; operating margin is rising over five fiscal years but down from 16.5% to 15.5% over 12 months. The filing attributes a Q3 gross margin fall to labour and commodity costs, partly offset by service mix (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1781335/000178133526000127/otis-20260930.htm) [Likely]. Leverage is rising: net debt/EBITDA 3.12x vs 2.48x in FY2023 [Certain]. CEO retirement is expected in H1 2027 (headline: https://www.tipranks.com/news/the-fly/otis-worldwide-ceo-judy-marks-to-retire-in-2027-thefly-news) [Likely].

## 3. Valuation
The price is below the whole of its own 5y range on every multiple: P/E 19.4x (range 22.5x–29.7x), EV/EBITDA 15.0x (16.4x–17.7x), P/FCF 17.9x (22.6x–25.7x) (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/otis/financials/ratios/) [Certain]. Versus peers (CARR, JCI, TT, LII) it trades at a 33% P/E discount and 19% EV/EBITDA discount [Certain]. FCF yield is 5.60% [Certain]. The mechanical fair value disagrees: base 68.86 vs close 77.50 (-11.1%), driven by a DCF of 57.20 against EV/EBITDA of 92.18; the reverse DCF implies 7.3% year-1 revenue growth [Certain]. The discount to history reflects a 12-month price change of -11.9% and slower margins [Likely]. Price is 2.4% above the 200-day MA, relative strength vs SPY +9.1pp over 3 months [Certain]. Cheap on multiples, fair rather than cheap on DCF.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
FCF conversion is 107.5% of net income and FCF margin 11.1% TTM, up from 9.2% a year earlier [Certain]. Shares fell 2.60% YoY, so buybacks are shrinking the count [Certain]. The quarterly dividend was declared (https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/otis-declares-quarterly-dividend-of-0-44-per-share-302924212.html) [Certain].
