# Otis Worldwide (OTIS) — Core initiation — 2026-11-07
## 1. Business
Otis makes and installs elevators and escalators (New Equipment) and, more importantly, maintains and modernises its installed base under recurring contracts (Service). The service annuity on a very large installed fleet drives the profit and cash flow. [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/otis/)

## 2. Quality
Returns are exceptional and rising: ROIC 58.0% TTM vs 31.2% in FY2021, gross margin 30.0% TTM vs 29.5% in FY2021, operating margin 15.5% TTM vs 15.2% in FY2021 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/otis/financials/ratios/). [Certain] The moat is the installed base plus route density in maintenance; switching is rare. [Likely]

The Q3 10-Q shows service volume growing faster than new equipment, but gross margin fell on higher labour and material costs: [company-reported] Q3 gross margin down 120 basis points, Service organic volume +7%, New Equipment +3% (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1781335/000178133526000127/otis-20260930.htm). TTM operating margin is below the FY2025 16.5%, so margin expansion has stalled for now. [Likely] China weakness and a CEO retirement expected in the first half of 2027 are the live uncertainties (source: https://www.tipranks.com/news/the-fly/otis-worldwide-ceo-judy-marks-to-retire-in-2027-thefly-news). [Likely] The SEC delta shows no substantive Risk Factor change. [Certain]

## 3. Valuation
At 78.96 USD, P/E is 19.6x, below its 5y minimum of 22.5x and 32% below the peer median (28.9x). EV/EBITDA is 15.1x vs a 5y range of 16.4x-17.7x and a peer median of 18.4x. P/FCF is 18.0x vs 22.6x-25.7x; FCF yield is 5.57% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/otis/statistics/). [Certain]

Base fair value is 70.99 USD (blend of DCF 60.40 and EV/EBITDA 92.18), so the price is 10.1% above the model's base. The gap comes from a DCF that is much lower than the multiple method; both rest on own-history assumptions. The reverse DCF implies 6.7% year-1 revenue growth, demanding versus the stalled margin. [Likely] The shares are cheap on every relative measure, but the mechanical fair value has been reached. The price is 12.3% above its 50-day average with RSI 68.1, so the recent rerating is already substantial. [Likely]

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Gross margin (income statement page) falls below 28.0% on a TTM basis.
- Net debt / EBITDA (ratios page) rises above 3.5x.
- FCF conversion (FCF/NI, cash flow page) falls below 90% on a TTM basis.
- ROIC (ratios page) falls below 45%.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Shares outstanding fell 2.60% year on year, so buybacks are shrinking the count. A quarterly dividend of 0.44 USD was declared (source: https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/otis-declares-quarterly-dividend-of-0-44-per-share-302924212.html). [Certain] FCF conversion is 107.5% TTM and FCF margin 11.1%, but leverage has crept up to 3.12x net debt / EBITDA from 2.62x in FY2021. Buybacks are partly debt-funded, which is acceptable at this cash generation but leaves less margin for error. [Likely]

## 6. Unusual items
CEO succession in the first half of 2027 (source: https://www.tipranks.com/news/the-fly/otis-worldwide-ceo-judy-marks-to-retire-in-2027-thefly-news) is a transition risk. [Likely] Short interest is 4.24% of float, not material. Decision: HOLD. The business is still compounding service profits and trades below its own history and peers, so the thesis has not played out. The model fair value is one estimate, and there is no evidence of deterioration that justifies a TRIM. An ADD is not warranted at 10.1% above base value after a sharp rally. [Likely]
