# Otis Worldwide Corporation (OTIS) — Core initiation — 2026-10-07

## 1. Business
Otis makes, installs and services elevators and escalators in 200+ countries. New Equipment (35% of 2025 net sales, 9% of segment profit) sells units to builders/developers; Service (65% of sales, 91% of segment profit) performs maintenance, repair and modernization on an installed base it maintains of ~2.5m units, earning high-margin recurring revenue over equipment lifecycles of decades [Certain] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1781335/000178133526000011/otis-20251231.htm).

## 2. Quality
Service's dominance of profit (91% of segment profit from 65% of sales) gives a recurring, high-margin base; ROIC 65.3% and ROCE 84.2% TTM, both rising over 5 years [Certain] (source: fact sheet RA). Moat: ~2.5m-unit maintenance portfolio, 37,000 mechanics across 1,400+ branches, ~4,600 issued patents — service-network scale and switching costs are hard to replicate [Certain] (source: 10-K, above). Operating margin rose FY21→FY25 (15.2%→16.5%) [Certain] (fact sheet IS), but Q2 2026 service margin fell 170bp on labor and aging-equipment material cost inflation [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/otis/transcripts/652920-q2-2026/), prompting a FY2026 operating-profit guidance cut. Net debt/EBITDA has risen to 3.04x TTM from 2.62x FY21, and FCF conversion has fallen from 128% to 113% TTM [Certain] (fact sheet RA/CF/IS) — leverage drift and conversion softness are the real quality risks, not the installed-base economics.

## 3. Valuation
At the 66.45 close, OTIS trades at 17.1x P/E, 12.6x EV/EBITDA, 2.2x EV/Sales and 14.8x P/FCF — each below its own 5-year range minimum and 16–39% below the JCI/CARR/HON/LII peer median [Certain] (fact sheet RA, P:peers). FCF yield is 6.77% [Certain] (fact sheet ST). The shares are down 27.4% over 12 months and 43.8pp behind SPY, near the 52-week low of 63.58 [Certain] (fact sheet HI/ST). The price assumes the FY2026 margin cut (operating profit guided "down $30m to flat") is a durable deterioration rather than a transitory labor/productivity headwind, and gives no credit to management's claim of accelerating modernization backlog (+26% YoY) [Likely] (source: transcript, above). With quality metrics still rising on a 5-year view, the discount to both own history and peers looks more sentiment/CEO-transition driven than fundamentally justified [Likely].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Service operating margin (quarterly filings/earnings release) falls below 20% for two consecutive quarters, vs 23.2% in Q2 2026.
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) rises above 3.5x, vs 3.04x TTM.
- New Equipment organic sales decline (earnings release) widens beyond -5% for two consecutive quarters, vs -1% in Q2 2026.
- Modernization backlog growth (earnings release) turns negative, vs +26% YoY in Q2 2026.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Shares outstanding fell 2.55% YoY, implying net buybacks [Certain] (fact sheet ST). FCF margin is 11.5% TTM with FCF conversion of 112.9% of net income [Certain] (fact sheet CF/IS), comfortably funding the $0.44/share quarterly dividend (source: headline cited in fact sheet, https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/otis-declares-quarterly-dividend-of-0-44-per-share-302833623.html) and buybacks without reliance on fresh debt. R&D spend is low at ~1.1% of sales ($152m, 2025), consistent with an asset-light service model [Certain] (10-K, above). The 10-K gives no specific buyback-pace target, so forward capital-return cadence beyond the share count trend is [data unavailable].
