# Otis Worldwide (OTIS) — Core initiation — 2026-11-08
## 1. Business
Otis makes and installs elevators and escalators (New Equipment) and maintains, repairs and modernises them (Service). Service was 65% of 2025 net sales and about 91% of segment operating profit [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1781335/000178133526000011/otis-20251231.htm). China is the key foreign exposure: about one fifth of global New Equipment sales, over half of unit volume; its total share of sales is not disclosed in the text read [company-reported] (same source). [Certain]

## 2. Quality
The moat is the installed base: Service contracts on equipment that cannot easily be switched are recurring and high-margin [Likely]. ROIC is 58.0% TTM (5y trend rising), gross margin 30.0%, FCF conversion 107.5% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/otis/financials/ratios/). [Certain]

Direction changes addressed:
- ROIC/ROCE sit 37.7pp / 35.6pp below 2-year highs, yet are up 6.8pp / 8.1pp on a year earlier. The peak was an outlier against a 5y base of 31-55%; not deterioration [Likely].
- Operating margin: 15.5% TTM versus 16.5% a year ago. Q3 gross margin fell 120bp on labour and material costs [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1781335/000178133526000127/otis-20260930.htm). Real, unresolved pressure [Likely].
- FCF margin recovered to 11.1% from 9.2%; the FY26 FCF range was narrowed (source: https://www.tipranks.com/news/the-fly/otis-worldwide-backs-fy26-adjusted-eps-view-4-01-4-05-consensus-4-04-thefly-news). [Likely]

## 3. Valuation
At 78.96 USD the stock trades below its own 5y minimum on every multiple: P/E 19.6x (5y 22.5-29.7x), EV/EBITDA 15.1x (16.4-17.7x), P/FCF 18.0x (22.6-25.7x), and 18-32% below the peer median (CARR, JCI, TT, KONE). FCF yield is 5.57%. [Certain]

The mechanical fair value is below the price: base 70.99, bear 56.96, bull 71.64 (DCF 60.40, EV/EBITDA 92.18). The price implies 6.7% year-1 revenue growth fading to terminal. The market pays for a de-rating's reversal, not a cash-flow bargain [Likely]. The stock is 12.3% above its 50-day average, RSI 68.1, and 13.5% down over 12 months. (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/otis/statistics/)

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Service organic sales growth (10-Q MD&A) below 3% in any quarter.
- TTM operating margin (income statement, trailing page) below 14.5%.
- Net debt / EBITDA (ratios page) above 3.5x.
- FY26 adjusted free cash flow guidance (earnings release) cut below 1.4bn USD.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Shares are down 2.60% YoY via buybacks, and the board declared a quarterly dividend (source: https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/otis-declares-quarterly-dividend-of-0-44-per-share-302924212.html). FCF conversion is 107.5% of net income. Net debt/EBITDA has crept up to 3.12x from 2.62x in FY2021, so returns are partly debt-funded [Likely]. The UpLift restructuring is nearly complete [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1781335/000178133526000127/otis-20260930.htm). [Certain]
