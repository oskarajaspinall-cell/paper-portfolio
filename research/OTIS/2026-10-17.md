# Otis Worldwide Corporation (OTIS) — Core initiation — 2026-10-17

## 1. Business
Otis makes, installs and services elevators and escalators in 200+ countries. New Equipment (35% of 2025 net sales, 9% of segment profit) [company-reported] sells units to builders/developers; Service (65% of sales, 91% of segment profit) [company-reported] performs maintenance, repair and modernization on an installed base of ~2.5m units, earning high-margin recurring revenue over equipment lifecycles of decades [Certain] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1781335/000178133526000011/otis-20251231.htm).

## 2. Quality
TTM returns are unchanged since initiation: ROIC 65.3% and ROCE 84.2%, operating margin 16.6% and FCF margin 11.5%, all still above the invalidation triggers set at entry (15.2%/10.0%) [Certain] (fact sheet RA/IS/CF). The SEC 10-Q delta confirms the mix driving that quality: organic volume grew 9% in Service for H1 2026 vs a 1-3% New Equipment decline, and the UpLift program delivered ~$200m of run-rate savings, with ~$103m of annual recurring savings approved and $51m realized in H1 2026 [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1781335/000178133526000113/otis-20260630.htm). The German tax litigation payable to RTX fell to $55m from $56m, a small de-risking [company-reported, same URL]. Net debt/EBITDA has drifted further to 3.04x TTM from 2.62x FY2021 [Certain] (fact sheet RA) — leverage drift, not the installed-base economics, remains the real quality risk, unchanged from initiation.

At the 71.33 close, OTIS trades at 18.3x P/E, 13.3x EV/EBITDA, 2.4x EV/Sales and 15.9x P/FCF — still below its own 5-year range minimum on every multiple and 5-40% below the JCI/HON/KONE peer median [Certain] (fact sheet RA, P:peers). FCF yield is 6.30% [Certain] (fact sheet ST). The base fair value is now 71.95 (bear 56.55 / bull 72.62), essentially level with the close (+0.9%) [Certain] (fact sheet valuation table); the scan's fair-value-reached trigger reflects the stock closing in on a single model estimate, not evidence the thesis has played out — fair value is one estimate, never the thesis itself [Likely]. ## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Operating margin (ratios page) falls below the FY2021 5-year low of 15.2%, vs 16.6% TTM now.
- FCF margin (ratios page) falls below the FY2025 5-year low of 10.0%, vs 11.5% TTM now.
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) rises above 3.5x, vs 3.04x TTM now.
- Service operating margin (Oct 28 earnings release) falls below 20% for two consecutive quarters, or modernization backlog growth turns negative.
