# Macro overlay: Otis Worldwide Corporation (OTIS) — 2026-10-17
## Assessment
Otis is a quality service compounder (91% of segment profit from recurring maintenance) [Certain] (note §1-2), not a classic macro-cyclical, but two variables touch its valuation and translated earnings. (1) Discount rate: the DCF base fair value ($71.95) is essentially level with the $71.33 close and uses the FRED 10y Treasury as risk-free (5.10%, 2026-10-15, +0.53pp/3m); the real yield (DFII10) rose +0.43pp to 2.78% over the same window [Certain] (https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=DGS10, https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=DFII10). Breakeven inflation (T10YIE) is flat (+0.11pp to 2.35%, 2026-10-16, https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=T10YIE) — **(inference)** nearly all the nominal move is a real-rate/discount-rate rise, not an inflation surprise. A further rise would push the already-slim +0.9% margin of safety below zero. (2) FX: the broad dollar index jumped +3.72 over 1m to 121.6 vs only +1.1 over 3m (https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=DTWEXBGS, 2026-10-09) — a late, sharp move postdating Q3 (ended Sept) that could pressure Q4 translation of Otis's international (incl. China) revenue [Likely] **(inference)**, given its 200+-country footprint. Neither variable sits inside the core thesis (service mix, leverage), so this stays secondary, not primary.
**macro_bull**: real yields/dollar plateau or retrace — DCF fair value holds or rises, multiple re-rates toward the peer median.
**macro_bear**: real yields and dollar keep climbing (higher-for-longer Fed) — discount rate rises further, fair value falls below the close, and Q4 FX translation trims reported international revenue/EPS.
**macro_tilt**: small, toward bear — the margin/leverage thesis dominates; macro only erodes the already-thin valuation cushion.
