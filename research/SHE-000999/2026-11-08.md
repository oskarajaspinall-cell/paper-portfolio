# China Resources Sanjiu Medical & Pharmaceutical (SHE:000999) — Core initiation — 2026-11-08
## 1. Business
Chinese branded pharmaceutical maker (industry per stockanalysis.com: Drug Manufacturers - Specialty & Generic), selling mainly non-prescription and prescription medicines through pharmacy and hospital channels, within the China Resources group [Likely]. Revenue is earned overwhelmingly in mainland China, reported in CNY; no material foreign-country share is evident, though a split is [data unavailable] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/she/000999/).

## 2. Quality
Profitable and cash-generative, but returns have stepped down. ROIC was 17.7% in FY2024, 13.8% in FY2025 and 10.4% TTM; ROCE 15.8% to 10.2% to 10.3% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/she/000999/financials/ratios/) [Certain]. Operating margin is steady at 14.2% TTM vs 14.0% in FY2025, gross margin 55.6% TTM, so the decline is in capital efficiency and net margin (10.4% TTM vs 12.2% FY2024) rather than pricing power [Certain]. Direction change: over the last 12 months ROIC rose from 9.1% to 10.4% and operating margin from 12.3% to 14.2% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/she/000999/financials/ratios/?p=trailing), so the fall looks to have troughed in 2025, but it remains well below the 17.5% two-year high [Likely]. The moat (consumer brand recognition, distribution, state-owned parent) is plausible but not evidenced in the fact sheet [Guessing]. Net cash (net debt/EBITDA -0.61x), Piotroski 7, Altman Z 3.20 [Certain].

## 3. Valuation
P/E 13.4x is below its range of 13.8x-18.9x (median 16.9x); EV/EBITDA 9.5x is below its 11.1x-12.9x range; P/B 1.1x is below the range minimum; P/FCF 12.4x is near the low end (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/she/000999/statistics/) [Certain]. Peer medians (Yunnan Baiyao, Pientzehuang, Tong Ren Tang, Dong-E-E-Jiao) are far higher: P/E 29.5x (-55% for us), EV/EBITDA 16.2x (-41%) [Certain], though the peers are premium-brand names with higher returns [Likely]. FCF yield is 8.07% [Certain]. Mechanical fair value (DCF 67%, P/E 33%): base 34.55 CNY vs 25.95 close (+33.2%), bear 20.80 (-19.8%), bull 50.08 (+93.0%), with a 3.5% owner-set China risk premium in the cost of capital [Certain]. The reverse DCF implies only 2.6% year-1 revenue growth fading to terminal [Certain]. The price therefore assumes no recovery in returns; the margin of safety rests on ROIC holding near the current level, not on re-rating to peers [Likely].
