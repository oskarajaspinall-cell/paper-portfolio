# General Mills, Inc. (GIS) — Core initiation — 2026-10-12

## 1. Business
General Mills makes and markets packaged food — cereal, snacks, baking mixes, convenience meals and pet food (Blue Buffalo) — sold mainly through North America Retail, Pet, North America Foodservice and International segments, largely via grocery, club and mass retailers [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/gis/).

## 2. Quality
Historically a solid, if unspectacular, branded-foods franchise: TTM ROIC 13.8% and ROCE 12.3%, and gross margin has held a tight 32.6–35.0% band over five years, implying retained pricing power [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/gis/financials/ratios/, https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/gis/financials/income-statement/). But FY2026 broke that pattern sharply: ROE swung to -1.0% (from +24.6% FY2025), net margin to -0.5% and FCF conversion to -1,857% of net income, while operating margin slipped to 15.7% from 19.0% two years earlier [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/gis/financials/ratios/). The fact sheet does not show the cause of this one-year collapse (no impairment or charge line is broken out); a swing of this size most plausibly reflects a large non-cash write-down [Likely], but the specific driver is [data unavailable]. Net debt/EBITDA has also risen steadily, to 3.86x TTM from 2.83x in FY2022 [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/gis/financials/ratios/). The brand moat (Cheerios, Pillsbury, Betty Crocker) is real but FY2026 shows returns and margins no longer durably protected from cost and volume pressure.

## 3. Valuation
The market is pricing GIS below its own five-year multiple history on EV/EBITDA (8.9x vs a 9.1–16.4x range) and EV/Sales (1.7x vs 1.7–3.0x), both at or below the five-year floor [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/gis/financials/ratios/). Yet versus packaged-food peers (K, CPB, KHC, SJM) it is not obviously cheap: EV/EBITDA is +7% above the peer median, P/FCF +35% above and P/B +25% above, while only EV/Sales is cheaper (-14%) [Certain] (source: peer statistics pages). P/E is unavailable because TTM EPS is negative [Certain]. The DCF-based base fair value of $48.97 implies +51.6% upside to the $32.29 close, but the reverse-DCF shows the price already assumes -5.4% revenue growth in year one fading to terminal growth — a pessimism level only justified if the FY2026 margin collapse is structural rather than one-off [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/gis/financials/ratios/). The stock is a show-me story, not a clean statistical bargain.

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) rises further from the current 3.86x without a disclosed deleveraging plan.
- FCF margin (cash flow vs income statement) stays negative, or recovers to below 5%, over the next two quarters.
- Gross margin (income statement) breaks below its five-year floor of ~32.6%, signaling lost pricing power.
- Net margin/ROE (ratios page) fails to recover from FY2026's -0.5%/-1.0% levels within two further quarters, confirming the collapse is structural rather than a one-off charge.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Shares outstanding fell 2.80% year over year, showing continued buybacks despite the earnings collapse [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/gis/statistics/). The company declared its regular quarterly dividend on 2026-09-29, signaling no near-term cut [Certain] (source: https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260929240468/en/General-Mills-Quarterly-Dividend-Declared/, as listed in the fact sheet). It also priced and completed cash tender offers for senior notes in early October 2026, consistent with active liability management as leverage has risen [Likely] (source: https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20261009314327/en/General-Mills-Announces-Pricing-Terms-Expiration-and-Results-of-Cash-Tender-Offers-for-its-Senior-Notes/, as listed in the fact sheet). FCF margin collapsed to 8.5% TTM from a 10.4–14.5% range in FY2022–2025 [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/gis/financials/ratios/), so cash conversion cannot currently be relied on as a cushion.

## 6. Unusual items
Board named insider COO Dana McNabb as CEO, succeeding Jeff Harmening, announced 2026-09-30 — an internal succession that preserves continuity but comes amid the weakest reported results in five years [Likely] (source: as listed in the fact sheet, https://www.reuters.com/business/general-mills-names-insider-dana-mcnabb-ceo-2026-09-30/). Short interest is elevated at 8.42% of float, up slightly month over month, with press noting heavy bearish put activity, consistent with the market pricing real distress risk into the shares [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/gis/statistics/, headline as listed in the fact sheet).
