# Macro overlay: General Mills, Inc. (GIS) — 2026-10-12
## Assessment
GIS is domestic staples, but carries elevated leverage (net debt/EBITDA 3.86x TTM, rising) and is actively managing its debt stack (senior-note tender offers priced 2026-10-09, per fact sheet). That makes rates secondary, not primary: material to the discount rate and refinancing cost, not the core margin-recovery thesis.

**Facts** [Certain]: US 10y real yield (DFII10) 2.87% on 2026-10-08, +44bp 1m / +56bp 3m (https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=DFII10). Nominal 10y (DGS10) 5.22%, +42bp/+68bp. 10y breakeven inflation (T10YIE) roughly flat at 2.33%, -4bp 1m (https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=T10YIE) — the yield rise is a real-rate move, not inflation expectations. Fed funds (DFF) 3.88%, +25bp 1m (https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=DFF). HY credit spread 3.15%, +48bp 1m/+45bp 3m (https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=BAMLH0A0HYM2) — widening, not yet stressed.

**Market pricing** (proxy, **(inference)**): 2y yield (4.75%) sits ~87bp above fed funds (3.88%), implying the market isn't pricing near-term cuts and may expect "higher for longer" [Guessing].

**Chain**: real yields -> cost of equity in GIS's DCF (base case uses 5.22% 10y as risk-free) and cost of refinancing -> fair value range and interest expense, amplified by 3.86x leverage.

**Bull**: real yields/fed funds roll over -> discount rate falls, DCF band widens upward, refinancing cheaper mid-tender. **Bear**: real yields keep rising on sticky core CPI -> discount rate rises further, compressing the fair value band, refinancing coupons reset higher on a balance sheet with negative TTM FCF conversion — compounding the margin problem.

**Tilt**: moderate toward bear — a genuine real-rate rise (inflation expectations flat), hitting a levered, FCF-negative-TTM issuer mid-refinancing, adding downside to an already reverse-DCF "show-me" valuation.
