# GSK plc (LON:GSK) — Core initiation — 2026-10-19
## 1. Business
GSK plc is a UK-based biopharma earning revenue from prescription medicines (HIV via its ViiV franchise, respiratory, immuno-inflammation, oncology) and vaccines (Shingrix, Arexvy, meningitis/MMR), sold globally to health systems, payers and distributors, plus partner royalty/licensing income. [Certain]

## 2. Quality
ROIC has risen from 13.5% (FY21) to 26.4% TTM and ROCE from 10.2% to 24.1%, with operating margin expanding to 29.2% (ratios/income-statement pages) [Certain]. Durable edges: Shingrix and the ViiV HIV portfolio carry patent protection and high switching costs (physician/payer formulary inertia), and vaccine manufacturing scale plus regulatory approval limit new entrants [Likely]. Net debt/EBITDA is moderate at 1.40x TTM, down from 2.48x in FY21 (ratios page) [Certain]. The main flag: FCF margin has fallen from 28.4% (FY21) to 20.8% TTM and FCF conversion from 159.7% to 143.1% (cash-flow/income-statement pages), a five-year declining trend worth monitoring given continued capex, including a US manufacturing expansion (source: https://www.reuters.com/business/healthcare-pharmaceuticals/pennsylvania-says-gsk-invest-more-than-800-million-biopharma-hub-create-300-jobs-2026-10-09/, 2026-10-09) [Likely]. Beta of 0.28 (statistics page) signals a defensive earnings profile typical of large pharma. [Certain]

## 3. Valuation
At 15.8x P/E, 8.1x EV/EBITDA, 2.7x EV/Sales and 10.8x P/FCF, GSK trades 29-34% below the peer median (AZN, PFE, SNY, NVS) on every earnings/cash-flow multiple, and EV/EBITDA sits near the bottom of its own five-year range (17th percentile) [Certain]. Only P/B (4.3x) is above peers (+23%), consistent with a franchise still printing high ROE (33.4% TTM) rather than a value trap [Likely]. FCF yield of 9.24% (statistics page) is high for a large-cap pharma [Certain]. The fact sheet's DCF/P/E fair-value model could not be computed — [data unavailable — no FX rate for GBP] — so there is no model anchor; the discount must be judged on relative multiples alone, which suggest the market is pricing in patent-cliff/pipeline risk that is not yet showing up in the margin and ROIC trend already in the data [Likely].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROIC (ratios page) falls below 15%, reversing the five-year rising trend (FY21 13.5% → TTM 26.4%)
- FCF margin (cash-flow/income-statement pages) falls below 12%, continuing the five-year declining trend (FY21 28.4% → TTM 20.8%)
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) rises above 2.5x, back toward the FY21 peak of 2.48x
- Operating margin (income-statement page) falls below 20%, reversing the five-year expansion to 29.2%

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
FCF conversion remains well above net income (143.1% TTM; cash-flow/income-statement pages) [Certain], funding both the dividend and continued investment; shares outstanding fell 1.36% YoY (statistics page), indicating net buybacks [Certain]. Net debt/EBITDA of 1.40x TTM leaves headroom for bolt-on M&A and the announced US manufacturing expansion without material re-leveraging [Likely]. The declining five-year FCF margin trend (Quality section) means cash conversion, while still strong, is less of a buffer than three years ago [Likely].

## 6. Unusual items
GSK is cutting up to roughly 1,200 roles in Belgium under a restructuring plan, with the Belgian government saying it will monitor it closely (source: https://www.nasdaq.com/articles/belgian-minister-closely-monitor-gsk-restructuring-plan, 2026-10-16) [Likely] — a cost/footprint action not yet visible in reported margins; watch for one-off charges. GSK also reported first global data for its B7-H3 ADC risvutatug rezetecan in relapsed small-cell lung cancer (source: https://www.tipranks.com/news/the-fly/gsk-announces-global-data-for-risvutatug-rezetecan-thefly-news, 2026-10-18) [Guessing] on commercial significance — an early pipeline readout, not yet a valuation driver. Institutional ownership is high at 84.5% (statistics page) [Certain]; Altman Z of 2.20 sits in the grey zone typical for asset-light large pharma, not a distress signal given Piotroski F of 7 [Likely].
