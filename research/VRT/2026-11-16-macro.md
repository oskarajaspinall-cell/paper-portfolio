# Macro overlay: Vertiv Holdings Co (VRT) — 2026-11-16
## Assessment
**Weight: secondary.** No foreign country is disclosed at ~10%+ of sales, so there is no country-risk item.

**Chain:** real yields -> discount rate -> multiple of a long-duration, high-beta (2.11) AI-capex name. Credit spreads and risk appetite are a second link, through customer capex financing and the equity risk premium.

**Facts (FRED, https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=DFII10, 2026-11-12):** the 10y real yield is 2.86%, up 0.47pp over 3 months. The 2y is 4.86% against fed funds of 3.88%. The high-yield spread is 3.20% (+0.49pp over 3m) and VIX is 18.7. Breakevens are 2.24% (-0.03pp over 3m), so the rise is real-rate driven.

**Pricing (proxy):** the 2y about 100bp above fed funds implies hikes are priced **(inference)** [Likely]. VRT is -29.6% over 3m against SPY -3.0% (fact sheet), and P/E is below its 5y minimum, so much of the rate effect is probably in the price **(inference)** [Guessing]. The rest looks company- or policy-specific (5 Nov midterm worries in the note).

**Bull:** real yields ease toward 2.5% and spreads hold near 3%; part of the de-rating reverses with growth unchanged.
**Bear:** real yields above 3.25% and spreads above 4%; the multiple compresses further and customer financing tightens.
**Tilt:** toward bear, small. The rate move has largely happened, and the thesis rests on AI demand.
