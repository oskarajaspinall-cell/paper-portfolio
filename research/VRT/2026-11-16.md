# Vertiv Holdings Co (VRT) — Core initiation — 2026-11-16
## 1. Business
Vertiv sells power (UPS, switchgear, busway), thermal (liquid and air cooling) and monitoring equipment plus service for data centers; AI/high-performance compute is the growth driver [Certain] (source: https://www.vertiv.com). Q3 2026 net sales were $4,312.8m [company-reported]: Americas $2,988.8m, Asia Pacific $779.3m, EMEA $544.7m (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1674101/000162828026067893/vrt-20260930.htm). No single foreign country is disclosed at ~10%+ [Likely].

## 2. Quality
Returns are exceptional and still rising: TTM ROIC 43.8% vs 27.4% a year earlier, operating margin 21.1% (FY2022: 4.0%), FCF margin 24.0%, FCF/NI 150.0%, net cash (net debt/EBITDA -0.11x) (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/vrt/financials/ratios/?p=trailing) [Certain]. Q3 operating profit rose 82.4% YoY on operating leverage [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1674101/000162828026067893/vrt-20260930.htm). Moat is moderate: installed base, service, systems integration and Nvidia collaboration (ATLAS validation, 800V DC roadmap from 2H27) [Likely]; but the 2022 margin of 4.0% shows the business was low-return before this AI cycle, and returns depend on AI capex staying high [Likely]. Beta 2.11 shows how cyclical sentiment is [Certain].

## 3. Valuation
P/E 35.4x is below its 5y minimum (39.8x), P/FCF 23.7x is at its 5y minimum, EV/EBITDA 23.3x sits at 59% of its range and about in line with peers (23.8x); P/E is 31% above peers, P/FCF 15% above (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/vrt/statistics/) [Certain]. FCF yield is 4.22%. Mechanical fair value base is 93.70 vs close 202.07; bull 175.38 is still below the price, and the reverse DCF needs 29.4% year-1 revenue growth [Certain]. The DCF is blunt for a business whose returns tripled, so the gap overstates the downside [Guessing]. Price assumes AI build-out growth continues for years; the stock is 25.6% below its 200-day average, so the trend is broken, not healthy [Certain].

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Cash conversion is strong (FCF/NI 150.0%) and the balance sheet moved from 5.80x leverage (FY2022) to net cash. Management is raising capex materially to add capacity, and has raised its dividend (source: https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/vertiv-declares-quarterly-dividend-302938565.html) [Likely]. Share count creep is small (0.77% YoY). New credit-agreement covenant risk factors (leverage cap of 4.00:1.00, stepping up with large acquisitions) are boilerplate with ample headroom at net cash [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1674101/000162828026067893/vrt-20260930.htm) [Likely].
