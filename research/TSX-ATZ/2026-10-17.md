# Aritzia Inc. (TSX:ATZ) — Tactical initiation — 2026-10-17

## 1. Catalyst and why now
On 2026-10-09 ATZ closed +20.5% on 3.5x average volume after Q2 FY27 results; the fact sheet's earnings-reaction row shows that three sessions later the stock has held 109% of that jump, more than the entire move (source: fact sheet, HI row) [Certain]. Headlines report a broad beat across revenue, comparable sales and margins, plus a raised FY27 revenue and gross-margin outlook driven by US demand (sources: https://financialpost.com/news/retail-marketing/aritzia-raises-2027-outlook-q2-revenue-up; https://www.wsj.com/business/retail/aritzia-lifts-guidance-as-u-s-sales-support-higher-profit-revenue-202583d7) [Likely]. Full retention of the jump plus a guidance raise points to a genuine re-rating rather than a one-off pop [Likely].

## 2. What the price already assumes
At 30.1x TTM P/E, 18.4x EV/EBITDA and 4.0x EV/Sales, ATZ trades above its own 5-year range (EV/Sales at 105% of range and P/B at 117%, both above the 5-year max) and far above its apparel-retail peer median (P/E +185%, EV/EBITDA +137%, EV/Sales +262% vs. LULU/URBN/ANF/GPS) [Certain]. The market is pricing sustained US-led growth and margin expansion well beyond the quarter just reported [Likely]. A 14-day RSI of 70.2 confirms the stock is already overbought [Certain].
