# Macro overlay: Aritzia Inc. (TSX:ATZ) — 2026-10-17

## Assessment
ATZ's tactical thesis is idiosyncratic (a Q2 beat + raised FY27 guidance, +20.5% on 3.5x volume, held) [Certain], but the setup already prices a lot: 30.1x TTM P/E, 4.0x EV/Sales (105% of its 5y range) and a 1.73 beta [Certain, fact sheet]. That makes the multiple sensitive to the discount rate, so one macro variable can shift the odds within the 8-week hold: **real yields → discount rate on an extended, high-beta multiple → re-rating risk**.

Facts (FRED, 2026-10-15/16): the 10y real yield (DFII10) is 2.78%, +43bp in ~3m; the 10y nominal yield is 5.10% (+53bp/3m) while breakevens are flat at 2.35% (+11bp/3m) — most of the yield rise is real, not inflation expectations, consistent with firmer growth pricing rather than an inflation scare [Certain fact, interpretation **(inference)**]. The 2y yield (4.77%) sits above fed funds (3.88%), a proxy that the market isn't pricing near-term cuts **(inference)** [Likely]. VIX is low and falling (16.46, -0.74/1m), supportive of risk appetite; HY spreads widened modestly (3.23%, +0.47/1m) but stay far below stress levels (5.0%) [Certain].

**Bull**: real yields stabilize/ease, low VIX persists → discount-rate pressure eases, the market tolerates the stretched multiple, drift continues toward the 180.34 target. **Bear**: real yields extend their 3-month climb and/or VIX/credit spreads turn → discount rate rises further on an already-stretched multiple → compounds the extended RSI (70.2), raising odds of hitting the 133.45 stop before the target. **Tilt**: toward bear, small — the idiosyncratic beat dominates; macro only adds a secondary valuation headwind given beta and multiple.
