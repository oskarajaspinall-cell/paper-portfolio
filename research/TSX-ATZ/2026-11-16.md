# Aritzia Inc. (TSX:ATZ) — Core initiation — 2026-11-16
## 1. Business

## 2. Quality
Strong and improving. TTM ROIC 25.2% vs 16.0% a year earlier; gross margin 46.9%, the highest in five years; operating margin 16.5% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tsx/ATZ/financials/ratios/?p=trailing) [Certain]. Direction change addressed: operating and FCF margins fell over five fiscal years because FY2024 was depressed (operating margin 6.8%), but the last 12 months rose to 16.5% and 15.3%, so the five-year "falling" label reflects a trough-to-peak start point, not decay [Likely]. Net debt/EBITDA 0.78x, Altman Z 6.29 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tsx/ATZ/statistics/) [Certain]. Moat is brand and product-led pricing power, shown by margin expansion alongside rapid growth. Fashion is fickle, so durability is unproven [Guessing]. Q2 results beat management's expectations and guidance was lifted (source: https://www.wsj.com/business/retail/aritzia-lifts-guidance-as-u-s-sales-support-higher-profit-revenue-202583d7) [Likely].

## 3. Valuation
Not cheap, and the dip is no dip: the 155.45 CAD close is 12.3% above the 50-day and 13.9% above the 200-day average (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tsx/ATZ/history/). P/E 30.2x sits at the 22% position of its 5y range (24.7x–49.6x), EV/EBITDA 19.2x at 51%, P/FCF 26.4x at 36%; EV/Sales 4.2x and P/B 11.8x exceed their five-year maxima (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tsx/ATZ/financials/ratios/) [Certain]. Peers (LULU, ANF, AEO, GIL) trade at a median 11.7x P/E, a premium of 157% [Certain]. Mechanical fair value is base 72.08 CAD and bull 155.38 CAD; the price equals the bull case and implies 29.8% year-1 revenue growth, fading [Certain]. The DCF probably undercounts a long growth runway, but the price already pays for execution [Likely]. FCF yield 3.79%.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
FCF conversion 112.5% of net income TTM and FCF margin 15.3%, so growth is self-funded [Certain]. Leverage fell from 2.93x in FY2024 to 0.78x. Shares change YoY 1.09%, mild dilution. Management targets 15%–17% annual growth and about 100 bps of EBITDA margin expansion to fiscal 2032 [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tsx/ATZ/transcripts/766695-investor-day-2026/). Past FCF volatility (FY2023 negative, -2.2% margin) shows inventory and capex swings in expansion years [Certain].
