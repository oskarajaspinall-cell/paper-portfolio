# Regeneron Pharmaceuticals (REGN) — Core initiation — 2026-10-12

## 1. Business
Regeneron discovers, develops and commercializes biologic medicines, mostly antibodies [Certain]. Revenue is driven by three Regeneron-discovered products: Dupixent (IL-4/IL-13 antibody, profit-shared ~50/50 in the US with Sanofi, sliding 55/45-65/35 ex-US favoring Sanofi, after Regeneron reimburses 30-50% of Sanofi's development spend) [Certain] (source: SEC 10-K); Eylea/Eylea HD (anti-VEGF retinal disease, sold directly by Regeneron) [Certain]; and Libtayo (oncology) [Certain]. It also earns collaboration/manufacturing fees from Sanofi and Bayer [Certain].

## 2. Quality
Formerly exceptional returns are fading fast: ROIC 60.1%→13.5% TTM, ROE 54.2%→14.0%, gross margin 67.0%→44.5%, operating margin 56.0%→26.9% over FY21-TTM [Certain] (source: fact sheet, ratios/income-statement pages). Driver: legacy Eylea net sales fell from $8.10bn (FY24) to $5.32bn (FY25) as Eylea HD and competing anti-VEGF products erode the franchise, only partly offset by Eylea HD growth ($1.44bn→$2.57bn) [Certain] (source: SEC 10-K). 77% of Regeneron-discovered product revenue now comes from Dupixent, where Regeneron captures only shared economics with Sanofi, not the full upside [Certain] (source: SEC 10-K) — durable (broad label, entrenched biology) but not fully owned. Balance sheet is a strength: net cash on both net-debt/EBITDA definitions, Altman Z 6.47, FCF conversion rising to 86.9% TTM [Certain] (source: fact sheet). The Oct-2026 Sanofi alliance expansion (four new immunology antibodies, $1bn upfront/up to $7bn milestones) extends the runway, but pivotal data isn't due before 2027-28 [Likely].

## 3. Valuation
REGN trades at 18.5x TTM P/E — 67% up its own 5y range (avg 17.6x) but a 38% discount to the peer median (29.7x) [Certain] (source: fact sheet, ratios page). EV/EBITDA 12.6x is 15% below peers, EV/Sales 3.8x is 45% below peers; P/FCF 19.8x is the one multiple where REGN isn't cheap (+9% vs peers) [Certain]. The discount looks earned, not a free lunch: it prices in the margin/ROIC deterioration above and Dupixent's shared economics, not a compounder the market is missing. The fact sheet's own-history fair value (EV/Revenue, 100% weight — rNPV/pipeline-value inputs are [data unavailable] on stockanalysis.com) puts base fair value at $780.30 vs last close $746.54 — only +4.5% upside [Certain]; bear/bull are -11.8%/+25.5% [Certain]. A near-flat base case and a ~12% bear case, against a bull case needing early-stage pipeline optionality to read through, is a thin margin of safety for a franchise whose growth engine is already cracking.

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Gross margin (financials/income-statement page) stabilizes or rises back above 48% TTM, signalling the Eylea erosion has bottomed.
- ROIC (financials/ratios page) stops falling and prints two consecutive FY readings at/above 20%.
- Dupixent net sales growth (income statement / 10-K segment disclosure) decelerates below 10% y/y, removing the one offsetting growth engine.
- Net debt/EBITDA (balance sheet) turns positive, indicating capital discipline on buybacks/R&D has slipped.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Self-funded and net cash; TTM FCF margin 24.2% with 86.9% FCF conversion of net income [Certain] (source: fact sheet). Shares outstanding fell 4.76% YoY, confirming an active buyback [Certain] (source: fact sheet, statistics page). No meaningful debt on either net-debt/EBITDA definition [Certain]. The Sanofi collaborations (Dupixent, and the new Oct-2026 four-antibody deal) externalize part of R&D cost and risk — Regeneron reimburses 30-50% of Sanofi's Dupixent development spend from shared profits, capped at 20% of quarterly profit [Certain] (source: SEC 10-K) — a capital-efficient way to fund pipeline breadth without further dilution.

## 6. Unusual items
Short interest is low and falling (2.24% of float, -14.9% month-on-month) [Certain] (source: fact sheet); institutional ownership is high (94.1%) [Certain]. Beta is unusually low at 0.15, flattering risk-adjusted metrics but also meaning the stock hasn't re-rated with the broader healthcare tape. No governance or accounting red flags; Piotroski F-Score of 5 is middling, consistent with a business past its peak-margin phase rather than one in distress.
