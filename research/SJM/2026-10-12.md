# The J. M. Smucker Company (SJM) — Core initiation — 2026-10-12

## 1. Business
Smucker makes and markets branded packaged foods: coffee (Folgers), pet food/snacks (Milk-Bone, Meow Mix), consumer foods (Jif, Smucker's, Uncrustables) and Sweet Baked Snacks (Hostess, acquired Nov 2023), sold mainly through US retail. Walmart is 34% of net sales [Certain] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/91419/000009141926000050/sjm-20260430.htm).

## 2. Quality
ROIC and ROCE rose over five years (+3.4pp, +1.6pp) but ROE swung to -1.2% (FY23) and -17.9% (FY25) before recovering to 3.9% TTM; FCF conversion vs net income swung to -785%/-833% those years — consistent with non-cash, goodwill-type charges, likely tied to Sweet Baked Snacks weakness: Barron's reported its sales fell 7% YoY last quarter [Likely] (source: https://www.barrons.com/articles/pepsico-smucker-food-stocks-gas-prices-4ec5b565). Gross (38.0% TTM) and operating margins (18.6% TTM) held up, and FCF margin is rising (17.3% TTM) — the core brands (Folgers, Jif, Uncrustables) still convert sales to cash well. The durable risk is structural: Walmart is 34% of net sales, giving one customer outsized leverage [Certain], and net debt/EBITDA stays elevated at 3.05x TTM (peaked 4.53x post-Hostess) [Certain]. Altman Z of 1.54 flags balance-sheet risk for a staple [Likely] — a watch item, offset for now by Piotroski F of 8.

## 3. Valuation
Headline P/E of 55.8x looks extreme (548% above its 5y range, +145% vs peer median) but it is a TTM net-margin artefact (2.5% TTM, depressed by the charges above), not overvaluation. Cash-flow multiples are less demanding: EV/EBITDA 8.7x is below its 5y range and +9% vs peer median; P/FCF 8.1x is below its 5y minimum and -5% vs peer median, with a 12.4% FCF yield. Fair-value base case ($217.63, +82% vs the $119.83 close) is driven mostly by DCF; the bear/bull spread ($69–$227) is unusually wide, so treat it cautiously [Guessing]. Reverse DCF implies the price assumes -12.9% revenue decline in year one fading to terminal growth — worse than guidance of a 1–2% FY2027 decline [Certain] (fact sheet, 2026-08-26). On cash-generative measures the stock is cheaper than its own history and in line with peers.

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) stops declining from the TTM 3.05x and rises back above 4x.
- ROIC (ratios page) falls back below 7% for two consecutive fiscal years.
- Gross margin (income statement) falls below its 5y low of 34.0%, signalling lost pricing power.
- Walmart's share of net sales (10-K) rises further or is renegotiated materially against Smucker.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
FCF margin is rising (12.8% FY26, 17.3% TTM) and FCF yield is 12.4%, well ahead of reported net margin — cash generation is outrunning accounting earnings. Management said on the August earnings call it hit its leverage targets early, enabling an increased dividend and potential buybacks [Likely] (source: fact sheet news, Q1 2027 transcript, 2026-08-26). Share count is essentially flat (+0.33% YoY) — no dilution. Net debt/EBITDA (3.05x TTM) is still above typical packaged-food peer levels but has fallen from its post-Hostess peak of 4.53x.

## 6. Unusual items
Short interest is 4.07% of float and falling (-5.6% month-on-month) — not a crowded short. Institutional ownership is high at 89.3%. Altman Z-score of 1.54 is a balance-sheet flag worth monitoring given the debt-funded Hostess acquisition, though Piotroski F of 8 and improving leverage trend offset it for now [Likely].
