# Macro overlay: Best Buy Co., Inc. (BBY) — 2026-10-09
## Assessment
BBY sells discretionary, often-financed big-ticket electronics/appliances, so real rates/consumer-credit cost are a genuine, secondary variable; the researched thesis itself (margin mix, ROIC decline) is fundamentals-led, not macro-led [Likely].

**Chain**: real yields -> consumer financing/credit-card cost -> big-ticket discretionary demand (comps); also real yields -> BBY's own DCF discount rate -> fair value.

**Facts** [Certain, FRED, 2026-10-07/08]: 10y Treasury (DGS10) 5.28%, +0.72pp/3m; 10y real yield (DFII10) 2.92%, +0.61pp/3m — almost all the nominal move is real, not inflation (breakeven, T10YIE, 2.35%, +0.12pp/3m); fed funds (DFF) 3.88%, +0.26pp/3m; HY spread (BAMLH0A0HYM2) 3.09%, +0.39pp but still low; VIX 15.1, -1.8pp — no stress. **Market pricing (proxy, inference)**: 2y yield (4.77%) sits ~0.9pp above fed funds, consistent with the market pricing continued policy firmness, not cuts, over the next two years.

**Inference**: rising real yields raise financing cost on BBY's store-card/installment purchases, a plausible drag on comps into the Nov 24 holiday print [Likely]; the factsheet's DCF already uses today's 5.28% risk-free rate, so the base case's thin 6% premium to last close has little cushion left against a further rise [Likely].

**Bull**: real yields roll over -> easier financing, firmer comps, lower discount rate -> fair value and multiple lift. **Bear**: real yields stay elevated/rise further -> tighter holiday financing, higher discount rate -> fair value compresses, compounding the existing margin/ROIC erosion. **Tilt**: toward bear, moderate — the asymmetric valuation (large downside tail, thin upside) is exactly what a further real-yield rise worsens mechanically, right before the Nov 24 earnings test.
