# Best Buy Co., Inc. (BBY) — Core initiation — 2026-10-09

## 1. Business
Best Buy is a U.S./Canada specialty retailer of consumer electronics, appliances and computing, selling through ~1,000 big-box and smaller-format stores plus bestbuy.com. Revenue comes from product sales (hardware, mostly thin-margin) plus services (Geek Squad installation/repair, memberships), third-party marketplace commissions and Best Buy Ads retail media, the latter two carrying materially higher margin [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/bby/transcripts/681799-q2-2027/).

## 2. Quality
Returns are still decent but eroding: ROIC fell from 68.4% (FY22) to 26.0% TTM and ROE from 64.5% to 43.1%, a structural decline, not one bad year [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/bby/financials/ratios/). Operating margin is thin at 4.3% TTM and net margin 3.0%, typical of a low-differentiation box retailer competing with Amazon, Walmart and Costco on identical SKUs [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/bby/financials/income-statement/). Memory/component cost inflation is a live headwind: management said computing ASPs rose "mid-teens" while units fell "high single digits," i.e. price rises are offsetting a real unit decline [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/bby/transcripts/681799-q2-2027/). Marketplace ($1.3bn GMV guided) and Best Buy Ads (~$900m, +10% growth) are genuine higher-margin levers but remain small next to total revenue — a mix tailwind, not yet a moat [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/bby/transcripts/681799-q2-2027/). Net debt/EBITDA is low at 0.66x, so balance-sheet risk is not the issue [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/bby/financials/ratios/). Durable but low-moat, margin-constrained, not a compounder.

## 3. Valuation
BBY trades at 14.7x P/E and 7.7x EV/EBITDA, 50-60% below the peer median (WMT/TGT/COST/AMZN, 30.2x/19.4x), but those peers are structurally higher-quality or higher-growth, so the discount is largely warranted, not a bargain signal [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/bby/statistics/). On its own history, P/E sits at the 50th percentile of its 5-year range (9.6x-19.8x) — mid-pack, not cheap [Certain]. P/FCF looks cheap at the 8th percentile of its 5-year range, but TTM/FY25 FCF conversion (139-150% of net income) is well above the FY23/FY24 run-rate (54-63%), likely flattered by working-capital timing, overstating cheapness [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/bby/financials/cash-flow-statement/). The fact sheet's DCF/EV-EBITDA blend puts base fair value at $83.12 versus a last close of $88.40 (price 6% above base case), bull case only $96.97 (+9.7%), bear case $33.62 (-62%) [Certain] (source: research/BBY/factsheet-2026-10-09.md). Asymmetry is unfavourable: limited upside, large downside tail.

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROIC (ratios page) stabilizes and holds above 30% for two consecutive fiscal years, reversing the 5-year decline.
- Operating margin (income statement) expands above 5.0%, showing marketplace/ads mix is becoming a real margin driver rather than offsetting hardware deflation.
- Comparable sales (next two quarterly reports) stay positive and gross margin (income statement) holds at/above 22.5% despite memory cost inflation, evidencing durable pricing power.
- Marketplace GMV or Best Buy Ads revenue (quarterly filings/transcripts) grows to a scale that visibly lifts consolidated gross margin, not just management commentary.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Buybacks are modest: $37m spent on 0.4m shares in H1 FY27 at $82.19 average, with $3.0bn left on a $5.0bn authorization (no expiry) — capital return is dividend-led, not buyback-led [Certain] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/764478/000076447826000042/bby-20260801.htm). Dividends paid were $405m H1 FY27 ($1.92/share); combined with TTM FCF margin of 4.2% and net debt/EBITDA of 0.66x, the payout looks covered but leaves little room for aggressive buybacks without FCF improving [Likely] (same source).

## 6. Unusual items
Short interest is 8.12% of float and rose 13.7% month-over-month, a meaningfully elevated and rising bearish position for a large-cap retailer [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/bby/statistics/). Best Buy received $34m of tariff refunds in Q2 FY27 plus a further $41m post-quarter (September 2026), covering "substantially all" of its IEEPA tariff refund claims — a one-off tailwind, not recurring, and tariff/trade policy remains an unresolved risk per the filing [Certain] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/764478/000076447826000042/bby-20260801.htm).
