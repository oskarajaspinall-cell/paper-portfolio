# Roche Holding AG (SWX:ROP) — Core initiation — 2026-11-16
## 1. Business
Roche sells patented prescription medicines (oncology, neurology, immunology, ophthalmology) and diagnostics (lab analysers, assays, tissue and molecular tests), the latter giving a recurring reagent stream on an installed base [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/swx/ROP/). Revenue split by division and by country: [data unavailable]; the company site could not be fetched (not on allowlist), so whether one foreign market is ~10%+ of revenue is unverified, and the US is likely material [Guessing]. Reports in CHF; the 5y beta is 0.32 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/swx/ROP/statistics/).

## 2. Quality
A high-return franchise: TTM ROIC 28.1% and ROCE 30.1%, TTM operating margin 33.1% on gross margin 74.2%, FCF margin 25.0%, FCF/net income 127.6% [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/swx/ROP/financials/ratios/). Balance sheet is conservative: net debt/EBITDA 0.98x, Altman Z 4.48 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/swx/ROP/statistics/) [Certain].

Direction: five-year ROIC is falling (38.3% in FY2021 to 28.1% TTM) and sits 2.5pp below its 2-year high of 30.6%, but the last 12 months are flat (-0.5pp) while operating margin rose 1.2pp to 33.1% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/swx/ROP/financials/ratios/?p=trailing). So the decline is a multi-year fade from a high base, not a current deterioration [Likely]. Moat: patent-protected drugs plus a diagnostics installed base; it is time-limited by patent expiries, so it depends on the pipeline refilling [Likely]. Pipeline news is steady (CHMP recommendation of Gazyva in lupus; source: https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/11/13/3402454/0/en/chmp-recommends-eu-approval-of-roche-s-gazyva-gazyvaro-for-systemic-lupus-erythematosus-sle.html) [Certain], but one label extension does not change the thesis.

## 3. Valuation
The price has re-rated. P/E 26.4x, EV/EBITDA 14.5x and P/FCF 20.5x are all above their 5y maxima (24.8x, 13.1x, 17.8x) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/swx/ROP/financials/ratios/). Against large-cap pharma peers (NVS, MRK, LLY, ABBV) it is cheaper: P/E 46% below the peer median, EV/EBITDA 12% below, P/FCF 22% below; but P/B is 22% above (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/swx/ROP/statistics/). Peer multiples are lifted by LLY and ABBV, so that discount flatters [Likely]. FCF yield is 4.87%.

Mechanical fair value (DCF 67%, P/E 33%) is base 285.29 CHF (bear 209.03, bull 306.59) against a 404.70 CHF close, so even the bull case sits 24.2% below the price. The reverse DCF implies 9.5% year-1 revenue growth, which is demanding for a large mature pharma [Likely]. The case rests on trend, not value: the price is 16.8% above its 200-day average, 12-month change +39.4% and relative strength against SPY positive (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/swx/ROP/history/) [Certain].

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
FCF conversion has run above 100% every year (FY2021-FY2025), with 127.6% on a TTM basis; FY2024 at 200.1% reflects depressed net income (net margin 13.3%) rather than a cash surge [Likely]. Shares outstanding rose 0.31% year on year, so no buyback shrinkage (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/swx/ROP/statistics/) [Certain]. Leverage is low, giving room for bolt-on M&A; deal discipline cannot be assessed from the fact sheet [Guessing].
