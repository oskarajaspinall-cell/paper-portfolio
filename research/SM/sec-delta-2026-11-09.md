## MD&A
- **Added**: Our purpose. Our purpose is to improve communities by providing affordable, reliable energy. We are a premier operator of top-tier assets, using state-of-the-art digital technology, data analytics, and artificial intelligence in our operations, and continually innovating to optimize capital efficiency and well performance, while reducing our impact on shared natural resources and operating efficiently, safely, and responsibly.
- **Added**: Strategic vision and value creation. Our asset portfolio consists of high-quality assets in the Midland Basin and Delaware Basin, both of which are part of the larger Permian Basin of West Texas and New Mexico; the DJ Basin of Northeast Colorado; the Maverick Basin of South Texas; and the Uinta Basin of Northeast Utah. We believe our assets are capable of generating strong returns in the current macroeconomic environment and provide resilience to commodity price risk and volatility. Through disciplined capital allocation, active portfolio management, and continued development and optimization, we seek to maximize returns and increase the long-term value of our asset portfolio.
- **Added**: Our long-term vision and strategy are focused on sustainably growing value for all of our stakeholders by maintaining and optimizing our high-quality asset portfolio, generating cash flows, and maintaining a strong balance sheet. Our team executes our strategy by prioritizing safety, technological innovation, and stewardship of natural resources, which are foundational to our corporate culture. Our near-term strategic focus is continuing to optimize operations across our multi-basin portfolio; maintaining safe operations; delivering consistent operational execution; maximizing free cash flow; and bolstering our balance sheet.
- **Added**: Responsible operations. We believe responsible operations and strong financial performance are mutually reinforcing. We are committed to operating safely and efficiently, managing environmental and operational risks, investing in our workforce, maintaining constructive relationships with the communities where we operate, and transparently reporting our performance. The Operations and Environmental, Health and Safety Committee of our Board of Directors assists the Board of Directors in overseeing operational performance, environmental stewardship, health and safety, sustainability reporting, and the management of operational and environmental risks. In addition, compensation for our executives and employees under certain aspects of our compensation plans is based in part on Company-wide performance metrics that include financial, operational, environmental, health and safety measures.
