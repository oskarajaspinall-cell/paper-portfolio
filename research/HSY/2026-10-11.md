# The Hershey Company (HSY) — Core initiation — 2026-10-11

## 1. Business
Hershey makes and markets branded confectionery (Reese's, Hershey's, Kit Kat under US licence, Cadbury) and salty snacks (Dot's Pretzels), sold mainly through North American retail/wholesale, with a smaller international segment. Revenue comes from volume plus price/mix on branded, shelf-leading products; cocoa and sugar are the main input costs [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/hsy/financials/income-statement/).

## 2. Quality
Hershey holds a near-duopoly position in US chocolate with Mars, and Reese's is the top-selling Halloween candy franchise [Likely] (source: https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/reeses-is-on-the-hunt-to-find-the-trick-or-treatiest-street-in-america-302896072.html). Historically this earned very high returns: ROIC ran 24.7–29.4% and ROCE 26.0–33.2% in FY2021–24 [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/hsy/financials/ratios/). FY2025 shows the limit of that moat: a cocoa-price spike crushed gross margin to 33.5% (from 47.4% in FY2024) and ROIC/ROCE to 11.3%/13.7% [Certain] (same source), as pricing passed through with a lag rather than protecting margin in real time. Q2 2026 confirms the squeeze is cyclical, not structural: gross margin recovered to 45.3% from 30.5% a year earlier as cocoa costs fell and prior pricing took hold [Certain] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/47111/000162828026050900/hsy-20260628.htm). Management calls 2026 a "profit restoration" year, expecting the 2%–4% long-term organic growth algorithm to resume in 2027, though North American confectionery volumes fell meaningfully in Q2 on pricing elasticity [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/hsy/transcripts/736623-barclays-19th-annual-global-consumer-conference/). Net debt/EBITDA rose to 2.44x in FY2025, easing to 1.89x TTM [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/hsy/financials/ratios/).

## 3. Valuation
P/E of 22.2x sits at the 26th percentile of its own 5y range (15.4x–41.8x) and 43% below the peer median (39.0x, n=2) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/hsy/statistics/). But EV/EBITDA (13.9x, +58% vs. peer median 8.8x), EV/Sales (+61%) and P/FCF (+53%) all show HSY at a large premium to Mondelez/General Mills/Kraft Heinz/Smucker, reflecting materially higher margins and returns on capital even after the FY2025 cocoa shock [Certain] (same source). Base fair value (DCF 67%/P/E 33% blend, cost of equity from FRED 10y Treasury + beta) is $188.32, 15.7% above the $162.74 last close; bear $109.82 (-32.5%), bull $210.96 (+29.6%) [Likely] (source: factsheet fair-value table). The price sits between bear and base, consistent with the market still discounting whether margin recovery sticks through 2027 rather than pricing in a clean restoration [Guessing].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Gross margin (income statement page) fails to hold above 40% for two consecutive quarters after Q2 2026's 45.3%, signalling cocoa/pricing pass-through is stalling.
- ROIC (ratios page) stays below 20% through FY2027, indicating the FY2025 collapse was structural, not a one-year commodity shock.
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) rises back above 2.44x (the FY2025 peak), showing buybacks/dividends are outrunning deleveraging.
- North American confectionery volumes (quarterly filings/earnings releases) keep declining for more than two further quarters, showing pricing elasticity is worse than management's "resilient consumer" view.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
TTM FCF margin is 18.3% with FCF/net income conversion of 149.6%, strong even through the margin shock [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/hsy/financials/ratios/). In H1 2026 Hershey paid $574.4m of dividends and repurchased $439.4m of stock (1.9m shares) while funding $204.4m of capex, against net debt of roughly $4.8bn [Certain] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/47111/000162828026050900/hsy-20260628.htm). Shares outstanding rose only 0.16% YoY, so buybacks are roughly offsetting dilution, not shrinking the float [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/hsy/statistics/).

## 6. Unusual items
New CFO Dave Hulays (an internal promotion) started September 2026, with predecessor Steve Voskuil staying through early 2027 for an orderly transition — a planned, not forced, change [Likely] (source: https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/the-hershey-company-names-dave-hulays-chief-financial-officer-302867924.html). Short interest is 6.33% of float and fell 11.2% versus the prior month, not a crowded short [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/hsy/statistics/). Reported 5y beta of 0.09 is unusually low for a staple and looks like a data artefact rather than a true low-volatility signal [Guessing] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/hsy/statistics/); not thesis-relevant.
