# Macro overlay: Regency Centers Corporation (REG) — 2026-10-12
## Assessment
REG's bull case rests on a multiple below its own 5y range (EV/EBITDA -12%, EV/Sales -11%; fact sheet), giving a $94.06 base fair value (+30.6% vs $72.05); that gap and the stock's 22.6pp 6-month underperformance vs SPY line up with the rate move [Likely] **(inference)**. Facts: 10y real yield (DFII10) rose 0.56pp/3m to 2.87% (2026-10-08, FRED); 10y Treasury (DGS10) rose 0.68pp/3m to 5.22% (2026-10-08, FRED) [Certain] — the same risk-free rate used in REG's CAPM fair value. Higher real yields raise cap rates and REIT discount rates market-wide, compressing multiples [Likely] **(inference)**. Chain: real yields → cap rate/discount rate → P/FFO & NAV multiple → equity value; secondarily, nominal yields → debt cost → development-spread economics. Market pricing: the 2y Treasury (4.75%) sits ~87bp above effective fed funds (3.88%, both 2026-10-08, FRED), a proxy implying the market expects rates to stay elevated rather than cut soon [Likely] **(inference, proxy)**. Breakevens are flat (T10YIE 2.33%, -4bp/1m), so this is a real-rate/term-premium move, not an inflation surprise. Bull: real yields stabilize/fall → multiple re-rates toward its 5y range, closing the gap faster; cheaper debt widens development spreads. Bear: real yields keep rising → discount rate rises further, multiple stays compressed, development-spread/FFO growth narrows. Tilt: moderate toward bear — official data still show yields rising, not falling, working against REG's own re-rating thesis despite strong fundamentals (record occupancy, raised guidance).
