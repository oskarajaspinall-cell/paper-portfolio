# Barrick Mining Corporation (TSX:ABX) — Core initiation — 2026-10-07

## 1. Business
Barrick Mining (formerly Barrick Gold) is a large-cap gold miner, classified Materials/Gold [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tsx/ABX/), operating large, long-life assets including the Nevada Gold Mines joint venture and the Loulo-Gounkoto complex in Mali [Likely] (source: https://www.reuters.com/business/world-at-work/multiple-unions-threaten-strikes-barricks-flagship-mali-gold-mine-documents-show-2026-09-18/). Revenue is the gold (and some copper) produced sold at spot/contract prices; profitability tracks the gold price less costs, so it is a price-taker [Likely].

## 2. Quality
Returns have stepped up sharply: TTM ROIC 23.7% (vs 9.4% FY2021), ROCE 21.8% (vs 9.6%), ROE 27.0% (vs 10.3%) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tsx/ABX/financials/ratios/). Margins expanded every year for five years — gross margin 56.3% TTM (vs 40.7% FY2021), operating margin 51.9% (vs 35.8%), net margin 31.6% (vs 16.9%) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tsx/ABX/financials/income-statement/). This is largely gold-price-driven operating leverage on a mostly fixed-cost base, not a structural moat: gold miners have no pricing power, so these returns are not protected from a gold-price reversal [Likely]. FCF conversion (FCF/NI) fell from ~96-100% in FY2021-22 to 79.5% TTM as capex rose [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tsx/ABX/financials/cash-flow-statement/). The balance sheet is net cash (net debt/EBITDA -0.10x) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tsx/ABX/financials/balance-sheet/). Piotroski F-Score 6/9 and Altman Z 3.68 (safe zone) indicate solid but not exceptional health [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tsx/ABX/statistics/). The durable edge is low-cost, long-life ore bodies and scale, not pricing power [Likely].

## 3. Valuation
TTM P/E of 10.4x is below ABX's own 5-year minimum (12.6x) and 24% below the peer median (13.6x: NEM/AEM/KGC/AU) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tsx/ABX/financials/ratios/). EV/EBITDA 5.4x is below its 5-year minimum (5.9x) and 19% below peers (6.7x) [Certain]. P/FCF 12.7x is below its own 5-year minimum (17.4x) but is the one multiple not cheap vs peers (+10% vs 11.6x) [Certain]. P/B 1.8x sits near the top of its own range (79th percentile) yet 47% below the peer median (3.4x), reflecting peers (notably AEM) on richer book multiples [Certain]. FCF yield is 7.84% [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tsx/ABX/statistics/). The market prices ABX cheaper than its own history and peers on earnings/EBITDA despite record returns, consistent with discounting Mali labour and IPO execution risk, or expecting margins to mean-revert [Likely]. At current multiples the thesis needs margins not to deteriorate, not to expand further [Likely].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Gross margin (income statement) falls back below 40% (toward FY2022-23 levels), signalling margin reversion.
- FCF conversion, FCF/NI (cash-flow statement), falls below 50%, signalling capex overrun or working-capital deterioration.
- Net debt/EBITDA (balance sheet/ratios) rises above 0.5x, signalling releveraging (e.g., to fund IPO costs or Mali disruption).
- ROIC (ratios page) falls back below 10%, erasing the step-change in capital returns.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Shares outstanding are down 3.0% year-on-year, indicating net buybacks [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tsx/ABX/statistics/). The company holds net cash (net debt/EBITDA -0.10x) [Certain]. FCF margin is a strong 25.1% TTM, but FCF conversion of net income fell to 79.5% from ~100% in FY2021-22, implying rising capex intensity, plausibly tied to the Nevada growth plan and North American IPO preparation reported in the press [Likely] (source: https://www.marketbeat.com/instant-alerts/event-barrick-mining-targets-year-end-ipo-as-nevada-growth-plan-takes-shape-2026-09-29/). The fact sheet shows no dividend data, so a reported dividend yield figure from a third-party article cannot be verified and is not used [data unavailable].

## 6. Unusual items
Short interest is low in absolute terms (0.72% of float) but rose 43.5% versus the prior month [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tsx/ABX/statistics/) — rising but still small, not yet material. Multiple unions issued strike notices at the flagship Loulo-Gounkoto mine in Mali as of September 18, 2026, a labour risk concentrated at a key asset [Likely] (source: https://www.reuters.com/business/world-at-work/multiple-unions-threaten-strikes-barricks-flagship-mali-gold-mine-documents-show-2026-09-18/). The planned North American IPO of Nevada/US gold assets has reportedly been pushed from year-end 2026 to 2027 amid investor opposition, adding execution-timeline uncertainty though not a current fundamental issue [Likely] (source: https://financialpost.com/commodities/mining/barrick-weigh-delaying-north-america-gold-ipo-2027). Agnico Eagle has publicly said it is not interested in participating in that IPO, a competitor signal on deal attractiveness rather than a Barrick-specific red flag [Likely] (source: https://www.reuters.com/business/agnico-eagle-says-it-is-not-interested-participating-barricks-north-american-ipo-2026-09-15/).
