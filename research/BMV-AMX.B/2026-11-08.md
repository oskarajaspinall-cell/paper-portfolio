# América Móvil (BMV:AMX.B) — Core initiation — 2026-11-08
## 1. Business
Latin America's largest mobile operator, also fixed broadband and pay TV; revenue is subscription and usage fees, reported in MXN (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/bmv/AMX.B/). ## 2. Quality
Solid, not exceptional. TTM ROIC 11.7% and ROCE 14.5% are flat over five years but rising over the last 12 months (+2.4pp and +1.2pp) (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/bmv/AMX.B/financials/ratios/?p=trailing) [Certain]. Gross margin has risen steadily to 62.7% and TTM FCF margin is a five-year high of 18.4% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/bmv/AMX.B/financials/cash-flow-statement/) [Certain]. FCF is about twice net income because net margin (9.4%) is depressed by below-the-line items, probably financing and FX costs [Likely]. The durable edge is scale and spectrum/network density in largely concentrated markets, but returns sit only modestly above a likely cost of equity [Likely]. Leverage is 1.90x net debt/EBITDA, down from 2.38x in FY2024, but Altman Z is 1.77 and beta 0.13 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/bmv/AMX.B/statistics/) [Certain]. Q3 reporting showed postpaid growth and higher FCF; management cited Mexico competitive pricing as a near-term concern (source: https://www.fool.com/earnings/takeaways/2026/10/28/america-movil-sab-de-cv-3-key-takeaways-for-long-term-investors/, https://www.wsj.com/business/earnings/america-movil-posts-higher-third-quarter-profit-317d8c15) [Likely].

## 3. Valuation
At 23.95 MXN the stock trades on 5.8x EV/EBITDA, 8% of its 5y range and 17% below peers, and an 8.0x P/FCF (12% of range) with a 12.54% FCF yield [Certain]. P/E of 15.8x is above peers (13.5x, n=2) and P/B of 3.1x is at the top of its 5y range, reflecting buybacks and a leveraged equity base [Likely]. The reverse DCF implies year-1 revenue shrinking 9.8%, far below recent growth, so the price assumes decline [Certain]. Base fair value is 40.00 MXN (+67.0%), but the two methods diverge: DCF 49.06 against EV/EBITDA 21.89, so the DCF-driven upside leans on sustained FCF margin [Likely]. Bear case 18.72 (-21.9%). Cash-flow multiples look cheap, equity multiples do not; the discount is real only if FCF margin holds [Likely]. The stock is 16.5% above its 50-day average with RSI 75.0, so entry timing is stretched; this is not a thesis point [Certain].

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
FCF conversion is 196.9% of net income on a TTM basis, and share count fell 0.13% year over year, so buybacks are small (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/bmv/AMX.B/statistics/) [Certain]. Management said cash flow funded investment, debt reduction and shareholder returns (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/bmv/AMX.B/) [Likely]. The May 2026 outlook reportedly held capex flat (source: https://www.reuters.com/business/media-telecom/mexican-telecoms-giant-america-movil-targets-steady-growth-holds-capex-7-billion-2026-05-27/) [Likely]. Deleveraging from 2.38x to 1.90x is the clearest positive [Certain].

## 6. Unusual items

Mexican regulatory/competition risk and MXN and other LatAm currency exposure are material; the fact sheet's "next earnings Oct 20, 2026" is stale as Q3 was reported 21 Oct [Certain].
