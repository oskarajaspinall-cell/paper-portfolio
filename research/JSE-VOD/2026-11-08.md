## 2. Quality
A good, cash-generative business [Certain]. TTM ROIC is 16.9% and ROCE 20.2%, both up over the last year (+2.1pp and +1.2pp); operating margin is 23.8% (+2.0pp) and gross margin 55.1% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/jse/VOD/financials/ratios/?p=trailing). FCF margin is 19.5% and FCF/net income is 158.7%, so earnings are backed by cash, though that gap partly reflects heavy depreciation [Likely]. Net margin has fallen 4.4pp over five years to 12.3%, so below-the-line costs (interest, tax, minorities) absorb a lot [Likely]. Piotroski F is 7 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/jse/VOD/statistics/). The moat is spectrum, network scale and distribution in concentrated markets [Likely]; the fact sheet cannot show market share or regulatory risk. Net debt/EBITDA is 1.16x and has crept up 0.17x over five years, consistent with Safaricom funding [Likely].
