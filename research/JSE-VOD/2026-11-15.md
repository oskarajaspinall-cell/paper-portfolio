# Vodacom Group (JSE:VOD) — Core initiation — 2026-11-15
## 1. Business
Sub-Saharan mobile operator: voice, data, fintech (mobile money) and fibre, billed mostly prepaid and contract in South Africa, with subsidiaries in Egypt, Tanzania and other African markets. Safaricom (Kenya) is being consolidated, adding fintech scale [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/jse/VOD/transcripts/686215-h1-2027/). Revenue share by country: [data unavailable]; the company's site is not allowlisted, so no `[company-reported]` split. Egypt, Tanzania and Kenya are plausibly each ~10%+ [Guessing]; the macro overlay should check them.

## 2. Quality
Good, not exceptional. Gross margin is stable at 56.1% TTM (5y flat) and operating margin is 26.0% TTM, up from 22.6% a year earlier [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/jse/VOD/financials/income-statement/?p=trailing). FCF margin is 20.8% TTM, with FCF conversion of 158.7% in FY2026 [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/jse/VOD/financials/cash-flow-statement/). Telecom licences, spectrum and network scale are a durable local moat [Likely].

Conflict to address: ROIC is 14.7% TTM vs 18.4% a year earlier, and ROCE 15.3% vs 20.4%, although margins rose (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/jse/VOD/financials/ratios/?p=trailing). The likely cause is a larger capital base from the Safaricom deal and TTM net income inflated by a one-off (net margin 24.3% vs 12.3% in FY2026) [Guessing]. Returns are above 14% but falling. Net debt/EBITDA is 1.74x vs 1.16x [Certain].

## 3. Valuation
P/E is 7.8x, below its 5y minimum of 11.7x, but TTM earnings look inflated (net margin 24.3%), so treat it as unreliable [Likely]. EV/EBITDA is 6.6x (5y median 6.4x, peers 5.2x), P/FCF is 8.8x (median 8.6x), and P/B is 3.2x, the top of its 5y range [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/jse/VOD/financials/ratios/). FCF yield is 11.30% [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/jse/VOD/statistics/). The price assumes the current cash flows hold, with no re-rating cushion against the 5y median. Fair value: [data unavailable] (no ZAR FX rate). The price is 17.7% above its 200-day average, 12-month change +30.0%, RSI 79.1: a working stock, but extended in the short run [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/jse/VOD/history/).

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROIC TTM (ratios page, trailing) falls below 12%.
- Operating margin TTM (income statement, trailing) falls below 22%.
- FCF margin TTM (cash flow statement, trailing) falls below 16%.
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) rises above 2.5x.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Cash conversion is strong: FCF/NI was above 100% in each of the last five fiscal years, and FCF margin has held 17-20%. Management raised dividends at the FY2026 results [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/jse/VOD/transcripts/666160-h2-2026-q-a/). Leverage is rising with the Safaricom consolidation. Shares outstanding grew 0.71% YoY [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/jse/VOD/statistics/). The deal is the main capital allocation question.

## 6. Unusual items
- Safaricom transaction remains subject to a Kenyan legal proceeding, per the interim call [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/jse/VOD/transcripts/766201-h1-2027-q-a/). An adverse ruling is a binary risk.
- Emerging-market currency exposure (rand, Egyptian pound, Kenyan shilling) against ZAR reporting. Altman Z is 1.69 [Certain].
