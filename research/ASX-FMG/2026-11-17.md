# Fortescue Ltd (ASX:FMG) — Core initiation — 2026-11-17
## 1. Business
Fortescue is a Pilbara iron ore miner, the third-largest in Australia, selling mainly to Chinese steelmakers, and is also building renewable-power, electrified-mining and copper/lithium-adjacent ventures [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/asx/FMG/). Revenue mix by country is [data unavailable]; China is the dominant buyer [Likely], so the macro overlay should check China risk. It reports in USD and is priced in AUD (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/asx/FMG/).

## 2. Quality
A low-cost, scaled miner with a clean balance sheet, but returns have halved from their peak. ROIC fell from 38.3% (FY2022) to 17.0% TTM and operating margin from 52.3% to 32.1% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/asx/FMG/financials/ratios/). Over the last year ROIC and FCF margin were flat, operating margin fell 1.1pp; all four metrics sit well below their 2-year highs (ROIC 12.0pp, operating margin 14.6pp) (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/asx/FMG/financials/ratios/?p=trailing). FCF conversion is strong at 127.4% and net debt/EBITDA is 0.10x (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/asx/FMG/financials/cash-flow-statement/). The moat is ore-body cost position, not pricing power: margins track iron ore prices [Likely]. Quality is good, not exceptional; decline is cyclical rather than a broken franchise [Likely]. Altman Z 4.36, Piotroski F 5 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/asx/FMG/statistics/).

## 3. Valuation
The price assumes depressed iron ore economics persist. EV/EBITDA is 3.6x, below its 5y minimum of 3.7x and 31% below peers (BHP, MIN, VALE) at 5.2x; P/FCF 7.9x vs 5y minimum 8.6x; FCF yield 12.61%. P/E of 10.1x is mid-range (50%) and 48% below peers' 19.5x (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/asx/FMG/statistics/). Mechanical fair value (EV/EBITDA only; NAV [data unavailable]) is bear 14.00, base 16.70, bull 18.88 AUD against a last completed close of 14.42 on 2026-11-16 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/asx/FMG/). Price is 23.0% below the 200-day average, 12-month change -32.7%, relative strength -42.8pp vs SPY: the market prices a commodity or China-access problem [Likely]. Cheap, but falling. Conflict: the caller states a 13.62 close on 2026-11-17; the fact sheet's last completed close is 14.42 (2026-11-16) and 13.62 appears only as its 52-week low.

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROIC (ratios page, TTM) falls below 12%.
- FCF margin (cash-flow page, TTM) falls below 15%.
- Net debt/EBITDA (statistics page) rises above 1.0x.
- Company cuts iron ore shipment guidance, or announces a failed or loss-making China Mineral Resources Group arrangement (company releases) [Likely].

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
FCF margin 21.5% TTM, FCF/net income 127.4%, share count change +0.06% YoY (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/asx/FMG/statistics/). The AGM transcript describes a $1.08 dividend and a 65% payout, with spend aimed at renewable power, electrified mining and diversified growth [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/asx/FMG/transcripts/654293-agm-2026/). Green-energy capex is the key allocation risk: it competes with dividends for a mining cash flow that has fallen [Guessing]. A power deal with a lithium producer shows some external revenue (source: https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/australias-pls-signs-20-year-power-deal-with-fortescue-unit-pilgangoora-lithium-2026-10-27/) [Likely].
