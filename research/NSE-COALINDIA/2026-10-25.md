# Coal India Limited (NSE:COALINDIA) — Core initiation — 2026-10-25
## 1. Business
State-controlled miner of thermal coal, the largest coal producer worldwide. It sells mainly to Indian power utilities under fuel supply agreements, plus auctions. It is diversifying into iron ore, critical minerals trading and lithium assets, and is planning IPOs of subsidiaries (headlines: https://www.reuters.com/world/india/coal-india-enters-iron-ore-mining-after-odisha-block-win-2026-08-06/, https://www.reuters.com/world/india/coal-india-unit-mahanadi-coalfields-files-ipo-2026-09-01/) [Likely]. (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/nse/COALINDIA/)

## 2. Quality
Highly profitable and net cash, but returns are normalising down from a peak. ROIC fell from 158.9% (FY2023) to 30.0% (FY2026), 28.9% TTM; ROE 27.8% FY2026; gross margin 74.5%; operating margin 18.6% FY2026, 17.8% TTM (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/nse/COALINDIA/financials/ratios/, https://stockanalysis.com/quote/nse/COALINDIA/financials/income-statement/) [Certain]. Net debt/EBITDA is -1.04x TTM, so balance-sheet risk is low. FCF conversion is volatile (3.4% in FY2024, 98.8% in FY2026) [Certain]. The moat is regulatory and geological: dominant domestic supply to utilities, with administered pricing [Likely]. That same state control caps pricing and can direct capital to policy goals [Likely]. Quality is high but not improving: ROIC, ROCE and margins all trend down [Certain]. Demand from power plants is reported strong (57 plants critically low on stock, https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/india/cil-offers-extra-fuel-57-plants-now-report-critically-low-stock/articleshow/133814003.cms) [Likely].

## 3. Valuation
P/E is 7.9x against a 5y median of 6.9x (68% of range) and peers at 7.2x. EV/EBITDA is 4.9x against a 4.6x median and peers at 5.0x. P/FCF is 8.0x against a 9.7x median, and FCF yield is 12.54% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/nse/COALINDIA/statistics/) [Certain]. P/B is 2.0x, below its 5y minimum. Multiples sit near mid-range, with no clear discount to history or peers. The mechanical base fair value is 549.71 INR, +38.3% against the 397.45 INR close, but it rests on a through-cycle DCF that ranges from 30.58 to 1,337.85 INR across bear and bull cases, so it is wide and low-confidence [Likely]. The reverse DCF implies -7.3% year-1 revenue growth, so the price already discounts decline [Likely]. The stock is below its 50- and 200-day averages, with 6-month relative strength of -19.1pp versus SPY; this is context only [Certain].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROIC (ratios page) TTM falls below 20%.
- Operating margin (income statement page) TTM falls below 14%.
- Net debt / EBITDA (ratios page) turns positive, above 0.5x, from debt-funded diversification or acquisitions.
- FCF margin (cash-flow and income statement pages) for the fiscal year below 8%.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Net cash and no dilution (shares change YoY -0.02%) [Certain]. FCF conversion averaged well below earnings in FY2023-FY2025, then recovered to 98.8% in FY2026, so capex and working capital swing widely [Certain]. Management is pushing into iron ore, critical minerals and a possible lithium acquisition (https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/coal-india-weighs-buying-canadas-wealth-minerals-unit-chile-lithium-assets-2026-08-11/): returns on this unproven diversification are a risk to the high ROIC [Likely]. The dividend record is not in the fact sheet [data unavailable].

## 6. Unusual items
State control: pricing, capex and diversification can follow policy rather than shareholder returns [Likely]. Subsidiary IPOs (SECL, MCL) may be planned, which could change the structure of the group (https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/city/kolkata/coal-india-plans-secl-mcl-ipos-by-year-end/articleshow/133657803.cms) [Likely]. Earnings are due Oct 29, 2026, days away (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/nse/COALINDIA/statistics/). Long-term decarbonisation of Indian power is a structural risk to volumes [Guessing].
