# Macro overlay: Airbnb (ABNB) — 2026-11-15
## Assessment
**Weight: secondary.** Chain: real yields -> discount rate -> multiple. The stock trades at 35.1x P/E, above its 5y maximum, with a reverse DCF implying 31.8% year-1 growth (fact sheet 2026-11-15) [Certain]. The 10y TIPS yield is 2.86%, up 0.47pp in ~3m (FRED DFII10, 2026-11-12) [Certain]. A second chain is dollar -> FX translation -> reported revenue. EMEA is 47.2% of Q3 revenue [company-reported, SEC 10-Q]. The euro fell about 3.5% against the dollar over ~3m (DEXUSEU 1.1154, 2026-11-06, -0.0405), and the broad dollar rose about 2.4% (DTWEXBGS 121.88, +2.82) [Certain].

**Priced:** the stock is up 53.5% over 12m and jumped 11.7% on earnings, despite the higher real yields. So the market is not pricing a de-rating, and the 2y yield of 4.86% sits 0.98pp above fed funds of 3.88% (DGS2, DFF), a proxy for no easing and possibly hikes **(inference)** [Likely]. Breakevens are flat at 2.24%, so the move is real rate, not inflation. Credit is calm (HY 3.2%, VIX 18.7).

**Macro bull:** real yields stabilise or fall and the dollar reverses. The multiple holds and EMEA translation turns neutral or positive.
**Macro bear:** real yields rise past about 3.25% and the dollar strengthens further. The multiple compresses toward the mechanical base value, and translation trims reported growth by a few points on Europe.

**Tilt:** toward bear, small. The valuation sits on the rate-sensitive end, but the demand data is not macro-driven.
