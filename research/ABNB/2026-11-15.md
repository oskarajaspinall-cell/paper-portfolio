# Airbnb, Inc. (ABNB) — Core initiation — 2026-11-15
## 1. Business
Airbnb runs a two-sided marketplace for short-term stays, experiences and services, taking a service fee at booking and recognising revenue at check-in [Certain] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1559720/000155972026000032/abnb-20260930.htm). By listing location, EMEA was 47.2% of Q3 2026 revenue and North America 39.9% [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1559720/000155972026000032/abnb-20260930.htm). Europe is therefore the largest region; no single country is disclosed.

## 2. Quality
Asset-light, high-return marketplace: gross margin 83.1% TTM, operating margin 22.5%, ROCE 25.7%, ROE 35.8%, net cash (net debt/EBITDA -3.20x), Piotroski 7 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/abnb/financials/ratios/) [Certain]. The moat is two-sided network scale: supply of unique homes that hotel chains cannot replicate, and high direct and repeat traffic [Likely].

Direction is mixed. ROCE is down from 30.0% to 25.7% over 12 months and FCF margin from 38.4% to 36.0% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/abnb/financials/ratios/?p=trailing) [Certain]. Management attributes the FCF dip partly to growth in "reserve now, pay later" (RNPL), which moves cash receipt closer to check-in, and to a New York property purchase [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1559720/000155972026000032/abnb-20260930.htm). That makes it timing, not economics [Likely].

## 3. Valuation
The price is 186.78 USD after an 11.7% earnings-day jump on 2.9x volume. P/E of 35.1x sits above its 5y maximum of 32.8x and 18% above the peer median (BKNG, EXPE, MAR, HLT). EV/EBITDA (31.7x) is low in its own range but 66% above peers. P/FCF 21.9x is in line with peers (-1%) and FCF yield is 4.57% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/abnb/statistics/) [Certain].

The mechanical fair value is 94.44 USD base (DCF-led), 49.4% below the price; only the bull case (214.62) is above it. The reverse DCF implies 31.8% year-1 revenue growth fading to terminal, which is demanding [Certain]. The market is paying for sustained double-digit growth, international expansion and margin stability; the stock is a business that is working (+53.5% over 12 months, 25.3% above its 200-day average) priced above its own-history fair value [Likely].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROCE (ratios page, TTM) falls below 20%.
- Operating margin (income statement page, TTM) falls below 18%.
- FCF margin (cash flow page, TTM) falls below 30% without an RNPL/timing explanation in the 10-Q.
- Revenue growth (income statement page, quarterly YoY) falls below 8%, e.g. from AI agents or direct booking bypassing the platform.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
FCF conversion is 156.3% of net income TTM and the share count fell 3.98% YoY (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/abnb/statistics/) [Certain]. The company repurchased $2,840 million of stock in the nine months to September 2026 [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1559720/000155972026000032/abnb-20260930.htm). Net cash and no dilution are positives; buying at 35x earnings returns less per dollar than in prior years [Likely].

## 6. Unusual items
- 10-Q delta: no material change in risk factors versus the 2025 10-K; MD&A notes the Q3 revenue growth of 19% outpaced costs, growth led by Latin America and Asia Pacific, and a slight rise in chargeback rate [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1559720/000155972026000032/abnb-20260930.htm) [Certain].
- AI disintermediation: the CEO says AI agents are bringing few bookings, and the company is building its own agent (source: https://www.businessinsider.com/airbnb-ceo-ai-agents-muse-instinct-done-few-bookings-traffic-2026-11) [Likely]. Still a medium-term risk [Guessing].
- Revenue raised guidance drove the November 6 jump (source: https://www.reuters.com/business/airbnb-rises-upbeat-outlook-bucking-broader-industry-caution-2026-11-06/) [Likely].
- Insiders own 26.32% and short interest is 3.15% of float; beta 1.27 [Certain].
