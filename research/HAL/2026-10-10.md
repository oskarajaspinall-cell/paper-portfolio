# Halliburton Company (HAL) — Core initiation — 2026-10-10

## 1. Business
Halliburton provides oilfield services across two segments: Completion & Production (hydraulic fracturing, cementing, completions, production chemicals) and Drilling & Evaluation (drilling, wireline, reservoir/logging technology) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/hal/). Revenue spans North America, Latin America, Europe/Africa/CIS and Middle East/Asia; North America was the largest segment at $4.41bn of H1 2026 revenue [Certain] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/45012/000004501226000061/hal-20260630.htm).

## 2. Quality
Returns sit above typical cost of capital: ROIC 13.5% TTM (flat over 5y) and ROCE 14.9% TTM, up 5.7pp over 5y [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/hal/financials/ratios/). Operating margin has expanded +2.1pp to 13.3% TTM while net margin fell 3.7pp to 7.2%, pointing to below-the-line items rather than lost pricing power [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/hal/financials/income-statement/). FCF conversion rose to 107.7% TTM (from 76% in FY2021) and leverage nearly halved (net debt/EBITDA 1.49x vs 2.75x FY2021) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/hal/financials/ratios/). The moat is thin and cyclical: oilfield services is commoditized and competes directly with SLB and BKR; HAL's edge is NA pressure-pumping scale and completions technology, which earns a premium in up-cycles but no pricing power once activity falls. International mix is diverging — Latin America +18.1% and Europe/Africa/CIS +17.6% YoY in H1 2026, but Middle East/Asia fell 11.7% [Certain] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/45012/000004501226000061/hal-20260630.htm). A cash-generative business, but returns track the services cycle, not a defensible position.

## 3. Valuation
HAL trades at a discount to peers on most multiples: P/E 17.1x vs peer median 21.0x (-19%), EV/EBITDA 8.0x vs 9.5x (-16%), EV/Sales 1.5x vs 1.6x (-9%), P/B 2.5x vs 2.8x (-12%); only P/FCF is at a premium, 15.7x vs 14.3x (+10%) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/hal/financials/ratios/). Against its own 5y history, P/E (57th pct) and P/FCF (31st) sit mid-range, while EV/EBITDA (41st) and P/B (9th, near the 5y low) look cheap [Certain] (source: fact sheet). Base fair value $35.21 implies +8.2% to last close ($32.55); bear $24.23 (-25.5%), bull $59.38 (+82.4%) [Certain] (source: fact sheet). The reverse DCF embedded in the price implies just 1.7% revenue growth in year 1, fading to terminal growth — the market prices a mature, low-growth services business [Certain] (source: fact sheet).

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) rises above 2.0x, reversing the 5y deleveraging trend.
- FCF conversion (cash flow + income statement) falls back below 80%, its FY2021-23 average.
- ROCE (ratios page) falls below 10%, back toward the FY2021 trough.
- Middle East/Asia revenue (SEC filings, segment note) declines further beyond H1 2026's -11.7% YoY.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
H1 2026 shareholder returns were $593m (buybacks $308m + dividends $285m), down from $799m in H1 2025, a more conservative stance amid softer activity [Certain] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/45012/000004501226000061/hal-20260630.htm). The $0.17/share quarterly dividend held steady. FCF margin is 7.7% TTM and FCF conversion 107.7%, funding distributions and deleveraging without raising net debt [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/hal/financials/ratios/).

## 6. Unusual items
Two items, neither yet thesis-changing: (1) non-binding Venezuela MOUs with Eneva and WESCA (announced 2026-09-21) — early-stage, no sanctions exposure disclosed in the Q2 2026 10-Q [Likely] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/45012/000004501226000061/hal-20260630.htm; headline: https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/halliburton-signs-venezuela-energy-pacts-with-eneva-wesca-2026-09-21/). (2) An unresolved IRS dispute over the tax classification of the $3.5bn Baker Hughes termination-fee deduction from 2016; HAL states no resolution is expected within 12 months, a contingent liability material relative to the $27.1bn market cap if decided adversely [Likely] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/45012/000004501226000061/hal-20260630.htm). Short interest is modest at 4.2% of float though it rose 12.6% month-on-month [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/hal/statistics/).
