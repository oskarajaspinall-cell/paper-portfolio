# Incyte Corporation (INCY) — Core initiation — 2026-10-07
## 1. Business
Incyte discovers, develops and commercializes small-molecule and biologic therapies in hematology/oncology, dermatology and inflammation/autoimmunity [Certain] (source: https://www.incyte.com). Revenue is driven mainly by US sales of Jakafi (ruxolitinib, myelofibrosis/polycythemia vera/GVHD), royalties on ex-US Jakavi via Novartis, and growing Opzelura (atopic dermatitis/vitiligo) sales, plus newer approvals Niktimvo (axatilimab), Monjuvi, Zynyz and Pemazyre [Certain] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/879169/000087916926000010/incy-20251231.htm).

## 2. Quality
Returns are strong and rising: ROIC 86.5% TTM (5y range 0.6–69.6%, FY2024 a one-off dip), ROCE 28.1% TTM, operating margin 31.7% TTM vs 2.0% in FY2024 [Certain] (source: factsheet RA/IS). FCF conversion is 119% TTM, net debt/EBITDA -2.3x (net cash) [Certain] (source: factsheet CF/BS). The moat rests on patent-protected franchises (Jakafi, Opzelura) plus orphan/first-in-class positioning (Niktimvo) rather than scale or brand — durable only while patents hold [Likely]. Key vulnerability: Jakafi/Jakavi US patents expire around late-2028; the 10-K flags single-franchise dependence as a principal risk [Certain] (source: 10-K incy-20251231.htm). Diversification is underway — approvals/expansions since September 2026 (Atebrioz with Mirum, Niktimvo Brazil, expanded Opzelura partnership) and guidance to 15–20% CAGR from five late-stage programs post-2029 [Likely] (source: factsheet news section) — but pipeline revenue is not yet large enough to fully offset a Jakafi step-down on schedule [Likely].

## 3. Valuation
At 112.73 USD, INCY trades on 9.6x EV/EBITDA, 14.4x P/E, 3.2x EV/Sales and 12.1x P/FCF TTM — below its own 5-year minimum on EV/EBITDA, P/E and P/FCF, and 16–41% below the peer median (EXEL, BMRN, ALNY, UTHR, JAZZ) on every multiple [Certain] (source: factsheet RA/ST + peer statistics pages). FCF yield is 8.4% [Certain] (source: factsheet ST). The discount implies the market prices the 2028 Jakafi patent cliff close to a worst case, giving little credit for the post-2029 pipeline [Likely]. P/B of 3.6x sits almost exactly at the peer median, so the cheapness is an earnings/cash-flow-multiple story, not a balance-sheet one [Likely]. If diversification revenue executes in line with guidance, today's multiples look too low; if Jakafi erodes earlier than 2028 or pipeline reads out poorly, the discount could still widen [Guessing].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Jakafi/Jakavi combined revenue (income statement / 10-K disclosure) declines more than a high-single-digit % y/y outside a guided step-down, signalling earlier-than-2028 share loss
- Opzelura, Niktimvo and other pipeline products fail to grow as a share of total product revenue (income statement) over the next four quarters
- Gross margin (income statement) falls back toward the FY2024 trough (31.2%) outside of a one-off charge
- ROIC/ROCE (ratios page) reverse their 5-year rising trend for two consecutive fiscal years

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
FCF margin is 33.0% TTM (5y range 5.9–26.3%, rising) with FCF/NI conversion of 119% TTM [Certain] (source: factsheet CF/IS). Net debt is negative at -2.3x EBITDA TTM, giving room for continued bolt-on M&A (e.g., the Vega Therapeutics acquisition into hematology) without leverage risk [Likely] (source: https://www.incyte.com). Shares outstanding rose 3.08% y/y, consistent with funding deals rather than net buybacks [Certain] (source: factsheet ST). No dividend; capital is being redeployed into pipeline/franchise diversification rather than returned to shareholders, appropriate given the Jakafi cliff [Likely].

## 6. Unusual items
Short interest is 5.86% of float and rose 6.6% versus the prior month — a moderate increase, not extreme [Certain] (source: factsheet ST). FY2024 was anomalous across all return/margin metrics (ROIC 0.6%, operating margin 2.0%); FY2025/TTM confirm normalization, with no evidence of recurrence [Likely]. RSI of 35.7 shows a modest oversold pullback from the 6-month high; not a factor for a core thesis. Otherwise none.
