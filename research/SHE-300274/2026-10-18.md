# Sungrow Power Supply Co., Ltd. (SHE:300274) — Core initiation — 2026-10-18
## 1. Business
Sungrow designs and makes solar PV inverters and battery energy-storage systems (BESS) for utility, commercial and residential customers, plus EV drives and hydrogen electrolyzer equipment [Certain]. Revenue is product sales (hardware plus EMS software), with storage now the faster-growing segment as grid-scale battery demand rises [Likely]. It is widely described as the world's largest producer of solar inverters and energy-storage systems (source: https://www.scmp.com/business/banking-finance/article/3328118/chinese-solar-inverter-and-energy-storage-giant-sungrow-seeks-hong-kong-ipo) [Likely].

## 2. Quality
Returns are exceptional and rising: ROIC 43.1% TTM (vs 21.1% FY21), ROCE 19.8% (vs 8.6%), ROE 22.0%, gross margin 30.9% and operating margin 16.7% — both 5-year highs [Certain]. FCF conversion is 132% of net income TTM, and the balance sheet is net cash (net debt/EBITDA -2.01x) [Certain]. Global #1 shipment scale and vertical integration into storage/EMS software support the margin trend [Likely]. But durability is in question: Q1 2026 net income fell 40% y/y on intensifying storage-market competition (source: https://financialpost.com/pmn/business-pmn/sungrow-first-quarter-profit-slides-as-competition-intensifies) [Certain], and the US has moved to bar certain foreign-made grid equipment and related software — a direct threat to a major export market (source: https://www.scmp.com/economy/global-economy/article/3365554/chinese-power-stocks-slump-after-us-grid-ban-sell-warranted) [Likely]. Net: a genuinely high-return business, but rivals (Huawei, Deye, GoodWe, SMA, SolarEdge) and geopolitics are now pressuring the edge that produced those returns [Likely].

## 3. Valuation
At 76.86 CNY, the stock trades on 14.4x TTM P/E, 9.5x EV/EBITDA, 10.9x P/FCF and 3.0x P/B — near or below the bottom of its own 5-year range (1%, -1%, -38% and -9% percentile) and 47-50% below the SEDG/ENPH/SHA:688390 peer median on P/E, EV/EBITDA and P/FCF [Certain]. FCF yield is 9.15%. Only EV/Sales (1.7x) sits above peers (+30%), consistent with margins still above the peer group [Certain]. The reverse-DCF implies the price assumes ~0% revenue growth in year 1 fading to terminal growth — near-stagnation — despite an 18.9% TTM FCF margin and >100% FCF conversion [Likely]. Base fair value of 144.51 CNY implies +88% upside; the bear case is negative, reflecting a tail scenario of a sharp margin reversal rather than a base expectation [Guessing].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Gross margin (ratios/income statement page) falls below 25% for two consecutive quarters, undoing the 5-year margin recovery.
- ROIC (ratios page) falls below 20%, back toward FY21-22 levels, signalling returns are being competed away.
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios/balance sheet page) turns positive (net cash position lost) without an offsetting capex or working-capital explanation.
- Revenue (income statement page) turns negative y/y for two consecutive quarters, confirming share loss rather than a cyclical air pocket.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
The balance sheet is net cash (-2.01x net debt/EBITDA TTM) and FCF conversion is >100% of net income, giving ample internal funding for capex and working capital in a growing storage business [Certain]. Shares outstanding fell 1.07% y/y, consistent with no material dilution to date [Certain]. Sungrow has refiled for a Hong Kong listing to raise offshore capital (source: https://www.scmp.com/business/china-business/article/3351515/chinas-solar-giant-sungrow-revives-hong-kong-ipo-plan-amid-rising-demand-clean-energy) [Likely] — a secondary listing that could dilute existing A-shares or simply broaden the investor base; terms are not yet known and should be revisited once filed.

## 6. Unusual items
US restrictions on foreign-made grid equipment and software are a direct, company-relevant regulatory risk given exposure to the US market (source: https://www.scmp.com/economy/global-economy/article/3365554/chinese-power-stocks-slump-after-us-grid-ban-sell-warranted) [Likely]. Beta is low (0.71) and Altman Z of 4.18 / Piotroski F of 6 indicate low distress risk [Certain]. Short interest and its monthly change are [data unavailable] on stockanalysis.com.
