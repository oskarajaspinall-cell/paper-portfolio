# Macro overlay: Sungrow Power Supply Co., Ltd. (SHE:300274) — 2026-10-18
## Assessment
Chain: global real yields → solar/storage project financing cost (developer IRRs) → capex order growth for inverters/BESS → Sungrow revenue growth → the multiple the market already pays (reverse-DCF implies ~0% year-1 growth) [Likely] **(inference)**. US 10y real yield (DFII10) is 2.78% on 2026-10-15, up +0.16pp in ~1m and +0.43pp in ~3m [Certain, https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=DFII10 (2026-10-15)]; effective fed funds is 3.88%, up +0.25pp both windows [Certain, https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=DFF (2026-10-15)]; the 2y yield (4.77%) sitting ~89bp above fed funds is a market proxy for more tightening priced in, not cuts [Likely] **(inference)**. Breakeven inflation (T10YIE) is flat at 2.35% (+0.02pp 1m), so the yield rise is mostly real, raising the hurdle rate for new renewable projects rather than reflecting inflation fears [Likely] **(inference)**. HY spreads ticked up to 3.23% (+0.47pp 1m) but remain historically tight, so credit access is not yet binding [Likely]. This is secondary: it reinforces, but doesn't drive, a thesis already dominated by competition and the US grid-equipment ban (note).
- **Bull**: real yields roll over / Fed pauses → project financing eases → order growth surprises above the ~0% priced in → re-rating toward base fair value (144.51 CNY).
- **Bear**: real yields and fed funds stay elevated or rise further → global storage/solar capex slows further → growth stays near zero, validating the depressed multiple.
- **Tilt**: toward bear, small — the 3-month rate move is real but modest next to the competition/regulatory risks already driving the stock; macro nudges the odds rather than sets them.
