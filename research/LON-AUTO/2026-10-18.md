# Autotrader Group plc (AUTO) — Core initiation — 2026-10-18
## 1. Business
Auto Trader runs the UK's largest online marketplace for new and used vehicles. Revenue comes mainly from monthly subscription packages sold to car retailers (listings, data and AI tools), plus smaller consumer-facing finance/insurance referrals and manufacturer display advertising. The model is asset-light: no inventory risk, high incremental margins. [Certain]

## 2. Quality
Returns are exceptional and durable so far: TTM ROIC 49.0%, ROCE 62.3%, net margin 47.1%, FCF/NI conversion 100.5% (source: ratios/income-statement/cash-flow pages). The moat is a two-sided network effect: management describes a large, durable lead in consumer time-spent and unique audience share versus its nearest competitor, citing independent comscore data [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/lon/AUTO/transcripts/411876-h2-2026/). That audience scale is what lets retailers keep paying up even as their own margins compress.

The flip side: every margin line has fallen over 5 years (gross -4.7pp, operating -7.2pp, net -9.5pp, FCF margin -14.9pp) and management itself acknowledged retailer-pricing growth decelerating toward a lower historical range [Likely] (same source). A dealer backlash over the "Deal Builder" product forced a slower rollout, though management did not abandon it [Likely] (same source) — pricing power has a ceiling set by retailer tolerance, not just audience share.

## 3. Valuation
At 16.0x P/E the stock sits near the bottom (25th percentile) of its own 5-year range (13.1x-24.6x) and below the peer median of 18.4x — on earnings alone it looks the cheapest it has been and cheap vs peers. But EV/EBITDA (10.8x, +17% vs peer median), P/FCF (14.5x, +53%) and especially EV/Sales (7.2x, +172%) are all well above the peer set (LON:RMV, CARG, CARS, LON:MONY), reflecting Auto Trader's structurally higher margins rather than a stretched multiple. The 12-month share price is down 29.6% even though the stock trades 8.7%/9.5% above its 50-/200-day averages — the de-rating happened earlier in the period and the multiple has only partly recovered. No DCF/EV-Revenue fair value is computable here [data unavailable — no FX rate for GBP], so this is a relative, not absolute, valuation read. [Certain]

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROIC (ratios page) falls below 40%, signalling the moat is eroding faster than the margin decline already priced in.
- Net margin (income statement) falls below 40% on a trailing-twelve-month basis, showing margin compression is structural, not cyclical.
- FCF/NI conversion (cash-flow + income statement) falls below 85%, indicating cash generation is no longer tracking reported profit.
- Net debt/EBITDA (balance sheet + income statement) rises above 1.0x, showing buyback-funded leverage is outpacing EBITDA growth.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
FCF/NI conversion has stayed at or above 100% every year shown (FY2022-FY2026, falling from 110.0% to 100.5%), so reported profit is real cash. [Certain] Net debt/EBITDA moved from net cash (-0.14x, FY2022) to 0.48x (TTM), consistent with sustained buybacks: shares outstanding fell 3.65% year-on-year (source: statistics page). [Certain] ROE rose to 60.2% TTM (+7.6pp over 5 years) partly reflecting a shrinking equity base from buybacks rather than purely higher returns. [Likely] Management also indicated continued dividends and buybacks funded from free cash flow [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/lon/AUTO/transcripts/411876-h2-2026/), though the exact split is not a stockanalysis.com figure and is omitted.

## 6. Unusual items
A dealer backlash over the "Deal Builder" product led management to slow its rollout rather than cancel it — a qualitative sign that retailer pushback can constrain future monetisation, not currently visible in the margin data. [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/lon/AUTO/transcripts/411876-h2-2026/) Institutional ownership is high (93.1%) with minimal insider ownership (0.48%) (source: statistics page) — not itself a red flag but leaves little insider skin in the game.
