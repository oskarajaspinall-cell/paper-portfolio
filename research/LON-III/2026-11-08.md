# 3i Group plc (LON:III) — Core initiation — 2026-11-08
## 1. Business
3i is a UK-listed private equity and infrastructure investor. It earns returns mainly from fair-value uplifts and realisations on a concentrated, long-held portfolio, plus dividends and fees. The WSJ headline says one holding, the retailer Action, accounts for the bulk of portfolio value [Likely] (source: https://www.wsj.com/business/3i-group-shares-plunge-after-slowdown-at-retailer-action-bdd881c0). Revenue-by-country mix is [data unavailable]; the company IR page was blocked, so I could not confirm the share of Action's sales from France or any other single country.

## 2. Quality
Returns are high but have fallen for five years. ROIC fell from 34.2% (FY2022) to 19.1% (FY2026) and is 17.3% TTM; ROE is 19.1% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/lon/III/financials/ratios/). [Certain]
Direction is still down: ROIC and ROCE are each about 2.3-2.4pp lower than a year ago and about 5pp below their 2-year highs (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/lon/III/financials/ratios/?p=trailing). [Certain]
Operating margin is 97.6% and leverage is minimal, at 0.11x net debt/EBITDA (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/lon/III/financials/income-statement/). [Certain]
These margins reflect an investment-holding income statement, not operating moat. The durable advantage is long-term sourcing and ownership of a few compounders. [Guessing]
The risk is concentration. After the May results, shares fell on weaker profitability at Action, and the 12-month change is -42.1% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/lon/III/statistics/). [Likely] A single-asset reversal can overwhelm the rest of the portfolio. [Likely]

## 3. Valuation
P/E is 4.7x, equal to its 5y median of 4.7x (range 3.3x-7.1x), and about 68% below the peer median of 14.6x (ICG, Partners Group, EQT). P/B is 0.8x, the bottom of its 5y range (0.8x-1.4x), against a peer median of 4.2x (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/lon/III/financials/ratios/). [Certain]
P/FCF of 25.7x is not meaningful for an investment company, because cash flow is not earnings. [Likely]
Fair value: [data unavailable]. The model lacked a GBP FX rate, so no DCF or P/E estimate is computed.
The price implies that carrying values, mainly Action's, are overstated or will be marked down. The discount to book is a margin of safety only if the marks hold. [Likely]
The stock sits 6.0% below its 200-day average, and next results are due Nov 12, 2026. [Certain]
Peers are asset managers earning fee income, not holders of a concentrated balance sheet, so the multiple gap overstates the discount. [Likely]

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROIC (ratios page, TTM) falls below 12%.
- ROE (ratios page, TTM) falls below 12%.
- Shares change YoY (statistics page) rises above 4%, meaning equity-funded dilution.
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) exceeds 1.0x.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
The company announced a buyback and a higher dividend at the May results [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/lon/III/transcripts/422339-h2-2026/). Yet shares outstanding rose 1.56% YoY (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/lon/III/statistics/), so the buyback has not yet shrunk the share count. [Certain]
FCF conversion is 18.8% of net income, up 6.6pp over five years. Most reported profit is unrealised valuation gain, not cash. [Certain]
Net debt is negligible.
