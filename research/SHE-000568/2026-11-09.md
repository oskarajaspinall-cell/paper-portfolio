## 2. Quality
Historically excellent: gross margin 86.6% and operating margin 54.2% in FY2025, ROIC 41.8% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/she/000568/financials/ratios/) [Certain]. The pricing power of a premium liquor brand is a durable-moat candidate [Likely].

But the last 12 months broke the pattern (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/she/000568/financials/ratios/?p=trailing) [Certain]:
- ROIC 58.3% → 19.3%; ROCE 31.4% → 14.3%.
- Operating margin 54.8% → 39.8% (income-statement trailing page).
- TTM gross margin 83.4% vs 86.6% in FY2025.

This is a sharp, fast deterioration, consistent with demand or price weakness in baijiu [Likely]; the cause is not established here [Guessing]. Balance sheet is net cash (net debt/EBITDA -2.25x), Altman Z 9.75, so distress is no concern [Certain]. Piotroski F is only 5.

## 3. Valuation

Multiples look mid-range, not cheap, on already-depressed trailing earnings: the price assumes profits stabilise near current levels [Likely].

Mechanical fair value base 84.00 CNY vs close 81.17 (+3.5%); bear 58.07 (-28.5%), bull 129.63 (+59.7%). Only EV/EBITDA worked; the DCF failed because the base cash-flow margin is negative [Certain]. Upside is thin and the bear case is larger than the base [Likely]. The stock is -39.1% over 12 months.
