## 2. Quality
Good business, high returns [Certain]: TTM ROIC 17.6%, ROCE 27.2%, ROE 38.3%, gross margin 57.4%, operating margin 12.8% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/wse/LPP/financials/ratios/). Piotroski F 7, Altman Z 3.04 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/wse/LPP/statistics/).
Direction is mixed [Certain]: ROIC is 5.0pp below its 2-year high of 22.6%, and ROCE slipped from 28.6% to 27.2% over 12 months, but operating margin rose 1.2pp and FCF margin recovered from 2.7% to 13.4% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/wse/LPP/financials/ratios/?p=trailing). Fiscal-year FCF is lumpy (FY2023 -3.6%), so the TTM rebound may partly be working-capital timing [Likely]. Net debt/EBITDA 1.54x, down from 2.70x in FY2026. Durable edge: vertically integrated speed and cost, plus store density in Central Europe [Likely]; June guidance was cut on fewer openings and operational headwinds (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/wse/LPP/transcripts/543278-q1-26-27/) [Likely].
