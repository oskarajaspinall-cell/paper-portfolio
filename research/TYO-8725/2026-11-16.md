## 2. Quality
Returns are decent but cyclical. ROIC was 8.6%, 7.1%, 11.1%, 14.6%, 8.4% over FY2022–FY2026 and 11.3% TTM; the 5y trend is flat [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tyo/8725/financials/ratios/). ROE is 16.1% TTM versus 9.8% in FY2026. Direction change: TTM ROIC fell 3.9pp in 12 months (15.2% to 11.3%) and sits 6.4pp below its 2-year high of 17.7% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tyo/8725/financials/ratios/?p=trailing). Earnings swing with catastrophe losses and investment gains, so a durable moat is not proven by this data [Likely]. FCF conversion is high (179.9% in FY2026), but insurer cash flow is noisy [Likely]. Piotroski F is 6. Beta is low at 0.27 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tyo/8725/statistics/). Conflict: TTM net debt/EBITDA is 1.26x on debt less cash and short-term investments but -0.33x on the site's definition; not resolved.

## 3. Valuation
P/E is 8.9x, at 28% of its 5y range (7.0x–13.6x) and 25% below the peer median of 11.9x (peers: TYO:8766, TYO:8630, CB, TRV). P/B is 1.1x, at 90% of its range and 43% below peers; FCF yield is 11.99% [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tyo/8725/statistics/). EV/EBITDA is 5.4x, at 94% of its 5y range, so the discount is not uniform across measures. The price is 5,316.00 JPY, +17.1% above the 200-day average. Mechanical fair value (P/B+ROE blended with P/E) is 4,163.92 JPY base, 21.7% below the price, with bull at 5,539.68 JPY, only 4.2% above it [Certain]. The market is therefore pricing roughly bull-case earnings and returns to persist; the cheapness versus peers is partly a Japan discount [Likely]. Cheap on earnings and cash yield, not on book or fair value.
