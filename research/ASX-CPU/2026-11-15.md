# Computershare (ASX:CPU) — Core initiation — 2026-11-15
## 1. Business
Share registry, employee equity plans and corporate trust/mortgage services. It earns recurring fees plus margin income on client cash balances, so earnings are rate-sensitive [Likely]. Per the AGM headline, all three core businesses grew in FY2026 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/asx/CPU/) [Likely]. Revenue by country is [data unavailable]: the company site was blocked. The US is probably a large share, so the macro overlay should check USD/AUD exposure [Guessing]. Financials are reported in USD, the shares quote in AUD.

## 2. Quality
Returns have risen sharply: ROIC 6.3% (FY2022) to 26.2% TTM, ROCE 6.7% to 22.0%, ROE 28.0% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/asx/CPU/financials/ratios/) [Certain]. Operating margin is 28.5% TTM against 12.4% in FY2022, FCF margin 24.5%, and FCF/net income 129.5% [Certain]. Net debt/EBITDA is 0.28x, Altman Z 5.30, Piotroski 7 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/asx/CPU/statistics/) [Certain].

Moat: switching costs and scale in registry and plan administration, with sticky issuer contracts [Likely]. Caveat: part of the margin gain is cyclical interest on client balances, which falls with rates [Likely]. Recent direction is flat to up: ROIC +2.3pp over the year, operating margin -0.6pp and FCF margin -0.4pp (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/asx/CPU/financials/ratios/?p=trailing) [Certain]. Profits sit near their peak, not mid-cycle [Likely].

## 3. Valuation
P/E is 29.0x, at 33% of its 5y range (21.1x-45.0x), and EV/EBITDA is 16.5x, mid-range at 49%. P/FCF is 22.3x, 95% of its range. EV/Sales and P/B are above their 5y maxima (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/asx/CPU/financials/ratios/) [Certain]. Against peers (BR, SSNC, ASX:ASX) it trades at a premium of +26% on P/E, +26% on EV/EBITDA and +36% on P/FCF [Certain]. FCF yield is 4.48% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/asx/CPU/statistics/) [Certain].

The mechanical DCF base is 26.15 AUD against a close of 44.73, so the price implies 17.4% year-1 revenue growth (reverse DCF) [Certain]. The P/E method gives 39.28 base. A DCF on FY2026 margins is arguably harsh if rate-linked income persists, but the price still assumes durable margins [Likely]. Trend is strong: +23.8% above the 200-day average and 12-month change +27.6%, but RSI is 73.1 and the price is at its 52-week high [Certain]. The setup is a good business that is working, but not cheap [Likely].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROIC (ratios page, TTM) falls below 18%.
- Operating margin (income statement, TTM) falls below 24%.
- FCF margin (cash flow statement, TTM) falls below 20%.
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) rises above 2.0x without an acquisition that adds earnings.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Shares outstanding fell 1.45% YoY, which points to buybacks (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/asx/CPU/statistics/) [Certain]. FCF conversion of 129.5% of net income is healthy, though below FY2022's 198.4% [Certain]. Management reaffirmed FY2027 guidance and says it will stay disciplined on acquisitions (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/asx/CPU/) [Likely]. Leverage is minimal.
