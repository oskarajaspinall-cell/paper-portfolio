# Macro overlay: Computershare (ASX:CPU) — 2026-11-15
## Assessment
**Weight: secondary.** Chain: policy rates -> yield on client cash balances -> margin income -> operating margin (28.5% TTM vs 12.4% in FY2022 per the note) -> earnings and multiple. The note flags part of the margin gain as cyclical interest income [Likely].

**Facts (FRED):** fed funds 3.88% (2026-11-12, +0.25pp over ~3m); US 2y 4.86% (+0.71pp ~3m); 10y real yield 2.86% (+0.47pp ~3m); 10y breakeven 2.24% (-0.03pp ~3m) (https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=DGS2, https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=DFF, https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=DFII10).

**Pricing (proxy):** the 2y sits ~1pp above fed funds, so markets price higher rates ahead, mostly real not inflation-driven **(inference)**. That supports float income but is already in the price: P/E is 29.0x and the DCF base is 26.15 vs a 44.73 close (fact sheet) [Likely].

**Hurts:** a rate-cut surprise cuts margin income; a further real-yield rise compresses a 29x multiple. **Helps:** rates staying higher for longer.

**Bull:** rates hold or rise, margin income persists, margin stays above 24%. **Bear:** the 2y falls under ~4% and real yields rise; margin income fades and the multiple de-rates. **Tilt:** toward bear, small. Higher rates are largely priced and the earnings downside is asymmetric from near-peak profits [Guessing].

**Country risk:** the US is likely a large share of revenue, but the mix is [data unavailable] in the note, so this is unverified. Reporting is in USD, quotes in AUD. FRED has no AUD series. The broad dollar index is 121.88 (2026-11-06, +2.82 ~3m). I did not check US politics or regulation.
