# Colgate-Palmolive Company (CL) — Core initiation — 2026-10-09
## 1. Business
Colgate-Palmolive sells oral, personal and home care products (Colgate, Palmolive, Speed Stick, Softsoap) and, via Hill's Pet Nutrition, premium pet food, through a global distribution network with leading share in oral care in most markets [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/cl/).

## 2. Quality
Returns on capital are high and still rising: TTM ROIC 45.6% (5y: +5.5pp) and ROCE 40.8% (5y: +10.0pp) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/cl/financials/ratios/). Gross margin is stable at ~60% (5y: +0.6pp), consistent with brand/scale pricing power in oral care [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/cl/financials/income-statement/). But operating margin (-1.1pp) and net margin (-2.0pp) have drifted down over 5 years [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/cl/financials/income-statement/), and Q2 2026 showed real North America stress: category softness, heavier competitive activity and retailer destocking cut shipments despite flat consumption [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/cl/transcripts/659046-q2-2026/). Management's response (premium innovation, surgical promotions, higher H2 ad spend) lifted gross margin +100bps in Q2 and the full-year gross-margin guide to "roughly flat" [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/cl/transcripts/659046-q2-2026/), suggesting the moat (brand strength, shelf position, Hill's premium pet franchise) is intact, but North America is not currently a source of growth.

## 3. Valuation
The market is pricing CL for continued execution, not for the North America wobble. TTM P/E of 34.8x sits at the 81st percentile of its own 5-year range (25.7x–36.9x) and is +76% above the peer median (19.7x; PG, KMB, CHD, UL) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/cl/financials/ratios/). EV/EBITDA (15.3x, +8% vs peers) and EV/Sales (3.6x, +5%) tell the same story, though P/FCF (18.2x) is actually 6% below the peer median, reflecting CL's unusually high cash conversion [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/cl/statistics/). The fact sheet's own-history DCF/P/E blend puts fair value at $73.62 base (-16.4% vs the $88.10 close), with even the bull case ($78.65) 10.7% below the price [Likely] (source: research/CL/factsheet-2026-10-09.md). P/B (297.6x) is not meaningful given a near-zero book value from years of buybacks. At face value, the stock requires sustained double-digit-ROIC growth beyond what the base case already assumes to earn today's multiple.

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Gross margin (income statement page) falls below 58% for two consecutive quarters, undoing the pricing-power thesis.
- North America organic/net sales growth (quarterly filings) stays negative for two more quarters, showing the H2 2026 actions failed.
- ROIC (ratios page) falls below 35%, back toward its FY2022 trough, signalling returns are no longer structurally rising.
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) rises above 2.0x, indicating capital discipline has slipped (e.g., to fund a brand-portfolio reshuffle).

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Cash conversion is excellent and improving: FCF margin 18.3% (5y: +2.0pp) and FCF/net income 189.3% (5y: +43.1pp) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/cl/financials/cash-flow-statement/). H1 2026 free cash flow rose 18% and the company returned $1.4bn to shareholders [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/cl/transcripts/659046-q2-2026/); the board declared a $0.53/share quarterly dividend on 2026-09-17, payable 2026-11-13 [Certain] (source: fact sheet news item, https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260916785260/en/Colgate-Palmolive-Declares-Regular-Quarterly-Dividend/). Leverage is conservative and falling: net debt/EBITDA 1.29x TTM (5y: -0.13x) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/cl/financials/ratios/). Shares outstanding fell 1.49% YoY, consistent with ongoing buybacks [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/cl/statistics/).

## 6. Unusual items
Reuters reported (sourcing unnamed) that Colgate is exploring a sale of mass-market personal-care brands including Softsoap, Irish Spring and Speed Stick [Guessing] (source: headline listed in fact sheet, https://www.reuters.com/world/colgate-palmolive-seeks-divest-some-personal-care-brands-sources-say-2026-09-11/ — article itself not independently verifiable; not confirmed by the Q2 2026 earnings call, which made no mention of it). If confirmed, a sale would sharpen the portfolio toward oral care/Hill's but proceeds and margin impact are unknown; this is a watch item for the next earnings release (2026-10-30), not yet a fact to size around. Short interest is low (2.46% of float) and institutional ownership high (85.8%); neither is material [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/cl/statistics/).
