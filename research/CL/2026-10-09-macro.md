# Macro overlay: Colgate-Palmolive Company (CL) — 2026-10-09
## Assessment
CL's thesis is bottom-up (North America volume softness vs. premium global brand/moat), but the stock trades at the 81st percentile of its own 5y P/E range and +76% above peers [Certain] (fact sheet, stockanalysis.com/stocks/cl/financials/ratios/, 2026-10-09), making its premium multiple unusually exposed to the discount rate. **Facts**: US 10y real yield (TIPS, DFII10) is 2.92% (2026-10-07), up +0.49pp in ~1m and +0.61pp in ~3m; the 10y nominal (DGS10) is 5.28%, which is the risk-free rate already used in the fact sheet's DCF [Certain] (https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=DFII10, id=DGS10, both 2026-10-07). The dollar index (DTWEXBGS) is 121.4, up +2.86 (~2.4%) in the last month alone vs +0.70 over 3m — most of the quarter's dollar strength is recent [Certain] (https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=DTWEXBGS, 2026-10-02). **Market pricing (proxy)**: 2y Treasury (4.77%) sits ~89bp above effective fed funds (3.88%), consistent with the market still pricing a higher-for-longer path rather than imminent cuts [Likely, proxy] (https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=DGS2, id=DFF, 2026-10-07). **Inference (ours)**: further real-yield rises would most likely compress CL's rich multiple toward peer/historical norms, and continued dollar strength would drag on reported EPS given material international/emerging-market sales (Latin America, Europe cited on the Q2 call) **(inference)**. Both channels point the same way as the existing bear case (-51% at $43.18), not the bull.
