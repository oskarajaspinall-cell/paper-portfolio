# Macro overlay: Hess Midstream LP (HESM) — 2026-10-19
## Assessment
HESM's revenue is fee/tariff-based under minimum-volume-commitment contracts with Chevron, not directly commodity- or GDP-cyclic [Likely] (factsheet-2026-10-19.md). The ~24% three-month decline is overwhelmingly a company-specific event: Chevron's 2026-10-06 announcement restructuring Bakken/DJ Basin midstream contracts (Reuters, 2026-10-06, as cited in 2026-10-19.md), not a macro move [Likely] **(inference)**.

A secondary discount-rate channel exists given leverage: net debt/EBITDA is 3.00x TTM and H1 2026 buybacks were revolver-funded [company-reported] (factsheet). Facts (FRED, official): US 10y real yield (DFII10) 2.78% on 2026-10-15, +0.43pp over 3m; US HY credit spread (BAMLH0A0HYM2) 3.23% on 2026-10-15, +0.52pp over 3m; effective fed funds (DFF) 3.88% on 2026-10-15, +0.25pp over both 1m and 3m (reports/macro/2026-10-19.md). Market pricing: the 2y yield (DGS2, 4.77%, +0.61pp/3m) moved roughly in line with the 10y (+0.53pp/3m), keeping the curve narrow — a proxy suggesting the market isn't pricing near-term cuts **(inference)**.

Chain: rising real yields/credit spreads raise the discount rate on HESM's distribution yield and the cost of refinancing its revolver-funded buybacks — a multiple effect, not an earnings effect (fee/MVC structure insulates cash flow).

Bull: real yields/spreads plateau, easing cost-of-capital pressure, supporting a multiple recovery once deal terms are clarified. Bear: continued 3m-trend widening in yields/spreads compounds the deal overhang, keeping the multiple compressed. Tilt: toward bear, small — macro is a secondary layer behind the dominant, company-specific restructuring catalyst.
