# Macro overlay: Hess Midstream LP (HESM) — 2026-10-18
## Assessment
HESM's thesis is dominated by the Chevron restructuring (idiosyncratic, not macro) [Certain]. Fixed-fee/cost-of-service contracts with an 80% minimum revenue commitment insulate cash flow from commodity cycles [Likely], but the stock is partly a high-yield bond proxy (FCF yield 13.25%, fact sheet ST) and carries leverage (net debt/EBITDA ~3.0x TTM, rated BBB-/Ba1), so rates/credit are a secondary overlay on the valuation multiple and refinancing cost, not on contracted revenue.

**Facts**: US 10y Treasury yield 5.10% on 2026-10-15, +53bp over 3 months; 10y real yield (DFII10) 2.78%, +43bp over 3 months; 2y yield 4.77% vs effective fed funds 3.88% (both FRED, 2026-10-15); US HY credit spread 3.23%, +52bp over 3 months (FRED BAMLH0A0HYM2, 2026-10-15) — still low versus history, not stressed. **Market pricing (proxy, inference)**: the 2y ~90bp above effective fed funds suggests the market is not pricing near-term cuts, consistent with core CPI acceleration (+2.83 index points over 3m, FRED, 2026-09-01) **(inference)**. **Inference**: rising real yields raise the discount rate for yield/distribution-sensitive midstream equity, pressuring the multiple independent of the Chevron deal.

**Bull**: real yields/credit spreads stabilize → discount-rate pressure eases → partial multiple recovery toward peers, on top of any deal resolution. **Bear**: real yields and HY spreads keep rising → discount rate stays elevated and refinancing the 3x-levered balance sheet gets costlier → further multiple compression layered on the Chevron de-rating. **Tilt**: small toward bear — the magnitude is modest (spreads still tight) next to the company-specific catalyst.
