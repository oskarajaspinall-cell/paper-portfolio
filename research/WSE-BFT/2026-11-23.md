## 2. Quality
Good business with high returns on capital: ROIC 15.2% TTM, ROE 35.5%, operating margin 17.9%, gross margin 35.2% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/wse/BFT/financials/ratios/). [Certain] Cash conversion is strong: FCF/NI 116.7% TTM. [Certain]
Direction caveat: ROIC peaked at 27.7% (TTM Sep-2024) and is 12.5pp below it; it fell 2.1pp over the last year even as ROCE (+2.8pp) and FCF margin (13.3% to 18.5%) rose (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/wse/BFT/financials/ratios/?p=trailing). [Certain] Returns have normalised from a post-pandemic rebound, not collapsed. [Likely]
Moat: [Likely] network effects of a multi-venue card (employers want breadth of clubs; clubs want volume) plus scale in Poland. [Guessing] on durability; not verified from a primary source.
Leverage moderate: net debt/EBITDA 2.22x vs 1.08x in FY2023; Altman Z 3.02 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/wse/BFT/statistics/).
