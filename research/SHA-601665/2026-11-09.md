P/E is 6.8x against a 5y range of 4.6x-9.1x (median 5.8x, 47th percentile) and peers' 6.7x, so in line [Certain]. P/B is 0.7x against 0.4x-0.8x (median 0.6x, 68th percentile) and peers' 0.8x, 11% below [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sha/601665/statistics/). With ROE near 11% a P/B of 0.7x implies the market doubts reported book value or earnings durability, consistent with the reverse DCF implying -8.7% year-1 revenue growth [Likely]. Base fair value is 10.11 CNY against a 6.52 close, but the fact sheet flags it unreliable: methods disagree 5.8x (P/B+ROE 7.64, FCFE DCF 15.03, dividend discount 2.59) [Certain]. The FCFE inputs are distorted for a bank, so I weigh only the P/B+ROE estimate, modestly above the price [Guessing]. Only marginally cheap versus own history; no clear mispricing.
