# Bristol-Myers Squibb (BMY) — Core initiation — 2026-10-07

## 1. Business
BMY discovers, develops and sells prescription biopharmaceuticals across oncology, hematology, immunology, cardiovascular and neuroscience [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/bmy/). Revenue mixes legacy drugs facing patent expiry (e.g. Eliquis, Revlimid) with a "Growth Portfolio" of newer branded drugs (Opdivo, Camzyos, Breyanzi, Reblozyl, Opdualag, Sotyktu, Cobenfy) that already supplies 55% of revenue, with 7 assets each annualizing over $1bn [Certain] (source: https://www.bms.com/investors.html).

## 2. Quality
ROIC and ROCE have risen steadily, TTM 21.7% and 22.7% versus 10.8%/9.8% in FY2021 [Certain] (fact sheet RA). That rise, plus a Piotroski F-score of 7, points to improving capital efficiency, not a one-off [Certain] (fact sheet ST). Durability rests on the Growth Portfolio's breadth, not any single drug: late-stage programs span milvexian (cardiovascular), iberdomide/mezigdomide/arlo-cel (myeloma), golcadomide (lymphoma), Cobenfy (neuroscience), iza-bren/pumitamig (oncology) and Sotyktu (immunology) [Certain] (source: https://www.bms.com/research-and-development/pipeline.html). Recent proof points: an expanded FDA pediatric approval for Camzyos in oHCM on 2026-09-30 [Certain] (source: https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260930516104/en/U.S.-Food-and-Drug-Administration-Approves-Expanded-Indication-for-Bristol-Myers-Squibb%E2%80%99s-CAMZYOS%C2%AE-mavacamten-for-the-Treatment-of-Symptomatic-Obstructive-Hypertrophic-Cardiomyopathy-oHCM-in-Adults-and-Pediatric-Patients*/), and a blood-cancer cell therapy meeting its primary endpoint in a mid-stage trial (2026-09-08) [Certain] (source: https://www.reuters.com/business/healthcare-pharmaceuticals/bristol-myers-blood-cancer-cell-therapy-meets-main-goal-mid-stage-trial-2026-09-08/). The counterweight: gross margin has fallen 5.9pp over five years and FCF margin/conversion are also declining (see §5) [Certain] (fact sheet IS, CF). Patent protection is time-limited by design, so this is a finite moat clock, not a permanent one.

## 3. Valuation
BMY trades at 13.1x P/E (1st percentile of its own 5y range of 13.0x-25.2x) and 8.2x EV/EBITDA (31st percentile of 7.5x-9.8x) [Certain] (fact sheet RA). Against peers (PFE, MRK, ABBV, LLY) it is cheaper on every multiple: P/E -77%, EV/EBITDA -47%, EV/Sales -56%, P/FCF -55%, P/B -34% [Certain] (fact sheet RA, peer statistics pages). FCF yield is 9.4% [Certain] (fact sheet ST). The current price assumes the market is treating BMY's legacy-drug patent cliffs as a permanent earnings-power impairment rather than a transition being offset by a Growth Portfolio already at 55% of revenue and with rising ROIC/ROCE. That looks too pessimistic given the fact-sheet trend data, though the discount is not free money — it reflects real execution risk in the pipeline above.

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROIC (ratios page) falls back below 15% in a full fiscal year, reversing the 5y uptrend.
- FCF margin (cash flow + income statement, computed) drops below 20% TTM, versus 23.3% now.
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) rises above 2.5x, versus 1.80x TTM, signalling balance-sheet strain funding the pipeline.
- Growth Portfolio revenue share (bms.com investor disclosures) falls back below 50%, indicating the new drugs are not offsetting legacy LOE.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
FCF margin is still solid at 23.3% TTM but has fallen from 32.8% in FY2021; FCF/net income conversion has fallen from 217.8% to 123.2% over the same period [Certain] (fact sheet CF, IS). Net debt/EBITDA has drifted up from 1.47x to 1.80x TTM [Certain] (fact sheet BS, IS). Shares outstanding are essentially flat (+0.47% YoY), so there is no material dilution funding this [Certain] (fact sheet ST). The company declared a dividend on 2026-09-16, consistent with its capital-return history [Certain] (source: https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260916783123/en/Bristol-Myers-Squibb-Announces-Dividend/); the fact sheet does not give the per-share amount or yield, so that figure is [data unavailable].

## 6. Unusual items
FY2024 shows a net margin of -18.5% and ROE of -38.9%, a sharp reversal from positive prior and subsequent years (FY2025 net margin 14.6%, ROE 40.4%) [Certain] (fact sheet IS, RA); this is consistent with a one-off charge rather than a change in the underlying earnings trend, but the specific cause is [data unavailable] from the fact sheet. Altman Z-score is 2.59, in the "grey zone" rather than clearly safe, worth monitoring alongside the rising net debt/EBITDA trend [Certain] (fact sheet ST). Short interest is low (2.06% of float) and fell a further 8.5% month-on-month, and institutional ownership is high at 83.1% [Certain] (fact sheet ST) — neither is a red flag.
