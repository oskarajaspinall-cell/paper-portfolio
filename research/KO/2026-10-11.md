# The Coca-Cola Company (KO) — Core initiation — 2026-10-11

## 1. Business
Coca-Cola owns beverage brands (Coca-Cola, Sprite, Fanta, water, coffee/tea, juice — ~200 brands) and sells concentrate/syrup to independent and owned bottlers, who make, distribute and sell finished product [Likely]. Revenue is asset-light: marketing and brand investment drive bottler volume, royalties and concentrate pricing (source: fact sheet margins, https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/ko/financials/income-statement/).

## 2. Quality
Returns are excellent and still improving: ROIC 20.1% TTM (+3.6pp over 5y), ROCE 19.3% (+2.6pp), gross margin 61.9% and operating margin 31.8%, both rising [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/ko/financials/ratios/, https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/ko/financials/income-statement/). The concentrate/bottling model and scaled distribution are a durable advantage few beverage peers can replicate at this margin [Likely]. Leverage is low and falling (net debt/EBITDA 1.64x TTM vs 2.45x in FY2021) [Certain] (source: ratios page). One quality wrinkle: FCF margin fell from 29.1% (FY21) to 10-11% (FY24-25) before rebounding to 28.5% TTM, and FCF/NI conversion dropped to ~40-45% in FY24-25 before recovering to 99.9% TTM [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/ko/financials/cash-flow-statement/) — the multi-year swing is large enough to watch, though the TTM figure is back near historical norms. Management change: Rob Gehring (ex-Monster Beverage) becomes North America president effective Dec 1, 2026, succeeding John Murphy [Certain] (source: https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260925081299/en/The-Coca-Cola-Company-Names-Rob-Gehring-President-of-North-America-Operating-Unit/) — a leadership transition, not yet a quality signal either way [Guessing].

## 3. Valuation
The price assumes continued premium growth the fact sheet's own model doesn't support. KO trades at 23.9x EV/EBITDA and 8.1x EV/Sales vs peer (PEP, KDP, MNST, CCEP) medians of 15.0x and 3.1x — +60% and +166% premiums — both above their own 5-year range maximum (152% and 164% of range) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/ko/financials/ratios/ and peer statistics pages). P/E (26.5x) and P/FCF (26.5x) sit at or above peers too (+1%/+33%). The blended DCF/P/E fair value is 62.39 base, bear 33.02, and bull only 69.87 — all below the last close of 88.05 (-29.1% base, -20.6% bull) (source: fact sheet fair-value block, https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/ko/, https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=DGS10). The reverse-DCF implies ~15.5% year-1 revenue growth fading to terminal — above KO's recent single-digit growth [Likely]. A quality compounder priced for perfection, with no margin of safety even in the bull case.

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Revenue growth (income statement) sustains mid-to-high single digits for two consecutive fiscal years, validating the reverse-DCF's implied growth path.
- Operating margin (income statement) continues expanding past 32%, showing pricing/mix power that could justify the premium multiple.
- FCF margin (cash flow statement) stabilizes above 25% for two consecutive fiscal years rather than reverting to the 10-11% seen in FY24-25.
- ROIC (ratios page) holds above 19%, confirming the moat is intact and returns aren't being competed away.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Shares outstanding fell only 0.09% YoY — minimal buyback activity [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/ko/statistics/). Net debt/EBITDA improved to 1.64x TTM from 2.45x in FY2021, giving flexibility [Certain] (source: ratios page). FCF conversion (FCF/NI) was volatile — 115% (FY21) down to 40-45% (FY24-25) and back to 99.9% TTM — so cash generation should be checked against the next print before sizing up [Certain] (source: cash-flow and income-statement pages). Reported plans to divest Costa Coffee (acquired for ~$5bn) would be a modest simplification, not a red flag, if confirmed [Likely] (source: https://www.reuters.com/legal/transactional/coca-cola-looks-sell-costa-coffee-semafor-reports-2026-10-08/, headline only).

## 6. Unusual items
Reuters/Semafor reported (2026-10-08) that Coca-Cola is seeking to unwind its ~$5bn Costa Coffee acquisition after an earlier sale process failed [Likely] (source: https://www.reuters.com/legal/transactional/coca-cola-looks-sell-costa-coffee-semafor-reports-2026-10-08/). Short interest is low (1.05% of float) but rose 3.5% versus the prior month [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/ko/statistics/). Beta is very low (0.32), consistent with a defensive staples profile [Certain] (source: statistics page). Q3 2026 earnings are due Oct 27, 2026, shortly after this note [Certain] (source: https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260929949392/en/The-Coca-Cola-Company-Announces-Timing-of-Third-Quarter-2026-Earnings-Release/).
