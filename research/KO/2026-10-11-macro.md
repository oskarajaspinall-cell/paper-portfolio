# Macro overlay: The Coca-Cola Company (KO) — 2026-10-11
## Assessment
KO is a low-growth, low-beta (0.32 raw [Certain], source: stockanalysis.com/stocks/ko/statistics/) staple already priced ~29% above its own base DCF fair value (62.39 vs 88.05 close). As a bond-proxy on a record-premium multiple, valuation is sensitive to the discount rate: **real/nominal yields -> CAPM cost of equity -> DCF fair value / multiple**. Facts: US 10y Treasury (DGS10) 5.22% on 2026-10-08, +0.68pp/3m; 10y real yield (DFII10) 2.87%, +0.56pp/3m (fred.stlouisfed.org, id=DGS10, DFII10). The fair-value model already uses this 5.22% risk-free rate, so the recent yield backup is partly embedded in the -29% gap; further rises would widen it [Likely] **(inference)**. Proxy (not fact): 2y yield (DGS2) 4.75% sits ~87bp above fed funds (DFF) 3.88%, consistent with a "higher-for-longer" path, not near-term cuts **(inference)**. Secondary: the dollar index (DTWEXBGS) rose to 121.385 (2026-10-02, +2.86/1m) — a plausible FX headwind on KO's international sales, unquantified here [Guessing]. HY spreads widened (+0.45pp/3m to 3.15%) while VIX stayed low (15.41).
**Bull**: yields roll over -> discount rate falls -> fair value drifts toward mid-$60s/$70s, narrowing (not closing) the gap to price.
**Bear**: real yields keep rising (sticky core CPI, hawkish Fed) -> fair value falls further below the 62.39 base, widening the overvaluation gap.
**Tilt**: moderate toward bear — a persistent 3-month real-yield rise stacks onto a stock with no margin of safety in its bull case; clamped beta (floor 0.8) dampens but doesn't remove the sensitivity.
