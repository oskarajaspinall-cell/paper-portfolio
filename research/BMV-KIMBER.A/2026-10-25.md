# Kimberly-Clark de México (BMV:KIMBER.A) — Core initiation — 2026-10-25
## 1. Business
Mexican maker of tissue, diapers, feminine care and wipes, selling consumer brands plus away-from-home professional products; earns margin from scale manufacturing, brand strength and retail distribution in Mexico (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/bmv/KIMBER.A/) [Likely]. Costs are pulp and petrochemical-based inputs, so margins swing with commodity prices and the peso [Likely].

## 2. Quality
Strong. TTM ROIC 51.9% and ROCE 35.6%, up from 25.3% and 24.9% in FY2021; operating margin 22.9% vs 17.8%; FCF conversion 132.4% of net income (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/bmv/KIMBER.A/financials/ratios/) [Certain]. Net debt/EBITDA 0.87x, Altman Z 3.53, Piotroski 6 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/bmv/KIMBER.A/statistics/) [Certain]. ROE above 100% reflects low equity after payouts, not a pure operating signal [Likely]. Moat: category-leading brands and scale in a staples category with local manufacturing and shelf presence [Likely]. Margin gain was partly cost-cycle driven, so some may reverse [Likely]. Q3 call: flat sales, weak demand expected through year-end, gradual improvement in 2027 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/bmv/KIMBER.A/transcripts/653996-q3-2026/) [Likely]. Beta 0.18 [Certain].

## 3. Valuation
P/E 14.3x (27% of own range, vs peers' median 24.3x), EV/EBITDA 8.5x (33%; peers 15.8x), P/FCF 10.6x (4%; peers 20.1x), FCF yield 9.41% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/bmv/KIMBER.A/financials/ratios/) [Certain]. P/B 20.5x sits at the top of its range, reflecting the high-ROE, low-equity structure [Likely]. Reverse DCF: price implies -5.5% revenue growth in year 1, fading to terminal growth; base fair value 51.50 MXN vs close 37.86 (+36.0%), bear 28.23 (-25.4%) (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/bmv/KIMBER.A/) [Likely]. The peer discount is partly warranted: Mexico-only exposure, higher country risk and flat volumes [Likely]. Even so, the price assumes sustained shrinkage while margins and cash conversion hold [Likely].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Gross margin (income statement page, TTM) falls below 36%.
- ROIC (ratios page, TTM) falls below 30%.
- FCF margin (cash-flow and income statement pages, TTM) falls below 10%.
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) rises above 2.0x.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Cash conversion is excellent: TTM FCF/NI 132.4%, FCF margin 18.7%. Share count down 0.97% YoY, so buybacks are modest (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/bmv/KIMBER.A/statistics/) [Certain]. Leverage is falling, leaving room for payouts and buybacks. Q4 call cited debt repayment, dividends and buybacks (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/bmv/KIMBER.A/transcripts/367698-q4-2025/) [Likely].

## 6. Unusual items
MXN exposure feeds into the USD return; non-USD fee applies [Certain]. Institutional ownership only 16.7%, likely a controlled company with a thin float [Guessing]. Price trails SPY over 3m/6m/12m, and shares sit below both moving averages, but this is not a thesis input [Certain]. Q3 sales were flat with weak demand guided into year-end [Likely].
