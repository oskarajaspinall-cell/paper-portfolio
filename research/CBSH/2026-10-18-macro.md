# Macro overlay: Commerce Bancshares, Inc. (CBSH) — 2026-10-18
## Assessment
CBSH is a rate-sensitive regional bank: short rates -> net interest margin (asset repricing vs. CBSH's low-cost core deposits) -> earnings; and the 10y Treasury -> CAPM discount rate in the fact sheet's fair value -> multiple [Certain].

Official facts (FRED, 2026-10-15/16): effective fed funds 3.88%, +0.25pp 3m; 2y Treasury 4.77%, +0.61pp 3m; 10y Treasury 5.10%, +0.53pp 3m; 10y real yield 2.78%, +0.43pp 3m; 10y breakeven 2.35%, +0.11pp 3m; HY spread 3.23%, +0.52pp 3m (https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=DFF,DGS2,DGS10,DFII10,T10YIE,BAMLH0A0HYM2).

Market pricing (proxy, **inference**): 2y yield (4.77%) above fed funds (3.88%), plus fed funds itself up this quarter, implies the market prices policy staying higher for longer rather than near-term cuts [Likely]. Most of the long-yield rise is real (+0.43pp) rather than breakeven inflation (+0.11pp) — a real-rate/term-premium move, not a fresh inflation scare [Likely].

The fact sheet's DCF already uses the current 5.10% risk-free rate, so the question is direction from here, not the level already priced.

**Macro bull**: rates plateau/ease as inflation cools -> NIM expansion (Q2: "expanding net interest margin, lower funding costs") continues, discount rate steady -> supports the $65.95 base fair value.
**Macro bear**: 10y/real yields keep climbing on sticky core CPI -> discount rate rises further, compressing fair value even if NIM holds; HY spread widening (still a low 3.23% level) is a funding-cost tail risk to watch.
**Tilt**: toward bear, small — low beta (0.59) and sticky deposits blunt the sensitivity; Q3 earnings (2026-10-20) will show whether NIM momentum offsets the drag.
