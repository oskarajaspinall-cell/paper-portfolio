# Macro overlay: Commerce Bancshares (CBSH) — 2026-11-08
## Assessment
**Weight: secondary.** A US regional bank is rate- and credit-sensitive, and the last month moved the risk premium, not just the level of rates. No foreign country is ~10% of revenue, so there is no country risk [Likely] (research note, section 1).

**Chain:** credit spreads and risk appetite -> expected credit losses and the equity risk premium -> bank multiple (P/B). Secondary chain: the curve -> net interest margin (NIM) on the FineMark-enlarged loan book.

**Facts (FRED, 2026-11-05/06):**
- HY spread 3.48% (+0.45pp 1m, +0.77pp 3m).
- VIX 28.18 (+13.2 1m).
- 10y yield 4.84% (-0.43 1m); 2y 4.46% (-0.33 1m); fed funds 3.88% (flat 1m, +0.25 3m).
- 10y real yield 2.60%; 10y breakeven 2.23%.
Source: https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=BAMLH0A0HYM2 (and the other series in reports/macro/2026-11-08.md).

**Pricing (proxy):** the 2y sits about 58bp above fed funds, so the market is pricing further hikes [Likely]. A 10y-2y slope of +0.38pp is positive but thin **(inference)**. Spreads are still low in absolute terms (3.48% against the 7% stress anchor), so this is a repricing of risk, not a credit event [Likely].

**Inference:** the surprise that hurts is a continued spread widening or a flattening/inversion, which compresses NIM and raises provisions. The fall in long yields helps asset values (securities marks) and the discount rate. The fundamentals note already shows slipping ROE and falling loan yields (-31bp). Macro does not change the 3 conviction on its own.
