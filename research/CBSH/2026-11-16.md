# Commerce Bancshares (CBSH) — Core initiation — 2026-11-16
## 1. Business
Kansas City-based regional bank holding company: net interest income on commercial, consumer and personal real estate loans, plus fee income from wealth, payments and, increasingly, investment banking. [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/cbsh/). It closed the FineMark acquisition on 2026-01-01, adding Florida, Arizona and South Carolina wealth-oriented banking [company-reported] (https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/22356/000002235626000213/cbsh-20260930.htm). Domestic; no foreign country is near 10% of revenue [Likely].

## 2. Quality
ROE is 15.1% TTM against 16.0% in FY2025 and a 17.8% peak in FY2023: still high for a bank but drifting down (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/cbsh/financials/ratios/) [Certain]. Net margin 31.4% TTM vs 33.1% in FY2025 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/cbsh/financials/income-statement/) [Certain]. Beta of 0.60 and a deposit-funded model suggest low earnings volatility [Likely]; the moat is a sticky, low-cost deposit base [Likely]. ROIC and ROCE are [data unavailable]; Piotroski F is 4 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/cbsh/statistics/).
Direction: FCF margin rose over 12 months (11.2% to 67.3%) but fell over five years; bank cash flow is distorted by loan-sale and working-capital timing, so I give it no weight [Likely]. Net interest income growth is FineMark-driven, acquired not organic [Likely]. Shares rose 2.07% year on year [Certain].

## 3. Valuation
P/E is 13.4x, near the 5y minimum of 13.0x (median 15.9x); P/B of 1.9x sits at the 5y minimum (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/cbsh/financials/ratios/) [Certain]. Versus peers (UMBF, BOKF, CFR, WTFC) it trades at a premium: P/E +13%, P/B +32% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/umbf/statistics/) [Certain], explained by higher returns and lower beta [Likely].
Mechanical fair value is 65.19 base (bear 58.67, bull 70.27) vs a 55.74 close, +17.0% to base, flagged unreliable: the dividend-discount value (17.08) is an outlier; P/B + ROE gives 64.07 and FCFE 67.44 [Certain]. FCF yield (16.36%) and P/FCF (6.1x) are distorted and discounted.
Why the price fell: 3-month return is -7.1% vs SPY -3.0%, from a 6-month high of 60.92 on 2026-07-29 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/cbsh/history/). Q3 results (2026-10-20) showed no guidance change I can identify; this looks like sector and multiple compression, not a thesis break [Guessing]. The buyback was reauthorized on 2026-11-03 (https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20261103236034/en/Commerce-Bancshares-Inc.-Board-of-Directors-Reauthorizes-Share-Repurchase-Program/) [Likely].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROE (ratios page) stays below 12% for two consecutive quarters.
- Net margin (income statement page, TTM) falls below 28%.
- Efficiency ratio in the 10-Q MD&A rises above 62%, vs 58.34% for the first nine months of 2026 [company-reported] (https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/22356/000002235626000213/cbsh-20260930.htm).
- Shares outstanding growth (statistics page) exceeds 4% year on year without a further acquisition.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Buybacks are active (reauthorized 2026-11-03) and the company pays regular cash and stock dividends (https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20261030234545/en/Commerce-Bancshares-Inc.-Declares-Stock-Dividend-and-Quarterly-Cash-Dividend-on-Common-Stock/) [Certain]. Per-share growth lags net income: nine-month net income +7.2% but diluted EPS only +3.6% [company-reported] (https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/22356/000002235626000213/cbsh-20260930.htm). Bolt-ons (FineMark, Nolan & Associates, 2026-10-01) are fee-oriented [Likely]. FCF/NI conversion is unreliable for a bank [Likely].
