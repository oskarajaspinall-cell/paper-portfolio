# Commerce Bancshares, Inc. (CBSH) — Core initiation — 2026-10-18

## 1. Business
Commerce Bancshares operates Commerce Bank, a Kansas City-based super-regional bank: it earns net interest income lending against low-cost core deposits, plus fee income from wealth/trust management, card and payments, and consumer/commercial lending [Certain] (source: https://www.commercebank.com/about-us; sector/industry per https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/cbsh/).

## 2. Quality
A granular, low-cost deposit base and a large trust/wealth franchise are hard for new entrants to replicate, and underwriting is conservative (5y beta 0.59) [Likely] (ST). ROE has slipped from 17.8% (FY23) to 14.7% TTM, and the net margin trend is falling (-2.5pp over 5y, 31.6% TTM) [Certain] (RA, IS) — some margin give-back after the post-2022 rate cycle. The FineMark Holdings acquisition closed January 1, 2026, adding ~$4.0bn total assets, $2.6bn loans, $3.1bn deposits and $8.7bn assets under administration across 13 offices in Florida, Arizona and South Carolina [company-reported] (https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/22356/000002235626000190/cbsh-20260630.htm) — extends the trust/wealth fee base into higher-growth Sunbelt markets [Likely]. Commerce Bank also completed the acquisition of Nolan & Associates, a boutique middle-market investment bank, on 2026-10-01, adding fee-based capability [Likely] (https://www.tipranks.com/news/the-fly/commerce-bancshares-completes-nolan-associates-acquisition-thefly-news). Piotroski F-score is a middling 4/9 [Likely] (ST).

## 3. Valuation
P/E of 13.6x sits near the bottom of its own 5-year range (14th percentile) and roughly in line with peer median (13.3x, +2%) [Certain] (RA, peers UMBF/WTFC/FFIN/FHN). P/FCF (6.6x) is below its 5y range minimum and 35% cheap to peers, though bank free-cash-flow metrics are noisy and driven by deposit/loan balance swings rather than true distributable cash [Likely] (CF, IS). P/B (1.8x) is below its own 5y minimum but 36% above the peer median (1.4x) — rich on book value given the peer set, which the higher ROE within that group partly justifies [Likely] (RA). Own-history fair value: base $65.95 (+18.4% vs last close $55.70), bear $59.24 (+6.4%), bull $70.72 (+27.0%) (IS,BS,CF,RA,ST,HI). The reverse DCF implies the market is pricing roughly -0.8% revenue growth in year 1 fading to terminal growth — a conservative embedded assumption [Likely].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROE (ratios page) falls below 12% on a TTM basis (vs 14.7% now) — franchise/margin deterioration beyond the rate cycle.
- Net margin (income statement) falls below 28% TTM (vs 31.6% now; 5y trend already -2.5pp) — credit or funding-cost pressure not offset by fee income.
- Efficiency ratio [company-reported, 10-Q: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/22356/000002235626000190/cbsh-20260630.htm] rises above 63% (vs 59.19% in H1 2026) — FineMark integration costs running above plan.
- Short interest (statistics page) rises above 7% of float, sustained for a full quarter (vs 4.98% now) — building market concern on asset quality or integration execution.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
The board declared a $0.275/share quarterly dividend in July 2026 [Certain] (https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260731839732/en/Commerce-Bancshares-Inc.-Declares-Cash-Dividend-on-Common-Stock/). TTM FCF/NI conversion is 207.9%, but this is noisy for a bank given balance-sheet funding swings rather than a clean distributable-cash signal (CF, IS) [Likely]. Shares outstanding rose 1.15% YoY, consistent with FineMark deal-related share issuance rather than buybacks (ST) [Likely]. Capital has been deployed into bolt-on fee-income M&A (FineMark, closed Jan 2026; Nolan & Associates, closed Oct 2026) [Certain], and the company tendered legacy Visa Class B-2 shares and repositioned investment securities in May 2026, realising value from a long-held non-operating asset [Likely] (https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260512821808/en/Commerce-Bancshares-Inc.-Announces-Visa-Inc.%E2%80%99s-Acceptance-of-Class-B-2-Common-Stock-and-Investment-Securities-Repositioning/).

## 6. Unusual items
The FineMark merger closed January 1, 2026, and the prior-year merger-completion risk factors (deal-break, regulatory-approval, integration and retention risk) have been removed from the 10-Q now that the deal is done [Certain] (sec-delta, 10-Q above). Next earnings are due 2026-10-20, two days after this note, so the figures above will be refreshed almost immediately [Certain] (ST/OV). Short interest (4.98% of float, +1.3% month-on-month) is not elevated [Likely] (ST).
