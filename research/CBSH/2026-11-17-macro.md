# Macro overlay: Commerce Bancshares (CBSH) — 2026-11-17
## Assessment
**Weight: secondary.** Chain: market-implied hikes -> higher short yields -> deposit costs and securities marks vs. loan repricing (NIM and tangible book), plus credit spreads -> credit costs -> bank risk premium [Likely].

**Facts (FRED, 2026-11-13/16):** 2y Treasury 4.88% (+0.71pp in ~3m) vs effective fed funds 3.88% (+0.25pp); 10y 5.15% (+0.47pp); 10y TIPS 2.91% (+0.5pp); 10y breakeven 2.2% (-0.08pp); high-yield spread 3.23% (+0.56pp in 3m, -0.02pp in 1m); VIX 22.91 (+7.7 in 3m). Source: https://fred.stlouisfed.org (reports/macro/2026-11-17.md).

**Market pricing (proxy):** the 2y sitting ~100bp above fed funds implies the market prices further hikes **(inference)**; the 10y-2y spread is only +0.27pp, so the curve is flat. Breakevens are falling, so the rise is real yields, not inflation scares.

**Inference:** CBSH has a low-cost deposit franchise and beta of 0.60 [Likely]. Its P/B (1.9x) is at its 5y minimum, so higher discount rates and risk aversion appear already partly priced. A flat curve limits NIM gains from higher rates; deposit competition and unrealised securities losses are the risks. Its NII growth is acquisition-led (10-Q), so macro does not rescue organic growth [Likely]. Spreads are still tight in absolute terms; no credit stress yet.

**Bull:** policy hikes stay shallow and the curve steepens: NIM edges up, multiple rebuilds toward peers. **Bear:** hikes plus spread widening above ~4.5%: deposit costs rise, credit costs rise, the multiple compresses further below 1.9x P/B.

**Tilt:** neutral, small. Helpful deposit franchise offsets the rate headwind; the priced-in valuation limits further downside.
