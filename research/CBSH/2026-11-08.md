# Commerce Bancshares (CBSH) — Core initiation — 2026-11-08
## 1. Business
Commerce Bank (Kansas City) earns net interest income lending against a low-cost core deposit base, plus fees from trust/wealth, cards and payments, and now investment banking [Certain] (source: https://www.commercebank.com; https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/cbsh/). No foreign country is ~10% of revenue; it is a US domestic bank [Likely].

## 2. Quality
Franchise moat is a granular, cheap deposit base and a large trust business [Likely]. Returns are good but drifting: ROE 17.8% (FY23) to 15.1% TTM; net margin 31.4% TTM, 5y trend falling (-2.5pp) [Certain] (https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/cbsh/financials/ratios/). ROIC/ROCE are [data unavailable] for banks. Recent direction: the fact sheet flags FCF margin up from 29.6% to 65.8% over 12 months, but for a bank this reflects deposit/loan balance swings, not distributable cash, so I give it no weight [Likely] (https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/cbsh/financials/cash-flow-statement/?p=trailing). The 10-Q shows the FineMark deal (closed 1 January 2026) lifted average loans 18.2% and net interest income 10.5% in the quarter, while average loan rates fell 31bp; nine-month net income rose 7.2% but diluted EPS only 3.6% (to $3.14), i.e. growth is bought partly with dilution; nine-month annualised ROE 14.03%, efficiency ratio 58.34% [company-reported] (https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/22356/000002235626000213/cbsh-20260930.htm). Risk factors unchanged [Certain] (sec-delta). Piotroski F-score 4 [Certain]. Quality is good, not exceptional [Likely].

## 3. Valuation
P/E 13.3x is at the 6th percentile of its 5y range (13.0x–17.6x) and 2% below peers (13.5x; FITB, HBAN, CFR, UMBF) [Certain] (https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/cbsh/financials/ratios/). P/B 1.9x is at its 5y minimum but 29% above the peer median 1.4x [Certain]; P/FCF 6.4x is not meaningful for a bank [Likely]. Fair value base $67.93 (bear $61.12, bull $73.22) against a close of $55.04, +23.4% to base; the reverse DCF implies -2.4% year-1 revenue growth [Certain] (fact sheet). Upside is moderate and rests on a P/B+ROE method with ROE already slipping; there is no discount to peers on earnings, and the premium on book is what the above-peer ROE earns. Upside to base has widened since the prior note as the price fell, but the business evidence has not improved [Likely]. This is fairly priced to modestly cheap, short of the margin of safety for conviction 4.
