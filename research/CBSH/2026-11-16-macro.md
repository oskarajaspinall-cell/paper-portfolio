# Macro overlay: Commerce Bancshares (CBSH) — 2026-11-16
## Assessment
**Weight: secondary.** Chain: policy path and curve -> deposit costs and securities marks -> net interest margin and the P/B multiple. Domestic bank; no country risk.

**Facts (FRED):** 2y yield 4.86% (+0.71pp over ~3m), 10y 5.10% (+0.47pp), real 10y 2.86% (+0.47pp), fed funds 3.88% (+0.25pp over 3m), so the 10y-2y slope is only 0.24pp, flatter than three months ago. HY spread 3.2% (+0.49pp over 3m), VIX 18.7 (+4.07 over 3m) (https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=DGS2, DGS10, DFII10, DFF, BAMLH0A0HYM2, VIXCLS; latest observations 2026-11-12/13). [Certain]

**Pricing (proxy):** the 2y yield sits about 1pp above fed funds, so the market is pricing further hikes, not cuts. [Likely] Breakevens are flat (2.24%), so the rise in yields is real-rate driven. [Certain]

**Inference:** higher-for-longer lifts loan yields. A flat curve and deposit competition squeeze the margin, higher yields mark down the securities book, and rising real yields and spreads lift the bank risk premium. This fits the 3m share-price fall of -7.1% against SPY -3.0% (fact sheet). **(inference)** Only a surprise from here matters.

**Bull:** the curve steepens on growth with stable credit. NIM holds and P/B re-rates from the 5y low of 1.9x.
**Bear:** the curve inverts and spreads pass ~4.5%. Deposit repricing and credit worries keep the multiple compressed and EPS growth below the 3.6% nine-month rate.

**Tilt:** toward bear, small. Low beta (0.60) and P/B at its 5y minimum already discount a lot. [Guessing]
