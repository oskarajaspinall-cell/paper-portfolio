# Commerce Bancshares (CBSH) — Core initiation — 2026-11-17
## 1. Business
Kansas City-based regional bank holding company (Banks - Regional) earning net interest income on loans and securities, plus fee income from payments, trust and wealth management (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/cbsh/). It completed the FineMark acquisition on January 1, 2026, extending into Florida wealth banking, and bought Nolan & Associates, an investment bank, on 2026-10-01 [Certain] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/22356/000002235626000213/cbsh-20260930.htm; https://www.tipranks.com/news/the-fly/commerce-bancshares-completes-nolan-associates-acquisition-thefly-news). No foreign country is disclosed as ~10% of revenue in the sources read [Likely].

## 2. Quality
ROE has eased from 17.8% (FY2023) to 15.1% TTM, flat over five years (+0.2pp); net margin fell from 33.1% (FY2025) to 31.4% TTM (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/cbsh/financials/ratios/). The 9-month return on assets was 1.75% and the efficiency ratio 58.34% `[company-reported]` (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/22356/000002235626000213/cbsh-20260930.htm) [Certain]. These are strong for a regional bank, consistent with a low-cost deposit franchise and conservative credit culture [Likely]; that is the durable edge, not a patent-like moat [Guessing]. Beta is 0.60 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/cbsh/statistics/). ROIC, margin and leverage rows are [data unavailable] (normal for banks). The 10-Q reports no material change to risk factors [Certain].

## 3. Valuation
P/E is 13.3x, at the bottom of its 5y range (13.0x-17.6x; position 7%) and +14% above the peer median of 11.7x (UMBF, BOKF, WTFC, CFR). P/B is 1.9x, at the 5y minimum, +32% above peers at 1.4x. The market pays a premium for quality yet at its own cheapest in five years [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/cbsh/statistics/). Reverse DCF implies -1.1% year-1 revenue growth. Base fair value 64.70 vs last close 55.51 (+16.6%); P/B+ROE alone gives 63.59, but the sheet flags it unreliable because the dividend model gives 16.95. P/FCF 6.1x and FCF yield 16.33% are not meaningful for a bank (TTM FCF margin jumped to 67.3%, likely working-capital/loan-sale timing) [Guessing]. Price is 1.8% above the 200-day MA.

## 4. What would prove this wrong

- Net margin (income statement page) falls below 27%.
- Efficiency ratio (10-Q MD&A, `[company-reported]`) rises above 62% for two quarters, indicating FineMark/Nolan integration costs outrunning revenue.
- Shares change YoY (statistics page) stays above 4% after the buyback reauthorization, i.e. dilution persists.
