# OMV Aktiengesellschaft (VIE:OMV) — Core initiation — 2026-11-16
## 1. Business
Austrian integrated oil and gas group: upstream oil and gas (Romania Neptun Deep project in development), refining and fuels marketing, and chemicals (polyolefins, including the Borouge International stake). Earnings track commodity prices and refining indicator margins. Country revenue split: [data unavailable]; the company site was not on the allowlist, so no foreign-country share is confirmed. [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/vie/OMV/)

## 2. Quality
Average, cyclical business, not a compounder. ROIC fell from 11.9% (FY2021) to 5.3% (FY2025) and is back at 10.7% TTM; FCF margin 9.0% TTM vs 2.9% a year earlier (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/vie/OMV/financials/ratios/?p=trailing). [Certain] The recovery is price-driven: the Q3 call cites higher energy prices and exceptional refining margins, offsetting weaker Chemicals and disrupted Middle East liftings (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/vie/OMV/transcripts/632415-q3-2026/). [Likely] Operating margin is 17.4% TTM vs 18.3% a year ago, so there is no structural margin gain. Net debt/EBITDA is 0.66x, Piotroski F 7, Altman Z 2.00 (weak). No evident moat beyond integration scale; the 5-year ROIC trend is falling and the last-12-month rise is cyclical. [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/vie/OMV/statistics/)

## 3. Valuation
P/E 7.6x is at the 30% position of its 5y range and 22% below peers; EV/EBITDA 4.4x is at 96% of range and in line with peers; P/FCF 9.4x is 17% above peers; FCF yield is 10.60%. [Certain] The FCF yield and P/E are on peak-cycle TTM earnings, so they flatter. [Likely] Mechanical DCF base is 93.41 EUR vs a last close of 73.35 (+27.3%), but the range is wide (bear 26.36, bull 135.39) and the SOTP/NAV methods are unavailable, so it is one method only. Its reliability is limited. [Guessing] The reverse DCF implies -9.7% year-1 revenue growth, i.e. the price assumes a fade from current conditions. Price is +16.1% above the 200-day average with 12-month change +49.8%. (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/vie/OMV/statistics/)
