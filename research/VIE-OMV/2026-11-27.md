## 2. Quality
Average, cyclical; not a compounder. ROIC fell from 11.9% (FY2021) to 5.3% (FY2025) and is 11.0% TTM; FCF margin is 9.0% TTM vs 2.9% a year earlier (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/vie/OMV/financials/ratios/?p=trailing). [Certain] The recovery is price-driven: the Q3 call cites higher energy prices and exceptional refining margins, offsetting weaker Chemicals and disrupted Middle East liftings (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/vie/OMV/transcripts/632415-q3-2026/). [Likely] Operating margin is 17.4% TTM vs 18.3% a year ago: no structural gain. Net debt/EBITDA 0.66x, Piotroski F 7, Altman Z 2.00 (weak) (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/vie/OMV/statistics/). [Certain] No evident moat beyond integration scale; the 5-year trend is falling and the 12-month rise is cyclical. [Likely]
