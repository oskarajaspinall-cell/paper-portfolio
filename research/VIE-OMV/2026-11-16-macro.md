**Facts:** Brent was $92.14 on 2026-11-11, vs $129.95 on 2026-10-12 and $93.26 on 2026-08-11 (FRED DCOILBRENTEU, https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=DCOILBRENTEU). The spike has fully reversed. The note says TTM ROIC (10.7%) and FCF margin (9.0%) rose on this spike and on exceptional refining margins. [Certain]

**Pricing (inference):** the reverse DCF implies -9.7% year-1 revenue growth, so a fade is partly priced, but the price is still +49.8% over 12 months. Refining margins are not in this snapshot. [Guessing]

**Bull:** Middle East disruption persists, Brent and margins re-spike, FCF holds near 9% of sales. **Bear:** normalisation takes the FCF margin toward 3-6% (the thesis-break zone in note section 4) and the multiple de-rates from 4.4x EV/EBITDA (96% of its 5y range).

**Tilt:** toward bear, small. TTM figures still carry the spike, so Q4/Q1 prints are at risk against that base. This is a commodity call, not a rates call.

**Country risk:** no foreign share confirmed (revenue split [data unavailable]); Romania and Middle East exposures are named, nothing verified.
