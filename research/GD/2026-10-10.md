# General Dynamics Corporation (GD) — Core initiation — 2026-10-10

## 1. Business
GD is a diversified US defense/aerospace prime across four segments: Aerospace (Gulfstream business jets plus Jet Aviation services, 25% of FY2025 revenue), Marine Systems (Electric Boat nuclear submarines, Bath Iron Works/NASSCO surface ships, 32%), Combat Systems (Abrams tanks, Stryker vehicles, munitions, 17%) and Technologies (GDIT IT services, Mission Systems C5ISR, 26%) [Certain] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/40533/000004053326000006/gd-20251231.htm).

## 2. Quality
Marine Systems and Combat Systems are durable, sole-source government franchises: Electric Boat is the Navy's sole prime/lead shipyard for nuclear-powered submarines, with the Columbia-class program alone worth more than $125bn of Navy program value; Land Systems is the sole-source producer of Abrams tanks and Strykers [Certain] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/40533/000004053326000006/gd-20251231.htm). Multi-decade backlogs (Virginia/Columbia run to 2034+) and a ~3,000-supplier submarine ecosystem create switching costs rivals cannot replicate [Likely]. ROIC and ROCE have risen over five years (ROIC 13.0%→14.6% TTM; ROCE 12.1%→13.7% TTM) while net debt/EBITDA fell from 2.20x to 0.78x, showing improving capital discipline [Certain] (fact sheet). Operating margin has drifted down (-1.1pp to 10.4% TTM), reflecting Gulfstream/IT-services mix [Certain] (fact sheet). CEO succession is orderly: insider President Danny Deep succeeds Phebe Novakovic as CEO on 1 Jan 2027, with Novakovic becoming Executive Chairman, limiting strategy-continuity risk [Likely] (source: https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/deep-elected-ceo-of-general-dynamics-novakovic-to-executive-chairman-302901643.html, per fact sheet).

## 3. Valuation
GD trades at a premium to its four-peer median (LMT, NOC, RTX, HII) on P/E (20.2x vs 17.3x, +17%) and EV/EBITDA (14.3x vs 12.7x, +13%), but at a discount on P/B (-12%) and P/FCF (13.9x vs 18.7x peer median, -26%) — 59% below its own 5-year P/FCF minimum [Certain] (fact sheet); FCF yield is 7.19% [Certain]. The stock has lagged: -4.4% over 12 months vs SPY +15.7% (a 20.1pp gap), below its 50- and 200-day averages with RSI 30.6, suggesting these concerns are priced in [Likely] (fact sheet). The reverse DCF implies just 0.2% year-1 growth fading to terminal — a low bar given backlog visibility [Likely]. Base-case fair value of $392.42 implies +18.5% upside from the $331.27 close, with bear/bull of -28.1%/+38.2% [Certain] (fact sheet). The P/FCF discount looks more informative than the P/E premium, given improving FCF conversion (TTM 143.4%) and deleveraging [Likely].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Operating margin (income statement, stockanalysis.com) falls below 9% for two consecutive quarters, reversing the recent stabilization
- Net debt/EBITDA (balance sheet + income statement, stockanalysis.com) rises back above 1.6x, signaling re-leveraging
- ROIC (ratios page, stockanalysis.com) falls below 11%, reversing the five-year improving trend
- A disclosed multi-year delay, cost overrun or cancellation on the Columbia-class or Virginia-class submarine program (10-K/earnings release), impairing the core Marine Systems franchise

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
TTM FCF margin is 11.7%, well above the FY2025 level of 7.5%, with FCF conversion (FCF/NI) of 143.4% TTM, indicating earnings are converting to cash more efficiently than in recent years [Certain] (fact sheet). Net debt/EBITDA has fallen to 0.78x from 2.20x five years ago, giving balance-sheet capacity for buybacks, dividends and bolt-on investment without straining the capital structure [Certain] (fact sheet). Shares outstanding fell -0.30% year-over-year, a modest buyback rather than large-scale repurchase [Certain] (fact sheet).

## 6. Unusual items
CEO succession announced 2026-10-07: Danny Deep (current President) becomes CEO effective 1 Jan 2027; Phebe Novakovic becomes Executive Chairman [Likely] (source: https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/deep-elected-ceo-of-general-dynamics-novakovic-to-executive-chairman-302901643.html). An insider promotion with a defined handover date limits disruption risk; nothing in the disclosure points to a strategy change. Short interest is low at 1.06% of float and falling, and institutional ownership is high at 81.1%, consistent with a stable, non-contested shareholder base [Certain] (fact sheet).
