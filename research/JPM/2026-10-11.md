# JPMorgan Chase & Co. (JPM) — Core initiation — 2026-10-11
## 1. Business
JPMorgan is the largest U.S. bank by assets, classified Banks - Diversified (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/jpm/) [Certain]. It earns net interest income on loans/deposits and fees from trading, underwriting, payments, card and asset/wealth management across consumer and institutional clients [Likely].

## 2. Quality
ROE is high and broadly stable: 16.9%/12.8%/16.0%/17.4%/16.1% FY21-FY25, 17.8% TTM, roughly flat over five years (-0.7pp) (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/jpm/financials/ratios/) [Certain]. Net margin is high at 34.9% TTM but has drifted down 3.0pp over five years (36.9%→34.9%) (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/jpm/financials/income-statement/) [Certain]. As a global systemically important bank, scale, deposit funding cost advantages, regulatory capital barriers to entry and revenue diversification across consumer, markets, banking and wealth management support durable returns [Likely], though these are not quantified on the fact sheet. The Piotroski F-Score is low at 2 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/jpm/statistics/) [Certain] — a mechanical weak signal, though this test is a poor fit for a structurally leveraged bank balance sheet and should not be read as deteriorating fundamentals on its own [Likely]. Institutional ownership is high at 75.0% and short interest low at 0.91% of float (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/jpm/statistics/) [Certain], consistent with a widely-owned, low-controversy large-cap.

## 3. Valuation
P/E of 14.3x sits at the 74th percentile of its five-year range (10.1x-15.8x), 8% above the peer median of 13.2x (BAC, WFC, GS, MS) (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/jpm/financials/ratios/) [Certain]. P/B of 2.5x is above the entire five-year range (max 2.4x) and 25% above the peer median of 2.0x (source: same) [Certain]. The fact sheet's own-history fair value model gives base fair value of $274.70 (-17.5% vs the $332.99 last close) and bull-case $320.88, still -3.6% below last close (source: fact sheet fair value table) [Certain]. Price exceeds even the model's most optimistic scenario [Likely]. Price is -4.9% below the 50-day MA, +3.4% above the 200-day MA, RSI 38.3: a cool-off inside an intact uptrend, not a valuation signal (source: fact sheet price table) [Certain].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROE (ratios page) falls below 13%, near the FY2022 five-year trough of 12.8%, for two consecutive quarters.
- Net margin (income statement page) falls below 30%, extending the five-year downtrend from 36.9% toward credit-cycle-type compression.
- Shares outstanding (statistics page) stop shrinking and turn to net issuance, reversing the current -3.56% YoY buyback-driven reduction and signalling weaker capital-return discipline.
- Piotroski F-Score (statistics page) stays at or below 2 for consecutive quarters alongside a falling ROE, confirming a genuine (not just mechanical) quality deterioration.
