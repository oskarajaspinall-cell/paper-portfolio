# Oscar Health (OSCR) — Core initiation — 2026-11-15
## 1. Business
US health insurer selling ACA individual-market plans on federal and state exchanges; substantially all revenue is member premiums, so revenue tracks membership, risk-adjusted pricing and medical cost ratio. About 2.7 million effectuated members at 30 Sep 2026, up about 27% YoY [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1568651/000156865126000086/oscr-20260930.htm). No material foreign revenue [Likely]. Healthcare Plans industry (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/oscr/).

## 2. Quality
Not yet a proven-quality business. ROIC was negative in four of the last five fiscal years and TTM ROIC is 33.8% after -27.6% in FY2025 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/oscr/financials/ratios/). Latest TTM versus a year earlier is +46.6pp (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/oscr/financials/ratios/?p=trailing). That swing is cyclical underwriting recovery, not a durable return stream [Likely]. Moat is thin: ACA plans are price-driven and heavily regulated, and the real edge is a full-stack tech platform and a pricing and risk-adjustment discipline that has worked for one year [Guessing]. Membership grew while the market shrank after enhanced subsidies expired [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1568651/000156865126000086/oscr-20260930.htm). Management raised 2026 guidance at Q3 results (source: https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20261105823197/en/Oscar-Health-Announces-Financial-Results-for-Third-Quarter-2026-and-Raises-Full-Year-2026-Outlook/) [Likely]. Piotroski F is 7 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/oscr/statistics/).

## 3. Valuation
P/E is 11.7x vs peer median 23.3x (-50%); EV/EBITDA 5.9x vs 13.7x (-57%); P/FCF 2.3x vs 15.1x (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/oscr/statistics/). P/B is 4.3x, above its own 5y max of 3.9x, and 84% above peers; FCF yield is 42.98%. The market is not paying for a durable earnings level: it prices trough-like multiples on peak-like TTM profit, which implies it expects margins to mean-revert [Likely]. The FCF yield is inflated by insurer working-capital timing (claims reserves, risk-adjustment payables) and is not a reliable cash-return measure [Likely]. Beta 2.49: very volatile.

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- TTM ROIC (ratios page, ?p=trailing) falls below 10%.

- FY operating earnings guidance (earnings release, Business Wire) is cut at any quarterly update.
- Net debt/EBITDA on the fact sheet's debt − cash definition rises above 3.0x.

## 6. Unusual items
- Regulation is the dominant risk. The 10-Q added language that expired enhanced subsidies, income verification rules in the 2027 payment notice, new identity proofing for enrollment from 12 Oct 2026, and a new lawsuit (Columbus II) challenging reintroduced provisions could shrink the market and worsen morbidity [Certain] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1568651/000156865126000086/oscr-20260930.htm). CMS member-eligibility inquiries can remove members and trigger premium refunds [company-reported] (same filing).
