# Macro overlay: T. Rowe Price Group, Inc. (TROW) — 2026-10-08
## Assessment
TROW's revenue is fees on AUM (~38% equity, ~33% fixed income, 31.6% target-date) [Certain] (fact sheet/10-K), so market levels and discount rates move both earnings and the multiple on this high-beta (1.47) [Certain] stock. **Chain**: real yields -> bond (and, via discount rate, equity) price levels -> AUM mark-to-market -> fee revenue; and risk-free rate -> discount rate/equity risk premium -> TROW's own multiple, already the cheapest in peer set.

**Facts**: 10y real yield (DFII10) 2.91% on 2026-10-06, +0.48pp 1m / +0.61pp 3m; 10y nominal (DGS10) 5.27%, +0.72pp 3m; breakeven inflation (T10YIE) 2.36%, +0.11pp 3m; fed funds (DFF) 3.88%; HY credit spread (BAMLH0A0HYM2) 3.03%, +0.36pp 3m; VIX 15.01, -1.12pp 3m (all FRED, fred.stlouisfed.org, 2026-10-06/07).

**Pricing (inference)**: nearly flat breakevens alongside a sharp real-yield rise means the market is pricing higher real rates/term premium, not an inflation surprise [Likely]. 2y yield (4.79%) sitting ~0.9pp above effective fed funds (proxy for the implied path) suggests the market expects no near-term easing [Likely]. Low VIX and contained credit spreads show risk appetite still benign despite the yield move — equity markets (SPY +17.9% 6m, factsheet) have absorbed it so far [Likely]. TROW's own -17.3pp 3m/12m underperformance vs SPY looks driven by stock-specific flows/passive-share concerns, not macro [Likely] (**inference**).

**Bull**: yields plateau/ease, equities hold gains -> AUM grows, multiple re-rates toward peers. **Bear**: real yields keep climbing with credit spreads widening -> fixed-income AUM marks down, equity risk premium rises, multiple compresses further on top of outflows.
