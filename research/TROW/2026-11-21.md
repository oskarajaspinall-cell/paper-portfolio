# T. Rowe Price Group (TROW) — Core initiation — 2026-11-21
## 1. Business
Active asset manager, mainly US mutual funds, target-date retirement products, collective trusts, ETFs and SMAs. About 90% of net revenues are investment advisory fees on average assets under management, so revenue tracks markets and net flows [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1113169/000111316926000005/trow-20260930.htm). No single foreign country is disclosed as ~10%+ of revenue in the sources read [Likely].

## 2. Quality
Profitable and cash-generative with a net cash balance sheet: TTM ROIC 21.0%, operating margin 34.2%, net debt/EBITDA -0.96x (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/trow/financials/ratios/). The last 12 months improved (ROIC +1.7pp, operating margin +2.5pp, FCF margin +4.5pp; https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/trow/financials/ratios/?p=trailing) against a 5-year fall in all of them from the FY2021 peak: that peak was a market-driven high, and current levels are near the FY2024-25 range [Likely]. The moat is narrow: brand and retirement-plan distribution, but active management faces fee pressure and passive shift [Likely]. Q3 2026 AUM was $1,862.7 billion with net outflows of $28.3 billion; target-date outflows were $6.5 billion [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1113169/000111316926000005/trow-20260930.htm). Outflows are the central weakness; management expects them to stay elevated [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/trow/transcripts/721330-q3-2026/). Margins held because markets lifted average AUM [Certain].

## 3. Valuation
The price is 100.99 USD, down 10.0% over 3 months and 17% below the 52-week high of 122.00 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/trow/history/). P/E 10.2x and EV/EBITDA 6.1x are below their 5-year minimums (11.0x and 7.2x), and 37% and 49% below peers. FCF yield is 7.42%. The reverse DCF implies -0.7% revenue growth in year 1, i.e. continued outflows are largely priced in [Likely]. Mechanical base fair value is 120.41 (+19.2%), bear 89.48, bull 164.73 (fact sheet fair value section). P/B 1.9x sits at the bottom of its range. The discount fits a business with shrinking client assets, so cheapness alone is not the case; it depends on outflows stabilising [Likely]. Price is 2.9% below the 200-day average, so the trend is not intact.

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Net outflows (company monthly AUM release, troweprice.com) worse than $40 billion in a quarter, or AUM below $1,700 billion at a quarter-end.
- Operating margin TTM (income statement, trailing page) falls below 30%.
- ROIC TTM (ratios page, trailing) falls below 15%.
- FCF margin TTM (cash flow statement, trailing page) falls below 16%.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Net cash, shares down 2.44% year on year (buybacks), and a quarterly dividend declared 2026-11-04 (source: https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/t-rowe-price-group-inc-declares-quarterly-dividend-302929875.html). FCF conversion is 73.1% of net income TTM, below the 104.2% of FY2021 but recovering from 50.9% in FY2023. Capital returns look covered by FCF [Likely].
