# T. Rowe Price Group, Inc. (TROW) — Core initiation — 2026-10-08

## 1. Business
T. Rowe Price is an active asset manager earning advisory fees as a % of AUM: $1,775.6bn at FY2025-end, up $169.0bn YoY on +$216.7bn of market gains offset by $56.9bn of net outflows [Certain] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1113169/000162828026008002/trow-20251231.htm). Revenue mix is ~55% U.S. mutual funds, the rest CITs, subadvised funds, SMAs and alternatives; AUM is ~38% equity, ~33% fixed income, ~17% multi-asset, ~12% alternatives [Certain] (same source). Target-date funds are 31.6% of AUM [Certain] (same source).

## 2. Quality
Returns are high in absolute terms but structurally declining: ROIC fell from 35.5% (FY2021) to 21.6% TTM, ROE from 31.7% to 19.4%, net margin from 40.2% to 29.3%, each falling every year of the 5y window [Certain] (source: fact sheet ratios/income-statement). FCF conversion (FCF/NI) dropped to 75.3% TTM from >100% in FY2021 [Certain]. The 10-K itself concedes the moat is eroding: "investment advisors that emphasize passive products have gained and may continue to gain market share from active managers like us," while FY2025 saw $56.9bn of net outflows against $216.7bn of market-driven AUM growth [Certain] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1113169/000162828026008002/trow-20251231.htm). Brand, an 85-year track record and scale support pricing power versus smaller active peers, but do not protect share against passive products — a real competitive threat, not a durable moat [Likely]. The balance sheet is net cash at -0.94x EBITDA, a quality buffer [Certain] (source: fact sheet ratios page).

## 3. Valuation
At 10.4x P/E and 6.6x EV/EBITDA, TROW trades below its own 5-year range (5y min 11.0x and 7.2x) and at roughly half the peer median (22.1x P/E, 12.8x EV/EBITDA; peers BEN, IVZ, AMG, BLK) [Certain] (source: fact sheet valuation table). P/FCF (13.3x) and P/B (2.0x) sit closer to own history (5y median 15.1x, 2.3x) but still a 55%/6% discount to peers [Certain]. FCF yield is 7.54% [Certain] (source: statistics page). Part of the discount is structural: BLK's scale/ETF franchise and alternatives platforms at BEN/AMG command premium multiples a pure active long-only manager with persistent outflows cannot [Likely]. The price assumes continued AUM erosion and margin compression consistent with the 5y trend above, not stabilization; it does not appear to price a scenario where outflows moderate or the ETF/SMA build-out (F/m Investments deal, announced 2026-08-20) offsets legacy active redemptions [Guessing].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROIC (ratios page) falls below 15%, through its FY2023 trough of 16.5%, confirming returns on capital no longer clearly exceed peers' cost of capital.
- Net margin (income statement) falls below 25%, through its 5y low of 24.0% (FY2022), confirming fee/cost pressure has overwhelmed scale benefits.
- FCF conversion, FCF/NI (cash-flow statement & income statement) falls below 50%, through its FY2023 low of 50.9%, signaling deteriorating cash quality of earnings.
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) turns positive (currently -0.94x net cash), showing the firm has levered up to fund buybacks or M&A rather than funding them from its net-cash position.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
FCF margin fell from 41.9% (FY2021) to 22.0% TTM, and FCF conversion (FCF/NI) from 104.2% to 75.3%, tracking the margin compression in Section 2 rather than a working-capital deterioration [Certain] (source: fact sheet cash-flow/income-statement). The balance sheet remains net cash at -0.94x EBITDA, leaving room to fund the F/m Investments acquisition — announced 2026-08-20 to expand fixed-income ETF/SMA capabilities — and continued buybacks/dividends without leverage [Certain] (source: fact sheet ratios page; headline: https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/t-rowe-price-to-expand-fixed-income-etf-and-sma-capabilities-by-acquiring-fm-investments-302856818.html). Shares outstanding fell 2.09% YoY, consistent with ongoing buybacks [Certain] (source: statistics page).

## 6. Unusual items
Short interest is elevated at 13.12% of float and still rising (+4.6% month-on-month); RSI (14-day) is neutral at 41.5 [Certain] (source: statistics page). Beta of 1.47 is high for an asset manager, so the stock amplifies broader market/AUM-correlated drawdowns [Certain] (source: statistics page). Institutional ownership is high at 90.0% [Certain] (source: statistics page). None is disqualifying alone, but position size should reflect the stock's elevated market-beta sensitivity.
