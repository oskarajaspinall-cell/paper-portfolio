# L3Harris Technologies, Inc. (LHX) — Core initiation — 2026-10-12

## 1. Business
Defense and technology contractor generating over $21.9bn revenue across three segments, serving government/military customers in 100+ countries (source: https://www.l3harris.com/) [Certain]. Revenue comes from long-cycle, often sole-source programs spanning tactical communications, space/ISR systems, and missile/munitions production (counter-UAS, missile-defense propulsion, space-based missile warning) [Certain] (source: https://www.l3harris.com/).

## 2. Quality
Returns are improving: ROIC rose to 8.1% TTM from 6.3% FY2021, ROCE to 8.6% from 7.0% (source: ratios page) [Certain]. FCF/NI conversion is 149.8% TTM (+40pp in 5y); FCF margin held near 12.2% despite gross margin compressing 4.5pp to 25.5% and net margin falling 3.0pp to 8.2% (source: income statement, cash-flow statement) [Certain]. Management targets 16% operating margin (now 12.9%, +1.1pp in 5y) via mix shift to production and scale efficiencies [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/lhx/transcripts/738873-jefferies-global-industrials-conference-2026/). The moat is proprietary technology in long, qualified programs: open-architecture radios, a counter-UAS system (Wraith Shield) said to resist cheap commercial jamming, and prime status on all five Golden Dome satellite tranches — high switching cost once qualified [Likely] (source: https://www.l3harris.com/; https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/lhx/transcripts/743383-morgan-stanley-s-14th-annual-laguna-conference/). Net debt/EBITDA is elevated and rising over 5y (2.68x FY2025, 2.29x TTM); management calls leverage "slightly elevated versus peers" [Certain]/[Likely] (source: ratios page; Laguna transcript).

## 3. Valuation
LHX trades near the bottom of its own 5y multiple range on every metric but P/E: EV/EBITDA 12.9x is below its 5y minimum (13.2x), P/FCF 15.7x below minimum (17.8x), P/B 2.2x at the 21st percentile, vs P/E 23.9x at the 9th percentile of its 22.7x-37.3x range (source: ratios/statistics pages). Versus the RTX/NOC/GD/LMT peer median, LHX is cheaper on EV/EBITDA (-8%), P/FCF (-4%) and materially cheaper on P/B (-42%, 2.2x vs 3.8x), but pricier on P/E (+23%) and EV/Sales (+27%) — the P/E premium reflects its lower net margin. FCF yield is 6.36%. Blended base fair value is $270.27 vs last close $236.97 (+14.1%); bear/bull $170.48/-28.1% and $310.34/+31.0% (source: fact sheet fair-value section). The reverse-DCF implies the market prices only 3.2% year-1 growth fading to terminal — a bar the missile-capacity and space backlog commentary suggests is beatable [Likely], though the discount also reflects leverage and margin risk.

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Operating margin (income statement) fails to progress and stays below 13% for three or more consecutive quarters, undermining the path to management's 16% target.
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) rises above 3.0x (vs 2.29x TTM) without a stated deleveraging plan, signaling capital discipline has slipped.
- ROIC or ROCE (ratios page) reverses its five-year uptrend and falls back toward FY2021 levels (6.3% / 7.0%).
- Loss of prime/sole-source status on a major program (e.g., a Golden Dome tranche or missile-propulsion contract), reported in a filing or company release.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Management's stated hierarchy is: (1) business investment — R&D and a $2bn+ missile-capacity expansion; (2) buybacks ($525m in H1 2026); (3) debt reduction (~$1.8bn of maturing debt against elevated leverage); (4) dividends (24-year growth streak, targeting "dividend aristocrat" status); (5) M&A, explicitly de-prioritized [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/lhx/transcripts/743383-morgan-stanley-s-14th-annual-laguna-conference/). Shares outstanding fell only 0.82% YoY, so buybacks are modest net of dilution (source: statistics page). FCF conversion of 149.8% TTM means cash funds this hierarchy without relying on further leverage, consistent with management's stated priority of paying down debt first [Likely].

## 6. Unusual items
Leadership transition: a new CEO took over recently; management and the outgoing CFO publicly framed it as continuity, not restructuring [Likely] (source: Laguna transcript, 2026-09-16). Short interest rose 28.2% month-over-month but remains low in absolute terms (2.42% of float) — a sentiment data point, not a fundamental one [Certain] (source: statistics page). A law firm (SBS Law) issued a press release soliciting investors for a "fraud investigation" (2026-08-30); this is a generic plaintiff-firm solicitation following the share-price decline, not a disclosed regulatory or accounting finding, and no fact-sheet figure corroborates an accounting issue [Guessing] (source: https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260830994316/en/LHX-Investors-Have-Opportunity-to-Join-L3Harris-Technologies-Inc.-Fraud-Investigation-with-SBS-Law/).
