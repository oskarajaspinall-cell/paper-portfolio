# Macro overlay: M&T Bank Corporation (MTB) — 2026-10-09
## Assessment
Chain: policy-rate path and long yields → net interest margin and funding cost / AFS securities marks (AOCI, hits book value) → earnings and book value → P/E, especially P/B multiple (MTB's P/B of 1.2x already sits above its own 5y max of 1.1x per the fact sheet, leaving little cushion). [Likely] **(inference)**

Facts (FRED, https://fred.stlouisfed.org/, data to 2026-10-07/08): the 10y Treasury yield (DGS10) is 5.28%, +0.72pp over 3 months; the 10y real yield (DFII10) is 2.92%, +0.61pp; the effective fed funds rate (DFF) is 3.88%, +0.26pp; the high-yield credit spread (BAMLH0A0HYM2) is 3.09%, +0.39pp but still well below stress levels; VIX is 15.08, calm and falling. [Certain]

Market pricing (proxy, not fact): the 2y Treasury yield (4.77%) sits ~0.89pp above effective fed funds (3.88%), implying the market prices further tightening ahead rather than cuts — a reversal from the cutting-cycle narrative priced earlier this year. [Likely] **(inference)** Breakevens are flat (T10YIE 2.35%, -0.02pp 1m), so the yield rise looks like real-rate/term-premium repricing, not an inflation scare.

What hurts MTB: further rate rises pressure funding costs and deepen unrealized losses on its securities book (AOCI), while the research note's flagged risk — rising criticized CRE/office loans — is the kind of exposure that would show up first as HY spreads widen from today's tight 3.09%. What helps: rates stabilizing or falling would support NIM without the funding-cost offset and ease the AOCI/book-value drag, letting the stock re-rate toward its FCFE-based fair value range.
