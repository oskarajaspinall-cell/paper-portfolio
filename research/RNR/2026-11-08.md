# RenaissanceRe Holdings Ltd. (RNR) — Core initiation — 2026-11-08
## 1. Business
Bermuda-based property-catastrophe and casualty/specialty reinsurer. It earns underwriting profit on premiums, fee income from managing third-party capital vehicles (DaVinci, Fontana, Upsilon), and investment income on float [Certain] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/913144/000091314426000098/rnr-20260930.htm). ## 2. Quality
Returns are cyclical, not stable. ROIC went from -9.9% (FY2022) to 22.9% (FY2023), 16.8% (FY2024), 18.1% (FY2025) and 13.1% TTM; ROE is 14.0% TTM (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/rnr/financials/ratios/) [Certain]. TTM ROIC is 11.5pp below its 2-year high of 24.5% (2024-09-30), although it is +0.7pp on a year earlier (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/rnr/financials/ratios/?p=trailing). The fall from peak is cycle normalisation: Q3 premiums earned fell on property rate reductions, and reduced exposure plus more retrocession in casualty [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/913144/000091314426000098/rnr-20260930.htm) [Likely].
Moat: catastrophe-modelling skill, broker relationships and the fee-earning capital-partner model [Likely]. This is a skill edge in a commodity-priced, rate-cycle business; it is not a durable structural moat [Likely]. Q3 2026 had a net loss from large-loss events and mark-to-market losses as yields rose; the combined ratio was 79.7% [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/913144/000091314426000098/rnr-20260930.htm). Underlying underwriting stayed profitable [Certain]. Piotroski F is 4 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/rnr/statistics/).

## 3. Valuation
P/E is 9.7x against a 5y median of 5.0x (max 7.2x) and a peer median of 8.4x, +17% vs peers (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/rnr/statistics/) [Certain]. P/B is 1.3x against a 5y range of 0.7x–0.8x, in line with the peer median of 1.3x [Certain]. The 5y fiscal-year-end range looks low against recent price history, so I give it little weight [Guessing]. TTM earnings are depressed by the Q3 mark-to-market loss [Likely]. EV/EBITDA, P/FCF and FCF yield are [data unavailable].
Model fair value is 469.06 / 528.15 / 699.12 (bear/base/bull) against a close of 347.35. P/B+ROE gives 703.55, assuming the sustainable ROE holds; P/E gives 177.36, far below the price. The methods disagree widely, so the base case rests on the ROE assumption [Certain]. The price assumes a soft-market ROE in the low teens, near TTM, with no collapse, and no clear discount to peers [Likely].
