# Giant Network Group Co., Ltd. (SHE:002558) — Core initiation — 2026-10-17
## 1. Business
Giant Network develops, operates and publishes online games (PC and mobile) in China and abroad, plus mobile-internet products, earning virtual-item and subscription revenue, historically led by its long-running Zhengtu MMORPG franchise [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/she/002558/). Revenue is hit-driven: operating margin jumped from a 24.2-28.3% FY2021-24 band to 46.9% TTM, consistent with one or two recent titles dominating sales rather than a broad-based improvement [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/she/002558/financials/income-statement/).

## 2. Quality
Returns have surged: ROIC rose from a 6.5-8.3% FY2021-24 band to 16.4% (FY2025) and 33.7% (TTM); ROCE and operating margin show the same pattern (operating margin 24.2%→46.9% TTM) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/she/002558/financials/ratios/; https://stockanalysis.com/quote/she/002558/financials/income-statement/). Net margin tells a different story: it fell FY2021-25 (46.8%→34.8%, -12.1pp) even as TTM (38.7%) ticked back up, implying volatile below-operations items (likely non-operating gains/losses) periodically distort net income versus the operating business [Likely] (same source). The moat is thin: Chinese PC/mobile gaming has low switching costs and depends on continual hit content, so a recent return surge driven by one or two launches is not evidence of a structural edge [Likely]. Balance-sheet quality is a genuine strength: net cash exceeds EBITDA by 1.9-4.3x across all five years, Altman Z is 13.48 (no distress) and Piotroski F is 7/9 [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/she/002558/financials/ratios/; https://stockanalysis.com/quote/she/002558/statistics/).

## 3. Valuation
At 20.95 CNY, P/E (12.7x) sits below its own 5y range floor (16.5x) and the peer median (81.1x, n=4) [Certain], but that peer median looks skewed by a depressed-earnings peer; EV/EBITDA (8.5x) is a cleaner cross-check, in line with the peer median (9.0x) yet 69% below Giant's own 5y range minimum (21.5x) [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/she/002558/financials/ratios/; peer statistics pages). FCF yield is 10.6% [Certain]. Base fair value of 40.28 CNY implies +92.3% upside, bear 10.97 (-47.6%), bull 55.85 (+166.6%); the reverse DCF shows the price assumes revenue falls -4.9% in year 1, fading to terminal growth, i.e. the market is treating the TTM margin surge as a peak to be fully reversed [Likely] (source: fact sheet fair-value section). That is a harsh assumption even allowing for the quality caveats above, leaving a wide margin of safety if any of the recent improvement holds [Likely].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Revenue growth (income statement) decelerates to flat or negative for two consecutive quarters, showing the TTM margin surge was a single-title spike, not durable.
- Gross margin (income statement) falls below 85%, below the FY2021-24 band, signalling a shift away from the current high-margin mix.
- ROIC (ratios page) retreats back into its FY2021-24 range (6.5-8.3%), erasing the FY2025/TTM improvement.
- Net cash position deteriorates: net debt/EBITDA (balance sheet, ratios page) rises above -1.0x, showing cash being consumed faster than earnings growth.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Shares outstanding rose 3.20% year over year — mild dilution [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/she/002558/statistics/). Cash conversion is strong: TTM FCF margin is 52.1% and FCF exceeds net income (134.8% conversion), funded from operations rather than debt, given the net-cash balance sheet [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/she/002558/financials/cash-flow-statement/; https://stockanalysis.com/quote/she/002558/financials/ratios/). Durability of this cash generation is unproven beyond the current hit cycle and should be checked each quarter.
