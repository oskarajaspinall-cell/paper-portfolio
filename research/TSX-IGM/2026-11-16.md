# IGM Financial (TSX:IGM) — Core initiation — 2026-11-16
## 1. Business
Canadian wealth and asset manager. It earns recurring fees on client assets (advisory wealth through IG Wealth Management, plus Mackenzie Investments asset management), so revenue tracks assets under management and net flows. It also holds a stake in Wealthsimple, a fintech platform. [Likely] Revenue is overwhelmingly Canadian, so no foreign-country share is material. [Guessing] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tsx/IGM/)

## 2. Quality
A good business [Likely]: TTM ROIC 14.9% and operating margin 41.9%, both at 2-year highs, FCF conversion 91.2% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tsx/IGM/financials/ratios/?p=trailing). ROIC is down over five fiscal years (16.6% to 13.7%) but rising over the last 12 months (12.6% to 14.9%). The recovery coincides with operating-margin expansion [Likely]. FCF margin is 24.9%, still 2.5pp below its 2024 high of 27.4%. Net debt/EBITDA is 0.60x, a light balance sheet [Certain]. Moat: scale and sticky advisor-client relationships with high switching friction [Likely]. Management cites record flows and cost savings reinvested in AI (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tsx/IGM/transcripts/663772-q2-2026/) [Likely]. Fees are market-sensitive: an equity drawdown cuts earnings [Certain]. Altman Z 2.07 is unhelpful for an asset manager.

## 3. Valuation
The price assumes the improved margins persist. P/E 15.5x, EV/EBITDA 10.3x and P/B 2.1x all sit above their own 5y maximums (13.2x, 9.0x, 1.7x) [Certain]. Against peers it looks cheaper: P/E -20%, P/FCF -32%, P/B -23% below the peer median, though the peers are insurers and a diversified alternatives manager, so the comparison is loose [Likely]. FCF yield is 6.05%. Mechanical fair value: base 74.33 CAD vs close 89.34 CAD (-16.8%), bull 92.53 (+3.6%) [Certain]. The reverse DCF implies 6.8% year-1 revenue growth [Certain]. The stock is up 55.9% over 12 months, 14.4% above its 200-day average, and beats SPY over 3, 6 and 12 months. It is a working business, but the re-rating already prices a lot of the improvement [Likely].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Operating margin (income statement, TTM) falls below 38%.
- ROIC (ratios page, TTM) falls below 12%.
- FCF conversion (FCF/NI, cash-flow and income statements, TTM) falls below 75%.
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) rises above 1.5x.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Shares outstanding fell 1.21% year on year, so buybacks are modest net of issuance [Certain]. Management describes substantial capital returns (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tsx/IGM/transcripts/720321-q3-2026/) [Likely]. Cash conversion is 91.2% of net income (TTM) [Certain]. It is committing further capital to Wealthsimple (source: https://www.nasdaq.com/articles/igm-financial-invest-additional-96-mln-wealthsimple) [Likely]. That is an optional-growth investment whose return is hard to verify [Guessing].

## 6. Unusual items
Short interest is 3.87% of float, with little change [Certain]. Institutions own 13.3% [Certain], which suggests a controlling shareholder (Power Corporation group) [Likely]. Minority holders therefore rely on the parent's governance and related-party discipline [Likely]. The most recent quarter was reported on Nov 6, 2026, with growth in client assets and flows (source: https://www.marketbeat.com/instant-alerts/transcript-what-igm-financial-igm-said-on-its-q3-earnings-call-2026-11-08/) [Likely].
