# Qualys, Inc. (QLYS) — Core initiation — 2026-11-15
## 1. Business
Qualys sells cloud-delivered subscription security and compliance software (vulnerability management, asset inventory, TruRisk risk platform) to enterprises and governments. Revenue is recurring and sold both directly and through channel partners; partners were 55.3% of Q3 2026 revenue [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1107843/000110784326000044/qlys-20260930.htm). The geographic split sits in the 10-Q's segment note, which I could not read, so any single-country exposure of ~10%+ is [data unavailable]. [Certain]

## 2. Quality
A high-quality, cash-rich software franchise. TTM gross margin 83.3%, operating margin 34.5% and FCF margin 45.0% have all risen over five years; ROIC is 107.5% and net debt/EBITDA is -1.80x (net cash) (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/qlys/financials/ratios/). [Certain]
Direction check: ROIC is 45.5pp below its 2-year high but +12.7pp on a year earlier; the 2-year high was a peak year (2024-09-30), so I read the gap as normalisation rather than erosion. ROCE is flat (-0.4pp) and 3.6pp below its high. [Likely]
Moat: embedded in security and compliance workflows with sticky subscriptions and a large partner channel. Still, vulnerability scanning is contested by larger platforms; margin resilience is the evidence. [Likely]
Q3 beat and raised FY26 revenue and EPS guidance (source: https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/qualys-announces-third-quarter-2026-financial-results-302933782.html). [Likely]

## 3. Valuation
Price 172.94 sits at the low end of its own history: P/E 28.8x (9% of 5y range), EV/EBITDA 20.2x (15%), P/FCF 18.6x (20%). FCF yield is 5.37% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/qlys/statistics/). [Certain]
Versus peers it is cheaper on P/E (-48%) and EV/EBITDA (-18%) but dearer on EV/Sales (+63%) and P/FCF (+30%), which reflects its far higher margins. [Certain]
Mechanical fair value: base 154.59 (-10.6% vs close), bear 112.39, bull 202.88. The reverse DCF implies 12.9% year-1 revenue growth, fading, which is demanding against low-double-digit reported growth. [Likely]
Price is +99.9% over 6 months but -11.7% over 3 months and 9.4% below its 50-day average; it is above the 200-day average (+25.7%). A good business in an uptrend that has pulled back, fairly priced rather than cheap. [Likely]

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
FCF conversion is 152.1% of net income, down from earlier years but still strong. Shares fell 3.01% year on year; buybacks were $185.6M in the first nine months of 2026 [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1107843/000110784326000044/qlys-20260930.htm). No debt burden. Stock-based comp is the offset; the buyback is partly absorbing it. [Likely]
