# Vår Energi ASA (OSL:VAR) — Core initiation — 2026-10-17

## 1. Business
Vår Energi is an independent upstream oil & gas producer on the Norwegian Continental Shelf, majority owned by Eni International B.V. [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/osl/VAR/). It earns revenue producing and selling crude oil and natural gas liquids from production, development and exploration licenses [Certain] (same source). In July 2026 it agreed a $1.3bn deal for BlueNord, adding European gas-infrastructure exposure; the deal had not closed as of the September 30, 2026 filing [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/osl/VAR/filings/3417489/).

## 2. Quality
TTM ROIC is 25.6% and ROCE 24.6%, both rising over 5y (+3.2pp / +1.2pp) [Certain] (source: fact sheet, ratios page). Operating margin is 56.2% TTM but net margin only 13.0% — the gap is Norway's petroleum tax wedge, not a cost problem [Likely]. Net debt/EBITDA is low at 0.41x TTM, falling over 5 years [Certain]. FCF margin, still a high 28.0% TTM, has fallen 10.6pp over 5 years and FCF conversion 93.7pp, so cash generation is less consistent than the income statement implies [Certain]. ROE jumped to 94.0% TTM / 121.6% FY2025 from 25-63% in FY2021-24 — too large a swing to be a clean profitability read; more likely a thin, volatile equity base, not to be relied on [Likely]. As a commodity E&P, Vår Energi has no pricing power; its edge is low cost, concentrated NCS licenses and Eni's backing as majority owner [Likely], not a moat that survives a sustained price downturn.

## 3. Valuation
Trailing multiples look cheap: P/E 10.7x vs peer median 18.1x (-41%, 8th percentile of its own 5y range); P/FCF 4.7x vs 13.4x (-65%); EV/EBITDA 2.0x vs 2.8x (-29%); FCF yield 21.3% [Certain] (source: fact sheet ratios/statistics pages). P/B is the outlier at 7.1x vs a 2.2x peer median (+219%), which reflects a small book-equity base rather than overvaluation [Likely]. The fact sheet's own EV/EBITDAX-based fair value (NAV [data unavailable]) is NOK 33.16 base, NOK 17.24 bear, NOK 54.49 bull, against a last close of NOK 55.76 — the market is pricing close to the bull case and 40.5% above base [Certain] (source: fact sheet). That is the key tension: cheap versus peers on current earnings, but the stock's own reverse-DCF read implies today's price already needs sustained current-cycle oil/gas prices and a clean BlueNord integration to be justified [Likely].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) rises above 1.0x, signalling the BlueNord deal or a price downturn is straining the balance sheet.
- ROIC (ratios page) falls back below its 5y average (~13%), showing the current return spike was cyclical, not structural.
- FCF margin (cash flow/income statement) falls below 15%, confirming the 5y declining cash-conversion trend continues.
- Operating margin (income statement) drops below 45%, below its 5y range, indicating cost or volume pressure beyond the tax wedge.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
FCF margin TTM is 28.0% and FCF yield 21.3%, both strong in absolute terms, though both have fallen over the 5y window (FCF margin -10.6pp, FCF conversion -93.7pp) [Certain] (source: fact sheet cash-flow/ratios pages). Net debt/EBITDA of 0.41x TTM leaves room to fund the $1.3bn BlueNord deal without obvious near-term balance-sheet strain, assuming financing terms don't re-rate [Likely] (source: fact sheet balance sheet; https://stockanalysis.com/quote/osl/VAR/filings/3417489/). Dividend/buyback cash amounts beyond this are [data unavailable] on stockanalysis.com; management has only called dividend guidance "robust" on the Q1 2026 call [Guessing] (source: fact sheet "News & sentiment").

## 6. Unusual items
Majority owner is Eni International B.V., with institutional ownership at only 19.9% and insiders 0.76% — a small, concentrated free float that limits minority-holder influence over capital allocation and BlueNord's terms [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/osl/VAR/). The pending $1.3bn BlueNord acquisition is a real integration/financing risk not yet reflected in TTM financials and had not closed as of the September 30, 2026 filing [Likely] (source: fact sheet news; https://stockanalysis.com/quote/osl/VAR/filings/3417489/). Reported 5y beta of -0.48 is an unusual reading for an oil producer; treat as a possible data artefact rather than genuine negative market correlation [Guessing] (source: fact sheet "Other").
