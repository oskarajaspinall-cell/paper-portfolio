Market pricing (proxy): 2y yield (4.77%, +0.61pp/3m) sits ~0.9pp above effective fed funds (3.88%), implying the market still prices further tightening/"higher for longer" **(inference)** [Likely]. The fact sheet's own cost of equity already uses today's 5.10% risk-free rate, so it is in the NOK 33.16 base/54.49 bull fair value; a further rise compresses it more.

No Brent/WTI FRED series exists, so oil-price direction is unconfirmed here [Certain]. The dollar's rise is a historically oil-negative signal **(inference)** [Guessing]. Bull: dollar/yields plateau, fair value drifts toward the NOK 54.49 bull case near the NOK 55.76 close. Bear: dollar/yields keep rising and HY spreads widen, raising BlueNord financing cost, pressuring revenue and the multiple while price already sits above base fair value.
