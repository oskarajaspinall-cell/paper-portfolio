# Eastroc Beverage (Group) Co., Ltd. (SHA:605499) — Core initiation — 2026-10-17

## 1. Business
Eastroc makes and distributes functional beverages in China, led by its "Dongpeng Special Drink" energy drink plus newer electrolyte/hydration lines, sold through an extensive wholesale and retail distribution network with refrigerated point-of-sale placement [Certain]. Revenue is overwhelmingly single-category (energy drinks), scaling via volume growth and geographic expansion beyond its historical southern-China base.

## 2. Quality
Exceptional returns: ROIC 130.2% TTM, ROE 34.7%, ROCE 28.3%, all rising over five years (ROIC +76.2pp) [Certain]. Gross margin 46.6% TTM and operating margin 25.0% TTM, both trending up [Certain]. Net debt/EBITDA is -2.78x — a net-cash balance sheet [Certain]. The moat is brand recognition plus the densest refrigerated point-of-sale network for energy drinks in China, built over two decades, which is costly and slow for rivals (including Red Bull China) to replicate [Likely]. The durability risk: the business remains concentrated in one beverage category while domestic rivals (Want Want, Nongfu Spring, Red Bull China) add energy/hydration lines, and FCF conversion has weakened (123.3% FY2024 to 88.4% TTM) alongside FCF margin falling 2.3pp over five years [Certain] — a sign that working-capital or capex intensity is rising as growth broadens into new products and regions.

## 3. Valuation
At 16.9x TTM P/E and 10.3x EV/EBITDA, the stock trades below its own five-year minimum (31.5x P/E, 24.4x EV/EBITDA) and roughly half the peer median (32.9x P/E, 14.1x EV/EBITDA; peers HKG:9633, SHA:600887, MNST, CELH) [Certain]. P/B of 4.2x is similarly far below its 5y range (11.5x-17.2x) [Certain]. The stock is down 49.1% over 12 months and sits 25.4% below its 200-day average [Certain], so the price is discounting a sharp, durable slowdown in growth and/or margin compression relative to the last five years, not merely a pause. The base-case fair value of CNY140.04 implies +22.6% upside from the last close of CNY114.25; even the bear case (CNY103.71) is only -9.2% below [Certain]. FCF yield of 5.58% is high for a business compounding returns at this rate [Certain].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Gross margin (ratios/income-statement page) falls below 40% for a full fiscal year, signalling lost pricing power to competition.
- ROIC (ratios page) drops below 80% for two consecutive fiscal years, indicating the distribution-network moat is eroding.
- Net margin (income-statement page) falls below 15%, suggesting the single-category concentration risk is crystallising.
- FCF conversion, FCF/NI (cash-flow and income-statement pages), stays below 80% for two consecutive fiscal years, implying working-capital or capex intensity is structurally higher.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
A net-cash balance sheet (net debt/EBITDA -2.78x TTM) gives flexibility [Certain]. FCF margin of 20.1% TTM and FCF yield of 5.58% remain healthy even after the recent dip in conversion (88.4% TTM vs 123.3% FY2024) [Certain]. Shares outstanding rose 6.50% year-over-year [Certain] — a dilution rate worth monitoring, since the fact sheet shows no offsetting buyback; it should be weighed against the net-income growth it funds at the next earnings report.

## 6. Unusual items
Insider/founder ownership is very high at 60.67% versus only 15.2% institutional ownership [Certain] — this aligns management incentives with long-term value but concentrates control, so minority-shareholder influence is correspondingly limited [Likely]. Reported five-year beta of 0.17 is unusually low for this stock's realised volatility and should not be relied on for risk sizing [Certain]; the 2.5% daily ATR and the -49.1% 12-month decline both show material price risk despite the low beta.
