# Eastroc Beverage (Group) Co., Ltd. (SHA:605499) — Core initiation — 2026-10-28
## 1. Business
Eastroc makes and distributes functional beverages in China, led by its energy drink and newer hydration lines, sold through a wholesale and refrigerated point-of-sale network [Likely]. Revenue is concentrated in one category, so growth depends on volume and geographic expansion (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sha/605499/).

## 2. Quality
Reported quality is still excellent: TTM ROIC 130.2%, gross margin 46.6%, operating margin 25.0%, net debt/EBITDA -2.78x (net cash) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sha/605499/financials/ratios/). But TTM ROCE (28.3% vs 51.7% in FY2025) and ROE (34.7% vs 51.6%) dropped sharply [Certain], and FCF conversion slid to 92.4% TTM from 123.3% in FY2024 [Certain]. Shares outstanding rose 6.50% YoY [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sha/605499/statistics/). Why it fell: the fact sheet's only headlines are filing notices, with no event, guidance cut or regulatory action [Certain]. The decline (12-month -53.2%, 3-month momentum -19.6%, relative strength vs SPY -23.7pp over 3 months) is steady and unexplained by any dated catalyst [Certain]. Most likely it is a derating of a growth story as expansion matures and competitors add energy/hydration lines [Guessing]. Moat (brand plus distribution density) is plausible but unverified here [Likely].

## 3. Valuation
P/E 15.3x vs a 5y range of 31.5x–61.0x (median 38.8x) and a peer median of 19.9x; EV/EBITDA 9.1x vs 24.4x–45.9x; P/FCF 16.3x; FCF yield 6.14% [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sha/605499/financials/ratios/). Every multiple is below its 5y minimum. Caveat: the valuation file flags TTM EPS as a peak versus its 5y median, so the P/E-based value uses the 5y average earnings [Certain] (source: research/SHA-605499/2026-10-28-valuation.md). Base fair value 141.43 CNY is +37.0% above the 103.21 close; the bear value of 104.58 is +1.3% above it [Certain]. The reverse DCF implies 7.8% year-1 revenue growth fading to terminal [Certain]. The price therefore assumes a sharp slowdown; whether it is excessive is untestable before results due Oct 29, 2026 [Certain].
