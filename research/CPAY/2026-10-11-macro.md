# Macro overlay: Corpay, Inc. (CPAY) — 2026-10-11
## Assessment
CPAY is a premium-rated compounder (P/E 24.2x, above its own 5y max of 23.5x; EV/EBITDA 12.0x, +66% vs peer median) carrying elevated leverage (net debt/EBITDA 2.70x TTM, 3.23x FY2025) — both make it sensitive to the discount rate and funding costs, even though its business (B2B payment fees, fuel/FX spreads) is not directly cyclical [Likely] **(inference)**.

**Official data**: US 10y real yield (DFII10) 2.87% on 2026-10-08, up +0.44pp over ~1m and +0.56pp over ~3m (https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=DFII10); 10y Treasury (DGS10) 5.22%, +0.42pp/+0.68pp (https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=DGS10); HY credit spread (BAMLH0A0HYM2) 3.15%, +0.48pp/+0.45pp (https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=BAMLH0A0HYM2); broad dollar index (DTWEXBGS) 121.4 on 2026-10-02, +2.86 (~2.4%) over 1m (https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=DTWEXBGS).

**Market pricing (proxy)**: 2y Treasury (4.75%, 2026-10-08) trading above effective fed funds (3.88%) implies the market is pricing further tightening or no near-term cuts, not easing — a hawkish signal relative to a few months ago **(inference)**.

**Chain**: real yields → discount rate → multiple compression, amplified by leverage (financing cost) → equity risk premium. Rising real yields and widening HY spreads are a headwind to CPAY's premium rating and refinancing cost; a reversal would be a tailwind.

**Bull**: real yields/HY spreads roll over → discount rate eases → premium multiple holds/expands. **Bear**: real yields and HY spreads keep rising → multiple compression on an already-stretched valuation, higher interest expense on ~2.7-3.2x leverage. **Tilt**: despite the macro headwind, the stock is near 52-week highs (+36% 12m) on 21% revenue growth and margin expansion — fundamentals have dominated the rate move so far [Likely] **(inference)**.
