# Corpay, Inc. (CPAY) — Core initiation — 2026-10-11
## 1. Business
Corpay (formerly FleetCor) runs a B2B payments platform in three segments: Corporate Payments (AP automation, commercial cards, cross-border FX), Vehicle Payments (fuel/EV cards, tolls, parking, maintenance) and Lodging Payments (managed lodging for workforce/airline crews) [Certain] (source: https://www.corpay.com/about-us). Revenue is mainly transaction/program fees and FX spreads on client payment volume, not balance-sheet risk [Certain].

## 2. Quality
Returns are high and improving: ROIC 14.5% TTM (flat, +0.6pp over 5y), ROCE 21.8% TTM (+2.4pp), ROE 29.2% TTM (+2.1pp) (source: ratios page). Gross margin 80.5% and operating margin 49.0% (+3.5pp over 5y) point to a durable moat from embedded, switching-cost-heavy fuel-card and cross-border FX relationships across 800,000+ clients [Likely] (source: https://www.corpay.com/about-us). But net margin TTM 22.7% is down 6.0pp over 5y and FCF margin 32.6% is down 9.6pp, with FCF/NI conversion easing to 143.6% TTM from a 198% FY2023 peak [Certain] (source: cash flow/income statement). Leverage is elevated and rising: net debt/EBITDA 2.70x TTM, 3.23x FY2025 (source: ratios/balance sheet). Recent execution is strong: Q2 2026 revenue +21% to $1.34bn, cash EPS +36%, with FY26 guidance raised [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/cpay/transcripts/662696-q2-2026/).

## 3. Valuation
P/E 24.2x sits above CPAY's own 5y range (14.2x/20.8x/23.5x), while EV/EBITDA (12.0x, 22% of range) is below its own 5y median of 13.7x — a mixed signal on own history. Versus peers (WEX, GPN, FIS, EEFT), CPAY trades at large premiums on EV/EBITDA (+66%), EV/Sales (+171%) and P/B (+382%), but at a discount on P/FCF (15.9x vs 17.7x median, -10%) — the premium looks like a profitability premium, not a cash-flow one [Likely]. FCF yield is 6.27%. Base-case fair value (DCF 67%/EV-Revenue 33% blend) is $451.17, 13.4% above the $397.92 last close; bear/bull are -52.7%/+107.2%. The reverse DCF implies the market is pricing only 3.3% year-1 revenue growth fading to terminal — below the 21% growth just posted — leaving room if growth persists, but this is model output, not a forecast [Guessing].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) rises above 3.5x without a stated deleveraging plan
- Operating margin (income statement) breaks its 5y uptrend and falls below 44% (the FY2023 level)
- FCF margin (cash flow/income statement) extends its 5y decline below 25% TTM
- A further FTC/regulatory action against the Vehicle Payments marketing practices that were just settled

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
FCF margin TTM (32.6%) still exceeds net margin TTM (22.7%); FCF/NI conversion is 143.6% TTM despite the 5y downward drift from a 198% FY2023 peak (source: cash flow/income statement). Net debt/EBITDA of 2.70x TTM (3.23x FY2025) funds ongoing M&A and buybacks; shares outstanding fell 3.27% YoY (source: statistics page), so the buyback is shrinking the count even as leverage stays elevated — a trade-off to monitor, not yet a red flag [Likely].

## 6. Unusual items
Corpay reached a proposed settlement with the FTC (announced 2026-09-18) resolving previously disclosed allegations on marketing/disclosure practices [Certain] (source: https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260917736158/en/Corpay-Agrees-to-Settle-FTC-Matter/). This removes a legal overhang, but fine/compliance terms are not shown on stockanalysis.com and were not found in the primary filings checked, so residual compliance cost is [Guessing]. Short interest is 4.95% of float, up 24.1% in the past month (source: statistics page) — rising bearish positioning worth watching. Altman Z is 1.52, in the classic distress zone (<1.8), though the model fits asset-light, high-margin payments businesses poorly; flagged given the coincident rise in leverage [Likely].
