# Macro overlay: RLI Corp. (RLI) — 2026-10-19
## Assessment
RLI is a specialty P&C underwriter with very low market beta (0.32) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/rli/statistics/); its thesis is underwriting discipline, not a macro trade. One channel is real, though: rate level/path → reinvestment yield on RLI's bond float → net investment income → ROE and its P/B-based fair value. Management already flagged this working in its favor: Q2 2026 investment income rose 17% y/y (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/rli/transcripts/653721-q2-2026/, 2026-07-23) [Likely].

**Facts**: fed funds 3.88% (+0.25pp/3m), 10y Treasury 5.10% (+0.53pp/3m), 2y Treasury 4.77% (+0.61pp/3m), 10y breakeven 2.35% (+0.11pp/3m) — all https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id={DFF,DGS10,DGS2,T10YIE} (2026-10-15/16). Core CPI index +2.83pts/3m to 338.9 (2026-09-01, https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=CPILFESL), a ~3.3% annualized run-rate [Likely] **(inference)**. HY credit spread 3.23% (+0.52pp/3m, https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=BAMLH0A0HYM2).

**Market pricing**: the 2y yield (4.77%) sitting ~89bp above effective fed funds (3.88%) is a proxy for a market still pricing further tightening or a higher terminal rate, not cuts [Likely] **(inference)**; stable breakevens suggest no fresh inflation-surprise is priced despite the CPI uptick.

**Bull**: rates stay elevated without a growth shock → NII keeps compounding, supporting ROE and the P/B multiple. **Bear**: rapid cuts (recession) or a credit-spread shock cut reinvestment yields, while accelerating claims/social inflation (core CPI) pressures casualty combined ratios — both squeeze EPS/ROE. **Tilt**: small — RLI's low beta and underwriting-led earnings dominate; rates are a known, already-visible tailwind, not a surprise.
