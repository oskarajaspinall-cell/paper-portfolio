# RLI Corp. (RLI) — Core initiation — 2026-10-19

## 1. Business
RLI is a US specialty property & casualty insurer writing niche casualty (personal umbrella, commercial transportation, professional/executive liability), property (commercial property, marine, Hawaii homeowners) and surety lines through admitted and E&S channels. It earns underwriting profit on premiums plus investment income on the float it holds between collecting premiums and paying claims [Certain] (source: https://www.rlicorp.com).

## 2. Quality
RLI's TTM ROE is 25.2% and ROIC 21.3%, both rising over 5y (ROE +0.8pp, ROIC +4.7pp) (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/rli/financials/ratios/) [Certain]. FCF/NI conversion is 117.9% TTM and net debt/EBITDA is low (0.56x) (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/rli/financials/ratios/) [Certain], consistent with a capital-light underwriting model, not reserve-funded earnings. The moat is underwriting discipline in long-tail specialty niches built over decades: the Q2 2026 10-Q shows the casualty segment combined ratio at 98.2% and the surety segment at 90.4% for H1 2026 — both profitable even as property pricing softened (property combined ratio 59.4%) [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/84246/000110465926086576/rli-20260630x10q.htm). Management describes walking away from mispriced business (e.g., declining general liability and commercial surety volumes rather than chase price) [Likely] (source: same 10-Q URL). RLI was named to Ward's 50 Top Performing Insurance Companies list for the 36th consecutive year, the only insurer recognized every year since 1991 [Likely] (source: https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260709618796/en/RLI-Named-to-Ward%E2%80%99s-50%C2%AE-Top-Performing-Insurance-Companies-List-for-36th-Consecutive-Year/). Beta is very low (0.32), reflecting a diversified, non-correlated book (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/rli/statistics/) [Certain].

## 3. Valuation
At 12.8x P/E, RLI sits at the 23rd percentile of its own 5y range (10.2x–21.8x) and roughly in line with the specialty-insurer peer median (12.9x; WRB, MKL, AFG, CB) (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/rli/statistics/) [Certain]. EV/EBITDA (10.3x) is similarly close to peer median (10.0x). P/B (3.2x) is below its own 5y minimum (3.3x) but 45% above peer median (2.2x); P/FCF is a 25% premium to peers. The P/B premium is consistent with RLI's above-peer ROE, though the fact sheet does not show peer ROE to confirm the size of that gap [Likely]. The owner's P/B+ROE/P/E blended fair value is $69.54 base (+13.9% vs the $61.07 close), bear $56.73 (-7.1%), bull $73.96 (+21.1%) (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/rli/statistics/, ratios/financials pages). The setup is a quality underwriter priced near the cheap end of its own earnings-multiple history, not a deep-value name.

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Casualty or overall combined ratio (10-Q MD&A, company-reported) exceeds 100% for two consecutive quarters.
- ROE (ratios page) falls below 15% for a full fiscal year, breaking the 5y trend.
- FCF/NI conversion (cash-flow/income-statement pages) falls below 80%, signalling earnings quality has weakened.
- Casualty segment prior-year reserve development (10-Q MD&A, company-reported) turns adverse rather than favorable for two consecutive quarters.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
FCF conversion is strong (117.9% TTM, rising over the 5y window) and leverage is low (net debt/EBITDA 0.56x, falling) (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/rli/financials/ratios/) [Certain]. Shares outstanding fell 0.30% YoY (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/rli/statistics/), and RLI declared a regular $0.18/share quarterly dividend in August 2026 [Certain] (source: https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260813233962/en/RLI-Declares-Regular-Dividend/). Capital return is modest and steady rather than aggressive, fitting a specialty underwriter that retains capital to support growth in favorably-priced casualty lines.

## 6. Unusual items
Short interest is 10.81% of float and rose 4.4% over the prior month (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/rli/statistics/) [Certain] — elevated for a low-beta insurer and worth monitoring, though institutional ownership is high (96.8%) [Certain]. Q3 2026 earnings are due after market close on 2026-10-19, the same day as this note [Certain] (source: https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20261001534830/en/RLI-Third-Quarter-Earnings-Release-Teleconference/), so the next print is an immediate near-term catalyst/risk not yet reflected in these figures.
