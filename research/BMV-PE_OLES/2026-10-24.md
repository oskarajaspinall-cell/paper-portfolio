# Industrias Peñoles (BMV:PE_OLES) — Core initiation — 2026-10-24
## 1. Business
Mexican miner and smelter-refiner of silver, gold, zinc, lead and copper; revenue is metal price times volume sold, with margin set by realised prices against mine cost [Likely]. It reports in USD and is priced in MXN, so earnings track metals cycles, not local demand (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/bmv/PE_OLES/). Segment mix is [data unavailable] in the fact sheet and was not researched.

## 2. Quality
Returns are cyclical and currently at a peak, not a through-cycle norm [Certain]. TTM ROIC is 38.7%, against 0.9% in FY2023 and 3.3% in FY2024; operating margin is 39.1% against 1.1% in FY2023 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/bmv/PE_OLES/financials/ratios/). The five-year path shows no durable return spread: ROIC was 5.9% in FY2022. A miner has no pricing power, so the moat is only ore-body cost position and the integrated smelting/refining, which I could not verify from the fact sheet [Guessing]. The balance sheet is a clear strength: net debt/EBITDA is -0.14x TTM and Piotroski F is 8, Altman Z 5.28 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/bmv/PE_OLES/statistics/) [Certain]. Quality today reflects metal prices, not a structural edge [Likely].

## 3. Valuation
The headline multiples look cheap: P/E 8.7x and EV/EBITDA 3.9x, both below the 5y minimum (11.7x and 4.2x), and 48-54% below the peer median (SCCO, FRES, NEM, HL) (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/bmv/PE_OLES/statistics/) [Certain]. FCF yield is 12.59%. But EV/Sales is 1.8x against a 1.4x 5y median and P/B 2.2x against 0.9x, so the price already embeds high margins [Certain]. Low multiples on peak earnings are a classic cyclical trap [Likely]. The mechanical base fair value is 596.48 MXN, 27.4% below the 821.97 close; the bear case is 365.93 and the bull 1,730.18, a very wide range (EV/EBITDA only, NAV [data unavailable]). The price assumes margins largely hold [Likely].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Operating margin (income statement page, TTM) falls below 25%.
- ROIC (ratios page, TTM) falls below 15%.
- FCF conversion (FCF/NI, cash-flow page) falls below 70% for two consecutive reporting periods.
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) rises above 1.0x.

Net cash replaced net debt of 2.61x EBITDA in FY2023. Shares change YoY is [data unavailable], so dilution cannot be assessed. Reinvestment returns and dividend policy were not researched.
