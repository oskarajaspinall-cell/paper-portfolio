# Macro overlay: Industrias Peñoles (BMV:PE_OLES) — 2026-10-24
## Assessment
**Weight: secondary.** Peñoles is a silver, gold, zinc and lead producer reporting in USD. Its operating margin went from 1.1% (FY2023) to 39.1% TTM [Certain] (https://stockanalysis.com/quote/bmv/PE&OLES/, fact sheet 2026-10-24), so earnings are geared to metal prices.

**Chain:** real yields and the dollar -> precious-metal prices -> revenue and operating margin.

**Facts (FRED):**
- 10y TIPS yield 2.64% on 2026-10-22, +0.21pp over ~3m (https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=DFII10).
- Broad dollar index 121.4 on 2026-10-16, +2.48 over ~1m (https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=DTWEXBGS).
- Fed funds 3.88%, 2y yield 4.65% on 2026-10-22 (https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=DGS2).

**Pricing (proxy):** the 2y sits about 77bp above fed funds, implying no near-term cuts **(inference)**. 10y breakeven is 2.34%, +0.08pp over 3m (https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=T10YIE, 2026-10-23), so the rise in yields is mostly real, a headwind for non-yielding metals **(inference)**. The stock is -13.4% over 6m, RSI 36 (fact sheet), so part of this is likely priced [Likely]. Metal prices are not in the snapshot [Guessing].

**Bull:** real yields fall below ~2.3% or the dollar weakens; metal prices rise, margins hold near TTM, the multiple (EV/EBITDA 3.9x) holds.
**Bear:** real yields above ~3% and a firmer dollar; metal prices fall, margins compress from a peak-like 39%, the multiple de-rates.

**Tilt:** toward bear, small [Likely].
