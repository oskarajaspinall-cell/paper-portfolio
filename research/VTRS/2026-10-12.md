# Viatris Inc. (VTRS) — Core initiation — 2026-10-12

## 1. Business
Viatris sells off-patent generic drugs and a smaller branded/specialty portfolio (e.g., Wakix, Xulane) across more than 165 countries, formed from the 2020 Pfizer Upjohn–Mylan merger [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/vtrs/). Revenue comes from high-volume, low-price generics plus higher-margin specialty and complex-generic products; growth now also relies on bolt-on M&A, most recently agreeing to buy Pacira BioSciences for $1.65bn cash to add non-opioid pain brands EXPAREL and ZILRETTA [Certain] (source: https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/viatris-agrees-to-acquire-pacira-biosciences-advancing-its-innovative-medicines-strategy-and-becoming-a-leader-in-non-opioid-pain-therapies-302902353.html).

## 2. Quality
Returns are weak but improving: TTM ROIC 5.9% and ROCE 5.5%, up from 2.4%/2.4% in FY2021, still likely below the cost of capital [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/vtrs/financials/ratios/). Net margin and ROE are negative TTM (-2.8%) and have deteriorated 17.5pp/15.2pp over five years, driven by goodwill/intangible impairments typical of the Mylan-era generics book [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/vtrs/financials/ratios/, https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/vtrs/financials/income-statement/). Gross margin (40.3% TTM) and operating margin (10.6% TTM) have both expanded over five years despite generic pricing pressure, and FCF margin is stable near 13-14% [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/vtrs/financials/cash-flow-statement/). The moat is thin: generics are largely commoditized with little pricing power, so durability rests on scale, manufacturing breadth and a specialty mix that competitors can erode over time; Altman Z of 1.10 sits in the distress zone, a balance-sheet quality flag despite falling leverage [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/vtrs/statistics/).

## 3. Valuation
VTRS trades at EV/EBITDA 7.5x and P/FCF 10.4x, both above their own 5-year range highs (133% and 220% of the range respectively), yet 28% and 62% below the peer median (TEVA, OGN, AMRX, LON:HIK) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/vtrs/financials/ratios/). P/B (1.4x) is 71% below peers. FCF yield is 9.65% [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/vtrs/statistics/). The base DCF fair value is $22.22 (+26% to the $17.64 close); bear is $17.77 (roughly today's price); bull is $29.91 (+69.5%) [Likely] (source: fact sheet fair value table). The reverse DCF implies the market is pricing in perpetual revenue decline of 4.6% a year — harsher than the five-year trend of rising ROIC and stable FCF margin suggests, which is the asymmetry: the stock is expensive versus its own quiet history but cheap versus peers, and the gap reflects a market discount for low net income quality and distress-zone leverage metrics rather than an obvious value trap [Likely].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROIC (ratios page) falls back below the FY2023 low of 1.4%, undoing the five-year improvement
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) rises back above 3.5x, reversing the deleveraging trend as the Pacira cash deal adds debt
- Gross margin (income statement) falls below the FY2025 level of 39.5%, signalling generics pricing pressure is intensifying faster than mix can offset
- FCF margin (cash flow/income statement) falls below 10%, the bottom of its five-year range

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Net debt/EBITDA has fallen from 4.10x (FY2021) to 2.82x TTM, a genuine deleveraging track record, and shares outstanding are down 2.44% YoY [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/vtrs/financials/ratios/, https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/vtrs/statistics/). That discipline is now being redirected: Viatris agreed on 2026-10-08 to acquire Pacira BioSciences for $1.65bn in cash, which management says will be "immediately accretive" to guidance, but which will add leverage back onto a balance sheet whose Altman Z already signals distress-zone risk [Likely] (source: https://www.reuters.com/legal/litigation/viatris-acquire-pacira-biosciences-165-billion-deal-2026-10-08/, https://www.tipranks.com/news/the-fly/viatris-pacira-deal-expected-to-be-immediately-accretive-to-financial-guidance-thefly-news).
