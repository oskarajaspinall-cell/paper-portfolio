## 2. Quality
Returns are high: ROIC 28.9% TTM and ROCE 53.4% in FY2025 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/msm/OQEP/financials/ratios/) [Certain]. Operating margin 54.3% TTM and gross margin 79.7% TTM indicate low-cost barrels (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/msm/OQEP/financials/income-statement/) [Likely]. Leverage is light: net debt/EBITDA 0.21x [Certain].

Direction is mixed. ROIC has risen over the last 12 months (25.0% to 28.9%), but ROCE fell from 64.2% to 54.4% and operating margin from 61.8% to 54.3% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/msm/OQEP/financials/ratios/?p=trailing) [Certain]. FCF margin is 21.1%, well below the 2-year high of 33.2% [Certain]. Net margin of 25.9% is far below FY2023's 58.3%, so earnings quality over the cycle is uneven [Certain]. A durable moat is unproven: returns depend on resource quality and the oil price, not on a competitive position [Likely]. The listed history is short, so cycle resilience is untested [Likely].

## 3. Valuation
P/E is 10.4x against a 5y range of 8.4x–10.4x (median 9.4x), so it sits at the top of its own short range. EV/EBITDA is 3.9x against 3.0x–3.4x, and P/FCF is 13.2x against about 10.8x (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/msm/OQEP/statistics/) [Certain]. Against peers (ADNOC Drilling, Saudi Aramco, Harbour Energy) it is cheaper: P/E -40%, EV/EBITDA -48%, P/FCF -32%, though P/B is +4%. The peers are a loose comparison; a driller, a national oil major and a UK E&P are not like-for-like [Likely]. FCF yield is 7.55%. Fair value is [data unavailable]: neither the DCF nor the EV/EBITDA method could be computed. The price already assumes current margins hold, since multiples are above every year-end in the range. The discount to peers is the main support, and it may reflect Omani country and liquidity discounts (institutional ownership is 0.1%) rather than mispricing [Guessing].
