# Protagonist Therapeutics (PTGX) — Core initiation — 2026-11-15
## 1. Business
Peptide-drug discoverer that earns licence, milestone and collaboration revenue rather than product sales. Icotrokinra (oral IL-23R peptide, approved for plaque psoriasis) is licensed to J&J; rusfertide (MIMRYLO, polycythemia vera, approved August 2026) to Takeda. Earlier oral IL-17 and obesity peptides are in development [Certain] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1377121/000110465926123620/ptgx-20260930x10q.htm). No foreign country is flagged as a revenue concentration in the fact sheet [Likely].

## 2. Quality
Profitability has just inflected: TTM operating margin is 58.6% versus 9.1% a year earlier, and TTM ROCE is 29.6% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/ptgx/financials/ratios/?p=trailing) [Certain]. But earnings are lumpy: FY2025 operating margin was -343.6% and FY2024 was 58.2%, so the margin tracks milestone timing, not a stable base [Certain]. FCF margin is falling over the last 12 months (30.5% to 19.8%) and FCF conversion is 31.9% of net income, so reported profit is partly non-cash or receivable [Certain]. Moat: two approved first-in-class peptides and a platform, with royalties on partner-run launches. Phase 3 psoriatic arthritis and ICONIC-ASCEND data were positive for icotrokinra (non-inferior to ustekinumab, not superior) [Certain] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1377121/000110465926123620/ptgx-20260930x10q.htm). Exposure to partner execution (J&J, Takeda) is the key dependency [Likely]. Net cash position (net debt/EBITDA -2.44x) [Certain].

## 3. Valuation
At 130.95 USD the market cap is 8.5bn USD. P/E is 26.6x (-20% vs peers, n=2) but P/FCF is 78.3x, 78% of its own 5y range and +244% vs peers [Certain]. EV/Sales of 14.0x sits low in its own range (10th percentile) yet +35% above peers [Certain]. Mechanical fair value is 144.52 USD base (+10.4%), bear 43.50, bull 385.39: an EV/Revenue-only blend, as rNPV is unavailable, so wide and unreliable [Likely]. The price assumes partner launches deliver royalties and milestones persistently; little margin of safety on cash flow. Trend is healthy: +9.8% above the 200-day, 12-month +54.3%, though 3-month -13.5% and 50-day -6.7% [Certain].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- TTM operating margin (income statement, trailing page) falls below 20%.
- TTM ROCE (ratios page, trailing) falls below 10%.
- Shares change YoY (statistics page) rises above 12%, despite the announced buyback.
- Icotrokinra or MIMRYLO suffers a label restriction, safety action or partner termination (company or SEC filings).

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
The board authorised a share buyback in September 2026 [Likely] (headline, source: https://www.accessnewswire.com/newsroom/en/healthcare-and-pharmaceutical/protagonist-therapeutics-announces-300-million-share-repurchase-progr-1218162), yet shares are up 8.19% YoY, so dilution persists [Certain]. FCF conversion of 31.9% is weak against 62.0% TTM net margin [Certain]. Net cash balance sheet limits financing risk [Likely].
