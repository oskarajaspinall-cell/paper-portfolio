- **Added**: ICOTYDE® (icotrokinra) was approved in the United States in March 2026 for the treatment of moderate-to-severe plaque psoriasis in adults and pediatric patients 12 years of age or older who weigh at least 40 kg and are candidates for systemic therapy or phototherapy. ICOTYDE is the first and only targeted oral peptide that precisely blocks the Interleukin-23 receptor (“IL-23R”), which underpins the inflammatory response in psoriasis and offers potential in other IL-23-mediated diseases. ICOTYDE is licensed to Janssen Biotech, Inc., a Johnson & Johnson company (“JNJ”), under a license and collaboration agreement initially entered into in 2017. - **Added**: In October 2026 JNJ submitted an abstract accepted for presentation at the American College of Rheumatology Convergence 2026 outlining Week 24 results from a Phase 3, randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled study evaluating icotrokinra in biologic-naïve participants with active psoriatic arthritis. The publicly released abstract reported that in biologic-naïve patients with active psoriatic arthritis, once-daily ICOTYDE 200 mg demonstrated both joint and skin improvements versus placebo at Week 16, with 400 mg showing no additional benefit beyond the 200 mg dose. Continued improvements were observed through Week 24 for both joint and skin responses. Adverse event profiles were comparable across groups with no new safety signals identified.

- **Added**: PN-881. We are developing PN-881, a potential best-in-class oral peptide IL-17 antagonist, for the treatment of immune-mediated skin diseases. PN-881 targets three IL-17 dimers (IL-17 AA, AF and FF), and may offer potential treatment options for plaque psoriasis, psoriatic arthritis, hidradenitis suppurativa, and spondyloarthritis.
- **Added**: In October 2025, the first human subject was dosed in our Phase 1 trial of PN-881 (ClinicalTrials.gov identifier NCT07153146) evaluating its safety, tolerability, pharmacokinetics and pharmacodynamics in healthy adults. We believe data from our completed Phase 1 study supports our decision to advance PN-881 into a comprehensive Phase 2b psoriasis program, with initiation of a dose range-finding study in patients with plaque psoriasis expected in early first quarter 2027. Phase 1 data are expected to be presented at a future medical meeting.
- **Added**: PN-477. In June 2025, we nominated PN-477, a potential best-in-class novel triple GLP-1, GIP and GCG receptor agonist peptide, as a development candidate for the treatment of obesity. In the subcutaneously administered PN-477sc single ascending dose study, we observed positive dose-related pharmacokinetic, pharmacodynamic and clinical activity. Based on these highly encouraging findings, we have made a strategic decision to prioritize the orally administered PN-477o clinical development program. The initiation of a Phase 1 study of PN-477o is anticipated in the first half of 2027.
