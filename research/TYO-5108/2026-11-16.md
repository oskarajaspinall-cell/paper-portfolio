# Bridgestone Corporation (TYO:5108) — Core initiation — 2026-11-16
## 1. Business
Global tyre maker (passenger, truck/bus, mining and aircraft), with replacement-tyre demand the main profit pool, plus diversified products. Reports in JPY; yen weakness lifts reported profit [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tyo/5108/transcripts/727158-q3-2026/). ## 2. Quality
Solid but not exceptional. TTM ROIC 13.9% and operating margin 13.3% are the best of the five years, though FY2021-25 ROIC drifted from 12.9% to 11.5% and net margin fell from 12.1% to 7.4% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tyo/5108/financials/ratios/). The last 12 months reverse that: ROIC rose from 10.6% to 13.9% and operating margin from 8.9% to 13.3% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tyo/5108/financials/ratios/?p=trailing) [Certain]. Per the Q3 call, nine-month revenue and adjusted operating profit were records and revenue guidance was raised, while raw-material and Middle East costs and the Tosu plant fire remain pressures [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tyo/5108/transcripts/727158-q3-2026/). Part of the margin gain is yen depreciation (Q1 call) rather than structural [Likely]. The moat is scale, brand and dealer networks; tyres are a mature, cyclical-lite category [Likely]. Net debt/EBITDA is 0.17x; FCF margin is 9.9% TTM.

## 3. Valuation
P/E 12.6x sits at 76% of its 5y range (8.8x-13.8x) and is 42% above the peer median of 8.9x; EV/EBITDA 5.7x is at 96% of its range and in line with peers; P/B 1.5x is above its 5y max (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tyo/5108/statistics/). P/FCF 11.6x is near its 5y low and FCF yield is 8.59% [Certain]. So earnings multiples price in the margin recovery, while cash flow still looks reasonable. Mechanical base fair value is 5,555 JPY vs the 4,402 JPY close (+26.2%), but it is DCF-led (67%) and the EV/EBITDA leg gives 4,101, below the DCF figure [Likely]. The price implies -4.3% year-1 revenue growth, undemanding if margins hold [Likely]. Bear case 1,483 JPY shows how sensitive the DCF is to margin reversal.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
FCF conversion is 106.3% TTM and 125.1% in FY2025, so earnings are cash-backed. Shares outstanding fell 3.23% YoY, consistent with buybacks, and the company guided to continued buybacks with higher CapEx and R&D [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tyo/5108/transcripts/395521-q4-2025/). It sold its China/Thailand tyre-cord business to Bekaert, a sensible pruning of non-core assets [Likely] (source: https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/05/01/3285658/0/en/Bekaert-completes-acquisition-of-tire-cord-business-from-Bridgestone-in-China-and-Thailand.html). Balance sheet is a net-cash-like 0.17x leverage.
