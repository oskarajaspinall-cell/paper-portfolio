# Vinhomes JSC (HOSE:VHM) — Core initiation — 2026-10-20
## 1. Business
Vinhomes develops and sells large-scale residential/mixed-use "Vinhomes"-branded urban areas in Vietnam, monetising mainly through bulk and retail unit sales, plus office leasing and property-management, construction, design and supervision services [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hose/VHM/company/). It is a subsidiary of Vingroup JSC [Certain] (same source).

## 2. Quality
Returns have compressed hard: ROIC fell from 28.7% (FY2021) to 10.3% (FY2025, TTM 19.2%, -18.4pp over 5y), ROE from 35.3% to 18.4% (-16.8pp), gross margin from 57.0% to 30.2% (TTM 43.8%, -26.8pp) [Certain] (fact sheet Quality table). Net debt/EBITDA rose from 0.22x (FY2021) to 2.38x (FY2025, TTM 1.57x) [Certain]. Scale in Vietnam's largest land bank is real, but margins and ROIC are falling together, consistent with a capital-intensive, cyclical developer rather than a business with a durable moat [Likely]. FCF turned volatile (FCF margin -32.3% FY2024 to +16.8% FY2025, FCF/NI -103.9% to 61.5%, TTM 83.4%) [Certain], likely reflecting lumpy handover-based revenue recognition rather than stable cash generation [Likely]. Piotroski F-score 6/9; Altman Z-score 1.22 is in the distress zone (<1.8), flagging balance-sheet fragility scale does not offset [Certain] (fact sheet Other table).

## 3. Valuation
VHM trades at P/E 6.2x (15th percentile of its 5y range) and EV/EBITDA 6.7x (below its 5y minimum), both ~60-65% below the peer median (P/E 15.2x, EV/EBITDA 19.3x; n=4) [Certain] (fact sheet Valuation table). That looks cheap, but P/B is 1.8x versus a peer median of 0.7x (+172%) — peers trade at/below book, more plausibly reflecting sector-wide distress in Vietnamese real estate than VHM being overvalued [Likely]. FCF yield is 13.45%. Base fair value (DCF 67%/EV-EBITDA 33%) is 115,433 VND, +91.4% above the 60,300 VND close, but the bear case is -98,202 VND and reverse DCF implies -6.9% year-1 revenue growth priced in — dispersion is wide; read as low-confidence [Guessing]. The stock fell 11.9%/17.3% over 3/6 months, sits 13.5%/9.0% below its 50/200-day averages, and underperformed SPY by 15.2/26.1/11.4pp over 3/6/12 months [Certain] (fact sheet Price table) — the market is pricing real distress, not a cyclical dip.

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) rises above 3.0x (TTM 1.57x, FY2025 2.38x, trending up +2.16x over 5y).
- Gross margin (income statement) falls below 25% for a full fiscal year (TTM 43.8%, FY2025 30.2%, falling -26.8pp over 5y).
- ROIC (ratios page) stays below 8% for two consecutive fiscal years (FY2025 10.3%, 5y trend -18.4pp).
- FCF/NI conversion (cash flow + income statement) turns negative again for a full fiscal year (FY2025 61.5%, TTM 83.4%, but FY2023/24 were -25.4%/-103.9%).
