# Trex Company (TREX) — Core initiation — 2026-11-16
## 1. Business
Trex makes wood-alternative composite decking and railing, plus fencing, cladding, lighting and fasteners, sold through distributors to dealers and contractors. Revenue is US-centred residential repair-and-remodel demand [Likely]. The distribution network was consolidated, with Specialty Building Products as sole national decking and railing distributor (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1069878/000119312526447251/trex-20260930.htm) [Certain]. No foreign country is shown as ~10% of revenue in the sources read [Guessing].

## 2. Quality
Still a good business, but returns have clearly fallen. ROIC is 14.3% TTM against 43.4% in FY2021 and 17.0% a year ago; operating margin is 19.2% against 24.2% a year ago (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/trex/financials/ratios/?p=trailing) [Certain]. This is the Direction change flagged in the fact sheet: the last 12 months confirm the 5-year decline, not a reversal. Cause: gross margin was 36.0% in the latest quarter against 40.5% a year earlier, from depreciation, diesel-driven freight and labour as the Arkansas plant started [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1069878/000119312526447251/trex-20260930.htm) [Certain]. These are ramp costs, which should fade if volumes fill the plant [Likely]. FCF margin rose to 15.7% from 2.0% as capex eased [Certain]. Moat: brand and a recycled-material cost position, but the cladding and fencing push shows growth is being bought with new categories [Guessing]. Net debt/EBITDA is 0.67x, with Altman Z at 7.89 [Certain].

## 3. Valuation
P/E is 30.8x, 20% up its 5y range (19.8x-74.5x) and below the peer median of 34.2x. EV/EBITDA is 17.1x, 16% up its range but 33% above peers. P/FCF is 26.2x, below its 5y minimum (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/trex/financials/ratios/) [Certain]. FCF yield is 3.81%. The price is 20.8% above the 200-day average, with +59.6% over 12 months against SPY's +10.4% [Certain]. The reverse DCF implies 27.5% year-1 revenue growth fading to terminal, which is demanding. The mechanical fair value (base 32.03 vs 52.76 close) is flagged unreliable, as the two methods disagree 3.2x, so weigh it lightly [Certain]. The market is pricing a margin recovery once Arkansas is absorbed, and much of that appears priced after the rerating [Likely].

## 4. What would prove this wrong

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Shares are down 3.13% YoY on buybacks; the board added to the repurchase authorisation and a new revolving credit facility was signed in March (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1069878/000119312526447251/trex-20260930.htm) [Certain]. Cash was thin at quarter-end, so buybacks are financed partly with the revolver [Likely]. FCF conversion is 112.9% TTM, flattered by the capex cycle ending [Likely]. Nine-month operating cash flow fell on higher receivables from strong sales [company-reported] (same filing) [Certain].

## 6. Unusual items
