# Hiscox (LON:HSX) — Core initiation — 2026-11-16
## 1. Business
Specialty and retail property & casualty insurer plus reinsurance (Hiscox Re). Earns underwriting profit on premiums plus investment income on float; retail (small-business and personal lines in the UK, US and Europe) is the growth engine, big-ticket and reinsurance the cyclical part [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/lon/HSX/). Country revenue split: [data unavailable] from the fact sheet; no primary source read, so the US share of premiums is unverified and may be material [Guessing].

## 2. Quality
- ROIC TTM 14.5% vs 7.0% in FY2021; ROE TTM 16.5% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/lon/HSX/financials/ratios/) [Certain]. Returns stepped up after FY2022 and have held, but are off the FY2023 peak (ROE 24.0%) as rates soften [Likely].
- ROIC is 3.2pp below its 2-year high of 17.8% (2024-06-30), though up 0.7pp on a year earlier (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/lon/HSX/financials/ratios/?p=trailing). The fall from peak looks like normalising from a hard-market high, not a break [Likely].
- Moat is modest: specialty underwriting skill and brand in small-business lines, not structural [Likely]. Management says it is tempering big-ticket growth as rates soften (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/lon/HSX/transcripts/761022-q3-2026-tu/), the discipline a durable underwriter shows [Likely].
- Piotroski F 7; shares down 3.35% YoY from buybacks (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/lon/HSX/statistics/) [Certain].
- Margin lines are [data unavailable].

## 3. Valuation
P/E 13.6x sits at 48% of its 5y range (6.5x–21.3x), 14% below peers' 15.8x (n=3: Beazley, Lancashire, Admiral). P/B 2.1x is above its 5y max of 1.7x, in line with peers. EV/EBITDA 10.2x is 30% above peers (insurer EBITDA is a weak yardstick) [Likely]. FCF yield 8.99%, but insurer FCF is float-driven and noisy [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/lon/HSX/statistics/).

Mechanical fair value (P/B+ROE 67%, P/E 33%): base 1,751.53 GBX vs close 1,952.00 (-10.3%), bull 2,317.21, bear 808.75 (source: fact sheet, https://stockanalysis.com/quote/lon/HSX/financials/ratios/). The price assumes ROE holds mid-teens with no earnings fall as the cycle softens [Likely]. Trend is healthy: +12.9% above the 200-day, 12-month +43.9%, beating SPY by +33.5pp (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/lon/HSX/history/). A May report of a possible bid approach (source: https://www.reuters.com/business/shares-hiscox-jump-after-media-report-potential-bid-insurer-2026-05-15/) may be partly in the price [Guessing].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROIC TTM (ratios page, ?p=trailing) falls below 10%.
- ROE FY (ratios page) falls below 12%.
- Net debt/EBITDA (debt less cash and ST investments, fact sheet definition) rises above 2.0x.
- Shares change YoY (statistics page) turns positive above +3% (dilution).

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Net buyback: shares down 3.35% YoY [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/lon/HSX/statistics/). Management cites a continuing buyback and portfolio repositioning (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/lon/HSX/transcripts/761022-q3-2026-tu/) [Likely]. FCF conversion is erratic: TTM 114.3% vs FY2025 11.8%, as expected for insurance cash flow timing [Likely]. Leverage is low; the two net-debt definitions conflict (1.10x vs -0.32x; see fact sheet) [Certain].

## 6. Unusual items
- Institutions own 91.5%, insiders 0.15% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/lon/HSX/statistics/) [Certain]. Short interest [data unavailable].
- Possible takeover approach reported by media in May (source: https://www.reuters.com/business/shares-hiscox-jump-after-media-report-potential-bid-insurer-2026-05-15/); unconfirmed, no later follow-up in the fact sheet [Guessing].
