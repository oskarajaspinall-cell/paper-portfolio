# McCormick & Company, Incorporated (MKC) — Core initiation — 2026-10-09

## 1. Business
McCormick makes and markets spices, seasonings, condiments and flavorings through two segments: Consumer (retail brands) and Flavor Solutions (branded/private-label flavors sold to foodservice and packaged-food makers), across ~150 countries [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/mkc/). Growth comes from pricing, volume/mix and bolt-on M&A; the McCormick de Mexico acquisition helped drive Q3 2026 sales growth [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/mkc/transcripts/700420-q3-2026/).

## 2. Quality
ROIC drifted from 9.8% (FY21) to 8.9% TTM; ROCE from 11.4% to 10.3% [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/mkc/financials/ratios/). Gross margin fell from 39.7% to 38.9% and operating margin from 17.5% to 16.3% over five years [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/mkc/financials/income-statement/) — category leadership in spices has not stopped input-cost/mix pressure outpacing pricing [Likely]. FCF margin improved to 12.0% TTM (62.1% FCF/net income) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/mkc/financials/cash-flow-statement/), but net debt/EBITDA is 3.12x, still elevated for staples [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/mkc/financials/ratios/). The moat is brand recognition and retail shelf-space incumbency, not rising returns. A proposed, transformational acquisition of the majority of Unilever's foods business is under active UK competition-authority investigation since 2026-09-16 [Certain] (source: https://www.reuters.com/legal/litigation/mccormick-unilever-food-deal-faces-uk-competition-scrutiny-2026-09-16/) — likely debt-funded on an already-leveraged balance sheet, an execution/regulatory risk, not yet a demonstrated quality gain [Likely].

## 3. Valuation
TTM P/E of 8.3x looks far below its own 5y range (22.9x–33.5x) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/mkc/financials/ratios/), but TTM net margin (19.4%) and ROE (22.6%) sit well above the FY21–25 ranges (10.2–12.0%; 13.9–18.1%) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/mkc/financials/ratios/), consistent with a one-off item inflating trailing EPS; GAAP Q3 profit fell to $97.6m from $225.5m a year earlier even as sales rose [Likely] (source: https://www.wsj.com/business/retail/mccormick-posts-lower-profit-on-higher-sales-edb1b56d). The "cheap" P/E is probably not representative. On EV/EBITDA, EV/Sales, P/FCF and P/B, MKC trades 34–53% above the peer median (KHC, GIS, CAG, HRL) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/mkc/financials/ratios/) — rich versus packaged-food peers on every multiple except the unreliable (n=1) peer P/E. Base-case DCF fair value of $66.91 is +45.6% above the $45.94 close, but bear case ($33.64) is -26.8% below [Certain] (source: fact sheet fair value). Net: cheap only against a likely-distorted own-history multiple; rich against peers on every other measure.

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Gross margin (income statement page) stays below 39% through FY2026 and FY2027, confirming the five-year erosion is structural, not cyclical.
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) rises above 3.5x once Unilever Foods financing is set, before any synergy delivery.
- ROIC (ratios page) falls below 8% for two consecutive fiscal years.
- The UK competition authority clears the Unilever Foods deal without material remedies or delay, removing the key execution-risk overhang.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
FCF margin improved to 12.0% TTM with 62.1% FCF/net-income conversion [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/mkc/financials/cash-flow-statement/). Net debt/EBITDA is already 3.12x [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/mkc/financials/ratios/), ahead of any financing for the proposed Unilever Foods acquisition, whose terms are not yet confirmed [Certain]. Shares outstanding fell only -0.05% YoY — buybacks are minimal [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/mkc/statistics/); the board maintained a $0.48 quarterly dividend [Likely] (source: https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/mccormick-declares-0-48-quarterly-dividend-302889785.html). Capital is being directed at integration and a prospective transformational deal, not shareholder returns, raising leverage risk if that deal proceeds debt-funded [Likely].

## 6. Unusual items
Short interest rose +20.3% month-over-month to 6.0% of float [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/mkc/statistics/) — rising bearish positioning. TTM net margin and ROE are well above the five-year range, consistent with a one-off item flattering trailing earnings [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/mkc/financials/ratios/). The proposed acquisition of the majority of Unilever's foods business remains under active UK competition-authority investigation (opened 2026-09-16), an outcome-uncertain, scale-changing event [Certain] (source: https://www.reuters.com/legal/litigation/mccormick-unilever-food-deal-faces-uk-competition-scrutiny-2026-09-16/). A Barclays-conference transcript cites a $600m synergy target for the combination [Guessing] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/mkc/transcripts/737705-barclays-19th-annual-global-consumer-conference/) — unconfirmed by any primary filing read for this note.
