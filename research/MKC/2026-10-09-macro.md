# Macro overlay: McCormick & Company (MKC) — 2026-10-09

## Assessment
MKC's thesis is company-specific (Unilever Foods deal risk, margin erosion, leverage) [Certain], but a leveraged balance sheet (net debt/EBITDA 3.12x TTM) is macro-sensitive to the cost of debt, and the EMEA/APAC/Mexico sales growth driving Q3 is dollar-sensitive via FX translation [Likely] **(inference)**.

Chain: real yields -> cost of debt for a prospective debt-funded acquisition and the DCF discount rate; US dollar -> FX translation of foreign sales growth into reported USD earnings.

What's priced: Facts [Certain] — US 10y real yield (DFII10) 2.92% on 2026-10-07, up 0.49pp in ~1m and 0.61pp in ~3m (https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=DFII10); US 10y Treasury 5.28%, up 0.72pp in 3m (DGS10); fed funds 3.88%, up 0.26pp in 3m (DFF); 10y breakeven 2.35%, roughly flat (T10YIE); broad dollar index 121.385, up ~2.4% in 1m (DTWEXBGS, 2026-10-02). Market pricing (proxy) **(inference)**: the 2y yield (4.77%) sitting above fed funds (3.88%) implies the market still expects further tightening, not cuts. A rising-real-yield, strengthening-dollar regime is already partly priced, but the pace (1m moves exceeding 3m moves in real yields and the dollar) is accelerating, not stabilizing.

Bull [Likely] **(inference)**: real yields/dollar stabilize or ease -> cheaper acquisition financing, lower discount rate, better FX translation of EMEA/APAC/Mexico growth. Bear [Likely] **(inference)**: real yields and dollar keep climbing into the Unilever Foods financing decision -> higher leverage cost stacking on an already-flagged invalidation risk (net debt/EBITDA >3.5x trigger), plus a translation drag on reported growth. Tilt: toward bear, small — macro amplifies an existing fundamental risk rather than creating a new one.
