# Industrial Bank Co., Ltd. (SHA:601166) — Core initiation — 2026-10-18
## 1. Business
Industrial Bank (601166.SH) is a Chinese joint-stock commercial bank headquartered in Fuzhou. It earns net interest income on corporate, retail and interbank loans funded mainly by deposits and interbank borrowing, plus fee income from wealth management, custody, bond underwriting and card services [Likely]. Versus the big state-owned banks, its balance sheet leans more on interbank/treasury assets and wholesale funding, raising sensitivity to funding-cost swings in China's interbank market [Likely].

## 2. Quality
ROE fell every year shown, from 12.7% (FY2021) to 8.2% TTM (-4.1pp), and net margin slipped from 53.8% to 49.7% over the same period [Certain] [RA,IS]. This tracks sector-wide NIM compression and rising credit costs as property and local-government debt exposure work through Chinese joint-stock banks [Likely]. Piotroski F-score is 4 (of 9), below the mid-point, consistent with weakening rather than improving fundamentals [Certain] [ST]. ROIC, ROCE and gross/operating margin are not computed for banks on stockanalysis.com [data unavailable]. FCF margin and conversion swing from -257.1% to +470.6% and -478.2% to +947.5% across the five years because a bank's reported "free cash flow" is dominated by loan/deposit balance changes, not a repeatable economic flow [Likely]; neither is a reliable quality signal here. No fact-sheet page shows the asset-quality ratio (NPLs) that would normally anchor a bank quality call [data unavailable].

## 3. Valuation
At 18.45 CNY, P/E is 5.8x, 19% below peers' 7.1x median (SHA:600036, SHA:600016, SHA:600000, SHA:601998) but near the top of its own 5-year range (4.2x-6.1x, 81st percentile) — cheap versus peers, not versus its own history [Certain] [RA]. P/B is 0.4x, below its own 5-year minimum (0.4x/0.5x/0.6x) and 12% below the 0.5x peer median [Certain] [RA]. P/FCF (0.5x) is also below its own range and peers but not a meaningful read given the FCF distortions in section 2. Base fair value of 29.60 CNY (+60.5%) uses P/B plus sustainable ROE as its primary method, assuming ROE stabilises near a sustainable level [Certain] [FV] — in tension with the five-year falling ROE trend, so base-case upside may be overstated unless margin pressure eases [Likely]. The reverse DCF implies the price already prices in -12.4% revenue growth in year one, fading to terminal growth by year ten [Certain] [FV].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROE (ratios page) falls below 7% TTM, confirming margin compression has not stabilised.
- Net margin (income statement) falls below 48%, indicating credit costs or NIM compression are accelerating rather than levelling off.
- Piotroski F-score (statistics page) falls to 3 or below, confirming fundamental deterioration rather than stabilisation.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Shares outstanding rose only 0.20% year-on-year, indicating minimal dilution from capital raises [Certain] [ST]. Dividend-discount fair value (10.17 CNY base) is far below the P/B+ROE and FCFE estimates (29.60-30.55 CNY), implying the modelled payout is well short of what the bank could sustain if profitability holds [Likely] [FV]; stockanalysis.com does not show the payout ratio directly [data unavailable]. Reported FCF margin and conversion are not usable to judge cash generation for a bank, since loan/deposit balance swings dominate them (section 2) [Likely], so capital adequacy and dividend cover cannot be verified from the fact sheet alone.

## 6. Unusual items
Beta (5y) is 0.20, unusually low even for a bank, consistent with limited free-float/foreign participation in A-shares rather than genuinely low risk [Likely] [ST]. Short interest and insider ownership are not available for this A-share listing [data unavailable]; institutional ownership is 32.3% [Certain] [ST]. A May 2025 headline alleged the bank offered elite-internship access to wealthy clients' children, drawing public criticism — a reputational item, not a figure, and over a year old by this initiation date (source: https://www.scmp.com/economy/china-economy/article/3312415/fury-chinese-bank-offers-help-rich-clients-kids-gain-top-internships) [Likely]. Country and regulatory risk (CNY reporting, capital controls, state influence over credit allocation) is material for any mainland China bank holding [Certain].
