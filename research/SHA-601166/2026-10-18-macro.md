# Macro overlay: Industrial Bank Co., Ltd. (SHA:601166) — 2026-10-18
## Assessment
This stock's own valuation model prices its cost of equity off the US 10-year Treasury yield plus a static CNY country-risk premium (beta floor 0.8 applies, since raw beta 0.20 is clamped), so a US-yield move mechanically shifts this CNY-denominated, China-domestic bank's fair value even though its revenue has no US exposure.

Facts [Certain]: US 10y Treasury 5.10% (2026-10-15, +0.53pp/3m) [FRED DGS10]; 10y real yield 2.78% (+0.43pp/3m) [FRED DFII10]; 10y breakeven 2.35% (+0.11pp/3m) [FRED T10YIE] — the rise is real-rate led, not an inflation scare. CNY per USD 6.692 (2026-10-09, -0.0846/3m) — CNY appreciated vs the dollar even as the broad dollar index rose +1.1/3m [FRED DEXCHUS, DTWEXBGS]. PBOC's DR007 overnight repo rate was ~1.49% on 2026-10-18 [pbc.gov.cn], an accommodative level.

Market pricing (proxy) [Likely]: the 2y Treasury (4.77%) sits 0.89pp above effective fed funds (3.88%) [FRED DGS2, DFF, 2026-10-15], proxying a market-priced higher-for-longer US path rather than near-term cuts (inference).

Bull: US yields reverse lower → discount rate falls → base fair value (29.60 CNY) re-rates up; easy PBOC liquidity supports the NIM-stabilization assumption the bull case needs.
Bear: US yields keep grinding higher → discount rate rises → fair value range compresses mechanically, independent of China's own credit cycle (the bigger earnings risk, already tracked via this stock's text-only triggers).

Tilt: small, toward bear — the UST-yield channel is live and currently adverse; the offsetting CNY/PBOC-easing channel is already established, not a fresh surprise.
