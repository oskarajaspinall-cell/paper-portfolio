DOC's -8.5% vs its 50-day MA and -16.4pp relative to SPY over 3m suggest much of this is already reflected in price **(inference)**. Bull: real yields stabilize/fall from 2.87% -> cap-rate compression resumes -> P/FFO re-rates toward the $21.53 base fair value faster. Bear: real yields and fed funds keep rising -> bridge debt refinanced more expensively, FFO guidance ($1.73-1.77) at risk, multiple stays trough-bound. Tilt: moderate toward bear — the core thesis (capital recycling, peer discount) is fundamental, but active Fed hikes and rising real yields are a genuine, not-yet-reversing headwind to the re-rating and leverage path.
