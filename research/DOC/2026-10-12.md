# Healthpeak Properties, Inc. (DOC) — Core initiation — 2026-10-12

## 1. Business
Healthpeak is a healthcare REIT owning outpatient medical office buildings, lab/life-science space, and senior housing via the newly public, 73.6%-owned Janus Living [Certain] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/765880/000162828026053373/peak-20260630.htm). Revenue comes from long-duration triple-net and RIDEA-operating leases to health systems, life-science tenants and senior-housing operators, plus JV fee income from partners such as Brookfield [Certain] (same source).

## 2. Quality
Tenant relationships are sticky: hospital-adjacent medical office and licensed senior-housing assets face high relocation costs and CON/zoning barriers, a durable moat [Likely]. Healthpeak just sold 86 outpatient buildings (~$2.1bn) into a Brookfield JV while retaining management and a buy-back option, evidencing institutional demand that validates book values [Certain] (source: SEC 10-Q above). Reported ROIC is only 2.5-3.0% TTM, well below an estimated ~10% cost of equity (5.22% risk-free + 0.99 beta x 5% ERP), but this understates REIT economic returns because real estate sits at depreciated historical cost rather than market value [Likely]. Piotroski F-score is a middling 5/9 (source: stockanalysis.com/stocks/doc/statistics/). Net debt/EBITDA (site definition) is roughly flat at 5.5-6.2x over five years, but the fact sheet's own-calculated measure shows a mild rising trend to 6.57x TTM, as recent acquisitions were bridged with bank debt ahead of permanent capital (source: stockanalysis.com/stocks/doc/financials/ratios/) [Certain].

## 3. Valuation
DOC trades near the bottom of its own five-year range and at a wide discount to peers: P/E 54.6x (21st percentile of 5y range, -47% vs WELL/VTR/OHI/HR median 102.2x), EV/EBITDA 13.9x (3rd percentile, -29% vs peer 19.5x), EV/Sales 7.4x (below its 5y minimum, -36% vs peers), P/B 1.7x (-38% vs peers) [Certain] (source: stockanalysis.com/stocks/doc/financials/ratios/ and peer statistics pages). The P/FFO-proxy fair-value model gives $19.54-$21.69 (bear-bull), base $21.53 vs the last close of $18.80, implying +14.5% upside even before the bull case [Certain] (source: fact sheet valuation table). Peer multiples are also lifted by different leverage/earnings mixes (e.g. senior-housing operators), so part of the gap may reflect portfolio mix rather than pure mispricing [Likely]. The price appears to assume the leverage build persists and no re-rating toward peer multiples, even as capital recycling (Brookfield JV, Janus Living IPO proceeds) delevers the balance sheet [Likely].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page, site definition) rises above 7.0x, showing the acquisition-funded leverage build is not being repaid with recycling proceeds.
- Same-store cash NOI growth (next earnings release/filing) turns negative, signalling the outpatient/lab/senior-housing demand backdrop has reversed.
- FY2026 adjusted FFO per share guidance (company earnings release) is cut below the raised $1.73-$1.77 range, indicating the Q2 beat was a one-off.
- ROIC (ratios page) falls below 2.0% for two consecutive fiscal years, indicating returns are structurally deteriorating rather than merely lagging a REIT's typical economic-return profile.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Shares outstanding fell 0.69% YoY, so there is no meaningful dilution to Healthpeak's own share count from the Janus Living IPO (source: stockanalysis.com/stocks/doc/statistics/) [Certain]. FCF yield is 2.58% TTM (source: stockanalysis.com/stocks/doc/statistics/); FCF margin and conversion are [data unavailable] on stockanalysis.com. Management is recycling capital rather than raising common equity: the Brookfield JV ($1.025bn proceeds), the Janus Living IPO/follow-on (~$1.69bn gross) and the SWF JV buyout ($312m) were all funded through JV/subsidiary structuring, not new DOC shares [Certain] (source: SEC 10-Q above). Monthly common dividends continue into Q4 2026 [Certain] (source: https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20261001414165/en/Healthpeak-Properties-Declares-Monthly-Common-Stock-Cash-Dividends-for-the-Fourth-Quarter-of-2026/).

## 6. Unusual items
Since March 2026 Healthpeak consolidates the newly public, 73.6%-owned Janus Living, so reported revenue/EBITDA/debt include 100% of Janus Living with a noncontrolling-interest deduction below the line — multiples and margins versus pre-2026 history and non-consolidating peers are not fully like-for-like [Likely] (source: SEC 10-Q above). Short interest rose 13% month-over-month to 3.69% of float, modest and not a red flag on its own [Certain] (source: stockanalysis.com/stocks/doc/statistics/).
