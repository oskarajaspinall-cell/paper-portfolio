## 2. Quality
Returns are high and rising: ROIC 32.4% TTM vs 22.4% a year earlier, though 7.2pp below its 2-year high of 39.6%. Operating margin 58.5% TTM (50.9% FY2025); gross margin 72.3%. [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tsx/DPM/financials/ratios/?p=trailing)
Much of the margin expansion reflects the metal price rather than a moat: a miner is a price taker, and the durable edge is only low cost and grade. [Likely]
FCF margin fell to 46.6% from 59.4% a year earlier, as spending on growth projects rose. [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tsx/DPM/financials/cash-flow-statement/?p=trailing)
Balance sheet is net cash (net debt/EBITDA -0.94x); Altman Z 11.83, Piotroski 7. [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tsx/DPM/statistics/)
Q3 results were released 2026-11-13; the call transcript headline says guidance was on track and Vareš began commercial production early [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tsx/DPM/transcripts/721368-q3-2026/).
