# China Oilfield Services (HKG:2883) — Core initiation — 2026-10-25
## 1. Business
Offshore oilfield services: drilling rigs, well services (logging, directional drilling, cementing), marine support and geophysical survey. Revenue is day-rate and contract work, mostly for offshore E&P operators, chiefly its CNOOC-group affiliates [Likely]. The technology segments carry the profit: 55% of 2025 revenue and 72% of profit (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/2883/transcripts/379581-h2-2025/) [Likely].

## 2. Quality
Returns are modest but improving: ROIC rose from 1.9% (FY2021) to 9.6% (FY2025), 8.8% TTM; operating margin 12.4% TTM against 7.6% in FY2022 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/2883/financials/ratios/) [Certain]. Piotroski F is 7 and net debt/EBITDA has fallen from 2.00x to 0.66x (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/2883/statistics/) [Certain]. FCF conversion is 151.8% of net income, flattered by heavy depreciation on a rig fleet [Likely].
Moat is narrow. The advantage is captive access to the parent's offshore programme and local-content preference, not technology leadership; returns are cyclical and ROE of 8.5% barely clears the cost of equity [Likely]. Quality is "decent and improving", not "compounder". Altman Z of 2.45 is grey-zone [Certain].

## 3. Valuation
P/E 7.8x, EV/EBITDA 4.7x and P/B 1.0x all sit below the bottom of their 5y ranges (P/E min 13.8x, EV/EBITDA min 5.8x, P/B min 1.1x) and 52-63% below the peer median (SLB, HAL, BKR, CNOOC). FCF yield is 12.00% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/2883/statistics/) [Certain].
The reverse DCF implies revenue shrinking 15.0% in year 1 (source: fact sheet fair-value section), a collapse scenario for a business whose revenue and margins have been rising [Likely]. Base fair value 21.43 HKD vs 7.41 close; even bear case 13.55 HKD is far above price [Certain]. Part of the discount is a Hong Kong/China and parent-controlled governance discount, which the owner's 3.5% country premium already loads [Likely].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Operating margin (income statement page) falls below 9% on a TTM basis.
- ROIC (ratios page) falls below 6% on a TTM basis.
- FCF margin (cash flow statement page) falls below 6% on a TTM basis.
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) rises above 2.0x.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Deleveraging has been steady (net debt/EBITDA 0.35x at FY2025) and the share count is shrinking 0.51% a year, so there is no dilution (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/2883/statistics/) [Certain]. FCF margin of 11.5% TTM exceeds net margin of 7.6%, but capex needs for the fleet are high, so FCF overstates distributable earnings [Likely]. Dividend policy is not shown in the fact sheet [data unavailable].

## 6. Unusual items
- Controlled by CNOOC group, also its dominant customer: related-party pricing and minority-shareholder risk [Likely].
- Middle East instability and FX losses flagged as risks on the Q1 2026 call (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/2883/transcripts/623662-q1-2026/) [Likely].
- Oil-price-linked capex cycle; 6-month relative strength vs SPY is -27.2pp [Certain]. Q3 2026 results are listed on the site but not yet analysed here.
