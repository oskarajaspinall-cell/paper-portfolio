# China Oilfield Services (HKG:2883) — Core initiation — 2026-11-27
## 1. Business
Offshore oilfield services: drilling rigs and vessels, technical services (logging, directional drilling, cementing), marine support and survey, sold on day-rate and contract terms, chiefly to CNOOC-group affiliates [Likely]. Technology segments carried 55% of 2025 revenue and 72% of profit (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/2883/transcripts/379581-h2-2025/) [Likely]. Overseas (Norway, Brazil, Central Asia) is a minority of revenue; no primary-source share was fetched [Likely].

## 2. Quality
Returns are modest but rising: ROIC 1.9% (FY2021) to 9.6% (FY2025), 8.8% TTM, up 0.7pp on a year earlier; operating margin 12.4% TTM vs 11.3% a year earlier (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/2883/financials/ratios/?p=trailing) [Certain]. Net debt/EBITDA is 0.66x TTM against 2.00x in FY2022; Piotroski F 7; Altman Z 2.45, grey zone (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/2883/statistics/) [Certain].
Direction change: FCF margin is falling over five fiscal years (-1.8pp) but rose over the last 12 months, 6.2% to 11.5% [Certain]. Management said 9M capex ran below its full-year guidance, with some projects cut or deferred, and operating cash flow turned positive in Q3, so part of the recent FCF gain is capex timing rather than structural [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/2883/transcripts/739591-q3-2026/).
Moat is narrow: captive access to the parent's offshore programme and local-content preference, not technology leadership. Returns are cyclical [Likely].

## 3. Valuation
At 6.47 HKD the stock trades at P/E 6.9x, EV/EBITDA 4.5x, P/B 0.9x and P/FCF 7.9x, every one below the minimum of its own 5y range (P/E min 13.8x, EV/EBITDA min 5.8x, P/B min 1.1x). Against peers (SLB, HAL, CNOOC Ltd, Sinopec Oilfield Service) the discount is 49% on P/E and EV/EBITDA and 63% on P/B. FCF yield is 12.71% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/2883/statistics/) [Certain].
The reverse DCF implies revenue falling 17.8% in year 1 [Certain], at odds with Q3 revenue growth of 3% and management guidance of slight full-year growth [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/2883/transcripts/739591-q3-2026/).
Fair value is flagged unreliable: base 21.69 HKD is +235% above the price (bear 13.70). Weigh it lightly [Certain]. Much of the discount reflects a China/parent-controlled governance discount, already loaded via the 3.5% country premium [Likely].

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Share count is down 0.51% YoY, so no dilution [Certain]. Leverage has fallen steadily. FCF conversion is 151.8% of net income, flattered by depreciation and by capex underspend against guidance; plans to buy drilling vessels remain, so capex should catch up [Likely]. Dividend policy is not on the fact sheet [data unavailable].

## 6. Unusual items
- Why the price fell: no company-specific announcement is listed after the Q3 results of 2026-10-26 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/2883/filings/3875532/). The Q3 call showed steady margins and day rates, but 9M FX losses of CNY 720 million, capex deferrals, a slight Middle East impact and a more demanding five-year-plan timeline [Likely]. The fall is mostly stock-specific (the market fell less). The cause is not identified: I suspect de-rating on oil/offshore sentiment and FX-loss drag, not a change in the business [Guessing].
- The stock is 11.2% below its 50-day and 18.9% below its 200-day average, with 6-month relative strength vs SPY of -16.0pp and RSI 36.6; the 52-week low is 6.09 HKD [Certain].
- Controlled by CNOOC group, also its dominant customer: related-party pricing and minority-shareholder risk [Likely].
