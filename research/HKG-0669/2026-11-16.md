# Techtronic Industries (HKG:0669) — Core initiation — 2026-11-16

## 1. Business
TTI makes cordless power tools (Milwaukee), outdoor power equipment and floorcare (Ryobi, Hoover), sold mainly through large retailers and trade channels. It reports in USD and is quoted in HKD [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/0669/). Milwaukee and Ryobi drove record H1 2026 revenue, profit and margins [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/0669/transcripts/579903-h1-2026/ via the listed headline). ## 2. Quality
Good business, improving lately [Likely]. TTM ROIC is 19.1%, up from 17.2% a year earlier, though below FY2021's 21.0%. Fact-sheet flag: ROIC fell over five fiscal years but has risen over the last 12 months, so the five-year decline looks like a trough rather than a trend (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/0669/financials/ratios/?p=trailing) [Likely]. Gross margin rose from 38.8% to 42.6% TTM, which suggests pricing power and a premium-brand mix [Likely]. Operating margin is flat at 9.1% (FY2021: 8.9%), so the gain stays in gross margin and is not yet reaching operating profit. FCF margin is 12.5% with FCF/NI of 149.8%, so earnings turn into cash. Net cash (net debt/EBITDA -0.32x). Piotroski F is 8 and Altman Z is 4.81. A durable moat in professional cordless tools (battery-platform lock-in) is plausible but not evidenced by the fact sheet [Guessing].

## 3. Valuation
P/E is 25.3x against a 5y median of 21.6x (49th percentile of its range). EV/EBITDA is 17.2x against a 16.5x median (28th percentile). P/FCF is 16.9x (24th percentile). FCF yield is 5.93%. Against peers (SWK, SNA, Makita) it trades 32-33% above on P/E and EV/EBITDA, a premium for higher returns and growth [Likely]. The mechanical fair value is below the price: base 103.07 HKD against a 141.80 HKD close (-27.3%), and bull 113.80 HKD is also below it. The reverse DCF implies 14.7% year-1 revenue growth fading to terminal, a demanding assumption against recent growth [Likely]. The price rests on a trend case: +56.7% over 12 months, 14.4% above the 200-day average, 6-month RS +18.3pp vs SPY. Beta is 1.75. Own-history multiples are mid-range, but the DCF does not support the price [Likely].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Gross margin (income statement page) falls below 40% on a TTM basis, reversing the pricing-and-mix gain.
- TTM ROIC (ratios page, trailing) falls below 15%.
- TTM FCF margin (cash flow page, trailing) falls below 8%, or FCF/NI below 100%.
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) rises above 1.0x, which would end the net-cash position through debt-funded M&A or buybacks.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Net cash has built from 1.31x net debt/EBITDA in FY2021 to -0.32x TTM while the share count is nearly flat (-0.17% YoY) [Certain]. FCF conversion of 149.8% is well above 100%, helped by working-capital release after the FY2021 inventory build (FCF margin -6.4%) [Likely]. Management raised its FCF target at H1 2026 and is running a buyback, per the listed earnings headlines [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/0669/transcripts/579903-h1-2026/). Insiders own 22.24%, which aligns interests [Certain].
