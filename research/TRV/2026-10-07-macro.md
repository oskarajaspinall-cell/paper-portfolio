# Macro overlay: The Travelers Companies, Inc. (TRV) — 2026-10-07
## Assessment
TRV's thesis is underwriting/reserve-driven, but rates touch two macro-sensitive levers: net investment income on its float and the book-value denominator of an already-premium P/B multiple. Chain: rates -> reinvestment yield on TRV's fixed-income float -> net investment income (tailwind if rates rise); rates -> mark-to-market on existing AFS bonds -> book value -> P/B multiple (TRV 2.3x vs peer median 1.8x, above its own 5y max, per factsheet) more exposed to re-rating.

Facts [Certain], FRED, dated 2026-10-05/06: US 10y real yield (DFII10) 2.95%, +71bp ~3m (fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=DFII10); 10y nominal (DGS10) 5.31%, +83bp 3m; 2y (DGS2) 4.84%, +71bp 3m; fed funds (DFF) 3.88%, +25bp 1m/3m; 10y breakeven (T10YIE) 2.36%, +11bp 3m; HY spread (BAMLH0A0HYM2) 3.12%, +40bp 3m, still historically tight.

Market pricing **(inference)**: 2y yield (4.84%) above fed funds (3.88%) is a proxy for the market pricing further tightening/term-premium build, not cuts. Breakeven inflation barely moved (+11bp) while real yields jumped (+71bp), so the back-up looks term-premium/growth-driven, not an inflation scare [Likely] **(inference)**.

Bull: yields stay elevated, no growth scare -> higher reinvestment yields lift net investment income, sustaining ROE/buybacks; P/B premium holds.
Bear: real yields keep climbing on growth fears, or reverse sharply on a hard landing (NII tailwind fades as underwriting normalizes) -> TRV's above-peer P/B and EV/Sales premiums compress.
Tilt: small, toward bear — low beta (0.44) and short duration cap sensitivity, but TRV's premium sits in book value, most exposed to further real-yield moves.
