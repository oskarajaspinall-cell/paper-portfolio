# Texas Instruments (TXN) — Core initiation — 2026-11-16
## 1. Business
Analog and embedded chips sold to industrial, automotive, personal electronics and data-center customers, made in TI's own fabs. Analog is the bulk of revenue, Embedded Processing the next largest [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/97476/000009747626000169/txn-20260930.htm). China is 23% of Q3 2026 revenue and the US 39%, by customer headquarters [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/97476/000009747626000169/txn-20260930.htm). [Certain]

## 2. Quality
Historically excellent, currently recovering. ROIC fell from 73.0% (FY2021) to 21.5% (FY2025) as the heavy 300mm capex cycle raised the capital base and the downturn cut margins; TTM ROIC is back to 30.1% and operating margin to 41.1% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/txn/financials/ratios/). The five-year decline is real, but the last 12 months reverse it: ROIC +9.0pp, operating margin +6.2pp, FCF margin 12.0% to 35.2% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/txn/financials/ratios/?p=trailing). [Certain]

Moat: owned 300mm capacity and a long-tail catalogue with sticky designs-in. Management says broad revenue growth across all end markets signals a cyclical recovery [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/97476/000009747626000169/txn-20260930.htm). [Likely] Part of the margin rebound is operating leverage on a cyclical upturn and may not persist through the next downturn. [Likely] Piotroski 8, Altman Z 11.02. [Certain]

## 3. Valuation
The price assumes a full cyclical recovery and more. P/E 39.4x is above its 5y max of 35.8x, though 10% below the peer median (43.9x). EV/EBITDA 26.4x is above its 5y max and 50% above peers; EV/Sales 13.6x is 122% above peers. P/FCF 37.9x sits at 14% of its 5y range only because FCF was depressed by capex in prior years. FCF yield is 2.64% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/txn/statistics/). [Certain]

The mechanical fair value (base 102.25 against a 303.60 close) is flagged unreliable, with the methods disagreeing 4.4x, so weigh it lightly. The reverse DCF implies 46.9% year-one revenue growth, which the price cannot be underwriting literally. [Likely] Trend is strong: close is 14.7% above the 200-day average and the 12-month change is +86.2%. [Certain] This is a good business that is working, but not cheap.

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- TTM operating margin (income statement, trailing page) falls below 34%.
- TTM ROIC (ratios, trailing page) falls below 21%.
- TTM FCF margin (cash flow, trailing page) falls below 20%.
- Net debt / EBITDA (ratios page) rises above 2.0x, e.g. from the Silicon Labs deal funding.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
TTM FCF conversion is 102.9% of net income versus 52.0% in FY2025, as capex falls. Management guides to lower 2026 capex, helped by CHIPS Act funding [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/97476/000009747626000169/txn-20260930.htm). [Certain] Dividends dominate payouts; buybacks were small this year and share count change is 0.08% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/txn/statistics/). The planned all-cash Silicon Labs acquisition, expected to close in the first half of 2027, is funded with cash and debt, and TI priced $2.0 billion of notes on 2026-10-26 (source: https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/texas-instruments-prices-2-0-billion-of-investment-grade-notes-302920061.html). [Certain] Net debt/EBITDA is 0.51x. Integration and leverage risk is moderate. [Likely]

## 6. Unusual items
- China concentration: 23% of revenue (section 1), so macro overlay should check CNY and US-China trade and export policy. [Certain]
- Pending Silicon Labs acquisition needs regulatory approval, including in China [Likely] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/97476/000009747626000169/txn-20260930.htm).
- The 10-Q risk factors show no substantive change (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/97476/000009747626000169/txn-20260930.htm). The MD&A removes the line that TI was "mostly through" its elevated capex cycle. [Certain]
- Q4 revenue guidance beat expectations, led by industrial and data center (source: https://www.reuters.com/business/analog-chipmaker-texas-instruments-sees-quarterly-sales-above-estimates-2026-10-21/). [Likely]
- Short interest 2.01% of float; beta 1.39. [Certain]
