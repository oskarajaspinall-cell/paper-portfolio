# Macro overlay: Aon plc (AON) — 2026-10-08
## Assessment
Chain: rising long-end yields and widening credit spreads → higher coupon on the ~$17.0bn all-new-debt USI financing (closing Q4 2026) → deeper near-term EPS dilution and slower deleveraging; partially offset by rising short rates → higher fiduciary investment income on client premium float AON holds before remitting to insurers [Likely] **(inference)**.

Facts (FRED, official): 10y Treasury yield 5.27% on 2026-10-06, +0.49pp/1m, +0.72pp/3m; 10y real yield (DFII10) 2.91%, +0.48pp/1m, +0.61pp/3m; 10y breakeven 2.36%, +0.01pp/1m, +0.11pp/3m — the long-yield rise is almost entirely real yield, not inflation expectations [Certain] (https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=DGS10, DFII10, T10YIE, 2026-10-06/07). Fed funds 3.88%, +0.25pp both windows [Certain] (DFF). US HY credit spread 3.03%, +0.35pp/1m, widening [Certain] (BAMLH0A0HYM2).

Market pricing (proxy, **inference**): 2s10s has steepened from ~0.36pp three months ago to 0.48pp now, consistent with less-dovish near-term pricing rather than imminent cuts [Likely]. Widening HY spreads proxy tighter conditions into AON's note window, though AON itself is investment-grade (Baa2/A-) [Likely].

Bull: yields/spreads stabilize before the new notes price → financing cost lighter than feared, dilution milder, multiple (bottom of its 5y range) re-rates toward peers. Bear: yields and HY spreads keep widening into Q4 close → higher coupons, deeper dilution through 2027, slower deleveraging below the 2.50x path. Tilt: toward bear, moderate — financing risk is dated and sized to an already-dilutive deal; the fiduciary-income offset is real but likely smaller than the financing-cost delta on debt this size [Guessing] **(inference)**.
