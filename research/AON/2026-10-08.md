# Aon plc (AON) — Core initiation — 2026-10-08

## 1. Business
Aon is a global professional-services firm that brokers insurance and reinsurance risk between corporate/carrier clients and underwriters, and advises on human-capital, health and retirement programs, earning fee and commission income [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/aon/). It is asset-light: it does not underwrite risk on its own balance sheet [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/aon/).

## 2. Quality
The franchise has strengthened structurally: operating margin is 28.5% TTM (+10.0pp over 5y), net margin 22.3% (+11.2pp) and ROIC 16.1% TTM (+3.2pp over 5y), with gross margin rising to 48.2% [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/aon/financials/income-statement/, https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/aon/financials/ratios/). The brokerage/consulting model benefits from recurring client relationships, embedded switching costs and scale among a narrow set of global peers (MMC, WTW, AJG, BRO), a durable structural advantage [Likely].

The main threat to quality is the balance sheet, not the franchise. Net debt/EBITDA is already 2.50x TTM (down from 4.00x in FY2021 but still leveraged) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/aon/financials/ratios/), and FCF conversion (FCF/NI) has fallen from 162.9% in FY2021 to 83.0% TTM (-75.9pp over 5y) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/aon/financials/cash-flow-statement/, https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/aon/financials/income-statement/), so cash is covering net income less fully even before the debt-funded USI deal described in Unusual items [Certain].

## 3. Valuation
At the 270.47 USD close on 2026-10-07, AON trades on 14.9x TTM P/E, below its own 5y range (20.5x-52.8x) and 27% below the peer median of 20.3x (MMC, WTW, AJG, BRO) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/aon/financials/ratios/, peer statistics pages). EV/EBITDA of 12.2x is close to the peer median (12.4x, -2%) and well below its own 5y range (16.3x-30.8x) [Certain]. EV/Sales (4.1x, +4% vs peers) and P/FCF (17.7x, +6% vs peers) sit near peer levels, while P/B (6.0x) is +100% above the peer median (3.0x) [Certain] — a less meaningful gauge for an asset-light, buyback-shrunk-equity business [Likely]. FCF yield is 5.66% [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/aon/statistics/).

The discount is concentrated in earnings-based multiples (P/E, P/FCF vs own history) rather than EV-based multiples, which sit near peers — consistent with the market pricing integration execution risk and higher leverage from the USI deal rather than a broad franchise re-rating [Likely].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) stays above 4.0x beyond FY2027, showing post-USI deleveraging is not on track.
- FCF conversion, FCF/NI (cash flow + income statement pages), falls below 70% TTM, showing cash generation deteriorating further.
- Operating margin (income statement page) falls below 24%, showing pricing or integration-cost pressure.
- ROIC (ratios page) falls below 12% TTM, showing returns are not sustained on the larger capital base.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
FCF margin is 18.5% TTM (5y range 16.8%-24.2%) and FCF conversion (FCF/NI) is 83.0% TTM, down from 162.9% in FY2021 [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/aon/financials/cash-flow-statement/, https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/aon/financials/income-statement/). Net debt/EBITDA is 2.50x TTM [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/aon/financials/ratios/). Shares outstanding fell 1.08% YoY on buybacks [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/aon/statistics/), but Aon has now suspended repurchases to fund the ~$17.0bn all-debt USI acquisition (expected to close Q4 2026), prioritizing debt repayment and a stable/growing dividend while defending its Baa2 (Moody's)/A- (S&P) ratings [Certain] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/315293/000119312526375328/d108801dex991.htm).

## 6. Unusual items
On 31 Aug 2026 Aon announced a $17.0bn (net $16.7bn) acquisition of USI Insurance Services from KKR, funded entirely with new debt across a range of maturities, expected to close in Q4 2026 pending regulatory approval [Certain] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/315293/000119312526375328/d108801dex991.htm). Aon targets $395m of annual run-rate net adjusted EBITDA synergies but expects adjusted EPS accretion only from 2028, i.e. the deal is dilutive through 2027 [Certain] (same source). Buybacks are suspended to prioritize deleveraging [Certain] (same source) — a multi-year balance-sheet and integration risk layered on top of the quality franchise above.

Short interest rose 9.7% month-over-month to a still-low 1.77% of float [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/aon/statistics/) — not material on its own. Separately, Aon's new "Power Lifecycle Program" (28 Sep 2026) adds up to $2.5bn of capacity for gas-power/data-centre infrastructure risk, a product-line expansion rather than a balance-sheet item [Likely] (source: https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/aon-launches-power-lifecycle-program-to-support-conventional-gas-power-projects-powering-digital-infrastructure-growth-302890617.html).
