# Largan Precision (TPE:3008) — Core initiation — 2026-11-16
## 1. Business
Taiwan-listed maker of optical lens modules, classed as Electronic Components (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tpe/3008/) [Certain]. It earns high margins from precision lenses sold into consumer electronics [Likely]. Revenue mix by customer, product or country is [data unavailable] in the fact sheet and was not fetched, so any foreign-country share of ~10%+ is unverified.

## 2. Quality
Exceptional balance sheet and returns, but eroding. Net debt/EBITDA is -4.13x (net cash) and TTM ROIC is 29.7%. Gross margin fell from 59.9% (FY2021) to 47.2% TTM and operating margin from 49.3% to 35.5% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tpe/3008/financials/income-statement/) [Certain]. ROIC is down 7.7pp in 12 months and 15.6pp below its 2-year high (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tpe/3008/financials/ratios/?p=trailing) [Certain]. FCF margin is up 1.3pp over 12 months to 32.5%, and FCF conversion is 89.0%, so cash earnings hold up better than accounting margins [Certain]. The durable-moat case rests on lens-making know-how, but falling margins over five years show pricing power is weakening [Likely]. Piotroski F is only 5 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tpe/3008/statistics/) [Certain].

## 3. Valuation
The price assumes a lot. P/E is 28.0x against a 5y range of 12.0x–21.4x and 18.3x for peers (+53%); EV/EBITDA is 18.8x against a 5y max of 11.8x; P/B is 3.6x, above its 5y max (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tpe/3008/financials/ratios/) [Certain]. FCF yield is 2.91%. The mechanical base fair value is 2,615.86 TWD, -50.7% versus the 5,310.00 close, and even the bull case (4,790.84) is below the price. The reverse DCF implies 32.4% year-1 revenue growth (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tpe/3008/) [Certain]. The shares rose 137.6% in 12 months, and sit 14.7% below the 50-day average after a 7,995.00 high on 2026-09-01. Margins are falling while the multiple expands, so the price discounts a recovery that the reported numbers do not yet show [Likely].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Operating margin (TTM, income-statement trailing page) falls below 33%.
- ROIC (TTM, ratios trailing page) falls below 25%.
- FCF margin (TTM, cash-flow trailing page) falls below 25%.
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) rises above 0x, i.e. net cash is lost.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Share count is down 1.82% year on year (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tpe/3008/statistics/), consistent with modest buybacks [Likely]. FCF conversion is 89.0% TTM against 64.6% in FY2025, with a 5-year range of 55.6%–158.9% [Certain]. Dividend policy [data unavailable] in the fact sheet.
