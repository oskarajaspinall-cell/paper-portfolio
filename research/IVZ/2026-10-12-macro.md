# Macro overlay: Invesco Ltd. (IVZ) — 2026-10-12
## Assessment
Chain: real yields → risk appetite for duration-sensitive growth equities (Nasdaq-100; QQQ is 19% of IVZ's AUM, ETFs/Index 29%) → equity market levels → IVZ's AUM and fee revenue; real yields also set IVZ's own cost of equity (beta 1.63 [Certain], factsheet:ST), amplifying any multiple move [Likely] **(inference)**.

Market pricing — facts: US 10y real yield (DFII10) 2.87% on 2026-10-08, +56bp over 3m; US 10y nominal (DGS10) 5.22%, +68bp 3m; 10y breakeven (T10YIE) 2.33%, roughly flat (-4bp 1m, +9bp 3m) [Certain, fred.stlouisfed.org, 2026-10-08/09]. The nominal-yield rise is almost entirely real, i.e. tightening financial conditions, not an inflation surprise [Likely]. VIX is low/falling (15.41, -0.43/3m) and HY spreads are contained (3.15%, +45bp/3m) [Certain, FRED] — no risk-off yet. 2y yield (4.75%) above fed funds (3.88%) is a proxy that near-term cuts aren't priced [Guessing]. IVZ rallied +23%/12m and +25%/6m, beating SPY, over the same window real yields climbed 56bp — it re-rated alongside, not despite, rising real yields, suggesting the valuation risk higher real yields pose to its Nasdaq-100-linked AUM isn't priced [Guessing] **(inference)**.

Bull: real yields plateau/ease, risk appetite persists, QQQ/Nasdaq-100 AUM keeps growing, fee revenue/FCF margin rise further, supporting re-rating. Bear: real yields extend higher, growth-equity multiples compress, equity markets fall, AUM (29% ETF/Index, 19% QQQ) marks down, fee revenue falls, and IVZ's high-beta multiple compresses on a higher cost of equity.
