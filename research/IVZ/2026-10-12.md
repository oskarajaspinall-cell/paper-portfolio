# Invesco Ltd. (IVZ) — Core initiation — 2026-10-12
## 1. Business
Invesco is a global asset manager earning fees on AUM ($2.17tn at FY2025-end), split retail 70% / institutional 30% [Certain] (10-K). By asset class: ETFs/Index 29% (incl. QQQ 19%), fixed income 14%, fundamental equities 14%, China JV 6%, private markets 6%, liquidity 9% [Certain] (10-K). Revenue is "substantially all" investment-management fees, so it scales with AUM level and with the fee rate of the product mix [Certain] (10-K).

## 2. Quality
TTM ROIC 9.1%/ROCE 5.2% are modest for an asset-light fee business; the 5y trend is flat-to-falling (ROIC -0.8pp, ROCE -1.1pp) [Certain] (factsheet:RA). GAAP profitability is unreliable: net margin was negative in FY2023 (-1.7%), FY2025 (-4.4%) and TTM (-0.9%), and ROE is negative TTM (-0.6%), even as FCF margin is healthy and rising (27.8% TTM vs 14.1% FY2021), pointing to non-cash or one-off charges rather than a cash problem [Likely]; the specific cause is not shown on stockanalysis.com [data unavailable]. The 10-K cites scale, 120+-country distribution and independence from distribution conflicts as advantages, but also discloses fee compression as clients shift to passive/ETF and alternatives [Certain] (10-K). ~48% of AUM sits in ETFs/Index, including the single QQQ Trust (19%), a concentration tied to Nasdaq-100/AI-stock performance [Likely] (10-K). Scale is real, but larger low-cost rivals cap pricing power in its mix [Likely].

## 3. Valuation
At 29.51 USD, EV/EBITDA is 9.5x (in line with the 9.5x peer median; 24th percentile of its own 5y range), but EV/Sales 2.0x is 23% below peers and below its own 5y min, and P/FCF 6.8x is 64% below the 18.6x peer median and below its own 5y min [Certain] (factsheet). FCF yield is 14.74% [Certain] (factsheet:ST). P/E is unavailable (TTM EPS negative/missing) [data unavailable]. P/B 1.3x is 34% below peers but 199% above its own 5y max (0.5x-0.9x), as book value shrank in the negative-earnings years [Likely]. The gap between a rich P/B and cheap P/FCF/EV/Sales shows the market is pricing earnings and book-value erosion, not crediting the cash engine; re-rating needs net margin/ROE durably positive, not just FCF [Likely]. Fair value is not computable: both DCF (negative base cash-flow margin) and P/E (missing TTM EPS) are blocked [Certain] (factsheet).

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Operating margin (income statement page) stays below 15% for two consecutive fiscal years (below the FY2023 trough), showing fee compression overwhelming scale benefits.
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) rises above 1.5x (vs 0.49x TTM / 0.93x FY2025), showing balance-sheet strain.
- ROIC (ratios page) falls back below 5% for two consecutive fiscal years (its FY2023/24 level), undoing the FY2025/TTM recovery.
- Net margin (income statement page) stays negative for a third consecutive fiscal year, confirming the charges are recurring, not one-off.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
FCF/NI is not a usable gauge while net income is negative/erratic (FCF/NI swung to -2,977.8% TTM) [Certain] (factsheet:CF,IS); FCF margin itself is strong and rising (27.8% TTM, from 14.1% FY2021) [Certain]. Net debt/EBITDA is low at 0.49x TTM (0.93x FY2025) [Certain] (factsheet:RA), and ~$2.5bn of perpetual preferred stock remains outstanding [Certain] (10-K). The 10-K states a focus on "financial flexibility" and operating leverage but the excerpt reviewed does not disclose a specific dividend/buyback split [data unavailable: payout detail]. Shares outstanding fell 1.04% YoY, consistent with net buybacks exceeding dilution [Certain] (factsheet:ST).

## 6. Unusual items
Short interest rose 17.9% month-on-month to 3.67% of float [Certain] (factsheet:ST) — modest in absolute level but rising. Beta is high at 1.63, and Altman Z-score (1.16) sits in the model's distress zone, though Z-score is poorly calibrated for an asset manager's balance sheet [Likely] (factsheet). MassMutual can "significantly influence" the business as Invesco's largest shareholder [Certain] (10-K) — a governance overhang separate from the AUM/QQQ concentration noted in Quality.
