# Union Pacific Corporation (UNP) — Core initiation — 2026-10-10

## 1. Business
Union Pacific runs the largest Class I freight railroad in the western United States, moving bulk (coal, grain), industrial and intermodal freight over a 23-state network that cannot practically be duplicated. Revenue is per-carload/ton-mile pricing under limited rail competition; the largely fixed network gives high operating leverage. [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/unp/)

## 2. Quality
A genuinely high-quality business: ROIC 16.0% and ROCE 15.8% (TTM) have held a tight 14.7–17.0% band for five years, operating margin is 40.8% and FCF conversion 88.7% TTM — all well above typical industrials [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/unp/financials/ratios/, https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/unp/financials/income-statement/, https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/unp/financials/cash-flow-statement/). The moat is durable: a second transcontinental network cannot be built, and US rail is a stable Class-I duopoly/oligopoly in most corridors — advantages no competitor can take away [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/unp/). Margins and cash conversion have nonetheless drifted down over five years (operating margin -2.3pp, FCF conversion -16.4pp), partly fuel-cost related [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/unp/financials/income-statement/, https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/unp/financials/cash-flow-statement/). Net debt/EBITDA of 2.26x is mid-range and manageable today [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/unp/financials/balance-sheet/). Sitting on top of this stable core is a proposed $85bn Norfolk Southern combination, which would materially change execution, integration, financing and regulatory risk versus the standalone business [Likely] (source: https://www.up.com/, https://www.foxbusiness.com/markets/union-pacific-ceo-makes-case-85b-norfolk-southern-merger-amid-pushback).

## 3. Valuation
At the last close of 278.34, the stock trades 56% above the DCF-based base fair value of 178.57 and 31% above even the bull case of 211.62; the reverse DCF shows the price implies 15.3% revenue growth in year one fading to terminal growth, above UNP's own trend [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/unp/ fact sheet fair value table). Peer multiples are mixed: P/E 22.5x is 17% below the 4-peer median and P/FCF 25.4x is 32% below, but P/B 8.0x is 78% above the peer median and sits in the bottom 14% of UNP's own 5-year P/B range — priced for returns well above its own history [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/unp/statistics/, peer statistics pages for CSX, NSC, CNI, CP). There is no margin of safety: on the fact sheet's own DCF the current price already assumes execution beyond the standalone business — it is partly pricing in an unapproved, contested merger [Likely].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROIC (ratios page) holds above 17% for two consecutive fiscal years, closing the gap the current P/B premium implies.
- Operating margin (income statement) stabilises above 41% for two consecutive quarters, reversing the five-year decline.
- Net debt/EBITDA (balance sheet + income statement) stays below 2.8x through any Norfolk Southern deal financing, showing the balance sheet isn't stretched to fund the deal.
- FCF conversion (FCF/net income, cash flow + income statement) recovers above 85% on a trailing-twelve-month basis.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
UNP converts earnings to cash well (FCF margin 25.6% TTM, FCF conversion 88.7% TTM) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/unp/financials/cash-flow-statement/). Shares outstanding fell 1.50% year over year, consistent with continued buybacks [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/unp/statistics/). The proposed $85bn Norfolk Southern acquisition would be the largest capital-allocation decision in the company's history; neither stockanalysis.com nor UP's own site discloses financing mix or synergy quantification beyond qualitative network-benefit claims [data unavailable]. The eventual financial impact is [Guessing] (source: https://www.up.com/).

## 6. Unusual items
Material M&A event risk: UNP is pursuing an $85bn acquisition of Norfolk Southern that management says would create a combined railroad with roughly 40% of US ton-miles and 43% of carloadings [Likely] (source: https://www.up.com/); labor unions and farm groups are opposing the deal and Surface Transportation Board approval is not assured [Certain] (source: https://www.foxbusiness.com/markets/union-pacific-ceo-makes-case-85b-norfolk-southern-merger-amid-pushback). Short interest rose 5.7% month-on-month to 4.63% of float — a modest but rising negative signal [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/unp/statistics/).
