# Endeavour Mining (TSX:EDV) — Core initiation — 2026-10-24
## 1. Business
Endeavour is a West African gold producer (Côte d'Ivoire and neighbouring jurisdictions, per the company's own presentations). It sells gold at the spot price, so revenue tracks the gold price times ounces. Profit is the margin between that price and all-in cost. Growth is expected from the Assafou and Sabodala-Massawa projects (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tsx/EDV/transcripts/751891-mining-forum-americas-2026/) [Likely].
## 2. Quality
Returns have swung widely: ROIC was -20.7% in FY2024 and 35.3% TTM; operating margin went from 14.9% to 43.2% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tsx/EDV/financials/ratios/). That is a price-driven margin expansion, not evidence of a moat [Likely]. A gold miner is a price-taker; cost position and reserve life are the only durable edges, and neither is on stockanalysis.com [data unavailable]. Positives: net debt/EBITDA is -0.06x, Piotroski F is 8, Altman Z is 5.05, and FCF margin is 30.2% TTM (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tsx/EDV/statistics/) [Certain]. Negative: operating in higher-risk jurisdictions with a fatality at Ity on 7 October (source: https://www.tipranks.com/news/the-fly/endeavour-mining-reports-death-at-ity-mine-thefly-news) [Certain]. Overall: good balance sheet, cyclical returns at peak-like levels [Likely].
## 3. Valuation
Multiples look cheap on the surface: EV/EBITDA 4.3x is below its 5y minimum of 4.4x and 33% under peers; P/FCF 8.5x is at the 5y minimum; FCF yield is 11.74% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tsx/EDV/statistics/). But these rest on peak-cycle earnings [Likely]; P/E of 14.9x is 47% above the peer median and P/B of 3.3x sits at 87% of its 5y range. The mechanical fair value (EV/EBITDA, NAV unavailable) is base 44.77 CAD, bear 37.54, bull 88.06 against a 71.89 CAD close, so base is 37.7% below the price and only the bull case supports it (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tsx/EDV/). The price assumes gold-price strength persists [Likely]. No margin of safety on base case [Certain].
## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Operating margin (income statement page) falls below 30% on a trailing-twelve-month basis.
- FCF margin (cash flow page) falls below 15% TTM.
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) rises above 1.0x.
- Shares outstanding (statistics page) up more than 5% year on year (dilution).
## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
FCF conversion is 171.0% of net income TTM and share count is up only 0.18% year on year (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tsx/EDV/statistics/) [Certain]. Management cites nearly $2 billion of shareholder returns since 2021 and record H1 free cash flow (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tsx/EDV/transcripts/751891-mining-forum-americas-2026/) [Likely]. Disciplined, but the FCF is cycle-high.
## 6. Unusual items
Short interest is 1.27% of float, and institutions own 63.9%. Next results are due on Nov 12, 2026 (source: https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/10/14/3380331/0/en/endeavour-to-announce-its-q3-2026-results-on-12-november-2026.html). Gold-price and West African sovereign/permit risk are the main exposures [Likely]. Lean AVOID: quality fine, price not.
