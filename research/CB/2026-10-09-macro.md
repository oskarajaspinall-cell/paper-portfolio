# Macro overlay: Chubb Limited (CB) — 2026-10-09
## Assessment
Chubb's bond float and its CAPM-based fair value both key off risk-free rates, so this is **secondary**, not background noise. Chain: rising long-dated yields → higher reinvestment yield on CB's bond portfolio → net investment income tailwind; higher risk-free rate → higher CAPM discount rate → multiple compression. These largely offset. [Likely] **(inference)**

Facts: 10y Treasury yield 5.28% (2026-10-07, FRED DGS10), up +0.72pp/3m; 10y real yield (TIPS) 2.92%, up +0.61pp/3m (FRED DFII10, 2026-10-07); effective fed funds 3.88%, up +0.26pp/3m (FRED DFF). Breakeven inflation flat at 2.35% (FRED T10YIE, 2026-10-08) — the move is real-rate, not inflation-expectations, driven. [Certain]

Market pricing: the 2y yield (4.77%) sits ~0.9pp above fed funds (3.88%), a proxy for the market pricing policy staying restrictive rather than near-term cuts. [Likely] **(inference)**. CB's fair-value model already uses today's 5.28% risk-free rate and still shows +10.3% base-case upside, so the rate move is largely priced in. HY credit spreads widened modestly (+0.39pp/3m to 3.09%, FRED BAMLH0A0HYM2) — not yet stress-level for an IG-heavy insurer.

**Bull**: yields stay elevated without a credit event → NII compounds, modest EPS tailwind, multiple pressure contained given CB's low beta (0.37). **Bear**: a disorderly rate move (spread blowout or fast cut cycle) → NII reverses or bond mark-to-market losses hit book value. **Tilt**: small, toward bull — rates have already risen a lot with no credit stress and CB's thesis is fundamentals-led (buybacks, ROE), not rate-dependent.
