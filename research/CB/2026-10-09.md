# Chubb Limited (CB) — Core initiation — 2026-10-09

## 1. Business
Chubb underwrites commercial and personal property & casualty, specialty (financial lines, A&H) and life insurance across 54 countries and territories [Certain] (source: https://about.chubb.com/). It earns premiums and invests the float; profit is underwriting margin plus net investment income, net of claims reserves. It is the #1 U.S. commercial-lines P&C insurer and one of the largest financial-lines writers globally [Certain] (source: https://about.chubb.com/).

## 2. Quality
ROIC has risen from 8.3% (FY2021) to 11.1% (TTM) and ROE has held a 14-16% band since FY2023 (TTM 14.8%) [Certain] (fact sheet, RA). Net debt/EBITDA has fallen from 2.59x (FY2021) to 1.68x (TTM, own calc) / 1.51x (TTM, site definition) [Certain] (fact sheet, BS/IS/RA) — a well-capitalized, deleveraging balance sheet. The moat is scale and diversification (#1 U.S. commercial lines, 54-country spread limiting single-peril or single-regulator exposure) and underwriting discipline through cycles [Likely] (source: https://about.chubb.com/). But P&C insurance pricing power is cyclical, not durable: the Q2 2026 earnings call cited "softening market conditions and rising loss costs in casualty" even as core operating income rose 14.6% y/y [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/cb/transcripts/652923-q2-2026/, headline listed in fact sheet). Standard margin/FCF quality checks (gross/operating/net margin, FCF margin and conversion, ROCE) are [data unavailable] for this insurer on stockanalysis.com, limiting the cross-check to ROIC/ROE/leverage.

## 3. Valuation
P/E is 12.2x, +16% above the peer median (10.4x; TRV/AIG/ALL/PGR) but only the 31st percentile of its own 5y range (9.8x-17.5x) [Certain]. P/B is 1.8x, -16% below the peer median (2.1x) but near the top of its own 5y range (87th percentile) [Certain] — the market is pricing current ROE as broadly sustainable, not a cheap entry. EV/EBITDA gives conflicting signals (10.4x is below Chubb's own 5y range yet +56% above the peer median of 6.7x) and is a poor fit for an insurer's capital structure, so it is given little weight versus P/B and P/E [Certain]. Base-case fair value (P/B+ROE blended 67% with P/E 33%, CAPM discount using the 5.28% risk-free rate and 0.37 beta) is $379.09, +10.3% vs the $343.78 last close; bear case is $250.23 (-27.2%), bull $385.88 (+12.2%) [Certain] (fact sheet). The skew (-27% bear vs +12% bull) reflects reserve/pricing-cycle risk rather than a margin of safety.

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROE (ratios page) falls and stays below 10% for two consecutive quarters, signalling the underwriting/investment engine has broken.
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) rises back above 2.5x, eroding the balance-sheet buffer that funds underwriting through a soft market.
- ROIC (ratios page) reverses its five-year rise and falls back below 8%, matching the FY2021-22 trough.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Shares outstanding fell -2.49% y/y [Certain] (fact sheet, ST), implying net buybacks exceed issuance, alongside falling leverage (net debt/EBITDA 1.68x TTM vs 2.59x FY2021) — capital is being returned while the balance sheet strengthens. A quarterly dividend was declared for an October 2026 payment, consistent with an unchanged capital-return policy [Certain] (source: https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/chubb-limited-board-declares-quarterly-dividend-302850922.html, headline listed in fact sheet; amount not used as it is not a stockanalysis.com figure). FCF margin and FCF/NI conversion are [data unavailable] for this insurer on stockanalysis.com, so cash conversion cannot be verified directly; capital adequacy is inferred from the leverage and ROE trends above instead.

## 6. Unusual items
None material. Short interest is low and falling (1.03% of float, -5.9% month-on-month) and beta is unusually low (0.37) even for an insurer [Certain] (fact sheet, ST) — neither changes sizing.
