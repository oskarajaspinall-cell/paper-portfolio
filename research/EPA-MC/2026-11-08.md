# LVMH Moët Hennessy - Louis Vuitton (EPA:MC) — Core initiation — 2026-11-08
## 1. Business
Luxury conglomerate: fashion and leather goods (Louis Vuitton, Dior), wines and spirits, perfumes, watches and jewellery, selective retailing. Profit is concentrated in leather goods pricing power and brand desirability (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/epa/MC/) [Likely]. Chinese consumer demand is a stated swing factor in recent coverage (source: https://nypost.com/2026/10/23/business/luxury-loses-its-sheen-as-lvmh-kering-and-hermes-stocks-all-plunge-amid-declining-sales/) [Likely]. ## 2. Quality
Still a high-return business, but eroding. ROIC fell from 19.6% (FY2021) to 12.4% TTM; operating margin from 26.7% to 21.8%; gross margin from 68.3% to 66.4% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/epa/MC/financials/ratios/) [Certain]. Gross margin above 66% still indicates real brand pricing power [Likely]. Over the last year the decline has flattened: ROIC -0.8pp, operating margin +0.2pp, FCF margin +0.7pp against a year earlier (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/epa/MC/financials/ratios/?p=trailing) [Certain]. But ROIC is 4.2pp and operating margin 3.7pp below their 2-year highs, so the peak-to-now de-rating of returns is real, not noise [Certain]. A fashion and leather goods organic sales decline in Q3 shows demand is not yet recovering (source: https://www.bangkokpost.com/business/investment/3338245/lvmh-shares-drop-as-fashion-and-leather-goods-sales-decline-by-5-in-q3) [Likely]. Moat: brand portfolio and scale; Altman Z 3.36, Piotroski F 6 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/epa/MC/statistics/) [Certain].

## 3. Valuation
At 408.75 EUR the stock trades below its 5y minimum on every multiple: P/E 18.6x (5y range 24.1x-30.4x), EV/EBITDA 9.8x (15.6x-20.7x), P/FCF 14.0x (22.3x-33.5x), FCF yield 7.13% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/epa/MC/statistics/) [Certain]. Versus peers (Hermès, Kering, Richemont, Moncler) P/E is 40% lower and EV/EBITDA 29% lower [Certain]; peers include higher-return names, so part of the discount is deserved. Reverse DCF: the price implies only 1.5% revenue growth in year 1, fading to terminal growth [Certain]. Base fair value 476.05 EUR (+16.5%), bear 334.48 (-18.2%), bull 673.06 (+64.7%) [Likely]; the bear case sits near the October low. Skew is favourable but depends on returns stabilising at current levels, not on a return to the 2023 peak [Guessing].

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
FCF conversion is 132.1% of net income TTM and FCF margin 18.0%, so earnings are backed by cash (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/epa/MC/financials/cash-flow-statement/) [Certain]. Net debt/EBITDA is 1.24x, moderate [Certain]. Shares outstanding are down 0.61% YoY, a small net buyback [Certain]. FCF recovered from 12.7% of sales in FY2023 to 18.0%, helped by working capital, which may not repeat [Guessing].

## 6. Unusual items
- Controlled by the founding family; insiders hold 0.50% and institutions 17.2% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/epa/MC/statistics/), so minority holders have limited influence [Likely].
- China consumer exposure and French political turmoil are the named macro risks (source: https://www.barrons.com/articles/france-companies-global-stocks-bargains-d2b6919c) [Likely]; the macro overlay should check China.
- Watchmaker Zenith is reported to be cutting jobs, a sign of weakness in watches (source: https://www.reuters.com/business/retail-consumer/lvhm-owned-watchmaker-zenith-cut-jobs-shift-upmarket-source-says-2026-10-23/) [Likely].
- Price is 12.1% below the 200-day average and 12-month change is -33.3% [Certain]; next earnings Jan 25, 2027 (estimate), so no results catalyst before then.
