# LVMH Moët Hennessy - Louis Vuitton (EPA:MC) — Core initiation — 2026-11-17
## 1. Business
Luxury conglomerate: fashion and leather goods (Louis Vuitton, Dior), wines and spirits, watches and jewellery, perfumes and selective retailing. Profit is driven by pricing power in a few flagship houses. ## 2. Quality
Still a high-margin franchise, but returns have eroded. ROIC fell from 19.6% (FY2021) to 12.4% TTM and operating margin from 26.7% to 21.8%; gross margin is 66.4%, still elite (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/epa/MC/financials/ratios/). [Certain] The last year is flat (ROIC -0.8pp, operating margin +0.2pp, FCF margin +0.7pp), so the decline has stopped, not reversed; ROIC is 4.2pp below its 2-year high (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/epa/MC/financials/ratios/?p=trailing). [Certain] Net debt/EBITDA is 1.24x and Altman Z 3.36 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/epa/MC/statistics/). The moat is brand scarcity and scale in distribution; nine-month organic growth was described as slow, with China weak and Europe, US and Japan supporting (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/epa/MC/transcripts/716218-q3-2026/). [Likely] Brand-level price cuts at rivals are a risk to pricing power (source: https://www.wsj.com/business/retail/gucci-is-cutting-prices-louis-vuitton-is-reluctant-to-follow-the-trend-50e4b218). ## 3. Valuation
The price (404.00 EUR at the last fact-sheet close) sits near the entry level, after a 12-month decline of 38.3%; the share is 12.2% below its 200-day average. Every multiple sits below its 5-year minimum: P/E 17.9x vs 24.1x low, EV/EBITDA 9.5x vs 15.6x, P/FCF 13.6x vs 22.3x; FCF yield 7.36% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/epa/MC/statistics/). [Certain] Versus peers it is about 43-59% cheaper on each multiple, but the peer set includes Hermès, whose premium is structural. Mechanical fair value: base 463.50 EUR (+14.7% vs the 404.00 close), bear 327.27, bull 653.72; the reverse DCF implies 2.2% year-1 revenue growth (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/epa/MC/). [Likely] The price assumes returns do not recover; the bear case is 19.0% below the last close, so downside is real if China keeps slowing. Fair value is one model estimate.
