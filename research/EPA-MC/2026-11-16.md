## 2. Quality
Still a high-return business, but returns have fallen. ROIC went from 19.6% (FY2021) to 12.4% (TTM). Operating margin went from 26.7% to 21.8%. Gross margin of 66.4% is still very high [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/epa/MC/financials/ratios/). The last year looks flat: TTM ROIC is down 0.8pp, and operating margin is up 0.2pp. Both remain well below their 2-year highs (ROIC 16.6%, operating margin 25.5%) [Certain]. FCF margin of 18.0% is the TTM high and FCF conversion is 132.1% of net income. Net debt/EBITDA is 1.24x and Altman Z is 3.36 [Certain]. The moat is brand scarcity and pricing power. A WSJ headline says the sector is weighing price cuts, and that Louis Vuitton is reluctant to follow [Guessing] (source: https://www.wsj.com/business/retail/gucci-is-cutting-prices-louis-vuitton-is-reluctant-to-follow-the-trend-50e4b218). A moat that holds is still plausible [Likely], but the margin fall since FY2023 is real.

## 3. Valuation
The price assumes little. P/E is 18.4x against a 5y range of 24.1x–30.4x, so it is below the 5y minimum. EV/EBITDA is 9.8x against 15.6x–20.7x. P/FCF is 13.9x. FCF yield is 7.17%. Against peers (Hermès, Kering, L'Oréal), P/E is about 47% lower and EV/EBITDA about 45% lower [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/epa/MC/statistics/). Much of that gap is probably Hermès's premium and L'Oréal's quality [Likely].
Mechanical fair value is 464.54 EUR base, 15.1% above the 403.45 close, with a bear case of 327.87 and a bull case of 655.33. The DCF is 419.26 and the P/E method 555.11 [Certain]. The reverse DCF implies 2.1% year-1 revenue growth. The multiples are on trough-ish earnings, so cheapness partly reflects falling margins. The price is 12.5% below the 200-day average and 36.6% below a year ago, so the trend is down [Certain].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Operating margin (income statement, TTM) falls below 19%.
- ROIC (ratios page, TTM) falls below 10%.
- Gross margin (income statement, TTM) falls below 63%, which would show pricing power eroding.
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) rises above 2.0x.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Cash conversion is strong: FCF/net income is 132.1% TTM, against 72.0% in FY2023. The share count is down 0.61% year on year, so there is a small net buyback [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/epa/MC/statistics/). Leverage is moderate at 1.24x.
