# Wolters Kluwer N.V. (AMS:WKL) — Core initiation — 2026-10-18

## 1. Business
Wolters Kluwer sells subscription research, software and workflow tools to professionals across legal & regulatory, tax & accounting, health, and finance, risk & compliance (sector/industry: Industrials / Specialty Business Services) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/ams/WKL/). Revenue is predominantly recurring digital subscriptions (CCH, UpToDate, Libra, Tagetik brands, per recent product-launch headlines) [Likely] (source: https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20261007978787/en/Wolters-Kluwer-brings-AI-powered-drafting-directly-into-the-Libra-legal-workspace/).

## 2. Quality
Returns are high and rising: ROIC 24.8% TTM, up 6.8pp over five years; ROCE 27.6% TTM, up 9.7pp [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/ams/WKL/financials/ratios/). Gross margin (74.1% TTM), operating margin (25.5% TTM) and net margin (21.9% TTM) are all at five-year highs and still rising [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/ams/WKL/financials/income-statement/). The moat is workflow/content switching costs: regulatory, clinical and tax content embedded in daily professional workflows, reinforced by the steady cadence of AI feature launches (Libra, Tagetik, TaxWise) that extend rather than disrupt the installed base [Likely] (source: https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20261005207096/en/Wolters-Kluwer-launches-agentic-tax-preparation-in-TaxWise-helping-tax-preparers-increase-return-preparation-capacity/). FCF margin is high (22.8% TTM) though FCF conversion (FCF/NI) has fallen from 144.5% to 104.2% over five years as net income has caught up with cash generation — still full conversion, not a red flag [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/ams/WKL/financials/cash-flow-statement/). Net debt/EBITDA has drifted up from 1.50x to 2.08x, a moderate increase consistent with ongoing capital return rather than deteriorating operations [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/ams/WKL/financials/ratios/).

## 3. Valuation
Every multiple sits below its own five-year range and well below the peer median (RELX, Thomson Reuters, S&P Global, Experian): P/E 13.9x vs peer 24.8x (-44%); EV/EBITDA 11.1x vs 17.3x (-36%); EV/Sales 3.6x vs 6.2x (-41%); P/FCF 13.1x vs 19.4x (-33%) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/ams/WKL/financials/ratios/). The reverse DCF implies the price assumes -0.4% year-one revenue growth fading to terminal growth, below the rising-ROIC quality profile above [Certain] (source: fact sheet DCF). Base fair value EUR117.66 implies +44.3% upside; the bear case (EUR80.48) sits almost exactly at the last close (EUR81.52, -1.3%), so the model's downside case is close to priced in [Certain] (source: fact sheet fair value). P/B (19.3x) is +282% above peers (5.1x) even as P/E is far below them — a thin equity base from buybacks, not higher earnings power, so should not be read as expensive [Likely].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROIC (ratios page) falls below 20% for two consecutive fiscal years — the moat is eroding faster than priced.
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) rises above 3.0x — capital return turning into balance-sheet risk.
- FCF margin (cash flow/income statement pages) falls below 18%, versus 22-24% normal range — cash generation weakening.
- Revenue growth (income statement page) is negative for two consecutive fiscal years — confirms the market's near-zero-growth pricing rather than a temporary dip.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
FCF margin is 22.8% TTM with FCF conversion of 104.2% of net income — full cash backing of earnings, though conversion has declined from 144.5% five years ago as net income growth outpaced FCF growth [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/ams/WKL/financials/cash-flow-statement/). Shares outstanding fell 3.36% year-on-year, consistent with ongoing buybacks [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/ams/WKL/statistics/). The company completed a capital reduction on 2026-09-18, returning capital to shareholders [Likely] (source: https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/09/18/3364828/0/en/wolters-kluwer-completes-capital-reduction.html). Net debt/EBITDA has risen to 2.08x from 1.50x over five years, funding this capital return — still a moderate level but worth monitoring against the trigger in Section 4 [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/ams/WKL/financials/ratios/).

## 6. Unusual items
ROE has spiked to 146.1% TTM from 111.7% in FY2025, a +79.3pp five-year move, while net margin rose only 6.1pp over the same period [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/ams/WKL/financials/ratios/). This is consistent with the capital reduction shrinking the equity base rather than an operating step-change [Likely], and explains the P/B anomaly in Section 3; it is a structural feature of a heavily-bought-back balance sheet, not a distress signal, but means ROE/P/B are not comparable to peers' more book-heavy balance sheets. Separately, the stock is down 25.7% over the past 12 months despite a beta of only 0.18, with no headline in the fact sheet's news window explaining the decline (the listed items are all routine product launches and PR) [Guessing] — the cause of the prior drawdown (now partly retraced, +30% over 3 months) is a data gap worth the evaluator's attention before sizing.
