## 2. Quality
High-return, high-margin franchise [Certain]. TTM ROIC is 24.8% (rising from 18.6% in FY2021), ROCE 27.6%, gross margin 74.1% and operating margin 25.5%, both at 5-year highs (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/ams/WKL/financials/ratios/). The rolling-12-month table shows ROIC up 1.6pp and flat operating and FCF margins (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/ams/WKL/financials/ratios/?p=trailing). Moat: embedded regulatory and clinical content plus workflow lock-in [Likely]. The 12-month share price change is -2.9% despite improving fundamentals. The market is probably pricing AI-disruption risk to content and software seats [Guessing]. Recent launches (agentic tax, MCP server, AI clinical decision support via the AgileMD acquisition) show it is responding [Likely] (source: https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/11/13/3402862/0/en/wolters-kluwer-to-acquire-ai-powered-clinical-decision-support-platform-agilemd.html). Beta is 0.20.

## 3. Valuation
The price is 90.84 EUR. P/E is 15.5x against a 5-year range of 15.3x–36.8x (median 30.7x), and EV/EBITDA is 12.1x, below its 5-year minimum of 12.5x (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/ams/WKL/statistics/). Peer median P/E is 29.4x and EV/EBITDA 19.2x, so WKL trades about 47% and 37% below peers (RELX, Thomson Reuters, S&P Global, Verisk) [Certain]. FCF yield is 6.87%. The reverse DCF says the price implies only 2.1% year-1 revenue growth, fading to terminal growth [Certain]. Mechanical base fair value is 116.24 EUR (+28.0%), with bear 79.39 (-12.6%) and bull 128.12 [Likely]. The stock has re-rated sharply (3-month +33.0%, 6-month +59.1%, 17.7% above its 50-day average, RSI 65.6), so part of the discount has closed. It is still cheap against its own history and peers. P/B of 21.5x reflects buyback-shrunk equity, not valuation.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Shares outstanding are down 3.36% YoY, so buybacks are active (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/ams/WKL/statistics/). Net debt/EBITDA has risen from 1.50x in FY2021 to 2.08x, which is buyback and M&A funded; this is acceptable at the current level [Likely]. FCF conversion of net income has fallen from 144.5% to 104.2%. Part of that is higher net margin, but it is the weak spot [Likely]. Bolt-on deals continue (AgileMD) alongside pruning (the German consumer tax divestiture) (source: https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/10/30/3392049/0/en/wolters-kluwer-to-divest-german-consumer-tax-business.html) [Certain].
