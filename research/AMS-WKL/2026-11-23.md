## 2. Quality
Strong. ROIC is 24.3% TTM vs 18.6% in FY2021; ROCE 27.6%; gross margin 74.1% and operating margin 25.5% have both risen five years running (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/ams/WKL/financials/ratios/, https://stockanalysis.com/quote/ams/WKL/financials/income-statement/) [Certain]. Last-12-month ROIC is flat (-0.6pp vs a year earlier) and operating and FCF margins are flat, so improvement has plateaued; no direction-change flag beyond that (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/ams/WKL/financials/ratios/?p=trailing) [Certain]. Moat: embedded workflow software and proprietary content with high switching costs [Likely]. Key debate is generative-AI disintermediation of information products; the 12-month price change of -9.7% and the June low of 54.64 EUR reflect that fear [Likely]. Company is shipping AI products across divisions and bought AgileMD, a clinical decision-support platform (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/ams/WKL/, headline list in fact sheet) [Likely]. Piotroski F is 6 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/ams/WKL/statistics/) [Certain].
