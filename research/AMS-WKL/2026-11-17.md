## 2. Quality
A high-return, high-margin franchise. ROIC is 24.8% TTM, up from 18.6% in FY2021; gross margin is 74.1% and operating margin 25.5%, both rising over five years (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/ams/WKL/financials/ratios/). [Certain] Rolling-12-month ROIC is +1.6pp year on year, operating and FCF margins flat, so no direction break (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/ams/WKL/financials/ratios/?p=trailing). [Certain]
Moat: proprietary content and regulated workflows with high switching costs. [Likely] Weaker points: FCF conversion has fallen to 104.2% from 144.5% in FY2021, and net debt/EBITDA has risen to 2.08x from 1.50x (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/ams/WKL/financials/balance-sheet/). [Certain] Main debate is whether generative AI erodes content and seat-based software; the company is shipping agentic tax and MCP-server products (source: businesswire links in the fact sheet). [Guessing]

## 3. Valuation
P/E is 15.0x against a 5-year range of 15.3x–36.8x (median 30.7x), so it sits below its own minimum; EV/EBITDA is 11.9x against 12.5x–22.2x; P/FCF is 14.3x. Peer medians are 29.3x, 19.8x and 21.8x, discounts of 49%, 40% and 34% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/ams/WKL/statistics/). FCF yield is 7.01%. [Certain]
Mechanical fair value base is 115.98 EUR (bear 79.19, bull 127.82), +27.7% vs the 90.84 close of 2026-11-16 (DCF 98.71, EV/EBITDA 150.53). The reverse DCF implies 2.2% year-1 revenue growth fading to terminal, which is undemanding for a business with this margin record. [Likely]
The multiple is low partly because the price fell sharply before recovering: 12-month change is -6.6%, but 3-month is +32.6% and the price is 33.7% above its 200-day average (low 54.64 in June, 6-month high 93.38). Much of the derating has already reversed. [Certain]

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROIC (ratios page, TTM) falls below 18%, or operating margin (income statement, TTM) falls below 23%.
- FCF conversion (FCF/NI, cash-flow page) stays below 90% for a full fiscal year, or net debt/EBITDA (balance sheet) rises above 3.0x.
- Organic growth in a trading update or results release (company RNS) turns negative, i.e. AI substitution showing up in revenue.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Shares outstanding fell 3.36% year on year, so buybacks are material (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/ams/WKL/statistics/). [Certain] Leverage has crept up (2.08x) as buybacks and bolt-ons run ahead of cash generation; FCF margin is flat at 22.8%. M&A is small and AI-focused: AgileMD (clinical decision support) and the German consumer tax business disposal (source: https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/11/13/3402862/0/en/wolters-kluwer-to-acquire-ai-powered-clinical-decision-support-platform-agilemd.html; https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/10/30/3392049/0/en/wolters-kluwer-to-divest-german-consumer-tax-business.html).
