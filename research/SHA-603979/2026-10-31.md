## 2. Quality
Returns have risen every year. TTM ROIC is 26.3% against 11.2% in FY2021, and TTM ROE is 25.5% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sha/603979/financials/ratios/) [Certain]. Operating margin rose from 15.7% to 27.9% TTM, and gross margin from 26.6% to 36.8% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sha/603979/financials/income-statement/) [Certain]. The balance sheet has net cash on TTM figures (net debt/EBITDA -0.23x), and Piotroski F is 7 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sha/603979/statistics/) [Certain].

Is the margin gain durable? A contractor's edge rests on switching costs and operating know-how in hard jurisdictions. [Guessing] Part of the lift likely reflects a commodity cycle, which would pass through its owned-resource earnings and contract pricing. Contract miners are normally price-takers, and the sustainability of a 27.9% operating margin is unproven. FCF conversion is volatile (-105.0% in FY2022, 126.1% in FY2025, 62.9% TTM).

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
The business was a net cash consumer in FY2022 and FY2023 (FCF margin -12.0% and -7.9%), consistent with heavy capex to build capacity. It turned strongly cash-generative in FY2024 and FY2025. TTM FCF conversion has slipped to 62.9%. Shares outstanding rose 2.26% YoY, a mild dilution (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sha/603979/statistics/) [Certain]. Reinvestment returns look high, but conversion is not yet consistent.
