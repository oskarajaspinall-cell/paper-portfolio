# Evolution AB (STO:EVO) — Core initiation — 2026-11-15
## 1. Business
Evolution supplies live-dealer casino and game-show content to licensed online casino operators, earning a revenue share and fees from them rather than facing players. It reports in EUR and is quoted in SEK (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sto/EVO/). Regional mix is [data unavailable]: the company site was blocked and the fact sheet has no split. Q1 and Q3 2026 call summaries name Brazil, Latin America, North America, Europe and Asia as relevant regions [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sto/EVO/).

## 2. Quality
Very high returns and cash generation: ROIC 32.1% TTM, ROCE 31.1%, FCF margin 59.3%, FCF conversion 116.5% of net income, and net cash (net debt/EBITDA -0.44x) (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sto/EVO/financials/ratios/) [Certain]. The moat is scale in live-studio capacity and game content, which small rivals struggle to match; this is my judgement [Likely].

Direction is soft. Operating margin is 57.0% TTM vs 59.1% a year earlier and 62.1% at its 2024 peak (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sto/EVO/financials/income-statement/?p=trailing). ROIC is 2.5pp below its 2-year high; FCF margin is 3.0pp below its high. Returns are still extreme, but the margin trend is down and growth is slow: Q1 revenue fell and Q3 grew modestly, per call summaries (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sto/EVO/transcripts/552836-q3-2026/) [Likely]. The Altman Z of 6.92 is strong, while the Piotroski F of 4 is middling.

## 3. Valuation
At 871.00 SEK the stock trades at 14.3x P/E, 10.5x EV/EBITDA and 11.3x P/FCF, all in the bottom 10% of their 5-year ranges. FCF yield is 8.87% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sto/EVO/statistics/) [Certain]. Peers sit at a median 29.5x P/E (n=1) and 16.0x EV/EBITDA, so EVO is at a 35-51% discount, though the peers are lower-margin operators and the comparison flatters EVO [Likely].

The mechanical base fair value is 1,320.92 SEK (+51.7%), bear 888.76 and bull 1,814.22. The reverse DCF says the price implies -1.4% revenue growth in year 1. The market is pricing a regulatory-driven plateau or decline [Likely]. The price is 23.7% above its 200-day average with a +33.9% 12-month change, so this is a business that is working and not a washed-out value case [Certain].

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Capital allocation is shareholder-friendly. Shares outstanding fell 4.06% YoY through buybacks, and a large buyback programme was launched in July, continuing at a slower pace in Q3 per call summaries (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sto/EVO/transcripts/551131-q2-2026/) [Likely]. The company holds net cash, and FCF exceeds net income at 116.5% conversion [Certain]. Reinvestment needs are low, and returns on capital stay above 30%. The risk is paying up for buybacks while margins drift down, though the current multiples make buybacks accretive [Likely].
