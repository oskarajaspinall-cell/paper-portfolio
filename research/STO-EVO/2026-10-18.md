# Evolution AB (publ) (STO:EVO) — Core initiation — 2026-10-18

## 1. Business
Evolution AB is a B2B supplier of live-dealer casino and RNG (random-number-generator) games, streaming tables from its own studios and licensing slot/table content (via the NetEnt and Red Tiger acquisitions) to online gambling operators worldwide [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sto/EVO/). Revenue is largely a royalty/commission on operators' gross gaming revenue, so growth tracks regulated online-casino volumes plus EVO's own content market share [Likely].

## 2. Quality
Returns are high and still rising: ROIC climbed from 22.7% (FY21) to 31.2% TTM, ROCE from 18.5% to 26.9%, ROE from 20.5% to 26.7% [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sto/EVO/financials/ratios/). The moat is studio/content scale plus multi-jurisdiction gambling licensing — a high regulatory barrier few B2B suppliers can replicate — reinforced by new exclusive titles management calls best-sellers (Hasbro-branded Monopoly games, Ice Fishing) [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sto/EVO/transcripts/551131-q2-2026/). But margins are compressing: operating margin fell 2.9pp over five years to 57.8% TTM and net margin fell 5.3pp to 51.8%, which management ties to tightening European gambling taxation/regulation and to Asian competitors illegally copying and redistributing EVO's product [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sto/EVO/transcripts/551131-q2-2026/; figures: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sto/EVO/financials/income-statement/). Despite this, cash generation keeps improving — FCF margin rose to 60.2% TTM and FCF conversion to 116.3% [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sto/EVO/financials/cash-flow-statement/) — so the margin pressure has not yet reached the cash line.

## 3. Valuation
EVO trades at 13.6x P/E and 10.0x EV/EBITDA, near the bottom of its own five-year range (8th and 4th percentile) and 60%/16% below the peer median (Playtech, IGT, Kambi) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sto/EVO/statistics/). P/FCF of 11.1x is 19% below peers. EV/Sales (+202%) and P/B (+57%) screen above peers, but that reflects EVO's far higher margins rather than a richer valuation — P/E, EV/EBITDA and P/FCF are the cleaner comps [Likely]. The mechanical reverse DCF implies the price is pricing in a -3.7% year-1 revenue decline fading to terminal growth, which sits awkwardly against still-rising ROIC and FCF margin [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sto/EVO/). Base fair value of 1,320.58 SEK is 65.1% above the last close (800.00 SEK); even the bear case (888.09 SEK) sits 11% above it [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sto/EVO/). The gap looks too wide to be explained by margin compression alone and is more plausibly tied to the unresolved ownership situation in Unusual items.

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROIC (ratios page) falls below 20% for a full fiscal year (vs 31.2% TTM) — source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sto/EVO/financials/ratios/
- Operating margin (income statement) falls below 50% (vs 57.8% TTM, already down from 61.3% FY21) — source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sto/EVO/financials/income-statement/
- FCF conversion, FCF/NI (cash flow & income statement) falls below 90% for two consecutive fiscal years (vs 116.3% TTM) — source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sto/EVO/financials/cash-flow-statement/
- Net debt/EBITDA, site definition (ratios page) turns positive, i.e. the net-cash position is lost (vs -0.85x TTM) — source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sto/EVO/financials/ratios/

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Cash conversion is strong and improving (FCF/NI 116.3% TTM, up from 93.4% FY21) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sto/EVO/financials/cash-flow-statement/), and the balance sheet carries net cash, not debt (net debt/EBITDA -0.85x TTM) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sto/EVO/financials/ratios/). Shares outstanding fell 3.23% year-on-year, consistent with active buybacks [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sto/EVO/statistics/); management also described what it called the largest share buyback programme in Nasdaq Stockholm's history, launched in May 2026 [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sto/EVO/transcripts/551131-q2-2026/). Combined with rising returns on capital, this signals spare capacity and management confidence despite the margin softness noted above.

## 6. Unusual items
An investment vehicle linked to Kenneth Dart ("Candle Lake") crossed Sweden's mandatory-bid ownership threshold in July 2026 and separately made an unsolicited approach to buy Evolution, both reported below the current share price of 800.00 SEK [Likely] (sources: https://www.wsj.com/business/media/kenneth-darts-candle-lake-crosses-mandatory-bid-threshold-for-swedens-evolution-b0dfc2c4, https://www.reuters.com/business/candle-lake-offers-buy-online-casino-firm-evolution-14-billion-2026-08-13/). No further headline on the bid's outcome appears in the fact sheet's news window through this date, so whether it lapsed, was topped, or remains live is [Guessing]. Consistent with a large holder, insider ownership is unusually high at 34.37% versus 20.5% institutional [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sto/EVO/statistics/). This ownership/takeover overhang is a governance risk to monitor even though it is not a core-thesis trigger. Short interest is [data unavailable].
