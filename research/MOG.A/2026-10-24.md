# Moog Inc. (MOG.A) — Core initiation — 2026-10-24
## 1. Business
Moog designs and makes precision motion-control components and systems for commercial aircraft, military aircraft, space and defense, and industrial markets (including data-center cooling pumps). Revenue comes from OEM programs plus higher-margin aftermarket spares and repair (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/67887/000162828026051294/mog-20260627.htm). [Certain]

## 2. Quality
Returns are improving: ROIC rose from 8.2% (FY2021) to 13.6% (TTM) and operating margin from 8.4% to 12.0% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/mog.a/financials/ratios/). Gross margin is flat at about 28%, so the margin gain is mostly operating leverage. [Certain] Switching costs on flight-qualified, sole-source content are a plausible moat, but returns are still modest and FCF margin has been volatile (-1.1% in FY2023, 8.1% TTM) (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/mog.a/financials/cash-flow-statement/). [Likely]
Part of the TTM margin is not repeatable: the company booked $30 million of IEEPA tariff refunds as a reduction of cost of sales [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/67887/000162828026051294/mog-20260627.htm). Backlog rose in every segment, led by defense, space and data-center pumps. [company-reported] [Certain]

## 3. Valuation
The price assumes a lot. EV/EBITDA is 18.8x against a 5y range of 9.0x–15.5x, and EV/Sales and P/B are also above their 5y maxima (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/mog.a/financials/ratios/). P/E of 29.0x is 89% of the way up its range. Versus peers CW, WWD, HEI and TDG, it is 14–16% cheaper on P/E and EV/EBITDA, but the peers are higher-quality, higher-margin franchises (TDG especially). [Likely]
The fact sheet's base fair value is 152.05 against a 341.34 close, and even the bull case (219.61) is below the price (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/mog.a/). The reverse DCF needs 20.7% year-one revenue growth. FCF yield is 3.24%. That is a demanding bar for a business with about 10–12% operating margins. [Likely]

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Operating margin (income statement page) falls below 10.0% on a TTM basis.
- FCF margin (cash-flow/statistics pages) falls below 3.0% on a TTM basis.
- ROIC (ratios page) falls below 10.0%.
- Net debt / EBITDA (ratios page) rises above 2.5x.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
TTM FCF/net income is 92.9%, up from 54.6% in FY2025, and net debt/EBITDA has fallen to 1.71x from 2.72x (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/mog.a/financials/balance-sheet/). Shares are down 0.36% year on year, so there is modest buyback with no dilution. Management says it will prioritise organic investment and opportunistic acquisitions [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/67887/000162828026051294/mog-20260627.htm).
