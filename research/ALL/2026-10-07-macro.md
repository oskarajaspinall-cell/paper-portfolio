# Macro overlay: The Allstate Corporation (ALL) — 2026-10-07
## Assessment
Allstate is a P&C insurer with very low broad-market beta (0.13) [Certain] (factsheet, stockanalysis.com), so the operative macro channel is not the equity risk premium but the yield earned on its fixed-income investment portfolio funded by policyholder float. **(inference)** Chain: policy rate path → reinvestment yield on the bond book → net investment income → EPS — a secondary add-on to the fundamental, non-macro thesis (cyclical ROE/earnings-multiple reversion).

Facts [Certain], all FRED: 10y real yield (DFII10) 2.95%, +71bp over 3m (2026-10-05); 10y nominal (DGS10) 5.31%, +83bp over 3m (2026-10-05); effective fed funds (DFF) 3.88%, +25bp over both 1m and 3m — a hike, not a cut (2026-10-05); 10y breakeven (T10YIE) 2.36%, roughly flat, +11bp (2026-10-06); HY credit spread (BAMLH0A0HYM2) 3.12%, +40bp over 3m (2026-10-05). Source: https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=<series>.

Market pricing (proxy, **(inference)**): the 2y yield (4.84%) sits ~96bp above effective fed funds (3.88%), implying the curve prices further tightening ahead rather than the cutting cycle markets had leaned toward — a hawkish surprise versus recent expectations.

Bull [Likely]: continued rate rises lift reinvestment yields on Allstate's bond portfolio, adding to net investment income and partly offsetting underwriting-margin mean reversion. Bear [Likely]: credit-spread widening (+40bp/3m) continuing toward genuine risk-off would mark down the available-for-sale portfolio and could coincide with softer consumer demand. Tilt: toward bull, small — rate moves modestly help earnings but don't change the core cyclical-reversion thesis or its already deep P/E discount (4.5x vs 10.2x peer median).
