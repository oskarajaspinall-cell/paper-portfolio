# The Allstate Corporation (ALL) — Core initiation — 2026-10-07

## 1. Business
Allstate underwrites personal auto and homeowners insurance (plus commercial lines and Allstate Protection Plans) in the US, earning premiums priced against claims/expenses and investing policyholder float [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/all/). Distribution is agent-led plus direct/independent channels under its "Transformative Growth" strategy [Likely].

## 2. Quality
Profitability has swung hard: ROE went from -6.3% (FY2022) to 39.5% (FY2025) and 46.1% TTM; ROIC from -4.9% to 32.6% TTM [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/all/financials/ratios/). This is the P&C underwriting cycle, not a permanent edge: 2022 losses came from unrepriced inflation in auto claims severity, and the current TTM figures reflect post-repricing margins that are historically elevated and can mean-revert as competition re-enters on rate. Recurring, lumpy catastrophe losses are structural to the homeowners book (see Unusual items) and pressure margins in any given quarter regardless of underlying pricing strength. Allstate's moat is scale in claims handling, brand and a large agent network, which support pricing/renewal retention but do not insulate margins from the cycle or from state rate regulation. Balance sheet quality is a genuine strength: net debt/EBITDA is low and falling (0.44x TTM vs 1.04x FY2021) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/all/financials/ratios/), giving flexibility to keep buying back stock through soft patches.

## 3. Valuation
At 4.5x P/E, ALL trades below its own 5-year range (5.4x-22.5x) and 56% below the 4-peer (PGR, TRV, CB, HIG) median of 10.2x; EV/EBITDA (3.7x) is similarly 53% below peer median and below its own 5y floor [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/all/financials/ratios/, peer statistics pages). P/B (1.8x) is a smaller discount to peers (-11%) and sits mid-range historically. The gap between an extreme earnings-multiple discount and a much smaller book-value discount suggests the market is treating current TTM earnings (46.1% ROE) as unsustainably high — i.e., pricing in reversion toward a more normal ROE closer to its own 5y pattern, rather than assigning Allstate a genuine quality premium. FCF yield of 21.6% [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/all/statistics/) is high but FCF conversion has been volatile (92.1% TTM vs 186.9% FY2024), so the cash-based valuation case is less clean than the P/E alone implies.

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROE (ratios page) falls and stays below 15% for two consecutive fiscal years, showing the underwriting-cycle tailwind has fully reversed.
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) rises above 1.5x, signalling the balance sheet cushion funding buybacks is eroding.
- FCF conversion, FCF/NI (cash flow + income statement) turns negative for two consecutive quarters, questioning the quality of the reported earnings recovery.
- ROIC (ratios page) falls back below its FY2021 level (15.5%), indicating the current return profile was cycle-driven, not structural.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Shares outstanding fell 1.96% YoY [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/all/statistics/), consistent with ongoing buybacks funded by a conservative balance sheet (net debt/EBITDA 0.44x TTM). Preferred dividends of ~$29.3m were declared payable Oct 15, 2026 [Certain] (source: https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/allstate-announces-preferred-dividends-payable-oct-15-2026-302879727.html, as listed in the fact sheet). FCF conversion is solidly positive TTM (92.1%) but has been volatile across the cycle (-2,106.9% FY2023, 295.6% FY2021), so near-term cash generation should be read with that swing in mind [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/all/financials/cash-flow-statement/).

## 6. Unusual items
Monthly catastrophe-loss disclosures are routine for Allstate's homeowners book, not a red flag: August 2026 estimated cat losses of $748m ($591m after-tax) and July 2026 of $682m ($539m after-tax) were each roughly 1% of market cap [Certain] (source: https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/august-2026-monthly-release-302882017.html, https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/july-2026-monthly-release-302856389.html, as listed in the fact sheet). Short interest is negligible (0.01% of float, up 9% month-on-month) and institutional ownership is high at 85% [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/all/statistics/). None of these change position sizing.
