# The Gap, Inc. (GAP) — Core initiation — 2026-11-09
## 1. Business
Gap Inc. sells mass-market apparel through Old Navy, Gap, Banana Republic and Athleta, in stores and online, and earns a credit-card revenue share. The latest 10-Q names Old Navy Global, Gap Global and Athleta Global as reporting units; Old Navy and Athleta sales fell and Gap sales rose in the second quarter (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/39911/000162828026059345/gap-20260801.htm) [Certain]. No single foreign country is shown as ~10% of revenue in the sources read [Guessing].

## 2. Quality
Mediocre, cyclical, brand-dependent. Apparel retail has weak moats: fashion risk, promotions and tariffs dominate. Recovery is real: ROIC rose from -0.8% (FY2023) to 12.7% (FY2026) and net debt/EBITDA fell to 2.20x TTM (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/gap/financials/ratios/) [Certain]. But the last year points down: ROIC 13.6% to 10.1% and operating margin 7.9% to 6.2% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/gap/financials/ratios/?p=trailing) [Certain]. Gross margin is flat over five years (40.5% TTM). Second-quarter cost of goods sold was helped by net IEEPA tariff recoveries, and management says gross margin may not be indicative of the rest of the year (10-Q above) [Certain]. Net margin of 8.1% TTM is flattered by one-offs [Likely]. Durable moat: [Guessing] only scale in sourcing and Old Navy's value positioning.

## 3. Valuation
The price implies little: reverse DCF needs 1.6% year-1 revenue growth, then fade (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/gap/). P/E 6.5x is below the 5y minimum of 10.8x, and 44% below peers (11.6x). EV/EBITDA 7.3x is at the 5y low and 12% under peers. P/FCF 7.9x, FCF yield 12.68% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/gap/statistics/) [Certain]. Mechanical base fair value 21.85 USD vs close 21.48 USD (+1.7%), bull +48.8%, bear about -100%: the range is wide and the base offers no margin of safety [Certain]. Cheapness partly reflects TTM earnings inflated by non-recurring tariff refunds and a litigation gain, so P/E understates the true multiple [Likely]. Beta 2.06 supports a high discount rate [Certain].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Operating margin (TTM, income statement trailing page) falls below 5%.
- ROIC (TTM, ratios trailing page) falls below 8%.
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) rises above 3.0x.
- Gross margin (income statement page) falls below 38% once tariff recoveries stop flowing through.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Share count fell 1.04% YoY (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/gap/statistics/). FCF conversion was 100.9% in FY2026 but 76.7% TTM; FCF margin 6.2% TTM, up from 5.1% a year earlier [Certain]. The 10-Q shows a tariff refund receivable of $417 million [company-reported] (https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/39911/000162828026059345/gap-20260801.htm), so operating cash flow is flattered by timing and one-offs [Likely].
