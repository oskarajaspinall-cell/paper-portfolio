# Macro overlay: Omnicom Group Inc. (OMC) — 2026-10-12
## Assessment
OMC's thesis is merger-integration recovery, not a macro call, but the bull case leans on a DCF fair value ($147.22 base, +92.5% vs $76.48 close) discounted at the 10y Treasury [Certain] (source: factsheet, https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=DGS10, 2026-10-08). That yield has risen +0.68pp over 3 months to 5.22%, almost entirely a real-yield move (DFII10 +0.56pp to 2.87%, https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=DFII10, 2026-10-08) with breakeven inflation flat-to-down (T10YIE -0.04pp over 1m to 2.33%, https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=T10YIE, 2026-10-09) [Certain]. Chain: real yields → CAPM discount rate → DCF fair-value range. **Market pricing (proxy, not fact):** the 2y yield (4.75%) sits 0.87pp above effective fed funds (3.88%, +0.25pp/1m), implying the market still prices further tightening, not cuts [Likely] **(inference)**. A hawkish surprise would compress the fair-value ceiling further; a dovish surprise (yields back to ~4.5-4.8%, their level 3 months ago) would widen the gap. High-yield spreads widened modestly (+0.45pp to 3.15%, below the 5.0% "stressed" level in this book's framework) and the dollar strengthened (+2.86%/1m on DTWEXBGS, https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=DTWEXBGS, 2026-10-02), a plausible secondary translation headwind on OMC's international billings; geographic mix is not in the fact sheet [Guessing] **(inference)**. **Tilt: small, toward bear** — real yields rising faster than inflation expectations is a genuine discount-rate headwind to the DCF bull case, but OMC's cheap cash multiples (EV/EBITDA 7.9x vs 8.5x peers, FCF yield 11.4%) and contained leverage (2.2x net debt/EBITDA vs a 3.5x covenant) leave a cushion that fundamentals should decide first.
