# Macro overlay: Albemarle Corporation (ALB) — 2026-10-12
## Assessment
ALB is a lithium producer with spot/index-linked revenue and a DCF-heavy fair value (bear case negative, base +82.6% to $101.27) — a long-duration, commodity-sensitive equity where macro can move both earnings and the discount rate [Likely] **(inference)**.

**Facts** (fred.stlouisfed.org, 2026-10-08/10-02): 10y real yield (DFII10) 2.87%, +56bp/3m; 10y Treasury (DGS10) 5.22%, +68bp/3m; 2y (DGS2) 4.75%, +59bp/3m; fed funds (DFF) 3.88%, +26bp/3m; broad dollar index (DTWEXBGS) 121.385, +2.86 (~2.4%)/1m; HY spread (BAMLH0A0HYM2) 3.15%, +45bp/3m; 10y breakeven (T10YIE) 2.33%, -4bp/1m.

**Market pricing (proxy)**: 2y yield sits 87bp above fed funds, consistent with the market pricing further tightening, not near-term cuts [Likely — proxy].

**Inference**: rising real yields, a sharply stronger dollar, and widening HY spreads are a tightening combination that pressures USD-priced commodities (lithium included) and raises the discount rate on back-loaded DCF cash flows — both work against ALB's bull case [Likely] **(inference)**; flat breakevens show this is a real-rate/dollar move, not an inflation-expectations shift.

**Bull**: dollar/real yields roll over → commodity reflation lifts spot lithium pricing and revenue, discount rate falls → fair value drifts toward/above the $184.89 base.
**Bear**: dollar and real yields keep rising → commodity pricing stays soft, discount rate rises further → outcome tracks the reverse-DCF's embedded -5% y1 revenue case toward the (already negative) bear fair value.
**Tilt**: moderate toward bear — the quarter's facts run against reflation/commodity trades, consistent with ALB's -24.7pp 3m underperformance vs SPY, though no FRED series directly prices lithium.
