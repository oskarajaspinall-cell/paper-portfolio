# Albemarle Corporation (ALB) — Core initiation — 2026-10-12

## 1. Business
Albemarle mines/processes lithium (Atacama brine in Chile, Nevada brine, Greenbushes hard-rock in Australia) into hydroxide and carbonate for EV/battery cathodes, sold on a mix of contracted and spot/index-linked pricing (Energy Storage segment) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/alb/). A Specialties segment makes bromine flame retardants and lithium specialties/catalysts; it sold its Refining Solutions (Ketjen) catalyst business in H1 2026 [Certain] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/915913/000091591326000102/alb-20260630.htm).

## 2. Quality
TTM ROIC is 4.1%, still recovering from FY2024's negative return (-4.6%); the 5y trend is -6.4pp [Certain] (source: ratios page). Gross margin collapsed from 42.0% (FY22) to 1.6% (FY24) as lithium prices crashed, recovering to 24.0% TTM [Certain] (source: income statement). FCF margin swung from -18.5% (FY24) to 22.7% TTM, but TTM cash was boosted by $525.2m of divestiture proceeds used to cut long-term debt from $3.119bn to $1.802bn in H1 2026 — not repeatable [Likely] (source: 10-Q). The edge is real but narrow: bottom-quartile-cost brine and Greenbushes give a genuine cost advantage, but lithium is a traded commodity with no pricing power, and Chinese converter oversupply caps margins even in upcycles [Likely]. Management halted Kemerton Train 3 and idled Train 2 given weak lithium prices, after >$726m of FY2024 Energy Storage impairments — rational now, evidence of past capital misallocation [Certain] (source: 10-Q).

## 3. Valuation
TTM P/E of 207.7x is not meaningful given near-zero trailing earnings [Certain] (source: ratios page). EV/EBITDA of 8.3x sits below ALB's own 5y low (9.7x) and is +26% above the peer median of 6.6x (SQM, LAC, PLL, MOS; n=2 with data) [Certain]. EV/Sales (2.1x, +29% vs peers) and P/B (1.5x, +140% vs peers) show a resource/balance-sheet premium peers don't command [Certain]. P/FCF of 8.9x looks cheap against its own 5y range (24.0x-39.3x) and near peer median (9.3x), but TTM FCF is inflated by one-off divestiture cash [Likely]. The DCF+EV/EBITDA blended base fair value is $184.89, +82.6% above the $101.27 close; the reverse DCF implies the price embeds a -5.0% year-one revenue decline fading to terminal growth — a bearish demand assumption [Likely] (source: fact sheet valuation table). The bear-case fair value is negative, so the blend has no credible floor if the cycle stays depressed [Likely].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROIC (ratios page) stays below 5% for two more fiscal years, showing continued through-cycle value destruction.
- Gross margin (income statement) fails to recover above 25% as lithium prices stabilize, below its historical through-cycle level.
- Net debt/EBITDA, site definition (ratios page) rises back above 3x, reversing the H1 2026 deleveraging.
- Further Energy Storage impairments (10-Q disclosures) exceed the $70-90m already guided through 2027.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Management used $525.2m of divestiture proceeds to cut long-term debt from $3.119bn to $1.802bn, leaving $1.632bn cash at quarter-end [Certain] (source: 10-Q). It repurchased notes below par for a $12.5m debt-extinguishment gain [Certain] (source: 10-Q). Capex discipline improved: Kemerton Train 3 stopped, Train 2 in care and maintenance, with $70-90m more decommissioning charges guided into 2027 [Certain] (source: 10-Q). Shares outstanding rose 7.95% YoY [Certain] (source: statistics page) — dilution from downturn-era raises, a cost of surviving the trough.

## 6. Unusual items
Short interest is 9.45% of float, up 8.6% in the past month [Certain] (source: statistics page) — sentiment stays bearish despite the deleveraging. Ragnar Udd (ex-BHP mining executive) becomes President & CEO effective February 1, 2027, with Kent Masters moving to Executive Chairman [Certain] (source: https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/albemarle-announces-ceo-succession-plan-302868256.html). A mining-industry hire signals a resource-first strategic emphasis — a leadership-transition risk to watch over the next two quarters [Guessing].
