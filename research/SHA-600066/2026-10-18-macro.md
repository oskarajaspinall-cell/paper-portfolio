# Macro overlay: Yutong Bus Co.,Ltd. (SHA:600066) — 2026-10-18
## Assessment
Yutong's thesis is domestic-demand- and multiple-driven (fleet-renewal/subsidy cycle, cheap P/E vs history/peers); exports to Asia/Africa/Latin America/the Middle East are named in the note as "the swing factor" **(inference)** [Likely]. Chain: global dollar/financing conditions and CNY -> export order economics and RMB-translated revenue -> earnings; discount-rate sensitivity is weak since beta (5y) is 0.13 [Certain] (stockanalysis.com statistics page), consistent with an A-share priced on domestic flows, not global ERP.

**Facts** (FRED, all dated 2026-10-15/16 unless noted): US 10y Treasury 5.10% (+0.53pp/3m); 2y Treasury 4.77% (+0.61pp/3m); fed funds 3.88% (+0.25pp/3m); US HY spread 3.23% (+0.52pp/3m, widening); broad dollar index 121.6 (2026-10-09, +1.1/3m); yuan/USD 6.692 (2026-10-09, -0.0846/3m, RMB modestly firmer).

**Market pricing (proxy):** 2y yield 0.89pp above fed funds implies little priced near-term easing **(inference)** [Likely] — a headwind for global liquidity and EM buyer financing in Yutong's export markets.

**Inference:** widening HY spreads and a firmer dollar are a modest, not dominant, headwind to the export swing factor; mild RMB strength is a small drag on translated export revenue, but neither changes the base case (domestic margin/ROIC durability). No China PBOC/CPI series sits in the FRED snapshot, so the bigger domestic subsidy-cycle driver isn't macro-overlay-observable here.

**Bull:** dollar/spreads ease -> cheaper EM financing -> export orders firm, adding a few points to revenue growth. **Bear:** dollar/spreads keep widening -> EM buyers delay orders, export growth turns negative, pressuring the re-rating case. **Tilt:** small, toward bear.
