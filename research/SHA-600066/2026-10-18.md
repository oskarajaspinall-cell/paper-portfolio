# Yutong Bus Co.,Ltd. (SHA:600066) — Core initiation — 2026-10-18

## 1. Business
China's largest bus and coach manufacturer: large/medium/light diesel, NEV and fuel-cell buses and coaches, plus after-market parts and service, sold to domestic transit operators/governments and exported to Asia, Africa, Latin America and the Middle East [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sha/600066/).

## 2. Quality
Returns have re-rated sharply: ROIC 88.5% (FY2025), 213.2% TTM, ROE 38.8% TTM, up from near-zero in FY2021-22 [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sha/600066/financials/ratios/). Gross margin rose each year to 25.6% TTM, operating margin to 13.7% TTM [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sha/600066/financials/income-statement/). TTM ROIC is extreme vs trend, likely reflecting a shrinking invested-capital base (net cash build) as much as operating gains [Likely].

The moat is scale and network, not hard technology: China's #1 bus maker by volume, with a nationwide sales/service/parts network costly for smaller rivals (e.g., King Long) to replicate, and a decade-built export franchise new entrants cannot buy quickly [Likely]. NEV/fuel-cell subsidy expertise and municipal-tender relationships add switching costs [Likely]. Main threat: cyclicality, as domestic demand depends on fleet-renewal/subsidy cycles and export growth is the swing factor [Likely]. Net debt/EBITDA -1.99x TTM (net cash every year shown) funds the network without leverage risk [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sha/600066/financials/ratios/).

## 3. Valuation
At 11.7x TTM P/E the stock trades below its own 5y range (13.0x-40.6x), in line with peers (11.5x median, n=3) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sha/600066/financials/ratios/). P/FCF 7.3x sits near the bottom of its 5y range, near peers (6.4x) [Certain]. P/B 4.4x is at the top of its own 5y range and above peer median (1.5x), consistent with ROE far above peers, not hype [Likely]. The EV/EBITDA peer comparison (1.8x median, n=2) looks distorted by a thin peer set, not used [Certain] (source: fact sheet valuation table).

The reverse DCF embedded in the close implies only 2.9% year-1 revenue growth fading to terminal growth — a modest bar [Certain] (source: fact sheet fair-value table). Base fair value CNY31.50 is +9.7% above the CNY28.71 close; bear/bull (CNY8.22/CNY78.56) bracket a wide range given the ROIC extremity above [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sha/600066/). Overall: cheap vs history, in line with peers on P/E, modest embedded growth — modestly favorable, not a re-rating case.

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Gross margin (income statement page) falls below 20% for two consecutive quarters, reversing the 5y uptrend (currently 25.6% TTM, 5y range 18.1%-23.7% at fiscal year-ends).
- ROIC (ratios page) falls below 15% on a TTM basis, signalling the return expansion was transitory (currently 213.2% TTM, 88.5% FY2025).
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) moves from net cash to net debt above 1.0x, indicating the balance sheet buffer funding the service/export network has eroded (currently -1.99x TTM).
- FCF margin (cash flow/income statement pages) falls below 5% for two consecutive fiscal years, indicating cash conversion has broken down (currently 21.4% TTM, FY2025 6.0%).

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
The business is self-funding: FCF margin 21.4% TTM and FCF/NI conversion 160.6% TTM, both improved from FY2021 lows [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sha/600066/financials/cash-flow-statement/). Conversion is lumpy year to year (FY2025 dipped to 44.4% from 158.3% in FY2024), consistent with working-capital swings typical of order-driven bus manufacturing rather than a structural issue [Likely]. The company carries net cash in every year shown (net debt/EBITDA -1.50x to -9.27x across FY2021-25), leaving capacity for shareholder returns or export-capacity investment without raising debt [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sha/600066/financials/ratios/). Shares outstanding are essentially flat (+0.07% YoY), so there is no meaningful dilution from equity issuance [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sha/600066/statistics/).
