# Radian Group (RDN) — Core initiation — 2026-11-08
## 1. Business
Radian is a US private mortgage insurer that, since acquiring Inigo (a Lloyd's specialty insurer) in early 2026, also runs a Specialty segment; it is divesting Title and has sold Real Estate Services [Certain] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/890926/000119312526451985/rdn-20260930.htm). Earnings come from mortgage premiums and investment income, plus Lloyd's underwriting. Q3 revenue was $624M [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/890926/000119312526451985/rdn-20260930.htm). The Specialty share of revenue and its UK/Lloyd's share are [data unavailable]; sterling exposure is material [Likely] and should go to the macro overlay.

## 2. Quality
- ROIC is 11.3% TTM against 11.6% in FY2025 and 14.4% in FY2022. The last-12-month table shows it rising 1.5pp from a year earlier but below the 2-year high of 11.8% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/rdn/financials/ratios/?p=trailing) [Certain]. ROE has drifted from 18.2% (FY2022) to 12.4% TTM (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/rdn/financials/ratios/) [Certain].
- Mortgage insurance is an oligopoly with GSE-set capital rules (PMIERs) and high barriers to entry [Likely]. Returns are cyclical and tied to housing credit, not to a unique edge versus the peer group [Likely].
- Specialty is a new, unseasoned earnings stream. Q3 benefited from benign catastrophe losses (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/rdn/transcripts/726451-q3-2026/) [Likely]. A normal-catastrophe year would show lower Specialty earnings [Guessing].
- Piotroski F is 6 and net debt/EBITDA is 1.3x (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/rdn/statistics/) [Certain]. Quality is average, not exceptional.

## 3. Valuation
P/E is 8.8x, above its 5y maximum of 8.4x (median 7.2x) and in line with the peer median of 8.8x. P/B is 1.0x, at the top of a 0.8x–1.0x 5y range and 11% below peers' 1.1x. EV/EBITDA is 6.1x against a 5y median of 6.0x and a peer median of 6.7x. P/FCF of 3.2x and a 31.05% FCF yield look cheap, but insurer cash flow is lumpy: FCF conversion has swung from -110% to 253% [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/rdn/). The mechanical base fair value is 55.49 against a 35.97 close, built mainly on P/B with a sustainable ROE (bear 45.52, bull 62.33) [Likely]. That method assumes ROE holds near today's levels, which is generous given the downtrend since FY2022 [Likely]. The price assumes mid-cycle earnings with no re-rating for Specialty, and sits on the cheap side of peers on book value [Likely]. It is not cheap on earnings against its own history [Certain].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROIC (ratios page, TTM) falls below 9%.
- ROE (ratios page, TTM) falls below 10%.
- Net debt/EBITDA (site definition) rises above 2.0x.
- Specialty combined ratio, reported in the quarterly release on radian.com, exceeds 100% for two consecutive quarters.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
The share count fell 4.92% YoY [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/rdn/statistics/). Management paid about $1.7bn [company-reported] for Inigo, partly from a $600M intercompany loan from Radian Guaranty that reduces the mortgage subsidiary's PMIERs cushion (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/890926/000119312526451985/rdn-20260930.htm) [Certain]. Goodwill and intangibles are now $410M [company-reported] (same source). Whether buybacks can continue at the old pace while Specialty absorbs capital is untested [Guessing]. FCF conversion is erratic (see section 3).
