# Macro overlay: Kinross Gold Corporation (TSX:K) — 2026-10-11
## Assessment
Kinross has zero pricing power over gold [Certain] (factsheet/note), so the gold price — driven mainly by real yields and the dollar — is the dominant swing factor for revenue, margin and multiple: a "miners and commodities" primary case.

**Chain**: real yields + dollar → gold price → Kinross revenue/margin (no cost offset; AISC already +13% YoY to $1,571/oz, source: SEC 40-F Ex 99.2) → earnings and EV/EBITDA multiple.

**Official data** (FRED, 2026-10-08/09): 10y real yield (DFII10) 2.87%, +0.44pp 1m/+0.56pp 3m; broad dollar index (DTWEXBGS) 121.4, +2.86 1m; 10y breakeven inflation (T10YIE) 2.33%, roughly flat; effective fed funds (DFF) 3.88%, +0.25pp 1m — actual tightening, not just expectations. Source: https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=[series].

**Market pricing (proxy, inference)**: 2y Treasury (4.75%) sits ~87bp above fed funds (3.88%), consistent with a higher/hawkish path rather than imminent cuts [Likely] (proxy). Flat breakevens plus rising real yields is a classic gold headwind — a real-rate story, not an inflation one.

The stock is already down -25.4% over 6 months and -16.9% vs its 200-day average, so part of this real-yield/dollar move is likely already reflected in price **(inference)**; the risk is the trend (still rising, not reversing) continuing.

**Bull**: real yields/dollar stabilize or roll over → gold recovers → high operating leverage expands margin fast; cheap multiple re-rates.
**Bear**: real yields/dollar keep rising → gold falls further on top of rising AISC → margin compresses from a cyclical peak; multiple stays depressed or falls further.
**Tilt**: toward bear, moderate — the move is real, recent and ongoing, and Kinross has no operational offset.
