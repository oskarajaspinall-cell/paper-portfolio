# Kinross Gold Corporation (TSX:K) — Core initiation — 2026-10-11

## 1. Business
Kinross mines and sells gold (plus byproduct silver) from five main operations: Paracatu (Brazil), Tasiast (Mauritania), Fort Knox/Manh Choh (Alaska), La Coipa (Chile) and Round Mountain/Bald Mountain (Nevada) [Certain] (source: SEC 40-F Ex 99.1, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/701818/000110465926035268/kgc-20251231xex99d1.htm). Proven and probable reserves were 20.9 Moz gold / 11.6 Moz silver at FY2025-end, calculated at a $2,000/oz gold assumption [Certain] (same source). Revenue is entirely sold at prevailing gold prices; the company has no pricing power over its own product [Certain].

## 2. Quality
Returns have risen sharply over five years — ROIC -0.6%→39.8% TTM, operating margin 5.1%→50.7% TTM (source: fact sheet RA/IS) — but gold mining is a commodity business: margins track the gold price, not a durable operating edge, and all-in sustaining cost rose 13% YoY to $1,571/oz in FY2025 from $1,388/oz [Certain] (source: SEC 40-F Ex 99.2, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/701818/000110465926035268/kgc-20251231xex99d2.htm), showing cost inflation eating into the price tailwind. Peers (AEM, ABX, NEM, AU) share the same gold-price driver; none has demonstrated moat-level, through-cycle returns [Likely]. Net debt/EBITDA is now -0.35x TTM, i.e. net cash (source: fact sheet BS/IS,RA) — real balance-sheet flexibility, but itself a function of the current cash-flow peak. There is no durable reason competitors (or Kinross itself in a lower gold-price regime) can't see returns compress automatically.

## 3. Valuation
Trading multiples look cheap: P/E 9.1x is below Kinross's own 5y minimum (12.0x) and the peer median (13.8x); EV/EBITDA 4.9x is below its 5y minimum (5.0x) and peer median (6.8x) (source: fact sheet). But the fact sheet's own fair-value model (EV/EBITDA, the only computable method; NAV unavailable) puts base fair value at 18.04 CAD and bear at 14.50 CAD — 46.7%/57.2% below the 33.85 CAD close; only the bull case (53.02 CAD) sits above it [Certain] (source: fact sheet). The gap is explained by TTM profitability sitting at a 5-year high (operating margin 50.7% TTM vs a 5.1%-57.3% range over FY21-25): the model's peak-earnings rule uses 5-year-average EBITDA rather than the TTM peak for bear/base once earnings exceed a threshold [Likely]. The "cheap" multiples are cheap against cyclically elevated earnings; against normalized profitability the stock is not demonstrably cheap.

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROIC (ratios page) sustains above 20% for two more fiscal years even after any gold-price pullback, showing durable (not purely cyclical) returns.
- AISC per ounce (future 40-F/MD&A) stops rising and falls back toward the FY2024 level ($1,388/oz), preserving margin independent of the gold price.
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) stays below 1x through a gold-price decline, confirming the balance sheet can absorb a down-cycle without dilution.
- The Great Bear project (AIF) delivers its ~12-year mine life and ~$594/oz cost-of-sales guidance without material capex overruns, extending reserve life as flagged.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
FY2025 free cash flow funded $700m of term-loan repayment, $600.3m of NCIB buybacks and $1,175.2m of growth capex [Certain] (source: SEC 40-F Ex 99.2). TTM FCF margin is 36.1%, but 5y FCF conversion (FCF/NI) is falling (-13.6pp to 96.2% TTM) (source: fact sheet CF,IS). Shares outstanding fell 2.14% YoY via buybacks (source: fact sheet ST). 2026 guidance is ~$1,500m capex ($450m sustaining/$1,050m growth) [Certain] (same SEC source). There is no dividend; capital return is opportunistic buybacks plus debt paydown — appropriate in a strong-FCF window but discretionary and likely to be cut if the gold price falls.

## 6. Unusual items
Several plaintiff law-firm "investor alert"/"fraud investigation" releases (Pomerantz, SBS Law) ran Oct 1-8, 2026 soliciting Kinross investors (source: fact sheet news, https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/investor-alert-pomerantz-law-firm-investigates-claims-on-behalf-of-investors-of-kinross-gold-corporation---kgc-302902142.html). These are generic solicitations without a specific disclosed claim, and the 40-F's risk factors only generically flag "potential" securities-litigation exposure, not a confirmed proceeding [Guessing]. Short interest is low (0.83% of float) but rose 28.1% versus the prior month (source: fact sheet ST) — a flag, not material alone. Beta is 1.52, above-average volatility for the sector (source: fact sheet ST).
