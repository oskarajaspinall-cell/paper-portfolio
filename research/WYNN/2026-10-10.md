# Wynn Resorts, Limited (WYNN) — Core initiation — 2026-10-10

## 1. Business
Owns and operates integrated casino resorts — Wynn/Encore Las Vegas, Wynn Palace and Wynn Macau (under a Macau gaming concession), and Encore Boston Harbor — plus a 40% equity interest in Wynn Al Marjan Island (UAE), expected to open September 2027 [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/wynn/ ; https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1174922/000117492226000055/wynn-20260630.htm). Revenue is gaming (tables, slots), rooms, food & beverage and entertainment [Certain].

## 2. Quality
Casino licenses are scarce and slow to replicate — Macau's limited-concession system and prime Las Vegas Strip real estate are real barriers to entry [Likely]. Profitability has recovered sharply since the pandemic: ROIC rose from -2.1% (FY22) to 9.4% TTM, ROCE to 11.6% TTM, operating margin to 16.0%, net margin to 6.1%, FCF conversion 176% TTM [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/wynn/financials/ratios/). The balance sheet is a real weakness: net debt/EBITDA 5.66x TTM, Altman Z-score 1.20 (distress zone) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/wynn/statistics/). The Q2-2026 10-Q shows a $906.7m stockholders' deficit and a 0.91 current ratio — negative book equity, negative working capital [Certain] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1174922/000117492226000055/wynn-20260630.htm). Management says it was in covenant compliance but discloses no specific thresholds [Certain] (same source). In September 2026 Wynn Resorts Finance priced $900m of new senior notes at 6.875% due 2035 [Certain] (source: https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/wynn-resorts-announces-pricing-of-private-offering-of-900-million-of-wynn-resorts-finance-6-875-senior-notes-due-2035--302875813.html). High leverage leaves less flexibility than better-capitalised peers (LVS) to invest through a downturn [Likely].

## 3. Valuation
WYNN is cheap against its own pandemic-distorted 5-year history but not against peers today. EV/EBITDA (9.9x) and EV/Sales (2.4x) sit below their 5-year range minimums (10.9x, 2.7x), yet are +19% and +6% above the 4-peer median [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/wynn/financials/ratios/). P/E (18.0x, 17th percentile of own range) is +28% above peer median; P/FCF (9.6x, 4th percentile) is +42% above [Certain]. FCF yield is 10.38% [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/wynn/statistics/). Base fair value is $256.98 (+242% vs the $75.15 close); the reverse DCF implies the price assumes -8.6% revenue decline in year one fading to terminal growth, more pessimistic than the actual trend of rising net margin and ROIC [Certain] (source: fair value table, fact sheet). The bear case is negative, an artefact of already-negative book equity, not a usable floor [Certain]. At 5.66x leverage, equity value is highly geared to EBITDA — part of the discount compensates for that risk [Likely].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) rises above 7x, showing leverage is not being worked down.
- Altman Z-score (statistics page) falls further or the stockholders' deficit widens materially in a future 10-Q, showing distress rather than steady deleveraging.
- Operating margin (income statement) falls below 12%, showing a loss of pricing power in Macau or Las Vegas.
- FCF conversion, FCF/NI (cash flow and income statement), drops below 100%, showing cash generation supporting debt service is deteriorating.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
FCF margin is 10.7% TTM with 176% FCF conversion [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/wynn/financials/cash-flow-statement/). The company raised $900m of 6.875% notes due 2035 in September 2026, consistent with refinancing nearer-dated maturities rather than funding new growth [Likely] (source: PRNewswire, above). H1-2026 operating cash flow was $645.4m against ongoing capex including the 40%-owned Al Marjan Island project [Certain] (source: SEC 10-Q). Shares outstanding fell 3.24% YoY, consistent with buybacks [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/wynn/statistics/). Specific dividend/buyback amounts were not found on the allowlisted company pages within the fetch budget [Guessing].

## 6. Unusual items
Stockholders' deficit of $906.7m and a current ratio of 0.91 (negative working capital) per the Q2-2026 10-Q [Certain] (source: SEC 10-Q, above). Altman Z-score of 1.20 places WYNN in the distress zone [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/wynn/statistics/). Short interest is elevated at 11.2% of float, though down 0.5pp over the past month [Certain] (same source). Together these flag market concern about balance-sheet risk distinct from the operating recovery.
