# Macro overlay: Wynn Resorts, Limited (WYNN) — 2026-10-10
## Assessment
WYNN's equity is unusually geared to financing cost: net debt/EBITDA 5.66x, book equity negative (-$906.7m deficit), Altman Z 1.20 (distress zone) [Certain] (fact sheet, SEC 10-Q). Chain: real yields and high-yield credit spreads → cost of debt/refinancing and equity risk premium → the levered equity "sliver" of enterprise value **(inference)**.

What's priced: US 10y real yield (DFII10) 2.87%, +44bp ~1m/+56bp ~3m; 10y nominal (DGS10) 5.22%, +68bp 3m; HY credit spread (BAMLH0A0HYM2) 3.15%, +45bp 3m (FRED, 2026-10-08) [Certain, official]. Breakeven inflation (T10YIE) roughly flat (+9bp 3m), so the real-yield rise looks like a term-premium/policy move, not an inflation surprise [Likely] **(inference)**. The 2y yield (4.75%) ~87bp above fed funds (3.88%) proxies the market pricing higher-for-longer, not near-term cuts [Likely, proxy] **(inference)**. VIX (15.41, -0.43 3m) shows no broad risk-off — a financing-conditions story specific to levered names, not a general risk-premium shock [Likely] **(inference)**. WYNN priced $900m of notes at 6.875% in Sept 2026, consistent with this backdrop [Certain] (fact sheet).

**Bull**: real yields/HY spreads stabilise or reverse → cheaper refinancing, lower risk premium, multiple re-rates up — leverage magnifies the gain **(inference)**.
**Bear**: real yields and spreads keep rising → higher discount rate, wider risk premium on a distress-zone balance sheet → further multiple compression — leverage magnifies the loss **(inference)**.
**Tilt**: moderate, toward bear — spreads and real yields are already moving against a 5.66x-levered, negative-equity name mid-refinancing, though the 3.15% HY spread is not yet historically stressed.
