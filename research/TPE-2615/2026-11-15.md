# Wan Hai Lines (TPE:2615) — Core initiation — 2026-11-15
## 1. Business
Taiwan-listed container carrier (Marine Shipping, Industrials) earning freight revenue on liner services, mainly intra-Asia trades [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tpe/2615/). Earnings are driven by freight rates and vessel supply, so profits swing violently through the cycle. Revenue split by country/region: [data unavailable] (not read from a primary source this run).

## 2. Quality
Cyclical, not a compounder [Certain]. ROIC ran from 124.6% (FY2021) to -5.8% (FY2023), then 21.3% (FY2024), 13.6% (FY2025) and 17.5% TTM (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tpe/2615/financials/ratios/). The 5-year trend is falling, but the last 12 months are rising: ROIC 15.4% to 17.5%, operating margin 26.5% to 28.9%, FCF margin 7.9% to 18.3% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tpe/2615/financials/ratios/?p=trailing). All remain below their 2-year highs [Certain]. No durable moat is evident: shipping is commodity-like, so returns reflect the rate cycle, not competitive advantage [Likely]. Strengths are the balance sheet (net debt/EBITDA -1.34x, i.e. net cash) and a Piotroski F of 7 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tpe/2615/statistics/). FCF conversion is 60.9% TTM, down from 95.1% in FY2021, reflecting fleet capex [Likely].

## 3. Valuation
Close 107.50 TWD. P/E 5.9x sits at 76% of its 5y range (2.4x-7.0x) but 21% below peers (7.5x); EV/EBITDA 3.4x is at 7% of its range and 18% below peers; P/B 1.0x is at 9% of its range; P/FCF 9.7x is above peers (+18%). FCF yield 10.31% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tpe/2615/statistics/). Mechanical base fair value 106.14 TWD (EV/EBITDA only; NAV unavailable) is 1.3% below the price, bull 111.97, bear 48.84 [Likely]. The price therefore assumes rates stay near current profitable levels; a normalising freight market is the main downside, as the bear value shows. Earnings multiples look cheap largely because profits are near cycle highs [Likely]. Upside to fair value is nil. The case rests on trend: price +19.5% above the 200-day average, +31.7% over 12 months, and net cash.

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROIC (ratios page, TTM) falls below 10%.
- Operating margin (income statement, TTM) falls below 20%.
- FCF margin (cash flow page, TTM) falls below 8%.
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) rises above 0.5x, i.e. net cash is lost to debt-funded fleet growth.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Share count is stable (-0.12% YoY), so no dilution. FCF margin collapsed to -48.3% in FY2023 and recovered to 18.3% TTM, showing heavy, lumpy fleet spend funded from cash [Likely]. Dividend and capex policy were not read from a primary source [data unavailable]. Cash conversion of 60.9% is acceptable but below the FY2021-22 level of ~92-95%.
