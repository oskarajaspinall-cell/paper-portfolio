# Wells Fargo & Company (WFC) — Core initiation — 2026-10-11

## 1. Business
Wells Fargo is one of the four largest U.S. banks, operating through Consumer Banking & Lending, Commercial Banking, Corporate & Investment Banking, and Wealth & Investment Management [Certain] (source: https://www.wellsfargo.com/about/investor-relations/). It earns net interest income from the spread between loans/securities and deposits/borrowings, plus fee income from investment banking, trading, wealth management and mortgage servicing [Certain] (source: fact sheet, income statement).

## 2. Quality
TTM ROE of 12.6% is the best in five years, but the five-year trend is flat (-0.9pp) [Certain] (source: fact sheet, ratios page) — a calmer rate backdrop, not a step-change in returns. Net margin rose steadily to 27.2% TTM from 18.8% in FY2022 [Certain] (source: fact sheet, income statement). ROIC, ROCE, gross margin and FCF margin are correctly [data unavailable], not meaningful for a deposit-funded bank [Certain] (source: fact sheet, ratios page). The Piotroski F-score is 3/9 [Certain] (source: fact sheet, statistics page) — a generic screen not built for banks, but still a flag on profitability/balance-sheet quality. The moat is scale and a low-cost deposit franchise, raising barriers to a comparable national deposit base [Likely], yet returns still trail large-bank peers, and the business now carries a fresh, open HUD probe into its Black-homeownership lending programs (opened 2026-10-07), exposure unknown [Guessing] (source: https://www.tipranks.com/news/wells-fargo-stock-wfc-falls-on-news-of-federal-probe).

## 3. Valuation
At the 83.55 last close [Certain] (source: fact sheet, price history), WFC trades at 12.1x P/E (56% of its own 5y range, 3% below the peer median of 12.6x) and 1.5x P/B (91% of its 5y range, near the top, 5% above the peer median of 1.5x) [Certain] (source: fact sheet, valuation table). EV/EBITDA, EV/Sales and P/FCF are [data unavailable], not meaningful for a bank. The fair-value model (P/B + sustainable ROE primary, FCFE/DDM secondary) gives a base fair value of 71.61, 14.3% below the last close, and a bull case of 77.76, still 6.9% below it [Certain] (source: fact sheet, fair value table) — the price already exceeds all three scenarios, including bull. The reverse DCF implies roughly -1.3% year-1 revenue growth fading to terminal growth [Certain] (source: fact sheet, fair value table) — not aggressive, but it leaves no margin of safety at today's price.

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROE (ratios page) falls back below 10%, reversing the TTM improvement that underpins the quality case.
- Piotroski F-score (statistics page) stays at or below 3 for another full fiscal year, confirming persistent balance-sheet/profitability weakness rather than a one-off reading.
- Net margin (income statement) reverses below 20%, erasing the efficiency gains made since FY2022.
- The HUD fair-lending probe, or a related regulatory action, results in a material penalty, consent order or mortgage-business restriction disclosed in an SEC filing.
