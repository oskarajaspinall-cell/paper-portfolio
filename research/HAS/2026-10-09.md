# Hasbro, Inc. (HAS) — Core initiation — 2026-10-09
## 1. Business
Hasbro designs and licenses toys, games and entertainment IP across three segments: Wizards of the Coast & Digital Gaming (Magic: The Gathering, Dungeons & Dragons — now 58% of TTM-pace revenue), Consumer Products (Transformers, Nerf, My Little Pony, Play-Doh), and Entertainment (small, shrinking licensing/TV business) [Certain] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/46080/000004608026000050/has-20260628.htm).

## 2. Quality
Returns are rising fast: ROIC 28.3% TTM (from 10.4% FY21) and ROCE 27.1% TTM, with gross margin up to 63.8% and operating margin to 23.6% [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/has/financials/ratios/, https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/has/financials/income-statement/). The driver is mix shift toward Wizards of the Coast: segment revenue grew 27% YoY YTD, tabletop gaming +30% to $989.0M, led by Magic: The Gathering [Certain] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/46080/000004608026000050/has-20260628.htm). Magic's 30-year collectible-card-game network effects and D&D's brand strength are a genuine moat that generic toy makers can't replicate [Likely]. Consumer Products is the weaker, lower-margin, tariff-exposed segment competing in commoditized toys [Likely]. FY2023/FY2025 net margin and ROE were negative only because of a $1.022bn tariff-driven goodwill impairment in 2025, not operating deterioration — Q2-26 operating profit was $252.5M (22.1% margin) vs a $798.2M loss a year earlier [Certain] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/46080/000004608026000050/has-20260628.htm). Net debt/EBITDA has fallen to 1.92x from 5.72x in FY23 [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/has/financials/ratios/).

## 3. Valuation
At 92.50 USD, HAS trades on 16.5x P/E — 21% below its own 5-year range minimum (20.5x) — and 12.0x EV/EBITDA, roughly mid-range. It trades above toy-peer (MAT, FNKO, JAKK, BBW) medians on P/E (+39%) and EV/EBITDA (+17%), reflecting its higher-margin, IP-driven mix, but below peers on P/FCF (-9%) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/has/statistics/, https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/mat/statistics/, https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/fnko/statistics/, https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/jakk/statistics/, https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/bbw/statistics/). P/B of 18.5x vs peers' 1.9x is not meaningful given book value was depressed by the 2025 impairment [Likely]. The stock's own fair-value model puts base case at 124.54 (+34.6% vs last close), driven by DCF (130.08); bear case of 34.99 assumes a severe reversal of the WizCo growth and margin trend [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/has/ — fact sheet fair-value section). The reverse-DCF implies the market is pricing -8.9% revenue decline next year, which looks too pessimistic against 15-16% YTD growth, though segment concentration risk (below) justifies some discount [Likely].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Wizards of the Coast & Digital Gaming segment revenue growth (10-Q MD&A) falls below high-single digits for two consecutive quarters.
- Gross margin (income statement page) falls back below 55%, signalling the mix shift toward Consumer Products is reversing.
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) rises back above 3.0x.
- Consumer Products operating margin stays negative or tariff costs keep eroding segment profit materially beyond the $10.8M Q2 cyber-incident hit already disclosed.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
FCF margin is 24.4% TTM (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/has/financials/ratios/). Management is prioritizing deleveraging over buybacks: YTD debt repayment of $123.3M vs. buybacks of only $41.5M, alongside $197.6M of dividends paid ($1.40/share declared) [Certain] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/46080/000004608026000050/has-20260628.htm). Shares outstanding still rose 1.43% YoY (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/has/statistics/), implying stock-comp issuance is outpacing the modest buyback — a mild dilution drag to watch [Likely].

## 6. Unusual items
A March 2026 unauthorized network-access incident disrupted order processing/shipping, costing $10.8M in Q2 and hitting Consumer Products sales [Certain] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/46080/000004608026000050/has-20260628.htm; corroborated by https://www.wsj.com/cfo-journal/something-was-wrong-hasbros-lessons-from-a-cyberattack-3fd08584). Press reported WSJ speculation of Mattel takeover interest on 2026-10-01 (HAS +2% to $89.70 same day) — unconfirmed M&A speculation, not a fundamental fact [Guessing] (source: https://www.tipranks.com/news/the-fly/hasbro-up-2-to-89-70-after-wsj-report-of-mattel-takeover-interst-thefly-news). Wizards of the Coast workers began unionizing on 2026-10-06 — a minor labor-relations risk, not yet a cost impact [Likely] (source: https://www.tipranks.com/news/the-fly/workers-at-hasbros-wizards-of-the-coast-to-form-union-thefly-news). Short interest is moderate at 5.17% of float, up 0.8% vs prior month (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/has/statistics/). Next earnings Oct 20, 2026 — a nearby catalyst but not an input to this core thesis (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/has/).
