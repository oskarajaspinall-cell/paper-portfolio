# The People's Insurance Company (Group) of China (HKG:1339) — Core initiation — 2026-11-09
## 1. Business
Chinese state-controlled insurance group. It earns underwriting margin and investment income, mainly from property & casualty insurance, with life/health and asset management alongside [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/1339/). Stockanalysis.com classes it as Insurance - Property & Casualty. It reports in CNY and its shares are priced in HKD. Segment mix is [data unavailable] because no primary source was fetched. Revenue is domestic to China [Likely]; no foreign country is ~10%+.

## 2. Quality
Returns are mediocre and volatile. ROIC was 11.2% in FY2025 but 7.4% TTM, against 13.2% a year earlier (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/1339/financials/ratios/?p=trailing). ROE was 15.9% in FY2025 and 9.4% TTM. [Certain] The 5-year trend is rising but the last 12 months are falling: the Direction change flag applies, so the FY2024-25 peak should not be read as the run rate. [Certain] Piotroski F is 5 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/1339/statistics/), which is average. A durable moat is plausible from state backing, distribution scale and licensing barriers [Likely], but returns have not been protected by it: ROIC 5.6% in FY2021, 11.7% in FY2024. Margin and ROCE lines are [data unavailable] for an insurer, so quality rests on ROE/ROIC alone. Beta is 0.61 (same statistics page).

## 3. Valuation
The price assumes little. P/E is 6.9x, below the 5y minimum of 7.2x and a 13% discount to the peer median of 7.9x. P/B is 0.6x, in the lowest quarter of its 5y range (24%) and 52% below the peer median of 1.3x. P/FCF of 2.6x is below its 5y minimum, but insurer "FCF" includes premium float, so I give it little weight. [Likely] EV/EBITDA of 10.0x sits at 62% of its range and 16% below peers (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/1339/financials/ratios/). The mechanical fair value (P/B+ROE 67%, P/E 33%) is bear 6.17 / base 7.69 / bull 10.27 HKD against a 5.37 close, i.e. +43.4% to base (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/1339/). The base rests on the P/B+ROE leg, which is sensitive to sustaining ROE. TTM ROE of 9.4% is well below the FY2025 15.9% it would need. [Guessing] The 12-month change is -26.8% (statistics page): the market is pricing lower earnings. [Likely]
