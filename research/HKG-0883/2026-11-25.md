# CNOOC Limited (HKG:0883) — Core initiation — 2026-11-25
## 1. Business
Pure upstream offshore oil and gas producer, China's largest. Revenue is realised crude and gas prices times volume; the state-owned parent gives preferential access to Chinese offshore acreage and policy backing [Likely]. Earnings are largely a leveraged call on oil prices, with low unit costs (gross margin 76.7% TTM) (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/0883/financials/income-statement/). Revenue by country is [data unavailable] on the fact sheet; the company reports in CNY and the share trades in HKD, so China exposure is total [Certain].

## 2. Quality
Returns are high: TTM ROIC 24.1% (21.0% a year earlier), ROCE 18.3%, operating margin 43.7%, net margin 31.6%; FY2021 ROIC was 15.1% and FY2022 28.3%, so the range reflects the oil cycle (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/0883/financials/ratios/) [Certain]. Net cash (net debt/EBITDA -0.98x), Piotroski F 7, Altman Z 3.76 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/0883/statistics/) [Certain].
Direction flags: ROCE is 2.3pp below its 2-year high of 20.6%, but ROIC and margins rose over the last 12 months, so no deterioration; FCF margin fell over five fiscal years yet rose from 23.1% to 27.5% in the last year [Certain].
Moat: low-cost reserves and a state licence position, not pricing power [Likely]. Q3 headline: record nine-month output and higher profit (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/0883/transcripts/739787-q3-2026/) [Likely]. A new ultra-deep Bohai discovery adds to the resource base (source: https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/chinas-cnooc-reports-major-ultra-deep-gas-discovery-bohai-sea-2026-11-12/) [Likely].
