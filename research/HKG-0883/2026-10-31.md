# CNOOC Limited (HKG:0883) — Core initiation — 2026-10-31
## 1. Business
Pure upstream offshore oil and gas producer, China's largest. Revenue is realised crude and gas prices times volume; the parent's state mandate gives preferential access to Chinese offshore acreage and security-of-supply policy backing [Likely]. Earnings are mostly a leveraged call on oil prices, with low costs per barrel (gross margin 76.7% TTM) (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/0883/financials/income-statement/).

## 2. Quality
Returns are high and consistent: TTM ROIC 23.9%, ROCE 18.3%, operating margin 43.7%, net margin 31.6%, versus a five-year ROIC low of 15.1% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/0883/financials/ratios/) [Certain]. The balance sheet is net cash (net debt/EBITDA -0.98x TTM), Piotroski F 7, Altman Z 3.76 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/0883/statistics/) [Certain].
Moat: low-cost reserves and state-backed licence position, not pricing power. Returns are commodity-driven, so a five-year ROIC range spanning 15.1% to 28.3% reflects the oil cycle [Likely]. Policy to raise domestic output supports volume (source: https://oilprice.com/Energy/Crude-Oil/Beijing-Pushes-Domestic-Oil-Production-as-Iranian-Supply-Dries-Up.html) [Likely]. Q3 results were released on the last trading day; the transcript headline describes record volume and higher profit (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/0883/transcripts/739787-q3-2026/) [Likely]. Current profits likely include elevated war-driven oil prices, i.e. near-peak [Likely].

## 3. Valuation
At 24.70 HKD the stock trades at P/E 7.2x, EV/EBITDA 2.8x, P/FCF 8.6x and an 11.58% FCF yield (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/0883/statistics/). These sit at 82-90% of the own five-year range, and P/B 1.2x is above the five-year max, so the stock is not cheap against its own history [Certain]. Against peers (HKG:0857, HKG:0386, COP, XOM) it is at a 55% P/E discount and 57% EV/EBITDA discount, partly reflecting China/state-owned discount and sector rerating [Likely].
Mechanical base fair value is 21.36 HKD (bear 15.73, bull 26.09), below the price, with the EV/EBITDA method only; reserve NAV is [data unavailable] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/0883/). The price assumes sustained high oil prices; the margin of safety is thin on own-history [Likely].

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
FCF conversion is 87.2% TTM versus 70.7% in FY2024, FCF margin 27.5% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/0883/financials/cash-flow-statement/) [Certain]. Net cash is growing, shares outstanding fell 0.41% YoY [Certain]. Dividend detail is not covered here [data unavailable]. Production restart at the Lingshui 17-2 gas field after an accident is guided for Q4 (source: https://www.reuters.com/world/asia-pacific/cnooc-resume-production-china-deep-sea-gasfield-q4-after-accident-2026-10-30/) [Likely].
