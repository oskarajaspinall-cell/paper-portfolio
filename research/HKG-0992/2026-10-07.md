# Lenovo Group (HKG:0992) — Tactical initiation — 2026-10-07

## 1. Catalyst and why now
Next print is Nov 5, 2026 (Est. Earnings), four weeks out (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/0992/) [Certain]. The setup is the AI-PC/server upcycle: Lenovo launched "Lenovo AI Express" with NVIDIA on Sept 30 and added Schneider Electric to its AI-infrastructure partner program on Sept 21 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/0992/) [Likely]. Trade press frames device makers as raising prices into a memory-chip shortage, which "so far" is working (WSJ, Sept 4 and Sept 23, headlines listed in the fact sheet) [Likely]. Offsetting this, the US ITC opened investigation No. 337-TA-1523 on Sept 24 naming Micron, Supermicro, HPE and Lenovo — an unresolved legal overhang into the print (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/0992/) [Certain].

## 2. What the price already assumes
At 35.40 HKD the stock is already up 209.3% over 12 months and 73.0% over three months, trading at 70.4x TTM P/E — 823% above its own 5-year range high of 14.2x — and 6.9x P/B, 242% above the 3-peer (DELL, HPQ, HKG:1810) median [Certain]. EV/EBITDA (14.4x, -4% vs peers) and EV/Sales (0.6x, -40% vs peers) look far less stretched, because TTM net margin has compressed to 0.9% from 2.3% in FY2026 [Certain]. The price assumes the AI/ASP tailwind sustains revenue growth and margin recovers quickly from that depressed TTM base [Likely] — most of the near-term good news already looks priced in.
