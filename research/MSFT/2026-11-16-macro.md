**Pricing (proxy):** the 2y at 4.86% against fed funds at 3.88% (DFF, 2026-11-12) implies the market prices hikes, roughly 1pp. The rise in the 10y yield is almost all real yield, because breakevens are flat. [Likely]

**Inference:** the 3-month rise is a discount-rate shock, not an earnings one. Part of it is already in the price: the 12-month change is only +3.0%. Credit spreads remain tight at 3.2%, so the risk premium is not yet repriced. **(inference)**

**Bull:** yields stall and the 2y falls back toward fed funds. The multiple holds near 27x P/E and the capex payback story carries the stock.
**Bear:** the 10y TIPS yield rises above ~3.25% or spreads widen. Cash-based valuation compresses the multiple. Earnings are barely affected, because margins are 47%.

**Tilt:** toward bear, small. Rate sensitivity is real, but the P/E is only at the 39th percentile of its range and fundamentals (ROIC, FCF conversion) dominate.
