## 2. Quality
Earnings are high-margin but cyclical and low-return. Operating margin has risen from 40.2% (FY2021) to 56.8% TTM and net margin to 44.7% TTM (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/6066/financials/income-statement/) [Certain]. Returns do not match: ROE fell from 13.8% to 8.4% (FY2025), 10.7% TTM, and ROIC is 2.4% TTM, below any plausible cost of capital (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/6066/financials/ratios/) [Certain]. Margins are at a cyclical high while ROE sits well below FY2021, which suggests leverage and equity growth diluting returns [Likely]. FCF margin swings between -91.5% and 358.3% because broker cash flow is dominated by client-fund and trading-book movements, so FCF carries no signal [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/6066/financials/cash-flow-statement/). I see no durable moat beyond licence and scale in a crowded, state-influenced industry [Likely]. Piotroski F is 5, neutral (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/6066/statistics/).

## 3. Valuation
P/E is 6.3x against a 5y range of 20.5x–29.1x, below the minimum, and +7% versus a peer median of 5.9x (n=3). P/B is 1.3x against 1.6x–2.5x, and 87% above peers at 0.7x (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/6066/statistics/) [Certain]. The 50.89% FCF yield and 2.0x P/FCF are artefacts of client-money flows and are not used [Likely]. The mechanical fair value (FCFE DCF, built on that same FCF) gives base 16.08 HKD, +49.4% against the 10.76 close, bear 8.25 (-23.3%) [Guessing]. The reverse DCF implies -12.6% year-1 revenue growth. The stock is cheap on earnings but sits at peak margins; applying the 5y median multiple to peak EPS overstates value. It trades at a premium to cheaper peers on book value [Likely].
