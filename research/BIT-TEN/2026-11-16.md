# Tenaris S.A. (BIT:TEN) — Core initiation — 2026-11-16
## 1. Business
Tenaris makes seamless steel tubes and related services, mainly for oil and gas drilling, completion and pipelines, and sells them to energy producers worldwide. It earns on volume, pricing and mill utilisation, so results follow upstream drilling activity. It reports in USD; the shares are quoted in EUR (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/bit/TEN/). Country and regional revenue mix: [data unavailable] in the fact sheet and not fetched. The Middle East, specifically Iraq, is a named delivery risk in recent calls [Likely] (source: https://www.marketbeat.com/instant-alerts/tenaris-q2-earnings-call-highlights-2026-08-09/).

## 2. Quality
A good business through the cycle, not a compounder. [Certain] TTM ROIC is 12.2% and ROCE 12.3%. FY2023 was a peak (ROIC 26.5%), and ROIC has since fallen 6.1pp below its 2-year high; it was flat over the last year (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/bit/TEN/financials/ratios/). Operating margin is 18.2%, against 11.1% in FY2021 and a 21.5% 2-year high. FCF conversion is 96.5% of net income. The balance sheet is a net cash position: net debt/EBITDA is -0.90x, Altman Z is 6.52 and Piotroski F is 7 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/bit/TEN/statistics/). [Likely] The moat is scale, premium connections and local-to-local supply, which earn a margin above the commodity-tube peers. I have not verified it from a primary source. The Q3 print beat consensus [Likely] (source: https://www.tipranks.com/news/the-fly/tenaris-reports-q3-eps-1-01-consensus-83c-thefly-news), and management guides to a higher Q4 [Likely] (source: https://www.fool.com/earnings/call-transcripts/2026/11/05/tenaris-ts-q3-2026-earnings-call-transcript/). The cycle is the risk, not solvency.

## 3. Valuation
[Certain] The stock is not cheap on its own history. P/E is 16.1x against a 5y median of 10.0x and a max of 11.2x. EV/EBITDA is 10.0x against a median of 5.8x and a max of 9.7x. P/B is 1.9x against a max of 1.5x. P/FCF is 16.6x, mid-range at 53% of its 5y band, and the FCF yield is 6.01%. Against peers it is mixed: P/E is 36% below the 25.1x peer median, but EV/EBITDA is 21% above the 8.3x median (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/bit/TEN/statistics/). Mechanical fair value base is 25.64 EUR against a 27.66 close (-7.3%); the bear-to-bull range (3.44 to 57.65) is very wide. The reverse DCF implies 3.2% year-1 revenue growth. [Likely] The price assumes margins hold near current levels, with no further cycle upswing. The stock is working: above its 50-day and 200-day averages, +55.4% over 12 months.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
[Certain] FCF conversion is 96.5% of net income (FY2024 109.0%, FY2025 105.1%). Shares outstanding fell 5.42% YoY, a buyback in effect (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/bit/TEN/statistics/). [Likely] The board doubled the interim dividend in August, on top of the buyback (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/bit/TEN/transcripts/553290-q2-2026/). Net cash and cash conversion give room to keep returning cash through a downturn. No dilution.
