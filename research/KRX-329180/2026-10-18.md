# HD Hyundai Heavy Industries Co., Ltd. (KRX:329180) — Core initiation — 2026-10-18

## 1. Business
HD Hyundai Heavy Industries builds and sells commercial vessels (container ships, tankers, LNG/gas carriers, bulk and car carriers), offshore plants for oil and gas operators, and marine engines, across four divisions: Shipbuilding, Offshore Plants, Engine Machinery and Others [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/krx/329180/). Contracts are multi-year newbuilds booked to an order backlog and recognized as vessels are delivered [Likely].

## 2. Quality
Returns have swung from loss-making (ROIC -10.8%, ROE -14.9% FY2021) to ROIC 39.3%, ROCE 25.4%, ROE 30.1% TTM, with gross margin rising from -2.6% to 19.1% and operating margin from -9.7% to 14.1% (source: fact sheet, ratios/income statement pages) [Certain]. Management attributes this to higher vessel prices, productivity gains and the Mipo merger (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/krx/329180/transcripts/553970-q1-2026/, https://stockanalysis.com/quote/krx/329180/transcripts/661910-q2-2026/) [Likely]. The moat is partial, not structural: Korean yards hold a technical edge in high-spec LNG/gas carriers under tightening emissions rules, but face Chinese yards scaling fast on price, and shipbuilding margins have historically been strongly cyclical — FY2021-23 shows negative gross margins [Certain]. TTM EBITDA is well above its 5-year median, i.e. earnings look peak-cycle, not a new durable run-rate (source: research/KRX-329180/2026-10-18-valuation.md) [Likely]. Net debt/EBITDA of -1.42x (net cash) gives balance-sheet resilience through the next downcycle [Certain].

## 3. Valuation
P/E of 13.7x sits below its own 5-year range (37.7x-463.9x) only because those years had thin or negative earnings, not because the stock has derated — today's multiple reflects genuinely higher TTM profit [Likely]. Versus peers (KRX:009540, KRX:010140, KRX:042660): EV/EBITDA is 11% cheaper, but P/FCF is 123% more expensive, P/B 32% more expensive and P/E 20% more expensive [Certain]. Base fair value — blended DCF (67%) and EV/EBITDA (33%), with the EV/EBITDA leg using the 5-year average EBITDA rather than TTM to correct for the peak — is 581,387 KRW, 65.4% above the last close of 351,500 KRW; bear case is -85.1%, bull +214.1% (source: fact sheet/valuation note) [Likely]. Reverse DCF implies the price already assumes -5.8% revenue decline in year 1 fading to terminal growth — the market is pricing a sharp cycle reversal, which is the crux of the bull case but also the risk if it is right [Likely].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Operating margin (ratios page) falls back below 5% (FY2024 level), signalling the upcycle has reversed faster than the fair-value model's own normalization already assumes.
- FCF margin (cash flow/income statement) turns negative again, as in FY2021-23, confirming a cyclical downturn in cash generation.
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios/balance sheet) swings materially positive (net cash position erodes), reducing resilience through the next downcycle.
- Shares outstanding (statistics page) keeps rising well above the 10.65% YoY already seen, diluting per-share value faster than earnings growth.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
FCF margin is 16.3% TTM and FCF conversion is 141.7% of net income — cash profit exceeds accounting profit, a sign of working-capital discipline through the upcycle [Certain]. The balance sheet carries net cash (net debt/EBITDA -1.42x) [Certain]. Shares outstanding grew 10.65% YoY, likely reflecting the Mipo merger share issuance (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/krx/329180/transcripts/553970-q1-2026/), which has diluted the per-share benefit of that cash generation [Likely].

## 6. Unusual items
