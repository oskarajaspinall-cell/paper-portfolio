## Assessment

Market pricing **(proxy, inference)**: 2y yield above fed funds by ~89bp implies markets expect no near-term cuts, consistent with continued dollar/KRW strength rather than reversal.

This is a secondary, not dominant, driver: the stock's 30.7% 12-month decline tracks a cyclical order-backlog/China-competition and peak-margin-normalization story (reverse DCF implies -5.8% yr-1 revenue), not a macro shock — the FX backdrop has actually been a tailwind the sell-off isn't crediting [Likely] **(inference)**. Bull: dollar/KRW stays weak, HY spreads contained → FX gains persist, shipowner financing intact. Bear: dollar reverses (faster Fed cuts) → FX tailwind flips to a margin headwind; HY spreads widen further → newbuild order deferrals, reinforcing the cyclical bear case already in the price.
