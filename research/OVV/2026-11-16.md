# Ovintiv Inc. (OVV) — Core initiation — 2026-11-16
## 1. Business
Independent oil, NGL and natural gas producer, selling at market prices; revenue tracks commodity prices and volumes. Core assets are the Permian (US) and Alberta Montney (Canada), after selling Anadarko and buying NuVista (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1792580/000119312526445603/ovv-20260930.htm) [Certain]. Canada is a large share of the asset base, but the segment revenue split is [data unavailable]. The filing excerpt I read did not show it. CAD/USD and Canadian regulation therefore matter [Likely].

## 2. Quality
Cyclical price-taker; no durable moat beyond cost position and inventory depth [Likely]. Returns are cycle-dependent: ROIC 12.2% in FY2025 against a 5y peak of 34.0% in FY2022, now 19.6% TTM, a year ago 9.7% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/ovv/financials/ratios/?p=trailing) [Certain]. Operating margin 31.4% TTM versus 23.0% in FY2024. The recent rebound is largely higher oil and condensate prices, not structural change [Likely]. FCF margin was -11.9% in FY2025, depressed by acquisition-related spending [Guessing], and is 17.6% TTM. Net debt/EBITDA is 0.91x, the lowest in five years (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/ovv/financials/ratios/). Piotroski F is 8 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/ovv/statistics/). Peak-cycle earnings are the main quality caveat [Likely].

## 3. Valuation
P/E is 13.4x, above its own 5y max of 9.4x, and +4% against the peer median of 12.9x (DVN, FANG, EOG, CTRA) [Certain]. EV/EBITDA is 4.2x, in the lower half of its 5y range (3.4x–5.3x) and 24% below peers at 5.5x. P/FCF is 9.9x, near the 5y max of 10.4x, and the FCF yield is 10.09% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/ovv/statistics/). The mechanical base fair value is 60.09 USD against a 65.39 close (-8.1%), and the bull case is 61.51 (-5.9%) (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/ovv/). It rests on EV/EBITDA alone because the NAV method is unavailable [Certain]. The 12-month change is +71.0%. The price assumes sustained strong commodity prices; on earnings it is not cheap [Likely]. The discount is on EBITDA and sales multiples, a fair but not compelling setup.

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROIC TTM (ratios page, ?p=trailing) falls below 10%.
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) rises above 2.0x, e.g. from debt-funded M&A.
- FCF margin TTM (cash-flow page, ?p=trailing) falls below 5%.
- Shares change YoY (statistics page) stays above 5% for a further year, i.e. the dilution continues.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Deal-heavy: NuVista (cash and stock) closed in February 2026, Anadarko was sold in April, and the Logan acquisition of about $890 million all-cash was announced 26 October 2026 [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1792580/000119312526445603/ovv-20260930.htm). Shares are up 3.80% YoY, mainly from the NuVista issuance [Likely]. The updated framework returns 50%–100% of cash flow above capex via dividends and buybacks [company-reported] (same URL). FCF conversion is 134.1% TTM against -83.1% in FY2025 [Certain]. Leverage is low, giving room to absorb Logan [Likely].
