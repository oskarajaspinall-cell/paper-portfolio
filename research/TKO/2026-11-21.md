# TKO Group Holdings (TKO) — Core initiation — 2026-11-21
## 1. Business
Owns UFC, WWE and IMG/On Location, plus PBR and boxing initiatives. Revenue is mainly media-rights fees (UFC–Skydance, WWE–Netflix/ESPN), live events and hospitality, and sponsorship and licensing (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1973266/000119312526447266/tko-20260930.htm) [Certain]. A foreign country or region at ~10%+ of revenue: [data unavailable] (not in the filing excerpt read).

## 2. Quality
Rolling-12-month returns are recovering: ROIC 9.2% vs 6.5% a year earlier; operating margin 23.9% vs 20.6%; FCF margin 31.1% vs 20.3% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/tko/financials/ratios/?p=trailing). Over five fiscal years all are lower (ROIC FY2022 16.4%, operating margin 47.7%), so the Direction-change flags are real: the recent rise is a rebound from post-merger lows (WWE/IMG consolidation), not a new peak [Likely]. The moat is scarce, owned live-sports IP with rising media-rights contracts; the 10-Q shows UFC revenue up 60% in the quarter on the Skydance deal and a record gate, and IMG up 16% on tennis and soccer commissions, while WWE was down 1% on fewer premium live events (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1973266/000119312526447266/tko-20260930.htm) [company-reported]. Revenue is lumpy by event calendar [Likely]. Altman Z 1.70 and net debt/EBITDA 2.39x are adequate but not fortress.

## 3. Valuation
P/E 47.9x sits at 3% of its 5y range, but that range is distorted by merger-year earnings. EV/EBITDA 21.0x is above its 5y max (20.3x) and 25% below the peer median; EV/Sales 6.8x is above range. P/FCF 19.5x, FCF yield 5.13% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/tko/statistics/). Fair value base 223.34 vs close 177.99 (+25.5%) but flagged unreliable: DCF 303.80 vs EV/EBITDA 62.43, a 4.9x gap, so weigh lightly. Reverse DCF: price implies 7.9% year-1 revenue growth fading to terminal. Price is 2.9% below the 50-day and 7.5% below the 200-day, so no trend support; the shares are near their 52-week low of 170.14 [Likely]. The stock is fairly priced on cash flow, expensive on EBITDA history; not obviously cheap.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Shares outstanding fell 24.48% YoY (statistics page), from buybacks, including repurchases in the latest quarter and a quarterly dividend (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1973266/000119312526447266/tko-20260930.htm) [company-reported]. FCF conversion is 600% of net income, flattered by low net income (amortization, minority interests) [Likely]. Long-term debt rose in the nine months on a new term loan, and a senior secured notes offering was priced on 2026-11-05 (https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20261105404187/en/TKO-Announces-Pricing-of-Senior-Secured-Notes-Offering/) [Likely]: buybacks are partly debt-funded.
