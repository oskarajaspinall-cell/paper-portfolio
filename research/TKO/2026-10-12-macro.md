## Assessment

TKO is not FX- or commodity-exposed (US media-rights/live-events revenue); the relevant transmission is cost of capital, not earnings.

Facts [Certain]: 10y Treasury (DGS10) 5.22% on 2026-10-08, +0.68pp over 3m; 10y real yield (DFII10) 2.87%, +0.56pp over 3m; HY credit spread (BAMLH0A0HYM2) 3.15%, +0.45pp over 3m; VIX 15.41, falling (fred.stlouisfed.org, 2026-10-08). The fact sheet's own DCF already uses the current 5.22% risk-free rate, so this level is priced into base fair value ($209.58); the 3-month *rise* is the incremental, not-yet-fully-digested move.

Market pricing **(inference)**: 2y yield (4.75%) above fed funds (3.88%) is a proxy for a market still pricing further tightening, consistent with core CPI still drifting up (+1.64pp index change/3m). This is a headwind, not a tailwind, backdrop for a name whose EV/EBITDA (23.5x) already sits above its own 5y range.

Bull: rates/spreads stabilize or ease -> lower discount rate -> DCF re-rates toward base/bull ($209-421). Bear: yields and HY spreads keep widening -> higher discount rate and refinancing cost on the growing debt stack -> fair value compresses toward bear ($56-61, EV/EBITDA method). Tilt: toward bear, small — the move is real but the base case already embeds today's 5.22% risk-free rate, and leverage/governance (per the note) remain the larger driver of the thesis.
