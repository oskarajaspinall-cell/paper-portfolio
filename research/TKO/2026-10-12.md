# TKO Group Holdings, Inc. (TKO) — Core initiation — 2026-10-12

## 1. Business
TKO owns and operates premium live-sports/entertainment IP: UFC (mixed martial arts) and WWE (wrestling), plus IMG (sports marketing/agency), On Location (hospitality) and PBR [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/tko/, https://tkogrp.com). Revenue comes mainly from media-rights fees, live-event ticketing/sponsorship, and consumer products/licensing [Certain] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1973266/000119312526330592/tko-20260630.htm).

## 2. Quality
5y returns are falling (ROIC -5.2pp, ROCE -3.0pp) but recovering from the FY2023 trough tied to the Sept-2023 WWE-UFC merger (ROIC: 5.3%→3.9%→6.3%→7.8% TTM) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/tko/financials/ratios/). FCF margin is strong at 31.5% TTM [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/tko/financials/cash-flow-statement/). Net debt/EBITDA has fallen to 2.69x TTM from 4.35x in FY2021 [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/tko/financials/ratios/), yet the 10-Q shows gross long-term debt jumped to $4.583bn at 30-Jun-26 from $3.724bn at 31-Dec-25 [Certain], a one-quarter increase the filing excerpt available does not explain [Guessing] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1973266/000119312526330592/tko-20260630.htm). The moat is scarcity value: UFC and WWE are the two dominant global MMA/wrestling properties with long-run media-rights pricing power [Likely]. But Endeavor entities hold ~51% of voting power via 116.2mm Class B shares vs 74.3mm Class A [Certain], with an ongoing related-party Transition Services Agreement with Endeavor OpCo [Certain] — a controlling-shareholder governance overhang (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1973266/000119312526330592/tko-20260630.htm).

## 3. Valuation
EV/EBITDA (23.5x) sits above the top of TKO's own 5-year range (18.4x-20.3x), while EV/Sales (7.2x) is 54% above the peer median [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/tko/financials/ratios/). P/FCF (20.1x) and P/B (3.9x) are below peer medians (-30%, -80%) [Certain]. P/E (60.9x) looks low in its 5y range only because that range is distorted by near-zero/negative earnings years around the 2023 merger — not a reliable read [Likely]. Base-case fair value (DCF 67%/EV-EBITDA 33% blend) is $209.58, +17.7% above the $178.01 close; bear case is $55.85 (-68.6%) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/tko/financials/ratios/, https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/tko/statistics/, https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/tko/history/). Reverse DCF shows the price already assumes 9.4% revenue growth fading to terminal by year 10 [Certain] — plausible against FY26 guidance of $5.775bn-$5.825bn revenue (per the earnings headline), but it leaves little room for error given EV/EBITDA is already above its own history.

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) rises above 3.5x (vs 2.69x TTM), confirming the Jun-26 debt jump is structural, not temporary
- ROIC (ratios page) falls back toward the FY2023/24 trough (below 4%) instead of continuing its post-merger recovery
- FCF margin (cash flow + income statement) falls below 15%, reversing the recovery from the FY2023 low of 5.3%
- Gross margin (income statement) resumes its 5y decline below 45% (TTM 59.5%, down from 71.4% in FY2022)

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
FCF conversion (FCF/NI) of 728.5% TTM reflects GAAP net income still suppressed by merger-related items rather than unusually strong cash generation [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/tko/financials/cash-flow-statement/). Shares outstanding fell 13.63% YoY, a sizable buyback [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/tko/statistics/), but gross long-term debt rose ~$0.86bn in the same period per the 10-Q, so buybacks/dividends may be partly debt-funded [Guessing]. A Q3 2026 dividend was declared 2026-09-03, confirming an ongoing capital-return program (source: https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260903376710/en/TKO-Declares-Third-Quarter-2026-Dividend/) [Certain].

## 6. Unusual items
Short interest is elevated at 13.95% of float and rising (+8.4% month-over-month) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/tko/statistics/) — large for an S&P 500 name, suggesting a sizable bear thesis in the market, likely around leverage or growth durability [Guessing]. Altman Z-score of 1.65 sits in the distress/grey zone (<1.8) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/tko/statistics/), though this is common for asset-light, high-FCF, leveraged media businesses and should be read alongside the falling net-debt/EBITDA trend [Likely]. Controlling shareholder: Endeavor holds ~51% of voting power via Class B shares, a governance risk given the related-party Transition Services Agreement [Certain] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1973266/000119312526330592/tko-20260630.htm).
