# Churchill Downs (CHDN) — Core initiation — 2026-11-08
## 1. Business
Owns the Kentucky Derby and Churchill Downs Racetrack (live racing, sponsorship, hospitality), runs historical racing machine (HRM) venues, regional casinos, and the TwinSpires/United Tote wagering businesses. Profit comes from Derby-week events, HRM and casino gaming win, and wagering fees. No single foreign country is near 10% of revenue [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/chdn/).

## 2. Quality
Decent, not exceptional. Operating margin is 25.8% TTM, up from 19.3% in FY2021, and FCF margin is 17.6% TTM; FCF conversion is 116.5% of net income (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/chdn/financials/ratios/). [Certain] ROIC is only 9.2% and below FY2021's 11.4%, so returns are modest against the capital tied up in casinos and HRM venues. [Certain] The moat is the Derby, which has no substitute, plus state-licensed HRM and casino footprints; but regulatory fragility is real, as Louisiana HRM operations ended on 8 May 2025 after a court ruling [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/20212/000002021226000084/chdn-20260930.htm). [Certain] Leverage is high (net debt/EBITDA 4.65x, Altman Z 1.50), though falling from 8.86x in FY2022. [Certain] Q3 beat on revenue and Adjusted EBITDA per the call summary (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/chdn/transcripts/719958-q3-2026/). [Likely]

## 3. Valuation
Every multiple sits below its own 5y minimum: P/E 15.1x (5y median 23.0x), EV/EBITDA 11.4x (median 19.5x), P/FCF 12.7x (median 40.6x), FCF yield 7.89% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/chdn/statistics/). [Certain] Against peers (BYD, PENN, RRR, MCRI) P/E is 12% lower but EV/EBITDA is 7% higher, so the discount is mainly versus its own past premium rating, not versus regional gaming. [Certain] The price implies -4.7% revenue growth in year 1, fading to terminal growth; the mechanical base fair value (DCF-led) is 231.67 against a 97.94 close, with a bear of 25.89 (leverage-sensitive). [Certain] Inform-only: the DCF is sensitive to FCF durability. Stock is up 12.7% in three months with RSI 73.1, so near-term chasing risk exists but is not a thesis issue. [Likely]

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Net debt / EBITDA (ratios page) rises above 5.5x, i.e. deleveraging stalls.
- TTM FCF margin (cash-flow and income statement pages, trailing view) falls below 12%.
- TTM ROIC (ratios page, trailing view) falls below 8%.
- Asset sale not completed by 2027-03-31 or a regulatory loss of HRM authorisation in Kentucky or Virginia (company filings on sec.gov).

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Shares outstanding fell 4.77% YoY (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/chdn/statistics/). [Certain] On 2026-10-28 the board approved a new $750M buyback replacing the prior programme (source: https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/10/28/3390725/12388/en/churchill-downs-incorporated-announces-new-750-million-share-repurchase-program.html). [Certain] Management is exploring a sale of nine regional gaming properties representing $280 to $300 million of annual Adjusted EBITDA [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/20212/000002021226000084/chdn-20260930.htm); the call said sales are expected by year-end to fund deleveraging and Derby/HRM/TwinSpires investment (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/chdn/transcripts/719958-q3-2026/). [Likely] Buying back stock while leverage is 4.65x is a tension; sale proceeds decide it. [Likely]
