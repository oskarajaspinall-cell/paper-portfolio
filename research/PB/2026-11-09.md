# Prosperity Bancshares (PB) — Core initiation — 2026-11-09
## 1. Business
Texas-focused commercial bank holding company (Houston). It earns net interest income from lending funded by a low-cost, granular deposit base, plus modest fee income. Growth is by serial acquisition: American (closed Jan 2026), Southwest (Feb 2026) and Stellar (Jul 2026) were all merged in this year (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1068851/000119312526338254/pb-20260630.htm) [Certain]. No material foreign revenue; US-domestic [Likely].

## 2. Quality
Adequate rather than exceptional. ROE is 6.5% TTM versus 8.3% in FY2021, a falling trend (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/pb/financials/ratios/) [Certain]. Net margin of 36.0% TTM is below the FY2025 43.4% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/pb/financials/income-statement/) [Certain]. Returns are depressed partly by acquisition goodwill and merger expenses, which swell equity; the 10-Q reports merger-related expenses in the first half (https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1068851/000119312526338254/pb-20260630.htm) [company-reported] [Likely]. The moat is a cheap, sticky Texas deposit franchise and conservative underwriting; it is real but not wide, since peers compete for the same deposits [Likely]. FCF margin is rising over the last 12 months (26.3% to 42.4%) but falling over five years (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/pb/financials/cash-flow-statement/?p=trailing). Beta of 0.65 reflects stable earnings [Likely]. ROIC, ROCE and leverage: [data unavailable] (normal for banks).

## 3. Valuation
P/E 13.2x sits at 40% of its 5y range (11.8x–15.1x) and 12% above the peer median of 11.7x. P/B of 0.9x is at 19% of its range and 34% below peers at 1.3x, which looks cheap but is explained by the lower ROE [Likely]. P/FCF of 14.6x is 78% of its range and 42% above peers. FCF yield is 6.86% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/pb/statistics/).

Mechanical fair value base is 69.91 USD versus the 71.87 close (-2.7%), range 56.66–85.00, but flagged unreliable: methods disagree 2.1x (P/B+ROE 60.59, FCFE DCF 88.54, DDM 41.39). The price therefore assumes roughly fair value with little margin of safety; the reverse DCF implies -2.4% year-1 revenue growth, undemanding if the merged banks hold earnings [Likely]. Not a bargain on earnings, cheap only on book.

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROE (ratios page) stays below 5% on a TTM basis after 2027 integration, i.e. merger synergies fail to lift returns.
- Net margin (income statement page) TTM falls below 30%.
- Shares change YoY (statistics page) stays above 15% on further issuance, showing acquisitions are dilutive rather than accretive.
- Net charge-offs rising above 0.50% in a quarterly release on the company site (https://www.prosperitybankusa.com) or a Texas credit event forcing provisions after management reported none in the first half.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Acquisitive: shares outstanding rose 8.89% YoY (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/pb/statistics/), and the Stellar deal used both stock and cash (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1068851/000119312526338254/pb-20260630.htm) [company-reported]. A dividend increase to 0.64 USD per quarter was declared (source: https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/prosperity-bancshares-inc-increases-common-stock-dividend-302914888.html) [Certain]. FCF conversion was 95.2% of net income in FY2025 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/pb/financials/cash-flow-statement/), historically volatile (88.5% to 146.0%) [Certain].

## 6. Unusual items
The SEC filing delta shows Risk Factors unchanged, but MD&A now lists integration risks: deposit and customer attrition, higher costs than planned and failed synergies from three simultaneous deals [Certain] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1068851/000119312526338254/pb-20260630.htm). A new accounting standard on purchased loans (ASU 2025-08) changes day-one provisioning, which complicates comparisons with prior periods [Certain]. Q3 results are out (source: https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/prosperity-bancshares-inc-reports-third-quarter-2026-earnings-302921536.html); the transcript headline suggests flat-to-slight loan growth, so the 2027 case relies on cost savings [Likely]. Short interest 4.07% of float, not material.
