## Assessment
**Weight: secondary.** For a US regional bank the variable that matters now is risk premium and credit, not the policy path.

**Chain:** credit spreads / risk appetite -> equity risk premium -> P/B multiple; secondary link: curve shape -> net interest margin.

**Facts (FRED, 2026-11-05):** HY spread 3.48%, +0.77pp over 3m (https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=BAMLH0A0HYM2). VIX 28.18, +13.2 over 1m (https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=VIXCLS). The 10y yield is 4.84% and the 2y 4.46%, a positive 0.38pp curve (https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=DGS10). Fed funds 3.88% (https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=DFF).

**Pricing (proxy):** the 2y sits above fed funds, so no cuts are priced, though falling yields over 1m show that expectation easing **(inference)**. The risk-off move is the new information; the spread is still low against a 7% stress anchor.

**Inference:** P/B of 0.9x already discounts weak returns and beta is 0.65. A credit scare would hit the multiple, then provisions, with three deals still being integrated. Nothing in the macro data confirms Texas credit stress.

**Bull:** spreads retrace and the curve steepens; modest re-rating, firmer NIM. **Bear:** spreads keep widening and risk-off persists; the multiple compresses and provisions rise.

**Tilt:** toward bear, small: the shock is recent but modest, and the price is near mechanical fair value.
