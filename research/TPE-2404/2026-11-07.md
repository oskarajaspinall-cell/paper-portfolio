# United Integrated Services (TPE:2404) — Core initiation — 2026-11-07
## 1. Business
Taiwan-listed engineering and construction contractor; I understand it builds cleanroom and process-system turnkey projects for semiconductor and electronics fabs [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tpe/2404/). Revenue is project-based and lumpy, tied to customers' fab capex. Segment mix and backlog are [data unavailable] in the fact sheet and were not researched from primary sources.

## 2. Quality
Reported quality is exceptional. TTM ROCE is 79.8% and ROE 81.3%, up from 29.8% and 28.3% in FY2021 [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tpe/2404/financials/ratios/). Operating margin rose from 12.3% to 17.7% TTM and gross margin from 16.0% to 21.3% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tpe/2404/financials/income-statement/) [Certain]. Net cash every year (net debt/EBITDA -4.21x TTM) [Certain].

The caution is durability. Contractor margins of this size, with FCF conversion of 230.4% of net income, are likely helped by customer prepayments and a capex upcycle, not only a moat [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tpe/2404/financials/cash-flow-statement/). Gross margin was 10.9% in FY2023, so margins are cyclical [Certain]. A moat from fab-qualification and relationships is plausible but unverified [Guessing]. Piotroski F is only 5 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tpe/2404/statistics/).

## 3. Valuation
At 886.00 TWD, P/E is 13.5x against a 5y median of 12.5x and peers at 12.3x. EV/EBITDA is 6.6x (5y median 7.2x, peers 9.4x), and P/FCF 5.8x (peers 8.7x). FCF yield is 17.25%. P/B of 9.2x sits at the top of its 5y range because ROE is so high (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tpe/2404/statistics/) [Certain].

The mechanical base fair value is 2,164.43 TWD (+144.3%), but it is a DCF extrapolating peak FCF margins (44.0% FY2025). Bear is 636.13 (-28.2%) [Certain]. The price implies year-1 revenue falling 26.7% (reverse DCF), i.e. the market treats current earnings as peak [Likely]. Cheap if margins hold; fairly priced if they mean-revert towards the FY2023 level. Price is 20.5% below the 50-day average with RSI 23.3 and a 24.3% fall in 3 months, with no news explaining it [Certain]. Unexplained fall plus earnings on Nov 12, 2026 argue for caution [Likely].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Operating margin (income statement page) falls below 12% on a TTM basis.
- ROCE (ratios page) falls below 40% TTM.
- FCF margin (cash flow page) falls below 15% on a TTM basis, signalling prepayments reversing.
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) turns positive.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Shares change YoY is -0.10%, so dilution is nil [Certain]. FCF/NI was 320.5% in FY2025 and 230.4% TTM, far above earnings, which I read as working-capital inflows (customer advances) that can reverse [Likely]. Payout and capex detail were not researched [data unavailable].
