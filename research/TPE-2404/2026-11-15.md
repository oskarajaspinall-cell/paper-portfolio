# United Integrated Services (TPE:2404) — Core initiation — 2026-11-15

[Likely] It earns project revenue from turnkey cleanroom and fab facility build-outs for high-tech manufacturers, so revenue is lumpy and tied to customers' capex cycles. ## 2. Quality
[Certain] Returns are exceptional for a contractor: TTM ROCE 76.7%, ROE 72.7%, operating margin 19.2%, gross margin 22.9%, up from 16.0% in FY2021 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tpe/2404/financials/ratios/). [Certain] Net cash every year (net debt/EBITDA -2.64x TTM) and FCF conversion of 283.1% of net income. Piotroski F is 6 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tpe/2404/statistics/).
[Certain] Direction is mixed: ROCE is 4.9pp below its 2-year high, and FCF margin fell from 46.7% to 42.1% over 12 months.
[Likely] FCF far above earnings suggests customer advance payments and working-capital inflow, which reverse when project awards slow. [Guessing] The moat is execution record and customer relationships, not structural; margins above the 5-year range look cyclical.

## 3. Valuation
[Certain] P/E 13.5x against a 5y median of 12.5x (44% of range) and peers at 14.9x; EV/EBITDA 7.7x vs 7.2x median and peers 11.3x; P/B 8.4x, 82% of range, 39% above peers. FCF yield is 21.11% and P/FCF 4.7x, below its 5y minimum (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tpe/2404/statistics/).
[Certain] The mechanical fair value is flagged unreliable (methods disagree 7.5x; base +167%); I weigh it lightly. The reverse DCF implies year-1 revenue falling 26.6%.
[Likely] The price assumes today's peak-like FCF and margins fade materially. Earnings-based multiples are only fair, so the cheapness is in cash flow, which is the least durable part.
