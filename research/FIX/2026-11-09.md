# Comfort Systems USA (FIX) — Core initiation — 2026-11-09
## 1. Business
US mechanical, electrical and plumbing contractor. Mostly project work: about 94.7% of revenue is earned on a project basis, bid at a fixed price and margin [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1035983/000110465926118159/fix-20260930x10q.htm). Demand is led by technology customers, especially data centers (same source) [Certain]. US-only; no foreign country is ~10% of revenue [Likely]. Market cap 35.5bn USD (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/fix/).

## 2. Quality
Returns are exceptional and still rising: TTM ROIC 103.2% vs 41.9% in FY2024, operating margin 16.8% vs 10.6%, FCF margin 19.5% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/fix/financials/ratios/) [Certain]. Net cash (net debt/EBITDA -0.81x) and Altman Z 7.27 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/fix/statistics/) [Certain].
Moat is modest: local labour, project execution and scarce skilled capacity, not IP. Margins roughly doubled in three years on a data-center boom, and bid-based project work means pricing power is cyclical [Likely]. Management says cost and supply-chain pressure will persist for several quarters (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1035983/000110465926118159/fix-20260930x10q.htm) [company-reported]. Same-store revenue grew 46.3% in Q3 [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1035983/000110465926118159/fix-20260930x10q.htm). Good business, but TTM returns are likely peak-level [Likely].

## 3. Valuation
P/E 22.0x sits at 34% of its 5y range (median 24.8x) and below the peer median 23.0x; EV/EBITDA 14.8x is at 14% of range but 17% above peers; P/FCF 14.6x is at 6% of range, 35% below peers (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/fix/financials/ratios/) [Certain]. FCF yield 6.87% [Certain].
The price has fallen 40.6% in three months and sits 36.6% under the 200-day average, so the market is de-rating peak earnings (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/fix/history/) [Likely]. Reverse DCF: the price implies 9.5% year-1 revenue growth fading to terminal [Certain]. Mechanical fair value base is 991.73 against a close of 1,017.50, but it is flagged unreliable (DCF and EV/EBITDA disagree 3.8x), so weigh it lightly [Certain]. Cheap versus history on cash flow, not versus peers on sales or book.

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Gross margin (income statement page) falls below 22% on a TTM basis.
- Operating margin (income statement, TTM) falls below 12%.
- FCF margin (cash flow page, TTM) falls below 10%.
- Reported backlog (10-Q/10-K MD&A) declines sequentially for two consecutive quarters.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Net cash and a raised quarterly dividend (headline: https://www.tipranks.com/news/the-fly/comfort-systems-usa-raises-quarterly-dividend-to-1-00-per-share-from-90c-thefly-news) [Likely]. Shares change -0.73% YoY, so no dilution (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/fix/statistics/) [Certain]. Acquisitions are small bolt-ons, adding 7.4% to Q3 revenue [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1035983/000110465926118159/fix-20260930x10q.htm). FCF/NI TTM 149.6%, but the 10-Q attributes much of the operating cash flow rise to billings in excess of costs and deferred revenue (same 10-Q) [company-reported]. That is working-capital timing that can reverse [Likely].
