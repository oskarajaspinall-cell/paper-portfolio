# OceanaGold Corporation (TSX:OGC) — Core initiation — 2026-10-11

## 1. Business
OceanaGold operates four producing mines — Haile (South Carolina, US), Macraes and Waihi (New Zealand) and Didipio (Philippines, gold-copper) — selling gold (and copper concentrate from Didipio) at prevailing market prices [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tsx/OGC/). It is a price-taking commodity producer: revenue and margin are driven mainly by the gold price, not by pricing power [Certain].

## 2. Quality
Returns have surged: ROIC 43.4% TTM and ROCE 39.9% TTM, up from -1.0%/-0.8% in FY2021, operating margin 49.0% TTM vs 21.8% FY2024, net debt/EBITDA -0.42x (net cash) (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tsx/OGC/financials/ratios/). This is [Likely] driven mainly by the gold price cycle rather than a structural cost or pricing advantage — gold mining has no durable moat against peers capturing the same price; the "moat," if any, is relative reserve/cost quality, which stockanalysis.com does not disclose (no mine-level AISC/reserve data). Diversification across three jurisdictions (US, NZ, Philippines) reduces single-asset risk but adds permitting/political exposure — recent NZ fast-track permit applications for Macraes Phase 4 are pending [Likely] (source: https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/oceanagold-submits-permit-application-and-provides-technical-and-exploration-update-supporting-mine-life-extension-at-macraes-302873359.html). Shares outstanding fell 4.2% YoY (buybacks), supporting per-share quality (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tsx/OGC/statistics/). FCF conversion (FCF/NI) is falling on a 5y trend (-1,624.7pp from an unusually high FY2021 base) though TTM conversion is still 85.2% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tsx/OGC/financials/ratios/).

## 3. Valuation
OGC trades at a steep discount to peers (AEM, KGC, EQX, NGD) and its own 5y range: P/E 7.3x (peer median 12.5x, -41%), EV/EBITDA 3.9x (peer median 8.2x, -53%), P/FCF 8.4x, below its 5y minimum (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tsx/OGC/financials/ratios/). But the fact sheet's fair-value model (EV/EBITDA, 100% weight) puts bear/base case at 15.64/17.76 CAD — 55-60% below the 39.36 CAD close — with bull case (using peak TTM earnings) at only 46.78 CAD, +18.9% (source: fact sheet fair value table). The gap implies the model treats current TTM earnings as a cyclical peak and normalizes bear/base cases to a lower, through-cycle level [Likely]. So the apparent cheapness on reported multiples is largely a function of currently elevated gold-price margins, not a structural re-rating opportunity: the current price already assumes near-current gold-price conditions persist, with limited further re-rating room even in the bull case.

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROIC or ROCE (ratios page) falls back below 15% from the current 43.4%/39.9% TTM, confirming margin expansion was cyclical, not structural.
- Operating margin (income statement) compresses below 30% from 49.0% TTM, signalling cost inflation is outrunning the gold price.
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) turns positive and exceeds 1.0x, reversing the current net-cash position and reducing balance-sheet flexibility.
- FCF margin (cash flow/income statement) falls below 10% from 28.6% FY2025/30.0% TTM.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Net debt/EBITDA is -0.42x TTM (net cash) (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tsx/OGC/financials/ratios/), and shares outstanding have fallen 4.2% YoY, indicating active buybacks (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tsx/OGC/statistics/). FCF margin has risen from -8.5% (FY2021) to 30.0% TTM (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tsx/OGC/financials/cash-flow-statement/), funding both growth capex and buybacks without leverage. [Likely] this is supportive of continued capital returns, though conversion (FCF/NI) is on a declining 5y trend from an unusually high FY2021 base.

## 6. Unusual items
CEO Gerard Bond announced his retirement, effective April 2027 [Certain] (source: https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/oceanagold-president-and-ceo-to-retire-in-q2-2027-302888238.html) — a leadership-transition risk over the next ~18 months. The company has outlined a growth plan centred on Waihi North and a proposed Ausgold acquisition [Likely] (source: https://www.marketbeat.com/instant-alerts/event-oceanagold-maps-30-production-growth-with-waihi-north-ausgold-deal-2026-10-05/); this is unfunded/undisclosed in scale on stockanalysis.com and could add M&A integration or dilution risk. Beta (5y) is 1.50, above the market, consistent with gold-equity volatility (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tsx/OGC/statistics/). Short interest is [data unavailable].
