# Macro overlay: OceanaGold Corporation (TSX:OGC) — 2026-10-11
## Assessment
`macro_weight: primary` — OGC is a price-taking gold/copper miner (Haile, Macraes, Waihi, Didipio); the thesis debates TTM returns (ROIC 43.4%, operating margin 49.0% [Certain], stockanalysis.com ratios/income statement) vs. the evaluator's view this is a cyclical gold-price peak, not structural (research/TSX-OGC/2026-10-11.md).

**Chain**: real yields (opportunity cost of gold) + trade-weighted dollar (gold's USD price) → gold spot price → OGC revenue and, since AISC costs are largely fixed, operating-leverage-amplified margin/FCF → earnings and the EV/EBITDA multiple.

**What's priced** — facts: 10y real yield (DFII10) 2.87% on 2026-10-08, +0.44pp/1m, +0.56pp/3m; dollar index (DTWEXBGS) 121.4 on 2026-10-02, +2.9/1m; breakeven (T10YIE) flat at 2.33% (fred.stlouisfed.org). The real-yield rise is a genuine real-rate move, not an inflation-expectation shift — historically most negative for gold [Likely] **(inference)**. Proxy: 2y yield (4.75%) sits 0.87pp above fed funds (3.88%, DFF), consistent with policy still priced higher-for-longer [Likely] **(inference, proxy)**. VIX fell to 15.41 while HY spreads widened only modestly (3.15%, +0.48pp/1m) — a hawkish-but-calm repricing, not a safe-haven bid for gold [Likely] **(inference)**. OGC's tape (-15.6% 6m, +11.7% 3m, +19.0% 12m) suggests only partial pass-through so far, company news offsetting the macro drag [Guessing].

**Bull**: real yields/dollar stabilise or reverse → gold holds/rises → fixed costs preserve margin, supporting re-rating toward peers (EV/EBITDA 8.2x vs 3.9x). **Bear**: real yields/dollar keep rising → gold falls → margin compresses faster than revenue, confirming the bear fair value (15.64-17.76 CAD, -55/-60% vs close). **Tilt**: toward bear, moderate.
