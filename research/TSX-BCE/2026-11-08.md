# BCE Inc. (TSX:BCE) — Core initiation — 2026-11-08
## 1. Business
Canada's largest telecom: wireless, home internet and TV, business services and fibre, plus Bell Media. Revenue is subscription-based and regulated, with a minor U.S. fibre presence [Likely]. Geographic split not verified from a primary source; no single foreign country is known to be ~10%+ of revenue [Guessing] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tsx/BCE/).

## 2. Quality
Adequate, not exceptional, and weakening. Gross margin of 44.9% TTM is up from 43.0% in FY2021, and operating margin is steady at 21.7% vs 22.4% [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tsx/BCE/financials/income-statement/). Returns are thin for a capital-heavy network: ROIC 6.1% TTM, falling 1.9pp over 12 months; ROCE 7.8% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tsx/BCE/financials/ratios/?p=trailing). FY2025 ROE of 32.0% and net margin of 26.4% are one-offs, not run-rate [Likely]. Moat is the oligopoly and spectrum/fibre scale in Canada [Likely], but returns are not above cost of capital by a wide margin. Net debt/EBITDA has risen from 3.53x to 4.67x; Altman Z is 0.75 and Piotroski F is 6 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tsx/BCE/statistics/) [Certain]. Q3 results showed revenue and adjusted EBITDA growth, better wireless retention, and reaffirmed guidance (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tsx/BCE/) [Likely].

## 3. Valuation
Cheap on earnings and assets, with the cash-flow decline as the catch. P/E is 11.9x vs a 5y median of 22.1x and peers at 17.7x (-33%). EV/EBITDA of 7.6x is below its 5y minimum of 8.3x and 8% under peers. P/B of 1.2x is below its 5y minimum. FCF yield is 8.85%. But P/FCF is 11.3x, 30% above the peer median of 8.7x, and FCF margin has fallen from 16.2% to 10.1% in 12 months [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tsx/BCE/statistics/). Reverse DCF implies revenue shrinking 9.7% in year one, which looks too pessimistic for a subscription business [Likely]. Mechanical base fair value is 70.96 CAD vs a 30.57 close; the bear case of 54.76 still sits well above the price. Read the fair value cautiously: the DCF leans on a FCF base that is now lower, and the EV/EBITDA leg gives 48.64 [Likely].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- FCF margin (cash-flow page, TTM) stays below 10% for the next two quarterly updates.
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) rises above 5.0x.
- Operating margin (income statement, TTM) falls below 20%.
- ROIC (ratios page, TTM) falls below 5%.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Cash conversion is erratic: FCF/NI was 51.0% in FY2025 and 898.5% in FY2024 because earnings were distorted, and is 98.9% TTM. Leverage has climbed for five years while shares outstanding rose 0.91% YoY, so equity is being diluted rather than bought back [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tsx/BCE/statistics/). Management is selling a minority stake in a new wireless tower company to La Caisse, with proceeds said to support growth and deleveraging, and reaffirmed its leverage target (source: https://www.nasdaq.com/articles/bce-inc-announces-agreement-la-caisse-sell-499-stake-wireless-tower-infrastructure-company) [Likely]. Deleveraging by asset sale lowers future EBITDA and cash flow, so it is not free [Likely].

## 6. Unusual items
Altman Z of 0.75 flags balance-sheet stress [Certain]. Tower sale to La Caisse is a dated catalyst (source: https://www.bnnbloomberg.ca/business/technology/2026/10/27/bce-sells-499-stake-in-new-wireless-tower-company-to-la-caisse-for-245b/) [Likely].
