# Verizon Communications Inc. (VZ) — Core initiation — 2026-10-11
## 1. Business
Verizon is the largest US wireless carrier by revenue, earning recurring service revenue from postpaid/prepaid wireless data and voice priced on owned nationwide spectrum and network infrastructure, plus Fios broadband/video and a declining enterprise wireline segment. Scale and owned spectrum let it monetize heavy, continuous network capex across a large subscriber base [Certain] (source: fact sheet OV).

## 2. Quality
Verizon's moat rests on owned low/mid-band spectrum and a nationwide network few can replicate given its capex intensity, in a US wireless market that is effectively a duopoly/triopoly with AT&T and T-Mobile [Certain]. But returns are eroding: 5y ROIC fell 3.2pp to 8.3% TTM, ROCE fell 2.2pp to 9.2%, and net margin fell 4.1pp to 11.6% (source: fact sheet RA/IS) [Certain]. Net debt/EBITDA is high at 3.76x for a low-growth business (source: fact sheet BS/IS) [Certain]. New-entrant risk: SpaceX agreed to buy a nationwide low-band spectrum portfolio to expand Starlink Mobile's direct-to-device service; multiple outlets framed this as a structural threat to the big three carriers' spectrum-scarcity advantage, though the deal's near-term commercial/financial impact on VZ is unconfirmed [Guessing] (source: https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/09/verizon-att-tmobile-stocks-spacex-network.html).

## 3. Valuation
VZ trades at 10.9x P/E, 48% above the peer median (T, TMUS, VOD, BCE: 7.3x) but mid-range of its own 5y history (52nd percentile) (source: fact sheet RA) [Certain]. EV/EBITDA (7.1x) and P/FCF (8.0x) sit below peer medians (-11%, -7%), and P/FCF is below its own 5y low (-13%), with a 12.44% FCF yield — on cash generation the market prices VZ worse than its peers or its own history [Certain]. P/B (1.7x) is 38% above peers, consistent with VZ's higher ROE. The fact sheet's DCF-based fair value (base $88.25) implies +111.9% upside to the $41.65 close, but the reverse DCF shows the close already prices in an 11.4% revenue decline in year 1 — an unusually bearish embedded assumption versus VZ's roughly flat historical revenue — so the fair-value gap should be read directionally, not as a precise target [Likely] (source: fact sheet).

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROIC (ratios page) falls below 7% (vs 8.3% TTM), confirming returns keep eroding without an offsetting competitive response.
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) rises above 4.0x (vs 3.76x TTM), reducing balance-sheet flexibility.
- FCF margin (cash-flow/income statement) falls below 10% (vs 15.5% TTM) on step-up capex or wireless price competition.
- Postpaid wireless subscriber losses turn persistent, or filings confirm material market-share loss to satellite/direct-to-device entrants (text-only trigger, monitored each release).

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
FCF conversion is strong: TTM FCF margin 15.5% on FCF/NI of 133.1% (source: fact sheet CF/IS) [Certain]. Shares outstanding fell 0.33% YoY, implying modest net buybacks (source: fact sheet ST) [Certain]. Leverage (net debt/EBITDA 3.76x) is elevated for a mature, low-growth telecom and likely keeps capital allocation skewed toward debt service and the dividend rather than aggressive buybacks or M&A [Likely] (source: fact sheet BS/IS). ## 6. Unusual items
Altman Z-score is 1.22, in the distress zone (<1.8), and Piotroski F-score is a middling 4/9 (source: fact sheet ST) [Certain]. Short interest is low (2.03% of float) but rose 10.1% month-on-month into the SpaceX-spectrum selloff (source: fact sheet ST) [Certain]. Beta (5y) is only 0.25 — low for a stock that just had an outsized single-day move — suggesting the move was idiosyncratic/sector-specific rather than market-beta-driven [Likely] (source: fact sheet ST).
