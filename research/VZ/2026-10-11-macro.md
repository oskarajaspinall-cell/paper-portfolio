# Macro overlay: Verizon Communications Inc. (VZ) — 2026-10-11
## Assessment
VZ's bull/bear case is fundamentals-driven (spectrum moat vs. SpaceX's low-band spectrum purchase), but its leverage and bond-proxy dividend profile make it secondarily rate-sensitive [Likely] **(inference)**. Chain: real/nominal yields → cost of equity & debt → VZ's DCF discount rate and refinancing cost on its 3.76x net debt/EBITDA (fact sheet BS/IS) → fair-value multiple and dividend-yield appeal vs. the risk-free rate.

Market pricing: US 10y real yield (DFII10) is 2.87% on 2026-10-08, up 0.56pp/3m and 0.44pp/1m (FRED, https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=DFII10) [Certain]. Nominal 10y (DGS10) is 5.22%, up 0.68pp/3m (same date/source) [Certain] — this is also the risk-free rate the fact sheet's own DCF already uses, so the higher rate is already in the base fair value. HY credit spreads (BAMLH0A0HYM2) widened to 3.15%, +0.48pp/1m (FRED, 2026-10-08) [Certain]. Breakeven inflation (T10YIE) is flat at 2.33% (-0.04pp/1m), so the rise looks like a real-rate/term-premium move, not an inflation surprise [Likely] **(inference)**. The 2y yield (4.75%) sitting 0.87pp above fed funds (3.88%) proxies a market not pricing near-term cuts **(inference)**.

Bull: yields stabilize/fall, financing eases, VZ's bond-proxy multiple re-rates up. Bear: yields keep rising, discount rate and refinancing cost climb further given VZ's leverage, compressing the multiple. Tilt: moderate toward bear — rates already moved against VZ, and its balance sheet (Altman Z 1.22, distress zone, fact sheet ST) is more exposed than peers' to a further rise.
