# The Cigna Group (CI) — Core initiation — 2026-11-08
## 1. Business
Two engines: Evernorth (pharmacy benefit services, specialty pharmacy, fee-based health services) earns per-claim and service fees; Cigna Healthcare sells employer-funded and insured medical plans, earning premiums less medical costs. Revenue is overwhelmingly US-based; no single foreign country is shown as ~10%+ [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/ci/). The HCSC transaction shrank premiums year on year [Certain] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1739940/000173994026000071/ci-20260930.htm).

## 2. Quality
Returns are decent and stable rather than exceptional: TTM ROIC 12.7% (5y trend rising), ROCE 10.7%, ROE 15.6% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/ci/financials/ratios/) [Certain]. Operating margin 3.8% and FCF margin 2.9% are thin, as for any scaled intermediary, and gross margin has fallen from 13.2% to 9.1% over five years [Certain]. Direction table: ROIC, ROCE, operating and FCF margin are all flat to slightly up over the last year (+0.7pp, +1.0pp, +0.1pp, +0.1pp), though ROIC sits 1.6pp below its March 2026 peak; no Direction change line fired (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/ci/financials/ratios/?p=trailing) [Certain]. Moat: PBS scale and integration with medical and specialty pharmacy; policy risk on rebates is the threat [Likely]. Q3 adjusted results beat and 2026 guidance was raised (source: https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/the-cigna-group-reports-strong-third-quarter-2026-results-raises-2026-outlook-302930018.html) [Likely]. Net debt/EBITDA 1.87x, Piotroski 7 [Certain].

## 3. Valuation
The price assumes decline: reverse DCF implies -2.7% year-1 revenue growth, fading to terminal growth [Certain]. P/E 13.0x is near the bottom of its 5y range (12.3x–22.4x, median 15.1x) and 42% below the peer median; EV/EBITDA 7.9x is below the 5y minimum; P/FCF 9.4x; FCF yield 10.69% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/ci/financials/ratios/) [Certain]. Peers (UNH, ELV, CVS, HUM, CNC) are depressed by managed-care cost trends, so the peer discount partly reflects a different, more insurance-heavy mix [Likely]. Mechanical fair value: bear 268.11, base 421.42, bull 852.56 against a 293.59 close; base is +43.5%, bear -8.7% [Certain]. The wide bull case is DCF-driven and I give it little weight [Guessing]. Skew is favourable, with the market pricing in PBS rebate and GLP-1 pressure already [Likely].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROIC (ratios page, trailing) falls below 10% on a TTM basis.
- Operating margin (income statement, trailing) falls below 3.0%.
- FCF margin (cash flow statement, trailing) falls below 2.0%.
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) rises above 2.5x.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
FCF conversion is 138.0% of net income TTM (5y rising); shares are down 2.66% year on year, so buybacks are shrinking the count (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/ci/statistics/) [Certain]. A quarterly dividend was declared (source: https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/the-cigna-group-declares-quarterly-dividend-302922675.html) [Certain]. Leverage has fallen from 2.84x to 1.87x net debt/EBITDA [Certain]. The new Accelerate to One programme targets cost savings through 2030, partly reinvested (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1739940/000173994026000071/ci-20260930.htm) [Certain].

## 6. Unusual items
SEC delta: Risk Factors unchanged; MD&A adds the Accelerate to One initiative and notes the planned exit from Individual and Family Plans medical business from the start of 2027 [Certain] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1739940/000173994026000071/ci-20260930.htm). Management flagged 2027 growth headwinds from PBS rebate changes and GLP-1 costs (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/ci/transcripts/719833-q3-2026/) [Likely]. Short interest 2.58% of float, beta 0.25; nothing governance-related [Certain].
