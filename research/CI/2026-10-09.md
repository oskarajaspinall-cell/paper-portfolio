# The Cigna Group (CI) — Core initiation — 2026-10-09

## 1. Business
Two segments: Cigna Healthcare sells employer-sponsored medical insurance (premiums/admin fees) to 18M+ members [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/ci/transcripts/749380-investor-day-2026/). Evernorth Health Services runs pharmacy-benefit management (the new fee-based "Signature" model replacing rebates) and specialty pharmacy/distribution (Accredo), managing ~$140bn of drug spend for 117M Americans [Likely] (same source). Evernorth is ~37% of earnings and the faster-growing unit [Likely] (same source).

## 2. Quality
Returns are improving and above cost of capital: ROIC 8.8%→13.4% TTM, ROE 11.1%→16.8% TTM, both rising over five years [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/ci/financials/ratios/). FCF conversion (FCF/NI) has averaged well above 100% every year, 142.0% TTM [Certain] (same source), and net debt/EBITDA has fallen from 2.84x to 1.93x [Certain] (same source) — a deleveraging, cash-generative franchise, not a value trap. The moat is scale rather than brand: Evernorth's negotiating scale in PBM and its specialty-distribution network (330+ limited-distribution drugs) are genuine switching-cost advantages for plan sponsors [Likely] (source: investor-day transcript, above), but UNH, ELV, HUM and CVS have comparable scale, so this is an industry oligopoly advantage, not Cigna-specific. Gross margin has fallen 3.9pp over five years (13.2%→9.1%) and operating margin is flat-to-down (4.7%→3.9%) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/ci/financials/income-statement/), consistent with industry-wide medical-cost-ratio and PBM-reform pressure rather than company-specific share loss.

## 3. Valuation
The stock is priced as structurally impaired versus history and peers: P/E 11.5x is 8% below its own five-year minimum and 51% below the UNH/ELV/HUM/CVS median (23.6x); EV/EBITDA 7.7x is 17% below its five-year min and 43% below peers; P/FCF 8.1x is 42% below peers; only P/B (1.7x, -21% vs peers) is near historical norms [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/ci/financials/ratios/, peer statistics pages). The reverse DCF implies -16% year-one revenue fading to terminal growth [Certain] (source: fact sheet valuation block) — a distress-level assumption guidance direction does not support [Likely] (source: fact sheet news, 2026-09-30 headline). DCF fair value ($793 base vs. $281 close) is directionally supportive but sensitive to terminal assumptions; the multiples cross-check (uniformly below own-history and peer levels) is the more robust mispricing evidence [Likely].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROIC (ratios page) falls back below 9%, giving up the five-year improvement.
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios/balance sheet pages) rises back above 2.5x, reversing the deleveraging trend.
- FCF conversion (cash flow/income statement) drops below 100% on a trailing-twelve-month basis, signalling deteriorating earnings quality.
- Net margin (income statement) falls below 1.5% TTM, confirming margin compression is structural rather than cyclical.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Shares outstanding fell 3.55% year-on-year, indicating ongoing buybacks funded by the 142% FCF-to-net-income conversion [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/ci/statistics/, cash flow statement). Management is redirecting some of that cash into a $3bn multi-year "Accelerate to One" modernization program aimed at workflow/AI efficiency [Likely] (source: https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/the-cigna-group-hosts-2026-investor-day-introduces-lead-to-one-to-drive-durable-growth-through-complex-care-leadership-and-differentiated-capabilities-302894288.html, per fact sheet news). Net leverage continues to fall (1.89x-2.08x range over five years to 1.93x TTM), leaving balance-sheet capacity for further buybacks or bolt-on deals [Certain] (source: ratios page).

## 6. Unusual items
Short interest is low (2.5% of float) but rose 25.2% month-on-month [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/ci/statistics/) — not yet material given the small base. Beta is low (0.30), consistent with defensive managed-care positioning [Certain] (same source). No governance or accounting red flags identified (Piotroski F-score 6/9, Altman Z 2.81) [Certain] (same source).
