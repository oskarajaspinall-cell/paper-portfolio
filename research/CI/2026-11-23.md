# The Cigna Group (CI) — Core initiation — 2026-11-23
## 1. Business
Two engines: Evernorth (pharmacy benefit services, specialty pharmacy, care services), which earns fees and pharmacy revenue, and Cigna Healthcare, which earns premiums and fees on commercial and international health plans. Revenue is mostly pass-through drug spend, hence thin margins (operating margin 3.8%, gross margin 9.1%) (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/ci/financials/income-statement/). No foreign country is disclosed here as ~10%+ of revenue [data unavailable]. [Likely]

## 2. Quality
Returns are decent and improving: ROIC 12.6% TTM vs 8.8% in FY2021, ROCE 10.7% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/ci/financials/ratios/). [Certain] Direction check: ROIC is 2.1pp below its 2-year high of 14.7%, but +0.8pp vs a year earlier; the dip is mild, not a break. [Certain] Gross margin fell 3.9pp over five years and operating margin is flat, so profit growth has come from volume, buybacks (shares -2.66% YoY) and capital efficiency, not margin. [Likely] FCF conversion is strong at 138% of net income; leverage fell to 1.87x net debt/EBITDA. [Certain] Moat: scale in pharmacy benefits and specialty, with 2027 rebate and GLP-1 headwinds flagged by management (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/ci/transcripts/719833-q3-2026/). [Likely] Moat is real but politically exposed. Cigna is exiting Individual and Family Plans from 1 January 2027 (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1739940/000173994026000071/ci-20260930.htm). [Certain]

## 3. Valuation
P/E 12.0x is below its 5y minimum (12.3x, median 15.1x) and 38% under the peer median of 19.4x; EV/EBITDA 7.5x is also below its 5y minimum; P/FCF 8.6x sits at the bottom of its range. FCF yield is 11.58%. [Certain] The reverse DCF says the price implies -3.9% revenue growth in year 1. [Certain] Mechanical fair value: bear 262.71, base 411.73, bull 828.98 vs a 270.90 close (base +52.0%); the bear is only -3.0% away, though the bull rests on a DCF that is highly sensitive. [Certain] Price is 4.3% below the 200-day average, with 3-month relative strength +8.3pp vs SPY but a 12-month change of -0.4%. The market is pricing sector regulatory risk (Medicare Advantage audit rule sell-off in UNH/HUM; CI named in the fall) more than company deterioration. [Likely]

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROIC (ratios page, TTM) falls below 10%.
- FCF margin (cash flow / income statement TTM) falls below 2%.
- Net debt / EBITDA (ratios page) rises above 3.0x.
- Operating margin (income statement TTM) falls below 3.0%.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Buybacks are shrinking the share count (-2.66% YoY) and leverage is falling, so deleveraging and repurchases are both funded from FCF at 138% of net income. [Certain] Management has announced a multi-year productivity programme, Accelerate to One, running through 2030, with part of the savings reinvested (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1739940/000173994026000071/ci-20260930.htm). [Certain] Altman Z 2.82 and Piotroski F 7 are acceptable. Execution risk on the programme and on 2027 pharmacy headwinds is the swing factor. [Likely]
