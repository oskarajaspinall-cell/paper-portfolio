# The Cigna Group (CI) — Core initiation — 2026-11-16
## 1. Business
Cigna earns fees and spreads on two engines: Evernorth (pharmacy benefit services, specialty pharmacy, care delivery) and Cigna Healthcare (employer-funded and insured medical plans, international). Most profit comes from fee-based services and rebate/pharmacy economics, not underwriting risk. [Likely] Revenue is overwhelmingly US; no single foreign country is disclosed as ~10%+ in the sources read [Guessing]. (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/ci/)

## 2. Quality
Returns are decent and improving: ROIC TTM 12.7% vs 8.8% in FY2021, ROE 15.6% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/ci/financials/ratios/) [Certain]. Margins are thin by construction (operating margin 3.8%) and gross margin has fallen 3.9pp over five years, so the moat is scale and employer/PBM relationships, not pricing power [Likely]. Cash conversion is strong: FCF/NI 138.0% TTM, net debt/EBITDA 1.87x, Piotroski 7. Rolling-12-month ROIC is +0.7pp year on year but below its 2-year high of 14.3%; FCF margin is flat at 2.9% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/ci/financials/ratios/?p=trailing). Q3 results lifted the 2026 outlook (source: https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/the-cigna-group-reports-strong-third-quarter-2026-results-raises-2026-outlook-302930018.html) [Certain]. Management flags 2027 pressure from PBM rebate reform, GLP-1 costs and the individual ACA exit (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/ci/transcripts/719833-q3-2026/) [Likely].

## 3. Valuation
P/E 13.2x sits at 8% of its 5y range (12.3x–22.4x); EV/EBITDA 8.1x is below the 5y minimum; P/FCF 9.6x; FCF yield 10.40% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/ci/statistics/). Versus the peer median (UNH, ELV, CVS, HUM, CNC) P/E is -44% and EV/EBITDA -39%, though peers are depressed by managed-care cost trouble, so the discount is partly a business-mix difference (CI is more fee-based) [Likely]. Mechanical base fair value is 408.94 vs a 297.00 close (+37.7%); bear 261.15 (-12.1%). The reverse DCF implies -1.5% revenue growth in year 1, which looks undemanding for a business that just raised guidance [Likely]. Fair value is a model output, with DCF carrying 67% weight and a bull case far above the base, so treat the upside as indicative [Guessing]. Price is above its 50- and 200-day averages (+3.2% / +5.0%).

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROIC TTM (ratios page, trailing) falls below 10%.
- Operating margin TTM (income statement, trailing) falls below 3.0%.
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) rises above 2.5x.
- FCF margin TTM (cash flow statement, trailing) falls below 2.0%, or FCF/NI below 100%.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Shares outstanding fell 2.66% YoY, so buybacks are shrinking the count rather than offsetting dilution (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/ci/statistics/) [Certain]. FCF conversion of 138.0% TTM supports this, and leverage has fallen to 1.87x from 2.84x in FY2021 [Certain]. The company calls its multi-year Accelerate to One programme a cost-saving initiative, with part of the savings reinvested (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1739940/000173994026000071/ci-20260930.htm) [Certain]. Management's long-term growth framework leans on specialty growth (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/ci/transcripts/798786-ubs-global-healthcare-conference/) [Likely].

## 6. Unusual items
- The Q3 10-Q has no substantive Risk Factor change. MD&A discloses the planned exit from Individual and Family Plans medical business and the absence of prior-year gains on business sales, which depress Q3 net income year on year (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1739940/000173994026000071/ci-20260930.htm) [Certain].
- 2027 PBM rebate and GLP-1 headwinds are the main regulatory/earnings risk [Likely].
- Beta 0.25, short interest 2.38% of float, so defensive and uncrowded (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/ci/statistics/) [Certain].
