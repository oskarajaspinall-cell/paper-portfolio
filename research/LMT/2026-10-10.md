# Lockheed Martin Corporation (LMT) — Core initiation — 2026-10-10

## 1. Business
LMT designs, builds and sustains defense systems across four segments: Aeronautics (F-35, fighter jets), Missiles and Fire Control (PAC-3, precision munitions), Rotary and Mission Systems (helicopters, sensors, radar) and Space (satellites, missile defense) (source: https://www.lockheedmartin.com/en-us/who-we-are/business-areas.html) [Certain]. Revenue is driven by multi-year U.S. government and allied-nation contracts, with margins from program execution and aftermarket sustainment [Certain].

## 2. Quality
ROIC is high but declining (38.4%→23.9% FY21-25, TTM 27.4%); ROCE drifted down too (21.2%→19.2%, TTM 21.7%); margins compressed: gross 13.6%→10.2%, operating 11.7%→9.3%, net 9.4%→6.7% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/lmt/financials/ratios/) [Certain]. This points to program-cost pressure (fixed-price classified/development work), not a one-off. The moat is real: sole-source, decades-long platforms (F-35, PAC-3) with high switching costs and government demand, evidenced by Beta of 0.10 and continued order flow — a large PAC-3 seeker production contract and a new PAC-3 Edge interceptor (source: fact sheet news, headlines dated 2026-10-05/10-09) [Likely]. Returns falling despite steady revenue points to the firm's own program mix eroding capital returns, not competitor encroachment [Likely]. FCF conversion is strong and rising (138.8% TTM), a quality offset [Certain]. Net debt/EBITDA rose to 1.73x TTM (2.25x FY25) — a leverage trend to watch, not yet a solvency concern (Altman Z 3.47, Piotroski F 7) (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/lmt/statistics/) [Certain].

## 3. Valuation
At 18.8x P/E and 13.9x EV/EBITDA, LMT trades mid-range of its own 5-year history (48%/51% percentile) and at a discount to peers on P/E (-15%), EV/Sales (-18%) and P/FCF (-22%), roughly in line on EV/EBITDA (+2%) (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/lmt/financials/ratios/) [Certain]. P/B at 13.4x looks extreme vs peers (+276%) but is not meaningful given the high-ROE, low-tangible-equity model common to defense primes [Certain]. The reverse-DCF shows the price implies -1.3% revenue growth in year 1 fading to 2.5% terminal growth — a pessimistic embedded assumption against rising global defense budgets and recent order flow [Likely]. DCF-based base fair value is $612.84 (+20.3% vs last close $509.59); EV/EBITDA-based base is $581.63 [Certain]. A below-peer multiple plus a pessimistic embedded growth assumption is the core of the valuation case [Likely].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Gross margin (income statement page) falls below 9% on a trailing-twelve-month basis, confirming program cost overruns are structural, not transitory.
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) rises above 2.5x, indicating leverage is funding working-capital or program losses rather than returns-accretive investment.
- ROIC (ratios page) falls below 20% TTM, showing the moat is no longer translating into superior capital returns.
- FCF conversion (cash flow + income statement) falls below 90% TTM, signalling cash quality of earnings has deteriorated.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
FCF margin is 11.3% TTM and FCF conversion versus net income is 138.8% TTM, both improving from FY24 lows (7.4%/99.1%) (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/lmt/financials/cash-flow-statement/) [Certain]. The board raised the quarterly dividend to $3.60/share (+$0.15) on Oct 9, 2026, continuing a long dividend-growth record (source: fact sheet news & sentiment, headline dated 2026-10-09, https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/lockheed-martin-declares-fourth-quarter-2026-dividend-302903859.html) [Certain]. Shares outstanding fell 1.97% YoY (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/lmt/statistics/), indicating continued buybacks alongside the dividend [Certain]. Rising net debt/EBITDA (1.73x TTM, up from 0.90x FY21) means more of the balance sheet now funds this distribution policy and program investment, a trend to monitor but not yet a red flag [Likely].

## 6. Unusual items
Short interest is low (1.22% of float) and institutional ownership is high (75.9%), both unremarkable (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/lmt/statistics/) [Certain]. Beta of 0.10 is unusually low for an industrial and reflects defense's historically low correlation to the broader market — a diversification benefit, not a flag [Certain]. No governance or legal items in the news feed changed the thesis; recent headlines (PAC-3 Edge unveiling, Fortem counter-drone investment, OpenAI collaboration on F-35) are incremental product/partnership news, not catalysts that alter capital allocation or risk [Guessing].
