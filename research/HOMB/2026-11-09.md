# Home BancShares (HOMB) — Core initiation — 2026-11-09
## 1. Business
Bank holding company for Centennial Bank, Conway, Arkansas. Earns net interest income on loans funded by deposits, plus fee income; grows by acquisition, most recently MCBI in Q2 2026 (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1331520/000133152026000124/homb-20260930.htm). US domestic; no foreign country at ~10% of revenue [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/homb/).

## 2. Quality
Profitability is solid but not exceptional: ROE 11.1% TTM, flat over five years (-0.4pp), net margin 43.5% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/homb/financials/ratios/). ROIC and ROCE are [data unavailable] (not meaningful for banks). Moat is a low-cost deposit franchise in smaller Southern markets plus acquisition skill [Likely]; not proven durable. FCF margin falls over five years (-17.8pp) but has risen over the last 12 months (35.5% to 39.1%). Direction change addressed: the five-year decline reflects lumpy cash flow items, and ROE and net margin are flat, so earnings power is stable rather than eroding [Guessing]. Q3 net interest margin was 4.53% vs 4.56% a year earlier, with a high loan yield and little provisioning [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1331520/000133152026000124/homb-20260930.htm). Credit is the quality question (section 4).

## 3. Valuation
P/E 12.2x sits at the 20th percentile of its 5y range (11.5x-15.2x), 2% below the peer median of 12.4x. P/B 1.3x equals its 5y median and is 10% above peers. P/FCF 13.8x is at 83% of its range and 27% above peers. FCF yield 7.24%. The reverse DCF implies -3.8% year-1 revenue growth, so the price assumes stagnation [Likely]. Mechanical base fair value 34.34 vs close 29.86 (+15.0%), but flagged unreliable: methods disagree 3.0x (14.67 to 44.73). Weigh lightly. Net: fairly priced for a bank earning an ROE near 11%, cheap on earnings only against its own history [Likely]. No margin of safety is evident.

## 4. What would prove this wrong

- ROE (ratios page) TTM falls below 9.5%.
