# Macro overlay: Home BancShares (HOMB) — 2026-11-09
## Assessment
**Weight: secondary.** Chain: credit spreads / risk appetite -> expected credit losses and bank equity risk premium -> P/B multiple. [Likely] The US-only regional bank (no foreign country near 10% of revenue per the note) is mostly a credit-cycle story, because its own non-performing loans already rose to $200.8m with coverage down to 162% (note section 6).

**Facts (FRED, snapshot 2026-11-09):** HY spread 3.48% on 2026-11-05 (+0.45pp 1m, +0.77pp 3m); VIX 28.18 (+13.2 1m); 10y 4.84% (-0.43pp 1m); 2y 4.46% (-0.33pp 1m); fed funds 3.88% (flat 1m) (https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=BAMLH0A0HYM2, ...id=VIXCLS, ...id=DGS2, ...id=DFF).

**Market pricing (proxy):** the 2y at 4.46% is 58bp above fed funds, so no near-term cuts are priced **(inference)**. Spreads are low in level (config anchors: 3.0 calm, 7.0 stress) but widening fast; the VIX jump is a sudden risk-premium repricing.

**Inference:** the harmful surprise is credit-driven: wider spreads would lift provisions from a near-zero Q3 base and compress P/B (1.3x, at its 5y median, so no cushion). Falling long yields are roughly neutral for NIM (4.53%).

**Bull:** spreads retrace, the large non-accrual loan resolves, P/B re-rates toward 1.4x.
**Bear:** spreads above ~4.5% plus a loss on the $92.1m relationship; provisions return, ROE toward the 9.5% tripwire, P/B toward 1.1x.
**Tilt:** toward bear, small: the widening is real but from low levels, and the idiosyncratic loan matters more.
