# RingCentral (RNG) — Core initiation — 2026-11-09
## 1. Business
Cloud communications seller: subscriptions (UCaaS such as RingEX, contact-centre CCaaS such as RingCX, AI products priced per seat or usage) provide over 90% of revenue [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1384905/000138490526000061/rng-20260930.htm). The vast majority of revenue is in the U.S. and Canada, so no foreign country reaches ~10% [company-reported] (same URL). Devices and services are minor. [Certain]

## 2. Quality
Profitability has improved sharply: operating margin rose from -18.9% (FY2021) to 8.4% TTM, FCF margin from 7.7% to 25.5%, gross margin steady at 72.2% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/rng/financials/income-statement/). [Certain]
Direction check: TTM ROIC is 24.0%, up from 13.6% a year earlier but 11.4pp below its 2-year high of 35.4%. ROCE fell from 33.0% to 18.5% over 12 months (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/rng/financials/ratios/?p=trailing). Operating margin and FCF margin are at 2-year highs, so the fall from the December 2025 peak looks like a capital-base and tax effect, not eroding operations. [Likely]
Moat is moderate: the 10-Q lists Microsoft, Zoom, Twilio, Five9 and carriers as competitors and adds AI-agent disruption and third-party LLM dependence as risks (source: SEC delta, 10-Q above). Switching costs support retention. [Likely] Q3 beat and guidance was raised (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/rng/transcripts/714252-q3-2026/). [Likely]

## 3. Valuation
At 72.07 USD the stock trades at EV/EBITDA 15.7x (6% of its 5y range, 5% above the peer median 14.9x), EV/Sales 2.7x (11% of range; peers 2.8x), P/FCF 9.1x (4% of range; peers 11.8x) and a 10.96% FCF yield. [Certain]
The 9.5x P/E is flattered: net margin of 25.3% against an 8.4% operating margin reflects a one-off tax benefit from releasing $544.9 million of valuation allowance [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1384905/000138490526000061/rng-20260930.htm). Ignore P/E. [Certain]
Base fair value is 75.85 (+5.2%), bull 96.16; the bear case is negative and unreliable. Reverse DCF implies only 2.4% year-1 revenue growth, so the price needs little growth but leaves modest upside. [Likely]

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Operating margin TTM (income statement, trailing page) falls below 6%.
- FCF margin TTM (cash flow statement, trailing page) falls below 20%.
- Gross margin (income statement) falls below 68%.
- Net debt / EBITDA (ratios page) rises above 3.25x.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
FCF conversion is 100.7% of net income TTM, but net income is inflated by the tax benefit; FCF margin 25.5% is the cleaner measure. Shares fell 5.00% YoY. Debt is being retired: $150.0 million of 2030 Senior Notes were repurchased in Q3, with an $8.3 million loss on extinguishment [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1384905/000138490526000061/rng-20260930.htm). Net debt / EBITDA is 2.10x versus 3.25x at FY2025. [Likely]

## 6. Unusual items
