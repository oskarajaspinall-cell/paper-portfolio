# lululemon athletica inc. (LULU) — Core initiation — 2026-10-07

## 1. Business
LULU designs and markets premium athletic apparel, footwear and accessories under its own brand, sold mostly direct-to-consumer (stores, e-commerce) with a smaller international/wholesale tail [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/lulu/). It targets an affluent, loyalty-driven athleisure customer, with the Americas as its historical core market and China/International as the growth engine [Certain] (source: https://corporate.lululemon.com/investors).

## 2. Quality
This was a high-return compounder but is now showing a genuine fundamentals downturn, not just sentiment. ROIC has fallen from 44.0% (FY2022) to 23.7% TTM and ROCE from 38.9% to 27.6% [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/lulu/financials/ratios/). Gross margin slipped to 54.9% TTM and operating margin to 16.6% TTM from the low-20s% [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/lulu/financials/income-statement/). Per the Q2 FY2026 earnings release, total comparable sales fell 9% (Americas −12%, International −3%) and FY2026 revenue guidance was cut to $10.35–10.5bn (down 5–7%), with Q3 revenue guided down 10–11% [Certain] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1397187/000139718726000126/lulu-20260802xex991.htm). This points to eroding pricing power as Alo Yoga, Vuori, On and Nike contest the premium-athleisure space [Likely]. The balance sheet stays conservative (net debt/EBITDA ~0.3x TTM, previously net cash) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/lulu/financials/ratios/), but short interest is elevated and rising (13.55% of float, +2.3% MoM) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/lulu/statistics/).

## 3. Valuation
P/E is 7.7x versus a 5y range of 13.0x–46.4x (below the 5y min) and a peer (NKE/DECK/ONON/UAA) median of 16.6x; EV/EBITDA is 4.7x versus peer median 13.9x (−66%); P/FCF 7.7x versus peer median 20.8x [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/lulu/financials/ratios/). The price assumes the Americas slowdown and margin compression are structural, pricing LULU as a distressed retailer rather than a premium brand. FCF yield of 13.07% [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/lulu/statistics/) looks attractive only if FCF margin stabilizes — it has instead fallen to 12.2% TTM from 15–17% in FY2024–25, with FCF conversion dropping 43.6pp to 95.5% TTM [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/lulu/financials/cash-flow-statement/). The cheap multiples reflect justified skepticism about durability of growth, not an obvious mispricing.

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Total comparable sales (future earnings release/10-Q) turn positive, with Americas comps above 0%.
- Gross margin (income statement page) recovers back above 58%, ex one-off items.
- ROIC (ratios page) stabilizes above 35% rather than extending its 5y decline (currently 23.7% TTM, down from 44.0%).
- FCF margin (cash flow/income statement pages) recovers above 15% of revenue (currently 12.2% TTM).

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Net debt/EBITDA is roughly 0.3x TTM, essentially debt-free [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/lulu/financials/ratios/). Shares outstanding fell 4.31% YoY via buybacks, with no dividend [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/lulu/statistics/). FCF conversion remains high (95.5% TTM) but has been volatile (38.3% in FY2023) and is trending down from its FY2024 peak (106.1%) alongside the margin pressure described above [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/lulu/financials/cash-flow-statement/).

## 6. Unusual items
Multiple plaintiff law firm (BFA Law) notices since Sept 22, 2026 announce a securities-fraud investigation tied to the ~17% share-price drop following the Q2 guidance cut [Likely] (these are standard plaintiff-firm solicitations after any large drop and do not by themselves establish wrongdoing, but add legal/reputational overhang and headline risk) (source: https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/10/07/3376299/0/en/lulu-investor-reminder-bfa-law-reminds-lululemon-investors-that-lost-money-when-stock-dropped-17-of-the-ongoing-securities-fraud-investigation.html). Short interest is high and rising at 13.55% of float (+2.3% MoM) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/lulu/statistics/), indicating the market is actively positioned for further deterioration.
