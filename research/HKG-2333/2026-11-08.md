# Great Wall Motor (HKG:2333) — Core initiation — 2026-11-08
## 1. Business
Chinese automaker (Auto Manufacturers) selling vehicles under its own brands, reporting in CNY and quoted in HKD (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/2333/). Profit comes from vehicle sales margin, with a thin operating margin (0.8% TTM). It is expanding overseas, with a Bulgarian plant for the Ora 5 planned for Q1 2027 and at least 10 new European models planned (source: https://www.reuters.com/business/autos-transportation/great-wall-motor-build-ora-5-compact-suv-bulgaria-2026-10-27/) [Likely]. ## 2. Quality
Not a high-return business today. [Certain] Over five fiscal years ROIC ranged from 7.6% to 17.3%, but TTM ROIC is 3.1%, against 11.2% a year earlier (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/2333/financials/ratios/?p=trailing). Operating margin fell from 3.3% to 0.8% over the same period, and gross margin is 17.5%. [Certain]

The balance sheet is strong. Net debt/EBITDA is -3.43x (net cash) and the Piotroski F-score is 6 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/2333/statistics/). [Certain]

FCF margin is 5.6% TTM, down from 10.6% a year earlier. FCF conversion of 254.8% of net income reflects low earnings rather than strong cash generation. [Likely]

I see no durable moat. Chinese auto competition is intense, and the stock is down 58.0% over 12 months with relative strength of -66.5pp against SPY. The market appears to be pricing price-war margin compression. [Likely] Returns have trended down for a year, so this is a cyclical, share-loss-prone franchise rather than a compounder. [Guessing]

## 3. Valuation
The stock looks cheap on every multiple, but earnings are depressed. [Certain]
- P/E is 9.2x, below its 5y minimum of 15.2x, against a peer median of 18.3x.
- EV/EBITDA is 4.9x against a peer median of 9.0x.
- P/B is 1.1x, below its 5y minimum of 2.0x.
- FCF yield is 12.78%, and P/FCF is 7.8x against a peer median of 4.4x (n=2).
(source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/2333/statistics/)

The price assumes margins stay near today's trough, with no recovery to the FY2024 operating margin of 5.3%. The model's base fair value of 32.63 HKD against a 6.40 close is not credible: the bear case is 0.20 and the through-cycle DCF bear is negative, so I place little weight on it. [Likely] Cheapness is partly a trough-earnings trap: TTM ROIC is far below its own history. The fair value includes a 3.5% country risk premium for CNY reporters. [Guessing]
