# Essent Group (ESNT) — Core initiation — 2026-11-08
## 1. Business
Bermuda-domiciled holding company whose main earner is US private mortgage insurance (Essent Guaranty), which collects premiums on low-down-payment loans and bears default losses. It also reinsures GSE mortgage risk through Essent Re, has reinsured property and casualty risk since the start of 2026, and sells title insurance in a small Corporate & Other line (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1448893/000144889326000032/esnt-20260930.htm) [Certain]. Investment income on float adds to earnings. Revenue is overwhelmingly US [Likely]; no foreign country is evidently ~10%+.

## 2. Quality
Profitable and cash-generative, but returns are drifting down rather than compounding. ROIC fell from 15.5% in FY2021 to 11.6% TTM, and ROE from 16.8% to 12.0% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/esnt/financials/ratios/) [Certain]. The last year is flat (ROIC 11.6% vs 11.8%; source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/esnt/financials/ratios/?p=trailing) [Certain]. FCF/NI is 125.5% TTM, and net debt/EBITDA is 0.58x, so leverage is low [Certain]. Shares are down 9.10% YoY (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/esnt/statistics/) [Certain].

The moat is regulatory and scale-based: GSE approval, capital and underwriting data that new entrants lack, plus high persistency in a high-rate world. The filing says rates keep persistency high and lift investment income (10-Q above) [Likely]. It is a small oligopoly, so differentiation is thin and returns are cyclical with credit [Likely]. Q3 EPS missed consensus on a revenue beat, and defaults rose, per the headline summary [Likely] (source: https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/11/06/3397400/0/en/essent-group-ltd-announces-third-quarter-2026-results-and-declares-quarterly-dividend.html).

## 3. Valuation
P/E 8.7x sits at 90% of its 5y range (5.0x–9.1x), i.e. near the top, against a peer median of 8.9x [Certain]. P/B 1.0x is at 30% of its range and 11% under peers; P/FCF 6.5x and EV/Sales 4.3x are below their 5y minimums; FCF yield is 15.29% [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/esnt/statistics/). EV/EBITDA 6.9x is 12% above peers [Certain].

The price therefore assumes roughly stable ROE near 12% and no credit deterioration; it is cheap on book and cash flow, not on earnings. Mechanical fair value is 98.66 base (bear 75.74) against a 63.41 close, but it leans on P/B+ROE at 67% weight, where the P/E leg gives 57.89 base, below the price [Certain]. The P/B leg looks generous for a falling-ROE business [Guessing]. The upside is real but sensitive to the ROE assumption.

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROE (ratios page, TTM) falls below 10%.
- FCF conversion (FCF/NI, fact sheet quality table) falls below 90%.
- Net debt / EBITDA (site definition, ratios page) rises above 1.0x.
- FHFA adopts a pilot or rule cancelling private mortgage insurance on future loans, cutting persistency or NIW (10-Q risk language; checked against each new filing).

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Capital return is the thesis's pillar. Shares fell 9.10% YoY, FCF conversion is 125.5% TTM and leverage is low (0.58x) [Certain]. A new buyback authorization was reported by the call-transcript headline [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/esnt/transcripts/727843-q3-2026/). Buybacks at about 1.0x book are accretive if returns hold, but they also shrink the capital base while growth is muted [Likely]. A dividend is declared quarterly [Certain].

## 6. Unusual items
- Policy: a late-October executive order directs the FHFA Director to consider a pilot for cancelling private mortgage insurance on future loans; the company will evaluate it once terms are final (10-Q above) [Certain]. The FHFA Director's posts hit the sector in September (source: https://www.tipranks.com/news/the-fly/mgic-investment-nmi-holdings-essent-fall-after-pulte-posts-thefly-news) [Likely]. This is the main binary risk [Likely].
- Tax: Bermuda income is untaxed under a limited-international-presence exception that lasts a limited period or until the criteria fail (10-Q above) [Certain].
- Short interest 4.49% of float, up 33.1% on the month [Certain]. Price is 3.3% below the 50-day average and 0.5% above the 200-day [Certain].
- New P&C reinsurance adds catastrophe-loss exposure the company has no history in [Likely].
