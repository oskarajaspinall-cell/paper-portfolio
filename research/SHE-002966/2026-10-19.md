# Bank of Suzhou Co., Ltd. (SHE:002966) — Core initiation — 2026-10-19

## 1. Business
A city commercial bank headquartered in Suzhou, Jiangsu province, China [Certain]. It gathers retail and corporate deposits and extends loans to local SMEs, corporates and retail customers across the affluent Jiangsu region, earning a net interest margin on its balance sheet plus fee income from wealth management and settlement services [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/she/002966/).

## 2. Quality
Returns are modest and stable rather than exceptional: ROE has held a narrow 9.2–11.0% band over five years, essentially flat (-0.6pp) through TTM, and net margin has risen steadily to 48.3% TTM (+6.0pp over five years) (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/she/002966/financials/ratios/, https://stockanalysis.com/quote/she/002966/financials/income-statement/) [Certain]. As a regional bank its moat is a local deposit franchise and licensing in a wealthy catchment area, but city commercial banks compete intensely for the same SME/retail customers and NIM is set largely by PBoC policy, not company-specific pricing power [Likely]. ROIC, ROCE, gross/operating margin and net debt/EBITDA are not meaningful for a bank and are [data unavailable] on the fact sheet. FCF margin/conversion swing wildly (-110% to +278% over five years) because bank operating cash flow is dominated by loan and securities balance changes, not true free cash generation — this metric should be discounted, not used to judge quality [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/she/002966/financials/cash-flow-statement/). Piotroski F-Score is a middling 4 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/she/002966/statistics/) [Certain].

## 3. Valuation
At 7.6x TTM P/E the stock sits above its own 5-year range (5.3x–7.4x) and 14% above the peer median (6.7x; Bank of Ningbo, Nanjing, Jiangsu, Ping An Bank) [Certain]. P/B of 0.6x is mid-range (52% of its 5-year band) and matches the peer median (0.6x), the more reliable multiple for a bank [Certain]. P/FCF (1.2x) looks cheap but FCF is unreliable for banks (see Quality) and should not drive this view (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/she/002966/financials/ratios/). Base fair value is CNY 10.91 (+21.2% vs the CNY 9.00 close), bear CNY 9.07 (+0.8%), bull CNY 12.40 (+37.7%), from P/B + sustainable ROE blended with an FCFE DCF [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/she/002966/). The reverse DCF implies -6.4% revenue decline in year 1 fading to terminal growth — pessimistic versus recent single-digit growth history, suggesting the market prices in NIM/asset-quality stress current ratios don't yet show [Likely].

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Growth is funded mainly through retained earnings supporting loan books and regulatory capital, the standard model for a deposit-funded bank; traditional FCF-based cash-conversion analysis is not meaningful here given the -110% to +278% five-year swing in FCF margin, which reflects loan/securities balance changes rather than distributable cash (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/she/002966/financials/cash-flow-statement/) [Likely]. Shares outstanding are roughly flat (+0.72% YoY), implying no recent dilutive capital raise (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/she/002966/statistics/) [Certain]. ## 6. Unusual items
Institutional ownership is low at 10.7% and insider ownership near zero (0.07%) (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/she/002966/statistics/) [Certain] — a narrow institutional/insider base typical of Chinese bank free floats, which can mean thinner liquidity support in a sell-off [Likely]. Beta (5y) is very low at 0.13 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/she/002966/statistics/) [Certain], likely reflecting limited foreign/index ownership and administered pricing rather than genuinely low business risk [Guessing].
