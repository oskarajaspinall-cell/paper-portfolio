# Bank of Suzhou (SHE:002966) — Core initiation — 2026-11-16
## 1. Business
Regional commercial bank (Banks - Regional) headquartered in Suzhou, Jiangsu; it earns net interest income on loans and bond holdings plus fees. Reports in CNY; no foreign-country revenue is shown on the fact sheet, so the business is domestic China [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/she/002966/).

## 2. Quality
- Returns are modest and flat: ROE 9.2% TTM versus 10.0% in FY2021, a 5-year change of -0.6pp [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/she/002966/financials/ratios/). ROIC/ROCE and leverage are [data unavailable], as usual for banks.
- Net margin rose from 42.0% to 48.7% TTM [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/she/002966/financials/income-statement/), but ROE did not follow, so the gain looks like mix or leverage, not a durable edge [Likely].
- FCF margin is meaningless for a bank (deposit flows) and fell from 128.4% to 54.9% over 12 months [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/she/002966/financials/cash-flow-statement/?p=trailing). I do not treat it as an operating signal [Guessing].
- Moat: local franchise in a wealthy region, no evidence of pricing power; the Piotroski F-score is 4 [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/she/002966/statistics/). Average quality, not compounding quality [Likely].

## 3. Valuation
- P/E is 8.0x, above its 5y maximum of 7.4x (5y median 7.3x) and 17% above the peer median of 6.8x [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/she/002966/financials/ratios/). Earnings multiple is at the top of its own history.
- P/B is 0.7x against a 0.6x median and 0.8x for peers, 14% below peers [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/she/002966/statistics/). FCF yield 14.96% is bank-cash-flow noise [Likely].
- Mechanical fair value: base 11.35 CNY, 17.9% above the last close of 9.63, but it is flagged unreliable (methods disagree 4.2x: P/B+ROE 9.31, FCFE 15.43, DDM 3.65) [Certain]. Only the P/B+ROE leg (base 9.31, below the price) is anchored to returns, and it says the stock is fairly priced to slightly rich [Likely].
- Trend is healthy: +13.7% above the 200-day average, 12-month change +13.6%, ahead of SPY by 17.1pp over 3 months [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/she/002966/history/). Priced for steady returns, with no discount [Likely].
