HY credit spread (BAMLH0A0HYM2) 3.03%, +0.36pp 3m — modestly wider, still low, relevant to refinancing VRSK's 2.68x net debt/EBITDA [Likely].
Bull: yields stabilize/ease → discount-rate pressure lifts → EV/EBITDA/EV/Sales premium re-rates toward VRSK's own 5y range (currently below it). Bear: yields grind higher → premium multiples compress further even as subscription fundamentals (ROIC 31.7%, FCF conversion 139.6%) hold, capping re-rating and nudging up refinancing cost. Tilt: small, toward bear — VRSK's beta (0.69) and >80% subscription revenue insulate earnings from the cycle, and its multiples already sit below 5y lows, so most of the real-yield repricing looks done; a further leg up in real yields would be a modest incremental drag, not a thesis-breaker.
