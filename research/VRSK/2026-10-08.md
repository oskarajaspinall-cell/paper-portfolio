# Verisk Analytics, Inc. (VRSK) — Core initiation — 2026-10-08
## 1. Business
Verisk is a single-segment data/analytics provider to the global P&C insurance industry: underwriting (forms, rules, loss costs), claims (fraud, property/casualty analytics, ClaimSearch), catastrophe modeling and specialty/life solutions (10-K, FY2025). Over 80% of 2025 revenue came from annual subscriptions or long-term prepaid agreements; all top 100 U.S. P&C carriers use at least one Verisk solution [Certain] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1442145/000143774926004452/vrsk20251231_10k.htm).

## 2. Quality
ROIC 31.7% TTM, up from 11.5% in FY2021; ROCE 41.7% TTM (RA) [Certain]. Gross margin 70.2%, operating margin 44.9%, FCF margin 39.4%, FCF conversion 139.6% of net income TTM (IS,CF) [Certain] — all rising over five years. The >80%-subscription model gives high revenue visibility [Certain]. Moat: proprietary/contributory P&C risk datasets built since 1971, deep embedding in carrier claims/underwriting workflows and near-universal penetration of the largest U.S. insurers create high switching costs; peers (MCO, SPGI, EFX, FDS) compete in adjacent but largely distinct data niches, limiting direct share capture [Likely] (10-K). Net debt/EBITDA rose to 2.68x TTM from 1.83x at FY2025-end (RA) [Certain], likely reflecting buyback-funded leverage rather than operating deterioration given FCF strength, but worth monitoring.

## 3. Valuation
P/E of 26.0x is below its own 5y minimum of 28.9x (-11%, cheapest in 5 years) and close to the 4-peer median of 24.6x (+6%) (RA) [Certain]. EV/EBITDA (17.2x) and EV/Sales (8.3x) run 22% and 34% above peer medians respectively, though both remain below VRSK's own 5y lows (19.0x/11.1x) (RA) [Certain]. P/FCF (17.8x) is essentially in line with peers (-1%) and far below VRSK's own 5y minimum (26.2x) [Certain]. FCF yield is 5.63% (ST). The price — down 31.2% over 12 months, 10.9% below its 200-day average, near its 52-week low of 155.94 (HI,ST) [Certain] — already discounts a chunk of the EV/EBITDA premium; on an FCF basis the de-rating looks largely complete, while the EV/EBITDA/Sales premium to peers still assumes superior margins and recurring-revenue quality persist.

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROIC (ratios page) falls below 20%, signalling returns compressing toward peer-average levels.
- Gross margin (income statement page) falls below 65% (below its 5y range), indicating pricing-power erosion.
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) rises above 3.5x, constraining the buyback/M&A capital-allocation model.
- Subscription/long-term-agreement share of revenue (10-K) falls below 75%, indicating loss of the recurring-revenue model.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Priority order: organic reinvestment, then selective bolt-on M&A (10 acquisitions since 2022, including ExactLogix/AccuLynx, Jul-2025), then shareholder returns via dividend and a $2.0bn revolving repurchase facility, funded through multiple accelerated share repurchase tranches in 2025 (10-K) [Certain]. Divested non-core Marketing Solutions on Dec-31-2025 for $80m net cash, sharpening insurance-industry focus [Certain]. FCF conversion of 139.6% of net income TTM comfortably funds buybacks and bolt-on deals without heavy reliance on debt (CF,IS) [Certain], though net debt/EBITDA has ticked up to 2.68x TTM (RA) [Certain].

## 6. Unusual items
Short interest rose 36.9% month-over-month to 3.58% of float (ST) [Certain] — a modest but rising bearish bet. Institutional ownership is reported at 100.7% of float (ST), a data artifact of overlapping 13F filings rather than a literal over-100% holding [Likely]. Verisk "strongly disagrees" with an August-2026 Delaware Chancery Court ruling concerning its AccuLynx acquisition; the headline gives no detail on quantum or remedy, so financial impact cannot be quantified from available data [Guessing] (source: https://www.tipranks.com/news/the-fly/verisk-analytics-strongly-disagrees-with-court-ruling-regarding-acculynx-thefly-news). Management guided (Sept-2026 conference transcripts) to organic constant-currency growth accelerating to 6%-8% in H2 2026 [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/vrsk/transcripts/738890-barclays-24th-annual-global-financial-services-conference/).
