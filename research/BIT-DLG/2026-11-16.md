# De'Longhi (BIT:DLG) — Core initiation — 2026-11-16

## 1. Business
De'Longhi sells small domestic appliances, led by automatic and espresso coffee machines, plus cooking, home-care and climate products (De'Longhi, Kenwood, Braun brands). It also has a fast-growing Professional division selling coffee solutions to commercial customers (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/bit/DLG/transcripts/550250-q2-2026/) [Likely]. Revenue is earned from hardware sales through retailers, e-commerce and direct channels. Regional mix: [data unavailable] on the fact sheet; no single foreign country was verified at ~10%+ of revenue, so no country-risk flag is raised from this note.

## 2. Quality
A good business with strong returns on capital [Certain]: ROIC TTM 20.9% and ROCE 18.7%, operating margin 13.4%, gross margin 45.0% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/bit/DLG/financials/ratios/). Cash conversion is excellent: FCF margin 13.4% and FCF/NI 149.6% TTM, with net cash (net debt/EBITDA -0.65x) [Certain]. Piotroski F is 7 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/bit/DLG/statistics/).

Direction: ROIC fell over five fiscal years (23.1% FY2021 to 19.6% FY2025, trough 12.6% in FY2022) but is rising over the last 12 months (17.8% to 20.9%) and gross margin is up over five years (42.7% to 45.1%). The latest rise is mix-driven, with Professional growth and margin expansion cited by management (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/bit/DLG/transcripts/671366-q3-2026/) [Likely]. Moat: brand and premium espresso-machine leadership; durability of Professional growth is [Guessing]. Appliances are cyclical and tariff exposed.

## 3. Valuation
At 47.40 EUR the stock trades on 19.9x P/E, above its entire 5y range (14.6x–18.3x), and 10.7x EV/EBITDA, at 78% of its 5y range. P/B 3.3x is above its 5y max. P/FCF is 13.3x, near the bottom of its range, and FCF yield is 7.52% [Certain]. Versus peers (SN, WHR, EPA:SK) P/E and EV/EBITDA are at a discount (-32%, -19%), but the peer set is weak (n=2–3) and low-quality, so the comparison flatters [Likely].

Mechanical fair value base is 44.19 EUR (bear 17.71, bull 54.59), i.e. -6.8% vs the close; the reverse DCF implies 5.9% year-1 revenue growth. The wide bear-to-bull spread reflects the DCF's sensitivity (source: fact sheet Fair value section). The price assumes the margin gains hold. Trend is strong (+25.0% vs 200-day, RSI 75.1), so it is extended in the short term [Certain].
