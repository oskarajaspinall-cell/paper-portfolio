# Valley National Bancorp (VLY) — Core initiation — 2026-11-16
## 1. Business
Regional commercial bank holding company: earns net interest income on commercial real estate, commercial and industrial and consumer loans funded by deposits, plus fees (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/vly/) [Certain]. Branch network is 55% New Jersey, 18% New York, 19% Florida [company-reported] (https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/714310/000071431026000049/vly-20260930.htm). Entirely US; no foreign country near 10% of revenue [Likely].

## 2. Quality
Mediocre-to-improving, not a moat story. ROE is 9.4% TTM versus 5.4% in FY2024 and 9.9% in FY2022, a five-year trend of falling (-1.9pp) (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/vly/financials/ratios/) [Certain]. Net margin recovered to 35.8% TTM from 25.0% in FY2024 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/vly/financials/income-statement/) [Certain]. ROIC, ROCE and rolling-12-month data are [data unavailable] for banks. Piotroski F is only 4 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/vly/statistics/) [Certain].
Franchise is a regional deposit and commercial lending business in dense markets; durable edge is limited to local relationships [Likely]. Credit is the watch item: net charge-offs were $37.1 million in Q3 2026 versus $14.6 million a year earlier [company-reported] (https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/714310/000071431026000049/vly-20260930.htm) [Certain]. Management says deposits and efficiency improved in Q3 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/vly/transcripts/713921-q3-2026/) [Likely].

## 3. Valuation
P/E is 11.4x, equal to its 5y median of 11.4x and at 28% of its 5y range; 12% below the peer median of 12.9x (ZION, WBS, FHN, CFG). P/B is 1.0x, 79% of its 5y range (0.7x-1.1x), 19% below peers at 1.3x (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/vly/financials/ratios/) [Certain]. Cheap versus peers, not versus itself on book.
Fair value base is 12.03 against a 14.34 close (-16.1%); bull 15.58. Flagged unreliable: methods disagree 3.1x (P/B+ROE 9.18, FCFE DCF 17.74, DDM 5.65) [Certain]. FCF yield of 11.85% is a bank cash-flow artefact; weigh lightly [Likely]. Reverse DCF implies -1.6% year-1 revenue growth, a low bar [Certain]. Price is above both averages (+6.5% vs 50-day, +5.7% vs 200-day); 12-month change +29.4% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/vly/history/) [Certain]. The price already discounts a good part of the earnings recovery.

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROE (ratios page) falls below 7% on a TTM basis.
- Quarterly net loan charge-offs (10-Q MD&A) exceed $50 million for two consecutive quarters.
- Shares change YoY (statistics page) exceeds 5% without matching earnings growth, i.e. dilution from the acquisitions.
- Net margin (income statement page) falls below 28% TTM.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Two pending acquisitions announced in Q3: Providence Financial (about $246 million, early 2027 close) and Bluevine (about $340 million, about $255 million of it cash) [company-reported] (https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/714310/000071431026000049/vly-20260930.htm). Management expects the Bluevine deal to be accretive to 2028 EPS (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/vly/transcripts/759938-m-a-announcement/) [Likely]. Share count is stable (0.19% YoY) [Certain]. Cash conversion is [data unavailable] for a bank. Execution risk rises with two deals at once [Likely].

## 6. Unusual items
- Short interest 11.05% of float, up 5.2% on the prior month (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/vly/statistics/) [Certain].
- 10-Q delta: no change in risk factors; added risk language on completing both deals, regulatory approval, Providence shareholder vote and realising synergies (https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/714310/000071431026000049/vly-20260930.htm) [Certain].
- Bluevine's current banking-partner relationship ends, with deposits moving to Valley from April 2027: transition risk [Likely].
