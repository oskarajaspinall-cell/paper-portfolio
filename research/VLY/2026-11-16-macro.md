# Macro overlay: Valley National Bancorp (VLY) — 2026-11-16
## Assessment
**Chain (inference):** rates path -> deposit costs and loan repricing -> net interest margin; credit spreads -> CRE/C&I credit losses -> provisions and the risk premium on a regional bank. Country risk: none; the branch network is New Jersey, New York and Florida only [Likely].
**Facts (FRED):** the 2y yield is 4.86% (2026-11-12, +0.71pp over 3m) against fed funds at 3.88% (+0.25pp). The 10y is 5.10% (+0.47pp), real yield 2.86%, breakeven 2.24% (https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=DGS2). The HY spread is 3.2% (+0.49pp over 3m), VIX 18.7 (+4.07).
**Pricing (proxy):** a 2y about 1pp above fed funds implies the market expects further hikes (inference). The 10y-2y slope is +0.24pp and flattened ~0.24pp in 3m. Credit spreads are still tight in level.
**Hurts:** a flatter or inverted curve with higher deposit costs, and any spread widening, since net charge-offs already rose to $37.1m from $14.6m [company-reported] (https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/714310/000071431026000049/vly-20260930.htm). **Helps:** a steeper curve, and stable spreads.
**Bull:** loans reprice up faster than deposits, margin widens, ROE moves above 10% and P/B holds ~1.0x+. **Bear:** the flattening continues, deposit beta rises, CRE losses push provisions up, the multiple compresses back to the lower end of its range (0.7x P/B).
**Tilt:** toward bear, small. The stock is up 29% and already discounts the recovery; the move in rates is a risk to the multiple, not a changed earnings path yet.
