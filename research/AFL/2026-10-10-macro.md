## Assessment
Chain: US rates -> AFL's AFS bond portfolio marks -> AOCI/book value -> the P/B+ROE fair value (the fact sheet's primary method); secondarily, dollar strength -> translation of Aflac Japan's yen earnings -> reported USD EPS [Likely] **(inference)**.

Facts: 10y Treasury 5.22% on 2026-10-08, +68bp/3m; 10y real yield 2.87%, +56bp/3m; 10y breakeven 2.33%, +9bp/3m (fred.stlouisfed.org). Broad dollar index 121.385 on 2026-10-02, +2.86%/1m. No JPY series exists in the snapshot, so dollar strength is only a directional proxy for yen-translation risk [Guessing] **(inference)**.

Pricing: 2y yield (4.75%) sits ~87bp above effective fed funds (3.88%), a proxy for the market pricing continued Fed firmness over cuts; since the back-up is mostly real-yield (not breakevens), it reads as "higher for longer," not an inflation scare **(inference)**. The fact sheet's fair value already uses today's 5.22% risk-free rate, so the current level is priced; a further move either way is not.

Bull: real yields plateau/fall -> AOCI losses reverse, book value recovers, P/B fair value drifts toward the $150 bull case; a softer dollar lifts reported Japan earnings with no change in yen profits. Bear: real yields extend higher on sticky core CPI -> further AOCI erosion pulls book value toward the $89 bear case; continued dollar strength shrinks reported USD EPS from Japan. Tilt: small, toward bear — AFL's duration-matched ALM and disclosed currency-neutral EPS limit the damage, but the rate back-up is a real, incremental headwind to the book-value-based valuation.
