# Aflac Incorporated (AFL) — Core initiation — 2026-10-10
## 1. Business
Aflac sells voluntary supplemental health and life insurance (cancer, medical, life, disability, dental, vision) in the US and Japan, collecting premiums and investing float. Japan is the larger, legacy profit pool; the US is the smaller but faster-growing segment, newer lines (group life, disability, dental/vision, direct-to-consumer) still scaling [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/afl/transcripts/744402-kbw-insurance-conference-2026/).

## 2. Quality
Returns are solid but decelerating: ROE fell from 22.6% (FY2024) to 13.1% (FY2025), TTM 16.9%; ROIC fell from 16.7% to 9.8%, TTM 11.1% — a flat 5y trend masks a sharp FY2025 step-down [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/afl/financials/ratios/). Piotroski F-Score is 5/9, mid-table [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/afl/statistics/). Net debt/EBITDA (standard definition) rose from 1.48x (FY2024) to 2.67x TTM [Certain]. Management attributes part of the FY2025 move to a deliberate ~5% Japan bond-portfolio repositioning (selling USD assets at gains, JGBs at losses), lifting net investment income ~$50m/year and cutting impairment risk — a one-off drag, not structural [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/afl/transcripts/744402-kbw-insurance-conference-2026/). The durable moat is distribution scale: decades-built US worksite/broker/agent networks and a dominant Japan franchise (Japan Post, banks) costly to replicate, plus a long-duration, matched liability book. Shares outstanding fell 5.75% YoY (continued buybacks) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/afl/statistics/).

## 3. Valuation
AFL trades at 12.2x P/E (46th percentile of its 5y range 9.1x–15.9x) and 11.1x EV/EBITDA (56th percentile of 7.9x–13.7x), an 18-26% discount to the 4-peer median (14.9x P/E, 15.0x EV/EBITDA) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/afl/financials/ratios/, peer statistics pages). P/B of 1.9x is roughly in line with peers (1.7x median), below its own 5y median of 2.2x [Certain]. EV/Sales is far above peers (+186%) but reflects insurance revenue-mix accounting, not overvaluation, and isn't the primary multiple for an insurer. Model fair value (P/B+ROE primary, P/E secondary) gives a base of $116.44 vs the $114.54 close — already near fair value (+1.7%), bear/bull $89.38/$150.45 [Certain] (source: fact sheet valuation table). The price mostly assumes the FY2025 ROE step-down persists rather than the one-off Japan effect described above; if US premium growth (3-6% target) and margin recovery play out there is bull-case room, but the base case offers limited margin of safety.

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROE (ratios page) falls and stays below 12% for two consecutive fiscal years, signalling the FY2025 step-down is structural, not transitory.
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page, standard definition) rises above 3.0x, indicating leverage is becoming a balance-sheet risk rather than a one-off repositioning effect.
- US segment premium growth (company disclosure) falls persistently below the 3-6% target range management has set, showing the newer US lines are failing to scale.
- Piotroski F-Score (statistics page) falls to 3 or below, a broad-based deterioration signal.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Shares outstanding declined 5.75% YoY, consistent with sustained buybacks [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/afl/statistics/). FCF margin, FCF conversion and P/FCF are [data unavailable] on stockanalysis.com for this insurer (cash-flow-statement presentation doesn't map cleanly to FCF); ROE/ROIC and the balance sheet are the best available proxies for capital efficiency, and both show the FY2025 deceleration flagged above.

## 6. Unusual items
