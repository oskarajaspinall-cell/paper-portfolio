# AppLovin Corporation (APP) — Core initiation — 2026-10-08
## 1. Business
AppLovin runs AppLovin Ads, a performance-advertising platform: advertisers set a target return on ad spend and the AXON AI engine auto-bids across its ad exchange to hit it [Likely] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1751008/000175100826000059/app-20260630.htm). Clients range from indie app developers to Meta and Google [Likely] (same source). It divested its Apps Business to Tripledot in June 2025 for $715.6m, becoming a pure-play, single-segment advertising business [Certain] (same source).

## 2. Quality
Returns are exceptional and rising: TTM ROIC 132.7%, ROCE 75.4%, operating margin 77.4%, all up sharply over FY2021-25 [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/app/financials/ratios/, https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/app/financials/income-statement/). FCF margin is 66.3% TTM and net debt/EBITDA has fallen to 0.09x, so the model needs almost no capital to scale [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/app/financials/cash-flow-statement/, https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/app/financials/ratios/). The moat is the AXON data/algorithm flywheel (more spend → more data → better targeting → more spend), reinforced by direct developer relationships [Likely] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1751008/000175100826000059/app-20260630.htm). The clearest threat is credibility of the AI claims underpinning that flywheel: since late September several law firms have filed securities-fraud suits alleging AppLovin misled investors on "the strength of its AI models and feasibility of its AI video creative tool" [Certain] (source: https://www.newsfilecorp.com/release/316996/Robbins-LLP-is-Investigating-Allegations-that-AppLovin-Corporation-Misled-Investors-Regarding-the-Strength-of-its-AI-Models-and-Feasibility-of-its-AI-Video-Creative-Tool); allegations unproven [Guessing]. A court this month also denied AppLovin's restraining order against Unity's rival Ad Quality SDK [Certain] (source: https://www.tipranks.com/news/the-fly/judge-denies-applovin-restraining-order-against-unity-says-wedbush-thefly-news). The 10-Q for the period to 30 June 2026, filed before these suits, reported no material loss contingencies [Certain] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1751008/000175100826000059/app-20260630.htm).

## 3. Valuation
TTM P/E of 21.6x sits below its own 5-year minimum (37.8x) and EV/EBITDA of 17.5x is in the bottom quarter of its 5-year range, both depressed by the stock's -56.8% 12-month decline rather than by a change in profitability, which has instead kept improving [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/app/financials/ratios/, https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/app/history/). Versus peers (TTD, META, GOOGL, U), APP trades at a premium on EV/Sales (+58%) and P/B (+342%) but a discount on EV/EBITDA (-15%) and P/FCF (-49%) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/app/statistics/, peer statistics pages). The P/FCF discount despite best-in-class FCF margin suggests the market is pricing in meaningful risk to the growth/margin trajectory — consistent with the litigation and competitive overhangs above — rather than paying up for quality the way it does for peers.

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Operating margin (income statement page) falls back below 60% for two consecutive quarters, signalling the AXON advantage is eroding.
- FCF margin (cash flow/income statement pages) drops below 50%, indicating monetization efficiency is deteriorating.
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) rises back above 1.0x, suggesting capital is being consumed rather than generated.
- ROIC (ratios page) falls below 50%, removing the evidence of a durable competitive advantage this thesis rests on.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
FCF conversion (FCF/NI) is 102.7% TTM, down from triple-digit/erratic prior years but still full conversion of profit into cash [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/app/financials/cash-flow-statement/). Shares outstanding fell 1.81% YoY, implying net buybacks rather than dilution [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/app/statistics/). Net debt/EBITDA of 0.09x leaves balance-sheet capacity for further buybacks or bolt-on M&A without strain [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/app/financials/ratios/).

## 6. Unusual items
Material: since 30 September 2026 at least four law firms (Kaplan Fox, BFA Law, Robbins LLP) have filed or publicized securities class actions alleging AppLovin misled investors on AI model strength/feasibility, with a lead-plaintiff deadline of 16 November 2026 [Certain] (source: https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/10/05/3374326/0/en/app-court-notification-applovin-has-been-sued-for-securities-fraud-over-ai-progress-issues-bfa-law-reminds-investors-of-the-november-16-court-deadline.html). Separately, San Diego County's new Consumer Fairness and Public Protection Unit has sued AppLovin alongside Roblox and Polymaker [Certain] (source: https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20261005827865/en/San-Diego-Launches-New-Consumer-Protection-Unit-With-Lawsuits-Against-AppLovin-Roblox-and-Polymaker/). Short interest is 4.12% of float and rose 3.7% over the past month, and institutional ownership is high at 68.8% [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/app/statistics/). None of this yet appears in AppLovin's own loss-contingency disclosure as of 30 June 2026 [Certain] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1751008/000175100826000059/app-20260630.htm), so outcome and financial impact are unknown [Guessing].
