# Macro overlay: AppLovin Corporation (APP) — 2026-10-08

## Assessment
APP is a zero-debt (net debt/EBITDA 0.09x [Certain], source: stockanalysis.com/stocks/app/financials/ratios/), high-multiple (EV/Sales 13.9x, +58% vs peers [Certain]) advertising platform with a 5y beta of 2.56 [Certain] (source: stockanalysis.com/stocks/app/statistics/). With no debt to refinance, the rates channel runs through the discount rate applied to a long-duration, high-growth cash-flow stream, not through funding cost: **real yields → discount rate → equity multiple**, amplified by the stock's high beta.

Facts: the 10y real yield (DFII10) is 2.91% (2026-10-06), up +0.48pp over 1m and +0.61pp over 3m [Certain] (fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=DFII10, 2026-10-06). 10y breakeven inflation (T10YIE) is flat at 2.36% (+0.01pp 1m) [Certain] (fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=T10YIE, 2026-10-07), so the real-yield rise is a genuine real-rate/term-premium move, not an inflation re-rating — the channel that bites hardest on long-duration multiples. The 2y yield (4.79%) sitting ~0.9pp above effective fed funds (3.88%) **(inference, proxy)** suggests the market is not pricing near-term cuts. VIX is low (15.0, -1.12pp 3m) [Certain] (fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=VIXCLS), i.e. no risk-off yet.

**Bull**: real yields stabilize/retrace (e.g. softer growth data, confirmed cut path) → discount-rate relief → disproportionate multiple recovery given beta 2.56. **Bear**: real yields grind higher → further multiple compression stacks on top of the litigation/competitive overhang already driving the -56.8% 12m decline, with high beta amplifying the hit. **Tilt**: moderate, toward bear — the stock-specific overhang (AI/securities litigation, Unity competition) remains the dominant driver **(inference)**, but rising real yields are a live, independent headwind this high-beta name is unusually exposed to.
