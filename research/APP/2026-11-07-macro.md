# Macro overlay: AppLovin Corporation (APP) — 2026-11-07
## Assessment
**Weight: secondary.** Chain: risk appetite and real yields -> discount rate / equity risk premium -> multiple. Beta 2.50 and a reverse DCF needing 35.8% year-1 revenue growth (https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/app/statistics/, 2026-11-07) make APP's value sensitive to the discount rate.

**Facts (FRED, snapshot 2026-11-07):**
- 10y TIPS yield 2.60% (2026-11-05), -0.31pp over 1m, +0.17pp over 3m (https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=DFII10).
- VIX 28.18 (2026-11-05), +13.2 over 1m (https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=VIXCLS).
- HY spread 3.48%, +0.77pp over 3m (https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=BAMLH0A0HYM2).
- Fed funds 3.88% vs 2y yield 4.46% (https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=DGS2). [Certain]

**Market pricing (proxy):** a 2y yield above fed funds suggests no near-term cuts are priced. Breakevens of 2.23% are flat over 3m (https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=T10YIE), so the real-yield move is not an inflation story. [Likely]

**Inference:** falling real yields help the multiple, but the VIX spike points to a rising risk premium. APP's recent jump is company-specific, since it rose on earnings while the Nasdaq-100 fell (https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/app/). **(inference)**

**Bull:** real yields keep falling and the VIX normalises; the multiple holds.
**Bear:** the VIX stays high, spreads widen or real yields rebound above ~2.8%; ERP and discount rate rise and the multiple compresses sharply for a beta-2.5 name.

**Tilt:** toward bear, small. Earnings dominate.
