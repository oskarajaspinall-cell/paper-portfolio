# Macro overlay: Cintas Corporation (CTAS) — 2026-10-08
## Assessment
CTAS is a domestic, essential B2B services compounder (uniform/facility services) with low beta (0.92 [Certain], factsheet) — demand is non-discretionary and revenue is largely USD, so macro earnings pass-through is weak [Likely] **(inference)**. The visible channel is valuation: CTAS trades at a premium multiple (38.9x P/E, 26.0x EV/EBITDA [Certain], factsheet), so real yields → discount rate → equity multiple is the one chain worth tracking for this 3-month tactical trade.

Facts (FRED, official): US 10y real yield (DFII10) 2.91% on 2026-10-06, +0.61pp over 3m; 10y Treasury (DGS10) 5.27%, +0.72pp/3m; effective fed funds (DFF) 3.88%, +0.25pp/1m; 10y breakeven inflation (T10YIE) 2.36%, +0.11pp/3m — essentially flat. Rates, not inflation expectations, are the mover. Market pricing (proxy, **inference**): 2y Treasury (4.79%) above fed funds (3.88%) implies the market isn't pricing near-term cuts and may expect further tightening — a rough read, not a forecast. These moves pre-date today and are already reflected in CTAS's 10% pullback from its 219.17 July high despite a record Q1 FY27 beat and a raised FY27 guide (no earnings-reaction flag) — the pullback looks rate-driven rather than fundamental **(inference)**.

Bull: real yields stabilize/ease → multiple re-rates toward the top of its 5y range, helping reach the 216.69 target. Bear: real yields extend higher → further compression, pressuring toward the 187.44 stop before fundamentals are reflected. Tilt: small toward bear — rates have risen into the trade, but low beta and strong fundamentals dominate; macro is a secondary swing on the valuation range, not the setup's driver.
