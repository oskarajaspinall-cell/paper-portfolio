# Qinghai Salt Lake Industry Co.,Ltd (SHE:000792) — Core initiation — 2026-10-18

## 1. Business
Qinghai Salt Lake Industry extracts potassium chloride (potash) fertiliser and lithium carbonate from brine at the Qarhan Salt Lake in Qinghai, China [Likely]. Revenue comes from selling potash to domestic Chinese agriculture and lithium carbonate to battery-material buyers; both products are priced off commodity benchmarks that the company does not control, not long-term contracts [Likely].

## 2. Quality
Returns have fallen sharply from the 2022 commodity spike: ROIC dropped from 126.2% (FY2022) to 25.4–39.4% (FY2024–25, TTM 57.0%) and ROE from 98.0% to 13.2–20.5% (TTM 28.0%) (source: ratios page) [Certain]. Margins stayed high throughout — operating margin never fell below 35.3%, net margin rose to 56.6% TTM (source: income statement) [Certain] — consistent with a low-cost resource producer, not a branded or contracted business. The likely durable edge is the scarce, low-cost Qarhan brine deposit and China's protection of domestic potash supply, which competitors cannot replicate quickly [Likely]; but potash and lithium prices are cyclical and set in global/domestic markets, so margins keep swinging with the cycle — inherent volatility, not moat erosion [Likely]. The balance sheet is net cash (net debt/EBITDA −2.68x TTM, source: balance sheet + income statement) [Certain], funding the resource base without leverage risk. Net: a low-cost producer, not a buy-and-forget compounder — returns should mean-revert with commodity prices.

## 3. Valuation
P/E of 10.1x sits in the bottom 6% of its own 5-year range (7.9x–42.9x) and 30% below the peer median of 14.4x (SQM, ALB, NTR, MOS) (source: ratios/statistics pages) [Certain]. EV/EBITDA of 7.3x is in line with peers (−1%) and near the bottom of its own range (9th percentile) [Certain]. But EV/Sales (4.4x, +139% vs peers), P/FCF (14.1x, +61%) and P/B (2.3x, +70%) are all well above peer medians — consistent with net margins (56.6% TTM) far richer than diversified peers Mosaic and Nutrien, rather than outright overvaluation on sales or book [Likely]. The model's base fair value is CNY 24.96 vs last close CNY 23.06 (+8.2%), with bear/bull of −43.0%/+45.6% (source: fair-value table) [Certain]. A modest base-case upside for a cyclical whose ROIC/ROE have fallen for three straight years suggests the market has already priced in reversion — this is not a clearly mispriced cheap stock [Likely].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
