# PPG Industries, Inc. (PPG) — Core initiation — 2026-10-09
## 1. Business
PPG makes and sells protective and decorative coatings (paints, sealants, adhesives) for aerospace, automotive OEM/refinish, industrial, packaging and architectural customers, split roughly evenly between Performance and Industrial Coatings segments [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/ppg/). Revenue is global, pricing-plus-volume driven, with raw materials (resins, pigments, solvents) the main cost input.

## 2. Quality
Returns are solid, not expanding: ROIC 11.6% TTM (+0.8pp over 5y), ROCE 12.5% (+1.8pp). Gross margin rose 2.6pp to 41.2% and operating margin 3.0pp to 13.2%, evidence of pricing power against input costs [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/ppg/financials/ratios/). Coatings is a durable, consolidated oligopoly (PPG, Sherwin-Williams, Akzo Nobel) with switching costs from OEM qualification cycles and distribution relationships — management calls PPG a "consolidating" acquirer [Likely] (source: https://investor.ppg.com/). Q2 2026 supports the moat thesis: Aerospace grew double digits with a $300m backlog, and Industrial Coatings posted its best organic growth in five years (+5%) on OEM share gains and >20% two-year-stacked Packaging growth [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/ppg/transcripts/656790-q2-2026/). The offset: Automotive Refinish fell double digits on destocking, though management says that is "behind us" [Likely] (same source). FCF conversion is volatile (46%-149% over 5y, 89.5% TTM) and ROE (19.3% TTM, -2.5pp trend) shows no structural improvement — a good, not great, business [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/ppg/financials/ratios/).

## 3. Valuation
At 15.1x P/E, 10.8x EV/EBITDA and 16.6x P/FCF, PPG trades 25%, 12% and 8% below peer (SHW/AXTA/RPM) medians and near or below its own five-year lows on every multiple shown (P/E at the 4th percentile of its five-year range, EV/EBITDA, EV/Sales, P/FCF and P/B all below their five-year minimums) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/ppg/financials/ratios/). The fact sheet's base fair value of $100.06 is 5% below the last close of $105.46, with a wide bear/bull range ($39.58/$127.17) reflecting DCF sensitivity to the reverse-DCF implied 8.2% year-one growth versus the EV/EBITDA method's materially higher $156.95 base [Certain] (source: fact sheet fair-value table). The gap between the DCF and multiple-based estimates, and the depressed starting multiples despite margin and backlog improvement, suggests the market is pricing in continued Automotive Refinish weakness and macro-led industrial softness rather than crediting the Aerospace/Industrial inflection — a reasonable entry point if that inflection continues [Likely].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Gross margin (ratios page) falls below 39.0% (the FY2021 five-year low), signalling lost pricing power.
- ROCE (ratios page) falls below 10.5% (the FY2022 low), signalling the margin/returns inflection has reversed.
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page, site definition) rises above 2.95x (the FY2022 high), signalling balance-sheet deterioration beyond the stated deleveraging path.
- Aerospace or Industrial Coatings organic growth turns negative for two consecutive quarters (earnings releases/transcripts), indicating the stated growth engines have stalled.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Net debt/EBITDA fell over $400m to 1.9x in Q2 2026 (adjusted) while PPG issued CHF 320m of bonds at ~1.4% weighted average rate, extending low-cost funding [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/ppg/transcripts/656790-q2-2026/). Buybacks totaled $75m in Q2 ($175m YTD), paced slower on working-capital gains; the dividend was recently raised [Likely] (same source). Management is directing >$500m of capex into aerospace capacity and says there is "nothing for sale right now" on major divestitures, with only small bolt-ons [Likely] (same source). TTM FCF margin is 8.6% and FCF/NI conversion 89.5%, within historical range [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/ppg/financials/ratios/).

## 6. Unusual items
Short interest rose 23.8% month-over-month to 2.93% of float — still low in absolute terms, not a governance or distress signal [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/ppg/statistics/). Institutional ownership is high at 94.1% and Altman Z (3.72) / Piotroski F (6) show no distress [Certain] (same source). News flow since August shows several segment-leadership and IR-personnel changes (Industrial Coatings leaders, a new VP-IR) alongside a $70m Huntsville aerospace investment — routine operating news, not thesis-relevant on its own [Likely] (source: https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20261007048988/en/PPG-announces-70-million-investment-to-expand-Aerospace-operations-in-Huntsville-Alabama/).
