# Altria Group (MO) — Core initiation — 2026-11-21
## 1. Business
Sells cigarettes (Marlboro), cigars (Black & Mild), smokeless (Copenhagen, Skoal), oral pouches (on!) and e-vapor (NJOY) almost entirely to US adult nicotine consumers [Certain] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/764180/000076418026000114/mo-20260930.htm). Profit comes from pricing power on a shrinking cigarette volume base, returned via dividends and buybacks. No foreign country is a material revenue share [Likely].

## 2. Quality
Very high returns on capital: ROIC TTM 37.4%, operating margin 61.2%, FCF margin 44.0%, net debt/EBITDA 1.66x (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/mo/financials/ratios/). [Certain]

Direction: ROIC is rising over five fiscal years but down over the last 12 months (38.8% to 37.4%), and 8.0pp below its 2-year high; FCF margin also slipped (45.6% to 44.0%) (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/mo/financials/ratios/?p=trailing). The 2-year highs look like a one-off peak; the TTM figures sit within the FY2021-25 range. [Likely]

Moat: brand and distribution scale in US cigarettes, but the volume decline is structural. In Q3 2026 the discount share of the cigarette category rose 2.0 points year on year, and Altria's adjusted domestic cigarette volume fell an estimated 4% [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/764180/000076418026000114/mo-20260930.htm). Pricing has so far offset volume loss. [Likely]

## 3. Valuation
P/E 15.5x sits at 28% of its 5y range (median 14.0x), +2% vs peers. EV/EBITDA 11.3x, P/FCF 13.7x and EV/Sales 7.1x are above their 5y maxima but in line with peers apart from EV/Sales (+45%) (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/mo/statistics/). FCF yield 7.28%. [Certain]

Mechanical fair value base 79.70 vs close 74.87 (+6.4%); bear 55.66, bull 81.87. The reverse DCF implies -2.0% year-1 revenue growth, which fits the volume trend: little margin of safety. [Likely]

Dip: the close is below the 79.97 high of 2026-11-16 but +7.0% above the 200-day average (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/mo/history/). The radar's stock-specific signal rests on a negative fitted beta during a market sell-off; 5y beta is 0.46, so it is mostly a regression artefact. No results, guidance or filing news explains it (Q3 raised guidance). [Likely]
