# Altria Group, Inc. (MO) — Core initiation — 2026-10-07

## 1. Business
Altria sells cigarettes (Marlboro, ~76% operating margin segment) and oral nicotine (on! pouches) almost entirely in the US, plus NJOY e-vapor and a wine business. Revenue comes from per-unit pricing power offsetting a structurally declining cigarette volume base; profit is driven by price/mix and cost discipline, not unit growth [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/mo/financials/income-statement/).

## 2. Quality
Returns are exceptional and rising: ROIC 46.1% TTM (5y: 26.4%→43.5%→46.1%), ROCE 60.7% TTM, operating margin 76.0%, gross margin 87.0% [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/mo/financials/ratios/, https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/mo/financials/income-statement/). The moat is regulatory and distribution-based: FDA's pre-market tobacco authorization (PMTA) regime and decades of brand/retail entrenchment (Marlboro's US share) keep new entrants out of cigarettes, letting Altria push price faster than volume falls [Likely]. The durability question is whether that pricing algebra keeps working as cigarette volumes shrink secularly and nicotine alternatives (pouches, vapor) see faster, FDA-encouraged product approval — the FDA is reportedly preparing to speed up vape/pouch approvals, which could let competitors (e.g., Juul2, authorized 2026-08-28) erode Altria's on!/NJOY share in the one growth category it has [Likely] (source: https://www.reuters.com/legal/litigation/us-fda-plans-move-speed-up-vape-pouch-approvals-wsj-reports-2026-09-23/, fact sheet News & sentiment). FCF conversion (FCF/NI) has fallen from 332.8% to 114.3% over five years, still comfortably >100% but the trend is the wrong direction [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/mo/financials/cash-flow-statement/).

## 3. Valuation
At 14.3x P/E (23rd percentile of its own 7.9x–35.3x 5y range) and 8.7x EV/EBITDA, MO trades 45% and 34% below peer medians (25.9x P/E, 13.2x EV/EBITDA; peers PM, BTI, TPB, UVV) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/mo/financials/ratios/). That discount is consistent with Altria's US-only litigation/regulatory concentration versus PM/BTI's global diversification, not obviously excessive. But on EV/Sales (159% of its own 5y range, above the 5y max) and P/FCF (166%, also above the 5y max), MO is richer than at any point in the last five years [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/mo/financials/ratios/) — the market is paying up for margin/FCF-margin expansion (44.6% TTM) rather than for earnings growth. FCF yield of 7.98% is attractive in absolute terms for a low-beta (0.49) name [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/mo/statistics/). Net: price assumes the current margin structure holds and the earnings multiple discount to global peers is justified by regulatory risk, not by a deteriorating core franchise.

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROIC (ratios page) falls below 35% for two consecutive fiscal years, signalling pricing power is breaking down.
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios/balance sheet page) rises above 2.0x and stays there, eroding the capital-return capacity that underpins the thesis.
- Gross margin (income statement) falls below 80%, indicating cigarette price/mix can no longer offset volume decline.
- FCF margin (cash flow/income statement) drops below 35%, below its 5y range minimum of 38.9%.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Altria returned $33.6bn in dividends and $8.9bn in buybacks over FY2021–25 [Certain] (source: https://www.altria.com/investors). The quarterly dividend was raised 4.7% to $1.11/share in August 2026, continuing an unbroken multi-decade increase streak [Certain] (source: fact sheet News & sentiment, https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260826405562/en/Altria-Increases-Quarterly-Dividend-to-1.11-Per-Share/). TTM FCF margin is 44.6% and FCF conversion 114.3% of net income, funding the payout without incremental leverage; net debt/EBITDA is a moderate 1.41x, down from 1.93x in FY2021 [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/mo/financials/cash-flow-statement/, https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/mo/financials/ratios/). Shares outstanding fell 1.0% YoY, consistent with continued buybacks [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/mo/statistics/).

## 6. Unusual items
Altria sued the FDA on 2026-09-02 over how the agency reviews new nicotine products [Certain] (source: https://www.reuters.com/world/altria-sues-us-fda-court-records-show-2026-09-02/, per fact sheet News & sentiment). Combined with the FDA's reported move to speed up vape/pouch approvals and Juul2's 2026-08-28 authorization, regulatory risk is now two-sided: faster approvals could help Altria's own pipeline but also accelerate competition in its only growth category [Likely]. Altria and Philip Morris International signed contract manufacturing agreements on 2026-08-24 to grow cigarette imports/exports between them [Certain] (source: fact sheet News & sentiment). Short interest is low (2.34%, falling 3.8% MoM) and institutional ownership is 65%, with Altman Z 5.34 and Piotroski F 7 — no distress signal [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/mo/statistics/).
