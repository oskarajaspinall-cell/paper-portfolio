# Macro overlay: Altria Group, Inc. (MO) — 2026-10-07
## Assessment
MO is US-only, low-beta (0.49) tobacco with a ~6.5% dividend yield (**(inference)**: $4.44 annualized dividend / $68.55 close, fact sheet), a classic bond-proxy. The core thesis (pricing power vs FDA-regulated nicotine competition) is fundamental, but the multiple also trades off competing risk-free yields. **Chain**: real yields -> relative attractiveness of MO's dividend yield vs the risk-free rate -> equity risk premium / multiple for yield-sensitive, low-growth equities like MO.

**Facts** [Certain]: US 10y real yield (DFII10) 2.95% on 2026-10-05, +52bp 1m, +71bp 3m (fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=DFII10). 10y nominal (DGS10) 5.31%, +83bp 3m; 10y breakeven inflation (T10YIE) only +11bp 3m to 2.36% (same source, 2026-10-05/06) — most of the 3m yield rise is real, not an inflation-expectations move, so it raises discount rates directly. Fed funds (DFF) 3.88%, +25bp over both 1m and 3m, implying a recent hike. MO's 3m return trailed SPY by 8.3pp [Certain] (fact sheet), consistent **(inference)** with bond-proxy de-rating as yields rose.

**Market pricing** (proxy, **(inference)**): 2y yield (4.84%) sits 96bp above fed funds (3.88%), implying the market still prices further tightening ahead.

**Bull**: real yields plateau/retreat -> MO's yield re-rates, narrowing its 45% P/E discount to peers. **Bear**: the 3m real-yield rise extends -> further multiple compression regardless of cigarette/pouch fundamentals. **Tilt**: moderate, toward bear — the real-yield move is already large and the 2y/fed-funds spread implies more tightening priced, a headwind the FDA/regulatory thesis doesn't address.
