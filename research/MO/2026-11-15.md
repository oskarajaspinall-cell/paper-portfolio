# Altria Group (MO) — Core initiation — 2026-11-15
## 1. Business
Altria sells cigarettes and other nicotine products to US adult consumers only. Marlboro is the core brand. Others: Black & Mild, Copenhagen, Skoal, on! pouches and NJOY e-vapor [Certain] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/764180/000076418026000114/mo-20260930.htm). Profit comes from pricing power on a shrinking cigarette volume base, with very high margins and cash returned to holders. No foreign country is material to revenue [Likely].

## 2. Quality
A high-return franchise: ROIC 37.4% TTM, operating margin 61.2%, FCF margin 44.0% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/mo/financials/ratios/) [Certain]. The moat is brand, distribution and regulation: the FDA authorisation barrier keeps new entrants out [Likely].

The direction is mixed. ROIC is 1.4pp below a year ago and 8.0pp below its 2-year high. FCF margin is down 1.6pp over 12 months (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/mo/financials/ratios/?p=trailing). Operating margin is 16.6pp below its 2-year high, which looks like an unusually high base rather than erosion, because the 5-year trend is rising [Guessing].

Volume is the structural weakness. The filing says discount brands' share of cigarettes was 34.1% in Q3, +2.0 share points year on year, and Altria's adjusted cigarette volume fell an estimated 4% [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/764180/000076418026000114/mo-20260930.htm). Q3 adjusted EPS grew 4.8% and FY26 guidance was raised [Certain] (source: https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20261028923631/en/Altria-Reports-2026-Third-Quarter-and-Nine-Months-Results-Announces-Expanded-Share-Repurchase-Program-Raises-2026-Full-Year-Earnings-Guidance/).

## 3. Valuation
P/E is 16.2x against a 5y median of 14.0x, at the 30% point of its 5y range, and in line with peers (16.0x) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/mo/financials/ratios/). EV/EBITDA is 11.8x and P/FCF is 14.4x, both above their 5y maxima, so the stock is no longer cheap on cash multiples. FCF yield is 6.93% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/mo/statistics/).

The mechanical base fair value is 78.67 against a last close of 78.64: the price is at fair value, with bear 54.87 and bull 80.81, so skew is poor [Likely]. The reverse DCF implies -0.6% year-1 revenue growth, a low bar for a business with pricing power. The stock is up 35.5% over 12 months and 12.9% above its 200-day average. The thesis is "good business that is working", not a discount [Likely].

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Shares outstanding fell 0.87% YoY, and net debt/EBITDA is 1.66x, down from 1.93x in FY2021 [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/mo/statistics/). Payouts are funded by FCF: FCF conversion was 111.8% of net income TTM, and the FCF yield is 6.93%. The company announced an expanded buyback with Q3 results [Certain] (source: https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20261028923631/en/Altria-Reports-2026-Third-Quarter-and-Nine-Months-Results-Announces-Expanded-Share-Repurchase-Program-Raises-2026-Full-Year-Earnings-Guidance/). Altria impaired its NJOY goodwill and intangibles in 2025 (acquisition value destroyed) and carried the e-vapor unit near fair value at year-end [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/764180/000076418026000114/mo-20260930.htm).

## 6. Unusual items
