# Macro overlay: Citizens Financial Group, Inc. (CFG) — 2026-10-11

## Assessment
CFG is a regional bank: earnings and valuation are directly geared to the rate cycle via net interest margin (NIM) and cost of equity, so `macro_weight` is primary [Certain] (banks-and-rates is the canonical primary case). Chain: fed funds path & 2y/10y curve -> NIM -> NII/ROE; 10y real yield -> cost of equity -> fair-value multiple; HY spreads -> provisioning.

Priced: 2y Treasury (4.75%, 2026-10-08) sits ~87bp above effective fed funds (3.88%, 2026-10-08) — a proxy (not forecast) for the market pricing further tightening, not cuts **(inference)**. The 3m rise in the 10y nominal yield (+0.68pp to 5.22%) is almost entirely a real-yield move (DFII10 +0.56pp to 2.87%) with breakevens flat (+0.09pp to 2.33%) [Certain, FRED, 2026-10-08/09] — growth/term-premium repricing, not an inflation surprise. HY spreads widened +0.45pp over 3m to 3.15% but stay historically tight; VIX fell to 15.4 — no credit-stress signal yet [Certain]. CFG's fair-value model already uses today's 5.22% risk-free rate, so most of this back-up is embedded in the $43.38 base / $56.40 bull estimates, already below the $63.57 last close (fact sheet).

**Bull**: curve steepening continues, growth resilient -> NIM expands, assets reprice faster than deposits -> closes the ROE gap.
**Bear**: real yields and HY spreads keep climbing -> cost of equity rises further on a rich multiple, provisioning increases -> ROE slide persists.

**Tilt**: small, toward bear — the dominant 3m move is a real-yield back-up (raises CoE), coinciding with CFG underperforming SPY by 12.6pp over 3m **(inference)**.
