# NetApp (NTAP) — Core initiation — 2026-11-15
## 1. Business
NetApp sells enterprise data storage: all-flash and hybrid arrays running ONTAP software (Hybrid Cloud), plus public-cloud storage services and support contracts. In Q1 FY2027 product revenue was $987M and services $1,038M of $2,025M total [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1002047/000119312526380207/ntap-20260731.htm). Services (support) are recurring and anchor the installed base. Revenue by country: [data unavailable]; the filing text I could read gave no split.

## 2. Quality
A high-return, asset-light franchise [Certain]: TTM ROIC 313.2%, ROCE 30.1%, operating margin 26.0%, gross margin 70.6%, net cash (net debt/EBITDA -0.50x) (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/ntap/financials/ratios/). ROIC is inflated by buybacks shrinking book capital, so ROCE is the cleaner gauge [Likely]. The moat is ONTAP lock-in and the support base, plus hyperscaler integration (AWS, Azure, Google Cloud) [Likely]. Direction: ROCE and operating margin are rising, but FCF margin is falling over the last 12 months (25.2% to 22.3%, 2-year high 27.0%) (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/ntap/financials/cash-flow-statement/?p=trailing) [Certain]. The filing reports memory and component cost inflation hitting gross margin, with price rises offsetting [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1002047/000119312526380207/ntap-20260731.htm). The company is a price-taker on components, a cyclical weakness [Likely].

## 3. Valuation
The price assumes a lot [Certain]. P/E 25.6x vs a 5y range of 10.6x-21.2x (141%, above max); EV/EBITDA 16.5x vs 9.5x-13.9x; P/FCF 21.6x vs 11.5x-16.5x; all above their 5y maxima. Against peers the P/E premium is +14% and P/FCF is -16% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/ntap/statistics/). FCF yield is 4.63%. Mechanical fair value (DCF plus EV/Revenue) is base 87.50 USD, bull 105.13, against a last close of 181.37; the reverse DCF implies 25.5% year-1 revenue growth [Certain]. The stock is +62.6% over 12 months and +17.7% above its 200-day, but 11.0% below its 50-day and down 11.5% in 3 months, off a 240.87 high (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/ntap/history/). The rerating is AI-storage-driven [Likely]; the fair-value model may understate that, but the gap is large [Guessing].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Gross margin (income statement, TTM) falls below 66%, meaning price rises fail to cover memory costs.
- Operating margin (income statement, TTM) falls below 22%.
- FCF margin (cash-flow trailing page) falls below 18%.
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) rises above 0.5x from net cash, or ROCE falls below 22%.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Shares are down 3.14% YoY (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/ntap/statistics/). Q1 FY2027: $200M buybacks and $102M dividends [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1002047/000119312526380207/ntap-20260731.htm). Bolt-on AI deals: DataPelago for $193M [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1002047/000119312526380207/ntap-20260731.htm) and PEAK:AIO, completed 2026-11-02 (source: https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20261102208472/en/NetApp-Completes-Acquisition-of-PEAKAIO-to-Accelerate-Zettabyte-scale-AI-Deployments/) [Certain]. FCF conversion TTM is 116.4% of net income, but down from 146.5% in FY2026 [Certain]. Buying back stock at record multiples earns a lower return [Likely].

## 6. Unusual items
- The Q1 FY2027 10-Q added risk language on non-cancelable component purchase commitments at fixed prices and hard-to-cut fixed costs if demand falls [Certain] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1002047/000119312526380207/ntap-20260731.htm).
