# Macro overlay: NetApp (NTAP) — 2026-11-15
## Assessment
**Chain:** real yields / risk appetite -> equity risk premium -> multiple. NTAP trades at 25.6x P/E against a 5y range of 10.6x-21.2x (fact sheet, https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/ntap/statistics/), with beta 1.51 and a reverse DCF needing 25.5% year-1 revenue growth. Its value rests on a multiple that rising discount rates compress. Earnings are not the macro channel (the revenue-by-country split is [data unavailable], so no country check is possible).

**Facts (FRED, 2026-11-12):** the 10y TIPS yield is 2.86%, +0.47pp over ~3m and -0.02pp over ~1m. The 2y is 4.86% (+0.71pp over 3m) against fed funds of 3.88%. HY spread is 3.2% (+0.49pp over 3m). VIX is 18.7 (+3.4 over 1m). Breakeven is 2.24% (flat over 3m). Source: reports/macro/2026-11-15.md (https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=DFII10).

**Pricing (proxy, inference):** a 2y yield about 1pp above fed funds implies the market prices hikes, or at least no cuts. Breakevens are flat, so the 3m rise is real-rate driven, which is the worst mix for long-duration multiples. The stock's -11.5% 3m move (fact sheet) fits this **(inference)**. [Likely]

**Bull:** real yields stabilise below 3% and AI-storage demand holds. The multiple stops compressing, and the macro effect is neutral.
**Bear:** the real yield passes 3.25% or credit spreads widen. The multiple drifts toward its 5y maximum of about 21x, roughly 15-20% off the P/E at constant earnings. [Guessing]

**Tilt:** toward bear, small. Fundamentals and the 12-month +62.6% run already carry the stock, and the move is the surprise that matters, not the level.
