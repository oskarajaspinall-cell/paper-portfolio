# Hyundai Rotem Company (KRX:064350) — Core initiation — 2026-10-18
## 1. Business
Hyundai Rotem makes railway rolling stock (trains, trams, EMUs) and defense land systems (armored vehicles) for the Korean government and export customers; stockanalysis.com classifies it Industrials/Railroads (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/krx/064350/). It is grouped with Korean defense primes Hanwha Aerospace, Korea Aerospace Industries and LIG Nex1 in sector coverage (source: https://www.cnbc.com/2025/03/04/south-korean-defense-stocks-surge-as-global-defense-spending-picks-up.html). [Certain]

## 2. Quality
Returns and margins rose every year for five years: ROIC 3.9%→187.2% TTM, ROCE 4.0%→25.8% TTM (+24.4pp over FY21-25), operating margin 2.8%→17.2% (FY25)/15.9% TTM, net margin 2.3%→13.2%/12.8% TTM [Certain] (source: fact sheet ratios/income-statement pages). The balance sheet flipped from 7.89x net debt/EBITDA (FY21) to net cash (-1.07x FY25, -2.30x TTM) [Certain]. This looks like a contract-manufacturing margin recovery as backlog converts to delivery, not a new structural barrier: FCF margin swung from -3.7% to 21.6% to 1.4% within the same five years, showing cash recognition is lumpy and contract-dependent [Certain]. TTM ROIC of 187.2%, a step up from FY25's 43.9%, likely reflects a thin invested-capital base, not a repeatable return level [Guessing]. Protection rests on government relationships and export-credit-backed contracts rather than a clear product moat versus Hanwha Aerospace or KAI; the fact sheet has no backlog or market-share data [Guessing].

## 3. Valuation
Every multiple sits well below its own 5-year range and the peer median: P/E 13.1x vs 5y range 13.3x-34.1x (below the 5y minimum) and peer median 43.8x (-70%); EV/EBITDA 7.6x vs peer 25.6x (-70%); P/FCF 6.3x vs peer 62.1x (-90%); P/B 3.1x vs peer 5.3x (-41%) [Certain]. The reverse DCF embedded in the price implies revenue falling -14.1% in year 1, fading to terminal growth by year 10 [Certain] — harsher than the TTM trend of rising margins and net cash. Base fair value is 180,828 KRW, +86.6% above the 96,900 KRW last close (inform-only, model-based) [Certain]. The stock is down -54.6% over 12 months and -39.1% over three months, -22.8%/-46.8% below its 50-/200-day averages and far below its 282,000 KRW 52-week high [Certain]. That drawdown could instead reflect a genuine fundamental reset not yet visible in the FY2025/TTM numbers, which section 4 is designed to catch [Guessing].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Operating margin (income statement page) falls back below ~10% (the FY2024 level) for two consecutive quarters, signalling the margin expansion was a peak, not a step-change.
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) reverses from net cash back to positive leverage, indicating contract losses or working-capital deterioration.
- ROIC or ROCE (ratios page) reverses its five-year rising trend for two consecutive fiscal years.
- FCF margin (cash flow + income statement pages) turns negative outside a single bad year, as it did in FY2021.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
FCF margin is 26.4% TTM and FCF conversion (FCF/NI) is 206.3% TTM — cash generated exceeds reported net income [Certain]. Net debt/EBITDA moved from 7.89x (FY21) to net cash (-2.30x TTM), material deleveraging over the same period margins expanded, consistent with a working-capital unwind as backlog converts into delivered, cash-collected revenue [Likely].
