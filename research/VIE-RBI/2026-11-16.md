## 2. Quality
Mediocre and volatile, with no data to show a durable moat. ROE was 9.5% in FY2021, peaked at 19.5% in FY2022, then fell to 7.6% in FY2025; TTM is 10.2% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/vie/RBI/financials/ratios/). [Certain] Net margin TTM is 23.3% against 15.3% in FY2025 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/vie/RBI/financials/income-statement/). ROIC, ROCE and leverage are [data unavailable], as expected for a bank. FCF is meaningless for a bank: FCF margin swung from 94.9% in FY2025 to 19.5% TTM, which reflects deposit and loan flows, not economics (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/vie/RBI/financials/cash-flow-statement/). [Likely] Management reports Q3 2026 record net interest income and loan growth, and says the Rasperia asset freeze and two pending acquisitions shape guidance (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/vie/RBI/transcripts/662869-q3-2026/). [Likely] Piotroski F is 4 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/vie/RBI/statistics/). [Certain]

## 3. Valuation
P/E is 10.5x against a 5y median of 6.2x and a 5y max of 10.0x, so it sits above its own range. P/B is 0.9x against a 5y max of 0.6x (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/vie/RBI/financials/ratios/). [Certain] Against peers (Erste, UniCredit, BAWAG) it is -14% on P/E and -45% on P/B, but those peers earn a cleaner return on equity, so the discount is partly deserved. [Likely] The mechanical fair value is flagged unreliable because methods disagree 4.4x. Base is 59.67 EUR against a 62.60 EUR close, i.e. about -4.7%. The price implies roughly flat revenue growth in year 1 on the reverse DCF. [Certain] The stock is up 91.3% over 12 months and sits 20.7% above its 200-day average, so the re-rating from Russia-discount levels has largely happened. [Certain] The price assumes a successful Russia resolution without a major loss. [Guessing]

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROE (ratios page) falls below 7% on a TTM basis.
- Net margin (income statement page) falls below 15% on a TTM basis.
- Piotroski F score (statistics page) falls to 3 or below.
- A court ruling, sanction or regulator action that forces a write-off or capital-ratio hit on the Russian business (company filings; checked by the weekly reviewer).
