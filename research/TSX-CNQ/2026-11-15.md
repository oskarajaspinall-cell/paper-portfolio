# Canadian Natural Resources (TSX:CNQ) — Core initiation — 2026-11-15
## 1. Business
Large, low-decline oil and gas producer: oil sands mining and upgrading (Horizon), thermal and conventional heavy oil, plus natural gas and smaller international assets. Revenue is commodity-price driven; profit comes from long-life, low-sustaining-cost barrels. [Likely] Revenue is overwhelmingly Canadian-based, so no single foreign country is plainly ~10%+ [Guessing]. This was not verified in a filing. (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tsx/CNQ/)

## 2. Quality
Good, cyclical business. Moat is asset-based: long-life reserves, integration and scale, not pricing power [Likely]. Five-year fiscal-year trends are falling (ROIC 13.7% to 10.8%, operating margin 31.6% to 21.1%), but the last 12 months reverse this: TTM ROIC 17.9% vs 12.5% a year earlier, operating margin 30.7% vs 23.0%, FCF margin 27.0% vs 21.0% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tsx/CNQ/financials/ratios/?p=trailing). [Likely] The rebound tracks higher crude prices and output (source: https://www.reuters.com/business/canadian-natural-resources-beats-quarterly-profit-estimate-2026-11-05/), so it is cycle, not structural improvement. Balance sheet strong: net debt/EBITDA 0.67x, Piotroski 8, Altman Z 2.86 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tsx/CNQ/statistics/). FCF conversion 91.6% of net income, below the 130% of FY2021-22.

## 3. Valuation
Price 70.91 CAD, FCF yield 8.99%. P/E 10.5x is 37% up its 5y range and 17% below peers, but EV/EBITDA 7.0x is 89% of range (peers 5.6x, +26%), and P/B 3.1x and EV/Sales 3.3x exceed 5y maxima. P/FCF 11.1x is in line with peers. [Likely] The price discounts mid-cycle-or-better earnings. Mechanical fair value is 60.55 CAD base (-14.6%), 42.52 bear, 61.22 bull; Reserve NAV is [data unavailable], so this rests on EV/EBITDA alone and is low-reliability [Likely]. Trend support: 9.4% above the 200-day, +56.1% over 12 months, beating SPY by 45.7pp. [Certain] The case is a good business working, not a discount.

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- TTM ROIC (ratios page, trailing view) falls below 12%.
- TTM FCF margin (cash flow page) falls below 18%.
- Net debt / EBITDA (ratios page) rises above 1.5x.
- Shares change YoY (statistics page) turns positive, i.e. buybacks reversed by issuance.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
[Likely] Management reported reaching its net debt target and now directs all free cash flow to buybacks, with a dividend maintained (source: https://www.nasdaq.com/articles/canadian-natural-resources-limited-achieves-13-billion-net-debt-target-and-targets-100). Shares change YoY is -0.91%, so buybacks are modest so far (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tsx/CNQ/statistics/). Buying back stock at above-fair-value multiples is a mild negative [Guessing]. FCF conversion of 91.6% is adequate, not exceptional.

## 6. Unusual items
Early Horizon maintenance completion supports next-quarter production [Likely] (source: https://www.marketbeat.com/instant-alerts/transcript-canadian-natural-resources-q3-earnings-call-highlights-2026-11-06/). Earnings were reported 2026-11-05 and beat estimates per headlines; no earnings-type price reaction in the last 10 sessions (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tsx/CNQ/history/). Oil-price and Canadian heavy-oil differential exposure is the main macro risk [Certain]. Short interest 1.21% of float: not material.
