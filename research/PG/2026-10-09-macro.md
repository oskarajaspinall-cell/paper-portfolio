# Macro overlay: The Procter & Gamble Company (PG) — 2026-10-09
## Assessment
PG's DCF discounts at risk-free + beta x ERP; with beta just 0.38, the risk-free rate still drives most of its discount-rate sensitivity [Certain]. Facts: the 10y Treasury yield is 5.28% (2026-10-07, FRED DGS10), up 0.72pp in 3 months; the 10y real yield (TIPS) is 2.92% (2026-10-07, FRED DFII10), up 0.61pp in 3 months [Certain]. The base-case DCF already uses today's 5.28% rate, so the current level is priced; direction from here matters. Market pricing (proxy): 2s10s is +0.51pp (DGS10 5.28% - DGS2 4.77%) and the 10y sits 1.40pp above effective fed funds (3.88%), a steep curve consistent with the market pricing policy normalization, not cuts [Likely]. Separately, the broad dollar index rose 2.86% in one month to 121.4 (2026-10-02, FRED DTWEXBGS) [Certain]; PG earns ~40-45% of sales abroad, so dollar strength is an FX translation headwind to EPS (inference) [Likely]. Bull: real yields retreat toward ~2.5% and/or the dollar weakens - cost of equity falls, narrowing the -25.6% base-case overvaluation gap; FX turns to a tailwind. Bear: real yields push through ~3.25% and the dollar extends its rise - cost of equity rises further, widening the overvaluation gap, and FX drag compounds the organic-growth slowdown already flagged. Tilt: toward bear, small - the valuation call is already fundamentals-driven (price 34% above even bull fair value); rising rates reinforce, not create, that view, and PG's low beta/defensive demand cushions the risk-premium leg.
