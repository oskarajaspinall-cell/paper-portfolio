# Victory Capital Holdings (VCTR) — Core initiation — 2026-11-15
## 1. Business
Multi-boutique US asset manager earning investment-management fees on client assets across equity, fixed income, Solutions and alternatives, sold through retail, institutional and ETF channels. Growth comes from acquisitions folded into one shared platform; Pioneer Investments (Amundi US) was added on April 1, 2025 and is now the largest franchise [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1570827/000119312526448759/vctr-20260930.htm). No foreign country share was found in the sources read [data unavailable]. [Certain]

## 2. Quality
Good, scale-driven business. TTM ROIC 17.4%, operating margin 47.0% and FCF margin 39.0%, FCF conversion 136.3% of net income (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/vctr/financials/ratios/). [Certain] Five-year fiscal-year trends are falling (ROIC -2.1pp, ROE -15.4pp), but the last 12 months reversed: ROIC 11.9% to 17.4%, FCF margin 28.2% to 39.0% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/vctr/financials/ratios/?p=trailing). The fiscal-year decline mostly reflects the Pioneer deal absorbing capital before synergies; the TTM recovery is consistent with that. [Likely]
Organic momentum is real: Q3 net inflows of $11.7bn against net outflows a year earlier, with $15.2bn net inflows in nine months [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1570827/000119312526448759/vctr-20260930.htm). [Certain] Flows came from fixed income, Solutions and non-US equity; US mid, small and large cap equity are still in outflow, so the core active-equity franchise is not growing. [Certain] Moat is modest: cost scale and distribution, not pricing power; active fees are exposed to passive shift. [Likely]

## 3. Valuation
The price assumes a lot. P/E 22.9x is above its entire 5y range (6.7x to 15.2x, median 10.7x) and 69% above the peer median of 13.6x. EV/EBITDA 10.3x sits at 85% of its range, P/FCF 12.5x at 99%, though FCF yield remains 8.02% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/vctr/statistics/). [Certain]
Mechanical fair value base is 93.82 against a 125.26 close (-25.1%); bull 140.32 (+12.0%). The reverse DCF implies 10.5% year-1 revenue growth. [Certain] Shares are up 97.1% in 12 months, 36.4% above the 200-day average, RSI 64.6. The mandate allows a working uptrend, but the stock is priced for sustained flow-led growth plus synergies, with little room for a flow stumble. [Likely]

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Quarterly net long-term flows (earnings release, company IR) turn negative for two consecutive quarters.
- Operating margin (statistics/income statement page, TTM) falls below 42%.
- TTM ROIC (ratios page) falls below 13%.
- Net debt / EBITDA (ratios page) rises above 2.0x without a matching rise in earnings (acquisition integration failing).

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Acquisitive but disciplined so far: net debt / EBITDA fell from 2.46x (FY2021) to 0.96x TTM, and shares are down 3.73% YoY, so buybacks outrun dilution. [Certain] Cash conversion is strong (FCF 136.3% of net income). The pending First Eagle deal, targeted to close by end of Q1 2027, will raise leverage and execution risk; synergy delivery is the key test. [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/vctr/transcripts/726127-q3-2026/)
