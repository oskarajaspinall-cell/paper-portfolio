# ADNOC Logistics & Services plc (ADX:ADNOCLS) — Core initiation — 2026-10-31
## 1. Business
Integrated marine logistics for the oil and gas chain (crude, product, LPG/LNG and chemical shipping plus terminal and ancillary services), predominantly for parent ADNOC under long-term contracts [Likely]. Revenue follows contracted fleet deployment more than spot freight rates [Likely]. The owned fleet is being expanded through vessel purchases and newbuild orders (source: https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/adnoc-ls-expands-fleet-with-13-billion-acquisition-11-vessels-2026-08-07/, headline only) [Likely].

## 2. Quality
Unchanged from the 2026-10-20 initiation. ROIC rose from 3.6% (FY2021) to 19.4% TTM, operating margin from 4.9% to 26.0% TTM, and net debt/EBITDA is 0.13x TTM (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/adx/ADNOCLS/financials/ratios/) [Certain]. The edge is the captive ADNOC relationship and contract visibility, consistent with a beta of 0.42 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/adx/ADNOCLS/statistics/) [Certain]. TTM margins sit far above FY2025's 18.1% operating margin, so part may be cyclical [Likely]. Hormuz risk remains direct: Iran blacklisted tankers using the strait in August (source: https://oilprice.com/Energy/Energy-General/Iran-Blacklists-45-Tankers-as-Hormuz-Standoff-Escalates.html, headline does not name this company) [Likely].

## 3. Valuation
Why it fell: I found no cause. The fact sheet and the stockanalysis.com quote page list no headline, filing or results after the 2026-08-24 headline; the latest results (Q2 2026, 2026-08-11) predate our last research, and the next report is due Nov 11, 2026 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/adx/ADNOCLS/) [Certain]. The dip is unexplained, and an RSI of 36.6 describes a pullback, not a broken business [Likely]. No obstacle from the AVOID has been removed.

At 6.85 AED: P/E 8.9x (5y min 12.4x), EV/EBITDA 6.5x (5y min 9.1x), P/FCF 22.6x (5y min 42.4x); premium to peers on P/E (+30%), EV/EBITDA (+9%) and P/FCF (+75%) (source: fact sheet, ratios page) [Certain]. Base fair value 8.18 AED (+19.4%); the reverse DCF implies 9.7% year-1 revenue growth (source: fact sheet) [Certain]. Upside is a modest margin of safety given possible peak margins and an unexplained fall [Likely].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- TTM operating margin (statistics page) falls below 15%.
- TTM ROIC (statistics page) falls below 10%.
- TTM FCF margin (statistics page) turns negative.
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) rises above 2.0x.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
FCF conversion improved from -479.8% (FY2021) to 39.4% TTM and FCF margin is 9.8% TTM, while net debt/EBITDA stays 0.13x despite fleet additions (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/adx/ADNOCLS/financials/cash-flow-statement/) [Certain]. Shares outstanding changed -0.04% YoY, so growth is not funded by dilution [Certain]. Conversion remains well below net income, so heavy capex is still absorbing cash [Likely].

## 6. Unusual items
Institutional ownership 4.6% and insider 0.03% imply ADNOC controls most of the equity and the free float is thin, so minority holders have little say in capital allocation and a price drop may reflect thin liquidity, not news [Likely]. The Q2 guidance raise reported by Reuters is headline-only and unverified here (source: https://www.reuters.com/world/middle-east/adnoc-ls-lifts-annual-outlook-q2-profit-quadruples-2026-08-11/) [Guessing].
