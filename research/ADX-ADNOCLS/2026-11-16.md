# ADNOC Logistics & Services (ADX:ADNOCLS) — Core initiation — 2026-11-16
## 1. Business
Majority-ADNOC-owned shipper: charters crude, gas (LNG/LPG) and product tankers plus offshore logistics, largely for its parent's export flows, with spot exposure to freight rates. [Likely] Revenue by country/region [data unavailable]; the fact sheet gives no mix, and none was fetched. (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/adx/ADNOCLS/)

## 2. Quality
Returns are at a cyclical high: TTM ROIC 25.6% vs 12.8% in FY2025 and a prior FY peak of 15.0%; operating margin 27.6% vs a 22.9% FY peak; FCF margin 12.6% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/adx/ADNOCLS/financials/ratios/?p=trailing). [Certain] Balance sheet is clean: net debt/EBITDA 0.05x, Piotroski 7. [Certain] Moat is parent-linked volume, not freight-rate power; a tanker fleet is a commodity asset. [Likely] The Q3 call links results to high freight rates and fleet growth, with 2027 clouded by volatility and geopolitics (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/adx/ADNOCLS/transcripts/729910-q3-2026/). [Likely] TTM profitability is roughly double its through-cycle level, so treat it as peak. [Likely] FCF conversion is only 47.8% of net income because of heavy fleet capex. [Certain]

## 3. Valuation
P/E 6.3x vs 5y median 14.2x and peers 17.4x; EV/EBITDA 4.9x vs 10.1x and peers 10.3x; P/FCF 13.2x; FCF yield 7.56% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/adx/ADNOCLS/statistics/). [Certain] Every multiple sits below its 5y minimum, which is what the market charges for peak shipping earnings: the reverse DCF implies -4.2% year-1 revenue growth, i.e. a rate normalisation. [Likely] Fair value is flagged unreliable (methods disagree 4.6x; DCF base 18.33 vs EV/EBITDA 3.96 AED vs price 6.74). [Certain] Cheap on peak earnings, not clearly cheap through the cycle. Price is 9.6% above its 200-day average, 12-month change +24.1%. [Certain]

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROIC (ratios page, TTM) falls below 12%, i.e. back to FY2025 level, meaning the rate windfall has fully reversed.
- Operating margin (income statement, TTM) falls below 18%.
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) rises above 1.5x as fleet purchases are debt-funded.
- FCF margin (cash flow page, TTM) turns below 3% while capex continues.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Fleet expansion is the main use of cash: the company bought vessels and ordered LNG carriers in July–August 2026 (source: https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/adnoc-ls-expands-fleet-with-13-billion-acquisition-11-vessels-2026-08-07/). [Likely] Shares outstanding fell 6.97% YoY (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/adx/ADNOCLS/statistics/), so buybacks or cancellations are visible. [Certain] Buying ships at a rate peak risks poor returns. [Guessing] FCF margin has risen from negative in FY2021-22 to 12.6% TTM. [Certain]

## 6. Unusual items
- Controlled by ADNOC (state oil company); minority holders have little say. Institutions own 4.6%, insiders 0.03%. [Certain]
- Geopolitics: Hormuz tanker standoff reported (source: https://oilprice.com/Energy/Energy-General/Iran-Blacklists-45-Tankers-as-Hormuz-Standoff-Escalates.html). The same disruption lifts rates and threatens operations. [Likely]
