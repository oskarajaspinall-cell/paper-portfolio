# ADNOC Logistics & Services plc (ADX:ADNOCLS) — Core initiation — 2026-10-20
## 1. Business
ADNOC L&S provides integrated marine logistics for the oil and gas value chain — crude, product, LPG/LNG and chemical shipping plus terminal, agency and ancillary petroleum services — predominantly for parent ADNOC under long-term contracts [Likely]. Revenue tracks shipped volumes and fleet deployment rather than spot freight rates the way independent tanker owners' revenue does [Likely]. It has been expanding its owned fleet with large vessel orders (source: stockanalysis.com news feed, see Sources) [Likely].

## 2. Quality
Returns and margins have risen steadily over five years: ROIC 3.6%→19.4% TTM, ROCE 2.6%→18.0% TTM, ROE 3.6%→22.5% TTM; operating margin 4.9%→26.0% TTM; FCF margin −22.1%→9.8% TTM (source: ratios/income statement/cash flow pages) [Certain]. Net debt/EBITDA is low at 0.13x TTM (source: ratios page) [Certain]. The durable edge is a captive relationship with ADNOC, Abu Dhabi's national oil producer, under long-term contracts that give revenue visibility competitors in open tanker markets (peers STNG, FRO, DHT) lack [Likely] — consistent with a much lower 5-year beta of 0.40 versus typical tanker-owner volatility (source: statistics page) [Certain]. The flip side is customer concentration: minimal bargaining power and dependence on ADNOC's own production and capex decisions [Likely]. Shipping through the Strait of Hormuz also carries geopolitical risk — Iran blacklisted 45 tankers transiting the strait in August 2026, threatening fines, detention and cargo confiscation (source: https://oilprice.com/Energy/Energy-General/Iran-Blacklists-45-Tankers-as-Hormuz-Standoff-Escalates.html; headline does not name ADNOC L&S vessels specifically) [Likely].

## 3. Valuation
Multiples sit below the 5-year range: P/E 9.6x (5y min/median/max 12.4x/14.2x/14.5x), EV/EBITDA 7.0x (vs 9.1x/10.1x/10.2x), EV/Sales 2.5x (vs 2.6x/2.8x/3.1x), P/FCF 24.3x (vs 42.4x/45.1x/158.6x); P/B 1.8x is mid-range (1.6x/1.8x/2.2x) (source: ratios page) [Certain]. Versus peers (STNG, FRO, DHT, Bahri/TADAWUL:4030), it trades at a premium on earnings multiples — P/E +51%, EV/EBITDA +24%, P/FCF +105% to peer medians — but a discount on EV/Sales (−32%) and P/B (−18%) (source: statistics pages) [Certain]. The earnings-multiple premium fits lower volatility than spot-chartered peers; the EV/Sales/P/B discount sits oddly against the ROE/ROIC trend above. Base fair value is 8.04 AED vs the 7.37 AED last close (+9.0%); bull 11.69 AED (+58.6%) (source: fact sheet, inform-only) [Certain]. The model's bear case (−11.78 AED) is a DCF artifact and not usable. The price already captures most of the margin/return re-rating; upside now needs contract growth, not a re-rating.

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) rises above 2.0x, signalling fleet-expansion debt is outpacing EBITDA growth.
- Operating margin (income statement) falls below 15%, signalling weaker contract economics with ADNOC.
- FCF margin (cash flow + income statement) turns negative again, signalling capex outpacing cash generation.
- ROIC (ratios page) falls below 10%, signalling returns compressing toward open-market tanker-peer levels.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
FCF conversion (FCF/NI) improved from −479.8% in FY2021 to 39.4% TTM, i.e., heavy early fleet capex is now converting into cash more consistently (source: cash flow/income statement pages) [Certain]. Net debt/EBITDA remains very low at 0.13x TTM despite recent large vessel purchases (source: ratios page) [Certain]; the stockanalysis.com news feed lists an 11-vessel, $1.3bn acquisition and a 4-ship, $900m LNG-carrier order, funded through cash/debt not quantified on the fact sheet [Likely]. Shares outstanding are essentially flat (−0.04% YoY), so no material dilution funds this growth (source: statistics page) [Certain].

## 6. Unusual items
Institutional ownership is low at 4.5% and insider ownership near zero at 0.03% (source: statistics page) [Certain], consistent with ADNOC retaining a large majority stake and leaving a small free float — a liquidity/concentration risk not otherwise captured in the multiples above.
