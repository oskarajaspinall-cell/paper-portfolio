Beta of -0.04 reflects a state-influenced, low-liquidity-driven stock, not low risk [Guessing].

## 3. Valuation
P/E is 6.8x against a 5y range of 4.2x–7.0x (92nd percentile) and a peer median of 6.7x (+1%), so earnings are not cheap versus its own history or peers (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sha/601169/financials/ratios/) [Certain]. P/B is 0.3x, at the bottom of its 5y range (0.3x–0.4x) and 46% below the peer median of 0.5x [Certain]. The discount to book is roughly what ROE of 5.3% would justify when cost of equity carries a 3.5% owner-set country premium on a 4.96% risk-free rate [Likely]. Mechanical fair value: bear 5.41, base 8.19, bull 8.77 CNY versus a 5.41 close; the base rests on P/B+ROE (7.52) and FCFE (9.54), and FCFE is unreliable for banks; the dividend-discount value is 2.68 [Likely]. Bear equals the current price, but a cheap P/B with falling ROE is a classic value trap [Likely]. The reverse DCF implies -13.5% year-1 revenue growth, so the price assumes decline [Certain].
