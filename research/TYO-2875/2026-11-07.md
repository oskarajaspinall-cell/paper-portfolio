# Toyo Suisan Kaisha (TYO:2875) — Core initiation — 2026-11-07
## 1. Business
Packaged foods (industry per stockanalysis.com), best known for branded instant noodles. It earns money by selling branded, shelf-stable and chilled food through retail channels. Segment mix is [data unavailable] in the fact sheet and was not fetched, so I do not size the segments here. [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tyo/2875/)

## 2. Quality
Returns are high and have improved. ROIC rose from 11.4% (FY2022) to 24.4% (FY2026), and the TTM figure is 23.4%. Gross margin rose from 25.0% to 31.3% TTM, and operating margin from 8.2% to 16.6%. [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tyo/2875/financials/ratios/)

The balance sheet is net cash (net debt/EBITDA -2.23x), Altman Z is 9.38 and Piotroski F is 7. Shares are down 2.66% YoY. [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tyo/2875/statistics/)

Brand and scale in a staple category plausibly protect returns, but I did not verify a moat from primary documents. [Guessing] The margin expansion is large for a food company and part of it may be cyclical (input costs, pricing, yen). Cash conversion was weak in FY2026 (61.7%) before recovering to 86.1% TTM. [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tyo/2875/financials/cash-flow-statement/)

## 3. Valuation
The price is 10,405 JPY. P/E is 13.6x against a 5y range of 13.9x–19.9x, so it is below the five-year minimum. EV/EBITDA is 6.7x, at the bottom of its 5y range. P/FCF is 15.5x, also below its range, and FCF yield is 6.46%. [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tyo/2875/financials/ratios/)

Peer median P/E is 22.0x and EV/EBITDA 12.7x, so the stock trades at discounts of 38% and 47%. [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tyo/2875/statistics/)

The reverse DCF implies -0.8% year-1 revenue growth, which is undemanding for a business with this margin trend. Base fair value is 12,909.85 JPY (+24.1%), with a bear case of 8,363.48 (-19.6%). That is a mechanical, inform-only estimate. [Likely] The risk is that the discount reflects margins being at a peak. [Guessing]

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Operating margin (income statement page, TTM) falls below 13.6%, the FY2024 level, meaning the gain has reversed.
- ROIC (ratios page, TTM) falls below 15%.
- Gross margin (income statement page, TTM) falls below 28.7%.
- FCF conversion (cash flow page, FCF/NI) stays below 70% on a TTM basis.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
The company holds net cash, and the share count is shrinking (-2.66% YoY), which suggests buybacks. [Likely] FCF margin is 11.5% TTM, against 8.1% in FY2026. FCF conversion swung between 93.2% (FY2024) and 61.7% (FY2026), so the pattern is lumpy, probably from capex timing. [Guessing] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tyo/2875/financials/cash-flow-statement/)
