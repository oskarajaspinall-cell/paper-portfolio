# Stanley Black & Decker, Inc. (SWK) — Core initiation — 2026-10-12
## 1. Business
Makes and sells hand/power tools and outdoor equipment (DEWALT, STANLEY, CRAFTSMAN, BLACK+DECKER) plus industrial engineered fastening products, sold mainly through retail and professional/OEM channels [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/swk/).

## 2. Quality
TTM ROIC 6.5% and ROCE 8.3% are both down from 11.2%/9.8% in FY2021 — five-year trend falling on both measures [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/swk/financials/ratios/). Operating margin TTM 8.5% is well below FY2021's 12.5% and short of management's 2028 target of mid-to-high-teens adjusted EBITDA margin and 35-37% gross margin (vs 32.2% TTM) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/swk/financials/income-statement/; https://www.stanleyblackanddecker.com/investors). The $2bn Global Cost Reduction Program closed end-2025, delivering ~$2.1bn run-rate savings above target [Certain] (source: 10-K, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/93556/000009355626000009/swk-20260103.htm) — the main driver of the margin recovery off the FY2022-23 trough, but that easy phase is now done; the rest relies on unproven-at-scale platforming/automation [Likely] (source: Laguna transcript, https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/swk/transcripts/748334-morgan-stanley-s-14th-annual-laguna-conference/). Brand strength (DEWALT, CRAFTSMAN) gives some pricing power, but the category is competitive (TTI/Milwaukee, Bosch) and margins remain well below best-in-class industrial peers, so no clear durable moat protects returns [Likely].

## 3. Valuation
TTM multiples are mixed: P/E 21.7x sits mid-5y-range (35th percentile), +5% vs peer median (20.6x); but EV/EBITDA 10.0x is below its own 5y minimum (11.0x) and 26% below peer median (13.5x); P/FCF 10.4x and EV/Sales 1.2x are also well below peers/own history [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/swk/financials/ratios/ and peer statistics pages). The disconnect (cheap on EV/EBITDA and FCF, average on earnings) reflects TTM earnings still depressed by the margin shortfall — the market is pricing a business unproven on its 2028 targets. Base fair value is $103.85 (+17.3% vs last close $88.57), driven mainly by DCF ($92.66) blended with EV/EBITDA ($126.22); the bear case is negative, showing high sensitivity to leverage/margin assumptions [Certain] (source: fact sheet fair-value section). Reverse DCF implies the price assumes roughly flat (-0.6%) year-1 revenue growth fading to terminal growth — a low bar management's mid-single-digit long-term goal should clear if execution continues [Likely].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Operating margin (income statement page) falls back below 7% on a trailing-twelve-month basis, reversing the post-2023 recovery.
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) rises back above 4.0x, signalling the CAM-proceeds deleveraging plan has stalled.
- ROIC (ratios page) fails to recover above 8% within the next two fiscal years, showing the cost program's benefits aren't converting into returns.
- FCF margin (cash flow + income statement) falls back below 3%, reversing the recent improvement (TTM 8.5% vs FY2021 0.9%).

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
FCF conversion (FCF/NI) TTM is 207.8%, up sharply from deeply negative FY2022-23 readings, though the multi-year series is volatile (-187%, -275%, +256%, +171%, +208%) reflecting working-capital swings rather than a clean trend [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/swk/financials/cash-flow-statement/). Shares outstanding are roughly flat (+0.05% YoY) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/swk/statistics/). Management's stated priorities are organic growth, a maintained/growing dividend, opportunistic buybacks, and using divestiture proceeds (pending CAM sale, ~$1.8bn cash) to cut debt — buybacks are currently the near-term priority ahead of M&A [Likely] (source: 10-K; Laguna transcript). Net debt/EBITDA TTM 2.58x has improved from a FY2023 peak of 7.38x [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/swk/financials/ratios/).

## 6. Unusual items
Short interest is 4.75% of float and rose 7.5% over the past month — a moderately elevated bearish positioning worth monitoring but not yet extreme [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/swk/statistics/). Institutional ownership is very high (99.7%) with minimal insider ownership (0.24%) [Certain] (source: statistics page). Tariff exposure is a live risk: management targets ~5% China-consumed U.S. production by year-end and USMCA-qualified sourcing, but the 10-K flags no assurance of full mitigation, and China's rare-earth export restrictions have already caused component delays [Likely] (source: 10-K). Altman Z-score of 2.30 is in the "grey zone" (not safe, not distressed), consistent with the leveraged balance sheet [Certain] (source: statistics page).
