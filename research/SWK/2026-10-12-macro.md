# Macro overlay: Stanley Black & Decker, Inc. (SWK) — 2026-10-12
## Assessment
Chain: real yields -> (a) mortgage rates -> housing turnover/DIY & pro tool demand -> SWK revenue, and (b) discount rate -> DCF fair value, both amplified by SWK's leverage (net debt/EBITDA 2.58x TTM, Altman Z 2.30 "grey zone") [Certain] (source: factsheet, https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/swk/financials/ratios/ and /statistics/).

Facts: 10y real yield (DFII10) 2.87% (2026-10-08), +0.56pp/3m; 10y Treasury (DGS10) 5.22%, +0.68pp/3m; breakeven inflation (T10YIE) 2.33%, flat (+0.09pp/3m) [Certain] (FRED DFII10/DGS10/T10YIE, 2026-10-08/09). The rise is almost entirely real, not breakeven — a growth/term-premium move, not an inflation scare **(inference)**. Market pricing: 2y yield (4.75%) sits above fed funds (3.88%, FRED DFF, 2026-10-08) — a proxy, not a forecast — implying no near-term cuts priced, i.e. "higher for longer." SWK's own DCF already uses today's 5.22% risk-free rate (factsheet), so the level is priced; the risk is a further rise. HY spreads widened modestly (+0.45pp/3m to 3.15%, BAMLH0A0HYM2) — not stress, but relevant given leverage.

Bull: yields roll over (growth cools, Fed pivots) -> mortgage rates ease -> housing turnover and tool demand recover -> lower discount rate lifts the DCF fair value range. Bear: yields grind higher -> housing-linked demand stays soft, compounding the unproven margin-recovery thesis, and a higher discount rate pressures an already fragile valuation (bear-case fair value is negative, showing high sensitivity) [Certain] (factsheet).

Tilt: moderate toward bear — leverage and housing exposure amplify a real move already underway, though the core thesis is execution-, not macro-, driven.
