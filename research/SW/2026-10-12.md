# Smurfit Westrock Plc (SW) — Core initiation — 2026-10-12

## 1. Business
Smurfit Westrock makes corrugated boxes, containerboard/paperboard, packaging machinery and retail displays, sold to consumer and industrial customers across Europe, North America, South America and Asia-Pacific [Certain] (source: https://www.smurfitwestrock.com). It is vertically integrated: ~23m tons/year of containerboard/paperboard capacity from its own mills, plus owned/certified forests and recycled fibre, feeds its converting plants, capturing margin from forest to finished box [Certain] (source: https://www.smurfitwestrock.com).

## 2. Quality
Formed by the 2024 WestRock merger; post-merger returns have fallen sharply: ROIC 4.1% TTM vs 11.6% FY2021, ROCE 5.1% vs 12.2%, ROE 2.7% vs 17.3% (factsheet RA) [Certain]. Operating margin compressed to 6.1% TTM from 10.3-12.7% pre-merger and gross margin to 18.2% from ~22-25% (factsheet IS) [Certain], consistent with integration costs and elevated freight (management cited $300m of extra freight cost in Q2) (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/sw/transcripts/657858-q2-2026/) [Likely]. Net debt/EBITDA rose to 2.95x TTM from 1.39-1.84x pre-merger (factsheet RA/BS/IS) [Certain]; Altman Z-score is 1.47, below the 1.8 distress threshold (factsheet ST) [Certain]. Scale (a leading containerboard producer in the Americas and Europe) and fibre integration are real advantages, but haven't shown up in returns: management says paper grades are "sold out globally" with price gains flagged to mainly benefit 2027 (same transcript) [Likely], so quality rests on an unproven recovery, not current results.

## 3. Valuation
At the 41.29 last close, P/E is 43.9x — only the 44th percentile of its 5y range (9.3x-87.5x) because TTM earnings are depressed by merger costs, and +150% above the IP/PKG/GPK/AMCR peer median of 17.5x (factsheet) [Certain]. EV/EBITDA, less distorted by one-offs, is 7.7x, the 22nd percentile of its own 5y range (5.7x-14.5x) and 6% below the peer median of 8.2x; P/B of 1.2x is 15% below the peer median (factsheet) [Certain]. The base fair value of $49.15 (DCF 67%/EV-EBITDA 33% blend) implies +19% upside, but the bear case is -$10.99 (negative equity value under a leverage/margin-stress scenario) against a bull of +169% (factsheet) [Certain] — an unusually wide, asymmetric spread showing the market is pricing real doubt that margins and leverage normalize, not just a depressed-earnings optical illusion. On EV/EBITDA the stock screens cheap; on earnings and the bear scenario it screens exposed.

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) stays above 3.0x for two consecutive quarters, showing leverage isn't falling despite the CMPC deal
- ROIC (ratios page) fails to recover above 6% within four quarters, showing merger synergies aren't materializing
- Operating margin (income statement) stays below 8% beyond FY2026, showing freight/integration costs are structural, not transitory
- Altman Z-score (statistics page) falls further below the current 1.47, widening distress risk

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
FCF margin is thin at 3.2% TTM, but FCF conversion (FCF/NI) is 204% TTM: net income is depressed well below cash generation by non-cash merger/integration charges (factsheet CF/IS) [Certain]. The company is simultaneously raising leverage to fund the ~$420m CMPC Chile containerboard acquisition announced 2026-09-23 per the headline (terms not independently verified beyond it) (source: https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260923252862/en/Smurfit-Westrock-Announces-Agreement-to-Acquire-CMPC%E2%80%99s-Chilean-Containerboard-and-Corrugated-Business/) [Likely]. With net debt/EBITDA already at 2.95x, further debt-funded M&A before deleveraging is complete adds execution risk: capital is going to consolidation ahead of balance-sheet repair [Likely].

## 6. Unusual items
Altman Z-score of 1.47 sits in the distress zone (<1.8) (factsheet ST) [Certain], material given the business is also levering up for the CMPC acquisition. Short interest rose 14% in the past month to 4.6% of float (factsheet ST/News) [Certain], consistent with the market questioning the pace of earnings recovery.
