# ConocoPhillips (COP) — Core initiation — 2026-10-11
## 1. Business
Pure-play upstream E&P: explores for and produces oil, natural gas and NGLs across the Lower 48 (Permian, Eagle Ford, Bakken), Alaska, Canada and international assets, selling at prevailing commodity prices [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/cop/). No refining/downstream; increasingly also an LNG offtaker, signing a new 20-year, 1mtpa supply deal with Venture Global [Certain] (source: https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20261001098196/en/Venture-Global-and-ConocoPhillips-Announce-20-Year-LNG-Sales-and-Purchase-Agreement/).

## 2. Quality
Returns have fallen every year since the 2022 price spike: ROIC 31.3%→9.6% (FY22-25, TTM 11.6%), ROCE 33.7%→11.4% (TTM 13.3%), ROE 40.0%→12.4% (TTM 14.2%); gross, operating and net margins all down 3-7pp over 5 years; FCF conversion fell 54pp over the period (TTM 108.4%) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/cop/financials/ratios/). This is a commodity producer with no pricing power — scale and low-cost basins (Permian/Eagle Ford/Bakken) give cost advantage, not a moat that stops returns reverting with the oil price. Balance sheet is conservative: net debt/EBITDA 0.58x TTM, Altman Z 3.10 (safe zone), Piotroski F 6/9 [Certain] (source: same). Management change announced 2026-09-24: Andy O'Brien becomes President/CEO, Ryan Lance moves to Executive Chair, Konnie Haynes-Welsh becomes CFO — an execution-continuity question at the top [Certain] (source: https://www.conocophillips.com/news/).

## 3. Valuation
Current multiples sit at or above the top of COP's own 5-year range: P/E 17.8x (150% of the 5y max), EV/EBITDA 6.6x (141% of max), EV/Sales 2.7x (130% of max); P/FCF 16.0x is at the 5y max [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/cop/financials/ratios/, https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/cop/statistics/). Vs the XOM/CVX/EOG/OXY peer median COP looks cheaper on P/E (-6%) and EV/EBITDA (-8%) but pricier on EV/Sales (+8%) and P/FCF (+12%) — a mixed picture, not a bargain [Certain]. Base fair value (EV/EBITDAX, the industry's 2nd-best method; NAV, the best method, needs reserve data not on stockanalysis.com) is $107.11, 20.1% below the last close of $134.10; even the bull case ($112.55) sits 16.1% below the current price [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/cop/). The market is pricing a more favorable cycle than COP's own history or bull-case assumptions support.

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROIC (ratios page) stabilizes and recovers back above 15% for two consecutive quarters, reversing the 5-year decline.
- FCF margin (ratios page) recovers back toward its FY21-22 levels (above 22%) rather than continuing to compress.
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) stays below 1.0x through a full down-cycle, confirming the balance sheet can absorb a lower oil price without forcing a dividend/buyback cut.
- The new CEO (effective after the announced succession) delivers at least two quarters of capital discipline consistent with guidance, with no unplanned capex increase.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Shares outstanding roughly flat YoY (+0.27%), so net buybacks have stalled even as FCF conversion remains >100% of net income TTM (108.4%) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/cop/financials/ratios/, https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/cop/statistics/). Net debt/EBITDA is low and stable (0.58x-0.79x over 5 years), leaving room to sustain the dividend through a downturn [Certain] (source: same). Management (chairman) has publicly flagged a rising oil-price floor near $70/bbl and a $65-70 mid-cycle view, which — if realized — underpins capital returns, but this is a forward view, not a stockanalysis.com figure, and is not used as valuation input here [Likely] (source: fact sheet headline, https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/conocophillips-sees-oil-price-floor-rising-70-barrel-chairman-says-2026-10-05/).

## 6. Unusual items
CEO/CFO succession announced 2026-09-24 (Andy O'Brien to CEO; Ryan Lance to Executive Chair; Konnie Haynes-Welsh to CFO) — a leadership transition during a period of declining returns [Certain] (source: https://www.conocophillips.com/news/). Company is evaluating a sale of its Norway business and UK Teesside asset after an unsolicited offer — portfolio-simplification, outcome and proceeds not yet known [Likely] (source: fact sheet headline, https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/conocophillips-explores-sale-norway-business-uk-teesside-asset-2026-10-01/). Short interest is low (1.34% of float) and fell 4.7% month-over-month — no crowding risk [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/cop/statistics/).

**Decision: AVOID.** Five years of falling ROIC/ROCE/ROE and margins, multiples at or above the top of COP's own 5-year range, and a price already above even the bull-case fair value estimate leave no margin of safety; a leadership transition adds near-term execution uncertainty.
