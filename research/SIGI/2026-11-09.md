# Selective Insurance Group (SIGI) — Core initiation — 2026-11-09
## 1. Business
Regional US property and casualty insurer: standard commercial lines, standard personal lines and E&S (surplus) lines, earning underwriting profit plus investment income on float. Writes business only in the United States, with no direct Middle East exposure; no foreign country is near 10% of revenue [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/230557/000023055726000027/sigi-20260930.htm). Recently added Standard Commercial states produced $351 million of premium in nine months, about 8% of direct premiums written [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/230557/000023055726000027/sigi-20260930.htm). [Certain]

## 2. Quality
Decent, not exceptional. TTM ROIC 12.4% and ROE 14.7%, but ROIC ranged from 7.1% (FY2024) to 12.2% (FY2025) and ROE 6.8% to 14.1% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/sigi/financials/ratios/). Swings track underwriting and reserve results, so returns are cyclical rather than moat-protected [Likely]. Direction is up: TTM ROIC +1.6pp year on year (ratios page, trailing view). Q3 2026 combined ratio 96.8% and operating ROE 16.9%, with no prior-year casualty reserve development [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/230557/000023055726000027/sigi-20260930.htm). [Certain] Edge is agent relationships and granular pricing, but management admits remediation is restraining commercial premium growth (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/sigi/transcripts/715112-q3-2026/). [Likely] Net debt/EBITDA 1.23x: balance sheet sound. Beta 0.21.

## 3. Valuation
The price assumes returns hold near today's level, not a repeat of FY2024. P/E 11.9x sits at 5% of its 5y range and EV/EBITDA 9.2x at 1%, while P/B 1.8x is mid-range (57%). Against peers (CINF, THG, AFG, WRB, HIG): P/E +3% and EV/EBITDA +9% vs the median, P/B 26% below (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/sigi/statistics/). Peers are not cheaper on earnings, so the 5y-low reading reflects sector de-rating plus SIGI's volatile earnings, not a unique discount [Likely]. Base fair value 109.63 vs last close 99.44 (+10.2%); bear 55.66 is far lower because the P/B + ROE bear case assumes a return to FY2024 profitability [Likely]. P/FCF 5.2x and FCF yield 19.41% are flattered by float growth (FCF/NI 222.8%); do not treat as owner earnings [Likely]. Modest upside, not a deep discount.

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Q3-style underwriting reverses: quarterly combined ratio (10-Q MD&A) above 100% for two consecutive quarters.
- Reserve adequacy: adverse prior-year casualty reserve development (10-Q MD&A table) in any full year.
- ROIC TTM (ratios page, trailing view) falls below 8%.
- Net debt / EBITDA (fact sheet quality table) rises above 2.5x.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Returned $81 million to common holders in Q3 via dividends and opportunistic buybacks [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/230557/000023055726000027/sigi-20260930.htm). Share count fell 1.55% year on year (statistics page): modest, accretive. Cash conversion is high, but insurance float inflates it [Likely]. Leverage is low, so reinsurance and reserve quality matter more than the balance sheet.
