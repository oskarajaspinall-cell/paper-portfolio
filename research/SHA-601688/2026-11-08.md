# Huatai Securities (SHA:601688) — Core initiation — 2026-11-08
## 1. Business
Chinese full-service broker (Shanghai A-share, also listed in Hong Kong and London). It earns from brokerage, investment banking and IPO sponsorship, asset management, and proprietary and trading income, so profits track Chinese equity-market turnover and issuance [Likely]. It is expanding overseas, funded by convertible bonds [Likely] (source: https://www.scmp.com/business/article/3342184/chinas-huatai-securities-raise-us13-billion-fund-overseas-push). Revenue mix by country is [data unavailable]; the business is predominantly mainland China [Likely]. Reports in CNY.

## 2. Quality
Mediocre, cyclical and not moat-protected. ROIC is 2.8% TTM against 2.5% in FY2021, flat (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sha/601688/financials/ratios/). ROE is 8.8% TTM, down from 9.6% in FY2021 [Certain]. Operating margin rose from 34.2% to 58.3% TTM and net margin is 42.8% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sha/601688/financials/income-statement/). That margin is a market-activity peak, not a durable edge [Likely]. Brokerage is a licensed, fee-compressing industry where scale peers (CITIC) compete on the same products [Likely]. Piotroski F is 5 and the share count rose 4.87% YoY (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sha/601688/statistics/). Recent direction: ROIC flat (+0.4pp); operating margin is just below its 2-year high [Certain]. No durable reason competitors can't take returns away [Likely].

## 3. Valuation
P/E 9.4x is below its 5y minimum of 9.6x and in line with peers at 9.6x. P/B 0.7x sits at 20% of its 5y range and is 22% below peers (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sha/601688/statistics/) [Certain]. FCF yield of 33.60% and P/FCF of 3.0x (above its 5y max) are meaningless for a broker: FCF swings between -167.0% and 219.6% of revenue with trading-book and client-fund flows, so ignore them [Likely]. Fair value base is 20.24 CNY (+13.5% vs 17.84), bear 12.50 (-29.9%), bull 26.62. The DCF methods are weak for a broker, and the P/E cross-check gives 19.13 [Likely]. Reverse DCF implies -1.1% year-1 revenue growth. The price asks little, but earnings are at peak-cycle margins on 8.8% ROE, so the discount to book is fair rather than cheap [Likely]. The trend is down: -16.1% over 12 months and -24.6pp vs SPY [Certain].
