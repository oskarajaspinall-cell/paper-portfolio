Revenue is overwhelmingly net interest income — the spread earned lending to corporates, SMEs, government-related borrowers and retail customers in Chengdu/Sichuan funded by deposits — plus fee/wealth-management income; net margin of 60-62% is typical of the deposit-funded banking model (source: stockanalysis.com income statement) [Certain].

Piotroski F-Score is a middling 4/9; Altman Z and ROIC/ROCE are [data unavailable] (not meaningful for a leveraged bank balance sheet). FCF margin and FCF/NI swing wildly (-457% to +560% over five years) and FCF yield prints 114% — bank cash-flow statements are dominated by deposit/loan balance movements, so these are not reliable quality signals and are excluded from the thesis [Certain]. The durable part of the franchise is licensing/regulatory barriers to entry and a local deposit base typical of Chinese city commercial banks, but the declining ROE trend argues competitors (or sector-wide NIM compression) are already eroding the return advantage [Likely].
