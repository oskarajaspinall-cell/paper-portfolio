# UnitedHealth Group (UNH) — Core initiation — 2026-11-19
## 1. Business
UnitedHealthcare sells health insurance (Medicare Advantage, Medicaid, employer and individual plans) and earns premiums less medical costs. Optum supplies care delivery, pharmacy benefits and data services, partly to its own insurer. Revenue is US-centred; the remaining South American operations are being exited [Certain] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/731766/000073176626000266/unh-20260930.htm). No foreign country appears to be ~10% of revenue [Likely].

## 2. Quality
Returns have fallen: ROIC 16.8% (FY2023) to 12.4% (TTM); ROE 25.0% to 15.8% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/unh/financials/ratios/) [Certain]. ROIC is 2.5pp below its 2-year high, though flat over the last year (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/unh/financials/ratios/?p=trailing) [Certain]. FCF conversion of 143.7% of net income is strong [Certain]. Leverage rose: net debt/EBITDA 2.57x TTM vs 1.78x in FY2023 [Certain].
The moat is scale and an integrated insurer-provider-PBM model, but it is being eroded by pricing that lags cost. The 10-Q says Medicare Advantage rates remain below medical cost trend, Medicaid rates lag, and commercial cost trend is elevated. It also says UnitedHealthcare served 1.8 million fewer people on benefit and pricing actions (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/731766/000073176626000266/unh-20260930.htm) [company-reported]. Shrinking to restore margin is a repair, not a moat [Likely]. Piotroski F is 6 [Certain].

## 3. Valuation
The price fell sharply on 2026-11-18 after a proposed CMS rule to extend Medicare Advantage audit extrapolation to payments from 2018 (source: https://www.barrons.com/articles/unitedhealth-humana-cvs-stock-medicare-advantage-audits-1a7e683d) [Likely]. Results do not explain the fall [Likely].
At 349.91 USD, P/E is 19.2x against a 5y range of 21.8x–32.3x (below the minimum). EV/EBITDA is 12.5x (5y min 14.8x) and P/FCF 13.7x (min 18.6x); FCF yield is 7.28% [Certain]. Against peers it is still at a premium: P/E +34%, EV/EBITDA +17% (n=4) [Certain]. RSI is 26.6 [Certain].
Mechanical fair value is bear 380.66, base 433.95, bull 478.70, from P/E alone because the DCF had no usable input [Certain]. That P/E rests on TTM earnings a retroactive clawback would reduce, so base is probably too high [Guessing]. The market prices a lasting margin reset plus an unsized regulatory tail.

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROIC TTM (ratios page, trailing) falls below 10%.
- Net debt/EBITDA (site definition, ratios page) exceeds 2.5x.
- FCF conversion (cash-flow and income statement pages) falls below 100%.
- The CMS audit rule is finalised with extrapolation back to 2018 payments, and company-reported people served at UnitedHealthcare keeps falling in the next 10-Q while ROIC stays under 12%.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Shares fell 0.98% year on year, a modest buyback (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/unh/statistics/) [Certain]. The 10-Q shows net repayment of debt and cash received from dispositions, so portfolio pruning is funding deleveraging [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/731766/000073176626000266/unh-20260930.htm). Quarterly dividend is 2.32 USD (source: https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20261104368533/en/UnitedHealth-Group-Board-Authorizes-Payment-of-Quarterly-Dividend/) [Certain]. Divestiture gains lift reported profit, so part of it is non-recurring [Likely]. A reported bid for Surgery Partners was rejected (source: https://www.tipranks.com/news/the-fly/surgery-partners-turned-down-28-per-share-bid-from-unitedhealth-bloomberg-says-thefly-news) [Guessing], so M&A appetite remains.

## 6. Unusual items
