# UnitedHealth Group (UNH) — Core initiation — 2026-10-12

## 1. Business
UnitedHealthcare earns premiums for insuring employer, Medicare Advantage, Medicaid and individual members, profiting on the gap between premiums and medical/claims costs. Optum (Health, Insight, Rx) sells care delivery, data/analytics and pharmacy-benefit-management services to external payers/providers and internally to UnitedHealthcare, vertically integrating the insurance-to-care chain. [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/unh/)

## 2. Quality
Returns have compressed sharply over five years: ROIC fell from 16.8% (FY2023) to 10.2% (FY2025)/11.4% TTM; ROE fell from 25.0% (FY2023) to 12.5% (FY2025)/14.2% TTM, a -11.6pp swing [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/unh/financials/ratios/). Net debt/EBITDA (site definition) more than doubled, from 0.94x (FY2021) to 2.44x (FY2025)/1.68x TTM; on a stricter own calc it reached 2.75x TTM from 1.76x [Certain] (same source). FCF conversion is a genuine offsetting strength, rising from 115% (FY2021) to 167% TTM [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/unh/financials/cash-flow-statement/). Scale in Medicare Advantage and Optum's insurer/PBM/care-delivery integration are real structural advantages, but the cost pressure behind the margin give-back is industry-wide: UnitedHealthcare and CVS's Aetna both moved to narrower-network 2027 Medicare Advantage plans to defend margin [Likely] (source: https://www.reuters.com/legal/litigation/unitedhealthcare-aetna-say-2027-medicare-advantage-plans-offer-more-limited-2026-10-01/). That the largest player needed two years of return erosion before acting suggests medical-cost trend is currently stronger than the moat.

## 3. Valuation
UNH trades above peer medians on every multiple: P/E 24.4x vs 20.5x (+19%), EV/EBITDA 14.4x vs 11.8x (+22%), P/FCF 14.4x vs 11.8x (+22%), EV/Sales 0.9x vs 0.4x (+106%), P/B 3.5x vs 1.9x (+86%) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/unh/statistics/). Against its own 5y range it looks cheaper: P/E sits at the 25th percentile (21.8x–32.3x range), and EV/EBITDA and P/FCF are both below their own 5y minimums [Certain] (same source) — the whole peer group has re-rated down, not just UNH. The fact sheet's base fair value (P/E-based; DCF unavailable, [data unavailable] for TTM revenue/share count) is $385.28, +1.6% above the $379.30 last close; bear/bull are -10.9%/+12.1% [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/unh/). The price sits close to the base case with a roughly symmetric, unexciting range either side — it is not pricing a quick recovery in returns, but it also is not paying an investor to own the still-falling ROIC/ROE trend.

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROIC (ratios page) falls further below the TTM 11.4%, toward single digits, confirming erosion rather than mean-reversion
- Net debt/EBITDA, site definition (ratios page) rises above 3.0x, from 1.68x TTM, showing leverage is not self-correcting
- FCF/NI conversion (cash flow + income statement) falls back below 100% from 167% TTM, reversing the one offsetting strength
- ROE (ratios page) falls below the FY2025 low of 12.5% even after the 2027 Medicare Advantage network changes take effect

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
FCF conversion is high and rising (143.7% FY2024, 167.2% TTM) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/unh/financials/cash-flow-statement/), but leverage is rising just as fast — net debt/EBITDA (site definition) more than doubled from 0.94x (FY2021) to 2.44x (FY2025) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/unh/financials/ratios/). Shares outstanding fell -1.47% YoY, showing continued buybacks even as leverage climbs [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/unh/statistics/). The September 2026 sale of a partial WellMed/Optum Health Florida stake to TPG reads as portfolio pruning to fund buybacks and deleverage rather than a strategy change [Likely] (source: https://invezz.com/news/2026/09/09/unitedhealth-sells-wellmed-to-tpg-what-it-means-for-unh-stock/).

## 6. Unusual items
Texas Attorney General Ken Paxton sent civil investigative demands over potential Deceptive Trade Practices Act violations (Oct 5, 2026); outcome and any fine are unknown [Likely] (source: https://www.wsj.com/health/healthcare/texas-ag-paxton-launches-investigation-into-unitedhealth-group-bc6ce142). Next earnings are Oct 13, 2026 — the day after this note — so results could move the valuation inputs quickly [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/unh/). Short interest is low (1.78% of float) and institutions own 89.1%, so this is not a crowding or squeeze risk [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/unh/statistics/).
