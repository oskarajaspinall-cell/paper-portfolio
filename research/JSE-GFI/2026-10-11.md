# Gold Fields Limited (JSE:GFI) — Core initiation — 2026-10-11

## 1. Business
Gold Fields mines and sells gold (plus copper/silver by-products) from a globally diversified portfolio across South Africa, Australia, Ghana, Peru and Chile, with a newly agreed nickel-concentrator bolt-on in Western Australia. Revenue is production volume × the prevailing market gold price, less all-in sustaining costs; the company is a price-taker with no control over its principal selling price (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/jse/GFI/). [Certain]

## 2. Quality
TTM returns look exceptional — ROIC 41.7%, ROCE 43.5%, ROE 58.7%, net margin 38.7%, FCF margin 38.9%, roughly double FY2021 levels (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/jse/GFI/financials/ratios/) [Certain]. But this is a commodity miner with no pricing power or moat beyond ore-body cost position; the surge tracks the gold price, not a structural improvement competitors can't replicate [Likely]. The balance sheet is currently pristine — net debt/EBITDA 0.06x TTM vs 0.82x in FY2024 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/jse/GFI/financials/ratios/) [Certain] — but management is actively pursuing Northern Star Resources after an initial non-binding proposal was rejected, and just agreed to buy BHP's Kambalda nickel concentrator (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/jse/GFI/transcripts/751937-mining-forum-americas-2026/; headline: https://www.reuters.com/legal/transactional/bhp-sell-kambalda-nickel-concentrator-tenements-gold-fields-2026-10-06/) [Certain] — capital-allocation moves that could re-lever the balance sheet or dilute shares if a sweetened bid succeeds [Likely].

## 3. Valuation
P/E 7.6x and P/FCF 7.5x both sit below their own 5-year minimums (9.4x/12.7x) and under peer medians (10.1x/10.8x) (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/jse/GFI/financials/ratios/) [Certain], but that reflects earnings and FCF running at a cyclical peak, not a structural re-rating [Likely]. P/B, the least cyclical multiple, sits at the top of its 5y range (61% position) and 24% above the peer median (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/jse/GFI/financials/ratios/) [Certain] — the market is already paying up for book value. The fact sheet's own history-based fair value (EV/EBITDA method on normalized earnings) puts the base case at 26,410 ZAc, 56.7% below last close, and bear at 23,382 ZAc, 61.7% below; only the bull case (86,578 ZAc) sits above the current price (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/jse/GFI/). So cheap trailing multiples coexist with a base-case fair value far below the quote — the price assumes record margins and the gold price persist; any mean reversion would expose this gap [Likely].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROIC (ratios page) falls back below 20% (its FY2021-24 average, vs 41.7% TTM) — the return surge was gold-price cyclicality, not a durable improvement.
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) rises above 1.0x — the Northern Star pursuit or further bolt-ons are being funded with real leverage, not cash.
- Shares outstanding growth YoY (statistics page) exceeds 15% — large dilutive stock issuance to fund a sweetened Northern Star bid.
- FCF margin (cash-flow/income statement) falls below 20% — cash conversion normalizing off its current 38.9% TTM peak.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
FCF conversion is 100.7% of net income TTM and FCF margin 38.9%, both far above the FY2021-24 norm (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/jse/GFI/financials/ratios/) [Certain]. Management's stated policy is a 35% free-cash-flow dividend plus discretionary buybacks and growth capex — $250m of special dividends and $300m of buybacks in H1 2026 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/jse/GFI/transcripts/751937-mining-forum-americas-2026/) [Certain]. Shares outstanding are up only 0.22% YoY (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/jse/GFI/statistics/) [Certain], so no dilution yet, but that policy now competes with a possible Northern Star acquisition and the just-agreed Kambalda nickel purchase from BHP [Likely].

## 6. Unusual items
Gold Fields is actively pursuing Northern Star Resources after an initial non-binding proposal was rejected, and management says it may return with an enhanced bid (source: https://www.reuters.com/legal/transactional/gold-fields-aims-woo-northern-star-investors-after-miners-rebuff-2026-09-30/; https://stockanalysis.com/quote/jse/GFI/transcripts/751937-mining-forum-americas-2026/) [Certain]. This is material: a large, partly stock-funded acquisition would change the leverage and share-count assumptions used above [Likely]. Institutional ownership is high at 95.8% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/jse/GFI/statistics/) [Certain]; short-interest data is unavailable (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/jse/GFI/statistics/).
