# Macro overlay: Gold Fields Limited (GFI) — 2026-10-11

## Assessment
Gold Fields is a pure gold-price taker with no disclosed hedge book; revenue is production × spot gold price — the gate's own textbook primary case via real yields + dollar → gold price → revenue/margin → multiple [Certain].

Facts (FRED, 2026-10-02/08/09): the 10y real yield (DFII10) rose to 2.87%, up +0.44pp 1m / +0.56pp 3m; the nominal 10y (DGS10) rose to 5.22% while the 10y breakeven (T10YIE) stayed flat at 2.33% (-0.04pp 1m) — the yield rise is almost entirely real, not inflation. The broad dollar index (DTWEXBGS) rose +2.86 in ~1m to 121.4. Market pricing (proxy): the 2y yield (4.75%) sits 0.87pp above fed funds (3.88%), implying the market prices further tightening, not cuts [Likely, proxy].

Inference (general macro relationship, not stockanalysis.com/FRED-sourced): rising real yields raise the opportunity cost of holding non-yielding gold, and dollar strength raises its cost to non-USD buyers; both are headwinds to the gold price GFI realizes, not yet visible in GFI's own 3-month +9.7% bounce (factsheet) [Likely] **(inference)**.

Bull: real yields/dollar reverse → gold price rises → GFI's elevated 53% TTM operating margin widens further, multiple re-rates toward peers.
Bear: real yields/dollar extend their rise → gold price falls → margin reverts toward its FY2021-24 ~32-37% norm → earnings fall toward the base-case fair value (26,410 ZAc, -56.7% vs spot) already implied by normalized EV/EBITDA.
Tilt: toward bear, moderate — the headwind is accelerating and not yet reflected in the stock's recent bounce.
