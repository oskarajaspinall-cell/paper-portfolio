# Macro overlay: Western Digital Corporation (WDC) — 2026-10-09
## Assessment
WDC has a clamped CAPM beta of ~2.0 [Certain, factsheet]; the fact sheet's DCF already uses the current 10y Treasury yield (5.28%, FRED DGS10, 2026-10-07) as risk-free, so the discount-rate channel is already embedded in the $134 base fair value. 10y real yield (DFII10) rose 0.61pp over 3 months to 2.92% (2026-10-07); nominal 10y rose 0.72pp to 5.28%; breakevens were roughly flat (+0.12pp to 2.35%) [Certain, FRED] — a real-rate/term-premium move, not an inflation re-rating **(inference)**. The 2y yield (4.77%) sits 0.89pp above effective fed funds (3.88%), a proxy for a market-priced further-hikes/higher-for-longer path **(inference, not a forecast)**. A further real-yield rise would push WDC's cost of equity up (beta amplifies it), widening the gap between the $393 close and fair value, which already sits below the bear case on cash-flow multiples. VIX is low and falling (15.08, -1.82/3m) [Certain, FRED] — the stock's 28.5% 3-month drawdown looks idiosyncratic (Toshiba/TDK capacity reports, hyperscaler-capex headlines), not macro risk-off [Likely]. Net cash makes the credit-spread channel immaterial [Certain, factsheet].
**Macro bull**: real yields fall (DFII10 toward ~2.3-2.5%) → cost of equity falls → fair value range rises, narrowing (not closing) the gap.
**Macro bear**: real yields keep rising → cost of equity rises further → fair value falls further below the close, adding to any sector-driven de-rating.
**Tilt**: the HDD supply/Toshiba/hyperscaler-capex story dominates; rates are a second-order lever.
