# Western Digital Corporation (WDC) — Core initiation — 2026-10-09

## 1. Business
Western Digital is now a pure-play maker of hard disk drives (HDDs) — nearline/enterprise, client and consumer storage — after spinning off its flash/NAND business as SanDisk (SNDK) in 2025 [Certain]. Cloud/enterprise is its largest end market, 89% of FY2026 revenue; top 10 customers are 73% of revenue, with three customers each ≥10% (source: SEC 10-K, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/106040/000162828026057139/wdc-20260703.htm).

## 2. Quality
Current returns are extraordinary — ROIC 52.9% TTM, ROCE 48.1%, gross margin 48.9% vs 38.8% a year ago (factsheet RA/IS) — but the 5y history shows how cyclical this business is: ROIC was -2.2% (FY2023), 0.7% (FY2024) [Certain]. HDD is a tight oligopoly: the 10-K names only Seagate and Toshiba as HDD competitors [Certain], some pricing discipline, but the margin surge looks like an AI/cloud storage supply shortage, not a structural moat; Toshiba is reportedly pursuing TDK's magnetic-heads business to add capacity (https://invezz.com/news/2026/10/06/seagate-western-digital-stocks-are-falling-again-on-tuesday-heres-why/) [Likely] — a risk to the shortage underpinning pricing. Customer concentration (three customers ≥10% of revenue each, cloud 89%) is a demand-side risk if hyperscaler capex slows [Certain]. FCF conversion (FCF/NI) fell from 97.9% (FY2024) to 37.3% TTM even as margins rose (factsheet CF,IS) [Certain]. TTM net margin (72.9%) is far above operating margin (35.8%), inflating ROE (130.9%) — treat ROE/net margin as unreliable; use ROIC/ROCE/operating margin instead [Guessing].

## 3. Valuation
P/E (16.2x) is 40% below the peer median (27.2x), mid-range of its own 5y history (66th percentile), but EV/EBITDA (29.3x, +48% vs peers), P/FCF (41.9x, +74% vs peers) and a 2.38% FCF yield show it expensive on cash-flow multiples — consistent with the net-margin anomaly distorting the P/E (factsheet RA, peers STX/MU/SNDK/NTAP). The fact sheet's own-history fair value (5y-average normalised, as current earnings look like a peak) gives bear -$17.38, base $134.18 (-65.9% vs the $393.31 last close), bull $421.84 (+7.3%) — price sits above even the bull case [Certain]. The reverse DCF implies 48.1% year-1 revenue growth fading to terminal — close to management's guided 42-49% YoY Q1 FY27 revenue growth (SEC 8-K exhibit 99.1, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/106040/000162828026053305/a4ex991-pressreleaseq426.htm) [Likely], so near-term growth may validate this, but sustaining both that growth rate and this margin for the DCF's full horizon is a high bar for a cyclical hardware maker.

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Gross margin (income statement/ratios page) falls back below its FY2025 level of 38.8%, signalling the supply shortage easing.
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) rises back above 1.0x, signalling capital discipline breaking down.
- Any of the three ≥10%-of-revenue customers (10-K disclosure) is reported lost or materially cutting orders.
- ROIC (ratios page) falls back into its FY2022-FY2024 range (-2.2% to 11.5%), confirming mean reversion to pre-shortage levels.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
FY2026 buybacks totalled $2.592bn (Q4 alone $672m), alongside a $0.15/share quarterly dividend (source: SEC 8-K exhibit 99.1, as above) [Certain]. Net debt/EBITDA is now -0.08x (net cash) — the strongest balance sheet in the 5y fact-sheet history — giving flexibility to keep returning capital through a downturn [Certain]. But FCF conversion (FCF/NI) has fallen from 97.9% (FY2024) to 37.3% TTM even as earnings rose, so a growing share of reported profit is not yet converting to free cash flow; capex/working-capital intensity is worth watching given guided Q1 FY27 revenue growth of 42-49% YoY [Likely].

## 6. Unusual items
TTM net margin (72.9%) is far above operating margin (35.8%), a ~37pp gap the fact sheet's income-statement lines cannot explain on their own — likely a one-off/non-operating item (possibly related to the 2025 SanDisk separation or tax items) that materially inflates reported ROE (130.9%) [Guessing]; size any position on ROIC/operating-margin quality, not ROE. Beta (5y) is high at 2.17, and the stock has already fallen 28.5% over three months (vs SPY +3.8%) with RSI at 37.2 — elevated realised volatility to reflect in sizing. Short interest is 4.65% of float, falling 13.1% month-on-month (factsheet ST).
