# 37 Interactive Entertainment (SHE:002555) — Core initiation — 2026-11-09

## 2. Quality
Returns remain good in absolute terms but are in clear decline. ROIC fell from 61.1% (FY2021) to 30.8% (FY2025) and 17.7% TTM; the last 12 months alone cost 15.7pp (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/she/002555/financials/ratios/?p=trailing) [Certain]. Operating margin is 13.9% TTM vs 19.1% a year earlier; FCF margin 10.5% vs 20.3% [Certain]. Gross margin slid from 84.8% to 75.9% by FY2025, which suggests rising traffic or platform costs or weaker titles [Likely]. Game publishing has hit-driven revenue and weak moats beyond the title pipeline and user acquisition skill, so durable excess returns are not evidenced [Likely]. Balance sheet is strong: net cash (net debt/EBITDA -2.07x), Altman Z 4.51, Piotroski F only 4 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/she/002555/statistics/) [Certain]. Dilution is negligible (0.08%) [Certain].

## 3. Valuation
P/E 17.4x sits at 59% of its 5y range (12.9x–20.7x, median 15.7x); EV/EBITDA 14.6x vs median 12.4x; P/FCF 22.9x is above its 5y max (22.7x) as FCF compressed [Certain]. Versus peers it looks cheap on P/E (-40%) and EV/EBITDA (-54%), but peer samples are small (n=2–3) and peers are higher-multiple gaming names [Likely]. The mechanical fair value is flagged unreliable: DCF base 7.52 vs EV/Revenue base 16.40 CNY against a 16.32 close; the reverse DCF implies 26.1% year-1 revenue growth [Certain]. Price is not cheap against depressed earnings; FCF yield 4.36%. The shares are down 26.9% over 12 months, the market already prices declining returns, but nothing shows margins have stabilised [Likely].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
