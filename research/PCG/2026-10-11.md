# PG&E Corporation (PCG) — Core initiation — 2026-10-11
## 1. Business
PG&E Corporation is the holding company for Pacific Gas and Electric Company, a rate-regulated electric and natural-gas utility serving Northern and Central California [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/pcg/). Revenue is earned on a regulator-approved rate base (generation, transmission, distribution, gas delivery) set by the California Public Utilities Commission, which sets allowed returns on capital spent largely on wildfire-hardening and grid upgrades [Certain].

## 2. Quality
A geographic monopoly faces no competitive threat [Certain], but returns are structurally weak: ROCE is 4.4% TTM and ROE 9.3% TTM, both low for a regulated utility, reflecting wildfire-liability and credit overhang [Certain] (source: fact sheet Quality table, RA). Net margin has risen to 11.8% TTM, yet FCF margin is deeply negative (-16.5% TTM) and FCF/NI conversion is -139.5% TTM: PG&E burns cash beyond reported profit, funded by continued debt and equity issuance [Certain] (source: fact sheet Quality table, CF/IS). Net debt/EBITDA is 6.19x — high for a utility — though down from 7.65x in FY2022 [Certain] (source: fact sheet, RA). Durability rests on regulatory outcomes (liability caps, allowed ROE), not any advantage the company controls [Likely]. Management has launched a review to regain investment-grade credit, including a $2bn cut to 2027 capex, implying the balance sheet is not seen as durable [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/pcg/transcripts/742515-status-update/).

## 3. Valuation
PCG trades at 9.5x P/E, 8.9x EV/EBITDA and 0.9x P/B — 51%, 26% and 43% below the peer median (EIX, SRE, ED, AEP) — and below its 5-year range minimums on every multiple except EV/Sales [Certain] (source: fact sheet Valuation table). The discount reflects real risk: below-average allowed returns, wildfire-liability uncertainty and the credit-repair review [Likely]. Yet the fact sheet's own-history fair value (dividend-discount 67% + P/E 33% blend) puts base fair value at $10.13 and even the bull case at $10.66, both below the $13.13 last close (-22.9% and -18.8%) [Certain] (source: fact sheet Fair value table). So while PCG looks cheap against peers, it looks rich against its own cash generation: the $0.05/share quarterly dividend is small for a $13 stock, capping a DDM-anchored value well below what peer-relative multiples imply [Certain]. This is a genuine conflict between relative and intrinsic valuation.

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROE (ratios page) falls below 8% TTM (vs 9.3% now), showing regulatory returns deteriorating further.
- Net debt/EBITDA, site definition (ratios page) rises back above 7.0x (vs 6.10x now), showing deleveraging has stalled.
- FCF margin (cash flow/income statement) fails to narrow from -16.5% TTM over the next two fiscal years, showing the capital-reduction plan is not cutting cash burn.
- Piotroski F-Score (statistics page) falls below 5 (vs 6 now), signaling broad fundamental deterioration.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Shares outstanding grew 3.06% YoY — PG&E is still issuing equity, not buying back stock, likely to fund wildfire-hardening capex and balance-sheet repair [Certain] (source: fact sheet Other table, ST). The quarterly dividend was reinstated at just $0.05/share, a fraction of pre-bankruptcy levels, prioritizing balance-sheet repair and capex over cash returns [Likely] (source headline, 2026-09-18: https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/pge-sets-dates-for-quarterly-stock-dividends-302882677.html). FCF/NI conversion is -139.5% TTM: cash conversion from earnings to free cash is currently negative, consistent with a multi-year capital-intensive rebuild rather than a cash-returning franchise [Certain] (source: fact sheet Quality table).

## 6. Unusual items
A California wildfire-liability reform bill was shelved in 2026; the CEO said this "crushed" the stock [Certain] (source headline, 2026-09-02: https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/02/pge-ceo-calls-on-california-to-pass-wildfire-reform-after-the-shelved-effort-crushed-the-stock.html). PG&E filed a 10-year, 5,000-mile electric-undergrounding plan targeting a 98% cut in wildfire ignition risk and 90% fewer outages; if approved it adds to the rate base but also to capex and financing needs [Likely] (source headline, 2026-10-01: https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/pge-files-10-year-electrical-undergrounding-plan-to-deliver-permanent-wildfire-protection-and-improve-safety-reliability-and-affordability-302895694.html). Short interest rose 9.4% in the past month to a still-low 1.88% of float [Certain] (source: fact sheet News & sentiment, ST). Beta of 0.28 understates real risk: PCG's volatility is idiosyncratic (wildfire/legislative), not market-driven [Likely].
