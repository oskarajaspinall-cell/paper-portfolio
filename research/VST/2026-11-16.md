# Vistra Corp. (VST) — Core initiation — 2026-11-16
## 1. Business
Vistra is a US integrated power producer and retailer. It earns money selling electricity from its nuclear, gas and coal fleet into wholesale markets (Texas, PJM, the East) and to retail customers, with hedging smoothing the result [Certain] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1692819/000169281926000025/vistra-20260930.htm). Reports in USD; no foreign-country revenue exposure to flag [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/vst/).

## 2. Quality
Returns are cyclical but now strong. ROCE is 14.1% TTM vs 7.2% a year earlier, FCF margin 12.7% vs 9.6%, and net debt/EBITDA 2.77x vs 4.03x in FY2025 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/vst/financials/ratios/?p=trailing) [Certain]. But the 3-year FCF margin change is -8.6pp, and FY2025 ROCE was 7.4%, so the TTM peak is not proven durable [Certain]. ROIC is [data unavailable].

The moat is scarce, hard-to-replicate dispatchable capacity, including nuclear, while supply-chain constraints raise the cost of new build (source: sec-delta 10-Q) [Likely]. Management is signing long-term power sales to large consumers, including a Meta nuclear PPA [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1692819/000169281926000025/vistra-20260930.htm) [Certain]. The Q3 release showed record adjusted EBITDA and a narrowed FY26 guide (source: https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/vistra-reports-third-quarter-2026-results-302931798.html) [Certain]. Earnings are still commodity-linked, and GAAP net income included an unrealised hedge gain [Certain].

P/FCF is 17.3x (36th percentile), FCF yield 5.78%. P/B at 11.6x is above its 5y max, a leverage and buyback effect [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/vst/statistics/).

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
FCF conversion is 96.3% of net income TTM and shares are down 1.81% YoY, so buybacks continue [Certain]. The Cogentrix acquisition closed on 2026-10-30 with cash, debt assumption and share issuance at a set price [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1692819/000169281926000025/vistra-20260930.htm). It adds gas capacity in PJM, ISO-NE and ERCOT. Funding used junior subordinated notes, with preferred stock redemptions following; leverage will rise from the TTM level and some dilution returns [Likely].
