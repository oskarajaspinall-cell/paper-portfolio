# Macro overlay: Vistra Corp. (VST) — 2026-11-16
## Assessment
**Weight: secondary.** Chain: real yields / term premium -> discount rate on a growth-priced (data-centre demand) cash-flow stream -> multiple; secondary leg: credit spreads -> refinancing cost of a fleet adding debt after Cogentrix. Earnings are driven mainly by power prices and contracts, not macro.

**Facts (FRED):** 10y TIPS 2.86% (2026-11-12), +0.47pp in ~3m; 10y Treasury 5.10%, +0.47pp; 2y 4.86%, +0.71pp vs fed funds 3.88% (+0.25pp); HY spread 3.2%, +0.49pp over 3m; VIX 18.7, +4.07 over 3m (https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=DFII10, ...?id=DGS2, ...?id=BAMLH0A0HYM2, ...?id=VIXCLS). The fact sheet's DCF uses that 5.10% risk-free rate.

**Pricing (proxy):** the 2y sitting about 1pp above fed funds implies the market expects hikes or no cuts **(inference)**. Breakevens are flat (2.24%), so the rise is real-rate, not inflation [Likely]. The stock is already -26.7% over 12 months and -9.1% over 3 months, so much of the repricing is in the price [Likely].

**Bull:** real yields retrace below ~2.5% and spreads stay near 3%: the multiple recovers and the DCF discount rate falls. Rough effect: multiple up, no change to earnings.
**Bear:** real yields above ~3.25% with HY above 4%: the multiple de-rates further and Cogentrix refinancing costs more; beta 1.38 amplifies it.

**Tilt:** toward bear, small. Reason: rates are rising faster than the policy rate implies, but the shares already reflect much of it, and company-specific drivers (Texas data-centre policy, PPAs) matter more [Guessing].
