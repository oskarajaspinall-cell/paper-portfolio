# Amgen Inc. (AMGN) — Core initiation — 2026-10-11

## 1. Business
Amgen discovers, manufactures and markets biologic and small-molecule medicines in oncology, inflammation, bone health, cardiovascular and rare disease, selling mostly in the US [Certain]. Revenue is anchored by branded biologics (Repatha, Enbrel, Prolia/Xgeva, Otezla, Tezspire, Uplizna), newer oncology launches (Imdelltra, Blincyto), and rare-disease drugs (Tepezza, Krystexxa) added via the 2023 Horizon Therapeutics acquisition (source: SEC 10-K, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/318154/000031815426000010/amgn-20251231.htm) [Certain]. Growth now leans on label expansions and an obesity pipeline, MariTide, in Phase 3 [Certain].

## 2. Quality
Returns are eroding: ROIC fell from 24.8% (FY21) to 19.8% TTM and ROCE is flat near 18% (source: fact sheet ratios page) [Certain]. Gross margin slipped 75.2%→71.9% and operating margin 35.2%→33.4% as legacy biologics face biosimilar pressure [Certain]. Prolia/Xgeva core RANKL patents expired in the US (Feb 2025) and EU (Nov 2025), opening a major franchise to biosimilar erosion; Enbrel protection fades through 2028-2038 by jurisdiction (source: 10-K) [Certain]. The moat is patent- and manufacturing-complexity based, but is time-limited and now eroding on two of the largest legacy products [Likely]. Newer launches (Repatha label expansion, Tezspire, Imdelltra, Uplizna) and MariTide (six Phase 3 studies, source: 10-K) are the offset, but are unproven at the scale needed to replace Prolia/Xgeva/Enbrel revenue [Likely]. Piotroski F-Score is strong (8/9) but Altman Z (1.89) sits in the distress grey zone, consistent with debt taken on for Horizon (source: fact sheet) [Certain].

## 3. Valuation
At 414.87, AMGN trades at a premium to peers (GILD, REGN, VRTX, BIIB) on most multiples: EV/EBITDA 15.5x (+15% vs. peer median, above its own 5y max), EV/Sales 7.0x (+29%, above 5y max), and P/FCF 22.0x (+29%, above 5y max); P/B 19.2x is +339% vs. peers, though that reflects a thin equity base from debt-funded buybacks rather than superior returns (source: fact sheet) [Certain]. Only P/E (25.8x) looks reasonable — below the peer median (29.7x) and mid-range of its own 5-year history (34th percentile) [Certain]. The fact sheet's blended DCF/P/E fair value is 376.99 base, so the price already sits 9.1% above the base case and inside the gap to the bull case (528.16, +27.3% from here) [Certain]. The market is paying up for the obesity/oncology pipeline outrunning legacy-franchise erosion; the margin and ROIC trend above suggests that bet is not yet showing up in the numbers [Likely].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) rises back above 3.5x, showing post-Horizon deleveraging has stalled
- Gross margin (income statement) falls below 68%, a new 5-year low signalling biosimilar erosion outpacing new launches
- ROIC (ratios page) falls below 15%, approaching the cost of capital
- FCF margin (cash flow/income statement, TTM) falls below 20%

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
The 2023 Horizon Therapeutics acquisition (added Tepezza, Krystexxa) was debt-funded, pushing net debt/EBITDA from 2.27x (FY22) to a peak of 3.96x (FY23); it has since been brought down to 2.51x TTM, still above the FY21-22 level (source: fact sheet ratios/balance sheet) [Certain]. FCF conversion is volatile rather than steadily strong (254% FY24, 105% FY25, 116% TTM) and FCF margin has fallen from 32.3% (FY21) to 26.7% (TTM) [Certain]. Shares outstanding rose slightly (+0.46% YoY), indicating cash is being prioritised for debt paydown over buybacks [Likely]. Dividend policy: [data unavailable] on the fact sheet.

## 6. Unusual items
The FDA requested a voluntary withdrawal of TAVNEOS in January 2026 over trial re-adjudication concerns affecting 9 of 331 patients; Amgen declined and disputes the FDA's position — an unresolved regulatory risk (source: 10-K) [Likely]. Altman Z-Score of 1.89 sits in the distress grey zone, consistent with Horizon-related leverage (source: fact sheet statistics page) [Certain]. Short interest is low (2.61% of float) but rose 8.4% month-on-month (source: fact sheet) [Certain] — not yet material.
