Returns are eroding: ROIC fell from 24.8% (FY21) to 19.8% TTM and ROCE is flat near 18% (source: fact sheet ratios page) [Certain]. Gross margin slipped 75.2%->71.9% and operating margin 35.2%->33.4% as legacy biologics face biosimilar pressure [Certain]. Prolia/Xgeva core RANKL patents expired in the US (Feb 2025) and EU (Nov 2025), opening a major franchise to biosimilar erosion; Enbrel protection fades through 2028-2038 by jurisdiction (source: 10-K) [Certain]. The moat is patent- and manufacturing-complexity based, but is time-limited and now eroding on two of the largest legacy products [Likely]. Newer launches (Repatha label expansion, Tezspire, Imdelltra, Uplizna) and MariTide (six Phase 3 studies, source: 10-K) are the offset, but are unproven at the scale needed to replace Prolia/Xgeva/Enbrel revenue [Likely]. Piotroski F-Score is strong (8/9) but Altman Z (1.89) sits in the distress grey zone, consistent with debt taken on for Horizon (source: fact sheet) [Certain].
