# Zheshang Securities (SHA:601878) — Core initiation — 2026-11-09
## 1. Business
Mainland Chinese brokerage and capital-markets group (Zhejiang-based): earnings come from trading and investment gains, brokerage, financing and advisory, all tied to domestic equity-market activity. Revenue is essentially China-domestic; no single foreign country is material [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sha/601878/). Reports in CNY.

## 2. Quality
Weak, cyclical, no evident moat [Likely]. ROIC is 2.2% TTM and flat over five years (2.2%-3.2%); ROE 6.8% TTM, down from 10.2% in FY2021 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sha/601878/financials/ratios/). Operating margin rose to 40.5% TTM from 12.4% in FY2023, but this is a market-driven earnings swing rather than structural gain [Likely]. Trailing-year direction: ROIC flat, operating margin up 1.4pp, but far below its 2-year high (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sha/601878/financials/income-statement/?p=trailing). FCF margin is -29.1% TTM versus 37.1% a year earlier: brokerage cash flow is swamped by trading-book and margin-lending balances, so it is not a useful quality gauge, yet it gives no cash support [Likely]. Piotroski F is 3 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sha/601878/statistics/). Chinese brokers are price-takers with commoditised commissions [Likely].

## 3. Valuation
P/E is 14.5x, below its own 5y minimum of 20.3x, but 54% above the peer median of 9.4x across four listed Chinese brokers [Certain]. P/B is 0.8x, below its 5y minimum of 1.0x, in line with peers at 0.8x. So it is cheap against its own history but not against peers, and TTM earnings are elevated versus the FY2021-23 level, which flatters the P/E [Likely]. EV-based multiples are [data unavailable]. FCF yield is -7.16%. Mechanical fair value is 4.73 CNY base against an 8.87 CNY close, but it is flagged unreliable (methods disagree 3.0x; the P/E method gives 14.20), so weigh it lightly. The reverse DCF implies 11.2% year-1 revenue growth, demanding for a cyclical [Likely]. The price assumes a sustained high-activity market (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sha/601878/statistics/).
