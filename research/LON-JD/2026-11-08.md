# JD Sports Fashion (LON:JD) — Core initiation — 2026-11-08
## 1. Business
Multi-brand sportswear and fashion retailer selling third-party brands (Nike is a major supplier) through stores and online in the UK, Europe and North America [Likely]. North America is described as its key market (source: https://www.reuters.com/business/retail-consumer/jd-sports-first-half-profit-hit-by-north-american-weakness-2026-09-23/) [Certain]. I could not fetch a primary source for the regional revenue split, so the exact North America share is [data unavailable]; the macro overlay should treat US and GBP/USD exposure as material [Likely].

## 2. Quality
Returns are good in absolute terms but have fallen for five years. ROIC is 11.0% TTM against 23.7% in FY2022. Operating margin is 7.0% against 11.9%. Gross margin is 46.9% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/lon/JD/financials/ratios/) [Certain]. The last 12 months continue the decline: ROCE is down 2.2pp and operating margin 1.4pp. FCF margin is the exception, up 1.5pp to 8.4% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/lon/JD/financials/ratios/?p=trailing) [Certain]. It cannot be called improving on that one line.
A retailer of other companies' brands has a thin moat: suppliers can favour their own channels, and the fall in margins fits that [Likely]. Management cut full-year profit guidance in August on pricing pressure and weak US sales (source: https://www.reuters.com/business/retail-consumer/britains-jd-sports-cuts-profit-outlook-us-sales-slump-2026-08-20/) [Certain]. First-half profit then fell, but the full-year outlook was maintained (source: https://www.reuters.com/business/retail-consumer/jd-sports-first-half-profit-hit-by-north-american-weakness-2026-09-23/) [Certain]. Scale and a Piotroski score of 7 are positives. Net debt/EBITDA has risen to 2.32x [Certain].

## 3. Valuation
The price assumes earnings keep shrinking. P/E is 8.4x, below the 5y minimum of 9.3x and well under the 10.9x median. EV/EBITDA is 4.3x against a 5.7x–9.3x range. P/FCF is 4.3x and FCF yield 23.38%. Against peers (NKE, FRAS, DKS, ONON) the discount is about 50% on P/E and 60% on EV/EBITDA (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/lon/JD/statistics/) [Certain]. Peer comparison flatters JD, because ONON and NKE are brand owners with higher margins [Likely].
Fair value is [data unavailable]: the model had no GBP FX rate, so there is no margin-of-safety anchor.
The market is pricing a structurally lower-return retailer with a US problem. Cash flow of this size supports the price only if margins stabilise near the current 7.0% [Guessing]. Beta is 1.65, so the shares are volatile [Certain].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Operating margin (income statement TTM) falls below 6.0%.
- ROIC (ratios page, TTM) falls below 9%.
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) exceeds 3.0x.
- FCF margin (cash flow TTM) falls below 5%.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
FCF conversion is 189.7% of net income TTM, helped by working capital and depreciation of leased stores [Likely]. Shares outstanding are down 3.37% YoY, so buybacks are shrinking the share count [Certain]. Leverage rose from 0.87x in FY2022 to 2.32x, so earlier acquisitions and lease obligations have consumed balance-sheet room [Likely]. A new chair, Peter Agnefjäll, started on 1 September (source: https://www.reuters.com/business/retail-consumer/uks-jd-sports-appoints-peter-agnefjll-chair-2026-08-07/) [Certain]. Buyback pace against rising debt is the item to watch (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/lon/JD/financials/cash-flow-statement/).
