# Broadridge Financial Solutions, Inc. (BR) — Core initiation — 2026-10-08

## 1. Business
Broadridge runs two segments: Investor Communication Solutions (~74% of revenue) — proxy processing, regulatory communications, fund/issuer services and customer communications — and Global Technology and Operations (~26%) — capital markets, wealth management and investment-management technology platforms [Certain] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1383312/000162828026052243/br-20260630.htm). Revenue is largely transaction- and position-based, outsourced infrastructure embedded in clients' daily operations.

## 2. Quality
Broadridge's ICS unit is the dominant US outsourced proxy-distribution utility, a position reinforced by SEC e-delivery/proxy regulation; GTO sells mission-critical trade processing, clearing and settlement systems with high switching costs once embedded [Certain] (source: SEC 10-K, link above). The 10-K flags client concentration among a small number of large clients as a structural risk [Certain] (same source). ROIC rose from 10.6% (FY2022) to 17.5% (FY2026, 17.0% TTM), ROCE from 11.1% to 17.3%, and net debt/EBITDA fell from 3.14x to 1.72x [Certain] (source: stockanalysis.com ratios page). FCF conversion is 113.7% of net income (TTM), above 100%, indicating high earnings quality [Certain] (source: stockanalysis.com cash-flow/income-statement pages). Broadridge is extending GTO into tokenized markets (Distributed Ledger Repo processed $7.5tn in September 2026; new "DLX" platform) and signed an MOU with Korea Securities Depository on proxy-voting modernization — plausible but unquantified extensions of the moat [Likely] (source: https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/broadridges-distributed-ledger-repo-processes-7-5-trillion-in-september-302901441.html).

## 3. Valuation
BR trades at 16.7x TTM P/E, only the 13th percentile of its own 5-year range (14.1x–34.0x) and a 23% discount to the peer median (21.8x; FIS, SSNC, JKHY, ADP). EV/EBITDA (11.7x) is 13% below peers and EV/Sales (2.8x) 29% below peers; FCF yield is 7.07% [Certain] (source: stockanalysis.com ratios/statistics pages). P/FCF (14.1x) is modestly above the peer median (+12%) and P/B (6.4x) is well above peers (+66%), but the latter reflects a buyback-shrunk equity base and a 40.9% ROE rather than overvaluation [Likely]. The price has fallen 32.4% over 12 months versus SPY +15.7% (a -48.1pp relative gap), while every quality metric above has improved each year — the current price assumes the multiple derating continues or that growth/margins stall, which the fundamentals trend does not yet support [Likely] (source: stockanalysis.com history/statistics pages).

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROIC (ratios page) falls below 14%, the FY2023 level, signalling the return trend reversing.
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) rises back above 2.5x, signalling the deleveraging trend reversing.
- FCF conversion, FCF/net income (cash-flow and income-statement pages) falls below 100%, signalling earnings-quality deterioration.
- Gross margin (income-statement page) falls below 29%, the FY2023 level.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Shares outstanding fell 1.01% YoY even as the company pursued active M&A (Signal Agency, CQG, Acolin Group acquisitions per the FY2026 10-K) [Certain] (source: SEC 10-K, link above). A dividend was declared subsequent to fiscal year-end per the 10-K; the exact per-share amount is [data unavailable] on stockanalysis.com. FCF margin is 17.1% and FCF conversion 113.7% of net income (TTM) [Certain] (source: stockanalysis.com cash-flow/income-statement pages), comfortably funding buybacks, dividend and M&A while net debt/EBITDA continues to fall (1.72x).

## 6. Unusual items
Board change: Amit Zavery is stepping down after seven years and Srinivas Tallapragada has been nominated as director — routine governance, not thesis-relevant [Certain] (source: https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/broadridge-nominates-srinivas-tallapragada-to-its-board-of-directors-302896586.html). Client concentration among a small number of large clients is a disclosed structural risk [Certain] (source: SEC 10-K, link above). Short interest is low (2.61% of float) and fell 11.9% month-on-month; Altman Z-score 4.43 and Piotroski F-score 7 show no distress signals [Certain] (source: stockanalysis.com statistics page).
