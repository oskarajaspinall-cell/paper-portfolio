# EPAM Systems (EPAM) — Core initiation — 2026-11-09
## 1. Business
Digital engineering and IT services: EPAM bills clients for engineering talent on time-and-materials and fixed-price projects, with large delivery centres in Eastern Europe. Revenue by client location, nine months 2026: Americas 56.9%, EMEA 41.2%, APAC 1.9% [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1352010/000135201026000052/epam-20260930.htm). No single foreign country is disclosed at ~10%+ in that excerpt; Ukraine and Belarus are delivery locations, not client markets [Certain].

## 2. Quality
Returns remain good but have eroded: ROIC 45.7% (FY2021) to 16.7% TTM, operating margin 14.5% to 11.4%, gross margin 33.9% to 29.9% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/epam/financials/ratios/). Last 12 months show stabilisation: ROIC +0.9pp, ROCE +2.4pp, operating margin +0.5pp year on year; ROIC remains 11.3pp below its 2-year high, so the improvement is partial [Certain]. Net cash (net debt/EBITDA -1.06x), Altman Z 6.82, Piotroski 7 [Certain]. Moat is modest: talent-based services with AI-driven price deflation risk; the 10-Q now names AI as competition to the services sold (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1352010/000135201026000052/epam-20260930.htm) [Likely]. Q3 beat and organic growth of 2.5% are positive but low (source: https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/epam-reports-results-for-third-quarter-2026-302930064.html) [Certain].

## 3. Valuation
At 159.31 after a +20.6% earnings-day jump and +71.2% in three months, P/E is 19.7x, EV/EBITDA 9.5x, P/FCF 17.3x: all below their 5y minimums (29.2x, 14.5x, 18.5x) [Certain]. Against peers it is at a premium on P/E (+28%), P/FCF (+41%), EV/EBITDA (+9%). FCF yield 5.79%. The cheapness versus own history reflects structurally lower returns, not just mispricing [Likely]. Mechanical fair value base 168.91 (+6.0%) is flagged unreliable: DCF 86.91 versus EV/EBITDA 332.91; the reverse DCF says the price implies 22.7% year-1 revenue growth, far above delivered growth [Certain]. The stock sits 29.8% above its 50-day average with RSI 69.7; the easy rebound from 73.06 is done [Likely]. (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/epam/statistics/)

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Operating margin TTM (income statement, trailing page) falls below 10%.
- ROIC TTM (ratios, trailing page) falls below 14%.
- Reported revenue growth (income statement) turns negative year on year for two consecutive quarters.
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) rises above 0x, i.e. net cash is lost.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Buybacks are active: $488.9M repurchased in nine months 2026 [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1352010/000135201026000052/epam-20260930.htm); shares change YoY -6.08% [Certain]. Debt is minimal. FCF conversion TTM 110.6% of net income, but FCF margin TTM 8.3% is 4.0pp below its 2-year high [Certain]. A 2026-08-31 activist letter urged a larger accelerated repurchase [Likely] (source: https://www.tipranks.com/news/the-fly/engine-capital-urges-epam-systems-board-to-initiate-750m-asr-program-thefly-news).
