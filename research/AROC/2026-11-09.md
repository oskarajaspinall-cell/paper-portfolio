# Archrock (AROC) — Core initiation — 2026-11-09
## 1. Business
Archrock owns and operates natural gas compression fleets under fee-based contracts for US producers, processors and pipelines (contract operations), plus aftermarket services (parts, overhaul) for customer-owned equipment [Certain]. Contract operations is the large majority of revenue: $331,155 thousand of $370,811 thousand in Q3 2026 [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1389050/000138905026000039/aroc-20260930x10q.htm). Operations are US-only; no foreign country approaches 10% [Likely].

## 2. Quality
ROIC rose from 3.9% (FY2021) to 11.2% TTM, operating margin from 17.5% to 38.8%, net debt/EBITDA fell from 4.91x to 2.73x (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/aroc/financials/ratios/) [Certain]. The trend is still rising over the last 12 months: ROIC +1.4pp, operating margin +2.5pp. Compression is a must-run service with large installed horsepower and scarce, long-lead equipment, so pricing power and renewals are the durable edge [Likely]. Weaknesses: ROIC of 11.2% is moderate for a capital-heavy lessor; Altman Z 1.28 reflects leverage; Q3 aftermarket revenue fell and costs outpaced revenue, lowering adjusted gross margin (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1389050/000138905026000039/aroc-20260930x10q.htm) [Certain]. The edge is real but exposed to gas volumes and peer fleet additions [Guessing].

## 3. Valuation
At 27.54 USD, P/E is 14.2x vs a 5y median of 25.7x and below the 5y minimum; EV/EBITDA 8.3x is also below its 5y range (8.5x min) and 9% under peers (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/aroc/statistics/) [Certain]. FCF yield is 6.27%, P/FCF 15.9x vs 13.3x for peers; P/B 3.0x is near the top of its range. FCF margin jumped from 1.3% to 20.3% in 12 months as growth capex eased, which is why the FCF multiple looks only fair [Likely]. Fair value is flagged unreliable (DCF base 45.46 vs EV/EBITDA base 12.88); the reverse DCF implies only 2.2% year-1 revenue growth, a low bar [Likely]. The stock is 19.5% below its 200-day average, 6-month momentum -29.6%: the market may be pricing a peak in the compression cycle [Guessing].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROIC (ratios page, TTM) falls below 9%.
- Operating margin (income statement page, TTM) falls below 34%.
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) rises above 3.5x.
- Contract operations revenue (10-Q segment note) declines year on year for two consecutive quarters.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
FCF conversion of 88.6% TTM is up from 37.1% in FY2025 as capex slowed (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/aroc/financials/cash-flow-statement/) [Certain]. Nine-month capex was $329,355 thousand vs $414,667 thousand a year earlier [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1389050/000138905026000039/aroc-20260930x10q.htm). Buybacks are small, $4,422 thousand in nine months [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1389050/000138905026000039/aroc-20260930x10q.htm), and share count is flat (0.01% YoY); the dividend is the main return of cash [Likely]. The 2026 refinancing removed the near-dated notes (SEC delta) [Likely].

## 6. Unusual items
Q3 revenue missed while adjusted EPS beat, with lower aftermarket revenue and cost pressure (source: https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/11/02/3393755/0/en/archrock-reports-third-quarter-2026-results.html) [Likely]. NGCS acquisition integration risk factors were dropped from the latest 10-Q, so the deal is treated as integrated (SEC delta) [Likely]. Short interest 3.86% of float: not material.
