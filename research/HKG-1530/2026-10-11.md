# 3SBio Inc. (HKG:1530) — Core initiation — 2026-10-11

## 1. Business
3SBio is a China-based biopharmaceutical holding company that develops, manufactures and sells biopharmaceutical products in China, the US and internationally [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/1530/). Its established franchise centres on two biologics: TPIAO (chemotherapy-induced and immune thrombocytopenia) and EPIAO (anaemia from chronic kidney disease/chemotherapy) [Certain] (same source). Financials report in CNY; sales are overwhelmingly domestic, sold through China's hospital/insurance reimbursement system.

## 2. Quality
Balance-sheet quality is strong: net cash (net debt/EBITDA -1.42x TTM) [Certain], Altman Z 10.01, Piotroski F 7 [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/1530/financials/ratios/, https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/1530/statistics/). But profitability underwent an extreme step-change that is central to any quality judgement: ROIC rose from 14.0% (FY2024) to 63.0% (FY2025)/62.0% (TTM); gross margin 86.0%→92.4%/91.9%; operating margin 28.7%→59.3%/60.3%; net margin 23.0%→47.9%/46.3% [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/1530/financials/ratios/, https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/1530/financials/income-statement/), against a stable, far lower FY2021-24 pattern. No allowlisted source checked (company overview, ratios page, stockanalysis.com's HKG:1530 filing index) explains the driver [Guessing] (cause unconfirmed). Biosimilars like TPIAO/EPIAO do not structurally carry 90%+ gross margins, so this looks more like a large non-recurring or quasi-recurring item than a repeatable competitive advantage. Until the driver is confirmed, I cannot conclude competitors are durably locked out of these returns — the moat claim currently rests on an unverified number.

## 3. Valuation
TTM multiples are cheap on both an absolute and relative basis: P/E 4.0x (5y range 6.4x-10.6x, i.e. below its own 5y low; peer median 24.7x, n=3) [Certain]; EV/EBITDA 1.8x (5y low 4.8x; peer 20.9x) [Certain]; EV/Sales 1.1x (5y low 1.6x; peer 8.1x) [Certain]; P/FCF 3.6x (peer 10.8x) [Certain]; only P/B 1.1x sits inside its 5y range (0.7x-1.8x) and is still well below peers' 4.7x [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/1530/financials/ratios/, https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/1530/statistics/). FCF yield is 27.75% [Certain] (same source). Multiples far below both history and peers, while P/B is merely mid-range, is consistent with the market treating the FY2025/TTM earnings surge as low quality or non-recurring — discounting the P&L but not yet the book value. The EV/Revenue-based fair value (rNPV inputs [data unavailable]) gives base HKD 25.52 (+69% vs last close 15.07) [Certain] (source: income-statement/balance-sheet/ratios/statistics pages), but it inherits the same unexplained margin assumption and should be treated cautiously until confirmed.

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Gross margin (income-statement page) falls back below 88% without a replacement revenue source — confirms the FY2025 margin surge was non-recurring.
- ROIC (ratios page) falls back below 20% for two consecutive reporting periods — shows the step-change in returns did not persist.
- Net margin (income-statement page) compresses below 25% — removes the basis for calling current multiples statistically cheap.
- Revenue (income-statement page) declines — signals the core TPIAO/EPIAO franchise is shrinking as the elevated-margin item runs off.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Net cash position, net debt/EBITDA -1.42x TTM [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/1530/financials/balance-sheet/, ratios page). FCF conversion (FCF/NI) is 109.2% TTM, up sharply from 33.3% in FY2021 [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/1530/financials/cash-flow-statement/, income-statement page) — more cash generated than accounting profit, consistent with working-capital release or the same unexplained item behind the margin spike.
