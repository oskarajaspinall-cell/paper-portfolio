# Oil and Natural Gas Corporation (NSE:ONGC) — Core initiation — 2026-11-09
## 1. Business
India's state-owned upstream oil and gas explorer and producer, classified as Oil & Gas Integrated (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/nse/ONGC/). Profit follows realised crude and gas prices and production volumes; overseas assets sit in ONGC Videsh, and the group holds a stake in refiner HPCL (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/nse/ONGC/transcripts/680706-q1-26-27/) [Likely]. Foreign revenue share: [data unavailable]; not verified against a primary source.

## 2. Quality
Mediocre returns, solid cash generation. TTM ROIC is 8.7% and ROCE 10.1%, with ROIC falling 2.6pp over five fiscal years and 5.0pp over three (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/nse/ONGC/financials/ratios/) [Certain]. TTM operating margin of 9.5% is below the FY2026 level of 11.2%. Latest-year direction is flat on ROIC/ROCE but margins are slipping (operating -0.8pp, FCF -0.8pp vs a year ago) [Certain]. FCF conversion is 111.3% of net income and net debt/EBITDA is 1.23x, so the balance sheet is sound. The moat is state-backed access to Indian acreage and domestic gas, not a durable cost edge. Returns are commodity-price dependent, and pricing and policy are set partly by the government [Likely]. Quality is average; the case rests on price.

## 3. Valuation
The price is 234.80 INR (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/nse/ONGC/history/). P/E is 5.9x against a 5y median of 6.9x (34th percentile of range) and a peer median of 8.5x (-30%). EV/EBITDA of 3.8x is below its 5y minimum of 4.2x; P/FCF of 5.3x is also at the bottom of its range; P/B is 0.7x versus peers' 1.3x. FCF yield is 18.88% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/nse/ONGC/statistics/) [Certain]. The reverse DCF implies year-1 revenue shrinking 17.5%, a very pessimistic assumption. The mechanical fair value (base 704.94 INR) is flagged unreliable (+200%); weigh it lightly. Cheap on every multiple, but cyclical earnings near a decent level mean multiples flatter; the discount to peers partly reflects government pricing and capital-allocation risk [Likely]. Shares are -8.0% vs the 200-day average; 6-month momentum is -16.4% [Certain].

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Cash conversion is strong: FCF is 8.0% of revenue on a TTM basis and 111.3% of net income. Share count is flat (+0.03% YoY), so no dilution (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/nse/ONGC/statistics/) [Certain]. Leverage is moderate and falling. The risk is deployment: overseas ventures (Venezuela operatorship talks, which depend on US and Venezuelan authorities) and state-directed investments such as HPCL could absorb cash at low returns [Likely] (source: https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/indias-ongc-hopes-get-rights-operate-2-venezuelan-oil-projects-six-months-2026-11-06/). Treat the Venezuela outcome as unconfirmed [Guessing].
