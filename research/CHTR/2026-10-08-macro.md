# Macro overlay: Charter Communications, Inc. (CHTR) — 2026-10-08
## Assessment
CHTR carries 4.41x net debt/EBITDA and an Altman Z of 0.67 — distress zone [Certain] (ratios/statistics pages, research/CHTR/2026-10-08.md). For a balance-sheet-heavy, low-growth leveraged equity, the relevant chain is rates/credit → cost of refinancing and equity discount rate → multiple, not an earnings-growth channel **(inference)**.

Official data (FRED, 2026-10-06): 10y real yield (DFII10) 2.91%, +0.48pp/1m, +0.61pp/3m; 10y nominal (DGS10) 5.27%, +0.49/+0.72; 2y (DGS2) 4.79%, +0.42/+0.60; HY credit spread (BAMLH0A0HYM2) 3.03%, +0.35/+0.36. Market pricing: the rise in both real yields and the 2y suggests markets have pushed out the Fed's easing path versus 1-3 months ago — a proxy, not certainty [Likely]. Facts are the levels/changes; the "hawkish repricing" read is inference.

This mostly compounds, rather than drives, CHTR's thesis: the stock is down ~62% over 12 months on Cox-integration execution, subscriber/competitive pressure (fiber, fixed wireless) and a CFO transition — all company-specific [Certain] (factsheet, note). But higher real yields and a widening HY spread raise the discount rate and risk premium applied to a 4.4x-levered, Altman-Z-distressed credit, independent of operating results **(inference)**.

**Bull**: real yields/HY spreads roll over (growth slows without credit stress) → refinancing costs ease, risk premium on leveraged credits compresses → modest multiple relief even with flat fundamentals.
**Bear**: real yields and HY spreads keep rising (sticky inflation, hawkish Fed) → higher cost of capital for an already-distressed balance sheet → further multiple compression, compounding competitive/integration risk.
**Tilt**: toward bear, moderate — leverage amplifies rate/spread sensitivity more than for peers.
