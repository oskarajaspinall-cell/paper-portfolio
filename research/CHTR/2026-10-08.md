# Charter Communications, Inc. (CHTR) — Core initiation — 2026-10-08
## 1. Business
Charter, operating as Spectrum, is the second-largest US cable operator, selling residential/business broadband, video, mobile and voice bundled over its hybrid fiber-coaxial network [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/chtr/transcripts/743320-goldman-sachs-communacopia-technology-conference-2026/). It closed its acquisition of Cox Communications in August 2026, expanding to ~70 million passings across 45 states [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/chtr/transcripts/743320-goldman-sachs-communacopia-technology-conference-2026/; corroborated by an Item 2.01 8-K filed 2026-08-20, source: https://www.sec.gov/cgi-bin/browse-edgar?action=getcompany&CIK=CHTR&type=8-K&dateb=&owner=include&count=10), competing on broadband/mobile convergence against fiber overbuilders, fixed wireless and satellite.

## 2. Quality
TTM ROIC (8.5%) and ROCE (9.2%) have been flat for five years (ratios page), only modestly above cost of capital given 4.41x net debt/EBITDA (balance sheet, ratios page); leverage isn't clearly value-accretive [Certain]. Gross (55.2%) and operating margins (24.0%) are rising (income statement), but FCF margin fell from 16.6% to 8.0% and FCF conversion from 185% to 88.5% over five years (cash flow statement) as capex absorbed cash for rural build-out and network upgrades [Certain]. ROE of 27.2% is flattered by an 11.0% YoY share-count cut (statistics page), not operating improvement [Likely]. Altman Z-score is 0.67 (statistics page) — distress zone — though Piotroski F-score of 6 suggests no near-term deterioration [Certain]. The moat is last-mile HFC infrastructure, eroded at both ends: fiber at the high end, fixed wireless/satellite at the value end [Likely] (Goldman Sachs Communacopia transcript); returns are unlikely to re-rate without subscriber stabilization.

## 3. Valuation
At 106.96 USD, CHTR trades on 2.8x P/E, 5.2x EV/EBITDA, 4.0x P/FCF and 0.8x P/B — all below its own 5-year range (source: ratios page) and below peer (CMCSA, ATUS, CABO, TMUS) medians except P/FCF, which is 10% above the 3.6x peer median (source: ratios page, peer statistics pages) [Certain]. FCF yield is 24.91% (source: statistics page) [Certain]. The price assumes continued secular subscriber erosion from fiber/fixed-wireless competition and no re-rating of the leveraged balance sheet, consistent with the stock's -62.2% 12-month return and -77.9pp underperformance vs SPY (source: statistics page, history pages) [Likely]. The discount looks largest on raw cash generation (P/FCF cheaper than peers) but is unexceptional on EV/EBITDA and EV/Sales (-21%/-19% vs peers), which better reflect leverage [Likely]. This reads as a distressed-value setup, not an obvious mispricing: the market is pricing real balance-sheet and competitive risk, not just sentiment.

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) rises above 5.0x, signalling the Cox integration is not delivering the guided >$1bn of synergies.
- FCF margin (cash flow statement + income statement) stays below 5% for two consecutive fiscal years, confirming capex intensity is structural rather than transitional.
- Altman Z-score (statistics page) falls further, e.g. below 0.3, indicating balance-sheet distress is accelerating rather than stabilizing.
- ROIC (ratios page) falls below 7%, confirming the last-mile moat is being competed away by fiber/fixed wireless.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Charter has run aggressive buybacks, cutting shares outstanding 11.0% YoY (source: statistics page), which materially boosts ROE and EPS/share even though operating returns are flat [Certain]. Management guides capex down from the mid-$11bn range to under $8bn as the subsidized rural build (ending 2026) and network-evolution upgrade (largely complete by end-2027) conclude [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/chtr/transcripts/743320-goldman-sachs-communacopia-technology-conference-2026/), which should lift FCF margin back toward its historical mid-teens level if realized. FCF conversion (FCF/NI) is 88.5% TTM (source: cash flow statement, income statement), below its 2021 peak of 184.9% but recovered from the 2024 trough of 62.2% [Certain].

## 6. Unusual items
CFO Jessica Fischer is stepping down October 15, 2026, with Kevin Howard named interim CFO, disclosed mid-integration of the Cox acquisition [Certain] (source: https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/charter-announces-chief-financial-officer-transition-302865300.html). Short interest is elevated at 13.93% of float and rose 2.8% in the past month (source: statistics page) [Certain]; 14-day RSI of 27.7 shows the stock is deeply oversold (source: statistics page) [Certain] — not a fundamentals item, but relevant to sizing/timing given extreme recent volatility (-62.2% 12-month return).
