# Macro overlay: PulteGroup, Inc. (PHM) — 2026-10-11
## Assessment
Homebuilders are a textbook primary macro case: 10y Treasury yield -> mortgage rates -> housing affordability/demand (orders, ASP, incentives) -> PHM revenue and gross margin; the same yield (+ beta) -> PHM's DCF discount rate -> fair value multiple [Certain] **(inference: standard transmission mechanism)**.

**Facts** (fred.stlouisfed.org, 2026-10-08): the 10y Treasury yield is 5.22%, up +42bp over 1m and +68bp over 3m; the 10y real yield (DFII10) is 2.87%, up +44bp/+56bp; effective fed funds is 3.88%, up +25bp/+26bp — the Fed is still tightening, not cutting [Certain]. High-yield credit spreads widened to 3.15%, +48bp/1m, a mild risk-off move [Certain].

**Market pricing**: the 2y yield (4.75%) sits ~87bp above effective fed funds (3.88%) — a proxy that the market expects further hikes, not cuts, over the policy horizon [Likely] **(inference)**. Sell-side commentary (Barrons, 2026-10-08; interpretation, not data) frames housing stocks at "maximum pessimism," contingent on mortgage rates falling — not yet met [Guessing].

PHM already prices much bad news (-12.2% 12m, -27.8pp vs SPY, below both moving averages, RSI 36), but rates rose, not eased, this past quarter — the opposite of what a re-rating needs.

**Bull**: a rate pause/cut eases mortgage rates -> orders/margin stabilize -> multiple re-rates up from a depressed base.
**Bear**: 10y sustains above ~5.5% -> incentives rise, gross margin breaches the 24% invalidation level, discount rate rises -> fair value falls further below the $89 base.
**Tilt**: toward bear, moderate — rates and credit spreads keep moving against a stock whose depressed price assumes relief that hasn't arrived [Likely].
