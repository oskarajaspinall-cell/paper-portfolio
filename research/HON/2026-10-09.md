# Honeywell International Inc. (HON) — Core initiation — 2026-10-09

## 1. Business
Honeywell Technologies (rebranded from Honeywell International) sells automation hardware/software, process-technology licensing (UOP), building/industrial automation and defense/aerospace systems, monetised via equipment sales and recurring aftermarket/service revenue [Certain] (source: stockanalysis.com overview). In 2026 it is separating into standalone units: it sold Productivity Solutions and Services to Brady Corporation and completed a major disposition on 2026-06-25 (SEC Item 2.01), consistent with the announced Solstice Advanced Materials spin-off; an Aerospace separation appears still in progress [Likely] (source: honeywell.com/us/en/news; sec.gov 8-K 000077384026000084).

## 2. Quality
Returns remain high but have fallen steadily for five years: ROIC 21.4%→13.5% (TTM), ROCE 16.6%→13.2% (TTM) [Certain] (source: ratios page). Operating margin slipped from 21.7% (FY2021) to 18.7% (TTM) [Certain] (source: income statement). These levels still imply pricing power from an installed base (process licensing, aftermarket service, building-automation retrofits), but the trend shows returns eroding, not a stable moat. TTM net margin (21.6%) and ROE (46.6%) are far above the FY2021-25 range (12.6-17.1%, 26.1-30.3%) — almost certainly inflated by a one-off gain tied to the mid-2026 disposition rather than organic improvement [Likely] (source: SEC 8-K Item 2.01, filed 2026-06-29; ratios page); these TTM figures should not be read as a step-change in quality. Net debt/EBITDA has risen every year, from 1.05x (FY2021) to 3.03x (TTM) [Certain] (source: ratios page), a leverage trend to watch through the separation.

## 3. Valuation
On fact-sheet multiples HON looks exceptionally cheap: P/E 8.0x vs. its own 5y range 24.4x-29.0x and 33.6x peer median (GE, RTX, MMM, EMR); EV/EBITDA 10.7x vs. 17.7x-20.1x historically and 17.1x peer median [Certain] (source: ratios/statistics pages). This gap is too large to take at face value: TTM net income (and likely TTM EBITDA) carries the one-off disposition gain, and the FY2025/TTM period mixes pre- and post-separation results, so these TTM multiples are not like-for-like this year [Likely]. The fact sheet's DCF-based fair value is more moderate: base 199.75 (-3.3% vs. last close 206.60), bear 164.78 (-20.2%), bull 284.73 (+37.8%) [Certain] (source: fact sheet fair value). The DCF is the more reliable read: price looks close to fair value, not a bargain.

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROIC (ratios page) falls below 10% (TTM), extending the five-year decline past a level consistent with a quality industrial franchise.
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) rises above 3.5x for two consecutive quarters once post-separation balance sheets settle, showing the leverage trend is structural.
- Operating margin (income statement) falls below 17% sustained, showing core automation-segment margins are eroding, not stabilising.
- FCF margin (cash flow/income statement) stays below 12% for two consecutive quarters once separation costs should have rolled off.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
The board maintained its quarterly dividend at $0.70/share, announced 2026-09-25 [Certain] (source: https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260925740546/en/Honeywell-Technologies-Announces-Quarterly-Dividend/). Shares outstanding fell 1.87% year-on-year, consistent with continued buybacks [Certain] (source: statistics page). FCF margin was 14.5% in FY2025 but only 10.5% TTM [Certain] (source: cash flow/income statement) — likely depressed by separation-related cash costs rather than core deterioration [Likely]. Net debt/EBITDA has more than doubled over five years to 3.03x TTM [Certain] (source: ratios page), worth monitoring as the Aerospace separation, if it proceeds, resets the balance sheet and earnings base.

## 6. Unusual items
A material corporate separation is underway: a disposition completed 2026-06-25 (SEC Item 2.01) [Certain] (source: sec.gov), the Productivity Solutions and Services business sold to Brady Corporation [Likely] (source: honeywell.com/us/en/news), and comments from Honeywell's own Aerospace CEO on a rival's M&A suggest the Aerospace unit had not yet been separated as of mid-September 2026 [Guessing] (source: https://www.reuters.com/legal/transactional/honeywell-aero-ceo-calls-planned-ge-aerospace-cpp-deal-positive-industry-2026-09-15/). Short interest rose 15.0% month-on-month to 2.49% of float — still low, not a crowding signal [Certain] (source: statistics page). TTM profitability and leverage ratios are less comparable than usual during this separation and should be re-tested once post-separation financials settle.
