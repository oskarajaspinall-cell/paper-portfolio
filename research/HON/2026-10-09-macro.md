# Macro overlay: Honeywell International Inc. (HON) — 2026-10-09
## Assessment
**Facts (FRED, 2026-10-07/08):** 10y real yield (DFII10) 2.92%, +61bp/3m, +49bp/1m; 10y nominal yield (DGS10) 5.28%, +72bp/3m; effective fed funds (DFF) 3.88%, +25bp over both 1m and 3m (a hike, not a cut); 10y breakeven (T10YIE) 2.35%, roughly flat (-2bp/1m); HY credit spread (BAMLH0A0HYM2) 3.09%, +39bp/3m but still historically tight; dollar index (DTWEXBGS) 121.4, +2.9pts/1m [Certain].

**Market pricing (proxy, inference):** 2y yield (4.77%) sits ~89bp above fed funds (3.88%), a proxy that the market expects further tightening rather than cuts [Likely] **(inference)**. Flat breakevens alongside the real/nominal yield jump point to a rising term premium, not an inflation-expectations shift **(inference)**.

**Link to HON:** HON's own DCF fair value uses the 10y Treasury as its risk-free rate (5.28%), and net debt/EBITDA is at a five-year high (3.03x TTM vs 1.05x FY2021) mid-way through an active corporate separation needing financing [Certain]. Rising real yields raise HON's discount rate; widening HY spreads raise its refinancing cost on that leverage [Likely].

**Bull:** real yields/spreads ease (DFII10 toward ~2.5%) → discount rate falls, fair value drifts toward bull case (284.73, +38%).
**Bear:** real yields/spreads keep widening → discount rate and refinancing cost both rise on near-5y-high leverage → fair value compresses toward/through bear case (164.78, -20%), pressuring the already-weak 10.5% TTM FCF margin.
**Tilt:** moderate toward bear — a large, still-running 3m real-yield rise lands on a balance sheet at 5-year-high leverage mid-separation.
