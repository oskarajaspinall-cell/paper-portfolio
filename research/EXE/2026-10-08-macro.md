# Macro overlay: Expand Energy Corporation (EXE) — 2026-10-08
## Assessment
EXE's thesis is fundamental (peer-discount valuation, post-Twin Eagle cash conversion), but the company is actively in debt markets ($500m notes priced 2026-09-15; net debt/EBITDA swung from 0.28x to 4.42x in FY2024, per the research note) [Certain], so the rates/credit chain is live: **real yields & HY credit spreads → cost of debt and buyback funding → leverage trajectory and equity risk premium → multiple**.

Facts (FRED, 2026-10-06/07): the 10y real yield (DFII10) is 2.91%, +48bp/~1m and +61bp/~3m; the 10y nominal yield (DGS10) is 5.27%, +72bp/~3m; HY credit spreads (BAMLH0A0HYM2) are 3.03%, +36bp/~3m; effective fed funds (DFF) rose 25bp both windows to 3.88%. Market pricing (proxy, **(inference)**): DGS2 (4.79%) above DFF (3.88%) implies the market still prices further tightening, consistent with breakeven inflation (T10YIE) drifting up slightly (+11bp/3m) to 2.36% — a mild inflation-surprise, hawkish-Fed backdrop. This is the same ~3-month window EXE underperformed SPY by 29.9pp (fact sheet), so much of the rate/credit deterioration already looks priced into EXE's multiple (EV/EBITDA at the 4th percentile of its own 5y range) [Likely] **(inference)**.

**Bull**: real yields/spreads stabilize or ease → cheaper refinancing, buyback funding intact → modest multiple recovery toward peers. **Bear**: real yields and HY spreads keep rising → refinancing cost up, buyback pace cut, leverage trigger (2.0x) risk rises → further multiple compression. **Tilt**: small, toward bear — EXE's low current leverage (0.46x TTM) and the modest HY-spread move limit the impact next to the dominant gas-price driver, outside this FRED-based overlay.
