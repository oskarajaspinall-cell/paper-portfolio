# Expand Energy Corporation (EXE) — Core initiation — 2026-10-08

## 1. Business
Natural gas E&P across the Haynesville and Northeast/Southwest Appalachia basins (source: https://www.expandenergy.com). [Certain] Since the September 2026 Twin Eagle acquisition it is also vertically integrating into gas marketing, transport and storage, aiming to be "North America's Leading Integrated Natural Gas Company" (source: https://www.expandenergy.com). [Certain] Revenue is produced-gas sales plus, increasingly, marketing margin.

## 2. Quality
This is a commodity producer, not a business with pricing power: returns swing with gas prices (ROIC -2.8% in FY2024, 36.6% in FY2022; ratios page) [Certain]. TTM figures look healthier (ROIC 13.3%, ROCE 15.1%, FCF margin 19.6%, FCF conversion 89.1%, net debt/EBITDA 0.46x; ratios/cash-flow pages) [Certain], but the 5y trend in ROIC is falling (-6.3pp) [Certain]. The durable edge, if any, is scale and low-cost acreage from the Chesapeake-Southwestern merger plus Twin Eagle, which management says raises its marketing/commercial FCF target to $750m from $500m and improves breakeven by $0.15-0.25/unit (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/exe/transcripts/656934-q2-2026/) [Likely]. Management's own mid-cycle gas price assumption of $3.50-4.00/MMBtu to "balance the market" (same source) [Likely] implies the business needs a specific commodity level to earn its cost of capital — competitors with similar low-cost Haynesville/Appalachia acreage face the same constraint, so there is no moat against price, only a relative cost-position edge.

## 3. Valuation
EXE trades at a wide discount to the peer group (EQT, AR, RRC, CTRA, CNX) on every multiple: P/E 7.6x vs 11.0x peer median, EV/EBITDA 3.5x vs 5.8x, EV/Sales 1.9x vs 3.5x, P/FCF 8.2x vs 12.5x, P/B 1.1x vs 1.3x (valuation table) [Certain]. It also sits near the bottom of its own 5-year range (EV/EBITDA 4th percentile, P/FCF 0th percentile) [Certain]. FCF yield is 12.1% (statistics page) [Certain]. The shares are down 18.8% over 12 months and have underperformed SPY by 34.5pp over that period, trading below both the 50- and 200-day moving averages (price table) [Certain]. The price assumes the market distrusts the TTM profitability recovery and/or discounts further dilution and gas-price downside; the discount to peers is large enough that even a partial re-rating toward peer multiples would be a meaningful re-rate, but the setup also reflects genuine commodity and leverage risk, not just sentiment.

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) rises and holds above 2.0x for two consecutive quarters
- FCF margin (cash-flow/income-statement pages) turns negative for two consecutive quarters
- ROCE (ratios page) falls below 8% (approx. cost of capital) for a full fiscal year
- Operating margin (income statement) falls back below 15%, undoing the FY2024→FY2025 recovery

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Management's stated priority order is reinvestment, then dividends, then balance sheet, then buybacks (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/exe/transcripts/656934-q2-2026/) [Likely]. In Q1 2026 the company paid down $1.3bn of debt and repurchased $850m (about 4%) of shares, with a further $1bn buyback authorization added (same source) [Likely]. Despite this, shares outstanding are up 13.24% year-on-year (statistics page) [Certain], consistent with merger- and acquisition-related issuance outpacing buybacks — a genuine offset to the per-share buyback benefit. FCF conversion has swung from -1.1% (FY2024) to 89.1% (TTM) (ratios/cash-flow pages) [Certain], and net debt/EBITDA is low at 0.46x TTM versus a FY2024 spike to 4.42x (ratios page) [Certain].

## 6. Unusual items
EXE priced a $500m senior notes offering on September 15, 2026 (fact sheet news item, source: https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/09/15/3362641/0/en/expand-energy-corporation-announces-pricing-of-senior-notes-offering.html) [Certain] — new leverage layered on top of the Twin Eagle acquisition that should be watched against the net debt/EBITDA trigger above. Short interest is a modest 4.15% of float but rose 13.8% versus the prior month (fact sheet) [Certain]. Shares outstanding up 13.24% YoY, as noted above [Certain].
