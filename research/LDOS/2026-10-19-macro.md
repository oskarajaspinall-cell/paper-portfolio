# Macro overlay: Leidos Holdings (LDOS) — 2026-10-19
## Assessment
LDOS is a leveraged (net debt/EBITDA 2.45x TTM, factsheet) US government IT contractor that just raised $1.4bn of new notes for the Entrust/Analogic deals, so rates and credit feed both its DCF discount rate and interest expense — secondary on top of a fundamentals-driven valuation gap.

**Facts (FRED, 2026-10-15 unless noted)**: 10y Treasury yield 5.10%, +53bp/3m (…id=DGS10); 10y real (TIPS) yield 2.78%, +43bp/3m (…id=DFII10); US HY credit spread 3.23%, +52bp/3m (…id=BAMLH0A0HYM2); effective fed funds 3.88%, +25bp over 1m and 3m (…id=DFF); core CPI index +2.83pts/3m, ≈3.4% annualised (inference) (…id=CPILFESL).

**Market pricing (proxy, inference)**: 2y Treasury (4.77%) ~89bp above effective fed funds (3.88%) implies the market prices a higher/slower policy path, not near-term cuts.

**Inference**: rising nominal/real yields, a still-climbing fed-funds rate, and widening HY spreads raise LDOS's DCF discount rate (already using the current 5.10% risk-free rate) and new-debt cost — a headwind against closing the 124% gap to base fair value, via financing cost and multiple compression, not revenue [Likely].

**Bull**: yields/spreads roll over → lower discount rate, cheaper refinancing, faster re-rating toward fair value.
**Bear**: yields/spreads keep rising → discount rate stays elevated, interest expense rises further, multiple compression persists despite strong ROIC/margins.
**Tilt**: moderate toward bear — rates and spreads moved against the stock over 1-3 months just as leverage rose for M&A, though the valuation gap itself is fundamentals-driven, not macro-driven [Likely] **(inference)**.
