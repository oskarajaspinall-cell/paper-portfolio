# Leidos Holdings (LDOS) — Core initiation — 2026-11-16
## 1. Business
Leidos sells IT, cyber, intelligence, defense and health-systems services, mainly under multi-year U.S. government contracts. About 83% of Q3 revenue came from the U.S. government [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1336920/000133692026000293/ldos-20261002.htm) [Certain]. It reports four segments from fiscal 2026: Intelligence & Digital, Health, Homeland and Defense. The Entrust acquisition closed in the first half of 2026, and a majority of the SES business was sold on 5 October 2026 (same URL) [Certain]. No single foreign country is disclosed at ~10% in the material read [Likely].

## 2. Quality
Returns have improved sharply over five years: ROIC 9.9% (FY2021) to 15.2% TTM, operating margin 8.5% to 11.8%, FCF margin 6.8% to 11.0% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/ldos/financials/ratios/) [Certain]. The moat is incumbency: security clearances, recompete know-how and long contracts, which give predictable revenue but not pricing power [Likely].

Direction change: ROIC (16.6% to 15.2%) and ROCE (19.3% to 17.1%) fell over the last 12 months and sit below their January 2026 peaks (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/ldos/financials/ratios/?p=trailing). Operating margin is flat and FCF margin is rising, so the fall looks like the larger post-Entrust capital base and higher debt rather than weaker operations [Likely]. Management says Q3 operating income rose on program wins and mix, offset by completed higher-margin contracts (10-Q URL above) [Certain]. Net debt/EBITDA is 2.46x TTM against 1.77x at FY2025, a deliberate rise to fund the acquisition [Likely].

## 3. Valuation
The price assumes a shrinking business. The stock trades below its own five-year minimum on P/E (11.7x), EV/EBITDA (9.1x) and P/FCF (8.2x), with a 12.27% FCF yield (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/ldos/statistics/) [Certain]. It is 25% below the peer median on P/E and 24% below on EV/EBITDA (peers BAH, CACI, SAIC, KBR) [Certain]. P/B (3.1x) is mid-range, which fits unusually high ROE [Likely].

The mechanical base fair value is 259.21 against a close of 130.53, and the reverse DCF implies -11.0% year-1 revenue growth [Certain]. That DCF-led figure is probably too high, since EV/EBITDA gives 178.85 [Likely]. The 12-month change is -32.0%, and the price is 5.5% below its 200-day average (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/ldos/history/) [Certain]. I read the derating as fear about 2027 earnings (VBA uncertainty) and government funding, not a business collapse [Guessing].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Operating margin TTM (income statement, trailing page) falls below 10.5%.
- FCF margin TTM (cash flow statement, trailing page) falls below 8%.
- ROIC TTM (ratios, trailing page) falls below 12%.
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) rises above 3.0x without a matching acquisition.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
FCF conversion is 138.1% of net income TTM, and FCF margin rose 3.0pp in a year [Certain]. The share count is down 3.42% YoY (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/ldos/statistics/) [Certain]. Debt rose to $6.0bn from $4.6bn at the start of the year after $600m and $800m of notes issued to fund Entrust [company-reported] (10-Q URL above) [Certain]. The buyback is real, but leverage is higher than in FY2025 [Likely].

## 6. Unusual items
- Federal funding runs only to 11 December 2026 under a continuing resolution, so a shutdown is possible [company-reported] (10-Q URL above) [Certain].
- Q3 EPS beat and full-year EPS guidance was raised, while revenue guidance was cut after the SES divestiture (source: https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/leidos-delivers-strong-third-quarter-and-enhances-full-year-guidance-302926904.html) [Certain]. The call headline flags VBA uncertainty weighing on 2027 earnings growth (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/ldos/transcripts/723688-q3-2026/) [Likely].
- The 10-Q risk factors are unchanged, and the new items are the Entrust and SES transactions [Certain].
- Short interest is 3.23% of float and beta is 0.55 [Certain].
