# Leidos Holdings (LDOS) — Core initiation — 2026-11-23
## 1. Business
US government IT, intelligence, defense, homeland-security and health services contractor, run in four segments since fiscal 2026 (Intelligence & Digital, Health, Homeland, Defense). Revenue is mostly multi-year cost-plus and fixed-price contracts. About 84% of nine-month revenue came from the US government [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1336920/000133692026000293/ldos-20261002.htm). No foreign country is ~10% of revenue disclosed in the filing delta. [Certain]

## 2. Quality
Returns are high and structurally better than FY2021-23: ROIC TTM 15.2% vs 9.9% in FY2021; operating margin 11.8% vs 8.5%; FCF margin 11.0% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/ldos/financials/ratios/). [Certain]
Direction change: ROIC and ROCE are rising over five years but down over 12 months (ROIC 16.6% to 15.2%, 2.3pp below its 2-year high), while operating margin is flat and FCF margin rose 3.0pp (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/ldos/financials/ratios/?p=trailing). The dip is consistent with acquisition capital (Entrust) entering the base before its profit contributes [Likely]; margins held, so it is not an operating deterioration [Likely].
Moat: incumbency, security clearances and recompete switching costs on classified/mission programs, not pricing power; peers (CACI, BAH, SAIC) share it [Likely]. Beta 0.55 (statistics page). [Certain]

## 3. Valuation
The price assumes a shrinking business. P/E 10.9x, EV/EBITDA 8.6x and P/FCF 7.6x all sit below their 5-year minimums (15.7x, 11.2x, 13.4x) and 24-31% under peers; FCF yield 13.14% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/ldos/statistics/). Reverse DCF implies -12.5% year-1 revenue growth. [Certain]
The mechanical fair value (base 265.01 vs close 122.21) is flagged unreliable (>+100%); the EV/EBITDA method alone gives 178.85 base, bear 166.47, and even the bear DCF (131.30) is above the price. Weigh lightly. [Certain]
Stock is 35.4% off a year ago, 10.7% below its 200-day, so the trend is not supportive. The market is pricing government-spending/contract-recompete risk; management says a contract-vehicle uncertainty weighs on 2027 earnings growth (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/ldos/transcripts/723688-q3-2026/). [Likely]

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Operating margin TTM (income statement ?p=trailing page) falls below 10.5%.
- ROIC TTM (ratios ?p=trailing page) falls below 12% with no recovery in the next two quarters.
- FCF margin TTM (cash-flow ?p=trailing page) falls below 8%.
- Net debt / EBITDA (ratios page) rises above 3.0x.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Cash conversion is strong: FCF/NI 138.1% TTM, though the 5-year trend is falling from an FY2023 outlier [Certain]. Shares are down 3.42% YoY (statistics page), so buybacks continue. Debt rose to $6.0bn from $4.6bn at fiscal year-end to fund the Entrust acquisition [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1336920/000133692026000293/ldos-20261002.htm). Leverage ratio 2.46x vs 1.77x at FY2025 [Certain]. The SES business sale closed 5 October with a retained minority stake in the venture [company-reported] (same filing); Q4 revenue will be lower as a result. [Certain]

## 6. Unusual items
- Federal funding runs only through 11 December 2026 under a continuing resolution; a lapse means a shutdown [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1336920/000133692026000293/ldos-20261002.htm). [Certain]
- Q3 EPS beat and EPS guidance raised, with revenue guidance trimmed (source: https://www.tipranks.com/news/the-fly/leidos-ups-fy26-eps-view-to-12-50-12-70-from-12-20-12-50-consensus-12-41-thefly-news). [Likely]
- Risk Factors: no substantive change in the 10-Q delta. Short interest 3.23% of float; institutions own 82.8%. [Certain]
- The low tax rate in the quarter came from research credits and lower unrecognized tax benefits, so part of EPS growth is non-recurring [company-reported] (same filing). [Likely]
