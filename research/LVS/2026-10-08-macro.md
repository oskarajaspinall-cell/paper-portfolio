## Assessment
LVS's bear case already prices a China-consumption downturn (stock -31.5% over 12m, RSI 19); the only FRED-visible variable with a credible, mechanical link to Macao revenue is USD/CNY, via mainland outbound travel and gaming budgets (dollar/CNY move -> wealth effect -> Macao mass/VIP volumes) **(inference)**. US 10y real yield (DFII10) rose to 2.91% (+0.61pp 3m) and the 2y yield (4.79%) sits ~0.9pp above fed funds (3.88%) — a proxy for the market pricing "higher for longer" rather than near-term cuts [Likely] **(inference)**. Market pricing: these FX/rate moves are modest, and on CNY mildly favourable, which is not consistent with a 31% equity drawdown — price action is reading Macao-specific demand/VIP/renovation and China-domestic factors (outside FRED) more than currency or rate shifts [Likely] **(inference)**. Bull: CNY stabilizes/strengthens and the recent broad-dollar rally fades, restoring mainland travel budgets, supporting re-rating toward peer multiples. Bear: the broad dollar extends its ~2.4% monthly rally, CNY resumes depreciation, and real yields keep climbing, pressuring both Macao volumes and the equity risk premium, keeping LVS's discount intact even as fundamentals recover.
