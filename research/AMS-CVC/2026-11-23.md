# CVC Capital Partners plc (AMS:CVC) — Core initiation — 2026-11-23
## 1. Business
Amsterdam-listed private-markets manager. It earns recurring management fees on fee-paying assets across private equity, credit, secondaries and infrastructure, plus carried interest when funds realise investments [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/ams/CVC/transcripts/557778-h1-2026/). Revenue by country or region: [data unavailable] on the fact sheet; no single foreign country is confirmed at ~10%+ from a primary source.

## 2. Quality
Good business, with the usual alternative-asset caveat that carry is lumpy. TTM ROIC is 30.4% and operating margin 51.2% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/ams/CVC/financials/ratios/). Net debt/EBITDA is 1.15x, down from 2.73x in FY2023 [Certain].
The moat is fund franchise and LP relationships, which are sticky because capital is locked up for years and re-ups follow track record [Likely].
Direction check: ROIC is 5.4pp below its 2-year high but flat over the last year (-0.7pp). Operating margin (-5.9pp vs its high) and FCF margin (-7.7pp) are both below their peaks yet rose over the last 12 months (+2.5pp each) (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/ams/CVC/financials/income-statement/?p=trailing). I read this as normalisation from a strong 2024 on carry timing, not a structural decline [Likely]. ROCE is falling over five years but rising over 12 months [Certain].
FCF conversion (73.4% of net income TTM) is well below FY2024 [Certain].

## 3. Valuation
At 13.37 EUR the stock trades at 17.9x P/E, 6% of the way up its 5-year range and 34% below the peer median (BX, KKR, APO, ARES) (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/ams/CVC/statistics/). EV/EBITDA (13.4x) and P/FCF (19.8x) are below their 5-year minimums; FCF yield is 5.04% [Certain]. P/B (8.9x) is 26% above peers, reflecting higher ROE [Likely].
Mechanical fair value is 24.54 base (+83.5%), 6.20 bear, 51.45 bull: a very wide range from an AUM/fee DCF [Guessing]. The reverse DCF implies 5.2% year-1 revenue growth, fading to terminal growth, which is undemanding [Likely]. Price is near its 50- and 200-day averages, so the case rests on value, not trend.

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROIC (ratios page, TTM) falls below 20%.
- Operating margin (income statement page, TTM) falls below 42%.
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) rises above 2.0x.
- Shares change YoY (statistics page) exceeds 5%, showing dilution.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Leverage is falling, but share count is up 1.16% YoY, so buybacks are not yet shrinking the base net (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/ams/CVC/statistics/) [Certain]. The H1 call pointed to a dividend and a buyback programme (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/ams/CVC/transcripts/557778-h1-2026/) [Likely]. FCF margin is 35.3% TTM; conversion at 73.4% is lower than in 2023-24 and depends on realisations [Certain].
