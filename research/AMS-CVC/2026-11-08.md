# CVC Capital Partners plc (AMS:CVC) — Core initiation — 2026-11-08
## 1. Business
Listed private-equity and credit manager (Amsterdam). It earns recurring management fees on fund capital, plus performance fees and carry when it sells portfolio companies; it also deploys capital into buyouts, credit, secondaries and private wealth products [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/ams/CVC/). Revenue by country/region: [data unavailable]; the company site was not on the allowlist, so no regional share is stated.

## 2. Quality
- Returns are high: TTM ROIC 27.9%, ROCE 20.6%, operating margin 51.2%, FCF margin 35.3% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/ams/CVC/financials/ratios/) [Certain].
- Direction: ROCE is falling over five fiscal years (-6.0pp) but rising over the last 12 months (18.9% to 20.6%). Operating margin is 5.9pp below its 2-year high of 57.1%, and FCF margin 7.7pp below its high of 43.0% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/ams/CVC/financials/ratios/?p=trailing). Margins are lumpy because performance fees depend on exit timing [Likely].
- Cash conversion is weaker than in FY2024: FCF/NI 73.4% TTM vs 298.4% in FY2024. FY2025 net margin of 63.9% looks inflated by non-operating items [Guessing].
- Moat: sticky, multi-year fund capital and a long track record in European buyouts [Likely]. Fee-paying capital is not easily displaced, but fundraising is cyclical, and Blackstone, KKR and EQT compete for the same investors [Likely].
- The H1 2026 call headline cites record realizations and strong fundraising (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/ams/CVC/transcripts/557778-h1-2026/) [Likely].

## 3. Valuation
At 14.46 EUR the stock trades at 18.7x P/E, against a 5y median of 56.6x (7% of its 5y range) and a peer median of 28.7x (-35%) (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/ams/CVC/statistics/) [Certain]. EV/EBITDA of 14.2x is below its 5y minimum of 15.7x. P/FCF of 21.3x is below its 5y minimum, 17% under peers. FCF yield is 4.70%. P/B of 6.7x is 55% above peers, which is less informative for an asset-light manager [Likely].

The mechanical base fair value is 25.01 EUR (+72.9%), with a bear case of 6.32 and a bull case of 52.23. The wide range shows how earnings-sensitive the stock is [Likely]. The reverse DCF implies 6.5% year-1 revenue growth, fading to terminal growth. The 5y multiples come from a short post-IPO history that was inflated by depressed earnings, so the discount to that range overstates cheapness [Guessing]. The price needs steady fee growth with no collapse in exits.

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Operating margin (income-statement TTM, https://stockanalysis.com/quote/ams/CVC/financials/income-statement/?p=trailing) falls below 45%.
- ROIC (ratios page, TTM) falls below 20%.
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) rises above 2.5x.
- FCF margin (cash-flow TTM) falls below 28%.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Leverage has fallen from 2.73x in FY2023 to 1.15x TTM net debt/EBITDA [Certain]. Shares outstanding rose 1.16% YoY, so there is mild dilution, probably from equity compensation [Guessing]. FCF conversion is volatile because of performance-fee timing (FY2025 53.0%, TTM 73.4%). The firm is active in deals (Recordati bid with GBL; Irca purchase from Advent), which come with fund capital rather than only the balance sheet [Likely] (source: https://www.reuters.com/business/cvc-gbl-launch-107-bln-euro-full-takeover-offer-italys-recordati-2026-05-22/).
