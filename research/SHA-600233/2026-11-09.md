# YTO Express Group (SHA:600233) — Core initiation — 2026-11-09
## 1. Business
YTO is a Chinese express-parcel network operator (industry: Integrated Freight & Logistics) (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sha/600233/). [Likely] It earns revenue mainly from per-parcel fees on a franchise-style network, where partners collect and deliver and YTO runs the trunk-line sorting and the platform. All revenue is domestic, so no foreign-country exposure applies. Segment mix not verified from a primary source. [Guessing]

## 2. Quality
Returns are good and recovering. TTM ROIC is 16.2% against 12.0% a year earlier, and TTM operating margin is 9.2% against 6.6% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sha/600233/financials/ratios/?p=trailing). FY ROIC peaked at 20.5% in FY2022 and slid to 13.9% in FY2025, so the TTM rebound is not yet proven through a full year. [Certain]
The balance sheet is net cash (net debt/EBITDA -0.24x TTM; Piotroski 6; Altman Z 4.32) (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sha/600233/statistics/). [Certain]
Moat: scale in a network business with a thin margin. Margins stayed positive through the price war, which suggests a durable franchise network, but rivals can still take share on price. [Likely]
Gross margin is 11.7% TTM against 9.5% in FY2025 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sha/600233/financials/income-statement/). [Certain]

## 3. Valuation
The price is 18.79 CNY. P/E is 10.5x, below the 5y minimum of 11.4x (median 13.0x), and in line with peers at 11.1x. EV/EBITDA is 6.1x, near the bottom of its 5y range (5.6x to 13.3x), and +5% against peers. P/FCF is 25.2x, below its 5y minimum, but far above the peer median of 11.1x because FCF is only 3.2% of sales (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sha/600233/statistics/). [Certain]
Fair value is flagged unreliable: DCF base 10.80 against EV/EBITDA base 21.96, blended base 14.52, which is below the price. The reverse DCF implies 23.0% year-one revenue growth, which is not credible, so the DCF is likely too harsh on a business whose FCF is volatile. [Likely]
Net: the stock is cheap on earnings against its own history, fair against peers, and not clearly cheap on cash. [Likely]

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- TTM ROIC (ratios page, trailing) falls below 12%.
- TTM operating margin (income statement, trailing) falls below 7%.
- TTM FCF margin (cash flow statement, trailing) turns negative again.
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) moves above 0.0x, meaning net debt.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Cash conversion is the weak spot: FCF/NI was -17.5% in FY2025 and -22.7% in FY2024, and is 41.9% TTM. Heavy capex has absorbed earnings. The share count is shrinking slightly (-0.52% YoY), a small positive. Dividends are not covered in this note. [Certain]
