# Julius Bär Gruppe AG (SWX:BAER) — Core initiation — 2026-11-08
## 1. Business
Swiss-based private bank earning fees and commissions on client assets under management, plus net interest income on client lending and deposits. Revenue rises with markets and net new money, so it is cyclical. Regional revenue mix: [data unavailable] on the fact sheet; not verified from a primary source. [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/swx/BAER/)

## 2. Quality
Returns are good but volatile. ROE was 16.4% in FY2021, 7.3% in FY2023, 10.9% in FY2025 and 16.2% TTM, a 5y trend of -5.6pp (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/swx/BAER/financials/ratios/). Net margin is 27.0% TTM against 14.0% in FY2023 [Certain]. ROIC, ROCE and operating margin are [data unavailable] (not meaningful for a bank). FCF is not a reliable quality gauge for a bank, because client-flow and balance-sheet movements swing it (-51.6% in FY2022, 115.1% TTM) [Likely]. Piotroski F is 4 [Certain].
Moat: client relationships and Swiss booking-centre reputation, but it is narrow. FINMA said the bank seriously breached risk management and anti-money-laundering rules (source: https://www.reuters.com/business/finance/julius-baer-seriously-breached-risk-money-laundering-rules-swiss-regulator-says-2026-09-29/) [Certain]. De-risking weighs on net new money, which management expects to normalise by 2028 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/swx/BAER/transcripts/550407-h1-2026/) [Likely]. Record profit and AUM were reported for H1 2026 [Likely].

## 3. Valuation
P/E is 12.2x against a 5y range of 11.4x–21.4x (median 11.8x), the 8th percentile, and 19% below the peer median of 15.2x [Certain]. P/B is 1.9x, at the top of its 5y range and 7% below peers [Certain]. So the cheap P/E partly reflects TTM earnings that are above normal: net margin is 27.0% against a 5y low of 14.0% [Likely]. P/FCF of 2.9x is not meaningful for a bank [Likely]. Fair value base is 74.05 CHF, +9.1% against the 67.90 close; bear is 45.07 and bull 85.31 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/swx/BAER/). The reverse DCF implies -2.6% year-1 revenue growth [Certain]. Modest upside with a wide bear case: the price assumes earnings stay near current levels, not a re-rating. [Likely]

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROE (ratios page) falls below 10% on a TTM basis.
- Net margin (income statement page) falls below 18% on a TTM basis.
- Shares change YoY (statistics page) exceeds 3%, which would be dilution.
- Any new FINMA or other regulatory sanction that restricts client onboarding or business (company releases).

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
A share buyback of up to 600 million CHF was announced after FINMA closed its enforcement proceedings (source: https://www.reuters.com/legal/transactional/swiss-bank-julius-baer-announces-723-million-share-buyback-program-2026-10-02/) [Likely]. Shares change YoY is 0.09% [Certain]. Management says capital and liquidity are robust [Likely]. Cash conversion is not meaningful for a bank: FCF/NI was 129.0% in FY2025 and 426.5% TTM, after deeply negative FY2022–23 [Certain]. A new CFO started in August [Likely] (source: https://www.reuters.com/business/finance/julius-baer-appoints-peter-burrill-cfo-2026-07-03/).
