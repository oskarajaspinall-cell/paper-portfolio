# Kuaishou Technology (HKG:1024) — Core initiation — 2026-10-25
## 1. Business
Short-video and live-streaming content community in China. Revenue comes from advertising, live-streaming and e-commerce, and from the Kling AI video-generation unit, which is growing fast from a small base [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/1024/). Gross margin is 53.2% TTM (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/1024/financials/income-statement/).

## 2. Quality
Profitability has turned from loss-making to solid: operating margin 10.8% TTM against -34.2% in FY2021, ROIC 29.4% TTM, ROE 20.6% [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/1024/financials/ratios/). Net cash: net debt/EBITDA -1.97x. The moat is network scale and engagement in a duopoly-like Chinese short-video market, but it is contested by Douyin and Tencent, and returns are fading from the peak: ROIC fell from 61.5% in FY2024, and FCF margin fell from 17.1% to 6.3% TTM, with FCF conversion 57.3% [Likely]. Revenue growth has slowed to low single digits in Q1 and Q2 2026 while AI capex rises (third-party headline: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/1024/transcripts/680856-q2-2026/) [Likely]. Piotroski F of 4 and Altman Z of 0.77 are weak, though Z is distorted for a net-cash internet platform [Guessing].

## 3. Valuation
The price assumes decline. P/E is 6.8x versus a 5y range of 10.9x to 32.6x (median 13.3x) and a peer median of 22.8x. EV/EBITDA is 2.6x against a 5y minimum of 7.6x, and P/B is 1.3x. FCF yield is 8.64% and P/FCF is 11.6x, in line with peers (11.8x), so cheapness is on earnings, not cash. The reverse DCF implies -1.6% revenue growth in year 1. Base fair value is 48.04 HKD against a 27.90 close (+72.2%), but the bear value is negative, so the range is wide [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/1024/statistics/). Stock is down 62.2% over 12 months, 42.6% below its 200-day average. Cheap versus history, with cash earnings lagging accounting earnings.

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Operating margin (income statement page, TTM) falls below 7%.
- Revenue growth (income statement page, year on year) turns negative for two consecutive quarters.
- FCF margin (cash flow page, TTM) falls below 3%.
- Net debt/EBITDA (statistics page) rises above 0x, i.e. net cash is lost to AI capex or the Kling funding.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Heavy AI and compute capex guided for 2026 (third-party headline: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/1024/transcripts/616926-q1-2026/) is compressing FCF conversion to 57.3% TTM from 141.7% in FY2024. Share count change is 0.89% YoY, mild dilution [Certain]. Kling AI outside funding from Alibaba and Tencent reduces own capital need but dilutes ownership of a possible crown jewel (https://www.reuters.com/world/china/alibaba-tencent-back-kuaishous-kling-ai-28-billion-fundraise-2026-07-03/) [Likely].

## 6. Unusual items
- Tencent unit sought to sell Kuaishou shares: a supply overhang from a major holder (https://www.reuters.com/world/asia-pacific/tencent-unit-seeks-up-155-billion-kuaishou-share-sale-term-sheet-shows-2026-07-06/) [Likely].
- Planned Kling spin-off and listing could move value out of the parent's listed equity (headline listed in the fact sheet, source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/1024/) [Guessing].
- China/HK macro and geopolitical stress; beta 1.27. Insiders own 19.31% [Certain].
