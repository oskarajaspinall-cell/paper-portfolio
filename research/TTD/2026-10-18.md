# The Trade Desk, Inc. (TTD) — Core initiation — 2026-10-18
## 1. Business
TTD runs an independent demand-side platform (DSP) for programmatic digital advertising: agencies and brands use it to buy ad inventory across CTV, display, audio and mobile, paying TTD a share of gross ad spend plus fees for data and value-added services. It does not own ad inventory, which it markets as an objectivity advantage over supply-side rivals [Certain].

## 2. Quality
Historically high returns and margins: TTM ROIC 24.8%, ROCE 19.7% (both rising over 5y), operating margin 19.6%, FCF margin 28.9%, net cash (net debt/EBITDA −1.51x), Altman Z 4.72, Piotroski F 6/9 (source: stockanalysis.com ratios/statistics) [Certain]. But revenue growth has stalled: the FY2026 Q2 10-Q reports revenue up only 3% YoY in Q2 and 7% for H1 2026, "primarily driven by... a decrease in gross spend from existing clients" [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1671933/000167193326000086/ttd-20260630.htm) — a sharp break from TTD's historical double-digit growth. The same filing adds new risk-factor language on intensifying competition, AI-driven disruption, agencies/suppliers trading directly and holding-company consolidation shrinking its client base [Likely] (same URL). Gross margin has also drifted down, 81.5%→76.9% TTM (source: income statement) [Certain]. The independence moat is real, but whether it still withstands Amazon- and Google-scale DSP/retail-media competition is unproven.

## 3. Valuation
Multiples sit at or below the bottom of TTD's own 5y range and well below peers (PUBM, MGNI, GOOGL, META): P/E 14.4x (5y min 41.4x; peer median 21.3x), EV/EBITDA 6.8x (peer 22.6x), P/FCF 6.7x (peer 29.8x), FCF yield 14.9% (source: fact sheet valuation table) [Certain]. The reverse DCF implies the price assumes revenue falls 0.3% in year 1, fading to terminal growth — the market is pricing near-zero recovery [Certain]. Base fair value is $45.13 (DCF $19.45, EV/EBITDA $96.51, blended 67/33) above a $12.23 close [Certain], but the EV/EBITDA leg borrows multiples from faster-growing GOOGL/META and likely overstates a fair multiple for a name now growing low-single-digits; the DCF-only leg is the more defensible floor and still sits above the close. Upside on that conservative estimate needs only that growth stabilizes near its current depressed rate, not a reacceleration — a low bar, but one TTD has not yet cleared.

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Quarterly revenue growth YoY (income statement) stays below 5% for two more consecutive quarters after Q3 FY2026 — confirms the stall is structural, not one-off.
- A subsequent 10-Q/10-K MD&A again cites a decrease in gross spend from existing clients (sec.gov) — confirms client losses are continuing, not a single quarter.
- Gross margin (income statement) falls below 75% — confirms further erosion of pricing power/value-added-service mix.
- FCF margin (cash flow + income statement) falls below 20% — confirms cash generation deteriorating alongside growth.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Net cash balance sheet (net debt/EBITDA −1.51x TTM) and strong but volatile FCF conversion (FCF/NI: 235%→870%→308%→163%→179%→212% TTM) (source: cash flow/income statement) [Certain]. Shares outstanding down 4.27% YoY, consistent with continued buybacks; no dividend (source: statistics page) [Certain]. Management announced a restructuring into smaller operating "pods" including workforce reductions (source: https://www.barrons.com/articles/trade-desk-stock-job-cuts-0386897b, headline date 2026-09-04) [Likely] — the stated magnitude is a third-party headline figure and is not used as data here; its cash cost is not yet visible in the financials shown.

## 6. Unusual items
Short interest is 20.06% of float — elevated for a name of this size, though down 5% month-on-month (source: statistics page) [Certain], signalling persistent bearish positioning on the growth-stall thesis. SEC EDGAR shows an 8-K reporting exit/disposal costs [company-reported], filed 2026-09-04 (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1671933/000119312526382690/0001193125-26-382690-index.htm), consistent with the restructuring [Likely]. The fair-value model's two legs diverge sharply ($19.45 DCF vs $96.51 EV/EBITDA), which should be read as reduced confidence in the EV/EBITDA leg for a decelerating business, not confirmation of the $45.13 blend.
