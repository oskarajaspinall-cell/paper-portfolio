# Macro overlay: The Trade Desk, Inc. (TTD) — 2026-10-18
## Assessment
TTD's base fair value ($45.13) is discounted at CAPM = 10y Treasury + beta × ERP (fact sheet, https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/ttd/). Rising real/nominal yields and widening credit spreads raise that discount rate and risk premium on a depressed, high-short-interest small-cap, secondary to the dominant company-specific thesis (client-spend losses, competition) **(inference)**.

**Facts** [Certain]: 10y Treasury (DGS10) 5.10% on 2026-10-15, +53bp over 3m (https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=DGS10); 10y real yield (DFII10) 2.78%, +43bp over 3m (https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=DFII10); 10y breakeven inflation (T10YIE) 2.35%, +11bp over 3m (https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=T10YIE) — the yield rise is mostly real, not inflation; US high-yield spread (BAMLH0A0HYM2) 3.23%, +52bp over 3m (https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=BAMLH0A0HYM2); 2y Treasury (DGS2) 4.77% vs effective fed funds (DFF) 3.88%.

**Market pricing** (proxy): 2y yield 89bp above fed funds implies the market isn't pricing near-term cuts **(inference)** — a headwind, not tailwind, for TTD's DCF discount rate.

**Bull**: yields/spreads ease (10y toward ~4.7%, HY toward ~2.7%) → cost of equity falls → fair value drifts modestly higher; doesn't fix the growth stall. **Bear**: 10y toward/above 5.5% and HY toward 4%+ → cost of equity rises → fair value compresses, compounding the already-depressed multiple on a name priced for near-zero growth (reverse DCF, factsheet). **Tilt**: macro is a second-order amplifier — the thesis (research/TTD/2026-10-18.md) hinges on revenue stabilizing, not on yields.
