# FactSet Research Systems (FDS) — Core initiation — 2026-11-15
## 1. Business
Subscription financial data, analytics and workflow software (workstations, portfolio analytics, enterprise data feeds, AI solutions, managed services) sold to asset managers, bankers and wealth managers. Revenue is recurring and mostly Americas-led: fiscal 2026 revenue grew 6.7%, all organic, with more than 9,200 clients and about 260,000 users [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1013237/000162828026066391/fds-20260831.htm). No single foreign country share is disclosed in the delta; [data unavailable].

## 2. Quality
Good, but not improving. ROIC 18.0% TTM (5y trend falling, -5.4pp); ROCE 23.6% rising; FCF margin 28.6% and FCF/NI 132.6% are strong (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/fds/financials/ratios/) [Certain]. Operating margin fell to 29.1% from 32.3% a year earlier, the fact sheet's flagged direction change. The 10-K attributes it to restructuring charges, higher variable pay and one-time CEO pay costs [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1013237/000162828026066391/fds-20260831.htm), so part is likely non-recurring [Likely]. Moat: embedded workflows and switching costs [Likely]. Offsetting this, the new 10-K risk factors say AI may let clients build their own tools and reduce demand, and that FactSet is going through significant organisational change [Certain]. Headcount fell 2.8% on restructuring [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1013237/000162828026066391/fds-20260831.htm). Net debt/EBITDA 1.42x is comfortable.

## 3. Valuation
Multiples sit at the bottom of the 5y range: P/E 22.8x (9th percentile of range, median 29.9x), EV/EBITDA 14.4x (4%), P/FCF 16.6x (5%), and 27-65% below peers MSCI, SPGI, MCO and VRSK (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/fds/statistics/) [Certain]. FCF yield is 6.03%. The DCF fair value disagrees: base 247.28 USD vs a 332.69 close (-25.7%), bull 334.10, with a 5.10% risk-free rate. The P/E method gives 436.31 base. The reverse DCF implies 11.9% year-1 revenue growth, well above the 6.7% organic growth reported [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1013237/000162828026066391/fds-20260831.htm). Cheap versus history and peers, expensive on DCF; the price has risen 64.8% in six months, so some of the de-rating has already reversed [Likely]. Peers' premium multiples reflect higher margins, not necessarily a mispricing [Guessing].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Operating margin (income statement page, TTM) stays below 27% after FY2027 restructuring costs roll off.
- Organic revenue growth (10-K/10-Q MD&A) falls below 4% for two consecutive quarters.
- ROIC TTM (ratios page) falls below 15%.
- Client count (10-K/10-Q) declines year on year, which would signal AI-driven disintermediation.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Shares outstanding fell 4.62% year on year, so buybacks are material, and FCF conversion of 132.6% funds them with leverage at 1.42x net debt/EBITDA (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/fds/statistics/) [Certain]. Management hosted an Investor Day on 2026-11-10 and reaffirmed its fiscal 2027 outlook (source: https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/11/10/3399323/7768/en/factset-hosts-investor-day-2026.html) [Likely]. Partnerships (Hamilton Lane, Nasdaq Private Market) extend the private-markets data set rather than adding debt-funded M&A [Likely].

## 6. Unusual items
Short interest is 10.99% of float, up 21.4% on the prior month, which signals real doubt about AI disruption [Certain]. New 10-K risk factors add cybersecurity, concentrated cloud supplier dependence and data-centre/compute capacity constraints [Certain]. About 8,652 of 12,444 employees are in Asia Pacific [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1013237/000162828026066391/fds-20260831.htm), so the cost base is exposed to Asian currencies [Likely]. The price is 31.5% above its 200-day average and RSI is 58.6.
