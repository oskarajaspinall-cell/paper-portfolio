# FactSet Research Systems Inc. (FDS) — Core initiation — 2026-10-08

## 1. Business
FactSet sells subscription access to a connected financial data, analytics and AI platform (workstations, portfolio analytics, enterprise data, managed services, data feeds/APIs) to ~9,000 institutional clients. Institutional buyside is 82% of organic ASV; Americas/EMEA/APAC are 65%/25%/10% of revenue [Certain] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1013237/000162828025045769/fds-20250831.htm).

## 2. Quality
Annual ASV retention exceeds 95% and client retention is 91%, reflecting high switching costs: clients embed FactSet in daily workflow and hand it proprietary portfolio data [Certain] (source: SEC 10-K). ROCE is 22.9% TTM (+7.8pp over 5y) but ROIC fell from 23.2% to 17.5% TTM and ROE from 33.8% to 25.5%, with gross margin down 2.2pp to 50.6% — margin give-back even as cash conversion improved (FCF margin 28.6%, +2.2pp; FCF/NI 132.6%) [Certain] (source: fact sheet, RA/IS/CF). The 10-K names Bloomberg, S&P Market Intelligence, LSEG Data & Analytics, BlackRock Aladdin, MSCI and Morningstar as competitors and flags AI letting rivals erode demand — a real, not-yet-visible moat risk [Likely] (source: SEC 10-K). Management acquired BCC Group International on 2026-09-22 to expand real-time market data [Likely] (source: GlobeNewsWire release, below). Net leverage is moderate (net debt/EBITDA 1.45x TTM), roughly half the 2.67x of five years ago.

## 3. Valuation
At 272.78 USD, FDS trades on 18.7x P/E and 12.5x EV/EBITDA, both ~11% below the bottom of its own 5-year range (P/E 20.9-41.5x; EV/EBITDA 14.1-29.1x) and 29%/32% below the peer median (MSCI, SPGI, MCO, MORN) [Certain] (source: fact sheet, RA). EV/Sales (4.4x vs peer median 9.1x, -51%) and P/FCF (13.7x vs 23.1x, -41%) show an even wider discount. FCF yield is 7.3%. The price assumes FactSet's margin erosion and AI-competition risk persist and that it never re-rates toward its historical multiple or peer group, despite 95%+ ASV retention and rising FCF conversion. That looks too pessimistic for a business with this retention profile and no sign of client losses in the quarter's results.

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Gross margin (income statement page) falls below 48%, extending the FY2024-FY2026 decline from 54.1% to 50.6%.
- ROIC (ratios page) falls below 15%, extending the 5-year decline from 23.2% to 17.5% TTM.
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) rises back above 2.0x, reversing the deleveraging from 2.67x five years ago.
- FCF margin (cash flow/income statement) falls below 24%, reversing the current 28.6% TTM level.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
FCF conversion is strong (FCF/NI 132.6% TTM, FCF margin 28.6%) and shares outstanding fell 4.62% YoY, consistent with continued buybacks funded from free cash flow [Certain] (source: fact sheet, ST/CF/IS). Net debt/EBITDA of 1.45x leaves room to keep funding M&A (e.g., BCC Group) and repurchases without balance-sheet strain [Likely] (source: fact sheet, RA/BS).

## 6. Unusual items
Short interest is 10.86% of float and rose 7.0% month-on-month — elevated and rising for a business with this retention profile, consistent with a live bear case on AI/competitive disruption rather than a balance-sheet or accounting concern [Likely] (source: fact sheet, ST). Piotroski F-score is 6/9 (mid-range, no red flag).
