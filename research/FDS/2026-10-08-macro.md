# Macro overlay: FactSet Research Systems Inc. (FDS) — 2026-10-08
## Assessment
FDS's bull case is a valuation re-rating: 18.7x P/E vs its 5y range (20.9x-41.5x) and 26.3x peer median (MSCI/SPGI/MCO/MORN) [Certain] (fact sheet, 2026-10-08). Chain: real yields → discount rate/risk premium → ceiling on re-rating; FDS's ~95% ASV/client retention is largely macro-insensitive on earnings [Likely] (note, SEC 10-K).

Facts (FRED, fred.stlouisfed.org): US 10y real yield (DFII10) 2.91% (2026-10-06), +48bp/1m, +61bp/3m; nominal 10y (DGS10) 5.27%, +72bp/3m; effective fed funds (DFF) 3.88%, +25bp/3m; HY credit spread (BAMLH0A0HYM2) 3.03%, +36bp/3m; 10y breakeven inflation (T10YIE) 2.36%, +11bp/3m (2026-10-07).

Market pricing (proxy, not fact): the 2y yield (DGS2, 4.79%) sitting ~91bp above effective fed funds implies the market expects further tightening on average over the next two years, not cuts **(inference)**. Rising real yields alongside widening HY spreads read as a hawkish, mildly risk-off repricing — not yet stress.

This hurts the re-rating bull case (higher discount rate tightens the ceiling) and helps nothing on earnings. But FDS underperformed peers facing the same rate backdrop by -16.5pp vs SPY over 12m [Certain] (fact sheet), so idiosyncratic AI-competition/margin concerns, not macro, explain most of the discount [Likely] **(inference)**.

Bull: real yields roll over (DFII10 toward ~2.3%, DGS10 <4.75%) → risk premium compresses → multiple re-rates toward peers/history even absent fundamental change.
Bear: real yields and credit spreads keep rising → discount-rate ceiling tightens → multiple stays depressed regardless of ASV growth/retention strength, delaying the thesis.
Tilt: small, toward bear.
