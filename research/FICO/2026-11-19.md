# Fair Isaac Corporation (FICO) — Core initiation — 2026-11-19
## 1. Business
FICO earns revenue from two segments. Scores licenses the FICO Score to lenders and credit bureaus; the U.S. mortgage market is a significant part of it (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/814547/000081454726000042/fico-20260930.htm) [Certain]. Software sells the FICO Platform and decisioning and fraud analytics [Certain]. Scores drove most of fiscal 2026 revenue growth, from both higher unit price and higher mortgage volume [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/814547/000081454726000042/fico-20260930.htm). No single foreign country is shown at ~10%+ of revenue in the material read [Likely].

## 2. Quality
Very high quality on the numbers. TTM ROIC is 80.2% and ROCE 111.9%. Operating margin is 51.0% and FCF margin 42.2%, all rising year on year and over three years (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/fico/financials/ratios/?p=trailing) [Certain]. Gross margin is 86.1%. Piotroski F is 7 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/fico/statistics/) [Certain].

The moat is the FICO Score's standing as the lending standard and the cost of switching it out [Likely]. It is under real attack. The new risk factors say the Federal Housing Finance Agency's "lender choice" regime lets mortgage lenders pick VantageScore, a product of the three bureaus. They say this has already hurt the stock and could hurt revenue (source: sec-delta, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/814547/000081454726000042/fico-20260930.htm) [Certain]. Management is moving to bespoke mortgage pricing, and the FICO Preferred Program has signed lenders (source: https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20261104153916/en/DHI-Mortgage-LoanDepot-Paramount-Residential-Mortgage-Group-and-CMG-Financial-Join-Growing-Roster-of-FICO%C2%AE-Preferred-Program-Lenders/) [Likely]. The pricing power behind recent margins is therefore likely to be renegotiated downward [Likely].

## 3. Valuation
The price assumes the margin expansion is over. P/E is 18.1x against a 5y median of 50.3x (4th percentile of its range). EV/EBITDA is 14.9x and P/FCF 13.5x, both near their 5y minimum. FCF yield is 7.38%. Against peers, P/E is -37% and P/FCF -41% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/fico/statistics/) [Certain].

The reverse DCF implies 8.6% year-1 revenue growth, fading (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/fico/). Fair value is flagged unreliable, with methods disagreeing 2.0x: base 982.86 against a 677.75 close, DCF-only 732.28 [Certain]. Weigh it lightly. Because management withheld guidance (source: https://www.pymnts.com/earnings/2026/fico-withholds-2027-guidance-mortgage-pricing-model-shifts/), the market is pricing an unknown pricing reset, not a known forecast [Likely]. The trend is broken: price is -36.5% versus the 200-day average, with 12-month change -60.6% and relative strength -71.3pp against SPY [Certain].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Operating margin (income statement, TTM) falls below 45%.
- ROIC (ratios page, TTM) falls below 50%.
- FCF margin (cash flow page, TTM) falls below 35%.
- Scores segment revenue falls year on year in a 10-Q or 10-K, evidencing mortgage price or volume loss to VantageScore.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Conversion is strong: FCF/NI is 128.1%. Shares are down 6.40% year on year. Buybacks are debt-funded, including a term loan for an accelerated repurchase [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/814547/000081454726000042/fico-20260930.htm). Net debt/EBITDA has risen to 3.87x from 3.16x over five years. That is manageable at current cash flow but leaves less room if pricing resets [Likely].

## 6. Unusual items
- Short interest is 12.24% of float, up 12.7% on the prior month (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/fico/statistics/) [Certain].
- FY27 guidance withheld at the Q4 results (source: https://www.wsj.com/business/earnings/fico-declines-to-share-guidance-amid-price-structure-change-27edfe82) [Certain].
- Roughly 15% of positions were cut in Q4 (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/814547/000081454726000042/fico-20260930.htm) [Certain].
- Julie May was named President of B2B Scores (source: https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20261117693337/en/FICO-Promotes-Julie-May-to-President-B2B-Scores/) [Certain].
- The price drop is explained by the guidance withdrawal and mortgage-pricing uncertainty, not by weaker reported results [Likely].
- The prior 10-K's Goodwill impairment assessment was removed from the critical accounting estimates (source: sec-delta) [Certain].
