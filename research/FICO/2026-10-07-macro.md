# Macro overlay: Fair Isaac Corporation (FICO) — 2026-10-07
## Assessment
Chain: (1) long/mortgage rates → origination & refinance volumes → unit volume in FICO's bureau-distributed Scores line (51% of FY2025 revenue, each mortgage credit pull triggers a fee) [Certain] (10-K, cited in note); (2) real yields → discount rate → equity multiple for a high-ROIC compounder [Likely] **(inference)**.

Facts (FRED): 10y Treasury (DGS10) 5.31%, 2026-10-05, +0.83pp/3m; 10y real yield (DFII10) 2.95%, +0.71pp/3m; 10y breakeven (T10YIE) 2.36%, flat (+0.11pp); effective fed funds (DFF) 3.88%, +0.25pp/3m; HY spread (BAMLH0A0HYM2) 3.12%, +0.4pp/3m, still low in absolute terms.

Market pricing (proxy, not fact): 2y yield (4.84%) sits above effective fed funds (3.88%), consistent with the market pricing further tightening, not cuts — a policy-path proxy, not a hard signal.

Inference: flat breakevens with rising real/nominal yields imply a real-rate/policy repricing, not an inflation surprise [Likely] **(inference)**. Higher-for-longer rates would (a) keep mortgage origination depressed, compounding Scores-volume weakness, and (b) keep the discount rate elevated, capping multiple recovery.

This is secondary, not primary: the stock's -64% 12-month move and sub-trough multiple are dominated by the FHFA mortgage-pricing/VantageScore regulatory shock (idiosyncratic, from the note), not macro. Macro bull: rates ease, origination volumes recover, partial multiple relief. Macro bear: rates stay elevated, origination stays weak, no multiple relief even if regulatory risk resolves. Tilt: moderate toward bear — macro reinforces, not offsets, the bear case, though it is not the swing factor.
