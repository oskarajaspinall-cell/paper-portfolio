# Macro overlay: Comcast Corporation (CMCSA) — 2026-10-08
## Assessment
CMCSA's thesis is firm-specific (broadband share loss, NBCUniversal separation execution), but the Treasury yield level has moved enough this quarter to shift the valuation range and refinancing cost, given 2.43x net debt/EBITDA and the pending debt split with NBCUniversal [Certain] (https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/cmcsa/financials/ratios/).

**Facts (FRED, 2026-10-06/07):** 10y Treasury yield (DGS10) 5.27%, +0.72pp/3m; 10y real yield (DFII10) 2.91%, +0.61pp/3m; 10y breakeven (T10YIE) +0.11pp to 2.36%; HY credit spread (BAMLH0A0HYM2) 3.03%, +0.36pp/3m (https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=DGS10, id=DFII10, id=T10YIE, id=BAMLH0A0HYM2).

**Inference:** the rise is almost entirely real-rate/term-premium, not an inflation surprise — raising CMCSA's cost of debt and the risk-free input the fact sheet's DCF already uses (5.27%), so today's depressed fair-value range already reflects this level, not a stale one **(inference)**. The 2y yield (4.79%) above fed funds (3.88%) is a market-pricing proxy for a higher-for-longer path, which if realized would raise refinancing costs just as the separation reallocates debt [Guessing].

**Bull:** yields/HY spreads plateau, refinancing stays cheap, discount rate falls → fair-value range ($70–$122) migrates up, aiding re-rating off 6.8x P/E.
**Bear:** yields/spreads grind higher into the separation, raising pro-forma leverage cost → fair-value range compresses further, reinforcing the depressed multiple.
**Tilt:** small toward bear — rates amplify an already-dominant, company-specific bear case.
