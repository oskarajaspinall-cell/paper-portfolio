# Comcast Corporation (CMCSA) — Core initiation — 2026-10-08

## 1. Business
Comcast earns most profit from Connectivity & Platforms: Xfinity broadband/video, Xfinity Mobile and Comcast Business, sold over its owned last-mile network. Content & Experiences (NBCUniversal: cable networks, Peacock, Universal theme parks, film/TV) and Sky (UK/Europe pay-TV) add the rest. It completed the Versant cable-network spin-off on 2026-01-02 (tax-free, 1 Versant share per 25 Comcast shares) and has disclosed a planned NBCUniversal separation targeted for mid-2027, not yet finalized [Likely] (source: SEC 10-Q, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1166691/000162828026049360/cmcsa-20260630.htm).

## 2. Quality
Returns on capital are respectable but not expanding: ROIC 8.1% TTM and ROCE 8.2% TTM, both roughly flat over five years [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/cmcsa/financials/ratios/). Gross margin rose to 71.7% (FY25) on mix shift toward broadband/streaming, but operating margin slipped from 18.8% (FY24) to 14.7% TTM as NBCUniversal/theme-park cost inflation and streaming investment ate the gain [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/cmcsa/financials/income-statement/). FCF margin is strong at 16.4% TTM and net debt/EBITDA improved to 2.43x from 2.85x five years ago [Certain]. The core moat — last-mile broadband with few local competitors — is eroding: domestic broadband revenue fell to $6.3bn in Q2 2026 from $6.6bn a year earlier as fiber overbuild and fixed-wireless (T, VZ) take share [Certain] (source: SEC 10-Q, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1166691/000162828026049360/cmcsa-20260630.htm). NBCUniversal diversifies earnings but shares the structural linear-TV decline and streaming losses seen across peers (WBD). The planned separation adds near-term uncertainty over how capital and debt split between the two entities.

## 3. Valuation
CMCSA trades at P/E 6.8x (6th percentile of its 5.4x–28.2x five-year range), EV/EBITDA 4.6x (below the five-year minimum of 5.4x), P/FCF 3.6x versus a 9.2x peer median (CHTR, T, VZ, WBD; -60%), and an FCF yield of 27.5% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/cmcsa/statistics/, https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/cmcsa/financials/ratios/). Every multiple sits well below own history and peers. The fact sheet's reverse DCF shows the price implies a -26.5% revenue decline in year one fading to terminal growth — far more pessimistic than reported broadband softness, implying the market prices a structural worst case (faster cord-cutting, spin-off execution risk, capital-structure uncertainty), not current trading performance [Likely]. The stated base-case DCF fair value of $111.76 versus a $20.94 close is too large a gap to treat as the thesis itself; it more likely reflects doubt about cash-flow durability post-separation and the 2.43x net debt/EBITDA than a modeling error [Guessing].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Domestic broadband segment revenue (10-Q, SEC) falls more than the -4.5% YoY seen in Q2 2026 for two further consecutive quarters, confirming accelerating share loss.
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) rises above 3.0x, signalling the planned separations are degrading balance-sheet quality.
- FCF margin (cash flow + income statement pages) falls below 10%, showing broadband/DOCSIS 4.0 capital intensity is outrunning the margin-mix benefit.
- Terms of the NBCUniversal separation (SEC filings) leave the remaining entity with materially higher pro-forma leverage or a smaller dividend-paying earnings base than today's combined company.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
In H1 2026 Comcast paid $2.4bn of dividends and repurchased $2.5bn of stock, funded by a TTM FCF margin of 16.4% [Certain] (source: SEC 10-Q, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1166691/000162828026049360/cmcsa-20260630.htm). FCF/NI of 182.5% TTM is flattered by working-capital timing, not a representative run rate [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/cmcsa/financials/cash-flow-statement/). Net debt/EBITDA improved to 2.43x from 2.85x five years ago even through the Versant spin. The planned NBCUniversal separation (mid-2027, still subject to final approval [Likely]) will reallocate the asset and debt base and could change dividend/buyback capacity at the remaining entity — an unknown to monitor, not yet a known negative.

## 6. Unusual items
The planned NBCUniversal separation (targeted mid-2027) remains unfinalized per the company's own risk disclosure [Likely] (source: SEC 10-Q, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1166691/000162828026049360/cmcsa-20260630.htm) — a structural change to what current shareholders will ultimately own. Short interest is low at 2.28% of float and falling (-3.7% month-on-month); institutional ownership is high at 90.0% — no crowding risk [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/cmcsa/statistics/). Altman Z-score of 1.40 sits in the grey/distress zone, though this is typical for asset-heavy telecom/media balance sheets with the leverage trend improving, not disqualifying on its own [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/cmcsa/statistics/).
