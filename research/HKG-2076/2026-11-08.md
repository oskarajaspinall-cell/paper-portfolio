# Kanzhun Limited (HKG:2076) — Core initiation — 2026-11-08
## 1. Business
Kanzhun runs BOSS Zhipin, China's direct-chat online recruitment platform. Revenue is mainly employer subscriptions and credits for job postings plus AI candidate-matching tools; job seekers use it free [Likely] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1842827/000110465926050959/bz-20251231x20f.htm). Operations and customers are in mainland China, so China is effectively the whole revenue base (financials in CNY, shares quoted in HKD) [Likely] (same source). No other foreign market is material.

## 2. Quality
Returns and margins have risen on both the fiscal-year and rolling-12-month views. ROCE is 14.0% TTM vs 4.3% in FY2021 and 10.6% a year earlier; operating margin is 34.8% vs 23.2% a year earlier; gross margin is 85.9% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/2076/financials/ratios/). Net debt/EBITDA is -5.79x, i.e. large net cash (source: factsheet Quality table).

**Direction change (FCF margin):** 50.9% TTM is 4.0pp below its 2-year high of 54.9% (2026-03-31), but 3.4pp above a year earlier: a pullback from a peak, not a reversal [Likely]. More telling: FCF conversion fell to 96.4% of net income from 162.1% in FY2025, while net margin (52.8%) far exceeds operating margin (34.8%). Part of reported profit is probably non-operating and not recurring [Guessing]; operating and FCF margins are cleaner gauges.

Moat: two-sided network effect in direct chat [Likely] (source: 20-F above).

## 3. Valuation
P/E is 12.2x and EV/EBITDA 8.8x, both below the bottom of the 5y fiscal-year range (24.3x and 15.0x minimums), and 33% and 51% below the peer median (peers ZIP, TYO:6098, DHX, UPWK) (source: factsheet Valuation table). P/FCF is 12.1x, 7% above peers; FCF yield is 8.28%. EV/Sales (3.8x) is above peers, which are low-multiple staffing/marketplace businesses, so peers are an imperfect yardstick [Likely].

The model's base fair value is 66.74 HKD vs a 68.55 close (-2.6%); bear 35.80 (-47.8%), bull 121.28 (+76.9%). The reverse DCF implies 25.2% year-1 revenue growth fading to terminal, a demanding hurdle [Likely]. The base fell by roughly half since the 2026-10-11 run, so this estimate is unstable and should carry little weight [Guessing]. The stock rose 27.2% in 6 months but is still -17.5% over 12 months (source: factsheet Price table).

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Operating margin (income statement, TTM) falls below 25% (vs 34.8%).
- FCF margin (cash-flow statement, TTM) falls below 40% (vs 50.9%), or FCF conversion stays below 80% of net income for two further quarters.
- ROCE (ratios page) falls below 9% (vs 14.0%).
- Revenue (income statement) declines year on year for two consecutive quarters.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Net cash and no leverage. The company declared an annual cash dividend and a shareholder-return plan in September 2026 [Certain] (source: https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/09/22/3365989/0/en/kanzhun-limited-declares-annual-cash-dividend-and-shareholder-return-plan.html). Yet shares outstanding rose 4.74% YoY (source: factsheet Other table), so buybacks are not offsetting dilution, probably from share-based compensation [Guessing]. FCF conversion has dropped to 96.4% TTM from 162.1% in FY2025.

## 6. Unusual items
Board turnover: a non-executive director resigned on 2026-10-23 (stated reason: other commitments) [Certain] (source: https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/10/23/3387283/0/en/kanzhun-limited-announces-board-change.html), following the 2026-09-01 change of directors' executive positions [Likely] (source: https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/09/01/3354013/0/en/kanzhun-limited-announces-change-of-directors-executive-positions.html). Not yet decision-changing, but watch for further departures. Next earnings are expected 2026-11-13 (source: factsheet Price table). Beta is 0.49; VIE structure and US-China listing/regulatory risk persist [Certain] (source: 20-F above). Short interest [data unavailable].
