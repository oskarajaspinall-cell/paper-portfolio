# Kanzhun Limited (HKG:2076) — Core initiation — 2026-10-11

## 1. Business
Kanzhun operates BOSS Zhipin, China's leading direct-chat online recruitment platform, connecting job seekers with employers via app-based messaging rather than resume databases [Likely] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1842827/000110465926050959/bz-20251231x20f.htm). Revenue is mainly enterprise subscriptions/credits for job postings and AI-powered candidate-matching tools, spanning key-account, mid-sized and small employers, and separate white-collar, gold-collar and blue-collar job-seeker products [Likely] (same source).

## 2. Quality
Returns and margins have improved sharply: ROCE rose from 4.3% (FY2021) to 14.0% TTM, net margin from -25.1% to 52.8% TTM, and operating margin from 11.0% to 34.8% TTM (source: factsheet Quality table). FCF margin is 50.9% TTM with FCF conversion of 96.4% of net income, and the balance sheet carries a large net cash position (net debt/EBITDA -5.79x) (source: factsheet). The moat is a two-sided network effect in the direct-chat model plus proprietary AI matching ("Nanbeige") that competitors with "greater financial... resources" have so far not displaced [Likely] (source: 20-F, sec.gov). Sales & marketing fell to 20.5% of revenue in 2025 from 33.5% in 2023 — evidence the network effect is reducing the need to buy growth [Likely] (source: 20-F). Key durability risk: the VIE contractual structure through which foreign holders access the Chinese operating entity, and reliance on China's domestic hiring cycle [Certain] (source: 20-F).

## 3. Valuation
At 10.4x TTM P/E and 7.2x EV/EBITDA, the stock trades below the bottom of its own 5-year range (5y P/E min/median/max 24.3x/37.2x/566.1x) and 61-63% below the peer median (28.1x P/E, 18.7x EV/EBITDA; peers ZIP, TYO:6098, DHX, UPWK) (source: factsheet Valuation table). FCF yield is 9.28% (source: factsheet). The fair-value model (DCF 67%/EV-Revenue 33%, CAPM using FRED 10y 5.22% plus a 3.5% CNY country-risk premium) gives bear/base/bull of 71.75/131.83/237.23 HKD against a 60.00 HKD close — even the bear case implies +19.6% [Guessing] (source: factsheet Fair value table). The 12-month share decline of -36.2% (source: factsheet Price table) suggests the market is pricing China-ADR/regulatory risk well ahead of the fundamentals, which the fact sheet shows have kept improving.

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Gross margin (ratios page) falls below 80% (vs 85.9% TTM), signalling pricing/competitive pressure.
- Revenue growth (income statement) turns negative for two consecutive quarters, ending the enterprise-adoption trend behind the 14.1% YoY Q2 2026 growth reported (source: https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/09/21/3365903/0/en/boss-zhipin-reports-accelerated-second-quarter-growth-and-strengthened-shareholder-returns.html).
- Operating margin (income statement) falls below 20% (vs 34.8% TTM), evidence the AI-matching cost advantage is eroding.
- ROCE (ratios page) falls below 8% (vs 14.0% TTM), evidence returns on the business are no longer improving.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Net cash balance sheet with no leverage (net debt/EBITDA -5.79x) (source: factsheet). The board adopted an annual dividend policy in August 2025, approving a US$0.084/Class A share dividend (~US$77.9m) in 2025 after a US$0.09/share special dividend in November 2023, plus buyback authorizations in 2023, March 2024, August 2024 and an amendment in August 2025 [Certain] (source: 20-F). FCF conversion is 96.4% of net income TTM (source: factsheet), so shareholder returns are backed by cash, not accounting profit alone.

## 6. Unusual items
The company announced a change of directors' executive positions on 2026-09-01; impact not yet assessable [Likely] (source: https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/09/01/3354013/0/en/kanzhun-limited-announces-change-of-directors-executive-positions.html). Beta is low (0.49) despite the -36.2% 12-month decline (source: factsheet, Other table), indicating the move was largely idiosyncratic rather than broad-market driven. HFCAA/PCAOB inspection and VIE-enforceability risk apply to the Nasdaq ADR line and, via sentiment, to these HK-listed shares [Certain] (source: 20-F).
