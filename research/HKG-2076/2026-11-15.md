# Kanzhun Limited (HKG:2076) — Core initiation — 2026-11-15
## 1. Business
Kanzhun runs BOSS Zhipin, China's direct-chat online recruitment platform. Revenue comes mainly from employer subscriptions and job-posting credits plus AI candidate-matching tools; job seekers use it free [Likely] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1842827/000110465926050959/bz-20251231x20f.htm). Operations and customers are in mainland China, so China is effectively the whole revenue base (financials in CNY, shares quoted in HKD) [Likely] (same source). No other foreign market is material.

## 2. Quality
Returns and margins are rising on both fiscal-year and rolling-12-month views: ROCE 14.0% TTM vs 10.6% a year earlier and 4.3% in FY2021; operating margin 34.8% vs 23.2% a year earlier; gross margin 85.9% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/2076/financials/ratios/). Net debt/EBITDA is -5.79x, i.e. large net cash [Certain].

**Direction change (FCF margin):** 50.9% TTM is 4.0pp below its 2-year high of 54.9% (2026-03-31) but 3.4pp above a year earlier: a pullback from a peak, not a reversal [Likely]. More telling, FCF conversion fell to 96.4% of net income from 162.1% in FY2025, while net margin (52.8%) far exceeds operating margin (34.8%). Part of reported profit is probably non-operating and non-recurring [Guessing]; operating and FCF margins are the cleaner gauges.

Moat: two-sided network effect in direct chat [Likely] (source: 20-F above).

## 3. Valuation
P/E is 8.6x and EV/EBITDA 5.0x, both below the bottom of their 5y fiscal-year ranges (24.3x and 15.0x minimums), and 53% and 70% below the peer median (ZIP, TYO:6098, DHX, UPWK). P/FCF is 8.9x (16% below peers); FCF yield is 11.27% (source: factsheet Valuation table). EV/Sales (2.1x) is 44% above peers, which are low-multiple staffing/marketplace names, so peers are an imperfect yardstick [Likely].

The reverse DCF implies only 8.9% year-1 revenue growth fading to terminal, a far lower hurdle than the prior note's reverse DCF at its higher price [Likely]. Base fair value is 66.29 HKD vs a 48.54 close (+36.6%); bear 35.90 (-26.0%), bull 119.68 (+146.6%). The base has moved sharply between runs, so the estimate is unstable and should carry little weight [Guessing]. The stock is -40.3% over 12 months.

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Operating margin (income statement, TTM) falls below 25% (vs 34.8%).
- FCF margin (cash-flow statement, TTM) falls below 40% (vs 50.9%), or FCF conversion stays below 80% of net income for two further quarters.
- ROCE (ratios page) falls below 9% (vs 14.0%).
- Revenue (income statement) declines year on year for two consecutive quarters.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Net cash and no leverage. The company declared an annual cash dividend and a shareholder-return plan in September 2026 [Certain] (source: https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/09/22/3365989/0/en/kanzhun-limited-declares-annual-cash-dividend-and-shareholder-return-plan.html). Yet shares outstanding rose 4.74% YoY (source: factsheet Other table), so buybacks are not offsetting dilution, probably from share-based compensation [Guessing]. FCF conversion has dropped to 96.4% TTM from 162.1% in FY2025.
