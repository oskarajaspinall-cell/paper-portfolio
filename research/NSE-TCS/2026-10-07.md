# Tata Consultancy Services Limited (NSE:TCS) — Core initiation — 2026-10-07

## 1. Business
TCS is India's largest IT services provider, earning revenue from long-term outsourced technology contracts — applications, consulting, cloud, AI and BPO services — for large global enterprises, mostly on time-and-materials or fixed-price/managed-service terms [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/nse/TCS/). Growth is increasingly layered through proprietary AI platforms (TCS BaNCS, TCS Chroma) on top of the core delivery model [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/nse/TCS/).

## 2. Quality
TCS earns exceptional returns on capital — TTM ROIC 66.4%, ROCE 55.8%, ROE 47.7%, all up versus FY2022 — from a net-cash balance sheet (net debt/EBITDA -0.47x) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/nse/TCS/financials/ratios/). The moat is switching costs: enterprise clients run multi-year, deeply embedded outsourced contracts that are costly and risky to re-tender, and TCS's scale (584,519 employees [Certain], source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/nse/TCS/) gives a delivery-cost edge matched mainly by Infosys, Wipro, HCLTech and Accenture. However, margins are drifting down: gross margin -3.0pp and net margin -1.6pp over five years, operating margin roughly flat [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/nse/TCS/financials/income-statement/), consistent with pricing/wage pressure in a commoditising offshore-services industry. Recent AI-linked wins — a ~$1.5bn five-year Porsche platform deal, the €320m MHP acquisition, and taking over Best Buy's India GCC mandate from Accenture [Likely] (source: https://www.reuters.com/world/india/indias-tcs-buy-porsches-it-unit-373-million-2026-08-24/) — support the pipeline but have not yet shown up in the margin trend.

## 3. Valuation
At 2,100 INR, TCS trades on 15.3x TTM P/E, 9.8x EV/EBITDA, 2.6x EV/Sales, 15.8x P/FCF and 6.9x P/B — every multiple below its own 5-year range (P/E -11% vs a 5y minimum of 17.3x) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/nse/TCS/financials/ratios/), so the shares carry the cheapest multiples TCS has had in five years on its own history. But against listed peers (Infosys, Wipro, HCLTech, Accenture) it is still expensive on every measure: P/E +11%, EV/EBITDA +15%, EV/Sales +47%, P/FCF +30% and P/B +70% versus peer medians [Certain] (source: fact sheet valuation table). FCF yield is 6.34% [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/nse/TCS/statistics/). The own-history de-rating looks sector-wide — peer multiples have compressed too — rather than TCS-specific value emerging, so paying a premium to global/Indian IT peers for a business with eroding margins is not an attractive entry point [Likely].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Gross margin (income-statement page) recovers above 42% (FY2023 level) for two consecutive fiscal years, reversing the current erosion.
- EV/EBITDA (ratios page) falls to at or below the current peer median of 8.5x without a profit decline, closing the valuation premium to peers.
- Net margin (income-statement page) drops below 17% (vs 18.1% TTM), signalling pricing pressure is deepening rather than stabilising.
- ROIC (ratios page) falls below 55% (versus FY2022's 64.6%), indicating the capital-return advantage over peers is eroding.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
FCF conversion (FCF/NI) is strong and stable at 96.7% TTM, in line with the 92-98% range over the past five years [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/nse/TCS/financials/cash-flow-statement/). The balance sheet carries net cash (net debt/EBITDA -0.47x) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/nse/TCS/financials/balance-sheet/), giving headroom to keep funding bolt-on deals like the €320m MHP acquisition and the $1.5bn Porsche contract from internal cash without raising debt or equity [Likely] (source: https://www.ndtv.com/india-news/tcs-to-acquire-porsches-it-consulting-arm-mhp-for-320-million-euros-11954679#publisher=newsstand). Institutions own 89.3% of shares and insiders 0.01% [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/nse/TCS/statistics/), a widely-held, promoter-light reported free float typical of an Indian IT major.

## 6. Unusual items
TCS reports in INR but bills clients mostly in USD/GBP/EUR, so currency translation (not just market beta, which is low at 0.18 [Certain], source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/nse/TCS/statistics/) is a meaningful swing factor in reported results. Otherwise none material: short interest and shares-outstanding change are not disclosed on stockanalysis.com [data unavailable].
