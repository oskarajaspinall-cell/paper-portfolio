# Macro overlay: PepsiCo, Inc. (PEP) — 2026-10-09
## Assessment
PEP's thesis is fundamental (North America turnaround vs. international growth, peer valuation gap), but two macro variables can shift the odds and valuation range. **Chain 1 — real yields → discount rate → multiple**: PEP is low-beta (0.36) [Certain, stockanalysis.com/stocks/pep/statistics/], a "bond-proxy" staple whose multiple is sensitive to the risk-free rate in its DCF. 10y real yield (TIPS, DFII10) is 2.92% (2026-10-07), +0.49pp/1m, +0.61pp/3m [Certain, https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=DFII10], while breakevens (T10YIE) are flat at 2.35% [Certain, https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=T10YIE] — a real-rate/policy move, not an inflation scare, so it mechanically raises PEP's cost of equity. **Chain 2 — dollar → FX translation**: PEP's one growth bright spot is 8% international organic growth [Certain, note §1]; the broad dollar index (DTWEXBGS) rose +2.86pts/1m to 121.4 [Certain, https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=DTWEXBGS], which would mute that growth in USD.

**Market pricing (proxy, inference)**: the 2y yield (4.77%) sits ~89bp above effective fed funds (3.88%) [Certain, FRED], implying the market prices further tightening, not near-term cuts **(inference)** — a headwind for bond-proxy re-rating hopes. HY spreads (3.09%, +0.4pp) and VIX (15.08, low) show no broad risk-premium stress [Certain, FRED]: this is a discount-rate story, not a credit/sentiment one.

**Bull**: real yields roll over toward ~2.5% and the dollar weakens → faster re-rating toward peers, better FX translation. **Bear**: real yields push above ~3.25% and the dollar extends gains → more multiple compression plus FX drag, on top of the guidance cut. **Tilt**: small, toward bear — rates/dollar add a modest headwind, but North America execution stays the dominant driver.
