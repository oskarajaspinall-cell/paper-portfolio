# Macro overlay: Grab Holdings Limited (GRAB) — 2026-10-08
## Assessment
Two channels: (1) US real yields → discount rate → GRAB's growth multiple (it trades on EV/Sales 2.1x and EV/EBITDA 23.2x against still-thin TTM profitability, factsheet) [Certain]; (2) broad dollar strength → SEA local-currency (IDR/MYR/VND/PHP) revenue → USD-reported revenue/margin via translation [Likely] **(inference, no direct ASEAN FX series in the FRED snapshot)**.

Market pricing — facts [Certain], FRED, 2026-10-06/07: 10y real yield (DFII10) 2.91%, +61bp over 3m; 10y nominal (DGS10) 5.27%, +72bp 3m; 2y (DGS2) 4.79% vs effective fed funds 3.88%; breakeven inflation (T10YIE) 2.36%, +11bp 3m; HY credit spread (BAMLH0A0HYM2) 3.03%, +36bp 3m; broad dollar index (DTWEXBGS) 121.4, +2.86 over 1m (recent acceleration), +0.70 over 3m. Proxy/inference [Likely]: 2y yield sitting ~90bp above fed funds implies the market is pricing a tightening-biased path, not cuts — a genuine financial-conditions tightening, reinforced by the real-yield rise, not just lagging core-CPI data (July/Aug). This raises the discount rate applied to GRAB's 2028 EBITDA guidance and the multiple the market pays for it.

GRAB's own -51% 12m move and -25.6pp 3m underperformance vs SPY (factsheet) is far larger than real-yield-driven multiple compression alone would produce for a diversified index facing the same move; company-specific drivers (Atome cash deal, Indonesia commission-cap risk, take-rate pressure) dominate [Likely] **(inference)**. Macro is an amplifier, not the thesis.

Bull: real yields plateau/fall, dollar stabilizes → multiple re-rates, translation drag fades.
Bear: real yields and the dollar extend their recent rise → further multiple compression and FX translation drag, compounding weak sentiment.
