# Lear Corporation (LEA) — Core initiation — 2026-11-09
## 1. Business
Tier-1 auto supplier with two segments, Seating and E-Systems (wiring, connection and high-voltage power distribution), selling to global automakers on multi-year awards, with production tied to industry volumes. Nine-month 2026 net sales of $17,805.2m were split North America $7,259.9m, Europe and Africa $6,609.5m, Asia $3,188.0m `[company-reported]` (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/842162/000084216226000092/lear-20261003.htm). Europe and Asia each exceed ~10% of sales; the filing gives no separate China figure.

## 2. Quality
Mediocre-margin but steady operator. Gross margin 7.8% and operating margin 4.6% TTM are flat over five years (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/lea/financials/income-statement/) [Certain]. ROIC 11.4% TTM, rising from 9.5% in FY2021, and ROCE 12.9%, both at their 2-year high (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/lea/financials/ratios/?p=trailing) [Certain]. Net debt/EBITDA 1.54x is moderate; Altman Z 2.86, Piotroski F 7 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/lea/statistics/). Moat is narrow: scale, vertical integration in seating and just-in-time plant proximity raise switching costs for programs in production, but OEMs hold pricing power and re-bid [Likely]. Q3 revenue and core operating earnings grew for a fourth straight quarter despite weaker industry production; FY26 revenue guidance was cut for China and FX, core operating earnings guidance held (source: https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/lear-reports-third-quarter-2026-results-year-over-year-growth-in-revenue-core-operating-earnings-net-income-and-earnings-per-share-302924380.html) [Likely].

## 3. Valuation
The price sits below its own 5y minimum on every multiple: P/E 9.7x (5y median 14.4x), EV/EBITDA 4.6x (6.6x), P/FCF 7.6x (13.2x), P/B 1.1x (1.5x); FCF yield 13.19% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/lea/statistics/) [Certain]. Versus peers (ADNT, BWA, APTV, MGA, VC) P/E is -42% and EV/EBITDA -6%, so the EV/EBITDA discount is small while P/E is wide [Certain]. The reverse DCF implies year-1 revenue shrinking -8.4%, i.e. the market prices a cyclical downturn plus margin pressure [Likely]. Mechanical fair value is base 200.42 against a 110.28 close, but the bear case is 31.11 and DCF and EV/EBITDA disagree, so treat the base as optimistic [Guessing]. Price is -14.1% vs the 200-day average with 6-month momentum -19.8%: the market sees auto-cycle and tariff risk.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
FCF conversion is 119.9% of net income TTM and shares are down -5.53% YoY (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/lea/statistics/) [Certain]. Nine-month 2026 buybacks cost $350.0m cash and dividends $119.2m `[company-reported]` (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/842162/000084216226000092/lear-20261003.htm). Authorization was raised to $1.5B on 2026-09-24 (source: https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/lear-increases-share-repurchase-authorization-to-1-5-billion-and-extends-authorization-302889718.html) [Certain]. Buying below history at low multiples is sensible, with leverage kept moderate.

## 6. Unusual items
- Tariffs/USMCA: the 10-Q says tariffs did not materially affect gross profit in the first nine months, but the U.S. declined to extend USMCA in its current form on 2026-07-01, triggering rolling annual reviews; Mexico, Canada and China sourcing exposure persists (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/842162/000084216226000092/lear-20261003.htm) [Certain]. The prior tariff risk-factor detail was replaced by a reference to the 10-K.
- CFO succession: Jared Fedele succeeds Jason Cardew effective 2027-03-01 (source: https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/lear-announces-cfo-succession-plan-302913024.html) [Certain]; orderly.
