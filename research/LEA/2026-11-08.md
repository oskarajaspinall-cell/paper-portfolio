# Lear Corporation (LEA) — Core initiation — 2026-11-08
## 1. Business
Lear sells automotive seating systems and E-Systems (wire harnesses, electronic and high-voltage power distribution) to vehicle makers, on multi-year programmes. Q3 2026 sales were Seating $4,326.1M and E-Systems $1,446.9M [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/842162/000084216226000092/lear-20261003.htm). Europe and Africa contributed $1,982.2M and Asia $977.7M of Q3 sales [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/842162/000084216226000092/lear-20261003.htm); China is not broken out separately. [Certain]

## 2. Quality
A thin-margin, scale-driven supplier, not a high-return franchise. Operating margin is 4.6% and gross margin 7.8%, both flat over five years; ROIC is 11.4% TTM vs 9.6% a year earlier, a genuine rise, and the fiscal-year path is 9.5% to 11.0% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/lea/financials/ratios/). [Certain] Piotroski F is 7 and Altman Z 2.86, adequate rather than strong (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/lea/statistics/). Net debt/EBITDA is 1.54x, stable. The moat is vertical integration, customer-qualified programmes and switching costs in seating, which protect against share loss but not against pricing pressure from OEMs. [Likely] FCF conversion is 119.9% of net income, but FCF margin is 2.9% against 3.2% a year earlier, so cash generation is not improving. [Certain] Q3 revenue and operating earnings grew despite weaker global production, and core operating earnings guidance was held while revenue guidance was cut for China and FX (headline: https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/lear-reports-third-quarter-2026-results-year-over-year-growth-in-revenue-core-operating-earnings-net-income-and-earnings-per-share-302924380.html). [Likely]

## 3. Valuation
The price assumes structural decline. P/E of 9.7x, EV/EBITDA of 4.6x, EV/Sales of 0.3x, P/FCF of 7.6x and P/B of 1.1x are all at or below the bottom of their five-year ranges (P/E median 14.4x, EV/EBITDA median 6.6x). FCF yield is 13.19%. Against peers, EV/EBITDA (+3%) and P/FCF (+8%) are in line; P/E is 52% below the peer median. [Certain] The reverse DCF implies year-1 revenue shrinking 8.4%, at odds with Q3 growth. [Likely] Inform-only base fair value is 200.42 vs a 110.28 close, but it is DCF-led and the bear case is 31.11; the EV/EBITDA leg gives 167.91. The DCF looks generous for a low-margin cyclical. [Guessing] The stock is 14.1% below its 200-day average and trails SPY by 20.0pp over six months. (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/lea/statistics/)

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROIC (ratios page, TTM) falls below 9%.
- Operating margin (income statement, TTM) falls below 4.0%.
- FCF margin (cash flow and income statement, TTM) falls below 2.0%.
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) rises above 2.0x.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Shares outstanding are down 5.53% year on year, so buybacks are real and funded from FCF (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/lea/statistics/). The board increased and extended the repurchase authorization on 2026-09-24 (https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/lear-increases-share-repurchase-authorization-to-1-5-billion-and-extends-authorization-302889718.html). [Certain] At a 13.19% FCF yield and below-median multiples, repurchases are accretive if cash flow holds. Leverage stayed near 1.5x throughout, so the buyback has not stretched the balance sheet. [Likely] Acquisitions are in automation and AI tooling and appear small. [Likely]

## 6. Unusual items
- Tariffs and trade: the latest 10-Q says tariffs did not materially affect gross profit in the first nine months of 2026, but now discusses tariff offset credits and a refund-related reduction of 2026 sales, and notes the US declined to extend USMCA in its current form [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/842162/000084216226000092/lear-20261003.htm). The old tariff risk factor was removed and the discussion moved into MD&A. [Certain] Mexico-based production makes USMCA a live exposure. [Likely]
- Management: CFO succession announced, with the new CFO effective March 2027 (https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/lear-announces-cfo-succession-plan-302913024.html). Orderly; low risk. [Certain]
- Short interest is 6.31% of float, up 43.2% on the prior month; beta is 1.28 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/lea/statistics/). [Certain]
