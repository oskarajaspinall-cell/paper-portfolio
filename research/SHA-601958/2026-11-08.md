## 2. Quality
Returns are high and have risen over five years. TTM ROIC is 27.9% (FY2021 6.5%) and TTM ROCE is 21.8%. TTM operating margin is 32.3% (FY2021 9.8%) and net margin is 25.1% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sha/601958/financials/ratios/). [Certain]

Most of that gain is a commodity upcycle, not proven structural advantage. Margins stepped up in FY2023 and then held. Net debt/EBITDA is -1.19x, so it carries net cash, and Altman Z is 13.18 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sha/601958/statistics/). [Certain]

The moat is probably ore-body ownership and low cost [Guessing]. I could not verify reserves or cost position, because the NAV input is [data unavailable].

Cash conversion has weakened. FCF margin fell to 13.8% TTM from 21.2% a year earlier, and FCF/NI is 55.0%. Piotroski F is 4 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sha/601958/financials/cash-flow-statement/?p=trailing). [Certain]

## 3. Valuation
At 16.65 CNY the stock trades at 13.9x P/E, which is 12% of its 5y range (median 15.9x), and 8.8x EV/EBITDA, which is 22% of its range (median 10.2x). Both are cheap against its own history [Certain].

Against peers (Zijin, China Molybdenum, Xiamen Tungsten) it is dearer: P/E +28%, EV/EBITDA +39% and EV/Sales +179%. P/FCF is 25.3x, 62% up its range, and FCF yield is 3.95%, so cash earnings are not cheap (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sha/601958/statistics/). [Certain]

The price is 14.7% below its 50-day average and 3-month momentum is -27.2%. The market is pricing a fall from peak molybdenum prices and margins [Likely]. The mechanical fair value (EV/EBITDA only, no NAV) is 19.55 CNY base, 13.49 bear and 27.26 bull: +17.4% upside against -19.0% downside. That is a modest skew for a cyclical [Likely].
