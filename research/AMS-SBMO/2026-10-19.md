# SBM Offshore N.V. (AMS:SBMO) — Core initiation — 2026-10-19

## 1. Business
SBM Offshore designs, builds, leases and operates Floating Production, Storage and Offloading vessels (FPSOs) for offshore oil & gas producers, mainly under 15-20 year lease-and-operate contracts with national/major oil companies (e.g., Petrobras) [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/ams/SBMO/). Revenue also includes turnkey/EPC construction fees recognized through the build cycle and fees from joint-venture operating stakes (e.g., FSO Chalchi) [Likely] (source: https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/06/29/3318650/0/en/SBM-Offshore-completes-US-465-million-financing-of-FSO-Chalchi.html).

## 2. Quality
Returns have improved materially: TTM ROIC 12.9% and ROCE 14.5%, up from 6.6%/7.2% in FY2021, with gross margin up to 35.2% and operating margin to 30.5% TTM (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/ams/SBMO/financials/ratios/) [Certain]. Piotroski F-score is 7/9 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/ams/SBMO/statistics/) [Certain]. FPSO leasing has real barriers to entry: capital intensity, track-record requirements in tenders, and limited global shipyard capacity (SBM, Modec, Yinson are the main independent lessors) support pricing power on new awards [Likely], a thesis judgement rather than a fact-sheet figure. The business stays leverage-intensive: net debt/EBITDA fell from 8.28x (FY2021) to 3.26x (TTM) via asset sales (same source) [Certain], but Altman Z is 1.38, in the distress band of an industrial model that fits a leveraged, long-contracted lessor poorly (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/ams/SBMO/statistics/) [Likely]. Customer concentration (Petrobras) and new-build execution risk are the main competitive risks [Guessing].

## 3. Valuation
SBMO is cheap on equity multiples but not on enterprise multiples versus four listed peers (FTI, BIT:SPM, TYO:6269, KLSE:YINSON). P/E of 7.5x is 74% below the peer median (29.3x) and in the bottom quintile of its own 5-year range; P/FCF (2.1x) and P/B (1.0x) are 83% and 66% below peer medians (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/ams/SBMO/financials/ratios/) [Certain]. But EV/EBITDA (8.1x) and EV/Sales (2.5x) are 30% and 55% above peer medians [Certain] — the market still prices SBMO's historically higher leverage even as net debt/EBITDA has fallen to 3.26x. The DCF-based fair value (base +635.5% vs last close; bear -68.31 EUR) looks like a modeling artifact: TTM FCF conversion of 354% of net income is unusually high and likely non-repeating, so a DCF extrapolating it is implausible [Likely] and is excluded here. Cheap equity multiples against rich EV/EBITDA imply the market pays for continued deleveraging, not cash generation already achieved.

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page, site definition) rises back above 6x, reversing the deleveraging behind the cheap equity multiples.
- ROIC (ratios page) falls below 8%, back toward the FY2021-2022 level, signaling fading project returns.
- FCF margin (cash flow vs income statement) turns negative, reverting to the FY2021-2023 pattern of capex outspending operating cash flow.
- Gross margin (income statement) falls below 24%, reversing the margin recovery since FY2024.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
TTM FCF conversion is 354.3% of net income and FCF margin 56.0%, both well above the 5-year average, flattered by asset-sale proceeds and project-financing receipts rather than pure operating cash generation (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/ams/SBMO/financials/cash-flow-statement/) [Likely]. Shares outstanding fell 3.13% year-on-year, consistent with an active buyback (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/ams/SBMO/statistics/) [Certain]; weekly repurchase transactions were still being disclosed as of October 2026 (source: https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/10/07/3376712/0/en/weekly-share-repurchase-program-transaction-details.html) [Certain]. The company is also monetizing minority JV stakes (intended divestment of its FSO Chalchi interest to NYK) (source: https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/06/04/3307033/0/en/SBM-Offshore-intends-to-divest-minority-interest-in-FSO-Chalchi.html) [Likely], funding deleveraging and buybacks together.

## 6. Unusual items
Altman Z-score of 1.38 sits in the conventional distress zone despite falling leverage and rising returns; this model fits long-contracted, asset-leasing businesses poorly but is flagged for monitoring (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/ams/SBMO/statistics/) [Likely]. The fact sheet's DCF fair value (Section 3) is an outlier, excluded from the valuation judgement. Beta is low at 0.65; institutional ownership is 43.9% and insider ownership only 0.47% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/ams/SBMO/statistics/) [Certain]. Short interest is [data unavailable].
