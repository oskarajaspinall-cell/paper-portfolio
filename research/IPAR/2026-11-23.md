# Interparfums (IPAR) — Core initiation — 2026-11-23
## 1. Business
Licensed-brand fragrance house: it develops, manufactures and distributes perfumes under long-term licences from fashion and luxury brands (Montblanc, Jimmy Choo, Coach, Lacoste, Guess and others), run through US-based and European-based operations (source: https://www.interparfumsinc.com) [Certain]. ## 2. Quality
Good, not exceptional, business. ROIC 18.3% TTM against 21.7% in FY2025 and a 23.5% peak in FY2024; gross margin 59.8%; net cash (net debt/EBITDA -0.10x); Altman Z 6.87 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/ipar/financials/ratios/) [Certain].
Direction change: ROIC, ROCE and operating margin (17.9% vs 19.3% a year earlier) are all falling over the last 12 months despite rising 5-year trends. Q3 sales were stable and nine-month sales up only slightly, with US gift-set delays cited (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/ipar/transcripts/725208-q3-2026/) [Likely]. Returns are slipping on flat sales, not collapsing. [Likely]
Moat is licence-based: renewals depend on the brand owners, so it is a contract moat, not an owned-brand moat. A new Puma licence shows the pipeline is still being fed (source: https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/09/22/3366892/7735/en/interparfums-inc-announces-exclusive-worldwide-license-agreement-with-puma.html) [Certain].
