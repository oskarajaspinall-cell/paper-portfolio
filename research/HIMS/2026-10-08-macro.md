# Macro overlay: Hims & Hers Health, Inc. (HIMS) — 2026-10-08
## Assessment
HIMS is a domestic US telehealth retailer with no FX or commodity exposure; the core thesis (FDA action against compounded GLP-1 products, margin collapse, litigation) is company-specific [Certain]. But HIMS is high-beta (5y beta 2.43 [Certain], factsheet statistics), FCF-negative-conversion, trading at 78.4x P/FCF and 2.8x EV/Sales [Certain] (factsheet valuation table) — a multiple that leans on a future-earnings narrative, so the discount rate is a live secondary lever.

**Chain**: real yields -> discount rate / equity risk premium -> valuation multiple on a richly-priced, FCF-negative growth stock.

**What's priced**: US 10y real yield (DFII10) is 2.91% on 2026-10-06, up 0.48pp ~1m / 0.61pp ~3m [Certain] (fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=DFII10, 2026-10-06) — a recent, genuine tightening, not a lagging series. The 2y Treasury (4.79%) sits well above effective fed funds (3.88%) [Certain] (FRED, 2026-10-06); **as a proxy** this implies the market prices further policy tightening, not near-term cuts **(inference)**. High-yield spreads also widened (+0.35pp 1m) [Certain] (FRED, 2026-10-06), a mild risk-appetite headwind for a distressed small-cap (Altman Z 1.98, Piotroski F 2/9).

**Bull**: real yields stabilize/retreat, VIX stays low -> compression pressure eases, leaving the regulatory/margin story to decide the stock.
**Bear**: real yields and credit spreads keep rising on sticky inflation -> multiple compression stacks on the regulatory/margin deterioration, amplifying downside.
**Tilt**: toward bear, small — macro adds a second headwind but the FDA/margin/litigation thesis stays dominant **(inference)**.
