# Hims & Hers Health, Inc. (HIMS) — Core initiation — 2026-10-08
## 1. Business
Direct-to-consumer telehealth platform selling subscription access to prescription and OTC treatments (weight loss/GLP-1, sexual health, hair loss, dermatology, mental health) via affiliated medical groups, fulfilled through owned 503A/503B pharmacies and a peptide manufacturing facility [Certain] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1773751/000177375126000022/hims-20251231.htm).

## 2. Quality
Growth has relied on heavy direct-to-consumer marketing, not a defensible moat; the 10-K cites only "brand trust," "technology" and "clinical excellence," not switching costs or scale economics, in a market of "multiple competitors" [Certain] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1773751/000177375126000022/hims-20251231.htm). Profitability has reversed from FY2025: TTM operating margin is -3.8% (vs +5.2% FY2025), net margin -5.5% (vs +5.5%), ROE -32.0% (vs +25.2%) and ROCE -4.5% (vs +6.9%) [Certain] (source: factsheet, IS/RA pages). Gross margin has slipped from an 82.0% FY2023 peak to 69.5% TTM [Certain]. The compounded-GLP-1 weight-loss line, the main growth driver, faces direct regulatory risk: two FDA warning letters in September 2025, a further warning letter in December 2025, and a February 2026 FDA statement naming Hims while manufacturers push to add semaglutide/tirzepatide to the restricted-compounding list [Certain] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1773751/000177375126000022/hims-20251231.htm). Piotroski F-score is 2/9 and Altman Z is 1.98 (distress grey zone) [Certain] (source: factsheet statistics page).

## 3. Valuation
At a 6.4bn USD market cap, HIMS trades on 2.8x EV/Sales (peer median 0.6x, +409%), 78.4x P/FCF (peer median 4.8x, +1539%) and 21.3x P/B (peer median 1.3x, +1491%) against telehealth peers TDOC, GDRX, LFMD and AMWL [Certain] (source: factsheet valuation table). P/B is also 179% of its own 5-year range (above the 5-year max); P/FCF sits at the 71st percentile of its 5-year range [Certain]. No DCF, rNPV or P/E fair value could be computed from stockanalysis.com data (FCF/EPS history too volatile or negative) [Certain] (source: factsheet fair-value section), so there is no own-history anchor to lean on. The price assumes the weight-loss franchise keeps compounding growth despite the FDA actions above, and that margins recover quickly from their TTM deterioration — a high bar given 24.76% of float is sold short [Certain] (source: factsheet statistics page).

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- TTM operating margin (income statement) turns and sustains above the FY2025 level of 5.2% for two consecutive quarters.
- Gross margin (income statement) stabilizes at or above 75% rather than continuing its slide from 82.0% (FY2023) to 69.5% (TTM).
- Piotroski F-score (statistics page) rises from 2 to 6 or above, evidencing a genuine fundamental turn.
- No further FDA warning letters or compounding-restriction actions against Hims's GLP-1 products within the next two reporting periods (10-Q/10-K disclosures).

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
FCF margin has collapsed to 3.2% TTM from a 14.2% FY2024 peak, and FCF conversion (FCF/NI) is -58.5% TTM — cash generation is now running behind, not ahead of, accounting earnings [Certain] (source: factsheet CF/IS pages). Net debt/EBITDA is not meaningful (TTM EBITDA near zero, producing a -69.3x ratio) [Certain]. Shares outstanding fell 7.33% YoY, consistent with buybacks, but this is a secondary consideration against the margin and cash-conversion deterioration above [Certain] (source: factsheet statistics page).

## 6. Unusual items
Short interest is 24.76% of float (down 9.8% month-on-month) [Certain] (source: factsheet statistics page) — a heavily contested stock. Multiple plaintiff law firms (Kaplan Fox, Robbins LLP) are soliciting investors ahead of a November 2, 2026 lead-plaintiff deadline in a securities class action against Hims & Hers [Likely] (source: https://www.newsfilecorp.com/release/317760/Kaplan-Fox-Kilsheimer-LLP-Reminds-Hims-Hers-Health-Inc.-HIMS-Investors-of-the-Approaching-Lead-Plaintiff-Deadline-on-November-2-2026); the underlying allegations are not detailed in these notices and are unconfirmed by any filing read for this note. The company appointed a new Chief Accounting Officer effective October 7, 2026, filed as an SEC Item 5.02 8-K [Certain] (source: https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20261007133997/en/Hims-Hers-Names-Jon-Franklin-as-Chief-Accounting-Officer/).
