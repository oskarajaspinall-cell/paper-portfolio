# Powszechny Zaklad Ubezpieczen (WSE:PZU) — Core initiation — 2026-11-08
## 1. Business
Poland's largest insurer: property & casualty (motor and non-motor), life, health, plus investment income on float and a banking stake [Likely]. Revenue is earned premiums and investment returns; the franchise is domestic, so exposure is chiefly Polish rates, claims inflation and regulation. Revenue split by country is [data unavailable] in the fact sheet (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/wse/PZU/). [Likely]

## 2. Quality
Returns are high for an insurer: ROE 20.3% in FY2025 versus 13.0% in FY2021, ROIC 18.5% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/wse/PZU/financials/ratios/) [Certain]. The durable edge is scale and distribution in a concentrated home market, a plausible but unverified moat [Likely]. Direction is the concern: TTM ROIC is 15.2%, down from 19.0% a year earlier and below its 2-year high of 19.2% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/wse/PZU/financials/ratios/?p=trailing) [Certain]. FY2022-FY2024 earnings were lifted by a hard-market and rate backdrop that may normalise [Guessing]. Piotroski F is 5, middling (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/wse/PZU/statistics/) [Certain]. Operating and net margins are [data unavailable], so underwriting quality cannot be tested from the fact sheet.

## 3. Valuation
At 73.00 PLN: P/E 9.7x, above its 5y maximum of 9.1x (median 8.1x), but 26% below the peer median of 13.2x (Allianz, AXA, Generali, NN) [Certain]. P/B 0.9x is also above its 5y range (0.6x-0.8x) yet 50% below peers at 1.8x; EV/EBITDA 4.8x sits at 26% of its range. FCF yield of 21.21% is inflated by insurance float movements and is not a reliable yield [Likely]. Model fair value (own-history, inform-only) is 77.65 PLN base, 52.65 bear and 81.84 bull, so base upside is +6.4% and bear downside -27.9% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/wse/PZU/). The price already assumes ROE holds near 20%; the discount to Western peers is a Poland/governance discount rather than an obvious mispricing [Likely]. Asymmetry is poor.
