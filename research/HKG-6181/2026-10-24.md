# Laopu Gold Co., Ltd. (HKG:6181) — Core initiation — 2026-10-24
## 1. Business
Laopu Gold is a Beijing-based jeweller selling gold jewellery under its own brand, classified as Luxury Goods (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/6181/) [Certain]. It earns a gross margin near 40% and reports in CNY, with shares quoted in HKD (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/6181/financials/income-statement/) [Certain]. It is expanding its store footprint, including a leased floor in Hong Kong's Central district (source: https://www.scmp.com/business/article/3370495/laopu-gold-deepens-hong-kong-footprint-entire-floor-lease-central-tower-sources) [Likely].

## 2. Quality
Returns are very high: TTM ROIC 39.9%, ROCE 68.5%, ROE 65.8%, operating margin 26.4% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/6181/financials/ratios/) [Certain]. Operating margin rose from 12.7% in FY2021 [Certain].

Three weaknesses:
- Cash conversion is negative: TTM FCF margin -8.0% and FCF/NI -40.5%, after -25.6% in FY2025 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/6181/financials/cash-flow-statement/) [Certain]. Profit is being absorbed by growth spending, probably stock and stores [Guessing].
- Gross margin slipped from 41.2% in FY2024 to 37.6% in FY2025, recovering to 39.5% TTM [Certain]. Falling gross margin hints at weaker pricing power [Guessing].
- Piotroski F is 3 and shares rose 3.72% YoY (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/6181/statistics/) [Certain].

Leverage is modest at 0.47x net debt/EBITDA [Certain]. The brand moat is plausible but unproven by cash generation [Guessing].

The share price has fallen 59.6% over 12 months to 284.60 HKD, and 52-week low 274.00 was set on the last close day (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/6181/history/) [Certain].
