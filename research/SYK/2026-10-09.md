# Stryker Corporation (SYK) — Core initiation — 2026-10-09

## 1. Business
Stryker designs and sells medical technology across MedSurg & Neurotechnology (surgical equipment, endoscopy, neurovascular) and Orthopaedics (joint replacement, trauma, spine, Mako robotics) [Certain] (source: https://www.stryker.com/us/en/about.html). $25.1bn global sales in 2025, sold mainly to hospitals via a direct sales/capital-equipment model with recurring implant and instrument revenue [Certain] (source: https://www.stryker.com/us/en/about.html).

## 2. Quality
Returns are solid but not clearly widening: TTM ROIC 12.5% is flat vs 5 years ago (-0.2pp), while ROCE has risen to 14.9% (+2.2pp) and net/FCF margins are both trending up (net margin 14.4% TTM, FCF margin 18.2% TTM) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/syk/financials/ratios/, https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/syk/financials/income-statement/). FCF conversion is strong (126% TTM) and leverage is falling (net debt/EBITDA 1.62x vs 2.13x in FY2021) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/syk/financials/cash-flow-statement/). Piotroski F-score 7/9 and Altman Z 5.21 show no distress [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/syk/statistics/). Moat is scale, patent base (14.6K patents) and surgeon/hospital switching costs embedded in capital equipment (e.g., Mako robotics) rather than a single blockbuster product [Likely] (source: https://www.stryker.com/us/en/about.html). A September 2026 management disclosure of "persisting peripheral vascular problems," reported third-party (via a law-firm investor-alert site, so treated cautiously), drove an 8.8% single-day price fall and is a live quality question into Q3 results (Oct 29) [Likely] (source: https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/stryker-nyse-syk-scrutinized-amid-disclosure-of-persisting-peripheral-vascular-problems----hbss-302896305.html).

## 3. Valuation
Despite a 25% 12-month decline, SYK is not cheap. The DCF/P-E blended base fair value is $271 vs the $276.97 close — essentially fair value, only -2.2% (base), with bear/bull of -35.1%/+14.2%, i.e. more downside than upside in the model's own range [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/syk/ fact sheet fair-value block). On every multiple, SYK sits below its own 5-year range (P/E 28.7x vs 35.9-50.6x historically) but still trades 28-91% above the peer median (ZBH, MDT, BSX, ISRG), e.g. P/E 28.7x vs 21.6x peer median, EV/EBITDA 16.1x vs 12.6x [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/syk/financials/ratios/, peer statistics pages). The re-rating looks like the market pricing in real uncertainty (vascular issue, slower growth vs history), not a mispriced quality discount — the stock's own reverse-DCF implies 12.8% year-1 revenue growth is still required to justify the price [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/syk/).

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROIC (ratios page) falls below 10% for two consecutive fiscal years, signalling a genuine return erosion rather than mix noise.
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios/balance sheet pages) rises back above 2.2x without an offsetting acquisition-driven EBITDA step-up, indicating balance-sheet discipline has slipped.
- FCF conversion (cash flow vs income statement) falls below 90% for two consecutive years, outside its FY2021-25 range, implying earnings quality has deteriorated.
- The peripheral vascular issue disclosed in September 2026 proves to be a recurring product/quality problem (e.g., a formal recall or FDA warning letter) rather than an isolated event, confirmed in a subsequent SEC filing or company statement.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
FCF conversion is strong (126% TTM, averaging >100% over 5 of the last 5 years bar FY2022) and improving, funding bolt-on M&A (ZuriMED, shoulder implants, terms undisclosed) without stretching the balance sheet — net debt/EBITDA has fallen from 2.13x (FY2021) to 1.62x (TTM) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/syk/financials/cash-flow-statement/, https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/08/31/3353675/0/en/stryker-signs-definitive-agreement-to-acquire-zurimed-to-enhance-its-shoulder-offering.html). Shares outstanding are essentially flat (+0.05% YoY), so returns are not being diluted [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/syk/statistics/).

## 6. Unusual items
CEO Kevin Lobo will move to executive chair effective January 1, 2027, with Spencer Stiles succeeding as CEO; this is a planned, board-run succession rather than a forced exit, but any core thesis should watch for strategy continuity at the Q3/Q4 calls [Likely] (source: https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/10/06/3375406/0/en/stryker-announces-leadership-succession.html). Short interest is low (2.2% of float) but rose 10.5% month-on-month, consistent with the stock's weak momentum rather than a crowded short [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/syk/statistics/).
