# Macro overlay: Stryker Corporation (SYK) — 2026-10-09
## Assessment
SYK's thesis is dominated by company-specific factors — the September vascular-device disclosure, CEO succession, and a valuation at a 28-91% premium to peers (ZBH/MDT/BSX/ISRG) [Certain] (factsheet). One macro variable still shifts the valuation range: real yields. Chain: rising real yields → higher DCF discount rate → compression of SYK's premium multiple (28.7x P/E vs 21.6x peer median), independent of earnings [Likely] **(inference)**.

Facts (FRED, 2026-10-07/08): 10y real yield (DFII10) 2.92%, +0.61pp/3m; nominal 10y (DGS10) 5.28%, +0.72pp/3m; 2y (DGS2) 4.77%; fed funds (DFF) 3.88%, +0.25pp/1m; 10y breakeven (T10YIE) 2.35%, +0.12pp/3m. Breakeven rose only 12bp vs the nominal 10y's 72bp, so the move is overwhelmingly real-yield (growth/policy), not inflation [Certain, arithmetic]. Market pricing (proxy, Likely): 2y (4.77%) sitting ~89bp above fed funds (3.88%) implies the market prices "higher for longer," a headwind for duration-sensitive multiples; SYK's DCF already uses today's 5.28% risk-free rate [Certain, factsheet].

**Bull**: real yields retrace toward ~2.5% → lower discount rate re-rates SYK's multiple toward its own 5y range, lifting the $271 base fair value.
**Bear**: real yields extend past ~3.25% (DFII10) / 5.5% (DGS10) on sustained "higher for longer" pricing → further multiple compression, widening the gap to peers regardless of fundamentals.
**Tilt**: small, toward bear — rates are a secondary overlay on a thesis set mainly by product/governance issues; low 5y beta (0.78) [Certain, ST] historically dampens rate sensitivity, but the real-yield rise is live, not reversing.
