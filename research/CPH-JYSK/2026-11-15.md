# Jyske Bank A/S (CPH:JYSK) — Core initiation — 2026-11-15
## 1. Business
Danish universal bank: mortgage credit, retail and corporate lending, and wealth/asset management. Earnings come mainly from net interest income, fee income and trading/value adjustments [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/cph/JYSK/). No foreign country is shown as ~10%+ of revenue in the sources read; segment and country mix not verified [Guessing].

## 2. Quality
Solid, not exceptional. ROE is 9.8% TTM vs 13.6% in FY2023, so it has slipped from its peak, though still above FY2021 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/cph/JYSK/financials/ratios/) [Certain]. Net margin is 36.3% TTM, in the 37-40% band of the last three years [Certain]. ROIC, operating margin and net debt/EBITDA are [data unavailable], as expected for a bank. FCF and its conversion are meaningless for a bank: the cash flow swings between hugely positive and negative with lending and deposit flows. The "Direction change" flag (FCF margin rising over 12 months, falling over 5 years) is therefore noise [Likely]. The share count is down 5.25% YoY, so buybacks are real (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/cph/JYSK/statistics/) [Certain]. Management reported Q3 EPS growth ex value adjustments and a cost/income improvement in Q2 (source: headlines on https://stockanalysis.com/quote/cph/JYSK/) [Likely]. Moat is a regional franchise and mortgage book, not a structural advantage [Guessing]. Piotroski F is 4 (weak-middling).

## 3. Valuation
P/E is 13.6x against a 5y range of 5.4x-9.9x (median 7.6x), above the max. P/B is 1.2x against 0.6x-1.0x (median 0.7x), also above the max [Certain]. Against Danish peers it is cheaper: P/E 28% below the peer median of 19.0x and P/B 29% below 1.7x (n=3, source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/cph/DANSKE/statistics/, https://stockanalysis.com/quote/cph/SYDB/statistics/, https://stockanalysis.com/quote/cph/RILBA/statistics/) [Certain]. The FCF yield of 17.31% is not meaningful for a bank. The mechanical base fair value of 1,004.83 DKK is 12.2% below the 1,144.00 close, with bull 1,202.51; the fair value is flagged unreliable (methods disagree 4.3x), so weigh it lightly [Certain]. The price has already re-rated well beyond its own history; the case for owning it rests on peer relative value and trend (12m +44.8%, +16.1% above the 200-day), not on a discount [Likely].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROE (ratios page) falls below 8% on a TTM basis.
- Net margin (income statement page) falls below 32% TTM.
- Shares outstanding (statistics page, shares change YoY) turns positive, i.e. buybacks stop or dilution begins.
