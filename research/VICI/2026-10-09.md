# VICI Properties Inc. (VICI) — Core initiation — 2026-10-09

## 1. Business
VICI is a triple-net-lease REIT owning 63 gaming and 40 non-gaming experiential properties (~130m sq ft, ~66,000 hotel rooms), including Caesars Palace, MGM Grand and The Venetian Las Vegas [Certain] (source: https://www.viciproperties.com/). Tenants pay rent plus all property taxes, insurance and maintenance; VICI collects fixed, escalating rent under long-dated master leases, with embedded call rights/ROFRs on tenant assets funding further growth [Certain] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1705696/000170569626000034/vici-20251231.htm).

## 2. Quality
TTM ROIC is 7.7% (flat vs FY21, -0.4pp over 5y), ROE 9.8% [Certain] (source: fact sheet, ratios page). The moat is the real estate itself — Strip-adjacent, effectively irreplaceable gaming assets under 15-32-year lease terms (5-30-year renewals), 15 of 17 leases with CPI-linked escalators — giving visible, inflation-protected rent with minimal competitive erosion [Certain] (source: 10-K). The key risk is tenant concentration: Caesars (39%) and MGM (35%) supply 74% of annualized rent [Certain] (source: 10-K); the 10-K flags Las Vegas Strip revenue concentration as a risk factor. Investment-grade ratings and triple-net structuring (tenants bear operating risk) partly offset this, but quality here is largely a bet on two operators' solvency [Likely]. Net debt/EBITDA rose from 3.5x (FY21) to 4.9x TTM [Certain] (source: fact sheet, ratios page), worth watching given no organic margin lever offsets higher leverage.

## 3. Valuation
VICI trades below both its own history and listed peers: P/E 8.8x (5y range 10.8x-27.9x, below the 5y minimum), EV/EBITDA 11.7x (5y range 13.1x-27.3x, also below minimum), P/B 0.9x (5y range 1.1x-1.6x) [Certain] (source: fact sheet, ratios/history pages). Peer median (GLPI, EPR, O, NNN) P/E is 18.6x and EV/EBITDA 14.1x, putting VICI 53% and 17% below peers [Certain] (source: fact sheet). FCF yield is 10.42% [Certain] (source: fact sheet, statistics page). The stock is down 28.8% over 12 months and 16.6% below its 200-day average [Certain] (source: fact sheet, history page). The fact sheet's own-history fair value (P/FFO-proxy) is $33.78 base ($27.89 bear, $48.02 bull) versus last close $22.79 — bear case alone implies 22% upside [Certain] (source: fact sheet, inform-only). The market is pricing persistent multiple de-rating across net-lease REITs (peers are also cheap) and/or Caesars/MGM concentration concern, but not an imminent default scenario.

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROIC (ratios page) falls below 6% (TTM 7.7%), signalling embedded rent escalators no longer cover cost of capital.
- Net debt/EBITDA, site definition (ratios page) rises above 6.0x (TTM 4.82x), threatening the investment-grade rating underpinning VICI's low cost of capital.
- Piotroski F-Score (statistics page) falls to 2 or below (currently 3), indicating broader profitability/balance-sheet deterioration.
- ROE (ratios page) falls below 7%, suggesting rent escalators are being offset by rising leverage cost rather than adding to equity returns.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
As a REIT, VICI must distribute substantially all taxable income [Certain] (source: 10-K). FCF yield is 10.42% TTM [Certain] (source: fact sheet, statistics page). Growth is funded mainly through embedded tenant options (call rights/ROFRs on assets including Canyon Ranch, Homefield, further Caesars assets) and acquisitions — pending Golden Entertainment (7 properties, announced Nov 2025) and a $2.58bn real-estate debt book with $623m of future funding commitments [Certain] (source: 10-K). Shares outstanding grew only 1.84% YoY [Certain] (source: fact sheet), modest dilution relative to the growth pipeline. P/FCF and FCF margin are [data unavailable] on stockanalysis.com.

## 6. Unusual items
Tenant concentration stands out: Caesars and MGM together account for 74% of annualized rent [Certain] (source: 10-K) — a structural dependency not captured in the standard ratios above and the single largest risk to the thesis. Short interest is modest at 2.46% of float, up 2.0% versus the prior month [Certain] (source: fact sheet, statistics page) — not a crowded short. Institutional ownership is high at 97.4% [Certain] (source: fact sheet, statistics page).
