# Macro overlay: VICI Properties Inc. (VICI) — 2026-10-09
## Assessment
VICI is a triple-net-lease REIT with long-dated, CPI-linked-but-fixed rent escalators and ~4.9x net debt/EBITDA (TTM) [Certain] (source: fact sheet, ratios page) — the equity multiple is a leveraged bet on the discount rate.

Chain: real yields -> cost of capital/cap rates -> REIT multiple; nominal yields -> refinancing cost on the ~4.9x-levered balance sheet -> FFO growth, partly offset by CPI escalators on 15 of 17 leases [Certain] (source: note, 10-K).

Facts (FRED, 2026-10-07/08): 10y real yield (DFII10) 2.92%, +0.49pp/1m, +0.61pp/3m; 10y Treasury (DGS10) 5.28%, +0.72pp/3m; 2y (DGS2) 4.77%; fed funds (DFF) 3.88%; 10y breakeven (T10YIE) 2.35%, +0.12pp/3m; HY spread (BAMLH0A0HYM2) 3.09%, +0.39pp/3m [Certain] (https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=DFII10 etc., 2026-10-07/08).

Market pricing (inference): the 2y yield ~0.9pp above fed funds proxies a market still pricing tightening/term premium, not cuts; breakevens rose only 0.12pp vs real yields' 0.61pp over 3m — a hawkish repricing, not an inflation scare [Likely] **(inference)**. This, not fundamentals (ROIC/ROE roughly flat), plausibly explains most of VICI's -28.8% 12-month decline and sub-200-day-MA price [Likely] **(inference)**.

Bull: real yields roll over (confirmed easing path) -> cap rates compress -> multiple re-rates toward its 5y range/peers (base fair value $33.78 vs $22.79 close already implies room without this).
Bear: real yields extend higher -> cap rates stay elevated, refinancing cost rises against leverage -> FFO growth throttled despite escalators, multiple stuck at/below 5y lows.
Tilt: moderate toward bear — the real-yield trend is still rising, not stabilizing, and fundamentals alone don't explain the scale of the de-rating.
