# Vedanta Limited (NSE:VEDL) — Core initiation — 2026-10-24
## 1. Business
Diversified metals and mining group (zinc, aluminium, oil and gas, power, iron and steel) in the fact sheet's "Other Industrial Metals & Mining" industry (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/nse/VEDL/). [Likely] Per the stockanalysis.com overview, the aluminium, iron and steel, oil and gas and power units were demerged into four separately listed companies around June 2026, so the listed parent is now a different, smaller entity from the one in the historical financials (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/nse/VEDL/). [Guessing] Which segments the parent retains is unverified.

## 2. Quality
Historical (pre-demerger) quality looks decent but cyclical: ROIC 12.7% in FY2026 and 19.4% TTM, against 22.9% in FY2022; operating margin 24.1% in FY2026 (27.5% in FY2022); net debt/EBITDA 0.61x in FY2026 after 2.88x in FY2025; Piotroski F 7 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/nse/VEDL/financials/ratios/, https://stockanalysis.com/quote/nse/VEDL/statistics/). [Certain] FCF conversion was 107.1% of net income in FY2026 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/nse/VEDL/financials/cash-flow-statement/). [Likely] These are commodity-price-driven returns, not evidence of a durable moat. [Likely] None of the statements can be assumed to describe the post-demerger company, so the quality evidence is not usable for the current entity. A court ruling on bauxite pricing went against the company on 2026-10-01 (source: https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/city/bhubaneswar/hc-dismisses-vedantas-plea-for-bauxite-supply-at-2004-rates/articleshow/134614742.cms) [Likely].

## 3. Valuation
Screen multiples look extremely cheap: P/E 4.5x (5y median 12.1x, peers 10.9x), EV/EBITDA 3.9x (5y median 5.2x, peers 6.4x), P/FCF 4.7x, FCF yield 21.06% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/nse/VEDL/statistics/). Mechanical base fair value is 377.55 INR vs a 226.50 INR close (source: fact sheet, Fair value section). [Likely] This is an artefact: the price has fallen 69.2% in six months (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/nse/VEDL/history/) while trailing earnings, EBITDA and FCF still appear to be the pre-demerger consolidated group's. Price and earnings are on different perimeters, so the multiples and fair value cannot be trusted. [Guessing] On a like-for-like basis the stock is probably not as cheap versus peers as shown. The mismatch cannot be resolved from the fact sheet, and nothing is estimated.

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Operating margin (income statement page) falls below 15% on post-demerger reported numbers.
- Net debt/EBITDA (statistics page) rises above 2.5x.
- FCF margin (cash-flow page) turns negative on a full post-demerger fiscal year.
