# Ambev S.A. (BVMF:ABEV3) — Core initiation — 2026-10-31
## 1. Business
Brazil-centred brewer and soft-drink bottler (Americas, Brewers industry), selling beer, soft drinks and non-alcoholic drinks through a dense distribution network. Earnings are in BRL, so currency and Brazilian consumer demand drive results [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/bvmf/ABEV3/).

## 2. Quality
A high-return, net-cash franchise [Certain]. ROIC is 25.3% TTM against 25.4% in FY2021, i.e. steady (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/bvmf/ABEV3/financials/ratios/). Operating margin rose from 23.7% to 26.1% TTM and gross margin is 51.8% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/bvmf/ABEV3/financials/income-statement/). Net debt/EBITDA is -0.72x and Piotroski F is 7, Altman Z 4.61 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/bvmf/ABEV3/statistics/). The durable reason returns persist is scale and route-to-market density in a category where distribution and brand share are hard to replicate [Likely]. Beta is 0.22, so the stock is defensive against the market, but not against Brazil-specific shocks [Likely].

## 3. Valuation
At 14.60 BRL: P/E 14.5x (26% of own range, peers 18.5x), EV/EBITDA 7.1x (18% of range, peers 8.9x), P/FCF 9.1x (6% of range, peers 11.5x), FCF yield 10.97% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/bvmf/ABEV3/statistics/). It trades at a discount to peers on earnings and cash multiples, and near the bottom of its own range. Mechanical fair value is bear 11.33 / base 18.03 / bull 19.30 BRL, base +23.5%; the reverse DCF implies -3.9% year-one revenue growth, a pessimistic assumption for a business whose margins are expanding [Likely]. P/B of 2.5x and EV/Sales are above peers, reflecting superior returns. Fair value is inform-only.

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROIC (ratios page) falls below 20% on a TTM basis.
- Operating margin (income statement page) falls below 22% on a TTM basis.
- FCF conversion (FCF/NI, cash-flow page) falls below 100% on a TTM basis.
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) turns positive above 1.0x.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
FCF conversion is 156.8% of net income TTM, with FCF margin 27.7%; the lowest fiscal year shown is 97.6% [Certain]. Shares fell 0.79% YoY. The board approved a new buyback programme alongside Q3 results [Likely] (source: https://www.nasdaq.com/articles/ambev-sa-board-approves-share-buyback-program-190000000-common-shares). The balance sheet holds net cash, so buybacks and dividends are self-funded.
