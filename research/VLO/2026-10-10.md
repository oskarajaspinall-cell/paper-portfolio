# Valero Energy Corporation (VLO) — Core initiation — 2026-10-10

## 1. Business
Valero is the world's largest independent petroleum refiner, running Gulf Coast, Mid-Continent, West Coast and North Atlantic refineries that convert crude oil into gasoline, diesel and jet fuel sold wholesale and for export; it also produces ethanol and renewable diesel (Diamond Green Diesel) [Certain] (source: https://www.valero.com/). Revenue is driven by the refining margin — product prices less crude and operating cost — a commodity spread Valero does not set. [Certain]

## 2. Quality
Returns have risen over five years (ROIC +4.5pp, ROCE +5.4pp) [Certain] (source: fact sheet, ratios page), but the path is sharply cyclical: ROIC spiked to 38.1%/26.9% in FY2022-23, fell to 8.9% in FY2024, and is back to 25.2% TTM alongside today's war-disrupted crack spreads [Certain] (source: fact sheet, ratios page) — returns track the refining cycle, not a structural step-change. Net debt/EBITDA is low at 0.26x TTM, down from 2.40x in FY2021 [Certain] (source: fact sheet, balance sheet/ratios), giving balance-sheet resilience into the next downturn; Altman Z 4.90 and Piotroski F 7/9 support near-term solvency [Certain] (source: fact sheet, statistics page). Refining itself has no durable moat: margins are set by global refined-product/crude spreads and industry capacity utilization, not Valero's pricing power. Scale and complex Gulf Coast configuration lower unit cost but do not prevent margin reversion once crack spreads normalize [Likely].

## 3. Valuation
At 433.75 [Certain] (source: fact sheet, price history), VLO trades on EV/EBITDA of 9.6x (105% of its own 5y range, above the 5y max of 9.3x), P/FCF of 12.3x (146% of 5y max) and P/B of 5.0x (703% of 5y max) [Certain] (source: fact sheet, valuation table) — richer than at any point in five years on most measures. Versus peers (MPC, PSX, PBF, DK) it is 14% dearer on P/E, 34% dearer on EV/Sales, 13% cheaper on EV/EBITDA [Certain] (source: fact sheet, valuation table). Base-case fair value (300.30) implies the price already sits 30.8% above a through-cycle normalized value; the bull case (471.87) is just 8.8% above it [Certain] (source: fact sheet, fair value table) — the market prices close to the best plausible scenario, with a reverse-DCF-implied 4.7% year-1 revenue growth on top of currently elevated, war-disrupted margins [Certain] (source: fact sheet, fair value table). Little margin of safety.

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROIC (ratios page) falls below its FY2024 trough of 8.9%, confirming margins reverted to the prior cyclical low rather than a sustained higher level.
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) rises above 1.5x, signalling balance-sheet erosion through the cycle.
- FCF margin (cash flow/income statement) falls below its 5y low of 3.9% (FY2021).
- Gross margin (income statement) falls below 10%, the FY2024 cyclical trough.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Net debt/EBITDA of 0.26x TTM, down from 0.92x FY2025 and 1.04x FY2024 [Certain] (source: fact sheet, balance sheet/ratios), shows Valero deleveraging through this up-cycle rather than returning the full windfall via buybacks alone. Shares outstanding fell 4.6% YoY [Certain] (source: fact sheet, statistics page), consistent with continued repurchases. FCF margin is 7.6% TTM, up from 4.3% FY2025 [Certain] (source: fact sheet, ratios page); FCF conversion (FCF/NI) is 140.3% TTM, with a falling 5y trend (-236.7pp) off an unusually high FY2021 base (451%, when net income was near zero) [Certain] (source: fact sheet, cash flow/income statement) — conversion is normalizing rather than deteriorating.

## 6. Unusual items
Current earnings power is geopolitically driven: a WSJ headline on the fact sheet says refiners' margins are elevated by wars shrinking global energy supply [Likely] (source: https://www.wsj.com/business/energy-oil/american-oil-refiners-are-printing-money-as-wars-shrink-global-energy-supplies-c9240231), a plausibly transient rather than durable driver. Barron's flags demand destruction and a possible diesel export ban as emerging risks [Likely] (source: https://www.barrons.com/articles/diesel-prices-valero-marathon-refining-stocks-4487b345). CNBC calls the refiner rally "nearly unprecedented" and notes such runs have historically mean-reverted — sentiment, not a fact-sheet figure [Guessing] (source: https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/17/refiner-stocks-are-on-a-nearly-unprecedented-run-history-says-it-could-end-soon.html). Short interest is low (3.39% of float) [Certain] (source: fact sheet, statistics page) and an independent director (Matt Audette) joined the board in September 2026 [Certain] (source: https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260917413865/en/Valero-Energy-Corporation-Elects-Matt-Audette-to-its-Board-of-Directors/) — neither is decision-relevant.
