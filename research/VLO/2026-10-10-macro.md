# Macro overlay: Valero Energy Corporation (VLO) — 2026-10-10
## Assessment
VLO's earnings swing on refining crack spreads (crude vs. product prices), currently elevated by war-disrupted supply [Certain] (research note) — a commodity/geopolitical driver with no FRED series, outside this overlay. The macro variable that touches the thesis is the discount rate: the fact sheet's DCF uses the 10y Treasury as risk-free rate, now 5.22% on 2026-10-08, up 68bp over 3m, with the 10y real yield (DFII10) at 2.87%, up 56bp over 3m (fred.stlouisfed.org). Higher yields raise VLO's cost of equity (beta 0.59, so muted) and compress DCF fair value just as price sits ~44% above base fair value (300.30) [Certain] (fact sheet). HY credit spreads widened 45bp over 3m to 3.15% (fred.stlouisfed.org, 2026-10-08), a mild add to the same compression, though net debt/EBITDA of 0.26x limits financing exposure [Certain] (fact sheet).

Market pricing **(inference)**: the 2y yield (4.75%) ~87bp above effective fed funds (3.88%, both 2026-10-08, FRED) proxies a market expecting the Fed to hold, not cut soon — "higher for longer," a continuation, not a surprise.

**Bull**: yields stabilize while the war-driven crack-spread premium persists → discount-rate drag eases, gap narrows toward the bull fair value (471.87) [Likely].
**Bear**: yields keep rising (DFII10 >3.25%) while spreads normalize (Barron's, 2026-09-22, flags demand destruction/export-ban risk) → fair value falls on both legs, widening the premium toward the bear case (153.36) [Likely] **(inference)**.
**Tilt**: toward bear, small — fundamentals (crack-spread reversion) already dominate; low beta and minimal leverage limit rate/spread sensitivity.
