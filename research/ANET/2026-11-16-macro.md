## Assessment
**Chain:** real yields -> discount rate -> multiple. At 50.0x P/E and 44.3x EV/EBITDA (above its 5y max) (https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/anet/statistics/), the value sits far out in the cash flows, so the stock is a long-duration asset [Likely]. Earnings run on AI data-centre capex, which macro affects only indirectly. **Facts (FRED):** 10y TIPS 2.86% on 2026-11-12, +0.47pp in 3m; 10y Treasury 5.10%, +0.47pp; 2y 4.86%, +0.71pp; fed funds 3.88%; HY spread 3.2%, +0.49pp in 3m; VIX 18.7, +3.43pp in 1m (https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=DFII10, https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=BAMLH0A0HYM2). **Pricing (proxy):** the 2y sits ~1pp above fed funds, so the market prices hikes or no cuts **(inference)**. The stock is +42.4% over 12 months through this rise in yields, so the market has so far looked past higher real yields [Certain, fact sheet]. **Inference:** a further real-yield jump or a credit-spread widening would hit the multiple before it hit earnings. Beta of 1.64 amplifies it. Lower real yields help less, because they are partly priced.
Country risk: no foreign country is disclosed at about 10% of revenue (research note section 1), so there is no Step 0 item.
