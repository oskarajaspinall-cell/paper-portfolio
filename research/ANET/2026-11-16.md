# Arista Networks (ANET) — Core initiation — 2026-11-16

## 1. Business
Sells Ethernet switches and routers with its EOS software and network-data subscriptions to cloud, AI and enterprise data centres, plus campus and WAN. Hardware-led, software-differentiated; contract manufacturers build in Malaysia, Vietnam and Mexico (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1596532/000159653226000254/anet-20260930.htm) [Certain]. Two end customers were 16% and 26% of FY2025 revenue [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1596532/000159653226000254/anet-20260930.htm). No single foreign country disclosed at ~10% in the filing delta [Likely].

## 2. Quality
Exceptional economics: operating margin 43.8% TTM, rising from 31.4% in FY2021; ROIC 239.9% TTM; net cash (net debt/EBITDA -2.71x); FCF conversion 105.6% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/anet/financials/ratios/) [Certain]. The moat is software (EOS) and open-standards design adopted by hyperscalers: a real but contestable edge, since merchant silicon lets rivals match hardware [Likely].
Weak spots the evaluator must address:
- ROIC is 44.9pp below its 2-year high of 284.8%; FCF margin fell from 47.9% to 42.2% over 12 months (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/anet/financials/cash-flow-statement/?p=trailing). Likely working capital and inventory build for supply-constrained components [Likely].
- The new 10-Q language adds supply-shortage, purchase-commitment and excess-inventory risk, and says the long-term AI trajectory "is unknown" (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1596532/000159653226000254/anet-20260930.htm) [Certain].
- Gross margin is slipping on higher product costs (same filing) [Certain].

## 3. Valuation
Price 197.29 USD. P/E 50.0x sits at 90% of its 5y range, EV/EBITDA 44.3x above its 5y max, EV/Sales 19.8x above max, P/B 15.2x above max; premium to peers of +47% (P/E) and +79% (EV/EBITDA) (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/anet/statistics/) [Certain]. FCF yield is 2.01%. Mechanical base fair value is 73.75 USD (bull 136.28), below the price; the reverse DCF needs 49.4% year-1 revenue growth fading to terminal [Certain]. The DCF is mechanical and likely understates a compounder, but the price already discounts the strong guide issued with Q3 results [Likely]. Trend is supportive: +17.7% above the 200-day average, +42.4% over 12 months, +32.0pp vs SPY over 12 months [Certain]. This is a good business that is working, but not cheap; the case rests on growth persisting [Likely].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Gross margin (income statement page, TTM) falls below 58%.
- Operating margin (income statement, TTM) falls below 38%.
- FCF margin (cash-flow page, TTM) falls below 30%, or inventory days rise sharply with no revenue to match.
- Either of the two largest customers reduces its share to under 10% of revenue without replacement growth, or the next 10-K discloses a customer loss [company-reported basis].

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Share count is flat (-0.14% YoY), so there is no dilution, but no meaningful buyback shrink either (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/anet/statistics/) [Certain]. The cash pile funds R&D and supply commitments. FCF conversion is above 100% on a fiscal-year basis but slipped on a rolling basis as inventory built [Likely]. Piotroski F is 6; Altman Z 15.85 [Certain].

## 6. Unusual items
- Customer concentration: two customers are a large share of revenue and order timing is lumpy (filing above) [Certain].
- Tariffs and Asia-based manufacturing are a new explicit risk factor (filing above) [Certain].
- Beta is 1.64, so expect a deep drawdown in a risk-off or AI-capex scare; short interest is low at 1.14% of float (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/anet/statistics/) [Certain].
- Q3 results on 2026-11-03 beat and the stock jumped on 2026-11-06 (+7.3% on 2.0x volume) (source: https://www.tipranks.com/news/anet-stock-soars-as-aristas-ai-growth-smashes-q3-estimates) [Likely].
- Fact sheet "Next earnings date" shows 2026-11-03, already past: stale [Certain].
