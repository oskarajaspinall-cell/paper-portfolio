# Fisher & Paykel Healthcare (NZE:FPH) — Core initiation — 2026-11-16
## 1. Business
Designs and makes respiratory humidification devices and consumables (hospital respiratory care, homecare sleep-apnea masks), selling mostly through hospitals and distributors, with recurring disposable consumables on an installed base [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/nze/FPH/). Reports in NZD; manufacturing is in New Zealand and Mexico, while sales are overwhelmingly offshore [Likely]. Regional revenue split: [data unavailable] (the company site is not on the allowlist, so no `[company-reported]` figure was fetched). US exposure is therefore likely above 10% of revenue [Guessing]; the macro overlay should check USD/NZD.

## 2. Quality
Strong. TTM ROIC 26.2% and ROCE 27.0%, with gross margin 63.7% and operating margin 27.6% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/nze/FPH/financials/ratios/) [Certain]. Net cash (net debt/EBITDA -0.41x), Altman Z 20.22, Piotroski 7, and shares change only 0.14% YoY (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/nze/FPH/statistics/) [Certain].
Direction: ROIC fell from 27.4% (FY2022) to 10.9% (FY2024) and recovered to 26.4% in FY2026; the TTM figure is up 4.8pp on a year earlier, so the 5-year "falling" label reflects a trough and recovery, not erosion [Likely]. FCF margin is 21.2%, 2.3pp below its 2-year high of 23.5% [Certain]; FCF/NI conversion 104.7%.
Moat: consumables on an installed base and clinical-evidence-led product design [Likely]. Margins hold above FY2023-24 levels, which supports pricing power [Likely].

## 3. Valuation
Expensive on every lens. P/E 61.3x (5y range 37.3x-113.0x, 32% of the way up the range; peer median 44.6x, +37%). EV/EBITDA 35.8x sits at 85% of its 5y range, against a peer median of 15.7x (+129%). EV/Sales 12.2x and P/B 13.5x are above their 5y maxima. FCF yield is 1.72% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/nze/FPH/statistics/) [Certain].
Mechanical fair value (base 21.52 NZD against a 48.48 close) is flagged unreliable because DCF and P/E disagree 3.6x (11.61 vs 41.33). The reverse DCF implies 46.6% year-1 revenue growth, an unrealistic hurdle [Certain].
The trend is strong: +18.7% above the 200-day MA, +49.7% over 6 months, and a 52-week high on 2026-10-21 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/nze/FPH/history/) [Certain]. Next results are due Nov 25, 2026 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/nze/FPH/) [Certain].

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Net cash, negligible dilution (0.14% YoY), and FCF equal to 104.7% of net income in FY2026, up from 20.1% in FY2023 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/nze/FPH/financials/cash-flow-statement/) [Certain]. The AGM transcript cites dividend increases and heavy R&D and manufacturing expansion (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/nze/FPH/transcripts/680720-agm-2026/) [Likely]. Cash flow has been consumed by capex in weaker years (FCF margin 3.2% in FY2023) [Certain]; the current conversion is not guaranteed to hold [Guessing].
