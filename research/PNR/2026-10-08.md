# Pentair plc (PNR) — Core initiation — 2026-10-08

## 1. Business
Pentair designs and sells water- and fluid-treatment equipment across three segments: Flow (pumps for residential/commercial/agricultural use), Water Solutions (filtration, softening) and Pool (pumps, filters, heaters, automation for residential/commercial pools), sold through distributors, dealers and direct channels [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/pnr/). It completed the acquisition of Taco Group Holdings on 2026-10-01, adding circulation-pump products to Flow [Certain] (source: https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20261001204647/en/Pentair-Completes-Acquisition-of-Taco-Group-Holdings/).

## 2. Quality
Returns are respectable and margins have risen for five years: TTM ROIC 14.0%, ROCE 15.9%, operating margin 22.8% (+6.4pp vs FY2021), FCF conversion 103.3% of net income [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/pnr/financials/ratios/). But near-term earnings quality and management credibility are impaired. A preliminary Q2 2026 release disclosed Pool-segment channel destocking "more pronounced... than previously estimated," forcing a deep guidance cut, the same day CFO Nicholas Brazis resigned "to pursue another opportunity" after ~4 months in the role [Certain] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/77360/000007736026000039/pnr-20260710.htm). It is the company's third CFO in a year (Fishman served as interim; Bob Hau starts 2026-11-01) [Certain] (source: https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260922021792/en/Pentair-Announces-Bob-Hau-as-Executive-Vice-President-and-Chief-Financial-Officer/). Multiple firms since filed securities class actions alleging investors were misled, lead-plaintiff deadline 2026-10-02 [Certain] (source: https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/09/28/3369753/1020/en/kaplan-fox-urges-investors-of-pentair-plc-nyse-pnr-to-seek-a-leadership-role-before-october-2-2026.html). ROE fell from 24.6% (FY2021) to 17.1% TTM and net debt/EBITDA rose from 1.24x to 1.62x [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/pnr/financials/ratios/). Pool's forecasting visibility is currently unreliable [Likely].

## 3. Valuation
PNR trades at a steep discount on every multiple to both its own 5-year history and peers (XYL, AOS, WTS, IEX): P/E 13.1x vs 5y range 15.4–26.6x and peer median 27.3x (-52%); EV/EBITDA 9.7x vs peer 16.0x (-40%); P/FCF 12.4x vs peer 25.8x (-52%); P/B 2.2x vs peer 4.2x (-48%); FCF yield 8.08% [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/pnr/financials/ratios/, statistics page). Being below the 5-year trough on P/E, EV/EBITDA, P/FCF and P/B simultaneously, immediately after a guidance cut and amid active fraud litigation, implies the market is pricing continued earnings downside or impaired guidance credibility, not simply a cyclical Pool air-pocket [Likely]. This is a classic statistically-cheap setup that can also be a value trap if destocking is structural rather than timing.

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROIC (ratios page) falls below 10% for two consecutive quarters, indicating destocking has become a structural margin problem rather than channel timing.
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) rises above 3.0x, indicating balance-sheet strain from debt-funded M&A (Taco) layered on a weak Pool quarter.
- FCF conversion (cash flow/income statement) falls below 70% sustained, indicating deteriorating earnings quality.
- Gross margin (income statement) falls below 38%, indicating pricing/mix pressure spreading beyond Pool into Flow/Water Solutions.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Cash generation remains a strength: TTM FCF margin 16.8%, FCF conversion 103.3% of net income, up from 100.0%/57.8%/87.2%/110.7% in FY2021–24 [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/pnr/financials/cash-flow-statement/). Shares outstanding fell 1.59% YoY, consistent with ongoing buybacks [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/pnr/statistics/). The company also took on new financial obligations in September 2026 (SEC 8-K item 2.03) and closed the debt-funded Taco acquisition on 2026-10-01 [Certain] (source: https://www.sec.gov/cgi-bin/browse-edgar?action=getcompany&CIK=0000077360&type=8-K), stacking M&A leverage on top of a net debt/EBITDA that has already risen to 1.62x (from 1.53x in FY2025) just as the core Pool segment is under stress [Likely].

## 6. Unusual items
Material governance/event risk: abrupt CFO resignation concurrent with a severe guidance cut; a third CFO change in ~12 months; multiple active securities class-action filings (lead-plaintiff deadline 2026-10-02 has passed, litigation now proceeds) [Certain] (source: https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/09/18/3364972/32719/en/pentair-plc-stockholders-with-significant-losses-should-contact-robbins-llp-before-the-october-2-2026-lead-plaintiff-deadline-for-information-about-recovering-their-losses.html). Short interest is only 3.97% of float and fell 16.3% month-on-month, so this is not a crowded short despite the news flow [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/pnr/statistics/). Next earnings are 2026-10-20, 12 days from this note, before the thesis can be tested [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/pnr/).
