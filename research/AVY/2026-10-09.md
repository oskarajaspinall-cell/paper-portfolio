# Avery Dennison Corporation (AVY) — Core initiation — 2026-10-09

## 1. Business
Avery Dennison converts base materials (films, papers, adhesives) into pressure-sensitive labeling and functional materials, graphics/reflective films, performance tapes, and RFID/intelligent-label inlays, sold to brand owners, retailers, logistics, apparel, food, pharma and auto customers [Certain] (source: https://www.averydennison.com/en/home/company/overview.html). "High-value categories" (RFID/intelligent labels, specialty materials) are now ~45% of sales, targeted at ~50% by 2030 [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/avy/transcripts/753873-investor-update/).

## 2. Quality
TTM ROIC 15.1% and ROCE 19.2% sit above cost of capital, but the 5y trend is falling (ROIC -3.4pp, ROE -13.2pp) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/avy/financials/ratios/). Gross margin is rising (+1.2pp to 29.0%), consistent with management's claim that high-value categories carry above-average, mix-accretive margins and grew mid-single-digit organically, faster than the core [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/avy/transcripts/753873-investor-update/; https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/avy/financials/income-statement/). The 10-K describes durable but not unassailable barriers: "entry of competitors... is limited by technical knowledge and capital requirements," citing scale, product breadth and distribution, while named competitors (UPM, Fedrigoni, 3M, Tesa, Nitto Denko) are credible and well-capitalized [Certain] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/8818/000000881826000015/avy-20251231.htm). Customer concentration is low — no customer >10% of 2025 sales [Certain] (source: same 10-K). Net debt/EBITDA 2.28x TTM is moderate but rising (+0.15x over 5y) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/avy/financials/ratios/). Overall: a good, cyclical materials-science business whose moat is scale/know-how, not pricing power; the RFID/specialty mix-shift is the swing factor for returns.

## 3. Valuation
AVY trades at a premium to peers (SON, SLGN, BERY, CCK) on every multiple — P/E 18.3x (+28% vs peer median 14.3x), EV/EBITDA 10.8x (+29%), EV/Sales 1.7x (+36%) — but at a discount to its own 5-year history, most starkly P/FCF 11.9x vs a 5y range of 19.7x–29.0x (84% below the 5y minimum) and in line with peers' 11.9x median [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/avy/financials/ratios/ and peer statistics pages). The fact sheet's reverse-DCF shows the price implies -3.3% revenue growth in year one fading to terminal, below management's guided 3%-4% organic growth for FY2026 [Likely] (source: fact sheet valuation section; headline https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260730701873/en/Avery-Dennison-Announces-Second-Quarter-2026-Results/). Base-case fair value $213.64 implies +27.5% upside to the $167.62 close (bear -25.6%, bull +37.3%) [Likely] (source: fact sheet). The gap between a rich peer multiple and a cheap own-history multiple suggests the market pays up for AVY's quality/mix-shift relative to peers, while still pricing in more growth deceleration than guided.

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROIC (ratios page) falls below 12% for two consecutive fiscal years.
- Gross margin (income statement) falls below 27%, reversing the mix-shift gain from high-value categories.
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) rises above 3.0x.
- High-value categories' share of sales (company investor disclosures) stalls below ~45% for two consecutive years.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Roughly half of internal capex is directed at high-value categories; the board raised the dividend and buys back stock opportunistically, and management cites ~$8bn of five-year capacity for M&A [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/avy/transcripts/753873-investor-update/; https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260723527735/en/Avery-Dennison-Declares-Quarterly-Dividend/). Shares outstanding fell -3.08% YoY, confirming active buybacks [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/avy/statistics/). TTM FCF margin (11.5%) and FCF/NI conversion (150.8%) are both well above the FY2025 level (8.0%/103.5%) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/avy/financials/cash-flow-statement/, income-statement) — flagged below as it may not be sustained.

## 6. Unusual items
TTM FCF margin and FCF/NI conversion jumped sharply versus FY2025 (8.0%→11.5% margin; 103.5%→150.8% conversion), likely working-capital timing rather than a structural step-change; treat the P/FCF "discount to history" with some caution until confirmed in FY2026 results (next earnings Oct 29, 2026) [Guessing] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/avy/financials/cash-flow-statement/, https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/avy/statistics/). Otherwise none: short interest is low (2.82%, down 21.2% month-on-month) and leverage/governance show nothing distressed [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/avy/statistics/).
