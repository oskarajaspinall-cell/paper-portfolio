# Willis Towers Watson Public Limited Company (WTW) — Core initiation — 2026-10-10

## 1. Business
WTW is a global insurance broking and advisory firm, classified by stockanalysis.com as Insurance Brokers (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/wtw/) [Certain]. It operates broadly across commercial insurance broking/risk advisory and human-capital, benefits and retirement consulting [Likely]. Revenue is largely fee- and commission-based, tied to client retention, renewal cycles and insurance-market pricing [Likely].

## 2. Quality
ROIC, ROCE and ROE have all improved over five years (ROIC 9.4%→14.5% TTM; ROCE 8.6%→15.7% TTM; ROE 17.8%→19.8% TTM) (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/wtw/financials/ratios/) [Certain]. FY2024 shows a sharp anomaly — ROIC -14.2%, ROE -1.0%, net margin -1.0%, FCF conversion -1,292.9% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/wtw/financials/ratios/, https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/wtw/financials/income-statement/, https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/wtw/financials/cash-flow-statement/) [Certain] — consistent with a large one-off charge rather than a change in underlying economics, since FY2025/TTM returns rebounded strongly. Gross margin has drifted down 3.4pp over five years to 42.5% TTM while operating margin rose 3.3pp to 23.7% TTM (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/wtw/financials/income-statement/) [Certain] — cost efficiency offsetting a less favourable revenue mix. Net debt/EBITDA rose from 0.34x to 1.93x TTM (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/wtw/financials/ratios/) [Certain], still moderate for a recurring-revenue advisory business. Brand, global broking scale and embedded client relationships are durable advantages in a concentrated industry (peers MMC, AON, AJG, BRO), though a softening commercial-insurance pricing cycle (see Unusual items) is a genuine headwind to broking commissions [Likely].

## 3. Valuation
At 296.26 USD, WTW trades at a discount to peers and its own history: P/E 18.2x vs 5y median 21.6x (58th percentile of its range) and 12% below the peer median 20.7x; EV/EBITDA 11.7x is below its own 5y minimum (12.2x) and 9% below peers' 12.9x; EV/Sales 3.3x is 22% below peers' 4.2x; P/FCF 16.3x is 8% below peers' 17.7x (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/wtw/financials/ratios/, https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/wtw/statistics/, peer statistics pages) [Certain]. Only P/B (3.6x) sits above its 5y median (2.6x) but is still 11% below peers. The fact sheet's fair value model (P/E-led, blended with EV/EBITDA) gives a base fair value of 347.25, 17.2% above last close, with bear/bull of -40.7%/+24.9% (source: fact sheet fair value table) [Likely]. The market appears to be pricing WTW as a slower-growth, cyclically exposed broker despite return metrics now ahead of or level with the sector.

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROIC (ratios page) falls back below 10% for two consecutive fiscal years
- Operating margin (income statement) falls below 20% for two consecutive fiscal years
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) rises above 2.5x
- Organic/commission revenue growth turns negative for two consecutive quarters (checked against filings/releases)

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
FCF margin is 16.7% TTM, up from 21.3% in FY2021 but on a volatile path (dipped to 7.6% in FY2022) (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/wtw/financials/cash-flow-statement/) [Certain]. FCF conversion (FCF/NI) was 107.9% TTM, implying FCF comfortably covers net income [Certain], though the FY2024 reading (-1,292.9%) reflects that year's one-off net-income hit rather than a cash shortfall. Shares outstanding fell 3.98% year-on-year (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/wtw/statistics/) [Certain], consistent with ongoing buybacks. Net debt/EBITDA of 1.93x TTM leaves headroom before leverage constrains further buybacks [Likely].

## 6. Unusual items
Altman Z-score is 1.39, in the model's traditional "distress" zone, though this metric is poorly calibrated for asset-light advisory/broking businesses with low tangible assets like WTW (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/wtw/statistics/) [Likely]. Institutional ownership is reported at 101.2% of float (source: same) [Certain] — a data artifact of double-counted positions, not a governance concern. WTW's own survey reports commercial property insurance rates fell 14.5% in Q2 2026, the sharpest decline in a decade (source: https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/10/01/3373107/0/en/commercial-property-rates-post-sharpest-decline-in-a-decade-willis-report-finds.html) [Likely] — a softening pricing cycle that pressures broking commissions industry-wide. Next earnings Oct 29, 2026, after this note; no drift signal currently (source: fact sheet price table).
