## 1. Business
US-focused insurer in three parts: specialty property and casualty (commercial lines, excess and surplus, warranty), title insurance (cyclical with mortgage and housing volume), and a small holding company. Earnings come from underwriting margin plus investment income on float. ## 3. Valuation
P/E 9.0x sits at 49% of its 5y range (4.8x–13.4x, median 10.7x); EV/EBITDA 7.9x at 53%; P/B 1.6x at 68% of its range (1.1x–1.9x), so book value is priced toward the upper half of history while earnings are mid-range [Certain]. Versus peers (WRB, CINF, TRV, CB) it trades at a 24% P/E discount and 31% P/B discount, consistent with lower perceived quality and a title cyclical [Likely]. Mechanical fair value: base 45.82, bear 25.15, bull 53.08 against a 39.61 close, i.e. +15.7% base upside and -36.5% bear downside (P/B+ROE 67%, P/E 33%) (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/ori/). The price assumes ROE roughly holds near current levels; no wide margin of safety [Likely]. Upside is moderate with a heavy bear tail.
