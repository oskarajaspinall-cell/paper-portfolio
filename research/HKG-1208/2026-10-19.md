# MMG Limited (HKG:1208) — Core initiation — 2026-10-19
## 1. Business
MMG is a Hong Kong-listed base-metals miner operating five mines — Las Bambas (Peru), Kinsevere (DRC), Khoemacau (Botswana), and Dugald River and Rosebery (Australia) — producing copper, zinc, gold, silver, molybdenum, cobalt and lead [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/1208/). It sells into global commodity markets at prevailing (largely LME-linked) prices, so revenue and margins swing with metal prices and volumes, not with pricing power [Certain].

## 2. Quality
MMG is a price-taker: copper and zinc are fungible commodities, so no durable moat protects margins from peers. ROIC/ROCE show a 5y trend of flat-to-rising (ROIC +0.6pp to 20.2% TTM; ROCE +2.4pp to 24.4% TTM) but gross, operating and net margins are all down over the 5y window (gross -5.8pp, operating -4.1pp, net -7.5pp) despite a strong TTM print, consistent with a commodity up-cycle rather than a structural improvement [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/1208/financials/ratios/, https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/1208/financials/income-statement/). The one clear, durable improvement is the balance sheet: net debt/EBITDA fell from 3.44x (FY2022) to 0.16x (TTM) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/1208/financials/ratios/). Record H1 2026 results (revenue +61% to $4.54bn, EBITDA margin 60%, net debt cut to $610m) [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/1208/transcripts/579897-h1-2026/) were price- and volume-driven, not evidence of a new competitive position. Asset/jurisdiction diversification (Peru, DRC, Botswana, Australia) spreads single-mine risk but adds country risk that a single-asset peer wouldn't carry [Likely].

## 3. Valuation
TTM multiples look statistically cheap: P/E 11.3x sits at the 3rd percentile of its own 5y range and 58% below peer median (26.9x); EV/EBITDA 3.7x is below its own 5y minimum (4.1x) and 64% below peers (10.2x) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/1208/financials/ratios/). But TTM earnings embed the record H1 2026 print, so the discount partly reflects the market pricing in margin reversion as metal prices normalize, not a true mispricing [Likely]. The model's base fair value (HKD 8.16, EV/EBITDA method — NAV unavailable from stockanalysis.com) is only +6.9% above last close (HKD 7.63); bear/bull are -32.4%/+133.1% [Certain] (source: fact sheet fair value table). That the model shows only modest base-case upside despite "cheap" trading multiples confirms the cheapness is largely cyclical, not a free lunch.

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROIC (ratios page) falls back below its own 5y average of ~7.3% for two consecutive periods — would show the TTM profitability was cyclical, not durable.
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) rises back above 2.0x — would reverse the deleveraging that is the thesis's one clear structural improvement.
- Net margin (income statement) falls back below 5% — would show the post-raise earnings power the market is paying for has not held.
- Shares outstanding (statistics page) rises more than a further 5% in a year — continued dilution beyond the 2026 raise would erode per-share value.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Cash conversion is strong: TTM FCF margin 31.9% and FCF/NI 237.1%, though both metrics have been volatile over 5 years (FCF margin fell 20.7pp from FY2021's unusually high base) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/1208/financials/cash-flow-statement/). Net debt/EBITDA has collapsed to 0.16x from 3.44x in FY2022 [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/1208/financials/ratios/). Management raised ~$1.6bn of capital alongside Q2 2026 results, framed as funding expansion and cost reduction at Las Bambas, Khoemacau and Rosebery [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/1208/transcripts/714438-q2-2026/); shares outstanding are up 3.43% YoY (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/1208/statistics/), a modest dilution funding growth capex rather than distress.

## 6. Unusual items
MMG was formerly named Minmetals Resources Limited (renamed 2012) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/1208/), consistent with its historic link to China's state-owned Minmetals group; current ownership % is [data unavailable] from allowlisted sources, so state-linked governance/geopolitical risk (e.g. around the DRC cobalt operation) cannot be sized here and should be treated as a qualitative watch item [Guessing]. Beta (5y) is high at 1.61 and Altman Z is 2.09 — the "grey zone", not distress but not comfortably safe either [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/1208/statistics/).
