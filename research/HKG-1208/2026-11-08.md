# MMG Limited (HKG:1208) — Core initiation — 2026-11-08
## 1. Business
MMG is a Hong Kong-listed base-metals miner with five mines: Las Bambas (Peru), Kinsevere (DRC), Khoemacau (Botswana), Dugald River and Rosebery (Australia). It sells copper, zinc and by-products at LME-linked prices, so revenue follows metal prices and volumes [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/1208/). Revenue split by country is [data unavailable]: the company site (mmg.com) is not on the allowlist. Peru (Las Bambas) is the largest asset by copper output, so a Peru share of 10% or more is [Likely] but unquantified.

## 2. Quality
MMG is a price-taker; copper and zinc are fungible, so there is no structural moat [Certain]. The Recent direction table is the new evidence: TTM ROIC 20.3% vs 10.2% a year earlier, ROCE 24.5% vs 12.8%, operating margin 42.6% vs 33.4%, FCF margin 31.9% vs 25.5%, all at 2-year highs (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/1208/financials/ratios/?p=trailing). Over five fiscal years, operating margin is down 3.9pp, net margin down 7.5pp and FCF margin down 20.7pp, and ROIC in FY2022-23 was 4.2% and 3.6% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/1208/financials/ratios/). The last year is therefore an up-cycle peak in returns, not proof of a durable edge [Likely]. Real improvement: net debt/EBITDA is 0.16x vs 3.44x in FY2022 [Certain]. H1 2026 was a record on volume and price, per the call summary (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/1208/transcripts/579897-h1-2026/) [Likely]. Piotroski F is 7 [Certain]. Verdict: a good-for-now cyclical, not a compounder.

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Operating margin (income statement, TTM) falls below 25%: the up-cycle earnings power is gone.
- ROIC (ratios page, TTM) falls below 10%: returns back to the FY2024-type range.
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) rises above 2.0x: reverses the deleveraging.
- Shares change YoY (statistics page) exceeds 8%: further dilution erodes per-share value.
