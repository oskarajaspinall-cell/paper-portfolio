## 2. Quality
Returns have turned up sharply: TTM ROIC 20.3% vs 11.7% in FY2025 and 3.6% in FY2023, and TTM operating margin 42.6% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/1208/financials/ratios/) [Certain]. The 5-year trend is flat to falling, but the last 12 months are rising (operating margin 33.4% to 42.6%; FCF margin 25.5% to 31.9%) [Certain]. This is cycle-driven. FY2023 ROIC of 3.6% and net margin of 0.2% show how fast profits go when metal prices or operations slip [Certain]. No durable moat is evident beyond asset quality and cost position; I could not verify reserves or cost curve position [data unavailable]. Quality is therefore good now, not proven through the cycle [Likely]. Piotroski F is 7; Altman Z is 2.09 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/1208/statistics/) [Certain].
