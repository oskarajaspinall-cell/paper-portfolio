# NIKE, Inc. (NKE) — Core initiation — 2026-10-11

## 1. Business
Nike designs, markets and sells athletic footwear, apparel and equipment globally under the Nike and Jordan brands, plus Converse. Revenue comes from wholesale accounts and Nike Direct (owned stores and digital), with scale giving it global distribution and marketing reach few rivals can match. [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/nke/)

## 2. Quality
Every return and margin metric has fallen over five years: ROIC 44.9%→18.9% TTM, ROCE 22.6%→15.7%, ROE 43.1%→21.6%, gross margin 46.0%→43.4%, operating margin 14.3%→9.0%, net margin 12.9%→6.7% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/nke/financials/ratios/, https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/nke/financials/income-statement/) [Certain]. Brand strength and scale still support a premium gross margin, but the moat is not protecting returns: Greater China revenue fell 22% reported / 26% currency-neutral in the quarter to August 2026, and management says the decline is structural, expected to "extend beyond fiscal 2027" (source: SEC 10-Q filed 2026-10-02, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/320187/000032018726000193/nke-20260831.htm) [Certain]. Nike launched a multi-year "Pace" restructuring program in October 2026 with ~$1.0bn of further pre-tax charges (on top of $0.3bn already booked in FY2026) targeting $2.5bn of cumulative savings through FY2031 (same 10-Q) [Certain] — an admission the current cost base no longer earns historical returns. Net debt/EBITDA has risen from -0.05x to 0.56x TTM as cash generation weakens (source: ratios page) [Certain].

## 3. Valuation
TTM multiples sit below NKE's own 5-year range on P/E (16.6x vs 22.0x min), EV/EBITDA (11.1x vs 14.5x min), EV/Sales and P/B, suggesting the market has marked down expectations already [Certain] (source: ratios, statistics pages). Yet NKE still trades at a premium to peer medians (LULU, SKX, DECK, UAA) on every multiple: P/E +42%, EV/EBITDA +29%, EV/Sales +13%, P/FCF +143%, P/B +61% [Certain], despite inferior and still-falling growth and returns. The fact sheet's base fair value ($26.52) is 23.6% below the last close; bear is 38.7% below, bull 47.8% above [Guessing] (source: fact sheet fair-value section, inform-only DCF/comp blend). The reverse DCF implies the price embeds roughly 27.2% year-1 revenue growth fading to terminal growth [Certain] — hard to reconcile with a business shrinking in its largest declining region, with no forward guidance. The low trailing P/E looks like depressed earnings, not a mispriced asset.

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROIC (ratios page) stabilizes or rises back above 20% TTM, instead of continuing to fall from 18.9%
- Gross margin (income statement page) holds at or above 43% TTM, instead of resuming its 5-year decline (46.0%→43.4%)
- FCF margin (cash-flow/income-statement pages) stays above 5% TTM, instead of falling further below the current 4.6%
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) stays below 1.0x, instead of continuing its rise from -0.05x to 0.56x

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Nike paused share repurchases entirely in the quarter to August 2026 ($0 spent) while still paying $610m of dividends ($0.41/share common) (source: SEC 10-Q) [Certain] — management is conserving cash through the Pace restructuring rather than returning it. Quarterly operating cash flow of $135m fell short of $199m capex, a negative free-cash-flow quarter [Certain]. TTM FCF conversion (FCF/NI) has fallen to 68.1% from a 116.1% peak in FY2024, and TTM FCF margin is 4.6%, down from 9.5-12.9% in FY2022-24 (source: ratios, cash-flow pages) [Certain].

## 6. Unusual items
Short interest is 7.27% of float, up 24.5% versus the prior month (source: stockanalysis.com statistics page) [Certain] — a material rise in bearish positioning alongside the stock's 49.8% 12-month decline. No governance or accounting red flags identified (Altman Z 4.14, Piotroski F 5, source: statistics page).
