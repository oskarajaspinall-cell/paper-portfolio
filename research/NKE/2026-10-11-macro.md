# Macro overlay: NIKE, Inc. (NKE) — 2026-10-11
## Assessment
NKE's thesis is overwhelmingly idiosyncratic — structural Greater China share loss and a margin/restructuring reset [Certain] (source: 10-Q, research note) — but two macro variables add a modest, same-direction overlay via two chains: `dollar -> FX translation -> reported earnings` and `real yields -> cost of equity -> DCF fair value`.

**Facts**: broad trade-weighted USD index 121.385, +2.86 (~1m) / +0.70 (~3m) [Certain] (FRED DTWEXBGS, 2026-10-02). US 10y real yield (TIPS) 2.87%, +0.44pp (~1m) / +0.56pp (~3m) [Certain] (FRED DFII10, 2026-10-08); 10y nominal 5.22%, +0.42pp (~1m) [Certain] (FRED DGS10, 2026-10-08) — this is the risk-free rate the fact sheet's DCF already uses. HY credit spread 3.15%, +0.48pp (~1m) [Certain] (FRED BAMLH0A0HYM2).

**Market pricing (proxy, labelled)**: 2y Treasury (4.75%) sits ~87bp above effective fed funds (3.88%) [Certain, FRED DGS2/DFF, 2026-10-08], consistent with the market pricing limited near-term cuts rather than easing — a "higher for longer" skew **(inference)**.

**Bull**: dollar reverses, real yields fall back → FX translation tailwind on international sales and a lower DCF discount rate lift the fair-value range modestly, independent of the China turnaround.
**Bear**: dollar and real yields keep rising → FX translation drag on reported EPS during an already-guided-down year, and a higher cost of equity pushes DCF fair value (already below last close) lower still.
**Tilt**: toward bear, **small** — macro reinforces rather than drives the bear case, which rests on company-specific China/margin facts, not a macro surprise.
