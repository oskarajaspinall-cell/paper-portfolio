# Contemporary Amperex Technology Co., Limited (SHE:300750) — Core initiation — 2026-10-24

## 1. Business
CATL is the world's largest maker of lithium-ion battery cells and packs, selling to automakers and energy-storage integrators; revenue follows battery volume, and profit depends on scale, raw-material pass-through and technology lead (LFP, fast-charging, swapping) [Likely]. A 2026-10-19 cooperation with Huawei-backed Yinwang on battery swapping is incremental, not decision-changing (source: https://www.reuters.com/world/asia-pacific/huawei-backed-smart-car-business-cooperate-with-catl-battery-swapping-2026-10-19/) [Likely].

## 2. Quality
Why it fell: Q3 results on 2026-10-20 showed a sharp slowdown in profit growth, which Reuters and SCMP attribute to intensifying EV-battery competition and cooling China auto sales growth; the stock then fell hard on 2026-10-21, dragging China EV/tech names (sources: https://www.reuters.com/business/autos-transportation/chinas-catl-profit-growth-slows-third-quarter-competition-intensifies-2026-10-20/, https://www.scmp.com/business/china-business/article/3371545/chinese-battery-giant-catl-hit-profit-slowdown-ev-headwind-fears-grow, https://www.ibtimes.com.au/chinese-stocks-fall-catl-consumer-demand-1877111) [Likely]. I could not open the filing itself (Reuters/CATL pages blocked), so the detailed cause rests on headlines [Guessing].

The fact sheet confirms deterioration: TTM gross margin 23.2% (FY2025 25.1%), operating margin 16.5% (18.0%), FCF margin 10.3% (21.4%), FCF conversion 68.1% (125.9%) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/she/300750/financials/ratios/). ROIC remains 54.3% TTM and net debt/EBITDA -1.99x (net cash) [Certain]. The moat (scale, technology) is intact but its pricing power is being tested [Likely].

## 3. Valuation
At 237.60 CNY: P/E 12.4x (5y min 16.3x, median 23.2x; peers 19.3x), EV/EBITDA 7.4x (5y min 8.6x; peers 17.3x), EV/Sales 1.5x (peers 2.4x), P/B 2.6x (peers 2.2x), FCF yield 5.40% [Certain]. Multiples are below every 5y low, but TTM FCF fell, so P/FCF 18.5x is near the 5y median 18.1x, not cheap [Certain]. Base fair value is now 230.59 CNY, 2.9% below the price (bear 71.37, bull 680.55); the reverse DCF implies 15.2% year-1 revenue growth [Certain]. The entry price set on 2026-10-19 rested on a higher base fair value, which has since fallen, so there is no margin of safety [Likely]. The market is pricing a further earnings reset, not a bargain [Guessing].
