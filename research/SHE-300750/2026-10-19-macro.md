# Macro overlay: Contemporary Amperex Technology (SHE:300750) — 2026-10-19
## Assessment
CATL's -25% 12-month decline is overwhelmingly a China EV-demand/overcapacity and US-political story (Ford/Transportation Secretary pressure, note §6), not a macro one [Likely]: SPY returned +14.8% over the same 12 months (stockanalysis.com) while CATL fell -25%, so idiosyncratic drivers dominate. Still, this fund's own DCF discounts CATL's cash flows at the US 10y Treasury yield (5.10%, 2026-10-15) plus a 3.5pp CNY country premium (factsheet-2026-10-19.md, https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=DGS10), so a further move in US yields mechanically shifts the bear/base/bull fair-value range the valuation thesis leans on.

Facts [Certain]: 10y UST (DGS10) +0.53pp over 3m to 5.10% (2026-10-15); 10y real yield (DFII10) +0.43pp to 2.78%; 10y breakeven inflation flat at 2.35% (+0.02pp) — the yield rise looks like a real-rate/term-premium move, not an inflation re-pricing **(inference)**. Market pricing, proxy **(inference)**: the 2y yield (4.77%) sits ~89bp above fed funds (3.88%), implying markets still price a higher-for-longer rate path rather than imminent cuts.

Bull: yields roll over toward ~4.5% → discount rate falls → DCF fair value (base 300.73 CNY) re-rates higher, reinforcing the cheap-vs-peers case [Guessing]. Bear: 10y pushes above 5.5% → fair value compresses further, deepening the de-rating even if fundamentals hold [Guessing]. Tilt: small and roughly neutral — rates are a real but secondary lever on the valuation range; the price action to date has been driven by overcapacity/demand and US political risk, which the core note's own triggers already cover.
