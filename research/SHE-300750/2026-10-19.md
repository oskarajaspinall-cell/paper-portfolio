# Contemporary Amperex Technology Co., Limited (SHE:300750) — Core initiation — 2026-10-19

## 1. Business
CATL is the world's largest maker of lithium-ion battery cells and packs for EVs and energy storage, selling to automakers (e.g. Ford) and grid/storage integrators, plus materials recycling [Certain]. Revenue tracks battery volume shipped across EV and non-EV (grid-storage) markets; profitability depends on scale, raw-material pass-through and technology leadership (LFP, sodium-ion, fast-charging) [Likely].

## 2. Quality
ROIC is 69.5% TTM, up from 35.6% FY21 (+33.1pp over 5y); operating margin 17.9% TTM (+5.5pp); FCF margin 16.8% TTM with FCF conversion 103% of net income [Certain]. Net cash (net debt/EBITDA -2.40x) gives balance-sheet flexibility [Certain]. Moat: #1 global EV/storage battery maker by volume, vertically-integrated materials/recycling, and a technology lead that has kept gross margin resilient (24.5% TTM vs a 19.1-25.1% 5y range) despite a commoditizing industry [Likely]. Risk to durability: Chinese battery capacity has been overbuilt and price competition intense (gross margin fell to 19.1-19.5% in FY22-23); a 2026-07-24 WSJ headline reports EV demand in China "weakening" while energy-storage demand is "booming" (https://www.wsj.com/business/autos/catl-profit-climbs-on-surging-demand-for-energy-storage-batteries-041b4b69), so growth is shifting toward a newer, less-tested segment [Likely].

## 3. Valuation
At 277.00 CNY, TTM P/E 14.8x sits below its own 5y range (16.3-86.0x, min) and 25% below peer median 19.8x; EV/EBITDA 8.5x is also below its 5y floor (8.6x) and 34% below peers (13.0x); P/FCF 14.8x is 62% below peer median (though that median, 39.0x, has n=1) [Certain]. On EV/Sales and P/B it trades at a premium (+46%, +38%), consistent with far higher ROIC/ROE than peers [Certain]. The price is 26.8% below its 200-day MA, near 6-month/52-week lows, implying the market is pricing continued margin/growth deceleration rather than current (record) profitability persisting [Likely]. The base-case fair value (300.73 CNY, +8.6%) assumes 7.7% year-1 revenue growth fading to terminal; bear (85.62, -69.1%) implies a return to FY22-23-type margin pressure, bull (623.63, +125.1%) assumes current returns persist [Guessing].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Gross margin (income statement page) below 19% for two consecutive quarters — signals a return to FY22-23 price war.
- ROIC (ratios page) below 40% TTM — the moat eroding faster than priced.
- FCF conversion (cash flow/income statement pages) below 80% of net income — working-capital or capex strain.
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) turns positive — shift from net-cash to net-debt funding.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
FCF conversion has exceeded 100% of net income for three straight years (134.2%, 129.7%, 125.9% FY23-25; 103.0% TTM) [Certain], funding a net-cash balance sheet (net debt/EBITDA -2.40x TTM) [Certain] even as shares outstanding grew 3.68% YoY — modest dilution [Certain]. Payout/buyback detail is [data unavailable] on the fact sheet.
