# Banco Santander (Brasil) (BVMF:SANB11) — Core initiation — 2026-10-28
## 1. Business
Brazilian universal bank, majority-owned by the Santander group. It earns net interest income on retail, SME, agribusiness and corporate loans, plus fees and market-making. Management is deliberately shifting from low-income unsecured lending toward high-income clients, secured credit and corporate/CIB, which carries structurally lower spreads (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/bvmf/SANB11/transcripts/659705-q3-2026/) [Likely].

## 2. Quality
Mediocre and deteriorating. ROE is 11.2% TTM versus 14.7% in FY2021 (falling, -4.2pp), and net margin slid from 33.1% to 28.7% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/bvmf/SANB11/financials/ratios/) [Certain]. ROIC, ROCE and operating margin are [data unavailable] (banks). FCF is erratic (FCF/NI -163.5% in FY2024, 279.8% TTM), so it is meaningless for a bank and I do not use it. Piotroski F is 4 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/bvmf/SANB11/statistics/) [Certain].
The Q3 2026 call (28 Oct) described ROE as below management's own targets, higher provisions in agribusiness, low- and middle-income unsecured and card lending, and about BRL half a billion of one-off wholesale cases. It also described client NII pressure from lower funding income and the consumer-finance portfolio sale (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/bvmf/SANB11/transcripts/659705-q3-2026/) [Likely]. Offsets: improving efficiency, falling short-term delinquency and better newer cohorts [Likely]. No durable moat beyond scale and the Santander franchise; peers Itaú and BPAC earn more [Guessing].

## 3. Valuation
The price is 25.87 BRL (27-Oct close). P/E is 6.7x, at the bottom of its 5y range (6.6x / 7.3x / 12.7x), 14% below the peer median of 7.8x. P/B is 0.8x (5y 0.7x / 1.0x / 1.1x) versus a peer median of 1.5x (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/bvmf/SANB11/statistics/) [Certain]. At 0.8x book with an ROE of about 11%, the market is pricing returns below the cost of equity (risk-free 5.00% plus a 3.0% country premium and a beta floor of 0.8), i.e. no recovery. Base fair value is 34.27 BRL (+32.5%), bear 23.54 (-9.0%), bull 41.80 [Likely]. The reverse DCF implies -8.4% revenue growth in year 1 [Certain]. Fair value rests on ROE stabilising; the dividend-discount cross-check is much lower (18.22), so the upside is not robust. Stock is -16.3% over 12 months and RSI is 21.5 (oversold) [Certain].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROE (ratios page) stays below 9% on the next reported TTM print, i.e. no recovery from 11.2%.
- Net margin (income statement page) falls below 22%.
- Management guidance for 2027 NII improvement is withdrawn or reversed in a subsequent quarterly release (earnings release).
- Shares change YoY (statistics page) rises above 3%, a sign of dilution to rebuild capital.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Share count is flat (0.22% YoY), so there is no dilution so far [Certain]. The Q3 call did not discuss dividends or payout, so the distribution outlook is unknown to me (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/bvmf/SANB11/transcripts/659705-q3-2026/). Management is rotating capital toward high-net-worth, large corporate and CIB. FCF conversion is not informative for a bank.
