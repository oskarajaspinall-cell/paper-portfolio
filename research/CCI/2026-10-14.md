# Crown Castle Inc. (CCI) — Core initiation — 2026-10-14
## 1. Business
Crown Castle leases space on ~40,000 U.S. towers to wireless carriers (AT&T, Verizon, T-Mobile) under long-term master lease agreements with annual escalators [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/cci/). After divesting fiber/small-cells it is now a pure-play U.S. tower operator [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/cci/transcripts/740217-goldman-sachs-communacopia-technology-conference-2026/).

## 2. Quality
Towers carry high switching costs and zoning makes new sites hard to replicate, giving CCI, AMT and SBAC an effective triopoly [Likely]. ROE rose from 13.1% (FY2021) to 37.2% (FY2024) [Certain] (source: fact sheet RA), but ROIC is flat near 6% (5.7%→6.1%, FY2021-FY2023) [Certain] — the gain still looks like leverage, not a better business. Net debt/EBITDA (site definition) is 8.31x TTM, up from 7.39x in FY2021 [Certain]; management has cited ~6.3x, a gap this note cannot reconcile — **Conflict**, so neither figure is used. Piotroski F-Score is 6/9 [Certain] (source: fact sheet ST). No new 8-K has been filed since the 2026-09-14 CFO/COO transition [Certain] (source: https://www.sec.gov/cgi-bin/browse-edgar?action=getcompany&CIK=0001051470&type=8-K), and the news feed carries no new company item since the prior note [Certain] (source: fact sheet News & sentiment) — nothing has changed the thesis in three days.

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROIC (ratios page) falls below 5.7%, the FY2021 low, showing the towers-only model is not lifting returns.
- Net debt/EBITDA, site definition (ratios page), is still above 8.0x at FY2026 Q3 results (due 2026-10-21), showing deleveraging from fiber-sale proceeds is stalling.
- ROE (ratios page) reverses back below 20% (from 37.2% in FY2024), showing the profitability jump was temporary.
- Piotroski F-Score (statistics page) falls below 4 (from 6), signalling broad-based financial deterioration.
