# Crown Castle Inc. (CCI) — Core initiation — 2026-10-11
## 1. Business
Crown Castle leases space on ~40,000 U.S. towers to wireless carriers (AT&T, Verizon, T-Mobile) under long-term master lease agreements with annual escalators [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/cci/). After divesting its fiber/small-cell segment it is now a pure-play U.S. tower operator [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/cci/transcripts/740217-goldman-sachs-communacopia-technology-conference-2026/).

## 2. Quality
Towers carry high switching costs: carriers rarely relocate equipment once sited, and zoning makes new towers hard to replicate, giving CCI, AMT and SBAC an effective triopoly [Likely]. ROE rose from 13.1% (FY2021) to 37.2% (FY2024) [Certain] (source: fact sheet RA), but ROIC is flat near 6% [Certain] (source: fact sheet RA) — the profitability gain looks like leverage, not a better business. Leverage is the key risk: net debt/EBITDA rose from 7.39x (FY2021) to 8.31x TTM [Certain] (source: fact sheet RA). Management targets 6.0-6.5x and says leverage was "6.3x" last quarter [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/cci/transcripts/753373-rbc-capital-markets-2026-global-communications-and-infrastructure-conference/) — **Conflict**: well below the 8.31x-8.70x stockanalysis.com implies; the gap is not reconcilable, so neither figure is used as fact. Piotroski F-Score is 6/9 [Certain] (source: fact sheet ST). Organic tower growth is "bottoming out in 2026 before improving" per management [Likely] (source: fact sheet headline, Bank of America conference transcript) — a weak near-term growth algorithm by its own admission.

## 3. Valuation
P/E of 32.5x sits 1% below its own 5-year minimum (33.3x) and 29% below the peer median (45.5x, AMT/SBAC/EQIX/DLR) [Certain] (source: fact sheet RA/ST). EV/EBITDA of 20.9x is also at a 5-year low and 8% below peer median [Certain]. EV/Sales (13.5x) is the one premium multiple — 11% above peer median — consistent with a higher-margin, now-simplified, towers-only model [Certain]. FCF yield is 7.10% [Certain] (source: fact sheet ST). The stockanalysis.com-derived fair value (P/AFFO proxy) is $97.83 base (+22.8% vs the $79.64 close), $62.23 bear (-21.9%) and $118.60 bull (+48.9%) [Certain] (source: fact sheet fair-value table). The wide bear/bull spread and multiples sitting at 5-year lows imply the market is pricing persistently high leverage and the 2026 growth trough as structural, not cyclical — a real debate given the leverage conflict above.

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROIC (ratios page) falls below 5% (below the FY2021 level), showing post-transformation returns are not actually improving.
- Net debt/EBITDA, site definition (ratios page), is still above 8.0x at FY2026 results (due 2026-10-21), showing deleveraging from fiber-sale proceeds is stalling.
- ROE (ratios page) reverses back below 20% (from 37.2% in FY2024), showing the profitability jump was temporary/non-recurring.
- Piotroski F-Score (statistics page) falls below 4 (from 6), signalling broad-based financial deterioration.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Shares outstanding are essentially flat (+0.57% YoY) [Certain] (source: fact sheet ST) — no material dilution. FCF yield is 7.10% [Certain]; margin/FCF-conversion rows are [data unavailable]. Management targets dividend coverage of 75-80% of AFFO on a $4.25/share dividend, with room to grow it as AFFO grows [Likely] (source: RBC transcript, URL above) — qualitative, not used as a valuation input. CCI entered a new credit facility in May 2026 (SEC Form 8-K, items 1.01/1.02/2.03, filed 2026-05-01), consistent with refinancing around the fiber/small-cell divestiture close [Likely] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1051470/000105147026000055/0001051470-26-000055-index.htm); exact terms are not disclosed in the filing index.
