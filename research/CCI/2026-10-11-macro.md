Three months of rising real yields/spreads is a headwind, consistent with CCI's -17.4% 12m return and 52-week low (65.32, 2026-10-05) [Certain, HI]. The +15.6% jump on 2026-10-09, 4.3x volume, no company catalyst [Certain], coincides with a weak September payrolls headline (29k vs ~84-90k expected, Invezz, 2026-10-05) that plausibly shifted near-term cut odds dovish **(inference, Guessing)** — but FRED data through 10/8 had not turned, so this is one point against the trend, not a confirmed reversal. Dollar/CNY/HKD are irrelevant: CCI is pure-play US domestic.
