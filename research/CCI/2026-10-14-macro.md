# Macro overlay: Crown Castle Inc. (CCI) — 2026-10-14
## Assessment
CCI is a highly levered (net debt/EBITDA 8.3x TTM [Certain], fact sheet RA), long-duration tower REIT valued on a P/AFFO proxy discounted at the 10y Treasury + beta×ERP. The chain is **real yields -> discount rate -> P/AFFO multiple**, plus **nominal yields -> refinancing cost on CCI's debt -> AFFO**.

Priced: FRED data (https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=DFII10, 2026-10-09) show the US 10y real yield (TIPS) at 2.91%, up +45bp/1m, +59bp/3m [Certain]. Nominal 10y (DGS10) is 5.24%, +41bp/1m, +68bp/3m, while 10y breakeven inflation (T10YIE, 2026-10-13) is roughly flat (-3bp/1m, +8bp/3m) [Certain] — the move is real, not inflation, so it hits CCI's discount rate directly, unoffset by lease escalators. Fed funds (DFF, 2026-10-12) rose +25bp/1m to 3.88% [Certain] — official data show policy still tightening, against the softer-labor headline in the news feed (third-party, not data) **(inference)**. CCI's fair-value model already uses the current 5.24% 10y, so today's level is priced; the risk is further rise.

**Bull**: real yields roll over (growth data forces a Fed pause) → discount rate eases → multiple re-rates toward/through the $97.83 base. **Bear**: real yields keep climbing on sticky core CPI (+2.83 index pts/3m, CPILFESL) → discount rate and refinancing cost rise on 8.3x leverage → fair value compresses toward the $62.23 bear case. **Tilt**: moderate toward bear — official data show rates still rising, not falling, and CCI is the most levered of its tower peers.
