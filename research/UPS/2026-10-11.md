# United Parcel Service, Inc. (UPS) — Core initiation — 2026-10-11

## 1. Business
UPS moves packages and freight for a fee: US Domestic (ground + air small package), International, and Supply Chain Solutions (healthcare logistics, forwarding, brokerage) (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/ups/). Profit is yield (revenue per piece) times volume, scaled over a fixed ground/air network; US Domestic is the core earnings engine [Certain].

## 2. Quality
UPS and FedEx form a US small-package duopoly with route density and last-mile infrastructure rivals can't cheaply replicate [Likely]. But returns have fallen hard: ROIC 39.0%→16.6% TTM, operating margin 13.2%→9.2% TTM, as Amazon's lower-yield volume diluted mix (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/ups/financials/ratios/, https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/ups/financials/income-statement/). Management says the "Amazon glide down and related network reconfiguration" completed in June 2026, and a transformation program (Network Reconfiguration, Efficiency Reimagined, Driver Choice) delivered ~$1.2bn of benefits in H1 2026 toward a $3bn 2026 target, with FY2026 guidance raised to ~$91.2bn revenue / $8.65bn adjusted operating profit (source: https://investors.ups.com/news-events/press-releases/detail/2164/ups-releases-2q-2026-earnings) [Likely]; this is a management claim not yet proven over a full fiscal year. Cash conversion stayed strong (FCF/NI 119.4% TTM) even as margins fell, but net debt/EBITDA rose to 1.99x TTM from 0.94x in FY2021 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/ups/financials/ratios/) [Certain] — leverage is building into the recovery, not shrinking.

## 3. Valuation
UPS trades at a 42-54% discount to the peer median (FDX, ODFL, XPO, CHRW) on every multiple (P/E 17.6x vs 31.1x, EV/EBITDA 8.6x vs 18.9x, P/FCF 14.7x vs 29.2x) and at/below its own 5-year range (EV/EBITDA and P/FCF both below their 5y minimum) (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/ups/statistics/). Much of the peer gap is ODFL's premium LTL rating, not a clean read; FedEx is the more relevant comp. The fact sheet's DCF-led fair value (base 90.22, bear 63.46, bull 127.02) puts the 94.57 close just 4.6% above base — essentially fair, not cheap on a reconstructed-cash-flow basis, with the reverse DCF implying only 4.6% year-1 growth is priced in (source: research/UPS/factsheet-2026-10-11.md). The statistical cheapness vs peers looks like the market pricing continued margin/leverage risk rather than a clear mispricing [Likely].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Operating margin (income statement page) falls back below the FY2024 trough of 8.8%, signalling the Amazon-mix/transformation recovery has failed.
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) rises above 2.2x, showing leverage is still building rather than stabilizing post-restructuring.
- FCF margin (cash flow/income statement pages) falls below the 5y low of 5.4%, meaning cash generation isn't funding the dividend and transformation costs.
- ROIC (ratios page) falls below 15%, showing no bottoming in returns on capital despite the stated network changes.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
FY2026 guidance: ~$3.0bn capex and ~$5.4bn dividends (subject to board approval); no buyback activity was mentioned in the 2Q26 release (source: https://investors.ups.com/news-events/press-releases/detail/2164/ups-releases-2q-2026-earnings) [Certain]. FCF conversion (FCF/NI) is strong at 119.4% TTM (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/ups/financials/ratios/), but ~$1.3-1.5bn of 2026 transformation/restructuring cash costs (incl. the Driver Choice Program) are being funded alongside capex and the dividend while net debt/EBITDA rises (1.99x TTM) [Certain] — capital priority currently looks like debt and dividend over buybacks.

## 6. Unusual items
A UPS employee reportedly missed a routing-block email, allowing F-35 fighter-jet parts to reach China via a UPS shipment, per Bloomberg (source: https://www.tipranks.com/news/the-fly/ups-employee-mistake-let-china-get-f-35-parts-bloomberg-says-thefly-news) [Likely], a third-party report not yet confirmed by UPS or a regulator, and an export-control/compliance risk of unknown financial magnitude [Guessing]. UPS also moved to a "global" operating model from 1 September 2026, consolidating international and domestic operations under new C-suite roles (source: https://investors.ups.com/news-events/press-releases/detail/2168/ups-announces-executive-leadership-changes-and-new-global) [Certain]; no cost or savings figures were disclosed, so financial impact is [Guessing]. Separately, Happy Returns (a UPS unit) signed an exclusive reverse-logistics deal with TikTok Shop, a diversification positive but immaterial to group financials near-term [Likely].
