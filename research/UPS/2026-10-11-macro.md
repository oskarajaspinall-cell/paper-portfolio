# Macro overlay: United Parcel Service, Inc. (UPS) — 2026-10-11
## Assessment
Chain: real yields and credit spreads → discount rate / cost of debt → UPS's DCF fair value and funding cost. UPS is capital-intensive and increasingly levered (net debt/EBITDA 1.99x TTM, up from 0.94x FY2021 [Certain], factsheet) with a margin-recovery thesis that has thin headroom (base fair value 90.22 vs 94.57 close, only +4.6% [Certain]), so the discount-rate channel is material this cycle, not just background.

Facts: 10y Treasury 5.22% (2026-10-08, +0.68pp/3m); 10y real yield (TIPS) 2.87% (+0.56pp/3m); 10y breakeven 2.33% (-0.04/1m) (fred.stlouisfed.org). High-yield OAS 3.15%, +0.45pp/3m (BAMLH0A0HYM2). Real yields rose roughly with nominal while breakevens stayed flat, so the move reads as real-rate/term-premium, not inflation **(inference)** — the channel that raises UPS's DCF discount rate (risk-free input already 5.22%). Pricing: 2y yield (4.75%) above fed funds (3.88%) is a proxy the market expects policy to stay firm, not ease soon — proxy, not fact.

Bull: real yields/credit spreads retrace toward summer levels → lower discount rate, HY spreads narrow → cheaper funding for UPS's transformation/debt load → fair value base could re-rate up from 90.22.
Bear: 10y yields keep climbing and HY spreads keep widening on a leveraged balance sheet → discount rate and interest cost both rise → DCF fair value falls further below the already-thin 4.6% cushion, compounding the fundamental margin/ROIC decline.
Tilt: toward bear, moderate — yields are rising fast while leverage is rising into an unproven recovery, cutting the valuation cushion from two sides at once.
