# Macro overlay: Capital One Financial Corporation (COF) — 2026-10-09
## Assessment
COF is a consumer card/auto lender (post-Discover) whose earnings hinge on NIM and credit-loss provisioning, both macro-sensitive [Certain]. Chain: fed funds/credit spreads → consumer debt-service burden and funding cost → delinquencies/charge-offs and NIM → provisions → EPS; the 10y Treasury also sets the DCF discount rate already used in the fact sheet's fair value (risk-free 5.28%, https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=DGS10, 2026-10-07) **(inference)**.

Facts (FRED, 2026-10-07/08): effective fed funds 3.88% (+0.25pp/1m); 10y Treasury 5.28% (+0.50pp/1m); 2y Treasury 4.77% (+0.40pp/1m); HY credit spread (BAMLH0A0HYM2) 3.09% (+0.41pp/1m, +0.39pp/3m, still below the 5.0% stress level used elsewhere in this book); breakeven inflation 2.35% (roughly flat); VIX 15.08 (falling).

Market pricing (proxy): the 2y yield sitting ~0.9pp above effective fed funds implies the market isn't pricing near-term cuts, leaning higher-for-longer [Likely] **(inference)**. Paired with a widening (not yet stressed) HY spread, this is a modestly negative directional signal for charge-off trends, but not large enough to override the note's dominant driver — company-specific Discover-merger dilution.

- **Macro bull**: rate cuts resume, HY spreads narrow → funding costs ease, provisions normalize faster → ROE recovery supports re-rating above the thin 8.7% base-case upside.
- **Macro bear**: higher-for-longer rates, HY spreads keep widening → delinquencies outpace integration synergies → provisions surprise higher → earnings miss the already-thin margin of safety.
- **Tilt**: secondary, not primary — macro shifts the odds but merger-dilution/margin normalization remains the core driver.
