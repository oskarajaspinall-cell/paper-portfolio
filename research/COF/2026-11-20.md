# Capital One Financial Corporation (COF) — Core initiation — 2026-11-20
## 1. Business
US consumer and commercial lender. It earns net interest income on credit cards, auto loans and commercial loans, plus interchange and network fees. Discover (acquired) added the Discover, PULSE and Diners Club networks, and Brex (closed 7 April 2026) added business cards and spend management (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/927628/000092762826000152/cof-20260930.htm) [Certain]. No foreign country is ~10% of revenue [Likely].

## 2. Quality
Returns are cyclical and recently depressed by acquisition effects. ROE was 20.4% in FY2021 and 2.4% in FY2025, and is 9.4% TTM (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/cof/financials/ratios/) [Certain]. Net margin is 22.2% TTM against 7.5% in FY2025 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/cof/financials/income-statement/) [Certain]. ROIC and operating margin are [data unavailable], as is net debt/EBITDA (meaningless for a bank). Piotroski F is 4 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/cof/statistics/) [Certain].

Moat: owning a network (Discover) alongside the issuer lets COF capture interchange and reissue debit cards onto it, which the 10-Q cites as lifting non-interest income (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/927628/000092762826000152/cof-20260930.htm) [Likely]. Underwriting data scale is real but the card market is competitive and credit-cycle exposed [Likely].

Direction: FCF margin is rising over five years but fell from 72.2% to 53.7% over the last 12 months (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/cof/financials/cash-flow-statement/?p=trailing). For a lender, FCF is distorted by loan flows, so I weight ROE more [Likely]. Shares outstanding rose 29.78% YoY (Discover and Brex issuance) [Certain].

## 3. Valuation
At 181.76 the stock trades at 10.8x P/E, in the 9th percentile of its 5y range (5.0x–70.6x, median 10.9x), 13% below the 12.5x peer median (AXP, SYF, JPM, BAC) [Certain]. P/B is 1.0x, at the 5y median, and 46% below peers (1.9x). FCF yield is 24.01%, but this is loan-flow noise for a bank [Likely]. EV/EBITDA is [data unavailable].

The mechanical fair value base is 228.30 (+25.6%), with a bear of 64.89 and a bull of 432.06, a very wide range that signals low reliability [Likely]. The P/E method gives 184.09, roughly the current price. The reverse DCF implies -3.0% revenue growth in year 1, so the price assumes stagnation, not growth [Certain]. The stock is 8.7% under both its 50- and 200-day averages, down 17.7% over 3 months against SPY at -6.9%, with RSI 30.0 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/cof/history/). The trend is weak, so the case rests on cheapness at an earnings trough [Likely].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROE TTM (ratios page) stays below 8.0% at the next two quarterly updates.
- Net margin TTM (income statement page) falls below 15%.
- Allowance coverage ratio (10-Q MD&A) is rebuilt above 5.5% on credit deterioration, from 4.92% at 30 September 2026 [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/927628/000092762826000152/cof-20260930.htm).
- Legacy Discover loan balances keep shrinking while the combined loan book stops growing (10-Q MD&A).

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Heavy returns: in the first nine months of 2026 COF paid $1.5 billion of dividends and repurchased $7.9 billion of stock [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/927628/000092762826000152/cof-20260930.htm). Brex cost about $4.5 billion, part paid in stock [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/927628/000092762826000152/cof-20260930.htm). Buybacks are offsetting dilution from the share issuance, but the share count is still up 29.78% YoY [Certain]. FCF conversion of 241.6% TTM is not a clean signal for a lender [Likely].

## 6. Unusual items
Filing delta: the risk factors are unchanged versus the 2025 10-K, with no new risk language [Certain] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/927628/000092762826000152/cof-20260930.htm). The 10-Q shows a net allowance release in Domestic Card on favourable credit; a smaller release would weigh on earnings [Likely]. Berkshire cut its COF stake in Q3 (source: https://nypost.com/2026/11/16/business/berkshire-hathaway-cuts-stakes-in-bank-of-american-capital-one/) [Likely], and I treat it as sentiment, not fundamentals. Short interest is 1.22% of float, so there is no squeeze or crowding issue [Certain]. The 16 November online banking outages do not change my view [Guessing].
