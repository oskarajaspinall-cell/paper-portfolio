# Alphabet Inc. (GOOGL) — Core initiation — 2026-11-16
## 1. Business
Alphabet earns most revenue from advertising (Search, YouTube, Network), plus subscriptions/devices, Google Cloud (infrastructure, Workspace, and now TPU system sales) and loss-making Other Bets (Waymo). Geography, Q3 2026 [company-reported]: United States 53%, EMEA 26%, APAC 16%, Other Americas 5% (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1652044/000165204426000084/goog-20260930.htm). No single foreign country is disclosed at ~10%+. [Certain]

## 2. Quality
Franchise economics remain high: gross margin 60.9% and operating margin 33.3% TTM, both rising over five years; net cash (net debt/EBITDA -0.62x) (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/googl/financials/ratios/). [Certain]

Direction is the issue. Over 12 months ROIC fell 31.5% to 23.8%, ROCE 28.4% to 18.1%, FCF margin 19.1% to 4.2% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/googl/financials/ratios/?p=trailing). The cause is the capex build, not weaker operations: nine-month capex roughly doubled and net PP&E rose from $246.6bn to $372.8bn [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1652044/000165204426000084/goog-20260930.htm). [Likely] Total revenue backlog is $567.7bn [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1652044/000165204426000084/goog-20260930.htm), with just over 55% to be recognised within 24 months [company-reported] (same URL), part tied to a related-party JV. [Certain] Whether capital is earning a return is unproven. [Guessing] Moat: search distribution and data, YouTube, TPU/cloud scale; AI-search and Amazon shopping-search are the contested flank. [Likely]

## 3. Valuation
P/E 15.1x is below its 5y minimum (19.1x) and 30% under peers, but TTM net margin of 50.0% against 33.3% operating margin implies large non-operating gains; the nine-month gains on securities are in the 10-Q (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1652044/000165204426000084/goog-20260930.htm). P/E therefore flatters. [Likely] On cash-based and enterprise measures it is not cheap: EV/EBITDA 19.0x (55% of 5y range, +13% vs peers), P/FCF 184.1x, FCF yield 0.54% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/googl/statistics/). [Certain]

Mechanical fair value is 256.59 base (-13.2% vs 295.77), bear 152.61, bull 278.04; DCF unusable (negative base cash-flow margin), so EV/Revenue alone drives it. The price assumes capex converts to Cloud/AI earnings and free cash flow recovers. [Likely] Shares are 12.6% below the 200-day, 6m momentum -25.5%, 27.1pp behind SPY: the market is already questioning returns. [Certain]

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROIC TTM (ratios ?p=trailing page) stays below 20% for two further quarterly updates.
- FCF margin TTM (cash-flow ?p=trailing page) is still below 5% while operating margin falls below 30%.
- Operating margin TTM (income-statement ?p=trailing page) falls below 30%.
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) turns positive above 0.5x.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Cash conversion has collapsed: FCF/NI 8.4% TTM vs 55.4% FY2025. Capex is funded increasingly externally: Q3 senior notes of $28.7bn net, ATM equity issuance (remaining capacity $27.6bn) and a $10.0bn Berkshire placement in June [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1652044/000165204426000084/goog-20260930.htm). Shares change YoY is only 0.33% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/googl/statistics/), but issuing equity while the price falls is a negative signal. [Likely] New risk factors flag backstops, guarantees for third-party data centres and hardware repurchase obligations (same 10-Q). [Certain]

## 6. Unusual items
- Off-balance-sheet-style commitments (backstops, guarantees, repurchase obligations) newly added to risk factors; extended asset-life estimates are a flagged sensitivity (SEC delta, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1652044/000165204426000084/goog-20260930.htm). [Certain]
- Waymo raised a $5.0bn secured term loan, non-recourse to Alphabet [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1652044/000165204426000084/goog-20260930.htm). [Certain]
- EC Android fine upheld; accrued fine paid in July 2026 (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1652044/000165204426000084/goog-20260930.htm). [Certain]
- Competition for shopping search: Amazon reportedly targeting it (https://www.tipranks.com/news/amazon-stock-amzn-climbs-on-reports-it-wants-to-be-go-to-search-engine-for-shoppers). [Guessing]
- Short interest 0.80% of float; no governance concern beyond dual-class structure. [Likely]
