# Bosideng International (HKG:3998) — Core initiation — 2026-11-16
## 1. Business
Bosideng designs and sells down and winter apparel, mainly under its own Bosideng brand, plus outdoor, children's, business and womenswear lines, through its own stores, wholesale and online. It reports in CNY, so its home market is China; no foreign country is visible as ~10%+ of revenue [Likely]. ## 2. Quality
- Returns are high and rising: ROIC 81.2% TTM vs 34.3% in FY2022, ROE 22.8% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/3998/financials/ratios/) [Certain].
- Operating margin rose from 16.1% to 19.9% even as gross margin slipped from 60.1% to 57.2%, so opex leverage is doing the work [Certain].
- Net cash: net debt/EBITDA -2.24x; Altman Z 5.25 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/3998/statistics/) [Certain].
- FCF margin 20.0% TTM, conversion 137.2% of net income, but lumpy (29.2% to 12.2% to 20.0%) [Certain].
- Direction flags: ROCE is 28.5% against a 2-year high of 34.3%, and FCF margin is below its 2-year high of 29.2%. Both reflect a peak-year working-capital and capex mix, not a decline: ROCE was flat over the last year and FCF margin is up 7.8pp year on year [Likely].
- Moat: brand leadership in China down apparel and owned distribution. Pricing power is the test; falling gross margin is the one crack [Likely]. Weather and fashion cyclicality remain [Likely].

## 3. Valuation
P/E 11.9x sits at 18% of its 5y range (10.3x–19.4x), in line with peers (median 11.9x). EV/EBITDA 5.1x is below its 5y minimum of 5.6x and 2% under peers. P/FCF 8.7x is 36% below peers; FCF yield 11.54% [Certain]. P/B 2.6x is a premium to peers (1.4x), justified by ROE [Likely].

The reverse DCF implies revenue falls 11.7% in year 1, which looks too bearish for a business growing earnings [Likely]. Mechanical fair value is HKD 7.63 base (bear 4.95, bull 13.15) against a 4.57 close, including a 3.5% China risk premium [Likely]. The price is 3.9% above its 200-day average, but the 50-day average is 3.2% below the 200-day, and the 12-month change is -10.1% [Certain]. This is cheap on cash flow, not yet confirmed by trend. Results are due Nov 25, 2026 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/3998/).
