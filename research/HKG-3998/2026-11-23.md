# Bosideng International (HKG:3998) — Core initiation — 2026-11-23
## 1. Business
Bosideng designs and sells down apparel, chiefly under its own Bosideng brand, and reports in CNY (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/3998/). Gross margin of 57.2% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/3998/financials/income-statement/) points to a brand-led, mostly own-retail and wholesale model [Likely]. China is the home market, so there is no separate foreign-country mix to disclose. I did not fetch a segment split [data unavailable].

## 2. Quality
- Returns are high and rising: ROIC 68.7% (65.4% in FY2025), ROE 22.8%, operating margin 19.9% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/3998/financials/ratios/) [Certain].
- Net cash: net debt/EBITDA is -2.24x. Altman Z is 5.25 and Piotroski F is 5 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/3998/statistics/) [Certain].
- Gross margin has slipped from 60.1% (FY2022) to 57.2%. That is the main sign of pricing or mix pressure [Certain].
- The brand is a plausible durable moat in the down-jacket category, but I have not verified market share [Guessing].
- Direction flags: ROCE is 5.8pp below its 2-year high of 34.3%, but it is flat over the last year (-0.2pp). FCF margin is 9.2pp below its 2-year high of 29.2%, but it rose 7.8pp over the last year to 20.0% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/3998/financials/ratios/?p=trailing). The FY2024 FCF margin of 29.2% looks like a working-capital peak, since FCF/NI was 220.5%, so the decline is largely normalisation [Likely].

## 3. Valuation
P/E is 11.5x against a 5y median of 12.8x (13% of its 5y range) and a peer median of 14.6x. EV/EBITDA is 4.9x, below its 5y minimum of 5.6x, and 37% below peers. FCF yield is 11.97% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/3998/statistics/) [Certain]. The mechanical fair value is 7.66 HKD base (bear 4.96, bull 13.22) against a 4.46 close. The reverse DCF implies revenue shrinking 12.8% in year 1 (source: research/HKG-3998/factsheet-2026-11-23.md). The price therefore assumes a sharp decline in a business with rising returns [Likely]. Caveats: a 3.5% China risk premium is built into the model, and a CNY consumer and policy discount may be justified [Guessing]. The price sits just above its 200-day average (+1.3%), so the trend is neutral rather than strong.

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Gross margin (income statement page) falls below 54%.
- ROIC (ratios page, TTM) falls below 40%.
- FCF margin (cash-flow page, TTM) falls below 10%.
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) turns positive, i.e. net cash is gone.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
FCF conversion is 137.2% of net income in FY2026 (90.1% in FY2025). The company is net cash. Shares outstanding rose 1.14% YoY, so there is mild dilution rather than buybacks (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/3998/statistics/) [Certain]. I have not verified the dividend policy [data unavailable].

## 6. Unusual items

- The business is a seasonal winter-apparel play. The 12-month price change is -13.5% with no identified company-specific cause in the listed headlines [Certain].
