# Bosideng International Holdings (HKG:3998) — Core initiation — 2026-10-17
## 1. Business
Bosideng is China's leading down-jacket and outerwear group, selling branded apparel (Bosideng, Bogner, Snow Flying, Moiselle womenswear) through owned and franchised stores plus e-commerce, alongside a smaller OEM/ODM manufacturing segment for third-party brands and a diversified-apparel unit [Certain]. Classified Consumer Discretionary / Apparel Manufacturing (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/3998/). Revenue and earnings are seasonally concentrated in the autumn/winter half [Likely].

## 2. Quality
Bosideng compounds high returns on capital: ROIC 81.2% TTM (up from 34.3% in FY2022), ROE 22.8% TTM, operating margin up to 19.9% from 16.1% over five years, and FCF conversion of 137% of net income [Certain] (source: fact sheet RA/IS/CF). The balance sheet carries net cash (net debt/EBITDA -2.24x) [Certain]. The durable element is decades of brand incumbency as China's default down-jacket brand, a category where brand recall at the point of a cold-weather purchase is high [Likely]. That said apparel is structurally a weaker moat than most businesses: peers Anta and Li Ning are both pushing into outerwear, and Moncler/Uniqlo compete at the premium and value ends respectively [Likely]. Results are weather-dependent (a mild winter cuts volume), and the five-year gross margin has drifted down 2.8pp even as operating margin rose, implying cost control rather than pricing power is driving the margin gain [Likely].

## 3. Valuation
Bosideng trades near the low end of its own five-year multiple range on P/E (12th percentile, 11.3x vs 10.3x/12.8x/19.4x) and below its own five-year range on EV/EBITDA and EV/Sales [Certain]. Against the three listed peers (Li Ning, Anta, Xtep) it is more expensive on EV/EBITDA (+44%), EV/Sales (+110%) and P/B (+137%), reflecting materially higher ROE/ROIC, but cheaper on P/FCF (-21%) [Certain]. FCF yield is 12.15% [Certain]. The fact sheet's own-history DCF (best method) puts base fair value at HKD 7.62, +73% above the HKD 4.41 last close; even the bear case (HKD 4.94) is +12.2% above the current price [Certain] (source: fact sheet fair-value table). The reverse-DCF implied growth (-13% revenue in year 1) is well below Bosideng's recent trajectory, suggesting the market is pricing in a demand or margin reset the historical numbers don't yet show [Likely].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Gross margin (income statement page, IS) falls below 55% for two consecutive half-years, signalling loss of pricing power rather than a mix shift.
- Net margin (income statement page, IS) falls below 10% on a TTM basis, indicating the brand premium has eroded.
- ROIC (ratios page, RA) falls below 40% TTM, a reversion toward peer-level returns that would undercut the competitive-moat thesis.
- FCF conversion (cash-flow/income-statement pages, CF/IS) falls below 80% of net income for a full fiscal year, suggesting working-capital/inventory stress typical of a demand down-cycle.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Shares outstanding rose 1.14% YoY [Certain] (source: ST), modest dilution consistent with employee incentive schemes rather than an equity raise [Guessing]. FCF conversion has averaged well above 100% of net income over five years (101-220%, TTM 137%) [Certain], funding the net-cash position (net debt/EBITDA -2.24x) without external capital [Certain]. High FCF conversion plus net cash gives Bosideng flexibility to sustain distributions through a weak retail season without balance-sheet stress [Likely]; the fact sheet does not show the dividend payout ratio or buyback amount, so the size of distributions is [data unavailable].

## 6. Unusual items
All revenue is earned in China (reporting currency CNY) with the stock listed in HKD — a single-country consumer-discretionary and currency exposure for a USD portfolio, already reflected in the fair-value country-risk premium [Certain]. Short-interest level and its change versus the prior month are unavailable (source: ST) [Certain]. No governance flags: institutional ownership 22.1%, beta 0.57, Altman Z 5.25, Piotroski F 5 [Certain].
