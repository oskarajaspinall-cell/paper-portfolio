# Bosideng International Holdings (HKG:3998) — Core initiation — 2026-11-08
## 1. Business
China's leading down-jacket group: branded outerwear (Bosideng, Bogner, Snow Flying) sold through owned and franchised stores and e-commerce, plus a smaller OEM/ODM manufacturing unit and womenswear [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/3998/). Earnings are concentrated in the autumn/winter half [Likely]. Revenue is overwhelmingly mainland China; the reporting currency is CNY and the shares quote in HKD [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/3998/). No single foreign country is ~10%+ of revenue [Guessing].

## 2. Quality
Returns on capital are high and the balance sheet is net cash: ROIC 81.2% TTM, ROE 22.8%, operating margin 19.9% (16.1% in FY2022), net debt/EBITDA -2.24x [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/3998/financials/ratios/).

Direction changes, addressed:
- ROCE is 28.5% against a 2-year high of 34.3% (Sept 2024), but flat year on year (-0.2pp): normalisation from a peak, not deterioration [Likely].
- FCF margin is 20.0%, below the 29.2% high (Mar 2024) but up 7.8pp year on year. FY2024's 220% conversion was a working-capital release; 90% in FY2025 and 137% now show the range [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/3998/financials/cash-flow-statement/).
- Gross margin is down 2.8pp over five years (57.2%) while operating margin rose: cost control, not pricing, drives profit [Likely].

Moat: brand incumbency, but weaker than most; Anta and Li Ning are entering outerwear and weather drives volume [Likely].
