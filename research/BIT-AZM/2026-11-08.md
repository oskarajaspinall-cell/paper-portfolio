## 2. Quality
Highly profitable but a cash-flow-lumpy, market-sensitive model [Likely]. TTM ROIC is 32.5% and operating margin 43.0% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/bit/AZM/financials/ratios/). Net debt/EBITDA is -0.31x, i.e. net cash [Certain]. FCF margin swings (FY2022 -8.5%, FY2024 -14.4%, TTM 50.4%), so single-year FCF is unreliable [Likely].

Direction is a negative: TTM ROIC is down 4.5pp from 37.0%, operating margin down 5.4pp year on year (48.4% to 43.0%), 6.2pp below its 2-year high (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/bit/AZM/financials/income-statement/?p=trailing). Likely performance-fee or mix normalisation, not confirmed [Guessing]. ROCE is low (6.0%) because the balance sheet carries large financial assets [Guessing]. Moat is distribution/advisor network and client stickiness, moderate rather than wide [Likely]. Management cited record net inflows and recurring-profit growth in the FY2025 call (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/bit/AZM/transcripts/406324-q4-2025/) [Likely].
