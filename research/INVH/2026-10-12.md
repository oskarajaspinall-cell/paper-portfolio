# Invitation Homes Inc. (INVH) — Core initiation — 2026-10-12

## 1. Business
Invitation Homes is the largest owner-operator of single-family rental homes in the US, leasing and managing a Sun Belt-weighted portfolio for institutional and household renters. Revenue is recurring rent plus ancillary fees; growth comes from occupancy, lease rate growth, new-construction ("ResiBuilt") partnerships with builders, and selective wholly-owned acquisitions, funded partly by disposing of non-core homes [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/invh/transcripts/658192-q2-2026/).

## 2. Quality
Scale gives local-market density advantages in maintenance/leasing costs that smaller landlords cannot easily replicate, and weak starter-home affordability structurally supports rental demand [Likely]. ROE rose from 2.9% (FY2021) to 7.1% TTM, and net debt/EBITDA (site definition) fell from 6.72x to 5.65x — a genuine deleveraging trend, though leverage stays high [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/invh/financials/ratios/). Piotroski F-Score is 5/9, neutral [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/invh/statistics/). Margin and ROIC/ROCE data are [data unavailable] for this REIT structure. Q2 2026 management reported 97.1% average occupancy, accelerating renewal rent growth (4.3% in July), and raised full-year core FFO/AFFO guidance [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/invh/transcripts/658192-q2-2026/). Management stated the new ROAD to Housing Act does not ban institutional single-family ownership, countering a third-party headline framing it that way [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/invh/transcripts/658192-q2-2026/; headline: https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/11/invitation-homes-ceo-institutional-homebuying-ban-prices.html).

## 3. Valuation
Every multiple sits below both its own 5-year range and the peer median: P/E 24.6x (5y min 29.0x; peer median 29.7x, -17%), EV/EBITDA 16.2x (5y min 16.9x; peer 18.7x, -13%), EV/Sales 8.6x (5y min 9.3x; peer 10.5x, -18%), P/B 1.7x (5y min 1.8x; peer 2.3x, -25%) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/invh/financials/ratios/; peers AMH/EQR/MAA/AVB statistics pages). The stock trades near a multi-year low on every lens despite an improving ROE and falling leverage. The fact sheet's own-history fair value (P/FFO-proxy method, best method for this industry; NAV unavailable) puts bear/base/bull at 30.05/41.16/41.78 vs last close 26.79 — +12.2%/+53.6%/+56.0% [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/invh/ fair value model). FCF yield of 0.04% is not a useful cross-check here: SFR REITs reinvest almost all operating cash flow into home acquisition/construction, so near-zero reported FCF is structural, not a distress signal [Likely].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROE (ratios page) falls back below 5%, reversing the five-year improving trend.
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page, site definition) rises back above 6.5x, reversing the deleveraging trend.
- Piotroski F-Score (statistics page) falls below 4.
- Occupancy or lease-growth guidance is cut at a future earnings release (next report: Oct 28, 2026), evidencing the H2 execution risk management itself flagged (seasonal turnover, property-tax uncertainty, ResiBuilt delays) [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/invh/transcripts/658192-q2-2026/).

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Since December, management has repurchased $600m of stock at an average $26.30 — a discount to the ~$450,000 average home sale price it cites — while raising disposition guidance by $300m to $850m and holding wholly-owned acquisition guidance at $250m [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/invh/transcripts/658192-q2-2026/). Shares outstanding fell 1.17% YoY, consistent with buybacks exceeding issuance [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/invh/statistics/). FCF-based conversion metrics are [data unavailable]/not meaningful for this REIT structure (see Valuation).

## 6. Unusual items
Short interest is 2.90% of float but rose 20.9% in the past month, and institutional ownership is high at 92.3% — neither extreme but worth monitoring given the regulatory headline noise around institutional SFR ownership [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/invh/statistics/). Beta of 0.83 implies below-market volatility [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/invh/statistics/).
