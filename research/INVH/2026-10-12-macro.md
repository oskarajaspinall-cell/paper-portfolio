# Macro overlay: Invitation Homes Inc. (INVH) — 2026-10-12
## Assessment
INVH is a highly levered (net debt/EBITDA 5.65x [Certain], source: research/INVH/factsheet-2026-10-12.md) SFR REIT, so it sits on the classic rate chain: **real yields -> discount rate/cap-rate assumptions -> valuation multiple**, plus **real yields -> refinancing cost -> FFO/margin**. Facts: 10y real yield (DFII10) 2.87% on 2026-10-08, +44bp in 1m / +56bp in 3m; 10y nominal (DGS10) 5.22%, +42bp/+68bp; effective fed funds (DFF) 3.88%, +25bp in 1m — all FRED, https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=DFII10|DGS10|DFF (2026-10-08) [Certain]. 10y breakeven (T10YIE) is roughly flat (-4bp 1m, +9bp 3m), so this rise is real-rate, not inflation, driven [Certain] — the kind of move that hits cap rates directly. Market pricing (proxy, not certain): 2y yield (4.75%) sits ~87bp above fed funds (3.88%), implying the market still prices further hikes, not cuts **(inference)**. A commentator source (ETF Trends, 2026-09-30) flags a possible further hike — commentator view, not fact. INVH's own fair-value model already uses today's 5.22% risk-free rate, so the stock's -17% to -25% discount to peers/own history already reflects this higher-rate backdrop; the surprise risk is whether yields keep climbing (hurts) or roll over (helps a re-rating and cheaper refinancing).

**Bull**: yields/Fed pause or reverse -> cost of equity and cap rates fall -> multiple re-rates toward peer median, refinancing cost pressure eases. **Bear**: real yields and fed funds keep rising -> discount rate/cap rate assumptions rise further, refinancing costs on the 5.65x leverage increase -> fair value estimate and relative multiple discount persist or widen.
