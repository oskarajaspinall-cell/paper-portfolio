# Engie Brasil Energia (BVMF:EGIE3) — Core initiation — 2026-11-08
## 1. Business
Brazilian renewable-weighted power generator (hydro, wind, solar) selling mostly under long-term contracts, plus a growing electricity transmission portfolio with inflation-indexed regulated revenue [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/bvmf/EGIE3/). Operations are domestic, so revenue and costs are in BRL; no material foreign revenue region [Likely]. The Q2 2026 call cited new wind/solar assets, the Jirau acquisition, transmission and battery projects as growth drivers [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/bvmf/EGIE3/transcripts/539647-q2-2026/).

## 2. Quality
Mixed. Returns are high but eroding: ROCE fell from 17.1% (FY2021) to 11.3% (FY2025), TTM 12.0%, only +0.3pp versus a year earlier and below the 13.5% two-year high [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/bvmf/EGIE3/financials/ratios/). ROE of 28.6% TTM is flattered by leverage and one-off gains; FY2025 was 21.8% and the Q4 2025 call noted absent one-time gains [Likely]. Net margin 28.9% TTM against 20.1% in FY2025 suggests TTM includes non-recurring items [Guessing]. Contracted, long-dated assets and a beta of 0.36 give a defensive moat via regulated or contracted tariffs and scarce grid-connected capacity [Likely]. Net debt/EBITDA (site definition) rose from 2.34x to 3.56x as the company invests heavily; Piotroski F is 5 [Certain]. Operating margin and ROIC are [data unavailable].

## 3. Valuation
P/E 7.8x sits at 8% of its 5y range (6.8x–20.0x, median 11.6x) and 11% below the peer median 8.9x [Certain]. EV/EBITDA 8.1x is 54% into its range and in line with peers (8.0x), so leverage-adjusted the discount disappears [Certain]. P/FCF 10.8x is below its 5y minimum, but TTM FCF margin of 26.1% followed a year of negative FCF (-23.1% in FY2024), so it is probably cyclical on capex timing [Likely]. P/B 2.4x is at its 5y low but 62% above peers. Fair value base is 31.06 BRL (+14.4% versus 27.15), bull 33.83; the bear case is negative and not meaningful [Certain]. The reverse DCF implies -0.7% year-1 revenue growth, which looks undemanding for a business adding capacity [Likely]. Upside is modest, not a large margin of safety.
