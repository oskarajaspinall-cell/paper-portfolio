# Engie Brasil Energia (BVMF:EGIE3) — Core initiation — 2026-11-16
## 1. Business
Brazilian independent power producer (hydro, wind, solar, thermal) selling mostly contracted power, expanding into transmission lines and battery storage; part of the Engie group. Revenue is in BRL and essentially all Brazilian, so no separate foreign-country exposure [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/bvmf/EGIE3/). Growth comes from new assets, not volume on a mature base [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/bvmf/EGIE3/transcripts/669525-q3-2026/).

## 2. Quality
Cash generative, but returns are eroding and leverage is rising.
- ROCE fell from 17.1% (FY2021) to 10.8% TTM, 2.4pp below its 2-year high of 13.2% [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/bvmf/EGIE3/financials/ratios/). Dilution and new, not-yet-earning assets plausibly depress it [Likely].
- FCF margin is 22.1% TTM vs -0.1% a year earlier, 4.0pp under its 2-year high; FY2024 was -23.1%, so FCF is lumpy with capex [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/bvmf/EGIE3/financials/cash-flow-statement/).
- Net debt/EBITDA is 4.14x (own definition) vs 3.15x in FY2021, a clear drift up [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/bvmf/EGIE3/financials/balance-sheet/).
- Moat: long-dated contracted assets and concessions, low beta of 0.36 [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/bvmf/EGIE3/statistics/). The moat protects cash flows, not rising returns on new capital.

## 3. Valuation
P/E 7.8x sits at the 8% point of its 5y range (6.8x–20.0x), 7% above the peer median of 7.3x. EV/EBITDA 7.8x is mid-range (37%) and 4% above peers. P/FCF 11.7x and P/B 1.7x are below their 5y minimums, with FCF yield 8.53% [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/bvmf/EGIE3/statistics/). Mechanical base fair value is 23.72 BRL, 9.6% below the 26.25 close; bull is 26.23, so the price already assumes the bull case (the reverse DCF implies 3.8% year-1 revenue growth) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/bvmf/EGIE3/financials/income-statement/). The multiples look cheap against own history, but earnings multiples fall while ROCE and leverage worsen, and no peer discount exists [Likely]. Not a margin-of-safety entry.

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROCE (ratios page, TTM) falls below 9%.
- Net debt / EBITDA (site definition, ratios page) rises above 4.5x.
- FCF margin (rolling 12 months, cash-flow page) falls below 10%.
- Shares outstanding (statistics page) rise a further 5% in a year, beyond the 5.01% already seen.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Shares rose 5.01% YoY after a mid-2026 equity raise, which funded growth rather than returning cash [Certain] (source: https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/engie-brasils-share-offering-raises-17-billion-2026-07-15/). Management still keeps a dividend payout policy and says it is focused on leverage [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/bvmf/EGIE3/transcripts/669525-q3-2026/). FCF/NI conversion is 78.6% TTM vs 61.5% in FY2025, improving [Certain]. Capex-heavy growth in transmission and batteries must earn above the cost of capital, and ROCE says it has not yet.

## 6. Unusual items
Why the price fell: the stock is 17.4% below its 200-day average and RSI is 30.1, after the equity offering and Q3 results on 2026-11-11 that showed only modest earnings growth [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/bvmf/EGIE3/transcripts/669525-q3-2026/). Brazilian high interest rates raise financing cost, as management noted in Q1 [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/bvmf/EGIE3/transcripts/530323-q1-2026/). Relative strength vs SPY is -27.4pp over 12 months. Piotroski F is 4 [Certain]. Short interest [data unavailable].
