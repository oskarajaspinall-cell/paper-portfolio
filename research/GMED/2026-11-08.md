# Globus Medical (GMED) — Core initiation — 2026-11-08
## 1. Business
Globus sells spine implants, trauma and neuromonitoring products (Musculoskeletal Solutions) plus robotics, navigation and imaging (Enabling Technologies, Excelsius), recurring via implant pull-through in surgeon-chosen procedures. It added spinal cord stimulation through the Nevro merger. International sales are a minority; the 10-Q names no single foreign country at ~10%+ [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1237831/000162828026071165/gmed-20260930.htm). [Likely]

## 2. Quality
Profitable and cash-generative with a net-cash balance sheet: TTM ROIC 13.7%, operating margin 23.8%, FCF margin 24.4%, net debt/EBITDA -0.40x, Altman Z 10.86, Piotroski 7 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/gmed/statistics/). [Certain] The last 12 months improved on every returns measure (ROIC +2.2pp, operating margin +5.0pp, FCF margin +3.5pp), reversing the FY2023-24 trough after the merger integration (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/gmed/financials/ratios/?p=trailing). [Certain] The 5-year "falling" FCF-margin label is an endpoint effect. Gross margin is 69.3% against 75.0% in FY2021, a real mix shift. [Certain]

The moat is surgeon preference and installed robots; it is moderate, not wide. Q3 revenue was down slightly year on year and FY26 revenue guidance was lowered, on Nevro weakness and delayed spine launches (source: https://www.tipranks.com/news/the-fly/globus-medical-sees-fy26-non-gaap-eps-4-95-5-05-consensus-5-00-thefly-news). [Likely] Spinal cord stimulation US trial volumes are falling, while the 10-Q says Enabling Technologies unit deployment rose 52% in the US [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1237831/000162828026071165/gmed-20260930.htm). [Certain] Nevro is not earning its keep yet. [Likely]

## 3. Valuation
At 71.05 USD, P/E is 17.5x, below the 5y minimum of 21.7x and 34% under the peer median (SYK, ZBH, MDT). EV/EBITDA is 8.4x (5y range 13.0x-24.0x), P/FCF 12.3x and FCF yield 8.14% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/gmed/statistics/). [Certain] The reverse DCF implies year-1 revenue growth of -0.3%, i.e. continued stagnation is priced in. [Certain] Inform-only base fair value is 131.26 USD, with bear 37.78 and bull 203.79; the spread is wide because the DCF is sensitive to the growth path. [Likely] Part of the discount to the 5y history reflects earlier merger-depressed earnings inflating historical P/E. [Likely] The shares fell 12.4% in 3 months and trade 8.0% under the 50-day average, so sentiment is poor and short interest is 7.50% of float, up 21.0% on the month. [Certain]

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
FCF conversion is 137.5% of net income on a TTM basis (FY2025: 109.5%). The share count is down 0.58% year on year, and a $500 million repurchase authorization increase was announced with Q3 results [company-reported] (source: https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/11/05/3397018/0/en/globus-medical-reports-third-quarter-2026-results-and-announces-a-500-million-increase-to-the-share-repurchase-program.html). [Likely] Buybacks at a depressed multiple are sensible. M&A is the risk: Synaptive Medical (cranial) closed on 2026-11-03 (source: https://www.tipranks.com/news/the-fly/globus-medical-acquires-cranial-neurosurgery-tech-firm-synaptive-medical-thefly-news). [Likely] After Nevro, further deals need proving. [Guessing]

## 6. Unusual items
The 10-Q shows a $24.6 million lower provision for litigation in Q3 [company-reported], which flatters SG&A comparisons (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1237831/000162828026071165/gmed-20260930.htm). [Certain] The 10-Q risk factors are unchanged. Insiders own 17.41%. Q3 cost of sales fell on cost-cutting and the absence of last year's inventory step-up charge. [Likely]
