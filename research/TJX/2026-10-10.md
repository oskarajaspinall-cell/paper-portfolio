# The TJX Companies, Inc. (TJX) — Core initiation — 2026-10-10

## 1. Business
TJX runs off-price retail banners (T.J. Maxx, Marshalls, HomeGoods/HomeSense, Winners, TK Maxx, Sierra) that buy excess, closeout and opportunistic branded inventory from thousands of vendors and sell it at a discount to department/specialty-store prices [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/tjx/). Revenue is driven by store count plus comp-store transactions and basket, across four divisions: Marmaxx, HomeGoods, Canada and International [Certain].

## 2. Quality
TTM ROIC is 30.1% and ROCE 31.9%, both rising over five years (+1.8pp, +5.6pp); FCF margin has risen to 9.4% and FCF conversion to 96.9%; net debt/EBITDA is a conservative 0.93x [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/tjx/financials/ratios/). The moat is the flexible, no-long-lead-time buying model: scale (5,000+ stores) and vendor breadth let TJX opportunistically absorb excess branded inventory that smaller off-price peers (ROST, BURL) cannot match, and the in-store "treasure hunt" format is structurally harder for e-commerce to substitute than conventional apparel retail [Likely]. Durability has a near-term wrinkle: Marmaxx, the largest division, grew comps far slower than HomeGoods, Canada and International last quarter, which management attributed to store-mix execution issues it says it is fixing [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/tjx/transcripts/562422-q2-2027/). Gross margin also benefited from tariff-cost favorability that management flagged will partly lap out in the back half, a timing tailwind rather than a permanent structural gain [Likely] (source: same transcript).

## 3. Valuation
Current multiples sit at a modest premium to the peer median (P/E +3%, EV/EBITDA +9%, EV/Sales +8%, P/FCF +2%; peers ROST, BURL, FIVE) but low in TJX's own five-year range (P/E 16th percentile, P/FCF 2nd percentile), so the market isn't paying up versus TJX's own history even as margins expand [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/tjx/financials/ratios/). P/B is 65% above peers, reflecting a buyback-shrunk book value/high ROE, not a useful signal here [Likely]. Base fair value is $98.57, 29% below the $138.76 close, while the EV/EBITDA estimate ($136.02) sits near the market price; the reverse DCF implies ~17% year-1 revenue growth fading to terminal, above TJX's recent high-single-digit comps [Certain] (source: fact sheet fair-value table). This likely reflects the DCF's growth/terminal-growth caps understating a high-ROIC compounder as much as overvaluation, but it's a real risk to flag [Guessing].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Gross margin (income statement page) falls below 29%, giving back the tariff/merchandise-margin gains behind FY2026's 31.0% level.
- ROIC (ratios page) falls below 28% (below the 5y low of 28.3%), signalling the off-price buying-scale advantage is eroding.
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) rises above 1.3x, above the 5y high of 1.26x, indicating capital discipline has slipped.
- FCF conversion, FCF/NI (cash flow and income statement pages) falls below 75%, reversing the structural improvement toward 97%.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
FCF conversion is 96.9% of net income TTM and FCF margin has risen to 9.4%, both at five-year highs [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/tjx/financials/ratios/). The balance sheet is conservative at 0.93x net debt/EBITDA, falling over five years [Certain]. Shares outstanding fell 1.1% year-on-year, consistent with ongoing buybacks, and TJX continues to pay a quarterly dividend [Certain] (source: fact sheet news item, dividend announcement). High cash generation and a clean balance sheet give management room to keep funding store growth, buybacks and the dividend without added leverage [Likely].

## 6. Unusual items
None material. One new non-executive director (Craig Pintoff) was added to the board in September 2026, a routine governance addition [Certain] (source: https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260917345665/en/The-TJX-Companies-Inc.-Elects-Craig-A.-Pintoff-to-Board-of-Directors/). Short interest is low and falling (1.68% of float, -6.5% month-on-month) and institutional ownership is high (93.9%) — no positioning red flags [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/tjx/statistics/). Scattered individual store closures were reported (Fast Company) but are immaterial against a 5,000+ store base [Likely] (source: https://www.fastcompany.com/91615224/tj-maxx-stores-closing-locations-2026).
