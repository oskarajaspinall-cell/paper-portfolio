# Masco Corporation (MAS) — Core initiation — 2026-10-07
## 1. Business
Masco designs, manufactures and distributes branded home-improvement products via two segments: Plumbing Products (Delta, Hansgrohe, Brizo faucets/fixtures) and Decorative Architectural Products (Behr/Kilz paint, Liberty hardware) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/mas/). It sells through home centers, wholesalers and specialty retailers in North America and internationally; The Home Depot is ~38% of FY2025 net sales and holds exclusive North American Behr distribution [Certain] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/62996/000006299626000005/mas-20251231.htm). Demand is driven by repair/remodel and new-construction spend on faucets, fixtures, paint and hardware.

## 2. Quality
ROIC is 30.8% TTM (down from 32.7% FY21) and ROCE 36.1% TTM (up from 28.4% FY21); operating margin has risen 4.4pp over five years to 17.8% TTM and gross margin to 37.2% TTM [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/mas/financials/ratios/). These returns reflect brand equity (Behr's exclusive Home Depot deal, Delta/Hansgrohe share leadership) and asset-light manufacturing, creating contractor/retailer switching costs [Likely] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/62996/000006299626000005/mas-20251231.htm). The main threat to durability is customer concentration: Home Depot at ~38% of sales gives the retailer shelf-space and pricing leverage [Certain] (source: same 10-K). Tariff and raw-material exposure (brass, resins, titanium dioxide, Asia-sourced plumbing parts) is an ongoing cost pressure the filing says is hard to fully pass through [Likely] (source: same 10-K). FCF conversion is volatile (127.2% TTM, down 88.7pp over five years) — more working-capital noise than structural decay, but worth monitoring. Piotroski F-score 6/9 and Altman Z 3.86 (safe zone) support balance-sheet quality [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/mas/statistics/).

## 3. Valuation
MAS trades at 15.7x P/E, 11.0x EV/EBITDA and 12.1x P/FCF — near the bottom of its own five-year range (11–21st percentile; P/FCF is actually below the 5y minimum) and 18–37% below the peer median (FBIN, ALLE, SHW, RPM) on every multiple except EV/Sales, where it sits 20% below peers but at the 76th percentile of its own range [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/mas/financials/ratios/). FCF yield is 8.29% [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/mas/statistics/). The market appears to be pricing continued margin/ROIC mean-reversion, tariff cost pressure and housing-demand softness — consistent with -0.6% 12-month share performance and roughly -17pp relative strength versus SPY over both 3 and 12 months [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/mas/history/). A premium-brand, high-ROIC business trading at a material discount to both its own history and listed peers is an attractive asymmetry if margin and returns hold near current levels.

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Gross margin (income statement page) falls below 34% (the 5y low) for two consecutive quarters
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) rises above 2.1x, above the 5y range
- ROIC (ratios page) falls below 28%, below the 5y range
- FCF margin (cash flow/income statement pages) falls below 9%, below the 5y low

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Masco returns nearly all free cash flow to shareholders: ~8.5m shares repurchased for ~$576m in FY2025, a new $2.0bn buyback authorized February 2026, and the quarterly dividend raised ~7% year over year [Certain] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/62996/000006299626000005/mas-20251231.htm). Shares outstanding fell 4.66% year over year [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/mas/statistics/). Net debt/EBITDA has stayed in a stable 1.7–2.1x band over five years [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/mas/financials/ratios/), leaving room for continued buybacks without a material leverage increase, assuming EBITDA holds.

## 6. Unusual items
Reported ROE is not a usable quality signal: years of buybacks have shrunk book equity toward zero, producing a TTM ROE of 5,862.5% (FY2024: 2,731.2%) that reflects a thin equity base, not economics [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/mas/financials/ratios/). Customer concentration (Home Depot ~38% of FY2025 sales, exclusive Behr distribution) is a single-retailer dependency [Certain] (source: 10-K above). Short interest rose 21.3% month over month to 4.57% of float [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/mas/statistics/) — modest in absolute terms, not a crowded short. Management raised FY2026 earnings guidance and cited a one-time tariff-related benefit on the Q2 2026 call; exact figures are headline-reported and excluded here per house rules, but the direction is a positive read on near-term execution [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/mas/transcripts/652841-q2-2026/).
