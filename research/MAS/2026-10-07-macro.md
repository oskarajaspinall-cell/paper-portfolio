# Macro overlay: Masco Corporation (MAS) — 2026-10-07

## Assessment
Masco's Plumbing and Decorative Architectural segments depend on home-turnover-linked repair/remodel spend, which tracks mortgage rates derived from the 10y Treasury yield; higher real yields also raise the discount rate on MAS's multiple. Chain: policy path -> 10y nominal/real yields -> mortgage rates -> home turnover/remodel demand -> MAS revenue, and separately real yields -> discount rate -> multiple.

Facts [Certain] (FRED, 2026-10-05/06): 10y real yield (DFII10) 2.95%, +0.52pp 1m, +0.71pp 3m; 10y nominal (DGS10) 5.31%, +0.83pp 3m; fed funds (DFF) 3.88%, the full +0.25pp move within the last month; 2y yield (DGS2) 4.84%; HY credit spread (BAMLH0A0HYM2) 3.12%, +0.44pp 1m. Market pricing (proxy, **(inference)**): 2y yield ~1pp above fed funds implies no near-term cuts priced, possibly further tightening — hawkish versus the "rates ease into 2027" narrative common in housing commentary. Breakeven inflation (T10YIE) barely moved (+0.11pp 3m), so the yield back-up is real-rate, not inflation, driven — raising MAS's discount rate and mortgage costs together.

MAS already fell 13.7% over 3 months (-17.4pp vs SPY), consistent with partial absorption of this repricing [Likely] (inference).

**macro_bull**: yields roll over (CPI cools, Fed pivots) -> mortgage rates ease, remodel/turnover demand recovers, multiple re-rates toward peer median.
**macro_bear**: real yields stay elevated/rise further -> mortgage rates stay high, remodel demand softens, MAS's cheap multiple stays cheap or compresses further, delaying the valuation-reversion thesis.
**Tilt**: moderate toward bear — the valuation thesis needs a re-rating catalyst, and the real-yield move is still in progress, not reversing.
