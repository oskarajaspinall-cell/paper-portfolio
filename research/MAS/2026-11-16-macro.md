# Macro overlay: Masco Corporation (MAS) — 2026-11-16
## Assessment
**Chain:** long rates -> mortgage cost and housing turnover -> repair-and-remodel demand and volumes; the same rates set the discount rate on a stock at 18.4x P/E [Likely]. The weight is `secondary`, not `primary`, because pricing power and 112.6% FCF conversion cushion the earnings link (research note, 2026-11-16).

**Facts (FRED):** the 10y Treasury is 5.10%, +0.47pp over ~3m (2026-11-12, https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=DGS10). The 10y real yield is 2.86%, +0.47pp (https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=DFII10). The 2y is 4.86%, +0.71pp, against fed funds of 3.88% (https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=DGS2). Breakevens are 2.24%, flat (2026-11-13, https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=T10YIE). The high-yield spread is 3.2% (2026-11-12, https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=BAMLH0A0HYM2).

**Pricing (proxy):** a 2y yield about 1pp above fed funds implies expected hikes, not cuts. **(inference)** The rise is real-yield driven, the worse mix for housing and multiples. Credit is calm. MAS sits 18.2% above its 200-day average, so the price already looks through higher rates.

**Bull:** yields fall, mortgage costs ease, volumes recover and the multiple holds.
**Bear:** real yields keep rising, housing turnover stays frozen, volumes fall, margin slips toward the 15% invalidation line and the multiple de-rates.

**Tilt:** toward bear, small. The price implies 8.2% year-1 growth against a flat housing backdrop.

**Country:** Europe is part of the ~21% international sales. The euro is down about 3.5% over ~3m (https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=DEXUSEU, 2026-11-06): a modest translation headwind.
