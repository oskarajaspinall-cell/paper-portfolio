# Masco Corporation (MAS) — Core initiation — 2026-11-16
## 1. Business
Branded home-improvement products. Plumbing Products is about 69% of sales and Decorative Architectural (paint, hardware) about 31% [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/62996/000006299626000033/mas-20260930.htm). International, all Plumbing, is about 21% of nine-month sales [company-reported] (same URL), mainly Europe. No single foreign country is disclosed at 10%+ in what I read [Likely]. Demand is repair-and-remodel led and priced up against tariffs and commodities.

## 2. Quality
High-return franchise [Certain]: TTM ROIC 26.7% and ROCE 35.3%, gross margin 36.6%, operating margin 16.9% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/mas/financials/ratios/). Brands and plumbing channel position support pricing power [Likely]: Q3 net pricing added about five points to sales while volumes were mixed [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/62996/000006299626000033/mas-20260930.htm).

Direction change, addressed: ROIC is down 7.0pp in 12 months (33.7% to 26.7%), 10.1pp below its 2-year high, while operating margin is flat (16.9% vs 17.0%) and FCF margin is up 2.5pp to 13.6% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/mas/financials/ratios/?p=trailing). Margins held, so the ROIC fall points to a larger invested-capital base rather than weaker profitability [Guessing]. Net debt/EBITDA is 1.98x; Altman Z is 3.83 [Certain].

Q3 EPS beat and FY26 adjusted EPS guidance was raised to $4.65-$4.75 (source: https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20261028013042/en/Masco-Corporation-Reports-Third-Quarter-2026-Results/) [Likely].

## 3. Valuation
Price 84.68 USD, 18.2% above the 200-day average, near the 52-week high of 87.86 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/mas/history/). P/E is 18.4x, 20% of the way up its 5y range and 23% below peers [Certain]. EV/EBITDA is 13.2x (53% of range, 10% above peers), P/FCF 15.7x (33%, 16% above peers) and EV/Sales is above its 5y max [Certain]. FCF yield is 6.39% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/mas/statistics/).

The mechanical fair value is 60.48 base (-28.6% vs the close), with a DCF of 54.95 and EV/EBITDA of 71.54; the reverse DCF implies 8.2% year-1 revenue growth [Certain]. The price already assumes a housing recovery. Management itself assumes no major market rebound for 2027 [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/mas/transcripts/792237-baird-2026-global-industrial-conference/). Multiples are fair on earnings but not cheap on cash flow or sales; the case rests on trend and quality, not a discount [Likely].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Operating margin (TTM, income statement trailing page) falls below 15.0%.
- TTM ROIC (ratios trailing page) falls below 22%.
- FCF margin (TTM, cash flow trailing page) falls below 10%.
- Net debt / EBITDA (statistics page) rises above 2.8x.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
FCF conversion is 112.6% of net income TTM and shares are down 4.95% YoY [Certain]. Nine-month buybacks were $852 million and dividends $191 million [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/62996/000006299626000033/mas-20260930.htm). Buybacks are heavy relative to the 6.39% FCF yield, so leverage must be watched [Likely].

## 6. Unusual items
- Tariffs: nine-month operating profit includes a net IEEPA tariff-refund benefit of about $90 million, mainly Plumbing, which is non-recurring [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/62996/000006299626000033/mas-20260930.htm). Underlying margin is lower than reported [Likely].
- The 10-Q adds language on lower market demand, elevated commodity, labour and China-tariff costs. Risk Factors are unchanged. Restructuring charges of about $50 million are expected for FY26 [company-reported] (same URL).
- Short interest 4.11% of float; RSI 64.6 [Certain].
