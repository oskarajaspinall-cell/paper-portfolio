# ANA Holdings (TYO:9202) — Core initiation — 2026-11-09
## 1. Business
Japan's largest full-service airline group (ANA brand plus low-cost carrier operations), earning revenue from passenger and cargo flights, with a travel and other segment. Industry classification: Airlines (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tyo/9202/). Revenue split by country or region: [data unavailable] in the fact sheet and not fetched; the macro overlay should treat international routes and inbound tourism as the foreign exposure. [Likely]

## 2. Quality
Post-pandemic recovery is complete: ROIC 11.4% TTM vs -10.5% in FY2022, operating margin 7.6% TTM vs 8.6% in FY2026 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tyo/9202/financials/ratios/). [Certain] Returns are above the 10% quality mark but not exceptional for a capital-intensive airline; the moat is slot and airport access in Japan, a duopoly with JAL, not pricing power. [Likely] Direction is mixed: over the last 12 months ROIC is flat (+0.2pp) but FCF margin fell from 6.3% to 5.2%, 3.8pp below its 2-year high of 9.0% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tyo/9202/financials/cash-flow-statement/?p=trailing). [Certain] FCF conversion is 89.9% TTM vs 135.1% in FY2026, consistent with heavy fleet capex. [Likely] Balance sheet is clean: net debt/EBITDA 0.15x, Piotroski F 6, Altman Z 1.57 (typical for airlines, but weak). [Certain]

## 3. Valuation
At 3,237 JPY: P/E 10.9x (44% of its 5y range, 10% below peers' 12.2x), EV/EBITDA 3.8x (16% of range, 35% below peers), P/B 1.0x (19% of range, 57% below peers), FCF yield 10.09%. P/FCF 9.9x is above its 5y max of 8.3x because FCF fell. [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tyo/9202/statistics/). The price is cheap against peers (JAL, Delta, United, IAG) on EV-based and book multiples, mostly the market's discount for Japanese airlines and cyclicality, not a mispricing proven by earnings. [Likely] The mechanical fair value (base 11,337 JPY) is flagged unreliable (methods disagree 4.6x, bear negative); ignore it. The EV/EBITDA method alone gives a base of 3,309.83 JPY, near the price: so the market already pays about mid-cycle earnings. [Likely] Reverse DCF implies -22.4% year-1 revenue decline, a pessimistic embedded assumption. [Guessing]

Shares outstanding fell 1.09% YoY, a modest buyback. [Likely] Leverage is minimal (0.15x), giving room for fleet spend and shareholder returns without dilution. [Certain]
