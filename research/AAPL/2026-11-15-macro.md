# Macro overlay: Apple Inc. (AAPL) — 2026-11-15
## Assessment
**Weight: secondary.** Chain: real yields -> discount rate -> multiple. At 39.7x P/E, above its 5y max of 37.7x (fact sheet), the price depends on the multiple holding, not on earnings. [Likely]

**Facts (FRED).** The 10y TIPS yield was 2.86% on 2026-11-12, up 0.47pp in 3m. The 2y was 4.86% (+0.71pp in 3m) and fed funds 3.88%. The 10y breakeven was 2.24% (-0.03pp in 3m), so the rise is real yield, not inflation. VIX was 18.7. [Certain]

**Pricing (proxy) and inference.** The 2y yield sits 0.98pp above fed funds, which suggests the market prices no near-term cuts. **(inference)** Rising real yields with flat breakevens is the discount-rate channel hitting a stock priced above its own history. The safe-haven bid in the note's headline is a risk-appetite effect that can reverse. [Guessing]

**Country risk: China (~17% of sales, 10-K).** CNY was 6.676 per USD on 2026-11-06 and little changed over 3m, so translation is neutral. The PBoC rate was not checked. The 10-K flags tariffs and a semiconductor investigation, none imposed; no new event verified (unverified).

**Bull:** real yields ease below ~2.5% and the multiple holds. **Bear:** the real yield passes ~3.2% or money rotates back to AI names, and the multiple falls toward its 5y median of 33.8x, about -15%, with no earnings change.

**Tilt:** toward bear, small; the stretched multiple makes rate surprises matter, while fundamentals and CNY are not a headwind.
