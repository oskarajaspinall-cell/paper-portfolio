# Apple Inc. (AAPL) — Core initiation — 2026-10-10

## 1. Business
Apple designs, manufactures and sells iPhone, Mac, iPad, wearables and accessories, monetized further through a growing Services segment (App Store, AppleCare, cloud, payments, advertising, subscriptions) layered on an installed base of >1bn devices (source: https://www.apple.com; https://www.sec.gov/cgi-bin/browse-edgar?action=getcompany&CIK=0000320193&type=10-K, 10-K filed 2025-10-31). [Certain]

## 2. Quality
Returns are exceptional and rising: TTM ROIC 101.6% and ROCE 66.2% (ROIC +11.8pp, ROCE +20.4pp over 5y), with gross margin up to 48.7% TTM from 41.8% in FY2021 (source: factsheet-2026-10-10.md, Quality table). [Certain] The moat is the ecosystem: hardware, software and a high-margin Services layer create switching costs rivals (Samsung, Android OEMs) cannot easily replicate, consistent with operating margin rising to 33.2% TTM from 29.8% in FY2021. [Likely] FCF conversion (106.0% TTM) has fallen from a FY2024 high of 116.1%, an inconsistent but not alarming trend (source: factsheet, Quality table). [Certain] Near-term risk: reports say Apple cut iPhone 18 Pro/Pro Max component orders ~15% in October on soft demand after a price rise (source: https://www.marketwatch.com/story/is-iphone-18-demand-cooling-off-heres-how-deep-apple-reportedly-is-cutting-component-orders-a44244ee). [Likely] A single product cycle, not evidence the moat has weakened, but worth watching into the Nov 2, 2026 print. [Guessing]

## 3. Valuation
The price assumes durable, above-history growth. P/E (38.6x) is above the 5y range max (113% of range) and 30% above the peer median (29.8x, MSFT/GOOGL/DELL/SONY); EV/EBITDA (28.9x) is 137% of range, +34% vs peers; EV/Sales (10.4x) is 144% of range, +73% vs peers (source: factsheet, Valuation table). [Certain] P/FCF (35.9x) is the one multiple below peers (-30%), reflecting Apple's lower FCF yield (2.78%). Stockanalysis.com's own-history DCF/EV-EBITDA blend puts base fair value at $155.60 (bear $122.17, bull $174.96) vs last close $336.64 — even the bull case is 48.0% below market (source: factsheet, Fair value table). [Certain] The reverse DCF implies ~31.5% year-1 revenue growth fading to terminal — far above any recent growth shown on the fact sheet, a valuation the business's own multiple range and cash flows do not support. [Certain]

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROIC (ratios page) falls below 70% for two consecutive fiscal years, signalling the ecosystem moat is eroding rather than strengthening.
- Gross margin (income statement) drops below 44% (the FY2023 level), indicating pricing power or mix has deteriorated.
- FCF conversion (FCF/NI, cash flow + income statement) falls below 90% for two consecutive years, suggesting cash quality is weakening structurally rather than cyclically.
- Net margin (income statement) falls below 24% (the FY2024 low), evidencing a genuine profitability reset rather than a one-quarter product-cycle wobble.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Shares outstanding fell 2.12% year-on-year, consistent with continued large buybacks funded by TTM FCF conversion of 106.0% of net income (source: factsheet, Other and Quality tables). [Certain] Net debt/EBITDA is 0.13x on a strict debt-less-cash-and-ST-investments basis, though the site's own definition (netting more investments) shows -0.37x net cash; this conflict is unresolved on the fact sheet. [Certain] Either way leverage is immaterial and does not constrain the buyback programme. [Likely]

## 6. Unusual items
Short interest is low (0.88% of float) and fell 7.9% in the past month; institutional ownership is high (65.7%) and insider ownership negligible (0.06%) — none of this is a red flag (source: factsheet, Other/News & sentiment tables). [Certain] The iPhone 18 demand/component-cut reports (section 2) are the only live catalyst; they affect near-term estimates but are not yet a thesis-level quality signal.
