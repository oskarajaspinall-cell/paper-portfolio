# Apple Inc. (AAPL) — Core initiation — 2026-11-15
## 1. Business
Designs the iPhone, Mac, iPad and wearables, and monetises the installed base through Services. FY2026 net sales were $478,834m, of which iPhone $254,098m and Services $123,301m [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/320193/000032019326000026/aapl-20260926.htm). Greater China was $82,378m, about 17% of sales [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/320193/000032019326000026/aapl-20260926.htm), so the macro overlay should check China. Services gross margin was 76.2% [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/320193/000032019326000026/aapl-20260926.htm). [Certain]

## 2. Quality
Exceptional and durable. TTM ROIC is 93.9% and the operating margin is 33.0% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/aapl/financials/ratios/). Gross margin has risen from 43.3% in FY2022 to 48.8%, and the FCF margin is 28.4% with FCF conversion of 102.9% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/aapl/financials/income-statement/). [Certain] The moat is the hardware, OS and services ecosystem, whose switching costs let it keep margins. [Likely]

Direction: ROIC is 10.4pp below its 2-year high of 104.3% and TTM ROCE slipped from 68.7% to 67.4%, while operating margin and FCF margin both rose over the last year (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/aapl/financials/ratios/?p=trailing). I read this as noise from a rising capital base, not erosion. [Likely]

## 3. Valuation
The price is demanding. P/E is 39.7x against a 5y range of 24.2x–37.7x, so it sits above the historical maximum. EV/EBITDA is 29.9x, also above its range, and P/FCF is 38.1x, near its top (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/aapl/statistics/). FCF yield is 2.63%. [Certain] Peer-median P/E is 17.8x, but those peers are poor comparables for Apple's returns. [Likely]

Mechanical fair value is 180.00 base against a 356.27 close. The sheet flags it as unreliable: the DCF and EV/EBITDA methods disagree 2.5x. Weigh it lightly. The reverse DCF implies 33.0% year-1 revenue growth, which means the price rests on multiple expansion and a safe-haven bid, not on the cash flows (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/aapl/history/). [Likely] Trend supports the stock: +18.3% over the 200-day average, +30.3% over 12 months, and +19.9pp against SPY over 12 months. [Certain]

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- TTM operating margin (income statement, trailing page) falls below 30%.
- TTM ROIC (ratios page, trailing) falls below 75%.
- TTM FCF margin (cash flow statement, trailing page) falls below 23%.
- Greater China net sales (next 10-Q segment note) fall year on year while iPhone net sales also fall.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
The share count fell 1.90% in the last year, so buybacks are shrinking it (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/aapl/statistics/). Net debt/EBITDA is 0.22x on debt less cash and short-term investments, but -0.25x on the site's definition (a flagged conflict; not resolved). Either way, leverage is low. FCF conversion is 102.9% against 88.2% in FY2025. [Certain] The 10-K discloses other purchase obligations of $27.3bn, $9.9bn due within 12 months [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/320193/000032019326000026/aapl-20260926.htm). R&D is rising on AI infrastructure. [Likely]

## 6. Unusual items
- New 10-K risk language says supply constraints and rising costs for advanced semiconductors, memory and storage are expected to intensify, and that price increases may not offset them and may reduce demand. This is a gross-margin risk [Likely] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/320193/000032019326000026/aapl-20260926.htm).
- The 10-K adds a risk factor on tariffs and on a US Commerce semiconductor investigation (no tariffs imposed yet), and on AI competition and compute-capacity access. Most manufacturing sits in China mainland, India, Japan, South Korea, Taiwan and Vietnam. [Certain] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/320193/000032019326000026/aapl-20260926.htm)
- Headlines say Apple is being bought as a safe haven during a tech sell-off after Nvidia's CEO resigned. That makes the price sensitive to a rotation back into AI names. [Guessing] (source: https://invezz.com/news/2026/11/13/apple-stock-gains-as-bernstein-highlights-safe-haven-appeal-amid-ai-selloff/)
- The shares rose 5.3% on 2.2x volume on 2026-11-04, after the 10-K filing. Short interest is 1.00% of float. [Certain]
