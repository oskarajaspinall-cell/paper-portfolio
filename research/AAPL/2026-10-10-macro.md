# Macro overlay: Apple Inc. (AAPL) — 2026-10-10
## Assessment
**Gate: secondary.** AAPL's thesis is fundamentals-led (ecosystem quality, iPhone cycle, Services growth), but the stock trades on an unusually stretched multiple — P/E 38.6x is 113% of its own 5y range and +30% vs peers (factsheet, stockanalysis.com, 2026-10-10) — so the discount rate matters more here than for a typical holding. [Certain]

**Chain:** real yields → discount rate → equity multiple. US 10y real yield (DFII10) is 2.87% on 2026-10-08, +0.44pp in ~1m, +0.56pp in ~3m; nominal 10y (DGS10) rose +0.68pp over 3m while breakeven inflation (T10YIE) rose only +0.09pp — nearly all the move is real, not inflationary (FRED, fred.stlouisfed.org). [Certain] Apple's own DCF already discounts at today's 5.22% risk-free rate and still derives base fair value $155.60 / bull $174.96 vs a $336.64 close (factsheet); further real-yield rises push that down, not up **(inference)**.

**Market pricing:** DGS2 (4.75%) sitting above effective fed funds (3.88%, +0.26pp/3m) is a proxy for the market expecting a shallower easing path than three months ago [Likely]. VIX (15.41, near 3m lows) shows calm risk appetite has let AAPL's multiple expand despite higher real yields — fundamentals/AI-platform optimism, not macro, has been the swing factor so far **(inference)**.

**Bull:** yields roll over → discount rate eases → supports further re-rating. **Bear:** real yields keep rising with no earnings offset → multiple compresses toward its 5y range, widening the fair-value gap; a stronger dollar (DTWEXBGS +2.9%/1m, 2026-10-02) trims translated international revenue. **Tilt:** small, toward bear — a secondary headwind on an already-stretched, fundamentals-driven valuation.
