# Brown-Forman Corporation (BF.B) — Core initiation — 2026-10-09

## 1. Business
Brown-Forman makes and markets branded spirits — Jack Daniel's whiskey family, Woodford Reserve, Herradura/el Jimador tequila and other wines/spirits — selling through wholesalers/distributors to retailers and on-premise accounts worldwide [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/bf.b/). Revenue is brand- and volume/price-mix driven, concentrated in the US and a handful of international markets [Certain] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/14693/000001469326000024/bfb-20260430.htm).

## 2. Quality
Still a good business but returns are eroding: ROIC fell from 22.3% (FY22) to 14.3% TTM and ROE from 31.1% to 18.1% [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/bf.b/financials/ratios/). Gross margin is stable at ~60.7% TTM, evidence the Jack Daniel's franchise still carries pricing power [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/bf.b/financials/income-statement/). The moat is brand/distribution scale plus long-dated aged-whiskey inventory competitors can't replicate quickly, reinforced by the Brown family's >50% voting control (a "controlled company" under NYSE rules), which supports a long-term orientation but also means BF.B (non-voting) holders have no say [Certain] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/14693/000001469326000024/bfb-20260430.htm). Category headwinds are real: tequila sales are slowing and the RTD joint venture with Pabst is being unwound (Brown-Forman takes back RTD supply/sales/marketing from July 2026), while Canadian provinces pulled US spirits, including Jack Daniel's, from shelves amid the tariff dispute [Likely] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/14693/000001469326000024/bfb-20260430.htm). Net debt/EBITDA has risen from 1.12x (FY22) to 1.80x TTM [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/bf.b/financials/ratios/).

## 3. Valuation
The market is pricing a real slowdown, not a healthy compounder. P/E (17.1x) and P/FCF (13.2x) sit near the bottom of their 5-year ranges (3% and -0%/below-min percentile) and below peer medians (-14% and -5%), while EV/EBITDA (12.1x), EV/Sales (3.7x) and P/B (3.1x) sit similarly low in BF.B's own history but above peer medians (+17%, +13%, +47%) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/bf.b/financials/ratios/). The reverse-DCF embedded in the last close implies -4.2% revenue growth in year one fading to terminal growth — a meaningfully bearish growth path versus the business's own recent trend [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/bf.b/). Fair value (DCF 67%/EV-EBITDA 33% blend) is base $38.73 (+45.9%), bear $15.94 (-40.0%), bull $43.04 (+62.2%) vs the $26.54 close [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/bf.b/). FCF yield is 7.6% [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/bf.b/statistics/).

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Gross margin (income statement page) falls below 57% for two consecutive quarters — brand pricing power breaking down.
- ROIC (ratios page) falls below 10% TTM — the moat no longer protecting returns.
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) rises above 3.0x — leverage threatens the capital-return capacity the thesis relies on.
- FCF margin (cash flow/income statement) falls back below 15% TTM, reversing the recent recovery from the FY23-25 trough.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
FCF conversion (FCF/NI) has swung from 40.9% (FY24) to 128.3% TTM and FCF margin to 23.6% TTM, the strongest cash generation in the 5-year window [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/bf.b/financials/cash-flow-statement/). Shares outstanding fell 2.03% YoY, consistent with ongoing buybacks [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/bf.b/statistics/). Management describes capital priorities as organic brand/geographic investment plus "opportunistic" M&A, alongside a stated dividend (amount not disclosed on the pages checked) [Likely] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/14693/000001469326000024/bfb-20260430.htm). It also priced a new $500m 5-year note in September 2026, adding fixed-rate debt against an already-rising net debt/EBITDA trend [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/bf.b/).

## 6. Unusual items
Short interest is 10.2% of float, up 24.8% versus the prior month — an elevated and rising bet against the stock [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/bf.b/statistics/). BF.B carries no voting rights; the Brown family controls >50% of voting power as a "controlled company," a durable governance constraint for minority holders [Certain] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/14693/000001469326000024/bfb-20260430.htm). Beta is very low (0.32), typical of a defensive staple but also meaning the stock may lag a market re-rating [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/bf.b/statistics/).
