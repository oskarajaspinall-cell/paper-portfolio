# Macro overlay: Brown-Forman Corporation (BF.B) — 2026-10-09
## Assessment
Dominant chain: real/nominal yields → BF.B's own DCF discount rate and the cost of any new/refinanced debt → fair value and net margin. The fact sheet's DCF already uses the 10y Treasury as its risk-free rate; FRED shows DGS10 at 5.28% (2026-10-07), up +0.72pp/~3m, and the 10y real yield (DFII10) at 2.92%, up +0.49pp/~1m and +0.61pp/~3m (fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=DGS10, id=DFII10) [Certain]. That rate rise is large enough to mechanically compress a DCF-based fair value, and it lands on a company with rising leverage (net debt/EBITDA 1.12x→1.80x TTM) that just priced $500m of new 5-year notes at 5.375% (2026-09-17, stockanalysis.com) [Certain].

Market pricing (proxy, **inference**): the 2y Treasury (4.77%) sits ~0.9pp above effective fed funds (3.88%), suggesting the market isn't pricing near-term cuts [Likely]. Whether this is already "in" BF.B's price is unclear: its 6-month move (-1.2%) and -20.4pp underperformance vs SPY (factsheet, 2026-10-08) look driven mainly by the tequila slowdown and RTD JV unwind, not rates, given BF.B's low beta (0.32) [Guessing] **(inference)**.

Bull: real yields ease toward ~2.0-2.5%, lowering the discount rate and lifting fair value toward/above the $38.73 base case; cheaper future refinancing. Bear: real yields hold above ~3% and HY spreads (3.09%, +0.39pp/3m) widen further, keeping the discount rate elevated and capping re-rating even if fundamentals stabilize. Tilt: small, toward bear — the new debt is fixed for 5 years, and BF.B's low beta/modest growth limit rate-duration sensitivity, so fundamentals remain the swing factor.
