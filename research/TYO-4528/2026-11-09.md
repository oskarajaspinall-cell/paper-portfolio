# Ono Pharmaceutical (TYO:4528) — Core initiation — 2026-11-09
## 1. Business
Osaka-based drug maker (Drug Manufacturers - General) selling prescription medicines in Japan and earning licence and royalty income on its oncology franchise partnered with overseas companies [Likely]. The high gross margin (74.1% TTM) is consistent with a royalty-rich, patent-protected mix (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tyo/4528/financials/income-statement/). Regional revenue split [data unavailable]: the company's own site was not on the allowlist, so no foreign-country share is confirmed.

## 2. Quality
Good but not great, and recovering from a trough [Likely]. ROIC fell from 19.3% (FY2024) to 7.6% (FY2025), then recovered to 9.9% TTM; operating margin went 31.9% -> 12.7% -> 20.0% TTM (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tyo/4528/financials/ratios/). The 5-year trend is falling, yet the last 12 months are rising (ROIC 6.4% -> 9.9%; operating margin 11.5% -> 20.0%), still below the 2-year highs of 11.9% and 24.6% [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tyo/4528/financials/ratios/?p=trailing). The FY2025 step-down looks like a one-off cost or R&D/deal charge, but the cause is unverified [Guessing]. Durable edge rests on patent exclusivity and pipeline replenishment, which is time-limited; there is no structural moat beyond that [Likely]. Cash generation is strong: FCF margin 25.9% TTM, FCF/NI 170.5%. Piotroski F 7, Altman Z 5.29 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tyo/4528/statistics/).

## 3. Valuation
Price 2,352.50 JPY. P/E 15.3x sits at 65% of its 5y range (median 15.0x), so earnings are not cheap on the depressed base; but EV/EBITDA 7.4x (26% of range), EV/Sales 2.0x (20%), P/B 1.3x (26%) and P/FCF 9.0x (1% of range) are low. Against peers (n=5) it trades at -18% on P/E, -45% on EV/EBITDA and -42% on P/FCF; FCF yield 11.12% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tyo/4528/statistics/). Net cash (net debt/EBITDA -1.10x). The mechanical fair value (base 4,197.11, +78.4%) is flagged unreliable (methods disagree 2.1x); the P/E method gives 2,385.99, near the price, so I weigh it lightly [Certain]. The price implies a shrinking top line (reverse DCF: -15.0% year-1 revenue growth), which is undemanding if royalties hold [Likely]. Cash flow is cheap; earnings are fairly valued.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Net cash balance sheet, share count stable (+0.07% YoY), so no dilution (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tyo/4528/statistics/). FCF conversion 170.5% TTM exceeds earnings, though part may reflect working-capital timing [Guessing]. Management is buying pipeline through discovery partnerships (Aitia, Mediar, Phylo) rather than large M&A [Likely] (sources: https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260901390619/en/Ono-Pharma-Enters-into-a-Drug-Discovery-Partnership-with-Aitia-in-Neurological-Diseases/, https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260806851824/en/Ono-Pharma-Enters-into-a-Drug-Discovery-Partnership-with-Mediar-Therapeutics-to-Create-Novel-Antibody-Therapeutics-for-Fibro-inflammatory-Diseases/). Dividend and buyback detail not reviewed.
