# ASE Technology Holding (TPE:3711) — Tactical initiation — 2026-10-07

## 1. Catalyst and why now
Next earnings is 2026-10-30 [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tpe/3711/). On the Q2 call (Jul 30) management guided Q3 revenue +21-22% QoQ, gross margin 20.5-21.5%, operating margin 11.5-12.5%, driven by AI-related advanced-packaging (LEAP/CoWoS) demand [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tpe/3711/transcripts/656634-q2-2026/). The company also raised 2026 capex by $2bn to roughly $10.5bn to add capacity [Certain] (source headline: https://www.reuters.com/world/asia-pacific/taiwans-ase-tech-raises-2026-capex-by-2-billion-strong-demand-2026-07-30/, listed in fact sheet; article itself could not be fetched). But blended utilization is already 80-85% and management called capacity "very close to being full" [Likely] (source: transcript above), so the Oct 30 print mainly confirms guidance already public rather than offering fresh upside surprise — a "sell the news" set-up.

## 2. What the price already assumes
At 56.4x P/E (245% of the 5y range, above the 26.9x max) and 24.0x EV/EBITDA (+123% vs the 3-peer median 10.8x), the price already assumes sustained hyper-growth and flawless execution across 13 greenfield/8 brownfield projects, beyond the already-guided Q3 ramp [Certain] (source: research/TPE-3711/factsheet-2026-10-07.md). The 12-month return of +349.6% and 6-month return of +94.3% show most of the AI-packaging re-rating has already happened [Certain] (same source).
