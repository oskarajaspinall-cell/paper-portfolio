# Equinor ASA (OSL:EQNR) — Core initiation — 2026-11-24
## 1. Business
Norwegian integrated oil and gas group: upstream production, mainly on the Norwegian continental shelf, plus marketing, midstream, refining and a low-carbon arm [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/osl/EQNR/). Earnings follow oil and European gas prices. Reports in USD, shares quoted in NOK. Country revenue split: [data unavailable]; no foreign-country share was verified, though a headline describes it as the largest pipeline gas supplier to Europe [Likely] (source: https://oilprice.com/Energy/Energy-General/Equinor-Hack-Adds-to-Wave-of-Cyberattacks-on-Energy-Companies.html).

## 2. Quality
Cyclical, not a compounder. ROIC peaked at 67.2% in FY2022 and fell to 10.3% in FY2025; TTM has rebounded to 22.9% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/osl/EQNR/financials/ratios/?p=trailing). Operating margin is 32.1% TTM vs 25.0% in FY2025; FCF margin 9.4% vs 5.6% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/osl/EQNR/financials/income-statement/). [Certain] The 12-month rise contradicts the five-year decline, but it tracks commodity prices (a headline cites a Hormuz crisis lifting European gas), so I treat TTM as above mid-cycle. [Likely]
Edge is cost position and reserve access, not pricing power. Net debt/EBITDA is 0.12x; Piotroski 7, Altman Z 2.72 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/osl/EQNR/statistics/). [Certain]

## 3. Valuation
At 380.50 NOK, P/E is 8.3x vs a 5y median of 7.9x and a peer median of 10.7x; EV/EBITDA is 2.0x vs 4.2x for peers; FCF yield is 12.02%. P/B is 2.1x, 85th percentile of own range (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/osl/EQNR/financials/ratios/). [Certain]
The DCF base (771.87 NOK) is flagged unreliable; I lean on multiples. The price assumes TTM earnings roughly persist; the reverse DCF implies -15.5% year-1 revenue growth. [Likely]
Why it fell: Equinor disclosed an IT cyberattack in the week of 2026-11-18, later said stopped with no production impact (source: https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/equinor-says-cyberattack-against-company-has-been-stopped-2026-11-20/). [Likely] The ex-dividend date also lowered the price. [Likely] A long-term VNG gas supply deal followed (source: https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/norways-equinor-signs-18-year-gas-supply-deal-with-germanys-vng-2026-11-24/). [Likely] The fall looks event-driven, not an earnings change. [Guessing]
