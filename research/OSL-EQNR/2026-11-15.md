# Equinor ASA (OSL:EQNR) — Core initiation — 2026-11-15
## 1. Business
Norwegian integrated oil and gas group: upstream oil and gas production, mainly on the Norwegian continental shelf, plus marketing, midstream, refining and a renewables/low-carbon arm [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/osl/EQNR/). Earnings follow oil and European gas prices. Reports in USD, shares quoted in NOK. Country revenue split: [data unavailable]; the company site was not on the fetch allowlist, so no foreign-country share was verified.

## 2. Quality
Cyclical, not a compounder. Returns peaked in FY2022 (ROIC 67.2%) and fell to 10.3% in FY2025; TTM has rebounded to 22.3% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/osl/EQNR/financials/ratios/?p=trailing). Operating margin 32.1% TTM vs 25.0% in FY2025; FCF margin 9.4% vs 5.6% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/osl/EQNR/financials/income-statement/). [Certain] The 12-month recovery contradicts the five-year decline, but it is a commodity-price recovery, so I treat TTM as above mid-cycle. [Likely]
Durable edge: low-cost, long-life North Sea assets and a low-risk balance sheet (net debt/EBITDA 0.12x; Piotroski 7, Altman Z 2.72) (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/osl/EQNR/statistics/). [Certain] There is no pricing power; the moat is cost position and reserve access. [Likely]

## 3. Valuation
At 407.60 NOK, P/E is 8.9x against a 5y median of 7.9x (64th percentile of range) and a peer median of 13.4x; EV/EBITDA is 2.2x vs 4.5x for peers; FCF yield is 11.26%. P/B is 2.2x, above its 5y maximum, and P/FCF is 8.9x, 83rd percentile of own range (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/osl/EQNR/financials/ratios/). [Certain]
The peer discount is partly a Norwegian-state/low-multiple domicile effect, and the mechanical DCF base of 719.28 NOK (+76.5%) sits beside a bull case of 2,236.97 NOK; I treat that spread as unreliable and lean on multiples. [Likely] The price assumes TTM earnings mostly persist, with no margin of safety if commodity prices normalise. Reverse DCF implies -12.7% year-1 revenue growth, so the market is already pricing a fall. [Likely] Trend is healthy: +12.1% above the 200-day, +66.9% over 12 months.

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROIC TTM (ratios page, trailing view) falls below 10%.
- FCF margin TTM (cash flow and income statement trailing pages) falls below 5%.
- Net debt / EBITDA (ratios page) rises above 1.0x.
- Shares change YoY (statistics page) turns positive, i.e. buybacks stop shrinking the count.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Shares are down 7.43% YoY, so buybacks are real (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/osl/EQNR/statistics/). [Certain] Buyback transactions continue under a fourth 2026 tranche, and the shares went ex-dividend on 2026-11-13 (source: https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/11/10/3399153/0/en/equinor-asa-share-buy-back-fourth-tranche-for-2026.html; https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/11/13/3402422/0/en/equinor-asa-ex-dividend-second-quarter-2026-today-ose.html). [Certain] FCF conversion is 96.0% of net income TTM and 118.5% in FY2025. Net margin 9.8% TTM leaves modest earnings to fund both payouts and capex if prices fall. [Likely]
