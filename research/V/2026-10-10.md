# Visa Inc. (V) — Core initiation — 2026-10-10

## 1. Business
Visa runs a four-party network linking card issuers, acquirers, merchants and consumers, earning service fees, data-processing fees, international-transaction fees and licensing/advisory revenue, net of client incentives paid to issuers and merchants [Certain] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1403161/000140316125000089/v-20250930.htm). It does not lend or set interchange rates; it monetizes volume and processing on its own rails [Certain] (same source).

## 2. Quality
Returns are exceptional and still rising: ROIC 54.8% TTM (+19.3pp over 5y), ROE 61.2% TTM, operating margin 66.9%, gross margin 97.7% [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/v/financials/ratios/). The moat is network effects and scale - a two-sided network with deep switching costs for issuers/acquirers embedded in payment rails [Likely] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1403161/000140316125000089/v-20250930.htm, which names American Express, Discover, Mastercard, UnionPay and stablecoin/blockchain entrants as competitors). Visa itself flags new entrants that "depart from traditional network payment models" as a competitive risk [Likely] (same source). A WSJ headline reports an interchange-reform bill has gained a prominent political ally - an early-stage but genuine threat to the fee pool Visa and issuers share [Likely] (source: https://www.wsj.com/finance/regulation/the-credit-card-bill-that-banks-fear-most-has-gained-trump-as-an-ally-013551b3). FCF margin and conversion are drifting down (FCF margin -6.3pp over 5y to 47.2%; FCF/NI -10.4pp to 93.0%) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/v/financials/cash-flow-statement/), a mild erosion worth watching. Net debt/EBITDA is low at 0.32x [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/v/financials/ratios/).

## 3. Valuation
The price assumes durable, elevated growth: the reverse DCF implies 22.6% year-one revenue growth fading to terminal by year 10 [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/v/statistics/), well above Visa's recent trend. Multiples are stretched on every measure: P/E 32.8x (71st percentile of its 5y range), EV/EBITDA 23.1x (62nd pct), P/FCF 33.7x (130% of 5y max), P/B 20.4x (151% of 5y max) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/v/financials/ratios/). Versus peers (MA, AXP, PYPL, FI), V trades at a +125% P/E, +195% EV/EBITDA, +476% EV/Sales and +220% P/FCF premium [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/ma/statistics/, https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/axp/statistics/, https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/pypl/statistics/, https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/fi/statistics/) - partly earned by superior margins and ROIC, but the magnitude is large. Fair value: base $211.42, bear $179.85, bull $292.83 vs a last close of $385.45 - even the bull case sits 24.0% below the current price [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/v/statistics/). FCF yield is just 2.97% [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/v/statistics/). This is a very good business priced for perfection, with no margin of safety in any scenario tested.

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROIC (ratios page) stays below 40% for two consecutive fiscal years
- FCF margin (cash flow/income statement) keeps falling and drops below 45%
- Net revenue growth (income statement) decelerates to high-single-digits or below while multiples stay near current levels, confirming the reverse-DCF growth assumption was too aggressive
- An interchange-reform bill (per WSJ reporting) advances to a floor vote, directly threatening the fee pool Visa and issuers share

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Shares outstanding fell 2.88% YoY, consistent with ongoing buybacks [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/v/statistics/). FCF conversion remains above 90% of net income (93.0% TTM) despite the recent decline, still a highly cash-generative model funding buybacks without leverage (net debt/EBITDA 0.32x) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/v/financials/cash-flow-statement/). The 10-K excerpt reviewed did not disclose explicit dividend/buyback targets [data unavailable] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1403161/000140316125000089/v-20250930.htm).

## 6. Unusual items
None material beyond the valuation stretch and the nascent interchange-legislation headline risk noted above; short interest is low (1.09% of float, down 16.7% month-on-month) and institutional ownership is high (82.7%) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/v/statistics/).
