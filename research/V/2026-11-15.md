# Visa Inc. (V) — Core initiation — 2026-11-15
## 1. Business
Visa runs a card-payments network: it earns fees on payments volume, cross-border volume and processed transactions, plus value-added services (consulting, risk, tokenization, marketing) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/v/). It does not lend, so it carries no credit risk. Cross-border and value-added growth are the swing drivers [Likely] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1403161/000140316126000134/v-20260930.htm). Country revenue mix: [data unavailable] in the fact sheet; I did not verify whether any one foreign country is about 10% of revenue.

## 2. Quality
Exceptional [Certain]. Gross margin 97.7% and operating margin 66.3% (TTM) are flat over five years; ROIC rose from 40.1% (FY2022) to 56.4% (TTM) and ROCE to 48.5% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/v/financials/ratios/). Net debt/EBITDA is 0.28x; Altman Z 8.32 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/v/statistics/). The moat is a two-sided network (issuers and merchants) with scale and tokenization/risk tooling [Likely].

Direction change to address: FCF margin is 48.1% TTM vs 53.9% a year earlier (-5.9pp) and 56.8% at its 2-year high; FCF conversion fell to 93.8% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/v/financials/cash-flow-statement/?p=trailing). Net margin is stable, so the gap sits between earnings and cash. The 10-K cites legal settlements and tax items among the drivers of the year's tax line [Likely], but I could not tie the FCF decline to a single cause [Guessing]. Operating expenses grew faster on people and World Cup/Olympics marketing, a partly one-off cost [Likely] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1403161/000140316126000134/v-20260930.htm).

## 3. Valuation
Demanding [Certain]. Every multiple sits above its 5y maximum: P/E 34.8x (range 24.9x-33.0x, median 27.8x), EV/EBITDA 25.5x (max 23.6x), P/FCF 36.4x (max 30.4x). Peer medians are far lower: P/E 14.7x, though peers (AXP, PYPL, FIS) are lower-quality, so that gap overstates. FCF yield is 2.75%.

The mechanical fair value is base 222.83 USD vs a 425.76 close (-47.7%); even the bull case is 290.25 (-31.8%) [Certain]. The reverse DCF implies 23.6% year-1 revenue growth fading to terminal; this is far above recent growth, so the price embeds long durability beyond the model [Likely]. The DCF is probably harsh on a capital-light compounder, but the position-in-range reads are independent of it. Trend is strong: +22.7% over the 200-day, +25.6% over 12 months, +19.5pp vs SPY over 3 months (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/v/history/).

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Share count is down 3.00% YoY, so buybacks are meaningful [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/v/statistics/). Conversion has slipped from 119.5% (FY2022) to 93.8%. Acquisitions are rising: Argentina's Prisma/Newpay for $1.5 billion cash and a pending $2.4 billion BioCatch fraud-intelligence deal [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1403161/000140316126000134/v-20260930.htm). Modest relative to cash generation [Likely]; watch whether M&A replaces buybacks.

## 6. Unusual items
- Regulation (filing delta): the 10-K's risk factors were expanded on interchange and routing. They flag recent conflicting federal court decisions on the Reg II debit cap, which could lower it significantly, a Fed proposal for further rate cuts, and the reintroduced Credit Card Competition Act [Certain] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1403161/000140316126000134/v-20260930.htm). This is the key thesis risk.
- Litigation: interchange claims for damages remain unresolved. Visa reports the amount at issue fell from $17.4 billion (May 2026) to $15.9 billion (1 Oct 2026) after the class B exchange offer [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1403161/000140316126000134/v-20260930.htm).
- Agentic commerce is a possible tailwind or disintermediation risk; OpenAI's wallet accepts Visa cards [Likely] (source: /news/openai-launches-chatgpt-wallet/ on https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/v/).
- Short interest 1.17% of float; beta 0.79; RSI 62.9 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/v/statistics/).
