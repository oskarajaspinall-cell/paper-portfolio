# Macro overlay: Visa Inc. (V) — 2026-10-10
## Assessment
Visa isn't a macro-earnings story: negligible debt (net debt/EBITDA 0.32x [Certain], factsheet RA), limited direct rate/FX revenue sensitivity. The one live channel is the **discount rate**: real yields → cost of equity → the fair-value multiple, acute because V trades above its own 5y valuation range (P/B 151% of max, P/FCF 130% of max [Certain], factsheet RA) with an FCF yield of 2.97% [Certain] barely above the 10y real yield.

**Facts** (FRED, 2026-10-08/09): 10y real yield (DFII10) 2.87%, +56bp/3m; 10y nominal (DGS10) 5.22%, +68bp/3m; 10y breakeven (T10YIE) 2.33%, +9bp/3m; fed funds (DFF) 3.88%, +25bp/1m; 2y yield (DGS2) 4.75%; VIX 15.41 (low, falling); HY spread 3.15% (+48bp/1m, still low).

**Inference**: ~80% of the 3m nominal-yield rise is real, not breakeven-driven (56 of 68bp) **(inference)** — a cost-of-capital repricing, not an inflation scare. DGS2 ~90bp above DFF is a rough **proxy** for the market pricing "higher for longer," not near-term cuts **(inference)**. Low VIX and contained spreads show no risk-off priced; pressure is specifically on duration/multiple, hitting an extended-multiple name like V regardless of its unchanged fundamentals.

**Bull**: real yields plateau/retrace → cost-of-equity relief → room for V's stretched multiple to hold or re-rate up on unchanged earnings. **Bear**: real yields grind higher → fair-value base compresses further, and a stock with almost no FCF-yield cushion over the real risk-free rate de-rates with no change in the business. **Tilt**: moderate, toward bear — reinforces the valuation-stretch bear case already flagged on fundamentals alone.
