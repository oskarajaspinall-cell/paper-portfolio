# Wintrust Financial (WTFC) — Core initiation — 2026-11-08
## 1. Business
Chicago-area and Wisconsin commercial and community bank holding company. It earns net interest income on commercial, commercial real estate and premium-finance loans funded by deposits, plus fees from wealth management, mortgage banking and operating leases. Non-interest income was $136.3 million in Q3 2026 vs $130.8 million a year earlier [company-reported] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1015328/000101532826000031/wtfc-20260930.htm). No foreign country is a material revenue share [Likely].

## 2. Quality
- ROE has risen from 10.8% (FY2021) to 12.9% TTM, with 12.1% in FY2025 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/wtfc/financials/ratios/) [Certain].
- Net margin rose from 26.3% to 32.8% TTM (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/wtfc/financials/income-statement/) [Certain].
- Q3 was a seventh consecutive record-earnings quarter with loan and deposit growth of mid-to-high single digits and stable credit, per the call summary (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/wtfc/transcripts/711337-q3-2026/) [Likely].
- The moat is local relationship banking and deposit franchises in a fragmented market. That is a modest, not wide, advantage; ROE of 12.9% is decent, not exceptional [Guessing].
- FCF margin fell over five years (60.7% to 32.7%) but is 57.0% on a rolling 12 months vs 33.5% a year earlier. Bank cash flow is noisy through working-capital swings, so I give it little weight against ROE and net margin [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/wtfc/financials/cash-flow-statement/?p=trailing).
- ROIC and ROCE are [data unavailable] (not meaningful for banks).
- The 10-Q risk factors show no substantive change (SEC delta) [Certain].

## 3. Valuation
- P/E is 11.6x, at 71% of its 5y range (9.6x–12.4x, median 11.8x) and in line with the peer median of 11.7x (PNFP, ONB, ZION, CFR, BOKF) (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/wtfc/financials/ratios/) [Certain].
- P/B is 1.5x, above its 5y maximum of 1.3x and 21% above peers; the market already pays for the higher ROE [Certain].
- P/FCF is 6.5x vs a 10.5x peer median, but bank FCF is unreliable, so the 15.41% FCF yield flatters [Likely].
- Fair value base is 172.52 vs a 151.27 close (+14.0%); bear 138.33, bull 193.44. The result is driven by the FCFE DCF (241.04), while P/B + ROE gives 138.25, below the price (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/wtfc/statistics/) [Likely].
- The price assumes ROE sustained near 13% with no deterioration. Valuation is fair, not cheap [Likely].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROE (ratios page) falls below 10.0% on a TTM basis.
- Net margin (income statement page) falls below 27.0% on a TTM basis.
- Shares outstanding change YoY (statistics page) exceeds 4.0%, i.e. dilution or equity-funded acquisitions.
- A future 10-Q shows a substantive new credit-risk factor on commercial real estate (SEC delta), or the reserve build outpaces loan growth for two consecutive quarters.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Shares change YoY is 0.62%, so there is little dilution (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/wtfc/statistics/) [Certain]. A quarterly dividend was declared (source: https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/10/22/3386895/11955/en/wintrust-financial-corporation-announces-cash-dividends.html) [Certain]. The growth is organic, which supports rising returns on equity. FCF conversion (FCF/NI) was 104.4% in FY2025, down from 230.4% in FY2021, but is back to normal levels [Likely]. Piotroski F is 4, middling [Certain].

## 6. Unusual items
Short interest is 4.42% of float, with a -0.4% change vs the prior month; institutions own 96.2%. Beta is 0.86. Nothing unusual was flagged in Q3 reporting [Likely]. The fact sheet shows a next-earnings date of Oct 19, 2026, which has already passed.
