**Bull:** VIX fades, spreads stabilise, multiple holds; little upside beyond fundamentals. **Bear:** spreads above 5% with CRE stress, higher provisions and P/B toward 1.3x, roughly a 10-15% multiple hit [Guessing].

**Tilt:** toward bear, small: premium valuation, but spreads are benign.
