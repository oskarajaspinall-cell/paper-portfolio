**Politics:** Lula's re-election on 2026-10-26 comes from fact-sheet headlines. I did not check it against tse.jus.br, so it is unverified officially [Likely]. No decree, pricing rule or dividend change is confirmed.

**Pricing (inference):** The stock is 11.0% below its 50-day average with RSI 29.2 (fact sheet), so part of the political discount is priced. The model's 3.0% country premium is a fixed owner assumption and does not track the market. The surprises that hurt are fuel-price or dividend intervention and a pause in Selic cuts. A stable payout and continued easing would help. Brent is not in the snapshot.

**Bull:** BRL stabilises and payouts hold, so the multiple rerates toward peers with little change to revenue.
**Bear:** Intervention plus a BRL slide compress the multiple and cut distributable cash.
**Tilt:** toward bear, small. The risk is partly priced and no policy action is confirmed. Q3 results on 2026-11-10 are the next test.
