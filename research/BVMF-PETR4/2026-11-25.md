# Petróleo Brasileiro S.A. - Petrobras (BVMF:PETR4) — Core initiation — 2026-11-25
## 1. Business
Brazil's state-controlled integrated oil company. Deep-water pre-salt exploration and production is the profit engine. Refining, fuel logistics, gas and biofuels sit downstream. Earnings follow oil prices and the state's fuel-pricing policy. The share is quoted in BRL and the fact sheet shows financials in BRL (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/bvmf/PETR4/) [Certain]. Brazil is the home market. No foreign-country revenue share was fetched, so I treat export mix as unknown [Guessing].

## 2. Quality
Returns are high but cyclical. TTM ROIC is 21.3% vs 16.4% in FY2025, a 12-month high, but the 5-year trend is falling (FY2022 peak 33.5%) (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/bvmf/PETR4/financials/ratios/?p=trailing) [Certain]. Operating margin is 38.1% TTM vs 29.6% a year earlier [Certain]. The weak spot is cash conversion. FCF margin is 19.0%, flat on a year ago and below its 2-year high of 28.2%. FCF/NI is 80.0% TTM vs 158.4% in FY2021, because capex absorbs profit [Certain]. Net debt/EBITDA is 1.11x and Piotroski F is 8 [Certain]. The moat is low-cost pre-salt reserves, a real scale and access advantage that is tied to oil and state policy [Likely]. Q3 results showed raised guidance and record refinery output (source: https://www.fool.com/earnings/call-transcripts/2026/11/13/petrobras-pbr-q3-2026-earnings-call-transcript/) [Likely].

## 3. Valuation
The price assumes little. P/E is 3.8x (17% of its 5y range; peers 11.7x), EV/EBITDA 2.7x (peers 5.7x), P/B 1.1x (peers 1.8x) and FCF yield 19.88% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/bvmf/PETR4/statistics/) [Certain]. P/FCF of 5.0x is above its 5y max, so part of the cheapness is a cash-flow story rather than an earnings one [Likely]. The reverse DCF implies year-1 revenue shrinking 15.1% [Certain]. The mechanical DCF fair value is flagged unreliable (base +145%, SOTP/NAV unavailable), so weigh it lightly. The peer discount mostly reflects state control, dividend and political risk [Likely]. Trend: the close of 40.55 is 9.7% below the 200-day average and 19.1% below the 50-day. RSI is 23.9, deeply oversold, and the 12-month change is +24.4% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/bvmf/PETR4/history/) [Certain].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROIC (ratios page, TTM) falls below 12%.
- FCF margin (cash-flow statement, TTM) falls below 12%.
- Net debt / EBITDA (ratios page) rises above 2.0x.
- Operating margin (income statement, TTM) falls below 28%.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
The cause of the fall is the new 2027-2031 strategic plan, published 2026-11-24. Headlines say it lifts the investment programme but leaves less cash for shareholders than expected (sources: https://www.marketbeat.com/instant-alerts/event-petrobras-unveils-122-billion-plan-sees-potential-for-10-billion-more-in-dividends-2026-11-24/ and https://www.tipranks.com/news/the-fly/jpmorgan-expects-negative-market-reaction-to-petrobras-five-year-plan-thefly-news) [Likely]. I take no figures from the headlines. An extraordinary dividend was approved on 2026-11-23 (source: https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/petrobras-approves-extraordinary-dividends-26-billion-2026-11-23/) [Likely]. Bolt-ons continue: corn-ethanol stakes (source: https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/petrobras-seek-more-corn-ethanol-deals-after-neomille-sources-say-2026-11-24/) [Likely] and a possible BP Bumerangue stake (unconfirmed) [Guessing]. The thesis now depends on returns on higher capex. FCF conversion is already falling, and the plan could push it lower [Likely]. Share-count change is [data unavailable].

## 6. Unusual items
- Governance: the state controls the company, so capex, dividends and fuel pricing can follow political aims. Scrutiny of fuel pricing came up on the Q3 call (source: https://www.nasdaq.com/articles/pbr-q3-earnings-call-highlights-output-growth-and-pricing-scrutiny) [Likely].
- Currency: BRL moves affect a USD holder. The macro overlay should check Brazil country risk (BRL, Selic, politics) [Likely].
