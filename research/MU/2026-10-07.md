# Micron Technology, Inc. (MU) — Core initiation — 2026-10-07
## 1. Business
Micron designs, manufactures and sells semiconductor memory (DRAM, including HBM) and NAND/NOR storage products, sold to data-center/hyperscale, PC, mobile, automotive and industrial customers via four segments: Cloud Memory, Core Data Center, Mobile/Client and Automotive/Embedded [Certain] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/723125/000072312525000028/mu-20250828.htm). Pricing is largely commodity-driven by global DRAM/NAND supply-demand balance [Certain].

## 2. Quality
Memory is a historically cyclical, capital-intensive, commodity business: FY2023 net margin was -37.5% and ROIC -7.4% [Certain] (fact sheet). Current TTM figures (ROIC 93.4%, ROCE 59.0%, gross margin 80.7%, operating margin 74.6%, FCF margin 44.3%) are far above the 5-year trend and reflect an AI/HBM-driven up-cycle, not a structural change in competitive position [Likely] (fact sheet; also https://investors.micron.com/, which cites "record fiscal 2026 results" and an "even stronger fiscal 2027"). The industry is a tight oligopoly (Micron, Samsung, SK hynix), giving some pricing discipline, but Micron's 10-K flags "intense competition" and advancing Chinese entrants (CXMT, YMTC) as a medium-term threat to pricing power [Likely] (source: SEC 10-K above). There is no durable moat beyond scale and node leadership; returns this high have not persisted through a full cycle historically [Likely].

## 3. Valuation
TTM P/E of 14.1x sits at the 5th percentile of its own 5-year range and 69% below peer median (44.8x); EV/EBITDA (10.2x) and P/FCF (20.0x) are similarly depressed vs history and peers (-70%/-64%) [Certain] (fact sheet). But P/B (8.5x) and EV/Sales (8.4x) are 111% and 107% above their 5-year maxima, though still below peer medians (-46%/-36%) [Certain] (fact sheet). This combination — a "cheap" P/E alongside record-high P/B/EV/Sales — is a classic cyclical-peak trap: the market may be pricing in durability of today's extraordinary margins rather than mean reversion. FCF yield is 4.99% [Certain] (fact sheet). The multiples look inexpensive only if TTM earnings are representative of a normal year, which the fact sheet's own 5-year quality trend argues against [Likely].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Gross margin (income statement page) falls below 50% for two consecutive quarters, signalling the up-cycle reversing toward historical norms.
- ROIC (ratios page) falls back below 20% (near the FY2025 level), indicating returns normalizing rather than structurally sustained.
- FCF margin (cash flow + income statement pages) turns negative, as it did in FY2023, signalling a demand/pricing downturn.
- Net debt/EBITDA (balance sheet + income statement pages) rises above 1.0x, indicating capex outpacing cash generation as the cycle turns.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
TTM FCF conversion (FCF/net income) is 69.4% and FCF margin 44.3%, strong cash generation funding heavy capex for leading-edge DRAM/HBM and NAND capacity, including CHIPS Act-supported U.S. fab expansion [Certain] (fact sheet; qualitative detail from SEC 10-K above). The balance sheet carries net cash (net debt/EBITDA -0.35x) [Certain] (fact sheet), giving flexibility to fund the capex cycle without added leverage. Shares outstanding rose 1.60% YoY despite buyback authorization, implying stock-based compensation issuance is offsetting repurchases rather than producing net buybacks [Likely] (fact sheet).

## 6. Unusual items
Micron reached a settlement and cross-licensing agreement with Netlist in October 2026 resolving a long-running patent dispute over memory technology, removing a litigation overhang; headline terms are third-party reporting and are not used as data here [Likely] (source: https://www.reuters.com/legal/litigation/micron-enters-600-million-settlement-netlist-patent-dispute-2026-10-06/). Stock-specific risk is elevated: beta is 2.23 and headlines (e.g. "Micron Stock's Make-or-Break Year") flag investor concern about the sustainability of the memory up-cycle once supply increases, which is relevant to position sizing [Likely] (source: https://www.barrons.com/articles/micron-stock-price-memory-bubble-d2329e7d). Short interest is modest (2.46% of float, down 7.0% month-on-month) [Certain] (fact sheet).
