# Macro overlay: Camden Property Trust (CPT) — 2026-10-12
## Assessment
Camden is a Sun Belt apartment REIT valued on P/FFO/NAV discounted at a cap rate tied to Treasury real yields, with leverage already elevated (net debt/EBITDA 5.40x TTM [Certain], factsheet). Dominant chain: real yields -> cap rate/discount rate -> valuation multiple; secondary chain: fed funds/mortgage rates -> renter-vs-buyer demand -> occupancy.

Facts [Certain] (FRED, 2026-10-08/09): 10y real yield (DFII10) 2.87%, +0.44pp/1m, +0.56pp/3m; 10y nominal (DGS10) 5.22%, +0.42/+0.68; breakeven (T10YIE) 2.33%, flat (-0.04/+0.09); fed funds (DFF) 3.88%, +0.25/+0.26; HY spread 3.15%, +0.48/+0.45 (still low); VIX 15.4, falling.

Market pricing (proxy) **(inference)**: 2y yield (4.75%) above fed funds (3.88%) implies a higher-for-longer path, not near-term cuts [Likely]. Flat breakevens mean this is a real-rate story, not inflation.

Already priced **(inference)**: CPT fell -13.9% over 3 months (SPY +3.6%) tracking the +0.56pp real-yield rise, and sits at the 4th percentile of its 5y EV/EBITDA range and -12%/-18%/-20% vs peers on EV/EBITDA, EV/Sales, P/B — most of this shock looks priced in [Likely].

Bull: real yields stabilize/fall -> cap-rate compression -> multiple re-rates toward peer median (EV/EBITDA ~19x), supporting the $107-151 fair-value range.
Bear: real yields extend higher -> cap rates widen further, refinancing cost rises on 5y-high leverage, pressuring the multiple and FFO coverage.
Tilt: toward bear, moderate — leverage is at a 5y high just as real yields trend up, adding funding-cost risk atop the re-rating thesis, though much of the 3-month move is already in the price.
