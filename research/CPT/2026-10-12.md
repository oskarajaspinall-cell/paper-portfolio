# Camden Property Trust (CPT) — Core initiation — 2026-10-12

## 1. Business
Camden is a US apartment REIT that owns and operates ~170 upscale communities, almost entirely in Sun Belt metros (Texas, Georgia, Florida, DC) after completing its exit from California in July 2026 [Certain] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/906345/000162828026051327/cpt-20260630.htm). Revenue is rental income; profit is driven by occupancy, rent growth and disciplined recycling of capital between markets.

## 2. Quality
ROIC has been flat and low (3.3%→3.2% TTM) and ROE flat (8.0%→7.9% TTM) over five years [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/cpt/financials/ratios/), typical of a capital-intensive, commodity rental business with limited pricing power — Sun Belt supply, not a moat, sets the cycle. Piotroski F-score is mid-pack at 5 and FCF yield is negative (-2.06%), consistent with ongoing development/recycling capex rather than distress [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/cpt/statistics/). Net debt/EBITDA (site definition) has risen to 5.40x TTM from 3.92x in FY2024, the high end of its five-year range, as 2026 asset-sale proceeds were deployed before debt paydown fully showed in trailing EBITDA [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/cpt/financials/ratios/). Management says pro forma net debt/EBITDA is already back near 4.5x after repaying all credit lines and commercial paper [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/cpt/transcripts/659238-q2-2026/). It is defensively positioned (beta 0.81) but not a high-return compounder; the case rests on valuation and cycle timing, not a moat.

## 3. Valuation
CPT trades at a discount to both its peers and its own history: P/E 31.8x (-7% vs. peer median, 22nd percentile of its own 5y range), EV/EBITDA 16.8x (-12%, 4th percentile), EV/Sales 9.5x (-18%, 1st percentile) and P/B 2.6x (-20%, 23rd percentile) (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/cpt/statistics/, peers EQR/AVB/UDR/ESS/MAA). The fact sheet's own-history fair value (P/FFO-proxy) is $88.64 bear / $106.96 base / $150.80 bull against a last close of $97.58 — base case +9.6%, bear only -9.2% [Certain] (source: fact sheet fair value table). The price already reflects weak Sun Belt supply/rent growth and the leverage spike noted above; it does not yet reflect management's view that new supply has passed its peak in most Camden markets [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/cpt/transcripts/659238-q2-2026/). Asymmetry (limited downside, larger upside optionality) is the core attraction, not business quality.

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Net debt/EBITDA (statistics page, site definition) rises above 5.6x, showing leverage keeps climbing rather than converging toward management's ~4.5x pro forma target.
- ROE (statistics page) falls below 5%, giving back the recovery from FY2024's 3.5% trough to the current 7.9% TTM.
- ROIC (statistics page) falls below 3.0%, below the already-low five-year range of 3.2%-3.7%.
- Sun Belt new-supply deliveries in Camden's core markets (Austin, Dallas, Houston, Atlanta, DC) re-accelerate to new multi-year highs, reversing management's "past peak" assessment — tested against each quarterly filing.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Camden sold its entire California portfolio (11 properties) for ~$1.6bn in July 2026 [Certain] (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/906345/000162828026051327/cpt-20260630.htm) and redeployed proceeds into buybacks, Sun Belt acquisitions and debt reduction, repaying all outstanding credit lines and commercial paper [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/cpt/transcripts/659238-q2-2026/). Shares outstanding fell 2.43% YoY, consistent with buybacks (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/cpt/statistics/). FCF yield is currently negative (-2.06%) (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/cpt/statistics/); FCF margin and conversion are [data unavailable] from the income/cash-flow statements, so cash generation cannot be fully verified from stockanalysis.com alone.
