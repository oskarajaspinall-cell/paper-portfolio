# Macro overlay: Raymond James Financial, Inc. (RJF) — 2026-10-12
## Assessment
RJF is a wealth manager/broker-dealer where rates hit two channels: the discount rate in its own DCF, and net interest income (NII) on client cash-sweep balances and RJ Bank deposits/loans; credit spreads and volatility affect capital-markets/underwriting fees [Likely] **(inference)**.

Facts (FRED, 2026-10-08/09): 10y real yield (DFII10) 2.87%, +44bp/1m, +56bp/3m; 10y Treasury (DGS10) 5.22%, +42bp/1m, +68bp/3m; 2y (DGS2) 4.75%, +36bp/1m; fed funds (DFF) 3.88%, +25bp/1m; 10y breakeven (T10YIE) 2.33%, roughly flat (-4bp/1m); HY spread (BAMLH0A0HYM2) 3.15%, +48bp/1m but still low; VIX 15.41, falling.

Market pricing (proxy, not fact): the 2y yield (4.75%) sits ~87bp above fed funds (3.88%), implying the market is not pricing near-term cuts and may expect a higher-for-longer path — a hawkish repricing versus a cutting-cycle narrative **(inference)**. Flat breakevens alongside the near-lockstep real/nominal yield rise point to a term-premium move, not an inflation scare **(inference)**.

RJF's fair value already uses today's 5.22% 10y as its risk-free rate, so the DCF is current, not stale. Bull: yields stabilise -> discount rate eases, cash-sweep NII stays supportive, low VIX/spreads keep underwriting open -> fair value drifts toward the $201 bull case. Bear: real yields keep rising -> discount rate rises further, pulling fair value below the $171 base; or a sharp cut cycle instead compresses cash-sweep NII. Tilt: neutral/moderate — the two rate channels offset, not yet large enough to override the fundamentals-led thesis (advisor recruiting, AUA growth, peer-discount valuation).
