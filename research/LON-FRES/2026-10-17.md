# Fresnillo plc (LON:FRES) — Core initiation — 2026-10-17

## 1. Business
Fresnillo is the world's largest primary silver producer and Mexico's largest gold producer, running eight mines plus brownfield/greenfield projects in Mexico and Canada (source: fact sheet news, https://www.marketbeat.com/instant-alerts/event-fresnillo-highlights-growth-pipeline-strong-cash-flow-and-bullish-metals-outlook-2026-09-29/) [Certain]. It sells silver, gold and by-product lead/zinc at prevailing market prices, so revenue and margins are levered directly to spot metal prices and to ore grades/volumes mined, not to any pricing power of its own [Certain].

## 2. Quality
Headline returns look exceptional — ROIC 65.7% TTM vs 5.4% FY2024, ROE 53.0% TTM vs 5.5% FY2024, operating margin 59.4% TTM vs 21.0% FY2024 (source: fact sheet ratios/income statement) [Certain] — but this is price-driven operating leverage, not an improving asset base. Two separate headlines this year report falling silver/gold production from lower ore grades at key mines (Q1 2026: Saucito, Fresnillo, Juanicipio; Q2 2026: unspecified) while full-year guidance was held (https://www.wsj.com/business/fresnillos-quarterly-silver-production-slips-6b71ccba, https://www.wsj.com/business/fresnillos-gold-silver-production-falls-on-lower-ore-grades-a35290a9) [Likely]. A separate item notes silver prices are down in 2026 (https://www.nasdaq.com/articles/zacks-industry-outlook-highlights-fresnillo-pan-american-silver-and-hecla-mining) [Guessing]. As a price-taker with declining grades at its legacy Mexican mines, Fresnillo has no mechanism to defend today's margins if metal prices normalise; peers face the identical commodity exposure, so there is no durable moat, only a currently net-cash balance sheet (net debt/EBITDA -0.41x TTM) and a high Piotroski F-Score of 7 (source: fact sheet ratios/statistics) [Certain].

## 3. Valuation
On trailing multiples the stock screens cheap: P/E 11.7x is below its own 5y range (min 21.1x) and the peer median (14.1x, -17%); EV/EBITDA 6.3x and P/FCF 11.7x are both below peer medians (source: fact sheet valuation table) [Certain]. But P/B is 4.8x, 68% above the peer median and at the 67th percentile of its own 5y range — the market is capitalising today's abnormal ROE, not a normalised one [Certain]. The fact sheet's fair-value model (EV/EBITDA method, normalised for peak TTM earnings) puts base fair value at GBX 1,440 — 47% below the last close of GBX 2,719 — with bear GBX 785 (-71%) and bull GBX 4,842 (+78%) [Certain]. The gap between cheap trailing multiples and an expensive normalised fair value says today's price assumes metal prices and margins stay near current elevated levels, inconsistent with the declining-grade production trend above [Likely].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Gross margin (income statement page) holds above 45% for two more quarters even as silver/gold spot prices ease, showing margin resilience beyond price pass-through.
- Production volumes/ore grades (company-reported, next quarterly filing) stabilise or rise, reversing the Q1/Q2 2026 declines, showing operational improvement rather than price luck.
- ROIC (ratios page) stays above 30% for full-year FY2026 even if average realised metal prices fall from TTM levels, showing return durability beyond the current cycle peak.
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) stays net-cash through the brownfield/greenfield capex ramp in Mexico and Canada, confirming capital discipline is not being sacrificed for growth.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
FCF margin swung from negative in FY2022 (-3.7%) and FY2023 (-2.1%) to 26.6% FY2024, 41.4% FY2025 and 37.7% TTM, with FCF conversion (FCF/NI) of 99.3% TTM (source: fact sheet cash flow/income statement) [Certain]. The balance sheet is net cash (net debt/EBITDA -0.41x TTM), giving headroom to fund the Mexico/Canada project pipeline without leverage [Certain]. Management highlighted continued dividend payments and brownfield/greenfield investment in H1 2026 results and at a September 2026 investor forum (https://www.marketbeat.com/instant-alerts/fresnillo-h1-earnings-call-highlights-2026-08-04/, /quote/lon/FRES/transcripts/751861-mining-forum-americas-2026/) [Likely]; specific dividend/capex amounts are not on the fact sheet and are not cited as data here.

## 6. Unusual items
Institutional ownership is low at 19.7% and insider ownership is 0.00% (source: fact sheet) [Certain] — a governance point worth monitoring but not itself a red flag for a long-established dual-listed miner. Next earnings are due 2026-10-20, three days after this note (source: fact sheet price table) [Certain]; the quarterly print should be read against the production-decline headlines above before any position is sized. RSI (14-day) of 45.0 and short interest are not material/not disclosed (source: fact sheet) [Certain].
