# Macro overlay: Fresnillo plc (FRES) — 2026-10-17
## Assessment
Fresnillo is a pure price-taker in silver and gold; it cannot offset declining ore grades except through the spot metal price, so earnings and the multiple track the gold/silver cycle, driven by real yields and the dollar [Certain]. Chain: real yields + USD strength → gold/silver spot → Fresnillo revenue/margin (price-taker) → earnings and multiple (already at P/B 4.8x, 68% above peers — fact sheet, 2026-10-17).

Facts (FRED, 2026-10-15/16): 10y real yield (DFII10) 2.78%, +0.43pp/3m; 10y nominal (DGS10) 5.10%, +0.53pp/3m; breakeven inflation (T10YIE) 2.35%, +0.11pp/3m — the nominal-yield rise is mostly real, not inflation [Certain]. Broad dollar index (DTWEXBGS, 2026-10-09) 121.6, +3.72 index pts/1m — a sharp recent move [Certain]. Effective fed funds (DFF) 3.88%, +0.25pp/1m, a hike just delivered [Certain].

Market pricing (proxy): 2y Treasury (DGS2) 4.77% vs fed funds 3.88%, a +0.89pp spread, implies the market prices further tightening, not cuts [Likely, proxy]. This matches the direction already flagged in the fact sheet's news (commentary, not fact: a stronger dollar and rising real rates cited as drivers of gold's 2026 correction), but the 1-month dollar acceleration is fresh, not yet reflected in that older commentary (inference) [Likely].

Bear: real yields/dollar keep rising → metal prices soften → today's peak margins (59% operating TTM) normalise, de-rating the stretched P/B. Bull: real yields/dollar stall (credit spreads already +0.47pp/1m to 3.23%, BAMLH0A0HYM2, a possible early brake on the Fed) → metals hold, cushioning the grade-decline problem in the fundamental note. Tilt: moderate toward bear — macro reinforces, not offsets, the fundamental bear case.
