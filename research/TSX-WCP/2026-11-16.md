# Whitecap Resources (TSX:WCP) — Core initiation — 2026-11-16
## 1. Business
Canadian oil and gas exploration and production company; revenue is commodity sales (crude, condensate, gas) priced off benchmarks, with a dividend and a drilling inventory supporting modest growth [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tsx/WCP/). Reports in CAD, listed on the TSX; the business is Canadian, so no foreign-country revenue share needs flagging [Likely]. Earnings and cash flow swing with oil prices.

## 2. Quality
- Returns have normalised, not compounded. ROIC fell from 51.6% (FY2021) to 10.7% (FY2025); TTM is 11.9% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tsx/WCP/financials/ratios/) [Certain]. This is a commodity-cycle pattern, not a durable moat; a price-taker has no pricing power [Likely].
- Direction change: the last 12 months rose. ROIC went 5.9% to 11.9%, and FCF margin went 14.6% to 30.6% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tsx/WCP/financials/ratios/?p=trailing). Operating margin is 28.5%, still below its 2-year high of 36.3% [Certain]. FY endpoints overstate the decline; TTM is near a cyclical high for FCF [Likely].
- Balance sheet is strong: net debt/EBITDA 0.51x TTM [Certain]. Piotroski F 7, Altman Z 2.10 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tsx/WCP/statistics/) [Certain].
- Moat: low-cost operations and inventory depth only, per management commentary [Guessing].

## 3. Valuation
P/E is 13.7x, 96% of its 5y range, in line with peers (13.7x). EV/EBITDA is 5.2x, 96% of range, 21% below the peer median. P/B is 1.8x, above the 5y max [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tsx/WCP/financials/ratios/). P/FCF is 8.8x (24% of range, 49% below peers) with an FCF yield of 11.36% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tsx/WCP/statistics/) [Certain].

Earnings multiples sit at 5y highs while the cash multiple is mid-range: the market is capitalising near-peak TTM cash flow at a normal multiple. The mechanical base fair value of 6.68 CAD against a 17.34 close rests only on EV/EBITDA, since the reserve NAV is [data unavailable]; treat it as unreliable, not as evidence [Likely]. The price assumes oil prices hold near current levels [Likely].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROIC (ratios page, TTM) falls below 8%.
- FCF margin (cash-flow/income statement, TTM) falls below 18%.
- Net debt / EBITDA (ratios page) rises above 1.5x.
- Shares change YoY (statistics page) shows further dilution above 10% in a year not tied to a disclosed acquisition.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
FCF conversion is 155.0% of net income TTM versus 61.9% in FY2025 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tsx/WCP/financials/cash-flow-statement/) [Certain]. Management raised the 2026 capital budget, production outlook and dividend after Q3 (source: https://www.marketbeat.com/instant-alerts/transcript-whitecap-resources-q3-earnings-call-highlights-2026-10-31/) [Likely]: a higher budget lowers the share of cash flow left for holders. Share count rose 45.30% YoY (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tsx/WCP/statistics/) [Certain]; the cause (likely acquisition-related issuance) is not verified [Guessing].

## 6. Unusual items
Oil-price cyclicality and heavy dilution are the main exposures. Short interest is 1.12% of float, down 11.4% on the month; beta 0.81 [Certain]. RSI 40.0 and the price 6.7% below its 50-day average after a 52-week high of 20.79 on 2026-10-23 [Certain].
