# General Motors Company (GM) — Core initiation — 2026-10-12
## 1. Business
GM designs, manufactures and sells vehicles under Chevrolet, GMC, Cadillac and Buick, wholesaling to dealers in North America and China JVs [Certain]. GM Financial, its captive lender, finances dealer and retail purchases and contributes financing income [Certain]. Management is pivoting the lineup toward hybrids as all-electric demand slows (source: https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/05/gm-hybrid-vehicles.html) [Likely].

## 2. Quality
ROIC is roughly flat at ~6% over five years, but gross, operating and net margins have compressed every year since FY2021 (gross −3.5pp, operating −2.8pp, net −6.4pp), and ROE has fallen from 17.2% to 3.2% TTM (source: factsheet RA/IS) [Certain]. Net margin is just 1.1% TTM [Certain]. There is no durable moat: Reuters reports the "Detroit Three," including GM, are losing US share to Asian rivals, and GM's Q3 unit sales fell 5.5% y/y with EV sales down across the board (source: https://www.reuters.com/business/autos-transportation/detroit-three-automakers-set-lose-market-share-asian-rivals-2026-10-01/) [Likely]. A lower federal fuel-economy standard and the hybrid pivot are modest offsetting tailwinds (source: https://stockanalysis.com/news/nhtsa-lowers-fuel-economy-standards/) [Likely]. Net debt/EBITDA of 6.32x looks high but is driven by GM Financial's $111.7bn of captive-lending debt: core automotive debt is only ~$16.0bn against $24.7bn of liquidity (source: SEC 10-Q) [Certain]. This is a capital-intensive, cyclical business with limited pricing power and active share loss to lower-cost rivals.

## 3. Valuation
TTM P/E of 41.7x is above the stock's own 5y maximum (24.0x), but net income is depressed by restructuring/EV charges, making P/E unreliable [Certain]. On cash flow GM looks statistically cheap: P/FCF of 5.0x sits near the 10th percentile of its 5y range (4.3x–11.1x) and FCF yield is 19.83% (source: factsheet RA/ST) [Certain]. EV/EBITDA of 10.7x is near the top of its own 5y range (98th percentile: 7.9x–10.8x), still below the peer median (24.8x, n=3, skewed by a high-multiple peer) [Certain]. The fair-value model's base case (158.74, +91.9%) requires margins to recover toward historical levels; its bear case (71.64, −13.4%) assumes margins stay near TTM lows [Certain]. The reverse DCF implies the price prices in a −6.4% year-1 revenue decline — the market already assumes continued share loss [Likely]. Cheap cash-flow multiples reflect depressed, not durable, earnings; re-rating needs margin stabilization not yet shown.

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Gross margin (income statement) recovers above 12% (FY2024 level) for two consecutive quarters, reversing the 5y decline.
- ROE (ratios page) stabilizes above 10% for a full fiscal year.
- Reported US quarterly vehicle sales stop declining for two consecutive quarters as the hybrid lineup ships.
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page, site definition) falls back toward 5.0x.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
GM repurchased $2.8bn of stock in H1 2026 ($0.8bn Q1, $2.0bn Q2) and paid ~$324m of dividends while margins were compressing — capital priority is shrinking the share count, not reinvesting for margin recovery (source: SEC 10-Q) [Likely]. H1 2026 operating cash flow was $9.3bn; TTM FCF margin is 7.8% but FCF/NI conversion of 738.7% is inflated by a depressed net-income base from write-downs, not a genuine quality signal (source: factsheet CF/IS) [Certain]. Automotive liquidity ($24.7bn) exceeds automotive debt (~$16.0bn); GM Financial's larger debt load is normal captive-lender funding (source: SEC 10-Q) [Certain].

## 6. Unusual items
Short interest is modest (2.38% of float, down 5.3% m/m) [Certain]. Beta of 1.40 and an Altman Z-score of 1.27 point to high cyclicality and balance-sheet sensitivity in a downturn [Certain]. Mexican-tariff policy is squeezing cross-border auto supply chains industry-wide and GM has meaningful Mexican manufacturing exposure (source: https://www.reuters.com/business/autos-transportation/mexican-auto-exports-slump-september-us-tariffs-take-toll-2026-10-08/) [Likely]. GM has also cut planned Bolt EV production by ~75% after federal EV support lapsed (source: https://www.reuters.com/business/autos-transportation/gms-pint-sized-bolt-ev-gets-smaller-production-run-2026-09-29/) [Likely].
