# Macro overlay: General Motors Company (GM) — 2026-10-12
## Assessment
Chain: rising real yields/fed funds → auto-loan APRs & GM Financial's funding cost → demand and financing margin; same real yield → cost of equity → valuation multiple. GM Financial carries ~$111.7bn debt (10-Q, 2026-06-30) [Certain], making GM more rate-sensitive than most industrials.

Facts (FRED, 2026-10-08/09): 10y real yield (DFII10) 2.87%, +0.56pp/3m; 10y Treasury (DGS10) 5.22%, +0.68pp/3m; fed funds (DFF) 3.88%, +0.26pp/3m; breakeven (T10YIE) 2.33%, ~flat; HY spread 3.15%, +0.45pp/3m; VIX 15.41, down/3m [Certain]. Proxy: 2y yield (4.75%) sits 0.87pp above fed funds, i.e. no near-term cuts priced [Likely] (proxy, inference).

Inference: flat breakevens with sharply higher real yields point to a real-rate/term-premium move, not an inflation scare **(inference)**. The DCF already discounts at today's 5.22% risk-free rate, so base fair value (158.74) is current; further rises would cut it, reversals lift it [Likely]. Spreads are only modestly wider and VIX low, so the market isn't pricing a credit-driven demand shock beyond the fundamental share-loss/margin story already in the note (Q3 sales -5.5% y/y, WSJ 2026-10-01) — rates are incremental, not core.

Bull: rates plateau/ease → loan rates stabilize, funding cost stops rising, sales decline flattens → supports re-rating off a depressed base.
Bear: rates keep rising → APRs climb, deepening the affordability-driven decline, squeezes funding spread, raises the discount rate → fair value falls below base.
Tilt: toward bear, moderate — the fundamental thesis is already soft; higher-for-longer real rates add to, not offset, demand and funding-cost pressure.
