# Macro overlay: Greatland Resources (ASX:GGP) — 2026-11-15
## Assessment
**Chain:** real yields and the dollar -> gold (and copper) price in USD -> AUD realised price on a mostly fixed cost base -> margin and EV/EBITDA multiple. A price-taker with a 52.2% operating margin has high operating leverage to the metal price (note section 2) [Likely].

**Facts (FRED):** US 10y real yield 2.86% on 2026-11-12, +0.47pp over ~3m (https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=DFII10). Broad dollar index 121.88 on 2026-11-06, +2.82 over ~3m (https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=DTWEXBGS). 10y breakeven 2.24%, -0.03 over ~3m (https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=T10YIE). No gold price or AUD series in the snapshot [data unavailable].

**Pricing (proxy):** 2y yield 4.86% vs fed funds 3.88% implies hikes priced. Flat breakevens with rising real yields suggest a real-rate tightening, not an inflation scare **(inference)**.

**Inference:** a headwind for gold, consistent with GGP's -30.4% 6-month move, but the gold price itself is unverified [Guessing]. EV/EBITDA of 4.3x already discounts a lower metal price or output [Likely]. Further real-yield rises hurt; falls or a weaker dollar help.

**Bull:** real yields fall, dollar weakens, gold recovers and the multiple rebuilds. **Bear:** real yields keep rising, metal price drops, margin compresses and Havieron funding tightens.

**Tilt:** slightly toward bear, small; the transition year dominates and much is priced. No foreign country identified at ~10%+ of revenue.
