# Macro overlay: Greatland Resources (ASX:GGP) — 2026-10-11
## Assessment
GGP is a pure-play, unhedged gold-copper producer and a price-taker [Certain] (note §2) — gold price is the dominant macro lever on its revenue, margin and multiple. **Chain**: US real yields & the dollar → USD gold price → GGP revenue/margin (no pricing power) → EV/EBITDA multiple.

**Facts** (FRED, official): 10y real yield (DFII10) 2.87% on 2026-10-08, +0.44pp/1m, +0.56pp/3m; broad dollar index (DTWEXBGS) 121.4 on 2026-10-02, +2.86/1m, +0.70/3m; 10y breakeven inflation (T10YIE) flat-to-down (-0.04/1m), so the nominal yield rise (DGS10 +0.42/1m) is a *real*-rate move, not an inflation story; fed funds (DFF) 3.88%, +0.25pp/1m and /3m. All: https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=&lt;series&gt; (dates above).

**Pricing/inference**: rising real yields plus a firmer dollar is the classic gold headwind (higher opportunity cost of a non-yielding asset, dearer USD gold) **(inference, not a FRED series)**. GGP's own share price has fallen -30.5% over 6 months and -7.1% over 3 months against a rising SPY (fact sheet HI/SPY-HI) — directionally consistent with the market already discounting a less supportive real-yield/dollar backdrop since Q2 2026 **(inference)**. AUD/USD itself isn't in our FRED set, so the partial AUD-cost offset to a weaker gold price can't be quantified here — a data gap, not a judgment.

**Bull**: real yields/dollar roll over → gold price firms → margin and the deeply discounted 4.2x EV/EBITDA multiple both re-rate. **Bear**: real yields/dollar extend higher → gold price soft on top of the already-guided FY2027 lower-output/higher-AISC transition (note §2) → margin compression, FCF yield falls, discount persists.
