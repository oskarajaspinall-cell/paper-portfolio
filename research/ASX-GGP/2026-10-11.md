# Greatland Resources Limited (ASX:GGP) — Core initiation — 2026-10-11

## 1. Business
Australian gold-copper miner. Owns and operates the Telfer mill and underground/open-pit mines in WA's Paterson province, and is developing the adjacent Havieron deposit (acquired, with Telfer, from Newmont in 2025). Revenue comes from selling gold and copper doré/concentrate at spot prices [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/asx/GGP/transcripts/710196-q4-2026/).

## 2. Quality
TTM ROIC 85.8%, ROCE 43.3%, ROE 48.4%, all rising sharply from negative FY22-24 readings [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/asx/GGP/financials/ratios/) — the step-change reflects the 2025 Telfer/Havieron acquisition, not an established run-rate. Durable edge: management describes owning "the third largest mill in Australia," running at only ~25% capacity (AUD 17/t processing cost) and underground infrastructure rated for 6Mt/yr vs 1.3Mt processed today — a scale/low-cost advantage new entrants cannot easily replicate [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/asx/GGP/transcripts/723212-diggers-dealers-mining-forum-2026/). FY2026 AISC of AUD 2,179/oz beat guidance (AUD 2,400-2,800) by >AUD 200/oz [Certain] (same source, Q4 transcript). But it is a price-taker: no pricing power, margins are gold/copper-price dependent, and FY2027 guidance is for lower output (260-310koz vs 329koz) and higher cost (AISC AUD 2,933-3,330) as stockpiles deplete and growth capex ramps [Certain] (source: Q4 2026 transcript). Operating margin 52.2%, net margin 38.2% TTM, both rising [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/asx/GGP/financials/income-statement/).

## 3. Valuation
Current price assumes the FY2027 step-down in output/cost persists and caps re-rating: P/E 8.0x vs peer median 19.6x (-59%), EV/EBITDA 4.2x vs 8.4x (-50%), P/FCF 8.7x vs 29.4x (-70%); all below GGP's own 5y multiple range [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/asx/GGP/financials/ratios/, peer statistics pages). FCF yield 11.5% [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/asx/GGP/statistics/). Base fair value (EV/EBITDA method, the only one stockanalysis.com data supports; NAV unavailable) is AUD 16.51 vs last close AUD 10.11 (+63.3%); even the bear case (AUD 11.71, +15.8%) sits above the current price [Likely] (source: fact sheet; fair value is inform-only, never a buy rule). The gap likely reflects the FY2027 guidance step-down and 28.5% YoY share dilution from acquisition funding, both already public, suggesting the discount is wider than the fundamentals justify [Likely].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROIC (ratios page) falls below 30%, reverting toward pre-acquisition levels and signalling the Telfer/Havieron returns were a one-off.
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) turns positive beyond 1.0x, showing growth capex is outrunning the cash position.
- FCF margin (income statement/cash-flow pages) falls below 15%, indicating capex is structurally outrunning operating cash generation.
- AISC (company reporting) stays above AUD 3,000/oz for more than one year beyond the guided FY2027 transition.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
FY2026 cash flow AUD 714m; GGP closed the year with AUD 1.3bn cash and zero debt, then arranged an additional AUD 500m facility (~AUD 1.8bn total liquidity) ahead of heavy Havieron (AUD 365-435m) and Telfer (AUD 315-335m) FY2027 growth capex [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/asx/GGP/transcripts/710196-q4-2026/). FCF margin fell from 44.3% to 34.7% and FCF conversion from 125.8% to 90.8% TTM [Certain] (source: income statement/cash-flow pages) — reinvestment into growth projects, not deteriorating core economics, given the cash buffer.

## 6. Unusual items
Shares outstanding +28.5% YoY, largely scrip issued to fund the 2025 Newmont (Telfer/Havieron) acquisition — material dilution already reflected in per-share metrics above [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/asx/GGP/statistics/). Next earnings due 2026-10-12, one day after this note [Certain] (source: fact sheet) — a near-term event but not the basis for this core thesis.
