# Greatland Resources (ASX:GGP) — Core initiation — 2026-11-15
## 1. Business
Gold-copper producer: sells gold and copper from the Telfer mine and is building the Havieron development, both in Western Australia [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/asx/GGP/transcripts/774787-q1-2027/). Earnings follow metal prices and ounces produced. Revenue-by-country split [data unavailable]; no foreign country is identified as ~10%+ of revenue in the sources read [Guessing].

## 2. Quality
Returns are exceptional but young. ROIC is 85.8% TTM (FY2024: -19.1%), ROCE 43.3%, operating margin 52.2%, gross margin 55.4% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/asx/GGP/financials/ratios/) [Certain]. Piotroski F 7 and Altman Z 7.08 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/asx/GGP/statistics/) [Certain].

Moat: low-cost position, not a durable franchise. A gold miner is a price-taker; margins reflect the metal price and a low cost base [Likely]. The last quarter beat plan on gold and copper output, with cost at the low end of guidance and guidance unchanged (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/asx/GGP/transcripts/774787-q1-2027/) [Likely].

Direction: FCF margin fell from 44.3% to 34.7% over 12 months, 11.1pp below its 2-year high; FCF conversion fell to 90.8% [Certain]. Management called FY2027 a transition year of lower production and higher investment (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/asx/GGP/transcripts/710196-q4-2026/) [Likely]. The decline fits that, but it is not yet proven temporary [Guessing].

## 3. Valuation
P/E 8.2x, EV/EBITDA 4.3x, P/FCF 8.9x: all below their 5y minimum (P/E min 8.9x, EV/EBITDA min 5.2x) and 50-74% below peer medians (P/E 21.8x, EV/EBITDA 8.6x; peers NST, EVN, RMS, CMM) [Certain]. FCF yield is 11.25% [Certain].

The price assumes earnings are at a peak and will drop: lower FY2027 output plus the 5y history covers only a short, loss-making-to-profitable period, so "below range" overstates cheapness [Likely]. Mechanical fair value is 16.51 AUD base (11.71 bear, 21.31 bull) vs a 10.36 close, +59.3% to base; it rests on EV/EBITDA alone because the NAV method lacks data [Certain]. The price is 13.6% below its 200-day average with 6-month momentum -30.4%, so the market is not yet confirming [Certain].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROIC (ratios page, TTM) falls below 30%.
- Operating margin (income statement, TTM) falls below 40%.
- FCF margin (cash flow statement, TTM) falls below 20%, i.e. the transition year erodes cash rather than just dips it.
- Net debt / EBITDA (ratios page) rises above 0.5x, i.e. Havieron spending consumes the net cash.
