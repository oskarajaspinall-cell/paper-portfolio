# CGI Inc. (TSX:GIB.A) — Core initiation — 2026-10-19

## 1. Business
CGI is a global IT and business consulting/systems-integration services firm (Information Technology Services industry) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tsx/GIB.A/). It earns fees from long-duration outsourcing, application management and consulting contracts across government, financial services, health and other industries, billed largely on a time-and-materials or managed-services basis, with proprietary IP solutions licensed alongside services [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tsx/GIB.A/).

## 2. Quality
Returns are solid: ROIC 14.3% TTM (14.8% to 16.5% over FY2021-FY2025) and ROCE 18.9% TTM (17.4% to 20.7%), both roughly flat over five years [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tsx/GIB.A/financials/ratios/). Operating margin is steady at 16.1% to 16.5% and gross margin flat near 20% for five straight years [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tsx/GIB.A/financials/income-statement/), consistent with long contracts that get re-priced rather than exposure to sudden margin shocks. FCF conversion is strong at 143% of net income TTM, down from 146% in FY2021 [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tsx/GIB.A/financials/cash-flow-statement/, https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tsx/GIB.A/financials/income-statement/).

The moat is switching costs, not technology: once embedded in a client's core operations (benefits administration, bank core processing), CGI is costly and risky to re-tender, and its acquisitive, decentralized model lets it absorb smaller rivals' client books, as with the Callibrity bolt-on completed in September 2026 [Likely] (source: https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/cgi-acquires-callibrity-strengthening-presence-in-cincinnati-and-expanding-production-ready-ai-and-platform-engineering-capabilities-302872898.html). This is a scale/relationship moat, not one rivals are barred from: Accenture and Capgemini contest the same accounts [Likely].

## 3. Valuation
GIB.A trades below its own five-year multiple floor on every metric shown: P/E 13.2x vs a 16.5x-20.7x five-year range and EV/EBITDA 8.4x vs 10.8x-14.2x [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tsx/GIB.A/financials/ratios/, https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tsx/GIB.A/history/). It is also cheaper than peers (ACN, DXC, EPA:CAP, INFY) on P/E (-18% vs peer median), EV/EBITDA (-10%), P/FCF (-17%) and P/B (-30%) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tsx/GIB.A/financials/ratios/, peer statistics pages). The reverse DCF implies the price assumes revenue shrinks about 9.4% in year one before fading to 2.5% terminal growth — far more pessimistic than the flat-to-growing margin history shows [Certain] (source: research/TSX-GIB.A/2026-10-19-valuation.md). Base fair value of CAD 196.18 is +84.3% above the CAD 106.42 close, and even the bear case (CAD 148.17) is +39.2% above [Certain] (source: research/TSX-GIB.A/2026-10-19-valuation.md). The stock's 52-week change is -12.4%, so the de-rating looks sentiment- rather than fundamentals-driven given flat margins [Likely].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROIC (ratios page) falls below 12% for two consecutive fiscal years, signalling the switching-cost moat is eroding.
- Operating margin (income statement) falls below 15% for two consecutive quarters, indicating pricing or cost discipline has broken.
- FCF conversion (FCF/NI, cash-flow + income statement) falls below 100% for a full fiscal year, indicating working-capital or capex deterioration beyond normal noise.
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) rises above 2.0x, signalling September 2026's new note issuance was the start of releveraging rather than routine refinancing.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
CGI converts earnings to cash well above 100% (143% TTM, 116.6% to 145.7% over FY2021-FY2025) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tsx/GIB.A/financials/cash-flow-statement/). Shares outstanding fell 5.1% year-on-year, indicating sustained buybacks [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tsx/GIB.A/statistics/). Net debt/EBITDA is moderate at 1.31x TTM, up from 0.71x in FY2024, after it priced a private offering of senior unsecured notes in two tranches in September 2026 [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tsx/GIB.A/financials/ratios/; https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/cgi-announces-private-offering-of-c500-million-in-aggregate-of-3-25-year-and-4-75-year-senior-unsecured-notes-302874496.html). It also funded the Callibrity bolt-on that same month [Certain] (source: https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/cgi-acquires-callibrity-strengthening-presence-in-cincinnati-and-expanding-production-ready-ai-and-platform-engineering-capabilities-302872898.html), consistent with its long-running tuck-in M&A strategy funded by free cash flow and modest debt.

## 6. Unusual items
Beta is unusually low at 0.19 (5-year) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tsx/GIB.A/statistics/), and short interest is low and falling (0.63% of float, -4.0% month-on-month) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tsx/GIB.A/statistics/) — no distress or crowded-short signal. Institutional ownership is high at 63.2% with minimal insider ownership (0.27%) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tsx/GIB.A/statistics/). Recent news flow is routine contract wins/partnerships (France's Ministry of Agriculture modernization, D-Wave quantum partnership, CGI Federal/DLA research agreement) [Likely], none large enough individually to move the thesis.
