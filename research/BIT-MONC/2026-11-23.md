# Moncler S.p.A. (BIT:MONC) — Core initiation — 2026-11-23

## 1. Business
Italian luxury outerwear group: the Moncler brand, sold mainly through its own stores and online (DTC) plus wholesale, and Stone Island, which grew in the latest quarter [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/bit/MONC/transcripts/652948-q3-2026/). Revenue by country/region: [data unavailable] (company site not fetchable under the allowlist). Tourist spending in Europe is a stated swing factor [Certain] (source: https://www.reuters.com/business/retail-consumer/moncler-reports-flat-q3-revenue-held-back-by-weaker-tourist-spending-europe-2026-10-21/).

## 2. Quality
Good business, with a high-margin franchise [Certain]: gross margin 78.2% TTM (77.1% in FY2023), operating margin 29.0%, net margin 20.0% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/bit/MONC/financials/income-statement/). Net debt/EBITDA is 0.10x and Altman Z is 6.13 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/bit/MONC/statistics/). The durable reason is brand pricing power, which shows in a gross margin that has held near 78% [Likely].

Direction, as the fact sheet requires: ROIC is 18.4% TTM against 23.2% in FY2022, and 2.6pp below its 2-year high of 21.0%. ROCE is 19.1% against 20.6% a year earlier. Returns are drifting down from a peak but remain well above 15% [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/bit/MONC/financials/ratios/?p=trailing). The cause looks like softer demand on a fixed store and brand cost base, not margin collapse: operating margin is flat (+0.3pp over 12 months) [Likely]. FCF margin is rising over 12 months (21.5% to 25.0%) even though it is falling over five years [Certain].

## 3. Valuation
At 39.49 EUR the shares trade below their own 5-year minimum on every multiple [Certain]: P/E 17.4x (5y min 21.6x, median 23.8x), EV/EBITDA 9.9x (min 13.6x), P/FCF 13.9x (min 17.4x). Against peers (Hermès, LVMH, Brunello Cucinelli, Kering) P/E is -40% and EV/EBITDA -21% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/bit/MONC/financials/ratios/). FCF yield is 7.44% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/bit/MONC/statistics/).

Mechanical base fair value is 46.59 EUR (+18.0%), bear 33.19, bull 70.07, a DCF-led blend. The reverse DCF says the price implies 3.1% year-1 revenue growth, fading to terminal growth, which is low for a brand that reported flat latest-quarter revenue [Likely]. The price is not at a trend-supported level: it is 20.5% below the 200-day average, with a 12-month change of -29.5% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/bit/MONC/history/). This is a value case, not a "working" uptrend case [Certain].

The balance sheet is close to net-cash neutral. Share count is up 0.28% year on year, so there is no buyback shrinkage [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/bit/MONC/statistics/). ## 6. Unusual items
Why the price fell (entry-price hit): the stock dropped after weaker European tourism hurt sales (source: https://www.wsj.com/business/retail/moncler-shares-fall-after-weaker-tourism-hurts-european-sales-40a261fa) [Likely]. Third-quarter revenue was flat, with DTC softer and warm weather weighing on traffic (source: https://www.reuters.com/business/retail-consumer/moncler-reports-flat-q3-revenue-held-back-by-weaker-tourist-spending-europe-2026-10-21/) [Likely].
