# Orient Securities (SHA:600958) — Core initiation — 2026-11-09
## 1. Business
Mainland Chinese full-service broker: commission income, proprietary and investment gains, asset management and financing. Earnings are driven by domestic A-share turnover, market level and regulation [Likely]. Revenue is essentially onshore China, so the macro overlay should check CNY and PBoC policy [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sha/600958/). Segment split not fetched [data unavailable].

## 2. Quality
Weak on returns, strong on cyclical margin. ROIC is 2.0% TTM versus 3.0% in FY2021, and ROE is 7.0% versus 8.6% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sha/600958/financials/ratios/) [Certain]. Operating margin has risen to 47.9% TTM from 30.2% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sha/600958/financials/income-statement/). That reflects a strong market cycle rather than a moat [Likely]. Brokers are commoditised, with no durable pricing power [Likely]. FCF margin is 33.7% TTM, down from 71.9% a year earlier, and swings wildly (-0.9% in FY2025; source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sha/600958/financials/cash-flow-statement/?p=trailing) [Certain]. Cash flow in brokers is driven by working-capital and trading-book movements, so it is not a reliable quality signal [Likely]. Piotroski F is 4 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sha/600958/statistics/). Leverage metrics are [data unavailable].

## 3. Valuation
P/E is 12.2x, below its 5y minimum of 16.0x (median 25.3x), but 29% above the peer median of 9.4x (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sha/600958/statistics/) [Certain]. P/B is 0.8x, below its 5y minimum of 0.9x and in line with peers at 0.8x [Certain]. Cheap versus its own history, not versus peers. Earnings are near a cyclical peak, so the P/E flatters [Likely]. Mechanical fair value is 5.71 CNY base against an 8.38 close, with methods disagreeing 2.1x (P/E method 12.05), so it is unreliable [Certain]. The price implies 6.4% year-1 revenue growth [Certain]. FCF yield is 7.51%. EV multiples are [data unavailable]. Stock is 21.8% lower over 12 months with a negative trend [Certain].

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROE (ratios page) stays below 6% on a TTM basis at the next two quarterly reports.
- Operating margin (income statement, TTM) falls below 35%.
- FCF margin (trailing cash-flow page) turns negative for two consecutive TTM periods.
- Shares outstanding (statistics page) rise more than 5% year on year through dilution or deal funding.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Shares changed 1.07% year on year (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sha/600958/statistics/) [Certain]. Reported consolidation talk, a buyout of a smaller rival under state-led consolidation, could bring integration risk and issuance [Likely] (source: https://www.scmp.com/business/china-business/article/3350685/orient-securities-buyout-create-us85b-brokerage-beijing-pushes-consolidation).
