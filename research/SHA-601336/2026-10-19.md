# New China Life Insurance Company Ltd. (SHA:601336) — Core initiation — 2026-10-19

## 1. Business
New China Life Insurance sells long-term savings, protection and annuity products to Chinese households through bancassurance, agency and group channels [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sha/601336/). Revenue is net premiums earned plus investment income on policy reserves; profit depends on underwriting margin, persistency, and the spread between investment returns and guaranteed policy rates [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sha/601336/financials/income-statement/).

## 2. Quality
ROIC swung from 9.6% (FY2021) to a 3.8% trough (FY2023) to 16.0% TTM, and ROE from 14.2% to 34.9% (FY2025) to 42.8% TTM [Certain] (source: ratios page). That volatility shows earnings follow investment-market swings and policy-spread economics, not a stable moat; TTM ROE sits well above the 5-year trend, a sign of a peak rather than a sustainable run-rate [Likely]. Net debt/EBITDA has risen to 3.25x (site definition 2.60x) from 2.20x in FY2022 [Certain] (source: ratios page) — distorted for an insurer by policy reserves booked as liabilities, so treat as directional only [Likely]. Management's Q2 2026 and Q4 2025 calls described continued premium growth, rising persistency and an improving investment yield, with record assets and bancassurance strength [Likely] (source: transcripts, see Sources). FCF conversion, though high (241.5% TTM), fell sharply from FY2023's 1,026.8%, consistent with a normalizing profit cycle [Certain] (source: ratios/cash-flow pages).
