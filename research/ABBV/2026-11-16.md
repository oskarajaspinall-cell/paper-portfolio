# AbbVie (ABBV) — Core initiation — 2026-11-16
## 1. Business
Research-based biopharma: immunology (Skyrizi, Rinvoq), neuroscience, oncology and aesthetics (Botox, Allergan). Revenue comes from patented branded drugs sold mainly in the US, so pricing policy and patent life drive earnings [Likely]. The filing describes leadership positions in these four areas (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1551152/000155115226000037/abbv-20260930.htm). Country mix: [data unavailable]; no foreign country was verified at ~10%+.

## 2. Quality
Strong franchise economics [Likely]. TTM gross margin is 73.7% and operating margin 36.6%, rising over five years (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/abbv/financials/income-statement/). TTM ROCE is 23.1% and ROIC 19.7% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/abbv/financials/ratios/). Net debt/EBITDA is 2.21x and Piotroski F is 7 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/abbv/statistics/).

Direction change, addressed: ROIC is 7.6pp below its 2-year high of 27.4%, and FCF margin fell from 33.0% to 28.6% in 12 months (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/abbv/financials/ratios/?p=trailing). Operating margin and ROCE are rising, so the weaker cash conversion is not an operating-margin problem. The cause (acquired-pipeline spending, working capital, interest) is not verified [Guessing]. The moat is the Skyrizi/Rinvoq immunology franchise, whose label keeps widening: new Skyrizi and Rinvoq approvals in the 10-Q (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1551152/000155115226000037/abbv-20260930.htm) [Likely]. Durability rests on patents and on replacing Humira, so it is finite [Likely].

## 3. Valuation
Expensive on history. P/E is 50.8x, 40% of its 5y range, and 23% above the peer median of 41.2x. EV/EBITDA is 18.6x, above its 5y max of 16.0x and 34% above peers. P/FCF is 27.6x, above its 5y max of 22.7x. FCF yield is 3.62% (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/abbv/statistics/) [Certain].

Mechanical fair value: base 180.45 USD (DCF 154.03, P/E 233.30) against a 295.03 close, so -38.8%. Only the bull case (301.26) covers the price. The reverse DCF implies 15.5% year-1 revenue growth. The price assumes sustained double-digit growth from Skyrizi/Rinvoq plus pipeline wins [Likely]. The trend is strong: +22.5% above the 200-day average, +41.5% over 6 months, and +39.9pp versus SPY (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/abbv/history/).

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
FCF conversion is 182.5% of net income TTM, but net income is depressed by acquired-R&D charges [Guessing]. FCF margin has fallen from 39.1% in FY2021 to 28.6%. The company pledged large US R&D and manufacturing investment in a voluntary agreement with the US government, which exempts it from tariffs and future price mandates for three years (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1551152/000155115226000037/abbv-20260930.htm) [Certain]. The CSO says it is open to buying obesity assets (https://www.tipranks.com/news/the-fly/abbvie-science-chief-says-interested-in-building-out-obesity-assets-thefly-news) [Likely]. That implies more deal spending and goodwill risk [Guessing]. Share-count change: [data unavailable].

## 6. Unusual items
- Botox was selected for Medicare price negotiation. A federal district court vacated the selection, subject to government appeal (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1551152/000155115226000037/abbv-20260930.htm) [Certain]. An appeal win would hurt aesthetics economics [Likely].
- The 10-Q Risk Factors show no substantive change. The MD&A adds Epkinly and etentamig Phase 3 results, one mixed (Epkinly DLBCL-1 missed overall survival) [Certain].
- The company says it is too early to assess the 340B rebate pilot (https://www.tipranks.com/news/the-fly/abbvie-says-too-early-to-determine-financial-impact-from-340b-rebate-pilot-thefly-news) [Likely].
- Short interest is 0.90% of float and beta is 0.19 (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/abbv/statistics/).
