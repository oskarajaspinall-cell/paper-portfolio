# The Progressive Corporation (PGR) — Core initiation — 2026-10-07
## 1. Business
Progressive underwrites personal auto (largest U.S. direct-premium writer [Likely], ~1-in-4 U.S. households use a Progressive product [Likely]) plus home/property and commercial lines, sold direct and via independent agents (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/pgr/transcripts/650481-q2-2026/). It earns underwriting profit (premiums less claims/expenses) plus investment income on float.

## 2. Quality
ROE and ROIC rose sharply over five years (ROE 19.0%→40.4%, ROIC 15.4%→32.9% FY21-FY25), TTM still elevated at 34.9%/28.7% [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/pgr/financials/ratios/). FY2022 was a trough (ROE 4.2%), showing underwriting-cycle cyclicality [Certain]. Net debt/EBITDA is low and falling (0.54x TTM vs 1.07x FY21) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/pgr/financials/ratios/). The edge is scale in data/pricing and a low-cost direct model: Progressive captured ~75% of 2025 industry auto premium growth, now the largest U.S. personal-auto writer by direct premiums [Likely] (source: transcript above). Management runs surgical state rate cuts (16 states, 37% of net written premium YTD) over chasing volume [Likely] (source: transcript above). Property combined ratio improved from above 100% (through 2022) to 78% YTD 2026 [Likely] (source: transcript above). Risk: management flags soft market conditions, with more carriers taking on risk, that could compress the pricing edge [Likely] (source: transcript above). Piotroski F-score is 4 (mid-range, not a red flag) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/pgr/statistics/).

## 3. Valuation
PGR trades on 10.7x P/E and 8.5x EV/EBITDA, both below its own 5-year minimum (11.8x/9.5x), and 7.7x P/FCF, also below the 5y min (7.8x) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/pgr/financials/ratios/). Versus peers (ALL, TRV, CB, AIG), P/E is in line (10.7x vs 10.7x median) but EV/EBITDA is a 31% premium and P/B a 106% premium (3.6x vs 1.7x median) [Certain] (source: fact sheet valuation table). FCF yield is a high 12.94% [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/pgr/statistics/). The combination — cheap on earnings/cash flow versus its own history, but expensive on book value versus peers — reads as the market pricing in continued above-peer ROE rather than mean reversion; the stock is already 13.7% below its 52-week high and underperforming SPY by 30.1pp over 12 months, suggesting some of the "soft market" risk is reflected [Likely] (source: fact sheet price table). The P/B premium is the key valuation risk if ROE normalizes toward the FY2022 trough.

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- ROE (ratios page) falls below 20% for two consecutive quarters, indicating the underwriting-cycle downturn seen in FY2022 (4.2%) is recurring rather than transitory.
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) rises above 2x, signalling balance-sheet or reserve deterioration.
- Personal Lines policies-in-force growth (quarterly filings/shareholder letters) turns negative, evidencing loss of the direct-channel growth advantage described by management.
- Property combined ratio (shareholder letters) rises back above 90%, reversing the turnaround cited by management.

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
FCF conversion (FCF/NI) is strong but decelerating (908% FY22 outlier down to 136.4% TTM) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/pgr/financials/cash-flow-statement/). Shares outstanding fell only 0.14% YoY, so buybacks are modest relative to size [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/pgr/statistics/). Management says it increased repurchases in 2026 using an "intrinsic value model," with reinvestment in the high-ROE business as "top priority" and capital return secondary [Likely] (source: transcript above); it targets 3.5x premium-to-surplus leverage by year-end 2026 to free up capital flexibility [Likely] (source: transcript above).

## 6. Unusual items
Short interest is low (1.08% of float) but rose 4.7% versus the prior month [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/pgr/statistics/) — not material at this level. One headline (TheFly, 2026-09-18) cites a sell-side analyst flagging that August operating EPS of $1.49 missed a $1.52 estimate on core loss ratio/net items; this is a third-party estimate comparison, not a stockanalysis.com figure, and sell-side estimates are excluded as an input, but it is directionally consistent with the "soft market" competitive pressure management itself describes [Guessing] (source: https://www.tipranks.com/news/the-fly/keefe-says-progressive-august-operating-earnings-missed-estimate-thefly-news). Otherwise none material.
