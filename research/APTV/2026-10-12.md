# Aptiv PLC (APTV) — Core initiation — 2026-10-12

## 1. Business
Aptiv designs and supplies electrical/electronic architecture, sensors, compute and software for vehicle electrification, connectivity and ADAS, sold directly to global OEMs [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/aptv/). It completed the separation of its legacy Electrical Distribution Systems (EDS) wiring business into discontinued operations in 2026, leaving a leaner, more software/compute-weighted portfolio with growing non-automotive revenue (robotics, drones, energy storage) [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/aptv/transcripts/660507-q2-2026/).

## 2. Quality
Core profitability is improving even as revenue stalls: operating margin rose to 11.0% TTM (+3.1pp over 5y) and gross margin to 19.3% (+3.5pp), while ROCE climbed to 15.0% TTM (+3.3pp) [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/aptv/financials/ratios/). ROIC (7.8% TTM, -7.7pp) and ROE (5.2% TTM, -5.4pp) have fallen, and net margin dropped to 1.1% TTM — likely distorted by one-off costs/gains tied to the 2026 EDS separation rather than core deterioration [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/aptv/transcripts/660507-q2-2026/). Net debt/EBITDA is a moderate 1.52x TTM; Piotroski F-score 7/9 and Altman Z 3.22 show no distress [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/aptv/statistics/). Moat: an integrated sensor/compute/software architecture that OEMs attempting in-house development "have had a very difficult time" replicating, per management [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/aptv/transcripts/720828-j-p-morgan-automotive-conference/). Risk to durability: China OEM in-sourcing and intensifying EV-supply-chain competition, evidenced by the 2026 guidance cut.

## 3. Valuation
At the 43.80 close, APTV trades near 5-year lows on EBITDA/book multiples: EV/EBITDA 4.3x sits 18% below its own 5-year minimum (7.3x) and 17% below the peer (MGA/LEA/BWA/VC) median (5.2x); P/B 1.0x is 14% below its 5-year min and 26% below peer median [Certain] (source: fact sheet valuation table). P/E (19.9x) is roughly in line with peers (+3%), while P/FCF (13.3x) and EV/Sales (0.7x) trade above peer medians (+55%/+28%) — cheapness is concentrated in EBITDA/book multiples, plausibly affected by the EDS separation changing the EBITDA and debt base mid-history, a comparability caveat [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/aptv/transcripts/660507-q2-2026/). The model's own-history base fair value (inform-only) implies +73.6% upside, with a reverse DCF showing the price assumes only 5.5% year-1 revenue growth fading to terminal — a low bar against 2026 guidance of ~2% growth plus 12% non-automotive growth [Certain] (source: fact sheet fair value table).

## 4. What would prove this wrong
- Operating margin (income statement page) falls below 9% (FY2023 level), reversing the margin-recovery trend
- ROCE (ratios page) falls back below 10%, erasing the 5-year improvement
- Non-automotive revenue growth (earnings release) turns negative, showing the robotics/drones/energy-storage diversification has stalled
- Net debt/EBITDA (ratios page) rises above 3.0x, indicating buybacks are straining the balance sheet into continued China weakness

## 5. Capital allocation and cash conversion
Management is committing effectively 100% of 2026 free cash flow to buybacks (minimum 50% of FCF going forward), and shares outstanding fell 7.54% YoY [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/aptv/transcripts/720828-j-p-morgan-automotive-conference/; shares figure [Certain] source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/aptv/statistics/). FCF margin improved to 3.3% TTM (7.5% FY2025); the TTM FCF/net-income ratio (310.9%) is not meaningful given the near-zero TTM net income base [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/aptv/financials/cash-flow-statement/). M&A is limited to small bolt-ons [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/aptv/transcripts/720828-j-p-morgan-automotive-conference/). Net debt/EBITDA of 1.52x leaves room for continued repurchases.

## 6. Unusual items
Aptiv completed the EDS separation into discontinued operations in 2026, materially changing the historical revenue/margin/debt base and likely distorting several reported FY2025/TTM ratios (net margin, ROE, FCF conversion) [Likely] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/aptv/transcripts/660507-q2-2026/); treat multi-year trend lines with caution until a full post-separation history exists. Short interest is low and falling (3.6% of float, -20.9% m/m); institutional ownership of 105.7% likely reflects index double-counting. No governance red flags found [Certain] (source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/aptv/statistics/).
