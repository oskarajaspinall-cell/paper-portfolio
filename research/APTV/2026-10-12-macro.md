# Macro overlay: Aptiv PLC (APTV) — 2026-10-12

## Assessment
APTV is a China/Europe-exposed auto-electronics supplier that cut 2026 guidance on China weakness [Certain], funds near-100% FCF buybacks [Likely], and trades at trough multiples (EV/EBITDA 4.3x, 18% below its own 5y min) [Certain] (factsheet). Chain: real yields & credit spreads → APTV's DCF discount rate (its model already uses the 10y Treasury as risk-free) and → auto-loan rates/OEM production → revenue and multiple.

Facts [Certain, FRED, 2026-10-08/09]: 10y real yield (DFII10) 2.87%, +56bp/3m; 10y nominal (DGS10) 5.22%, +68bp/3m; HY spread (BAMLH0A0HYM2) 3.15%, +45bp/3m; 10y breakeven (T10YIE) 2.33%, roughly flat (+9bp/3m). Breakevens barely moved while real yields drove the rise, so this is a genuine tightening of real financial conditions, not an inflation scare **(inference)**. APTV's own base fair value ($76.02) already discounts at today's 5.22% risk-free rate, so the level is priced in; what matters is the next move. Market proxy: the 2y yield (4.75%, DGS2) above effective fed funds (3.88%, DFF) implies the market expects the policy rate to stay elevated or rise, not cut soon — a proxy, not certain **(inference)**.

**Bull**: yields/spreads roll over (CPI cools, Fed signals an end to hikes) → lower discount rate lifts fair value and cheaper financing aids OEM volumes, reinforcing the base case's +74% upside.
**Bear**: yields/spreads keep rising → discount rate up, fair value down, financing costs add to the China/Europe weakness already in guidance.
**Tilt**: toward bear, moderate — rates/spreads move with the company-specific China/Europe weakness already flagged, compounding it, though fundamentals remain the dominant driver.
